# New York City Criminal Court Act § 90 — Inspection of penal and reformatory institutions

[New York City Criminal Court Act](../README.md) › [Article 6 — General Miscellaneous Provisions](README.md)

```text
  § 90. Inspection of penal and reformatory institutions. All judges of
the court shall visit at least once, preferably within three months
after their appointment, the workhouse, penitentiary and all other
reformatories and institutions to which they have power to commit, and
shall make further visits to such institutions as may be required by
rule or order of court, and shall report in writing to the appellate
divisions of the supreme court exercising supervisory and administrative
power over the court their observations with such recommendations as
they may deem proper.
```
