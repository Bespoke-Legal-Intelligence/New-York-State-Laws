# New York City Criminal Court Act § 93 — Display of flag

[New York City Criminal Court Act](../README.md) › [Article 6 — General Miscellaneous Provisions](README.md)

```text
  § 93. Display of flag. It shall be the duty of the officer, employee
or person having the care of any courtroom used by the court to display
the American flag in each such room during all hours that the court is
in session.
```
