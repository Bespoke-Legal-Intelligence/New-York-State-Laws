# New York City Criminal Court Act § 51 — Trials on Sunday

[New York City Criminal Court Act](../README.md) › [Article 5 — Miscellaneous Provisions Affecting Cases In Which Judge Sits As Magistrate](README.md)

```text
  § 51. Trials on Sunday. Notwithstanding any provision of law, a part
or parts of the court may be convened on Sunday to hear, try and
determine offenses of a grade less than misdemeanor in the same manner
and to the same extent as upon any other day of the week.
```
