# New York City Criminal Court Act § 59 — Liability of judge sitting as magistrate

[New York City Criminal Court Act](../README.md) › [Article 5 — Miscellaneous Provisions Affecting Cases In Which Judge Sits As Magistrate](README.md)

```text
  § 59. Liability of judge sitting as magistrate. A judge of the court,
sitting as a magistrate who, in good faith and having reason to believe
that a crime or offense has been committed, issues process for the
arrest of a person, shall not be liable therefor unless it is shown that
his action in so doing was malicious or a deliberate abuse of his
discretion.
```
