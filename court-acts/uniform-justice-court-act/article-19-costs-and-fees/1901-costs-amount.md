# Uniform Justice Court Act § 1901 — Costs; amount

[Uniform Justice Court Act](../README.md) › [Article 19 — Costs and Fees](README.md)

```text
§ 1901. Costs; amount.
  Costs upon issue joined and after trial shall be calculated as
follows:
  (a) for money only: for one hundred dollars or under, five dollars;
for every additional one hundred dollars or fractional part thereof,
five dollars.
  (b) for the recovery of a chattel: the amounts provided by subdivision
(a), based upon the value of the chattel as determined by the judgment
or claimed by the adverse party, as the case may be.
```
