# Uniform Justice Court Act § 1913 — Witnesses' fees

[Uniform Justice Court Act](../README.md) › [Article 19 — Costs and Fees](README.md)

```text
§ 1913. Witnesses' fees.
  Witnesses in an action or a special proceeding or before a
commissioner or justice of this court taking a deposition, are entitled
to the same fees, including mileage, as a witness in an action in the
supreme court.
```
