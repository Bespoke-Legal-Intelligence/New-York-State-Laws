# Uniform Justice Court Act § 1900 — Security for costs

[Uniform Justice Court Act](../README.md) › [Article 19 — Costs and Fees](README.md)

```text
§ 1900. Security for costs.
  Article 85 of the CPLR, entitled "security for costs," shall apply in
this court, except that the minimum undertaking of CPLR 8503 shall be
$50 rather than the amount therein provided.
```
