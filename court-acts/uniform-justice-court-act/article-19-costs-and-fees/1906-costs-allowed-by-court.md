# Uniform Justice Court Act § 1906 — Costs allowed by court

[Uniform Justice Court Act](../README.md) › [Article 19 — Costs and Fees](README.md)

```text
§ 1906. Costs allowed by court.
  The court may in its discretion impose costs not exceeding five
dollars in the following cases:
  (a) Upon granting or denying a motion.
  (b) Upon allowing an amendment of a pleading.
  (c) Upon adjournment of a trial.
```
