# Uniform Justice Court Act § 1705 — Printing; record and briefs

[Uniform Justice Court Act](../README.md) › [Article 17 — Appeals](README.md)

```text
§ 1705. Printing; record and briefs.
  The printing of neither the record nor the briefs shall be required.
```
