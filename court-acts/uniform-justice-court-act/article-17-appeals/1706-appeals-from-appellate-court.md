# Uniform Justice Court Act § 1706 — Appeals from appellate court

[Uniform Justice Court Act](../README.md) › [Article 17 — Appeals](README.md)

```text
§ 1706. Appeals from appellate court.
  Appeals from the judgment or order of an appelate court, on appeal
from this court, shall be governed by the CPLR.
```
