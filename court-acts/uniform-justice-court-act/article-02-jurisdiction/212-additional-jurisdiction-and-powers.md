# Uniform Justice Court Act § 212 — Additional jurisdiction and powers

[Uniform Justice Court Act](../README.md) › [Article 2 — Jurisdiction](README.md)

```text
§ 212. Additional jurisdiction and powers.
  Except as otherwise provided by law, in the exercise of its
jurisdiction the court shall have all of the powers that the supreme
court would have in like actions and proceedings.
```
