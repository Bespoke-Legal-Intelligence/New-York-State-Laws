# Uniform Justice Court Act § 209 — Provisional remedies

[Uniform Justice Court Act](../README.md) › [Article 2 — Jurisdiction](README.md)

```text
§ 209. Provisional remedies.
  A requisition to seize a chattel may issue out of the court if such
requisition might issue out of the supreme court in a like case. No
other provisional remedy shall issue by or out of the court.
```
