# Uniform Justice Court Act § 103 — Titles of justices; incidental powers

[Uniform Justice Court Act](../README.md) › [Article 1 — Organization](README.md)

```text
§ 103. Titles of justices; incidental powers.
  The title of each justice of:
  a. a town court shall be "Town Justice";
  b. a village court shall be "Village Justice"; and
  c. a court established in and for a city and governed by this act
shall be "City Justice".
  In addition to such judicial powers as are conferred by law in this
act or elsewhere, each justice shall have all of the powers conferred or
conferrable on non-judicial personnel of the court.
```
