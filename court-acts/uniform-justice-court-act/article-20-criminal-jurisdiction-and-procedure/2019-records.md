# Uniform Justice Court Act § 2019 — Records

[Uniform Justice Court Act](../README.md) › [Article 20 — Criminal Jurisdiction and Procedure](README.md)

```text
§ 2019. Records.
  All justices of courts governed by this act shall keep or cause to be
kept legible and suitable records and dockets of all criminal actions
and proceedings. The rules may govern the manner, form, care, custody
and disposition of such records.
```
