# Uniform Justice Court Act § 1304 — Time for rendering judgment or decision

[Uniform Justice Court Act](../README.md) › [Article 13 — Trial](README.md)

```text
§ 1304. Time for rendering judgment or decision.
  If a jury trial is not demanded or directed as provided in § 1303, the
court must render judgment within thirty days from the time when the
case is submitted for that purpose, except when further time is given by
the consent of the parties.
```
