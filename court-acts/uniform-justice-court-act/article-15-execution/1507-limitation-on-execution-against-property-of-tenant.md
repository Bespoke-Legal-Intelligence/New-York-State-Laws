# Uniform Justice Court Act § 1507 — Limitation on execution against property of tenant

[Uniform Justice Court Act](../README.md) › [Article 15 — Execution](README.md)

```text
§ 1507. Limitation on execution against property of tenant.
  No levy shall be made on the personal property of a tenant
dispossessed for non-payment of rent under any execution within
twenty-four hours of the time of dispossess, if the real property of
which the tenant is being dispossessed was his residence.
```
