# Uniform Justice Court Act § 1504 — Execution issued out of this court; requisites

[Uniform Justice Court Act](../README.md) › [Article 15 — Execution](README.md)

```text
§ 1504. Execution issued out of this court; requisites.
  An execution issued out of this court may be levied only against
personal property of the judgment-debtor. It must be subscribed by
either the clerk or the attorney for the judgment-creditor and must
bear, in addition to such other matter as is required by the CPLR, the
date of its delivery. It may be levied in any part of the county, and
for such purpose it is not necessary that the judgment have been
docketed with the county clerk.
```
