# Uniform District Court Act § 1904 — Certain costs added

[Uniform District Court Act](../README.md) › [Article 19 — Costs and Fees](README.md)

```text
§ 1904.  Certain costs added.
  Costs on appeal, and those awarded under § 1102 of this act, may be
added to the amount of costs otherwise applicable in the action.
```
