# Uniform District Court Act § 102 — Application of UDCA; court of record; seal

[Uniform District Court Act](../README.md) › [Article 1 — Organization](README.md)

```text
§ 102. Application of UDCA; court of record; seal.
  The jurisdiction of and practice and procedure in each district court
governed by the UDCA shall be as prescribed herein, and such court shall
be a court of record and a part of the unified court system for the
state.  Such court in each county shall have an official seal to be
furnished by the county upon which shall be engraved the words "District
Court of (insert name of county), New York, Seal".
```
