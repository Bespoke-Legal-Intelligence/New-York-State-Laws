# Uniform District Court Act § 412 — Accrual of interest

[Uniform District Court Act](../README.md) › [Article 4 — Summons](README.md)

```text
§ 412. Accrual of interest.
  In any action, petition, order to show cause or other proceeding
wherein interest accrues from the date of the inception of the action,
petition, order or proceeding, such entitlement to interest shall not
accrue until service is completed by the actual index number being
properly depicted on the summons and provided to the party to be charged
with payment of interest.
```
