# Uniform District Court Act § 1705 — Printing; record and briefs

[Uniform District Court Act](../README.md) › [Article 17 — Appeals](README.md)

```text
§ 1705. Printing; record and briefs.
  The printing of neither the record nor the briefs shall be required
except as the rules of the court to which the appeal is taken shall
provide in designated classes of appeals.
```
