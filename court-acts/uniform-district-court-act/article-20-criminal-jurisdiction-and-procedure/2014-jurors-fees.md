# Uniform District Court Act § 2014 — Jurors' fees

[Uniform District Court Act](../README.md) › [Article 20 — Criminal Jurisdiction and Procedure](README.md)

```text
§ 2014. Jurors' fees.
  A juror attending as such shall be entitled to the same fee for each
day such juror served or attended and the same mileage as jurors in the
supreme court of the county. No fee shall be paid to a juror who has
been excused from service as a juror, for the day or days so excused.
```
