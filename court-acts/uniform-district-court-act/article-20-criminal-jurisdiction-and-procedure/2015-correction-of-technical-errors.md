# Uniform District Court Act § 2015 — Correction of technical errors

[Uniform District Court Act](../README.md) › [Article 20 — Criminal Jurisdiction and Procedure](README.md)

```text
§ 2015. Correction of technical errors.
  Technical errors in the commitment of any accused person may be
corrected by the judge at any time after the commitment is signed. The
judge may alter or modify a sentence or correct any technical error in
the record not affecting the substantial rights of the defendant at any
time before the execution of the sentence has been commenced.
```
