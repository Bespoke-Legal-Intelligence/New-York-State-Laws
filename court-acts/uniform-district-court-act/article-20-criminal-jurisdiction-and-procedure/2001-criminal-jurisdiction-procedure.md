# Uniform District Court Act § 2001 — Criminal jurisdiction; procedure

[Uniform District Court Act](../README.md) › [Article 20 — Criminal Jurisdiction and Procedure](README.md)

```text
§ 2001. Criminal jurisdiction; procedure.
  (1) The court shall have such jurisdiction of criminal matters as is
prescribed by the criminal procedure law.
  (2) Unless otherwise specifically prescribed, the practice and
procedure in the court shall be governed by the criminal procedure law.
```
