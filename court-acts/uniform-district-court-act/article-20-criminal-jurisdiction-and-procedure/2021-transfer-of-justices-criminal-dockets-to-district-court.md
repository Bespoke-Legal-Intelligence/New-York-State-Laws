# Uniform District Court Act § 2021 — Transfer of justices' criminal dockets to district court

[Uniform District Court Act](../README.md) › [Article 20 — Criminal Jurisdiction and Procedure](README.md)

```text
§ 2021. Transfer of justices' criminal dockets to district court.
  In any county or part of a county when the district court system has
been duly adopted, all criminal dockets of the town justices then on
file or required to be filed in the office of the town clerk shall be
transferred to the office of the clerk of the district court and there
kept and maintained in the same manner as other official records of the
district court.
```
