# Uniform District Court Act § 802 — Tender and offer

[Uniform District Court Act](../README.md) › [Article 8 — Provisional Remedies](README.md)

```text
§ 802. Tender and offer.
  The provisions of rules 3219, 3220 and 3221 of the CPLR, treating
respectively of tender, offer to liquidate damages conditionally and
offer to compromise, shall be applicable in this court, with the
additional requirement that at the time of service upon the other party
of the "written tender", "written offer" or "written notice", as
referred to in said rules, a copy of such tender, offer or notice shall
be filed with the clerk.
```
