# Uniform District Court Act § 1002 — Motion to dismiss

[Uniform District Court Act](../README.md) › [Article 10 — Motions](README.md)

```text
  § 1002. Motion to dismiss. CPLR rule 3211, relating to a motion to
dismiss, shall apply in this court, except that, with reference to
subdivision (e) of said rule, a party's time to move to dismiss a cause
of action contained in a pleading to which no response is required shall
be within ten days after the service of such pleading. A motion based on
paragraphs two, seven or ten of subdivision (a) of said rule may, as
provided in its subdivision (e), be made at any time.
```
