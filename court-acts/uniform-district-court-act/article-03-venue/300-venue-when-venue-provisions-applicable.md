# Uniform District Court Act § 300 — Venue; when venue provisions applicable

[Uniform District Court Act](../README.md) › [Article 3 — Venue](README.md)

```text
§ 300. Venue; when venue provisions applicable.
  The provisions of this article shall apply only in a county having two
or more districts comprising its district court, and it shall not be
necessary that such districts within such county be contiguous.
```
