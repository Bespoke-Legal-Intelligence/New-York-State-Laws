# Uniform District Court Act § 307 — Venue; rules

[Uniform District Court Act](../README.md) › [Article 3 — Venue](README.md)

```text
§ 307. Venue; rules.
  Notwithstanding the provisions of this article, the rules may
establish a part or parts of the court where designated classes of cases
shall be brought or tried.
```
