# Uniform District Court Act § 303 — Summary proceedings; venue

[Uniform District Court Act](../README.md) › [Article 3 — Venue](README.md)

```text
§ 303. Summary proceedings; venue.
  A summary proceeding to recover possession of real property or to
remove tenants therefrom shall be brought in the district in which such
property or a part thereof is situated.
```
