# Uniform District Court Act § 302 — Mechanic's lien; venue

[Uniform District Court Act](../README.md) › [Article 3 — Venue](README.md)

```text
§ 302. Mechanic's lien; venue.
  An action to establish a mechanic's lien on real property shall be
brought in the district in which such property or a part thereof is
situated.
```
