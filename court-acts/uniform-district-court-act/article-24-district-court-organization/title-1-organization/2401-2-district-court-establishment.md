# Uniform District Court Act § 2401*2 — District court establishment

[Uniform District Court Act](../../README.md) › [Article 24 — District Court: Organization](../README.md) › [Title 1 — Organization](README.md)

*An asterisk in a number (for example Article 24\* or § 20-C\*2) is the Senate's mark for a number the Legislature has used more than once: the marked article or section is a different one from the unmarked one with the same number. \*2 means the second one enacted with that number.*

```text
  * § 2401. District court establishment. There shall be a district
court system established in the area or areas of the county of Suffolk
as herein provided. The Suffolk county district court shall be an
inferior court and the judges thereof shall have the civil and criminal
jurisdiction prescribed hereinafter. Such court shall not be a court of
record.
  * NB There are 2 § 2401's
```
