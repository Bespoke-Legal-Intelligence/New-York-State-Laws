# Uniform District Court Act § 2501 — Application of uniform district court act

[Uniform District Court Act](../../README.md) › [Article 25 — From Chapter 570 of 1963, As Amended, Is Supplemental - Not Part of Udca Article Xxv Civil and Criminal Jurisdiction and Procedure](../README.md) › [Title 1 — Jurisdiction, Practice and Procedure](README.md)

```text
  § 2501. Application of uniform district court act. The civil and
criminal jurisdiction of and practice and procedure in the district
court of Suffolk county shall be as set forth in the uniform district
court act.
```
