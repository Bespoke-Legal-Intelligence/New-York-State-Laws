# Uniform District Court Act § 201 — Jurisdiction; in general

[Uniform District Court Act](../README.md) › [Article 2 — Jurisdiction](README.md)

```text
§ 201. Jurisdiction; in general.
  The court shall have jurisdiction as set forth in this article and as
elsewhere provided by law. The phrase "$15,000", whenever it appears
herein, shall be taken to mean "$15,000 exclusive of interest and
costs".
```
