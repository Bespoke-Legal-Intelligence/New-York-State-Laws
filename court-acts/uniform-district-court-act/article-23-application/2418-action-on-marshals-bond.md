# Uniform District Court Act § 2418 — Action on marshal's bond

[Uniform District Court Act](../README.md) › [Article 23 — Application](README.md)

```text
  § 2418. Action on marshal's bond. An action upon the bond of a marshal
may be brought and prosecuted to judgment in this court, upon leave
obtained from a judge of this court, according to the provisions
relating to an action in the supreme court by a private person upon an
official bond.
```
