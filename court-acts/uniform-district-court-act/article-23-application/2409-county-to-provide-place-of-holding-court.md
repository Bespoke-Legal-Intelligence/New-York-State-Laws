# Uniform District Court Act § 2409 — County to provide place of holding court

[Uniform District Court Act](../README.md) › [Article 23 — Application](README.md)

```text
  § 2409. County to provide place of holding court. It shall be the duty
of the county to provide suitable places for holding court.
```
