# Family Court Act § 921 — Originating proceeding

[Family Court Act](../../README.md) › [Article 9 — Conciliation Proceedings](../README.md) › [Part 2 — Procedure](README.md)

```text
  § 921. Originating proceeding. A spouse may originate a conciliation
proceeding under this article by filing a petition stating that his or
her marriage is in difficulty and that the conciliation services of the
family court are needed.
```
