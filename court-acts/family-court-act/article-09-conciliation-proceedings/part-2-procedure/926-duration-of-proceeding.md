# Family Court Act § 926 — Duration of proceeding

[Family Court Act](../../README.md) › [Article 9 — Conciliation Proceedings](../README.md) › [Part 2 — Procedure](README.md)

```text
  § 926. Duration of proceeding. Unless both spouses consent to the
continuation of a conciliation proceeding under this article, it
terminates ninety days after the filing of the petition.
```
