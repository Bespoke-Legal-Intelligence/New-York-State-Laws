# Family Court Act § 914 — No effect on marital status

[Family Court Act](../../README.md) › [Article 9 — Conciliation Proceedings](../README.md) › [Part 1 — Purpose and Jurisdiction](README.md)

```text
  § 914. No effect on marital status. The family court in a proceeding
under this article may not issue any order affecting the marital status
of the petitioner or of the petitioner's spouse or relieving either of
any marital obligation.
```
