# Family Court Act § 911 — Purpose

[Family Court Act](../../README.md) › [Article 9 — Conciliation Proceedings](../README.md) › [Part 1 — Purpose and Jurisdiction](README.md)

```text
  § 911. Purpose. This article is designed to implement section
thirteen-c, subdivision six, of article six of the constitution by
making available an informal conciliation procedure to those whose
marriage is in trouble.
```
