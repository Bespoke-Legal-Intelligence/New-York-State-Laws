# Family Court Act § 113 — Establishment of court

[Family Court Act](../../README.md) › [Article 1 — Family Court Established](../README.md) › [Part 1 — Applicability of Act and Creation of Court](README.md)

```text
  § 113. Establishment of court. The family court of the state of New
York is established in each county of the state as part of the unified
court system for the state.
```
