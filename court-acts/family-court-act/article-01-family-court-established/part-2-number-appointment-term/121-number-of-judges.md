# Family Court Act § 121 — Number of judges

[Family Court Act](../../README.md) › [Article 1 — Family Court Established](../README.md) › [Part 2 — Number, Appointment, Term and Compensation of Judges Within the City of New York](README.md)

```text
  § 121. Number of judges. The family court within the city of New York
shall consist of sixty-seven judges, effective January first, two
thousand twenty-five. There shall be at least one family court judge
resident in each county of the city of New York.
```
