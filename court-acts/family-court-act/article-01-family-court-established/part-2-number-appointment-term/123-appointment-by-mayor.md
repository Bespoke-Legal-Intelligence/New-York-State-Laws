# Family Court Act § 123 — Appointment by mayor

[Family Court Act](../../README.md) › [Article 1 — Family Court Established](../README.md) › [Part 2 — Number, Appointment, Term and Compensation of Judges Within the City of New York](README.md)

```text
  § 123. Appointment by mayor. The mayor of the city of New York shall
appoint the judges of the family court in counties within the city of
New York for a term of ten years.
```
