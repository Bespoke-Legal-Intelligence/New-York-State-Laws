# Family Court Act § 126 — Vacancies

[Family Court Act](../../README.md) › [Article 1 — Family Court Established](../README.md) › [Part 2 — Number, Appointment, Term and Compensation of Judges Within the City of New York](README.md)

```text
  § 126. Vacancies. When a vacancy occurs, otherwise than by expiration
of term on the last day of any year, in the office of judge of the
family court in a county within the city of New York, the vacancy shall
be filled by appointment of the mayor of the city of New York for the
unexpired term.
```
