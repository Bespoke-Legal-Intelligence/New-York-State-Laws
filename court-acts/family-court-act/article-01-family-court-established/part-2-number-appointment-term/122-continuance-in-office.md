# Family Court Act § 122 — Continuance in office

[Family Court Act](../../README.md) › [Article 1 — Family Court Established](../README.md) › [Part 2 — Number, Appointment, Term and Compensation of Judges Within the City of New York](README.md)

```text
  § 122. Continuance in office. The justices of the domestic relations
court of the city of New York in office on the effective date of this
act, shall for the remainder of the term of their appointment continue
in office as judges of the family court in the county within the city of
New York in which they reside.
```
