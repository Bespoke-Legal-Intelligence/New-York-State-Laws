# Family Court Act § 144 — Activity in community organizations

[Family Court Act](../../README.md) › [Article 1 — Family Court Established](../README.md) › [Part 4 — Family Court Judges](README.md)

```text
  § 144. Activity in community organizations. Any judge discharging the
duties of family court judge is authorized to serve as an officer or
member of the governing body of any corporation or association organized
and maintained exclusively for religious, charitable, benevolent, or
educational purposes.
```
