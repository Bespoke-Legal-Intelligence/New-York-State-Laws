# Family Court Act § 145 — Liability of judge

[Family Court Act](../../README.md) › [Article 1 — Family Court Established](../README.md) › [Part 4 — Family Court Judges](README.md)

```text
  § 145. Liability of judge. Any family court judge who in good faith
issues process in any proceeding under this act shall not be liable
therefor unless it is shown that his action in so doing was malicious or
a deliberate abuse of his discretion.
```
