# Family Court Act § 153-C — Temporary order of protection

[Family Court Act](../../README.md) › [Article 1 — Family Court Established](../README.md) › [Part 5 — General Powers](README.md)

```text
  § 153-c. Temporary order of protection. Any person appearing at family
court when the court is open requesting a temporary order of protection
under any article of this act shall be entitled to file a petition
without delay on the same day such person first appears at the family
court, and a hearing on that request shall be held on the same day or
the next day that the family court is open following the filing of such
petition.
```
