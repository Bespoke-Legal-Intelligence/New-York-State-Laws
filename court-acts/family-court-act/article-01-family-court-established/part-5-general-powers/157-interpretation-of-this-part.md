# Family Court Act § 157 — Interpretation of this part

[Family Court Act](../../README.md) › [Article 1 — Family Court Established](../README.md) › [Part 5 — General Powers](README.md)

```text
  § 157. Interpretation of this part. If there is any conflict between
the application of any provision of this part to any proceeding under
this act and any provision of the article of this act governing the
proceeding, the article governing the proceeding controls.
```
