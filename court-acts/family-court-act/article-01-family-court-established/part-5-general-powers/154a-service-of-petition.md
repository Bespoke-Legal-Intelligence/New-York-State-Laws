# Family Court Act § 154-A — Service of petition

[Family Court Act](../../README.md) › [Article 1 — Family Court Established](../README.md) › [Part 5 — General Powers](README.md)

```text
  § 154-a. Service of petition. In every proceeding in family court, a
copy of the petition filed therein shall be served upon the respondent
at the time of service of process or, if that is not practicable, at the
first court appearance by respondent.
```
