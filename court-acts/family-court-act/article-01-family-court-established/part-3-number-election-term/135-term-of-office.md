# Family Court Act § 135 — Term of office

[Family Court Act](../../README.md) › [Article 1 — Family Court Established](../README.md) › [Part 3 — Number, Election, Term and Compensation of Judges Not Within the City of New York](README.md)

```text
  § 135. Term of office. The term of office of a judge elected to the
family court in a county outside the city of New York is ten years.
```
