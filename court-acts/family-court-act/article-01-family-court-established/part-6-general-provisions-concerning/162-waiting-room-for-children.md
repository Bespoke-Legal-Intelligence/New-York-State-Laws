# Family Court Act § 162 — Waiting room for children

[Family Court Act](../../README.md) › [Article 1 — Family Court Established](../README.md) › [Part 6 — General Provisions Concerning Hearings](README.md)

```text
  § 162. Waiting room for children. So far as possible a waiting room
with a competent person in charge shall be provided for the care of
children brought to the family court under this act.
```
