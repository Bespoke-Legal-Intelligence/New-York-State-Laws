# Family Court Act § 580-314 — Limited immunity of petitioner

[Family Court Act](../../README.md) › [Article 5-B — Uniform Interstate Family Support Act](../README.md) › [Part 3 — Civil Provisions of General Application](README.md)

```text
  * § 580-314. Limited immunity of petitioner. (a) Participation by a
petitioner in a proceeding before a responding tribunal, whether in
person, by private attorney, or through services provided by the support
enforcement agency, does not confer personal jurisdiction over the
petitioner in another proceeding.
  (b) A petitioner is not amenable to service of civil process while
physically present in this state to participate in a proceeding under
this article.
  (c) The immunity granted by this section does not extend to civil
litigation based on acts unrelated to a proceeding under this article
committed by a party while present in this state to participate in the
proceeding.
  * NB Effective until January 1, 2016
  * § 580-314. Limited immunity of petitioner. (a) Participation by a
petitioner in a proceeding under this article before a responding
tribunal, whether in person, by private attorney or through services
provided by the support enforcement agency, does not confer personal
jurisdiction over the petitioner in another proceeding.
  (b) A petitioner is not amenable to service of civil process while
physically present in this state to participate in a proceeding under
this article.
  (c) The immunity granted by this section does not extend to civil
litigation based on acts unrelated to a proceeding under this article
committed by a party while physically present in this state to
participate in the proceeding.
  * NB Effective January 1, 2016
```
