# Family Court Act § 580-104 — Remedies cumulative

[Family Court Act](../../README.md) › [Article 5-B — Uniform Interstate Family Support Act](../README.md) › [Part 1 — General Provisions](README.md)

```text
  * § 580-104. Remedies cumulative. (a) Remedies provided by this
article are cumulative and do not affect the availability of remedies
under other law or the recognition of a foreign support order on the
basis of comity.
  (b) This article does not:
  (1) provide the exclusive method of establishing or enforcing a
support order under the law of this state; or
  (2) grant a tribunal of this state jurisdiction to render judgment or
issue an order relating to child custody or visitation in a proceeding
under this article.
  * NB Effective January 1, 2016
```
