# Family Court Act § 580-103 — Remedies cumulative

[Family Court Act](../../README.md) › [Article 5-B — Uniform Interstate Family Support Act](../README.md) › [Part 1 — General Provisions](README.md)

```text
  * § 580-103. Remedies cumulative. Remedies provided by this article
are cumulative and do not affect the availability of remedies under
other law.
  * NB Effective until January 1, 2016
  * § 580-103. State tribunal and state support enforcement agencies.
(a) The family court is the tribunal of this state.
  (b) The office of temporary and disability assistance and/or a social
services district as defined in subdivision seven of section two of the
social services law are the support enforcement agencies of this state.
  * NB Effective January 1, 2016
```
