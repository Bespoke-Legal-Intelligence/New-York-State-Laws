# Family Court Act § 580-103 — Remedies cumulative

[Family Court Act](../../README.md) › [Article 5-B — Uniform Interstate Family Support Act](../README.md) › [Part 1 — General Provisions](README.md)

```text
  § 580-103. Remedies cumulative.  Remedies provided by this article are
cumulative and do not affect the availability of remedies under other
law.
```
