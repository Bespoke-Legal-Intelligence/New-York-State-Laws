# Family Court Act § 580-402 — Proceeding to determine parentage

[Family Court Act](../../README.md) › [Article 5-B — Uniform Interstate Family Support Act](../README.md) › [Part 4 — Establishment of Support Order](README.md)

```text
  * § 580-402. Proceeding to determine parentage. A tribunal of this
state authorized to determine parentage of a child may serve as a
responding tribunal in a proceeding to determine parentage of a child
brought under this article or a law or procedure substantially similar
to this article.
  * NB Effective January 1, 2016
```
