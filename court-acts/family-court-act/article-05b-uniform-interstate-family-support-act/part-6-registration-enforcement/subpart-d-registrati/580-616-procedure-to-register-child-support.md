# Family Court Act § 580-616 — Procedure to register child support order of foreign country for modification

[Family Court Act](../../../README.md) › [Article 5-B — Uniform Interstate Family Support Act](../../README.md) › [Part 6 — Registration, Enforcement and Modification of Support Order](../README.md) › [Subpart D — Registration and Modification of Foreign Child Support Order](README.md)

```text
  § 580-616. Procedure to register child support order of foreign
country for modification. A party or support enforcement agency seeking
to modify, or to modify and enforce, a foreign child support order not
under the Convention may register that order in this state under
sections 580-601 through 580-608 of this part if the order has not been
registered. A petition for modification may be filed at the same time as
a request for registration, or at another time. The petition must
specify the grounds for modification.
```
