# Family Court Act § 580-601 — Registration of order for enforcement

[Family Court Act](../../../README.md) › [Article 5-B — Uniform Interstate Family Support Act](../../README.md) › [Part 6 — Registration, Enforcement and Modification of Support Order](../README.md) › [Subpart A — Registration and Enforcement of Support Order](README.md)

```text
  § 580-601. Registration of order for enforcement. A support order or
income withholding order issued in another state or a foreign support
order may be registered in this state for enforcement.
```
