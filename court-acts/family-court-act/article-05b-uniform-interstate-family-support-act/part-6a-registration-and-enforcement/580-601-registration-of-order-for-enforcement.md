# Family Court Act § 580-601 — Registration of order for enforcement

[Family Court Act](../../README.md) › [Article 5-B — Uniform Interstate Family Support Act](../README.md) › [Part 6A — Registration and Enforcement of Support Order](README.md)

```text
  § 580-601. Registration of order for enforcement.  A support order or
an income-withholding order issued by a tribunal of another state may be
registered in this state for enforcement.
```
