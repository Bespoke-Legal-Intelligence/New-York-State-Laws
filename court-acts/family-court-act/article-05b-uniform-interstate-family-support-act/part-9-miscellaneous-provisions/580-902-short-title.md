# Family Court Act § 580-902 — Short title

[Family Court Act](../../README.md) › [Article 5-B — Uniform Interstate Family Support Act](../README.md) › [Part 9 — Miscellaneous Provisions](README.md)

```text
  * § 580-902. Short title. This article may be cited as the uniform
interstate family support act.
  * NB Effective until January 1, 2016
  * § 580-902. Severability. If any provision of this article or its
application to any person or circumstance is held invalid, the
invalidity does not affect other provisions or applications of this
article which can be given effect without the invalid provision or
application, and to this end the provisions of this article are
severable.
  * NB Effective January 1, 2016
```
