# Family Court Act § 580-901 — Uniformity of application and construction

[Family Court Act](../../README.md) › [Article 5-B — Uniform Interstate Family Support Act](../README.md) › [Part 9 — Miscellaneous Provisions](README.md)

```text
  § 580-901. Uniformity of application and construction. In applying and
construing this uniform act, consideration must be given to the need to
promote uniformity of the law with respect to its subject matter among
states that enact it.
```
