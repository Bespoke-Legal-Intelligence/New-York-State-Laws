# Family Court Act § 580-712 — Personal information; limit on use

[Family Court Act](../../README.md) › [Article 5-B — Uniform Interstate Family Support Act](../README.md) › [Part 7 — Support Proceedings Under Convention](README.md)

```text
  § 580-712. Personal information; limit on use. Personal information
gathered or transmitted under this part may be used only for the
purposes for which it was gathered or transmitted.
```
