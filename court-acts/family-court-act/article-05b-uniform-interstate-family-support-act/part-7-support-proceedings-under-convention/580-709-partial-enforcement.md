# Family Court Act § 580-709 — Partial enforcement

[Family Court Act](../../README.md) › [Article 5-B — Uniform Interstate Family Support Act](../README.md) › [Part 7 — Support Proceedings Under Convention](README.md)

```text
  § 580-709. Partial enforcement. If a tribunal of this state does not
recognize and enforce a Convention support order in its entirety, it
shall enforce any severable part of the order. An application or direct
request may seek recognition and partial enforcement of a Convention
support order.
```
