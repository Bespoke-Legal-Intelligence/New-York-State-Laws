# Family Court Act § 580-703 — Relationship of office of temporary and disability assistance to United States central authority

[Family Court Act](../../README.md) › [Article 5-B — Uniform Interstate Family Support Act](../README.md) › [Part 7 — Determination of Parentage](README.md)

```text
  * § 580-703. Relationship of office of temporary and disability
assistance to United States central authority. The office of temporary
and disability assistance is recognized as the agency designated by the
United States central authority to perform specific functions under the
Convention; provided, however, that a social services district of this
state may perform any function authorized under state law.
  * NB Effective January 1, 2016
```
