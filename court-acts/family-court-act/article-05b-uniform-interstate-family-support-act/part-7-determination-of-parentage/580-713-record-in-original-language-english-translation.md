# Family Court Act § 580-713 — Record in original language; English translation

[Family Court Act](../../README.md) › [Article 5-B — Uniform Interstate Family Support Act](../README.md) › [Part 7 — Determination of Parentage](README.md)

```text
  * § 580-713. Record in original language; English translation. A
record filed with a tribunal of this state under this part must be in
the original language and, if not in English, must be accompanied by an
English translation.
  * NB Effective January 1, 2016
```
