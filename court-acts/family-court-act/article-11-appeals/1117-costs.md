# Family Court Act § 1117 — Costs

[Family Court Act](../README.md) › [Article 11 — Appeals](README.md)

```text
  § 1117. Costs. When costs and disbursements on an appeal in a
proceeding instituted by a social services official are awarded to the
respondent, they shall be a county charge and be paid by the county.
```
