# Family Court Act § 1116 — Printed case and brief not required

[Family Court Act](../README.md) › [Article 11 — Appeals](README.md)

```text
  § 1116. Printed case and brief not required. In appeals under this
article, a printed case on appeal or a printed brief shall not be
required.
```
