# Family Court Act § 1111 — Appeals to appellate division

[Family Court Act](../README.md) › [Article 11 — Appeals](README.md)

```text
  § 1111. Appeals to appellate division. An appeal may be taken to the
appellate division of the supreme court of the judicial department in
which the family court whose order is appealed from is located.
```
