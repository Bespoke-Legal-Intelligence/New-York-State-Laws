# Family Court Act § 533 — Adjournment on application of party

[Family Court Act](../../README.md) › [Article 5 — Paternity Proceedings](../README.md) › [Part 3 — Hearings](README.md)

```text
  § 533. Adjournment on application of party. The court, on application
of either party, may for good cause shown grant such adjournments as may
be necessary. If an adjournment is granted upon the request of either
party, the court may require the respondent to give an undertaking for
appearance.
```
