# Family Court Act § 515 — Governmental obligation to child

[Family Court Act](../../README.md) › [Article 5 — Paternity Proceedings](../README.md) › [Part 1 — Jurisdiction and Duties to Support](README.md)

```text
  § 515. Governmental obligation to child. In case of the neglect or
inability of the parents to provide for the support and education of the
child, it shall be supported by the county, city or town chargeable
therewith under the provisions of the social welfare law.
```
