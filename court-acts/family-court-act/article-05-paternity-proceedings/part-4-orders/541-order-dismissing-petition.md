# Family Court Act § 541 — Order dismissing petition

[Family Court Act](../../README.md) › [Article 5 — Paternity Proceedings](../README.md) › [Part 4 — Orders](README.md)

```text
  § 541. Order dismissing petition. If the court finds the male party is
not the father of the child, it shall dismiss the petition. If a neglect
petition was filed in the paternity proceeding, the court retains
jurisdiction over the neglect petition whether or not it dismisses the
paternity petition.
```
