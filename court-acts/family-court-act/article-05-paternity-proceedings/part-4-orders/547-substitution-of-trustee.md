# Family Court Act § 547 — Substitution of trustee

[Family Court Act](../../README.md) › [Article 5 — Paternity Proceedings](../README.md) › [Part 4 — Orders](README.md)

```text
  § 547. Substitution of trustee. The court, on motion of a party or
otherwise, may at any time for good cause shown substitute another
trustee for the one designated and acting.
```
