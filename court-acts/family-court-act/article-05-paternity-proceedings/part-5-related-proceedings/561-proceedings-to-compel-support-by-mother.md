# Family Court Act § 561 — Proceedings to compel support by mother

[Family Court Act](../../README.md) › [Article 5 — Paternity Proceedings](../README.md) › [Part 5 — Related Proceedings](README.md)

```text
  § 561. Proceedings to compel support by mother.  Proceedings may be
initiated under article four of this act to compel a mother who fails to
support her child to do so in accord with the provisions of article four
of this act.
```
