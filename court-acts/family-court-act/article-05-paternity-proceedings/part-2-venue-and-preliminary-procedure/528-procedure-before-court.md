# Family Court Act § 528 — Procedure before court

[Family Court Act](../../README.md) › [Article 5 — Paternity Proceedings](../README.md) › [Part 2 — Venue and Preliminary Procedure](README.md)

```text
  § 528. Procedure before court. The court before whom the respondent is
taken under section five hundred twenty-seven may require an undertaking
to appear or in default thereof may place the respondent in custody.
```
