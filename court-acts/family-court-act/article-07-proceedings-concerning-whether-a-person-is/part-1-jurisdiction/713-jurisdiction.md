# Family Court Act § 713 — Jurisdiction

[Family Court Act](../../README.md) › [Article 7 — Proceedings Concerning Whether a Person Is In Need of Supervision](../README.md) › [Part 1 — Jurisdiction](README.md)

```text
  § 713. Jurisdiction. The family court has exclusive original
jurisdiction over any proceeding involving a person alleged to be a
person in need of supervision.
```
