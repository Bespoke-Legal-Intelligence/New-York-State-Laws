# Family Court Act § 716 — Substitution of petition

[Family Court Act](../../README.md) › [Article 7 — Proceedings Concerning Whether a Person Is In Need of Supervision](../README.md) › [Part 1 — Jurisdiction](README.md)

```text
  § 716. Substitution of petition. On its own motion and at any time in
the proceedings, the court may substitute a neglect petition under
article ten for a petition to determine whether a person is in need of
supervision.
```
