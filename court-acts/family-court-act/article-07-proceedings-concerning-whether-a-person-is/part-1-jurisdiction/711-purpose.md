# Family Court Act § 711 — Purpose

[Family Court Act](../../README.md) › [Article 7 — Proceedings Concerning Whether a Person Is In Need of Supervision](../README.md) › [Part 1 — Jurisdiction](README.md)

```text
  § 711. Purpose. The purpose of this article is to provide a due
process of law (a) for considering a claim that a person is in need of
supervision and (b) for devising an appropriate order of disposition for
any person adjudged in need of supervision.
```
