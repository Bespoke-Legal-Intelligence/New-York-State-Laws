# Family Court Act § 772 — Action on return from agency or institution

[Family Court Act](../../README.md) › [Article 7 — Proceedings Concerning Whether a Person Is In Need of Supervision](../README.md) › [Part 7 — Compliance With Orders](README.md)

```text
  § 772. Action on return from agency or institution.  If a person is
returned to the court under section seven hundred seventy-one, the court
may make any order that might have been made at the time the order of
placement was made, except that the maximum duration authorized for any
such order shall be decreased by the time spent in placement.
```
