# Family Court Act § 762 — Staying, modifying, setting aside or vacating order

[Family Court Act](../../README.md) › [Article 7 — Proceedings Concerning Whether a Person Is In Need of Supervision](../README.md) › [Part 6 — New Hearing and Reconsideration of Orders](README.md)

```text
  § 762. Staying, modifying, setting aside or vacating order. For good
cause, the court on its own motion or on motion of any interested person
acting on behalf of the respondent may stay execution of, arrest, set
aside, modify or vacate any order issued in the course of a proceeding
under this article.
```
