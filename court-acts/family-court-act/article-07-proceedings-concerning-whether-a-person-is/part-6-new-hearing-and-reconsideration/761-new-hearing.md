# Family Court Act § 761 — New hearing

[Family Court Act](../../README.md) › [Article 7 — Proceedings Concerning Whether a Person Is In Need of Supervision](../README.md) › [Part 6 — New Hearing and Reconsideration of Orders](README.md)

```text
  § 761. New hearing. On its own motion or on motion of any interested
person acting on behalf of the respondent, the court may for good cause
grant a new fact-finding or dispositional hearing under this article.
```
