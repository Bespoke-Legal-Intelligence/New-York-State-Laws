# Family Court Act § 768 — Successive petitions

[Family Court Act](../../README.md) › [Article 7 — Proceedings Concerning Whether a Person Is In Need of Supervision](../README.md) › [Part 6 — New Hearing and Reconsideration of Orders](README.md)

```text
  § 768. Successive petitions. If a petition under section seven hundred
sixty-four is denied, it may not be renewed for a period of ninety days
after the denial, unless the order of denial permits renewal at an
earlier time.
```
