# Family Court Act § 781 — Nature of adjudication

[Family Court Act](../../README.md) › [Article 7 — Proceedings Concerning Whether a Person Is In Need of Supervision](../README.md) › [Part 8 — Effect of Proceedings](README.md)

```text
  § 781. Nature of adjudication.  No adjudication under this article may
be denominated a conviction, and no person adjudicated a person in need
of supervision under this article shall be denominated a criminal by
reason of such adjudication.
```
