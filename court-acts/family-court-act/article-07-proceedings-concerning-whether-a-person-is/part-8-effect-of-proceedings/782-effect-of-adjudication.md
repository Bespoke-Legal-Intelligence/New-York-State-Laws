# Family Court Act § 782 — Effect of adjudication

[Family Court Act](../../README.md) › [Article 7 — Proceedings Concerning Whether a Person Is In Need of Supervision](../README.md) › [Part 8 — Effect of Proceedings](README.md)

```text
  § 782. Effect of adjudication. No adjudication under this article
shall operate as a forfeiture of any right or privilege or disqualify
any person from subsequently holding public office or receiving any
license granted by public authority.
```
