# Family Court Act § 729 — Duration of detention before filing of petition or hearing

[Family Court Act](../../README.md) › [Article 7 — Proceedings Concerning Whether a Person Is In Need of Supervision](../README.md) › [Part 2 — Custody and Detention](README.md)

```text
  § 729. Duration of detention before filing of petition or hearing. No
person may be detained under this article for more than seventy-two
hours or the next day the court is in session, whichever is sooner,
without a hearing under section seven hundred twenty-eight.
```
