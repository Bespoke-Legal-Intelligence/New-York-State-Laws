# Family Court Act § 752 — Findings

[Family Court Act](../../README.md) › [Article 7 — Proceedings Concerning Whether a Person Is In Need of Supervision](../README.md) › [Part 5 — Orders](README.md)

```text
  § 752. Findings.  If the allegations of a petition under this article
are established in accord with part three, the court shall enter an
order finding that the respondent is a person in need of supervision.
The order shall state the grounds for the finding and the facts upon
which it is based.
```
