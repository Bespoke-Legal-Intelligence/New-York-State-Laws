# Family Court Act § 365.2 — Appeal by permission

[Family Court Act](../../README.md) › [Article 3 — Juvenile Delinquency](../README.md) › [Part 6 — Post-dispositional Procedures](README.md)

```text
  § 365.2. Appeal by permission. An appeal may be taken by the
respondent, in the discretion of the appropriate appellate division,
from any other order under this article.
```
