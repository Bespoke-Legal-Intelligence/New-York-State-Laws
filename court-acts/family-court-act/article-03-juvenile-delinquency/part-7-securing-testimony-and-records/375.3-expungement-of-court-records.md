# Family Court Act § 375.3 — Expungement of court records

[Family Court Act](../../README.md) › [Article 3 — Juvenile Delinquency](../README.md) › [Part 7 — Securing Testimony and Records](README.md)

```text
  § 375.3. Expungement of court records. Nothing contained in this
article shall preclude the court's use of its inherent power to order
the expungement of court records.
```
