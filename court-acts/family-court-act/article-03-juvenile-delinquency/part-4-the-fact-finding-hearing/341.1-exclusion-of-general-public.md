# Family Court Act § 341.1 — Exclusion of general public

[Family Court Act](../../README.md) › [Article 3 — Juvenile Delinquency](../README.md) › [Part 4 — The Fact-finding Hearing](README.md)

```text
  § 341.1. Exclusion of general public. The general public may be
excluded from any proceeding under this article and only such persons
and the representatives of authorized agencies as have a direct interest
in the case shall be admitted thereto.
```
