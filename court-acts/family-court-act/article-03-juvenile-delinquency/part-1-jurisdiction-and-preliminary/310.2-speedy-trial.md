# Family Court Act § 310.2 — Speedy trial

[Family Court Act](../../README.md) › [Article 3 — Juvenile Delinquency](../README.md) › [Part 1 — Jurisdiction and Preliminary Procedures](README.md)

```text
  § 310.2. Speedy trial. After a petition has been filed, or upon the
signing of an order of removal pursuant to section 725.05 of the
criminal procedure law, the respondent is entitled to a speedy
fact-finding hearing.
```
