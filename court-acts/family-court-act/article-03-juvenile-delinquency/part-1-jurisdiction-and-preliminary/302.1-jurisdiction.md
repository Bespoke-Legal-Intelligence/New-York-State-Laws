# Family Court Act § 302.1 — Jurisdiction

[Family Court Act](../../README.md) › [Article 3 — Juvenile Delinquency](../README.md) › [Part 1 — Jurisdiction and Preliminary Procedures](README.md)

```text
  § 302.1. Jurisdiction. 1. The family court has exclusive original
jurisdiction over any proceeding to determine whether a person is a
juvenile delinquent.
  2. In determining the jurisdiction of the court the age of such person
at the time the delinquent act allegedly was committed is controlling.
```
