# Family Court Act § 214 — State administrator to prescribe forms

[Family Court Act](../../README.md) › [Article 2 — Administration, Medical Examinations, Attorneys For Children, Auxiliary Services](../README.md) › [Part 1 — Administration](README.md)

```text
  § 214. State administrator to prescribe forms. The state administrator
shall promulgate a uniform, statewide petition for adoption and may
prescribe such other forms as may be proper for the efficient and just
administration of this act, including forms for petitions, summons,
warrants, subpoenas, undertakings, and orders authorized by this act.
```
