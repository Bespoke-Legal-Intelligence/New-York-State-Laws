# Family Court Act § 216-A — Clerk of court

[Family Court Act](../../README.md) › [Article 2 — Administration, Medical Examinations, Attorneys For Children, Auxiliary Services](../README.md) › [Part 1 — Administration](README.md)

```text
  § 216-a. Clerk of court. There shall be a clerk of court for the
family court in each county. The clerk of court shall keep the court
records and seal and have such other responsibilities as may be provided
in accord with article seven-a of the judiciary law.
```
