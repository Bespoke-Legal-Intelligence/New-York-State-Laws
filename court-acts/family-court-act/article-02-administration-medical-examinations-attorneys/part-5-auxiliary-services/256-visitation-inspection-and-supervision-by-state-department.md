# Family Court Act § 256 — Visitation, inspection and supervision by state department of social services or board of social welfare

[Family Court Act](../../README.md) › [Article 2 — Administration, Medical Examinations, Attorneys For Children, Auxiliary Services](../README.md) › [Part 5 — Auxiliary Services](README.md)

```text
  § 256. Visitation, inspection and supervision by state department of
social services or board of social welfare. Any child placed or
committed under order of the court shall be subject to such visitation,
inspection and supervision as the state board of social welfare or
department of social services shall provide for or require.
```
