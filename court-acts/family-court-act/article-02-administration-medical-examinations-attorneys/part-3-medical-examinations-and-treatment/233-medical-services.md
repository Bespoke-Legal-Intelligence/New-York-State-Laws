# Family Court Act § 233 — Medical services

[Family Court Act](../../README.md) › [Article 2 — Administration, Medical Examinations, Attorneys For Children, Auxiliary Services](../README.md) › [Part 3 — Medical Examinations and Treatment](README.md)

```text
  § 233. Medical services. Whenever a child within the jurisdiction of
the court appears to the court to be in need of medical, surgical,
therapeutic, or hospital care or treatment, a suitable order may be made
therefor.
```
