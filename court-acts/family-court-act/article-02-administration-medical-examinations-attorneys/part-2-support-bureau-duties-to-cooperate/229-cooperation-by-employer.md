# Family Court Act § 229 — Cooperation by employer

[Family Court Act](../../README.md) › [Article 2 — Administration, Medical Examinations, Attorneys For Children, Auxiliary Services](../README.md) › [Part 2 — Support Bureau; Duties to Cooperate](README.md)

```text
  § 229. Cooperation by employer. Employers are authorized and required
to report to the court, when so requested, full information as to the
earnings of a petitioner or respondent in a proceeding under articles
four or five of this act.
```
