# Family Court Act § 242 — Attorney for the child

[Family Court Act](../../README.md) › [Article 2 — Administration, Medical Examinations, Attorneys For Children, Auxiliary Services](../README.md) › [Part 4 — Attorneys For Children](README.md)

```text
  § 242. Attorney for the child. As used in this act, "attorney for the
child" refers to an attorney admitted to practice law in the state of
New York and designated under this part to represent minors pursuant to
section two hundred forty-nine of this act.
```
