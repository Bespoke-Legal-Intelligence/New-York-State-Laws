# Family Court Act § 246 — Supervision by administrative board

[Family Court Act](../../README.md) › [Article 2 — Administration, Medical Examinations, Attorneys For Children, Auxiliary Services](../README.md) › [Part 4 — Attorneys For Children](README.md)

```text
  § 246. Supervision by administrative board. The administrative board
of the judicial conference may prescribe standards for the exercise of
the powers granted to the appellate divisions under this part and may
require such reports as it deems desirable.
```
