# Family Court Act § 844 — Reconsideration and modification

[Family Court Act](../../README.md) › [Article 8 — Family Offenses Proceedings](../README.md) › [Part 4 — Orders](README.md)

```text
  § 844. Reconsideration and modification.  For good cause shown, the
family court may after hearing reconsider and modify any order issued
under paragraphs (b), (c) and (d) of section eight hundred forty-one.
```
