# Family Court Act § 832 — Definition of "fact-finding hearing"

[Family Court Act](../../README.md) › [Article 8 — Family Offenses Proceedings](../README.md) › [Part 3 — Hearing](README.md)

```text
  § 832. Definition of "fact-finding hearing". When used in this
article, "fact-finding hearing" means a hearing to determine whether the
allegations of a petition under section eight hundred twenty-one are
supported by a fair preponderance of the evidence.
```
