# Family Court Act § 834 — Evidence

[Family Court Act](../../README.md) › [Article 8 — Family Offenses Proceedings](../README.md) › [Part 3 — Hearing](README.md)

```text
  § 834. Evidence. Only competent, material and relevant evidence may be
admitted in a fact-finding hearing; only material and relevant evidence
may be admitted in a dispositional hearing.
```
