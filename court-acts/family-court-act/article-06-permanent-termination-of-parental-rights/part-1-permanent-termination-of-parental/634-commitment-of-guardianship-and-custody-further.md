# Family Court Act § 634 — Commitment of guardianship and custody; further orders

[Family Court Act](../../README.md) › [Article 6 — Permanent Termination of Parental Rights, Adoption, Guardianship and Custody](../README.md) › [Part 1 — Permanent Termination of Parental Custody By Reason of Permanent Neglect](README.md)

```text
  § 634. Commitment of guardianship and custody; further orders. The
court may enter an order under section six hundred thirty-one committing
the guardianship and custody of the child to the petitioner on such
conditions, if any, as it deems proper.
```
