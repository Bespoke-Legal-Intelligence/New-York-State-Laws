# Family Court Act § 653 — Rules of court

[Family Court Act](../../README.md) › [Article 6 — Permanent Termination of Parental Rights, Adoption, Guardianship and Custody](../README.md) › [Part 3 — Custody](README.md)

```text
  § 653. Rules of court. Rules of court, not inconsistent with any law,
may authorize the probation service to interview such persons and obtain
such data as will aid the court in determining a habeas corpus or
custody proceeding under section six hundred fifty-one.
```
