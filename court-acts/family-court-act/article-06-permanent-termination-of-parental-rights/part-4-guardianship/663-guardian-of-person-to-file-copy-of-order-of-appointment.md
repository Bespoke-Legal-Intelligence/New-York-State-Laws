# Family Court Act § 663 — Guardian of person to file copy of order of appointment

[Family Court Act](../../README.md) › [Article 6 — Permanent Termination of Parental Rights, Adoption, Guardianship and Custody](../README.md) › [Part 4 — Guardianship](README.md)

```text
  § 663. Guardian of person to file copy of order of appointment. Upon
the appointment and qualification of guardian of the person of a minor
as provided in section six hundred sixty-one of this act, letters of
guardianship shall thereupon issue from this court.
```
