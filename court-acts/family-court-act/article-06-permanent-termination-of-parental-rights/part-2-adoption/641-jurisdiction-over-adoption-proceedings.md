# Family Court Act § 641 — Jurisdiction over adoption proceedings

[Family Court Act](../../README.md) › [Article 6 — Permanent Termination of Parental Rights, Adoption, Guardianship and Custody](../README.md) › [Part 2 — Adoption](README.md)

```text
  § 641. Jurisdiction over adoption proceedings. The family court has
original jurisdiction concurrent with the surrogate's courts over
adoption proceedings under article seven of the domestic relations law.
```
