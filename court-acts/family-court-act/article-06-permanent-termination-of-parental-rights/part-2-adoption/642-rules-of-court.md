# Family Court Act § 642 — Rules of court

[Family Court Act](../../README.md) › [Article 6 — Permanent Termination of Parental Rights, Adoption, Guardianship and Custody](../README.md) › [Part 2 — Adoption](README.md)

```text
  § 642. Rules of court. Rules of court, not inconsistent with any
provision of article seven of the domestic relations law, may authorize
the probation service to interview such persons and obtain such data as
will aid the court in determining a petition under that article.
```
