# Family Court Act § 581-703 — Parent under section seventy of the domestic relations law

[Family Court Act](../../README.md) › [Article 5-C — Judgments of Parentage of Children Conceived Through Assisted Reproduction or Pursuant to Surrogacy Agreements](../README.md) › [Part 7 — Miscellaneous Provisions](README.md)

```text
  § 581-703. Parent under section seventy of the domestic relations law.
The term "parent" in section seventy of the domestic relations law shall
include a person established to be a parent under this article or any
other relevant law.
```
