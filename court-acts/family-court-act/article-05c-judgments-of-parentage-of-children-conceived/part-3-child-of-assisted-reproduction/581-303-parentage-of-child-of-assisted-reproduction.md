# Family Court Act § 581-303 — Parentage of child of assisted reproduction

[Family Court Act](../../README.md) › [Article 5-C — Judgments of Parentage of Children Conceived Through Assisted Reproduction or Pursuant to Surrogacy Agreements](../README.md) › [Part 3 — Child of Assisted Reproduction](README.md)

```text
  § 581-303. Parentage of child of assisted reproduction. (a) An
individual who provides gametes for, or who consents to, assisted
reproduction with the intent to be a parent of the child with the
consent of the gestating parent as provided in section 581-304 of this
part, is a parent of the resulting child for all legal purposes.
  (b) The court shall issue a judgment of parentage pursuant to this
article upon application by any participant.
```
