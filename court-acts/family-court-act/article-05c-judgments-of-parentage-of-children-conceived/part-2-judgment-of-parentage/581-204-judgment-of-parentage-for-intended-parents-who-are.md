# Family Court Act § 581-204 — Judgment of parentage for intended parents who are spouses

[Family Court Act](../../README.md) › [Article 5-C — Judgments of Parentage of Children Conceived Through Assisted Reproduction or Pursuant to Surrogacy Agreements](../README.md) › [Part 2 — Judgment of Parentage](README.md)

```text
  * § 581-204. Judgment of parentage for intended parents who are
spouses.  Notwithstanding or without limitation on presumptions of
parentage that apply, a judgment of parentage may be obtained under this
part by intended parents who are each other's spouse. Nothing in this
section requires intended parents to be married to each other in order
to be jointly declared the parents of the child.
  * NB Effective February 15, 2021
```
