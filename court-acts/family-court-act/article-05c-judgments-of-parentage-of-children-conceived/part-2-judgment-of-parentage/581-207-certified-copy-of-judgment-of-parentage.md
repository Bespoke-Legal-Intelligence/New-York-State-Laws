# Family Court Act § 581-207 — Certified copy of judgment of parentage

[Family Court Act](../../README.md) › [Article 5-C — Judgments of Parentage of Children Conceived Through Assisted Reproduction or Pursuant to Surrogacy Agreements](../README.md) › [Part 2 — Judgment of Parentage](README.md)

```text
  § 581-207. Certified copy of judgment of parentage. Upon issuing a
judgment of parentage pursuant to section 581-202 or 581-203 of this
part, the issuing court shall provide a certified copy of such judgment
to the intended parent or parents.
```
