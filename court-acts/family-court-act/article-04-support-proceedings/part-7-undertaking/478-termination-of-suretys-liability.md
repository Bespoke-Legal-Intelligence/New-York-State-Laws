# Family Court Act § 478 — Termination of surety's liability

[Family Court Act](../../README.md) › [Article 4 — Support Proceedings](../README.md) › [Part 7 — Undertaking](README.md)

```text
  § 478. Termination of surety's liability. Whenever the liability on an
undertaking has ceased, the court shall make an order to that effect.
Upon receipt of a certified copy of the order, the county clerk shall
discharge of record the lien of the undertaking.
```
