# Family Court Act § 436 — Competence of spouse

[Family Court Act](../../README.md) › [Article 4 — Support Proceedings](../README.md) › [Part 3 — Hearing](README.md)

```text
  § 436. Competence of spouse. Wives and husbands are competent
witnesses against each other in a hearing under section four hundred
thirty-three and may testify to non-access in such a hearing.
```
