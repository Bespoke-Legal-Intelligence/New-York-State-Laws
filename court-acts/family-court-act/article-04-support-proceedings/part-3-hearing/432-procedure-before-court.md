# Family Court Act § 432 — Procedure before court

[Family Court Act](../../README.md) › [Article 4 — Support Proceedings](../README.md) › [Part 3 — Hearing](README.md)

```text
  § 432. Procedure before court. The court before whom the respondent is
taken under section four hundred thirty-one may require an undertaking
to appear or in default thereof may place the respondent in custody
until the hearing commences.
```
