# Family Court Act § 434-A — Order for temporary spousal support

[Family Court Act](../../README.md) › [Article 4 — Support Proceedings](../README.md) › [Part 3 — Hearing](README.md)

```text
  § 434-a. Order for temporary spousal support. The court may make an
order for temporary spousal support pending a final determination,
notwithstanding that information with respect to income and assets of
the respondent may be unavailable.
```
