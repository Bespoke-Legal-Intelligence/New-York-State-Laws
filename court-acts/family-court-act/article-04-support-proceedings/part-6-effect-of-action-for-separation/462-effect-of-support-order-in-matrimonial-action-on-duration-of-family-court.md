# Family Court Act § 462 — Effect of support order in matrimonial action on duration of family court support order for child

[Family Court Act](../../README.md) › [Article 4 — Support Proceedings](../README.md) › [Part 6 — Effect of Action For Separation, Divorce or Annulment](README.md)

```text
  § 462. Effect of support order in matrimonial action on duration of
family court support order for child. Any order of the family court
requiring support of a child terminates when the supreme court makes an
order in an action for divorce, separation or annulment providing for
the support of the child, unless the supreme court continues the order
of the family court.
```
