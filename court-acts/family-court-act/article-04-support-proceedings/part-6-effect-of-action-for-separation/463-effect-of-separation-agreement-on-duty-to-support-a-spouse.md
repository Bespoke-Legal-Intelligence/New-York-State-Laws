# Family Court Act § 463 — Effect of separation agreement on duty to support a spouse

[Family Court Act](../../README.md) › [Article 4 — Support Proceedings](../README.md) › [Part 6 — Effect of Action For Separation, Divorce or Annulment](README.md)

```text
  § 463. Effect of separation agreement on duty to support a spouse. A
separation agreement does not preclude the filing of a petition and the
making of an order under section four hundred forty-five of this article
for support of a spouse who is likely to become in need of public
assistance or care.
```
