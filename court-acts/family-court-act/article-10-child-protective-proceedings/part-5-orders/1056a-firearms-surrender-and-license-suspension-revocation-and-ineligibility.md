# Family Court Act § 1056-A — Firearms; surrender and license suspension, revocation and ineligibility

[Family Court Act](../../README.md) › [Article 10 — Child Protective Proceedings](../README.md) › [Part 5 — Orders](README.md)

```text
  § 1056-a. Firearms; surrender and license suspension, revocation and
ineligibility. Upon the issuance of an order of protection or temporary
order of protection, or upon a violation of such order, the court shall
make an order in accordance with section eight hundred forty-two-a of
this act.
```
