# Family Court Act § 1032 — Persons who may originate proceedings

[Family Court Act](../../README.md) › [Article 10 — Child Protective Proceedings](../README.md) › [Part 3 — Preliminary Procedure](README.md)

```text
  § 1032. Persons who may originate proceedings. The following may
originate a proceeding under this article:
  (a) a child protective agency, or
  (b) a person on the court's direction.
```
