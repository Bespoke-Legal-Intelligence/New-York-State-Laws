# Family Court Act § 1033-A — Initial appearance

[Family Court Act](../../README.md) › [Article 10 — Child Protective Proceedings](../README.md) › [Part 3 — Preliminary Procedure](README.md)

```text
  § 1033-a. Initial appearance. For the purposes of this section,
"initial appearance" means the proceeding on the date the respondent
first appears before the court after the petition has been filed and any
adjournments thereof.
```
