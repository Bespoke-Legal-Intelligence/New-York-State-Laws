# Family Court Act § 1066 — Successive motions

[Family Court Act](../../README.md) › [Article 10 — Child Protective Proceedings](../README.md) › [Part 6 — New Hearing and Reconsideration of Orders](README.md)

```text
  § 1066. Successive motions. If a motion under section one thousand
sixty-two of this part is denied, it may not be renewed for a period of
ninety days after the denial, unless the order of denial permits renewal
at an earlier time.
```
