# Family Court Act § 1068 — Action on return from agency or institution

[Family Court Act](../../README.md) › [Article 10 — Child Protective Proceedings](../README.md) › [Part 6 — New Hearing and Reconsideration of Orders](README.md)

```text
  § 1068. Action on return from agency or institution. If a person is
returned to the court under section one thousand sixty-seven of this
part, the court may make any order that might have been made at the time
of the order of placement.
```
