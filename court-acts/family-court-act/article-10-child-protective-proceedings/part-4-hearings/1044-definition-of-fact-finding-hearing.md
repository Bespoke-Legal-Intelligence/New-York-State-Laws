# Family Court Act § 1044 — Definition of "fact-finding hearing"

[Family Court Act](../../README.md) › [Article 10 — Child Protective Proceedings](../README.md) › [Part 4 — Hearings](README.md)

```text
  § 1044. Definition of "fact-finding hearing". When used in this
article, "fact-finding hearing" means a hearing to determine whether the
child is an abused or neglected child as defined by this article.
```
