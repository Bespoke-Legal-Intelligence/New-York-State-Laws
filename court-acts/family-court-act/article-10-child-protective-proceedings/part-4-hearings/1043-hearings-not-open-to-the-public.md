# Family Court Act § 1043 — Hearings not open to the public

[Family Court Act](../../README.md) › [Article 10 — Child Protective Proceedings](../README.md) › [Part 4 — Hearings](README.md)

```text
  § 1043. Hearings not open to the public.  The general public may be
excluded from any hearing under this article and only such persons and
the representatives of authorized agencies admitted thereto as have an
interest in the case.
```
