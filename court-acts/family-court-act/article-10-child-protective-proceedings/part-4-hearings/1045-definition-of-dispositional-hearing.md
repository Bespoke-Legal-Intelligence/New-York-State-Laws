# Family Court Act § 1045 — Definition of "dispositional hearing"

[Family Court Act](../../README.md) › [Article 10 — Child Protective Proceedings](../README.md) › [Part 4 — Hearings](README.md)

```text
  § 1045. Definition of "dispositional hearing". When used in this
article, "dispositional hearing" means a hearing to determine what order
of disposition should be made.
```
