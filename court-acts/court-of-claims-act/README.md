**Short title (from the law itself):** Section 1. Short title. This act shall be known as the "Court of Claims Act."

## Chapter text as published

```text
                               CHAPTER 860
AN ACT in relation to the court of claims and the jurisdiction, practice
and procedure therein
Became a law June 13, 1939, with the approval of the Governor. Passed,
    three-fifths being present
  The People of the State of New York, represented in Senate and
Assembly, do enact as follows:
                           COURT OF CLAIMS ACT
```
