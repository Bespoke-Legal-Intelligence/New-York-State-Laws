# Court of Claims Act § 6 — Seal of court

[Court of Claims Act](../README.md) › [Article 1 — The Court, Judges and Officers](README.md)

```text
  § 6. Seal of court.  The court shall adopt and procure an official
seal, with suitable device and inscription. A description of such seal,
with an impression thereof, shall be filed in the office of the
secretary of state. The expenses of procuring such seal shall be paid
out of the contingent fund of the court.
```
