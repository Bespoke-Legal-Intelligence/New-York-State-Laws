# Court of Claims Act § 21 — Compromise of canal claims

[Court of Claims Act](../README.md) › [Article 3 — Practice Provisions](README.md)

```text
  § 21. Compromise of canal claims.  No claims brought against the state
on account of the canal shall be settled or compromised for any amount
without the written consent thereto by the commissioner of
transportation or his duly authorized representative.
```
