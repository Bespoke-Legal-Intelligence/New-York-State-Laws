# Court of Claims Act § 18 — Expense of procuring testimony on commission or deposition

[Court of Claims Act](../README.md) › [Article 3 — Practice Provisions](README.md)

```text
  § 18. Expense of procuring testimony on commission or deposition.
When testimony is taken on commission or deposition at the instance of
the claimant, the expense thereof including the fees of the
commissioner, shall be paid by the claimant; and when taken at the
instance of the state, such fees and all expenses incurred by the
attorney-general shall be paid by the state.
```
