# Court of Claims Act § 13 — Court rooms for sessions

[Court of Claims Act](../README.md) › [Article 3 — Practice Provisions](README.md)

```text
  § 13. Court rooms for sessions.  The sheriff of any county, except
Albany, shall furnish for the use of the court suitable rooms in the
court house of his county for any session ordered to be held thereat and
shall, if requested, attend said session.  His fees for attendance shall
be paid out of the contingent fund of the court at the same rate as for
attending a term of the supreme court in that county.
```
