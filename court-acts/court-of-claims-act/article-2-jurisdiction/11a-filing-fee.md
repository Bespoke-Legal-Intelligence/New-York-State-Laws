# Court of Claims Act § 11-A — Filing fee

[Court of Claims Act](../README.md) › [Article 2 — Jurisdiction](README.md)

```text
  * § 11-a. Filing fee. 1. The clerk of the court of claims shall
require for the filing of a claim a fee of fifty dollars. The fee shall
be payable in advance, unless a motion, affidavit, or certification
pursuant to section eleven hundred one of the civil practice law and
rules is filed with the claim, in which case the provisions of such
section shall be applicable.
  2. The court shall award to a prevailing claimant as a taxable
disbursement the actual amount of any fee paid to file the claim.
  * NB Expires September 1, 2027
```
