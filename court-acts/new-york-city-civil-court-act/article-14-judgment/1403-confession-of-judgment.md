# New York City Civil Court Act § 1403 — Confession of judgment

[New York City Civil Court Act](../README.md) › [Article 14 — Judgment](README.md)

```text
  § 1403. Confession of judgment. The provisions of CPLR § 3218,
relating to judgment by confession, shall be applicable in this court
where the relief for which the judgment is confessed is within the
jurisdiction of this court. For such purpose the words "clerk of the
county" as used in CPLR § 3218 (b) shall be deemed a reference to the
clerk of this court in such county, and such judgment shall be entered
in this court and shall be enforcible in the same manner and with the
same effect as a judgment in an action in this court.
```
