# New York City Civil Court Act § 2202 — Referees

[New York City Civil Court Act](../README.md) › [Article 22 — Transition](README.md)

```text
  § 2202. Referees. An official referee of the city court of the city of
New York or of the municipal court of the city of New York in office at
the time this act takes effect shall for the remainder of his term of
office be an official referee of this court. He shall perform such
duties and discharge such functions in this court as would an official
referee of the supreme court perform and discharge therein, or such
duties and functions as may be assigned to him by the appellate
division.
```
