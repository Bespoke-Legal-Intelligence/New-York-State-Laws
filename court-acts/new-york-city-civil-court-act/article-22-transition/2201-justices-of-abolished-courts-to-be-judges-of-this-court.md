# New York City Civil Court Act § 2201 — Justices of abolished courts to be judges of this court

[New York City Civil Court Act](../README.md) › [Article 22 — Transition](README.md)

```text
  § 2201. Justices of abolished courts to be judges of this court.  The
justices of the city court of the city of New York and the justices of
the municipal court of the city of New York in office on the date such
courts are abolished shall, for the remainder of the term for which each
was elected or appointed, be judges of this court.
```
