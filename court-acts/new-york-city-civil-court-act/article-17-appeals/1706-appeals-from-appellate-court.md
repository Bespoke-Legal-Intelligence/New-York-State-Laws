# New York City Civil Court Act § 1706 — Appeals from appellate court

[New York City Civil Court Act](../README.md) › [Article 17 — Appeals](README.md)

```text
  § 1706. Appeals from appellate court. Appeals from the judgment or
order of an appellate court, on appeal from this court, shall be
governed by the CPLR.
```
