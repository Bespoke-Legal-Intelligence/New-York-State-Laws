# New York City Civil Court Act § 1509 — Contempt; extension of court's process

[New York City Civil Court Act](../README.md) › [Article 15 — Execution](README.md)

```text
  § 1509. Contempt; extension of court's process. In an instance where a
contempt of the court has been committed, the court's process or mandate
relating to the punishment of the contemptuous person may be served and
executed in any part of the state, and proceedings thereon shall follow
supreme court practice.
```
