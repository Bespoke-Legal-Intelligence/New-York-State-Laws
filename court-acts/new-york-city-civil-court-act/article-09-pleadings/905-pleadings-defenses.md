# New York City Civil Court Act § 905 — Pleadings; defenses

[New York City Civil Court Act](../README.md) › [Article 9 — Pleadings](README.md)

```text
  § 905. Pleadings; defenses. The court may consider any defense to a
cause of action or claim asserted by any party, whether such defense be
denominated or deemed legal or equitable in nature.
```
