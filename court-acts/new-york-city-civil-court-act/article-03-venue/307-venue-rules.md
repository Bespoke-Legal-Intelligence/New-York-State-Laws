# New York City Civil Court Act § 307 — Venue; rules

[New York City Civil Court Act](../README.md) › [Article 3 — Venue](README.md)

```text
  § 307. Venue; rules. Notwithstanding the provisions of this article,
the rules may establish a part or parts of the court where designated
classes of cases shall be brought or tried.
```
