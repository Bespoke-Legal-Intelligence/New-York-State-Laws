# New York City Civil Court Act § 303 — Summary proceedings; venue

[New York City Civil Court Act](../README.md) › [Article 3 — Venue](README.md)

```text
  § 303. Summary proceedings; venue. A summary proceeding to recover
possession of real property or to remove tenants therefrom shall be
brought in the county in which the real property or a part thereof is
situated.
```
