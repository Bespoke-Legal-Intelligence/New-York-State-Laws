# New York City Civil Court Act § 1900 — Security for costs

[New York City Civil Court Act](../README.md) › [Article 19 — Costs and Fees](README.md)

```text
  § 1900. Security for costs. Article 85 of the CPLR, entitled "security
for costs", shall apply in this court, except that the minimum
undertaking of CPLR 8503 shall be $200 rather than the amount therein
provided.
```
