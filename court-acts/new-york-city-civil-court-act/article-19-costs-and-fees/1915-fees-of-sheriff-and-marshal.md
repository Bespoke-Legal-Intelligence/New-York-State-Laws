# New York City Civil Court Act § 1915 — Fees of sheriff and marshal

[New York City Civil Court Act](../README.md) › [Article 19 — Costs and Fees](README.md)

```text
  § 1915. Fees of sheriff and marshal.  In performing a function within
their respective jurisdiction and powers as elsewhere provided in this
act, the sheriff of the city of New York and the city marshals shall be
allowed and paid the same fees to which a sheriff would be entitled for
like services in supreme court.
```
