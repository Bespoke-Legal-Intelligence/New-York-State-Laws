# New York City Civil Court Act § 1905 — No costs on plea of bankruptcy

[New York City Civil Court Act](../README.md) › [Article 19 — Costs and Fees](README.md)

```text
  § 1905. No costs on plea of bankruptcy.  Where the defendant recovers
judgment upon the defense of bankruptcy, he shall not be entitled to
costs.
```
