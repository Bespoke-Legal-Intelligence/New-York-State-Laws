# New York City Civil Court Act § 1913 — Witnesses' fees

[New York City Civil Court Act](../README.md) › [Article 19 — Costs and Fees](README.md)

```text
  § 1913. Witnesses' fees. Witnesses in an action or a special
proceeding or before a commissioner or judge of this court taking a
deposition, are entitled to the same fees, including mileage, as a
witness in an action in the supreme court.
```
