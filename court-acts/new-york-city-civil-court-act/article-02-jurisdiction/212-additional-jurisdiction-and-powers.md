# New York City Civil Court Act § 212 — Additional jurisdiction and powers

[New York City Civil Court Act](../README.md) › [Article 2 — Jurisdiction](README.md)

```text
  § 212. Additional jurisdiction and powers. In the exercise of its
jurisdiction the court shall have all of the powers that the supreme
court would have in like actions and proceedings.
```
