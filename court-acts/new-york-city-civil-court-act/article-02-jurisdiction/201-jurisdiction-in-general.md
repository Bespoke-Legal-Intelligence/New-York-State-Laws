# New York City Civil Court Act § 201 — Jurisdiction; in general

[New York City Civil Court Act](../README.md) › [Article 2 — Jurisdiction](README.md)

```text
  § 201. Jurisdiction; in general. The court shall have jurisdiction as
set forth in this article and as elsewhere provided by law. The phrase
"$25,000", whenever it appears herein, shall be taken to mean "$25,000
exclusive of interest and costs".
```
