# New York City Civil Court Act § 202 — Money actions and actions involving chattels

[New York City Civil Court Act](../README.md) › [Article 2 — Jurisdiction](README.md)

```text
  § 202. Money actions and actions involving chattels. The court shall
have jurisdiction of actions and proceedings for the recovery of money,
actions and proceedings for the recovery of chattels and actions and
proceedings for the foreclosure of liens on personal property where the
amount sought to be recovered or the value of the property does not
exceed $25,000.
```
