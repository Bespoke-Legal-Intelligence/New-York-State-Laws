# New York City Civil Court Act § 213 — Jurisdiction for rescission or reformation of certain transactions

[New York City Civil Court Act](../README.md) › [Article 2 — Jurisdiction](README.md)

```text
  § 213. Jurisdiction for rescission or reformation of certain
transactions. The court shall have jurisdiction of actions for
rescission or reformation of a transaction if the amount in controversy
does not exceed $50,000.
```
