# New York City Civil Court Act § 210 — Contempt

[New York City Civil Court Act](../README.md) › [Article 2 — Jurisdiction](README.md)

```text
  § 210. Contempt. All of the provisions of law governing civil and
criminal contempts in like instances in supreme court shall apply in
this court, except that this court shall have no power to punish for
contempt a judge or justice of any court.
```
