# New York City Civil Court Act § 1611 — Fees to the city of New York

[New York City Civil Court Act](../README.md) › [Article 16 — Marshals](README.md)

```text
  § 1611. Fees to the city of New York. 1. Every city marshal who serves
in office for any portion of the calendar year shall pay an annual fee
to the city of New York of fifteen hundred dollars.
  2. Every city marshal shall, in addition to the fee required by
subdivision one, pay annually to the city of New York 4.50 percent of
the gross fees, including poundage, received by the marshal during the
preceding calendar year.
  3. The fees paid to the city of New York pursuant to this section
shall be disbursed for the purposes of this article.
```
