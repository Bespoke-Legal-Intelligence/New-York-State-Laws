# New York City Civil Court Act § 1605 — Action on marshal's bond

[New York City Civil Court Act](../README.md) › [Article 16 — Marshals](README.md)

```text
  § 1605. Action on marshal's bond. An action upon the bond of a marshal
may be brought and prosecuted to judgment in this court, upon leave
obtained from a judge of this court, according to the provisions
relating to an action in the supreme court by a private person upon an
official bond.
```
