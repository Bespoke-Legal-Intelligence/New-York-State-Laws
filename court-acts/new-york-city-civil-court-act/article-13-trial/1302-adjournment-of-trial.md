# New York City Civil Court Act § 1302 — Adjournment of trial

[New York City Civil Court Act](../README.md) › [Article 13 — Trial](README.md)

```text
  § 1302. Adjournment of trial. The trial of an action may be adjourned:
  (a) By the court for good cause shown and upon such terms and
conditions as the court may deem just.
  (b) By stipulation of the parties with the approval of the court, such
stipulation to be filed with the clerk; or upon request of the plaintiff
where the defendant has made default; or, if the court approve, upon
consent of the parties in open court.
```
