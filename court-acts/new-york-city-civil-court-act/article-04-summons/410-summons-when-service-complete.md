# New York City Civil Court Act § 410 — Summons; when service complete

[New York City Civil Court Act](../README.md) › [Article 4 — Summons](README.md)

```text
  § 410. Summons; when service complete. The service of summons is
complete:
  (a) immediately upon personal delivery to the defendant, where § 402
(a) is applicable; or
  (b) upon the filing of proof of service, where § 402 (b) is
applicable.
```
