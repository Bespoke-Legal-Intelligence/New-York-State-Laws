# New York City Civil Court Act § 109 — Clerk of the court

[New York City Civil Court Act](../README.md) › [Article 1 — Organization](README.md)

```text
  § 109. Clerk of the court. There shall be a chief clerk of the court.
The chief clerk and such other non-judicial personnel as shall be
authorized by rule or order of court shall each have the power to
administer oaths, take acknowledgments and sign the process or mandate
of the court.
```
