# Uniform City Court Act § 903 — Pleadings; requirements of formal pleading inapplicable to indorsement pleading

[Uniform City Court Act](../README.md) › [Article 9 — Pleadings](README.md)

```text
§ 903. Pleadings; requirements of formal pleading inapplicable to
         indorsement pleading.
  The requirements of this act or of the CPLR applicable to a formal
pleading shall not be applicable to an indorsement pleading.
```
