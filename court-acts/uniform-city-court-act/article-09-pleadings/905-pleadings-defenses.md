# Uniform City Court Act § 905 — Pleadings; defenses

[Uniform City Court Act](../README.md) › [Article 9 — Pleadings](README.md)

```text
§ 905. Pleadings; defenses.
  The court may consider any defense to a cause of action or claim
asserted by any party, whether such defense be denominated or deemed
legal or equitable in nature.
```
