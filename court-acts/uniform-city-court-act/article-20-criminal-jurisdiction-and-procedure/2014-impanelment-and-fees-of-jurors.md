# Uniform City Court Act § 2014 — Impanelment and fees of jurors

[Uniform City Court Act](../README.md) › [Article 20 — Criminal Jurisdiction and Procedure](README.md)

```text
§ 2014. Impanelment and fees of jurors.
  The system for impaneling, and the fees of, trial jurors shall be as
provided by law for the particular court or, in the absence of such
governing provisions, as provided for the supreme court of the county in
which the court sits. No fee shall be paid to a juror who has been
excused from service as a juror, for the day or days so excused.
```
