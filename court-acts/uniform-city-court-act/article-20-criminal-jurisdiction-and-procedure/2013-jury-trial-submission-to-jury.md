# Uniform City Court Act § 2013 — Jury trial; submission to jury

[Uniform City Court Act](../README.md) › [Article 20 — Criminal Jurisdiction and Procedure](README.md)

```text
§ 2013. Jury trial; submission to jury.
  Whenever a case is submitted to the jury, the jury shall be placed in
charge of any peace officer, acting pursuant to his special duties, or
police officer designated by the court.
```
