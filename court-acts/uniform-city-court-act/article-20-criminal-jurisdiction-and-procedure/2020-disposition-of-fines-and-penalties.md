# Uniform City Court Act § 2020 — Disposition of fines and penalties

[Uniform City Court Act](../README.md) › [Article 20 — Criminal Jurisdiction and Procedure](README.md)

```text
§ 2020. Disposition of fines and penalties.
  All fines and penalties collected by the court shall be paid over to
the persons or agencies entitled to the same pursuant to law.
```
