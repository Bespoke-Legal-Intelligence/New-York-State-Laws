# Uniform City Court Act § 2019 — Records

[Uniform City Court Act](../README.md) › [Article 20 — Criminal Jurisdiction and Procedure](README.md)

```text
§ 2019. Records.
  All judges of the court shall keep or cause to be kept legible and
suitable records and dockets of all criminal actions and proceedings
separate and apart from the records and dockets of civil actions and
proceedings kept by them or by clerks of their respective courts.
```
