# Uniform City Court Act § 1905 — No costs on plea of bankruptcy

[Uniform City Court Act](../README.md) › [Article 19 — Costs and Fees](README.md)

```text
§ 1905. No costs on plea of bankruptcy.
  Where the defendant recovers judgment upon the defense of bankruptcy,
he shall not be entitled to costs.
```
