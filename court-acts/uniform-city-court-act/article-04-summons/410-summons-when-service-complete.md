# Uniform City Court Act § 410 — Summons; when service complete

[Uniform City Court Act](../README.md) › [Article 4 — Summons](README.md)

```text
§ 410. Summons; when service complete.
  The service of summons is complete when it would be complete in a like
instance in an action in the supreme court.
```
