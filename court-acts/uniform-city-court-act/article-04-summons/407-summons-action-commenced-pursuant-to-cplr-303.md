# Uniform City Court Act § 407 — Summons; action commenced pursuant to CPLR § 303

[Uniform City Court Act](../README.md) › [Article 4 — Summons](README.md)

```text
§ 407. Summons; action commenced pursuant to CPLR § 303.
  In any action in this court commenced by service of summons upon an
attorney or a clerk as agent, as authorized by CPLR § 303, such service
may be made in any part of the county or any adjoining county and shall
otherwise be made in such manner as would confer jurisdiction on the
supreme court in a like case.
```
