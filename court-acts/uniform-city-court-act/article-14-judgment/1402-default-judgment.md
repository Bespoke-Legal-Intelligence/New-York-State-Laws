# Uniform City Court Act § 1402 — Default judgment

[Uniform City Court Act](../README.md) › [Article 14 — Judgment](README.md)

```text
§ 1402. Default judgment.
  A judgment by default may be entered as provided in CPLR § 3215. A
summons stating the amount for which the plaintiff will take judgment if
the defendant fails to appear and answer, and containing a statement of
the nature and substance of the cause of action, or a summons
accompanied by a formal complaint, shall be deemed "the summons and the
complaints" referred to in subdivision (e) of said section.
```
