# Uniform City Court Act § 1507 — Limitation on execution against property of tenant

[Uniform City Court Act](../README.md) › [Article 15 — Execution](README.md)

```text
§ 1507. Limitation on execution against property of tenant.
  No levy shall be made on the property of a tenant dispossessed for
nonpayment of rent under any execution within twenty-four hours of the
time of eviction or dispossess.
```
