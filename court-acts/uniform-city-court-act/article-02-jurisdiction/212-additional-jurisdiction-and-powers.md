# Uniform City Court Act § 212 — Additional jurisdiction and powers

[Uniform City Court Act](../README.md) › [Article 2 — Jurisdiction](README.md)

```text
§ 212. Additional jurisdiction and powers.
  In the exercise of its jurisdiction the court shall have all of the
powers that the supreme court would have in like actions and
proceedings.
```
