# Uniform City Court Act § 210 — Contempt

[Uniform City Court Act](../README.md) › [Article 2 — Jurisdiction](README.md)

```text
§ 210. Contempt.
  All of the provisions of law governing civil and criminal contempts in
like instances in supreme court shall apply in this court, except that
this court shall have no power to punish for contempt a judge or justice
of any court.
```
