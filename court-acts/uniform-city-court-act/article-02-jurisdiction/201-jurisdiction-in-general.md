# Uniform City Court Act § 201 — Jurisdiction; in general

[Uniform City Court Act](../README.md) › [Article 2 — Jurisdiction](README.md)

```text
§ 201. Jurisdiction; in general.
  The court shall have jurisdiction as set forth in this article and as
elsewhere provided by law. Whenever a sum of money is set forth in this
article, or reference is made in this article to a sum, it shall be
construed as such sum exclusive of interest and costs.
```
