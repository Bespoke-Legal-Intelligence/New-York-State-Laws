# Uniform City Court Act § 205 — Interpleader

[Uniform City Court Act](../README.md) › [Article 2 — Jurisdiction](README.md)

```text
§ 205. Interpleader.
  The court shall have jurisdiction of an action of interpleader as and
defensive interpleader as defined and governed by the CPLR, provided
that the amount in controversy or the value of the property does not
exceed the sum referred to in § 202.
```
