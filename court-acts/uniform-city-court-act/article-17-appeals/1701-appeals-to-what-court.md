# Uniform City Court Act § 1701 — Appeals; to what court

[Uniform City Court Act](../README.md) › [Article 17 — Appeals](README.md)

```text
§ 1701. Appeals; to what court.
  Appeals in civil causes shall be taken to the county court, unless an
appellate term of the supreme court has been established by the
appellate division of the department and such appellate division has
directed that such appeals be taken to such term, in which case the
appeal shall be taken to the appellate term.
```
