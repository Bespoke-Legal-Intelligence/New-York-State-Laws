# Uniform City Court Act § 1304 — Time for rendering judgment or decision

[Uniform City Court Act](../README.md) › [Article 13 — Trial](README.md)

```text
§ 1304 Time for rendering judgment or decision.
  If a jury trial is not demanded or directed as provided in § 1303, the
court must render judgment within thirty days from the time when the
case is submitted for that purpose, except when further time is given by
the consent of the parties. If no decision is rendered within the time
thus limited, the case shall be placed upon such calendar as may be
provided by rule.
```
