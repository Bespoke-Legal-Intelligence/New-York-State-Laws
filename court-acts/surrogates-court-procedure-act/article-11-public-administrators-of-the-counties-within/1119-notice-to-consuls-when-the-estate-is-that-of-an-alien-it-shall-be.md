# Surrogate's Court Procedure Act § 1119 — Notice to consuls When the estate is that of an alien it shall be the duty of the public administrator when making application for letter...

[Surrogate's Court Procedure Act](../README.md) › [Article 11 — Public Administrators of the Counties Within the City of New York](README.md)

```text
§ 1119. Notice to consuls
  When the estate is that of an alien it shall be the duty of the public
administrator when making application for letters to mail a notice to
the consular representative of the nation of which the decedent was a
citizen or subject if any there be in the city of New York.
```
