# Surrogate's Court Procedure Act § 1117 — No separate bond or oath The public administrators within the counties of the city of New York shall not be required to file the designat...

[Surrogate's Court Procedure Act](../README.md) › [Article 11 — Public Administrators of the Counties Within the City of New York](README.md)

```text
§ 1117. No separate bond or oath
  The public administrators within the counties of the city of New York
shall not be required to file the designation, separate bond or oath
required by 708 before the issuance of letters to him.
```
