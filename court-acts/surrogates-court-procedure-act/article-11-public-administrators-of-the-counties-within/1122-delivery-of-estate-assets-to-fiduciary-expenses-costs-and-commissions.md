# Surrogate's Court Procedure Act § 1122 — Delivery of estate assets to fiduciary; expenses, costs and commissions When the public administrator has been superseded he shall accoun...

[Surrogate's Court Procedure Act](../README.md) › [Article 11 — Public Administrators of the Counties Within the City of New York](README.md)

```text
§ 1122. Delivery of estate assets to fiduciary; expenses, costs and
          commissions
  When the public administrator has been superseded he shall account and
deliver to the fiduciary superseding him all the personal property
belonging to the estate which he has in his possession or control after
deducting his expenses, costs and commissions as provided by law.
```
