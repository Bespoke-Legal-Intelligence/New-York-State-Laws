# Surrogate's Court Procedure Act § 1127 — Disposition of records after 10 years The public administrator is authorized to destroy the file and all of the papers relating to a dece...

[Surrogate's Court Procedure Act](../README.md) › [Article 11 — Public Administrators of the Counties Within the City of New York](README.md)

```text
§ 1127. Disposition of records after 10 years
  The public administrator is authorized to destroy the file and all of
the papers relating to a decedent after the lapse of 10 years from the
completion of the administration of his estate.  The public
administrator and the city of New York shall not be liable or held to
account for such action.
```
