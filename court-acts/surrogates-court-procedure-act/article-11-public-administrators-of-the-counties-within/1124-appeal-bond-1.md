# Surrogate's Court Procedure Act § 1124 — Appeal; bond 1

[Surrogate's Court Procedure Act](../README.md) › [Article 11 — Public Administrators of the Counties Within the City of New York](README.md)

```text
§ 1124. Appeal; bond
  1.  The public administrator is authorized to appeal from any order,
decree or judgment affecting an estate in his charge or in which he is a
party.
  2.  On such an appeal no bond on his part shall be required to stay
execution of the order, decree or judgment appealed from.
```
