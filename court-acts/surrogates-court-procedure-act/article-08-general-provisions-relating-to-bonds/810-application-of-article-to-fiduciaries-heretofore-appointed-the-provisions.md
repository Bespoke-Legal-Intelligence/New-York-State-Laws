# Surrogate's Court Procedure Act § 810 — Application of article to fiduciaries heretofore appointed The provisions of this article shall apply to fiduciaries heretofore appointed...

[Surrogate's Court Procedure Act](../README.md) › [Article 8 — General Provisions Relating to Bonds](README.md)

```text
§ 810. Application of article to fiduciaries heretofore appointed
  The provisions of this article shall apply to fiduciaries heretofore
appointed and to trusts created before this act takes effect except that
its provisions shall not affect in any manner the liability of sureties
on bonds theretofore executed.
```
