# Surrogate's Court Procedure Act § 807 — Bond of a legal life tenant Whenever a legal life tenant is entitled to the possession or control of property under a will, he shall if t...

[Surrogate's Court Procedure Act](../README.md) › [Article 8 — General Provisions Relating to Bonds](README.md)

```text
§ 807. Bond of a legal life tenant
  Whenever a legal life tenant is entitled to the possession or control
of property under a will, he shall if the court so directs execute and
file a bond unless the will expressly provides otherwise.
```
