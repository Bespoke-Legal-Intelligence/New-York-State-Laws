# Surrogate's Court Procedure Act § 806 — Bond of a testamentary trustee or executor acting as trustee Whenever a testamentary trustee is appointed by will or order of the court o...

[Surrogate's Court Procedure Act](../README.md) › [Article 8 — General Provisions Relating to Bonds](README.md)

```text
§ 806. Bond of a testamentary trustee or executor acting as trustee
  Whenever a testamentary trustee is appointed by will or order of the
court or an executor is appointed who is required to hold, manage or
invest real or personal property for the benefit of another, he shall
unless the will provides otherwise, execute and file a bond.
```
