# Surrogate's Court Procedure Act § 1916 — Provision for payment of undetermined claims and debts not yet due If any claim remains undetermined at the making of the decree or any d...

[Surrogate's Court Procedure Act](../README.md) › [Article 19 — Disposition of Real Property](README.md)

```text
§ 1916. Provision for payment of undetermined claims and debts not yet
          due
  If any claim remains undetermined at the making of the decree or any
debt is not yet due and the person holding it does not consent to its
present payment, the decree shall direct that sufficient funds be
retained by the fiduciary to meet the claim or demand when determined or
when payable and provide for the distribution of any surplus of the
amount so retained.
```
