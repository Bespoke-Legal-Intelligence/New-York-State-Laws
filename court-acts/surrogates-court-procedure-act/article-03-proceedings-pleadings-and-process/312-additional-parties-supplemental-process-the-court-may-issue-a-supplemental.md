[Surrogate's Court Procedure Act](../README.md) › [Article 3 — Proceedings, Pleadings and Process](README.md)

```text
§ 312. Additional parties; supplemental process
  The court may issue a supplemental process at any time and require any
party to procure it and cause it to be served in conformity with the
provisions of 307 and 308 on any person in any proceeding, so that any
person necessary or proper to a final determination therein may be made
a party thereto.
```
