# Surrogate's Court Procedure Act § 316 — Process to attorney general where persons unknown In every case where it appears that there is no distributee or beneficiary or that it i...

[Surrogate's Court Procedure Act](../README.md) › [Article 3 — Proceedings, Pleadings and Process](README.md)

```text
§ 316. Process to attorney general where persons unknown
  In every case where it appears that there is no distributee or
beneficiary or that it is not known whether or not there be such, the
process shall be issued to the attorney general of the state.
```
