# Surrogate's Court Procedure Act § 310 — Who may serve process 1

[Surrogate's Court Procedure Act](../README.md) › [Article 3 — Proceedings, Pleadings and Process](README.md)

```text
§ 310. Who may serve process
  1. Any person over the age of 18 years, although a party, may serve
process of the court within the state.
  2. Personal service of process without the state may be made in the
same manner as within the state by any of the persons authorized by CPLR
313 even though a party to the proceeding.
```
