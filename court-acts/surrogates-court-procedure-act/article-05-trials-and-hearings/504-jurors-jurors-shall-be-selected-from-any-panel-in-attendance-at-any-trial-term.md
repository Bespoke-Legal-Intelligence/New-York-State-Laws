# Surrogate's Court Procedure Act § 504 — Jurors Jurors shall be selected from any panel in attendance at any trial term of the supreme court or county court of the county

[Surrogate's Court Procedure Act](../README.md) › [Article 5 — Trials and Hearings](README.md)

```text
§ 504. Jurors
  Jurors shall be selected from any panel in attendance at any trial
term of the supreme court or county court of the county.
```
