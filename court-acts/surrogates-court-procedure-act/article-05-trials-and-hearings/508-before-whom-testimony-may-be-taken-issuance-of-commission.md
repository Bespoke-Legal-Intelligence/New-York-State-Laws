# Surrogate's Court Procedure Act § 508 — Before whom testimony may be taken; issuance of commission

[Surrogate's Court Procedure Act](../README.md) › [Article 5 — Trials and Hearings](README.md)

```text
§ 508. Before whom testimony may be taken; issuance of commission.
  1.  Testimony may be taken within or without the state or in a foreign
country by an attorney in addition to the persons enumerated in CPLR
3113 (a).
  2.  The court may issue a commission to a person authorized to take a
commission under CPLR 3113 or to an attorney of this state or of the
jurisdiction in which the commission is to be taken.
```
