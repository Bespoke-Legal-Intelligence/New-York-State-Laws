# Surrogate's Court Procedure Act § 509 — Effect of uncontroverted allegations of fact Except as otherwise prescribed by law, a petition, or account filed in a proceeding, unless ...

[Surrogate's Court Procedure Act](../README.md) › [Article 5 — Trials and Hearings](README.md)

```text
§ 509. Effect of uncontroverted allegations of fact
  Except as otherwise prescribed by law, a petition, or account filed in
a proceeding, unless denied by answer, objection or other proof, is due
proof of the facts therein stated.
```
