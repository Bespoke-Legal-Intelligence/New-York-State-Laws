# Surrogate's Court Procedure Act § 1218 — Appeal; bond The public administrator is authorized to appeal from any decree, order or judgment affecting an estate in his charge or in ...

[Surrogate's Court Procedure Act](../README.md) › [Article 12 — Public Administrators In the Counties of Erie, Monroe, Nassau, Onondaga, Suffolk and Westchester and County Treasurers Appointed Administrators](README.md)

```text
§ 1218. Appeal; bond
  The public administrator is authorized to appeal from any decree,
order or judgment affecting an estate in his charge or in which he is a
party.  No bond on his part shall be required to stay execution of the
order, decree or judgment from which an appeal is taken.
```
