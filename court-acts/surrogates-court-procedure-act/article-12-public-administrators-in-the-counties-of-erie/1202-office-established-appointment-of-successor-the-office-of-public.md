# Surrogate's Court Procedure Act § 1202 — Office established; appointment of successor The office of public administrator hereby is continued

[Surrogate's Court Procedure Act](../README.md) › [Article 12 — Public Administrators In the Counties of Erie, Monroe, Nassau, Onondaga, Suffolk and Westchester and County Treasurers Appointed Administrators](README.md)

```text
§ 1202. Office established; appointment of successor
  The office of public administrator hereby is continued. The public
administrator shall continue in office unless removed by the court.
Vacancy in such office for any reason shall be filled by the court of
the county by appointing a public administrator for the county
immediately. In the counties of Westchester, Nassau and Suffolk, the
court may appoint a deputy public administrator who shall act generally
for and in place of the public administrator whenever required.
```
