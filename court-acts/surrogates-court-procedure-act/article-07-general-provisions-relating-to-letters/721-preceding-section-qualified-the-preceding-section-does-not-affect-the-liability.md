# Surrogate's Court Procedure Act § 721 — Preceding section qualified The preceding section does not affect the liability of a person to whom money or other property has been deli...

[Surrogate's Court Procedure Act](../README.md) › [Article 7 — General Provisions Relating to Letters](README.md)

```text
§ 721. Preceding section qualified
  The preceding section does not affect the liability of a person to
whom money or other property has been delivered as distributee or
legatee to respond to the person lawfully entitled thereto, where
letters are revoked because a supposed decedent is living or because a
will is discovered after administration has been granted, in a case of
supposed intestacy or a prior will is revoked upon which letters were
granted.
```
