# Surrogate's Court Procedure Act § 723 — Copy of letters issued to chief fiscal officer of county to be transmitted to comptroller

[Surrogate's Court Procedure Act](../README.md) › [Article 7 — General Provisions Relating to Letters](README.md)

```text
§ 723. Copy of letters issued to chief fiscal officer of   county to be
         transmitted to comptroller.
  Within ten days after granting letters to the chief fiscal officer of
the county the court must transmit to the state comptroller a certified
copy thereof.
```
