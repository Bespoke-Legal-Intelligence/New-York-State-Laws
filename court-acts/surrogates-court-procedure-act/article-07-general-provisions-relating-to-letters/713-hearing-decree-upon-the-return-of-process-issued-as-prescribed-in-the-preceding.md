# Surrogate's Court Procedure Act § 713 — Hearing; decree Upon the return of process issued as prescribed in the preceding section the court may make a decree suspending, modifyin...

[Surrogate's Court Procedure Act](../README.md) › [Article 7 — General Provisions Relating to Letters](README.md)

```text
§ 713. Hearing; decree
  Upon the return of process issued as prescribed in the preceding
section the court may make a decree suspending, modifying or revoking
the letters issued to or removing the respondent or modifying the terms
of his appointment or may dismiss the proceeding upon such terms as
justice requires.
```
