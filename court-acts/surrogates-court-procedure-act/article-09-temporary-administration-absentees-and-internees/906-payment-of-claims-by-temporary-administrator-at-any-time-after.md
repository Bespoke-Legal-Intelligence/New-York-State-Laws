# Surrogate's Court Procedure Act § 906 — Payment of claims by temporary administrator At any time after the issuance of letters of temporary administration, process may issue to ...

[Surrogate's Court Procedure Act](../README.md) › [Article 9 — Temporary Administration--absentees and Internees](README.md)

```text
§ 906. Payment of claims by temporary administrator
  At any time after the issuance of letters of temporary administration,
process may issue to a temporary administrator requiring him to show
cause why he should not pay the petitioner's claim.  The court may
dismiss the petition or direct payment or satisfaction of the claim in
whole or in part and may require a refunding bond.
```
