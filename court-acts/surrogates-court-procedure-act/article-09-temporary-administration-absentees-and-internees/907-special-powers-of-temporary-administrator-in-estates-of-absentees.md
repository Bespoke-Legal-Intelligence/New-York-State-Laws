# Surrogate's Court Procedure Act § 907 — Special powers of temporary administrator in estates of absentees or internees 1

[Surrogate's Court Procedure Act](../README.md) › [Article 9 — Temporary Administration--absentees and Internees](README.md)

```text
§ 907. Special powers of temporary administrator in estates of absentees
         or internees
  1. Where it appears to the satisfaction of the court that the spouse,
a child or a dependent presumptive distributee of an absentee or
internee or the internee himself or herself requires provision out of
the estate for his or her benefit, health, maintenance, clothing or
education, the court may make such orders from time to time empowering
the temporary administrator to make such provision therefor out of all
property in his or her hands not needed for the payment of claims as the
court deems proper.
  2.  The court may by order empower the temporary administrator from
time to time to conduct any inquiry and to expend funds of the estate
therefor to ascertain the whereabouts or condition of an absentee or
internee.  The expenditures so made shall be deemed administration
expenses.
```
