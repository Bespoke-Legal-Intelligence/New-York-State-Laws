[Surrogate's Court Procedure Act](../README.md) › [Article 16 — Foreign Estates](README.md)

```text
§ 1614.  Authentication of foreign will or letters
  In any case in which a foreign will or letters are required to be
proved under this article, the will or letters shall be authenticated in
the manner prescribed by the CPLR.
```
