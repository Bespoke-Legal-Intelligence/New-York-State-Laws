# Surrogate's Court Procedure Act § 603 — Transcript of decree or order 1

[Surrogate's Court Procedure Act](../README.md) › [Article 6 — Orders and Decrees](README.md)

```text
§ 603. Transcript of decree or order
  1.  Clerk to furnish transcript.  Upon the application of any person,
the clerk must furnish to him a transcript of any decree or order.  If
it be for other than money only, the clerk shall insert in the
transcript a brief statement of the nature of the relief awarded.  Such
statement may be inserted under "remarks" as contained in the form set
forth in section 255-c of the judiciary law.
  2.  Docketing with county clerk.  A transcript of a decree or order of
the court may be filed in the office of the clerk of the county in which
the court is located and upon such filing the clerk shall docket the
decree or order in like manner and with like effect as a judgment
entered in the supreme court within the county.  The filing and
docketing and any subsequent filing and docketing with any other county
clerk shall be governed by the CPLR.
```
