# Surrogate's Court Procedure Act § 605 — Enforcement of decree by execution An execution shall not issue out of the court

[Surrogate's Court Procedure Act](../README.md) › [Article 6 — Orders and Decrees](README.md)

```text
§ 605. Enforcement of decree by execution
  An execution shall not issue out of the court.  The enforcement by
execution of any decree or order of the court shall take place only
after the docketing of a transcript as set forth in 603 of this act,
whereupon the decree or order shall be enforceable by execution as if it
were a judgment of the supreme court.
```
