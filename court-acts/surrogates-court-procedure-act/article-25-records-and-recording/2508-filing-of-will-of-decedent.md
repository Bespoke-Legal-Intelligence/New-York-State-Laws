# Surrogate's Court Procedure Act § 2508 — Filing of will of decedent

[Surrogate's Court Procedure Act](../README.md) › [Article 25 — Records and Recording](README.md)

```text
§ 2508. Filing of will of decedent.
  The court of any county may receive and file the will of any decedent
domiciled in that county at the date of death which any person shall
deliver, without payment of fee.  A record of the receipt and filing
thereof shall be made by the clerk and the will shall remain on file
subject to the further order of the court.
```
