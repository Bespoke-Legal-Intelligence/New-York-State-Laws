# Surrogate's Court Procedure Act § 2608 — Expenses of surrogate or clerk Where, upon the application of any party, the judge or clerk of the surrogate's court goes to a place othe...

[Surrogate's Court Procedure Act](../README.md) › [Article 26 — The Surrogate's Court and Its Officers](README.md)

```text
§ 2608. Expenses of surrogate or clerk
  Where, upon the application of any party, the judge or clerk of the
surrogate's court goes to a place other than the court in order to take
testimony, he shall be paid by such party his actual and necessary
expenses.
```
