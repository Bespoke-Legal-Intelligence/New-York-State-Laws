# Surrogate's Court Procedure Act § 1712 — Appointment of successor When no guardian by will or deed remains in office a guardian may be appointed by the court with all the powers ...

[Surrogate's Court Procedure Act](../README.md) › [Article 17 — Guardians and Custodians](README.md)

```text
§ 1712. Appointment of successor
  When no guardian by will or deed remains in office a guardian may be
appointed by the court with all the powers conferred by the will or deed
and with the effect prescribed in 706 of this act unless such
appointment would be contrary to the express provisions of the will or
deed.
```
