# Surrogate's Court Procedure Act § 2001 — Definition When used in this article the term fiduciary includes, where no fiduciary has been appointed, any person in actual or construc...

[Surrogate's Court Procedure Act](../README.md) › [Article 20 — Proceeding to Open Safe Deposit Box](README.md)

```text
§ 2001. Definition
  When used in this article the term fiduciary includes, where no
fiduciary has been appointed, any person in actual or constructive
possession of any property required to be included in the gross estate
or the New York gross estate of the decedent as determined under article
10-c or article 26 of the tax law.
```
