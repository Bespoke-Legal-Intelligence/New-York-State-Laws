# Surrogate's Court Procedure Act § 211 — When jurisdiction in personam obtained The court may exercise personal jurisdiction over any person as to any matter within the subject m...

[Surrogate's Court Procedure Act](../README.md) › [Article 2 — Jurisdiction and Powers](README.md)

```text
§ 211. When jurisdiction in personam obtained
  The court may exercise personal jurisdiction over any person as to any
matter within the subject matter jurisdiction of the court, if, on
analogous facts in an action in the supreme court, such person would be
subject to the personal jurisdiction of that court.
```
