# Surrogate's Court Procedure Act § 2404 — Expenses Where upon the application of any party the surrogate or any of the personnel of the court goes to a place other than the court ...

[Surrogate's Court Procedure Act](../README.md) › [Article 24 — Court Fees](README.md)

```text
§ 2404. Expenses
  Where upon the application of any party the surrogate or any of the
personnel of the court goes to a place other than the court in order to
take testimony or to produce records or for any purpose required by law,
the person so going shall be paid by such party his actual and necessary
expenses.
```
