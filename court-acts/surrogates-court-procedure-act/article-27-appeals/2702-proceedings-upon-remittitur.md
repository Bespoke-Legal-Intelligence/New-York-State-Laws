# Surrogate's Court Procedure Act § 2702 — Proceedings upon remittitur

[Surrogate's Court Procedure Act](../README.md) › [Article 27 — Appeals](README.md)

```text
§ 2702. Proceedings upon remittitur.
  Remittitur from the appellate court to this court shall also be as
provided in the CPLR, except that any decree or order to be entered in
this court upon the remittitur shall be as directed by the court.
```
