# Surrogate's Court Procedure Act § 2216 — Distribution in kind At any time during the administration of an estate or upon an accounting the court may direct the conveyance of any ...

[Surrogate's Court Procedure Act](../README.md) › [Article 22 — Accounting](README.md)

```text
§ 2216. Distribution in kind
  At any time during the administration of an estate or upon an
accounting the court may direct the conveyance of any unsold realty or
the delivery of any unsold chattel or the assignment of any uncollected
demand or any other personal property to a party or parties entitled to
payment or distribution, in lieu of the money value of the property at
the fair market value at the date of distribution.
```
