[Surrogate's Court Procedure Act](../README.md) › [Article 22 — Accounting](README.md)

```text
§ 2227. Summary statement
  Each decree whereby an account is judicially settled must contain in
the body thereof a summary statement of the account as settled or must
refer to such summary, which must be recorded.
```
