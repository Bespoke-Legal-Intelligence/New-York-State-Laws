# Surrogate's Court Procedure Act § 2212 — Accounting for profit and loss No profit shall be made by a fiduciary by the increase nor shall he sustain any loss by the decrease or lo...

[Surrogate's Court Procedure Act](../README.md) › [Article 22 — Accounting](README.md)

```text
§ 2212. Accounting for profit and loss
  No profit shall be made by a fiduciary by the increase nor shall he
sustain any loss by the decrease or loss without his fault of any part
of the estate, but he shall be charged with the increase and credited
for the decrease or loss on the settlement of his accounts.
```
