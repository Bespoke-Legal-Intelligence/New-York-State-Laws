# Surrogate's Court Procedure Act § 1509 — Power over lifetime trusts Except to the extent inconsistent with other provisions of this act, the surrogate having jurisdiction of a li...

[Surrogate's Court Procedure Act](../README.md) › [Article 15 — Trusts and Trustees](README.md)

```text
§ 1509. Power over lifetime trusts
  Except to the extent inconsistent with other provisions of this act,
the surrogate having jurisdiction of a lifetime trust shall have such
power over the lifetime trust and its trustee as a justice of the
supreme court having jurisdiction over the trust would have.
```
