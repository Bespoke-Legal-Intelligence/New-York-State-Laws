# Suffolk County Tax Act § 6 — Final completion and filing of assessment roll

[Suffolk County Tax Act](../README.md) › [Article 1 — Assessments; Collection of Tax By Collectors](README.md)

```text
  § 6. Final completion and filing of assessment roll. The assessment
roll shall be completed, verified and filed on or before the first day
```
