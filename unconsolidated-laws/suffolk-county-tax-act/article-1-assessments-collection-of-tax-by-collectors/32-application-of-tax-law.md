# Suffolk County Tax Act § 32 — Application of tax law

[Suffolk County Tax Act](../README.md) › [Article 1 — Assessments; Collection of Tax By Collectors](README.md)

```text
  § 32. Application of tax law. The tax law shall apply and govern in
all matters relating to taxation in Suffolk county not inconsistent with
the provisions of this act.
```
