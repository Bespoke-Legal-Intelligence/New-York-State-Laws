# Facilities Development Corporation Act § 13 — Tax exemption of monies and property of the corporation

[Facilities Development Corporation Act](README.md)

```text
  § 13. Tax exemption of monies and property of the corporation. The
monies and property of the corporation and its operations shall be
exempt from taxation.
```
