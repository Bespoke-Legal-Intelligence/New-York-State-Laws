# General City Model § 13 — Years for which imposed

[General City Model](../../README.md) › [Part 3 — Financial Corporation Tax](../README.md) › [Subpart 1 — Tax On State Banks, Trust Companies, Financial Corporations and Savings and Loan Associations](README.md)

```text
  § 13. Years for which imposed.  The tax imposed by section twelve of
this part is imposed for each calendar year beginning with the calendar
year nineteen hundred sixty-six.
```
