# General City Model § 24 — Years for which imposed

[General City Model](../../README.md) › [Part 3 — Financial Corporation Tax](../README.md) › [Subpart 2 — Tax On National Banking Associations and Production Credit Associations](README.md)

```text
  § 24.  Years for which imposed.  The tax imposed by section
twenty-three of this part is imposed for each calendar year beginning
with the calendar year nineteen hundred sixty-six.
```
