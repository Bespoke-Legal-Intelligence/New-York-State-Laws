# NYS Project Finance Agency Act § 20 — Inconsistent provisions in other laws superseded

[NYS Project Finance Agency Act](README.md)

```text
  § 20. Inconsistent provisions in other laws superseded.
  Insofar as the provisions of this act are inconsistent with the
provisions of any other law, general, special or local, the provisions
of this act shall be controlling.
```
