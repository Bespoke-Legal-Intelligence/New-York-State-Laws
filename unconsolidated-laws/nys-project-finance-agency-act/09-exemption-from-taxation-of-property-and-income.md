# NYS Project Finance Agency Act § 9 — Exemption from taxation of property and income

[NYS Project Finance Agency Act](README.md)

```text
  § 9. Exemption from taxation of property and income.
  The property of the agency and its income and operations shall be
exempt from taxation.
```
