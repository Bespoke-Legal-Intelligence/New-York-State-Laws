# Local Finance Law § 175.00 — Acts legalized or validated not affected

[Local Finance Law](../README.md) › [Article 3 — Effect of Chapter; Saving Clauses; Laws Repealed; When to Take Effect](README.md)

```text
  § 175.00 Acts legalized or validated not affected.  The repeal by this
chapter of any provisions of law legalizing or validating any act done
or committed shall in no way affect or impair such legalization or
validation.
```
