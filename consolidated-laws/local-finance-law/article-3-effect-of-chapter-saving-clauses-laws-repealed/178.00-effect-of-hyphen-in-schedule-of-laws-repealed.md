# Local Finance Law § 178.00 — Effect of hyphen in schedule of laws repealed

[Local Finance Law](../README.md) › [Article 3 — Effect of Chapter; Saving Clauses; Laws Repealed; When to Take Effect](README.md)

```text
  § 178.00 Effect of hyphen in schedule of laws repealed.  When two
numbers in the schedule of repeals hereto annexed are connected by a
hyphen both such numbers are included as well as all intermediate
numbers.
```
