# Local Finance Law § 170.00 — Local legislation relating to local finance

[Local Finance Law](../README.md) › [Article 3 — Effect of Chapter; Saving Clauses; Laws Repealed; When to Take Effect](README.md)

```text
  § 170.00 Local legislation relating to local finance.  Any county,
city, town or village may adopt local laws:
  1. Imposing duties in addition to those imposed by this chapter,
  2. Altering duties imposed by this chapter so as to make them more
stringent, or
  3. Restricting or prohibiting the exercise of any powers granted by
this chapter.
```
