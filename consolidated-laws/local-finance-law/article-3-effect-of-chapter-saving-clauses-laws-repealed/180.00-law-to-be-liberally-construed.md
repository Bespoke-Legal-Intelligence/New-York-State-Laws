# Local Finance Law § 180.00 — Law to be liberally construed

[Local Finance Law](../README.md) › [Article 3 — Effect of Chapter; Saving Clauses; Laws Repealed; When to Take Effect](README.md)

```text
  § 180.00 Law to be liberally construed.  This chapter shall be
liberally construed to effectuate the objects and purposes thereof and
the public policy of the state as hereby declared.
```
