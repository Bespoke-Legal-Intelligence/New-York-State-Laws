# Local Finance Law § 100.00 — Requirement of pledge of faith and credit

[Local Finance Law](../../README.md) › [Article 2 — Local Indebtedness](../README.md) › [Title 8 — Limitations On the Power to Contract Indebtedness](README.md)

```text
  § 100.00 Requirement of pledge of faith and credit.  Every
municipality, school district and district corporation shall pledge its
faith and credit for the payment of all indebtedness contracted by it.
```
