# Local Finance Law § 131.00 — Definition of obligations

[Local Finance Law](../../README.md) › [Article 2 — Local Indebtedness](../README.md) › [Title 10 — Statement of Debt-contracting Power of Municipalities, Fire Districts and Certain School Districts](README.md)

```text
  § 131.00 Definition of obligations.  As used in this title, the term
"obligations" shall mean bonds, notes or other evidences of
indebtedness.
```
