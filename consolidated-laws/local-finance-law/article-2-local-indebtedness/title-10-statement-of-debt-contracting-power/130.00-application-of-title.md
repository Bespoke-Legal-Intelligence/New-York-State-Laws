# Local Finance Law § 130.00 — Application of title

[Local Finance Law](../../README.md) › [Article 2 — Local Indebtedness](../README.md) › [Title 10 — Statement of Debt-contracting Power of Municipalities, Fire Districts and Certain School Districts](README.md)

```text
  § 130.00 Application of title.  This title shall apply only to
municipalities, school districts and fire districts which are required
to file a debt statement pursuant to section 109.00 of this chapter.
```
