# Local Finance Law § 162.00 — Exemption from taxation

[Local Finance Law](../../README.md) › [Article 2 — Local Indebtedness](../README.md) › [Title 12 — Miscellaneous Provisions](README.md)

```text
  § 162.00 Exemption from taxation.  All bonds and notes of a
municipality, school district or district corporation, until payable,
and the interest thereon, shall be exempt from taxation for municipal
and state purposes.
```
