# Local Finance Law § 63.10 — Cancellation of obligations

[Local Finance Law](../../README.md) › [Article 2 — Local Indebtedness](../README.md) › [Title 4 — Local Obligations: Terms, Form and Contents Thereof; Sale and Issuance Thereof](README.md)

```text
  § 63.10. Cancellation of obligations. When obligations are paid,
notwithstanding the provisions of any other general, special or local
law or of article forty-six of the education law relating to public
records, they shall be cancelled or destroyed in such manner as the
state comptroller shall prescribe by a rule or order to be filed in his
office and in such other offices as he may designate.
```
