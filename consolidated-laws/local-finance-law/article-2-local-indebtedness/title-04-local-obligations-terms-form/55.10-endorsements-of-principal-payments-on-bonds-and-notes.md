# Local Finance Law § 55.10 — Endorsements of principal payments on bonds and notes

[Local Finance Law](../../README.md) › [Article 2 — Local Indebtedness](../README.md) › [Title 4 — Local Obligations: Terms, Form and Contents Thereof; Sale and Issuance Thereof](README.md)

```text
  § 55.10 Endorsements of principal payments on bonds and notes.  A bond
or note shall not provide for the notation of payments of principal
thereon. Only one bond or note shall be set forth in a single instrument
and such bond or note, when paid, shall be surrendered for cancellation
or destruction in the manner provided in this chapter.
```
