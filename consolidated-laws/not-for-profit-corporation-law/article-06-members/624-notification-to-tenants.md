# Not-for-Profit Corporation Law § 624 — Notification to tenants

[Not-for-Profit Corporation Law](../README.md) › [Article 6 — Members](README.md)

```text
* § 624. Notification to tenants.
  Upon the adoption, amendment or repeal of the by-laws by the board of
directors pursuant to paragraph (g) of section 602 (By-laws), where such
change would have a direct effect on the resident's occupancy or the
rules of the building, the board of directors shall post the adopted,
amended, or repealed by-laws conspicuously to ensure tenants will be
aware of such adoption.
  * NB Effective January 24, 2020
```
