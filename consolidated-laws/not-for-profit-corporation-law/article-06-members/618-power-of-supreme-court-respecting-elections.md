# Not-for-Profit Corporation Law § 618 — Power of supreme court respecting elections

[Not-for-Profit Corporation Law](../README.md) › [Article 6 — Members](README.md)

```text
§ 618. Power of supreme court respecting elections.
  Upon the petition of any member aggrieved by an election and upon
notice to the persons declared elected thereat, the corporation and such
other persons as the court may direct, the supreme court at a special
term held within the judicial district where the office of the
corporation is located shall forthwith hear the proofs and allegations
of the parties, and confirm the election, order a new election, or take
such other action as justice may require.
```
