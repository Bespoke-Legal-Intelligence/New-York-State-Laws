# Not-for-Profit Corporation Law § 401 — Incorporators

[Not-for-Profit Corporation Law](../README.md) › [Article 4 — Formation of Corporations](README.md)

```text
§ 401. Incorporators.
  One or more natural persons at least eighteen years of age may act as
incorporators of a corporation to be formed under this chapter.
```
