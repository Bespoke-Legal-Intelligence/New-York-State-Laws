# Not-for-Profit Corporation Law § 1306 — Powers of authorized foreign corporations

[Not-for-Profit Corporation Law](../README.md) › [Article 13 — Foreign Corporations](README.md)

```text
§ 1306. Powers of authorized foreign corporations.
  An authorized foreign corporation shall have such powers as are
permitted by the laws of the jurisdiction of its incorporation but no
greater powers than those of a domestic corporation formed for the
purposes set forth in the application for authority.
```
