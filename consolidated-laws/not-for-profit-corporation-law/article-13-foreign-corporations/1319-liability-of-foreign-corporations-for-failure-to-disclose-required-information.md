# Not-for-Profit Corporation Law § 1319 — Liability of foreign corporations for failure to disclose required information

[Not-for-Profit Corporation Law](../README.md) › [Article 13 — Foreign Corporations](README.md)

```text
§ 1319. Liability of foreign corporations for failure to disclose
          required information.
  A foreign corporation conducting activities in this state shall, in
the same manner as a domestic corporation, disclose to its members of
record who are residents of this state the information required under
Article 5 and shall be liable as provided in section 521 (Liability for
failure to disclose required information) for failure to comply in good
faith with these requirements.
```
