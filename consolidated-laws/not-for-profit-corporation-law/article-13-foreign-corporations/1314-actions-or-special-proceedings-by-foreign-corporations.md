# Not-for-Profit Corporation Law § 1314 — Actions or special proceedings by foreign corporations

[Not-for-Profit Corporation Law](../README.md) › [Article 13 — Foreign Corporations](README.md)

```text
§ 1314. Actions or special proceedings by foreign corporations.
  An action or special proceeding may be maintained by a foreign
corporation, in like manner and subject to the same limitations, as an
action or special proceeding brought by a domestic corporation, except
as otherwise prescribed by statute.
```
