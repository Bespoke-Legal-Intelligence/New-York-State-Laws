# Not-for-Profit Corporation Law § 558 — Relation to Electronic Signatures in Global and National Commerce Act

[Not-for-Profit Corporation Law](../README.md) › [Article 5-A — Prudent Management of Institutional Funds Act](README.md)

```text
§ 558. Relation to Electronic Signatures in Global and National Commerce
Act.
  This article modifies, limits, and supersedes the Electronic
Signatures in Global and National Commerce Act, 15 U.S.C. Section 7001
et seq., but does not modify, limit, or supersede Section 101 of that
act, 15 U.S.C.  Section 7001(a), or authorize electronic delivery of any
of the notices described in Section 103 of that act, 15 U.S.C. Section
7003(b).
```
