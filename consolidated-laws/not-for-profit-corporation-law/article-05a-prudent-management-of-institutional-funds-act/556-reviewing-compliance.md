# Not-for-Profit Corporation Law § 556 — Reviewing compliance

[Not-for-Profit Corporation Law](../README.md) › [Article 5-A — Prudent Management of Institutional Funds Act](README.md)

```text
§ 556. Reviewing compliance.
  Compliance with this article shall be determined in light of the facts
and circumstances existing at the time a decision is made or action is
taken, and not retrospectively.
```
