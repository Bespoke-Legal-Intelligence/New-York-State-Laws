# Not-for-Profit Corporation Law § 557 — Application to existing institutional funds

[Not-for-Profit Corporation Law](../README.md) › [Article 5-A — Prudent Management of Institutional Funds Act](README.md)

```text
§ 557. Application to existing institutional funds.
  This article shall apply to institutional funds existing on or
established after the effective date of this article. As applied to
institutional funds existing on the effective date of this article, this
article shall govern only decisions made or actions taken on or after
that date.
```
