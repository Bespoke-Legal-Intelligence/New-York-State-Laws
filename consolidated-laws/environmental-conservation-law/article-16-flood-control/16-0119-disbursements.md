# Environmental Conservation Law § 16-0119 — Disbursements

[Environmental Conservation Law](../README.md) › [Article 16 — Flood Control](README.md)

```text
§ 16-0119. Disbursements.
  The moneys hereby appropriated shall be payable by the commissioner of
taxation and finance on the audit and warrant of the comptroller on
vouchers approved by the commissioner.
```
