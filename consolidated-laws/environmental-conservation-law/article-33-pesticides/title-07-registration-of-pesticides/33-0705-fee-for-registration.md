# Environmental Conservation Law § 33-0705 — Fee for registration

[Environmental Conservation Law](../../README.md) › [Article 33 — Pesticides](../README.md) › [Title 7 — Registration of Pesticides](README.md)

```text
§ 33-0705. Fee for registration.
  The applicant for registration shall pay a fee as follows:
  a. On or before July 1, 2017, six hundred dollars for each pesticide
proposed to be registered, provided that the applicant has submitted to
the department proof in the form of a federal income tax return for the
previous year showing gross annual sales, for federal income tax
purposes, of three million five hundred thousand dollars or less;
  b. On or before July 1, 2017, for all others, six hundred twenty
dollars for each pesticide proposed to be registered;
  c. After July 1, 2017, fifty dollars for each pesticide proposed to be
registered.
```
