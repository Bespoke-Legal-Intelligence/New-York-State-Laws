# Environmental Conservation Law § 33-0721 — Judicial review

[Environmental Conservation Law](../../README.md) › [Article 33 — Pesticides](../README.md) › [Title 7 — Registration of Pesticides](README.md)

```text
§ 33-0721. Judicial review.
  Final orders of the commissioner under this title shall be subject to
review under article 78 of the Civil Practice Law and Rules instituted
within thirty days after receipt by the applicant for registration or
registrant of the commissioner's order.
```
