# Environmental Conservation Law § 33-0709 — Requirements for registration

[Environmental Conservation Law](../../README.md) › [Article 33 — Pesticides](../README.md) › [Title 7 — Registration of Pesticides](README.md)

```text
§ 33-0709. Requirements for registration.
  If it appears to the commissioner that the composition of the
pesticide is such as to warrant the proposed claims for it, and if the
pesticide and its labeling and other material required to be submitted
comply with the requirements of this article, he shall register the
pesticide.
```
