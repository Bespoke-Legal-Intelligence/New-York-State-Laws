# Environmental Conservation Law § 33-0725 — Approved agricultural uses

[Environmental Conservation Law](../../README.md) › [Article 33 — Pesticides](../README.md) › [Title 7 — Registration of Pesticides](README.md)

```text
§ 33-0725. Approved agricultural uses.
  A pesticide shall be used in accordance with its labeling. Unless the
labeling specifically prohibits such use, a pesticide registered for use
in New York state may be used for agricultural purposes only in a
dosage, concentration or frequency less than that specified on the
labeling.
```
