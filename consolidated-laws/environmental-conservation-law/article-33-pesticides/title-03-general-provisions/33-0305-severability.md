# Environmental Conservation Law § 33-0305 — Severability

[Environmental Conservation Law](../../README.md) › [Article 33 — Pesticides](../README.md) › [Title 3 — General Provisions](README.md)

```text
§ 33-0305. Severability.
  If any provision of this article is declared unconstitutional, or the
applicability thereof to any person or circumstance is held invalid, by
a court of competent jurisdiction, the constitutionality of the
remainder of the article, and the applicability thereof to other persons
and circumstances shall not be affected.
```
