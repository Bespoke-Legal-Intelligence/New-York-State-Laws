# Environmental Conservation Law § 33-0304 — Fees

[Environmental Conservation Law](../../README.md) › [Article 33 — Pesticides](../README.md) › [Title 3 — General Provisions](README.md)

```text
§ 33-0304. Fees.
  All fees collected pursuant to this article shall be deposited into
the environmental conservation special revenue fund to the credit of the
environmental regulatory account; provided, however, that the first five
million dollars collected pursuant to this article shall be deposited
into the environmental protection fund established pursuant to section
ninety-two-s of the state finance law.
```
