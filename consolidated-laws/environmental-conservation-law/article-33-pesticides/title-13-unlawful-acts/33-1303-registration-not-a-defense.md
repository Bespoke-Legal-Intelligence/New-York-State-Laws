# Environmental Conservation Law § 33-1303 — Registration not a defense

[Environmental Conservation Law](../../README.md) › [Article 33 — Pesticides](../README.md) › [Title 13 — Unlawful Acts](README.md)

```text
§ 33-1303. Registration not a defense.
  In no event shall registration of a pesticide, be construed as a
defense for the commission of any offense prohibited under subdivisions
1 through 7 of section 33-1301.
```
