# Environmental Conservation Law § 42-0117 — Siena College revolutionary war institute

[Environmental Conservation Law](../README.md) › [Article 42 — Historic Saratoga-washington On the Hudson Partnership](README.md)

```text
* § 42-0117. Siena College revolutionary war institute.
  The partnership may enter into an agreement with the Siena College
revolutionary war institute to provide diverse, academically based
education opportunities based on the historic resources of the Historic
Saratoga-Washington on the Hudson Reserve for local schools, residents
in the reserve and visitors to the reserve to learn about our nation's
birth and democratic foundations.
  * NB Effective until notification of receipt by the commissioner of
environmental conservation
* § 42-0117. Siena College revolutionary war institute.
  The partnership may enter into an agreement with the Siena College
revolutionary war institute to provide diverse, academically based
education opportunities based on the historic resources of the Historic
Hudson-Hoosic Rivers Partnership Reserve for local schools, residents in
the reserve and visitors to the reserve to learn about our nation's
birth and democratic foundations.
  * NB Effective upon notification of receipt by the commissioner of
environmental conservation
```
