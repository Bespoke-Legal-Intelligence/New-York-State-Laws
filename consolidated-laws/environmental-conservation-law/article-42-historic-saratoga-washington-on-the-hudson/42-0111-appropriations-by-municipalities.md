# Environmental Conservation Law § 42-0111 — Appropriations by municipalities

[Environmental Conservation Law](../README.md) › [Article 42 — Historic Saratoga-washington On the Hudson Partnership](README.md)

```text
* § 42-0111. Appropriations by municipalities.
  To effectuate the purposes of this article, the local legislative
bodies of the towns, villages and city within the reserve and the
counties of Saratoga and Washington may appropriate and pay over to the
partnership moneys to be expended by the partnership to carry out its
functions thereunder.
  * NB Effective until notification of receipt by the commissioner of
environmental conservation
* § 42-0111. Appropriations by municipalities.
  To effectuate the purposes of this article, the local legislative
bodies of the towns, villages and city within the reserve and the
counties of Saratoga, Washington and Rensselaer may appropriate and pay
over to the partnership moneys to be expended by the partnership to
carry out its functions pursuant to this article.
  * NB Effective upon notification of receipt by the commissioner of
environmental conservation
```
