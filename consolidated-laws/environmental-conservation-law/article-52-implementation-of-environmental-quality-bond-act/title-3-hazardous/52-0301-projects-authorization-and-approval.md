# Environmental Conservation Law § 52-0301 — Projects; authorization and approval

[Environmental Conservation Law](../../README.md) › [Article 52 — Implementation of Environmental Quality Bond Act of 1986](../README.md) › [Title 3 — Hazardous Waste Site Remediation Projects](README.md)

```text
§ 52-0301. Projects; authorization and approval.
  The commissioner is authorized to directly develop and implement, and
to cause and to approve development and implementation by municipalities
or by other parties, of hazardous waste site remediation projects
consistent with title thirteen of article twenty-seven of this chapter
and section ninety-seven-b of the state finance law.
```
