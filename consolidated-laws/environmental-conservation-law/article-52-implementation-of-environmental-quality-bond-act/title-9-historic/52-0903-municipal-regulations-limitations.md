# Environmental Conservation Law § 52-0903 — Municipal regulations; limitations

[Environmental Conservation Law](../../README.md) › [Article 52 — Implementation of Environmental Quality Bond Act of 1986](../README.md) › [Title 9 — Historic Preservation, Municipal Park and Urban Cultural Parks Projects](README.md)

```text
§ 52-0903. Municipal regulations; limitations.
  A municipality which acquires, develops, improves, restores or
rehabilitates property with funds made available pursuant to this title
may establish reasonable rules and regulations by local law or otherwise
to assure the proper administration and development thereof, provided
that no such rule or regulation which restricts the use of such lands or
facilities by non-residents of the municipality shall be effective
without the express approval of the commissioner.
```
