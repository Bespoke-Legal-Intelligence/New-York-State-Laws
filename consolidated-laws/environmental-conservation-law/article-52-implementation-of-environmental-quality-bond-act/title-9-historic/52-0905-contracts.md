# Environmental Conservation Law § 52-0905 — Contracts

[Environmental Conservation Law](../../README.md) › [Article 52 — Implementation of Environmental Quality Bond Act of 1986](../README.md) › [Title 9 — Historic Preservation, Municipal Park and Urban Cultural Parks Projects](README.md)

```text
§ 52-0905. Contracts.
  The commissioner shall impose such contractual requirements and
conditions upon any municipality and any not-for-profit corporation
which receive funds pursuant to this title as may be necessary and
appropriate to assure that a public benefit shall accrue from the use of
public funds by such municipality and not-for-profit corporation.  Such
conditions may include, but shall not be limited to limitations on the
right of the municipality or not-for-profit corporation to demolish or
convey such property, provisions for public access where appropriate,
the granting of facade easements to the state, a requirement that all
plans for restoration, rehabilitation, improvement, demolition or other
physical change must be subject to the commissioner's approval, and such
other conditions which shall assure the preservation and protection of
the project.
```
