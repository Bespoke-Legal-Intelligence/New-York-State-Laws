# Environmental Conservation Law § 29-0309 — Tenure of the commission

[Environmental Conservation Law](../../README.md) › [Article 29 — Low-level Radioactive Waste Facilities](../README.md) › [Title 3 — Commission For Siting Low-level Radioactive Waste Disposal Facilities](README.md)

```text
* § 29-0309. Tenure of the commission.
  The commission shall continue in existence only until final judicial
review of the department's certification of the commission's selection
pursuant to section 29-0105 of this article.
  * NB Commission existence pursuant to this section.
```
