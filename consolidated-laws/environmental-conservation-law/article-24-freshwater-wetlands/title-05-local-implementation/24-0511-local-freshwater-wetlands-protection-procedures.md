# Environmental Conservation Law § 24-0511 — Local freshwater wetlands protection procedures in the Adirondack park

[Environmental Conservation Law](../../README.md) › [Article 24 — Freshwater Wetlands](../README.md) › [Title 5 — Local Implementation](README.md)

```text
§ 24-0511. Local freshwater wetlands protection procedures in the
             Adirondack park.
  Except for section 24-0509, this title shall not apply to freshwater
wetlands protection laws or ordinances adopted by towns or villages with
respect to their territories within the Adirondack park. Such laws or
ordinances are governed by title eight of this article.
```
