# Environmental Conservation Law § 71-1717 — Examination and inspection of public works

[Environmental Conservation Law](../../README.md) › [Article 71 — Enforcement](../README.md) › [Title 17 — Enforcement of Provisions Derived From Public Health Law](README.md)

```text
§ 71-1717. Examination and inspection of public works.
  For purposes of enforcing the provisions of this chapter listed in
section 71-1701, and of titles 17 through 21 inclusive of this article,
all persons having the control, charge or custody of any public
structure, work or ground, or of any plan, description, outline, drawing
or chart thereof or relating thereto, made, kept or controlled by or
under any public authority, shall permit and facilitate the examination,
inspection and copying thereof by the commissioner, or by any person
authorized by him to make such examination or inspection of such copies.
```
