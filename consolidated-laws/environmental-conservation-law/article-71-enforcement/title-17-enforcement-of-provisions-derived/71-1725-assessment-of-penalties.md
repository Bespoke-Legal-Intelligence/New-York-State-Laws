# Environmental Conservation Law § 71-1725 — Assessment of Penalties

[Environmental Conservation Law](../../README.md) › [Article 71 — Enforcement](../README.md) › [Title 17 — Enforcement of Provisions Derived From Public Health Law](README.md)

```text
§ 71-1725. Assessment of Penalties.
  The commissioner may assess any penalty prescribed for a violation of
or a failure to comply with any provision contained in this title or
listed in section 71-1701, or any lawful notice, order or regulation
prescribed by the commissioner under any such provision, one thousand
dollars for every such violation or failure, which penalty may be
assessed after a hearing or an opportunity to be heard.
```
