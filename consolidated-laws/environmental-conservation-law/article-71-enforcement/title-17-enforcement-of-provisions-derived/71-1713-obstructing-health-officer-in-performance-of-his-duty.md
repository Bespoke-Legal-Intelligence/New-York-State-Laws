# Environmental Conservation Law § 71-1713 — Obstructing health officer in performance of his duty

[Environmental Conservation Law](../../README.md) › [Article 71 — Enforcement](../README.md) › [Title 17 — Enforcement of Provisions Derived From Public Health Law](README.md)

```text
§ 71-1713. Obstructing health officer in performance of his duty.
  A person who wilfully opposes or obstructs a health officer or
physician charged with enforcement of the health laws, in performing any
legal duty, is guilty of a misdemeanor.
```
