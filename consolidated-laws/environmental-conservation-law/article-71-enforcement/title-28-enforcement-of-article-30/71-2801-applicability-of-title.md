# Environmental Conservation Law § 71-2801 — Applicability of title

[Environmental Conservation Law](../../README.md) › [Article 71 — Enforcement](../README.md) › [Title 28 — Enforcement of Article 30](README.md)

```text
§ 71-2801. Applicability of title.
  In addition to the provisions of sections 71-0101 and 71-0301 of this
article, the provisions of this title shall be applicable to the
enforcement of article 30 of this chapter and to this title.
```
