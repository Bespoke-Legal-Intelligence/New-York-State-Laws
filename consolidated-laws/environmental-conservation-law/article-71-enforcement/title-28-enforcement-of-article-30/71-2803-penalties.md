# Environmental Conservation Law § 71-2803 — Penalties

[Environmental Conservation Law](../../README.md) › [Article 71 — Enforcement](../README.md) › [Title 28 — Enforcement of Article 30](README.md)

```text
§ 71-2803. Penalties.
  Any person who violates section 30-0103 of this chapter shall be
liable for a civil penalty not to exceed thirty-seven thousand five
hundred dollars for the first day of the violation, not to exceed
seventy-five thousand dollars for the second day of the violation, and
not to exceed one hundred fifty thousand dollars per violation
thereafter. For purposes of this section, each day in which a violation
occurs shall be considered a separate violation.
```
