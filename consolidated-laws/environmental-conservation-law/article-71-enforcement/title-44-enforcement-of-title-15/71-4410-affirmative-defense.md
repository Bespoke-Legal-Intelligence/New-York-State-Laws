# Environmental Conservation Law § 71-4410 — Affirmative defense

[Environmental Conservation Law](../../README.md) › [Article 71 — Enforcement](../README.md) › [Title 44 — Enforcement of Title 15](README.md)

```text
§ 71-4410. Affirmative defense.
  In any prosecution or proceeding pursuant to title 15 of article 27 it
is an affirmative defense that the articles being discarded have been
treated in accordance with subdivision one of section 27-1505.
```
