# Environmental Conservation Law § 71-4405 — Unlawful release of regulated medical waste in the fourth degree

[Environmental Conservation Law](../../README.md) › [Article 71 — Enforcement](../README.md) › [Title 44 — Enforcement of Title 15](README.md)

```text
§ 71-4405. Unlawful release of regulated medical waste in the fourth
             degree.
  A person is guilty of unlawful release of regulated medical waste in
the fourth degree when such person with criminal negligence, engages in
conduct which causes the release to the environment of regulated medical
waste.
  Unlawful release of regulated medical waste in the fourth degree is a
class B misdemeanor.
```
