[Environmental Conservation Law](../../README.md) › [Article 71 — Enforcement](../README.md) › [Title 31 — Enforcement of Article 35](README.md)

```text
§ 71-3101. Applicability of this title.
  In addition to the provisions of sections 71-0101 and 71-0301 the
provisions of this title shall apply to the enforcement of article 35 of
this chapter.
```
