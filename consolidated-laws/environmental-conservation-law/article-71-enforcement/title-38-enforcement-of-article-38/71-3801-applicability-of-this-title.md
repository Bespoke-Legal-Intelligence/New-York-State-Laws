# Environmental Conservation Law § 71-3801 — Applicability of this title

[Environmental Conservation Law](../../README.md) › [Article 71 — Enforcement](../README.md) › [Title 38 — Enforcement of Article 38](README.md)

```text
§ 71-3801. Applicability of this title.
  In addition to the provisions of section 71-0101 and 71-0301 of this
chapter, the provisions of this title shall apply to the enforcement of
article thirty-eight of this chapter.
```
