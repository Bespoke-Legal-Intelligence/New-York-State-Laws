# Environmental Conservation Law § 71-0523 — Power of the department to settle or compromise an action

[Environmental Conservation Law](../../README.md) › [Article 71 — Enforcement](../README.md) › [Title 5 — Enforcement of Provisions Derived From Former Conservation Law](README.md)

```text
§ 71-0523. Power of the department to settle or compromise an action.
  The department shall empowered to settle or compromise in its
discretion any action or cause of action to recover a penalty under the
provisions of this chapter listed in section 71-0501 or under titles 5
through 15 inclusive and title 33 of this article, as it may deem
advantageous to the state.
```
