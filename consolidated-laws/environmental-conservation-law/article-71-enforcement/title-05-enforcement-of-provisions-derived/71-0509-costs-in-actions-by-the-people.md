# Environmental Conservation Law § 71-0509 — Costs in actions by the people

[Environmental Conservation Law](../../README.md) › [Article 71 — Enforcement](../README.md) › [Title 5 — Enforcement of Provisions Derived From Former Conservation Law](README.md)

```text
§ 71-0509. Costs in actions by the people.
  In case of recovery of any amount in an action brought for a penalty
under the provisions of this chapter listed in section 71-0501 or under
titles 5 through 15 inclusive and title 33, of this article or any
action authorized by such law as amended, in any court of record the
people shall be entitled to recover full costs, of course, and at the
rates as provided for civil actions.
```
