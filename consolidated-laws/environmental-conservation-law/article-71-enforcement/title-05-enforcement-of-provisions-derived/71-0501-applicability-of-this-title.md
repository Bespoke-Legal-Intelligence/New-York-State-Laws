# Environmental Conservation Law § 71-0501 — Applicability of this title

[Environmental Conservation Law](../../README.md) › [Article 71 — Enforcement](../README.md) › [Title 5 — Enforcement of Provisions Derived From Former Conservation Law](README.md)

```text
§ 71-0501. Applicability of this title.
  This title shall apply to sections 3-0109 through 3-0115 inclusive and
sections 3-0305 and 3-0307 of article 3; articles 9, 11, 13 and 15;
titles 7, 9, 11 and 13 of article 21; articles 23 and 24; and articles
43, 45 and 47 of this chapter.
```
