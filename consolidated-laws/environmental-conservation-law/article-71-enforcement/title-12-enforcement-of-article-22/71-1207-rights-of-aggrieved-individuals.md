# Environmental Conservation Law § 71-1207 — Rights of aggrieved individuals

[Environmental Conservation Law](../../README.md) › [Article 71 — Enforcement](../README.md) › [Title 12 — Enforcement of Article 22](README.md)

```text
§ 71-1207. Rights of aggrieved individuals.
  The provisions of this title shall not affect, impair or in any manner
restrict or limit the rights of any person seeking damages arising out
of a violation of article twenty-two of this chapter.
```
