# Environmental Conservation Law § 71-1203 — Penalties

[Environmental Conservation Law](../../README.md) › [Article 71 — Enforcement](../README.md) › [Title 12 — Enforcement of Article 22](README.md)

```text
§ 71-1203. Penalties.
  Any person who violates the provisions of article twenty-two of this
chapter shall be subject to a civil penalty not to exceed ten thousand
dollars for each day during which such violation occurred; provided,
however, that the total penalty to be imposed shall not exceed one
million dollars.
```
