# Environmental Conservation Law § 71-2905 — Immunity of witnesses

[Environmental Conservation Law](../../README.md) › [Article 71 — Enforcement](../README.md) › [Title 29 — Enforcement of Article 33](README.md)

```text
§ 71-2905. Immunity of witnesses.
  In any investigation, hearing or inquiry, conducted pursuant to
article 33 of this chapter or this title or the rules of the department
made pursuant thereto, the commissioner, or his deputy or other officer
presiding at such investigation, hearing or inquiry, may confer immunity
in accordance with the provisions of section 50.20 of the criminal
procedure law.
```
