# Environmental Conservation Law § 71-2909 — Aiders and abettors

[Environmental Conservation Law](../../README.md) › [Article 71 — Enforcement](../README.md) › [Title 29 — Enforcement of Article 33](README.md)

```text
§ 71-2909. Aiders and abettors.
  Any person knowingly aiding or abetting any other person in the
violation of any provision of article 33 of this chapter or any rule,
regulation, or order of the department made pursuant thereto shall be
subject to the same punishment and penalty as that prescribed by this
title for the violation by such other person.
```
