# Environmental Conservation Law § 71-0707 — Resisting or obstructing departmental agent or employee

[Environmental Conservation Law](../../README.md) › [Article 71 — Enforcement](../README.md) › [Title 7 — Enforcement of Article 9](README.md)

```text
§ 71-0707. Resisting or obstructing departmental agent or employee.
  Any person who resists or obstructs an authorized agent or employee of
the department while he is engaged in carrying out any provision of
section 9-0305 shall be guilty of a violation which shall be punishable
by a fine not exceeding one hundred dollars and by an additional fine of
not exceeding twenty-five dollars for each additional day of such
resistance or obstruction.
```
