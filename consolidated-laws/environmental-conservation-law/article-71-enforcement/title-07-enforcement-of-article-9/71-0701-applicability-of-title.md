# Environmental Conservation Law § 71-0701 — Applicability of title

[Environmental Conservation Law](../../README.md) › [Article 71 — Enforcement](../README.md) › [Title 7 — Enforcement of Article 9](README.md)

```text
§ 71-0701. Applicability of title.
  In addition to the provisions of sections 71-0101 and 71-0301, and
titles 5 and 40 of this article, the provisions of this title shall be
applicable to the enforcement of article 9 of this chapter.
```
