# Environmental Conservation Law § 71-1501 — Applicability of this title

[Environmental Conservation Law](../../README.md) › [Article 71 — Enforcement](../README.md) › [Title 15 — Enforcement of Title 25 of Article 27](README.md)

```text
§ 71-1501. Applicability of this title.
  In addition to the provisions of sections 71-0101 and 71-0301 of this
article, the provisions of this title shall apply to the enforcement of
title 25 of article 27 of this chapter.
```
