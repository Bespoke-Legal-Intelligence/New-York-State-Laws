# Environmental Conservation Law § 71-1907 — Enforcement of section 17-1707

[Environmental Conservation Law](../../README.md) › [Article 71 — Enforcement](../README.md) › [Title 19 — Enforcement of Titles 1 Through 11 and 15 Through 21 Inclusive of Article 17 and Spills of Bulk Liquids](README.md)

```text
§ 71-1907. Enforcement of section 17-1707.
  1. Every person violating any provision of section 17-1707 shall
forfeit to the municipality having a local board of health where the
violation occurs the sum of twenty-five dollars for the first day when
the violation takes place, and the sum of ten dollars for every
subsequent day that such violation is repeated or continued.
  2. The board of health of any such municipality shall examine into any
alleged offense against section 17-1707 and cause the same to be abated,
if found to exist.
```
