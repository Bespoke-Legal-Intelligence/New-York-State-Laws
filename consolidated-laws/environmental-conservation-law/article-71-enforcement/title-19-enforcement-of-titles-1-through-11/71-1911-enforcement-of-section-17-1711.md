# Environmental Conservation Law § 71-1911 — Enforcement of section 17-1711

[Environmental Conservation Law](../../README.md) › [Article 71 — Enforcement](../README.md) › [Title 19 — Enforcement of Titles 1 Through 11 and 15 Through 21 Inclusive of Article 17 and Spills of Bulk Liquids](README.md)

```text
§  71-1911. Enforcement of section 17-1711.
  Any person violating any provision of section 17-1711 shall be guilty
of an offense, and punishable by a fine of not more than seventy-five
dollars.
```
