# Environmental Conservation Law § 71-1917 — Approval and enforcement of orders issued under section 17-1717

[Environmental Conservation Law](../../README.md) › [Article 71 — Enforcement](../README.md) › [Title 19 — Enforcement of Titles 1 Through 11 and 15 Through 21 Inclusive of Article 17 and Spills of Bulk Liquids](README.md)

```text
§ 71-1917. Approval and enforcement of orders issued under section
             17-1717.
  An order issued under section 17-1717 shall not be valid until
approved by the Governor and the Attorney General, and when so approved
it shall be the duty of the Attorney General to enforce such order.
```
