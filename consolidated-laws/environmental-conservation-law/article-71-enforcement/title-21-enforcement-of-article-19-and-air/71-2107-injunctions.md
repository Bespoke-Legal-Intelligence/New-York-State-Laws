# Environmental Conservation Law § 71-2107 — Injunctions

[Environmental Conservation Law](../../README.md) › [Article 71 — Enforcement](../README.md) › [Title 21 — Enforcement of Article 19 and Air Pollution Emergency Rules and Regulations](README.md)

```text
§ 71-2107. Injunctions.
  It shall be the duty of the attorney general upon the request of the
commissioner to bring an action for an injunction against any person
violating a provision of article nineteen; any code, rule or regulation
promulgated pursuant thereto; or any order of the commissioner issued
pursuant thereto; provided, however, that the commissioner shall furnish
the attorney general with such material, evidentiary matter, or proof as
may be requested by the attorney general for the prosecution of such
action. In any action for injunction brought pursuant to this title, any
finding of the commissioner shall be prima facie evidence of the fact or
facts found therein.
```
