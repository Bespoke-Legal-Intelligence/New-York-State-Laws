# Environmental Conservation Law § 71-2101 — Applicability of this title

[Environmental Conservation Law](../../README.md) › [Article 71 — Enforcement](../README.md) › [Title 21 — Enforcement of Article 19 and Air Pollution Emergency Rules and Regulations](README.md)

```text
§ 71-2101. Applicability of this title.
  In addition to the provisions of section 71-0101 and 71-0301 and title
17 of this article, the provisions of this title shall be applicable to
the enforcement of article 19.
```
