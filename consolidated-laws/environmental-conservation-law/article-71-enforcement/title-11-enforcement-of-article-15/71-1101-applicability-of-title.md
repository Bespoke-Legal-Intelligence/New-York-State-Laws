# Environmental Conservation Law § 71-1101 — Applicability of title

[Environmental Conservation Law](../../README.md) › [Article 71 — Enforcement](../README.md) › [Title 11 — Enforcement of Article 15](README.md)

```text
§ 71-1101. Applicability of title.
  In addition to the provisions of sections 71-0101 and 71-0301 and
title 5 of this article, the provisions of this title shall be
applicable to the enforcement of article 15.
```
