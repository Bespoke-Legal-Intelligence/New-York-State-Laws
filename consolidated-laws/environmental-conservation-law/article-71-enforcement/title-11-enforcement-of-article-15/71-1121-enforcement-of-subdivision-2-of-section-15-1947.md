# Environmental Conservation Law § 71-1121 — Enforcement of subdivision 2 of section 15-1947

[Environmental Conservation Law](../../README.md) › [Article 71 — Enforcement](../README.md) › [Title 11 — Enforcement of Article 15](README.md)

```text
§ 71-1121. Enforcement of subdivision 2 of section 15-1947.
  Violation of subdivision 2 of section 15-1947 shall constitute a
violation, punishable by a fine of not more than one thousand dollars,
and in addition thereto, a civil penalty of not more than fifteen
hundred dollars.
```
