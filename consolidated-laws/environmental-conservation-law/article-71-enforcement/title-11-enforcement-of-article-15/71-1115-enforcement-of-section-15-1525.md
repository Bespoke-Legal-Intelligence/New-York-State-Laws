# Environmental Conservation Law § 71-1115 — Enforcement of section 15-1525

[Environmental Conservation Law](../../README.md) › [Article 71 — Enforcement](../README.md) › [Title 11 — Enforcement of Article 15](README.md)

```text
§ 71-1115. Enforcement of section 15-1525.
  Any person violating the provisions of section 15-1525 shall be guilty
of a violation punishable by a fine of not more than one thousand
dollars, and in addition thereto, shall be liable for a civil penalty of
not more than fifteen hundred dollars.
```
