# Environmental Conservation Law § 71-1129 — Emergencies excepted

[Environmental Conservation Law](../../README.md) › [Article 71 — Enforcement](../README.md) › [Title 11 — Enforcement of Article 15](README.md)

```text
§ 71-1129. Emergencies excepted.
  The civil liabilities imposed upon persons violating the provisions of
article 15, except section 15-1713, shall not be construed to include
any violation which was caused by an act of God, war, strike, riot or
other catastrophe as to which negligence or willful misconduct on the
part of such person was not the proximate cause.
```
