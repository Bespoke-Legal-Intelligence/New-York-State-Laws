# Environmental Conservation Law § 71-2701 — Applicability of title

[Environmental Conservation Law](../../README.md) › [Article 71 — Enforcement](../README.md) › [Title 27 — Enforcement of Article 27 and Article 71](README.md)

```text
§ 71-2701. Applicability of title.
  In addition to the provisions of sections 71-0101 and 71-0301 of this
article, the provisions of this title shall be applicable to the
enforcement of article 27 and to this title.
```
