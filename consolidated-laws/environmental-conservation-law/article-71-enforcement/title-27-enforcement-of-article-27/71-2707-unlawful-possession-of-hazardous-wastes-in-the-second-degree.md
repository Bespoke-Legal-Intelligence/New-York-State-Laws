# Environmental Conservation Law § 71-2707 — Unlawful possession of hazardous wastes in the second degree

[Environmental Conservation Law](../../README.md) › [Article 71 — Enforcement](../README.md) › [Title 27 — Enforcement of Article 27 and Article 71](README.md)

```text
§ 71-2707. Unlawful possession of hazardous wastes in the second degree.
  No person shall:
  1. Knowingly possess more than one hundred gallons or one thousand
pounds, whichever is less, of an aggregate weight or volume of hazardous
wastes at a place other than the site of generation; or
  2. Recklessly possess more than two hundred gallons or two thousand
pounds, whichever is less, of an aggregate weight or volume of hazardous
wastes at a place other than the site of generation.
  Unlawful possession of hazardous wastes in the second degree is a
class E felony.
```
