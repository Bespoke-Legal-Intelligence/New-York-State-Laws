# Environmental Conservation Law § 71-2307 — Abatement of pollution

[Environmental Conservation Law](../../README.md) › [Article 71 — Enforcement](../README.md) › [Title 23 — Enforcement of Article 24](README.md)

```text
§ 71-2307. Abatement of pollution.
  Where any freshwater wetlands have been damaged or endangered by
pollution or are subject to pollution, the commissioner shall take all
appropriate action to abate the pollution. The commissioner may restrict
or order cessation of solid waste disposal, deep well disposal, or
liquid waste disposal where such is polluting a given freshwater
wetland.
```
