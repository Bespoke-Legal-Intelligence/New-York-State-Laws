# Environmental Conservation Law § 71-2301 — Applicability of title

[Environmental Conservation Law](../../README.md) › [Article 71 — Enforcement](../README.md) › [Title 23 — Enforcement of Article 24](README.md)

```text
§ 71-2301. Applicability of title.
  In addition to the provisions of sections 71-0101, 71-0301 and title
five of this article, the provisions of this title shall be applicable
to the enforcement of article twenty-four of this chapter.
```
