# Environmental Conservation Law § 71-1301 — Applicability of this title

[Environmental Conservation Law](../../README.md) › [Article 71 — Enforcement](../README.md) › [Title 13 — Enforcement of Article 23](README.md)

```text
§ 71-1301. Applicability of this title.
  In addition to the provisions of sections 71-0101 and 71-0301 of this
article, the provisions of this title shall be applicable to the
enforcement of article 23 of this chapter except as otherwise provided
in article 23 of this chapter.
```
