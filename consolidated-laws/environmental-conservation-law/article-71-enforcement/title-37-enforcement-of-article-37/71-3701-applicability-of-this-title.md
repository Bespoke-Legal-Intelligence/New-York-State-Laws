# Environmental Conservation Law § 71-3701 — Applicability of this title

[Environmental Conservation Law](../../README.md) › [Article 71 — Enforcement](../README.md) › [Title 37 — Enforcement of Article 37](README.md)

```text
§ 71-3701. Applicability of this title.
  In addition to the provisions of sections 71-0101 and 71-0301 of this
article, the provisions of this title shall apply to the enforcement of
article 37 of this chapter.
```
