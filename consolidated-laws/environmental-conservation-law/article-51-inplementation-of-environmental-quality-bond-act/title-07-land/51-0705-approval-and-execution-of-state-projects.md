# Environmental Conservation Law § 51-0705 — Approval and execution of state projects

[Environmental Conservation Law](../../README.md) › [Article 51 — Inplementation of Environmental Quality Bond Act of 1972](../README.md) › [Title 7 — Land Preservation and Improvement Projects](README.md)

```text
§ 51-0705. Approval and execution of state projects.
  1. State forest preserve, stream rights acquisition, wetlands
preservation or restoration, unique area preservation, and public access
to state lands projects shall be undertaken pursuant to this title only
with the approval of the commissioner.
  2. Such projects as have been approved by the commissioner shall be
executed by the department in the manner provided for by applicable law.
```
