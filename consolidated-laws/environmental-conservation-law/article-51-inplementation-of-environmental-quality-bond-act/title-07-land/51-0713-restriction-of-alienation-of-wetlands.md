# Environmental Conservation Law § 51-0713 — Restriction of alienation of wetlands

[Environmental Conservation Law](../../README.md) › [Article 51 — Inplementation of Environmental Quality Bond Act of 1972](../README.md) › [Title 7 — Land Preservation and Improvement Projects](README.md)

```text
§ 51-0713. Restriction of alienation of wetlands.
  Any wetlands acquired or restored in part or in whole with state
moneys pursuant to this title shall not be sold, leased or otherwise
disposed of or used for any purpose inconsistent with the character or
value of such wetlands.
  No structure shall be placed thereon except water level regulation
works necessary to preserve, restore or maintain the biological
productivity thereof.
```
