# Environmental Conservation Law § 51-1105 — Approval and execution of projects

[Environmental Conservation Law](../../README.md) › [Article 51 — Inplementation of Environmental Quality Bond Act of 1972](../README.md) › [Title 11 — Park Lands Preservation Projects](README.md)

```text
§ 51-1105. Approval and execution of projects.
  1. Metropolitan park, open space preservation, waterway, and trailway
projects shall be undertaken pursuant to this title only with the
approval of the commissioner.
  2. Such projects as have been approved by the commissioner shall be
executed by the office in the manner provided by applicable law.
```
