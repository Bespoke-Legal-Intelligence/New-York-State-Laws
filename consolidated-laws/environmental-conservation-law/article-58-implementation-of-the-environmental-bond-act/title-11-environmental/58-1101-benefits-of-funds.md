# Environmental Conservation Law § 58-1101 — Benefits of funds

[Environmental Conservation Law](../../README.md) › [Article 58 — Implementation of the Environmental Bond Act of 2022 "restore Mother Nature"](../README.md) › [Title 11 — Environmental Justice and Reporting](README.md)

```text
* § 58-1101. Benefits of funds.
  The department shall make every effort practicable to ensure that
thirty-five percent of the funds pursuant to this article benefit
environmental justice communities.
  * NB Takes effect only if section 1 of part TT of chapter 59 of the
laws of 2021 enacting the environmental bond act of 2022 "restore mother
nature" is submitted to the people at the general election to be held in
November 2022 and is approved by the majority of votes
```
