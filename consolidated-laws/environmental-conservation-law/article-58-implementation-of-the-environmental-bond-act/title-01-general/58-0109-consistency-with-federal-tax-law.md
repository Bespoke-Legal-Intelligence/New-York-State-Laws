# Environmental Conservation Law § 58-0109 — Consistency with federal tax law

[Environmental Conservation Law](../../README.md) › [Article 58 — Implementation of the Environmental Bond Act of 2020 "restore Mother Nature"](../README.md) › [Title 1 — General Provisions](README.md)

```text
* § 58-0109. Consistency with federal tax law.
  All actions undertaken pursuant to this article shall be reviewed for
consistency with provisions of the federal internal revenue code and
regulations thereunder, in accordance with procedures established in
connection with the issuance of any tax exempt bonds pursuant to this
article, to preserve the tax exempt status of such bonds.
  * NB Effective upon a vote of the people in a general election held in
November 2020 approving the environmental bond act of 2020 set forth in
chapter 58 of 2020 Part QQ § 1
```
