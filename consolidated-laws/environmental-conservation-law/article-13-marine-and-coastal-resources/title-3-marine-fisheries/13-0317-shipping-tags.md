# Environmental Conservation Law § 13-0317 — Shipping tags

[Environmental Conservation Law](../../README.md) › [Article 13 — Marine and Coastal Resources](../README.md) › [Title 3 — Marine Fisheries](README.md)

```text
§ 13-0317. Shipping tags.
  All persons transporting, importing, exporting or otherwise
distributing shellfish shall label or tag the same as provided under
section 13-0319.
```
