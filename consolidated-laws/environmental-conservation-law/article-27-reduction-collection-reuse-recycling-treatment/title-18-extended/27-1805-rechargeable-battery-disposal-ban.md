# Environmental Conservation Law § 27-1805 — Rechargeable battery disposal ban

[Environmental Conservation Law](../../README.md) › [Article 27 — Reduction, Collection, Reuse, Recycling, Treatment and Disposal of Solid Waste](../README.md) › [Title 18 — Extended Producer Responsibility For Rechargeable Batteries](README.md)

```text
§ 27-1805. Rechargeable battery disposal ban.
  No person shall knowingly dispose of rechargeable batteries as solid
waste at any time in the state.
```
