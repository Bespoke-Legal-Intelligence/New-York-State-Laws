# Environmental Conservation Law § 27-0914 — Unauthorized possession, disposal and dealing in hazardous wastes

[Environmental Conservation Law](../../README.md) › [Article 27 — Reduction, Collection, Reuse, Recycling, Treatment and Disposal of Solid Waste](../README.md) › [Title 9 — Industrial Hazardous Waste Management](README.md)

```text
§ 27-0914. Unauthorized possession, disposal and dealing in hazardous
             wastes.
  1. No person shall possess hazardous wastes without authorization.
  2. No person shall dispose of hazardous wastes without authorization.
  3. No person shall deal in hazardous wastes without authorization.
```
