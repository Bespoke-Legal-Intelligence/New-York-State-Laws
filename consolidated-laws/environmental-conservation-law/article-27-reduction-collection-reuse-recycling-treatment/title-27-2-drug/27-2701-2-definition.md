# Environmental Conservation Law § 27-2701*2 — Definition

[Environmental Conservation Law](../../README.md) › [Article 27 — Reduction, Collection, Reuse, Recycling, Treatment and Disposal of Solid Waste](../README.md) › [Title 27*2 — Drug Management and Disposal](README.md)

*An asterisk in a number (for example Article 24\* or § 20-C\*2) is the Senate's mark for a number the Legislature has used more than once: the marked article or section is a different one from the unmarked one with the same number. \*2 means the second one enacted with that number.*

```text
* § 27-2701. Definition.
  As used in this title, "drug" means any drug, as defined in
subdivision seven of section sixty-eight hundred two of the education
law.
  * NB There are 2 § 27-2701's
```
