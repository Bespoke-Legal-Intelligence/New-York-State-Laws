# Environmental Conservation Law § 27-3207 — Preemption of local law

[Environmental Conservation Law](../../README.md) › [Article 27 — Reduction, Collection, Reuse, Recycling, Treatment and Disposal of Solid Waste](../README.md) › [Title 32 — Small Plastic Bottle Hospitality Personal Care Product Restrictions](README.md)

```text
§ 27-3207. Preemption of local law.
  Jurisdiction in all matters pertaining to restrictions on the
provision of small plastic bottle hospitality personal care products is
vested exclusively in the state.
```
