# Environmental Conservation Law § 27-2711 — Regulations

[Environmental Conservation Law](../../README.md) › [Article 27 — Reduction, Collection, Reuse, Recycling, Treatment and Disposal of Solid Waste](../README.md) › [Title 27 — Plastic Bag Reduction, Reuse and Recycling](README.md)

```text
§ 27-2711. Regulations.
  The department is authorized to promulgate any rules and regulations
necessary to implement the provisions of this title and shall
promulgate, and update the most recent version of, rules and regulations
pertaining to the definition of compostable as prescribed in American
Society for Testing and Materials Standard D6400.
```
