# Environmental Conservation Law § 49-0109 — Reports

[Environmental Conservation Law](../../README.md) › [Article 49 — Protection of Natural and Man-made Beauty](../README.md) › [Title 1 — Natural and Man Made Beauty](README.md)

```text
§ 49-0109. Reports.
  The department shall from time to time report to the Governor, and
shall make an annual report to the governor and the legislature, not
later than May first, concerning the work of the department pursuant to
this article, in the preceding calendar year.
```
