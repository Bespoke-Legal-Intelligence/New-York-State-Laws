# Environmental Conservation Law § 3-0119 — Laboratory tests

[Environmental Conservation Law](../../README.md) › [Article 3 — Department of Environmental Conservation; General Functions, Powers, Duties and Jurisdiction](../README.md) › [Title 1 — Department of Environmental Conservation](README.md)

```text
§ 3-0119. Laboratory tests.
  Any laboratory tests or sample analysis required pursuant to article
seventeen, nineteen or twenty-seven of this chapter for which the
commissioner of health issues certificates of approval pursuant to
section five hundred two of the public health law shall be conducted by
a laboratory which has been issued a certificate of approval.
```
