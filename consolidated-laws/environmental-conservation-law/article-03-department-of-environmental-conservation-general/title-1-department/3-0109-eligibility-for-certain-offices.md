# Environmental Conservation Law § 3-0109 — Eligibility for certain offices

[Environmental Conservation Law](../../README.md) › [Article 3 — Department of Environmental Conservation; General Functions, Powers, Duties and Jurisdiction](../README.md) › [Title 1 — Department of Environmental Conservation](README.md)

```text
§ 3-0109. Eligibility for certain offices.
  No person shall be eligible for or shall continue to hold the office
of commissioner, deputy commissioner, secretary to the department, if
any there be, or head of a division who is engaged in the business of
lumbering in any forest preserve county or who is engaged in any
business in the prosecution of which hydraulic power is used or in which
water is distributed or sold under any public franchise or who is an
officer or holder of stocks or bonds of any corporation engaged in such
business within the state.
```
