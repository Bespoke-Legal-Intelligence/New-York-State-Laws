# Environmental Conservation Law § 3-0311 — Municipal notification of environmental violations

[Environmental Conservation Law](../../README.md) › [Article 3 — Department of Environmental Conservation; General Functions, Powers, Duties and Jurisdiction](../README.md) › [Title 3 — General Functions, Powers, Duties and Jurisdiction](README.md)

```text
* § 3-0311. Municipal notification of environmental violations.
  Notwithstanding any other provision of law and except when any
criminal investigation is pending, the commissioner shall notify the
chief executive officer of the municipality together with the chief
executive officer of the county where any violations of article 17, 19,
24, 25 or 27 of this chapter are found to exist within the boundaries of
such municipalities including, but not limited to, any municipal
landfill or municipal solid waste disposal or recovery facility within
seventy-two hours of determining that sufficient evidence exists to
commence an enforcement action.
```
