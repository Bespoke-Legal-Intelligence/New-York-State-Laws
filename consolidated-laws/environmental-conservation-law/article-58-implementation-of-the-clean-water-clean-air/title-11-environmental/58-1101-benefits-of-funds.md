# Environmental Conservation Law § 58-1101 — Benefits of funds

[Environmental Conservation Law](../../README.md) › [Article 58 — Implementation of the "clean Water, Clean Air, and Green Jobs Environmental Bond Act of 2022 "](../README.md) › [Title 11 — Environmental Justice and Reporting](README.md)

```text
§ 58-1101. Benefits of funds.
  The department shall make every effort practicable to achieve a goal
that forty percent of the funds pursuant to this article benefit
disadvantaged communities; however, disadvantaged communities shall
receive no less than thirty-five percent of the benefit of the funds
pursuant to this article.
```
