# Environmental Conservation Law § 37-0215 — Rules and regulations

[Environmental Conservation Law](../../README.md) › [Article 37 — Substances Hazardous or Acutely Hazardous to Public Health, Safety or the Environment](../README.md) › [Title 2 — Hazardous Packaging](README.md)

```text
§ 37-0215. Rules and regulations.
  The commissioner shall have the power to adopt such rules and
regulations to provide for the effective application and enforcement of
this title. The commissioner, to the extent practicable and appropriate,
shall utilize the standards designated by the American Society for
Testing and Materials in determining "unit packaging", "intermediate
packages" and "shipping containers".
```
