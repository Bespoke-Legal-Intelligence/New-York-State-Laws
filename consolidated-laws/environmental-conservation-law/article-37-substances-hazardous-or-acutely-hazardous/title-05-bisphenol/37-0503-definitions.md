# Environmental Conservation Law § 37-0503 — Definitions

[Environmental Conservation Law](../../README.md) › [Article 37 — Substances Hazardous or Acutely Hazardous to Public Health, Safety or the Environment](../README.md) › [Title 5 — Bisphenol a](README.md)

```text
§ 37-0503. Definitions.
  As used in this title, "child care product" means all pacifiers and
unfilled beverage containers to be used by children under three years
old for the consumption of liquids including pacifiers, baby bottles,
baby bottle liners and cups, cup lids, straws and sippy cups.
```
