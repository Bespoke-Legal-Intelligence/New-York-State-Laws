# Environmental Conservation Law § 37-0119*2 — Coal tar; prohibited

[Environmental Conservation Law](../../README.md) › [Article 37 — Substances Hazardous or Acutely Hazardous to Public Health, Safety or the Environment](../README.md) › [Title 1 — Substances Hazardous to the Environment](README.md)

*An asterisk in a number (for example Article 24\* or § 20-C\*2) is the Senate's mark for a number the Legislature has used more than once: the marked article or section is a different one from the unmarked one with the same number. \*2 means the second one enacted with that number.*

```text
* § 37-0119. Coal tar; prohibited.
  1. A person shall not sell or offer for sale a pavement product
containing coal tar.
  ** 2. A person shall not apply a pavement product that is labeled as
containing coal tar on a driveway or parking area.
  ** NB Effective November 8, 2023
  3. For purposes of this section, the following terms shall have the
following meanings:
  a. "Coal tar" means a viscous substance obtained by the destructive
distillation of coal and containing levels of polycyclic aromatic
hydrocarbons (PAHs) in excess of ten thousand milligrams per kilogram.
  b. "Pavement product" means a material that is for use on an asphalt
or concrete surface, including but not limited to sealcoat.
  * NB There are 2 § 37-0119's
```
