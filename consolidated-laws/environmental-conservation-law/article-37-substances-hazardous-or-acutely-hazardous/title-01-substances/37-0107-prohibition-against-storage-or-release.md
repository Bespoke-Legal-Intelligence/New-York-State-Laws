# Environmental Conservation Law § 37-0107 — Prohibition against storage or release

[Environmental Conservation Law](../../README.md) › [Article 37 — Substances Hazardous or Acutely Hazardous to Public Health, Safety or the Environment](../README.md) › [Title 1 — Substances Hazardous to the Environment](README.md)

```text
§ 37-0107. Prohibition against storage or release.
  No person shall store or release to the environment substances
hazardous or acutely hazardous to public health, safety or the
environment in contravention of rules and regulations promulgated
pursuant hereto.
```
