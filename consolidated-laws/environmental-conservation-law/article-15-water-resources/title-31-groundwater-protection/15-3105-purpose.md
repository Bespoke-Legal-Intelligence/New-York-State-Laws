# Environmental Conservation Law § 15-3105 — Purpose

[Environmental Conservation Law](../../README.md) › [Article 15 — Water Resources](../README.md) › [Title 31 — Groundwater Protection and Remediation Program](README.md)

```text
§ 15-3105. Purpose.
  It is the intent of the legislature that the department develop a
strategy to address contaminated groundwater and implement a program to
remediate and manage groundwater resources in a manner that will ensure
long-term sustainability.
```
