# Environmental Conservation Law § 15-2719 — Cooperation clause

[Environmental Conservation Law](../../README.md) › [Article 15 — Water Resources](../README.md) › [Title 27 — Wild, Scenic and Recreational Rivers System](README.md)

```text
§ 15-2719. Cooperation clause.
  The commissioner and agency shall cooperate with each other and
coordinate their activities with respect to their respective
jurisdictions over the designated rivers within the Adirondack park to
ensure compliance for the general criteria and management objectives set
forth in this part.
```
