# Environmental Conservation Law § 15-2127 — Error in names

[Environmental Conservation Law](../../README.md) › [Article 15 — Water Resources](../README.md) › [Title 21 — River Regulation By Storage Reservoirs](README.md)

```text
§ 15-2127. Error in names.
  No error in the names of the owners of real estate, or in the
descriptions thereof, shall invalidate such apportionment or the levying
of assessments or taxes based thereon if sufficient description is given
to identify such real estate, and the owners thereof.
```
