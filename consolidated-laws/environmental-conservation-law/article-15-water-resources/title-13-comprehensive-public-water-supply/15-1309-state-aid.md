# Environmental Conservation Law § 15-1309 — State aid

[Environmental Conservation Law](../../README.md) › [Article 15 — Water Resources](../README.md) › [Title 13 — Comprehensive Public Water Supply Studies and Reports](README.md)

```text
§ 15-1309. State aid.
  The state Commissioner of Health, may, in the name of the state, make
or contract to make, within appropriations therefor, a state grant for
payment during any of the ten successive fiscal years of the state,
beginning with its fiscal year commencing April 1, 1964, to a local
agency to cover the entire cost, as determined by him, of the
preparation of a comprehensive study and report.
```
