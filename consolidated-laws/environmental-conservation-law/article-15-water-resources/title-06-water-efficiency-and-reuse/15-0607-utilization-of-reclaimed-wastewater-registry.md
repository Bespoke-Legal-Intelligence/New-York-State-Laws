# Environmental Conservation Law § 15-0607 — Utilization of reclaimed wastewater registry

[Environmental Conservation Law](../../README.md) › [Article 15 — Water Resources](../README.md) › [Title 6 — Water Efficiency and Reuse](README.md)

```text
§ 15-0607. Utilization of reclaimed wastewater registry.
  All persons utilizing reclaimed wastewater or greywater shall register
such project with the department. The department shall maintain such
registry.
```
