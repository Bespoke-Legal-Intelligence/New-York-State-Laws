# Environmental Conservation Law § 15-0909 — Payment of costs

[Environmental Conservation Law](../../README.md) › [Article 15 — Water Resources](../README.md) › [Title 9 — Administrative Procedures For Article 15](README.md)

```text
§ 15-0909. Payment of costs.
  The expense of any hearing initiated by petition shall be certified by
the department to the person or public corporation filing such petition,
and shall be paid by such petitioner within thirty days after the
receipt of the certificate.
```
