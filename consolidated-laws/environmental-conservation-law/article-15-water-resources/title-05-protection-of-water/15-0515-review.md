# Environmental Conservation Law § 15-0515 — Review

[Environmental Conservation Law](../../README.md) › [Article 15 — Water Resources](../README.md) › [Title 5 — Protection of Water](README.md)

```text
§ 15-0515. Review.
  A determination, order or issuance, modification or denial of a
permit, pursuant to sections 15-0501, 15-0503 and 15-0505 shall be
reviewable in a proceeding pursuant to article 78 of the Civil Practice
Law and Rules.
```
