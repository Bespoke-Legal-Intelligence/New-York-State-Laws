# Environmental Conservation Law § 15-1949 — Removal of beaver

[Environmental Conservation Law](../../README.md) › [Article 15 — Water Resources](../README.md) › [Title 19 — Drainage](README.md)

```text
§ 15-1949. Removal of beaver.
  In the event that any drainage improvement district is inhabited by or
is invaded by beaver and the work of those animals interferes with the
proper operation of the drainage system, the commissioner may cause such
beaver to be removed or destroyed as he shall determine.
```
