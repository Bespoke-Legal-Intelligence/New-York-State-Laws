# Environmental Conservation Law § 15-1953 — Drainage improvement district fund

[Environmental Conservation Law](../../README.md) › [Article 15 — Water Resources](../README.md) › [Title 19 — Drainage](README.md)

```text
§ 15-1953. Drainage improvement district fund.
  Moneys received under the provisions of title 19 of this article shall
constitute a fund to be known as the drainage improvement district fund,
and the portion thereof applicable to each improvement shall be
separately kept by the Comptroller, and the Comptroller is authorized
and directed to pay therefrom the principal and interest of such
obligations as the same mature and become payable by the terms thereof,
and the costs and expenses of such improvement and the maintenance
thereof upon the order of the department or the authorized officer
thereof.
```
