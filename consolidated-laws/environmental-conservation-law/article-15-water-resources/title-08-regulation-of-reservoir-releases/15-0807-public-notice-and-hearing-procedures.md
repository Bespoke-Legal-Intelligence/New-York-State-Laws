# Environmental Conservation Law § 15-0807 — Public notice and hearing procedures

[Environmental Conservation Law](../../README.md) › [Article 15 — Water Resources](../README.md) › [Title 8 — Regulation of Reservoir Releases](README.md)

```text
§ 15-0807. Public notice and hearing procedures.
  The promulgation of rules and regulations pursuant to this title shall
be exclusively governed by the rule-making procedures of the state
administrative procedure act, except that municipalities affected by the
releases which are the subject of such rules and regulations shall also
be provided an opportunity for consultation during the preparation of
such rules and regulations.
```
