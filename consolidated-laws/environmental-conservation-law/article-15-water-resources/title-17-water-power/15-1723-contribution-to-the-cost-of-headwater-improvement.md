# Environmental Conservation Law § 15-1723 — Contribution to the cost of headwater improvement

[Environmental Conservation Law](../../README.md) › [Article 15 — Water Resources](../README.md) › [Title 17 — Water Power](README.md)

```text
§ 15-1723. Contribution to the cost of headwater improvement.
  Licensees and persons exercising rights conferred by title 17 of this
article shall be liable for any payments or charges resulting from the
improvement of headwaters under the provisions of title 21 and title 23
of this article, according to their respective interests in the property
benefited.
```
