# Environmental Conservation Law § 15-1751 — Right to amend and repeal reserved

[Environmental Conservation Law](../../README.md) › [Article 15 — Water Resources](../README.md) › [Title 17 — Water Power](README.md)

```text
§ 15-1751. Right to amend and repeal reserved.
  The right to alter, amend or repeal title 17 of this article is
expressly reserved, but substantial rights acquired pursuant to title 17
of this article shall not be lost or impaired by such repeal or
amendment.
```
