# Environmental Conservation Law § 15-1747 — False entries, statements or reports

[Environmental Conservation Law](../../README.md) › [Article 15 — Water Resources](../README.md) › [Title 17 — Water Power](README.md)

```text
§ 15-1747. False entries, statements or reports.
  No person shall for the purpose of deceiving, make or cause to be made
any false entry in the books of accounts of a licensee, or for the
purpose of deceiving, make or cause to be made any false statement or
report in response to a request, order or direction from the department
for statements or reports under title 17 of this article.
```
