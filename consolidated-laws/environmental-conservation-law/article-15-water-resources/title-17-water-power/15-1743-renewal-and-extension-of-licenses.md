# Environmental Conservation Law § 15-1743 — Renewal and extension of licenses

[Environmental Conservation Law](../../README.md) › [Article 15 — Water Resources](../README.md) › [Title 17 — Water Power](README.md)

```text
§ 15-1743. Renewal and extension of licenses.
  The department may renew an expiring license to the same licensee on
the same or different terms, or may renew the privilege from year to
year under such terms as may be agreed upon.
```
