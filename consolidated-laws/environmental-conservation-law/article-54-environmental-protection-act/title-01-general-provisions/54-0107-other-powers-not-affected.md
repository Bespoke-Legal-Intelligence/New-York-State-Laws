# Environmental Conservation Law § 54-0107 — Other powers not affected

[Environmental Conservation Law](../../README.md) › [Article 54 — Environmental Protection Act](../README.md) › [Title 1 — General Provisions](README.md)

```text
§ 54-0107. Other powers not affected.
  Nothing in this article shall be construed to limit or restrict any
powers of the commissioner or any other agency pursuant to any other
provision of law.
```
