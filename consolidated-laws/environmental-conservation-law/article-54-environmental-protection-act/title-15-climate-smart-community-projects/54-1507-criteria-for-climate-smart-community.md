# Environmental Conservation Law § 54-1507 — Criteria for climate smart community projects

[Environmental Conservation Law](../../README.md) › [Article 54 — Environmental Protection Act](../README.md) › [Title 15 — Climate Smart Community Projects](README.md)

```text
§ 54-1507. Criteria for climate smart community projects.
  Costs incurred prior to April first, two thousand sixteen, shall not
be eligible for state assistance payments funded pursuant to this title.
Projects must demonstrate an ability to identify, mitigate and/or adapt
to climate change vulnerability and risk or demonstrate potential to
reduce greenhouse gas emissions outside the power sector.
```
