# Environmental Conservation Law § 54-1505 — Eligibility to receive state assistance payments for climate smart community projects

[Environmental Conservation Law](../../README.md) › [Article 54 — Environmental Protection Act](../README.md) › [Title 15 — Climate Smart Community Projects](README.md)

```text
§ 54-1505. Eligibility to receive state assistance payments for climate
             smart community projects.
  Any municipality may apply for state assistance payments toward the
cost of a climate smart community project. Any application must comply
with all applicable rules and regulations determined by the department.
```
