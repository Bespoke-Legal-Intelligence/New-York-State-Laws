# Environmental Conservation Law § 54-1111 — State projects

[Environmental Conservation Law](../../README.md) › [Article 54 — Environmental Protection Act](../README.md) › [Title 11 — Local Waterfront Revitalization Plans and Coastal Rehabilitation Projects](README.md)

```text
§ 54-1111. State projects.
  The commissioner is authorized, within amounts appropriated, to
directly undertake a coastal rehabilitation project pursuant to this
title in accordance with section 1 of chapter 535 of the laws of 1945,
as amended, or as specifically appropriated, on state lands or state
lands underwater.
```
