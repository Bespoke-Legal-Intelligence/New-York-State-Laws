# Environmental Conservation Law § 54-0301 — Definitions

[Environmental Conservation Law](../../README.md) › [Article 54 — Environmental Protection Act](../README.md) › [Title 3 — Open Space Land Conservation Projects](README.md)

```text
§ 54-0301. Definitions.
  For purposes of this title, "open space land conservation projects"
shall mean acquisition projects including the purchase of conservation
easements undertaken by the commissioner and/or the commissioner of the
office of parks, recreation and historic preservation listed in the
state open space land acquisition plan prepared pursuant to title 2 of
article 49 of this chapter.
```
