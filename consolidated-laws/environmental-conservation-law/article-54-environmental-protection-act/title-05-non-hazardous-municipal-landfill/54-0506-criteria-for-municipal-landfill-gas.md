# Environmental Conservation Law § 54-0506 — Criteria for municipal landfill gas management projects

[Environmental Conservation Law](../../README.md) › [Article 54 — Environmental Protection Act](../README.md) › [Title 5 — Non-hazardous Municipal Landfill Closure Projects and Municipal Landfill Gas Management Projects](README.md)

```text
§ 54-0506. Criteria for municipal landfill gas management projects.
  Municipal landfill gas management project costs incurred prior to
April first, nineteen hundred ninety-three shall not be eligible for
state assistance payments funded pursuant to this article.
```
