# Environmental Conservation Law § 17-1401 — Purpose

[Environmental Conservation Law](../../README.md) › [Article 17 — Water Pollution Control](../README.md) › [Title 14 — Nonpoint Source Water Pollution Control](README.md)

```text
§ 17-1401. Purpose.
  It is the purpose of this title to safeguard the waters of the state
from nonpoint source pollution by controlling and abating new and
existing sources of nonpoint source pollution.
```
