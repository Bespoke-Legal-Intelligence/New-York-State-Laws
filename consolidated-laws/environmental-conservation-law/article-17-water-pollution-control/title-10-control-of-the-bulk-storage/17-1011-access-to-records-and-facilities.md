# Environmental Conservation Law § 17-1011 — Access to records and facilities

[Environmental Conservation Law](../../README.md) › [Article 17 — Water Pollution Control](../README.md) › [Title 10 — Control of the Bulk Storage of Petroleum](README.md)

```text
§ 17-1011. Access to records and facilities.
  1. Every owner or operator shall, upon reasonable notice of the
commissioner or his designee, permit a duly designated officer or
employee of the department at all reasonable times to have access to and
to copy all books, papers, documents and records relating to the daily
measurement and inventory of petroleum stored at a facility.
  2. Any duly designated officer or employee of the department may, at
reasonable times and upon reasonable notice of the commissioner or a
designee, enter and inspect any facility, provided that such officer or
employee shall be accompanied by the owner or operator or their
designee.
```
