# Environmental Conservation Law § 17-0804 — Rules and regulations

[Environmental Conservation Law](../../README.md) › [Article 17 — Water Pollution Control](../README.md) › [Title 8 — State Pollutant Discharge Elimination System](README.md)

```text
§ 17-0804. Rules and regulations.
  The rules and regulations adopted by the department to implement this
title and the provisions of article 70 of this chapter and rules and
regulations thereunder shall govern permit applications, renewals,
modifications, suspensions and revocations under this title.
```
