# Environmental Conservation Law § 17-0807 — Prohibited discharges

[Environmental Conservation Law](../../README.md) › [Article 17 — Water Pollution Control](../README.md) › [Title 8 — State Pollutant Discharge Elimination System](README.md)

```text
§ 17-0807. Prohibited discharges.
  The following discharges into the waters of the state are hereby
prohibited.
  1. the discharge of any radiological, chemical or biological warfare
agent or high-level radioactive waste, as such terms are defined by the
Act or pursuant thereto.
  2. any discharge which would substantially impair anchorage and
navigation, as determined by the Secretary of the Army.
  3. any discharge to which the federal government has objected pursuant
to any right to object provided by the Act.
  4. any discharge not permitted by the provisions of this article,
rules and regulations adopted or applicable pursuant hereto, the Act, or
provisions of a permit issued hereunder.
```
