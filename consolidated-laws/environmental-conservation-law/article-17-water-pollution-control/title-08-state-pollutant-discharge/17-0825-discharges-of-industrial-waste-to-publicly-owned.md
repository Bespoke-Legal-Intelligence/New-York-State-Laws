# Environmental Conservation Law § 17-0825 — Discharges of industrial waste to publicly owned treatment works

[Environmental Conservation Law](../../README.md) › [Article 17 — Water Pollution Control](../README.md) › [Title 8 — State Pollutant Discharge Elimination System](README.md)

```text
§ 17-0825. Discharges of industrial waste to publicly owned treatment
             works.
  Persons discharging industrial waste to a publicly owned treatment
works shall comply with toxic effluent standards and pretreatment
standards and to monitoring, reporting, recording, sampling and entry
requirements provided by the Act or adopted pursuant thereto.
```
