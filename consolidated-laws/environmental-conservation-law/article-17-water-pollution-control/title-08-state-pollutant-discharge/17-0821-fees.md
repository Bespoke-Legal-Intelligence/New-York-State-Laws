# Environmental Conservation Law § 17-0821 — Fees

[Environmental Conservation Law](../../README.md) › [Article 17 — Water Pollution Control](../README.md) › [Title 8 — State Pollutant Discharge Elimination System](README.md)

```text
§ 17-0821. Fees.
  The department is hereby authorized to establish a schedule of
reasonable fees, such schedule taking into consideration the volume of
the discharge and treatment costs, for application for issuance of SPDES
permits to compensate the department for its expenses hereunder, giving
due recognition to the amount of federal assistance available; provided,
however, that the department shall not charge a publicly owned drinking
water treatment plant the rates charged industrial facilities, but shall
consider such plants to be municipal facilities for the purposes of the
imposition of fees pursuant to section 72-0602 of this chapter.
```
