# Environmental Conservation Law § 17-1511 — Duty of county clerk or register in respect to filing of plans and map

[Environmental Conservation Law](../../README.md) › [Article 17 — Water Pollution Control](../README.md) › [Title 15 — Realty Subdivisions: Sewerage Service](README.md)

```text
§ 17-1511. Duty of county clerk or register in respect to filing of
             plans and map.
  The county clerk or register shall not file nor record nor accept for
filing or recording any map or plat showing a subdivision of land in any
town, village or city having a population of less than one million
unless there is endorsed thereon or annexed thereto a certificate of the
department or city, county or part-county department of health having
jurisdiction approving the sewerage systems proposed or installed for
such subdivision and consenting to the filing of such map or plat.
```
