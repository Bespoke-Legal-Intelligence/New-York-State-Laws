# Environmental Conservation Law § 17-1509 — Cooperation with the Department of Health

[Environmental Conservation Law](../../README.md) › [Article 17 — Water Pollution Control](../README.md) › [Title 15 — Realty Subdivisions: Sewerage Service](README.md)

```text
§ 17-1509. Cooperation with the Department of Health.
  Notwithstanding any other provision of this title the Commissioner of
Environmental Conservation is empowered to make administrative
arrangements with the Commissioner of Health for joint or cooperative
administration of this title and title II of Article 11 of the Public
Health Law, such that only one plan must be filed and only one fee
totalling three dollars and fifty cents per lot must be paid, except
that where department approval is sought in connection with a particular
plan or map, such additional fee as may be specified in article 70 of
this chapter shall also be paid.
```
