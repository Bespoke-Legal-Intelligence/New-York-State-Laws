# Environmental Conservation Law § 17-1737 — Legal construction and limitations; riparian owners

[Environmental Conservation Law](../../README.md) › [Article 17 — Water Pollution Control](../README.md) › [Title 17 — Discharge of Sewage Into Waters](README.md)

```text
* § 17-1737. Legal construction and limitations; riparian owners.
  Nothing in titles 15 and 17 of this article shall be construed to
diminish or otherwise to modify the common law rights of riparian owners
in the quality of waters of streams covered by such rights, nor in the
case of actions brought against the pollution of waters to limit their
remedy to indemnities.
  * NB Expired September 1, 1973
```
