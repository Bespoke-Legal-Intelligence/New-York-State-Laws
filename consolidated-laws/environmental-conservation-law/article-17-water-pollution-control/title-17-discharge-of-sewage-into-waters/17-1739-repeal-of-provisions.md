# Environmental Conservation Law § 17-1739 — Repeal of provisions

[Environmental Conservation Law](../../README.md) › [Article 17 — Water Pollution Control](../README.md) › [Title 17 — Discharge of Sewage Into Waters](README.md)

```text
§ 17-1739. Repeal of provisions.
  The provisions of sections 17-1701, 17-1705, 17-1707, 17-1713 to
17-1737, inclusive, and 71-1925, shall remain in force and effect only
until September 1, 1973.
```
