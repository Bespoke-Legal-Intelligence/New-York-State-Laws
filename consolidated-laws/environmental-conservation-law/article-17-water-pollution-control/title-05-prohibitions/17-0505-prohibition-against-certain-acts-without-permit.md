# Environmental Conservation Law § 17-0505 — Prohibition against certain acts without permit

[Environmental Conservation Law](../../README.md) › [Article 17 — Water Pollution Control](../README.md) › [Title 5 — Prohibitions](README.md)

```text
§ 17-0505. Prohibition against certain acts without permit.
  The making or use of an outlet or point source discharging into the
waters of the state, and the operation or construction of disposal
systems, without a valid SPDES permit as provided by section 17-0701 or
title 8 hereof are prohibited.
```
