# Environmental Conservation Law § 17-0501 — General prohibition against pollution

[Environmental Conservation Law](../../README.md) › [Article 17 — Water Pollution Control](../README.md) › [Title 5 — Prohibitions](README.md)

```text
§ 17-0501. General prohibition against pollution.
  1. It shall be unlawful for any person, directly or indirectly, to
throw, drain, run or otherwise discharge into such waters organic or
inorganic matter that shall cause or contribute to a condition in
contravention of the standards adopted by the department pursuant to
section 17-0301.
```
