# Environmental Conservation Law § 17-1301 — Approval of marine toilet pollution control devices and establishing effluent standards therefor

[Environmental Conservation Law](../../README.md) › [Article 17 — Water Pollution Control](../README.md) › [Title 13 — Approval of Marine Toilet Pollution Control Devices and Establishing Effluent Standards Therefor](README.md)

```text
§ 17-1301. Approval of marine toilet pollution control devices and
             establishing effluent standards therefor.
  The department shall have all the functions, powers, obligations and
duties, regarding approval of marine toilet pollution control devices
and establishing effluent standards therefor, provided for in section
33-c of the Navigation Law.
```
