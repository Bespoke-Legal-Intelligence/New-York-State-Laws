# Environmental Conservation Law § 27-1014 — Authority to promulgate rules and regulations

[Environmental Conservation Law](../../README.md) › [Article 27 — Collection, Treatment and Disposal of Refuse and Other Solid Waste](../README.md) › [Title 10 — Litter and Solid Waste Control](README.md)

```text
§ 27-1014. Authority to promulgate rules and regulations.
  In addition to the authority of the commissioner, under sections
27-1009 and 27-1013 of this title, the commissioner shall have the power
to promulgate rules and regulations necessary and appropriate for the
administration of this title.
```
