# Environmental Conservation Law § 27-1111 — Public participation and education

[Environmental Conservation Law](../../README.md) › [Article 27 — Collection, Treatment and Disposal of Refuse and Other Solid Waste](../README.md) › [Title 11 — Industrial Siting Hazardous Waste Facilities](README.md)

```text
§ 27-1111. Public participation and education.
  The department shall establish a public information program which
addresses:
  1. The nature and dimension of the industrial hazardous waste
treatment, storage and disposal facility siting problem.
  2. The need for the establishment of a statewide hazardous waste
facility siting plan including the need to site any additional
industrial hazardous waste treatment, storage and disposal facilities
and the role of waste reduction, recycling and reuse in the planning
process.
  3. The necessity and opportunities for public participation in the
siting and planning process.
```
