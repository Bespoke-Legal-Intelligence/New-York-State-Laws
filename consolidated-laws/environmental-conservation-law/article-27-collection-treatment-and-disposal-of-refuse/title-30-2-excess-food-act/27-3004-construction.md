# Environmental Conservation Law § 27-3004 — Construction

[Environmental Conservation Law](../../README.md) › [Article 27 — Collection, Treatment and Disposal of Refuse and Other Solid Waste](../README.md) › [Title 30*2 — Excess Food Act](README.md)

* § 27-3004. Construction.

federal health laws or regulations regarding the handling of food.
