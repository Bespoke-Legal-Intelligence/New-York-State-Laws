# Environmental Conservation Law § 27-3005*2 — Enforcement

[Environmental Conservation Law](../../README.md) › [Article 27 — Collection, Treatment and Disposal of Refuse and Other Solid Waste](../README.md) › [Title 30*2 — Excess Food Act](README.md)

*An asterisk in a number (for example Article 24\* or § 20-C\*2) is the Senate's mark for a number the Legislature has used more than once: the marked article or section is a different one from the unmarked one with the same number. \*2 means the second one enacted with that number.*

```text
* § 27-3005. Enforcement.
  A supermarket that does not meet the requirements of this title shall
not be subject to the penalties specified in title twenty-seven of
article seventy-one of this chapter.
  * NB Effective June 21, 2021
  * NB There are 2 § 27-3005's
```
