# Environmental Conservation Law § 27-2621 — Disposition of fees

[Environmental Conservation Law](../../README.md) › [Article 27 — Collection, Treatment and Disposal of Refuse and Other Solid Waste](../README.md) › [Title 26 — Electronic Equipment Recycling and Reuse](README.md)

```text
§ 27-2621. Disposition of fees.
  All fees and charges collected pursuant to this title shall be
deposited into the environmental protection fund established pursuant to
section ninety-two-s of the state finance law.
```
