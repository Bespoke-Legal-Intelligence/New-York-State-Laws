# Environmental Conservation Law § 27-2501 — Definitions

[Environmental Conservation Law](../../README.md) › [Article 27 — Collection, Treatment and Disposal of Refuse and Other Solid Waste](../README.md) › [Title 25 — Phase-out of Creosote](README.md)

```text
§ 27-2501. Definitions.
  1. As used in this title, the term "creosote" means a substance
obtained from the high temperature treatment of coal and coal tar and
that is used primarily as a wood preservative.
  2. As used in this title, the term "marina" shall have the same
meaning as defined in section thirty-three-c of the navigation law.
```
