# Environmental Conservation Law § 7-0105 — Personnel

[Environmental Conservation Law](../README.md) › [Article 7 — Council of Environmental Advisers](README.md)

```text
§ 7-0105. Personnel.
  1. The council shall appoint an executive director and shall assign to
such director such powers, functions and duties as it shall deem
appropriate to fulfill the purposes of this article.
  2. The council shall have the power to employ and compensate, within
appropriations available therefor, such other personnel as it shall
determine necessary to carry out its duties under this article.
```
