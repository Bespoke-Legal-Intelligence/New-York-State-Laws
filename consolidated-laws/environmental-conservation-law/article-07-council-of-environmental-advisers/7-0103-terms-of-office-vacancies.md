# Environmental Conservation Law § 7-0103 — Terms of office; vacancies

[Environmental Conservation Law](../README.md) › [Article 7 — Council of Environmental Advisers](README.md)

```text
§ 7-0103. Terms of office; vacancies.
  1. The members of the council shall hold office at the pleasure of the
Governor.
  2. Vacancies shall be filled by appointment by the Governor, by and
with the advice and consent of the Senate.
```
