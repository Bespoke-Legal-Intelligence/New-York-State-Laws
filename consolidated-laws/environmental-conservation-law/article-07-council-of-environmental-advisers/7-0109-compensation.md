# Environmental Conservation Law § 7-0109 — Compensation

[Environmental Conservation Law](../README.md) › [Article 7 — Council of Environmental Advisers](README.md)

```text
§ 7-0109. Compensation.
  The members of the council shall receive no compensation for their
services but shall be allowed their actual and necessary expenses
incurred in the performance of their duties.
```
