# Environmental Conservation Law § 42-0113 — Cooperation of state agencies

[Environmental Conservation Law](../README.md) › [Article 42 — Historic Hudson-hoosic Rivers Partnership](README.md)

```text
§ 42-0113. Cooperation of state agencies.
  To effectuate the purposes of this article, the partnership may
request from any department, board, bureau, commission or other agency
of the state, and the same are authorized to provide, such cooperation
and assistance, services and data as will enable the partnership
properly to carry out its functions pursuant to this article.
```
