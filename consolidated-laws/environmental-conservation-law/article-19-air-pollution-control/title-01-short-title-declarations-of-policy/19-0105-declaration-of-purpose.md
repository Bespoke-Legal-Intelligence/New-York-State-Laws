# Environmental Conservation Law § 19-0105 — Declaration of purpose

[Environmental Conservation Law](../../README.md) › [Article 19 — Air Pollution Control](../README.md) › [Title 1 — Short Title; Declarations of Policy and Purpose; Definitions](README.md)

```text
§ 19-0105. Declaration of purpose.
  It is the purpose of this article to safeguard the air resources of
the state from pollution by: (1) controlling or abating air pollution
which shall exist when this artcle shall be enacted and (2) preventng
new air pollution, under a program which shall be consistent with the
declaration of policy above stated and in accordance with the provisions
of this article.
```
