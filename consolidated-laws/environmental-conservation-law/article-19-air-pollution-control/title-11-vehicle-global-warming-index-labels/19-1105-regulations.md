# Environmental Conservation Law § 19-1105 — Regulations

[Environmental Conservation Law](../../README.md) › [Article 19 — Air Pollution Control](../README.md) › [Title 11 — Vehicle Global Warming Index Labels](README.md)

```text
§ 19-1105. Regulations.
  The commissioner is hereby authorized and directed to promulgate rules
and/or regulations specifying labeling requirements necessary for the
implementation of this title provided, however, that any such rules
and/or regulations may allow for the use of labels adopted by the state
of California that generally meet the requirements of this title.
```
