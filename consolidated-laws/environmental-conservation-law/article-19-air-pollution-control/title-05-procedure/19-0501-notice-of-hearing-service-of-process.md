# Environmental Conservation Law § 19-0501 — Notice of hearing; service of process

[Environmental Conservation Law](../../README.md) › [Article 19 — Air Pollution Control](../README.md) › [Title 5 — Procedure](README.md)

```text
§ 19-0501. Notice of hearing; service of process.
  1. Notices of every public hearing held pursuant to the provisions of
this article shall specify the time, date, place and purpose of the
hearing.
  2. Service of all processes of the commissioner pursuant to the
provisions of this article, except subpoenas, shall be made in the same
manner as a summons in a civil action or by registered mail upon any
person to whom it is addressed, upon the mayor or counsel of a
municipality or upon an officer of a district, authority, commission,
private corporation or company, as the case may be.
  3. Subpoenas issued pursuant to the provisions of this article shall
be served upon a prospective witness personally and at the same time he
shall be paid such fees therefor as may be provided by law.
```
