# Environmental Conservation Law § 43-0113 — Appropriations by municipalities in certain counties

[Environmental Conservation Law](../README.md) › [Article 43 — Lake George Park Commission](README.md)

```text
§ 43-0113. Appropriations by municipalities in certain counties.
  The governing body of any municipality in the counties of Essex,
Warren or Washington may raise, appropriate and pay over to the
commission moneys to be expended by the commission for any of the
purposes hereof.
```
