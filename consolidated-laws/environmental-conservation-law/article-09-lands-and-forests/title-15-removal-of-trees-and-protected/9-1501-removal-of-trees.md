# Environmental Conservation Law § 9-1501 — Removal of trees

[Environmental Conservation Law](../../README.md) › [Article 9 — Lands and Forests](../README.md) › [Title 15 — Removal of Trees and Protected Plants](README.md)

```text
§ 9-1501. Removal of trees.
  No person shall cut, pull or dig up for the purpose of removal, injure
or destroy or cause to be so removed, injured or destroyed, any tree on
the lands of another without consent of the owner. Any peace officer,
acting pursuant to his or her special duties, or police officer may
enforce the provisions of this section.
```
