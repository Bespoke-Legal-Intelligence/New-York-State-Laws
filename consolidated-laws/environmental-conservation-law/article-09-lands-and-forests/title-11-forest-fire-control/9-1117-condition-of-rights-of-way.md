# Environmental Conservation Law § 9-1117 — Condition of rights of way

[Environmental Conservation Law](../../README.md) › [Article 9 — Lands and Forests](../README.md) › [Title 11 — Forest Fire Control](README.md)

```text
§ 9-1117. Condition of rights of way.
  1. The railroad right of way of all railroads which are operated
through forest lands shall be kept cleared of all inflammable material
whenever required by the department.
  2. Fire, live coals or hot ashes shall not be deposited unless
properly protected upon any track or railroad right of way on or near
forest land.
```
