# Environmental Conservation Law § 9-0809 — Public participation

[Environmental Conservation Law](../../README.md) › [Article 9 — Lands and Forests](../README.md) › [Title 8 — Forest Resources Planning](README.md)

```text
§ 9-0809. Public participation.
  To assure that all segments of the public have an opportunity to
participate in the development of the plan, the planning program will be
conducted with a structured program of public participation. The
department shall establish procedures to keep the public informed about
the planning process and to provide opportunities for the public to
contribute to that process.
```
