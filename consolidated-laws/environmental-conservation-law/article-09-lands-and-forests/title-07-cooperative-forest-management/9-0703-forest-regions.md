# Environmental Conservation Law § 9-0703 — Forest regions

[Environmental Conservation Law](../../README.md) › [Article 9 — Lands and Forests](../README.md) › [Title 7 — Cooperative Forest Management Program](README.md)

```text
§ 9-0703. Forest regions.
  The commissioner shall establish not to exceed nine forest regions of
one or more counties each, which shall include all counties of the
state.
```
