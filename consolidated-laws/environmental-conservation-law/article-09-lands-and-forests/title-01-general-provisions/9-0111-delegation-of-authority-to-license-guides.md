# Environmental Conservation Law § 9-0111 — Delegation of authority to license guides

[Environmental Conservation Law](../../README.md) › [Article 9 — Lands and Forests](../README.md) › [Title 1 — General Provisions](README.md)

```text
§ 9-0111. Delegation of authority to license guides.
  The commissioner may delegate the authority to administer the
licensing of guides pursuant to section 11-0533 of this chapter to the
office of public protection.
```
