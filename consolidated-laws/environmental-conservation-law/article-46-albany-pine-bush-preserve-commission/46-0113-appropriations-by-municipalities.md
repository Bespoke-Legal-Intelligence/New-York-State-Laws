# Environmental Conservation Law § 46-0113 — Appropriations by municipalities

[Environmental Conservation Law](../README.md) › [Article 46 — Albany Pine Bush Preserve Commission](README.md)

```text
§ 46-0113. Appropriations by municipalities.
  To effectuate the purposes of this article, the local legislative
bodies of the county of Albany, city of Albany and towns of Colonie and
Guilderland may appropriate and pay over to the commission moneys to be
expended by the commission to carry out its functions thereunder.
```
