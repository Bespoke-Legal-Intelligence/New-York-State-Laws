# Environmental Conservation Law § 46-0115 — Cooperation of state agencies

[Environmental Conservation Law](../README.md) › [Article 46 — Albany Pine Bush Preserve Commission](README.md)

```text
§ 46-0115. Cooperation of state agencies.
  To effectuate the purposes of this article, the commission may request
from any department, board, bureau, commission or other agency of the
state, and the same are authorized to provide, such cooperation and
assistance, services and data as will enable the commission properly to
carry out its functions thereunder.
```
