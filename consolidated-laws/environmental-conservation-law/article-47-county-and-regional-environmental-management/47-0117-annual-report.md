# Environmental Conservation Law § 47-0117 — Annual report

[Environmental Conservation Law](../README.md) › [Article 47 — County and Regional Environmental Management Councils](README.md)

```text
§ 47-0117. Annual report.
  The commissioner shall submit an annual report to the governor and the
legislature describing the operation of the local assistance program
established by this article. The annual report shall include a
description of activities conducted by each county and regional
environmental management council and the activities conducted by the
department in performing said activities pursuant to section 47-0115 of
this article.
```
