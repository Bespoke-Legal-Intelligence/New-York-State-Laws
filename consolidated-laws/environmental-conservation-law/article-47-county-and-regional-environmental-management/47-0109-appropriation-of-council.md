# Environmental Conservation Law § 47-0109 — Appropriation of council

[Environmental Conservation Law](../README.md) › [Article 47 — County and Regional Environmental Management Councils](README.md)

```text
§ 47-0109. Appropriation of council.
  The county may provide for compensation to be paid to the members of
the council and may make such appropriations as it may see fit for
expenses incurred by the council including appointment of such clerks
and other employees as may be necessary for the conduct of the council's
official business.
```
