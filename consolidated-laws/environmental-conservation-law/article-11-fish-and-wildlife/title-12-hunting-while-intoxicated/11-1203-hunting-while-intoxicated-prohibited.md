# Environmental Conservation Law § 11-1203 — Hunting while intoxicated prohibited

[Environmental Conservation Law](../../README.md) › [Article 11 — Fish and Wildlife](../README.md) › [Title 12 — Hunting While Intoxicated](README.md)

```text
§ 11-1203. Hunting while intoxicated prohibited.
  1. No person shall engage in hunting while he is in an intoxicated
condition.
  2. No person shall engage in hunting while his ability to engage in
such hunting without creating unreasonable risk of injury or death to
himself or other human life is impaired by his consumption of alcohol or
use of a drug.
```
