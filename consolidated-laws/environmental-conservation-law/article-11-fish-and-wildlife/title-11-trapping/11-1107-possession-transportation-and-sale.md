# Environmental Conservation Law § 11-1107 — Possession, transportation and sale

[Environmental Conservation Law](../../README.md) › [Article 11 — Fish and Wildlife](../README.md) › [Title 11 — Trapping](README.md)

```text
§ 11-1107. Possession, transportation and sale.
  Wildlife taken by trapping may be possessed, transported, bought and
sold as provided in section 11-0917, and shall not be possessed,
transported, bought or sold except as so provided.
```
