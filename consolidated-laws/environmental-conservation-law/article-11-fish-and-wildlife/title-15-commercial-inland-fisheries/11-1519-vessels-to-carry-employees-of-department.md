# Environmental Conservation Law § 11-1519 — Vessels to carry employees of department

[Environmental Conservation Law](../../README.md) › [Article 11 — Fish and Wildlife](../README.md) › [Title 15 — Commercial Inland Fisheries](README.md)

```text
§ 11-1519. Vessels to carry employees of department.
  Any person operating a boat used in taking fish shall permit
conservation officers or other employees of the department to board it
and inspect its contents and shall carry such persons for the purpose of
inspecting nets or taking fish eggs.
```
