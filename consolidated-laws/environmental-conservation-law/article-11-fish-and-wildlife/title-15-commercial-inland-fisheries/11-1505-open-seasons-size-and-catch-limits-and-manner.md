# Environmental Conservation Law § 11-1505 — Open seasons, size and catch limits and manner of taking and possession of fish; regulations

[Environmental Conservation Law](../../README.md) › [Article 11 — Fish and Wildlife](../README.md) › [Title 15 — Commercial Inland Fisheries](README.md)

```text
§ 11-1505. Open seasons, size and catch limits and manner of taking and
             possession of fish; regulations.
  The department may fix by regulation the manner of taking fish with
nets and other devices including but not limited to open seasons, size
and catch limits and manner of possession and transportation.
```
