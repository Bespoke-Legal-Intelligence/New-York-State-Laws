# Environmental Conservation Law § 11-1009 — Prohibitions

[Environmental Conservation Law](../../README.md) › [Article 11 — Fish and Wildlife](../README.md) › [Title 10 — Falconry](README.md)

```text
§ 11-1009. Prohibitions.
  (1) Raptors for use in falconry shall not be bought, sold or bartered,
or offered for sale or barter, except that this section shall not apply
to the captive bred progeny of lawfully acquired raptors.
  (2) No person shall possess or maintain in captivity any raptor or
raptors, except as otherwise provided by law, unless he shall first
obtain a falconry license.
```
