# Environmental Conservation Law § 11-1728 — Sale of live wild birds

[Environmental Conservation Law](../../README.md) › [Article 11 — Fish and Wildlife](../README.md) › [Title 17 — Importation and Sale of Fish, Wildlife and Game From Outside the State; Sale of Domestic and Foreign Game and Hatchery Trout; Bird Plumage; Taxidermy](README.md)

```text
§ 11-1728. Sale of live wild birds.
  Except as permitted by rule and regulation of the department, no
person shall sell live wild birds, as defined in paragraph b of
subdivision six of section 11-0103 of this chapter, unless such birds
were born and raised in captivity.
```
