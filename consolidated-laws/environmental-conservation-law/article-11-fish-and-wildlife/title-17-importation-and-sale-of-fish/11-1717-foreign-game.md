# Environmental Conservation Law § 11-1717 — Foreign game

[Environmental Conservation Law](../../README.md) › [Article 11 — Fish and Wildlife](../README.md) › [Title 17 — Importation and Sale of Fish, Wildlife and Game From Outside the State; Sale of Domestic and Foreign Game and Hatchery Trout; Bird Plumage; Taxidermy](README.md)

```text
§ 11-1717. Foreign game.
  1. Foreign game means pheasants of all species, Scotch grouse,
Norwegian ptarmigan, Norwegian white grouse, European black game,
European black plover, European gray-legged partridge, European
red-legged partridge, Egyptian quail, tinamou and species or subspecies
of birds not native to this state, and European red deer, fallow deer
and roebuck.
  2. The carcasses, or parts thereof, of foreign game imported from
outside the United States may be bought and sold.
```
