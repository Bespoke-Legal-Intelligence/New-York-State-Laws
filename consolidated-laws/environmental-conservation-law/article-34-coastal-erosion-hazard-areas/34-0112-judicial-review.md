# Environmental Conservation Law § 34-0112 — Judicial review

[Environmental Conservation Law](../README.md) › [Article 34 — Coastal Erosion Hazard Areas](README.md)

```text
§ 34-0112. Judicial review.
  Any person aggrieved by an act, order, determination or decision of
the commissioner made pursuant to this article may seek judicial review
pursuant to article seventy-eight of the civil practice law and rules in
the supreme court for the county in which the erosion hazard area
affected is located.
```
