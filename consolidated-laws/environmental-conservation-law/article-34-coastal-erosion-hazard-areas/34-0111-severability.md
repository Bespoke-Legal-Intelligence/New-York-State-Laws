# Environmental Conservation Law § 34-0111 — Severability

[Environmental Conservation Law](../README.md) › [Article 34 — Coastal Erosion Hazard Areas](README.md)

```text
§ 34-0111. Severability.
  The provisions of this article shall be severable, and if any clause,
sentence, paragraph, subdivision or part of this article shall be
adjudged invalid by a court of competent jurisdiction, such order or
judgment shall be confined in its operation to the controversy in which
it was rendered, and shall not affect or invalidate the remainder of any
provision of any section or the application of any part thereof to any
other person or circumstance.
```
