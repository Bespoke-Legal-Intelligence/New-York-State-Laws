# Environmental Conservation Law § 53-0105 — Reports

[Environmental Conservation Law](../../README.md) › [Article 53 — Tree Conservation and Urban Forestry](../README.md) › [Title 1 — Tree Conservation](README.md)

```text
§ 53-0105. Reports.
  The department shall from time to time report to the governor, and
shall make an annual report to the governor and the legislature, not
later than May first, concerning the effectiveness of this article in
carrying out the legislative purpose pursuant to this article, in the
preceding calendar year.
```
