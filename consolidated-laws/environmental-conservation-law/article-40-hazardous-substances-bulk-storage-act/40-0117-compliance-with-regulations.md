# Environmental Conservation Law § 40-0117 — Compliance with regulations

[Environmental Conservation Law](../README.md) › [Article 40 — Hazardous Substances Bulk Storage Act](README.md)

```text
§ 40-0117. Compliance with regulations.
  It shall be unlawful to operate, maintain or close a storage facility
other than in compliance with the regulations promulgated pursuant to
this article.
```
