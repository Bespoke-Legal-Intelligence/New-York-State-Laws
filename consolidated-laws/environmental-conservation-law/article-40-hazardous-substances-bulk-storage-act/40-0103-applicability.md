# Environmental Conservation Law § 40-0103 — Applicability

[Environmental Conservation Law](../README.md) › [Article 40 — Hazardous Substances Bulk Storage Act](README.md)

```text
§ 40-0103. Applicability.
  This article applies to all storage facilities which are used for the
storage of hazardous substances, except that this article shall not
apply to any storage facilities, regulated under:
  1. Title 7, 9 or 11 of article 27 of this chapter;
  2. The Natural Gas Pipeline Safety Act of 1968 (49 U.S.C. § 1671 et
seq.);
  3. The Hazardous Liquid Pipeline Safety Act of 1979 (49 U.S.C. § 2001
et seq.);
  4. The Natural Gas Act (15 U.S.C. § 717 et seq.);
  5. The Liquefied Natural and Petroleum Gas Act (Title 17 of article 23
of this chapter); or
  6. Provisions relating to liquid petroleum pipeline corporations
(article 3-C of the public service law); or
  7. The Atomic Energy Act of 1954, as amended (42 U.S.C. § 2011 et
seq.).
```
