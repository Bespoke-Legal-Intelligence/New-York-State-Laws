# Environmental Conservation Law § 40-0123 — Severability

[Environmental Conservation Law](../README.md) › [Article 40 — Hazardous Substances Bulk Storage Act](README.md)

```text
§ 40-0123. Severability.
  The provisions of this article shall be severable and if any phrase,
clause, sentence or provision of this article, or the applicability
thereof to any person or circumstance shall be held invalid, the
remainder of this article and the application thereof shall not be
affected thereby.
```
