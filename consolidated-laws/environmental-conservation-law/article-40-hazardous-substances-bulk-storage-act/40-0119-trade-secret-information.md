# Environmental Conservation Law § 40-0119 — Trade secret information

[Environmental Conservation Law](../README.md) › [Article 40 — Hazardous Substances Bulk Storage Act](README.md)

```text
§ 40-0119. Trade secret information.
  Disclosure to the public by the department of any information required
to be provided under this article which is claimed to be a trade secret
or confidential business information, shall be treated as such pursuant
to the provisions of the freedom of information law (article six of the
public officers law).
```
