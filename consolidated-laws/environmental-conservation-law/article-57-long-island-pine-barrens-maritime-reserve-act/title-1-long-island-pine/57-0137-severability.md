# Environmental Conservation Law § 57-0137 — Severability

[Environmental Conservation Law](../../README.md) › [Article 57 — Long Island Pine Barrens Maritime Reserve Act](../README.md) › [Title 1 — Long Island Pine Barrens Maritime Reserve Act](README.md)

```text
§ 57-0137. Severability.
  The provisions of this title shall be severable and if any portion
thereof or the applicability thereof to any person or circumstances
shall be held invalid, the remainder of this title and the application
thereof shall not be affected thereby.
```
