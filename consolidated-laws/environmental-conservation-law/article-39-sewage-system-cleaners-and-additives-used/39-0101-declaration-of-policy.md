# Environmental Conservation Law § 39-0101 — Declaration of policy

[Environmental Conservation Law](../README.md) › [Article 39 — Sewage System Cleaners and Additives Used In Restricted Geographical Areas](README.md)

```text
§ 39-0101. Declaration of policy.
  It is declared to be the public policy of this state to prevent the
pollution of water resources in restricted geographical areas through
the use of sewage system cleaners and additives by establishing a
regulatory program restricting the use of such products.
```
