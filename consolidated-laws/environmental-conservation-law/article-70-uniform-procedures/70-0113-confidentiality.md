# Environmental Conservation Law § 70-0113 — Confidentiality

[Environmental Conservation Law](../README.md) › [Article 70 — Uniform Procedures](README.md)

```text
§ 70-0113. Confidentiality.
  The provisions of section eighty-eight of article six of the public
officers law shall apply to information confidentially disclosed by
applicants to the department, except as otherwise provided in this
chapter.
```
