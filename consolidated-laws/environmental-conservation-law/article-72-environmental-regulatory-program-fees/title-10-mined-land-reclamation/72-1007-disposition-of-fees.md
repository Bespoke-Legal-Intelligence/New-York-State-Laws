# Environmental Conservation Law § 72-1007 — Disposition of fees

[Environmental Conservation Law](../../README.md) › [Article 72 — Environmental Regulatory Program Fees](../README.md) › [Title 10 — Mined Land Reclamation Program Fees](README.md)

```text
§ 72-1007. Disposition of fees.
  All fees collected pursuant to this title shall be paid into the
miscellaneous special revenue fund, to the credit of the mined land
reclamation program account. Moneys in such account shall be kept
separately from and shall not be commingled with any other moneys in the
joint or sole custody of the state comptroller.
```
