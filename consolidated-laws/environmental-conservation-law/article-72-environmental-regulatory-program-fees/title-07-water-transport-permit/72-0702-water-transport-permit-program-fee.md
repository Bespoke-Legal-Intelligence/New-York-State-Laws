# Environmental Conservation Law § 72-0702 — Water transport permit program fee

[Environmental Conservation Law](../../README.md) › [Article 72 — Environmental Regulatory Program Fees](../README.md) › [Title 7 — Water Transport Permit Program Fee](README.md)

```text
§ 72-0702. Water transport permit program fee.
  All persons and public corporations required to obtain a water
transport permit pursuant to section 15-1506 of this chapter shall
submit to the department a fee for each facility equal to one hundred
dollars per million gallons of fresh water withdrawn or transported
pursuant to such permit.
```
