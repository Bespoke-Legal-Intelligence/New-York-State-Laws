# Environmental Conservation Law § 72-0103 — Definitions

[Environmental Conservation Law](../../README.md) › [Article 72 — Environmental Regulatory Program Fees](../README.md) › [Title 1 — Declaration of Policy; Definitions](README.md)

```text
§ 72-0103. Definitions.
  When used in this article:
  "State environmental regulatory program" means any of the regulatory
or permit programs for which fees are assessed under this article.
```
