# Environmental Conservation Law § 21-1313 — Delegations of power

[Environmental Conservation Law](../../README.md) › [Article 21 — Pollution Control Compacts](../README.md) › [Title 13 — Susquehanna River Basin Compact](README.md)

```text
§ 21-1313. Delegations of power.
  No agency or officer of this state or any subdivision thereof shall
accept or exercise any delegation of power pursuant to section 11.1 of
the compact unless, in the absence of the compact, it would have the
constitutional or statutory power to exercise such power on its own
account.
```
