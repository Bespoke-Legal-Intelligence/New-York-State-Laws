# Environmental Conservation Law § 21-1321 — Inconsistent laws

[Environmental Conservation Law](../../README.md) › [Article 21 — Pollution Control Compacts](../README.md) › [Title 13 — Susquehanna River Basin Compact](README.md)

```text
§ 21-1321. Inconsistent laws.
  No provision of this chapter or of any other law of this state which
is inconsistent with the provisions of the compact shall be applicable
to the Susquehanna river basin commission or to any matter governed by
the compact.
```
