# Environmental Conservation Law § 21-1303 — Member and alternate

[Environmental Conservation Law](../../README.md) › [Article 21 — Pollution Control Compacts](../README.md) › [Title 13 — Susquehanna River Basin Compact](README.md)

```text
§ 21-1303. Member and alternate.
  1. As provided in section 2.2 of the compact, the Governor or his
designee shall be this state's member on the commission established
thereby. An officer of the Department of Environmental Conservation
shall be appointed as the designee if one is appointed. The Governor
shall appoint an alternate pursuant to section 2.3 of the compact. If
the Governor does not appoint a designee to act for him, he shall
appoint an officer of the Department of Environmental Conservation as
alternate.
  2. Any person serving on the Susquehanna river basin commission
pursuant to this section shall be reimbursed for all necessary expenses
incurred as an incident of such service and such reimbursement shall be
from the funds of said person's department or office.
```
