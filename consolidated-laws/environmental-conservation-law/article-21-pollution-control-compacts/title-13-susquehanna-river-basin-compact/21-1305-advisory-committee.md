# Environmental Conservation Law § 21-1305 — Advisory committee

[Environmental Conservation Law](../../README.md) › [Article 21 — Pollution Control Compacts](../README.md) › [Title 13 — Susquehanna River Basin Compact](README.md)

```text
§ 21-1305. Advisory committee.
  The Department of Environmental Conservation shall provide an advisory
committee with whom the member of the Susquehanna river basin commission
from this state may consult with respect to the conduct of New York
participation in the compact.
```
