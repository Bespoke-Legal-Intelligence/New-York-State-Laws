# Environmental Conservation Law § 21-1113 — Inconsistent laws

[Environmental Conservation Law](../../README.md) › [Article 21 — Pollution Control Compacts](../README.md) › [Title 11 — Champlain Basin Compact](README.md)

```text
§ 21-1113. Inconsistent laws.
  No provision of this chapter or of any other law, which is
inconsistent with the provisions of the compact shall be applicable to
the interstate commission on the Lake Champlain basin or to any matters
governed by the compact.
```
