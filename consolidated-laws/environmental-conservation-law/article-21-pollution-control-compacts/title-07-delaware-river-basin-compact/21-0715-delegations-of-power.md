# Environmental Conservation Law § 21-0715 — Delegations of power

[Environmental Conservation Law](../../README.md) › [Article 21 — Pollution Control Compacts](../README.md) › [Title 7 — Delaware River Basin Compact](README.md)

```text
§ 21-0715. Delegations of power.
  No agency or officer of this state or any subdivision thereof shall
accept or exercise any delegation of power pursuant to subdivision one
of section ten of article ten of the compact unless, in the absence of
the compact, it would have the constitutional or statutory power to
exercise such power on its own account.
```
