# Environmental Conservation Law § 21-0723 — Inconsistent laws

[Environmental Conservation Law](../../README.md) › [Article 21 — Pollution Control Compacts](../README.md) › [Title 7 — Delaware River Basin Compact](README.md)

```text
§ 21-0723. Inconsistent laws.
  No provision of this chapter or of any other law, which is
inconsistent with the provisions of the compact shill be applicable to
the Delaware river basin commission or to any matter governed by the
compact.
```
