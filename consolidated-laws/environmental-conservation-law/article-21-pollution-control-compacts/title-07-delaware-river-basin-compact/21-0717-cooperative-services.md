# Environmental Conservation Law § 21-0717 — Cooperative services

[Environmental Conservation Law](../../README.md) › [Article 21 — Pollution Control Compacts](../README.md) › [Title 7 — Delaware River Basin Compact](README.md)

```text
§ 21-0717. Cooperative services.
  Departments, agencies and officers shall provide technical and
administrative services to the Delaware river basin commission upon
request, within the limits of available appropriations and shall
cooperate generally with said commission for the purposes of the
compact.
```
