# Environmental Conservation Law § 21-0909 — Transmittal of documents

[Environmental Conservation Law](../../README.md) › [Article 21 — Pollution Control Compacts](../README.md) › [Title 9 — Great Lakes Basin Compact](README.md)

```text
§ 21-0909. Transmittal of documents.
  The Secretary of State is hereby authorized and directed to transmit a
duly authenticated copy of this title and the act by which it has been
added to the Environmental Conservation Law to each jurisdiction party
to the compact.
```
