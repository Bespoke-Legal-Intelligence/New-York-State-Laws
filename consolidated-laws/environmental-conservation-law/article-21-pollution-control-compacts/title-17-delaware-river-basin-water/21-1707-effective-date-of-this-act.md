# Environmental Conservation Law § 21-1707 — Effective date of this act

[Environmental Conservation Law](../../README.md) › [Article 21 — Pollution Control Compacts](../README.md) › [Title 17 — Delaware River Basin Water Commission Compact](README.md)

```text
§ 21-1707. Effective date of this act.
  The provisions of this act shall become effective immediately upon
final enactment; but the Governor shall not enter into the compact
herein set forth until the state of New Jersey and the commonwealth of
Pennsylvania shall have passed a substantially similar act embodying the
said compact between at least the states of New Jersey, New York and the
commonwealth of Pennsylvania.
```
