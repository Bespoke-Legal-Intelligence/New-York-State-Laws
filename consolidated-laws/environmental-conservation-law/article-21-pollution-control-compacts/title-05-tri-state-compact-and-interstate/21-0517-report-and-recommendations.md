# Environmental Conservation Law § 21-0517 — Report and recommendations

[Environmental Conservation Law](../../README.md) › [Article 21 — Pollution Control Compacts](../README.md) › [Title 5 — Tri-state Compact and Interstate Environmental Commission](README.md)

```text
§ 21-0517. Report and recommendations.
  The commission shall keep accurate accounts of all receipts and
disbursements and shall report to the Governor and the legislature of
this state on or before the twenty-fifth day of January in each year,
setting forth in detail the transactions conducted by it and shall make
recommendations for any legislative action deemed by it advisable,
including amendments to the statutes of the signatory states which may
be necessary to carry out the intent and purposes of the compact.
```
