# Environmental Conservation Law § 21-0509 — Powers to be additional; inconsistent provisions

[Environmental Conservation Law](../../README.md) › [Article 21 — Pollution Control Compacts](../README.md) › [Title 5 — Tri-state Compact and Interstate Environmental Commission](README.md)

```text
§ 21-0509. Powers to be additional; inconsistent provisions.
  Any powers herein granted to the interstate environmental commission
shall be regarded as in aid of and supplemental to, and in no case a
limitation upon, any other powers legally vested in said commission. No
provision of the Public Health Law and no provision of the Environmental
Conservation Law, if such provision of the Environmental Conservation
Law was derived from a provision of the Public Health Law, which is
inconsistent with the provisions of the tri-state compact shall be
applicable to the interstate environmental commission or to any matter
governed by the tri-state compact.
```
