# Environmental Conservation Law § 21-0309 — Powers to be additional

[Environmental Conservation Law](../../README.md) › [Article 21 — Pollution Control Compacts](../README.md) › [Title 3 — Ohio River Valley Water Sanitation Compact](README.md)

```text
§ 21-0309. Powers to be additional.
  Any powers herein granted to the commission shall be regarded as in
aid of and supplemental to and in no case a limitation upon any other
powers vested in said commission by other laws of the state of New York
or by any other jurisdiction, or by the terms of said compact.
```
