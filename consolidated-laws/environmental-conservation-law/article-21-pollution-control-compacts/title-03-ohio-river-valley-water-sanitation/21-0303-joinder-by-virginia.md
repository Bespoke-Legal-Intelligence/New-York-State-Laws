# Environmental Conservation Law § 21-0303 — Joinder by Virginia

[Environmental Conservation Law](../../README.md) › [Article 21 — Pollution Control Compacts](../README.md) › [Title 3 — Ohio River Valley Water Sanitation Compact](README.md)

```text
§ 21-0303. Joinder by Virginia.
  The state of New York hereby consents that the state of Virginia may
become a party to and a signatory state of the compact described in the
preceding section as fully as though such state had been expressly named
therein.
```
