# Environmental Conservation Law § 14-0113 — State agency assistance

[Environmental Conservation Law](../README.md) › [Article 14 — New York Ocean and Great Lakes Ecosystem Conservation Act](README.md)

```text
§ 14-0113. State agency assistance.
  Any state agency, department, public benefit corporation or division,
bureau or agency thereof may provide the council with the services of
its agents, employees and facilities without charge to the council for
the purpose of carrying out this article.
```
