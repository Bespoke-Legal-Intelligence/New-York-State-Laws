# Environmental Conservation Law § 56-0607 — Other air quality projects

[Environmental Conservation Law](../../README.md) › [Article 56 — Implementation of the Clean Water/clean Air Bond Act of 1996](../README.md) › [Title 6 — Air Quality Projects](README.md)

```text
§ 56-0607. Other air quality projects.
  Of monies received by the state from the sale of bonds pursuant to the
Clean Water/Clean Air Bond Act of 1996, up to twenty million dollars
($20,000,000) shall be available for disbursements for air quality
projects pursuant to this section.
  The commissioner is authorized in consultation with other state
agencies as may be necessary, to make state assistance payments or to
enter into contracts in the name of the state for projects that will
enhance the quality of the state's environment and the state's air
quality.
```
