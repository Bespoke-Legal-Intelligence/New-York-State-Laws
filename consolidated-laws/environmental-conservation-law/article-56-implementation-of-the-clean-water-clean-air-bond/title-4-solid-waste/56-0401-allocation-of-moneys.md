# Environmental Conservation Law § 56-0401 — Allocation of moneys

[Environmental Conservation Law](../../README.md) › [Article 56 — Implementation of the Clean Water/clean Air Bond Act of 1996](../README.md) › [Title 4 — Solid Waste Projects](README.md)

```text
§ 56-0401. Allocation of moneys.
  Of the moneys received by the state from the sale of bonds pursuant to
the Clean Water/Clean Air Bond Act of 1996 to be used for solid waste
projects, fifty million dollars ($50,000,000) shall be available for
disbursements for municipal landfill projects, excluding any landfill
closure project in the city of New York; fifty million dollars
($50,000,000) shall be available for disbursements for municipal
recycling projects, of which twenty-five million dollars ($25,000,000)
shall be available for municipal recycling projects in the city of New
York; and seventy-five million dollars ($75,000,000) shall be available
for disbursements for the closure of Fresh Kills Landfill in the city of
New York.
```
