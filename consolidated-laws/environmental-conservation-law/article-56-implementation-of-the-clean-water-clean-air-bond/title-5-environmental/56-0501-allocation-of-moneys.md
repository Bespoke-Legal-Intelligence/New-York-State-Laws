# Environmental Conservation Law § 56-0501 — Allocation of moneys

[Environmental Conservation Law](../../README.md) › [Article 56 — Implementation of the Clean Water/clean Air Bond Act of 1996](../README.md) › [Title 5 — Environmental Restoration Projects](README.md)

```text
§ 56-0501. Allocation of moneys. Of the moneys received by the state
from the sale of bonds pursuant to the Clean Water/Clean Air Bond Act of
1996, two hundred million dollars ($200,000,000) shall be available for
disbursements for environmental restoration projects.
```
