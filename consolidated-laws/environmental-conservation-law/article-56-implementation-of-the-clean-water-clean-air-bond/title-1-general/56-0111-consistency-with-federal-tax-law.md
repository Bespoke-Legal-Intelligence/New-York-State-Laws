# Environmental Conservation Law § 56-0111 — Consistency with federal tax law

[Environmental Conservation Law](../../README.md) › [Article 56 — Implementation of the Clean Water/clean Air Bond Act of 1996](../README.md) › [Title 1 — General Provisions](README.md)

```text
§ 56-0111. Consistency with federal tax law.
  All actions taken pursuant to this article shall be reviewed for
consistency with provisions of the federal internal revenue code and
regulations thereunder, in accordance with procedures established in
connection with the issuance of any tax exempt bonds pursuant to this
article, to preserve the tax exempt status of such bonds.
```
