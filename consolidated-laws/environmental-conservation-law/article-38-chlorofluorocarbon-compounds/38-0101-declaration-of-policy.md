# Environmental Conservation Law § 38-0101 — Declaration of policy

[Environmental Conservation Law](../README.md) › [Article 38 — Chlorofluorocarbon Compounds](README.md)

```text
§ 38-0101. Declaration of policy.
  It is hereby declared to be the policy of the state to protect present
and future New Yorkers from the dangers inherent in the depletion of the
world's ozone layer, by restricting the distribution and sale of
chlorofluorocarbon compounds as specified herein.
```
