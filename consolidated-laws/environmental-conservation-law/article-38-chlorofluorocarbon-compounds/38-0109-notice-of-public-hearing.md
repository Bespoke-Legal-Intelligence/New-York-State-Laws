# Environmental Conservation Law § 38-0109 — Notice of public hearing

[Environmental Conservation Law](../README.md) › [Article 38 — Chlorofluorocarbon Compounds](README.md)

```text
§ 38-0109. Notice of public hearing.
  Notice of public hearing shall be by publication in two newspapers
likely to give notice to the people of the state of such hearings at
least once in each of three successive weeks. In addition, the
commissioner shall give notice by letter to the manufacturers of
chlorofluorocarbon compounds known to the commissioner whenever
practicable.
```
