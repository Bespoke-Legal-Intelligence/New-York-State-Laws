# Education Law § 3024 — Teachers responsible for record books

[Education Law](../../README.md) › [Title 4 — Teachers and Pupils](../README.md) › [Article 61 — Teachers and Supervisory and Administrative Staff](README.md)

```text
  § 3024. Teachers responsible for record books.  School lists and
accounts of attendance shall be kept and prepared and entries shall be
made in regards thereto, in such manner, as shall be prescribed by the
commissioner of education by regulation or otherwise.
```
