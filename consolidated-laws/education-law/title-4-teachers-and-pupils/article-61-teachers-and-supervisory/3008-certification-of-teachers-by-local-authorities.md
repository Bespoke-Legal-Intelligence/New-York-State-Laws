# Education Law § 3008 — Certification of teachers by local authorities

[Education Law](../../README.md) › [Title 4 — Teachers and Pupils](../README.md) › [Article 61 — Teachers and Supervisory and Administrative Staff](README.md)

```text
  § 3008. Certification of teachers by local authorities.  A district
superintendent, a city superintendent of schools or such other authority
of a city school district as may be designated by law may issue such
certificate as may be authorized by law or by the regents general rules.
```
