# Education Law § 3019 — Penalty for teacher's failure to complete contract

[Education Law](../../README.md) › [Title 4 — Teachers and Pupils](../README.md) › [Article 61 — Teachers and Supervisory and Administrative Staff](README.md)

```text
  § 3019. Penalty for teacher's failure to complete contract.  Any
failure on the part of a teacher to complete an agreement to teach a
term of school without good reason therefor shall be deemed sufficient
ground for the revocation of the teacher's certificate.
```
