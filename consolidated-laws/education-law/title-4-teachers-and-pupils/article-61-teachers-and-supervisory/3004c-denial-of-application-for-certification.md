# Education Law § 3004-C — Denial of application for certification

[Education Law](../../README.md) › [Title 4 — Teachers and Pupils](../README.md) › [Article 61 — Teachers and Supervisory and Administrative Staff](README.md)

```text
  § 3004-c. Denial of application for certification. When the
commissioner determines that a certification should be denied, the
applicant shall be afforded notice and the right to be heard and offer
proof in opposition to such determination in accordance with the
regulations of the commissioner.
```
