# Education Law § 3201-A — Discrimination on account of sex

[Education Law](../../../README.md) › [Title 4 — Teachers and Pupils](../../README.md) › [Article 65 — Compulsory Education and School Census](../README.md) › [Part 1 — Compulsory Education](README.md)

```text
  § 3201-a. Discrimination on account of sex.  Notwithstanding any
general, special, local law or rule or regulation of the education
department to the contrary, no person shall be refused admission into or
be excluded from any course of instruction offered in the state public
and high school systems by reason of that person's sex.  No person shall
be disqualified from state public and high school athletic teams, by
reason of that person's sex, except pursuant to regulations promulgated
by the state commissioner of education.
```
