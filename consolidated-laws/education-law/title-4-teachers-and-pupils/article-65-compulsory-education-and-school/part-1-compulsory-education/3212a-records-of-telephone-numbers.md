# Education Law § 3212-A — Records of telephone numbers

[Education Law](../../../README.md) › [Title 4 — Teachers and Pupils](../../README.md) › [Article 65 — Compulsory Education and School Census](../README.md) › [Part 1 — Compulsory Education](README.md)

```text
  § 3212-a. Records of telephone numbers.  1. Each school shall maintain
a record of the telephone number of each pupil enrolled in the school
and each person in parental relation to such pupil including the
residential and business telephone numbers of persons in parental
relation to pupils unless such person or pupil chooses not to supply
such numbers.  The record of such telephone numbers shall, except as
otherwise provided by law, be accessible solely for emergency purposes.
  2. The provisions of this section shall not be applicable in any
school district in which the board of education has adopted a resolution
providing that the record otherwise required hereby shall not be
maintained.
```
