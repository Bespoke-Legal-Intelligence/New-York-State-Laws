# Education Law § 3219 — Consent of parent or guardian

[Education Law](../../../README.md) › [Title 4 — Teachers and Pupils](../../README.md) › [Article 65 — Compulsory Education and School Census](../README.md) › [Part 1 — Compulsory Education](README.md)

```text
  * § 3219. Consent of parent or guardian.  If the application is for a
full-time employment certificate a parent or guardian shall personally
appear before the issuing officer or school authorities to indicate
consent to the issuance of the application, provided, however, that such
personal appearance shall not be required if the applicant is a high
school graduate.
```
