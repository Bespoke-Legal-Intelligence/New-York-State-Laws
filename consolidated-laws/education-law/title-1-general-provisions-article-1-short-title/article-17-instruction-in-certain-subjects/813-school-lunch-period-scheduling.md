# Education Law § 813 — School lunch period; scheduling

[Education Law](../../README.md) › [Title 1 — General Provisions Article 1 Short Title and Definitions (§§](../README.md) › [Article 17 — Instruction In Certain Subjects](README.md)

```text
  § 813. School lunch period; scheduling.  Each school shall schedule a
reasonable time for each full day pupil attending pre-kindergarten
through grade twelve to consume lunch.
```
