# Education Law § 297 — Withdrawal

[Education Law](../../README.md) › [Title 1 — General Provisions Article 1 Short Title and Definitions (§§](../README.md) › [Article 6 — No title](README.md)

```text
  § 297. Withdrawal.  The commissioner of education is hereby designated
as the official who is authorized to send and receive the notices
provided in article eleven of the compact in the event of withdrawal of
this state or any other state from the compact.
```
