# Education Law § 344-C — Additional test subject rights

[Education Law](../../README.md) › [Title 1 — General Provisions Article 1 Short Title and Definitions (§§](../README.md) › [Article 7-A — Standardized Testing](README.md)

```text
  § 344-c. Additional test subject rights.  A test subject who must
retake a test because of an error by the test agency, may not be charged
for taking the retest.
```
