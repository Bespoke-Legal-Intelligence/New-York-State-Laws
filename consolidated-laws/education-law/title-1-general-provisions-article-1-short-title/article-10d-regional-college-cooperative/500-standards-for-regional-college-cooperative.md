# Education Law § 500 — Standards for regional college cooperative services board; review of plans

[Education Law](../../README.md) › [Title 1 — General Provisions Article 1 Short Title and Definitions (§§](../README.md) › [Article 10-D — Regional College Cooperative Services Board](README.md)

```text
  § 500. Standards for regional college cooperative services board;
review of plans.  The commissioner shall by regulation provide the
standards for regional boards and the plans of such boards shall be
reviewed by the commissioner at least every three years.
```
