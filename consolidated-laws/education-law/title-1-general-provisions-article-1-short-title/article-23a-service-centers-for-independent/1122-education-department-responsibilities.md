# Education Law § 1122 — Education department; responsibilities

[Education Law](../../README.md) › [Title 1 — General Provisions Article 1 Short Title and Definitions (§§](../README.md) › [Article 23-A — Service Centers For Independent Living](README.md)

```text
  § 1122. Education department; responsibilities.  The department shall
assist individuals and organizations in the planning and establishment
of such service centers. The department shall ensure program
accountability and shall monitor and evaluate such centers.
```
