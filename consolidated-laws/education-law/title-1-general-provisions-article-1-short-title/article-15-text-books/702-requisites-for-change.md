# Education Law § 702 — Requisites for change

[Education Law](../../README.md) › [Title 1 — General Provisions Article 1 Short Title and Definitions (§§](../README.md) › [Article 15 — Text-books](README.md)

```text
  § 702. Requisites for change.  1. When a text-book shall have been
designated for use in a school district or city as provided in
subdivision one of section seven hundred one, it shall not be lawful to
supersede such text-book by any other book within a period of five years
from the time of such designation, except upon a three-fourths vote of
the board of education, trustees or of such body or officer as performs
the function of such board.
```
