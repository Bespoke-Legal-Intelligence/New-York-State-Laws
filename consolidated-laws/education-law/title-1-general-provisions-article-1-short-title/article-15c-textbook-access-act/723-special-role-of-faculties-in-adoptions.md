# Education Law § 723 — Special role of faculties in adoptions

[Education Law](../../README.md) › [Title 1 — General Provisions Article 1 Short Title and Definitions (§§](../README.md) › [Article 15-C — Textbook Access Act](README.md)

```text
  § 723. Special role of faculties in adoptions. Each institution of
higher education shall institute policies that encourage faculty members
or the entity in charge of textbook adoptions to place their orders with
sufficient lead-time to enable the university, or contract-managed,
bookstore to confirm the availability of the requested materials and,
when appropriate, the availability of used textbooks or alternate
digital formats.
```
