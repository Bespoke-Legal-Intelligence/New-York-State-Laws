# Education Law § 494 — Article not affected if in part unconstitutional or ineffective

[Education Law](../../README.md) › [Title 1 — General Provisions Article 1 Short Title and Definitions (§§](../README.md) › [Article 10-B — City of Yonkers Educational Construction Fund](README.md)

```text
  § 494. Article not affected if in part unconstitutional or
ineffective.  If any section, subdivision, paragraph, sentence, clause
or provision of this article shall be unconstitutional or be ineffective
in whole or in part, to the extent that it is not unconstitutional or
ineffective, it shall be valid or effective and no other section,
subdivision, paragraph, sentence, clause or provision shall on account
thereof be deemed invalid or ineffective.
```
