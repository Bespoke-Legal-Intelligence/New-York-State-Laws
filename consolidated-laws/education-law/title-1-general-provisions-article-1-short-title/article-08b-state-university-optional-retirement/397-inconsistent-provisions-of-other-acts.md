# Education Law § 397 — Inconsistent provisions of other acts superseded

[Education Law](../../README.md) › [Title 1 — General Provisions Article 1 Short Title and Definitions (§§](../README.md) › [Article 8-B — State University Optional Retirement Program](README.md)

```text
  § 397. Inconsistent provisions of other acts superseded.  Insofar as
the provisions of this article are inconsistent with the provisions of
any other act, general or special, the provisions of this article shall
be controlling.
```
