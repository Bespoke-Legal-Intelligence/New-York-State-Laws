# Education Law § 395 — Social security

[Education Law](../../README.md) › [Title 1 — General Provisions Article 1 Short Title and Definitions (§§](../README.md) › [Article 8-B — State University Optional Retirement Program](README.md)

```text
  § 395. Social security.  Every electing employee shall have old-age,
survivors, and disability insurance coverage provided by the federal
social security act in accordance with the provisions of article three
of the retirement and social security law.
```
