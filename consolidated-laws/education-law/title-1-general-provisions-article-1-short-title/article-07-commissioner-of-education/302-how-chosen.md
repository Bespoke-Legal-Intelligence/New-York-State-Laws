# Education Law § 302 — How chosen

[Education Law](../../README.md) › [Title 1 — General Provisions Article 1 Short Title and Definitions (§§](../README.md) › [Article 7 — Commissioner of Education](README.md)

```text
  § 302. How chosen.  The commissioner of education shall be elected by
a majority vote of the regents.  Residence without the state of New York
shall not disqualify him from election.
```
