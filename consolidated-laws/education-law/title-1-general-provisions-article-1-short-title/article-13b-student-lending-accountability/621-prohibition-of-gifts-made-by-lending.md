# Education Law § 621 — Prohibition of gifts made by lending institutions to covered institutions and their employees

[Education Law](../../README.md) › [Title 1 — General Provisions Article 1 Short Title and Definitions (§§](../README.md) › [Article 13-B — Student Lending Accountability, Transparency and Enforcement Act](README.md)

```text
  § 621. Prohibition of gifts made by lending institutions to covered
institutions and their employees. 1. A lending institution may not,
directly or indirectly, offer or provide any gift to a covered
institution or a covered institution employee, in exchange for any
advantage or consideration provided to such lending institution related
to its educational loan activities.
  2. A lending institution may not engage in revenue sharing with a
covered institution.
```
