# Education Law § 628 — Proper execution of master promissory notes

[Education Law](../../README.md) › [Title 1 — General Provisions Article 1 Short Title and Definitions (§§](../README.md) › [Article 13-B — Student Lending Accountability, Transparency and Enforcement Act](README.md)

```text
  § 628. Proper execution of master promissory notes. A covered
institution shall not direct in any manner whatsoever potential
borrowers to any electronic master promissory notes or other loan
agreements that do not provide a reasonable and convenient alternative
for the borrower to complete a master promissory note with any federally
approved lending institution offering the relevant loan in this state.
```
