# Education Law § 415 — No schoolhouse shall be built on town line

[Education Law](../../README.md) › [Title 1 — General Provisions Article 1 Short Title and Definitions (§§](../README.md) › [Article 9 — School Buildings and Sites](README.md)

```text
  § 415. No schoolhouse shall be built on town line.  No schoolhouse
shall be built so as to stand on the division line of any two towns
unless the commissioner of education approves such location.
```
