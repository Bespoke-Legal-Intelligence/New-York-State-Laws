# Education Law § 407 — Application to certain districts

[Education Law](../../README.md) › [Title 1 — General Provisions Article 1 Short Title and Definitions (§§](../README.md) › [Article 9 — School Buildings and Sites](README.md)

```text
  § 407. Application to certain districts.  The provision of section
four hundred four of this article shall apply to union free school
districts and to districts organized under special laws; and the
trustees of such districts and the boards of education organized under
special laws shall be and are hereby clothed with all the powers vested
in trustees in the three preceding sections.
```
