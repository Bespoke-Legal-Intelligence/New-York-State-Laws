# Education Law § 357 — Statutory or contract colleges

[Education Law](../../README.md) › [Title 1 — General Provisions Article 1 Short Title and Definitions (§§](../README.md) › [Article 8 — State University and State Financial Assistance For Higher Education](README.md)

```text
  § 357. Statutory or contract colleges.  Statutory or contract colleges
shall continue to be operated pursuant to the provisions of this chapter
but such colleges shall be subject to the general supervision of the
state university trustees.
```
