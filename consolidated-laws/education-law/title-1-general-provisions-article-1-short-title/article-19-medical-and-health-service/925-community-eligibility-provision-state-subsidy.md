# Education Law § 925 — Community eligibility provision state subsidy

[Education Law](../../README.md) › [Title 1 — General Provisions Article 1 Short Title and Definitions (§§](../README.md) › [Article 19 — Medical and Health Service](README.md)

```text
  * § 925. Community eligibility provision state subsidy.
Notwithstanding any provision of law, rule or regulation to the
contrary, in the two thousand twenty-three--two thousand twenty-four
school year and thereafter, for each breakfast and lunch meal that is
served at a school participating in the federal community eligibility
provision program and that is reimbursed at the federal reimbursement
rate for a paid meal, the department shall reimburse the school food
authority the difference between (1) the combined state and federal
reimbursement rate for a paid meal for the current school year and (2)
the combined state and federal reimbursement rate for a free meal for
the current school year, provided that the total reimbursement rate for
each meal served shall equal the combined state and federal
reimbursement rate for a free meal for the current school year.
  * NB Effective July 1, 2023
```
