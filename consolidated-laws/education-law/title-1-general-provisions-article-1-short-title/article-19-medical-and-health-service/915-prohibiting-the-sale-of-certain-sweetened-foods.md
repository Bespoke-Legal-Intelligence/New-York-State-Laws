# Education Law § 915 — Prohibiting the sale of certain sweetened foods

[Education Law](../../README.md) › [Title 1 — General Provisions Article 1 Short Title and Definitions (§§](../README.md) › [Article 19 — Medical and Health Service](README.md)

```text
  § 915. Prohibiting the sale of certain sweetened foods. From the
beginning of the school day until the end of the last scheduled meal
period, no sweetened soda water, no chewing gum, no candy including hard
candy, jellies, gums, marshmallow candies, fondant, licorice, spun candy
and candy coated popcorn, and no water ices except those which contain
fruit or fruit juices, shall be sold in any public school within the
state.
```
