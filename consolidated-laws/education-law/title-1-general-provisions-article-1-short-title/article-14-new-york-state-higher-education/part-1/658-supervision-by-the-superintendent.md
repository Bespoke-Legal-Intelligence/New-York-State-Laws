# Education Law § 658 — Supervision by the superintendent of financial services

[Education Law](../../../README.md) › [Title 1 — General Provisions Article 1 Short Title and Definitions (§§](../../README.md) › [Article 14 — New York State Higher Education Services Corporation](../README.md) › [Part 1 — Corporate Structure](README.md)

```text
  § 658. Supervision by the superintendent of financial services. The
corporation in its administration of student loan programs shall be
subject to the supervision and examination of the superintendent of
financial services, but shall not be deemed to be a banking organization
nor required to pay a fee for any such supervision or examination.
```
