# Education Law § 695-G — Scholarships and financial aid

[Education Law](../../README.md) › [Title 1 — General Provisions Article 1 Short Title and Definitions (§§](../README.md) › [Article 14-A — New York State College Choice Tuition Savings Program](README.md)

```text
  § 695-g. Scholarships and financial aid.  Monies in a family tuition
account shall not be used toward the calculation of New York state
financial aid under a financial aid program administered by the state.
```
