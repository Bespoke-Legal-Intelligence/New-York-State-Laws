# Education Law § 1115 — Indebtedness of special education district

[Education Law](../../../README.md) › [Title 1 — General Provisions Article 1 Short Title and Definitions (§§](../../README.md) › [Article 23 — Career Education In Counties](../README.md) › [Part 2 — County Commission For Special Education In Westchester County](README.md)

```text
  § 1115. Indebtedness of special education district.  For the purpose
of giving effect to the provisions of part two of this article the board
of supervisors or other governing elective body of such county may issue
obligations of the county pursuant to the provisions of the local
finance law, which obligations shall be a charge upon the special
education district.  Whenever at the time of its enlargement the special
education district shall have an outstanding bonded indebtedness, such
outstanding indebtedness shall become a charge against the enlarged
district.
```
