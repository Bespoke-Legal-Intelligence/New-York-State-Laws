# Education Law § 246 — State medical library

[Education Law](../../../README.md) › [Title 1 — General Provisions Article 1 Short Title and Definitions (§§](../../README.md) › [Article 5 — University of the State of New York](../README.md) › [Part 2 — Libraries](README.md)

```text
  § 246. State medical library.  The state medical library shall be a
part of the New York state library under the same government and
regulations and shall be open for consultation to every citizen of the
state at all hours when the state library is open and shall be available
for borrowing books to every accredited physician residing in the state
of New York, who shall conform to the rules made by the regents for
insuring proper protection and the largest usefulness to the people of
the said medical library.
```
