# Education Law § 249-A — Libraries of public institutions of higher education; access and use

[Education Law](../../../README.md) › [Title 1 — General Provisions Article 1 Short Title and Definitions (§§](../../README.md) › [Article 5 — University of the State of New York](../README.md) › [Part 2 — Libraries](README.md)

```text
  § 249-a. Libraries of public institutions of higher education; access
and use.  The state university trustees and the board of higher
education of the city of New York are hereby authorized to establish
such rules and regulations as may be necessary and appropriate to make
provision for access and use by the residents of the state of the
libraries and library facilities of the public institutions of higher
education under their respective jurisdictions.
```
