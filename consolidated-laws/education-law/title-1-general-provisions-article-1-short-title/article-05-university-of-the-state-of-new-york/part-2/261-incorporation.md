# Education Law § 261 — Incorporation

[Education Law](../../../README.md) › [Title 1 — General Provisions Article 1 Short Title and Definitions (§§](../../README.md) › [Article 5 — University of the State of New York](../README.md) › [Part 2 — Libraries](README.md)

```text
  § 261. Incorporation.  Within one month after taking office, the first
board of trustees of any such public library or Indian library shall
apply to the regents for a charter in accordance with the vote
establishing the library.
```
