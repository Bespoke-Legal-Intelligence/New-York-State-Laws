# Education Law § 291 — Custodian of funds

[Education Law](../../../README.md) › [Title 1 — General Provisions Article 1 Short Title and Definitions (§§](../../README.md) › [Article 5 — University of the State of New York](../README.md) › [Part 3 — Federal Funds For Vocational Education](README.md)

```text
  § 291. Custodian of funds.  The state commissioner of taxation and
finance and the state comptroller are hereby constituted and appointed
the joint custodians of the moneys paid to the state of New York for
vocational education, under the provisions of such act, and such moneys
shall be paid out in the manner provided by such act for the purposes
therein specified.
```
