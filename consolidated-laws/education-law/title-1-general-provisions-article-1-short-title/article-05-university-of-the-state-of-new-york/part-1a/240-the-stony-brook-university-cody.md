# Education Law § 240 — The Stony Brook university Cody center for autism and developmental disabilities at Stony Brook university

[Education Law](../../../README.md) › [Title 1 — General Provisions Article 1 Short Title and Definitions (§§](../../README.md) › [Article 5 — University of the State of New York](../README.md) › [Part 1-A — Cody Center For Autism and Developmental Disabilities](README.md)

```text
  § 240. The Stony Brook university Cody center for autism and
developmental disabilities at Stony Brook university. The Stony Brook
university Cody center for autism and developmental disabilities
(hereinafter the "Cody center") is hereby established under the
jurisdiction of the state university of New York. The Cody center shall
be administered as a program of the state university in accordance with
the policies of the trustees of the state university.
```
