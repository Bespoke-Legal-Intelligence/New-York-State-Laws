# Education Law § 215-D — State university of New York report on economic development activities

[Education Law](../../../README.md) › [Title 1 — General Provisions Article 1 Short Title and Definitions (§§](../../README.md) › [Article 5 — University of the State of New York](../README.md) › [Part 1 — General Organization](README.md)

```text
  § 215-d. State university of New York report on economic development
activities. The chancellor of the state university of New York shall
report to the governor and to the legislature, on or before January
first, two thousand fifteen, on economic development activities
undertaken by the state university of New York. Such report shall
include, but not be limited to, expenditures of capital funds for
economic development activities received from the empire state
development corporation, SUNY 2020 challenge grant projects, capital
expenditures from other sources, and activities for the purpose of
securing START-UP NY approval.
```
