# Education Law § 208-A — Scheduling of examinations

[Education Law](../../../README.md) › [Title 1 — General Provisions Article 1 Short Title and Definitions (§§](../../README.md) › [Article 5 — University of the State of New York](../README.md) › [Part 1 — General Organization](README.md)

```text
  § 208-a. Scheduling of examinations. The department shall make a bona
fide effort to schedule state mandated examinations on days other than
days of religious observation. The commissioner may promulgate
regulations to implement this section.
```
