# Education Law § 212-C — Teacher accreditation review fees

[Education Law](../../../README.md) › [Title 1 — General Provisions Article 1 Short Title and Definitions (§§](../../README.md) › [Article 5 — University of the State of New York](../README.md) › [Part 1 — General Organization](README.md)

```text
  § 212-c. Teacher accreditation review fees. The costs of accreditation
reviews of teacher education programs shall be borne by the institution
seeking accreditation by the board of regents. The commissioner shall
establish and charge fees to cover the costs of the accreditation
visits, any other related visits the commissioner may determine to be
necessary, and related expenses. Fees collected shall be deposited to
the credit of the teacher education accreditation account and disbursed
pursuant to section ninety-nine-h of the state finance law. The
commissioner shall be authorized to adopt any regulations needed to
implement the provisions of this section.
```
