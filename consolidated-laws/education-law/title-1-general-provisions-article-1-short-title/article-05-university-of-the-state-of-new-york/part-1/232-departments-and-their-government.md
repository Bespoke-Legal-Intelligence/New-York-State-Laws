# Education Law § 232 — Departments and their government

[Education Law](../../../README.md) › [Title 1 — General Provisions Article 1 Short Title and Definitions (§§](../../README.md) › [Article 5 — University of the State of New York](../README.md) › [Part 1 — General Organization](README.md)

```text
  § 232. Departments and their government.  The state library and state
museum shall be departments of the university, and the regents may
establish such other departments and divisions therein as they shall
deem useful in the discharge of their duties.
```
