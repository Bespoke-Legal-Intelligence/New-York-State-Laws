# Education Law § 111 — Indian affairs

[Education Law](../../../README.md) › [Title 1 — General Provisions Article 1 Short Title and Definitions (§§](../../README.md) › [Article 3 — Education Department](../README.md) › [Part 1 — General Provisions](README.md)

```text
  § 111. Indian affairs.  The education department, by or through the
appropriate division, bureau or officer thereof, shall exercise and
perform the functions, powers and duties conferred or imposed by law in
relation to the education of children upon the Indian reservations.
```
