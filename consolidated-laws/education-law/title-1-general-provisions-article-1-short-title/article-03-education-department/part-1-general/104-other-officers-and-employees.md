# Education Law § 104 — Other officers and employees

[Education Law](../../../README.md) › [Title 1 — General Provisions Article 1 Short Title and Definitions (§§](../../README.md) › [Article 3 — Education Department](../README.md) › [Part 1 — General Provisions](README.md)

```text
  § 104. Other officers and employees.  The commissioner of education,
subject to the approval of the regents, shall have power, in conformity
with their rules, to appoint all other needed officers and employees and
fix their titles, duties and salaries.
```
