# Education Law § 106 — Joint seal

[Education Law](../../../README.md) › [Title 1 — General Provisions Article 1 Short Title and Definitions (§§](../../README.md) › [Article 3 — Education Department](../README.md) › [Part 1 — General Provisions](README.md)

```text
  § 106. Joint seal.  The regents of the university upon the
recommendation of the commissioner of education shall adopt, and may
modify at any time, a seal, which shall be used in common as the seal of
the education department and of the university; and copies of all
records thereof and of all acts, orders, decrees and decisions made by
the regents or by the commissioner of education, and of their official
papers, and of the drafts or machine copies of any of the foregoing, may
be authenticated under the said seal and shall then be evidence equally
with and in like manner as the originals.
```
