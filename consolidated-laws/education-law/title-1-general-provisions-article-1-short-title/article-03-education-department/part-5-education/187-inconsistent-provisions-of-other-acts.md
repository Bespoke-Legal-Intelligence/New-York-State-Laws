# Education Law § 187 — Inconsistent provisions of other acts superseded

[Education Law](../../../README.md) › [Title 1 — General Provisions Article 1 Short Title and Definitions (§§](../../README.md) › [Article 3 — Education Department](../README.md) › [Part 5 — Education Department Optional Retirement Program](README.md)

```text
  § 187. Inconsistent provisions of other acts superseded.  Insofar as
the provisions of this part V of this article are inconsistent with the
provisions of any other act, general or special, the provisions of this
article shall be controlling.
```
