# Education Law § 518-A — Supplemental retirement allowance fund

[Education Law](../../README.md) › [Title 1 — General Provisions Article 1 Short Title and Definitions (§§](../README.md) › [Article 11 — State Teachers' Retirement System For Public School Teachers](README.md)

```text
  § 518-a. Supplemental retirement allowance fund.  The supplemental
retirement allowance fund shall be the fund from which shall be paid the
supplemental retirement allowances provided by section five hundred
thirty-two of this article.
```
