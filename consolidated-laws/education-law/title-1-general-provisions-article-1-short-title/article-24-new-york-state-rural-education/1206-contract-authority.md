# Education Law § 1206 — Contract authority

[Education Law](../../README.md) › [Title 1 — General Provisions Article 1 Short Title and Definitions (§§](../README.md) › [Article 24 — New York State Rural Education Program Act](README.md)

```text
  § 1206. Contract authority. The center, after consultation with the
committee, is hereby empowered to enter into any agreement or contract
with any private or public agencies, corporations, or individuals
necessary or convenient to carry out the provisions of this article.
```
