# Education Law § 3816 — Duty of trustees or board of education to carry out order

[Education Law](../../README.md) › [Title 5 — Taxation and Financial Administration](../README.md) › [Article 77 — Fines, Penalties, Forfeitures and Costs](README.md)

```text
  § 3816. Duty of trustees or board of education to carry out order.  It
shall be the duty of the trustees or board of education of any school
district, within thirty days after service upon them or upon the
district clerk of a copy of an order of the county judge and notice
thereof to them or any two of them, to cause the same to be entered at
length in the book of record of said district, and to raise the amount
thereby directed to be paid, by a tax upon the district, to be by them
assessed and levied in the same manner as a tax voted by the district.
```
