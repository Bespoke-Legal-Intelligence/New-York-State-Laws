# Education Law § 3607 — Manner of payment of moneys apportioned

[Education Law](../../../README.md) › [Title 5 — Taxation and Financial Administration](../../README.md) › [Article 73 — Apportionment of Public Moneys](../README.md) › [Part 1 — General Provisions](README.md)

```text
  § 3607. Manner of payment of moneys apportioned.  Upon the audit of
vouchers certified by the commissioner of education, the comptroller
shall draw his warrant on the commissioner of taxation and finance for
payment of any moneys apportioned for the support of common schools.
Such payment shall be made to the treasurer, if there be one, otherwise
to the disbursing officer or collector of each school district, and to
the commissioner of finance of the city of New York for the city school
district of the city of New York, through the commissioner of education.
```
