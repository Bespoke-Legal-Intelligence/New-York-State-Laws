# Education Law § 3628 — Excess expenditures

[Education Law](../../../README.md) › [Title 5 — Taxation and Financial Administration](../../README.md) › [Article 73 — Apportionment of Public Moneys](../README.md) › [Part 2 — Aidable Transportation Expense](README.md)

```text
  § 3628. Excess expenditures. Unless otherwise provided in this part,
any expenditure for transportation in excess of the allowable
transportation expense as determined according to the provisions of this
part shall not be used in determining the amount of any other form of
state aid.
```
