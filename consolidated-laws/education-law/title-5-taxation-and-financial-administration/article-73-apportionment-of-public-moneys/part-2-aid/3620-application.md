# Education Law § 3620 — Application

[Education Law](../../../README.md) › [Title 5 — Taxation and Financial Administration](../../README.md) › [Article 73 — Apportionment of Public Moneys](../README.md) › [Part 2 — Aidable Transportation Expense](README.md)

```text
  § 3620. Application.  Part two of this article applies to all school
districts which receive a quota for the transportation of pupils.  It is
not intended by any provision in part two of this article to change or
modify in any manner the provisions of this chapter providing and
granting transportation to all children irrespective of the school they
legally attend.
```
