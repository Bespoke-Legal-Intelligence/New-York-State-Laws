# Education Law § 3650-C — Accident report data base

[Education Law](../../../README.md) › [Title 5 — Taxation and Financial Administration](../../README.md) › [Article 73 — Apportionment of Public Moneys](../README.md) › [Part 5 — Comprehensive School Bus Driver Safety Training Program](README.md)

```text
  § 3650-c. Accident report data base. The commissioner, in consultation
with the commissioner of motor vehicles, shall establish an electronic
data file containing accident reports relating to school buses.
```
