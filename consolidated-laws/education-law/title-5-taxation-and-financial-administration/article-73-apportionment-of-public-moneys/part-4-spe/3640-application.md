# Education Law § 3640 — Application

[Education Law](../../../README.md) › [Title 5 — Taxation and Financial Administration](../../README.md) › [Article 73 — Apportionment of Public Moneys](../README.md) › [Part 4 — Special Apportionments and Grants-in-aid](README.md)

```text
  § 3640. Application.  Part IV of this article shall apply to the
apportionment of public moneys for certain special apportionments and
grants-in-aid to school districts that are payable to the special aid
fund of the school district, and for certain grants-in-aid to other
entities.
```
