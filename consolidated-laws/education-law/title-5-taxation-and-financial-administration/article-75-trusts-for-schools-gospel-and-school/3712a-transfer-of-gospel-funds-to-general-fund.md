# Education Law § 3712-A — Transfer of gospel funds to general fund of town

[Education Law](../../README.md) › [Title 5 — Taxation and Financial Administration](../README.md) › [Article 75 — Trusts For Schools; Gospel and School Lots](README.md)

```text
  § 3712-a. Transfer of gospel funds to general fund of town.  In lieu
of the apportionment and payment of gospel funds as otherwise authorized
by this article, the town board of any town may, by resolution duly
adopted by it and approved by the voters of such town at any regular or
special town meeting, authorize the supervisor thereof to transfer such
funds to the general fund of the town, to be expended for any lawful
town purpose.
```
