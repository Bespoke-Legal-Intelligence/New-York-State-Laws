# Education Law § 3708 — Bond required of collector or treasurer

[Education Law](../../README.md) › [Title 5 — Taxation and Financial Administration](../README.md) › [Article 75 — Trusts For Schools; Gospel and School Lots](README.md)

```text
  § 3708. Bond required of collector or treasurer.  The collector or the
treasurer, if the district has a treasurer, of each of such school
districts shall execute and file with the supervisor of such town a bond
of twice the amount of such apportionment with sufficient sureties, to
be approved by such supervisor.
```
