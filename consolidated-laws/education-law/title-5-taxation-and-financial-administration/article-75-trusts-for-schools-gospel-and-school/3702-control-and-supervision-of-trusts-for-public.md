# Education Law § 3702 — Control and supervision of trusts for public schools

[Education Law](../../README.md) › [Title 5 — Taxation and Financial Administration](../README.md) › [Article 75 — Trusts For Schools; Gospel and School Lots](README.md)

```text
  § 3702. Control and supervision of trusts for public schools.  The
legislature may control and regulate the execution of all such trusts;
and the commissioner of education shall supervise and advise the
trustees, and hold them to a regular accounting for the trust property
and its income and interest at such times, in such forms, and with such
authentications, as he shall, from time to time, prescribe.
```
