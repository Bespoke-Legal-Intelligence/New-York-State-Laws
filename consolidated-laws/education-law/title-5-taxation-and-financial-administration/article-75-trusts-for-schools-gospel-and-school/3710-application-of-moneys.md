# Education Law § 3710 — Application of moneys

[Education Law](../../README.md) › [Title 5 — Taxation and Financial Administration](../README.md) › [Article 75 — Trusts For Schools; Gospel and School Lots](README.md)

```text
  § 3710. Application of moneys.  Such moneys shall be held by such
collector or treasurer and paid upon the written order of the trustee or
board of education of the district for such purposes as the annual or a
special meeting of the district shall direct.
```
