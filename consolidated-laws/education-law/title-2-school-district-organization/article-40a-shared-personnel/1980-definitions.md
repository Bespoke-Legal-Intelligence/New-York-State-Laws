# Education Law § 1980 — Definitions

[Education Law](../../README.md) › [Title 2 — School District Organization](../README.md) › [Article 40-A — Shared Personnel](README.md)

```text
  § 1980. Definitions.  When used in this article, the following terms
shall mean:
  1. The term "board of education" shall mean the trustee, trustees or
board of education of any common, union free, central, central high or
city school district, or a board of cooperative educational services.
  2. The term "school district" shall mean any common, union free,
central, central high or city school district, or a board of cooperative
educational services.
  3. The term "district superintendent" shall mean the chief
administrative officer of a supervisory district as defined in article
forty-five of this chapter.
```
