# Education Law § 2613 — Absentee ballots

[Education Law](../../README.md) › [Title 2 — School District Organization](../README.md) › [Article 53 — School Elections In City School Districts of Cities With Less Than One Hundred Twenty-five Thousand Inhabitants](README.md)

```text
  § 2613. Absentee ballots. The board of education of each city school
district to which this article applies shall provide for absentee
ballots in accordance with the provisions of section two thousand
eighteen-a of this chapter.
```
