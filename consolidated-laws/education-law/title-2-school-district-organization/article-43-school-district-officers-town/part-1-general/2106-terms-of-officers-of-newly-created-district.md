# Education Law § 2106 — Terms of officers of newly created district

[Education Law](../../../README.md) › [Title 2 — School District Organization](../../README.md) › [Article 43 — School District Officers--town and County Officials](../README.md) › [Part 1 — General Provisions](README.md)

```text
  § 2106. Terms of officers of newly created district.  The terms of all
officers other than trustees elected at the first meeting of a newly
created district shall expire on the first Tuesday of May, next
thereafter.
```
