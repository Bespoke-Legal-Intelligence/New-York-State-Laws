# Education Law § 2101 — Officers of district

[Education Law](../../../README.md) › [Title 2 — School District Organization](../../README.md) › [Article 43 — School District Officers--town and County Officials](../README.md) › [Part 1 — General Provisions](README.md)

```text
  § 2101. Officers of district.  1. Each common school district shall
have one or three trustees as the district determines, a collector
except in first class towns and except as may be otherwise provided by
law, and if the district so decides a treasurer.
  2. Each union free school district shall have a board of education
consisting of from three to nine trustees as the district shall
determine.
```
