# Education Law § 2129 — Deposit of moneys by treasurer and collector

[Education Law](../../../README.md) › [Title 2 — School District Organization](../../README.md) › [Article 43 — School District Officers--town and County Officials](../README.md) › [Part 2 — District Clerk; Treasurer; Collector](README.md)

```text
  § 2129. Deposit of moneys by treasurer and collector.  All moneys
received by the treasurer and collector shall be deposited and secured
in the manner provided by section ten of the general municipal law.
```
