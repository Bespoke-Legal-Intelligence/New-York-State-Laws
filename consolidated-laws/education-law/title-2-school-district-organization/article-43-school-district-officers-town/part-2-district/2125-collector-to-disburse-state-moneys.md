# Education Law § 2125 — Collector to disburse state moneys

[Education Law](../../../README.md) › [Title 2 — School District Organization](../../README.md) › [Article 43 — School District Officers--town and County Officials](../README.md) › [Part 2 — District Clerk; Treasurer; Collector](README.md)

```text
  § 2125. Collector to disburse state moneys.  In any school district
which does not have a treasurer it shall be the duty of the collector of
such district to disburse the money apportioned by the state.
```
