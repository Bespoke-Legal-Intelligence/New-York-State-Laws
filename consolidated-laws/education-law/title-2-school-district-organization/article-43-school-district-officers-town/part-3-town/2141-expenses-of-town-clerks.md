# Education Law § 2141 — Expenses of town clerks

[Education Law](../../../README.md) › [Title 2 — School District Organization](../../README.md) › [Article 43 — School District Officers--town and County Officials](../README.md) › [Part 3 — Town and County Officials](README.md)

```text
  § 2141.  Expenses of town clerks.  The necessary expenses and
disbursements of the town clerk in the performance of his said duties,
are a town charge, and shall be audited and paid as such.
```
