# Education Law § 1531 — Procedure

[Education Law](../../README.md) › [Title 2 — School District Organization](../README.md) › [Article 32 — Filing of Expenditure Statements By Candidates For Membership On Board of Education](README.md)

```text
  § 1531. Procedure.  A special proceeding under the foregoing
provisions of this article shall be heard upon a verified petition and
such oral or written proof as may be offered, and upon such notice to
such officers, persons or candidates as the court, justice or judge
shall direct and shall be summarily determined.
```
