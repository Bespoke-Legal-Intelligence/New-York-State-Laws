# Education Law § 2590-O*2 — Neutrality of school employees in community board elections

[Education Law](../../README.md) › [Title 2 — School District Organization](../README.md) › [Article 52-A — New York City Community School District System](README.md)

*An asterisk in a number (for example Article 24\* or § 20-C\*2) is the Senate's mark for a number the Legislature has used more than once: the marked article or section is a different one from the unmarked one with the same number. \*2 means the second one enacted with that number.*

```text
  * § 2590-o. Neutrality of school employees in community board
elections. 1. In their official capacities during school hours all
school, community district and city district employees shall maintain a
posture of complete neutrality with respect to elections of candidates
for community board. No school, community district or city district
facilities or supplies shall be used on behalf of any candidate.
  2. The city board shall have the authority and responsibility with
regard to the investigation and trial of employees against whom
violations of this section are charged. Wilful violations of this
section shall constitute cause for removal from office or termination of
employment.
  3. The city board shall adopt regulations to achieve the purposes of
this section.
  * NB There are 2 § 2590-o's
```
