# Education Law § 2590-T — New York city comptroller audits

[Education Law](../../README.md) › [Title 2 — School District Organization](../README.md) › [Article 52-A — New York City Community School District System](README.md)

```text
  * § 2590-t. New York city comptroller audits. The comptroller of the
city of New York shall have the authority to conduct operational and
programmatic audits, in addition to financial audits, of the city
district to the same extent that such comptroller has such authority for
agencies of the city of New York.
  * NB Repealed June 30, 2022
```
