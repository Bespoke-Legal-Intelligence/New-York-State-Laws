# Education Law § 1609 — Annual report of trustees of certain joint districts

[Education Law](../../README.md) › [Title 2 — School District Organization](../README.md) › [Article 33 — Common School Districts](README.md)

```text
  § 1609. Annual report of trustees of certain joint districts.  Where a
common school district lies in two or more counties, its trustees shall
file their annual report in the office of the district superintendent
having jurisdiction over the schools of the town in which the principal
schoolhouse of the district is situated.
```
