# Education Law § 1601 — Trustees constitute a board and body corporate

[Education Law](../../README.md) › [Title 2 — School District Organization](../README.md) › [Article 33 — Common School Districts](README.md)

```text
  § 1601. Trustees constitute a board and body corporate.  The sole
trustee or the trustees of a common school district shall constitute a
board for such district and such board is hereby created a body
corporate.
```
