# Education Law § 1613 — Trustees' right of action against predecessor

[Education Law](../../README.md) › [Title 2 — School District Organization](../README.md) › [Article 33 — Common School Districts](README.md)

```text
  § 1613. Trustees' right of action against predecessor.  The trustees
in office shall sue for and recover any district moneys in the hands of
any former trustee, or of his personal representatives, and apply them
to the use of the district.
```
