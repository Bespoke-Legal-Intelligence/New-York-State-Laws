# Education Law § 1611 — Penalty for failure of trustee to account

[Education Law](../../README.md) › [Title 2 — School District Organization](../README.md) › [Article 33 — Common School Districts](README.md)

```text
  § 1611. Penalty for failure of trustee to account.  By a wilful
neglect or refusal to render such account, a trustee forfeits any
unexpired term of his office, and becomes liable to the trustees for any
district moneys in his hands.
```
