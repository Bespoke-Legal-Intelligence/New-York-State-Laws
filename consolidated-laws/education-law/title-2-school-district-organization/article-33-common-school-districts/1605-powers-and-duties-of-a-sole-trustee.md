# Education Law § 1605 — Powers and duties of a sole trustee

[Education Law](../../README.md) › [Title 2 — School District Organization](../README.md) › [Article 33 — Common School Districts](README.md)

```text
  § 1605. Powers and duties of a sole trustee.  The sole trustee of a
common school district shall possess all the powers and be subject to
all the duties, liabilities and penalties which the law imposes upon a
board of three trustees.
```
