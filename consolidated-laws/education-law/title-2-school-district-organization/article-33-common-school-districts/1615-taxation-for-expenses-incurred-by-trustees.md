# Education Law § 1615 — Taxation for expenses incurred by trustees

[Education Law](../../README.md) › [Title 2 — School District Organization](../README.md) › [Article 33 — Common School Districts](README.md)

```text
  § 1615. Taxation for expenses incurred by trustees.  When trustees are
required or authorized by law, or by a vote of their district, to incur
any expenses for such district, and when any expenses incurred by them
are made, by express provision of law, a charge upon such district, they
may raise the amount thereof by tax in the same manner as if the
definite sum to be raised had been voted by a district meeting.
```
