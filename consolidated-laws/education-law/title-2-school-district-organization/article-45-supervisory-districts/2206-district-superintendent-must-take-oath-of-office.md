# Education Law § 2206 — District superintendent must take oath of office

[Education Law](../../README.md) › [Title 2 — School District Organization](../README.md) › [Article 45 — Supervisory Districts](README.md)

```text
  § 2206. District superintendent must take oath of office. A district
superintendent of schools before entering upon the discharge of the
duties of his office, and not later than five days after the date on
which his term of office is to commence, shall take the oath of office
prescribed by the constitution. Such oath may be taken before a county
clerk, a justice of the peace, or a notary public, and must be filed in
the office of the secretary of state.
```
