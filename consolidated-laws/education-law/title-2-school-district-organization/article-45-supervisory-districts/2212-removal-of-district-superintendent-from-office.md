# Education Law § 2212 — Removal of district superintendent from office

[Education Law](../../README.md) › [Title 2 — School District Organization](../README.md) › [Article 45 — Supervisory Districts](README.md)

```text
  § 2212. Removal of district superintendent from office.  A district
superintendent may be removed from office at any time upon the
affirmative vote of a majority of the members of the board of
cooperative educational services, or by the commissioner pursuant to
section three hundred six of this chapter.
```
