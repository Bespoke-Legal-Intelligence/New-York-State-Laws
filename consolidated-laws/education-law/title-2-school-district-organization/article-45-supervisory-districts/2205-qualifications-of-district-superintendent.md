# Education Law § 2205 — Qualifications of district superintendent

[Education Law](../../README.md) › [Title 2 — School District Organization](../README.md) › [Article 45 — Supervisory Districts](README.md)

```text
  § 2205. Qualifications of district superintendent. 1. To be eligible
to appointment to the office of district superintendent of schools a
person must be at least twenty-one years of age, a citizen of the United
States and a resident of the state. No person shall be ineligible on
account of sex.
  2. In addition thereto he shall possess or be entitled to a
superintendent's certificate as provided in section three thousand three
of this chapter.
  3. A district superintendent who is removed from office shall not be
eligible for appointment to such office in any supervisory district for
a period of five years.
```
