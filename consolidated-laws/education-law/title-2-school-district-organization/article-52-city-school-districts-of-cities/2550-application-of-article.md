# Education Law § 2550 — Application of article

[Education Law](../../README.md) › [Title 2 — School District Organization](../README.md) › [Article 52 — City School Districts of Cities With One Hundred Twenty-five Thousand Inhabitants or More](README.md)

```text
  § 2550. Application of article.  This article shall apply to the city
school districts of the following cities only: New York, Buffalo,
Rochester, Syracuse and Yonkers.
```
