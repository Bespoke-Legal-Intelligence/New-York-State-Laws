# Education Law § 2563 — Meetings of board of education

[Education Law](../../README.md) › [Title 2 — School District Organization](../README.md) › [Article 52 — City School Districts of Cities With One Hundred Twenty-five Thousand Inhabitants or More](README.md)

```text
  § 2563. Meetings of board of education.  1. The annual meeting of a
board of education shall be held on the second Tuesday in May, at four
o'clock in the afternoon, at which meeting the board shall select a
president for the ensuing year, except that the annual meeting of the
board of education of the city school district of the city of New York
shall be held on the first Tuesday in July.
  2. Each of such boards shall also fix the day and hour for holding
regular board meetings which shall be at least as often as once each
month and shall also prescribe a method for calling special meetings of
such board.
```
