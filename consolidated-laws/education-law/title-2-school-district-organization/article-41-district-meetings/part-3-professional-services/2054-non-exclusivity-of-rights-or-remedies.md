# Education Law § 2054 — Non-exclusivity of rights or remedies

[Education Law](../../../README.md) › [Title 2 — School District Organization](../../README.md) › [Article 41 — District Meetings](../README.md) › [Part 3 — Professional Services Providers](README.md)

```text
  § 2054. Non-exclusivity of rights or remedies. Nothing in this part
shall be construed to limit, in any matter, any rights or remedies
otherwise available under law against any party or to any person or
entity, including, but not limited to, the attorney general or the
comptroller of the state of New York.
```
