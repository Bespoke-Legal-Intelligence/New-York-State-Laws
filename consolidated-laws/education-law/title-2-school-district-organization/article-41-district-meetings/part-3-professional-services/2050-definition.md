# Education Law § 2050 — Definition

[Education Law](../../../README.md) › [Title 2 — School District Organization](../../README.md) › [Article 41 — District Meetings](../README.md) › [Part 3 — Professional Services Providers](README.md)

```text
  § 2050. Definition. As used in this part, a "lawyer" shall mean an
attorney or counselor governed by article fifteen of the judiciary law,
who receives remuneration or other compensation from a school district
or board of cooperative educational services in exchange for legal
services provided to such district or board.
```
