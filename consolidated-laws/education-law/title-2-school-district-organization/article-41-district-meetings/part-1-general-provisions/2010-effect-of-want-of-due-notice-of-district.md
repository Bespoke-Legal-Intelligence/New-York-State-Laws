# Education Law § 2010 — Effect of want of due notice of district meetings

[Education Law](../../../README.md) › [Title 2 — School District Organization](../../README.md) › [Article 41 — District Meetings](../README.md) › [Part 1 — General Provisions](README.md)

```text
  § 2010. Effect of want of due notice of district meetings.  The
proceedings of no district meeting, annual or special, shall be held
illegal for want of a due notice to all the persons qualified to vote
thereat, unless it shall appear that the omission to give such notice
was wilful and fraudulent.
```
