# Education Law § 2024 — Reference to commissioner of education

[Education Law](../../../README.md) › [Title 2 — School District Organization](../../README.md) › [Article 41 — District Meetings](../README.md) › [Part 1 — General Provisions](README.md)

```text
  § 2024. Reference to commissioner of education.  If any question shall
arise as to what are ordinary contingent expenses the same may be
referred to the commissioner of education, by a statement in writing,
signed by one or more of each of the opposing parties upon the question,
and the decision of the commissioner shall be conclusive.
```
