# Education Law § 2037 — Determination of meeting or election disputes

[Education Law](../../../README.md) › [Title 2 — School District Organization](../../README.md) › [Article 41 — District Meetings](../README.md) › [Part 1 — General Provisions](README.md)

```text
  § 2037. Determination of meeting or election disputes.  All disputes
concerning the validity of any district meeting or election or of any of
the acts of the officers of such meeting or election shall be referred
to the commissioner of education for determination and his decisions in
the matter shall be final and not subject to review.  The commissioner
may in his discretion order a new meeting or election.
```
