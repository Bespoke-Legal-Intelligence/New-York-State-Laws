# Education Law § 2029 — Poll list

[Education Law](../../../README.md) › [Title 2 — School District Organization](../../README.md) › [Article 41 — District Meetings](../README.md) › [Part 1 — General Provisions](README.md)

```text
  § 2029. Poll list.  A poll list containing the name and legal
residence of every person whose vote shall be received shall be kept by
the clerk and the assistant clerk or clerks of the meeting or election.
```
