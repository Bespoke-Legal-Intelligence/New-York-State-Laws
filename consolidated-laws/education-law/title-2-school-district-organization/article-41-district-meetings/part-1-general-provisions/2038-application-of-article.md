# Education Law § 2038 — Application of article

[Education Law](../../../README.md) › [Title 2 — School District Organization](../../README.md) › [Article 41 — District Meetings](../README.md) › [Part 1 — General Provisions](README.md)

```text
  § 2038. Application of article.  The provisions of part one of this
article shall apply to all school districts except as otherwise provided
by article fifty-three of this chapter, and except to city school
districts of cities with one hundred twenty-five thousand inhabitants or
more.
```
