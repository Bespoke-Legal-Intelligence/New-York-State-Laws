# Education Law § 2033 — Voting after closing of polls

[Education Law](../../../README.md) › [Title 2 — School District Organization](../../README.md) › [Article 41 — District Meetings](../README.md) › [Part 1 — General Provisions](README.md)

```text
  § 2033. Voting after closing of polls.  All qualified voters entitled
to vote who are in the place where the meeting or election is held at or
before the time of closing the polls shall be allowed to vote.
```
