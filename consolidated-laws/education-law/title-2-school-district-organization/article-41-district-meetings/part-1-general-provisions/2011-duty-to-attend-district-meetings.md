# Education Law § 2011 — Duty to attend district meetings

[Education Law](../../../README.md) › [Title 2 — School District Organization](../../README.md) › [Article 41 — District Meetings](../README.md) › [Part 1 — General Provisions](README.md)

```text
  § 2011. Duty to attend district meetings.  Whenever any district
meeting shall be duly called, it shall be the duty of the inhabitants
qualified to vote thereat, to assemble at the time and place fixed for
the meeting.
```
