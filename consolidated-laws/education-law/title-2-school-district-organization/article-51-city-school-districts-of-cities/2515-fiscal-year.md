# Education Law § 2515 — Fiscal year

[Education Law](../../README.md) › [Title 2 — School District Organization](../README.md) › [Article 51 — City School Districts of Cities With Less Than One Hundred Twenty-five Thousand Inhabitants](README.md)

```text
  § 2515. Fiscal year. The fiscal year for  city school districts of
cities with less than one hundred twenty-five thousand inhabitants shall
be the period commencing with July first and ending with June thirtieth
next following.
```
