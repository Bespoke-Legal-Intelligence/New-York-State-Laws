# Education Law § 1707 — Annual organizational meetings of boards of education

[Education Law](../../README.md) › [Title 2 — School District Organization](../README.md) › [Article 35 — Union Free School Districts](README.md)

```text
  § 1707. Annual organizational meetings of boards of education. 1.  The
annual organizational meeting of the board of education of every union
free school district shall be held on the first Tuesday in July of each
year, unless said day shall be a legal holiday, in which event it shall
fall on the first Wednesday in July.
  2.  The board of education may, however, by resolution, determine that
the annual meeting be held at any time during the first fifteen days in
the month of July.
```
