# Education Law § 1707 — Annual organizational meetings of boards of education

[Education Law](../../README.md) › [Title 2 — School District Organization](../README.md) › [Article 35 — Union Free School Districts](README.md)

```text
  § 1707. Annual organizational meetings of boards of education. The
annual organizational meeting of the board of education of every union
free school district shall be held in July on or before the Monday after
the twentieth day in July of each year.
```
