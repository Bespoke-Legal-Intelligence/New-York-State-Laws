# Education Law § 1706 — Power of removal of member of board of education

[Education Law](../../README.md) › [Title 2 — School District Organization](../README.md) › [Article 35 — Union Free School Districts](README.md)

```text
  § 1706. Power of removal of member of board of education.  For cause
shown, and after giving notice of the charge and opportunity of defense,
the commissioner of education may remove any member of a board of
education.  Wilful disobedience of any lawful requirement of the
commissioner of education, or a want of due diligence in obeying such
requirement or wilful violation or neglect of duty is cause for removal.
```
