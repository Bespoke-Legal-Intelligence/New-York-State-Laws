# Education Law § 1904 — Annual organizational meeting of board of education in the county of Nassau

[Education Law](../../README.md) › [Title 2 — School District Organization](../README.md) › [Article 39 — Central High School Districts](README.md)

```text
  § 1904.  Annual organizational meeting of board of education in the
county of Nassau.  The annual organizational meeting of the board of
education of every central high school district in the county of Nassau
shall be held on the second Tuesday in July of each year.
```
