# Education Law § 1912 — Formation of central high school districts in the county of Suffolk

[Education Law](../../README.md) › [Title 2 — School District Organization](../README.md) › [Article 39 — Central High School Districts](README.md)

```text
  § 1912. Formation of central high school districts in the county of
Suffolk.  A central high school district may be formed by the
combination of two or more central, union free or common school
districts in the county of Suffolk. The territory shall be contiguous.
```
