# Education Law § 1917 — Employees; employment rights

[Education Law](../../README.md) › [Title 2 — School District Organization](../README.md) › [Article 39 — Central High School Districts](README.md)

```text
  § 1917. Employees; employment rights.  Teachers and other staff
members of component districts, except the superintendent of schools,
whose services in the component districts are no longer needed because
of creation of a central high school district, shall be granted
employment rights in central high school districts in accordance with
length of service in each tenure area.
```
