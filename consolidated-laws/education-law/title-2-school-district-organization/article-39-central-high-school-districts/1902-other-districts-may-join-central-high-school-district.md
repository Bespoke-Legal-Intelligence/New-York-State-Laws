# Education Law § 1902 — Other districts may join central high school district

[Education Law](../../README.md) › [Title 2 — School District Organization](../README.md) › [Article 39 — Central High School Districts](README.md)

```text
  § 1902. Other districts may join central high school district.  Other
school districts that were not included in the establishment of the
original central high school district may join such district upon a vote
of the majority of the electors of such district present and voting at a
district meeting duly called for that purpose and upon the consent of
the board of education of the central high school district and the order
of the commissioner of education.
```
