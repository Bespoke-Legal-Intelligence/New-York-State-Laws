# Education Law § 1503 — Application of funds obtained from sale of school property

[Education Law](../../README.md) › [Title 2 — School District Organization](../README.md) › [Article 31 — School Districts](README.md)

```text
  § 1503. Application of funds obtained from sale of school property.
All moneys obtained from the sale of any school property authorized
under the provisions of this chapter shall be applied for the benefit of
the district as directed by the voters thereof in any annual or special
meeting.
```
