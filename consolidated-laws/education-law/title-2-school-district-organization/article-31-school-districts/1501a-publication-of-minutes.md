# Education Law § 1501-A — Publication of minutes

[Education Law](../../README.md) › [Title 2 — School District Organization](../README.md) › [Article 31 — School Districts](README.md)

```text
  § 1501-a. Publication of minutes.  Any school district board of
trustees, board of education or other corporate body may publish the
minutes of its proceedings in the official newspaper or if no official
newspaper has been designated, in any newspaper having general
circulation in the school district.
```
