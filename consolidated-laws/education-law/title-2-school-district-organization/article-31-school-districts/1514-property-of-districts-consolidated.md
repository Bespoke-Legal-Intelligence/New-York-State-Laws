# Education Law § 1514 — Property of districts consolidated

[Education Law](../../README.md) › [Title 2 — School District Organization](../README.md) › [Article 31 — School Districts](README.md)

```text
  § 1514. Property of districts consolidated.  When two or more
districts shall be consolidated into one, the new district shall succeed
to all the rights of property possessed by the annulled districts.
```
