**Short title (from the law itself):** Section 1. Short title. This chapter shall be known as the "Education Law".

## Chapter text as published

```text
                               CHAPTER 820
AN ACT to amend the Education Law, generally.
  Became a law April 14, 1947, with the approval of the Governor.
Passed, three-fifths being present.
  The People of the State of New York, represented in Senate and
Assembly, do enact as follows:
                   CHAPTER 16 OF THE CONSOLIDATED LAWS
                              EDUCATION LAW
```
