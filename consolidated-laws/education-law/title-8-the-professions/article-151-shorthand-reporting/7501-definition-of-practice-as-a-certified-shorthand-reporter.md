# Education Law § 7501 — Definition of practice as a certified shorthand reporter

[Education Law](../../README.md) › [Title 8 — The Professions](../README.md) › [Article 151 — Shorthand Reporting](README.md)

```text
  § 7501. Definition of practice as a certified shorthand reporter.  The
practice as a certified shorthand reporter is defined as engaging, under
such title, in making by a written system of either manual shorthand or
machine shorthand, a verbatim record of any oral court proceeding,
disposition, hearing or proceeding before any grand jury, referee,
commissioner, or arbitrator, or of any convention, deliberative
assembly, or meeting of like character.
```
