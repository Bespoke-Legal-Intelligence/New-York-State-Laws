# Education Law § 7502 — Use of title "certified shorthand reporter"

[Education Law](../../README.md) › [Title 8 — The Professions](../README.md) › [Article 151 — Shorthand Reporting](README.md)

```text
  § 7502. Use of title "certified shorthand reporter".  Only a person
licensed under this article shall be authorized to use the title
"certified shorthand reporter".
```
