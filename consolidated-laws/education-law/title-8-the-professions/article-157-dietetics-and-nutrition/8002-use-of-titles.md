# Education Law § 8002 — Use of titles

[Education Law](../../README.md) › [Title 8 — The Professions](../README.md) › [Article 157 — Dietetics and Nutrition](README.md)

```text
  § 8002. Use of titles.  Only a person certified  under this article
shall be authorized to use the title "certified dietitian", "certified
dietician", or "certified nutritionist".
```
