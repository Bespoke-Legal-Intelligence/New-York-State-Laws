# Education Law § 6844 — Requirements for licensure as a registered pharmacy technician

[Education Law](../../README.md) › [Title 8 — The Professions](../README.md) › [Article 137-A — Registered Pharmacy Technicians](README.md)

```text
  * § 6844. Requirements for licensure as a registered pharmacy
technician. To qualify for licensure as a "registered pharmacy
technician", an applicant shall fulfill the following requirements:
  1. Application: file an application with the department;
  2. Education: have received an education, including high school
graduation or its equivalent, as determined by the department;
  3. Certification from a nationally accredited pharmacy technician
certification program acceptable to the department;
  4. Age: at the time of application be at least eighteen years of age;
  5. Character: be of good moral character as determined by the
department; and
  6. Fee: pay a fee determined by the department for initial license and
for each triennial registration period.
  * NB Effective April 25, 2021
```
