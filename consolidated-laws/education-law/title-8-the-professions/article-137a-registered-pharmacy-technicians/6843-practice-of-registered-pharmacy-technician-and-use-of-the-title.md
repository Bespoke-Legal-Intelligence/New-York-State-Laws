# Education Law § 6843 — Practice of registered pharmacy technician and use of the title "registered pharmacy technician"

[Education Law](../../README.md) › [Title 8 — The Professions](../README.md) › [Article 137-A — Registered Pharmacy Technicians](README.md)

```text
  § 6843. Practice of registered pharmacy technician and use of the
title "registered pharmacy technician". Only a person licensed to
practice as a registered pharmacy technician under this article or
otherwise authorized shall practice as a registered pharmacy technician
or use the title "registered pharmacy technician."
```
