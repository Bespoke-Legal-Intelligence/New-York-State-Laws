# Education Law § 6512* — Construction of article

[Education Law](../../../README.md) › [Title 8 — The Professions](../../README.md) › [Article 130* — General Provisions](../README.md) › [Subarticle 4 — Unauthorized Acts](README.md)

*An asterisk in a number (for example Article 24\* or § 20-C\*2) is the Senate's mark for a number the Legislature has used more than once: the marked article or section is a different one from the unmarked one with the same number. \*2 means the second one enacted with that number.*

```text
  * § 6512.* Construction of article.  k. A corporation organized under
article nine-C of the insurance law from employing duly licensed
physicians or from entering into contracts with duly licensed physicians
or with partnerships, medical corporations organized under article
forty-four of the public health law, professional service corporations
organized under article fifteen of the business corporation law or other
groups of duly licensed physicians to practice medicine on its behalf
for persons insured under its contracts or policies.
  l.  Acts performed by duly registered physician's associates or
specialist's assistants who are working under the responsible
supervision of physicians licensed to practice medicine in the state of
New York.
  l.  The organization of a medical corporation under article forty-four
of the public health law or the organization of a professional service
corporation under article fifteen of the business corporation law.
  * NB (This section 6512 was repealed by 987/1971 which came under old
title VIII.  Although old 6512 was repealed, there were amendments made
to 6512 which are still live as noted above.  No reference to 6512 added
by 987/1971)
```
