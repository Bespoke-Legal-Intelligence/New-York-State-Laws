# Education Law § 6514* — Revocation of certificates; amendment of registrations

[Education Law](../../../README.md) › [Title 8 — The Professions](../../README.md) › [Article 130* — General Provisions](../README.md) › [Subarticle 4 — Unauthorized Acts](README.md)

*An asterisk in a number (for example Article 24\* or § 20-C\*2) is the Senate's mark for a number the Legislature has used more than once: the marked article or section is a different one from the unmarked one with the same number. \*2 means the second one enacted with that number.*

```text
  § 6514.* Revocation of certificates; amendment of registrations. (h)
That a physician is or has been guilty of negligence in assigning duties
or responsibilities to, or in supervising the practice of, a physician's
associate or specialist's assistant, or that he has violated any of the
provisions of the education law or of the public health law or any
regulations adopted pursuant thereto relating to such practice.
  * NB This section 6514 came under old title VIII. Although old 6514
was repealed, there were amendments made, which are still live. As noted
above, no reference to 6514 added by 987/1971.
```
