# Education Law § 6501-B — Affirmation of applications

[Education Law](../../../README.md) › [Title 8 — The Professions](../../README.md) › [Article 130* — General Provisions](../README.md) › [Subarticle 1 — Introductory Summary](README.md)

```text
  § 6501-b. Affirmation of applications. Notwithstanding any other
provision of law to the contrary, any application required by this title
to be filed with the department may, in lieu of being certified or sworn
under oath, be subscribed by the applicant and affirmed by the applicant
as true under penalties of perjury.
```
