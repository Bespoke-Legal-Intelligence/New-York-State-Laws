# Education Law § 6509-D — Limited exemption from professional misconduct

[Education Law](../../../README.md) › [Title 8 — The Professions](../../README.md) › [Article 130* — General Provisions](../README.md) › [Subarticle 3 — Professional Misconduct](README.md)

```text
  § 6509-d. Limited exemption from professional misconduct.
Notwithstanding any other provision of law to the contrary, it shall not
be considered professional misconduct pursuant to this sub-article for
any person who is licensed under title eight of this chapter and who
would otherwise be prohibited from prescribing or administering drugs
pursuant to the article that licenses such individual, to administer an
opioid antagonist in the event of an emergency.
```
