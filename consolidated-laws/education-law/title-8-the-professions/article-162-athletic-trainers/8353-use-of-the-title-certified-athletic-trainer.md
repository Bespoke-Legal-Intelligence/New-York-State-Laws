# Education Law § 8353 — Use of the title "certified athletic trainer"

[Education Law](../../README.md) › [Title 8 — The Professions](../README.md) › [Article 162 — Athletic Trainers](README.md)

```text
  § 8353. Use of the title "certified athletic trainer".  Only a person
certified or otherwise authorized under this article shall use the title
"certified athletic trainer".
```
