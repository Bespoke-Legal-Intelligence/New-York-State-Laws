# Education Law § 8350 — Introduction

[Education Law](../../README.md) › [Title 8 — The Professions](../README.md) › [Article 162 — Athletic Trainers](README.md)

```text
  § 8350. Introduction.  This article applies to the profession of
athletic training. The general provisions of all professions contained
in article one hundred thirty of this chapter shall apply to this
article.
```
