# Education Law § 6600 — Introduction

[Education Law](../../README.md) › [Title 8 — The Professions](../README.md) › [Article 133 — Dentistry and Dental Hygiene](README.md)

```text
  § 6600. Introduction.  This article applies to the professions of
dentistry and dental hygiene.  The general provisions for all
professions contained in article one hundred thirty of this title apply
to this article.
```
