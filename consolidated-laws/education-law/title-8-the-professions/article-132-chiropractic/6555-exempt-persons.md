# Education Law § 6555 — Exempt persons

[Education Law](../../README.md) › [Title 8 — The Professions](../README.md) › [Article 132 — Chiropractic](README.md)

```text
  § 6555. Exempt persons.  Nothing in this article shall be construed to
affect or prevent a student enrolled in a college of chiropractic in
this state from engaging in all phases of clinical practice under
supervision of a licensed chiropractor or physician in a curriculum
registered by the department.
```
