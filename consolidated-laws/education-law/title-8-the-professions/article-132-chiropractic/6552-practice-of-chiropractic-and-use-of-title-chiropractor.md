# Education Law § 6552 — Practice of chiropractic and use of title "chiropractor"

[Education Law](../../README.md) › [Title 8 — The Professions](../README.md) › [Article 132 — Chiropractic](README.md)

```text
  § 6552. Practice of chiropractic and use of title "chiropractor".
Only a person licensed or exempt under this article shall practice
chiropractic or use the title "chiropractor".
```
