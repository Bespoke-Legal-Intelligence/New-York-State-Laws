# Education Law § 7204 — Practice of land surveying and use of title "land surveyor"

[Education Law](../../README.md) › [Title 8 — The Professions](../README.md) › [Article 145 — Engineering, Land Surveying and Geology](README.md)

```text
  § 7204. Practice of land surveying and use of title "land surveyor".
Only a person licensed or otherwise authorized under this article shall
practice land surveying or use the title "land surveyor".
```
