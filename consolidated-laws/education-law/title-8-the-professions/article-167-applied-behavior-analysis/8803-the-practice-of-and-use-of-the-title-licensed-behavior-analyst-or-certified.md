# Education Law § 8803 — The practice of and use of the title "licensed behavior analyst" or "certified behavior analyst assistant"

[Education Law](../../README.md) › [Title 8 — The Professions](../README.md) › [Article 167 — Applied Behavior Analysis](README.md)

```text
  § 8803. The practice of and use of the title "licensed behavior
analyst" or "certified behavior analyst assistant". Only a person
licensed, certified or exempt under this article shall practice applied
behavior analysis. Only a person licensed or certified under this
article shall use the titles "licensed behavior analyst" or "certified
behavior analyst assistant."
```
