# Education Law § 7102 — Practice of optometry and use of title "optometrist"

[Education Law](../../README.md) › [Title 8 — The Professions](../README.md) › [Article 143 — Optometry](README.md)

```text
  § 7102. Practice of optometry and use of title "optometrist".  Only a
person licensed or exempt under this article shall practice optometry or
use the title "optometrist".
```
