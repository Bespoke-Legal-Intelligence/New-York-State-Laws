# Education Law § 6739 — Duties of licensed physical therapist assistants and the use of title "physical therapist assistant"

[Education Law](../../README.md) › [Title 8 — The Professions](../README.md) › [Article 136 — Physical Therapy and Physical Therapist Assistants](README.md)

```text
  § 6739. Duties of licensed physical therapist assistants and the use
of title "physical therapist assistant". Only a person licensed or
otherwise authorized under this article shall participate in the
practice of physical therapy as a licensed physical therapist assistant
and only a person licensed under this section shall use the title
"physical therapist assistant".
```
