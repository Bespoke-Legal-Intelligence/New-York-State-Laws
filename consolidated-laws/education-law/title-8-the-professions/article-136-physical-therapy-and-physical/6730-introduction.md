# Education Law § 6730 — Introduction

[Education Law](../../README.md) › [Title 8 — The Professions](../README.md) › [Article 136 — Physical Therapy and Physical Therapist Assistants](README.md)

```text
  § 6730. Introduction.  This article applies to the profession of
physical therapy and provides for the licensing of physical therapists
and for the certification of physical therapist assistants.  The general
provisions for all professions contained in article one hundred thirty
of this title apply to this article.
```
