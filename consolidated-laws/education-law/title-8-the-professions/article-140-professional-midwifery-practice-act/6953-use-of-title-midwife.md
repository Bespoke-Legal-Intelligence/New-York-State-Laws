# Education Law § 6953 — Use of title "midwife"

[Education Law](../../README.md) › [Title 8 — The Professions](../README.md) › [Article 140 — Professional Midwifery Practice Act](README.md)

```text
  § 6953. Use of title "midwife". Only a person licensed or exempt under
this article shall use the title "midwife".  Only a person licensed
under both this article and article one hundred thirty-nine of this
chapter may use the title "nurse-midwife".
```
