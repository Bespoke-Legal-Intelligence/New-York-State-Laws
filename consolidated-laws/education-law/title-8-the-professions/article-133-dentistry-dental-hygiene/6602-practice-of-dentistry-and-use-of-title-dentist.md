# Education Law § 6602 — Practice of dentistry and use of title "dentist"

[Education Law](../../README.md) › [Title 8 — The Professions](../README.md) › [Article 133 — Dentistry, Dental Hygiene, and Registered Dental Assisting](README.md)

```text
  § 6602. Practice of dentistry and use of title "dentist".  Only a
person licensed or otherwise authorized to practice under this article
shall practice dentistry or use the title "dentist".
```
