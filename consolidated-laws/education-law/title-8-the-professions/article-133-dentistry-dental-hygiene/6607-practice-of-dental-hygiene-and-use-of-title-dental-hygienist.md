# Education Law § 6607 — Practice of dental hygiene and use of title "dental hygienist"

[Education Law](../../README.md) › [Title 8 — The Professions](../README.md) › [Article 133 — Dentistry, Dental Hygiene, and Registered Dental Assisting](README.md)

```text
  § 6607. Practice of dental hygiene and use of title "dental
hygienist". Only a person licensed under section sixty-six hundred nine
of this article or exempt shall practice dental hygiene or use the title
"dental hygienist".
```
