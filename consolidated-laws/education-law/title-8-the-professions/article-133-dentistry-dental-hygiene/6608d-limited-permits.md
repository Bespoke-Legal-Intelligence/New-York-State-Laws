# Education Law § 6608-D — Limited permits

[Education Law](../../README.md) › [Title 8 — The Professions](../README.md) › [Article 133 — Dentistry, Dental Hygiene, and Registered Dental Assisting](README.md)

```text
  § 6608-d. Limited permits.  The department shall issue a limited
permit to an applicant who meets all requirements for admission to the
licensing examination. All practice under a limited permit shall be
under the direct personal supervision of a licensed dentist. Limited
permits shall be for one year and may be renewed at the discretion of
the department for one additional year. The fee for each limited permit
and for each renewal shall be forty dollars.
```
