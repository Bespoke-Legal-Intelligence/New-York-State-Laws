# Education Law § 7002 — Practice of podiatry and use of title "podiatrist"

[Education Law](../../README.md) › [Title 8 — The Professions](../README.md) › [Article 141 — Podiatry](README.md)

```text
  § 7002. Practice of podiatry and use of title "podiatrist".  Only a
person licensed or exempt under this article shall practice podiatry or
use the title "podiatrist" or "chiropodist".
```
