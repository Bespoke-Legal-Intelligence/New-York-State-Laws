# Education Law § 7005 — Exempt persons

[Education Law](../../README.md) › [Title 8 — The Professions](../README.md) › [Article 141 — Podiatry](README.md)

```text
  § 7005. Exempt persons. Nothing in this article shall be construed to
affect or prevent a student from engaging in clinical practice under
supervision of a licensed podiatrist as part of the program of an
approved school of podiatry.
```
