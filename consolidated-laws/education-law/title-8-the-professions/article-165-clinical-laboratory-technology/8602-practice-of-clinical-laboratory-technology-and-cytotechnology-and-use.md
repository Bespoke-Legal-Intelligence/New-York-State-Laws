# Education Law § 8602 — Practice of clinical laboratory technology and cytotechnology and use of the titles "licensed clinical laboratory technologist" and "lice...

[Education Law](../../README.md) › [Title 8 — The Professions](../README.md) › [Article 165 — Clinical Laboratory Technology Practice Act](README.md)

```text
  § 8602. Practice of clinical laboratory technology and cytotechnology
and use of the titles "licensed clinical laboratory technologist" and
"licensed cytotechnologist". No person shall practice clinical
laboratory technology or hold himself or herself out as a clinical
laboratory technologist or a cytotechnologist in this state unless he or
she is licensed or exempt pursuant to this article.
```
