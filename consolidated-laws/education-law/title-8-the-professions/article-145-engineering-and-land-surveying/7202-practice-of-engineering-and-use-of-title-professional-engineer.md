# Education Law § 7202 — Practice of engineering and use of title "professional engineer"

[Education Law](../../README.md) › [Title 8 — The Professions](../README.md) › [Article 145 — Engineering and Land Surveying](README.md)

```text
  § 7202. Practice of engineering and use of title "professional
engineer".  Only a person licensed or otherwise authorized under this
article shall practice engineering or use the title "professional
engineer".
```
