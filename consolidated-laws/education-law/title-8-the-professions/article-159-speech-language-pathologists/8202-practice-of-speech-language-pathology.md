# Education Law § 8202 — Practice of speech-language pathology

[Education Law](../../README.md) › [Title 8 — The Professions](../README.md) › [Article 159 — Speech-language Pathologists and Audiologists](README.md)

```text
  § 8202. Practice of speech-language pathology.  Only a person licensed
or otherwise authorized under this article shall practice
speech-language pathology or use the title of speech-language
pathologist.
```
