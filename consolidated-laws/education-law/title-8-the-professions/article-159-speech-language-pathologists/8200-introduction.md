# Education Law § 8200 — Introduction

[Education Law](../../README.md) › [Title 8 — The Professions](../README.md) › [Article 159 — Speech-language Pathologists and Audiologists](README.md)

```text
  § 8200. Introduction.  This article applies to the professions of
speech-language pathology and audiology. The general provisions for all
professions contained in article one hundred thirty of this title apply
to this article.
```
