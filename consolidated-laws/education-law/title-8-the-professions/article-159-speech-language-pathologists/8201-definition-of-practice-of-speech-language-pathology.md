# Education Law § 8201 — Definition of practice of speech-language pathology

[Education Law](../../README.md) › [Title 8 — The Professions](../README.md) › [Article 159 — Speech-language Pathologists and Audiologists](README.md)

```text
  § 8201. Definition of practice of speech-language pathology.  The
practice of the profession of speech-language pathology shall mean the
application of principles, methods and procedures of measurement,
prediction, non-medical diagnosis, testing, counselling, consultation,
rehabilitation and instruction related to the development and disorders
of speech, voice, swallowing, and/or language for the purpose of
preventing, ameliorating or modifying such disorder conditions in
individuals and/or groups of individuals.
```
