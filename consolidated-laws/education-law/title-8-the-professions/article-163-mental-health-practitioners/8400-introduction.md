# Education Law § 8400 — Introduction

[Education Law](../../README.md) › [Title 8 — The Professions](../README.md) › [Article 163 — Mental Health Practitioners](README.md)

```text
  § 8400. Introduction. This article applies to the professions of
mental health counseling, marriage and family therapy, creative arts
therapy, and psychoanalysis and provides for the licensing of such
practitioners.  The general provisions for all professions contained in
article one hundred thirty of this title apply to this article.
```
