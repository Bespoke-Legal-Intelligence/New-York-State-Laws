# Education Law § 7322 — Practice of landscape architecture and use of title "landscape architect"

[Education Law](../../README.md) › [Title 8 — The Professions](../README.md) › [Article 148 — Landscape Architecture](README.md)

```text
  § 7322. Practice of landscape architecture and use of title "landscape
architect".  Only a person licensed or otherwise authorized to practice
under this article shall practice landscape architecture or use the
title "landscape architect".
```
