# Education Law § 7302 — Practice of architecture and use of title "architect"

[Education Law](../../README.md) › [Title 8 — The Professions](../README.md) › [Article 147 — Architecture](README.md)

```text
  § 7302. Practice of architecture and use of title "architect".  Only a
person licensed or otherwise authorized to practice under this article
shall practice architecture or use the title "architect".
```
