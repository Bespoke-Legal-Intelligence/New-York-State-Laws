# Education Law § 8307 — Separability

[Education Law](../../README.md) › [Title 8 — The Professions](../README.md) › [Article 161 — Interior Design](README.md)

```text
  § 8307. Separability.  If any section of this article, or part
thereof, shall be adjudged by a court of competent jurisdiction to be
invalid, such judgment shall not affect, impair or invalidate the
remainder or any other section or part thereof.
```
