# Education Law § 8302 — Signature of a certified interior designer

[Education Law](../../README.md) › [Title 8 — The Professions](../README.md) › [Article 161 — Interior Design](README.md)

```text
  § 8302. Signature of a certified interior designer.  All original
final interior design documents, plans and specifications, prepared by a
certified interior designer or by a full-time or part-time subordinate
employed under his or her supervision, shall be manually  signed by the
certified interior designer.
```
