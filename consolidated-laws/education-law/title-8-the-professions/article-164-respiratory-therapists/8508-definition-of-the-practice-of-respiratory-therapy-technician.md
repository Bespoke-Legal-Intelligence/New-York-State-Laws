# Education Law § 8508 — Definition of the practice of respiratory therapy technician

[Education Law](../../README.md) › [Title 8 — The Professions](../README.md) › [Article 164 — Respiratory Therapists and Respiratory Therapy Technicians](README.md)

```text
  § 8508. Definition of the practice of respiratory therapy technician.
A respiratory therapy technician means a person licensed in accordance
with this article who works under the supervision of a licensed
respiratory therapist or a licensed or otherwise legally authorized
physician performing tasks and responsibilities within the framework of
the practice of respiratory therapy.
```
