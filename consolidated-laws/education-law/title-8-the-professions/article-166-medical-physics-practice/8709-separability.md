# Education Law § 8709 — Separability

[Education Law](../../README.md) › [Title 8 — The Professions](../README.md) › [Article 166 — Medical Physics Practice](README.md)

```text
  § 8709. Separability. If any section of this article, or part thereof,
shall be adjudged by any court of competent jurisdiction to be invalid,
such judgment shall not affect, impair or invalidate the remainder of
any other section or part thereof.
```
