# Education Law § 6808-A — Identification of Pharmacists

[Education Law](../../README.md) › [Title 8 — The Professions](../README.md) › [Article 137 — Pharmacy](README.md)

```text
  § 6808-a. Identification of Pharmacists.  Every pharmacist on duty
shall be identified by a badge designed by the state board of pharmacy,
which shall contain his name and title.
```
