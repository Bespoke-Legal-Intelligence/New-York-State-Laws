# Education Law § 6803 — Practice of pharmacy and use of title "pharmacist"

[Education Law](../../README.md) › [Title 8 — The Professions](../README.md) › [Article 137 — Pharmacy](README.md)

```text
  § 6803. Practice of pharmacy and use of title "pharmacist".  Only a
person licensed or otherwise authorized under this article shall
practice pharmacy or use the title "pharmacist" or any derivative.
```
