# Education Law § 7600 — Introduction

[Education Law](../../README.md) › [Title 8 — The Professions](../README.md) › [Article 153 — Psychology](README.md)

```text
  § 7600. Introduction. This article applies to the profession and
practice of psychology and to the use of the title "psychologist". The
general provisions for all professions contained in article one hundred
thirty of this title apply to this article.
```
