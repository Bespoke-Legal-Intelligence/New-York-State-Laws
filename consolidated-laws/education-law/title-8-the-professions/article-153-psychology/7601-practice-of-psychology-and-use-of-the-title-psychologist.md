# Education Law § 7601 — Practice of psychology and use of the title "psychologist"

[Education Law](../../README.md) › [Title 8 — The Professions](../README.md) › [Article 153 — Psychology](README.md)

```text
  § 7601. Practice of psychology and use of the title "psychologist".
Only a person licensed or otherwise authorized under this article shall
be authorized to practice psychology or to use the title "psychologist"
or to describe his or her services by use of the words "psychologist",
"psychology" or "psychological" in connection with his or her practice.
```
