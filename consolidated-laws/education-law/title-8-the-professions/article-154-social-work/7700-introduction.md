# Education Law § 7700 — Introduction

[Education Law](../../README.md) › [Title 8 — The Professions](../README.md) › [Article 154 — Social Work](README.md)

```text
  § 7700. Introduction. This article applies to the profession and
practice of social work, the practice of licensed master social work and
the practice of clinical social work, and to the use of the titles
"licensed master social worker", and "licensed clinical social worker".
The general provisions for all professions contained in article one
hundred thirty of this title apply to this article.
```
