# Education Law § 6006 — Property to belong to state

[Education Law](../../README.md) › [Title 7 — State and City Colleges and Institutions--cornell University](../README.md) › [Article 121 — State University of New York College of Environmental Science and Forestry](README.md)

```text
  § 6006. Property to belong to state.  All lands purchased and other
property acquired with money appropriated by the state for such college
shall be and remain the property of the state.  If real property is
purchased, the title thereto shall be conveyed to the people of the
state of New York and the sufficiency of such title and the form of
conveyance shall be approved by the attorney-general.
```
