# Education Law § 6001 — College continued

[Education Law](../../README.md) › [Title 7 — State and City Colleges and Institutions--cornell University](../README.md) › [Article 121 — State University of New York College of Environmental Science and Forestry](README.md)

```text
  § 6001. College continued.  The New York State College of Forestry at
Syracuse University shall hereafter be known as the State University of
New York College of Environmental Science and Forestry.  The college
shall be subject to the supervision of the state university trustees.
```
