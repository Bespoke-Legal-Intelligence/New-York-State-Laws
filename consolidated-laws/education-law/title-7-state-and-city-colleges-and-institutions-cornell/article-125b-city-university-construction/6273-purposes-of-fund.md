# Education Law § 6273 — Purposes of fund

[Education Law](../../README.md) › [Title 7 — State and City Colleges and Institutions-cornell University](../README.md) › [Article 125-B — City University Construction Fund](README.md)

```text
  § 6273. Purposes of fund.  The purposes of the fund shall be (i) to
provide facilities for the city university, and (ii) to support the
educational purposes of the city university.
```
