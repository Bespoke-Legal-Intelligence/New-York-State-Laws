# Education Law § 6283 — Procurements of the fund

[Education Law](../../README.md) › [Title 7 — State and City Colleges and Institutions-cornell University](../README.md) › [Article 125-B — City University Construction Fund](README.md)

```text
  * § 6283. Procurements of the fund. Notwithstanding subdivision two of
section one hundred twelve of the state finance law or any other law to
the contrary, fund procurements shall not be subject to the prior
approval of any state officer or agency.
  * NB Repealed June 30, 2016
```
