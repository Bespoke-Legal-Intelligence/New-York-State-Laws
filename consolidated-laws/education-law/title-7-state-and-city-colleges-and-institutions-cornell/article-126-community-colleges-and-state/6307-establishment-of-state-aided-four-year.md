# Education Law § 6307 — Establishment of state-aided four-year colleges

[Education Law](../../README.md) › [Title 7 — State and City Colleges and Institutions-cornell University](../README.md) › [Article 126 — Community Colleges and State-aided Four-year Colleges](README.md)

```text
  § 6307. Establishment of state-aided four-year colleges.  1.  Upon
recommendation of the state university trustees, counties or adjoining
cities not now adequately served by any four-year college programs, may
be empowered singly or jointly to establish and operate four-year
college programs.  Such colleges shall be financed and administered in a
manner similar to that provided for community colleges herein.
```
