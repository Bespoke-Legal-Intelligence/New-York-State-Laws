# Education Law § 6353 — Enforcement

[Education Law](../../README.md) › [Title 7 — State and City Colleges and Institutions-cornell University](../README.md) › [Article 127 — Military Service](README.md)

```text
  § 6353. Enforcement. Any violation of this article shall constitute an
unfair educational practice pursuant to section three hundred thirteen
of this chapter. Any student alleging a violation of this article may
commence an administrative or judicial proceeding pursuant to section
three hundred thirteen of this chapter.
```
