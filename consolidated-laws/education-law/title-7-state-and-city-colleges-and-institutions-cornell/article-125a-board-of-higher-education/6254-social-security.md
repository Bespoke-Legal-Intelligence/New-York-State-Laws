# Education Law § 6254 — Social security

[Education Law](../../README.md) › [Title 7 — State and City Colleges and Institutions-cornell University](../README.md) › [Article 125-A — Board of Higher Education Optional Retirement Program](README.md)

```text
  § 6254. Social security.  Every electing employee shall have old-age,
survivors, and disability insurance coverage provided by the federal
social security act in accordance with the provisions of article three
of the retirement and social security law.
```
