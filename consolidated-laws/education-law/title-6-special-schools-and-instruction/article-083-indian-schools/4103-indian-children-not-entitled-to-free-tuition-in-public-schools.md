# Education Law § 4103 — Indian children not entitled to free tuition in public schools

[Education Law](../../README.md) › [Title 6 — Special Schools and Instruction](../README.md) › [Article 83 — Indian Schools](README.md)

```text
  § 4103. Indian children not entitled to free tuition in public
schools.  Indian children residing on a reservation are not entitled to
free tuition in districts outside the reservation but may be received
into the schools of such districts on the approval of the trustees
thereof and the commissioner of education.
```
