# Education Law § 4106 — Duties of persons in parental relation to Indian children

[Education Law](../../README.md) › [Title 6 — Special Schools and Instruction](../README.md) › [Article 83 — Indian Schools](README.md)

```text
  § 4106. Duties of persons in parental relation to Indian children.
Any person in parental relation to an Indian child between six and
sixteen years of age in proper physical and mental condition to attend
school, shall cause such child to attend upon instruction as provided in
this article.
```
