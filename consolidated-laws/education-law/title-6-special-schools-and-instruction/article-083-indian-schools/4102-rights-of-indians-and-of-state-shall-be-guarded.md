# Education Law § 4102 — Rights of Indians and of state shall be guarded

[Education Law](../../README.md) › [Title 6 — Special Schools and Instruction](../README.md) › [Article 83 — Indian Schools](README.md)

```text
  § 4102. Rights of Indians and of state shall be guarded.  Any contract
which may be entered into with Indians for the use or occupancy of any
land for school grounds, sites or buildings, shall provide proper
protection for the title of the Indians to their lands, and shall
reserve to the state the right to remove or otherwise dispose of all
improvements made at the expense of the state.
```
