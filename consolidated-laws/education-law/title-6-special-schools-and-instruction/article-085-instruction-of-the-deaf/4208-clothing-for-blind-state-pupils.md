# Education Law § 4208 — Clothing for blind state pupils

[Education Law](../../README.md) › [Title 6 — Special Schools and Instruction](../README.md) › [Article 85 — Instruction of the Deaf and of the Blind](README.md)

```text
  § 4208. Clothing for blind state pupils.  Whenever the parents or
guardians are unable to furnish clothing to state pupils, the
commissioner of education is hereby required to apportion annually to
each institution caring for blind children legally appointed thereto the
sum of one hundred twenty dollars per pupil or so much thereof as may be
necessary to be applied to the cost of clothing for each of said pupils.
```
