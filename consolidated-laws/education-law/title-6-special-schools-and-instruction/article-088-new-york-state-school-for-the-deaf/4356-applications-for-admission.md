# Education Law § 4356 — Applications for admission

[Education Law](../../README.md) › [Title 6 — Special Schools and Instruction](../README.md) › [Article 88 — New York State School For the Deaf](README.md)

```text
  § 4356.  Applications for admission.  Applications for admission shall
be made to the department in such manner as shall be prescribed by the
department and shall be accompanied by such evidence of legal residence
and other information as the department may require.
```
