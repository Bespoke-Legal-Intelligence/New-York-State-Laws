# Education Law § 4902 — Management

[Education Law](../../README.md) › [Title 6 — Special Schools and Instruction](../README.md) › [Article 99 — New York Nautical School](README.md)

```text
  § 4902. Management.  The board of education of the city of New York
shall appoint annually at least three of its number who, subject to the
control, supervision and approbation of the board, shall constitute an
executive committee for the care, government and management of such
nautical school, under rules and regulations so prescribed, and whose
duty it shall be, among other things, to recommend the rules and
regulations which they deem necessary and proper for such school.
```
