# Education Law § 4904 — Expenses

[Education Law](../../README.md) › [Title 6 — Special Schools and Instruction](../README.md) › [Article 99 — New York Nautical School](README.md)

```text
  § 4904. Expenses.  After the establishment and organization of such
school the expenses thereof, and of carrying out the provisions of this
article, shall be defrayed from the moneys raised by law for the support
of public schools in the city of New York.
```
