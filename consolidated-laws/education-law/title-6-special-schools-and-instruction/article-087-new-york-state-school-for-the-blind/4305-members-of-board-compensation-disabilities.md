# Education Law § 4305 — Members of board; compensation; disabilities

[Education Law](../../README.md) › [Title 6 — Special Schools and Instruction](../README.md) › [Article 87 — New York State School For the Blind](README.md)

```text
  § 4305. Members of board; compensation; disabilities.  The members of
the board of visitors shall serve without pay, but they shall be paid
their actual expenses necessarily incurred in the discharge of their
official duties.  No member of such board shall be pecuniarily
interested in any contract for buildings pertaining to the school or in
furnishing supplies therefor.
```
