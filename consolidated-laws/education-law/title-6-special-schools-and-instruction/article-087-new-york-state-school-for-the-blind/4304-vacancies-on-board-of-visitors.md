# Education Law § 4304 — Vacancies on board of visitors

[Education Law](../../README.md) › [Title 6 — Special Schools and Instruction](../README.md) › [Article 87 — New York State School For the Blind](README.md)

```text
  § 4304. Vacancies on board of visitors.  In case of the declination of
any member of such board of visitors to act under his appointment, or of
the occurrence of any other vacancy in the board not caused by
expiration of term, the governor shall forthwith appoint some suitable
person to fill such vacancy, and the member so appointed shall serve out
the term of his predecessor.
```
