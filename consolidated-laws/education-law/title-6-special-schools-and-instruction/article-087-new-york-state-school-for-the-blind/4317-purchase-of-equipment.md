# Education Law § 4317 — Purchase of equipment

[Education Law](../../README.md) › [Title 6 — Special Schools and Instruction](../README.md) › [Article 87 — New York State School For the Blind](README.md)

```text
  § 4317. Purchase of equipment.  The education department shall
purchase all furniture, apparatus and other supplies necessary to the
equipment and maintenance of the school in the most efficient manner.
```
