# Education Law § 4303 — Appointment and terms of board of visitors

[Education Law](../../README.md) › [Title 6 — Special Schools and Instruction](../README.md) › [Article 87 — New York State School For the Blind](README.md)

```text
  § 4303. Appointment and terms of board of visitors.  The governor
shall appoint each year, by and with the consent of the senate, one
member of the board of visitors who shall serve for a term of seven
years.  Two of the board must be residents of the county of Genesee and
a majority must be residents within fifty miles of said school.
```
