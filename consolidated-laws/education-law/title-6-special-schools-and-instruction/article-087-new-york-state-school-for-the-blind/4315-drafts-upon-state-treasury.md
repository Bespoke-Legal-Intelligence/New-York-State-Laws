# Education Law § 4315 — Drafts upon state treasury

[Education Law](../../README.md) › [Title 6 — Special Schools and Instruction](../README.md) › [Article 87 — New York State School For the Blind](README.md)

```text
  § 4315. Drafts upon state treasury.  All moneys appropriated for such
school or derived from other sources in the course of the administration
thereof shall be expended upon vouchers approved by the commissioner of
education, when and in the manner authorized by the board of regents.
```
