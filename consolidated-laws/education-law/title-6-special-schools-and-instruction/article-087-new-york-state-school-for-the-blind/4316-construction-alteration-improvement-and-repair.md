# Education Law § 4316 — Construction, alteration, improvement and repair of buildings of school

[Education Law](../../README.md) › [Title 6 — Special Schools and Instruction](../README.md) › [Article 87 — New York State School For the Blind](README.md)

```text
  § 4316. Construction, alteration, improvement and repair of buildings
of school.  All of the provisions of section one hundred twenty-seven of
the state finance law shall apply, so far as applicable, to the
construction, alteration, improvements and repair of buildings for the
school.  For such purpose, the education department shall be the
department having jurisdiction as such expression is used in such
section.
```
