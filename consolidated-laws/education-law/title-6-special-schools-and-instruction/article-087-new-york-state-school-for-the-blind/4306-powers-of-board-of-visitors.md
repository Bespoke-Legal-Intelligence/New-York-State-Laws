# Education Law § 4306 — Powers of board of visitors

[Education Law](../../README.md) › [Title 6 — Special Schools and Instruction](../README.md) › [Article 87 — New York State School For the Blind](README.md)

```text
  § 4306. Powers of board of visitors.  The board of visitors shall
visit and inspect such school, recommend the appointment and approve the
removal of a superintendent of such school and have such other powers
and duties as may be required or authorized by the board of regents.
```
