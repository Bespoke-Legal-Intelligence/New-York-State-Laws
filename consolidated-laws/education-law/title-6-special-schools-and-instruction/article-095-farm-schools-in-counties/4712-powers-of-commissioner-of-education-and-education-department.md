# Education Law § 4712 — Powers of commissioner of education and education department

[Education Law](../../README.md) › [Title 6 — Special Schools and Instruction](../README.md) › [Article 95 — Farm Schools In Counties](README.md)

```text
  § 4712. Powers of commissioner of education and education department.
A school established as provided herein shall be deemed a part of the
public school system of the state, and shall be subject to the
supervision and control of the commissioner of education and the
education department in the same manner as other public schools, and
shall not be subject to any of the laws of the state relating to
charitable or penal institutions.
```
