# Benevolent Orders Law § 1-B — Definition

[Benevolent Orders Law](../README.md) › [Article 1 — Short Title](README.md)

```text
  § 1-b. Definition. The term "minor", as used in this chapter, means a
person who has not attained the age of eighteen years.
```
