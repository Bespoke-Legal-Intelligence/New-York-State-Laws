# Mental Hygiene Law § 13.03 — Definitions

[Mental Hygiene Law](../../README.md) › [Title C — Developmental Disabilities Act](../README.md) › [Article 13 — Office For People With Developmental Disabilities](README.md)

```text
§ 13.03 Definitions.
  As used in this title:
  1. "office" means the office for people with developmental
disabilities.
  2. "commissioner" means the head of the office for people with
developmental disabilities.
```
