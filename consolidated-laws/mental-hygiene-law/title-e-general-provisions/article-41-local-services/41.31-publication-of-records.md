# Mental Hygiene Law § 41.31 — Publication of records

[Mental Hygiene Law](../../README.md) › [Title E — General Provisions](../README.md) › [Article 41 — Local Services](README.md)

```text
§ 41.31 Publication of records.
  Every local governmental unit shall make available in its office, as a
public record, copies of its state-approved plans and proposals, and of
its rules, regulations, and contracting procedures.
```
