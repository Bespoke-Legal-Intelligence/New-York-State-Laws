# Mental Hygiene Law § 33.17 — Transportation of individuals

[Mental Hygiene Law](../../README.md) › [Title E — General Provisions](../README.md) › [Article 33 — Rights of Patients](README.md)

```text
§ 33.17 Transportation of individuals.
  Within staffing limitations and upon request, an individual who is
being transported to or from a facility may be accompanied by same
gender staff.
```
