# Mental Hygiene Law § 33.17 — Transportation of female patients

[Mental Hygiene Law](../../README.md) › [Title E — General Provisions](../README.md) › [Article 33 — Rights of Patients](README.md)

```text
§ 33.17 Transportation of female patients.
  Any female patient who is being transported to or from a facility
shall be accompanied by another female, unless accompanied by her
father, brother, husband, or son.
```
