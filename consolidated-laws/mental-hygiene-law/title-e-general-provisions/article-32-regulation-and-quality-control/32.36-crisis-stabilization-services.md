# Mental Hygiene Law § 32.36 — Crisis stabilization services

[Mental Hygiene Law](../../README.md) › [Title E — General Provisions](../README.md) › [Article 32 — Regulation and Quality Control of Chemical Dependence Services and Compulsive Gambling Services](README.md)

```text
§ 32.36 Crisis stabilization services.
  The commissioner shall be authorized, in conjunction with the
commissioner of the office of mental health, to create crisis
stabilization centers within New York state in accordance with article
thirty-six of this title, including the promulgation of joint
regulations and implementation of a financing mechanism to allow for the
sustainable operation of such programs.
```
