# Mental Hygiene Law § 32.17-A — Notification of emergency contact

[Mental Hygiene Law](../../README.md) › [Title E — General Provisions](../README.md) › [Article 32 — Regulation and Quality Control of Chemical Dependence Services and Compulsive Gambling Services](README.md)

```text
§ 32.17-a Notification of emergency contact.
  Every certified treatment program shall notify every patient of their
right to identify individuals who should be contacted in case of
emergency, as such term is defined by the commissioner.
```
