# Mental Hygiene Law § 32.02 — Regulation and quality control of compulsive gambling services

[Mental Hygiene Law](../../README.md) › [Title E — General Provisions](../README.md) › [Article 32 — Regulation and Quality Control of Chemical Dependence Services and Compulsive Gambling Services](README.md)

```text
§ 32.02 Regulation and quality control of compulsive gambling services.
  (a) The commissioner may adopt any regulation reasonably necessary to
regulate and ensure high quality of services to individuals suffering
from compulsive gambling.
  (b) The requirements of this article are applicable to this section.
```
