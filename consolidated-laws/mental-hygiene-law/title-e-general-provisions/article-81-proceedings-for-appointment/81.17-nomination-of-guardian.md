# Mental Hygiene Law § 81.17 — Nomination of guardian

[Mental Hygiene Law](../../README.md) › [Title E — General Provisions](../README.md) › [Article 81 — Proceedings For Appointment of a Guardian For Personal Needs or Property Management](README.md)

```text
§ 81.17 Nomination of guardian.
  In the petition, or in a written instrument duly executed,
acknowledged, and filed in the proceeding before the appointment of a
guardian, the person alleged to be incapacitated may nominate a
guardian.
```
