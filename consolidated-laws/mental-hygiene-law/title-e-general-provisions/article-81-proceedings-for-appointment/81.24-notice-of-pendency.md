# Mental Hygiene Law § 81.24 — Notice of pendency

[Mental Hygiene Law](../../README.md) › [Title E — General Provisions](../README.md) › [Article 81 — Proceedings For Appointment of a Guardian For Personal Needs or Property Management](README.md)

```text
§ 81.24 Notice of pendency.
  The petitioner shall, prior to judgment, file a notice of pendency if
real property or any interest therein is or may be affected by the
proceeding.
```
