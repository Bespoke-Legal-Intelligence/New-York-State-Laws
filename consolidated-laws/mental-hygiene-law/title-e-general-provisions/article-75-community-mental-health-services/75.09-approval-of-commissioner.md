# Mental Hygiene Law § 75.09 — Approval of commissioner

[Mental Hygiene Law](../../README.md) › [Title E — General Provisions](../README.md) › [Article 75 — Community Mental Health Services and Developmental Disabilities Services Companies](README.md)

```text
§ 75.09 Approval of commissioner.
  Whenever any such certificate shall be presented to the secretary of
state, he shall not file such certificate unless there is endorsed
thereon or annexed thereto the written approval of the commissioner in
accordance with section 13.22 of this chapter; nor shall any amendment
to the certificate of incorporation be filed unless it is accompanied by
a certificate of the commissioner approving such amendment.
```
