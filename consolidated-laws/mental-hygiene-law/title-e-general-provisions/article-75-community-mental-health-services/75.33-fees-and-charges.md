# Mental Hygiene Law § 75.33 — Fees and charges

[Mental Hygiene Law](../../README.md) › [Title E — General Provisions](../README.md) › [Article 75 — Community Mental Health Services and Developmental Disabilities Services Companies](README.md)

```text
§ 75.33 Fees and charges.
  The commissioner may by regulation establish and charge to any company
such fees and charges for inspection, regulation, supervision, and audit
as to the commissioner may appear just and reasonable in order to
recover the departmental costs in performing these functions.
```
