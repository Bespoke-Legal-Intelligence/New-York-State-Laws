# Mental Hygiene Law § 29.17 — Clothing and money to be furnished patients discharged or released

[Mental Hygiene Law](../../README.md) › [Title E — General Provisions](../README.md) › [Article 29 — General Provisions Relating to In-patient Facilities](README.md)

```text
§ 29.17 Clothing and money to be furnished patients discharged or
          released.
  No patient shall be discharged or released from a department facility
without suitable clothing adapted to the season in which he is
discharged or released; and if it cannot be otherwise obtained, the
facility shall upon the order of the director, or of the commissioner,
as the case may be, furnish the same, and money not exceeding fifty
dollars, to defray his expenses until he can reach his relatives or
friends, or find employment to earn a subsistence.
```
