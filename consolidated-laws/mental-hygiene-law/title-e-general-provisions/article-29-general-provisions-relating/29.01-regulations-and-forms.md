# Mental Hygiene Law § 29.01 — Regulations and forms

[Mental Hygiene Law](../../README.md) › [Title E — General Provisions](../README.md) › [Article 29 — General Provisions Relating to In-patient Facilities](README.md)

```text
§ 29.01 Regulations and forms.
  The commissioner shall have the power to make regulations governing
admissions to hospitals, schools, and alcoholism facilities and the
identification and processing of patients. He shall prescribe and
furnish forms for use in procedures for admission. Admission shall be
had only upon such forms.
```
