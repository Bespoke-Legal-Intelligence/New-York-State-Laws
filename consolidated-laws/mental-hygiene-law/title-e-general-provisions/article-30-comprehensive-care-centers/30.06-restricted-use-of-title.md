# Mental Hygiene Law § 30.06 — Restricted use of title

[Mental Hygiene Law](../../README.md) › [Title E — General Provisions](../README.md) › [Article 30 — Comprehensive Care Centers For Eating Disorders](README.md)

```text
§ 30.06 Restricted use of title.
  No person or entity shall claim, advertise or imply to consumers,
health plans or other health care providers that such provider or
practitioner is a state-identified comprehensive care center for eating
disorders unless it is qualified pursuant to section 30.04 of this
article.
```
