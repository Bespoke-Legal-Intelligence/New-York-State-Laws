# Mental Hygiene Law § 79.35 — Uniformity of construction

[Mental Hygiene Law](../../README.md) › [Title E — General Provisions](../README.md) › [Article 79 — Proceedings Relative to Incompetent Veterans and Infant Wards of the United States Veterans' Administration](README.md)

```text
§ 79.35 Uniformity of construction.
  This article shall be so interpreted and construed as to effectuate
its general purpose to make uniform the law of those states which enact
it.
```
