# Mental Hygiene Law § 79.37 — Invalidity of part of article

[Mental Hygiene Law](../../README.md) › [Title E — General Provisions](../README.md) › [Article 79 — Proceedings Relative to Incompetent Veterans and Infant Wards of the United States Veterans' Administration](README.md)

```text
§ 79.37 Invalidity of part of article.
  The invalidity of any portion of this article shall not affect the
validity of any other portion thereof which can be given effect without
such invalid part.
```
