# Mental Hygiene Law § 83.43 — Relation to electronic signatures in global and national commerce act

[Mental Hygiene Law](../../README.md) › [Title E — General Provisions](../README.md) › [Article 83 — Uniform Guardianship and Protective Proceedings Jurisdiction Act](README.md)

```text
§ 83.43 Relation to electronic signatures in global and national
          commerce act.
  This article modifies, limits and supersedes the federal Electronic
Signatures in Global and National Commerce Act, 15 U.S.C. Section 7001,
et seq., but does not modify, limit or supersede Section 101(c) of such
act, 15 U.S.C. Section 7001 (c), or authorize electronic delivery of any
of the notices described in Section 103(b) of such act, 15 U.S.C.
Section 7003(b).
```
