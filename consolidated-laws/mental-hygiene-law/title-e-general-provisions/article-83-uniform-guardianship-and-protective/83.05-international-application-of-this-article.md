# Mental Hygiene Law § 83.05 — International application of this article

[Mental Hygiene Law](../../README.md) › [Title E — General Provisions](../README.md) › [Article 83 — Uniform Guardianship and Protective Proceedings Jurisdiction Act](README.md)

```text
§ 83.05 International application of this article.
  A court of this state may treat a foreign country as if it were a
state for the purpose of applying sections 83.01 through 83.37 of this
article.
```
