# Mental Hygiene Law § 83.41 — Uniformity of application and construction

[Mental Hygiene Law](../../README.md) › [Title E — General Provisions](../README.md) › [Article 83 — Uniform Guardianship and Protective Proceedings Jurisdiction Act](README.md)

```text
§ 83.41 Uniformity of application and construction.
  In applying and construing this article, consideration must be given
to the need to promote uniformity of the law with respect to its subject
matter among states that enact it.
```
