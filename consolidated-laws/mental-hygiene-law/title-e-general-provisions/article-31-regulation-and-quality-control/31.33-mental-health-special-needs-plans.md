# Mental Hygiene Law § 31.33 — Mental health special needs plans

[Mental Hygiene Law](../../README.md) › [Title E — General Provisions](../README.md) › [Article 31 — Regulation and Quality Control of Services For the Mentally Disabled](README.md)

```text
* § 31.33 Mental health special needs plans.
  The commissioner, jointly with the commissioner of health, shall be
authorized to issue a certificate of authority to operate a mental
health special needs plan, in accordance with the procedures of section
forty-four hundred three-d of the public health law.
  * NB Repealed March 31, 2030
```
