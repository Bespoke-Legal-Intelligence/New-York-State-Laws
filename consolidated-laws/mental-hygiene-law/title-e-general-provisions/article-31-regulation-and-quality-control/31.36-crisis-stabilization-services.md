# Mental Hygiene Law § 31.36 — Crisis stabilization services

[Mental Hygiene Law](../../README.md) › [Title E — General Provisions](../README.md) › [Article 31 — Regulation and Quality Control of Services For the Mentally Disabled](README.md)

```text
§ 31.36 Crisis stabilization services.
  The commissioner shall be authorized, in conjunction with the
commissioner of the office of addiction services and supports, to create
crisis stabilization centers within New York state in accordance with
article thirty-six of this title, including the promulgation of joint
regulations and implementation of a financing mechanism to allow for the
sustainable operation of such programs.
```
