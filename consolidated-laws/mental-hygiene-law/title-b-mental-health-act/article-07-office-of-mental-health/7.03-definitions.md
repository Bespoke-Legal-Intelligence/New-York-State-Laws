# Mental Hygiene Law § 7.03 — Definitions

[Mental Hygiene Law](../../README.md) › [Title B — Mental Health Act](../README.md) › [Article 7 — Office of Mental Health](README.md)

```text
§ 7.03 Definitions.
  As used in this title:
  1. "Office" means the office of mental health.
  2. "Commissioner" means the head of the office of mental health.
```
