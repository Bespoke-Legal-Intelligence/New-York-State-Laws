# Mental Hygiene Law § 9.64 — Notice of admission determination to community provider

[Mental Hygiene Law](../../README.md) › [Title B — Mental Health Act](../README.md) › [Article 9 — Hospitalization of Persons With a Mental Illness](README.md)

```text
* § 9.64 Notice of admission determination to community provider.
  Upon an admission to a hospital or received as a patient in a
comprehensive psychiatric emergency program, the director of such
hospital or program shall, in accordance with section 33.13 of this
chapter, ensure that reasonable efforts are made to identify and
promptly notify of such determination any community provider of mental
health services that maintains such person on its caseload.
  * NB Effective August 7, 2025
```
