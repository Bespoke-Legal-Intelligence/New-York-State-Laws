# Mental Hygiene Law § 25.09 — Administrative costs

[Mental Hygiene Law](../../README.md) › [Title D — Alcoholism and Substance Abuse Act](../README.md) › [Article 25 — Funding For Services of the Office of Alcoholism and Substance Abuse Services](README.md)

```text
§ 25.09 Administrative costs.
  Subject to the approval of the director of the budget, the office
shall establish a limit on the amount of financial support which may be
advanced or reimbursed to a voluntary agency or a program operated by a
local governmental unit for the administration of a program.
```
