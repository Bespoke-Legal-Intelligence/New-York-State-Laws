# Mental Hygiene Law § 19.16 — Methadone Registry

[Mental Hygiene Law](../../README.md) › [Title D — Alcoholism and Substance Abuse Act](../README.md) › [Article 19 — Office of Alcoholism and Substance Abuse](README.md)

```text
§ 19.16 Methadone Registry.
  The office shall establish and maintain, either directly or through
contract, a central registry for purposes of preventing multiple
enrollment, ensuring accurate dosage delivery and facilitating disaster
management in methadone programs. The office shall require all methadone
programs to utilize such registry and shall have the power to assess
methadone programs such fees as are necessary and appropriate.
```
