# Mental Hygiene Law § 19.03 — Definitions

[Mental Hygiene Law](../../README.md) › [Title D — Alcoholism and Substance Abuse Act](../README.md) › [Article 19 — Office of Alcoholism and Substance Abuse](README.md)

```text
§ 19.03 Definitions. As used in this title:
  1. "Office" means the office of alcoholism and substance abuse
services.
  2. "Commissioner" means the commissioner of the office of alcoholism
and substance abuse services.
```
