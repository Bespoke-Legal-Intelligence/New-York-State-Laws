# Mental Hygiene Law § 19.10 — Status of facilities

[Mental Hygiene Law](../../README.md) › [Title D — Alcoholism and Substance Abuse Act](../README.md) › [Article 19 — Office of Alcoholism and Substance Abuse](README.md)

```text
§ 19.10 Status of facilities.
  A rehabilitation center, a medical examination facility, or such other
facility or facilities as may be established and operated by the office
pursuant to the provisions of section 19.09 of this article shall be
mental hygiene facilities in the department for the purpose of the
facilities development corporation act.
```
