# Arts and Cultural Affairs Law § 57.27 — Records with statewide significance

[Arts and Cultural Affairs Law](../../README.md) › [Title U — Divisions of History and Public Records](../README.md) › [Article 57-A — Local Government Records Law](README.md)

```text
  § 57.27. Records with statewide significance. The commissioner of
education is authorized to designate particular local government records
for permanent retention because of their enduring statewide
significance.
```
