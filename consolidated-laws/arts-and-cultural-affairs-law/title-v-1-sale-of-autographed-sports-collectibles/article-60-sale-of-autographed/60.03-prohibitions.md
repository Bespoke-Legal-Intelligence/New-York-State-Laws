# Arts and Cultural Affairs Law § 60.03 — Prohibitions

[Arts and Cultural Affairs Law](../../README.md) › [Title V-1 — Sale of Autographed Sports Collectibles](../README.md) › [Article 60 — Sale of Autographed Sports Collectibles](README.md)

```text
  § 60.03. Prohibitions. No dealer shall represent that a collectible is
autographed if said dealer knows, or has reason to believe, that it was
signed other than by the sports personality in his or her own hand.
```
