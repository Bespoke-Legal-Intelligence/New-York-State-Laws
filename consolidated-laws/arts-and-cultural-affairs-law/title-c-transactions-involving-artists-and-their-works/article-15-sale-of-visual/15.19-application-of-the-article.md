# Arts and Cultural Affairs Law § 15.19 — Application of the article

[Arts and Cultural Affairs Law](../../README.md) › [Title C — Transactions Involving Artists and Their Works](../README.md) › [Article 15 — Sale of Visual Art Objects and Sculptures Produced In Multiples](README.md)

```text
  § 15.19. Application of the article. This article shall apply to the
visual art objects governed by this article which are sold, offered for
sale, consigned or possessed with intent to sell in, into or from this
state. With respect to such multiples compliance with this article shall
commence six months after January first, nineteen hundred eighty-five.
```
