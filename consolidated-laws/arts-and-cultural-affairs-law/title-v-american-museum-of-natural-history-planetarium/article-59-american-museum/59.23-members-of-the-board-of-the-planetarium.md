# Arts and Cultural Affairs Law § 59.23 — Members of the board of the Planetarium Authority and employees not to profit

[Arts and Cultural Affairs Law](../../README.md) › [Title V — American Museum of Natural History Planetarium Authority](../README.md) › [Article 59 — American Museum of Natural History Planetarium Authority](README.md)

```text
  § 59.23. Members of the board of the Planetarium Authority and
employees not to profit. No officer, member of the board or employee of
the corporation shall receive or may be lawfully entitled to receive any
pecuniary profit from the operation thereof.
```
