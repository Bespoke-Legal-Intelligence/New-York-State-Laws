# Arts and Cultural Affairs Law § 59.27 — Application of law

[Arts and Cultural Affairs Law](../../README.md) › [Title V — American Museum of Natural History Planetarium Authority](../README.md) › [Article 59 — American Museum of Natural History Planetarium Authority](README.md)

```text
  § 59.27. Application of law. All of the general provisions of the
public authorities law or of any other law shall apply to the
Planetarium Authority herein continued in the same manner as if the
language of such law had been incorporated in full into this article,
except to the extent that such provisions are inconsistent with the
provisions of this article.
```
