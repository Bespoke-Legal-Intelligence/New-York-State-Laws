# Arts and Cultural Affairs Law § 20.43 — Inconsistent provisions of other laws superseded

[Arts and Cultural Affairs Law](../../README.md) › [Title E — New York State Cultural Resources Act](../README.md) › [Article 20 — New York State Cultural Resources Act](README.md)

```text
  § 20.43. Inconsistent provisions of other laws superseded. Insofar as
the provisions of this article and each special law enacted pursuant to
this article are inconsistent with the provisions of any other law,
general, special or local, the provisions of this article and each such
special law shall be controlling.
```
