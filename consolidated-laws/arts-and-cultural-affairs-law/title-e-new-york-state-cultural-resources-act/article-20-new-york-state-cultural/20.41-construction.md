# Arts and Cultural Affairs Law § 20.41 — Construction

[Arts and Cultural Affairs Law](../../README.md) › [Title E — New York State Cultural Resources Act](../README.md) › [Article 20 — New York State Cultural Resources Act](README.md)

```text
  § 20.41. Construction. This article and each special law enacted
pursuant to this article, being necessary for the welfare of the state
and its inhabitants, shall be liberally construed so as to effectuate
its purposes.
```
