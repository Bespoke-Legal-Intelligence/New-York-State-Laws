# Arts and Cultural Affairs Law § 20.45 — Separability

[Arts and Cultural Affairs Law](../../README.md) › [Title E — New York State Cultural Resources Act](../README.md) › [Article 20 — New York State Cultural Resources Act](README.md)

```text
  § 20.45. Separability. If any provision of any section of this article
and each special law enacted pursuant to this article or the application
thereof to any person or circumstance shall be judged invalid by a court
of competent jurisdiction, such order or judgment shall be confined in
its operation to the controversy in which it was rendered, and shall not
affect or invalidate the remainder of any provision of any section of
this article and each such special law or the application thereof to any
other person or circumstances, and to this end each provision of this
article and each such special law is hereby declared to be severable.
```
