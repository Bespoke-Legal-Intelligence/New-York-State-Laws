# Arts and Cultural Affairs Law § 33.07 — Imitation of a trade-mark defined

[Arts and Cultural Affairs Law](../../README.md) › [Title K — Trade-marks](../README.md) › [Article 33 — Offenses Against Trade-marks](README.md)

```text
  § 33.07. Imitation of a trade-mark defined. An "imitation of a
trade-mark" is that which so far resembles a genuine trade-mark as to be
likely to induce the belief that it is genuine, whether by the use of
words or letters, similar in appearance or in sound, or by any sign,
device or other means whatsoever.
```
