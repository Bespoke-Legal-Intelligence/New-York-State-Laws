# Arts and Cultural Affairs Law § 25.08 — Additional printing on tickets

[Arts and Cultural Affairs Law](../../README.md) › [Title G — Regulation of Sale of Theatre Tickets](../README.md) › [Article 25 — Tickets to Places of Entertainment](README.md)

```text
  * § 25.08. Additional printing on tickets. Every operator of a place
of entertainment having a permanent seating capacity in excess of five
thousand persons shall, if a price be charged for admission thereto,
print or endorse in a clear and legible manner on each ticket, "This
ticket may not be resold within one thousand five hundred feet from the
physical structure of this place of entertainment under penalty of law".
  * NB Repealed July 1, 2026
```
