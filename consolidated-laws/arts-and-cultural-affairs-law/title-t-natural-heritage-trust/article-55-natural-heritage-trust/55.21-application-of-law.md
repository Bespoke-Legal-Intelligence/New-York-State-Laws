# Arts and Cultural Affairs Law § 55.21 — Application of law

[Arts and Cultural Affairs Law](../../README.md) › [Title T — Natural Heritage Trust](../README.md) › [Article 55 — Natural Heritage Trust](README.md)

```text
  § 55.21. Application of law. All of the general provisions of the
public authorities law or of any other law shall apply to the natural
heritage trust herein continued in the same manner as if the language of
such law had been incorporated in full into this article, except to the
extent that such provisions are inconsistent with the provisions of this
article.
```
