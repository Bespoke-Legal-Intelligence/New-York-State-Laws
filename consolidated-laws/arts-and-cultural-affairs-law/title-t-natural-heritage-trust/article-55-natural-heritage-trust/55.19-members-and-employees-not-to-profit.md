# Arts and Cultural Affairs Law § 55.19 — Members and employees not to profit

[Arts and Cultural Affairs Law](../../README.md) › [Title T — Natural Heritage Trust](../README.md) › [Article 55 — Natural Heritage Trust](README.md)

```text
  § 55.19. Members and employees not to profit. No officer, member or
employee of the trust shall receive or may be lawfully entitled to
receive any pecuniary profit from the operation thereof except
reasonable compensation for services in effecting one or more of its
purposes herein set forth.
```
