# Arts and Cultural Affairs Law § 55.11 — Examination of title

[Arts and Cultural Affairs Law](../../README.md) › [Title T — Natural Heritage Trust](../README.md) › [Article 55 — Natural Heritage Trust](README.md)

```text
  § 55.11. Examination of title. The title to all real property proposed
to be acquired by the trust shall be examined and approved by the
attorney general of the state who shall also furnish any and all
necessary legal services and advice required to assist the trust in
accomplishing its corporate purposes.
```
