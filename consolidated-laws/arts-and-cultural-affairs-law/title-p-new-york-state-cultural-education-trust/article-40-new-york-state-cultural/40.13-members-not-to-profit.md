# Arts and Cultural Affairs Law § 40.13 — Members not to profit

[Arts and Cultural Affairs Law](../../README.md) › [Title P — New York State Cultural Education Trust](../README.md) › [Article 40 — New York State Cultural Education Trust](README.md)

```text
  § 40.13. Members not to profit. No member of the trust shall receive
or may be lawfully entitled to receive any pecuniary profit from the
operation of the trust.
```
