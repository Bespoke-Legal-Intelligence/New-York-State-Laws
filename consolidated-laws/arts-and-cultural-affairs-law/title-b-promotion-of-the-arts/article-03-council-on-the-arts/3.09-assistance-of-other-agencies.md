# Arts and Cultural Affairs Law § 3.09 — Assistance of other agencies

[Arts and Cultural Affairs Law](../../README.md) › [Title B — Promotion of the Arts](../README.md) › [Article 3 — Council On the Arts](README.md)

```text
  § 3.09. Assistance of other agencies. To effectuate the purposes of
this article, the council on the arts may request from any department,
board, bureau, commission or other agency of the state, and the same are
authorized to provide, such assistance, services and data as will enable
the council properly to carry out its powers and duties hereunder.
```
