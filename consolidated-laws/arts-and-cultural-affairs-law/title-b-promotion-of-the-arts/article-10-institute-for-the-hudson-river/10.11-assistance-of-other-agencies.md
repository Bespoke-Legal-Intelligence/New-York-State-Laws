# Arts and Cultural Affairs Law § 10.11 — Assistance of other agencies

[Arts and Cultural Affairs Law](../../README.md) › [Title B — Promotion of the Arts](../README.md) › [Article 10 — Institute For the Hudson River Collection](README.md)

```text
  § 10.11. Assistance of other agencies. The institute may request and
shall receive from any state agency or political subdivision of the
state, and the same are authorized to provide, such assistance and
information as will enable it to carry out its purposes and duties
pursuant to this article.
```
