# Arts and Cultural Affairs Law § 4.11 — Assistance of state agencies and political subdivisions

[Arts and Cultural Affairs Law](../../README.md) › [Title B — Promotion of the Arts](../README.md) › [Article 4 — Empire State Plaza Art Commission](README.md)

```text
  § 4.11. Assistance of state agencies and political subdivisions. The
commission may request and shall receive from any state agency or
political subdivision of the state such assistance and information as
will enable it to carry out its duties and accomplish its purposes.
```
