# Retirement and Social Security Law § 560 — Past service payments

[Retirement and Social Security Law](../README.md) › [Article 14-B — Special Retirement Plans For Sheriffs, Undersheriffs and Deputy Sheriffs Engaged In Law Enforcement Activities](README.md)

```text
  § 560. Past service payments.  Any past service payments required by a
county that adopts the provisions of this article may be paid over a
period of five or ten years determined by the county.
```
