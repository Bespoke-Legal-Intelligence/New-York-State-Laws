# Retirement and Social Security Law § 177-A — Investment in certain conventional mortgages

[Retirement and Social Security Law](../README.md) › [Article 4-A — Investments of Public Pension Funds](README.md)

```text
  § 177-a. Investment in certain conventional mortgages.
Notwithstanding any provision contained in this chapter or any other
general, special or local law or code to the contrary, within the
limitations authorized for investment in conventional mortgages, the
trustees of the fund may invest a part of its assets in first mortgages
on real property located anywhere within the boundaries of the United
States.
```
