# Retirement and Social Security Law § 159 — Miscellaneous

[Retirement and Social Security Law](../README.md) › [Article 3-B — Pension Forfeiture For Public Officials](README.md)

```text
  § 159. Miscellaneous. The remedies provided for in this article are
not intended to substitute for, limit or supersede the lawful authority
of any public officer, agency or other person to enforce any other right
or remedy provided for by law.
```
