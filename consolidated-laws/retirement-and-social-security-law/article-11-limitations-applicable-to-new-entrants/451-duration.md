# Retirement and Social Security Law § 451 — Duration

[Retirement and Social Security Law](../README.md) › [Article 11 — Limitations Applicable to New Entrants](README.md)

```text
  § 451. Duration.  Notwithstanding any other provision of this chapter
or of any other law, effective July first, nineteen hundred seventy-six,
all benefits provided by an actuarially funded public retirement system
of the state of New York or any municipality thereof shall continue with
respect to members to which article fourteen is applicable only until
December thirty-first, nineteen hundred seventy-six.
```
