# Retirement and Social Security Law § 333 — Election of certain optional retirement benefits by participating employers

[Retirement and Social Security Law](../../README.md) › [Article 8 — New York State Policemen's and Firemen's Retirement System Short Title](../README.md) › [Title 5 — Participation In System By Political Subdivisions and Other Organizations](README.md)

```text
  § 333. Election of certain optional retirement benefits by
participating employers.  By the adoption, filing and approval, where
required, of a resolution in the manner provided by section three
hundred thirty or section three hundred thirty-one of this article, a
participating employer may elect to provide for its own employees the
benefit provided for persons in the employ of the state listed in
subdivision j of section three hundred forty-one and may elect to
provide the benefits provided for employees of participating employers
in section three hundred sixty-b and section three hundred
seventy-five-i of this article.
  Such election may be for a limited period if such limitation is
included in the resolution, upon the expiration of which such election
will be deemed revoked.
```
