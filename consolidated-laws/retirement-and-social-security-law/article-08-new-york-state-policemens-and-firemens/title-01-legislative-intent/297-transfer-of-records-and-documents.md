# Retirement and Social Security Law § 297 — Transfer of records and documents

[Retirement and Social Security Law](../../README.md) › [Article 8 — New York State Policemen's and Firemen's Retirement System Short Title](../README.md) › [Title 1 — Legislative Intent; Transitory Provisions](README.md)

```text
  § 297. Transfer of records and documents.  Records and documents of
the employees' retirement system relating to policemen and firemen and
other persons who are members of such system pursuant to sections
eighty-one to eighty-eight, inclusive, shall be transferred to the
retirement system established by this article.
```
