# Retirement and Social Security Law § 805 — Records

[Retirement and Social Security Law](../README.md) › [Article 18 — Provisions Generally Applicable to Public Retirement Systems](README.md)

```text
  § 805. Records.  Nothing in this article shall be construed to affect
the authority of a public retirement system to require that evidence of
service be based upon records kept in the ordinary course of business.
```
