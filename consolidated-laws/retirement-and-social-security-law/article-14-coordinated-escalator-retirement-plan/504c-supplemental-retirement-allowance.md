# Retirement and Social Security Law § 504-C — Supplemental retirement allowance

[Retirement and Social Security Law](../README.md) › [Article 14 — Coordinated-escalator Retirement Plan](README.md)

```text
  § 504-c. Supplemental retirement allowance. Individuals who are
receiving benefits from the New York state and local employees'
retirement system pursuant to this article and who will not be eligible
for escalation pursuant to section five hundred ten of this article
shall be entitled to supplemental retirement benefits provided in
section seventy-eight of this chapter if they meet all of the
eligibility requirements provided in section seventy-eight of this
chapter.
```
