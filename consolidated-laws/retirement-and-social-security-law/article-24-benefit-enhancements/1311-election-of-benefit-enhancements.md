# Retirement and Social Security Law § 1311 — Election of benefit enhancements

[Retirement and Social Security Law](../README.md) › [Article 24 — Benefit Enhancements](README.md)

```text
  § 1311. Election of benefit enhancements. a. The state of New York may
elect to provide employees who hold a position represented by the
recognized collective bargaining units affiliated with the New York
state united teachers employee organization as certified by his or her
employer the benefit enhancements provided for in section thirteen
hundred twelve of this article.
  b. Such election is made by the governor to the retirement system upon
receipt of a request by the New York state united teachers employee
organization.
```
