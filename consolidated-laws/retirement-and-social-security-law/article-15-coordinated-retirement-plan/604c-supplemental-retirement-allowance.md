# Retirement and Social Security Law § 604-C — Supplemental retirement allowance

[Retirement and Social Security Law](../README.md) › [Article 15 — Coordinated Retirement Plan](README.md)

```text
  * § 604-c. Supplemental retirement allowance. Individuals who are
receiving benefits from the New York state and local employees'
retirement system pursuant to this article shall be entitled to
supplemental retirement benefits provided in section seventy-eight of
this chapter if they meet all of the eligibility requirements provided
in section seventy-eight of this chapter.
  * NB There are 3 § 604-c's
```
