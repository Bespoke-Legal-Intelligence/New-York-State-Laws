# Retirement and Social Security Law § 143 — Restriction on use of retirement or pension funds

[Retirement and Social Security Law](../README.md) › [Article 3 — Federal Old-age and Survivors Insurance Coverage For Certain Public Employees](README.md)

```text
  § 143. Restriction on use of retirement or pension funds.  No moneys
belonging to any public administered and operated retirement or pension
system or plan shall be appropriated or used for any purpose, or for any
payment, authorized or required by this article.
```
