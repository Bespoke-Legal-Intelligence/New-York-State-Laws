# Retirement and Social Security Law § 132 — New York state social security agency established

[Retirement and Social Security Law](../README.md) › [Article 3 — Federal Old-age and Survivors Insurance Coverage For Certain Public Employees](README.md)

```text
  § 132. New York state social security agency established.  There is
hereby established in the state retirement system an agency of the state
to be known as the "New York state social security agency." The director
shall have charge of such agency.
```
