# Retirement and Social Security Law § 145 — Limitations on obligations

[Retirement and Social Security Law](../README.md) › [Article 3 — Federal Old-age and Survivors Insurance Coverage For Certain Public Employees](README.md)

```text
  § 145. Limitations on obligations.  Old-age and survivors insurance
coverage provided pursuant to this article shall not constitute
membership in a pension or retirement system of the state or of a
political subdivision thereof, nor shall such coverage create a
contractual relationship between the state or a political subdivision
thereof, and any employee.
```
