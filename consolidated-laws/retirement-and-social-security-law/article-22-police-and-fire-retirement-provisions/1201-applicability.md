# Retirement and Social Security Law § 1201 — Applicability

[Retirement and Social Security Law](../README.md) › [Article 22 — Police and Fire Retirement Provisions](README.md)

```text
  § 1201. Applicability. Notwithstanding any provision of law to the
contrary, the provisions of this article shall be applicable to all
employees in the retirement system who first joined such system on or
after January first, two thousand ten.
```
