# Retirement and Social Security Law § 155 — Limitations

[Retirement and Social Security Law](../README.md) › [Article 3-A — Reporting and Disclosure](README.md)

```text
  § 155. Limitations.  Nothing contained in this article requiring
reporting and disclosure shall be construed to create, revive, extend,
or otherwise affect the entitlement of a member, retired member, or a
beneficiary to any retirement benefit.
```
