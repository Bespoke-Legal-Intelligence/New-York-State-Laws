# Retirement and Social Security Law § 20 — The funds of the retirement system

[Retirement and Social Security Law](../../README.md) › [Article 2 — New York State Employees' Retirement System](../README.md) › [Title 3 — Funds of the System; Members' Contributions and Employers' Contributions](README.md)

```text
  § 20. The funds of the retirement system.  The funds hereby continued
are the annuity savings fund, the annuity reserve fund, the pension
accumulation fund and the pension reserve fund.
```
