# Retirement and Social Security Law § 100 — Payment of retirement allowances

[Retirement and Social Security Law](../../README.md) › [Article 2 — New York State Employees' Retirement System](../README.md) › [Title 11 — Provisions Relating to Retired Members](README.md)

```text
  § 100. Payment of retirement allowances.  Retirement allowances shall
be payable on the first day of each and every month beginning on the
first day of the month following the effective date of retirement. Upon
the death of a retired member, however, the retirement allowance due for
that part of the month prior to his death shall be paid forthwith.
```
