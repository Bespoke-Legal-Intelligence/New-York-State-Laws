# Retirement and Social Security Law § 194 — Payment of supplemental retirement allowances

[Retirement and Social Security Law](../README.md) › [Article 6 — Supplemental Retirement Allowance Act](README.md)

```text
  § 194. Payment of supplemental retirement allowances.  On or before
the last day of each month, there shall be paid to each state retired
employee and state retired teacher, from the supplemental pension fund,
the monthly supplemental retirement allowance payment to which he may be
entitled under the provisions of this article.
```
