# Retirement and Social Security Law § 201 — Restriction on use of retirement or pension funds

[Retirement and Social Security Law](../README.md) › [Article 6 — Supplemental Retirement Allowance Act](README.md)

```text
  § 201. Restriction on use of retirement or pension funds.  No moneys
belonging to any publicly administered and operated retirement or
pension system or plan shall be appropriated or used for any purpose, or
for any payment, authorized or required by this article.
```
