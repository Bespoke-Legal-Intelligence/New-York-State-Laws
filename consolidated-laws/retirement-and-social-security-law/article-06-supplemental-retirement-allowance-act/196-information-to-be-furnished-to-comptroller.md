# Retirement and Social Security Law § 196 — Information to be furnished to comptroller

[Retirement and Social Security Law](../README.md) › [Article 6 — Supplemental Retirement Allowance Act](README.md)

```text
  § 196. Information to be furnished to comptroller.  The comptroller
shall have authority to require any department or agency of the state to
furnish him with such records, information and data as he may need to
carry out the provisions of this article.
```
