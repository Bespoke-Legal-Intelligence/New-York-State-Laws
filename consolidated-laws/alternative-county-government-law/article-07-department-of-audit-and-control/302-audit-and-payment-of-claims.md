# Alternative County Government Law § 302 — Audit and payment of claims

[Alternative County Government Law](../README.md) › [Article 7 — Department of Audit and Control](README.md)

```text
  § 302. Audit and payment of claims. All claims, accounts and demands
against the county shall be submitted, audited, allowed and paid in the
manner provided by section three hundred sixty-nine of the county law as
now in force or hereafter amended.
```
