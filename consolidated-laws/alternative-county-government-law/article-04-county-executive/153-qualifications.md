# Alternative County Government Law § 153 — Qualifications

[Alternative County Government Law](../README.md) › [Article 4 — County Executive](README.md)

```text
  § 153. Qualifications. A person holding the office of county executive
shall have the qualifications prescribed by section three of the public
officers law, except as otherwise provided in section fifty-one of this
chapter.
```
