# Alternative County Government Law § 151 — Compensation

[Alternative County Government Law](../README.md) › [Article 4 — County Executive](README.md)

```text
  § 151. Compensation. The county executive shall be paid an annual
compensation to be fixed by the board of supervisors of the county in
accordance with the provisions of the county law.
```
