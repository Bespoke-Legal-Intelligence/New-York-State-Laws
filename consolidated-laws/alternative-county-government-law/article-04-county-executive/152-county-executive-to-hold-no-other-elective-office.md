# Alternative County Government Law § 152 — County executive to hold no other elective office

[Alternative County Government Law](../README.md) › [Article 4 — County Executive](README.md)

```text
  § 152. County executive to hold no other elective office. The county
executive shall, during the term for which he was elected or appointed,
hold no other elective office, except as otherwise provided in section
fifty of this chapter.
```
