# Alternative County Government Law § 203 — Elective and appointive officers

[Alternative County Government Law](../README.md) › [Article 5 — County Departments and Offices](README.md)

```text
  § 203. Elective and appointive officers. Except as otherwise provided
in this chapter, all officers paid from county funds shall continue to
be elected or appointed as provided in the constitution and laws of this
state.
```
