# Alternative County Government Law § 206 — Organization of departments and offices

[Alternative County Government Law](../README.md) › [Article 5 — County Departments and Offices](README.md)

```text
  § 206. Organization of departments and offices. Subject to approval by
the board of supervisors of the county, the county executive may
organize departments and offices into such divisions, bureaus or offices
and make such assignments of functions, powers and duties among them,
and from time to time change such organization or assignments, as he may
consider advisable.
```
