# Alternative County Government Law § 601 — County finance

[Alternative County Government Law](../README.md) › [Article 13 — General Provisions](README.md)

```text
  § 601. County finance. The provisions of the local finance law shall
continue to apply to a county which has adopted an alternative form of
county government under this chapter.
```
