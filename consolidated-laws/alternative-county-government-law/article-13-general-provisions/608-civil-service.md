# Alternative County Government Law § 608 — Civil service

[Alternative County Government Law](../README.md) › [Article 13 — General Provisions](README.md)

```text
  § 608. Civil service. Appointments and promotions in the county
government shall continue to be made in accordance with the provisions
of the civil service law and rules of civil service.
```
