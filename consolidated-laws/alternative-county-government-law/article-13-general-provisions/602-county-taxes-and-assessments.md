# Alternative County Government Law § 602 — County taxes and assessments

[Alternative County Government Law](../README.md) › [Article 13 — General Provisions](README.md)

```text
  § 602. County taxes and assessments. The provisions of the tax law or
other applicable general or special statute shall continue to apply to a
county which has adopted an alternative form of county government.
```
