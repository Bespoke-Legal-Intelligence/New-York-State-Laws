# Alternative County Government Law § 604 — Administration of education

[Alternative County Government Law](../README.md) › [Article 13 — General Provisions](README.md)

```text
  § 604. Administration of education. Nothing in this chapter shall be
construed to affect or impair the administration of education within the
county.
```
