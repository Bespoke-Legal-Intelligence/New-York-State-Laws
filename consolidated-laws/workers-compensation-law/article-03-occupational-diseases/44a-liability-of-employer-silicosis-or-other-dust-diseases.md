# Workers' Compensation Law § 44-A — Liability of employer; silicosis or other dust diseases

[Workers' Compensation Law](../README.md) › [Article 3 — Occupational Diseases](README.md)

```text
  § 44-a. Liability of employer; silicosis or other dust diseases. The
employer in whose employment an employee was last exposed to an
injurious dust hazard shall be liable for the payments required by this
chapter when disability or death of the employee shall be due to
silicosis or other dust disease.
```
