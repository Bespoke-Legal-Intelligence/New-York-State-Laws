# Workers' Compensation Law § 42 — Date of disablement

[Workers' Compensation Law](../README.md) › [Article 3 — Occupational Diseases](README.md)

```text
  § 42. Date of disablement. For the purposes of this article the date
of disablement shall be such date as the board may determine on the
hearing on the claim.
```
