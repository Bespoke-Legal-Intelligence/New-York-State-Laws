# Workers' Compensation Law § 41 — Examining physicians

[Workers' Compensation Law](../README.md) › [Article 3 — Occupational Diseases](README.md)

```text
  § 41. Examining physicians. The chairman shall appoint one or more
physicians whose duty it shall be to examine any claimant under this
article and to make a report in such form as the chairman may require.
```
