# Workers' Compensation Law § 48 — Diseases which are accidents

[Workers' Compensation Law](../README.md) › [Article 3 — Occupational Diseases](README.md)

```text
  § 48. Diseases which are accidents. Nothing in this article shall
affect the rights of an employee to recover compensation in respect to a
disease to which this article does not apply if the disease is an
accidental personal injury within the meaning of subdivision seven of
section two of this chapter.
```
