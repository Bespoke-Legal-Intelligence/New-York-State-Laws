# Workers' Compensation Law § 37 — Definitions

[Workers' Compensation Law](../README.md) › [Article 3 — Occupational Diseases](README.md)

```text
  § 37. Definitions. Whenever used in this article: 1.  "Disability"
means the state of being disabled from earning full wages at the work at
which the employee was last employed.
  2. "Disablement" means the act of becoming so disabled as defined in
subdivision one.
```
