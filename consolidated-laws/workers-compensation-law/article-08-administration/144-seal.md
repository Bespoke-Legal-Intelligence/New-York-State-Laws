# Workers' Compensation Law § 144 — Seal

[Workers' Compensation Law](../README.md) › [Article 8 — Administration](README.md)

```text
  § 144. Seal. The board may adopt a seal and require that it be used
for the authentication of orders and proceedings and for such other
purposes as the board may prescribe.
```
