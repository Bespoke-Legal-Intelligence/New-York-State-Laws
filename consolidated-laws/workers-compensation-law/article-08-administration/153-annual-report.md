# Workers' Compensation Law § 153 — Annual report

[Workers' Compensation Law](../README.md) › [Article 8 — Administration](README.md)

```text
  § 153. Annual report. The board shall on or before the first day of
February in each year make an annual report in writing to the governor,
stating in detail the work it has done in hearing and deciding cases and
otherwise.
```
