# Workers' Compensation Law § 115 — Limitation of time

[Workers' Compensation Law](../README.md) › [Article 7 — Miscellaneous Provisions](README.md)

```text
  § 115. Limitation of time. No limitation of time provided in this
chapter shall run as against any person who is mentally incompetent or a
minor so long as he has no committee or guardian.
```
