# Workers' Compensation Law § 129 — Actions or causes of action pending

[Workers' Compensation Law](../README.md) › [Article 7 — Miscellaneous Provisions](README.md)

```text
  § 129. Actions or causes of action pending. This act shall not affect
any action pending or cause of action existing or which accrued prior to
July first, nineten hundred and twenty-two.
```
