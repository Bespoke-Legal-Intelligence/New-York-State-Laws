# Workers' Compensation Law § 121 — Depositions

[Workers' Compensation Law](../README.md) › [Article 7 — Miscellaneous Provisions](README.md)

```text
  § 121. Depositions. The chairman or board may cause depositions of
witnesses residing within or without the state to be taken in the manner
prescribed by law for like depositions in civil actions in the supreme
court.
```
