# Workers' Compensation Law § 128 — Unconstitutional provisions

[Workers' Compensation Law](../README.md) › [Article 7 — Miscellaneous Provisions](README.md)

```text
  § 128. Unconstitutional provisions. If any section or provision of
this chapter be decided by the courts to be unconstitutional or invalid,
the same shall not affect the validity of the chapter as a whole or any
part thereof other than the part so decided to be unconstitutional or
invalid.
```
