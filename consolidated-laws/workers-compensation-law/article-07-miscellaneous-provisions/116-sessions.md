# Workers' Compensation Law § 116 — Sessions

[Workers' Compensation Law](../README.md) › [Article 7 — Miscellaneous Provisions](README.md)

```text
  § 116. Sessions. The offices of the chairman and the board shall be
open for business during all business hours of all days except Sundays
and legal holidays. All sessions of the board shall be public. The
records of the board shall contain a record of each case considered, and
all awards, decisions or orders with respect thereto.  For convenience
of parties and prevention of delay or expense, the board may hold
sessions in cities other than Albany.
```
