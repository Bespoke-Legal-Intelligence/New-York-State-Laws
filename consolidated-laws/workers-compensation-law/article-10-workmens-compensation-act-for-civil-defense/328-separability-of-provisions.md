# Workers' Compensation Law § 328 — Separability of provisions

[Workers' Compensation Law](../README.md) › [Article 10 — Workmen's Compensation Act For Civil Defense Volunteers](README.md)

```text
  § 328. Separability of provisions. If any provisions of this act or
the application thereof to any person or circumstances is held invalid,
the remainder of this act and the application of such provision to other
persons or circumstances shall not be affected thereby.
```
