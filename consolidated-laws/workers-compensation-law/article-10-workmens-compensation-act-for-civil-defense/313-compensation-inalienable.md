# Workers' Compensation Law § 313 — Compensation inalienable

[Workers' Compensation Law](../README.md) › [Article 10 — Workmen's Compensation Act For Civil Defense Volunteers](README.md)

```text
  § 313. Compensation inalienable. Compensation payable under this
article shall not be assigned or released and shall be exempt from all
claims of creditors and from levy, execution and attachment or other
remedy for recovery or collection of a debt, which exemption may not be
waived.
```
