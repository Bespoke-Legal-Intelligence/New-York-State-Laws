# Workers' Compensation Law § 323 — Limitation of liability

[Workers' Compensation Law](../README.md) › [Article 10 — Workmen's Compensation Act For Civil Defense Volunteers](README.md)

```text
  § 323. Limitation of liability. The special fund for civil defense
volunteers created by this article shall be the sole and exclusive
source for payment of compensation provided by this article for civil
defense volunteers.
```
