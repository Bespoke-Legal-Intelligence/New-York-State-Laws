# Workers' Compensation Law § 321 — Reinsurance

[Workers' Compensation Law](../README.md) › [Article 10 — Workmen's Compensation Act For Civil Defense Volunteers](README.md)

```text
  § 321. Reinsurance. Funds credited to the special fund for the
purposes of this article may be used to effect insurance or reinsurance
with the war damage corporation or with any other authority or
instrumentality, public or private, or otherwise to distribute the
liability for compensation payable to civil defense volunteers.
```
