# Workers' Compensation Law § 304 — Evidence of authorized services

[Workers' Compensation Law](../README.md) › [Article 10 — Workmen's Compensation Act For Civil Defense Volunteers](README.md)

```text
  § 304. Evidence of authorized services. For the purpose of
administering this article a certified copy of regulations and orders of
the commission or state director shall be evidence thereof when filed
with the chairman; but if not so filed the burden of proving an
authorized civil defense service or duty or activity shall be on the
claimant.
```
