# Workers' Compensation Law § 322 — Rehabilitation

[Workers' Compensation Law](../README.md) › [Article 10 — Workmen's Compensation Act For Civil Defense Volunteers](README.md)

```text
  § 322. Rehabilitation. Fees for rehabilitation services to disabled
civil defense volunteers shall be paid out of the special fund in an
amount approved by the chairman, and shall be the reasonable and
necessary cost of such services, including services of a physician or
rehabilitation facility specially qualified to render rehabilitation
services. Expenses of rehabilitation may include travel, board and room,
when necessary.
```
