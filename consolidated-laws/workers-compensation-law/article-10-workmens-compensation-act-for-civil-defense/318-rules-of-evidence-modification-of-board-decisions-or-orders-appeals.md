# Workers' Compensation Law § 318 — Rules of evidence; modification of board decisions or orders; appeals

[Workers' Compensation Law](../README.md) › [Article 10 — Workmen's Compensation Act For Civil Defense Volunteers](README.md)

```text
  § 318. Rules of evidence; modification of board decisions or orders;
appeals.  The provisions of sections two hundred twenty-two, two hundred
twenty-three and two hundred twenty-four are made applicable to claims
for compensation under this article.
```
