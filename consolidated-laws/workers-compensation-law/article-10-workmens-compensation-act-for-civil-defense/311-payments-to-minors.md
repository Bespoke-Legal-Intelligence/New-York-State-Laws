# Workers' Compensation Law § 311 — Payments to minors

[Workers' Compensation Law](../README.md) › [Article 10 — Workmen's Compensation Act For Civil Defense Volunteers](README.md)

```text
  § 311. Payments to minors. Civil defense volunteers who are minors
shall be deemed to be sui juris for the purpose of receiving payment of
compensation under this article. Work as a civil defense volunteer shall
not be deemed an employment in violation of any of the provisions of the
labor law for purposes of section fourteen-a.
```
