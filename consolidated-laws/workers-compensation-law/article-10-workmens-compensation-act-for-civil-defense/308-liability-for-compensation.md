# Workers' Compensation Law § 308 — Liability for compensation

[Workers' Compensation Law](../README.md) › [Article 10 — Workmen's Compensation Act For Civil Defense Volunteers](README.md)

```text
  § 308. Liability for compensation. Compensation provided in this
article, including cash benefits, medical and chiropractic care and
funeral expenses, shall be paid by the chairman promptly out of the
special fund created under this article and, except as to a disallowed
claim, without decision by the board.
```
