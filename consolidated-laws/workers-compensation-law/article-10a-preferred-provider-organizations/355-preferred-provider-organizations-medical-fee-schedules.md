# Workers' Compensation Law § 355 — Preferred provider organizations; medical fee schedules

[Workers' Compensation Law](../README.md) › [Article 10-A — Preferred Provider Organizations](README.md)

```text
  § 355. Preferred provider organizations; medical fee schedules.  The
medical fee schedules authorized pursuant to section thirteen of this
chapter shall not apply to any medical services provided by a preferred
provider organization pursuant to the provisions of this article.
```
