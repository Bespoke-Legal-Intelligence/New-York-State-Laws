# Workers' Compensation Law § 32-A — Waivers of specific coverage prohibited

[Workers' Compensation Law](../README.md) › [Article 2 — Compensation](README.md)

```text
  § 32-a. Waivers of specific coverage prohibited. All employers doing
business in this state shall ensure that their employees working in New
York state are insured for all protections required under this chapter.
Waivers of coverage shall not be available to out of state employers.
```
