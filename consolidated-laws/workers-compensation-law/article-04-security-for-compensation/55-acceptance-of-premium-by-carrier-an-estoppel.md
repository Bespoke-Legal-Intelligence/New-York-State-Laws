# Workers' Compensation Law § 55 — Acceptance of premium by carrier an estoppel

[Workers' Compensation Law](../README.md) › [Article 4 — Security For Compensation](README.md)

```text
  § 55. Acceptance of premium by carrier an estoppel. Acceptance of a
premium on a policy securing to an employee compensation, either alone
or in connection with other insurance, shall estop the carrier so
accepting from pleading that the employment of such employee is not a
hazardous employment or the employment is not carried on for pecuniary
gain.
```
