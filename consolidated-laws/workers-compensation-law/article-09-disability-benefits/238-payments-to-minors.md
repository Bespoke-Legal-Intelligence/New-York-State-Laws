# Workers' Compensation Law § 238 — Payments to minors

[Workers' Compensation Law](../README.md) › [Article 9 — Disability Benefits](README.md)

```text
  § 238. Payments to minors. Minors shall be deemed to be sui juris for
the purpose of receiving payment of benefits under this article.
```
