# Workers' Compensation Law § 233 — Inspection of records of employers

[Workers' Compensation Law](../README.md) › [Article 9 — Disability Benefits](README.md)

```text
  § 233. Inspection of records of employers. All books, records and
payrolls of employers shall be open for inspection by the chairman or by
any officer or employee of the board designated by him for the purpose
of ascertaining the amount of wages and the number of employees and such
other information as may be necessary in the administration of this
article. Any person who refuses to allow the chairman or his authorized
representative to inspect any such books, records or payrolls relative
to the enforcement of this article shall be guilty of a misdemeanor.
```
