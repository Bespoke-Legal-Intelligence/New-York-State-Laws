# Workers' Compensation Law § 239 — Representation before the board

[Workers' Compensation Law](../README.md) › [Article 9 — Disability Benefits](README.md)

```text
  § 239. Representation before the board. Any person, firm, or
corporation licensed by the board under section twenty-four-a or
subdivision three-b of section fifty shall be deemed to be authorized to
appear in behalf of claimants or self insured employers, as the case may
be, in contested disability claims under this article.
```
