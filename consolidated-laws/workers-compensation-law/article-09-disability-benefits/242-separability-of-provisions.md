# Workers' Compensation Law § 242 — Separability of provisions

[Workers' Compensation Law](../README.md) › [Article 9 — Disability Benefits](README.md)

```text
  § 242. Separability of provisions. If any provision of this act or the
application thereof to any person or circumstances is held invalid, the
remainder of this act and the application of such provision to other
persons or circumstances shall not be affected thereby.
```
