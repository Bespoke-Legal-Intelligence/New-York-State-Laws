# Workers' Compensation Law § 230 — Destruction of records

[Workers' Compensation Law](../README.md) › [Article 9 — Disability Benefits](README.md)

```text
  § 230. Destruction of records. All records and documents relative to
this article required to be filed with the chairman or board may be
destroyed, in accordance with the state finance law.
```
