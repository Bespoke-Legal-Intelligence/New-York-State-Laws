# Workers' Compensation Law § 97 — Inspections

[Workers' Compensation Law](../README.md) › [Article 6 — State Insurance Fund](README.md)

```text
  § 97. Inspections. The commissioners shall have the right to inspect
the plants and establishments of employers insured in the state
insurance fund; and the inspectors designated by the commissioners shall
have free access to such premises during regular working hours.
```
