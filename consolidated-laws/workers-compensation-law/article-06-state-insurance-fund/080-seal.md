# Workers' Compensation Law § 80 — Seal

[Workers' Compensation Law](../README.md) › [Article 6 — State Insurance Fund](README.md)

```text
  § 80. Seal. The commissioners shall adopt a seal and shall require it
to be used for the authentication of records and documents as may be
necessary and proper.
```
