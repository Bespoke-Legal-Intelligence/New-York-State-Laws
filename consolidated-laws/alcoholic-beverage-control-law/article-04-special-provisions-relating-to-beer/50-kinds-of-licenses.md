# Alcoholic Beverage Control Law § 50 — Kinds of licenses

[Alcoholic Beverage Control Law](../README.md) › [Article 4 — Special Provisions Relating to Beer](README.md)

```text
  § 50. Kinds of licenses.  The following kinds of licenses may be
issued for the brewing and sale of beer:
  1. Brewer's licenses;
  2. Brewer's retail licenses;
  3. Wholesaler's licenses;
  4. Vendor's licenses;
  5. Licenses to sell beer at retail for consumption off the premises;
and
  6. Licenses to sell beer at retail for consumption on the premises.
```
