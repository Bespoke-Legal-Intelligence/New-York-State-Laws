# Alcoholic Beverage Control Law § 120 — Decisions by liquor authority

[Alcoholic Beverage Control Law](../README.md) › [Article 8 — General Provisions](README.md)

```text
  § 120. Decisions by liquor authority. A decision upon any application
or hearing submitted to or held by the liquor authority shall be
rendered within thirty days after such submission or hearing.
```
