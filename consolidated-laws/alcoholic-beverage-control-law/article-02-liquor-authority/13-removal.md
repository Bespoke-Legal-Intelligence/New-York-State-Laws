# Alcoholic Beverage Control Law § 13 — Removal

[Alcoholic Beverage Control Law](../README.md) › [Article 2 — Liquor Authority](README.md)

```text
  § 13. Removal.  Any member of the authority may be removed by the
governor for cause after an opportunity to be heard. A statement of the
cause of his removal shall be filed by the governor in the office of the
secretary of state.
```
