# Alcoholic Beverage Control Law § 12 — Expenses

[Alcoholic Beverage Control Law](../README.md) › [Article 2 — Liquor Authority](README.md)

```text
  § 12. Expenses.  Each member of the authority shall be entitled to his
expenses actually and necessarily incurred by him in the performance of
his duties.
```
