# Alcoholic Beverage Control Law § 61-B — Importer's license

[Alcoholic Beverage Control Law](../README.md) › [Article 5 — Special Provisions Relating to Liquor](README.md)

```text
  § 61-b. Importer's license. An importer's license shall authorize the
holder thereof to import alcoholic beverages into this state and sell
such alcoholic beverages to licensed wholesalers authorized to sell such
alcoholic beverages.
```
