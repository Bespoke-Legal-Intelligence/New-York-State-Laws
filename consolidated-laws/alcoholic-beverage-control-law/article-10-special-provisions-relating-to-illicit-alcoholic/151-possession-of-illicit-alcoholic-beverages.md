# Alcoholic Beverage Control Law § 151 — Possession of illicit alcoholic beverages

[Alcoholic Beverage Control Law](../README.md) › [Article 10 — Special Provisions Relating to Illicit Alcoholic Beverages and Stills](README.md)

```text
  § 151. Possession of illicit alcoholic beverages.  Any person who
shall knowingly possess or have under his control any illicit alcoholic
beverages is guilty of a misdemeanor.
```
