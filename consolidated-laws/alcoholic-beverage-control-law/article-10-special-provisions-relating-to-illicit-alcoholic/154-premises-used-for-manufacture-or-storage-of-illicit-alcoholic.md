# Alcoholic Beverage Control Law § 154 — Premises used for manufacture or storage of illicit alcoholic beverages

[Alcoholic Beverage Control Law](../README.md) › [Article 10 — Special Provisions Relating to Illicit Alcoholic Beverages and Stills](README.md)

```text
  § 154. Premises used for manufacture or storage of illicit alcoholic
beverages.  Any person who, being the owner, lessee, or occupant of any
room, shed, tenement, booth or building, float or vessel, or part
thereof, knowingly permits the same to be used for the manufacture,
distribution, purchase, sale, bottling, rectifying, blending, treating,
fortifying, mixing, processing, warehousing, transportation, distilling,
or storage of an illicit alcoholic beverage, is guilty of a misdemeanor.
```
