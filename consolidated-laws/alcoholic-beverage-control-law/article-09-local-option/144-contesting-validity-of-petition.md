# Alcoholic Beverage Control Law § 144 — Contesting validity of petition

[Alcoholic Beverage Control Law](../README.md) › [Article 9 — Local Option](README.md)

```text
  § 144. Contesting validity of petition.  Proceedings may be instituted
and maintained to contest the validity, sufficiency or legality of any
petition provided for in this article, in the manner provided by the
election law, as though such petition were a certificate of nomination.
```
