# Alcoholic Beverage Control Law § 145 — False statements and forgery

[Alcoholic Beverage Control Law](../README.md) › [Article 9 — Local Option](README.md)

```text
  § 145. False statements and forgery.  A person who forges the
signature of any person upon any petition or statement provided for in
this article shall be guilty of forgery in the third degree and on
conviction thereof be punished accordingly.
```
