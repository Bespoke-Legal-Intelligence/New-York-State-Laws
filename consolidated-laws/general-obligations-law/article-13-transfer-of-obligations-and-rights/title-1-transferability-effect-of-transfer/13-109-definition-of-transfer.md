# General Obligations Law § 13-109 — Definition of "transfer

[General Obligations Law](../../README.md) › [Article 13 — Transfer of Obligations and Rights](../README.md) › [Title 1 — Transferability; Effect of Transfer](README.md)

```text
  § 13-109. Definition of "transfer." As used in sections 13-101,
13-103, 13-105 and 13-107, the term "transfer" includes sale,
assignment, conveyance, deed and gift.
```
