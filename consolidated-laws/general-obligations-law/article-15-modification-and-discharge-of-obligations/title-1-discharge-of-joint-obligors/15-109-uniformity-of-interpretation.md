# General Obligations Law § 15-109 — Uniformity of interpretation

[General Obligations Law](../../README.md) › [Article 15 — Modification and Discharge of Obligations](../README.md) › [Title 1 — Discharge of Joint Obligors](README.md)

```text
  § 15-109. Uniformity of interpretation. This title shall be so
interpreted and construed as to effectuate its general purpose to make
uniform the law of those states which enact it.
```
