# General Obligations Law § 15-106 — Death of joint obligor

[General Obligations Law](../../README.md) › [Article 15 — Modification and Discharge of Obligations](../README.md) › [Title 1 — Discharge of Joint Obligors](README.md)

```text
  § 15-106. Death of joint obligor. On the death of a joint obligor in
contract, his estate shall be bound as such jointly and severally with
the surviving obligor or obligors.
```
