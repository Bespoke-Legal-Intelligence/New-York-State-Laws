# General Obligations Law § 15-102 — Co-obligor not a party

[General Obligations Law](../../README.md) › [Article 15 — Modification and Discharge of Obligations](../README.md) › [Title 1 — Discharge of Joint Obligors](README.md)

```text
  § 15-102. Co-obligor not a party. A judgment against one or more of
several obligors, or against one or more of joint, or of joint and
several obligors shall not discharge a co-obligor who was not a party to
the proceeding wherein the judgment was rendered.
```
