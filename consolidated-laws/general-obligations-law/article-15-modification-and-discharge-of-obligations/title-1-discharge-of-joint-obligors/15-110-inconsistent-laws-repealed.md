# General Obligations Law § 15-110 — Inconsistent laws repealed

[General Obligations Law](../../README.md) › [Article 15 — Modification and Discharge of Obligations](../README.md) › [Title 1 — Discharge of Joint Obligors](README.md)

```text
  § 15-110. Inconsistent laws repealed. All acts or parts of acts
inconsistent with this title are hereby repealed; but nothing in this
title shall be construed as repealing any of the provisions of the civil
practice law and rules or the partnership law.
```
