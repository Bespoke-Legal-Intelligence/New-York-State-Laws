# General Obligations Law § 15-303 — Release in writing without consideration or seal

[General Obligations Law](../../README.md) › [Article 15 — Modification and Discharge of Obligations](../README.md) › [Title 3 — Requirements For Effectiveness or Enforceability of Modification or Discharge](README.md)

```text
  § 15-303. Release in writing without consideration or seal. A written
instrument which purports to be a total or partial release of all
claims, debts, demands or obligations, or a total or partial release of
any particular claim, debt, demand or obligation, or a release or
discharge in whole or in part of a mortgage, lien, security interest or
charge upon personal or real property, shall not be invalid because of
the absence of consideration or of a seal.
```
