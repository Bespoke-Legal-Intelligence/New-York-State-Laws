# General Obligations Law § 18-403 — Severability

[General Obligations Law](../README.md) › [Article 18-C — Libor Discontinuance](README.md)

```text
  § 18-403. Severability. If any provision of this article or
application thereof to any person or circumstance is held invalid, the
invalidity shall not affect other provisions or applications of this
article that can be given effect without the invalid provision or
application, and to this end the provisions of this article shall be
severable.
```
