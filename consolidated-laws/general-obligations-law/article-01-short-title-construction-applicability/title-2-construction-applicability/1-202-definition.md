# General Obligations Law § 1-202 — Definition

[General Obligations Law](../../README.md) › [Article 1 — Short Title; Construction; Applicability of Certain Sections](../README.md) › [Title 2 — Construction; Applicability of Certain Sections](README.md)

```text
  § 1-202. Definition. As used in this chapter, the term "infant" or
"minor" means a person who has not attained the age of eighteen years.
```
