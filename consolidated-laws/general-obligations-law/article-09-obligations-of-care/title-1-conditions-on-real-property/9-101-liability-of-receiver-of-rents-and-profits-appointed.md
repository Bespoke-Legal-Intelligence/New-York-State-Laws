# General Obligations Law § 9-101 — Liability of receiver of rents and profits appointed in mortgage foreclosure

[General Obligations Law](../../README.md) › [Article 9 — Obligations of Care](../README.md) › [Title 1 — Conditions On Real Property](README.md)

```text
  § 9-101. Liability of receiver of rents and profits appointed in
mortgage foreclosure. A receiver of rents and profits appointed in an
action to foreclose a mortgage upon real property shall be liable, in
his official capacity, for injury to person or property sustained by
reason of conditions on the premises, in a case where an owner would
have been liable. Nothing herein contained shall be construed to enlarge
the liability of the receiver in his personal capacity.
```
