# General Obligations Law § 18-108 — Severability

[General Obligations Law](../README.md) › [Article 18 — Safety In Skiing Code](README.md)

```text
  § 18-108. Severability. If any provision of this article or the
application thereof to any person or circumstances is held invalid, such
invalidity shall not affect other provisions or applications of this
article that can be given effect without the invalid provision or
application, and to this end the provisions of this article are declared
to be severable.
```
