# General Obligations Law § 5-411 — Contracts on account of money or property wagered, bet or staked are void

[General Obligations Law](../../README.md) › [Article 5 — Creation, Definition and Enforcement of Contractual Obligations](../README.md) › [Title 4 — Contracts Relating to Wagering; Forfeiture and Recovery of Certain Property](README.md)

```text
  § 5-411. Contracts on account of money or property wagered, bet or
staked are void. All contracts for or on account of any money or
property, or thing in action wagered, bet or staked, as provided in
section 5-401, shall be void.
```
