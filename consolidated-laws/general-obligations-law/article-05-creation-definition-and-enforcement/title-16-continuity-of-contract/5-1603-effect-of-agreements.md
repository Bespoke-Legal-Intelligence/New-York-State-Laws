# General Obligations Law § 5-1603 — Effect of agreements

[General Obligations Law](../../README.md) › [Article 5 — Creation, Definition and Enforcement of Contractual Obligations](../README.md) › [Title 16 — Continuity of Contract](README.md)

```text
  § 5-1603. Effect of agreements. The provisions of this title shall not
alter or impair and shall be subject to any agreements between parties
with specific reference to or agreement regarding the introduction of
the euro.
```
