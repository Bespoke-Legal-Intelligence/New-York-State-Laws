# General Obligations Law § 5-1113 — Written or published promise or reward

[General Obligations Law](../../README.md) › [Article 5 — Creation, Definition and Enforcement of Contractual Obligations](../README.md) › [Title 11 — Consideration; Effect of Writing On Requirement of Consideration](README.md)

```text
  § 5-1113. Written or published promise or reward. A promise to pay a
reward for return of lost or mislaid property is not unenforceable
because of absence of consideration if the promise was made in writing
or the promisor caused it to be published.
```
