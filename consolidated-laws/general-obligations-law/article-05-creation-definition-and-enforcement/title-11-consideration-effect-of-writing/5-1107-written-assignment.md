# General Obligations Law § 5-1107 — Written assignment

[General Obligations Law](../../README.md) › [Article 5 — Creation, Definition and Enforcement of Contractual Obligations](../README.md) › [Title 11 — Consideration; Effect of Writing On Requirement of Consideration](README.md)

```text
  § 5-1107. Written assignment. An assignment shall not be denied the
effect of irrevocably transferring the assignor's rights because of the
absence of consideration, if such assignment is in writing and signed by
the assignor, or by his agent.
```
