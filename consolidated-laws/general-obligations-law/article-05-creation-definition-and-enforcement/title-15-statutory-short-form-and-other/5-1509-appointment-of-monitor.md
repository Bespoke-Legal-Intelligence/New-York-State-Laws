# General Obligations Law § 5-1509 — Appointment of monitor

[General Obligations Law](../../README.md) › [Article 5 — Creation, Definition and Enforcement of Contractual Obligations](../README.md) › [Title 15 — Statutory Short Form and Other Powers of Attorney For Financial and Estate Planning](README.md)

```text
  § 5-1509. Appointment of monitor. A principal may appoint a monitor or
monitors in the power of attorney who shall have the authority to
request, receive and compel the agent to provide a record of all
receipts, disbursements and transactions entered into by the agent on
behalf of the principal, to request and receive such records held by
third parties, and to request and receive a copy of the power of
attorney. Nothing in this title shall be construed to impose a fiduciary
duty on the monitor.
```
