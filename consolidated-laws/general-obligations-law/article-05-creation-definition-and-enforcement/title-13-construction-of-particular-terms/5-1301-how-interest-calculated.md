# General Obligations Law § 5-1301 — How interest calculated

[General Obligations Law](../../README.md) › [Article 5 — Creation, Definition and Enforcement of Contractual Obligations](../README.md) › [Title 13 — Construction of Particular Terms In Contractual Transactions](README.md)

```text
  § 5-1301. How interest calculated. Whenever, in any statute, act,
deed, written or verbal contract, or in any public or private instrument
whatever, any certain rate of interest is or shall be mentioned, and no
period of time is stated for which such rate is to be calculated,
interest shall be calculated at the rate mentioned, by the year, in the
same manner as if the words "per annum" or "by the year" had been added
to such rate.
```
