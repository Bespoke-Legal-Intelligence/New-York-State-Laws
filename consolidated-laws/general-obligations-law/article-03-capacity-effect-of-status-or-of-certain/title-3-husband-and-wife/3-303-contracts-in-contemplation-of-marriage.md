# General Obligations Law § 3-303 — Contracts in contemplation of marriage

[General Obligations Law](../../README.md) › [Article 3 — Capacity; Effect of Status or of Certain Relationships or Occupations Upon the Creation, Definition or Enforcement of Obligations](../README.md) › [Title 3 — Husband and Wife](README.md)

```text
  § 3-303. Contracts in contemplation of marriage. A contract made
between persons in contemplation of marriage, remains in full force
after the marriage takes place.
```
