# General Obligations Law § 3-307 — Liability of husband for antenuptial debts

[General Obligations Law](../../README.md) › [Article 3 — Capacity; Effect of Status or of Certain Relationships or Occupations Upon the Creation, Definition or Enforcement of Obligations](../README.md) › [Title 3 — Husband and Wife](README.md)

```text
  § 3-307. Liability of husband for antenuptial debts. A husband who
acquires property of his wife by antenuptial contract or otherwise, is
liable for her debts contracted before marriage, but only to the extent
of the property so acquired.
```
