# General Obligations Law § 3-111 — Negligence of parent or other custodian not imputed to infant

[General Obligations Law](../../README.md) › [Article 3 — Capacity; Effect of Status or of Certain Relationships or Occupations Upon the Creation, Definition or Enforcement of Obligations](../README.md) › [Title 1 — Infancy](README.md)

```text
  § 3-111. Negligence of parent or other custodian not imputed to
infant.  In an action brought by an infant to recover damages for
personal injury the contributory negligence of the infant's parent or
other custodian shall not be imputed to the infant.
```
