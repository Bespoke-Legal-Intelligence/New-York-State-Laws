# General Obligations Law § 12-103 — Liability established

[General Obligations Law](../README.md) › [Article 12 — Drug Dealer Liability Act](README.md)

```text
  § 12-103. Liability established. A person who knowingly participates
in a drug market within this state and has been convicted of a crime for
such participation in a drug market and is a drug trafficker shall be
liable for civil damages as provided in this article.
```
