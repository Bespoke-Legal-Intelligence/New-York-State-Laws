# Civil Service Law § 146 — Awards

[Civil Service Law](../README.md) › [Article 9 — Merit Awards For State Employees](README.md)

```text
  § 146. Awards. The commission may determine the nature and extent of
the merit awards to be made under this article which may include but
shall not be limited to certificates, medals or other appropriate
insignia, or cash awards in such amounts as may be fixed by the
commission.
```
