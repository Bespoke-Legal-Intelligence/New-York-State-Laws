# Civil Service Law § 116 — Classification defined

[Civil Service Law](../../README.md) › [Article 8 — Classification and Compensation of State Employees](../README.md) › [Title A — Classification and Allocation of Positions](README.md)

```text
  § 116. Classification defined. The terms "classification",
"reclassification", "classify", and "reclassify" where used in this
article refer to position classification as defined in section two of
this chapter, unless otherwise expressly stated or unless the context
requires a different meaning.
```
