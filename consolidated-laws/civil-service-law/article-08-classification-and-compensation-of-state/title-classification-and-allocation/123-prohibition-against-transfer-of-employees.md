# Civil Service Law § 123 — Prohibition against transfer of employees to competitive class

[Civil Service Law](../../README.md) › [Article 8 — Classification and Compensation of State Employees](../README.md) › [Title A — Classification and Allocation of Positions](README.md)

```text
  § 123. Prohibition against transfer of employees to competitive
class. Nothing in title A of this article shall be construed to effect a
transfer to the competitive class of the civil service of any employee
not heretofore included in such service.
```
