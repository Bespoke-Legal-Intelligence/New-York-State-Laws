# Civil Service Law § 55-C*2 — Acceptability of certain high school diplomas

[Civil Service Law](../../README.md) › [Article 4 — Recruitment of Personnel](../README.md) › [Title A — Examinations and Eligible Lists](README.md)

*An asterisk in a number (for example Article 24\* or § 20-C\*2) is the Senate's mark for a number the Legislature has used more than once: the marked article or section is a different one from the unmarked one with the same number. \*2 means the second one enacted with that number.*

```text
  * § 55-c. Acceptability of certain high school diplomas. Whenever a
high school diploma is required by the state civil service department or
a municipal commission as a minimum requirement of any competitive
examination, a high school individualized education plan diploma which
was granted to a child with handicapping conditions may be accepted by
such department or commission in fulfillment of such diploma
requirement.
  * NB There are 2 § 55-c's
```
