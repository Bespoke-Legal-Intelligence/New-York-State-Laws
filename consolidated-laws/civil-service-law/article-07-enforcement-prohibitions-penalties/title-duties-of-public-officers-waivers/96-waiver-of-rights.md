# Civil Service Law § 96 — Waiver of rights

[Civil Service Law](../../README.md) › [Article 7 — Enforcement; Prohibitions; Penalties](../README.md) › [Title A — Duties of Public Officers; Waivers of Rights](README.md)

```text
  § 96. Waiver of rights. No public officer nor any employee acting for
a public officer shall require a candidate for employment to sign any
document whereby such candidate for employment waives any right or
rights accruing to him under this chapter.
```
