# Civil Service Law § 202 — Right of organization

[Civil Service Law](../README.md) › [Article 14 — Public Employees' Fair Employment Act](README.md)

```text
  § 202. Right of organization.  Public employees shall have the right
to form, join and participate in, or to refrain from forming, joining,
or participating in, any employee organization of their own choosing.
```
