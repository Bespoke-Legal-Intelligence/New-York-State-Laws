# Civil Service Law § 43 — Labor class

[Civil Service Law](../../README.md) › [Article 3 — Jurisdictional Classification](../README.md) › [Title B — Classified Service](README.md)

```text
  § 43. Labor class. 1. The labor class shall comprise all unskilled
laborers in the service of the state and each of its civil divisions
except those whose positions can be examined for competitively.
  2. The state or municipal commission may require applicants for
employment in the labor class to qualify in such examinations of their
fitness for employment as may be deemed practicable.
```
