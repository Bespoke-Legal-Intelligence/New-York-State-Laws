# Civil Service Law § 83-D — Police advisory board meetings

[Civil Service Law](../../README.md) › [Article 5 — Personnel Changes](../README.md) › [Title E — Police Advisory Board](README.md)

```text
  § 83-d. Police advisory board meetings. The board will be convened by
the president of the state civil service department, who will indicate
the date, time and place of the first meeting.  Subsequent meetings will
be held in accordance with the majority determination of the board.
```
