# Civil Service Law § 83-G — Compensation of board members

[Civil Service Law](../../README.md) › [Article 5 — Personnel Changes](../README.md) › [Title E — Police Advisory Board](README.md)

```text
  § 83-g. Compensation of board members. No member of the board shall
receive any additional compensation from the state of New York or any of
its political subdivisions for any services rendered to the board nor
any expenses incurred in attendance at board meetings.
```
