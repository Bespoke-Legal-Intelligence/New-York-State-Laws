# Civil Service Law § 83-E — Duties of police advisory board

[Civil Service Law](../../README.md) › [Article 5 — Personnel Changes](../README.md) › [Title E — Police Advisory Board](README.md)

```text
  § 83-e. Duties of police advisory board. The board will establish
appropriate procedures and policies for the conduct of its affairs and
will make its recommendations to the president of the state civil
service commission.
```
