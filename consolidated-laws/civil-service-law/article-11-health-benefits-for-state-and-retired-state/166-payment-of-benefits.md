# Civil Service Law § 166 — Payment of benefits

[Civil Service Law](../README.md) › [Article 11 — Health Benefits For State and Retired State Employees](README.md)

```text
  § 166. Payment of benefits.  Any benefits payable under the plan may
be made either directly to the attending physicians, hospitals, medical
groups, or others furnishing the services upon which a claim is based,
or to the covered employee, upon presentation of valid bills for such
services, subject to such provisions to facilitate payment as may be
made by the president.
```
