# Parks, Recreation and Historic Preservation Law § 17.09 — Restriction on alienation

[Parks, Recreation and Historic Preservation Law](../../README.md) › [Title C — Parks, Recreation and Historic Preservation](../README.md) › [Article 17 — Outdoor Recreation Development Bond Act](README.md)

```text
  § 17.09 Restriction on alienation.  Real property acquired or
developed by a municipality with the aid of funds made available
pursuant to this article shall not be sold or disposed of or used for
purposes other than public park, marine, historic site or forest
recreation purposes without the express authority of an act of the
legislature.
```
