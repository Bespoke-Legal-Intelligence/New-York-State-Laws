# Parks, Recreation and Historic Preservation Law § 17.07 — Municipal regulations; limitations

[Parks, Recreation and Historic Preservation Law](../../README.md) › [Title C — Parks, Recreation and Historic Preservation](../README.md) › [Article 17 — Outdoor Recreation Development Bond Act](README.md)

```text
  § 17.07 Municipal regulations; limitations. A municipality which
acquires or develops real property with funds made available pursuant to
this article may establish reasonable rules and regulations to assure
proper administration and development of such lands, provided that no
rule or regulation restricting the use of such lands to the residents of
the municipality shall be effective without the express approval of the
commissioner.
```
