# Parks, Recreation and Historic Preservation Law § 19.23 — Lender's duty to the office

[Parks, Recreation and Historic Preservation Law](../../README.md) › [Title C — Parks, Recreation and Historic Preservation](../README.md) › [Article 19-A — Property Held By the Office of Parks, Recreation and Historic Preservation](README.md)

```text
  § 19.23 Lender's duty to the office.  The owner of property loaned to
the office is responsible for promptly notifying the office, in writing,
of any change of address or change in the ownership of the property.
```
