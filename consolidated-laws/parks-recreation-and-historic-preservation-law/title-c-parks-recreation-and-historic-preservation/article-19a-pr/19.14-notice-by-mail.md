# Parks, Recreation and Historic Preservation Law § 19.14 — Notice by mail

[Parks, Recreation and Historic Preservation Law](../../README.md) › [Title C — Parks, Recreation and Historic Preservation](../README.md) › [Article 19-A — Property Held By the Office of Parks, Recreation and Historic Preservation](README.md)

```text
  § 19.14 Notice by mail.  A notice given by the office under this
article must be mailed to the lender's last known address by certified
mail, return receipt requested. Service by mail is complete if the
office receives proof that the notice was received not more than thirty
days after it was mailed.
```
