# Parks, Recreation and Historic Preservation Law § 19.20 — Property acquired from the office

[Parks, Recreation and Historic Preservation Law](../../README.md) › [Title C — Parks, Recreation and Historic Preservation](../README.md) › [Article 19-A — Property Held By the Office of Parks, Recreation and Historic Preservation](README.md)

```text
  § 19.20 Property acquired from the office.  Any person who purchases
or otherwise acquires property from the office acquires good title to
such property if the office has acquired title in accordance with this
article.
```
