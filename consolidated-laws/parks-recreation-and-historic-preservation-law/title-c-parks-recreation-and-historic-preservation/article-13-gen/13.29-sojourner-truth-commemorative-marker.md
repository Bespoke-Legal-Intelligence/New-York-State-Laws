# Parks, Recreation and Historic Preservation Law § 13.29 — Sojourner Truth commemorative marker

[Parks, Recreation and Historic Preservation Law](../../README.md) › [Title C — Parks, Recreation and Historic Preservation](../README.md) › [Article 13 — General Provisions](README.md)

```text
  § 13.29 Sojourner Truth commemorative marker.  The office shall erect
and maintain a bronze and bluestone commemorative marker on the grounds
of the Ulster county court house in Kingston, New York honoring the
achievements and highlighting the events in the life of Sojourner Truth.
```
