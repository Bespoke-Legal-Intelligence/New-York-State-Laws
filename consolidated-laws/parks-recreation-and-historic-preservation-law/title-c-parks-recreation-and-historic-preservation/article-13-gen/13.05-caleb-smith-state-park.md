# Parks, Recreation and Historic Preservation Law § 13.05 — Caleb Smith State Park

[Parks, Recreation and Historic Preservation Law](../../README.md) › [Title C — Parks, Recreation and Historic Preservation](../README.md) › [Article 13 — General Provisions](README.md)

```text
  § 13.05 Caleb Smith State Park.  Notwithstanding any inconsistent
provision of law to the contrary, on and after the effective date of
this section the state park on the Nissequogue river known as the
Nissequogue State Park shall be known as the Caleb Smith State Park and
shall be suitably marked, in a manner to be prescribed by the
commissioner.
```
