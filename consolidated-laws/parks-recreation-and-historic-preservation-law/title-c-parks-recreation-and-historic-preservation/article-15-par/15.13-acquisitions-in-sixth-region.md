# Parks, Recreation and Historic Preservation Law § 15.13 — Acquisitions in sixth region

[Parks, Recreation and Historic Preservation Law](../../README.md) › [Title C — Parks, Recreation and Historic Preservation](../README.md) › [Article 15 — Park and Recreation Land Acquisition Bond Acts](README.md)

```text
  § 15.13 Acquisitions in sixth region.  As used in this article, the
term commissioner shall mean the commissioner of parks, recreation and
historic preservation or, for real property in the sixth region, the
commissioner of environmental conservation.
```
