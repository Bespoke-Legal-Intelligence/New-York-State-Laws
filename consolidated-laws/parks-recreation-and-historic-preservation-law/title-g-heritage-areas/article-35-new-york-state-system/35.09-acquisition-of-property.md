# Parks, Recreation and Historic Preservation Law § 35.09 — Acquisition of property

[Parks, Recreation and Historic Preservation Law](../../README.md) › [Title G — Heritage Areas](../README.md) › [Article 35 — New York State System of Heritage Areas](README.md)

```text
  § 35.09 Acquisition of property. The acquisition of interests and
rights in real property for heritage area purposes of preservation,
education, recreation or economic development within any state
designated heritage area shall constitute a public purpose for which
public funds may be expended or advanced.
```
