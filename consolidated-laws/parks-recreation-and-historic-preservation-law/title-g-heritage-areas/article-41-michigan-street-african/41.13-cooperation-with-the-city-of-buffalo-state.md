# Parks, Recreation and Historic Preservation Law § 41.13 — Cooperation with the city of Buffalo, state agencies and public corporations

[Parks, Recreation and Historic Preservation Law](../../README.md) › [Title G — Heritage Areas](../README.md) › [Article 41 — Michigan Street African American Heritage Corridor Commission](README.md)

**Repealed** (29 Apr 2022). The text below is what the Senate publishes for this repealed provision.

```text
  * § 41.13 Cooperation with the city of Buffalo, state agencies and
public corporations. 1. The city of Buffalo, any state agency or public
corporation may provide the commission with the services of its agents,
employees and facilities without charge to the commission. Persons
serving as officers or employees of the commission may be employees of
the city of Buffalo and the commission, and the commission may pay the
city of Buffalo an agreed portion of the compensation or costs of such
officers or employees.
  2. Nothing contained in this article shall be deemed to affect, impair
or supersede the provisions of any city charter, local law, rule or
other local requirements and procedures heretofore or hereafter adopted,
including, but not limited to, any such provisions relating to the
zoning and use of land.
  * NB Repealed December 31, 2020
```
