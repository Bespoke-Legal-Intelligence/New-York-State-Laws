# Parks, Recreation and Historic Preservation Law § 41.15 — Appropriations by the city of Buffalo

[Parks, Recreation and Historic Preservation Law](../../README.md) › [Title G — Heritage Areas](../README.md) › [Article 41 — Michigan Street African American Heritage Corridor Commission](README.md)

```text
  * § 41.15 Appropriations by the city of Buffalo. To effectuate the
purposes of this article, the city of Buffalo may appropriate and
transfer to the commission moneys to be expended by the commission to
carry out its functions pursuant to this article.
```
