# Parks, Recreation and Historic Preservation Law § 41.19 — State purpose

[Parks, Recreation and Historic Preservation Law](../../README.md) › [Title G — Heritage Areas](../README.md) › [Article 41 — Michigan Street African American Heritage Corridor Commission](README.md)

```text
  * § 41.19 State purpose. The development and implementation of a
Michigan Street African American Heritage Corridor plan is considered a
state purpose. Notwithstanding any law to the contrary, any state agency
or public corporation within amounts appropriated or available therefor
may offer assistance to the commission in support of its corporate
purpose through the lending of staff or other resources to accomplish
the purposes of this article.
  * NB Repealed December 31, 2019
```
