# Parks, Recreation and Historic Preservation Law § 41.07 — Commission purpose

[Parks, Recreation and Historic Preservation Law](../../README.md) › [Title G — Heritage Areas](../README.md) › [Article 41 — Michigan Street African American Heritage Corridor Commission](README.md)

```text
  * § 41.07 Commission purpose. The purpose of the commission is to
undertake all necessary actions to facilitate the creation of the
Michigan Street African American Heritage Corridor in downtown Buffalo,
complete a management plan to ensure the long term preservation and
maintenance of such heritage corridor and provide for the implementation
and sustainability of undertaking recommendations of the completed
management plan.
  * NB Repealed December 31, 2020
```
