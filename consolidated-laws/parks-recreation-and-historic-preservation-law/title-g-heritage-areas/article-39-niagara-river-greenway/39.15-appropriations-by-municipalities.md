# Parks, Recreation and Historic Preservation Law § 39.15 — Appropriations by municipalities

[Parks, Recreation and Historic Preservation Law](../../README.md) › [Title G — Heritage Areas](../README.md) › [Article 39 — Niagara River Greenway Commission](README.md)

```text
  § 39.15 Appropriations by municipalities. To effectuate the purposes
of this article, the local legislative bodies of any municipality may
appropriate and transfer to the commission moneys to be expended by the
commission to carry out its functions pursuant to this article.
```
