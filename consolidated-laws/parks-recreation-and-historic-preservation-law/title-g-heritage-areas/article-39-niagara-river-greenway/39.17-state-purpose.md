# Parks, Recreation and Historic Preservation Law § 39.17 — State purpose

[Parks, Recreation and Historic Preservation Law](../../README.md) › [Title G — Heritage Areas](../README.md) › [Article 39 — Niagara River Greenway Commission](README.md)

```text
  § 39.17 State purpose. The development of a Niagara greenway plan is
considered a state purpose. Notwithstanding any law to the contrary, any
state agency or public corporation within amounts appropriated or
available therefore may offer assistance to the commission in support of
its corporate purpose through the lending of staff or other resources to
accomplish the purposes of this article.
```
