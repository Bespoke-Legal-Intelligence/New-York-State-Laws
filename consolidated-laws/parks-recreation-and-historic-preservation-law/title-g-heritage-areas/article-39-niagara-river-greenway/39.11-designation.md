# Parks, Recreation and Historic Preservation Law § 39.11 — Designation

[Parks, Recreation and Historic Preservation Law](../../README.md) › [Title G — Heritage Areas](../README.md) › [Article 39 — Niagara River Greenway Commission](README.md)

```text
  § 39.11 Designation. The Niagara river greenway is hereby designated
to include those lands of state agencies, public corporations, and
municipalities adjacent to the Niagara river as identified in the
approved plan of the commission.
```
