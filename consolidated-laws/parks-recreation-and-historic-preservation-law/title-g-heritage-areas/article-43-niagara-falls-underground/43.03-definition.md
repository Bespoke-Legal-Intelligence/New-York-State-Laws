# Parks, Recreation and Historic Preservation Law § 43.03 — Definition

[Parks, Recreation and Historic Preservation Law](../../README.md) › [Title G — Heritage Areas](../README.md) › [Article 43 — Niagara Falls Underground Railroad Heritage Commission](README.md)

**Repealed** (13 Jan 2017). The text below is what the Senate publishes for this repealed provision.

```text
  * § 43.03 Definition. Whenever used in this article, unless a
different meaning clearly appears from the context, "Niagara Falls
underground railroad heritage commission" or "commission" shall mean the
Niagara Falls underground railroad heritage commission created pursuant
to this article.
  * NB Repealed December 31, 2015
```
