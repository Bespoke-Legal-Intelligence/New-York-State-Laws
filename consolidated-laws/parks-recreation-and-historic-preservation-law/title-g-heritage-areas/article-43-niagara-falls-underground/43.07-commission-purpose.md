# Parks, Recreation and Historic Preservation Law § 43.07 — Commission purpose

[Parks, Recreation and Historic Preservation Law](../../README.md) › [Title G — Heritage Areas](../README.md) › [Article 43 — Niagara Falls Underground Railroad Heritage Commission](README.md)

**Repealed** (13 Jan 2017). The text below is what the Senate publishes for this repealed provision.

```text
  * § 43.07 Commission purpose. The purpose of the commission is to
undertake all necessary actions to facilitate the creation of an
underground railroad heritage area in the city of Niagara Falls,
complete a management plan to ensure the long-term preservation and
maintenance of such heritage area and provide for the implementation and
sustainability of undertaking recommendations of the completed
management plan.
  * NB Repealed December 31, 2015
```
