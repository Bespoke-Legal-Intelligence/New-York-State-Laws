# Parks, Recreation and Historic Preservation Law § 43.15 — Appropriations by the city of Niagara Falls

[Parks, Recreation and Historic Preservation Law](../../README.md) › [Title G — Heritage Areas](../README.md) › [Article 43 — Niagara Falls Underground Railroad Heritage Commission](README.md)

**Repealed** (13 Jan 2017). The text below is what the Senate publishes for this repealed provision.

```text
  * § 43.15 Appropriations by the city of Niagara Falls. To effectuate
the purposes of this article, the city of Niagara Falls may appropriate
and transfer to the commission moneys to be expended by the commission
to carry out its functions pursuant to this article.
  * NB Repealed December 31, 2015
```
