# Parks, Recreation and Historic Preservation Law § 43.13 — Cooperation with the city of Niagara Falls, state agencies and public corporations

[Parks, Recreation and Historic Preservation Law](../../README.md) › [Title G — Heritage Areas](../README.md) › [Article 43 — Niagara Falls Underground Railroad Heritage Commission](README.md)

```text
  * § 43.13 Cooperation with the city of Niagara Falls, state agencies
and public corporations. 1. The city of Niagara Falls, any state agency
or public corporation may provide the commission with the services of
its agents, employees and facilities without charge to the commission.
Persons serving as officers or employees of the commission may be
employees of the city of Niagara Falls and the commission may pay the
city of Niagara Falls an agreed portion of the compensation or costs of
such officers or employees.
  2. Nothing contained in this article shall be deemed to affect, impair
or supersede the provisions of any city charter, local law, rule or
other local requirements and procedures heretofore or hereafter adopted,
including, but not limited to, any such provisions relating to the
zoning and use of land.
  * NB Repealed December 31, 2015
```
