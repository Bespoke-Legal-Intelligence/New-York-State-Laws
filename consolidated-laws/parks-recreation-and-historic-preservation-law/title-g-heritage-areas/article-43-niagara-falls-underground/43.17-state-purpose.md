# Parks, Recreation and Historic Preservation Law § 43.17 — State purpose

[Parks, Recreation and Historic Preservation Law](../../README.md) › [Title G — Heritage Areas](../README.md) › [Article 43 — Niagara Falls Underground Railroad Heritage Commission](README.md)

**Repealed** (13 Jan 2017). The text below is what the Senate publishes for this repealed provision.

```text
  * § 43.17 State purpose. The development of a Niagara Falls
underground railroad heritage management plan is considered a state
purpose.  Notwithstanding any law to the contrary, any state agency or
public corporation, within amounts appropriated or available therefor
may offer assistance to the commission in support of its corporate
purpose through the lending of staff or other resources to accomplish
the purposes of this article.
  * NB Repealed December 31, 2015
```
