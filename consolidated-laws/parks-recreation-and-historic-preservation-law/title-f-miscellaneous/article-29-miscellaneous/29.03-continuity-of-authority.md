# Parks, Recreation and Historic Preservation Law § 29.03 — Continuity of authority

[Parks, Recreation and Historic Preservation Law](../../README.md) › [Title F — Miscellaneous](../README.md) › [Article 29 — Miscellaneous](README.md)

```text
  § 29.03 Continuity of authority.  For the purpose of succession to all
functions, powers and duties transferred to the office by the provisions
of this act, the office shall be deemed and held to constitute the
continuation of the board, trust, council or commission from which such
functions, powers and duties were so transferred.
```
