# Parks, Recreation and Historic Preservation Law § 29.01 — Transfer of powers, functions, duties, employees and property

[Parks, Recreation and Historic Preservation Law](../../README.md) › [Title F — Miscellaneous](../README.md) › [Article 29 — Miscellaneous](README.md)

```text
  § 29.01 Transfer of powers, functions, duties, employees and property.
The powers, functions, duties, employees and property of any trust or
commission abolished by this act, of any council or commission continued
by this act, unless otherwise provided, or of the office of parks and
recreation, are hereby continued in or transferred to the office, as
continued by this act.
```
