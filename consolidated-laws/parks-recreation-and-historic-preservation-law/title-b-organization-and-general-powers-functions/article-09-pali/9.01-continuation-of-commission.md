# Parks, Recreation and Historic Preservation Law § 9.01 — Continuation of commission

[Parks, Recreation and Historic Preservation Law](../../README.md) › [Title B — Organization and General Powers, Functions and Duties of Office of Parks, Recreation and Historic Preservation](../README.md) › [Article 9 — Palisades Interstate Park Commission](README.md)

```text
  § 9.01 Continuation of commission.  The Palisades interstate park
commission, as established by compact between the states of New York and
New Jersey authorized by chapter one hundred seventy of the laws of
nineteen hundred thirty-seven, shall continue in the office with the
powers, functions and duties prescribed in this article.  The commission
shall continue to have the immediate custody, jurisdiction, management
and control of all lands, parks or parkways in this state which it now
has under its jurisdiction and control.
```
