# Parks, Recreation and Historic Preservation Law § 9.03 — Office

[Parks, Recreation and Historic Preservation Law](../../README.md) › [Title B — Organization and General Powers, Functions and Duties of Office of Parks, Recreation and Historic Preservation](../README.md) › [Article 9 — Palisades Interstate Park Commission](README.md)

```text
  § 9.03 Office.  The commission shall have and maintain a suitable
office where its maps, plans, documents, records and accounts shall be
kept, subject to public inspection at such times and under such
reasonable regulations as the commission shall determine.
```
