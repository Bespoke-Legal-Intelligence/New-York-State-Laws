# Parks, Recreation and Historic Preservation Law § 11.05 — Organization

[Parks, Recreation and Historic Preservation Law](../../README.md) › [Title B — Organization and General Powers, Functions and Duties of Office of Parks, Recreation and Historic Preservation](../README.md) › [Article 11 — State Board For Historic Preservation](README.md)

```text
  § 11.05 Organization.  1. The governor shall designate a chairman from
among the members of the board appointed by him, to hold such office at
his pleasure. The board may provide for its organization and appoint
such other officers from among its members as it deems appropriate.
  2. The commissioner, upon the approval of the board, shall appoint
staff within amounts made available by appropriation to serve the board.
The staff shall assist with the organization and implementation of the
board's duties; serve as a liaison between the commissioner and board;
inform the board of all developments germane to its duties and perform
other duties required of the board through provisions of article
fourteen of this chapter and any other laws affecting the board.
```
