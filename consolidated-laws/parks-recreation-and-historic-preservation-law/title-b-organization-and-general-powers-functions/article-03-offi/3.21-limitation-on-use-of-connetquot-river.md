# Parks, Recreation and Historic Preservation Law § 3.21 — Limitation on use of Connetquot river state park water resources

[Parks, Recreation and Historic Preservation Law](../../README.md) › [Title B — Organization and General Powers, Functions and Duties of Office of Parks, Recreation and Historic Preservation](../README.md) › [Article 3 — Office of Parks, Recreation and Historic Preservation; Commissioner](README.md)

```text
  § 3.21 Limitation on use of Connetquot river state park water
resources.  No person shall extract any surface or ground water within
the boundaries of the Connetquot river state park, except for public use
within the park.
```
