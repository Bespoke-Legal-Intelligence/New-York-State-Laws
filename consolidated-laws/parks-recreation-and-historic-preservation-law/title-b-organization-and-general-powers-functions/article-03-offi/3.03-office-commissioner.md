# Parks, Recreation and Historic Preservation Law § 3.03 — Office; commissioner

[Parks, Recreation and Historic Preservation Law](../../README.md) › [Title B — Organization and General Powers, Functions and Duties of Office of Parks, Recreation and Historic Preservation](../README.md) › [Article 3 — Office of Parks, Recreation and Historic Preservation; Commissioner](README.md)

```text
  § 3.03 Office; commissioner.  The office of parks, recreation and
historic preservation is hereby continued in the executive department.
The head of the office shall be the commissioner of parks, recreation
and historic preservation, who shall be appointed by the governor, by
and with the advice and consent of the senate, and hold office at the
pleasure of the governor.
```
