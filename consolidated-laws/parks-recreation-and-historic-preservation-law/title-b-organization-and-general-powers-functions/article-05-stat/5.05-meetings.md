# Parks, Recreation and Historic Preservation Law § 5.05 — Meetings

[Parks, Recreation and Historic Preservation Law](../../README.md) › [Title B — Organization and General Powers, Functions and Duties of Office of Parks, Recreation and Historic Preservation](../README.md) › [Article 5 — State Council of Parks and Recreation](README.md)

```text
  § 5.05 Meetings.  The council shall hold regular meetings at least
four times a year at such times and places as it may direct, and special
meetings when authorized and called as prescribed in its by-laws.
Members of the council may be represented at its meetings by a
commissioner of their respective commissions, or a deputy or assistant
commissioner of their respective departments.
```
