# Parks, Recreation and Historic Preservation Law § 5.03 — Chairman; officers

[Parks, Recreation and Historic Preservation Law](../../README.md) › [Title B — Organization and General Powers, Functions and Duties of Office of Parks, Recreation and Historic Preservation](../README.md) › [Article 5 — State Council of Parks and Recreation](README.md)

```text
  § 5.03 Chairman; officers.  The governor shall designate a chairman
and vice-chairman from among the members of the council, exclusive of
the commissioners of environmental conservation and parks, recreation
and historic preservation, to hold such offices at his pleasure. The
council may provide for its organization and appoint such other officers
from among its members as it deems appropriate.
```
