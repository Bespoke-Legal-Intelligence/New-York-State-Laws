# Parks, Recreation and Historic Preservation Law § 7.13 — Jones beach theatre

[Parks, Recreation and Historic Preservation Law](../../README.md) › [Title B — Organization and General Powers, Functions and Duties of Office of Parks, Recreation and Historic Preservation](../README.md) › [Article 7 — Park Regions and Regional Park and Recreation Commissions](README.md)

**Repealed** (22 Apr 2022). The text below is what the Senate publishes for this repealed provision.

```text
  § 7.13 Jones beach theatre.  1. Notwithstanding any other provision of
law, in addition to the powers, functions and duties conferred upon it
by the otherwise consistent provisions of this article, the Long Island
state park and recreation commission shall prepare an annual plan for
the Jones beach theatre within the ninth park region.
  2. Such plan shall include, but need not be limited to, selection of
the producer, types of program produced, and the advertising for the
promotion of events to be staged at such theatre.
  3. The Long Island state park, recreation and historic preservation
commission shall be empowered to approve the selection of the producer,
type of program produced and the advertising for the promotion of events
to be staged at the Jones beach theatre. Such approval shall not be
unreasonably withheld.
  4. The commission may request, and the commissioner is hereby
authorized to provide assistance in the preparation of the plan herein
provided for.
```
