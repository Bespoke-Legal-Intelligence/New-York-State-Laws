# Parks, Recreation and Historic Preservation Law § 7.09 — Meetings

[Parks, Recreation and Historic Preservation Law](../../README.md) › [Title B — Organization and General Powers, Functions and Duties of Office of Parks, Recreation and Historic Preservation](../README.md) › [Article 7 — Park Regions and Regional Park and Recreation Commissions](README.md)

```text
  § 7.09 Meetings.  Each commission shall hold regular meetings at least
four times a year at such times and places as it may direct, and special
meetings when authorized and called as prescribed by its rules.
```
