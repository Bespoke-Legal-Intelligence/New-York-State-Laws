# Partnership Law § 44 — Right to an account

[Partnership Law](../README.md) › [Article 4 — Relations of Partners to One Another](README.md)

```text
  § 44. Right to an account.  Any partner shall have the right to a
formal account as to partnership affairs:
  1. If he is wrongfully excluded from the partnership business or
possession of its property by his copartners,
  2. If the right exists under the terms of any agreement,
  3. As provided by section forty-three,
  4. Whenever other circumstances render it just and reasonable.
```
