# Partnership Law § 41 — Partnership books

[Partnership Law](../README.md) › [Article 4 — Relations of Partners to One Another](README.md)

```text
  § 41. Partnership books.  The partnership books shall be kept, subject
to any agreement between the partners, at the principal place of
business of the partnership, and every partner shall at all times have
access to and may inspect and copy any of them.
```
