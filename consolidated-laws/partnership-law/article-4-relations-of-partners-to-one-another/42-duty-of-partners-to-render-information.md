# Partnership Law § 42 — Duty of partners to render information

[Partnership Law](../README.md) › [Article 4 — Relations of Partners to One Another](README.md)

```text
  § 42. Duty of partners to render information.  Partners shall render
on demand true and full information of all things affecting the
partnership to any partner or the legal representative of any deceased
partner or partner under legal disability.
```
