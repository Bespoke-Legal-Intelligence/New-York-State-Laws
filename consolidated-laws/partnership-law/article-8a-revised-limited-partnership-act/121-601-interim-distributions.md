# Partnership Law § 121-601 — Interim distributions

[Partnership Law](../README.md) › [Article 8-A — Revised Limited Partnership Act](README.md)

```text
  § 121-601. Interim distributions. Except as provided in this article,
a partner is entitled to receive distributions from a limited
partnership before his withdrawal from the limited partnership and
before the dissolution and winding up thereof to the extent and at the
times or upon the happening of the events specified in the partnership
agreement.
```
