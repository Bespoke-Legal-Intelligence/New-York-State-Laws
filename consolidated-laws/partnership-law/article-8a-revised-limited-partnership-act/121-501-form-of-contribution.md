# Partnership Law § 121-501 — Form of contribution

[Partnership Law](../README.md) › [Article 8-A — Revised Limited Partnership Act](README.md)

```text
  § 121-501. Form of contribution. The contribution of a partner may be
in cash, property, or services rendered, or a promissory note or other
obligation to contribute cash or property or to render services.
```
