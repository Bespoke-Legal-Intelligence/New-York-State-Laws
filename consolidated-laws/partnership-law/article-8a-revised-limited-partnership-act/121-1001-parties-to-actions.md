# Partnership Law § 121-1001 — Parties to actions

[Partnership Law](../README.md) › [Article 8-A — Revised Limited Partnership Act](README.md)

```text
  § 121-1001. Parties to actions. A limited partner, unless he is also a
general partner, is not a proper party to proceedings by or against a
partnership, except where the object is to enforce a limited partner's
right against or liability to the partnership and except in cases
provided for in section 121-1002 of this article.
```
