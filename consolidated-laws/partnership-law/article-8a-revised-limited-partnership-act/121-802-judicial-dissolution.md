# Partnership Law § 121-802 — Judicial dissolution

[Partnership Law](../README.md) › [Article 8-A — Revised Limited Partnership Act](README.md)

```text
  § 121-802. Judicial dissolution. On application by or for a partner,
the supreme court in the judicial district in which the office of the
limited partnership is located may decree dissolution of a limited
partnership whenever it is not reasonably practicable to carry on the
business in conformity with the partnership agreement. A certified copy
of the order of dissolution shall be filed by the applicant with the
department of state within thirty days of its issuance.
```
