# Partnership Law § 121-107 — Nature of business

[Partnership Law](../README.md) › [Article 8-A — Revised Limited Partnership Act](README.md)

```text
  § 121-107. Nature of business. A limited partnership may carry on any
business that a partnership without limited partners may carry on except
as prohibited by law.
```
