# Partnership Law § 121-701 — Nature of partnership interest

[Partnership Law](../README.md) › [Article 8-A — Revised Limited Partnership Act](README.md)

```text
  § 121-701. Nature of partnership interest. An interest in a limited
partnership is personal property and a partner has no interest in
specific partnership property.
```
