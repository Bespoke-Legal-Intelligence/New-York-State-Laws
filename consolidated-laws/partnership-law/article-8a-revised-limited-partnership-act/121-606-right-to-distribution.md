# Partnership Law § 121-606 — Right to distribution

[Partnership Law](../README.md) › [Article 8-A — Revised Limited Partnership Act](README.md)

```text
  § 121-606. Right to distribution. Subject to sections 121-607 and
121-804 of this article, at the time a partner becomes entitled to
receive a distribution, he has the status of, and is entitled to all
remedies available to, a creditor of the limited partnership with
respect to the distribution.
```
