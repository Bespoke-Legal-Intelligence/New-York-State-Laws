# Partnership Law § 60 — Dissolution defined

[Partnership Law](../README.md) › [Article 6 — Dissolution and Winding Up](README.md)

```text
  § 60. Dissolution defined.  The dissolution of a partnership is the
change in the relation of the partners caused by any partner ceasing to
be associated in the carrying on as distinguished from the winding up of
the business.
```
