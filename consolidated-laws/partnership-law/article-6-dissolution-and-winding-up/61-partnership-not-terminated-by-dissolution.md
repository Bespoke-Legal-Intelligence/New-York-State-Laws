# Partnership Law § 61 — Partnership not terminated by dissolution

[Partnership Law](../README.md) › [Article 6 — Dissolution and Winding Up](README.md)

```text
  § 61. Partnership not terminated by dissolution.  On dissolution the
partnership is not terminated, but continues until the winding up of
partnership affairs is completed.
```
