# Partnership Law § 74 — Accrual of actions

[Partnership Law](../README.md) › [Article 6 — Dissolution and Winding Up](README.md)

```text
  § 74. Accrual of actions.  The right to an account of his interest
shall accrue to any partner, or his legal representative, as against the
winding up partners or the surviving partners or the person or
partnership continuing the business, at the date of dissolution, in the
absence of agreement to the contrary.
```
