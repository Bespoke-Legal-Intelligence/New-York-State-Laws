# Partnership Law § 71-A — Payment of wages by receivers

[Partnership Law](../README.md) › [Article 6 — Dissolution and Winding Up](README.md)

```text
  § 71-a. Payment of wages by receivers.  Upon the appointment of a
receiver of a partnership the wages of the employees of such partnership
shall be preferred to every other debt or claim.
```
