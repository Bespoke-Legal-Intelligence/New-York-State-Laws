# Partnership Law § 68 — Right to wind up

[Partnership Law](../README.md) › [Article 6 — Dissolution and Winding Up](README.md)

```text
  § 68. Right to wind up.  Unless otherwise agreed the partners who have
not wrongfully dissolved the partnership or the legal representative of
the last surviving partner, not bankrupt, has the right to wind up the
partnership affairs; provided, however, that any partner, his legal
representative, or his assignee, upon cause shown, may obtain winding up
by the court.
```
