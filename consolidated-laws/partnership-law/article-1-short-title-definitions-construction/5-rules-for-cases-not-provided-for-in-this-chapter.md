# Partnership Law § 5 — Rules for cases not provided for in this chapter

[Partnership Law](../README.md) › [Article 1 — Short Title; Definitions; Construction](README.md)

```text
  § 5. Rules for cases not provided for in this chapter. In any case not
provided for in this chapter the rules of law and equity, including the
law merchant, shall govern.
```
