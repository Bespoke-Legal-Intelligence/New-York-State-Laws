# Partnership Law § 50 — Extent of property rights of a partner

[Partnership Law](../README.md) › [Article 5 — Property Rights of a Partner](README.md)

```text
  § 50. Extent of property rights of a partner.  The property rights of
a partner are (a) his rights in specific partnership property, (b) his
interest in the partnership, and (c) his right to participate in the
management.
```
