# Partnership Law § 94 — Name not to contain surname of limited partner; exceptions

[Partnership Law](../README.md) › [Article 8 — Limited Partnerships](README.md)

```text
  § 94. Name not to contain surname of limited partner; exceptions.  (1)
The surname of a limited partner shall not appear in the partnership
name, unless
  (a) It is also the surname of a general partner, or
  (b) Prior to the time when the limited partner became such the
business had been carried on under a name in which his surname appeared.
  (2) A limited partner whose name appears in a partnership name
contrary to the provisions of subdivision one of this section is liable
as a general partner to partnership creditors who extend credit to the
partnership without actual knowledge that he is not a general partner.
```
