# Partnership Law § 110 — Death of limited partner

[Partnership Law](../README.md) › [Article 8 — Limited Partnerships](README.md)

```text
  § 110. Death of limited partner.  (1) On the death of a limited
partner his executor or administrator shall have all the rights of a
limited partner for the purpose of settling his estate, and such power
as the deceased had to constitute his assignee a substituted limited
partner.
  (2) The estate of a deceased limited partner shall be liable for all
his liabilities as a limited partner.
```
