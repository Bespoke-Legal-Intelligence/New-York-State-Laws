# Partnership Law § 104 — Compensation of limited partner

[Partnership Law](../README.md) › [Article 8 — Limited Partnerships](README.md)

```text
  § 104. Compensation of limited partner.  A limited partner may receive
from the partnership the share of the profits or the compensation by way
of income stipulated for in the certificate; provided, that after such
payment is made, whether from the property of the partnership or that of
a general partner, the partnership assets are in excess of all
liabilities of the partnership except liabilities to limited partners on
account of their contributions and to general partners.
```
