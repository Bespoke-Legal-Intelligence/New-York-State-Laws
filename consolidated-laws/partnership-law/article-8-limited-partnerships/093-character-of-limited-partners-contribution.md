# Partnership Law § 93 — Character of limited partner's contribution

[Partnership Law](../README.md) › [Article 8 — Limited Partnerships](README.md)

```text
  § 93. Character of limited partner's contribution.  The contributions
of a limited partner may be cash or other property, but not services.
```
