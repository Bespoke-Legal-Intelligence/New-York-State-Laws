# Partnership Law § 115 — Parties to actions

[Partnership Law](../README.md) › [Article 8 — Limited Partnerships](README.md)

```text
  § 115. Parties to actions.  A contributor, unless he is a general
partner, is not a proper party to proceedings by or against a
partnership, except where the object is to enforce a limited partner's
right against or liability to the partnership, and except in cases
provided for in section one hundred fifteen-a of this article.
```
