# Partnership Law § 90 — Limited partnership defined

[Partnership Law](../README.md) › [Article 8 — Limited Partnerships](README.md)

```text
  § 90. Limited partnership defined.  A limited partnership is a
partnership formed by two or more persons under the provisions of
section ninety-one, having as members one or more general partners and
one or more limited partners. The limited partners as such shall not be
bound by the obligations of the partnership.
```
