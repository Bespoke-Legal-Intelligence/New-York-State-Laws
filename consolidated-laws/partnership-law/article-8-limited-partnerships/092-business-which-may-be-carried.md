# Partnership Law § 92 — Business which may be carried on

[Partnership Law](../README.md) › [Article 8 — Limited Partnerships](README.md)

```text
  § 92. Business which may be carried on.  A limited partnership may
carry on any business which a partnership without limited partners may
carry on.
```
