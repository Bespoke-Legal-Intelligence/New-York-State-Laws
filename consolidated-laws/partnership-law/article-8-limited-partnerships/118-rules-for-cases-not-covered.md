# Partnership Law § 118 — Rules for cases not covered

[Partnership Law](../README.md) › [Article 8 — Limited Partnerships](README.md)

```text
  § 118. Rules for cases not covered.  In any case not provided for in
this article the rules of law and equity, including the law merchant,
shall govern.
```
