# Partnership Law § 97 — Admission of additional limited partners

[Partnership Law](../README.md) › [Article 8 — Limited Partnerships](README.md)

```text
  § 97. Admission of additional limited partners.  After the formation
of a limited partnership, additional limited partners may be admitted
upon filing an amendment to the original certificate in accordance with
the requirements of section one hundred and fourteen.
```
