# Rural Electric Cooperative Law § 71 — Separability

[Rural Electric Cooperative Law](../README.md) › [Article 7 — Construction; Separability; Effective Date](README.md)

```text
  § 71. Separability. If any provision of this chapter or the
application of such provision to any person or circumstance is held
invalid, the remainder of the chapter and the application of such
provisions to other persons or circumstances shall not be affected
thereby.
```
