# Rural Electric Cooperative Law § 70 — Construction

[Rural Electric Cooperative Law](../README.md) › [Article 7 — Construction; Separability; Effective Date](README.md)

```text
  § 70. Construction. This chapter shall be construed liberally. The
enumeration of any object, purpose, power, manner, method, or thing
shall not be deemed to exclude like or similar objects, purposes,
powers, manners, methods or things.
```
