# Rural Electric Cooperative Law § 10 — Purpose

[Rural Electric Cooperative Law](../README.md) › [Article 2 — Purpose; Organization and Management; Powers](README.md)

```text
  § 10. Purpose. Cooperative, non-profit, membership corporations may be
organized under this chapter for the purpose of supplying electric
energy and promoting and extending the use thereof in rural areas in
accordance with the provisions of this chapter.
```
