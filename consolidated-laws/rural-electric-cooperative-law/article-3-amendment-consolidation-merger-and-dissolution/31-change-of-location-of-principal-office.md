# Rural Electric Cooperative Law § 31 — Change of location of principal office

[Rural Electric Cooperative Law](../README.md) › [Article 3 — Amendment; Consolidation; Merger; and Dissolution](README.md)

```text
  § 31. Change of location of principal office. A cooperative may, upon
authorization of its board of directors or its members, change the
location of its principal office by filing a certificate reciting such
change of principal office, executed and acknowledged by its president
or vice-president under its seal attested by its secretary, in the
department of state.
```
