# Rural Electric Cooperative Law § 64 — Construction standards

[Rural Electric Cooperative Law](../README.md) › [Article 6 — General Provisions Governing Property and Affairs](README.md)

```text
  § 64. Construction standards. Construction of electric lines by a
cooperative shall, as a minimum requirement, comply with the standards
of the national electric safety code in effect at the time of such
construction.
```
