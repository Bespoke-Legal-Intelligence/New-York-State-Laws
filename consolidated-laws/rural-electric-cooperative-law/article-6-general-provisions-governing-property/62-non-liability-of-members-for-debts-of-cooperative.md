# Rural Electric Cooperative Law § 62 — Non-liability of members for debts of cooperative

[Rural Electric Cooperative Law](../README.md) › [Article 6 — General Provisions Governing Property and Affairs](README.md)

```text
  § 62. Non-liability of members for debts of cooperative. No member
shall be liable or responsible for any debts of the cooperative and the
property of the members shall not be subject to execution therefor.
```
