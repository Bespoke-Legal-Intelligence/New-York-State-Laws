# Civil Rights Law § 23 — No person to be arrested in civil proceedings without a statutory provision

[Civil Rights Law](../README.md) › [Article 3 — Privilege From Arrest](README.md)

```text
  § 23. No person to be arrested in civil proceedings without a
statutory provision. A person shall not be arrested in a civil action or
special proceeding, except as prescribed by statute. The writ of ne
exeat is abolished.
```
