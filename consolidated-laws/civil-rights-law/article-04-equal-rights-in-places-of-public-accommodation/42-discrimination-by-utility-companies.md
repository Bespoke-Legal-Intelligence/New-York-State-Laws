# Civil Rights Law § 42 — Discrimination by utility companies

[Civil Rights Law](../README.md) › [Article 4 — Equal Rights In Places of Public Accommodation and Amusement](README.md)

```text
  § 42. Discrimination by utility companies. It shall be unlawful for
any public utility company, as defined in the public service law, to
refuse to employ any person in any capacity in the operation or
maintenance of a public service on account of the race, creed, color or
national origin of such person.
```
