# Civil Rights Law § 9 — Freedom of elections

[Civil Rights Law](../README.md) › [Article 2 — Bill of Rights](README.md)

```text
  § 9. Freedom of elections. All elections ought to be free; and no
person by force of arms, malice, menacing, or otherwise, should presume
to disturb or hinder any citizen of this state in the free exercise of
the right of suffrage.
```
