# Civil Rights Law § 7 — Quartering soldiers

[Civil Rights Law](../README.md) › [Article 2 — Bill of Rights](README.md)

```text
  § 7. Quartering soldiers. No soldier can in time of peace be quartered
in any house, without the consent of the owner; nor in time of war, but
in a manner to be prescribed by law.
```
