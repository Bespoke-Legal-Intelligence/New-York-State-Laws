# Civil Rights Law § 11 — Fines must be reasonable and imposed only for cause

[Civil Rights Law](../README.md) › [Article 2 — Bill of Rights](README.md)

```text
  § 11. Fines must be reasonable and imposed only for cause. No citizen
of this state ought to be fined or amerced without reasonable cause, and
such fine or amercement should always be proportioned to the nature of
the offense.
```
