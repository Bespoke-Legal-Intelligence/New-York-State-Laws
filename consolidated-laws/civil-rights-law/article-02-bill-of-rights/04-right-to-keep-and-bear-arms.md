# Civil Rights Law § 4 — Right to keep and bear arms

[Civil Rights Law](../README.md) › [Article 2 — Bill of Rights](README.md)

```text
  § 4. Right to keep and bear arms. A well regulated militia being
necessary to the security of a free state, the right of the people to
keep and bear arms cannot be infringed.
```
