# Civil Rights Law § 101 — Face coverings prohibited

[Civil Rights Law](../README.md) › [Article 10 — Law Enforcement Face Coverings](README.md)

```text
  § 101. Face coverings prohibited. 1. A law enforcement officer shall
not wear any face covering that conceals, disguises, or obscures their
facial identity while interacting with the public in the performance of
their duties, except as authorized by this article.
  2. Any person who willfully violates this section shall for a first
offense be guilty of a violation and each subsequent offense shall be
guilty of a misdemeanor.
```
