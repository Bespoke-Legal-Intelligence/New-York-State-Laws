# Civil Rights Law § 1-A — Definitions

[Civil Rights Law](../README.md) › [Article 1 — Short Title](README.md)

```text
  § 1-a. Definitions. The term "minor" or "infant", as used in this
chapter, shall mean a person who has not attained the age of eighteen
years.
```
