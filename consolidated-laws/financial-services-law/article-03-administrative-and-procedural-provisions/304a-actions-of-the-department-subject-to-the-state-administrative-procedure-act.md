# Financial Services Law § 304-A — Actions of the department subject to the state administrative procedure act

[Financial Services Law](../README.md) › [Article 3 — Administrative and Procedural Provisions](README.md)

```text
  § 304-a. Actions of the department subject to the state administrative
procedure act. Unless otherwise specifically exempted in this chapter,
all rule making and adjudicatory proceedings shall be made in accordance
and consistent with the provisions of the state administrative procedure
act.
```
