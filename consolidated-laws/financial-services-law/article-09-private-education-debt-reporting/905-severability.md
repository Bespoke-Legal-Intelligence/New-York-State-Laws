# Financial Services Law § 905 — Severability

[Financial Services Law](../README.md) › [Article 9 — Private Education Debt Reporting](README.md)

```text
  § 905. Severability. If any provision of this article or the
application thereof to any person or circumstance is adjudged invalid by
a court of competent jurisdiction, that judgment shall not affect or
impair the validity of the other provisions of this article or the
application thereof to other persons and circumstances.
```
