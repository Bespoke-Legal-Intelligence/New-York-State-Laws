# Limited Liability Company Law § 601 — Nature of membership interest

[Limited Liability Company Law](../README.md) › [Article 6 — Members and Membership](README.md)

```text
  § 601. Nature of membership interest. A membership interest in the
limited liability company is personal property. A member has no interest
in specific property of the limited liability company.
```
