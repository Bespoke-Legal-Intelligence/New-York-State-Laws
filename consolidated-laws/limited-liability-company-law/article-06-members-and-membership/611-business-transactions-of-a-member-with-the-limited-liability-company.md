# Limited Liability Company Law § 611 — Business transactions of a member with the limited liability company

[Limited Liability Company Law](../README.md) › [Article 6 — Members and Membership](README.md)

```text
  § 611. Business transactions of a member with the limited liability
company. Except as may be provided in the operating agreement, a member
may lend money to, borrow money from, act as a guarantor or surety for,
provide collateral for the obligations of and transact other business
with the limited liability company and, subject to other applicable law,
has the same rights and obligations with respect thereto as a person who
is not a member.
```
