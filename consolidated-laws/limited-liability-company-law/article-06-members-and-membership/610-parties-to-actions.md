# Limited Liability Company Law § 610 — Parties to actions

[Limited Liability Company Law](../README.md) › [Article 6 — Members and Membership](README.md)

```text
  § 610. Parties to actions. A member of a limited liability company is
not a proper party to proceedings by or against a limited liability
company, except where the object is to enforce a member's right against
or liability to the limited liability company.
```
