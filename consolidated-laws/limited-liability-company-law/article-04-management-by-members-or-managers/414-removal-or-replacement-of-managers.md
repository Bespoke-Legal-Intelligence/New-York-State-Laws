# Limited Liability Company Law § 414 — Removal or replacement of managers

[Limited Liability Company Law](../README.md) › [Article 4 — Management By Members or Managers](README.md)

```text
  § 414. Removal or replacement of managers. Except as provided in the
operating agreement, any or all managers of a limited liability company
may be removed or replaced with or without cause by a vote of a majority
in interest of the members entitled to vote thereon.
```
