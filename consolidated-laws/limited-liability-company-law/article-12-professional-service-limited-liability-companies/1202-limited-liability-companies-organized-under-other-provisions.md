# Limited Liability Company Law § 1202 — Limited liability companies organized under other provisions of law

[Limited Liability Company Law](../README.md) › [Article 12 — Professional Service Limited Liability Companies](README.md)

```text
  § 1202. Limited liability companies organized under other provisions
of law. The provisions of this article shall not apply to limited
liability companies heretofore or hereafter duly formed under any other
provision of law.
```
