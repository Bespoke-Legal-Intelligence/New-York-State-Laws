# Limited Liability Company Law § 1309 — Licensing of individuals

[Limited Liability Company Law](../README.md) › [Article 13 — Foreign Professional Service Limited Liability Companies](README.md)

```text
  § 1309. Licensing of individuals. No member, manager or employee of a
foreign professional service limited liability company who is an
individual shall practice his or her profession in this state unless
such individual is duly licensed to practice such profession in this
state.
```
