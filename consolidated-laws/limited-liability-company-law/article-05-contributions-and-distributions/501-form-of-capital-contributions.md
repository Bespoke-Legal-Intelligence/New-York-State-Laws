# Limited Liability Company Law § 501 — Form of capital contributions

[Limited Liability Company Law](../README.md) › [Article 5 — Contributions and Distributions](README.md)

```text
  § 501. Form of capital contributions. The contribution of a member to
the capital of a limited liability company may be in cash, property or
services rendered or a promissory note or other obligation to contribute
cash or property or to render services, or any combination of the
foregoing.
```
