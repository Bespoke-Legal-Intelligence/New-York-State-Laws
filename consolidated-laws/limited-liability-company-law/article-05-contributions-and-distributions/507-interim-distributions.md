# Limited Liability Company Law § 507 — Interim distributions

[Limited Liability Company Law](../README.md) › [Article 5 — Contributions and Distributions](README.md)

```text
  § 507. Interim distributions. Except as provided in this chapter, to
the extent and at the times or upon the happening of events specified in
the operating agreement, a member is entitled to receive distributions
from a limited liability company before his or her withdrawal from the
limited liability company and before the dissolution and winding up of
the limited liability company.
```
