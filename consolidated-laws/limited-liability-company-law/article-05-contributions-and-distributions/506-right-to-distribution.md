# Limited Liability Company Law § 506 — Right to distribution

[Limited Liability Company Law](../README.md) › [Article 5 — Contributions and Distributions](README.md)

```text
  § 506. Right to distribution. Subject to sections five hundred eight
and seven hundred four of this chapter, at the time a member becomes
entitled to receive a distribution, such member has the status of, and
is entitled to all remedies available to, a creditor of the limited
liability company with respect to the distribution.
```
