# Employers' Liability Law § 17 — Construction

[Employers' Liability Law](../README.md) › [Article 3 — Laws Repealed; Construction; When to Take Effect](README.md)

```text
  § 17. Construction. The provisions of this chapter shall be construed
as a continuation of the provisions of sections two hundred to two
hundred and four of chapter thirty-six of the laws of nineteen hundred
and nine, as amended by chapter three hundred and fifty-two of the laws
of nineteen hundred and ten, and not as a new enactment, and nothing
contained in this chapter shall apply to employers or employees in
hazardous occupations as such occupations are defined by the workmen's
compensation law.
```
