# Election Law § 7-110 — Ballots; form for ballot proposals

[Election Law](../../README.md) › [Article 7 — Election Ballot](../README.md) › [Title 1 — Form of Ballots](README.md)

```text
  § 7-110. Ballots; form for ballot proposals. Ballot proposals shall
appear on the voting machine or ballot in a separate section. At the
left of, or below or above, each proposal shall appear two voting levers
or two voting squares, each at least one-half inch square. Next to the
first lever or square shall be printed the word "Yes," and next to the
second lever or square shall be printed the word "No." The proposals
shall be numbered consecutively on the voting machine or ballot. The
number of each proposal shall appear in front of its designation as an
amendment, proposition or question in the following form:  "Proposal
one, an amendment; proposal two, a proposition; proposal three, a
question".
```
