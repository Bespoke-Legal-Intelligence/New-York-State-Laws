# Election Law § 7-121 — Ballots which are counted by machine

[Election Law](../../README.md) › [Article 7 — Election Ballot](../README.md) › [Title 1 — Form of Ballots](README.md)

```text
  § 7-121. Ballots which are counted by machine. All ballots printed for
use on a voting system approved by the state board of elections may be
printed and arranged in a manner which would permit them to be counted
by such machine.
```
