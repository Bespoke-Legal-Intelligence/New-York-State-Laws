# Election Law § 7-209 — Elimination of punch card ballots

[Election Law](../../README.md) › [Article 7 — Election Ballot](../README.md) › [Title 2 — Voting Machines](README.md)

```text
  § 7-209. Elimination of punch card ballots. Notwithstanding any other
provision of law, on or after September first, two thousand six, no
punch card ballot or punch card voting system shall be used in any
manner in the conduct of any election.
```
