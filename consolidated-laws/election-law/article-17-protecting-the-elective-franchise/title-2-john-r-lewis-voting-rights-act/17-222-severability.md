# Election Law § 17-222 — Severability

[Election Law](../../README.md) › [Article 17 — Protecting the Elective Franchise](../README.md) › [Title 2 — John R Lewis Voting Rights Act of New York](README.md)

```text
  § 17-222. Severability. If any provision of this title or its
application to any person, political subdivision, or circumstance is
held invalid, the invalidity shall not affect other provisions or
applications of this title which can be given effect without the invalid
provision or application, and to this end the provisions of this title
are severable.
```
