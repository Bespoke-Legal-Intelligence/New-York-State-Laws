# Election Law § 17-162 — Judicial candidates not to contribute

[Election Law](../../README.md) › [Article 17 — Protecting the Elective Franchise](../README.md) › [Title 1 — Violations of the Elective Franchise](README.md)

```text
  § 17-162. Judicial candidates not to contribute. No candidate for a
judicial office shall, directly or indirectly, make any contribution of
money or other thing of value, nor shall any contribution be solicited
of him; but a candidate for a judicial office may make such legal
expenditures, other than contributions, authorized by this chapter.
```
