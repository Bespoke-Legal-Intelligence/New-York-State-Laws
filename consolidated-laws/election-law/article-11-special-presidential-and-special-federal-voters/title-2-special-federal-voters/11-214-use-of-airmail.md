# Election Law § 11-214 — Use of airmail

[Election Law](../../README.md) › [Article 11 — Special Presidential and Special Federal Voters and Special Ballots](../README.md) › [Title 2 — Special Federal Voters](README.md)

```text
  § 11-214. Use of airmail. All ballots, applications, notices or other
mail sent, pursuant to the provisions of this title, to addresses
outside the United States, Canada or Mexico, shall be sent by airmail.
```
