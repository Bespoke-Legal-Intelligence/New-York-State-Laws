# Election Law § 10-126 — Military voting; applicability of general provisions

[Election Law](../README.md) › [Article 10 — Voting By Members of Armed Forces](README.md)

```text
  § 10-126. Military voting; applicability of general provisions. The
general provisions of this chapter shall apply to this article, except
as they are inconsistent herewith. The provisions of this article shall
be liberally construed for the purpose of providing military voters the
opportunity to vote. The state board of elections shall have power to
adopt and promulgate regulations to effectuate the provisions of this
article.
```
