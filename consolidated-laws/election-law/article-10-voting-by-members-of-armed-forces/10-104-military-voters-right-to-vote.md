# Election Law § 10-104 — Military voters; right to vote

[Election Law](../README.md) › [Article 10 — Voting By Members of Armed Forces](README.md)

```text
  § 10-104. Military voters; right to vote. A military voter of this
state shall be entitled to vote as fully as if he were present at his
polling place and to register and vote in the manner hereinafter
provided except that the provisions of this article for absentee voting
in primary elections shall not apply to the party positions of members
of the ward, town, city or county committee.
```
