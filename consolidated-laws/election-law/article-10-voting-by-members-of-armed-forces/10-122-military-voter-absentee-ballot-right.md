# Election Law § 10-122 — Military voter; absentee ballot, right to

[Election Law](../README.md) › [Article 10 — Voting By Members of Armed Forces](README.md)

```text
  * § 10-122. Military voter; absentee ballot, right to. Any military
voter may vote by absentee ballot rather than military ballot provided
that he complies with the provisions of this chapter relating thereto.
  * NB Effective until January 1, 2024
  * § 10-122. Military voter; early mail or absentee ballot, right to.
Any military voter may vote by early mail ballot or absentee ballot
rather than military ballot provided that he complies with the
provisions of this chapter relating thereto.
  * NB Effective January 1, 2024
```
