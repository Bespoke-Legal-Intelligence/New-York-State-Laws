# Election Law § 15-114 — Number of voting machines

[Election Law](../README.md) › [Article 15 — Village Elections](README.md)

```text
  § 15-114. Number of voting machines. Any village that uses voting
machines for village elections shall provide one voting machine for each
eight hundred or fraction thereof of qualified voters in each election
district, as such voters are defined in section 15-110 of this article.
```
