# Election Law § 15-130 — Election of trustees by wards

[Election Law](../README.md) › [Article 15 — Village Elections](README.md)

```text
  § 15-130. Election of trustees by wards. The board of trustees of any
village may, by resolution, and subject to a mandatory referendum,
provide for the election of trustees by wards, alter existing ward
boundaries or abolish wards and the election of trustees by wards. If a
village elects trustees by wards separate ballot boxes or voting
machines shall be provided for each ward.
```
