# Election Law § 2-108 — State and county committees; new party

[Election Law](../README.md) › [Article 2 — Party Organization](README.md)

```text
  § 2-108. State and county committees; new party. The state committee
and county committees of a new political party, which meet prior to the
first primary for which members of such party shall have become
enrolled, shall be formed as provided by the rules of such party.
```
