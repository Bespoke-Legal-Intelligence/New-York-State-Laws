# Election Law § 2-100 — Party committees; provision for

[Election Law](../README.md) › [Article 2 — Party Organization](README.md)

```text
  § 2-100. Party committees; provision for. Party committees shall
consist of a state committee, county committees, and such other
committees as the rules of the party may allow.
```
