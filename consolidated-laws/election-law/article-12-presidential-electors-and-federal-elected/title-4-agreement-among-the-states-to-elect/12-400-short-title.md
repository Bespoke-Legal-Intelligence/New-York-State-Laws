# Election Law § 12-400 — Short title

[Election Law](../../README.md) › [Article 12 — Presidential Electors and Federal Elected Officers](../README.md) › [Title 4 — Agreement Among the States to Elect the President By National Popular Vote](README.md)

```text
  * § 12-400. Short title. This title shall be known and may be cited as
"agreement among the states to elect the president by national popular
vote".
  * NB Effective upon notification by the commissioner of the state
board of elections upon the occurence of the adoption of the agreement
among the states to elect the president by national popular vote by two
or more states.
```
