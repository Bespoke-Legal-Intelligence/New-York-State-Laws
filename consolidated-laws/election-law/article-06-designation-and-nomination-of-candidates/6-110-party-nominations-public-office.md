# Election Law § 6-110 — Party nominations; public office

[Election Law](../README.md) › [Article 6 — Designation and Nomination of Candidates](README.md)

```text
  § 6-110. Party nominations; public office. All other party nominations
of candidates for offices to be filled at a general election, except as
provided for herein, shall be made at the primary election.
```
