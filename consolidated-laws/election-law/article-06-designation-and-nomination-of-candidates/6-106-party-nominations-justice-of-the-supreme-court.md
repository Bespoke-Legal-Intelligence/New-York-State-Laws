# Election Law § 6-106 — Party nominations; justice of the supreme court

[Election Law](../README.md) › [Article 6 — Designation and Nomination of Candidates](README.md)

```text
  § 6-106. Party nominations; justice of the supreme court. Party
nominations for the office of justice of the supreme court shall be made
by the judicial district convention.
```
