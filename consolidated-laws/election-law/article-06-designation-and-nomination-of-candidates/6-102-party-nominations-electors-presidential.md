# Election Law § 6-102 — Party nominations; electors, presidential

[Election Law](../README.md) › [Article 6 — Designation and Nomination of Candidates](README.md)

```text
  § 6-102. Party nominations; electors, presidential. Party nominations
of candidates for the office of elector of president and vice president
of the United States, one for each congressional district and two at
large, shall be made by the state committee.
```
