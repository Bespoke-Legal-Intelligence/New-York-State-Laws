# Election Law § 6-130 — Designating petition; signer information

[Election Law](../README.md) › [Article 6 — Designation and Nomination of Candidates](README.md)

```text
  § 6-130. Designating petition; signer information.  The sheets of a
designating petition must set forth in every instance the name of the
signer, his or her residence address, town or city (except in the city
of New York, the county), and the date when the signature is affixed.
```
