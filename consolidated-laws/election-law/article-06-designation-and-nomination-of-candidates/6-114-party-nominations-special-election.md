# Election Law § 6-114 — Party nominations; special election

[Election Law](../README.md) › [Article 6 — Designation and Nomination of Candidates](README.md)

```text
  § 6-114. Party nominations; special election. Party nominations for an
office to be filled at a special election shall be made in the manner
prescribed by the rules of the party.
```
