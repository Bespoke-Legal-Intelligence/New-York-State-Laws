# Election Law § 5-214 — Registration cards for identification

[Election Law](../../README.md) › [Article 5 — Registration and Enrollment of Voters](../README.md) › [Title 2 — Registration and Enrollment](README.md)

```text
  § 5-214. Registration cards for identification. A board of elections
may provide identification cards for use in any city or town in such
county in which the board deems it necessary to facilitate voting.
However, the failure of a voter to present such card shall not deprive
such voter of the right to exercise the franchise, or any other right
provided under this chapter.
```
