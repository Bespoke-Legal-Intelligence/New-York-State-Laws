# Election Law § 5-1001 — Designating a member representative

[Election Law](../../README.md) › [Article 5 — Registration and Enrollment of Voters](../README.md) › [Title 10 — Multistate Voter List Maintenance Organization Membership Authorization](README.md)

```text
  § 5-1001. Designating a member representative. The state board of
elections shall designate the chief election official of the state of
New York as the representative to the board of directors of any
multistate voter list maintenance organization that the state is a
member of including, but not limited to, the electronic registration
information center or its successor.
```
