# Election Law § 5-1004 — Withdrawal from a multistate voter list maintenance organization

[Election Law](../../README.md) › [Article 5 — Registration and Enrollment of Voters](../README.md) › [Title 10 — Multistate Voter List Maintenance Organization Membership Authorization](README.md)

```text
  § 5-1004. Withdrawal from a multistate voter list maintenance
organization. Withdrawal of the state from a multistate voter list
maintenance organization shall require an affirmative vote of the state
board of elections commissioners pursuant to subdivision four of section
3-100 of this chapter.
```
