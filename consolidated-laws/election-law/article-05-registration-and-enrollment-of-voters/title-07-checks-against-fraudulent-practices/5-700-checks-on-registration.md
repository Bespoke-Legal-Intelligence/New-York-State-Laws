# Election Law § 5-700 — Checks on registration

[Election Law](../../README.md) › [Article 5 — Registration and Enrollment of Voters](../README.md) › [Title 7 — Checks Against Fraudulent Practices](README.md)

```text
  § 5-700. Checks on registration. The board of elections shall permit
only those persons to register or to remain registered who have the
qualifications prescribed by this article.
```
