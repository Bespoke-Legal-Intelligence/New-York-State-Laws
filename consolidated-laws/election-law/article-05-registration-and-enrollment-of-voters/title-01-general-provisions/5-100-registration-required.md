# Election Law § 5-100 — Registration; required

[Election Law](../../README.md) › [Article 5 — Registration and Enrollment of Voters](../README.md) › [Title 1 — General Provisions](README.md)

```text
  § 5-100. Registration; required. A person shall not be entitled to
vote in any election held pursuant to this chapter unless he shall be
registered, and if required, enrolled pursuant to the provisions of this
article unless he shall present a court order directing that he be
permitted to vote at such election. Where a specific provision of law
relating to the registration of voters exists in any other statute,
which is inconsistent with the provisions of this article, such
provision shall apply and the provisions of this article not
inconsistent therewith shall apply.
```
