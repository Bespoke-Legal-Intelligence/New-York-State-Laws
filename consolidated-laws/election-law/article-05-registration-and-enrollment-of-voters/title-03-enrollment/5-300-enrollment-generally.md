# Election Law § 5-300 — Enrollment; generally

[Election Law](../../README.md) › [Article 5 — Registration and Enrollment of Voters](../README.md) › [Title 3 — Enrollment](README.md)

```text
  § 5-300. Enrollment; generally. At the time a voter is registered or
completes an application for registration he may mark his party
enrollment within the circle or box underneath or next to the party of
his election on the application form.
```
