# Election Law § 17-112 — Soliciting media support

[Election Law](../../README.md) › [Article 17 — Protecting of the Elective Franchise](../README.md) › [Title 1 — Violations of the Elective Franchise](README.md)

```text
  § 17-112. Soliciting media support. Any person who solicits from a
candidate for an elective office money or other property as a condition
or consideration for a supporting article, report or advertisement in
any publication or news media in favor of such candidate, is guilty of a
misdemeanor.
```
