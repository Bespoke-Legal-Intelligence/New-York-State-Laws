# Election Law § 14-130 — Campaign funds for personal use

[Election Law](../../README.md) › [Article 14 — Campaign Receipts and Expenditures; Matching Financing](../README.md) › [Title 1 — Campaign Receipts and Expenditures](README.md)

```text
  § 14-130. Campaign funds for personal use. Contributions received by a
candidate or a political committee may be expended for any lawful
purpose. Such funds shall not be converted by any person to a personal
use which is unrelated to a political campaign or the holding of a
public office or party position.
```
