# Election Law § 16-118 — Proceedings to review removal of committee member or officer

[Election Law](../README.md) › [Article 16 — Judicial Proceedings](README.md)

```text
  § 16-118. Proceedings to review removal of committee member or
officer. The action of any political committee or independent body in
removing a member or officer thereof may be reviewed by a proceeding
pursuant to article seventy-eight of the civil practice law and rules.
```
