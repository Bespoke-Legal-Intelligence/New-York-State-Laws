# Executive Law § 354 — Cooperation and facilities of other departments

[Executive Law](../README.md) › [Article 17 — Veterans' Services](README.md)

**Repealed** (7 Apr 2023). The text below is what the Senate publishes for this repealed provision.

```text
  * § 354. Cooperation and facilities of other departments. To
effectuate the purposes of this article, the governor may direct any
department, division, board, bureau, commission or agency of the state,
or of any political subdivision thereof, to cooperate with and assist
and advise the division in the performance of its duties and functions,
and to provide such facilities, including personnel, materials and other
assistance and data as will enable the division or any of its agencies
to properly carry out its activities and effectuate its purposes under
this article.
  * NB Repealed April 1, 2023
```
