# Executive Law § 501-F — Commissioner for the interstate compact for juveniles

[Executive Law](../../README.md) › [Article 19-G — Office of Children and Family Services](../README.md) › [Title 1 — No title](README.md)

```text
  * § 501-f. Commissioner for the interstate compact for juveniles.
Pursuant to subdivision B of article III of section five hundred one-e
of this article, the commissioner shall be appointed by the governor in
consultation with the state council established pursuant to article IX
of section five hundred one-e and section five hundred one-g of this
article.
  * NB Repealed September 1, 2015
```
