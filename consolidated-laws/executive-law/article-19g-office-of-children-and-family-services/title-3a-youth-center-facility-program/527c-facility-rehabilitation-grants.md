# Executive Law § 527-C — Facility rehabilitation grants

[Executive Law](../../README.md) › [Article 19-G — Office of Children and Family Services](../README.md) › [Title 3-A — Youth Center Facility Program](README.md)

```text
  § 527-c. Facility rehabilitation grants. Within the limit of funds
made available by appropriation from the youth centers facility fund, as
established by section ninety-seven-ff of the state finance law, the
division is hereby authorized to award facility rehabilitation grants to
eligible applicants for all or a portion of project costs to
rehabilitate youth centers subject to the terms and conditions of this
title.
```
