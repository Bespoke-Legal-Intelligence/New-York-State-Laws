# Executive Law § 523-G — Review of policies concerning children

[Executive Law](../../../README.md) › [Article 19-G — Office of Children and Family Services](../../README.md) › [Title 3 — Programs and Services For Youth](../README.md) › [Subtitle B — Office of the Ombudsman](README.md)

```text
  § 523-g. Review of policies concerning children. In addition to other
powers and duties specified in this subtitle, the office of the
ombudsman may recommend changes in office policies and regulations
concerning children to improve services or to correct systemic problems
concerning services to children in the juvenile justice system.
```
