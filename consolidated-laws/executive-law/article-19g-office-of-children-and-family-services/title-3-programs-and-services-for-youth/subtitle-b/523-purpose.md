# Executive Law § 523 — Purpose

[Executive Law](../../../README.md) › [Article 19-G — Office of Children and Family Services](../../README.md) › [Title 3 — Programs and Services For Youth](../README.md) › [Subtitle B — Office of the Ombudsman](README.md)

```text
  § 523. Purpose. The purpose of this subtitle is to protect and promote
legal rights for youth in programs and facilities operated by the office
of children and family services, by codifying the office of the
ombudsman.
```
