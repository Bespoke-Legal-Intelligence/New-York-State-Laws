# Executive Law § 523-F — Notification to the office of the ombudsman of critical incidents

[Executive Law](../../../README.md) › [Article 19-G — Office of Children and Family Services](../../README.md) › [Title 3 — Programs and Services For Youth](../README.md) › [Subtitle B — Office of the Ombudsman](README.md)

```text
  § 523-f. Notification to the office of the ombudsman of critical
incidents. The office of the ombudsman shall receive copies of critical
incident reports involving a youth residing in a facility operated by
the office.
```
