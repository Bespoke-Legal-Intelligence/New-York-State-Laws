# Executive Law § 516 — Care of infants by division for youth

[Executive Law](../../../README.md) › [Article 19-G — Office of Children and Family Services](../../README.md) › [Title 3 — Programs and Services For Youth](../README.md) › [Subtitle A — Additional Services For Youth](README.md)

```text
  § 516. Care of infants by division for youth. The division for youth
is authorized to provide residential care in division facilities subject
to the regulations of the division, for infants born to or being nursed
by female residents placed with the division. Residential care for such
an infant may be provided for such period of time as is deemed desirable
for the welfare of the mother or infant.
```
