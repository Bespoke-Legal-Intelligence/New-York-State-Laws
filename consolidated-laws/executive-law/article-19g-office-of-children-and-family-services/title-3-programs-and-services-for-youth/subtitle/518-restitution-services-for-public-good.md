# Executive Law § 518 — Restitution; services for public good

[Executive Law](../../../README.md) › [Article 19-G — Office of Children and Family Services](../../README.md) › [Title 3 — Programs and Services For Youth](../README.md) › [Subtitle A — Additional Services For Youth](README.md)

```text
  § 518. Restitution; services for public good. The division shall
develop and operate programs for youths placed or referred to the
division or in conjunction with an order provided in section 353.6 of
the family court act. The division shall promulgate regulations as
required in such section.
```
