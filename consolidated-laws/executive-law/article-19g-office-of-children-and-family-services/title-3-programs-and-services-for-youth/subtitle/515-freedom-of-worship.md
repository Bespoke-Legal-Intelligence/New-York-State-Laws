# Executive Law § 515 — Freedom of worship

[Executive Law](../../../README.md) › [Article 19-G — Office of Children and Family Services](../../README.md) › [Title 3 — Programs and Services For Youth](../README.md) › [Subtitle A — Additional Services For Youth](README.md)

```text
  § 515. Freedom of worship. It shall be the duty of the division to
afford a child placed with it freedom of worship as provided for in the
state constitution.
```
