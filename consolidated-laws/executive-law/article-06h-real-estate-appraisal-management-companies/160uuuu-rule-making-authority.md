# Executive Law § 160-UUUU — Rule-making authority

[Executive Law](../README.md) › [Article 6-H — Real Estate Appraisal Management Companies](README.md)

```text
  * § 160-uuuu. Rule-making authority. The department may adopt rules
not inconsistent with the provisions of this chapter which may be
reasonably necessary to implement, administer, and enforce the
provisions of this chapter.
  * NB Effective April 27, 2019
```
