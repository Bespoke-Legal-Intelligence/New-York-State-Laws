# Executive Law § 160-HHHH — Owner requirements

[Executive Law](../README.md) › [Article 6-H — Real Estate Appraisal Management Companies](README.md)

```text
  § 160-hhhh. Owner requirements. An appraisal management company
applying for a certificate of registration shall not be owned in whole
or in part, directly or indirectly, by a person who has had a license,
registration or certificate to act as a real estate appraiser denied,
revoked, or surrendered in lieu of pending discipline in any state or by
a person holding ten percent or more of the company where that person
has had a license, registration or certificate to act as a real estate
appraiser denied, revoked, or surrendered in lieu of possible discipline
in any state.
```
