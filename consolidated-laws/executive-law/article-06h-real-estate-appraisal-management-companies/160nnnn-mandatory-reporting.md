# Executive Law § 160-NNNN — Mandatory reporting

[Executive Law](../README.md) › [Article 6-H — Real Estate Appraisal Management Companies](README.md)

```text
  * § 160-nnnn. Mandatory reporting. An appraisal management company
that has a reasonable basis to believe an appraiser within the appraisal
management company's appraisal panel is failing to comply with the
uniform standards of professional appraisal practice, is violating
applicable laws, or is otherwise engaging in unethical or unprofessional
conduct shall immediately refer such matter to the department.
  * NB Effective April 27, 2019
```
