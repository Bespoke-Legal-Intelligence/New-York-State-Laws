# Executive Law § 970 — Affect on other laws

[Executive Law](../README.md) › [Article 46 — Long Island South Shore Estuary Reserve](README.md)

```text
  * § 970. Affect on other laws. Nothing in this article shall affect
the police powers, local planning powers, zoning powers or authority to
regulate any activity by villages, towns or counties within the reserve
or the police powers of the state to regulate any activity within the
reserve or affect the authority of any state or public agency in the
management of any state or public lands within the reserve.
  * NB There are 2 § 970's
```
