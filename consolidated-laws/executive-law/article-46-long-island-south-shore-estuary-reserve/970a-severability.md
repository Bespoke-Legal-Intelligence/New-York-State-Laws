# Executive Law § 970-A — Severability

[Executive Law](../README.md) › [Article 46 — Long Island South Shore Estuary Reserve](README.md)

```text
  § 970-a. Severability. The provisions of this article shall be
severable and if any portion thereof or the applicability thereof to any
person or circumstances shall be held invalid, the remainder of this
article and the application thereof shall not be affected thereby.
```
