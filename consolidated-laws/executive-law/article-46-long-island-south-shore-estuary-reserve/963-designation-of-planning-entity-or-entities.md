# Executive Law § 963 — Designation of planning entity or entities

[Executive Law](../README.md) › [Article 46 — Long Island South Shore Estuary Reserve](README.md)

```text
  § 963. Designation of planning entity or entities. The secretary of
state, after consultation with the council, shall designate a planning
entity or entities for the purposes of assisting the council in
conducting research and developing the plan.
```
