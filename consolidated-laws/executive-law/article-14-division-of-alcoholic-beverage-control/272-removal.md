# Executive Law § 272 — Removal

[Executive Law](../README.md) › [Article 14 — Division of Alcoholic Beverage Control](README.md)

```text
  § 272. Removal. Any member of the authority may be removed by the
governor for cause after an opportunity to be heard. A statement of the
cause of his removal shall be filed by the governor in the office of the
secretary of state.
```
