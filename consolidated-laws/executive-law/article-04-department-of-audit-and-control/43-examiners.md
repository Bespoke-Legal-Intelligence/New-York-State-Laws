# Executive Law § 43 — Examiners

[Executive Law](../README.md) › [Article 4 — Department of Audit and Control](README.md)

```text
  § 43. Examiners.  1. Whenever the comptroller may deem it necessary to
enable him to perform the duties imposed upon him by law with regard to
the inspection, examination and audit of the fiscal affairs of the state
or of the several officers, departments, institutions, public
corporations or political subdivisions thereof, he may assign the work
of such inspection, audit and examination to any examiner or examiners
appointed by him pursuant to law.
  2. The comptroller is authorized to employ such examiners as he may
deem necessary to carry out the provisions of law in relation to his
duty as to money paid into court.
```
