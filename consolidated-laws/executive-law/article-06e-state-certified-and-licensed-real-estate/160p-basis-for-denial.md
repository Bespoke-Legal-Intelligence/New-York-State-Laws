# Executive Law § 160-P — Basis for denial

[Executive Law](../README.md) › [Article 6-E — State Certified and Licensed Real Estate Appraisers](README.md)

```text
  § 160-p. Basis for denial. The department may, in accordance with the
provisions of this article relating to hearings, deny the issuance of a
certificate as a state certified real estate appraiser, or license as a
state licensed real estate appraiser, or license as a state licensed
real estate appraiser assistant, to an applicant on any of the grounds
enumerated in this article.
```
