# Executive Law § 160-AA — Transitional licensing

[Executive Law](../README.md) › [Article 6-E — State Certified and Licensed Real Estate Appraisers](README.md)

```text
  § 160-aa. Transitional licensing. Consistent with the intent and
purpose of this article, and without the disapproval of the appraisal
subcommittee of the federal financial institutions examination council,
the board may prescribe requirements for transitional licenses which
shall expire no later than January first, nineteen hundred ninety-three.
```
