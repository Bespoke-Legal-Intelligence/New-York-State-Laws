# Executive Law § 36 — Findings by governor; issuance of reorganization plan

[Executive Law](../README.md) › [Article 3-A — Executive Reorganization Act of 2011](README.md)

**Repealed** (23 Jan 2015). The text below is what the Senate publishes for this repealed provision.

```text
  * § 36. Findings by governor; issuance of reorganization plan. 1.
Whenever the governor finds it in the public interest, he or she may
reorganize one or more agencies.
  2. Nothing in this article shall prohibit or limit the authority of
the governor or legislature to implement or enact a reorganization plan
pursuant to any other lawful process.
  * NB Repealed May 31, 2014
```
