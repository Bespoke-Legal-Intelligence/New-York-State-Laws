# Executive Law § 61 — Solicitor general

[Executive Law](../README.md) › [Article 5 — Department of Law](README.md)

```text
  § 61. Solicitor general. There shall be in the department of law a
solicitor general who shall be appointed by the attorney-general and who
shall perform such duties in the place and stead of the attorney-general
as may lawfully be assigned to him. The attorney-general shall fix his
compensation within the amounts appropriated therefor.
```
