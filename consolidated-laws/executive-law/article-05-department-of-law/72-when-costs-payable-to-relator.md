# Executive Law § 72 — When costs payable to relator

[Executive Law](../README.md) › [Article 5 — Department of Law](README.md)

```text
  § 72. When costs payable to relator. Where security is given by
relator in an action brought by the attorney-general on the relation or
information of a person having an interest, all costs and disbursements
taxed in favor of the plaintiff shall be payable to the relator.
```
