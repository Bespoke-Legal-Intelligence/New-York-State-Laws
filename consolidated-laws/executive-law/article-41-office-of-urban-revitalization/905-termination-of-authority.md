# Executive Law § 905 — Termination of authority

[Executive Law](../README.md) › [Article 41 — Office of Urban Revitalization](README.md)

```text
  * § 905. Termination of authority. Unless expressly extended by law,
the authority of the office to provide services under this article shall
terminate on March thirty-first, nineteen hundred eighty-five.
  * NB Expired March 31, 1985
```
