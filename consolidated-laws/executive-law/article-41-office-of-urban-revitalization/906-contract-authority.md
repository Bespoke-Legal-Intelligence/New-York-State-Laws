# Executive Law § 906 — Contract authority

[Executive Law](../README.md) › [Article 41 — Office of Urban Revitalization](README.md)

```text
  * § 906. Contract authority. The office is hereby empowered to enter
into any agreement or contract with any state agency necessary or
convenient to carry out the provisions of this article.
  * NB Expired March 31, 1985
```
