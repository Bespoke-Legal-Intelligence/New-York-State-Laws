# Executive Law § 898 — Assistance of other state agencies

[Executive Law](../README.md) › [Article 41 — Office of Urban Revitalization](README.md)

```text
  * § 898. Assistance of other state agencies. To effectuate the
purposes of this article, the director may request and shall be entitled
to receive from any state agency, and the same are authorized to
provide, such assistance, services, facilities, and data as will enable
the office to carry out its functions, powers, and duties, and such
temporarily or permanently assigned personnel as the director of the
budget may approve.
  * NB Expired March 31, 1985
```
