# Executive Law § 817 — Activities of the United States in the Adirondack park

[Executive Law](../README.md) › [Article 27 — Adirondack Park Agency](README.md)

```text
  § 817. Activities of the United States in the Adirondack park. 1.  It
is hereby declared to be the policy of this state that new land use or
development or acquisition of land by the United States within the
Adirondack park shall conform to the land use and development plan and
the master plan for the management of state lands so far as practicable,
and to any further extent as the Congress of the United States may by
law provide.
  2. The agency may, upon request of the United States, advise whether
any particular proposed land use and development or acquisition will
conform to the land use and development plan or the master plan.
```
