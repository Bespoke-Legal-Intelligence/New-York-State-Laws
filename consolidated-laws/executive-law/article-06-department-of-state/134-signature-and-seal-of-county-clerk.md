# Executive Law § 134 — Signature and seal of county clerk

[Executive Law](../README.md) › [Article 6 — Department of State](README.md)

```text
  § 134. Signature and seal of county clerk. The signature and seal of a
county clerk, upon a certificate of official character of a notary
public or the signature of a county clerk upon a certificate of
authentication of the signature and acts of a notary public or
commissioner of deeds, may be a facsimile, printed, stamped,
photographed or engraved thereon.
```
