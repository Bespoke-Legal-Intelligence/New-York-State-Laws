# Executive Law § 106-A — Internet access to the New York code, rules and regulations

[Executive Law](../README.md) › [Article 6 — Department of State](README.md)

```text
  § 106-a. Internet access to the New York code, rules and regulations.
The department of state shall post or maintain a link on its website to
an unofficial version of the New York codes, rules, and regulations,
which shall be provided at no cost to the end users. All state agencies
which have adopted rules and regulations shall maintain a link to the
department of state website which contains such link.
```
