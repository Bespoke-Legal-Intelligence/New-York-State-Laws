# Executive Law § 434 — Utilization of other agency assistance

[Executive Law](../README.md) › [Article 19-B — State Bingo Control Commission](README.md)

```text
  § 434. Utilization of other agency assistance. To effectuate the
purposes of this article, the governor may authorize any department,
division, board, bureau, commission or agency of the state or of or in
any political subdivision thereof to provide such facilities, assistance
and data as will enable the commission properly to carry out its
activities and effectuate its purposes hereunder.
```
