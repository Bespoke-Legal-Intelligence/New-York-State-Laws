# Executive Law § 439 — Filing and availability of rules and regulations

[Executive Law](../README.md) › [Article 19-B — State Bingo Control Commission](README.md)

```text
  § 439. Filing and availability of rules and regulations. A copy of
every rule and regulation adopted and promulgated by the commission
shall be filed in the office of the secretary of state before it shall
become effective and copies thereof shall be made available to the
various municipalities operating under the bingo licensing law.
```
