# Executive Law § 995-E — Applicability

[Executive Law](../README.md) › [Article 49-B — Commission On Forensic Science and Establishment of Dna Identification Index](README.md)

```text
  § 995-e. Applicability. This article shall not apply to a forensic DNA
laboratory operated by any agency of the federal government, or to any
forensic DNA test performed by any such federal laboratory.
```
