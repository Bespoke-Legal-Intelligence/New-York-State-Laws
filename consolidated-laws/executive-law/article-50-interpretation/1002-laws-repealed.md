# Executive Law § 1002 — Laws repealed

[Executive Law](../README.md) › [Article 50 — Interpretation](README.md)

```text
  § 1002. Laws repealed.  Chapter twenty-three of the laws of nineteen
hundred nine, entitled "An act in relation to executive officers,
constituting chapter eighteen of the consolidated laws, " and all acts
amendatory thereof and supplemental thereto, constituting the executive
law as heretofore in effect, are hereby repealed.
```
