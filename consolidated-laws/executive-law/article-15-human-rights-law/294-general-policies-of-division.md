# Executive Law § 294 — General policies of division

[Executive Law](../README.md) › [Article 15 — Human Rights Law](README.md)

```text
  § 294. General policies of division.  The division shall formulate
policies to effectuate the purposes of this article and may make
recommendations to agencies and officers of the state or local
subdivisions of government in aid of such policies and purposes.
```
