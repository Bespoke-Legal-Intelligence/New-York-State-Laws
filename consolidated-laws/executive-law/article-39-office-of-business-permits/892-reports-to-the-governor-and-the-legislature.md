# Executive Law § 892 — Reports to the governor and the legislature

[Executive Law](../README.md) › [Article 39 — Office of Business Permits](README.md)

```text
  * § 892. Reports to the governor and the legislature. The office shall
thereafter make an annual report to the governor and the legislature not
later than December thirty-first of each year.
  * NB Authority of office terminated per § 893 December 31, 1995
```
