# Executive Law § 159-H — Limitation of administrative costs

[Executive Law](../README.md) › [Article 6-D — Community Services Block Grant Program](README.md)

```text
  § 159-h. Limitation of administrative costs. Not more than five
percent of the community services block grant funds received by the
state shall be retained for administration at the state level.
```
