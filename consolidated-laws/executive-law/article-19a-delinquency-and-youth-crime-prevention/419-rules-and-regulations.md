# Executive Law § 419 — Rules and regulations

[Executive Law](../README.md) › [Article 19-A — Delinquency and Youth Crime Prevention](README.md)

```text
  § 419. Rules and regulations. The office may adopt, amend or rescind
all rules and regulations necessary to carry out the provisions of this
article, including the objective that state aid hereunder shall be
granted uniformly throughout the state, having regard for varying
conditions and needs in different parts of the state.
```
