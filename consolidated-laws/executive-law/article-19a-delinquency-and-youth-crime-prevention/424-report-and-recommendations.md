# Executive Law § 424 — Report and recommendations

[Executive Law](../README.md) › [Article 19-A — Delinquency and Youth Crime Prevention](README.md)

```text
  § 424. Report and recommendations. The office shall make an annual
report to the governor and legislature which shall include its
recommendations and program with respect to the provisions of this
article.
```
