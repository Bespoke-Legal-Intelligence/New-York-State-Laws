# Executive Law § 321 — Definitions

[Executive Law](../README.md) › [Article 15-B — New York State Martin Luther King, Jr Institute For Nonviolence Act](README.md)

```text
  § 321. Definitions. The following terms as used in this article shall
have the following meanings.
  1. "Board" shall mean the board of directors of the institute.
  2. "Institute" shall mean the New York state Martin Luther King, Jr.
institute for nonviolence established by section three hundred
twenty-two of this article.
```
