# Executive Law § 160-EE — Supervision of central dispatch facilities

[Executive Law](../README.md) › [Article 6-F — New York Black Car Operators' Injury Compensation Fund, Inc](README.md)

```text
  § 160-ee. Supervision of central dispatch facilities. A central
dispatch facility shall, with respect to the provisions of this article,
be subject to the supervision and oversight of the department and the
local licensing authority, if any, as provided in this article.
```
