# Executive Law § 160-LL — Exemption from taxes

[Executive Law](../README.md) › [Article 6-F — New York Black Car Operators' Injury Compensation Fund, Inc](README.md)

```text
  § 160-ll. Exemption from taxes.  The fund shall be exempt from payment
of all fees and taxes levied by this state or any of its subdivisions,
except taxes levied on real property.
```
