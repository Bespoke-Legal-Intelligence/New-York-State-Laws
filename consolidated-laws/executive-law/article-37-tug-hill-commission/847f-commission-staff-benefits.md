# Executive Law § 847-F — Commission staff; benefits

[Executive Law](../README.md) › [Article 37 — Tug Hill Commission](README.md)

```text
  § 847-f. Commission staff; benefits. The Tug Hill commission staff
shall be eligible for management/confidential benefits as are available
in subdivision two of section eleven of chapter four hundred sixty of
the laws of nineteen hundred eighty-two as amended.
```
