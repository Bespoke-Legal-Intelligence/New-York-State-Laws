# Executive Law § 328-A — Special administrations

[Executive Law](../README.md) › [Article 15-C — Religious Accommodation For Licensing Examinations](README.md)

```text
  § 328-a. Special administrations. When any licensing examination is
administered on a day of religious observance, a special administration
of such licensing examination or an equivalent examination shall be
offered to any affected test subject as soon after or before as is
possible, at a comparable time, place and cost, provided that in no
circumstances shall the special administration be more than thirty days
before or after the regular test administration.
```
