# Executive Law § 17 — Governor to communicate annually to legislature, reprieves, commutations and pardons

[Executive Law](../README.md) › [Article 2-A — Reprieves, Commutations and Pardons](README.md)

```text
  § 17. Governor to communicate annually to legislature, reprieves,
commutations and pardons. He must annually communicate to the
legislature, each case of reprieve, commutation or pardon; stating the
name of the convict, the crime of which he was convicted, the sentence
and its date, and the date of the commutation, pardon or reprieve.
```
