# Executive Law § 16 — His power; in respect to convictions for treason; duty of the legislature, in such cases

[Executive Law](../README.md) › [Article 2-A — Reprieves, Commutations and Pardons](README.md)

```text
  § 16. His power; in respect to convictions for treason; duty of the
legislature, in such cases. He may also suspend the execution of the
sentence, upon a conviction for treason, until the case can be reported
to the legislature, at its next meeting, when the legislature must
either pardon or commute the sentence, direct the execution thereof, or
grant a further reprieve.
```
