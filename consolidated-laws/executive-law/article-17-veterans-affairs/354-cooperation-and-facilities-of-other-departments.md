# Executive Law § 354 — Cooperation and facilities of other departments

[Executive Law](../README.md) › [Article 17 — Veterans' Affairs](README.md)

```text
  § 354. Cooperation and facilities of other departments.  To effectuate
the purposes of this article, the governor may direct any department,
division, board, bureau, commission or agency of the state, or of any
political subdivision thereof, to cooperate with and assist and advise
the division in the performance of its duties and functions, and to
provide such facilities, including personnel, materials and other
assistance and data as will enable the division or any of its agencies
to properly carry out its activities and effectuate its purposes under
this article.
```
