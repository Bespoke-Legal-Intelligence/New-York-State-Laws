# Executive Law § 943 — Utilization of other agency assistance

[Executive Law](../README.md) › [Article 44 — Council On Professional Career Opportunity](README.md)

```text
  § 943. Utilization of other agency assistance. To effectuate the
purposes of this article, any department, division, board, bureau,
commission or agency of the state or of any political subdivision
thereof shall, at the request of the council, provide to the council
such facilities, assistance and data as will enable the council properly
to carry out its powers and duties.
```
