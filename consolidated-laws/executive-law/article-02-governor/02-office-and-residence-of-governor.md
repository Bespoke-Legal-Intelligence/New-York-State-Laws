# Executive Law § 2 — Office and residence of governor

[Executive Law](../README.md) › [Article 2 — Governor](README.md)

```text
  § 2. Office and residence of governor.  The office of the governor
shall be known as the executive chamber, and his residence, as the
executive mansion.
```
