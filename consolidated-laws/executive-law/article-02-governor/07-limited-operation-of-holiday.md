# Executive Law § 7 — Limited operation of holiday

[Executive Law](../README.md) › [Article 2 — Governor](README.md)

```text
  § 7. Limited operation of holiday.  The governor in issuing any
proclamation appointing any day as a holiday or as a day of thanksgiving
or fasting and prayer or other religious observance, under section
twenty-four of the general construction law is authorized, in his
discretion, to limit or restrict the effect and operation of such
proclamation to any city or county to be designated by him in such
proclamation.
```
