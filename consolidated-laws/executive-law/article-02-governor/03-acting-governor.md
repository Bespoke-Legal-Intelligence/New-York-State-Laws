# Executive Law § 3 — Acting governor

[Executive Law](../README.md) › [Article 2 — Governor](README.md)

```text
  § 3. Acting governor.  Every provision of law relating to the governor
shall extend to the lieutenant-governor, to the president of the senate,
and to the speaker of the assembly respectively, while acting as
governor in pursuance of law.
```
