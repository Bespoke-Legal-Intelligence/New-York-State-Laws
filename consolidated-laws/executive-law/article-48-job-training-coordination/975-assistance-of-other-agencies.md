# Executive Law § 975 — Assistance of other agencies

[Executive Law](../README.md) › [Article 48 — Job Training Coordination](README.md)

```text
  § 975. Assistance of other agencies. All agencies, departments,
divisions, boards, officers and public corporations of the state shall
cooperate with the council and provide whatever assistance the commitee
may require to fulfill its purposes. Assistance may include, but need
not be limited to, consultation with and use of agency staff and the
provision of statistical and policy data.
```
