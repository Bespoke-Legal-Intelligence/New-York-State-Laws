# Executive Law § 212 — Equipment

[Executive Law](../README.md) › [Article 11 — Division of State Police](README.md)

```text
  § 212. Equipment. The superintendent is authorized on behalf of the
state to purchase, lease or lease-purchase, within the amounts
appropriated therefor, necessary equipment and supplies, including but
not limited to, vehicles and aircraft subject to the approval of the
director of the budget, and uniforms; when any state property shall
become unfit for use it shall be disposed of in accordance with the
provisions of the finance law.
```
