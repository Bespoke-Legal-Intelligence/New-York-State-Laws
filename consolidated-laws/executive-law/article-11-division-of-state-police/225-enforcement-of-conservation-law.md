# Executive Law § 225 — Enforcement of conservation law

[Executive Law](../README.md) › [Article 11 — Division of State Police](README.md)

```text
  § 225. Enforcement of conservation law. All the powers and duties now
conferred or imposed upon inspectors and game protectors under article
four of the conservation law, in relation to fish, birds and quadrupeds,
hereafter shall be exercised and performed by the state police,
inspectors and game protectors.
```
