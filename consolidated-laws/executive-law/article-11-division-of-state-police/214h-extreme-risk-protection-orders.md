# Executive Law § 214-H — Extreme risk protection orders

[Executive Law](../README.md) › [Article 11 — Division of State Police](README.md)

```text
  § 214-h. Extreme risk protection orders. The superintendent shall for
all members of the division of state police, including new and veteran
officers, develop, maintain and disseminate written policies and
procedures and educational materials regarding the availability of and
procedure for filing extreme risk protection orders under article
sixty-three-A of the civil practice law and rules, including the
requirements for police officers under section six thousand forty-one of
such law.
```
