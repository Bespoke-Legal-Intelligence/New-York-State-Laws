# Executive Law § 222 — Radio communication system

[Executive Law](../README.md) › [Article 11 — Division of State Police](README.md)

```text
  § 222. Radio communication system. The superintendent may establish a
system of police radio communication and for such purpose may purchase
or lease all necessary equipment for the operation thereof within the
appropriation.
```
