# Executive Law § 214 — Establishment of training school

[Executive Law](../README.md) › [Article 11 — Division of State Police](README.md)

```text
  § 214. Establishment of training school. The superintendent may
establish and maintain a training school and make rules and regulations
for the conduct thereof; provided, however, that such regulations shall
provide that the children or surviving spouse of a state police officer
killed in the line of duty shall, if otherwise qualified, have a prior
and preferred right over other applicants to appointment to such
training school or academy.
```
