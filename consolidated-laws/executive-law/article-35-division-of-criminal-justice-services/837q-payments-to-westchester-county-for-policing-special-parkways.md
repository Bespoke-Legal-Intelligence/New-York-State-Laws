# Executive Law § 837-Q — Payments to Westchester county for policing special parkways

[Executive Law](../README.md) › [Article 35 — Division of Criminal Justice Services](README.md)

```text
  § 837-q. Payments to Westchester county for policing special parkways.
The commissioner of the division of criminal justice services is hereby
authorized and directed, pursuant to annual appropriations, to make
payments to Westchester county for costs associated with policing
special parkways as defined in subdivision two of section seventy of the
transportation law.
```
