# Executive Law § 837-G — Livery vehicle safety training program establishment

[Executive Law](../README.md) › [Article 35 — Division of Criminal Justice Services](README.md)

```text
  § 837-g. Livery vehicle safety training program establishment. There
is hereby created within the division a livery vehicle safety program,
to be administered by the commissioner, to distribute funds to provider
agencies for the provision of program services to eligible drivers of
livery vehicles.
```
