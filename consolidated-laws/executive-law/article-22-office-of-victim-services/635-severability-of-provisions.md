# Executive Law § 635 — Severability of provisions

[Executive Law](../README.md) › [Article 22 — Office of Victim Services](README.md)

```text
  § 635. Severability of provisions. If any provision of this article or
the application thereof to any person or circumstances is held invalid,
the remainder of this article and the application of such provision to
other persons or circumstances shall not be affected thereby.
```
