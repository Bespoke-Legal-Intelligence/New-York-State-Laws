# Executive Law § 743 — Removal and vacancies

[Executive Law](../README.md) › [Article 26-B — Board of Social Welfare](README.md)

```text
  § 743. Removal and vacancies. Any member may be removed by the
governor for cause, an opportunity having been given him to be heard.  A
member who fails to attend three consecutive regular meetings of the
board, unless excused by formal vote of the board, shall be deemed to
have vacated his position.
```
