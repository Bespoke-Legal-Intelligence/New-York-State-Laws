# Executive Law § 741 — Qualifications of members

[Executive Law](../README.md) › [Article 26-B — Board of Social Welfare](README.md)

```text
  § 741. Qualifications of members. 1. No member of the board shall be
eligible to serve for more than two successive terms of five years, nor
for more than ten successive years.
  2. No member of the board shall qualify or enter upon the duties of
office or remain therein, while he is a trustee, manager, director,
officer or member of the governing board of any institution subject to
the visitation or inspection of such board.
```
