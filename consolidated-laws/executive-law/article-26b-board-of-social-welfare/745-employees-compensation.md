# Executive Law § 745 — Employees; compensation

[Executive Law](../README.md) › [Article 26-B — Board of Social Welfare](README.md)

```text
  § 745. Employees; compensation. The board may appoint and remove from
time to time, in accordance with law, such employees as it may deem
necessary for the efficient administration of the duties of the board.
The compensation of employees appointed by the board shall be determined
by it in accordance with law.
```
