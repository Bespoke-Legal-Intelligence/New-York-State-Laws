# Executive Law § 742 — Organization of the board

[Executive Law](../README.md) › [Article 26-B — Board of Social Welfare](README.md)

```text
  § 742. Organization of the board. The governor shall designate a
member of the board as chairman thereof to serve as such during the
pleasure of the governor. The board may designate other officers and may
choose from their number an executive committee with such functions as
shall be prescribed by the board.
```
