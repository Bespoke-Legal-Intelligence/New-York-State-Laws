# Executive Law § 746 — Offices of the board

[Executive Law](../README.md) › [Article 26-B — Board of Social Welfare](README.md)

```text
  § 746. Offices of the board. The board shall be provided with suitably
furnished rooms for its offices and for meetings of the board. The
principal office of the board shall be in the county of Albany.
```
