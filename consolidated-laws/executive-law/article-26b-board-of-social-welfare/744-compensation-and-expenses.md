# Executive Law § 744 — Compensation and expenses

[Executive Law](../README.md) › [Article 26-B — Board of Social Welfare](README.md)

```text
  § 744. Compensation and expenses. The chairman of the board, and the
other members of the board shall not receive a salary or other
compensation, but shall receive necessary expenses as shall have been
incurred by them in the performance of their duties. Payment of such
expenses shall be made on the audit and warrant of the comptroller from
the treasury of the state upon certification by the chairman.
```
