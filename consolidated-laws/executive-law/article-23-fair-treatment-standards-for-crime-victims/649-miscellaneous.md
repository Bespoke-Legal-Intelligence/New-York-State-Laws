# Executive Law § 649 — Miscellaneous

[Executive Law](../README.md) › [Article 23 — Fair Treatment Standards For Crime Victims](README.md)

```text
  § 649. Miscellaneous. Nothing in this article shall be construed as
creating a cause of action for damages or injunctive relief against the
state or any of its political subdivisions or officers or any agency
thereof.
```
