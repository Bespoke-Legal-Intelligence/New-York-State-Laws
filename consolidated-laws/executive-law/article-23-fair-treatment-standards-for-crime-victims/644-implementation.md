# Executive Law § 644 — Implementation

[Executive Law](../README.md) › [Article 23 — Fair Treatment Standards For Crime Victims](README.md)

```text
  § 644. Implementation. The commissioner of the division of criminal
justice services and the director of the office of victim services shall
assist criminal justice agencies in implementing the guidelines
promulgated by the commissioner.
```
