# Executive Law § 491 — Contract authority

[Executive Law](../README.md) › [Article 19-F — Rural Affairs Act](README.md)

```text
  § 491. Contract authority. The office is hereby empowered to enter
into any agreement or contract with any private or public agencies,
corporations or individuals necessary or convenient to carry out the
provisions of this article.
```
