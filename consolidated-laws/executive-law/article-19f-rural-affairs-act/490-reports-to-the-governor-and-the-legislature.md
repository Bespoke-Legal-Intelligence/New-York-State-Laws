# Executive Law § 490 — Reports to the governor and the legislature

[Executive Law](../README.md) › [Article 19-F — Rural Affairs Act](README.md)

```text
  § 490. Reports to the governor and the legislature. The office shall
hereafter make an annual report, to be received on or before February
first, to the governor and the legislature concerning the assistance
activities undertaken by the office, recommendations for legislative
proposals, data concerning program activities in rural areas and other
pertinent information which, in the opinion of the director, will
indicate the activities conducted by the office in the previous year.
```
