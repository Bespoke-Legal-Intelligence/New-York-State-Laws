# Executive Law § 600 — Application

[Executive Law](../README.md) › [Article 21-B — Manufactured Homes](README.md)

```text
  § 600. Application. This article shall be applicable to persons and
business entities engaged in the manufacture, sale, installation and
service of manufactured homes and to purchasers thereof.
```
