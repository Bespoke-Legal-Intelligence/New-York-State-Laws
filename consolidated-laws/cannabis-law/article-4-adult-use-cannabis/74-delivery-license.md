# Cannabis Law § 74 — Delivery license

[Cannabis Law](../README.md) › [Article 4 — Adult-use Cannabis](README.md)

```text
  § 74. Delivery license. A delivery license shall authorize the
delivery of cannabis and cannabis products by licensees independent of
another adult-use cannabis license, provided that each delivery licensee
may have a total of no more than twenty-five individuals, or the
equivalent thereof, providing full-time paid delivery services to
cannabis consumers per week under one license. For the purposes of this
section the state cannabis advisory board shall provide recommendations
to the board for the application process, license criteria, and scope of
licensed activities for this class of license. No person may have a
direct or indirect financial or controlling interest in more than one
delivery license. The granting of such licenses shall promote social and
economic equity applicants as provided for in this chapter.
```
