# Cannabis Law § 88 — Data collection and reporting

[Cannabis Law](../README.md) › [Article 4 — Adult-use Cannabis](README.md)

```text
  § 88. Data collection and reporting. The board shall collect
demographic data on owners and employees in the adult-use cannabis
industry and shall annually publish such data in its annual report.
```
