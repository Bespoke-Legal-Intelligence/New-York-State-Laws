# Cannabis Law § 106 — New York hemp product

[Cannabis Law](../README.md) › [Article 5 — Cannabinoid Hemp and Hemp Extract](README.md)

```text
  § 106. New York hemp product. The board may establish and adopt
official grades and standards for cannabinoid hemp, hemp extract and
products derived therefrom, as the board may deem advisable, which are
produced for sale in this state and, from time to time, may amend or
modify such grades and standards.
```
