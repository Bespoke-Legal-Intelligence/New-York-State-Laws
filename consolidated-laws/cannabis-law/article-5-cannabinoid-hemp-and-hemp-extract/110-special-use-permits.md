# Cannabis Law § 110 — Special use permits

[Cannabis Law](../README.md) › [Article 5 — Cannabinoid Hemp and Hemp Extract](README.md)

```text
  § 110. Special use permits. The board shall have the authority to
issue temporary permits for carrying on any activity related to
cannabinoid hemp, hemp extract and products derived therefrom, licensed
under this article. The board may set reasonable fees for such permits,
to establish the periods during which such permits are valid, and to
make rules and regulations to implement this section.
```
