# Cannabis Law § 138-A — Injunction for unlawful manufacturing, sale, or distribution of cannabis

[Cannabis Law](../README.md) › [Article 6 — General Provisions](README.md)

```text
  § 138-a. Injunction for unlawful manufacturing, sale, or distribution
of cannabis. The office of cannabis management shall have the authority
to request an injunction against any person who is unlawfully
cultivating, processing, distributing or selling cannabis in this state
without obtaining the appropriate registration, license, or permit
therefor, in accordance with this chapter and any applicable state law.
```
