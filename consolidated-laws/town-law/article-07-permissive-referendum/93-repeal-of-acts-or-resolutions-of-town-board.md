# Town Law § 93 — Repeal of acts or resolutions of town board

[Town Law](../README.md) › [Article 7 — Permissive Referendum](README.md)

```text
  § 93. Repeal of acts or resolutions of town board. Any act or
resolution of a town board may be rescinded or repealed at any time by
the town board and, in case the resolution so repealed be one subject to
a permissive referendum and a petition thereupon be filed, no further
proceedings shall be had thereunder and no referendum shall be held.
```
