# Town Law § 58 — Town manager

[Town Law](../README.md) › [Article 3-B — Town Manager](README.md)

```text
  § 58. Town manager. Any town may, by local law, establish the office
of town manager, provide for his appointment and delegate to such office
such powers and duties as may be prescribed, modified or revoked from
time to time by the town board.
```
