# Town Law § 28 — Power to administer oaths

[Town Law](../README.md) › [Article 3 — Town Officers, Powers, Duties and Compensation](README.md)

```text
  § 28. Power to administer oaths. Any town officer may administer any
necessary oath in any matter or proceeding lawfully before him, or in
connection with any paper to be filed with him as such officer.
```
