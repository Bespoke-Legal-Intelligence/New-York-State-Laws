# Town Law § 40 — Powers and duties of fence viewers

[Town Law](../README.md) › [Article 3 — Town Officers, Powers, Duties and Compensation](README.md)

```text
  § 40. Powers and duties of fence viewers. The assessors shall perform
all the duties and shall exercise all the powers hereinafter imposed and
conferred upon fence viewers. In a town having a sole appointed
assessor, the assessor and the members of the town board shall act as
fence viewers.
```
