# Town Law § 33 — Powers and duties of assessors

[Town Law](../README.md) › [Article 3 — Town Officers, Powers, Duties and Compensation](README.md)

```text
  § 33. Powers and duties of assessors. 1. The assessor or assessors
shall have such powers and shall perform such duties as are, or
hereafter may be, conferred or imposed by law.
  When required by the town board in a town of the first class, the
assessor or assessors shall keep such office hours for public
convenience as the town board shall determine.
```
