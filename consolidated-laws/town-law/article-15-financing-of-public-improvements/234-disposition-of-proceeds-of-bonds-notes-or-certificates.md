# Town Law § 234 — Disposition of proceeds of bonds, notes or certificates

[Town Law](../README.md) › [Article 15 — Financing of Public Improvements](README.md)

```text
  § 234. Disposition of proceeds of bonds, notes or certificates.  All
moneys derived from the sale of bonds or notes shall be kept as a
separate fund by the supervisor. Such moneys shall be disbursed only in
the manner provided by section one hundred nineteen of this chapter.
```
