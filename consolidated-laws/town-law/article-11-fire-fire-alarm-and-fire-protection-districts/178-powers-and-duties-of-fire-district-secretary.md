# Town Law § 178 — Powers and duties of fire district secretary

[Town Law](../README.md) › [Article 11 — Fire, Fire Alarm and Fire Protection Districts](README.md)

```text
  § 178. Powers and duties of fire district secretary. The fire district
secretary shall attend all meetings of the board of fire commissioners,
act as clerk thereof, and keep a complete and accurate record of the
proceedings of each meeting and of all propositions, rules and
regulations adopted pursuant to this article. The fire district
secretary shall have such additional powers and perform such additional
duties as the board of fire commissioners may determine, not
inconsistent with law.
```
