# Town Law § 340 — Laws repealed

[Town Law](../README.md) › [Article 19 — Laws Repealed; Temporary Provisions and Saving Clause](README.md)

```text
  § 340. Laws repealed. Chapter sixty-three of the laws of nineteen
hundred nine, entitled "An act relating to towns, constituting chapter
sixty-two of the consolidated laws," and all acts amendatory thereof and
supplemental thereto, constituting the town law as heretofore in force,
are hereby repealed, except as otherwise provided herein.
```
