# Town Law § 132 — Effect of town ordinances limited

[Town Law](../README.md) › [Article 9 — Ordinances and Licenses](README.md)

```text
  § 132. Effect of town ordinances limited. A rule, regulation or
ordinance of a town shall be effective and operative only in that
portion of such town outside of any incorporated village or city
therein, except as otherwise specifically provided by statute.
```
