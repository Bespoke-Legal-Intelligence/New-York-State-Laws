# Town Law § 131 — Definition of town ordinance

[Town Law](../README.md) › [Article 9 — Ordinances and Licenses](README.md)

```text
  § 131. Definition of town ordinance. A town ordinance includes also a
rule or regulation of the town board, for the violation of which a
penalty is imposed; and each provision of this chapter relating to the
enforcement of an ordinance applies to such rule or regulation.
```
