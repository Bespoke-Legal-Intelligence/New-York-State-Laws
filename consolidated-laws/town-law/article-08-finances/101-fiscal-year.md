# Town Law § 101 — Fiscal year

[Town Law](../README.md) › [Article 8 — Finances](README.md)

```text
  § 101. Fiscal year. The fiscal year of all towns shall begin on the
first day of January and end on the thirty-first day of December in each
year.
```
