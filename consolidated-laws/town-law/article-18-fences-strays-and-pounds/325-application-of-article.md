# Town Law § 325 — Application of article

[Town Law](../README.md) › [Article 18 — Fences, Strays and Pounds](README.md)

```text
  § 325. Application of article. The villages and cities of this state
shall be considered towns for the purpose of construing provisions of
this article relating to strays; and the trustees of the village and the
alderpersons, council members or members of the legislative body of the
city shall be fence viewers therein for such purposes.
```
