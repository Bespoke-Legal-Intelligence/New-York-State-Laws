# Town Law § 315 — Fees of fence viewers as to strays

[Town Law](../README.md) › [Article 18 — Fences, Strays and Pounds](README.md)

```text
  § 315. Fees of fence viewers as to strays. Each fence viewer shall be
entitled to receive ten cents for every mile he shall be obliged to
travel from his residence to the place where the beasts are kept, and
seventy-five cents for a certificate of the charges as ascertained by
them.
```
