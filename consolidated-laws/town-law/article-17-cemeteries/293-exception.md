# Town Law § 293 — Exception

[Town Law](../README.md) › [Article 17 — Cemeteries](README.md)

```text
  § 293. Exception. All provisions of the preceding sections of this
article which are inconsistent with the provisions of chapter
seventy-six of the laws of eighteen hundred sixty-nine, as amended by
chapter four hundred thirty-two of the laws of eighteen hundred
ninety-nine, shall not apply to Greenfield cemetery in Hempstead, Nassau
county, or to the trustees or management thereof.
```
