# Town Law § 190-F — Additional powers of sewer districts

[Town Law](../README.md) › [Article 12 — District and Special Improvements](README.md)

```text
  § 190-f. Additional powers of sewer districts. Notwithstanding any
other provision of law a sewer district may also exercise all the powers
of a wastewater disposal district if the map and plan prepared pursuant
to section one hundred ninety-c of this article includes on-site
wastewater disposal systems.
```
