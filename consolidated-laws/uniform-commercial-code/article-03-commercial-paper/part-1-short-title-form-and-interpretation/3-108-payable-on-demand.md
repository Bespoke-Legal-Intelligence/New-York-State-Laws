# Uniform Commercial Code § 3-108 — Payable on Demand

[Uniform Commercial Code](../../README.md) › [Article 3 — Commercial Paper](../README.md) › [Part 1 — Short Title, Form and Interpretation](README.md)

```text
Section 3--108. Payable on Demand.
  Instruments payable on demand include those payable at sight or on
presentation and those in which no time for payment is stated.
```
