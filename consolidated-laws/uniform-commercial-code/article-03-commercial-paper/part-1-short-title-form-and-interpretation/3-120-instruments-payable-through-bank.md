# Uniform Commercial Code § 3-120 — Instruments "Payable Through" Bank

[Uniform Commercial Code](../../README.md) › [Article 3 — Commercial Paper](../README.md) › [Part 1 — Short Title, Form and Interpretation](README.md)

```text
Section 3--120. Instruments "Payable Through" Bank.
  An instrument which states that it is "payable through" a bank or the
like designates that bank as a collecting bank to make presentment but
does not of itself authorize the bank to pay the instrument.
```
