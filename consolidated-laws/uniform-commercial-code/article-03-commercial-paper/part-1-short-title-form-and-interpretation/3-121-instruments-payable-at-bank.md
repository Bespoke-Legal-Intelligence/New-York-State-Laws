# Uniform Commercial Code § 3-121 — Instruments Payable at Bank

[Uniform Commercial Code](../../README.md) › [Article 3 — Commercial Paper](../README.md) › [Part 1 — Short Title, Form and Interpretation](README.md)

```text
Section 3--121. Instruments Payable at Bank.
  A note or acceptance which states that it is payable at a bank is the
equivalent of a draft drawn on the bank payable when it falls due out of
any funds of the maker or acceptor in current account or otherwise
available for such payment.
```
