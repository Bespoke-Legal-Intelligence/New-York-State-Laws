# Uniform Commercial Code § 3-402 — Signature in Ambiguous Capacity

[Uniform Commercial Code](../../README.md) › [Article 3 — Commercial Paper](../README.md) › [Part 4 — Liability of Parties](README.md)

```text
Section 3--402. Signature in Ambiguous Capacity.
  Unless the instrument clearly indicates that a signature is made in
some other capacity it is an indorsement.
```
