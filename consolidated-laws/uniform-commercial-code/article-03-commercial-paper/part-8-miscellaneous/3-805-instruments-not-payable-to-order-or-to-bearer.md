# Uniform Commercial Code § 3-805 — Instruments Not Payable to Order or to Bearer

[Uniform Commercial Code](../../README.md) › [Article 3 — Commercial Paper](../README.md) › [Part 8 — Miscellaneous](README.md)

```text
Section 3--805. Instruments Not Payable to Order or to Bearer.
  This Article applies to any instrument whose terms do not preclude
transfer and which is otherwise negotiable within this Article but which
is not payable to order or to bearer, except that there can be no holder
in due course of such an instrument.
```
