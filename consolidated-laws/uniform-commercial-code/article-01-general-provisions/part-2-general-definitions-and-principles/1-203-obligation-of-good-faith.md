# Uniform Commercial Code § 1-203 — Obligation of Good Faith

[Uniform Commercial Code](../../README.md) › [Article 1 — General Provisions](../README.md) › [Part 2 — General Definitions and Principles of Interpretation](README.md)

```text
Section 1--203. Obligation of Good Faith.
  Every contract or duty within this Act imposes an obligation of good
faith in its performance or enforcement.
```
