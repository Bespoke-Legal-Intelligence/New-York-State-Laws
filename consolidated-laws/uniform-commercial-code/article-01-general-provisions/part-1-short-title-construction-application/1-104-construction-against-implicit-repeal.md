# Uniform Commercial Code § 1-104 — Construction Against Implicit Repeal

[Uniform Commercial Code](../../README.md) › [Article 1 — General Provisions](../README.md) › [Part 1 — Short Title, Construction, Application and Subject Matter of the Act](README.md)

```text
Section 1--104. Construction Against Implicit Repeal.
  This Act being a general act intended as a unified coverage of its
subject matter, no part of it shall be deemed to be impliedly repealed
by subsequent legislation if such construction can reasonably be
avoided.
```
