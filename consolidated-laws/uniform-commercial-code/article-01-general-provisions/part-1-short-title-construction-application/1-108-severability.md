# Uniform Commercial Code § 1-108 — Severability

[Uniform Commercial Code](../../README.md) › [Article 1 — General Provisions](../README.md) › [Part 1 — Short Title, Construction, Application and Subject Matter of the Act](README.md)

```text
Section 1--108. Severability.
  If any provision or clause of this Act or application thereof to any
person or circumstances is held invalid, such invalidity shall not
affect other provisions or applications of the Act which can be given
effect without the invalid provision or application, and to this end the
provisions of this Act are declared to be severable.
```
