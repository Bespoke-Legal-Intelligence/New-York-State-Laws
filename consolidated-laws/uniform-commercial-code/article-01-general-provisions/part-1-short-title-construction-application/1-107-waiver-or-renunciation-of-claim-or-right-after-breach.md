# Uniform Commercial Code § 1-107 — Waiver or Renunciation of Claim or Right After Breach

[Uniform Commercial Code](../../README.md) › [Article 1 — General Provisions](../README.md) › [Part 1 — Short Title, Construction, Application and Subject Matter of the Act](README.md)

```text
Section 1--107. Waiver or Renunciation of Claim or Right After Breach.
  Any claim or right arising out of an alleged breach can be discharged
in whole or in part without consideration by a written waiver or
renunciation signed and delivered by the aggrieved party.
```
