# Uniform Commercial Code § 11-108 — Presumption that Rule of Law Continues Unchanged

[Uniform Commercial Code](../README.md) › [Article 11 — Provisions For Transition From Original Article 9 to Revised Article](README.md)

```text
Section 11--108. Presumption that Rule of Law Continues Unchanged.
  Unless a change in law has clearly been made, the provisions of
Revised Article 9 shall be deemed declaratory of the meaning of Original
Article 9.
```
