# Uniform Commercial Code § 11-107 — Transition Provisions as to Priorities

[Uniform Commercial Code](../README.md) › [Article 11 — Provisions For Transition From Original Article 9 to Revised Article](README.md)

```text
Section 11--107. Transition Provisions as to Priorities.
  Except as otherwise provided in this Article, Original Article 9 shall
apply to any questions of priority if the positions of the parties were
fixed prior to the effective date of Revised Article 9. In other cases
questions of priority shall be determined by Revised Article 9.
```
