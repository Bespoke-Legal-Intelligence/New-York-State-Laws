# Uniform Commercial Code § 4-206 — Transfer Between Banks

[Uniform Commercial Code](../../README.md) › [Article 4 — Bank Deposits and Collections](../README.md) › [Part 2 — Collection of Items: Depositary and Collecting Banks](README.md)

```text
Section 4--206. Transfer Between Banks.
  Any agreed method which identifies the transferor bank is sufficient
for the item's further transfer to another bank.
```
