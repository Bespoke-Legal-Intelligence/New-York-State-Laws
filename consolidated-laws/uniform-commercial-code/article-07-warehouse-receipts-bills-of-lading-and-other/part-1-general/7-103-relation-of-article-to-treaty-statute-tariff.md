# Uniform Commercial Code § 7-103 — Relation of Article to Treaty, Statute, Tariff, Classification or Regulation

[Uniform Commercial Code](../../README.md) › [Article 7 — Warehouse Receipts, Bills of Lading and Other Documents of Title](../README.md) › [Part 1 — General](README.md)

```text
Section 7--103. Relation of Article to Treaty, Statute, Tariff,
                  Classification or Regulation.
  To the extent that any treaty or statute of the United States,
regulatory statute of this State or tariff, classification or regulation
filed or issued pursuant thereto is applicable, the provisions of this
Article are subject thereto.
```
