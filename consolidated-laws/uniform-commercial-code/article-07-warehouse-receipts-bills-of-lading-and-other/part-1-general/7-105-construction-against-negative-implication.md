# Uniform Commercial Code § 7-105 — Construction Against Negative Implication

[Uniform Commercial Code](../../README.md) › [Article 7 — Warehouse Receipts, Bills of Lading and Other Documents of Title](../README.md) › [Part 1 — General](README.md)

```text
Section 7--105. Construction Against Negative Implication.
  The omission from either Part 2 or Part 3 of this Article of a
provision corresponding to a provision made in the other Part does not
imply that a corresponding rule of law is not applicable.
```
