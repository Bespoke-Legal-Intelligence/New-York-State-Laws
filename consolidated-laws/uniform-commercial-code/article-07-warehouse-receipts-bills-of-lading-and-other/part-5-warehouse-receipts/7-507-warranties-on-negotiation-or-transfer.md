# Uniform Commercial Code § 7-507 — Warranties on Negotiation or Transfer of Receipt or Bill

[Uniform Commercial Code](../../README.md) › [Article 7 — Warehouse Receipts, Bills of Lading and Other Documents of Title](../README.md) › [Part 5 — Warehouse Receipts and Bills of Lading: Negotiation and Transfer](README.md)

```text
Section 7--507. Warranties on Negotiation or Transfer of Receipt or
                  Bill.
  Where a person negotiates or transfers a document of title for value
otherwise than as a mere intermediary under the next following section,
then unless otherwise agreed he warrants to his immediate purchaser only
in addition to any warranty made in selling the goods
  (a)  that the document is genuine; and
  (b)  that he has no knowledge of any fact which would impair its
validity or worth; and
  (c)  that his negotiation or transfer is rightful and fully effective
with respect to the title to the document and the goods it represents.
```
