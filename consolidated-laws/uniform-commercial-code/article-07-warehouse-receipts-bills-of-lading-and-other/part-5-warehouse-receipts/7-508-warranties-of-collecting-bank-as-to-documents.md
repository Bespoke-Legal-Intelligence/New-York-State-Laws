# Uniform Commercial Code § 7-508 — Warranties of Collecting Bank as to Documents

[Uniform Commercial Code](../../README.md) › [Article 7 — Warehouse Receipts, Bills of Lading and Other Documents of Title](../README.md) › [Part 5 — Warehouse Receipts and Bills of Lading: Negotiation and Transfer](README.md)

```text
Section 7--508. Warranties of Collecting Bank as to Documents.
  A collecting bank or other intermediary known to be entrusted with
documents on behalf of another or with collection of a draft or other
claim against delivery of documents warrants by such delivery of the
documents only its own good faith and authority. This rule applies even
though the intermediary has purchased or made advances against the claim
or draft to be collected.
```
