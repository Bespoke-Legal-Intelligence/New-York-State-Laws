# Uniform Commercial Code § 2-301 — General Obligations of Parties

[Uniform Commercial Code](../../README.md) › [Article 2 — Sales](../README.md) › [Part 3 — General Obligation and Construction of Contract](README.md)

```text
Section 2--301. General Obligations of Parties.
  The obligation of the seller is to transfer and deliver and that of
the buyer is to accept and pay in accordance with the contract.
```
