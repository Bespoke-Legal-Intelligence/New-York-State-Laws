# Uniform Commercial Code § 2-717 — Deduction of Damages From the Price

[Uniform Commercial Code](../../README.md) › [Article 2 — Sales](../README.md) › [Part 7 — Remedies](README.md)

```text
Section 2--717. Deduction of Damages From the Price.
  The buyer on notifying the seller of his intention to do so may deduct
all or any part of the damages resulting from any breach of the contract
from any part of the price still due under the same contract.
```
