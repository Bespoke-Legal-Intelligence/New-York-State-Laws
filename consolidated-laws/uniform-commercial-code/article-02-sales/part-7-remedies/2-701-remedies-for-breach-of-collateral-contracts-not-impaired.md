# Uniform Commercial Code § 2-701 — Remedies for Breach of Collateral Contracts Not Impaired

[Uniform Commercial Code](../../README.md) › [Article 2 — Sales](../README.md) › [Part 7 — Remedies](README.md)

```text
Section 2--701. Remedies for Breach of Collateral Contracts Not
                  Impaired.
  Remedies for breach of any obligation or promise collateral or
ancillary to a contract for sale are not impaired by the provisions of
this Article.
```
