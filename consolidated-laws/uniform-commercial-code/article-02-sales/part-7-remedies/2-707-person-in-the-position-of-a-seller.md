# Uniform Commercial Code § 2-707 — "Person in the Position of a Seller"

[Uniform Commercial Code](../../README.md) › [Article 2 — Sales](../README.md) › [Part 7 — Remedies](README.md)

```text
Section 2--707. "Person in the Position of a Seller".
  (1) A "person in the position of a seller" includes as against a
principal an agent who has paid or become responsible for the price of
goods on behalf of his principal or anyone who otherwise holds a
security interest or other right in goods similar to that of a seller.
  (2) A person in the position of a seller may as provided in this
Article withhold or stop delivery (Section 2--705) and resell (Section
2--706) and recover incidental damages (Section 2--710).
```
