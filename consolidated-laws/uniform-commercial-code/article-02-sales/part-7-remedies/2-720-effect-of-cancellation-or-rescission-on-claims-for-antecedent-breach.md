# Uniform Commercial Code § 2-720 — Effect of "Cancellation" or "Rescission" on Claims for Antecedent Breach

[Uniform Commercial Code](../../README.md) › [Article 2 — Sales](../README.md) › [Part 7 — Remedies](README.md)

```text
Section 2--720. Effect of "Cancellation" or "Rescission" on Claims for
                  Antecedent Breach.
  Unless the contrary intention clearly appears, expressions of
"cancellation" or "rescission" of the contract or the like shall not be
construed as a renunciation or discharge of any claim in damages for an
antecedent breach.
```
