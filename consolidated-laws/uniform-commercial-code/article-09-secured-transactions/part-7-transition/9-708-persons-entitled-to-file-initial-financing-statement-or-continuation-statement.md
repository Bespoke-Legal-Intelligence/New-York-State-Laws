# Uniform Commercial Code § 9-708 — Persons Entitled to File Initial Financing Statement or Continuation Statement

[Uniform Commercial Code](../../README.md) › [Article 9 — Secured Transactions](../README.md) › [Part 7 — Transition](README.md)

```text
Section 9--708. Persons Entitled to File Initial Financing Statement or
                  Continuation Statement.
  A person may file an initial financing statement or a continuation
statement under this part if:
  (a) the secured party of record authorizes the filing; and
  (b) the filing is necessary under this part:
       (1) to continue the effectiveness of a financing statement filed
           before Revised Article 9 takes effect; or
       (2) to perfect or continue the perfection of a security interest.
```
