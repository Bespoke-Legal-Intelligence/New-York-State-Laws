# Uniform Commercial Code § 9-402 — Secured Party Not Obligated on Contract of Debtor or in Tort

[Uniform Commercial Code](../../README.md) › [Article 9 — Secured Transactions](../README.md) › [Part 4 — Rights of Third Parties](README.md)

```text
Section 9--402. Secured Party Not Obligated on Contract of Debtor or in
                  Tort.
  The existence of a security interest, agricultural lien, or authority
given to a debtor to dispose of or use collateral, without more, does
not subject a secured party to liability in contract or tort for the
debtor's acts or omissions.
```
