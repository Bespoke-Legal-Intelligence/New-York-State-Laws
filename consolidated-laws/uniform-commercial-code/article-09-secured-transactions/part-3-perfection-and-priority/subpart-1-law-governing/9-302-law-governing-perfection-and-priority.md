# Uniform Commercial Code § 9-302 — Law Governing Perfection and Priority of Agricultural Liens

[Uniform Commercial Code](../../../README.md) › [Article 9 — Secured Transactions](../../README.md) › [Part 3 — Perfection and Priority](../README.md) › [Subpart 1 — Law Governing Perfection and Priority](README.md)

```text
Section 9--302. Law Governing Perfection and Priority of Agricultural
                  Liens.
  While farm products are located in a jurisdiction, the local law of
that jurisdiction governs perfection, the effect of perfection or
nonperfection, and the priority of an agricultural lien on the farm
products.
```
