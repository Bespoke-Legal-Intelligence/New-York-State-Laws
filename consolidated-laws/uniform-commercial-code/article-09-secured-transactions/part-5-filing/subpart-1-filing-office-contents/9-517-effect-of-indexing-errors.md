# Uniform Commercial Code § 9-517 — Effect of Indexing Errors

[Uniform Commercial Code](../../../README.md) › [Article 9 — Secured Transactions](../../README.md) › [Part 5 — Filing](../README.md) › [Subpart 1 — Filing Office; Contents and Effectiveness of Financing Statement](README.md)

```text
Section 9--517. Effect of Indexing Errors.
  The failure of the filing office to index a record correctly does not
affect the effectiveness of the filed record.
```
