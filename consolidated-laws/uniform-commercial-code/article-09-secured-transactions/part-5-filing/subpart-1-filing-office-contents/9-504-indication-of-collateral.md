# Uniform Commercial Code § 9-504 — Indication of Collateral

[Uniform Commercial Code](../../../README.md) › [Article 9 — Secured Transactions](../../README.md) › [Part 5 — Filing](../README.md) › [Subpart 1 — Filing Office; Contents and Effectiveness of Financing Statement](README.md)

```text
Section 9--504. Indication of Collateral.
  A financing statement sufficiently indicates the collateral that it
covers if the financing statement provides:
  (1) a description of the collateral pursuant to Section 9--108; or
  (2) an indication that the financing statement covers all assets or
all personal property.
```
