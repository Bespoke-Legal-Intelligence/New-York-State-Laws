# Uniform Commercial Code § 2-A-301 — Enforceability of Lease Contract

[Uniform Commercial Code](../../README.md) › [Article 2-A — Leases](../README.md) › [Part 3 — Effect of Lease Contract](README.md)

```text
Section 2-A-301. Enforceability of Lease Contract.
  Except as otherwise provided in this Article, a lease contract is
effective and enforceable according to its terms between the parties,
against purchasers of the goods, and against creditors of the parties.
```
