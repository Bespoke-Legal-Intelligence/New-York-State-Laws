# Uniform Commercial Code § 2-A-302 — Title to and Possession of Goods

[Uniform Commercial Code](../../README.md) › [Article 2-A — Leases](../README.md) › [Part 3 — Effect of Lease Contract](README.md)

```text
Section 2-A-302. Title to and Possession of Goods.
  Except as otherwise provided in this Article, each provision of this
Article applies whether the lessor or a third party has title to the
goods, and whether the lessor, the lessee, or a third party has
possession of the goods, notwithstanding any statute or rule of law that
possession or the absence of possession is fraudulent.
```
