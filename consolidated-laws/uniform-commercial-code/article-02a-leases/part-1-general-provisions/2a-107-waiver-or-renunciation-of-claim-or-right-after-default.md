# Uniform Commercial Code § 2-A-107 — Waiver or Renunciation of Claim or Right After Default

[Uniform Commercial Code](../../README.md) › [Article 2-A — Leases](../README.md) › [Part 1 — General Provisions](README.md)

```text
Section 2-A-107. Waiver or Renunciation of Claim or Right After Default.
  Any claim or right arising out of an alleged default or breach of
warranty may be discharged in whole or in part without consideration by
a waiver or renunciation in a signed record delivered by the aggrieved
party.
```
