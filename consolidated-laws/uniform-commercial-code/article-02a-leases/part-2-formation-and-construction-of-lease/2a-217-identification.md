# Uniform Commercial Code § 2-A-217 — Identification

[Uniform Commercial Code](../../README.md) › [Article 2-A — Leases](../README.md) › [Part 2 — Formation and Construction of Lease Contract](README.md)

```text
Section 2-A-217. Identification.
  Identification of goods as goods to which a lease contract refers may
be made at any time and in any manner explicitly agreed to by the
parties. In the absence of explicit agreement, identification occurs:
  (1) when the lease contract is made if the lease contract is for a
lease of goods that are existing and identified;
  (2) when the goods are shipped, marked, or otherwise designated by the
lessor as goods to which the lease contract refers, if the lease
contract is for a lease of goods that are not existing and identified;
or
  (3) when the young are conceived, if the lease contract is for a lease
of unborn young of animals.
```
