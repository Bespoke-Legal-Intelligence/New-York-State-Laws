# Uniform Commercial Code § 2-A-203 — Seals Inoperative

[Uniform Commercial Code](../../README.md) › [Article 2-A — Leases](../README.md) › [Part 2 — Formation and Construction of Lease Contract](README.md)

```text
Section 2-A-203. Seals Inoperative.
  The affixing of a seal to a record evidencing a lease contract or an
offer to enter into a lease contract does not render the record a sealed
instrument and the law with respect to sealed instruments does not apply
to the lease contract or offer.
```
