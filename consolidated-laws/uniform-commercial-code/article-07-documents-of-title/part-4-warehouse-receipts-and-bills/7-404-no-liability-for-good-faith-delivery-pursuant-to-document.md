# Uniform Commercial Code § 7-404 — No Liability for Good-Faith Delivery Pursuant to Document of Title

[Uniform Commercial Code](../../README.md) › [Article 7 — Documents of Title](../README.md) › [Part 4 — Warehouse Receipts and Bills of Lading: General Obligations](README.md)

```text
Section 7--404. No Liability for Good-Faith Delivery Pursuant to
                  Document of Title.
  A bailee that in good faith has received goods and delivered or
otherwise disposed of the goods according to the terms of a document of
title or pursuant to this article is not liable for the goods even if:
  (1) the person from which the bailee received the goods did not have
authority to procure the document or to dispose of the goods; or
  (2) the person to which the bailee delivered the goods did not have
authority to receive the goods.
```
