# Uniform Commercial Code § 7-505 — Indorser Not Guarantor for Other Parties

[Uniform Commercial Code](../../README.md) › [Article 7 — Documents of Title](../README.md) › [Part 5 — Warehouse Receipts and Bills of Lading: Negotiation and Transfer](README.md)

```text
Section 7--505. Indorser Not Guarantor for Other Parties.
  The indorsement of a tangible document of title issued by a bailee
does not make the indorser liable for any default by the bailee or
previous indorsers.
```
