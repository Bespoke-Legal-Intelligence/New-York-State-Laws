# Uniform Commercial Code § 7-306 — Altered Bills of Lading

[Uniform Commercial Code](../../README.md) › [Article 7 — Documents of Title](../README.md) › [Part 3 — Bills of Lading: Special Provisions](README.md)

```text
Section 7--306. Altered Bills of Lading.
  An unauthorized alteration or filling in of a blank in a bill of
lading leaves the bill enforceable according to its original tenor.
```
