# Uniform Commercial Code § 5-104 — Formal requirements

[Uniform Commercial Code](../README.md) › [Article 5 — Letters of Credit](README.md)

```text
Section 5--104. Formal requirements.
  A letter of credit, confirmation, advice, transfer, amendment, or
cancellation may be issued in any form that is a record and is
authenticated:
  (a) by a signature, or
  (b) in accordance with the agreement of the parties or the standard
practice referred to in subsection (e) of section 5--108.
```
