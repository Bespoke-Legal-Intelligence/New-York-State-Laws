# Uniform Commercial Code § 5-105 — Consideration

[Uniform Commercial Code](../README.md) › [Article 5 — Letters of Credit](README.md)

```text
Section 5--105. Consideration.
  Consideration is not required to issue, amend, transfer, or cancel a
letter of credit, advice, or confirmation.
```
