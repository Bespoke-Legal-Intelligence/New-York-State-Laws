# Uniform Commercial Code § 1-304 — Obligation of Good Faith

[Uniform Commercial Code](../../README.md) › [Article 1 — No title](../README.md) › [Part 3 — Territorial Applicability and General Rules](README.md)

```text
Section 1--304. Obligation of Good Faith.
  Every contract or duty within this act imposes an obligation of good
faith in its performance and enforcement.
```
