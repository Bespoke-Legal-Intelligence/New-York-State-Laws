# Uniform Commercial Code § 1-306 — Waiver or Renunciation of Claim or Right After Breach

[Uniform Commercial Code](../../README.md) › [Article 1 — No title](../README.md) › [Part 3 — Territorial Applicability and General Rules](README.md)

```text
Section 1--306. Waiver or Renunciation of Claim or Right After Breach.
  A claim or right arising out of an alleged breach may be discharged in
whole or in part without consideration by agreement of the aggrieved
party in a signed record.
```
