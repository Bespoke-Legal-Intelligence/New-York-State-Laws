# Uniform Commercial Code § 1-308 — Performance or Acceptance Under Reservation of Rights

[Uniform Commercial Code](../../README.md) › [Article 1 — No title](../README.md) › [Part 3 — Territorial Applicability and General Rules](README.md)

```text
Section 1--308. Performance or Acceptance Under Reservation of Rights.
  A party that with explicit reservation of rights performs or promises
performance or assents to performance in a manner demanded or offered by
the other party does not thereby prejudice the rights reserved. Such
words as "without prejudice," "under protest," or the like are
sufficient.
```
