# Uniform Commercial Code § 1-108 — Relation to Electronic Signatures in Global and National Commerce Act

[Uniform Commercial Code](../../README.md) › [Article 1 — No title](../README.md) › [Part 1 — General Provisions](README.md)

```text
Section 1--108. Relation to Electronic Signatures in Global and National
                 Commerce Act.
  This article modifies, limits, and supersedes the federal Electronic
Signatures in Global and National Commerce Act, 15 U.S.C. Section 7001
et seq., except that nothing in this article modifies, limits, or
supersedes Section 7001(c) of that act or authorizes electronic delivery
of any of the notices described in Section 7003(b) of that act.
```
