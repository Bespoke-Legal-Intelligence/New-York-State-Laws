# Uniform Commercial Code § 1-102 — Scope of Article

[Uniform Commercial Code](../../README.md) › [Article 1 — No title](../README.md) › [Part 1 — General Provisions](README.md)

```text
Section 1--102. Scope of Article.
  This article applies to a transaction to the extent that it is
governed by another article of this act.
```
