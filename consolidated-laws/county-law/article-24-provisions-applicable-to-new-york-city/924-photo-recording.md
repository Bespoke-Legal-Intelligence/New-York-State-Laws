# County Law § 924 — Photo recording

[County Law](../README.md) › [Article 24 — Provisions Applicable to New York City](README.md)

```text
  § 924. Photo recording. The county clerks of the counties within the
city of New York may cause parts of documents to be separated in order
to facilitate recording and copying by use of a photo copying process,
provided that such documents are restored to their original form
immediately after the completion of such process.
```
