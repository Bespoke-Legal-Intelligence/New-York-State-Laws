# County Law § 914 — Duties of deputy county clerks

[County Law](../README.md) › [Article 24 — Provisions Applicable to New York City](README.md)

```text
  § 914. Duties of deputy county clerks. The deputy county clerks of
each of the counties comprising the city of New York shall, subject to
the provisions of the public officers law, have all the powers and
fulfill all the duties of the county clerk in his absence and shall be
subject to the same obligations and conditions prescribed for the county
clerk while he is performing his duties.
```
