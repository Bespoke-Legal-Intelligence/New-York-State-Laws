# County Law § 940 — Commissioner of finance as trustee of cemetery lots

[County Law](../README.md) › [Article 24 — Provisions Applicable to New York City](README.md)

```text
  § 940. Commissioner of finance as trustee of cemetery lots.  The
commissioner of finance of the city of New York shall perform the duties
as a trustee of cemetery lots in the same manner as imposed on county
treasurers by the provisions of this chapter.
```
