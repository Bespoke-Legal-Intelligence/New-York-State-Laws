# County Law § 934 — Cost of removed criminal trials

[County Law](../README.md) › [Article 24 — Provisions Applicable to New York City](README.md)

```text
  § 934.  Cost of removed criminal trials.  Each respective county of
New York, Bronx, Kings, Queens and Richmond in which an indictment is
found shall reimburse the county to which such indictment may be removed
for trial for the cost and expenses of such trial, in the same manner as
other counties are made liable under the provisions of this chapter.
```
