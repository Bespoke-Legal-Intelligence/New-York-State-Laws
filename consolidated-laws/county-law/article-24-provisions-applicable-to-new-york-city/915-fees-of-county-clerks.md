# County Law § 915 — Fees of county clerks

[County Law](../README.md) › [Article 24 — Provisions Applicable to New York City](README.md)

```text
  § 915. Fees of county clerks. All moneys to which the clerks of the
counties comprising the city of New York are entitled, by virtue of
their office, or which they may receive for any and all official
services rendered by them, or by any of their assistants, clerks,
employees, or subordinates shall belong to and be for the benefit of the
city of New York, and shall be collected by such clerks and accounted
for and paid over on the first day of each month into the treasury of
the city of New York.
```
