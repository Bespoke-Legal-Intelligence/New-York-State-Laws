# County Law § 904 — Court and trust fund register and liability of officers

[County Law](../README.md) › [Article 24 — Provisions Applicable to New York City](README.md)

```text
  § 904. Court and trust fund register and liability of officers.  The
county clerks of the counties comprising the city of New York shall
perform the same duties and shall be subject to the same penalties
imposed by the provisions of this chapter upon other county clerks of
the state with relation to court and trust fund registers and the
delivery of property or moneys to the commissioner of finance in
pursuance of any judgment, decree or order of a court of record of this
state.
```
