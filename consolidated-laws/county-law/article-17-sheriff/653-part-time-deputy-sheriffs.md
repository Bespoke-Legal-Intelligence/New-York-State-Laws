# County Law § 653 — Part time deputy sheriffs

[County Law](../README.md) › [Article 17 — Sheriff](README.md)

```text
  § 653. Part time deputy sheriffs. Within the limits of the
appropriation, the sheriff may employ such number of part time deputies,
as he may deem necessary, who shall be paid a salary or on a per diem
basis as the board of supervisors may determine. Such deputies shall be
appointed in the same manner as regular deputies and shall perform only
those duties and powers authorized by such sheriff. Whenever deemed
necessary, a sheriff may deputize persons to serve civil process and
execute civil mandates.
```
