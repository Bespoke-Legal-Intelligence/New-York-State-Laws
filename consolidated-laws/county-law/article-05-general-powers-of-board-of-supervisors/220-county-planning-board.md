# County Law § 220 — County planning board

[County Law](../README.md) › [Article 5 — General Powers of Board of Supervisors](README.md)

```text
  § 220. County planning board. The board of supervisors is empowered to
establish a county planning board pursuant to article twelve-b of the
general municipal law and to make the necessary appropriations therefor.
```
