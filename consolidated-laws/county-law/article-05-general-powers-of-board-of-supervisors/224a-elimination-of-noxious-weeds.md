# County Law § 224-A — Elimination of noxious weeds

[County Law](../README.md) › [Article 5 — General Powers of Board of Supervisors](README.md)

```text
  § 224-a. Elimination of noxious weeds. The board of supervisors of any
county may appropriate and expend such sums as it may deem proper for
the elimination of noxious weeds within the county.
  The moneys hereby authorized to be appropriated and spent may be
expended in such manner as the board of supervisors may determine,
including personal service, maintenance and operation, or by contract or
contracts for the purposes herein enumerated.
```
