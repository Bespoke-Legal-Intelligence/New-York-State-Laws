# County Law § 229 — Erection and dissolution of towns

[County Law](../README.md) › [Article 5 — General Powers of Board of Supervisors](README.md)

```text
  § 229. Erection and dissolution of towns. The board of supervisors
shall perform the duties prescribed by the town law relating to the
erection of new towns and the dissolution of towns.
```
