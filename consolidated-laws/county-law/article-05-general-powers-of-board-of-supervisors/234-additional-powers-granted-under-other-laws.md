# County Law § 234 — Additional powers granted under other laws

[County Law](../README.md) › [Article 5 — General Powers of Board of Supervisors](README.md)

```text
  § 234. Additional powers granted under other laws. Nothing herein
shall be deemed to affect or impair the provisions of any other act of
the legislature granting additional powers to appropriate and expend
county funds for county objects and purposes.
```
