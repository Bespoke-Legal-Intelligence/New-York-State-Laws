# County Law § 237 — Alcoholic beverage control

[County Law](../README.md) › [Article 5 — General Powers of Board of Supervisors](README.md)

```text
  § 237. Alcoholic beverage control. Pursuant to section seventeen of
the alcoholic beverage control law, the board of supervisors by
resolution may request the state liquor authority to further restrict
the hours during which alcoholic beverages may be sold at retail.
```
