# County Law § 217-A — Qualification for employment as a county correction officer

[County Law](../README.md) › [Article 5 — General Powers of Board of Supervisors](README.md)

```text
  § 217-a. Qualification for employment as a county correction officer.
A county may adopt the provisions contained in section twenty-two-a of
the correction law relating to qualifications of its officials who may
thereafter be appointed in a law enforcement capacity in any of its
penal correctional institutions. Any determination that would otherwise
be made by the commissioner or his or her designee of the department of
corrections and community supervision under the provisions of section
twenty-two-a of the correction law, shall, if such provisions are so
adopted, be made by the appointing authority for such officials.
```
