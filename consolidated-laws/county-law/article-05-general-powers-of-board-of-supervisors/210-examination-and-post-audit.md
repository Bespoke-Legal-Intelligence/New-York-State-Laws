# County Law § 210 — Examination and post audit

[County Law](../README.md) › [Article 5 — General Powers of Board of Supervisors](README.md)

```text
  § 210. Examination and post audit. The board of supervisors may at any
time cause an examination and post audit to be made of the books,
records and papers pertaining to the money, funds or other property
belonging to the county, or over which the county shall have control.
Such examination and post audit may be made by a standing or special
committee or by auditors employed for the purpose.
```
