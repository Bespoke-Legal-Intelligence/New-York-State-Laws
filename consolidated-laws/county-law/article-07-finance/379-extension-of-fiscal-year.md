# County Law § 379 — Extension of fiscal year

[County Law](../README.md) › [Article 7 — Finance](README.md)

```text
  § 379. Extension of fiscal year. The fiscal year of each county
expiring prior to December thirty-first, nineteen hundred fifty, shall
be and hereby is extended to include such December thirty-first. Nothing
in this article contained shall invalidate any contract or expenditure
for a lawful purpose entered into or made during the period of such
extension.
```
