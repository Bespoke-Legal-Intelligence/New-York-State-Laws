# County Law § 377 — Use of mechanical checksigner

[County Law](../README.md) › [Article 7 — Finance](README.md)

```text
  § 377. Use of mechanical checksigner. Each board of supervisors shall
have power to authorize checks drawn upon county funds to be signed with
the facsimile signature of the officer or officers authorized by law to
sign the same.
```
