# County Law § 350 — Application of article

[County Law](../README.md) › [Article 7 — Finance](README.md)

```text
  § 350. Application of article. This article shall apply to every
county except a county wholly contained within a city and except a
county containing a population of four hundred thousand or more as shown
by the federal census of nineteen hundred fifty.
```
