# County Law § 364 — Unencumbered balances

[County Law](../README.md) › [Article 7 — Finance](README.md)

```text
  § 364. Unencumbered balances. The board of supervisors by resolution
pursuant to section three hundred sixty-three may at any time
reappropriate for any lawful purpose all or any part of the unencumbered
balance of an existing appropriation.
```
