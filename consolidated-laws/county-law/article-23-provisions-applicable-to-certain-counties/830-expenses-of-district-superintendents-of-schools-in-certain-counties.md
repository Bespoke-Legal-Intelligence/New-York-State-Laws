# County Law § 830 — Expenses of district superintendents of schools in certain counties

[County Law](../README.md) › [Article 23 — Provisions Applicable to Certain Counties](README.md)

```text
  § 830. Expenses of district superintendents of schools in certain
counties. In the counties of Westchester, Suffolk, Monroe and Erie, the
board of supervisors may appropriate and provide for the payment upon
verified vouchers of expenses of the district superintendent of schools
therein for furniture, printing, office supplies, telephone service,
clerical assistance, or such other expenditures as may be authorized by
such board.
```
