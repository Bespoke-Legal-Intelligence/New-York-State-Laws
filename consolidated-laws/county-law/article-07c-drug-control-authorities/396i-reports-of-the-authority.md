# County Law § 396-I — Reports of the authority

[County Law](../README.md) › [Article 7-C — Drug Control Authorities](README.md)

```text
  § 396-i. Reports of the authority. The authority shall make an annual
report to the county legislature. When it deems advisable, the authority
may make interim reports to the county legislature with its
recommendations, in order to afford the county legislature an
opportunity to take immediate action thereon.
```
