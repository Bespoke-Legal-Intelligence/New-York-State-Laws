# County Law § 150 — Board of supervisors constituted

[County Law](../README.md) › [Article 4 — Board of Supervisors](README.md)

```text
  § 150. Board of supervisors constituted.  The supervisors of the
several cities and towns in each county, when lawfully convened, shall
constitute the board of supervisors of the county.
```
