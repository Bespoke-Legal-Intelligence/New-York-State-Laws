# County Law § 299-V — Jurisdiction of state agencies not affected

[County Law](../README.md) › [Article 5-D — County Small Watershed Protection Districts](README.md)

```text
  § 299-v. Jurisdiction of state agencies not affected. No provision of
this article shall be deemed to alter, repeal or modify the powers and
duties of the state departments of conservation, health and
transportation, the state water resources commission, the water
pollution and control board, and the state soil conservation committee.
```
