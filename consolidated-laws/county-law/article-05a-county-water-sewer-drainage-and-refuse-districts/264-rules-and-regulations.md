# County Law § 264 — Rules and regulations

[County Law](../README.md) › [Article 5-A — County Water, Sewer, Drainage and Refuse Districts](README.md)

```text
  § 264. Rules and regulations. The board of supervisors shall have
power to adopt, amend and repeal, from time to time, rules and
regulations for the operation of a county district and the use of water
in a water district including regulation of the manner of making
connections and the construction of the county system and all facilities
and appurtenances.
```
