# County Law § 276 — Jurisdiction of other state agencies

[County Law](../README.md) › [Article 5-A — County Water, Sewer, Drainage and Refuse Districts](README.md)

```text
  § 276. Jurisdiction of other state agencies. Nothing contained in this
article shall be held to alter or abridge the powers and duties of the
state department of health, the water pollution control board, or any
other state department or agency over district matters.
```
