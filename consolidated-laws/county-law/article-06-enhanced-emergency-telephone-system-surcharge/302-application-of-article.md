# County Law § 302 — Application of article

[County Law](../README.md) › [Article 6 — Enhanced Emergency Telephone System Surcharge](README.md)

```text
  § 302. Application of article. This article shall apply to every
county except a county wholly contained within a city and every city
having a population of a million or more persons.
```
