# County Law § 104 — Submission of proposition or question

[County Law](../README.md) › [Article 3 — Referendum](README.md)

```text
  § 104. Submission of proposition or question. Except as provided by
the municipal home rule law, a proposition or question required by or
pursuant to law to be approved by the qualified electors of the county
shall be submitted in the manner provided by section one hundred two of
this article.
```
