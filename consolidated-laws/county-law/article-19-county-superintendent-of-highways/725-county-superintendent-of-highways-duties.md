# County Law § 725 — County superintendent of highways; duties

[County Law](../README.md) › [Article 19 — County Superintendent of Highways](README.md)

```text
  § 725. County superintendent of highways; duties. The county
superintendent of highways shall perform the duties prescribed by law
relating to the construction, improvement and maintenance of highways
and bridges in the county. He shall perform such additional and related
duties as may be prescribed by law and directed by the board of
supervisors.
```
