# County Law § 50 — When county divided or boundaries altered

[County Law](../README.md) › [Article 2 — General Provisions](README.md)

```text
  § 50. When county divided or boundaries altered. Whenever the
legislature divides a county or alters its boundaries, the act shall
provide the method and manner of the disposition of assets and
apportionment of liabilities of such county or counties.
```
