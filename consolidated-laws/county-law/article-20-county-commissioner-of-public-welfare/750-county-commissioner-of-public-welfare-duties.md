# County Law § 750 — County commissioner of public welfare; duties

[County Law](../README.md) › [Article 20 — County Commissioner of Public Welfare](README.md)

```text
  § 750. County commissioner of public welfare; duties. The county
commissioner of public welfare shall perform the duties prescribed by
law in the administration of welfare assistance.  He shall perform such
additional and related duties as may be prescribed by law and directed
by the board of supervisors.
```
