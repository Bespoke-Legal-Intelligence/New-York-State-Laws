# County Law § 411 — Holding more than one elective office

[County Law](../README.md) › [Article 8 — Officers; General Provisions](README.md)

```text
  § 411. Holding more than one elective office. No county judge, family
court judge, surrogate, district attorney, sheriff, county clerk or any
elective county officer shall be eligible to hold at the same time any
other elective county or town office, or that of city supervisor.
```
