# County Law § 407 — Accounts of fees

[County Law](../README.md) › [Article 8 — Officers; General Provisions](README.md)

```text
  § 407. Accounts of fees. The county officer authorized to receive fees
or other form of compensation belonging to the county shall keep a
record showing the nature thereof, from whom received, date of receipt,
amount and when paid to the county treasurer.
```
