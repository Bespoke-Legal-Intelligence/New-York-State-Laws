# Correction Law § 630 — Applicability

[Correction Law](../README.md) › [Article 22-A — Prisoner Furloughs](README.md)

```text
  * § 630. Applicability. This article shall be applicable only to
prisoners sentenced to institutions operated by a department of
correction in cities having a population of one million or more or by a
county which elects to have this article apply thereto.
  * NB Repealed September 1, 2025
```
