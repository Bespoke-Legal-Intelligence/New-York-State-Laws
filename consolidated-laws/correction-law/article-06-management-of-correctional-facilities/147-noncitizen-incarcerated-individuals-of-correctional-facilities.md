# Correction Law § 147 — Noncitizen incarcerated individuals of correctional facilities

[Correction Law](../README.md) › [Article 6 — Management of Correctional Facilities](README.md)

```text
  § 147. Noncitizen incarcerated individuals of correctional facilities.
The commissioner shall within three months after admission of a
noncitizen incarcerated individual to a correctional facility cause an
investigation to be made of the record and past history of such
noncitizen and shall upon the termination of such investigation cause
the record of such noncitizen, together with all facts disclosed by such
investigation, and his or her recommendations as to deportation, to be
forwarded to the United States immigration authorities having such
matters in charge.
```
