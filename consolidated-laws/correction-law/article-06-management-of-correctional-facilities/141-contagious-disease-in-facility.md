# Correction Law § 141 — Contagious disease in facility

[Correction Law](../README.md) › [Article 6 — Management of Correctional Facilities](README.md)

```text
  § 141. Contagious disease in facility.  In case any pestilence or
contagious disease shall break out among the inmates in any of the
correctional facilities, or in the vicinity of such facilities, the
commissioner of correction may cause the inmates confined in such
facility, or any of them, to be removed to some suitable place of
security, where such of them as may be sick shall receive all necessary
care and medical assistance; such inmates shall be returned as soon as
may be feasible to the facility from which they were taken, to be
confined therein according to their respective sentences.
```
