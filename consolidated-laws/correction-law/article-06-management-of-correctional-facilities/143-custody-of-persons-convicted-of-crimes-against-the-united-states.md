# Correction Law § 143 — Custody of persons convicted of crimes against the United States

[Correction Law](../README.md) › [Article 6 — Management of Correctional Facilities](README.md)

```text
  § 143. Custody of persons convicted of crimes against the United
States.  The commissioner is authorized to enter into agreements for the
care and custody of persons convicted and sentenced to imprisonment by
the United States courts in this state. Persons may be confined in
correctional facilities pursuant to any such agreement and all
provisions of law applicable to the care and custody of inmates
sentenced by courts of this state, except provisions governing the
duration of sentence and other related incidents of the sentence
provided by federal law, shall apply to the care and custody of such
persons.
```
