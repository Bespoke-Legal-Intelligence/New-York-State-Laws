# Correction Law § 270 — Definitions

[Correction Law](../README.md) › [Article 12 — Local Conditional Release Commission](README.md)

```text
  § 270. Definitions. As used in this article, the following terms have
the following meanings:
  1. "Commission" means the local conditional release commission.
  2. "County" means each county in the state, except a county within the
city of New York.
  3. "County executive" means the county commissioner, county manager,
county director or county president.
  4. "Division" means the division of criminal justice services.
```
