# Correction Law § 324 — Report of committee; compensation

[Correction Law](../README.md) › [Article 13 — Care of Property of Person Confined For Life](README.md)

```text
  § 324. Report of committee; compensation. The committee so appointed
shall annually render an account to the court of his management and of
his receipts and disbursements, and transmit a copy thereof to the
person so convicted. The court may grant such compensation to the
committee as it deems proper, not exceeding, however, the amount that
may be allowed to an administrator.
```
