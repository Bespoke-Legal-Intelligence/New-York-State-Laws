# Correction Law § 80 — Transfer of adolescents from the department

[Correction Law](../README.md) › [Article 4 — Establishment of Correctional Facilities, Commitments to Department and Custody of Incarcerated Individuals](README.md)

**Repealed** (5 Aug 2022). The text below is what the Senate publishes for this repealed provision.

```text
  * § 80. Transfer of adolescents from the department. The department
and the office of children and family services shall jointly establish a
transition plan and protocol to be used in transferring custody of all
adolescent offenders and individuals under the age of eighteen from the
custody of the department to the custody of the office of children and
family services on or before October first, two thousand twenty. The
plan and protocol shall be completed on or before July first, two
thousand twenty.
  * NB Repealed October 1, 2021
```
