# Correction Law § 263 — Oath of witnesses

[Correction Law](../README.md) › [Article 11 — Executive Clemency](README.md)

```text
  § 263. Oath of witnesses. The governor or a person designated by him
to conduct a hearing in a matter pertaining to an application for
clemency, shall have power to administer an oath to a person brought
before him.
```
