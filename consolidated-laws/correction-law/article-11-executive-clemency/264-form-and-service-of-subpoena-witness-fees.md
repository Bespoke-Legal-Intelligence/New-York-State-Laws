# Correction Law § 264 — Form and service of subpoena; witness fees

[Correction Law](../README.md) › [Article 11 — Executive Clemency](README.md)

```text
  § 264. Form and service of subpoena; witness fees. A subpoena, or
subpoena duces tecum, issued in conformity with the provisions of this
article, shall be signed by the governor's secretary, and be attested by
the privy seal of the state. A subpoena issued under this section shall
be regulated by the civil practice law and rules.
```
