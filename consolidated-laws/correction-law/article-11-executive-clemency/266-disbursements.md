# Correction Law § 266 — Disbursements

[Correction Law](../README.md) › [Article 11 — Executive Clemency](README.md)

```text
  § 266. Disbursements. Any disbursements necessary to be made for any
of the purposes mentioned in this article shall be paid from the
treasury upon the approval of the governor's secretary, on the audit and
warrant of the comptroller, out of any moneys in the treasury not
otherwise appropriated.
```
