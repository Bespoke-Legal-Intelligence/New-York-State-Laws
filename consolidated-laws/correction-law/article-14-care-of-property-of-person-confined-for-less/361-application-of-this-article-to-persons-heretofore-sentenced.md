# Correction Law § 361 — Application of this article to persons heretofore sentenced

[Correction Law](../README.md) › [Article 14 — Care of Property of Person Confined For Less Than Life](README.md)

```text
  § 361. Application of this article to persons heretofore sentenced.
This article applies to a prisoner who has been sentenced before this
chapter takes effect, and to his property; except where one or more
trustees of his property have been theretofore appointed, by proceedings
taken in pursuance of a statute then in force.
```
