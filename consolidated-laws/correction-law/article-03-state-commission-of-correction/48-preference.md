# Correction Law § 48 — Preference

[Correction Law](../README.md) › [Article 3 — State Commission of Correction](README.md)

```text
  § 48. Preference. Any action or proceeding commenced by the commission
pursuant to this article shall have a preference over all other cases,
except habeas corpus proceedings, pending before the court.
```
