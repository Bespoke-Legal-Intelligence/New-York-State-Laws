# Correction Law § 20 — Library

[Correction Law](../README.md) › [Article 2 — Department of Corrections and Community Supervision](README.md)

```text
  § 20. Library. A library shall be provided in the department
containing the leading books on parole, probation and other correctional
activities, together with reports and other documents on correlated
topics of criminology and social work.
```
