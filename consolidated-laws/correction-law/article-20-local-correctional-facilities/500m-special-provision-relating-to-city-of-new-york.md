# Correction Law § 500-M — Special provision relating to city of New York

[Correction Law](../README.md) › [Article 20 — Local Correctional Facilities](README.md)

```text
  § 500-m. Special provision relating to city of New York. Unless
specifically provided by law to the contrary, within the city of New
York or the counties constituting such city the term sheriff as set
forth in this chapter shall be deemed to mean the commissioner of
correction of such city.
```
