# Correction Law § 86 — Purpose of alternate correctional facilities

[Correction Law](../README.md) › [Article 4-B — Alternate Correctional Facilities For the City of New York](README.md)

```text
  § 86. Purpose of alternate correctional facilities. The purpose of
this article is to provide an extraordinary method for relieving
existing emergency space pressures in New York city local correctional
facilities.
```
