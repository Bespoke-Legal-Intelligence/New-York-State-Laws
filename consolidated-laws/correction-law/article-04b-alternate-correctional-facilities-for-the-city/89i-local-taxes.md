# Correction Law § 89-I — Local taxes

[Correction Law](../README.md) › [Article 4-B — Alternate Correctional Facilities For the City of New York](README.md)

```text
  § 89-i. Local taxes. Alternate correctional facilities shall be exempt
from all local property taxes while fee title to such facilities is
vested in the city of New York.
```
