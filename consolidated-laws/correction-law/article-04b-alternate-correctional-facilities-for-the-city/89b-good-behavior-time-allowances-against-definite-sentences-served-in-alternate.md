# Correction Law § 89-B — Good behavior time allowances against definite sentences served in alternate correctional facilities

[Correction Law](../README.md) › [Article 4-B — Alternate Correctional Facilities For the City of New York](README.md)

```text
  § 89-b. Good behavior time allowances against definite sentences
served in alternate correctional facilities. Notwithstanding any other
provision of law, the commissioner shall be authorized to grant,
withhold, cause to be forfeited, or cancel time allowances as provided
in and in compliance with section eight hundred four of the correction
law.
```
