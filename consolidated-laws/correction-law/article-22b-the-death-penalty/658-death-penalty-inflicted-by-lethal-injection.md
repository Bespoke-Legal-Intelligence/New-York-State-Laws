# Correction Law § 658 — Death penalty inflicted by lethal injection

[Correction Law](../README.md) › [Article 22-B — The Death Penalty](README.md)

```text
  § 658. Death penalty inflicted by lethal injection. The punishment of
death shall be inflicted by lethal injection; that is, by the
intravenous injection of a substance or substances in a lethal quantity
into the body of a person convicted until such person is dead.
```
