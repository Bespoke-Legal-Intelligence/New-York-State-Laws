# Correction Law § 605 — Prisoners sentenced at one session of court to be transported at same time

[Correction Law](../README.md) › [Article 22 — Miscellaneous Provisions](README.md)

```text
  § 605. Prisoners sentenced at one session of court to be transported
at same time. All the prisoners who shall be sentenced to imprisonment
in the same state prison, at one session of a criminal court, shall be
transported at the same time, unless said court shall expressly direct
otherwise.
```
