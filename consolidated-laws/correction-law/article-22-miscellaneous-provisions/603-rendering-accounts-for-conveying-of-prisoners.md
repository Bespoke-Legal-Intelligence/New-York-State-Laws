# Correction Law § 603 — Rendering accounts for conveying of prisoners

[Correction Law](../README.md) › [Article 22 — Miscellaneous Provisions](README.md)

```text
  § 603. Rendering accounts for conveying of prisoners.  On the
conveying of any such prisoners to the warden of such prison and upon
returning home, the sheriff or other person having charge of the same,
shall make and render to the warden of the prison, an account showing
the distance and route traveled, the method of transportation and the
items of expenditure paid or incurred, allowable under section six
hundred and two; which count shall then, be subscribed and affirmed by
him as true under the penalties of perjury to which shall be added the
certificate of the warden of such prison, setting forth the number of
prisoners so conveyed, and the distance from such prison to the place of
their conviction.
```
