# Correction Law § 604 — Payment of accounts for transporting prisoners

[Correction Law](../README.md) › [Article 22 — Miscellaneous Provisions](README.md)

```text
  § 604. Payment of accounts for transporting prisoners.  The account,
certified and attested as provided in the preceding section, shall be
subject to audit by the comptroller, and be paid out of the treasury,
unless otherwise provided.
```
