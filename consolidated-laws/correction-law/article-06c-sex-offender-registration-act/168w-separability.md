# Correction Law § 168-W — Separability

[Correction Law](../README.md) › [Article 6-C — Sex Offender Registration Act](README.md)

```text
  § 168-w. Separability. If any section of this article, or part thereof
shall be adjudged by a court of competent jurisdiction to be invalid,
such judgment shall not affect, impair or invalidate the remainder or
any other section or part thereof.
```
