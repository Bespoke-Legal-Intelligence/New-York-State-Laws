# Highway Law § 349 — Application of chapter

[Highway Law](../README.md) › [Article 12-A — State Thruways](README.md)

```text
  § 349. Application of chapter. All the provisions of this chapter
relating to state highways and not inconsistent with the provisions of
this article shall apply to the construction or improvement and the
control and maintenance of state thruways in the same manner as though
they were designated as state highways.
```
