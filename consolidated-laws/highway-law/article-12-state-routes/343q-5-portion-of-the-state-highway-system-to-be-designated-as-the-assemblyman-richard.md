# Highway Law § 343-Q*5 — Portion of the state highway system to be designated as the "Assemblyman Richard H

[Highway Law](../README.md) › [Article 12 — State Routes](README.md)

*An asterisk in a number (for example Article 24\* or § 20-C\*2) is the Senate's mark for a number the Legislature has used more than once: the marked article or section is a different one from the unmarked one with the same number. \*2 means the second one enacted with that number.*

```text
  * § 343-q. Portion of the state highway system to be designated as the
"Assemblyman Richard H. Miller Memorial Highway". All that portion of
the state highway system located in the county of Broome constituting
state route 201 from its intersection with state route 434 in the town
of Vestal to its intersection with state route 17 in the village of
Johnson City shall be designated and known as the "Assemblyman Richard
H. Miller Memorial Highway".
  * NB There are 11 § 343-q's
```
