# Highway Law § 343-Q — Portion of the state highway system to be designated as the "New York State Troopers T

[Highway Law](../README.md) › [Article 12 — State Routes](README.md)

```text
  * § 343-q. Portion of the state highway system to be designated as the
"New York State Troopers T. Michael Kelly and Kenneth A. Poormon
Memorial Highway". That portion of state highway route 28, between state
highway route 375 and Maverick Road, which lies in the town of Hurley,
county of Ulster, shall be designated and known as the "New York State
Troopers T. Michael Kelly and Kenneth A. Poormon Memorial Highway".
  * NB There are 11 § 343-q's
```
