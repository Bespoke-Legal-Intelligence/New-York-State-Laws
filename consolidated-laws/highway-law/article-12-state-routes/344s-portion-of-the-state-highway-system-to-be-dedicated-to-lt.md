# Highway Law § 344-S — Portion of the state highway system to be dedicated to Lt

[Highway Law](../README.md) › [Article 12 — State Routes](README.md)

```text
  § 344-s. Portion of the state highway system to be dedicated to Lt.
Gary A. Scott. All that portion of the state highway system constituting
state route 19 within the town of LeRoy, county of Genesee, shall be
dedicated to Lt. Gary A. Scott.
```
