# Highway Law § 343-T*4 — Portion of the state highway system to be designated the "Pearl Harbor Veterans Memorial Highway"

[Highway Law](../README.md) › [Article 12 — State Routes](README.md)

*An asterisk in a number (for example Article 24\* or § 20-C\*2) is the Senate's mark for a number the Legislature has used more than once: the marked article or section is a different one from the unmarked one with the same number. \*2 means the second one enacted with that number.*

```text
  * § 343-t. Portion of the state highway system to be designated the
"Pearl Harbor Veterans Memorial Highway". All that portion of the state
highway system in the county of Steuben constituting state route
thirty-six from North Hornell to the Livingston county line shall be
designated and known as the "Pearl Harbor Veterans Memorial Highway".
  * NB There are 7 § 343-t's
```
