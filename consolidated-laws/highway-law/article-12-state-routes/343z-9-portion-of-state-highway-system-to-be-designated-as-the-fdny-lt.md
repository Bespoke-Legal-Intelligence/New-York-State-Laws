# Highway Law § 343-Z*9 — Portion of state highway system to be designated as the "FDNY Lt

[Highway Law](../README.md) › [Article 12 — State Routes](README.md)

*An asterisk in a number (for example Article 24\* or § 20-C\*2) is the Senate's mark for a number the Legislature has used more than once: the marked article or section is a different one from the unmarked one with the same number. \*2 means the second one enacted with that number.*

```text
  * § 343-z. Portion of state highway system to be designated as the
"FDNY Lt. Richard Nappi Memorial Bridge". The bridge on Patchogue-Mount
Sinai Road also known as County Road 83 which crosses interstate route
495 in the town of Brookhaven, county of Suffolk, shall be designated
and known as the "FDNY Lt. Richard Nappi Memorial Bridge".
  * NB There are 10 § 343-z's
```
