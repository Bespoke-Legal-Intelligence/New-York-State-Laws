# Highway Law § 342-EEE — Portion of the state highway system to be designated as the "Sidney Veterans' Memorial Highway"

[Highway Law](../README.md) › [Article 12 — State Routes](README.md)

```text
  § 342-eee. Portion of the state highway system to be designated as the
"Sidney Veterans' Memorial Highway". That portion of state highway route
eight, between the southern end of the bridge over the Susquehanna River
and interstate eighty-eight, which lies in the village of Sidney, county
of Delaware, shall be designated and known as the "Sidney Veterans'
Memorial Highway".
```
