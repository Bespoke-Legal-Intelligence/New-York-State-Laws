# Highway Law § 342-VVV — Portion of the state highway system to be designated as "The Woodstock Way"

[Highway Law](../README.md) › [Article 12 — State Routes](README.md)

```text
  § 342-vvv. Portion of the state highway system to be designated as
"The Woodstock Way". All that portion of the highway system constituting
state route 17B in the county of Sullivan shall be designated and known
as "The Woodstock Way".
```
