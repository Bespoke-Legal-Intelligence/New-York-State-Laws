# Highway Law § 344-H*5 — Portion of the state highway system to be designated as the "Lt

[Highway Law](../README.md) › [Article 12 — State Routes](README.md)

*An asterisk in a number (for example Article 24\* or § 20-C\*2) is the Senate's mark for a number the Legislature has used more than once: the marked article or section is a different one from the unmarked one with the same number. \*2 means the second one enacted with that number.*

```text
  * § 344-h. Portion of the state highway system to be designated as the
"Lt. Christopher J. Raguso FDNY-CFD Memorial Highway". All that portion
of the highway system constituting the Jericho Turnpike, beginning at
its intersection with East Deer Park Road, continuing in a generally
easterly direction, and terminating at St. Johnland Road, shall be
designated and known as the "Lt. Christopher J. Raguso FDNY-CFD Memorial
Highway".
  * NB There are 14 § 344-h's
```
