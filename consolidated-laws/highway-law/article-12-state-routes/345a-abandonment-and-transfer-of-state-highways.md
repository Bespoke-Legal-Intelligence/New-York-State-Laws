# Highway Law § 345-A — Abandonment and transfer of state highways

[Highway Law](../README.md) › [Article 12 — State Routes](README.md)

```text
  § 345-a. Abandonment and transfer of state highways. The abandonment
or transfer of any state highway to a county, town, city or village
shall be deemed a transfer of all right, title and interest of the state
of New York to and in such highway to such county, town, city or village
respectively unless expressly provided to the contrary.
```
