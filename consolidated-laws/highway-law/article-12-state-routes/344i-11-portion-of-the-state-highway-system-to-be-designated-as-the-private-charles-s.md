# Highway Law § 344-I*11 — Portion of the state highway system to be designated as the "Private Charles S

[Highway Law](../README.md) › [Article 12 — State Routes](README.md)

*An asterisk in a number (for example Article 24\* or § 20-C\*2) is the Senate's mark for a number the Legislature has used more than once: the marked article or section is a different one from the unmarked one with the same number. \*2 means the second one enacted with that number.*

```text
  * § 344-i. Portion of the state highway system to be designated as the
"Private Charles S. Cooper, Jr. Memorial Highway". All that portion of
the state highway system constituting state route sixty in the town of
Kiantone, county of Chautauqua, beginning at the Jamestown city line,
continuing in a generally southeasterly direction, and terminating at
the intersection with state route sixty-two, shall be designated and
known as the "Private Charles S. Cooper, Jr. Memorial Highway".
  * NB There are 11 § 344-i's
```
