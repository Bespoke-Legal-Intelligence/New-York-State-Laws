# Highway Law § 342-WWW — Portion of the state highway system to be designated as the "Adirondack Veterans' Memorial Highway"

[Highway Law](../README.md) › [Article 12 — State Routes](README.md)

```text
  § 342-www. Portion of the state highway system to be designated as the
"Adirondack Veterans' Memorial Highway". That portion of state highway
route I-87, known as the Adirondack Northway, between exit one and exit
forty-three shall be designated and known as the "Adirondack Veterans'
Memorial Highway".
```
