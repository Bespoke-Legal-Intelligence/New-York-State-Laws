# Highway Law § 344-N*21 — Portion of the state highway system to be designated as the "Ralph Calabrese Memorial Highway"

[Highway Law](../README.md) › [Article 12 — State Routes](README.md)

*An asterisk in a number (for example Article 24\* or § 20-C\*2) is the Senate's mark for a number the Legislature has used more than once: the marked article or section is a different one from the unmarked one with the same number. \*2 means the second one enacted with that number.*

```text
  * § 344-n. Portion of the state highway system to be designated as the
"Ralph Calabrese Memorial Highway". All that portion of the state
highway system constituting state route 21 (E. Gibson Street), from its
intersection with state route 332 (North Main Street) in the city of
Canandaigua, county of Ontario, traveling in a northeasterly direction
to its intersection with state route 488 in the town of Hopewell, county
of Ontario, shall be designated and known as the "Ralph Calabrese
Memorial Highway".
  * NB There are 26 § 344-n's
```
