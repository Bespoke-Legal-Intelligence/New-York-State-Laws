# Highway Law § 343-T*5 — Portion of the state highway system to be designated as the "M

[Highway Law](../README.md) › [Article 12 — State Routes](README.md)

*An asterisk in a number (for example Article 24\* or § 20-C\*2) is the Senate's mark for a number the Legislature has used more than once: the marked article or section is a different one from the unmarked one with the same number. \*2 means the second one enacted with that number.*

```text
  * § 343-t. Portion of the state highway system to be designated as the
"M. Paul Keesler Memorial Highway". All that portion of the state
highway system constituting part of state route twenty-eight from the
town of Poland, county of Chautauqua in a generally northwesterly
direction to its intersection with state route twelve in the village of
Barneveld, county of Oneida shall be designated and known as the "M.
Paul Keesler Memorial Highway".
  * NB There are 7 § 343-t's
```
