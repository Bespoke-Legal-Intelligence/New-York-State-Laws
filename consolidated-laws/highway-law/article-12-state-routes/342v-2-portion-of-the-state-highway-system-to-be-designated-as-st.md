# Highway Law § 342-V*2 — Portion of the state highway system to be designated as "St

[Highway Law](../README.md) › [Article 12 — State Routes](README.md)

*An asterisk in a number (for example Article 24\* or § 20-C\*2) is the Senate's mark for a number the Legislature has used more than once: the marked article or section is a different one from the unmarked one with the same number. \*2 means the second one enacted with that number.*

```text
  * § 342-v. Portion of the state highway system to be designated as
"St.  Regis Mohawk Indian Veterans Memorial Bridge". The bridge to be
erected across the St. Regis river on state route 37 in Franklin county
shall be designated and known as "St. Regis Mohawk Indian Veterans
Memorial Bridge".
  * NB There are 3 § 342-v's
```
