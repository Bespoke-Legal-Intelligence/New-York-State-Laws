# Highway Law § 344-D*8 — Portion of state highway system to be designated as the "Oneida County Vietnam Veterans Memorial Highway"

[Highway Law](../README.md) › [Article 12 — State Routes](README.md)

*An asterisk in a number (for example Article 24\* or § 20-C\*2) is the Senate's mark for a number the Legislature has used more than once: the marked article or section is a different one from the unmarked one with the same number. \*2 means the second one enacted with that number.*

```text
  * § 344-d. Portion of state highway system to be designated as the
"Oneida County Vietnam Veterans Memorial Highway". All that portion of
the state highway system constituting New York state route forty-nine in
the county of Oneida, beginning from the eastern city line of the city
of Rome and continuing in a generally southeasterly direction to the
western city line of the city of Utica shall be designated and known as
the "Oneida County Vietnam Veterans Memorial Highway".
  * NB There are 16 § 344-d's
```
