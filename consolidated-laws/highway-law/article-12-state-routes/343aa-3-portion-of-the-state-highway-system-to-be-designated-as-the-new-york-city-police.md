# Highway Law § 343-AA*3 — Portion of the state highway system to be designated as the "New York City Police Department Detective Peter Figoski Memorial Bridge"

[Highway Law](../README.md) › [Article 12 — State Routes](README.md)

*An asterisk in a number (for example Article 24\* or § 20-C\*2) is the Senate's mark for a number the Legislature has used more than once: the marked article or section is a different one from the unmarked one with the same number. \*2 means the second one enacted with that number.*

```text
  * § 343-aa. Portion of the state highway system to be designated as
the "New York City Police Department Detective Peter Figoski Memorial
Bridge". The bridge on Route 109 which crosses the Sunrise Highway in
the hamlet of West Babylon, county of Suffolk, shall be designated and
known as the "New York City Police Department Detective Peter Figoski
Memorial Bridge".
  * NB There are 5 § 343-aa's
```
