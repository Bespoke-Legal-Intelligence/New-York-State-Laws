# Highway Law § 343-Y*3 — Portion of the state highway system to be designated as the "Almond E

[Highway Law](../README.md) › [Article 12 — State Routes](README.md)

*An asterisk in a number (for example Article 24\* or § 20-C\*2) is the Senate's mark for a number the Legislature has used more than once: the marked article or section is a different one from the unmarked one with the same number. \*2 means the second one enacted with that number.*

```text
  * § 343-y. Portion of the state highway system to be designated as the
"Almond E. Fisher Memorial Highway". All that portion of the state
highway system constituting state route nineteen beginning at the
Allegany county line in the town of Hume, and continuing southerly along
state route nineteen to its intersection with Allegany County Road 3 in
the town of Hume, Allegany county, shall be designated and known as the
"Almond E. Fisher Memorial Highway".
  * NB There are 5 § 343-y's
```
