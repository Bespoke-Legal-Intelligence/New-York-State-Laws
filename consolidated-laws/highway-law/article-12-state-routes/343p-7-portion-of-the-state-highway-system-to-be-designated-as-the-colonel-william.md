# Highway Law § 343-P*7 — Portion of the state highway system to be designated as the "Colonel William O'Brien and Sergeant Thomas Baker Memorial Highway"

[Highway Law](../README.md) › [Article 12 — State Routes](README.md)

*An asterisk in a number (for example Article 24\* or § 20-C\*2) is the Senate's mark for a number the Legislature has used more than once: the marked article or section is a different one from the unmarked one with the same number. \*2 means the second one enacted with that number.*

```text
  * § 343-p. Portion of the state highway system to be designated as the
"Colonel William O'Brien and Sergeant Thomas Baker Memorial Highway".
All that portion of the state highway system constituting state route
forty which lies within the city of Troy, county of Rensselaer, shall be
designated and known as the "Colonel William O'Brien and Sergeant Thomas
Baker Memorial Highway".
  * NB There are 8 § 343-p's
```
