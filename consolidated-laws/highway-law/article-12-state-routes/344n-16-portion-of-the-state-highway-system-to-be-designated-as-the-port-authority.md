# Highway Law § 344-N*16 — Portion of the state highway system to be designated as the "Port Authority Police Officer James W

[Highway Law](../README.md) › [Article 12 — State Routes](README.md)

*An asterisk in a number (for example Article 24\* or § 20-C\*2) is the Senate's mark for a number the Legislature has used more than once: the marked article or section is a different one from the unmarked one with the same number. \*2 means the second one enacted with that number.*

```text
  * § 344-n. Portion of the state highway system to be designated as the
"Port Authority Police Officer James W. Kennelly Memorial Highway". All
that portion of the state highway system constituting Old Sunrise
Highway from its intersection with Carman Mill Road to its intersection
with County Line Road within the hamlet of Massapequa, town of Oyster
Bay, county of Nassau, shall be designated and known as the "Port
Authority Police Officer James W. Kennelly Memorial Highway".
  * NB There are 26 § 344-n's
```
