# Highway Law § 343-K*2 — Portion of state highway system to be designated as the "POW-MIA Bridge"

[Highway Law](../README.md) › [Article 12 — State Routes](README.md)

*An asterisk in a number (for example Article 24\* or § 20-C\*2) is the Senate's mark for a number the Legislature has used more than once: the marked article or section is a different one from the unmarked one with the same number. \*2 means the second one enacted with that number.*

```text
  * § 343-k. Portion of state highway system to be designated as the
"POW-MIA Bridge". All that portion of the state highway system
constituting the Route 14 bridge, located across the Erie Canal north of
route 31 in the town of Lyons, county of Wayne shall be known as the
"POW-MIA Bridge".
  * NB There are 7 § 343-k's
```
