# Highway Law § 343-C — Portion of state highway system in the county of Rockland to be designated as "The Korean War Veterans Memorial Highway"

[Highway Law](../README.md) › [Article 12 — State Routes](README.md)

```text
  * § 343-c. Portion of state highway system in the county of Rockland
to be designated as "The Korean War Veterans Memorial Highway". All that
portion of the state highway system in the county of Rockland
constituting State Route 59 shall be designated and known as "The Korean
War Veterans Memorial Highway".
  * NB There are 4 § 343-c's
```
