# Highway Law § 342-S — Portion of state highway system to be designated as "J

[Highway Law](../README.md) › [Article 12 — State Routes](README.md)

```text
  § 342-s. Portion of state highway system to be designated as "J.
Donald Synnett Memorial Bridge". The bridge on that portion of the state
highway system identified as state route 9D, in the village of
Wappingers Falls, in the county of Dutchess, shall be designated and
known as "J. Donald Synnett Memorial Bridge".
```
