# Highway Law § 343-U — Portion of the state highway system to be designated as the "Griffiss Veterans' Memorial Parkway"

[Highway Law](../README.md) › [Article 12 — State Routes](README.md)

```text
  * § 343-u. Portion of the state highway system to be designated as the
"Griffiss Veterans' Memorial Parkway". That portion of state highway
route 825, from the intersection of state routes 49 and 365 to state
route 46 shall be designated and known as the "Griffiss Veterans'
Memorial Parkway".
  * NB There are 8 § 343-u's
```
