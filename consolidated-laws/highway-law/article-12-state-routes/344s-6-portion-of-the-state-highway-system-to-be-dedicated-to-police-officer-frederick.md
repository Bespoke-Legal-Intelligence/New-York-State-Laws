# Highway Law § 344-S*6 — Portion of the state highway system to be dedicated to Police Officer Frederick G

[Highway Law](../README.md) › [Article 12 — State Routes](README.md)

*An asterisk in a number (for example Article 24\* or § 20-C\*2) is the Senate's mark for a number the Legislature has used more than once: the marked article or section is a different one from the unmarked one with the same number. \*2 means the second one enacted with that number.*

```text
  * § 344-s. Portion of the state highway system to be dedicated to
Police Officer Frederick G. Maley. All that portion of the state highway
system constituting state route one hundred six, also known as Newbridge
Road, from its intersection with North Jerusalem Road to its
intersection with East Meadow Avenue within the hamlet of North
Bellmore, town of Hempstead, county of Nassau, shall be dedicated to
Police Officer Frederick G. Maley.
  * NB There are 9 § 344-s's
```
