# Highway Law § 344-N*18 — Portion of the state highway system to be designated and known as the "MSgt Thomas P

[Highway Law](../README.md) › [Article 12 — State Routes](README.md)

*An asterisk in a number (for example Article 24\* or § 20-C\*2) is the Senate's mark for a number the Legislature has used more than once: the marked article or section is a different one from the unmarked one with the same number. \*2 means the second one enacted with that number.*

```text
  * § 344-n. Portion of the state highway system to be designated and
known as the "MSgt Thomas P. Madison Memorial Highway". All that portion
of the state highway system constituting state route 19, from its
intersection with Maple Avenue to its intersection with state route 39
and Griffith Road in the town of Pike, county of Wyoming, shall be
designated and known as the "MSgt Thomas P. Madison Memorial Highway".
  * NB There are 26 § 344-n's
```
