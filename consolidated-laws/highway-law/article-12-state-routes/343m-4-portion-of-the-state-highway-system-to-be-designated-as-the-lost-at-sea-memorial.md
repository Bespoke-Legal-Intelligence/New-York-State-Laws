# Highway Law § 343-M*4 — Portion of the state highway system to be designated as the "Lost At Sea Memorial Pike"

[Highway Law](../README.md) › [Article 12 — State Routes](README.md)

*An asterisk in a number (for example Article 24\* or § 20-C\*2) is the Senate's mark for a number the Legislature has used more than once: the marked article or section is a different one from the unmarked one with the same number. \*2 means the second one enacted with that number.*

```text
  * § 343-m. Portion of the state highway system to be designated as the
"Lost At Sea Memorial Pike". All that portion of the state highway
system beginning at the intersection of state route 114 and Toilsome
Lane in the town of East Hampton and continuing northerly along route
114 to the northern boundary line of the village of Sag Harbor shall be
designated and known as the "Lost At Sea Memorial Pike".
  * NB There are 4 § 343-m's
```
