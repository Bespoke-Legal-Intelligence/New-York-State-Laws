# Highway Law § 344-D*14 — Portion of the state highway system to be designated as the "Mendon Veterans Memorial Highway"

[Highway Law](../README.md) › [Article 12 — State Routes](README.md)

*An asterisk in a number (for example Article 24\* or § 20-C\*2) is the Senate's mark for a number the Legislature has used more than once: the marked article or section is a different one from the unmarked one with the same number. \*2 means the second one enacted with that number.*

```text
  * § 344-d. Portion of the state highway system to be designated as the
"Mendon Veterans Memorial Highway". All that portion of the state
highway system constituting state route fifteen A in the town of Mendon,
county of Monroe, beginning at the Rush and Mendon town line, continuing
in a generally southerly direction, and terminating at the Monroe and
Livingston county line, shall be designated and known as the "Mendon
Veterans Memorial Highway".
  * NB There are 16 § 344-d's
```
