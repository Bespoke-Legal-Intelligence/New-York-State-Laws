# Highway Law § 343-S — Portion of the state highway system to be designated as the "Doctor Bernard J

[Highway Law](../README.md) › [Article 12 — State Routes](README.md)

```text
  * § 343-s. Portion of the state highway system to be designated as the
"Doctor Bernard J. Burke Memorial Bridge". The bridge on route 167 over
the Erie Canal, Mohawk River, CSX Railroad and Route 5 (BIN 4038920), in
the city of Little Falls, county of Herkimer, shall be designated and
known as the "Doctor Bernard J. Burke Memorial Bridge".
  * NB There are 12 § 343-s's
```
