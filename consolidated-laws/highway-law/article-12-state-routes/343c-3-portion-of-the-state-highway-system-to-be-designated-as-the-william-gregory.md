# Highway Law § 343-C*3 — Portion of the state highway system to be designated as the "William Gregory Bypass"

[Highway Law](../README.md) › [Article 12 — State Routes](README.md)

*An asterisk in a number (for example Article 24\* or § 20-C\*2) is the Senate's mark for a number the Legislature has used more than once: the marked article or section is a different one from the unmarked one with the same number. \*2 means the second one enacted with that number.*

```text
  * § 343-c. Portion of the state highway system to be designated as the
"William Gregory Bypass". All that portion of the state highway system
of state route 93 known as the Lockport Bypass located in the town and
city Lockport between Robinson Road and state route 31, in the county of
Niagara, shall be designated and known as the "William Gregory Bypass".
  * NB There are 4 § 343-c's
```
