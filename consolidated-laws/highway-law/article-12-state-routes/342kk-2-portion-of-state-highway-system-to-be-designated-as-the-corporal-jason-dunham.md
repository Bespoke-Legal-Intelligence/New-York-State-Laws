# Highway Law § 342-KK*2 — Portion of state highway system to be designated as the "Corporal Jason Dunham Memorial Bridge"

[Highway Law](../README.md) › [Article 12 — State Routes](README.md)

*An asterisk in a number (for example Article 24\* or § 20-C\*2) is the Senate's mark for a number the Legislature has used more than once: the marked article or section is a different one from the unmarked one with the same number. \*2 means the second one enacted with that number.*

```text
  * § 342-kk. Portion of state highway system to be designated as the
"Corporal Jason Dunham Memorial Bridge". All that portion of the state
highway system constituting bridge number 1061660, located on state
route nineteen, also known as Transit Hill Road, which crosses over
interstate route eighty-six, at exit thirty, in the town of Amity,
county of Allegany, shall be designated and known as the "Corporal Jason
Dunham Memorial Bridge".
  * NB There are 2 § 342-kk's
```
