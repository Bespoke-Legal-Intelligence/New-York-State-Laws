# Highway Law § 342-DDD — Portion of the state highway system to be designated as the "Hancock Veterans Memorial Bridge"

[Highway Law](../README.md) › [Article 12 — State Routes](README.md)

```text
  * § 342-ddd. Portion of the state highway system to be designated as
the "Hancock Veterans Memorial Bridge". The bridge on state route
ninety-seven spanning the Delaware River, designated New York state
bridge number 1035490, in the town of Hancock, county of Delaware, shall
be designated and known as the "Hancock Veterans Memorial Bridge".
  * NB There are 3 § 342-ddd's
```
