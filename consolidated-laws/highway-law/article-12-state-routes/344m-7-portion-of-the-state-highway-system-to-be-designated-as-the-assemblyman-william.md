# Highway Law § 344-M*7 — Portion of the state highway system to be designated as the "Assemblyman William 'Bill' Magee Memorial Highway"

[Highway Law](../README.md) › [Article 12 — State Routes](README.md)

*An asterisk in a number (for example Article 24\* or § 20-C\*2) is the Senate's mark for a number the Legislature has used more than once: the marked article or section is a different one from the unmarked one with the same number. \*2 means the second one enacted with that number.*

```text
  * § 344-m. Portion of the state highway system to be designated as the
"Assemblyman William 'Bill' Magee Memorial Highway". All that portion of
the state highway system constituting state route thirteen within the
county of Madison shall be designated and known as the "Assemblyman
William 'Bill' Magee Memorial Highway".
  * NB There are 19 § 344-m's
```
