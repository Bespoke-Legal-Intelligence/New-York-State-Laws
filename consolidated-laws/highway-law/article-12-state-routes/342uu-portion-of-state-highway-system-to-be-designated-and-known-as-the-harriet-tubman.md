# Highway Law § 342-UU — Portion of state highway system to be designated and known as the "Harriet Tubman Memorial Highway"

[Highway Law](../README.md) › [Article 12 — State Routes](README.md)

```text
  * § 342-uu. Portion of state highway system to be designated and known
as the "Harriet Tubman Memorial Highway". All that portion of the state
highway system constituting state route thirty-four from its
intersection with state route one hundred four in the town of Hannibal
in Oswego county, and continuing southerly along state route thirty-four
to its intersection with state route seventeen-c in the town of Waverly
in Tioga county shall be designated and known as the "Harriet Tubman
Memorial Highway".
  * NB There are 2 § 342-uu's
```
