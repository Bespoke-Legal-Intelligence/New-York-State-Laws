# Highway Law § 344-G*2 — Portion of the state highway system to be designated as the "Detective Steven McDonald Memorial Highway"

[Highway Law](../README.md) › [Article 12 — State Routes](README.md)

*An asterisk in a number (for example Article 24\* or § 20-C\*2) is the Senate's mark for a number the Legislature has used more than once: the marked article or section is a different one from the unmarked one with the same number. \*2 means the second one enacted with that number.*

```text
  * § 344-g. Portion of the state highway system to be designated as the
"Detective Steven McDonald Memorial Highway". All that portion of the
highway system constituting the Southern State Parkway, beginning at the
interchange of the Belt Parkway and the Cross Island Parkway, continuing
in a generally easterly direction, and terminating at exit 22, the
intersection of the Meadowbrook State Parkway, shall be designated and
known as the "Detective Steven McDonald Memorial Highway".
  * NB There are 2 § 344-g's
```
