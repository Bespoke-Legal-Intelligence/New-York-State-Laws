# Highway Law § 345-D*2 — Portion of the state highway system to be dedicated to TSgt Kory Wade

[Highway Law](../README.md) › [Article 12 — State Routes](README.md)

*An asterisk in a number (for example Article 24\* or § 20-C\*2) is the Senate's mark for a number the Legislature has used more than once: the marked article or section is a different one from the unmarked one with the same number. \*2 means the second one enacted with that number.*

```text
  * § 345-d. Portion of the state highway system to be dedicated to TSgt
Kory Wade. All that portion of the state highway system constituting
state route twenty-one from its intersection with state route ninety-six
to its intersection with Latting Road and Standpipe Road, in the town of
Manchester, county of Ontario, shall be dedicated to TSgt Kory Wade.
  * NB There are 4 § 345-d's
```
