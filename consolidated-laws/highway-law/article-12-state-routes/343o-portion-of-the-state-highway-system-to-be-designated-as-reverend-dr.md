# Highway Law § 343-O — Portion of the state highway system to be designated as "Reverend Dr

[Highway Law](../README.md) › [Article 12 — State Routes](README.md)

```text
  * § 343-o. Portion of the state highway system to be designated as
"Reverend Dr. Bennett W. Smith, Sr. Way". All that portion of the state
highway system in the city of Buffalo constituting Goodell Street from
Locust Street to Main Street shall on and after the effective date of
this section be designated and known as "Reverend Dr. Bennett W. Smith,
Sr. Way".
  * NB There are 8 § 343-o's
```
