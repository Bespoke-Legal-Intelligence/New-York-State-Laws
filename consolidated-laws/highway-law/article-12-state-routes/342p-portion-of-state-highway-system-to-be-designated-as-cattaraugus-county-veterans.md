# Highway Law § 342-P — Portion of state highway system to be designated as "Cattaraugus County Veterans Memorial Bridge"

[Highway Law](../README.md) › [Article 12 — State Routes](README.md)

```text
  § 342-p. Portion of state highway system to be designated as
"Cattaraugus County Veterans Memorial Bridge". The bridge across the
Allegheny river on the Southern Tier Expressway shall be designated and
known as "Cattaraugus County Veterans Memorial Bridge".
```
