# Highway Law § 344-R*5 — Portion of the state highway system to be dedicated to 9-11 Fallen Firefighters

[Highway Law](../README.md) › [Article 12 — State Routes](README.md)

*An asterisk in a number (for example Article 24\* or § 20-C\*2) is the Senate's mark for a number the Legislature has used more than once: the marked article or section is a different one from the unmarked one with the same number. \*2 means the second one enacted with that number.*

```text
  * § 344-r. Portion of the state highway system to be dedicated to 9-11
Fallen Firefighters. All that portion of the state highway system
constituting the Long Island Expressway between exit 59 and exit 61 in
the town of Brookhaven, county of Suffolk, shall be dedicated to 9-11
Fallen Firefighters.
  * NB There are 12 § 344-r's
```
