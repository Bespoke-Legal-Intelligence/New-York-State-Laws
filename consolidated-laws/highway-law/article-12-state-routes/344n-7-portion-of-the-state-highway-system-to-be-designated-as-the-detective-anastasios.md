# Highway Law § 344-N*7 — Portion of the state highway system to be designated as the "Detective Anastasios Tsakos Memorial Bridge"

[Highway Law](../README.md) › [Article 12 — State Routes](README.md)

*An asterisk in a number (for example Article 24\* or § 20-C\*2) is the Senate's mark for a number the Legislature has used more than once: the marked article or section is a different one from the unmarked one with the same number. \*2 means the second one enacted with that number.*

```text
  * § 344-n. Portion of the state highway system to be designated as the
"Detective Anastasios Tsakos Memorial Bridge". All that portion of the
state highway system constituting the Francis Lewis Boulevard overpass
at the Long Island Expressway in the city of New York, county of Queens,
shall be designated and known as the "Detective Anastasios Tsakos
Memorial Bridge".
  * NB There are 8 § 344-n's
```
