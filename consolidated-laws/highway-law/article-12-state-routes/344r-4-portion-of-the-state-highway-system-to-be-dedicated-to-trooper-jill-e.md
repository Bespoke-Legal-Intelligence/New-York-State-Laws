# Highway Law § 344-R*4 — Portion of the state highway system to be dedicated to Trooper Jill E

[Highway Law](../README.md) › [Article 12 — State Routes](README.md)

*An asterisk in a number (for example Article 24\* or § 20-C\*2) is the Senate's mark for a number the Legislature has used more than once: the marked article or section is a different one from the unmarked one with the same number. \*2 means the second one enacted with that number.*

```text
  * § 344-r. Portion of the state highway system to be dedicated to
Trooper Jill E. Mattice. All that portion of the state highway system
constituting state route 23 in the town of Morris, county of Otsego,
from its intersection with county route 49 to its intersection with
Potato Farm Road, known as Patrick Hill Road, shall be dedicated to
Trooper Jill E. Mattice.
  * NB There are 12 § 344-r's
```
