# Highway Law § 344-O*2 — Portion of the state highway system to be designated as the "Port Authority Police Officer Pavlos D

[Highway Law](../README.md) › [Article 12 — State Routes](README.md)

*An asterisk in a number (for example Article 24\* or § 20-C\*2) is the Senate's mark for a number the Legislature has used more than once: the marked article or section is a different one from the unmarked one with the same number. \*2 means the second one enacted with that number.*

```text
  * § 344-o. Portion of the state highway system to be designated as the
"Port Authority Police Officer Pavlos D. Pallas Memorial Bridge". All
that portion of the state highway system constituting the bridge on 94th
Street, between the 82nd Street overpass and the 102nd Street overpass,
crossing over the Grand Central Parkway in the neighborhood of East
Elmhurst, county of Queens, shall be designated and known as the "Port
Authority Police Officer Pavlos D. Pallas Memorial Bridge".
  * NB There are 3 § 344-o's
```
