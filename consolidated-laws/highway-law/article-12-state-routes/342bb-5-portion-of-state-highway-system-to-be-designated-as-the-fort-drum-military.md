# Highway Law § 342-BB*5 — Portion of state highway system to be designated as "The Fort Drum Military Highway"

[Highway Law](../README.md) › [Article 12 — State Routes](README.md)

*An asterisk in a number (for example Article 24\* or § 20-C\*2) is the Senate's mark for a number the Legislature has used more than once: the marked article or section is a different one from the unmarked one with the same number. \*2 means the second one enacted with that number.*

```text
  * § 342-bb. Portion of state highway system to be designated as "The
Fort Drum Military Highway". That portion of state route three in
Jefferson county, beginning at route twelve and continuing north to the
Jefferson - Lewis county border shall be designated as "The Fort Drum
Military Highway".
  * NB There are 5 § 342-bb's
```
