# Highway Law § 343-M — Portion of the state highway system to be known as the "POW-MIA Memorial Highway"

[Highway Law](../README.md) › [Article 12 — State Routes](README.md)

```text
  * § 343-m. Portion of the state highway system to be known as the
"POW-MIA Memorial Highway". All that portion of the state highway system
constituting state route twelve beginning at the town of Morristown,
county of St. Lawrence and continuing south through the counties of
Jefferson, Lewis, Oneida, Madison and Chenango to its intersection with
state route twelve-a in the town of Chenango, county of Broome, shall be
designated and known as the "POW-MIA Memorial Highway".
  * NB There are 4 § 343-m's
```
