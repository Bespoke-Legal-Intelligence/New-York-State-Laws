# Highway Law § 344-M*2 — Portion of the state highway system to be designated as the "CPL William James Hillard II Memorial Bridge"

[Highway Law](../README.md) › [Article 12 — State Routes](README.md)

*An asterisk in a number (for example Article 24\* or § 20-C\*2) is the Senate's mark for a number the Legislature has used more than once: the marked article or section is a different one from the unmarked one with the same number. \*2 means the second one enacted with that number.*

```text
  * § 344-m. Portion of the state highway system to be designated as the
"CPL William James Hillard II Memorial Bridge". The bridge on interstate
route eighty-six, crossing over West Main Street through the town of
Randolph, county of Cattaraugus, shall be designated and known as the
"CPL William James Hillard II Memorial Bridge".
  * NB There are 10 § 344-m's
```
