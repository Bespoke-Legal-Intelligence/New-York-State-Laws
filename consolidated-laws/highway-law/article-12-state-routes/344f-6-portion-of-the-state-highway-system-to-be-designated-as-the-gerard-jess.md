# Highway Law § 344-F*6 — Portion of the state highway system to be designated as the "Gerard 'Jess' Fitzpatrick Memorial Bridge"

[Highway Law](../README.md) › [Article 12 — State Routes](README.md)

*An asterisk in a number (for example Article 24\* or § 20-C\*2) is the Senate's mark for a number the Legislature has used more than once: the marked article or section is a different one from the unmarked one with the same number. \*2 means the second one enacted with that number.*

```text
  * § 344-f. Portion of the state highway system to be designated as the
"Gerard 'Jess' Fitzpatrick Memorial Bridge". The bridge on United States
route two hundred nineteen, crossing over Cattaraugus Creek, in the
counties of Cattaraugus and Erie, shall be designated and known as the
"Gerard 'Jess' Fitzpatrick Memorial Bridge".
  * NB There are 7 § 344-f's
```
