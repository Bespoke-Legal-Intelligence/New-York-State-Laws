# Highway Law § 344-I*8 — Portion of the state highway system to be designated as the "Lance Corporal Aaron M

[Highway Law](../README.md) › [Article 12 — State Routes](README.md)

*An asterisk in a number (for example Article 24\* or § 20-C\*2) is the Senate's mark for a number the Legislature has used more than once: the marked article or section is a different one from the unmarked one with the same number. \*2 means the second one enacted with that number.*

```text
  * § 344-i. Portion of the state highway system to be designated as the
"Lance Corporal Aaron M. Swanson Memorial Highway". All that portion of
the state highway system constituting state route three hundred
ninety-four in the village of Lakewood, county of Chautauqua, shall be
designated and known as the "Lance Corporal Aaron M. Swanson Memorial
Highway".
  * NB There are 10 § 344-i's
```
