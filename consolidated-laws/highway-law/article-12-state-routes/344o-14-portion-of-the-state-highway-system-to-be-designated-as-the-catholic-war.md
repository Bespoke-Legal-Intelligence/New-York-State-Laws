# Highway Law § 344-O*14 — Portion of the state highway system to be designated as the "Catholic War Veterans Memorial Highway"

[Highway Law](../README.md) › [Article 12 — State Routes](README.md)

*An asterisk in a number (for example Article 24\* or § 20-C\*2) is the Senate's mark for a number the Legislature has used more than once: the marked article or section is a different one from the unmarked one with the same number. \*2 means the second one enacted with that number.*

```text
  * § 344-o. Portion of the state highway system to be designated as the
"Catholic War Veterans Memorial Highway". All that portion of the state
highway system constituting state route 32, from its intersection with
Quaker Avenue in the town of Cornwall, county of Orange, and continuing
north to its intersection with state routes 300 and 94, also known as
"Five Corners", in the hamlet of Vails Gate, county of Orange, shall be
designated and known as the "Catholic War Veterans Memorial Highway".
  * NB There are 18 § 344-o's
```
