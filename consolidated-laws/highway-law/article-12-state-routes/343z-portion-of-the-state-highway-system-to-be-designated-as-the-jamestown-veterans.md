# Highway Law § 343-Z — Portion of the state highway system to be designated as the "Jamestown Veterans Memorial Bridge"

[Highway Law](../README.md) › [Article 12 — State Routes](README.md)

```text
  * § 343-z. Portion of the state highway system to be designated as the
"Jamestown Veterans Memorial Bridge". The bridge across Steele Street
and the Chadakoin River on West Third Street in the city of Jamestown,
county of Chautauqua, shall be designated and known as the "Jamestown
Veterans Memorial Bridge".
  * NB There are 10 § 343-z's
```
