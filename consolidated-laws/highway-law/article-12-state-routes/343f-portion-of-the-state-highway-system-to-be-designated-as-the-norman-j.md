# Highway Law § 343-F — Portion of the state highway system to be designated as "The Norman J

[Highway Law](../README.md) › [Article 12 — State Routes](README.md)

```text
  * § 343-f. Portion of the state highway system to be designated as
"The Norman J. Levy Memorial Highway". All that portion of the state
highway system in the county of Nassau known as the Meadowbrook Parkway
shall on and after the effective date of this section be designated and
known as "The Norman J. Levy Memorial Highway".
  * NB There are 2 § 343-f's
```
