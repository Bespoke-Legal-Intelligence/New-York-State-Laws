# Highway Law § 344-E*3 — Portion of the state highway system to be designated as the "Col

[Highway Law](../README.md) › [Article 12 — State Routes](README.md)

*An asterisk in a number (for example Article 24\* or § 20-C\*2) is the Senate's mark for a number the Legislature has used more than once: the marked article or section is a different one from the unmarked one with the same number. \*2 means the second one enacted with that number.*

```text
  * § 344-e. Portion of the state highway system to be designated as the
"Col. David Ireland Memorial Highway". That portion of the state highway
system constituting state route four hundred thirty-four from its
intersection with Pennsylvania Avenue to its intersection with South
Washington Street, in the city of Binghamton, in the county of Broome,
shall each be designated and known as the "Col. David Ireland Memorial
Highway".
  * NB There are 13 § 344-e's
```
