# Highway Law § 344-P*2 — Portion of the state highway system to be designated as the "Peter Wheeler Memorial Bridge"

[Highway Law](../README.md) › [Article 12 — State Routes](README.md)

*An asterisk in a number (for example Article 24\* or § 20-C\*2) is the Senate's mark for a number the Legislature has used more than once: the marked article or section is a different one from the unmarked one with the same number. \*2 means the second one enacted with that number.*

```text
  * § 344-p. Portion of the state highway system to be designated as the
"Peter Wheeler Memorial Bridge". All that portion of the state highway
system constituting the bridge on state route thirty-four-B crossing
Salmon Creek in the town of Lansing, county of Tompkins, shall be
designated and known as the "Peter Wheeler Memorial Bridge".
  * NB There are 14 § 344-p's
```
