# Highway Law § 344-R*11 — Portion of the state highway system in Oneida county to be dedicated to Officer Michael E

[Highway Law](../README.md) › [Article 12 — State Routes](README.md)

*An asterisk in a number (for example Article 24\* or § 20-C\*2) is the Senate's mark for a number the Legislature has used more than once: the marked article or section is a different one from the unmarked one with the same number. \*2 means the second one enacted with that number.*

```text
  * § 344-r. Portion of the state highway system in Oneida county to be
dedicated to Officer Michael E. Jensen. All that portion of the state
highway system constituting state route forty-six in the city of Rome,
county of Oneida, beginning at its intersection with Prospect Street,
continuing in a generally northerly direction, and terminating at its
intersection with Dixon Drive in the town of Western, county of Oneida,
shall be dedicated to Officer Michael E. Jensen.
  * NB There are 12 § 344-r's
```
