# Highway Law § 343-O*2 — Portion of the state highway system to be designated as the "U

[Highway Law](../README.md) › [Article 12 — State Routes](README.md)

*An asterisk in a number (for example Article 24\* or § 20-C\*2) is the Senate's mark for a number the Legislature has used more than once: the marked article or section is a different one from the unmarked one with the same number. \*2 means the second one enacted with that number.*

```text
  * § 343-o. Portion of the state highway system to be designated as the
"U.S. Army 77th Infantry Division Expressway". All that portion of the
state highway system which is identified and known as the Clearview
Expressway (Interstate 295), shall be designated and known as the "U.S.
Army 77th Infantry Division Expressway".
  * NB There are 8 § 343-o's
```
