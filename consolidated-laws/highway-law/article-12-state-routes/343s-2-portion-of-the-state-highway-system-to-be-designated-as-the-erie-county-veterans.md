# Highway Law § 343-S*2 — Portion of the state highway system to be designated as the "Erie County Veterans' Memorial Highway"

[Highway Law](../README.md) › [Article 12 — State Routes](README.md)

*An asterisk in a number (for example Article 24\* or § 20-C\*2) is the Senate's mark for a number the Legislature has used more than once: the marked article or section is a different one from the unmarked one with the same number. \*2 means the second one enacted with that number.*

```text
  * § 343-s. Portion of the state highway system to be designated as the
"Erie County Veterans' Memorial Highway". All that portion of the state
highway system constituting United States route two hundred nineteen, in
Erie county from the interchange with interstate route ninety ("The
Thomas E. Dewey Thruway"), mile marker 219-5312-124.9, in the town of
West Seneca to the interchange at New York route three hundred
ninety-one, mile marker 219-5312-116.5, in the town of Hamburg shall be
designated and known as the "Erie County Veterans' Memorial Highway".
  * NB There are 12 § 343-s's
```
