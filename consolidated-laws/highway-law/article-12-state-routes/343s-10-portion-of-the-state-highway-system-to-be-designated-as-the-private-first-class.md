# Highway Law § 343-S*10 — Portion of the state highway system to be designated as the "Private First Class Gregory Huxley, Jr

[Highway Law](../README.md) › [Article 12 — State Routes](README.md)

*An asterisk in a number (for example Article 24\* or § 20-C\*2) is the Senate's mark for a number the Legislature has used more than once: the marked article or section is a different one from the unmarked one with the same number. \*2 means the second one enacted with that number.*

```text
  *  § 343-s. Portion of the state highway system to be designated as
the "Private First Class Gregory Huxley, Jr. Memorial Bridge". The
bridge on route 28 over the Forestport reservoir, BIN # 1020210, in the
county of Oneida, shall be designated and known as the "Private First
Class Gregory Huxley, Jr. Memorial Bridge".
  * NB There are 12 § 343-s's
```
