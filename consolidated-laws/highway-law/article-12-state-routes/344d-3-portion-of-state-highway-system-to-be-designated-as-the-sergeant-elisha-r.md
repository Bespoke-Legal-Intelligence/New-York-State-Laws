# Highway Law § 344-D*3 — Portion of state highway system to be designated as the "Sergeant Elisha R

[Highway Law](../README.md) › [Article 12 — State Routes](README.md)

*An asterisk in a number (for example Article 24\* or § 20-C\*2) is the Senate's mark for a number the Legislature has used more than once: the marked article or section is a different one from the unmarked one with the same number. \*2 means the second one enacted with that number.*

```text
  * § 344-d. Portion of state highway system to be designated as the
"Sergeant Elisha R. Parker Veterans Memorial Highway". That portion of
state route thirteen in the village of Camden in the county of Oneida
from its intersection with county route eighty-four, known as Florence
Hill Road, and continuing southerly to the village line, shall be
designated and known as the "Sergeant Elisha R. Parker Veterans Memorial
Highway".
  * NB There are 16 § 344-d's
```
