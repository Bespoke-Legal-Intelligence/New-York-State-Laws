# Highway Law § 343-V — Portion of the state highway system to be designated as the "Lance Cpl

[Highway Law](../README.md) › [Article 12 — State Routes](README.md)

```text
  * § 343-v. Portion of the state highway system to be designated as the
"Lance Cpl. Jordan Haerter Veterans' Memorial Bridge". The bridge on
state route 114 connecting the village of Sag Harbor with the village of
North Haven crossing Sag Harbor and Sag Harbor Cove shall be designated
and known as the "Lance Cpl. Jordan Haerter Veterans' Memorial Bridge."
  * NB There are 5 § 343-v's
```
