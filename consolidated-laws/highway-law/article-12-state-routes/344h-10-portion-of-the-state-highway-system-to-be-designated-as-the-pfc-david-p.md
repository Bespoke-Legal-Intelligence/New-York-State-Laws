# Highway Law § 344-H*10 — Portion of the state highway system to be designated as the "PFC David P

[Highway Law](../README.md) › [Article 12 — State Routes](README.md)

*An asterisk in a number (for example Article 24\* or § 20-C\*2) is the Senate's mark for a number the Legislature has used more than once: the marked article or section is a different one from the unmarked one with the same number. \*2 means the second one enacted with that number.*

```text
  * § 344-h. Portion of the state highway system to be designated as the
"PFC David P. Coveny Memorial Highway". All that portion of the state
highway system constituting state route nineteen in the county of
Wyoming, between Sayre Road and Fox Road, in the towns of Warsaw and
Middlebury, shall be designated and known as the "PFC David P. Coveny
Memorial Highway".
  * NB There are 14 § 344-h's
```
