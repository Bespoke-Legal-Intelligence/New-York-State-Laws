# Highway Law § 343-K*6 — Portion of the state highway system to be designated as the "Joseph A

[Highway Law](../README.md) › [Article 12 — State Routes](README.md)

*An asterisk in a number (for example Article 24\* or § 20-C\*2) is the Senate's mark for a number the Legislature has used more than once: the marked article or section is a different one from the unmarked one with the same number. \*2 means the second one enacted with that number.*

```text
  * § 343-k. Portion of the state highway system to be designated as the
"Joseph A. Scapelliti Memorial Bridge". The bridge crossing the New York
state barge canal on Exchange Street between Market Street and Van Buren
Street in the city of Lockport shall be designated and known as the
"Joseph A. Scapelliti Memorial Bridge".
  * NB There are 7 § 343-k's
```
