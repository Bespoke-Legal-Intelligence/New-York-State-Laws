# Highway Law § 344-F — Portion of state highway system to be designated as the "Specialist Matthew E

[Highway Law](../README.md) › [Article 12 — State Routes](README.md)

```text
  § 344-f. Portion of state highway system to be designated as the
"Specialist Matthew E. Baylis Memorial Bridge". The bridge on
Oakdale-Bohemia Road, exit number 47A over route 27 in the town of
Islip, county of Suffolk, shall be designated and known as the
"Specialist Matthew E. Baylis Memorial Bridge".
```
