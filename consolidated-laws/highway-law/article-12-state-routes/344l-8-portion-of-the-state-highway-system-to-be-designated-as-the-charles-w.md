# Highway Law § 344-L*8 — Portion of the state highway system to be designated as the "Charles W

[Highway Law](../README.md) › [Article 12 — State Routes](README.md)

*An asterisk in a number (for example Article 24\* or § 20-C\*2) is the Senate's mark for a number the Legislature has used more than once: the marked article or section is a different one from the unmarked one with the same number. \*2 means the second one enacted with that number.*

```text
  * § 344-l. Portion of the state highway system to be designated as the
"Charles W. Howard Memorial Highway". All that portion of the state
highway system constituting state route thirty-one from the intersection
of state route thirty-one and Transit Road to the intersection of state
route thirty-one and Wood Road, in the town of Albion, county of
Orleans, shall be designated and known as the "Charles W. Howard
Memorial Highway".
  * NB There are 8 § 344-l's
```
