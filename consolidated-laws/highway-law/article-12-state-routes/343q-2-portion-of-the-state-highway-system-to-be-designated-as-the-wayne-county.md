# Highway Law § 343-Q*2 — Portion of the state highway system to be designated as the "Wayne County Veteran's Memorial Highway"

[Highway Law](../README.md) › [Article 12 — State Routes](README.md)

*An asterisk in a number (for example Article 24\* or § 20-C\*2) is the Senate's mark for a number the Legislature has used more than once: the marked article or section is a different one from the unmarked one with the same number. \*2 means the second one enacted with that number.*

```text
  * § 343-q. Portion of the state highway system to be designated as the
"Wayne County Veteran's Memorial Highway". All that portion of the state
highway system constituting state route 31 in Wayne county, from the
Monroe county line to the Seneca county line, shall be designated and
known as the "Wayne County Veteran's Memorial Highway".
  * NB There are 11 § 343-q's
```
