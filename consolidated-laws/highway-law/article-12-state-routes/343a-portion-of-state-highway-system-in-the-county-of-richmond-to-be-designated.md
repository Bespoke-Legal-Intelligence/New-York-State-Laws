# Highway Law § 343-A — Portion of state highway system in the county of Richmond to be designated as "The Korean War Veterans Memorial Parkway"

[Highway Law](../README.md) › [Article 12 — State Routes](README.md)

```text
  * § 343-a. Portion of state highway system in the county of Richmond
to be designated as "The Korean War Veterans Memorial Parkway". All that
portion of the state highway system in the county of Richmond
constituting the Richmond Parkway from Staten Island Expressway to
Outerbridge Crossing Approach shall be designated and known as "The
Korean War Veterans Memorial Parkway".
  * NB There are 4 § 343-a's
```
