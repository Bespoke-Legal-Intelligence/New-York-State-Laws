# Highway Law § 344-N*13 — Portion of the state highway system in Oneida county to be designated as the "Oneida County Korean War Veterans Memorial Highway"

[Highway Law](../README.md) › [Article 12 — State Routes](README.md)

*An asterisk in a number (for example Article 24\* or § 20-C\*2) is the Senate's mark for a number the Legislature has used more than once: the marked article or section is a different one from the unmarked one with the same number. \*2 means the second one enacted with that number.*

```text
  * § 344-n. Portion of the state highway system in Oneida county to be
designated as the "Oneida County Korean War Veterans Memorial Highway".
All that portion of state route three hundred sixty-five beginning from
the westerly Rome city line to Lawrence Street in the city of Rome,
county of Oneida, shall be designated and known as the "Oneida County
Korean War Veterans Memorial Highway".
  * NB There are 13 § 344-n's
```
