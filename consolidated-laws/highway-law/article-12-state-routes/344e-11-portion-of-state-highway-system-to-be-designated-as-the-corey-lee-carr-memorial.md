# Highway Law § 344-E*11 — Portion of state highway system to be designated as the "Corey Lee Carr Memorial Bridge"

[Highway Law](../README.md) › [Article 12 — State Routes](README.md)

*An asterisk in a number (for example Article 24\* or § 20-C\*2) is the Senate's mark for a number the Legislature has used more than once: the marked article or section is a different one from the unmarked one with the same number. \*2 means the second one enacted with that number.*

```text
  * § 344-e. Portion of state highway system to be designated as the
"Corey Lee Carr Memorial Bridge". The pedestrian bridge across state
routes five, eight and twelve, also known as the north-south arterial,
in the city of Utica, county of Oneida, shall be designated and known as
the "Corey Lee Carr Memorial Bridge".
  * NB There are 13 § 344-e's
```
