# Highway Law § 344-E*7 — Portion of the state highway system to be designated as the "Sergeant Kevin W

[Highway Law](../README.md) › [Article 12 — State Routes](README.md)

*An asterisk in a number (for example Article 24\* or § 20-C\*2) is the Senate's mark for a number the Legislature has used more than once: the marked article or section is a different one from the unmarked one with the same number. \*2 means the second one enacted with that number.*

```text
  * § 344-e. Portion of the state highway system to be designated as the
"Sergeant Kevin W. White Memorial Bridge". The bridge on U.S. route
twenty, between Oak street and South Portage street, in the town of
Westfield, county of Chautauqua, shall be designated and known as the
"Sergeant Kevin W. White Memorial Bridge".
  * NB There are 14 § 344-e's
```
