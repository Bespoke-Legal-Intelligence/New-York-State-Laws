# Highway Law § 342-D — Portion of state highway system to be designated as the "Col

[Highway Law](../README.md) › [Article 12 — State Routes](README.md)

```text
  § 342-d. Portion of state highway system to be designated as the "Col.
George F. Chandler Drive." From exit nineteen of "The Governor Thomas E.
Dewey Thruway" and Edison Plaza at the traffic interchange in Ulster
County at Kingston, shall be designated as "Col. George F. Chandler
Drive."
```
