# Highway Law § 342-O — Portion of state highway system to be designated as "Chautauqua County Veterans Memorial Bridge"

[Highway Law](../README.md) › [Article 12 — State Routes](README.md)

```text
  § 342-o. Portion of state highway system to be designated as
"Chautauqua County Veterans Memorial Bridge". The bridge across
Chautauqua Lake from Bemus Point to Stow on the Southern Tier Expressway
shall be designated and known as "Chautauqua County Veterans Memorial
Bridge".
```
