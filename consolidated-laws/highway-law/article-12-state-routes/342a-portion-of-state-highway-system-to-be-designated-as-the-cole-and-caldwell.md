# Highway Law § 342-A — Portion of state highway system to be designated as "The Cole and Caldwell Memorial Bridge"

[Highway Law](../README.md) › [Article 12 — State Routes](README.md)

```text
  § 342-a. Portion of state highway system to be designated as "The Cole
and Caldwell Memorial Bridge". The bridge across the Racquette river at
Hogansburg on state highway eight thousand three hundred ninety-six
running through the St. Regis Indian reservation shall be designated and
known as "The Cole and Caldwell Memorial Bridge" in honor of the late
Francis Cole and Michael Caldwell, who were St. Regis Mohawk Indians,
and both of whom lost their lives because of accidents while engaged in
the course of their employment in the work of construction on such
bridge.
```
