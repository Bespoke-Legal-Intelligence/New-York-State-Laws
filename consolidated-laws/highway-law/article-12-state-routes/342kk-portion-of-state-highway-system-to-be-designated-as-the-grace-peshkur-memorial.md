# Highway Law § 342-KK — Portion of state highway system to be designated as the "Grace Peshkur Memorial Bridge"

[Highway Law](../README.md) › [Article 12 — State Routes](README.md)

```text
  * § 342-kk. Portion of state highway system to be designated as the
"Grace Peshkur Memorial Bridge". The bridge on Waverly Avenue which
crosses interstate route 495 in the town of Islip, county of Suffolk,
shall be designated and known as the "Grace Peshkur Memorial Bridge".
  * NB There are 2 § 342-kk's
```
