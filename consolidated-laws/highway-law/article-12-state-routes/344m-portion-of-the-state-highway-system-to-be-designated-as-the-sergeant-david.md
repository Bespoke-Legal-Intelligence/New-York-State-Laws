# Highway Law § 344-M — Portion of the state highway system to be designated as the "Sergeant David Lockwood Memorial Bridge"

[Highway Law](../README.md) › [Article 12 — State Routes](README.md)

```text
  * § 344-m. Portion of the state highway system to be designated as the
"Sergeant David Lockwood Memorial Bridge". The bridge over Schoolhouse
road on interstate route eighty-six, in the town of Randolph, county of
Cattaraugus, shall be designated and known as the "Sergeant David
Lockwood Memorial Bridge".
  * NB There are 22 § 344-m's
```
