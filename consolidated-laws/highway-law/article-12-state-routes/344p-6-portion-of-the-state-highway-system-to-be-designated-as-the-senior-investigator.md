# Highway Law § 344-P*6 — Portion of the state highway system to be designated as the "Senior Investigator Thomas M

[Highway Law](../README.md) › [Article 12 — State Routes](README.md)

*An asterisk in a number (for example Article 24\* or § 20-C\*2) is the Senate's mark for a number the Legislature has used more than once: the marked article or section is a different one from the unmarked one with the same number. \*2 means the second one enacted with that number.*

```text
  * § 344-p. Portion of the state highway system to be designated as the
"Senior Investigator Thomas M. O'Neill Memorial Bridge". All that
portion of the state highway system constituting the Crooked Hill Road
Overpass crossing over the Sagtikos Parkway in the town of Islip, county
of Suffolk, shall be designated and known as the "Senior Investigator
Thomas M. O'Neill Memorial Bridge".
  * NB There are 7 § 344-p's
```
