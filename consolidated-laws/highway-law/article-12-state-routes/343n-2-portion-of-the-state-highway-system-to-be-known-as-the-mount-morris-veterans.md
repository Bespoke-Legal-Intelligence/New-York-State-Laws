# Highway Law § 343-N*2 — Portion of the state highway system to be known as the "Mount Morris Veterans Memorial Highway"

[Highway Law](../README.md) › [Article 12 — State Routes](README.md)

*An asterisk in a number (for example Article 24\* or § 20-C\*2) is the Senate's mark for a number the Legislature has used more than once: the marked article or section is a different one from the unmarked one with the same number. \*2 means the second one enacted with that number.*

```text
  * § 343-n. Portion of the state highway system to be known as the
"Mount Morris Veterans Memorial Highway". All that portion of the state
highway system constituting state route thirty-six within the village of
Mount Morris, county of Livingston, shall be designated and known as the
"Mount Morris Veterans Memorial Highway".
  * NB There are 3 § 343-n's
```
