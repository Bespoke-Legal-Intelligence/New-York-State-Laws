# Highway Law § 342-OO — Portion of the state highway system to be designated as "The Bronx Veterans' Memorial Highway"

[Highway Law](../README.md) › [Article 12 — State Routes](README.md)

```text
  § 342-oo. Portion of the state highway system to be designated as "The
Bronx Veterans' Memorial Highway". All that portion of the state highway
system, currently known as the Bronx River Parkway, from the Westchester
county line to the Bruckner expressway shall be designated and known as
"The Bronx Veterans' Memorial Highway".
```
