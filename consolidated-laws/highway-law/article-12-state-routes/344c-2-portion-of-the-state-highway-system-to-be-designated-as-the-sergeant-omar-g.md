# Highway Law § 344-C*2 — Portion of the state highway system to be designated as the "Sergeant Omar G

[Highway Law](../README.md) › [Article 12 — State Routes](README.md)

*An asterisk in a number (for example Article 24\* or § 20-C\*2) is the Senate's mark for a number the Legislature has used more than once: the marked article or section is a different one from the unmarked one with the same number. \*2 means the second one enacted with that number.*

```text
  * § 344-c. Portion of the state highway system to be designated as the
"Sergeant Omar G. Blowers Memorial Highway". All that portion of the
state highway system constituting state route seventy in the county of
Allegany, beginning at the Allegany and Steuben county line continuing
in a generally westerly direction, and terminating at the Allegany and
Livingston county line, shall be designated and known as the "Sergeant
Omar G. Blowers Memorial Highway".
  * NB There are 7 § 344-c's
```
