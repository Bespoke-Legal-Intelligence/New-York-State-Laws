# Highway Law § 344-S*8 — Portion of the state highway system to be dedicated to Patrick LaFortune Jr

[Highway Law](../README.md) › [Article 12 — State Routes](README.md)

*An asterisk in a number (for example Article 24\* or § 20-C\*2) is the Senate's mark for a number the Legislature has used more than once: the marked article or section is a different one from the unmarked one with the same number. \*2 means the second one enacted with that number.*

```text
  * § 344-s. Portion of the state highway system to be dedicated to
Patrick LaFortune Jr. All that portion of the state highway system
constituting state route nine-W beginning where such state route crosses
the town line from the city of Albany into the town of Bethlehem, county
of Albany, and extending until the intersection of such state route and
Moriah road in the town of Bethlehem, county of Albany, shall be
dedicated to Patrick LaFortune Jr.
  * NB There are 9 § 344-s's
```
