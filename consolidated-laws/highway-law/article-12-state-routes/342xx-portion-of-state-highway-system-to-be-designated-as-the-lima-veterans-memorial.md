# Highway Law § 342-XX — Portion of state highway system to be designated as the "Lima Veterans Memorial Highway"

[Highway Law](../README.md) › [Article 12 — State Routes](README.md)

```text
  § 342-xx. Portion of state highway system to be designated as the
"Lima Veterans Memorial Highway". That portion of state route 15A
located within the boundaries of the town of Lima, county of Livingston,
a distance of approximately six and two-tenths miles, shall be
designated and known as the "Lima Veterans Memorial Highway".
```
