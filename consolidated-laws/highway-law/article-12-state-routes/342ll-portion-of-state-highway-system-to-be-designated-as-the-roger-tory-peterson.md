# Highway Law § 342-LL — Portion of state highway system to be designated as the "Roger Tory Peterson Memorial Bridge"

[Highway Law](../README.md) › [Article 12 — State Routes](README.md)

```text
  § 342-ll. Portion of state highway system to be designated as the
"Roger Tory Peterson Memorial Bridge". The bridge on interstate route
eighty-six which crosses Falconer - Kimball Stand Road/county route one
hundred thirty-eight in the town of Ellicott, county of Chautauqua,
shall be designated and known as the "Roger Tory Peterson Memorial
Bridge".
```
