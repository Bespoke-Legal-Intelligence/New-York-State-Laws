# Highway Law § 343-S*11 — Portion of the state highway system to be designated as the "Justin Wrisley Garvey Memorial Bridge"

[Highway Law](../README.md) › [Article 12 — State Routes](README.md)

*An asterisk in a number (for example Article 24\* or § 20-C\*2) is the Senate's mark for a number the Legislature has used more than once: the marked article or section is a different one from the unmarked one with the same number. \*2 means the second one enacted with that number.*

```text
  * § 343-s. Portion of the state highway system to be designated as the
"Justin Wrisley Garvey Memorial Bridge". The bridge on county road 12
across interstate 87, exit 32, in the town of Lewis, county of Essex,
shall be designated and known as the "Justin Wrisley Garvey Memorial
Bridge".
  * NB There are 12 § 343-s's
```
