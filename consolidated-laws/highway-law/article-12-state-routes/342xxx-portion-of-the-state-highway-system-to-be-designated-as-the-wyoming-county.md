# Highway Law § 342-XXX — Portion of the state highway system to be designated as the "Wyoming County Veterans Memorial Highway"

[Highway Law](../README.md) › [Article 12 — State Routes](README.md)

```text
  § 342-xxx. Portion of the state highway system to be designated as the
"Wyoming County Veterans Memorial Highway". All that portion of the
state highway system known as United States route 20A beginning at the
western county line of the county of Wyoming and ending at the eastern
county line of the county of Wyoming, shall be designated and known as
the "Wyoming County Veterans Memorial Highway".
```
