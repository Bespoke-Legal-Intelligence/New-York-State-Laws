# Highway Law § 342-GG — Portion of state highway system to be designated as the "Borden Bridge"

[Highway Law](../README.md) › [Article 12 — State Routes](README.md)

```text
  § 342-gg. Portion of state highway system to be designated as the
"Borden Bridge". All that portion of the state highway system
constituting bridge number 1003520, located at the junction of state
routes 6 and 22, crossing the Croton River shall be designated and known
as the "Borden Bridge".
```
