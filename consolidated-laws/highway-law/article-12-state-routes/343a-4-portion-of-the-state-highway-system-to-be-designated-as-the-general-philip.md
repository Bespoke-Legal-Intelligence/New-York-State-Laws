# Highway Law § 343-A*4 — Portion of the state highway system to be designated as the "General Philip Schuyler Commemorative Highway"

[Highway Law](../README.md) › [Article 12 — State Routes](README.md)

*An asterisk in a number (for example Article 24\* or § 20-C\*2) is the Senate's mark for a number the Legislature has used more than once: the marked article or section is a different one from the unmarked one with the same number. \*2 means the second one enacted with that number.*

```text
  * § 343-a. Portion of the state highway system to be designated as the
"General Philip Schuyler Commemorative Highway". All that portion of the
state highway system known as state route 29, bounded on the east by the
intersection of U.S. routes 4 and 32 and bounded on the west by the
intersection of Henning Road and state route 29, in the county of
Saratoga shall be designated and known as the "General Philip Schuyler
Commemorative Highway".
  * NB There are 4 § 343-a's
```
