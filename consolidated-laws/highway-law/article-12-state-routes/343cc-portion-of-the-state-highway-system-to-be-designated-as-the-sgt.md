# Highway Law § 343-CC — Portion of the state highway system to be designated as the "Sgt

[Highway Law](../README.md) › [Article 12 — State Routes](README.md)

```text
  * § 343-cc. Portion of the state highway system to be designated as
the "Sgt. Michael J. Esposito Jr. Memorial Bridge". The bridge on
Washington Avenue which crosses over Interstate four hundred ninety-five
in the hamlet of Brentwood, in the town of Islip, county of Suffolk,
shall be designated as the "Sgt. Michael J. Esposito Jr. Memorial
Bridge".
  * NB There are 2 § 343-cc's
```
