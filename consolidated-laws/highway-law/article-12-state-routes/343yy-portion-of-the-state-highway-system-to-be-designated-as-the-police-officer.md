# Highway Law § 343-YY — Portion of the state highway system to be designated as the "Police Officer Joseph P

[Highway Law](../README.md) › [Article 12 — State Routes](README.md)

```text
  * § 343-yy. Portion of the state highway system to be designated as
the "Police Officer Joseph P. Olivieri Jr. Memorial Bridge". The bridge
on New Hyde Park Road which crosses Interstate four hundred ninety-five
in the town of North Hempstead, county of Nassau, shall be designated as
the "Police Officer Joseph P. Olivieri Jr. Memorial Bridge".
  * NB There are 2 § 343-yy's
```
