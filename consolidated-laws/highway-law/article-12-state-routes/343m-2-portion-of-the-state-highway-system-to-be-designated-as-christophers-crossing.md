# Highway Law § 343-M*2 — Portion of the state highway system to be designated as "Christopher's Crossing"

[Highway Law](../README.md) › [Article 12 — State Routes](README.md)

*An asterisk in a number (for example Article 24\* or § 20-C\*2) is the Senate's mark for a number the Legislature has used more than once: the marked article or section is a different one from the unmarked one with the same number. \*2 means the second one enacted with that number.*

```text
  * § 343-m. Portion of the state highway system to be designated as
"Christopher's Crossing". Notwithstanding any other provision of law to
the contrary, all that portion of the state highway system in the county
of Queens constituting the overpass at Forty-sixth Avenue and the
Clearview expressway shall be designated and known as "Christopher's
Crossing".
  * NB There are 4 § 343-m's
```
