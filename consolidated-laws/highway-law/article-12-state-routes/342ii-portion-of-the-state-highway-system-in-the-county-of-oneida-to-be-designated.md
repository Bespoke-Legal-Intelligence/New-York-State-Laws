# Highway Law § 342-II — Portion of the state highway system in the county of Oneida to be designated as the "Military Highway"

[Highway Law](../README.md) › [Article 12 — State Routes](README.md)

```text
  * § 342-ii. Portion of the state highway system in the county of
Oneida to be designated as the "Military Highway". All that portion of
the state highway system in the county of Oneida constituting state
route 69 from the village of Camden to the hamlet of Taberg shall be
designated and known as the "Military Highway".
  * NB There are 3 § 342-ii's
```
