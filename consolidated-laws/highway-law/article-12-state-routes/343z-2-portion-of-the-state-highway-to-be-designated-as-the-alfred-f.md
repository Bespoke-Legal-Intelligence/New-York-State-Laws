# Highway Law § 343-Z*2 — Portion of the state highway to be designated as the "Alfred F

[Highway Law](../README.md) › [Article 12 — State Routes](README.md)

*An asterisk in a number (for example Article 24\* or § 20-C\*2) is the Senate's mark for a number the Legislature has used more than once: the marked article or section is a different one from the unmarked one with the same number. \*2 means the second one enacted with that number.*

```text
  * § 343-z. Portion of the state highway to be designated as the
"Alfred F. Jones Memorial Bridge". The bridge on state route
seventy-six, also known as Osborne street, which crosses interstate
route eighty-six in the village of Sherman, county of Chautauqua, shall
be designated and known as the "Alfred F. Jones Memorial Bridge".
  * NB There are 10 § 343-z's
```
