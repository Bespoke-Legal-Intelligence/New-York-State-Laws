# Highway Law § 344-O*17 — Portion of the state highway system to be designated and known as the "Specialist Peter J

[Highway Law](../README.md) › [Article 12 — State Routes](README.md)

*An asterisk in a number (for example Article 24\* or § 20-C\*2) is the Senate's mark for a number the Legislature has used more than once: the marked article or section is a different one from the unmarked one with the same number. \*2 means the second one enacted with that number.*

```text
  * § 344-o. Portion of the state highway system to be designated and
known as the "Specialist Peter J. Loretto, Jr. Memorial Bridge". The
bridge located on state route 75, designated as bridge number 1029890,
which crosses Eighteen Mile Creek, in the town of Eden, county of Erie,
shall be designated and known as the "Specialist Peter J. Loretto, Jr.
Memorial Bridge".
  * NB There are 18 § 344-o's
```
