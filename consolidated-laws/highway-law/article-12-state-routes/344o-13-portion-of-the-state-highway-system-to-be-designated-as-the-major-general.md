# Highway Law § 344-O*13 — Portion of the state highway system to be designated as the "Major General Norbert J

[Highway Law](../README.md) › [Article 12 — State Routes](README.md)

*An asterisk in a number (for example Article 24\* or § 20-C\*2) is the Senate's mark for a number the Legislature has used more than once: the marked article or section is a different one from the unmarked one with the same number. \*2 means the second one enacted with that number.*

```text
  * § 344-o. Portion of the state highway system to be designated as the
"Major General Norbert J. Rappl Memorial Highway". All that portion of
the state highway system constituting state route five hundred ninety
between Ridge Road in the Town of Irondequoit and the overpass carrying
Tryon Park over state route five hundred ninety in the city of
Rochester, County of Monroe, shall be known and designated as the "Major
General Norbert J. Rappl Memorial Highway".
  * NB There are 18 § 344-o's
```
