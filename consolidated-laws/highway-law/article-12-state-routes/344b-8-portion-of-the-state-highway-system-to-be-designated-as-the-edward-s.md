# Highway Law § 344-B*8 — Portion of the state highway system to be designated as the "Edward S

[Highway Law](../README.md) › [Article 12 — State Routes](README.md)

*An asterisk in a number (for example Article 24\* or § 20-C\*2) is the Senate's mark for a number the Legislature has used more than once: the marked article or section is a different one from the unmarked one with the same number. \*2 means the second one enacted with that number.*

```text
  * § 344-b. Portion of the state highway system to be designated as the
"Edward S. Tucker Memorial Boulevard". That portion of New York state
route 208 in the town of Shawangunk, in Ulster county starting at the
intersection with park avenue, proceeding northeasterly through said
town and terminating at its intersection with reservoir road shall be
designated and known as the "Edward S. Tucker Memorial Boulevard".
  * NB There are 8 § 344-b's
```
