# Highway Law § 343-R — Portion of the state highway system to be designated as the "Trooper Robert G

[Highway Law](../README.md) › [Article 12 — State Routes](README.md)

```text
  § 343-r. Portion of the state highway system to be designated as the
"Trooper Robert G. Dunning Memorial Bridge". The bridge across the
Taconic State Parkway on Bulls Head Road, in the town of Stanford,
county of Dutchess, shall be designated and known as the "Trooper Robert
G. Dunning Memorial Bridge".
```
