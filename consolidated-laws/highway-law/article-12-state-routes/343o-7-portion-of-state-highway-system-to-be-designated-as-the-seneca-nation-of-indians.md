# Highway Law § 343-O*7 — Portion of state highway system to be designated as the "Seneca Nation of Indians, City of Salamanca Veterans Memorial Bridge"

[Highway Law](../README.md) › [Article 12 — State Routes](README.md)

*An asterisk in a number (for example Article 24\* or § 20-C\*2) is the Senate's mark for a number the Legislature has used more than once: the marked article or section is a different one from the unmarked one with the same number. \*2 means the second one enacted with that number.*

```text
  * § 343-o. Portion of state highway system to be designated as the
"Seneca Nation of Indians, City of Salamanca Veterans Memorial Bridge".
The bridge crossing the Allegany river on Church street between Parkway
drive and Wildwood avenue in the city of Salamanca shall be designated
and known as the "Seneca Nation of Indians, City of Salamanca Veterans
Memorial Bridge".
  * NB There are 8 § 343-o's
```
