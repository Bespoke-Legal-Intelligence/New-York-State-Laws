# Highway Law § 344-M*13 — Portion of the state highway system to be designated and known as the "State Trooper Jose A

[Highway Law](../README.md) › [Article 12 — State Routes](README.md)

*An asterisk in a number (for example Article 24\* or § 20-C\*2) is the Senate's mark for a number the Legislature has used more than once: the marked article or section is a different one from the unmarked one with the same number. \*2 means the second one enacted with that number.*

```text
  * § 344-m. Portion of the state highway system to be designated and
known as the "State Trooper Jose A. Rosado Memorial Bridge". All that
portion of the state highway system constituting the Sunrise Highway
Speonk Riverhead Rd. overpass on route 27, in the town of Southampton,
county of Suffolk, shall be known as the "State Trooper Jose A. Rosado
Memorial Bridge".
  * NB There are 22 § 344-m's
```
