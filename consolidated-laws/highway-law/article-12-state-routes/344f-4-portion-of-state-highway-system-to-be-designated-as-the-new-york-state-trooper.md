# Highway Law § 344-F*4 — Portion of state highway system to be designated as the "New York State Trooper Timothy Pratt Memorial Bridge"

[Highway Law](../README.md) › [Article 12 — State Routes](README.md)

*An asterisk in a number (for example Article 24\* or § 20-C\*2) is the Senate's mark for a number the Legislature has used more than once: the marked article or section is a different one from the unmarked one with the same number. \*2 means the second one enacted with that number.*

```text
  * § 344-f. Portion of state highway system to be designated as the
"New York State Trooper Timothy Pratt Memorial Bridge". The bridge on
county route 33 (Ballard Road) which crosses interstate 87, exit 16, in
the town of Wilton, county of Saratoga, shall be designated and known as
the "New York State Trooper Timothy Pratt Memorial Bridge".
  * NB There are 6 § 344-f's
```
