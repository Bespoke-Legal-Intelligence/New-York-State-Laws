# Highway Law § 343-S*4 — Portion of the state highway system to be designated as the "Rensselaer County Veterans' Memorial Highway"

[Highway Law](../README.md) › [Article 12 — State Routes](README.md)

*An asterisk in a number (for example Article 24\* or § 20-C\*2) is the Senate's mark for a number the Legislature has used more than once: the marked article or section is a different one from the unmarked one with the same number. \*2 means the second one enacted with that number.*

```text
  * § 343-s. Portion of the state highway system to be designated as the
"Rensselaer County Veterans' Memorial Highway". That portion of the
state highway system constituting interstate route ninety in the county
of Rensselaer, from the Albany county line to the exit B-1 toll barrier
of the New York state thruway, shall be designated and known as the
"Rensselaer County Veterans' Memorial Highway".
  * NB There are 12 § 343-s's
```
