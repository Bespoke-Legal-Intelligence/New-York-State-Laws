# Highway Law § 343-X*3 — Portion of state highway system to be designated as "Cpl

[Highway Law](../README.md) › [Article 12 — State Routes](README.md)

*An asterisk in a number (for example Article 24\* or § 20-C\*2) is the Senate's mark for a number the Legislature has used more than once: the marked article or section is a different one from the unmarked one with the same number. \*2 means the second one enacted with that number.*

```text
  * § 343-x. Portion of state highway system to be designated as "Cpl.
Paulo Marko Pacificador Memorial Bridge". The bridge on the William
Floyd Parkway which crosses over State Route 27 in the town of
Brookhaven, Suffolk county, shall be designated and known as "Cpl. Paulo
Marko Pacificador Memorial Bridge".
  * NB There are 5 § 343-x's
```
