# Highway Law § 342-PP — Portion of state highway system to be designated as the "Mayor Frank Ratigan Memorial Bridge"

[Highway Law](../README.md) › [Article 12 — State Routes](README.md)

```text
  § 342-pp. Portion of state highway system to be designated as the
"Mayor Frank Ratigan Memorial Bridge". The bridge on the portion of
state route 3 in the village of Saranac Lake in the county of Franklin
shall be designated and known as the "Mayor Frank Ratigan Memorial
Bridge".
```
