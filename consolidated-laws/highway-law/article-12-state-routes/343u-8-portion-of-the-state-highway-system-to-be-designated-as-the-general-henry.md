# Highway Law § 343-U*8 — Portion of the state highway system to be designated as the "General Henry Halleck Memorial Bridge"

[Highway Law](../README.md) › [Article 12 — State Routes](README.md)

*An asterisk in a number (for example Article 24\* or § 20-C\*2) is the Senate's mark for a number the Legislature has used more than once: the marked article or section is a different one from the unmarked one with the same number. \*2 means the second one enacted with that number.*

```text
  * § 343-u. Portion of the state highway system to be designated as the
"General Henry Halleck Memorial Bridge". The bridge on
Stokes-Westernville Road crossing the Mohawk River in the county of
Oneida shall be designated and known as the "General Henry Halleck
Memorial Bridge".
  * NB There are 8 § 343-u's
```
