# Highway Law § 344-M*19 — Portion of the state highway system to be designated and known as the "Staff Sergeant Peter J

[Highway Law](../README.md) › [Article 12 — State Routes](README.md)

*An asterisk in a number (for example Article 24\* or § 20-C\*2) is the Senate's mark for a number the Legislature has used more than once: the marked article or section is a different one from the unmarked one with the same number. \*2 means the second one enacted with that number.*

```text
  * § 344-m. Portion of the state highway system to be designated and
known as the "Staff Sergeant Peter J. Blidy Memorial Bridge". All that
portion of the state highway system constituting the bridge on
Versailles Plank Road, designated bridge number 6050680, which crosses
Clear creek one and one-half miles northeast of Versailles, in the town
of Collins, county of Erie, shall be known as the "Staff Sergeant Peter
J. Blidy Memorial Bridge".
  * NB There are 19 § 344-m's
```
