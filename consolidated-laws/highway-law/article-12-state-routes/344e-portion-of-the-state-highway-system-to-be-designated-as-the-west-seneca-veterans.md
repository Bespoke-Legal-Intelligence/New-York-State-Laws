# Highway Law § 344-E — Portion of the state highway system to be designated as the "West Seneca Veterans Memorial Highway"

[Highway Law](../README.md) › [Article 12 — State Routes](README.md)

```text
  * § 344-e. Portion of the state highway system to be designated as the
"West Seneca Veterans Memorial Highway". All that portion of the state
highway system constituting state route two hundred seventy-seven in the
town of West Seneca, county of Erie, shall be designated and known as
the "West Seneca Veterans Memorial Highway".
  * NB There are 13 § 344-e's
```
