# Highway Law § 343-Q*6 — Portion of state highway system to be designated as the "Chautauqua County Veterans Memorial Highway"

[Highway Law](../README.md) › [Article 12 — State Routes](README.md)

*An asterisk in a number (for example Article 24\* or § 20-C\*2) is the Senate's mark for a number the Legislature has used more than once: the marked article or section is a different one from the unmarked one with the same number. \*2 means the second one enacted with that number.*

```text
  * § 343-q. Portion of state highway system to be designated as the
"Chautauqua County Veterans Memorial Highway". All that portion of the
state highway system in the county of Chautauqua constituting interstate
route eighty-six commencing at its intersection with interstate route
ninety and terminating at the Cattaraugus county border shall be
designated and known as the "Chautauqua County Veterans Memorial
Highway".
  * NB There are 11 § 343-q's
```
