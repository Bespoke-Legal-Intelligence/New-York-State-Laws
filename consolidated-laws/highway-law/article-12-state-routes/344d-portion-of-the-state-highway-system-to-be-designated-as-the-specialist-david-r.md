# Highway Law § 344-D — Portion of the state highway system to be designated as the "Specialist David R

[Highway Law](../README.md) › [Article 12 — State Routes](README.md)

```text
  * § 344-d. Portion of the state highway system to be designated as the
"Specialist David R. Fahey Jr. Memorial Bridge". The bridge across the
New Croton reservoir on state route one hundred twenty-nine, in the town
of Yorktown, county of Westchester, shall be designated and known as the
"Specialist David R. Fahey Jr. Memorial Bridge".
  * NB There are 4 § 344-d's
```
