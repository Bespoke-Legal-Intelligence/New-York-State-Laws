# Highway Law § 342-JJ — Portion of state highway system to be designated and known as the "John J

[Highway Law](../README.md) › [Article 12 — State Routes](README.md)

```text
  § 342-jj. Portion of state highway system to be designated and known
as the "John J. Okulsky Memorial Highway". All that portion of the state
highway system constituting state route two hundred sixty-eight from its
intersection with New York route ninety-seven to its intersection with
state route ten in Delaware county shall be designated and known as the
John J. Okulsky Memorial Highway.
```
