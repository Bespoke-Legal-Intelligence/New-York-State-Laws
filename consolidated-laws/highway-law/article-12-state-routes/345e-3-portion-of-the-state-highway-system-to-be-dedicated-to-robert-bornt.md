# Highway Law § 345-E*3 — Portion of the state highway system to be dedicated to Robert Bornt

[Highway Law](../README.md) › [Article 12 — State Routes](README.md)

*An asterisk in a number (for example Article 24\* or § 20-C\*2) is the Senate's mark for a number the Legislature has used more than once: the marked article or section is a different one from the unmarked one with the same number. \*2 means the second one enacted with that number.*

```text
  * § 345-e. Portion of the state highway system to be dedicated to
Robert Bornt. The bridge located on state route two, designated as
bridge number 1000250, which crosses over state route twenty-two in the
town of Petersburg, county of Rensselaer, shall be dedicated to Robert
Bornt.
  * NB There are 3 § 345-e's
```
