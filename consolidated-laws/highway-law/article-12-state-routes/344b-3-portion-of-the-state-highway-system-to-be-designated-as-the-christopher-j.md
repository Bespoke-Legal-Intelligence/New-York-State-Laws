# Highway Law § 344-B*3 — Portion of the state highway system to be designated as the "Christopher J

[Highway Law](../README.md) › [Article 12 — State Routes](README.md)

*An asterisk in a number (for example Article 24\* or § 20-C\*2) is the Senate's mark for a number the Legislature has used more than once: the marked article or section is a different one from the unmarked one with the same number. \*2 means the second one enacted with that number.*

```text
  * § 344-b. Portion of the state highway system to be designated as the
"Christopher J. Scott Memorial Highway". All that portion of the state
highway system constituting New York state route 14A in the town of
Reading, county of Schuyler, and in the town of Starkey and village of
Dundee, county of Yates, from its junction at its southern terminus with
New York state route 14, and proceeding generally northerly to its
junction with Pre Emption Road, shall be designated and known as the
"Christopher J. Scott Memorial Highway".
  * NB There are 8 § 344-b's
```
