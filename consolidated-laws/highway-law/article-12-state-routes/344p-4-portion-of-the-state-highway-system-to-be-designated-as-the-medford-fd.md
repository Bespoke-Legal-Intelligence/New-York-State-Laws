# Highway Law § 344-P*4 — Portion of the state highway system to be designated as the "Medford FD Commissioner Niel Marturiello Memorial Bridge"

[Highway Law](../README.md) › [Article 12 — State Routes](README.md)

*An asterisk in a number (for example Article 24\* or § 20-C\*2) is the Senate's mark for a number the Legislature has used more than once: the marked article or section is a different one from the unmarked one with the same number. \*2 means the second one enacted with that number.*

```text
  * § 344-p. Portion of the state highway system to be designated as the
"Medford FD Commissioner Niel Marturiello Memorial Bridge". All that
portion of the state highway system constituting the bridge on
Horseblock Road, also known as County Road 16, crossing interstate route
495 in the town of Brookhaven, county of Suffolk, shall be designated
and known as the "Medford FD Commissioner Niel Marturiello Memorial
Bridge".
  * NB There are 7 § 344-p's
```
