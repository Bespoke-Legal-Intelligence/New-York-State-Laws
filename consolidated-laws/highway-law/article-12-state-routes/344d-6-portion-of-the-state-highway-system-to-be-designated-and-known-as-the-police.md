# Highway Law § 344-D*6 — Portion of the state highway system to be designated and known as the "Police Officer Arthur Lopez Memorial Bridge"

[Highway Law](../README.md) › [Article 12 — State Routes](README.md)

*An asterisk in a number (for example Article 24\* or § 20-C\*2) is the Senate's mark for a number the Legislature has used more than once: the marked article or section is a different one from the unmarked one with the same number. \*2 means the second one enacted with that number.*

```text
  * § 344-d. Portion of the state highway system to be designated and
known as the "Police Officer Arthur Lopez Memorial Bridge". The bridges
on Jericho Turnpike crossing the Cross Island Parkway near exit 27E in
the Counties of Queens and Nassau shall each be designated and known as
the "Police Officer Arthur Lopez Memorial Bridge".
  * NB Effective November 11, 2015
  * NB There are 16 § 344-d's
```
