# Highway Law § 344-F*3 — Portion of the state highway system to be designated as the "Gordie Little Memorial Bridge"

[Highway Law](../README.md) › [Article 12 — State Routes](README.md)

*An asterisk in a number (for example Article 24\* or § 20-C\*2) is the Senate's mark for a number the Legislature has used more than once: the marked article or section is a different one from the unmarked one with the same number. \*2 means the second one enacted with that number.*

```text
  * § 344-f. Portion of the state highway system to be designated as the
"Gordie Little Memorial Bridge". The bridge on state route twenty-two-B
crossing the Saranac river, in the hamlet of Morrisonville within the
town of Schuyler Falls and the town of Plattsburgh, county of Clinton,
shall be designated and known as the "Gordie Little Memorial Bridge".
  * NB There are 6 § 344-f's
```
