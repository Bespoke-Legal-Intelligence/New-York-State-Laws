# Highway Law § 345-E*5 — Portion of the state highway system to be dedicated to Sergeant Gregory L

[Highway Law](../README.md) › [Article 12 — State Routes](README.md)

*An asterisk in a number (for example Article 24\* or § 20-C\*2) is the Senate's mark for a number the Legislature has used more than once: the marked article or section is a different one from the unmarked one with the same number. \*2 means the second one enacted with that number.*

```text
  * § 345-e. Portion of the state highway system to be dedicated to
Sergeant Gregory L. Wahl. The bridge over the eastbound and westbound
lanes of the Southern State Parkway on state route one hundred eleven in
the town of Islip, county of Suffolk, shall be dedicated to Sergeant
Gregory L. Wahl.
  * NB There are 7 § 345-e's
```
