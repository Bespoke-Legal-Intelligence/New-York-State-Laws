# Highway Law § 343-X — Portion of the state highway system to be designated as the "National Veterans Highway"

[Highway Law](../README.md) › [Article 12 — State Routes](README.md)

```text
  * § 343-x. Portion of the state highway system to be designated as the
"National Veterans Highway". All that portion of the state highway
system constituting United States route 62 within the state of New York
shall be designated and known as the "National Veterans Highway".
  * NB There are 5 § 343-x's
```
