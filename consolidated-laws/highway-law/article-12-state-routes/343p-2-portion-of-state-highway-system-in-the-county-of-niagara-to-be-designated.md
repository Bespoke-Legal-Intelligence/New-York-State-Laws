# Highway Law § 343-P*2 — Portion of state highway system in the county of Niagara to be designated as the "North Tonawanda POW-MIA Memorial Highway"

[Highway Law](../README.md) › [Article 12 — State Routes](README.md)

*An asterisk in a number (for example Article 24\* or § 20-C\*2) is the Senate's mark for a number the Legislature has used more than once: the marked article or section is a different one from the unmarked one with the same number. \*2 means the second one enacted with that number.*

```text
  * § 343-p. Portion of state highway system in the county of Niagara to
be designated as the "North Tonawanda POW-MIA Memorial Highway". All
that portion of the state highway system in the city of North Tonawanda,
county of Niagara, constituting River Road shall be designated and known
as the "North Tonawanda POW-MIA Memorial Highway".
  * NB There are 8 § 343-p's
```
