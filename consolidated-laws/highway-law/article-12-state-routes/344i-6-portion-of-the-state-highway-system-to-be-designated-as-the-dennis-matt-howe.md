# Highway Law § 344-I*6 — Portion of the state highway system to be designated as the "Dennis 'Matt' Howe Memorial Highway"

[Highway Law](../README.md) › [Article 12 — State Routes](README.md)

*An asterisk in a number (for example Article 24\* or § 20-C\*2) is the Senate's mark for a number the Legislature has used more than once: the marked article or section is a different one from the unmarked one with the same number. \*2 means the second one enacted with that number.*

```text
  * § 344-i. Portion of the state highway system to be designated as the
"Dennis 'Matt' Howe Memorial Highway". All that portion of the state
highway system located in the town of Nichols, county of Tioga,
consisting of state route seventeen beginning at exit sixty-two,
continuing generally easterly, and terminating at exit sixty-three shall
be designated and known as the "Dennis 'Matt' Howe Memorial Highway".
  * NB There are 11 344-i's
```
