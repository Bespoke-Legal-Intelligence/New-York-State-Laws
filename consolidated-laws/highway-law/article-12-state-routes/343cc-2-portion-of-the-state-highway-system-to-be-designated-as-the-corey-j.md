# Highway Law § 343-CC*2 — Portion of the state highway system to be designated as the "Corey J

[Highway Law](../README.md) › [Article 12 — State Routes](README.md)

*An asterisk in a number (for example Article 24\* or § 20-C\*2) is the Senate's mark for a number the Legislature has used more than once: the marked article or section is a different one from the unmarked one with the same number. \*2 means the second one enacted with that number.*

```text
  * § 343-cc. Portion of the state highway system to be designated as
the "Corey J. Swinson Memorial Bridge". The bridge on County Road 13
which crosses the Sunrise Highway in the hamlet of Bay Shore, county of
Suffolk, shall be designated and known as the "Corey J. Swinson Memorial
Bridge".
  * NB There are 2 § 343-cc's
```
