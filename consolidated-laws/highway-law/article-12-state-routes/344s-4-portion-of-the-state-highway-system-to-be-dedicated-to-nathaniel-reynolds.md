# Highway Law § 344-S*4 — Portion of the state highway system to be dedicated to Nathaniel Reynolds

[Highway Law](../README.md) › [Article 12 — State Routes](README.md)

*An asterisk in a number (for example Article 24\* or § 20-C\*2) is the Senate's mark for a number the Legislature has used more than once: the marked article or section is a different one from the unmarked one with the same number. \*2 means the second one enacted with that number.*

```text
  * § 344-s. Portion of the state highway system to be dedicated to
Nathaniel Reynolds. All that portion of the state highway system
constituting state route thirty-five, beginning at its intersection with
state route one hundred twenty-one and continuing in a generally
northeasterly direction to its intersection with North Salem road, in
the town of Lewisboro, county of Westchester, shall be dedicated to
Nathaniel Reynolds.
  * NB There are 9 § 344-s's
```
