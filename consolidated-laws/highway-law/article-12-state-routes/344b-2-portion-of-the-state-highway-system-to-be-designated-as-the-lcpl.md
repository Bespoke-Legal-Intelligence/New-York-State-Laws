# Highway Law § 344-B*2 — Portion of the state highway system to be designated as the "LCpl

[Highway Law](../README.md) › [Article 12 — State Routes](README.md)

*An asterisk in a number (for example Article 24\* or § 20-C\*2) is the Senate's mark for a number the Legislature has used more than once: the marked article or section is a different one from the unmarked one with the same number. \*2 means the second one enacted with that number.*

```text
  * § 344-b. Portion of the state highway system to be designated as the
"LCpl. Jeremy Lasher Memorial Highway". The section of state route three
hundred sixty-five-a beginning at its intersection with New York state
route five in the city of Oneida, county of Madison, and continuing its
intersection with New York state route three hundred sixty-five in the
city of Oneida, county of Madison, shall be designated and known as the
"LCpl. Jeremy Lasher Memorial Highway".
  * NB There are 8 § 344-b's
```
