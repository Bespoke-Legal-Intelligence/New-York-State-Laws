# Highway Law § 343-P*4 — Portion of state highway system to be designated as the "Apalachin/Campville Memorial Bridge"

[Highway Law](../README.md) › [Article 12 — State Routes](README.md)

*An asterisk in a number (for example Article 24\* or § 20-C\*2) is the Senate's mark for a number the Legislature has used more than once: the marked article or section is a different one from the unmarked one with the same number. \*2 means the second one enacted with that number.*

```text
  * § 343-p. Portion of state highway system to be designated as the
"Apalachin/Campville Memorial Bridge". The bridge spanning the
Susquehanna River and connecting state route 17 in the hamlet of
Apalachin and route 17c in the hamlet of Campville, in the town of Owego
and county of Tioga, shall be designated and known as the
"Apalachin/Campville Memorial Bridge".
  * NB There are 8 § 343-p's
```
