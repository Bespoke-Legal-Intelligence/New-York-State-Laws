# Highway Law § 344-B*6 — Portion of the state highway system to be designated as the "Davenport Veterans of Foreign Wars Memorial Highway"

[Highway Law](../README.md) › [Article 12 — State Routes](README.md)

*An asterisk in a number (for example Article 24\* or § 20-C\*2) is the Senate's mark for a number the Legislature has used more than once: the marked article or section is a different one from the unmarked one with the same number. \*2 means the second one enacted with that number.*

```text
  * § 344-b. Portion of the state highway system to be designated as the
"Davenport Veterans of Foreign Wars Memorial Highway". All that portion
of the state highway system known as state route twenty-three beginning
at the western town line of the town of Davenport and ending at the
eastern town line of the town of Davenport, county of Delaware, shall be
designated and known as the "Davenport Veterans of Foreign Wars Memorial
Highway".
  * NB There are 8 § 344-b's
```
