# Highway Law § 344-M*11 — Portion of the state highway system to be designated as the "Detective Betsy Horner-Miller Cutrone Memorial Bridge"

[Highway Law](../README.md) › [Article 12 — State Routes](README.md)

*An asterisk in a number (for example Article 24\* or § 20-C\*2) is the Senate's mark for a number the Legislature has used more than once: the marked article or section is a different one from the unmarked one with the same number. \*2 means the second one enacted with that number.*

```text
  * § 344-m. Portion of the state highway system to be designated as the
"Detective Betsy Horner-Miller Cutrone Memorial Bridge". The bridge
operating as a part of Bay Parkway, crossing over Wantagh State Parkway,
just above Jones Beach Field 4 and Jones Beach Field 5, within Jones
Beach State Park, located on Jones Beach Island within the hamlet of
Wantagh, county of Nassau, shall be designated and known as the
"Detective Betsy Horner-Miller Cutrone Memorial Bridge".
  * NB There are 22 § 344-m's
```
