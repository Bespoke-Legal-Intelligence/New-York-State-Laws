# Highway Law § 343-YY*2 — Portion of the state highway system to be designated as the "New York State Trooper Kevin P

[Highway Law](../README.md) › [Article 12 — State Routes](README.md)

*An asterisk in a number (for example Article 24\* or § 20-C\*2) is the Senate's mark for a number the Legislature has used more than once: the marked article or section is a different one from the unmarked one with the same number. \*2 means the second one enacted with that number.*

```text
  * § 343-yy. Portion of the state highway system to be designated as
the "New York State Trooper Kevin P. Dobson, Sr. Memorial Bridge". The
bridge on interstate two-hundred ninety which crosses New York route
four hundred twenty-five in the town of Tonawanda, county of Erie, the
"New York State Trooper Kevin P. Dobson, Sr. Memorial Bridge".
  * NB There are 2 § 343-yy's
```
