# Highway Law § 343-B — Portion of the state highway system to be designated as "The 94th Infantry Division Memorial Highway

[Highway Law](../README.md) › [Article 12 — State Routes](README.md)

```text
  § 343-b. Portion of the state highway system to be designated as "The
94th Infantry Division Memorial Highway." That portion of the state
highway system beginning at the New York-New Jersey state line, thence
running generally northeasterly on state highway 94 continuing to the
northwestern terminus of highway 94 shall be designated and known as
"The 94th Infantry Division Memorial Highway."
```
