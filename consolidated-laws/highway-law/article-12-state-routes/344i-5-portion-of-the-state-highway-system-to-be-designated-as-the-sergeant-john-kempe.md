# Highway Law § 344-I*5 — Portion of the state highway system to be designated as the "Sergeant John Kempe Winslow Memorial Highway"

[Highway Law](../README.md) › [Article 12 — State Routes](README.md)

*An asterisk in a number (for example Article 24\* or § 20-C\*2) is the Senate's mark for a number the Legislature has used more than once: the marked article or section is a different one from the unmarked one with the same number. \*2 means the second one enacted with that number.*

```text
  * § 344-i. Portion of the state highway system to be designated as the
"Sergeant John Kempe Winslow Memorial Highway". All that portion of the
state highway system constituting state route two hundred five in the
town of Hartwick, county of Otsego, beginning at the intersection with
Scotch Hill Road, continuing in a generally southerly direction, and
terminating at the intersection with Rod Gun Club Road, shall be
designated and known as the "Sergeant John Kempe Winslow Memorial
Highway".
  * NB There are 11 § 344-i's
```
