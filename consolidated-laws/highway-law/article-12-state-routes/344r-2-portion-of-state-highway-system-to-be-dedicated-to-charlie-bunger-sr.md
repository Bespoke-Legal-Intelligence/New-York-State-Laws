# Highway Law § 344-R*2 — Portion of state highway system to be dedicated to Charlie Bunger Sr

[Highway Law](../README.md) › [Article 12 — State Routes](README.md)

*An asterisk in a number (for example Article 24\* or § 20-C\*2) is the Senate's mark for a number the Legislature has used more than once: the marked article or section is a different one from the unmarked one with the same number. \*2 means the second one enacted with that number.*

```text
  * § 344-r. Portion of state highway system to be dedicated to Charlie
Bunger Sr. All that portion of the state highway system constituting
state route twenty-seven-a, also known as Montauk Highway, from its
intersection with Deer Park Avenue to its intersection with Totten Place
in the village of Babylon, county of Suffolk, shall be dedicated to
Charlie Bunger Sr.
  * NB There are 12 § 344-r's
```
