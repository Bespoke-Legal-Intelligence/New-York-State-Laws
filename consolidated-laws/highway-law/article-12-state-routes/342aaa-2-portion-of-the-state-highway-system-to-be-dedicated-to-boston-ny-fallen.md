# Highway Law § 342-AAA*2 — Portion of the state highway system to be dedicated to Boston NY Fallen Firefighters

[Highway Law](../README.md) › [Article 12 — State Routes](README.md)

*An asterisk in a number (for example Article 24\* or § 20-C\*2) is the Senate's mark for a number the Legislature has used more than once: the marked article or section is a different one from the unmarked one with the same number. \*2 means the second one enacted with that number.*

```text
  * § 342-aaa. Portion of the state highway system to be dedicated to
Boston NY Fallen Firefighters. The bridge on Rice Hill Road, in the town
of Boston, county of Erie, crossing over state route two hundred
nineteen, shall be dedicated to Boston NY Fallen Firefighters.
  * NB There are 2 § 342-aaa's
```
