# Highway Law § 342-SS — Portion of state highway system to be designated and known as the "New York State Vietnam Veterans' Memorial Highway of Valor"

[Highway Law](../README.md) › [Article 12 — State Routes](README.md)

```text
  * § 342-ss. Portion of state highway system to be designated and known
as the "New York State Vietnam Veterans' Memorial Highway of Valor". All
that portion of the state highway system constituting route thirty-eight
from its intersection with route one hundred four-a in the town of
Sterling in Cayuga county, and continuing southerly along route
thirty-eight to its intersection with route ninety-six in the town of
Owego in Tioga county, shall be designated and known as the "New York
State Vietnam Veterans' Memorial Highway of Valor".
  * NB There are 2 § 342-ss's
```
