# Highway Law § 342-R — Portion of state highway system to be designated as "James Aloysius Farley Memorial Bridge"

[Highway Law](../README.md) › [Article 12 — State Routes](README.md)

```text
  § 342-r. Portion of state highway system to be designated as "James
Aloysius Farley Memorial Bridge". The bridge across Lowland Creek on
state route 9W in the town of Stony Point in the county of Rockland
shall be designated and known as "James Aloysius Farley Memorial
Bridge".
```
