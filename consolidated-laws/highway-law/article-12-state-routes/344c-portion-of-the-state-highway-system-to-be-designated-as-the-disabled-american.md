# Highway Law § 344-C — Portion of the state highway system to be designated as the "Disabled American Veterans Memorial Bridge"

[Highway Law](../README.md) › [Article 12 — State Routes](README.md)

```text
  * § 344-c. Portion of the state highway system to be designated as the
"Disabled American Veterans Memorial Bridge". The bridge across the
Taconic state parkway on Bryant Pond road, in the town of Putnam Valley,
county of Putnam, shall be designated and known as the "Disabled
American Veterans Memorial Bridge".
  * NB There are 7 § 344-c's
```
