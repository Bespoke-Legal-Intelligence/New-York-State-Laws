# Highway Law § 342-HH — Portion of the state highway system to be designated as the "POW/MIA Remembrance Bridge"

[Highway Law](../README.md) › [Article 12 — State Routes](README.md)

```text
  § 342-hh. Portion of the state highway system to be designated as the
"POW/MIA Remembrance Bridge". The bridge across the Mohawk River/Erie
Canal on state route 28 in the villages of Herkimer and Mohawk in the
county of Herkimer, shall be designated and known as the "POW/MIA
Remembrance Bridge".
```
