# Highway Law § 344-P*12 — Portion of the state highway system to be designated as the "T Sgt Walter Shearing Memorial Highway"

[Highway Law](../README.md) › [Article 12 — State Routes](README.md)

*An asterisk in a number (for example Article 24\* or § 20-C\*2) is the Senate's mark for a number the Legislature has used more than once: the marked article or section is a different one from the unmarked one with the same number. \*2 means the second one enacted with that number.*

```text
  * § 344-p. Portion of the state highway system to be designated as the
"T Sgt Walter Shearing Memorial Highway". All that portion of the state
highway system constituting state route nineteen from Eddy Road in the
town of Gainesville, county of Wyoming, to East Mill Street in the
village of Gainesville, county of Wyoming, shall be designated and known
as the "T Sgt Walter Shearing Memorial Highway".
  * NB There are 14 § 344-p's
```
