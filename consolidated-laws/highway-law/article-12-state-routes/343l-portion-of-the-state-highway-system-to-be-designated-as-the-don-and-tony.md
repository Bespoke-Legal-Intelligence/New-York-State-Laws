# Highway Law § 343-L — Portion of the state highway system to be designated as the "Don and Tony DeMatteo Parkway"

[Highway Law](../README.md) › [Article 12 — State Routes](README.md)

```text
  § 343-l. Portion of the state highway system to be designated as the
"Don and Tony DeMatteo Parkway". All that portion of the state highway
system in the city of Yonkers constituting the Cross County Parkway
beginning at the intersection of the Cross County Parkway and the Saw
Mill River Parkway in Yonkers and continuing easterly along the Cross
County Parkway to the eastern boundary line of Yonkers shall be
designated and known as the "Don and Tony DeMatteo Parkway".
```
