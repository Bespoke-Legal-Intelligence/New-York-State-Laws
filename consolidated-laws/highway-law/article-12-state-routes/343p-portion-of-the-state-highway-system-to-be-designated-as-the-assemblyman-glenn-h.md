# Highway Law § 343-P — Portion of the state highway system to be designated as the "Assemblyman Glenn H

[Highway Law](../README.md) › [Article 12 — State Routes](README.md)

```text
  * § 343-p. Portion of the state highway system to be designated as the
"Assemblyman Glenn H. Harris Memorial Highway". All that portion of the
state highway system beginning at the intersection of route ten and
route twenty-nine-A in the vicinity of Pine Lake, continuing north on
route ten, and terminating at the intersection with route eight in the
vicinity of Piseco Lake shall be designated and known as the
"Assemblyman Glenn H. Harris Memorial Highway".
  * NB There are 8 § 343-p's
```
