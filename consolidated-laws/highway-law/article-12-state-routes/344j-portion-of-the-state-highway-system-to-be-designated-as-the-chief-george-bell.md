# Highway Law § 344-J — Portion of the state highway system to be designated as the "Chief George Bell Memorial Highway"

[Highway Law](../README.md) › [Article 12 — State Routes](README.md)

```text
  § 344-j. Portion of the state highway system to be designated as the
"Chief George Bell Memorial Highway". The portion of state route
twenty-two in the village of Cambridge, county of Washington shall be
designated and known as the "Chief George Bell Memorial Highway".
```
