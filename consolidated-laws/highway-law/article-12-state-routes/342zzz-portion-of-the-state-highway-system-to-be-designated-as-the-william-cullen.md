# Highway Law § 342-ZZZ — Portion of the state highway system to be designated as the "William Cullen Bryant Viaduct"

[Highway Law](../README.md) › [Article 12 — State Routes](README.md)

```text
  § 342-zzz. Portion of the state highway system to be designated as the
"William Cullen Bryant Viaduct". The section on New York State Route 25A
which spans over Hempstead Harbor in Nassau County known as the Roslyn
Viaduct shall hereafter be designated and known as the "William Cullen
Bryant Viaduct".
```
