# Highway Law § 226 — Co-ordination of projects

[Highway Law](../README.md) › [Article 8-A — Town Highway Improvement Program](README.md)

```text
  § 226. Co-ordination of projects. So far as practicable, the town
boards and town superintendents of adjoining towns, within the county,
contemplating projects with the county superintendent, may confer by
committees or their representatives, and may cooperate with a view to
co-ordinating projects to the end that improved town highways may
provide connecting routes.
```
