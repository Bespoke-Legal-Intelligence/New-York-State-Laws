# Highway Law § 229-C — Existing rights

[Highway Law](../README.md) › [Article 8-A — Town Highway Improvement Program](README.md)

```text
  § 229-c. Existing rights. The repeal of article eight-A of the highway
law, as added by chapter three hundred fifteen of the laws of nineteen
hundred sixty shall not affect or impair any act done or right accruing,
accrued or acquired, or liability incurred prior to the time such repeal
takes effect, but the same may be enjoyed, asserted, enforced,
prosecuted or inflicted, as fully and to the same extent as if such
repeal had not been effected.
```
