*An asterisk in a number (for example Article 24\* or § 20-C\*2) is the Senate's mark for a number the Legislature has used more than once: the marked article or section is a different one from the unmarked one with the same number. \*2 means the second one enacted with that number.*

**Short title (from the law itself):** § 1. Short title. This chapter shall be known as the "Highway Law."

## Chapter text as published

```text
                               CHAPTER 63
  AN ACT relating to highways, constituting chapter twenty-five of the
consolidated laws
  Became a law March 4, 1936, with the approval of the Governor.
Passed, three-fifths being present
  The People of the State of New York, represented in Senate and
Assembly, do enact as follows:
                   CHAPTER 25 OF THE CONSOLIDATED LAWS
```
