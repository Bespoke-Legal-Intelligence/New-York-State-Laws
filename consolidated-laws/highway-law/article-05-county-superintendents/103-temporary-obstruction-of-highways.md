# Highway Law § 103 — Temporary obstruction of highways

[Highway Law](../README.md) › [Article 5 — County Superintendents](README.md)

```text
  § 103. Temporary obstruction of highways. The necessary obstruction of
a highway by the removal of buildings or other temporary obstruction
shall only be allowed if a highway other than a state highway under a
permit granted by the county superintendent upon the written request of
the town superintendent, and if a state highway under a permit granted
by the commissioner of transportation.
```
