# Highway Law § 44 — Acceptance of state highways when completed

[Highway Law](../README.md) › [Article 3 — State Highways](README.md)

```text
  § 44. Acceptance of state highways when completed. Upon the completion
of a state highway, or section thereof, constructed or improved under a
contract let as provided in this article, the commissioner of
transportation shall cause the same to be inspected and if he approve,
accept the work.
```
