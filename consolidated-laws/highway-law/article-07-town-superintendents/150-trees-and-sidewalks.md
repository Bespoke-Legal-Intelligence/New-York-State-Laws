# Highway Law § 150 — Trees and sidewalks

[Highway Law](../README.md) › [Article 7 — Town Superintendents](README.md)

```text
  § 150. Trees and sidewalks. The town superintendent may, by an order
in writing, approved by a majority of the members of the town board,
authorize the owners of property adjoining the town highways, at their
own expense, to locate and plant trees and locate and construct
sidewalks along the town highways, in conformity with the topography
thereof, which order with a map or diagram, showing the location of the
sidewalk and tree planting, certified by the town superintendent, shall
be filed in the office of the town clerk, within ten days after the
making of the order.
```
