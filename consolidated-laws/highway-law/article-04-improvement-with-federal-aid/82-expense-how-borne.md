# Highway Law § 82 — Expense; how borne

[Highway Law](../README.md) › [Article 4 — Improvement With Federal Aid](README.md)

```text
  § 82. Expense; how borne. The proportion of the total cost of work
performed on any project, in addition to such elements thereof as are
not subject to federal aid, which shall be borne by the state of New
York shall be the difference between the funds contributed for such work
by the federal government and the actual cost thereof except as provided
in section two hundred and thirty, and less any amount which may be
provided by any municipality in accordance with the provisions of this
chapter.
```
