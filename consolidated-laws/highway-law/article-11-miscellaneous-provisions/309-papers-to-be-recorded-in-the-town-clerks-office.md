# Highway Law § 309 — Papers to be recorded in the town clerk's office

[Highway Law](../README.md) › [Article 11 — Miscellaneous Provisions](README.md)

```text
  § 309. Papers to be recorded in the town clerk's office. The town
superintendent shall annex to such verdict the application, and their
certificate that the road is laid out, and the same shall be filed and
recorded in the town clerk's office.
```
