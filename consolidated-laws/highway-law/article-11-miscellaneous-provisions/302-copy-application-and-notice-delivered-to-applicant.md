# Highway Law § 302 — Copy application and notice delivered to applicant

[Highway Law](../README.md) › [Article 11 — Miscellaneous Provisions](README.md)

```text
  § 302. Copy application and notice delivered to applicant. Such town
superintendent shall deliver to the applicant a copy of the application,
to which shall be added a notice of the time and place appointed for the
selection of the jury, addressed to the owners and occupants of the
land.
```
