# Highway Law § 324 — Penalty for falling trees

[Highway Law](../README.md) › [Article 11 — Miscellaneous Provisions](README.md)

```text
  § 324. Penalty for falling trees. If any person shall cut down any
tree on land not occupied by him, so that it shall fall into any
highway, river or stream, unless by the order and consent of the
occupant, the person so offending shall forfeit to such occupant the sum
of one dollar for every tree so fallen and the like sum for every day
the same shall remain in the highway, river or stream.
```
