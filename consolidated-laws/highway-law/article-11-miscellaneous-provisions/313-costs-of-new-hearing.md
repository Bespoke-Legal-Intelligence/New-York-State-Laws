# Highway Law § 313 — Costs of new hearing

[Highway Law](../README.md) › [Article 11 — Miscellaneous Provisions](README.md)

```text
  § 313. Costs of new hearing. If upon a new hearing, the damages
assessed are increased, the applicant shall pay the costs and expenses
thereof, otherwise the owner shall pay the same.
```
