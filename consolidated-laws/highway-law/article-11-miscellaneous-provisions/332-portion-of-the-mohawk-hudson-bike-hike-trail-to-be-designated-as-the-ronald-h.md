# Highway Law § 332 — Portion of the Mohawk-Hudson Bike-Hike Trail to be designated as the "Ronald H

[Highway Law](../README.md) › [Article 11 — Miscellaneous Provisions](README.md)

```text
  § 332. Portion of the Mohawk-Hudson Bike-Hike Trail to be designated
as the "Ronald H. Miller Memorial Bike Trail". All that portion of the
Mohawk-Hudson Bike-Hike Trail in the village of Menands, commencing at
the city of Watervliet border, continuing in a generally southerly
direction and terminating at the city of Albany border, shall be
designated and known as the "Ronald H. Miller Memorial Bike Trail".
```
