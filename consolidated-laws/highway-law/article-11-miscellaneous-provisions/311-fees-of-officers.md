# Highway Law § 311 — Fees of officers

[Highway Law](../README.md) › [Article 11 — Miscellaneous Provisions](README.md)

```text
  § 311. Fees of officers. Every juror serving on the jury, in
proceedings for a private road, shall be entitled to receive for his
services one dollar and fifty cents to be paid by the applicant.
```
