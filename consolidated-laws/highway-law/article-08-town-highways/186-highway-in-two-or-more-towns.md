# Highway Law § 186 — Highway in two or more towns

[Highway Law](../README.md) › [Article 8 — Town Highways](README.md)

```text
  § 186. Highway in two or more towns. When application is made to lay
out, alter or discontinue a highway located in two or more towns, all
notices or proceedings required to be served upon the town
superintendents shall be served upon the town superintendent of each
town; and the supreme court shall determine the amount of damages to be
paid by each town.
```
