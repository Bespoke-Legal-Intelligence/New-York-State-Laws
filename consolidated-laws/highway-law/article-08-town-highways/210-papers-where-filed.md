# Highway Law § 210 — Papers, where filed

[Highway Law](../README.md) › [Article 8 — Town Highways](README.md)

```text
  § 210. Papers, where filed. All applications, certificates,
appointments and other papers relating to the laying out, altering or
discontinuing of any highway shall be filed by the town superintendent
as soon as a decision shall have been made thereon in the town clerk's
office of the town.
```
