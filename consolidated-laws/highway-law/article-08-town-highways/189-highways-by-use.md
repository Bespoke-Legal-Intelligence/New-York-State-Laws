# Highway Law § 189 — Highways by use

[Highway Law](../README.md) › [Article 8 — Town Highways](README.md)

```text
  § 189. Highways by use. All lands which shall have been used by the
public as a highway for the period of ten years or more, shall be a
highway, with the same force and effect as if it had been duly laid out
and recorded as a highway, and the town superintendent shall open all
such highways to the width of at least three rods.
```
