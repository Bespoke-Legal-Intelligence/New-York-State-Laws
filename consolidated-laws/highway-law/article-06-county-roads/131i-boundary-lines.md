# Highway Law § 131-I — Boundary lines

[Highway Law](../README.md) › [Article 6 — County Roads](README.md)

```text
  § 131-i. Boundary lines. Wherever the words "upon its borders" are
used in this article in reference to the boundary line between two
towns, the same are and were intended and shall be construed to mean
"upon," "along," and "across its borders."
```
