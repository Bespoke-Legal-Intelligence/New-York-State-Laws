# Highway Law § 119 — Purchase of lands

[Highway Law](../README.md) › [Article 6 — County Roads](README.md)

```text
  § 119. Purchase of lands. The board of supervisors may, by resolution,
authorize its chairman, a member, or a committee to purchase the lands
to be acquired for the purposes specified in the preceding section.
```
