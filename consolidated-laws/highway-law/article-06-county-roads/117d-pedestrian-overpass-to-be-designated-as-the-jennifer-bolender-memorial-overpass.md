# Highway Law § 117-D — Pedestrian overpass to be designated as the "Jennifer Bolender Memorial Overpass"

[Highway Law](../README.md) › [Article 6 — County Roads](README.md)

```text
  § 117-d. Pedestrian overpass to be designated as the "Jennifer
Bolender Memorial Overpass". The pedestrian overpass spanning the
LaSalle Expressway in the city of Niagara Falls, in the county of
Niagara, known as the sixty-sixth street overpass, shall be designated
and known as the "Jennifer Bolender Memorial Overpass".
```
