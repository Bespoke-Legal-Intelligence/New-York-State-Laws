# Highway Law § 353 — Laws repealed

[Highway Law](../README.md) › [Article 13 — Saving Clause; Laws Repealed; When to Take Effect](README.md)

```text
  § 353. Laws repealed. Chapter thirty of the laws of nineteen hundred
and nine entitled "An act relating to highways, constituting chapter
twenty-five of the consolidated laws," and all acts amendatory thereof
and supplemental thereto, constituting the highway law as heretofore in
force, are hereby repealed, except as otherwise provided herein.
```
