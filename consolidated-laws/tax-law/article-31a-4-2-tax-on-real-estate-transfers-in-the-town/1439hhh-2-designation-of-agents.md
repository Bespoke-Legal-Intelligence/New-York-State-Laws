# Tax Law § 1439-HHH*2 — Designation of agents

[Tax Law](../README.md) › [Article 31-A-4*2 — Tax On Real Estate Transfers In the Town of Orangetown](README.md)

*An asterisk in a number (for example Article 24\* or § 20-C\*2) is the Senate's mark for a number the Legislature has used more than once: the marked article or section is a different one from the unmarked one with the same number. \*2 means the second one enacted with that number.*

```text
  * § 1439-hhh. Designation of agents. The treasurer is authorized to
designate the recording officer to act as the treasurer's agent for
purposes of collecting the tax authorized by this article. The treasurer
shall provide for the manner in which the recording officer may be
designated as agent subject to such terms and conditions as the
treasurer shall prescribe. The real estate transfer tax shall be paid to
such agent as provided in section fourteen hundred thirty-nine-ccc of
this article.
  * NB Effective February 19, 2026
  * NB Repealed December 31, 2045
  * NB There are 2 § 1439-hhh's
```
