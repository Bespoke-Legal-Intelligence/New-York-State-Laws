# Tax Law § 1616 — Continuation of licenses

[Tax Law](../README.md) › [Article 34 — New York State Lottery For Education](README.md)

```text
  § 1616. Continuation of licenses. All licenses issued by the
commissioner to lottery sales agents prior to the effective date of this
act shall continue in effect by their terms and subject to the general
jurisdiction of the division.
```
