# Tax Law § 1439-MM — Apportionment

[Tax Law](../README.md) › [Article 31-A-3 — Tax On Real Estate Transfers In the Town of Northeast](README.md)

```text
  * § 1439-mm. Apportionment. A local law adopted by the town of
Northeast, pursuant to this article, shall provide for a method of
apportionment for determining the amount of tax due whenever the real
property or interest therein is situated within and without the town.
  * NB Repealed December 31, 2028
```
