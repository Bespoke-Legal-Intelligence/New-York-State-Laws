# Tax Law § 1450-MM — Apportionment

[Tax Law](../README.md) › [Article 31-K — Tax On Real Estate Transfers In the Town of Clarkstown](README.md)

```text
  * § 1450-mm. Apportionment. A local law adopted by the town of
Clarkstown, pursuant to this article, shall provide for a method of
apportionment for determining the amount of tax due whenever the real
property or interest therein is situated within and without the town.
  * NB Effective August 30, 2026
  * NB Repealed December 31, 2046
```
