# Tax Law § 1450-HH — Designation of agents

[Tax Law](../README.md) › [Article 31-K — Tax On Real Estate Transfers In the Town of Clarkstown](README.md)

```text
  * § 1450-hh. Designation of agents. The treasurer is authorized to
designate the recording officer to act as their agent for purposes of
collecting the tax authorized by this article. The treasurer shall
provide for the manner in which such person may be designated as their
agent subject to such terms and conditions as the treasurer shall
prescribe. The real estate transfer tax shall be paid to such agent as
provided in section fourteen hundred fifty-cc of this article.
  * NB Repealed December 31, 2046
```
