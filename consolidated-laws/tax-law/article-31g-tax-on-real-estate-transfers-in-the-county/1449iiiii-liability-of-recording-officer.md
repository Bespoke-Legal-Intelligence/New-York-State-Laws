# Tax Law § 1449-IIIII — Liability of recording officer

[Tax Law](../README.md) › [Article 31-G — Tax On Real Estate Transfers In the County of Tompkins](README.md)

```text
  § 1449-iiiii. Liability of recording officer. A recording officer
shall not be liable for any inaccuracy in the amount of tax imposed
pursuant to this article that he shall collect so long as he shall
compute and collect such tax on the amount of consideration or the value
of the interest conveyed as such amounts are provided to him or her by
the person paying the tax.
```
