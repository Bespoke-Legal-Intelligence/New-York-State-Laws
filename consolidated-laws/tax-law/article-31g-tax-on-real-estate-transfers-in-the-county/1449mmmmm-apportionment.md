# Tax Law § 1449-MMMMM — Apportionment

[Tax Law](../README.md) › [Article 31-G — Tax On Real Estate Transfers In the County of Tompkins](README.md)

```text
  § 1449-mmmmm. Apportionment. The local law adopted by the legislative
body of the county of Tompkins shall provide for a method of
apportionment for determining the amount of tax due whenever the real
property or interest therein is situated within and without the county
of Tompkins.
```
