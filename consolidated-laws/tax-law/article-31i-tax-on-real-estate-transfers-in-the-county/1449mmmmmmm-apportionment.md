# Tax Law § 1449-MMMMMMM — Apportionment

[Tax Law](../README.md) › [Article 31-I — Tax On Real Estate Transfers In the County of Cortland](README.md)

```text
  * § 1449-mmmmmmm. Apportionment. The local law adopted by the
legislative body of the county of Cortland shall provide for a method of
apportionment for determining the amount of tax due whenever the real
property or interest therein is situated within and without the county
of Cortland.
```
