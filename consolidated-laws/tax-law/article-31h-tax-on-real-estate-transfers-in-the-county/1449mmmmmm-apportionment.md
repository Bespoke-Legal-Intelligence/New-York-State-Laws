# Tax Law § 1449-MMMMMM — Apportionment

[Tax Law](../README.md) › [Article 31-H — Tax On Real Estate Transfers In the County of Madison](README.md)

```text
  § 1449-mmmmmm. Apportionment. The local law adopted by the legislative
body of the county of Madison shall provide for a method of
apportionment for determining the amount of tax due whenever the real
property or interest therein is situated within and without the county
of Madison.
```
