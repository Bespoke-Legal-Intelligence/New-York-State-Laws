# Tax Law § 661 — Change of election

[Tax Law](../../README.md) › [Article 22 — Personal Income Tax](../README.md) › [Part 4 — Returns and Payment of Tax](README.md)

```text
  § 661. Change of election. Any election expressly authorized by this
article may be changed on such terms and conditions as the tax
commission may prescribe by regulation.
```
