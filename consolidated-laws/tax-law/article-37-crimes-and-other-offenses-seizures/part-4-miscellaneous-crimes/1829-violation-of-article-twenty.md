# Tax Law § 1829 — Violation of article twenty-A

[Tax Law](../../README.md) › [Article 37 — Crimes and Other Offenses, Seizures and Forfeitures](../README.md) › [Part 4 — Miscellaneous Crimes](README.md)

```text
  § 1829. Violation of article twenty-A. Any wholesale dealer or retail
dealer who violates any of the provisions of article twenty-A of this
chapter may be prosecuted and upon conviction shall be guilty of a class
B misdemeanor.
```
