# Tax Law § 1802 — Criminal tax fraud in the fifth degree

[Tax Law](../../README.md) › [Article 37 — Crimes and Other Offenses, Seizures and Forfeitures](../README.md) › [Part 2 — Tax Fraud Acts and Penalties](README.md)

```text
  § 1802. Criminal tax fraud in the fifth degree. A person commits
criminal tax fraud in the fifth degree when he or she commits a tax
fraud act. Criminal tax fraud in the fifth degree is a class A
misdemeanor.
```
