# Tax Law § 1449-MMMM — Apportionment

[Tax Law](../README.md) › [Article 31-F — Tax On Real Estate Transfers In the Town of Warwick](README.md)

```text
  * § 1449-mmmm. Apportionment. A local law adopted by the town of
Warwick, pursuant to this article, shall provide for a method of
apportionment for determining the amount of tax due whenever the real
property or interest therein is situated within and without the town.
```
