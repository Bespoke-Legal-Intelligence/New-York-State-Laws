# Tax Law § 1453-A — Computation of alternative entire net income

[Tax Law](../README.md) › [Article 32 — Franchise Tax On Banking Corporations](README.md)

**Repealed** (9 Jan 2015). The text below is what the Senate publishes for this repealed provision.

```text
  * § 1453-A. Computation of alternative entire net income.--(a)
Alternative entire net income means entire net income as determined
pursuant to section fourteen hundred fifty-three, except that the
deductions described in paragraphs eleven and twelve of subsection (e)
of section fourteen hundred fifty-three shall not be allowed.
  (b) Any election made pursuant to paragraph two of subsection (b) of
section fourteen hundred fifty-four with respect to the modification
provided for in subsection (f) of section fourteen hundred fifty-three
shall be deemed to have been made for purposes of computing alternative
entire net income.
  * NB Repealed January 1, 2015
```
