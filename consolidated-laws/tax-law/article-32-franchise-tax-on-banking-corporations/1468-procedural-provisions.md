# Tax Law § 1468 — Procedural provisions

[Tax Law](../README.md) › [Article 32 — Franchise Tax On Banking Corporations](README.md)

**Repealed** (9 Jan 2015). The text below is what the Senate publishes for this repealed provision.

```text
  * § 1468. Procedural provisions. The provisions of article
twenty-seven of this chapter shall apply to the provisions of this
article in the same manner and with the same force and effect as if the
language of such article twenty-seven had been incorporated in full into
this article and had expressly referred to the tax under this article,
except to the extent that any such provision is either inconsistent with
a provision of this article or is not relevant to this article.
  * NB Repealed January 1, 2015
```
