# Tax Law § 1439-PPP — Foreclosure proceedings

[Tax Law](../README.md) › [Article 31-A-4 — Tax On Real Estate Transfers In the Town of Chester](README.md)

```text
  * § 1439-ppp. Foreclosure proceedings. Where the conveyance consists
of a transfer of property made as a result of an order of the court in a
foreclosure proceeding ordering the sale of such property, the referee
or sheriff effectuating such transfer shall not be liable for any
interest or penalties that are authorized pursuant to this article or
article thirty-seven of this chapter.
  * NB Effective January 14, 2026
  * NB Repealed December 31, 2045
  * NB There are 2 § 1439-ppp's
```
