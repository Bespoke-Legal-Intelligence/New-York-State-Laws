# Estates, Powers and Trusts Law § 12-2.2 — Action may be joint or several; right to implead

[Estates, Powers and Trusts Law](../../README.md) › [Article 12 — Actions By Creditors and Other Persons Against Distributees and Testamentary Beneficiaries](../README.md) › [Part 2 — Rules Governing Action to Enforce Liability](README.md)

```text
§ 12-2.2 Action may be joint or several; right to implead
  An action may be brought against one or more of the persons subject to
liability under this article.  A person against whom an action is
brought may implead, pursuant to the provisions of CPLR article 10
governing impleader, any person who may be liable to him for indemnity
or contribution under paragraph (b) of 12-1.3.
```
