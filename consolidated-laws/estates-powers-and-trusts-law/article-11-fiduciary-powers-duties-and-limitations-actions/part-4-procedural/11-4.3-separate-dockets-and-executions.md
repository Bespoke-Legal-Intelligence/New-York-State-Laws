# Estates, Powers and Trusts Law § 11-4.3 — Separate dockets and executions

[Estates, Powers and Trusts Law](../../README.md) › [Article 11 — Fiduciary: Powers, Duties and Limitations; Actions By or Against In Representative or Individual Capacities](../README.md) › [Part 4 — Procedural Aspects of Actions By or Against Personal Representatives](README.md)

```text
§ 11-4.3 Separate dockets and executions
  In a case specified in 11-4.2 or where costs to be collected out of
the individual property of a personal representative are awarded in an
action or proceeding by or against him in his representative capacity,
so much of the judgment as awards a sum of money against him personally
may be separately docketed and a separate execution may be issued
thereupon, as if the judgment contained no award against him in his
representative capacity.
```
