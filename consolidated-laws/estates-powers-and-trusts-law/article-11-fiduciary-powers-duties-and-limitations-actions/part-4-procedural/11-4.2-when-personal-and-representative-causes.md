# Estates, Powers and Trusts Law § 11-4.2 — When personal and representative causes of action may be joined

[Estates, Powers and Trusts Law](../../README.md) › [Article 11 — Fiduciary: Powers, Duties and Limitations; Actions By or Against In Representative or Individual Capacities](../README.md) › [Part 4 — Procedural Aspects of Actions By or Against Personal Representatives](README.md)

```text
§ 11-4.2 When personal and representative causes of action may be joined
  Actions or proceedings brought against a personal representative
personally and in his representative capacity may be joined.  In such
case a judgment for the plaintiff must clearly indicate whether it is
awarded against the defendant personally or in his representative
capacity.
```
