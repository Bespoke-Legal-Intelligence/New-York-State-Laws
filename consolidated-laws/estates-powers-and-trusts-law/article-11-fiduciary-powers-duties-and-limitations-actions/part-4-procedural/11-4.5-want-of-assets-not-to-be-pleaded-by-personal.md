# Estates, Powers and Trusts Law § 11-4.5 — Want of assets not to be pleaded by personal representative

[Estates, Powers and Trusts Law](../../README.md) › [Article 11 — Fiduciary: Powers, Duties and Limitations; Actions By or Against In Representative or Individual Capacities](../README.md) › [Part 4 — Procedural Aspects of Actions By or Against Personal Representatives](README.md)

```text
§ 11-4.5 Want of assets not to be pleaded by personal representative
  In an action or proceeding against a personal representative, in his
representative capacity, in which the complaint demands judgment for a
sum of money, the non-existence or insufficiency of assets may not be
pleaded and the plaintiff's right of recovery is not affected thereby.
```
