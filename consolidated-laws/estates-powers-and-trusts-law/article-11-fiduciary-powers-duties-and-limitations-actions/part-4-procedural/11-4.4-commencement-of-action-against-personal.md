# Estates, Powers and Trusts Law § 11-4.4 — Commencement of action against personal representatives; rule

[Estates, Powers and Trusts Law](../../README.md) › [Article 11 — Fiduciary: Powers, Duties and Limitations; Actions By or Against In Representative or Individual Capacities](../README.md) › [Part 4 — Procedural Aspects of Actions By or Against Personal Representatives](README.md)

```text
§ 11-4.4 Commencement of action against personal representatives; rule
           when some of representatives not served
  Where an action or proceeding is commenced against two or more
personal representatives in their representative capacities,
jurisdiction of all is obtained by service of process upon any one of
them and any judgment recovered may be entered and execution issued
thereon against all of them, in their representative capacities, as if
all had been served.
```
