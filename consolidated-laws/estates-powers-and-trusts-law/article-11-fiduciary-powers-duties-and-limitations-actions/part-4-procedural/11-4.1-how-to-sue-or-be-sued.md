# Estates, Powers and Trusts Law § 11-4.1 — How to sue or be sued

[Estates, Powers and Trusts Law](../../README.md) › [Article 11 — Fiduciary: Powers, Duties and Limitations; Actions By or Against In Representative or Individual Capacities](../README.md) › [Part 4 — Procedural Aspects of Actions By or Against Personal Representatives](README.md)

```text
§ 11-4.1 How to sue or be sued
  Actions or proceedings brought by or against a personal representative
must be brought by or against him in his representative capacity.
```
