# Estates, Powers and Trusts Law § 11-3.1 — Actions

[Estates, Powers and Trusts Law](../../README.md) › [Article 11 — Fiduciary: Powers, Duties and Limitations; Actions By or Against In Representative or Individual Capacities](../README.md) › [Part 3 — Actions By or Against Personal Representatives](README.md)

```text
§ 11-3.1 Actions
  Any action, other than an action for injury to person or property, may
be maintained by and against a personal representative in all cases and
in such manner as such action might have been maintained by or against
his decedent.
```
