# Estates, Powers and Trusts Law § 11-3.4 — Action by representative of representative

[Estates, Powers and Trusts Law](../../README.md) › [Article 11 — Fiduciary: Powers, Duties and Limitations; Actions By or Against In Representative or Individual Capacities](../README.md) › [Part 3 — Actions By or Against Personal Representatives](README.md)

```text
§ 11-3.4 Action by representative of representative
  Except as otherwise prescribed by law, a personal representative of a
personal representative has no authority to commence or maintain any
action or proceeding relating to the estate, effects or rights of the
decedent of the first representative, or to take any charge or control
thereof, as such representative.
```
