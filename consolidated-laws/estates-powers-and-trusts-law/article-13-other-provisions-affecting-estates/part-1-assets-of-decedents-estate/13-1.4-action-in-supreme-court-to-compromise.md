# Estates, Powers and Trusts Law § 13-1.4 — Action in supreme court to compromise controversies between

[Estates, Powers and Trusts Law](../../README.md) › [Article 13 — Other Provisions Affecting Estates](../README.md) › [Part 1 — Assets of Decedent's Estate](README.md)

```text
§ 13-1.4 Action in supreme court to compromise controversies between
           claimants to estate assets
  An action may be maintained in the supreme court to compromise
controversies between claimants to the property of an estate in
accordance with and subject to the provisions of SCPA 2106.
```
