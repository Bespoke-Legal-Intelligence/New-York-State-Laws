# Estates, Powers and Trusts Law § 13-4.9 — Nontestamentary transfer on death

[Estates, Powers and Trusts Law](../../README.md) › [Article 13 — Other Provisions Affecting Estates](../README.md) › [Part 4 — Transfer-on-death Security Registration](README.md)

```text
§ 13-4.9 Nontestamentary transfer on death
  (a) A transfer on death resulting from a registration in beneficiary
form is effective by reason of the contract regarding the registration
between the owner and the registering entity and this part and is not
testamentary.
  (b) This part does not limit the rights of creditors of security
owners against beneficiaries and other transferees under other laws of
this state.
```
