# Estates, Powers and Trusts Law § 13-4.11 — Rules of construction

[Estates, Powers and Trusts Law](../../README.md) › [Article 13 — Other Provisions Affecting Estates](../README.md) › [Part 4 — Transfer-on-death Security Registration](README.md)

```text
§ 13-4.11 Rules of construction
  (a) This part shall be liberally construed and applied to promote its
underlying purposes and policy and to make uniform the laws with respect
to the subject of this part among states enacting it.
  (b) Unless displaced by the particular provisions of this part, the
principles of law and equity supplement its provisions.
```
