# Estates, Powers and Trusts Law § 13-4.5 — Form of registration in beneficiary form

[Estates, Powers and Trusts Law](../../README.md) › [Article 13 — Other Provisions Affecting Estates](../README.md) › [Part 4 — Transfer-on-death Security Registration](README.md)

```text
§ 13-4.5 Form of registration in beneficiary form
  Registration in beneficiary form may be shown by the words "transfer
on death" or the abbreviation "TOD", or by the words "pay on death" or
the abbreviation "POD", after the name of the registered owner and
before the name of the beneficiary.
```
