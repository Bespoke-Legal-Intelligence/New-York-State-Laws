# Estates, Powers and Trusts Law § 13-4.4 — Origination of registration in beneficiary form

[Estates, Powers and Trusts Law](../../README.md) › [Article 13 — Other Provisions Affecting Estates](../README.md) › [Part 4 — Transfer-on-death Security Registration](README.md)

```text
§ 13-4.4 Origination of registration in beneficiary form
  A security, whether evidenced by certificate or account, is registered
in beneficiary form when the registration includes a designation of a
beneficiary to take the ownership at the death of the owner or the
deaths of all multiple owners.
```
