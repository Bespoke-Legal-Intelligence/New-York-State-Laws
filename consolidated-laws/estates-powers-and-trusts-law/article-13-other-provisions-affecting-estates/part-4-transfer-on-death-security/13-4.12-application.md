# Estates, Powers and Trusts Law § 13-4.12 — Application

[Estates, Powers and Trusts Law](../../README.md) › [Article 13 — Other Provisions Affecting Estates](../README.md) › [Part 4 — Transfer-on-death Security Registration](README.md)

```text
§ 13-4.12 Application
  This part applies to registrations of securities in beneficiary form
made before, on or after January first, two thousand six, by decedents
dying on or after January first, two thousand six.
```
