# Estates, Powers and Trusts Law § 5-4.5 — Non-marital children

[Estates, Powers and Trusts Law](../../README.md) › [Article 5 — Family Rights](../README.md) › [Part 4 — Rights of Members of Family Resulting From Wrongful Act, Neglect or Default Causing Death of Decedent](README.md)

```text
§ 5-4.5 Non-marital children
  For the purposes of this part, a non-marital child is the distributee
of his father and paternal kindred and the father and paternal kindred
of a non-marital child are that child's distributees to the extent
permitted by 4-1.2.
```
