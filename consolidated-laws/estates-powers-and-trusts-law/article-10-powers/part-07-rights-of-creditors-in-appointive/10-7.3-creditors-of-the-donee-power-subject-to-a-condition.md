# Estates, Powers and Trusts Law § 10-7.3 — Creditors of the donee; power subject to a condition

[Estates, Powers and Trusts Law](../../README.md) › [Article 10 — Powers](../README.md) › [Part 7 — Rights of Creditors In Appointive Property](README.md)

```text
§ 10-7.3 Creditors of the donee; power subject to a condition
  A general power of appointment may be created subject to a condition
precedent or subsequent, and until the condition is fulfilled, it is not
subject to the provisions of 10-7.2.
```
