# Estates, Powers and Trusts Law § 10-5.1 — Scope of the authority of the donee

[Estates, Powers and Trusts Law](../../README.md) › [Article 10 — Powers](../README.md) › [Part 5 — Extent of Donee's Authority to Appoint or Contract to Appoint an Estate In Appointive Property](README.md)

```text
§ 10-5.1 Scope of the authority of the donee
  The scope of the donee's authority as to appointees and as to the time
and manner of the appointment is unlimited except as the donor manifests
a contrary intention.
```
