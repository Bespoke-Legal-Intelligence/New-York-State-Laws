# Estates, Powers and Trusts Law § 10-5.2 — Contract to appoint; power presently exercisable

[Estates, Powers and Trusts Law](../../README.md) › [Article 10 — Powers](../README.md) › [Part 5 — Extent of Donee's Authority to Appoint or Contract to Appoint an Estate In Appointive Property](README.md)

```text
§ 10-5.2 Contract to appoint; power presently exercisable
  The donee of a power of appointment which is presently exercisable, or
of a postponed power which has become exercisable, can contract to make
an appointment to the extent that the contract or the promised
appointment does not confer a benefit upon a person who is not a
permissible appointee under the power.
```
