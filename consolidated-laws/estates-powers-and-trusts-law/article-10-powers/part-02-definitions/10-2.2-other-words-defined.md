# Estates, Powers and Trusts Law § 10-2.2 — Other words defined

[Estates, Powers and Trusts Law](../../README.md) › [Article 10 — Powers](../README.md) › [Part 2 — Definitions](README.md)

```text
§ 10-2.2 Other words defined
  (a) Donor. A donor is the person who creates or reserves a power.
  (b) Donee. A donee is the person to whom a power is given or in whose
favor a power is reserved.
  (c) Appointee. An appointee is the person in whose favor a power of
appointment is exercisable.
  (d) Appointive property. Appointive property is property which is the
subject of a power of appointment.
```
