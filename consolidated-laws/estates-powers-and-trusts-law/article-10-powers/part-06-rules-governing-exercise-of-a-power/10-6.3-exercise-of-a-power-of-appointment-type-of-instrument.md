# Estates, Powers and Trusts Law § 10-6.3 — Exercise of a power of appointment; type of instrument

[Estates, Powers and Trusts Law](../../README.md) › [Article 10 — Powers](../README.md) › [Part 6 — Rules Governing Exercise of a Power of Appointment](README.md)

```text
§ 10-6.3 Exercise of a power of appointment; type of instrument
  A power of appointment can be exercised only by a written instrument
which would be sufficient to dispose of the estate intended to be
appointed if the donee were the actual owner.
```
