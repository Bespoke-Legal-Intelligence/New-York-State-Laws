# Estates, Powers and Trusts Law § 10-10.2 — Power to lease in tenant for life; scope

[Estates, Powers and Trusts Law](../../README.md) › [Article 10 — Powers](../README.md) › [Part 10 — Provisions Affecting Powers Other Than Powers of Appointment](README.md)

```text
§ 10-10.2 Power to lease in tenant for life; scope
  A power may be conferred upon a tenant for life to make leases of real
property for a term of not more than twenty-one years to commence in
possession during his lifetime.  If the power authorizes, or the life
tenant makes, a lease for a term in excess of twenty-one years, such
power or lease is valid for twenty-one years, but is void as to the
excess.
```
