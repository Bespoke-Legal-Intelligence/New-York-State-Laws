# Estates, Powers and Trusts Law § 10-10.6 — Effect of reserved unqualified power to revoke

[Estates, Powers and Trusts Law](../../README.md) › [Article 10 — Powers](../README.md) › [Part 10 — Provisions Affecting Powers Other Than Powers of Appointment](README.md)

```text
§ 10-10.6 Effect of reserved unqualified power to revoke
  Where a creator reserves an unqualified power of revocation, he
remains the absolute owner of the property disposed of so far as the
rights of his creditors or purchasers are concerned.
```
