# Estates, Powers and Trusts Law § 10-10.8 — Irrevocability of powers other than powers of appointment

[Estates, Powers and Trusts Law](../../README.md) › [Article 10 — Powers](../README.md) › [Part 10 — Provisions Affecting Powers Other Than Powers of Appointment](README.md)

```text
§ 10-10.8 Irrevocability of powers other than powers of appointment
  A power, other than a power of appointment, is irrevocable unless an
authority to revoke it is granted or reserved in the instrument creating
the power.
```
