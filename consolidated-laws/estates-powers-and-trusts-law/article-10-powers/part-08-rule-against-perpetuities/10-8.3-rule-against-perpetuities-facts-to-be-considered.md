# Estates, Powers and Trusts Law § 10-8.3 — Rule against perpetuities; facts to be considered

[Estates, Powers and Trusts Law](../../README.md) › [Article 10 — Powers](../README.md) › [Part 8 — Rule Against Perpetuities and Accumulations As Affected By Powers of Appointment](README.md)

```text
§ 10-8.3 Rule against perpetuities; facts to be considered
  When the permissible period of the rule against perpetuities must be
computed from the time of the creation of the power of appointment,
facts and circumstances existing on the effective date of the instrument
exercising the power shall be taken into account in determining the
validity of interests created by the instrument exercising the power.
```
