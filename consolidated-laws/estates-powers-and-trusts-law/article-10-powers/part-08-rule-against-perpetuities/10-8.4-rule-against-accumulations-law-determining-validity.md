# Estates, Powers and Trusts Law § 10-8.4 — Rule against accumulations; law determining validity in

[Estates, Powers and Trusts Law](../../README.md) › [Article 10 — Powers](../README.md) › [Part 8 — Rule Against Perpetuities and Accumulations As Affected By Powers of Appointment](README.md)

```text
§ 10-8.4 Rule against accumulations; law determining validity in
           exercise of a power of appointment
  When a direction for the accumulation of income is contained in an
instrument exercising a power, heretofore or hereafter created, the
validity of such direction is determined by the law in effect when the
power is exercised, and not by the law in effect when the power was
created.
```
