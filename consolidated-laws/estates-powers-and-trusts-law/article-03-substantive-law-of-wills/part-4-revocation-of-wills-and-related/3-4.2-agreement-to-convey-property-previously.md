# Estates, Powers and Trusts Law § 3-4.2 — Agreement to convey property previously disposed of by will not

[Estates, Powers and Trusts Law](../../README.md) › [Article 3 — Substantive Law of Wills](../README.md) › [Part 4 — Revocation of Wills and Related Subjects](README.md)

```text
§ 3-4.2 Agreement to convey property previously disposed of by will not
          a revocation
  An agreement made by a testator to convey any property does not revoke
a prior testamentary disposition of such property; but such property
passes under the will to the beneficiaries, subject to whatever rights
were created by such agreement.
```
