# Estates, Powers and Trusts Law § 3-3.1 — What a testamentary disposition includes

[Estates, Powers and Trusts Law](../../README.md) › [Article 3 — Substantive Law of Wills](../README.md) › [Part 3 — Rules Governing Testamentary Dispositions](README.md)

```text
§ 3-3.1 What a testamentary disposition includes
  Unless the will provides otherwise, a disposition by the testator of
all his property passes all of the property he was entitled to dispose
of at the time of his death.
```
