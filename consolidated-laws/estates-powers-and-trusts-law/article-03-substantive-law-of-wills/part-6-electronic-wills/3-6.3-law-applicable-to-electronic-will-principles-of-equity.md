# Estates, Powers and Trusts Law § 3-6.3 — Law applicable to electronic will; principles of equity

[Estates, Powers and Trusts Law](../../README.md) › [Article 3 — Substantive Law of Wills](../README.md) › [Part 6 — Electronic Wills](README.md)

```text
* § 3-6.3 Law applicable to electronic will; principles of equity
  An electronic will is a will for all purposes of the law of this
state.  The law of this state applicable to wills and principles of
equity apply to an electronic will, except as modified by this act.
  * NB Effective December 12, 2027
```
