# Estates, Powers and Trusts Law § 7-5.8 — Application

[Estates, Powers and Trusts Law](../../README.md) › [Article 7 — Trusts](../README.md) › [Part 5 — Bank Accounts In Trust Form](README.md)

```text
§ 7-5.8 Application
  This part shall apply to all funds in trust accounts, as defined in
paragraph (d) of section 7-5.1, which are in existence on its effective
date, except that its provisions shall not impair or defeat any rights
which have accrued prior to such date.
```
