# Estates, Powers and Trusts Law § 7-5.6 — Joint depositors

[Estates, Powers and Trusts Law](../../README.md) › [Article 7 — Trusts](../README.md) › [Part 5 — Bank Accounts In Trust Form](README.md)

```text
§ 7-5.6 Joint depositors
  If a trust account is established in the names of more than one
depositor, in form to be paid or delivered to any, or the survivor of
them, in trust for another, such account shall be subject to the terms
of this part, except that the title to the funds on deposit, as between
the depositors, shall be governed by article XIII-E of the banking law.
```
