# Estates, Powers and Trusts Law § 7-6.10 — Single custodianship

[Estates, Powers and Trusts Law](../../README.md) › [Article 7 — Trusts](../README.md) › [Part 6 — Uniform Transfers to Minors Act](README.md)

```text
§ 7-6.10 Single custodianship
  A transfer may be made only for one minor, and only one person may be
the custodian. All custodial property held under this part by the same
custodian for the benefit of the same minor constitutes a single
custodianship.
```
