# Estates, Powers and Trusts Law § 7-6.26 — Severability

[Estates, Powers and Trusts Law](../../README.md) › [Article 7 — Trusts](../README.md) › [Part 6 — Uniform Transfers to Minors Act](README.md)

```text
§ 7-6.26 Severability
  If any provisions of this part or its application to any person or
circumstances is held invalid, the invalidity does not affect other
provisions or applications of this part which can be given effect
without the invalid provision or application, and to this end provisions
of this part are severable.
```
