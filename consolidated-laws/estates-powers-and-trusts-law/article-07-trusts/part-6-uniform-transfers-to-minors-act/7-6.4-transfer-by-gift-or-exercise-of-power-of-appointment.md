# Estates, Powers and Trusts Law § 7-6.4 — Transfer by gift or exercise of power of appointment

[Estates, Powers and Trusts Law](../../README.md) › [Article 7 — Trusts](../README.md) › [Part 6 — Uniform Transfers to Minors Act](README.md)

```text
§ 7-6.4 Transfer by gift or exercise of power of appointment
  A person may make a transfer by irrevocable gift to, or the
irrevocable exercise of a power of appointment in favor of, a custodian
for the benefit of a minor pursuant to 7-6.9.
```
