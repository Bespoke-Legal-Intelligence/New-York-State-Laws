# Estates, Powers and Trusts Law § 7-6.20 — Termination of custodianship

[Estates, Powers and Trusts Law](../../README.md) › [Article 7 — Trusts](../README.md) › [Part 6 — Uniform Transfers to Minors Act](README.md)

```text
§ 7-6.20 Termination of custodianship
  The custodian shall transfer in an appropriate manner the custodial
property to the minor or to the minor's estate upon the earlier of:
  (a) the minor's attainment of twenty-one years of age with respect to
custodial property transferred under 7-6.4 or 7-6.5;
  (b) the minor's attainment of age eighteen or other statutory age of
majority of New York with respect to custodial property transferred
under 7-6.6 or 7-6.7; or
  (c) the minor's death.
```
