# Estates, Powers and Trusts Law § 7-6.8 — Receipt for custodial property

[Estates, Powers and Trusts Law](../../README.md) › [Article 7 — Trusts](../README.md) › [Part 6 — Uniform Transfers to Minors Act](README.md)

```text
§ 7-6.8 Receipt for custodial property
  A written acknowledgement of delivery by a custodian constitutes a
sufficient receipt and discharge for custodial property transferred to a
custodian pursuant to this part.
```
