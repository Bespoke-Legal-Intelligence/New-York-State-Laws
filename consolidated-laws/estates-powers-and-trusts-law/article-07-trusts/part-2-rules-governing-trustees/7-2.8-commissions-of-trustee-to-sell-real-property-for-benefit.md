# Estates, Powers and Trusts Law § 7-2.8 — Commissions of trustee to sell real property for benefit of

[Estates, Powers and Trusts Law](../../README.md) › [Article 7 — Trusts](../README.md) › [Part 2 — Rules Governing Trustees](README.md)

```text
§ 7-2.8 Commissions of trustee to sell real property for benefit of
          creditors
  A trustee of a trust to sell real property for the benefit of
creditors is entitled to the same commissions as an assignee for the
benefit of creditors.
```
