# Estates, Powers and Trusts Law § 7-2.2 — When estate of trustee ceases

[Estates, Powers and Trusts Law](../../README.md) › [Article 7 — Trusts](../README.md) › [Part 2 — Rules Governing Trustees](README.md)

```text
§ 7-2.2 When estate of trustee ceases
  When the purpose for which an express trust is created ceases, the
estate of the trustee also ceases.
```
