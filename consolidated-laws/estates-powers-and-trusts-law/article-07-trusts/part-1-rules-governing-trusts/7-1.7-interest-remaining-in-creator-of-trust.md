# Estates, Powers and Trusts Law § 7-1.7 — Interest remaining in creator of trust

[Estates, Powers and Trusts Law](../../README.md) › [Article 7 — Trusts](../README.md) › [Part 1 — Rules Governing Trusts](README.md)

```text
§ 7-1.7 Interest remaining in creator of trust
  Every legal estate and interest not embraced in an express trust and
not otherwise disposed of remains in the creator.
```
