# Estates, Powers and Trusts Law § 7-3.3 — Person paying money to the trustee protected

[Estates, Powers and Trusts Law](../../README.md) › [Article 7 — Trusts](../README.md) › [Part 3 — Rights of Purchasers, Creditors and Other Persons](README.md)

```text
§ 7-3.3 Person paying money to the trustee protected
  A person who in good faith transfers money or property to a trustee is
not responsible for the proper application of such money or property;
and any right or title derived by him from the trustee in consideration
of such transfer is not affected by the trustee's misapplication of such
money or property.
```
