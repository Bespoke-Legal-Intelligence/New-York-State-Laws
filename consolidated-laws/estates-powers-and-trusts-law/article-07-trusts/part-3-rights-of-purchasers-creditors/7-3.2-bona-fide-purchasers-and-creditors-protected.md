# Estates, Powers and Trusts Law § 7-3.2 — Bona fide purchasers and creditors protected

[Estates, Powers and Trusts Law](../../README.md) › [Article 7 — Trusts](../README.md) › [Part 3 — Rights of Purchasers, Creditors and Other Persons](README.md)

```text
§ 7-3.2 Bona fide purchasers and creditors protected
  An express trust not declared in the disposition to the trustee or an
implied or resulting trust does not defeat the title of a purchaser from
the trustee for value and without notice of the trust, or the rights of
a creditor who extended credit to the trustee in reliance upon his
apparent ownership of the trust property.
```
