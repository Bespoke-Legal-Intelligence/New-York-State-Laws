# Estates, Powers and Trusts Law § 2-1.1 — Heirs at law and next of kin defined

[Estates, Powers and Trusts Law](../../README.md) › [Article 2 — Rules Governing Dispositions Subject to This Law](../README.md) › [Part 1 — Substantive Rules Governing Dispositions](README.md)

```text
§ 2-1.1 Heirs at law and next of kin defined
  Whenever used in a statute or instrument, unless a contrary intention
is expressed therein, the term "heirs", "heirs at law", "next of kin" or
any term of like import means the distributees, as defined in 1-2.5.
```
