# Estates, Powers and Trusts Law § 2-1.4 — Words of inheritance unnecessary

[Estates, Powers and Trusts Law](../../README.md) › [Article 2 — Rules Governing Dispositions Subject to This Law](../README.md) › [Part 1 — Substantive Rules Governing Dispositions](README.md)

```text
§ 2-1.4 Words of inheritance unnecessary
  The word "heirs" or words of inheritance of like import are not
necessary to create or dispose  of a fee.
```
