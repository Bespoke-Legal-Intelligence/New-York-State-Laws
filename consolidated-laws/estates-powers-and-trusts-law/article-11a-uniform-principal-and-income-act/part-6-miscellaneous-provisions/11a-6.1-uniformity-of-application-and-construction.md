# Estates, Powers and Trusts Law § 11-A-6.1 — Uniformity of application and construction

[Estates, Powers and Trusts Law](../../README.md) › [Article 11-A — Uniform Principal and Income Act](../README.md) › [Part 6 — Miscellaneous Provisions](README.md)

```text
§ 11-A-6.1 Uniformity of application and construction
  In applying and construing this article, consideration must be given
to the need to promote uniformity of the law with respect to its subject
matter among states that enact it.
```
