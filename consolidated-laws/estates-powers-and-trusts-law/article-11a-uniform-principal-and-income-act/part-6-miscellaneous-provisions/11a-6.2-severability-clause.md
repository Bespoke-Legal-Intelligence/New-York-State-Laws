# Estates, Powers and Trusts Law § 11-A-6.2 — Severability clause

[Estates, Powers and Trusts Law](../../README.md) › [Article 11-A — Uniform Principal and Income Act](../README.md) › [Part 6 — Miscellaneous Provisions](README.md)

```text
§ 11-A-6.2 Severability clause
  If any provision of this article or its application to any person or
circumstance is held invalid, the invalidity does not affect other
provisions or applications of this article which can be given effect
without the invalid provision or application, and to this end the
provisions of this article are severable.
```
