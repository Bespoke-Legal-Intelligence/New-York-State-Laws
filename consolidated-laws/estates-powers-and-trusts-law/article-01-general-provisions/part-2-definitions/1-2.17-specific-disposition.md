# Estates, Powers and Trusts Law § 1-2.17 — Specific disposition.

[Estates, Powers and Trusts Law](../../README.md) › [Article 1 — General Provisions](../README.md) › [Part 2 — Definitions](README.md)

```text
§ 1-2.17 Specific disposition.
  A specific disposition is a disposition of a specified or identified
item of the testator's property.
```
