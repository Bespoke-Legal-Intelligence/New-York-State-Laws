# Estates, Powers and Trusts Law § 1-2.7 — Fiduciary

[Estates, Powers and Trusts Law](../../README.md) › [Article 1 — General Provisions](../README.md) › [Part 2 — Definitions](README.md)

```text
§ 1-2.7 Fiduciary
  A fiduciary is a person who meets the description, in this part, of a
"personal representative" or who is designated by the creator or by the
court to act as an assignee for the benefit of creditors, or a
committee, conservator, curator, custodian, guardian, trustee or donee
of a power during minority.
```
