# Estates, Powers and Trusts Law § 1-2.12 — Person

[Estates, Powers and Trusts Law](../../README.md) › [Article 1 — General Provisions](../README.md) › [Part 2 — Definitions](README.md)

```text
§ 1-2.12 Person
  The term "person" includes a natural person, an association, board,
any corporation, whether municipal, stock or non-stock, court,
governmental agency, authority or subdivision, partnership or other firm
and the state.
```
