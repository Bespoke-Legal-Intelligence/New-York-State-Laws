# Estates, Powers and Trusts Law § 1-2.6 — Estate

[Estates, Powers and Trusts Law](../../README.md) › [Article 1 — General Provisions](../README.md) › [Part 2 — Definitions](README.md)

```text
§ 1-2.6 Estate
  Depending upon the context, "estate" may mean:
  (a)  The interest which a person has in property.
  (b)  The aggregate of property which a person owns.
```
