# Estates, Powers and Trusts Law § 1-2.13 — Personal representative

[Estates, Powers and Trusts Law](../../README.md) › [Article 1 — General Provisions](../README.md) › [Part 2 — Definitions](README.md)

```text
§ 1-2.13 Personal representative
  A personal representative is a person who has received letters to
administer the estate of a decedent. The term does not include an
assignee for the benefit of creditors, or a committee, conservator,
curator, custodian, guardian, trustee or donee of a power during
minority.
```
