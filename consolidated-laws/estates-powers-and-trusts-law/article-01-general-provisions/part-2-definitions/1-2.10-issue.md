# Estates, Powers and Trusts Law § 1-2.10 — Issue

[Estates, Powers and Trusts Law](../../README.md) › [Article 1 — General Provisions](../README.md) › [Part 2 — Definitions](README.md)

```text
§ 1-2.10 Issue
  (a)  Unless a contrary intention is indicated:
  (1)  Issue are the descendants in any degree from a common ancestor.
  (2)  The terms "issue" and "descendants", in subparagraph (1), include
adopted children.
```
