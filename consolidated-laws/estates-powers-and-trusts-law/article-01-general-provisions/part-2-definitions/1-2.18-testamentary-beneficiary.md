# Estates, Powers and Trusts Law § 1-2.18 — Testamentary beneficiary

[Estates, Powers and Trusts Law](../../README.md) › [Article 1 — General Provisions](../README.md) › [Part 2 — Definitions](README.md)

```text
§ 1-2.18 Testamentary beneficiary
  A testamentary beneficiary is a person in whose favor a disposition of
property is made by will.
```
