# Estates, Powers and Trusts Law § 1-2.8 — General disposition

[Estates, Powers and Trusts Law](../../README.md) › [Article 1 — General Provisions](../README.md) › [Part 2 — Definitions](README.md)

```text
§ 1-2.8 General disposition
  A general disposition is a testamentary disposition of property not
amounting to a demonstrative, residuary or specific disposition.
```
