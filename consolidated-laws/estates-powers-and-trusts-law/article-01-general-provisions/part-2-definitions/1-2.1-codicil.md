# Estates, Powers and Trusts Law § 1-2.1 — Codicil

[Estates, Powers and Trusts Law](../../README.md) › [Article 1 — General Provisions](../README.md) › [Part 2 — Definitions](README.md)

```text
§ 1-2.1 Codicil
  A codicil is a supplement to a will, either adding to, taking from or
altering its provisions or confirming it in whole or in part by
republication, but not totally revoking such will.
```
