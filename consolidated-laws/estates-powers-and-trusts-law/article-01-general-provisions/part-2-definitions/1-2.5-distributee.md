# Estates, Powers and Trusts Law § 1-2.5 — Distributee

[Estates, Powers and Trusts Law](../../README.md) › [Article 1 — General Provisions](../README.md) › [Part 2 — Definitions](README.md)

```text
§ 1-2.5 Distributee
  A distributee is a person entitled to take or share in the property of
a decedent under the statutes governing descent and distribution.
```
