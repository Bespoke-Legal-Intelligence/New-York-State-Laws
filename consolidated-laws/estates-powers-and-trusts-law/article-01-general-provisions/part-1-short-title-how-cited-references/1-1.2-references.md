# Estates, Powers and Trusts Law § 1-1.2 — References

[Estates, Powers and Trusts Law](../../README.md) › [Article 1 — General Provisions](../README.md) › [Part 1 — Short Title; How Cited; References; Severability; Application](README.md)

```text
§ 1-1.2 References
  Unless otherwise stated, all references in this chapter to article,
part or section number refer to the articles, parts or section numbers
of this chapter, and all references in any section of this chapter to a
lettered or numbered paragraph or subparagraph refer to the paragraph or
subparagraph so lettered or numbered in such section.
```
