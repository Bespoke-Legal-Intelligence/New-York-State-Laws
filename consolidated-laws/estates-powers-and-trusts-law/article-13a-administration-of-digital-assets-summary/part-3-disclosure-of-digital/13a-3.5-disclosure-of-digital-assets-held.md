# Estates, Powers and Trusts Law § 13-A-3.5 — Disclosure of digital assets held in trust when trustee is

[Estates, Powers and Trusts Law](../../README.md) › [Article 13-A — Administration of Digital Assets Summary of Article](../README.md) › [Part 3 — Disclosure of Digital Assets to Fiduciary](README.md)

```text
§ 13-A-3.5 Disclosure of digital assets held in trust when trustee is
             original user
  Unless otherwise ordered by the court or provided in a trust, a
custodian shall disclose to a trustee that is an original user of an
account any digital asset of the account held in trust, including a
catalogue of electronic communications of the trustee and the content of
electronic communications.
```
