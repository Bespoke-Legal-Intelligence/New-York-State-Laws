# Estates, Powers and Trusts Law § 6-4.5 — Definition of a possibility of reverter

[Estates, Powers and Trusts Law](../../README.md) › [Article 6 — Classification, Creation, Definition Of, and Rules Governing Estates In Property](../README.md) › [Part 4 — Estates Defined](README.md)

```text
§ 6-4.5 Definition of a possibility of reverter
  A possibility of reverter is the future estate left in the creator or
in his successors in interest upon the simultaneous creation of an
estate that will terminate automatically within a period of time defined
by the occurrence of a specified event.
```
