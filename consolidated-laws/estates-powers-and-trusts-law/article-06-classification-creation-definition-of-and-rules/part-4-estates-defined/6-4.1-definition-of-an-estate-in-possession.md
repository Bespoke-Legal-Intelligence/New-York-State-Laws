# Estates, Powers and Trusts Law § 6-4.1 — Definition of an estate in possession

[Estates, Powers and Trusts Law](../../README.md) › [Article 6 — Classification, Creation, Definition Of, and Rules Governing Estates In Property](../README.md) › [Part 4 — Estates Defined](README.md)

```text
§ 6-4.1 Definition of an estate in possession
  An estate in possession is an estate which entitles the owner to the
immediate possession of property.
```
