# Estates, Powers and Trusts Law § 6-4.4 — Definition of a reversion

[Estates, Powers and Trusts Law](../../README.md) › [Article 6 — Classification, Creation, Definition Of, and Rules Governing Estates In Property](../README.md) › [Part 4 — Estates Defined](README.md)

```text
§ 6-4.4 Definition of a reversion
  A reversion is the future estate, other than a possibility of reverter
and a right of reacquisition, left in the creator or in his successors
in interest upon the simultaneous creation of one or more lesser estates
than the creator originally owned.
```
