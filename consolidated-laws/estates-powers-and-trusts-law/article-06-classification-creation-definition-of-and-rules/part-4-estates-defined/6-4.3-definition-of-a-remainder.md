# Estates, Powers and Trusts Law § 6-4.3 — Definition of a remainder

[Estates, Powers and Trusts Law](../../README.md) › [Article 6 — Classification, Creation, Definition Of, and Rules Governing Estates In Property](../README.md) › [Part 4 — Estates Defined](README.md)

```text
§ 6-4.3 Definition of a remainder
  A remainder is a future estate, as defined in 6-4.2, created in favor
of a person other than the creator.
```
