# Estates, Powers and Trusts Law § 6-1.3 — When estate for life of third person is real property; when

[Estates, Powers and Trusts Law](../../README.md) › [Article 6 — Classification, Creation, Definition Of, and Rules Governing Estates In Property](../README.md) › [Part 1 — Estates Classified As to Duration](README.md)

```text
§ 6-1.3 When estate for life of third person is real property; when
          personal property
  A disposition of real property for the life of a third person, whether
limited to heirs or otherwise, is real property only during the life of
the grantee or devisee; after his death it is personal property.
```
