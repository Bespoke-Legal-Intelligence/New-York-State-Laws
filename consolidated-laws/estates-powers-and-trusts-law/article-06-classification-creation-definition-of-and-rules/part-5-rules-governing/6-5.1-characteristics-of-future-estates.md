# Estates, Powers and Trusts Law § 6-5.1 — Characteristics of future estates

[Estates, Powers and Trusts Law](../../README.md) › [Article 6 — Classification, Creation, Definition Of, and Rules Governing Estates In Property](../README.md) › [Part 5 — Rules Governing Future Estates](README.md)

```text
§ 6-5.1 Characteristics of future estates
  Future estates are descendible, devisable and alienable, in the same
manner as estates in possession.
```
