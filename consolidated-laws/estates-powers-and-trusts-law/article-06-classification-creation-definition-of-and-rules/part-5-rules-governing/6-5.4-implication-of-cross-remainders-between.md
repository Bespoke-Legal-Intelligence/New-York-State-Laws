# Estates, Powers and Trusts Law § 6-5.4 — Implication of cross remainders between tenants in common

[Estates, Powers and Trusts Law](../../README.md) › [Article 6 — Classification, Creation, Definition Of, and Rules Governing Estates In Property](../README.md) › [Part 5 — Rules Governing Future Estates](README.md)

```text
§ 6-5.4 Implication of cross remainders between tenants in common
  When a limitation, if contained in a will, would create a tenancy in
common, with implied cross remainders, a like limitation, if contained
in a deed, has the same effect.
```
