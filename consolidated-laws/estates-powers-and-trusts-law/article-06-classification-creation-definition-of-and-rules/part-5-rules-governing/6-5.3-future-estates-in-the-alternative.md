# Estates, Powers and Trusts Law § 6-5.3 — Future estates in the alternative

[Estates, Powers and Trusts Law](../../README.md) › [Article 6 — Classification, Creation, Definition Of, and Rules Governing Estates In Property](../README.md) › [Part 5 — Rules Governing Future Estates](README.md)

```text
§ 6-5.3 Future estates in the alternative
  Two or more future estates may be created to take effect in the
alternative, so that if the first in order fails to vest, the next in
succession is substituted for it and takes effect accordingly.
```
