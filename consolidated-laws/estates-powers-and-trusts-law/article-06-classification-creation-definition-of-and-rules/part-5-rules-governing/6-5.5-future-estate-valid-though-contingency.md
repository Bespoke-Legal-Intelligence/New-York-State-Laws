# Estates, Powers and Trusts Law § 6-5.5 — Future estate valid though contingency improbable

[Estates, Powers and Trusts Law](../../README.md) › [Article 6 — Classification, Creation, Definition Of, and Rules Governing Estates In Property](../README.md) › [Part 5 — Rules Governing Future Estates](README.md)

```text
§ 6-5.5 Future estate valid though contingency improbable
  A future estate, otherwise valid, shall not be void on the ground of
the improbability of the contingency on which it is limited to take
effect.
```
