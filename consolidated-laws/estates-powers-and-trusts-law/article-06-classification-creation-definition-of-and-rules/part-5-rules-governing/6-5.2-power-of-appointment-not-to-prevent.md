# Estates, Powers and Trusts Law § 6-5.2 — Power of appointment not to prevent vesting

[Estates, Powers and Trusts Law](../../README.md) › [Article 6 — Classification, Creation, Definition Of, and Rules Governing Estates In Property](../README.md) › [Part 5 — Rules Governing Future Estates](README.md)

```text
§ 6-5.2 Power of appointment not to prevent vesting
  The existence of an unexecuted power of appointment does not prevent
the vesting of a future estate, limited in default of the execution of
the power.
```
