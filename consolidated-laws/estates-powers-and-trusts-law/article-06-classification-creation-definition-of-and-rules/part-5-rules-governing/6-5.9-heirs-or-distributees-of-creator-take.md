# Estates, Powers and Trusts Law § 6-5.9 — Heirs or distributees of creator take as purchasers

[Estates, Powers and Trusts Law](../../README.md) › [Article 6 — Classification, Creation, Definition Of, and Rules Governing Estates In Property](../README.md) › [Part 5 — Rules Governing Future Estates](README.md)

```text
§ 6-5.9 Heirs or distributees of creator take as purchasers
  Where a remainder is limited to the heirs or distributees of the
creator of an estate in property, such heirs or distributees take as
purchasers.
```
