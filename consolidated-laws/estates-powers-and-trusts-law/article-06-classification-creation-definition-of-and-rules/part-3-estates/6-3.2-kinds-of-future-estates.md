# Estates, Powers and Trusts Law § 6-3.2 — Kinds of future estates

[Estates, Powers and Trusts Law](../../README.md) › [Article 6 — Classification, Creation, Definition Of, and Rules Governing Estates In Property](../README.md) › [Part 3 — Estates Classified As to Time of Enjoyment and Creation](README.md)

```text
§ 6-3.2 Kinds of future estates
  (a)  Future estates are divided into:
  (1)  Estates left in the creator, consisting of:
  (A)  Reversions.
  (B)  Possibilities of reverter.
  (C)  Rights of reacquisition.
  (2)  Estates in favor of a person other than the creator, namely
remainders, that are:
  (A)  Indefeasibly vested.
  (B)  Vested subject to open.
  (C)  Vested subject to complete defeasance.
  (D)  Subject to a condition precedent.
```
