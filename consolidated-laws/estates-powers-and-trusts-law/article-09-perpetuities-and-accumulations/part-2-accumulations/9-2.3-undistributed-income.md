# Estates, Powers and Trusts Law § 9-2.3 — Undistributed income

[Estates, Powers and Trusts Law](../../README.md) › [Article 9 — Perpetuities and Accumulations](../README.md) › [Part 2 — Accumulations](README.md)

```text
§ 9-2.3 Undistributed income
  When income is not disposed of and no valid direction is given for its
accumulation it passes to the persons presumptively entitled to the next
eventual estate.
```
