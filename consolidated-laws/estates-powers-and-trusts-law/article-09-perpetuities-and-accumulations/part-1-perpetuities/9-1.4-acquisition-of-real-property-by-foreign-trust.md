# Estates, Powers and Trusts Law § 9-1.4 — Acquisition of real property by foreign trust

[Estates, Powers and Trusts Law](../../README.md) › [Article 9 — Perpetuities and Accumulations](../README.md) › [Part 1 — Perpetuities](README.md)

```text
§ 9-1.4 Acquisition of real property by foreign trust
  Where real property situated in this state is acquired by a trust
validly created under the law of another jurisdiction, whether there is
a violation of the rule against perpetuities and whether a direction for
the accumulation of rents and profits is valid are determined by the law
of this state in effect at the time of the acquisition of such property.
```
