# Religious Corporations Law § 301 — Application for incorporation

[Religious Corporations Law](../README.md) › [Article 16 — Spiritualist Churches Connected With the National Spiritualist Association](README.md)

```text
  § 301. Application for incorporation. An unincorporated Spiritualist
church may apply to the National Spiritualist Association for permission
to incorporate under this article, which written permission granted by
the board of trustees of the National Spiritualist Association shall be
attached to and become a part of the certificate of incorporation.
```
