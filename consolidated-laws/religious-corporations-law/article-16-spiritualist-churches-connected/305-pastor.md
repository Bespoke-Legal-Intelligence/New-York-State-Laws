# Religious Corporations Law § 305 — Pastor

[Religious Corporations Law](../README.md) › [Article 16 — Spiritualist Churches Connected With the National Spiritualist Association](README.md)

```text
  § 305. Pastor. The pastor of the church shall be called, settled or
removed and his or her salary fixed, only by the vote of a majority of
the members of the corporation duly qualified to vote at elections
present and voting at a meeting of such corporation called for that
purpose, subject to the written consent of the board of trustees of the
National Spiritualist Association.
```
