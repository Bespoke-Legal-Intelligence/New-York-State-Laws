# Religious Corporations Law § 433 — Right of self government

[Religious Corporations Law](../README.md) › [Article 20 — Assemblies of God Churches](README.md)

```text
  § 433. Right of self government. Each church shall have the right of
self government under Jesus Christ, its living head, and shall have the
power to choose, call or dismiss its minister, establish the minister's
salary, elect its trustees and other officials, and transact all other
business pertaining to its life as a local unit.
```
