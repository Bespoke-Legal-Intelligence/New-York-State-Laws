# Religious Corporations Law § 437 — District superintendent

[Religious Corporations Law](../README.md) › [Article 20 — Assemblies of God Churches](README.md)

```text
  § 437. District superintendent. The district superintendent shall by
virtue of his office be designated the president and presiding officer
of the district corporation. He shall exercise such powers as are
granted him by the constitution and by-laws.
```
