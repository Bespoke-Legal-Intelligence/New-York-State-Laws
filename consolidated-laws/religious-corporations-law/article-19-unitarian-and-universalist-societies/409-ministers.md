# Religious Corporations Law § 409 — Ministers

[Religious Corporations Law](../README.md) › [Article 19 — Unitarian and Universalist Societies](README.md)

```text
  § 409. Ministers. Any minister shall be called or removed and the
salary fixed or changed by a vote of the majority of the members present
and voting at a meeting of such corporation called for that purpose,
unless the by-laws provide otherwise.
```
