# Religious Corporations Law § 172 — Application of this article

[Religious Corporations Law](../README.md) › [Article 8 — Churches of the United Church of Christ, Congregational Christian and Independent Churches](README.md)

```text
  § 172. Application of this article. This article applies only to
churches of the United Church of Christ, Congregational Christian
churches, whether or not part of the United Church of Christ, and
Independent churches.
```
