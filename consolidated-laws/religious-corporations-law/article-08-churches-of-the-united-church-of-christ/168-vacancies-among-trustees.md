# Religious Corporations Law § 168 — Vacancies among trustees

[Religious Corporations Law](../README.md) › [Article 8 — Churches of the United Church of Christ, Congregational Christian and Independent Churches](README.md)

```text
  § 168. Vacancies among trustees.  If any trustee of any such
incorporated church declines to act, resigns or dies, or ceases to be a
qualified voter at a corporate meeting thereof, his office shall be
vacant; and such vacancy may be filled by the remaining trustees until
the next annual corporate meeting of such church; at which meeting the
vacancy shall be filled for the unexpired term.
```
