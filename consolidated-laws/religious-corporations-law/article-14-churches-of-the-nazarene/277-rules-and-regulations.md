# Religious Corporations Law § 277 — Rules and regulations

[Religious Corporations Law](../README.md) › [Article 14 — Churches of the Nazarene](README.md)

```text
  § 277. Rules and regulations. Any church incorporated or
reincorporated under this article, shall be subject to the rules and
regulations of the general assembly of the church of the Nazarene.
```
