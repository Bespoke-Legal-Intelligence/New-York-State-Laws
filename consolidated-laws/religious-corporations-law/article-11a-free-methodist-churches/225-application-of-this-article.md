# Religious Corporations Law § 225 — Application of this article

[Religious Corporations Law](../README.md) › [Article 11-A — Free Methodist Churches](README.md)

```text
  § 225. Application of this article. This article applies only to
churches, societies, districts, conferences, and such other authorized
religious organizations of the Free Methodist Church of North America as
are or shall be situated in the state of New York.
```
