# Religious Corporations Law § 182 — Vacancies in boards of trustees

[Religious Corporations Law](../README.md) › [Article 9 — Free Churches](README.md)

```text
  § 182. Vacancies in boards of trustees. Any vacancies occurring in the
said board of trustees shall be supplied by the remaining trustees at
any legal meeting of the members; but there shall always be at least
five members of the board who are not ministers of the gospel or priests
of any denomination.
```
