# Religious Corporations Law § 169-A — Application for incorporation

[Religious Corporations Law](../README.md) › [Article 8-A — Churches of the Ukrainian Orthodox Churches of America](README.md)

```text
  § 169-a. Application for incorporation. Any Ukrainian Orthodox church
of the Greek rite may become incorporated in this state as a church of
the Ukrainian Orthodox Church of America by executing, acknowledging and
filing a certificate of incorporation as hereinafter provided.
```
