# Religious Corporations Law § 27 — Reservation as to Baptist churches, churches of the United Church of Christ and Congregational Christian churches

[Religious Corporations Law](../README.md) › [Article 2 — General Provisions](README.md)

```text
  § 27. Reservation as to Baptist churches, churches of the United
Church of Christ and Congregational Christian churches. Sections
twenty-five and twenty-six are not applicable to a Baptist church, a
church of the United Church of Christ, a Congregational Christian church
or to any other religious corporation having a congregational form of
government.
```
