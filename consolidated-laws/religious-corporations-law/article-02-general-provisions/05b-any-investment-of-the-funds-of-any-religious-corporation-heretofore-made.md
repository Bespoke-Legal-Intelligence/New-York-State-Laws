# Religious Corporations Law § 5-B — Any investment of the funds of any religious corporation heretofore made by the trustees thereof shall not be deemed to have been restric...

[Religious Corporations Law](../README.md) › [Article 2 — General Provisions](README.md)

```text
  § 5-b. Any investment of the funds of any religious corporation
heretofore made by the trustees thereof shall not be deemed to have been
restricted to securities which are lawful for the investment of trust
funds.
```
