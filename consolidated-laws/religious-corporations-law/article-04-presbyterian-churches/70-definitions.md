# Religious Corporations Law § 70 — Definitions

[Religious Corporations Law](../README.md) › [Article 4 — Presbyterian Churches](README.md)

```text
  § 70. Definitions. The words "spiritual officers," as used in this
article, include the pastor or pastors, the ruling elders, and the
deacons, of any church to which this article is applicable.
```
