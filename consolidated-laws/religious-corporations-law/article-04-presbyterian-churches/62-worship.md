# Religious Corporations Law § 62 — Worship

[Religious Corporations Law](../README.md) › [Article 4 — Presbyterian Churches](README.md)

```text
  § 62. Worship.  Nothing in this chapter contained shall authorize the
fixing or changing of the times, nature or order of public worship of
any particular Presbyterian church in any other manner, or by any other
authority than in the manner and by the authority provided in the
constitution of the Presbyterian Church (U.S.A.).
```
