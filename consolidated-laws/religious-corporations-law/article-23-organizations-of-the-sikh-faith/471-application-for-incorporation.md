# Religious Corporations Law § 471 — Application for incorporation

[Religious Corporations Law](../README.md) › [Article 23 — Organizations of the Sikh Faith](README.md)

```text
  § 471. Application for incorporation. Any unincorporated church or any
heretofore incorporated church wishing to incorporate under this article
shall execute, acknowledge and file a certificate of incorporation as
hereinafter provided.
```
