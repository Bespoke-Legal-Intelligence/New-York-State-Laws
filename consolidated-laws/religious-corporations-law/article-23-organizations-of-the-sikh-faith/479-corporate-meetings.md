# Religious Corporations Law § 479 — Corporate meetings

[Religious Corporations Law](../README.md) › [Article 23 — Organizations of the Sikh Faith](README.md)

```text
  § 479. Corporate meetings. 1. Each church shall determine the
requirements for a quorum in their by-laws that shall not exceed fifty
percent of the members.
  2. The action of the meeting upon any matter or question shall be
decided in a manner provided by the by-laws.
  3. The presiding officer of any meeting shall be the senior granthi or
guru of the church or if none, as shall be set forth in the by-laws of
the church.
```
