# Religious Corporations Law § 481 — Application for incorporation

[Religious Corporations Law](../README.md) › [Article 24 — Organizations of the Islamic Faith](README.md)

```text
  § 481. Application for incorporation. Any unincorporated church or any
heretofore incorporated church wishing to incorporate under this article
shall execute, acknowledge and file a certificate of incorporation as
hereinafter provided.
```
