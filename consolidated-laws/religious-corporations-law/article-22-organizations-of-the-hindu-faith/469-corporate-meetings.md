# Religious Corporations Law § 469 — Corporate meetings

[Religious Corporations Law](../README.md) › [Article 22 — Organizations of the Hindu Faith](README.md)

```text
  § 469. Corporate meetings. 1. Each church shall determine the
requirements for a quorum in their by-laws that shall not exceed fifty
percent of the members.
  2. The action of the meeting upon any matter or question shall be
decided in a manner provided by the by-laws.
  3. The presiding officer of any meeting shall be the senior pandit,
swami or guru of the church or if none, as shall be set forth in the
by-laws of the church.
```
