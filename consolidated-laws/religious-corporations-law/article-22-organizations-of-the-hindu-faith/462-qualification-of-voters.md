# Religious Corporations Law § 462 — Qualification of voters

[Religious Corporations Law](../README.md) › [Article 22 — Organizations of the Hindu Faith](README.md)

```text
  § 462. Qualification of voters. The following parties and no others
shall be qualified voters for all purposes under this article: all
persons eighteen years of age or over who are members in good and
regular standing of the church or body by admission into membership
therewith, in accordance with the standards for membership in the local
church as determined by the local church itself.
```
