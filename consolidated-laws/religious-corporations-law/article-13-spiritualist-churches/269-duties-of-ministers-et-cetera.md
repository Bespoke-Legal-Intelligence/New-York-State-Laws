# Religious Corporations Law § 269 — Duties of ministers, et cetera

[Religious Corporations Law](../README.md) › [Article 13 — Spiritualist Churches](README.md)

```text
  § 269. Duties of ministers, et cetera. Ministers and clerical
attendants shall perform such duties as the by-laws shall direct in
accordance with the rules and regulations of the General Assembly of
Spiritualists.
```
