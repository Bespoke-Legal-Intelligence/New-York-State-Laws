# General City Law § 56 — Issue of licenses to connect with sewers and water mains restricted

[General City Law](../README.md) › [Article 4 — Plumbing and Drainage](README.md)

```text
  § 56. Issue of licenses to connect with sewers and water mains
restricted. The commissioner of public works of any city, or the officer
or officers acting in a like capacity in any of the cities of this
state, and having charge of the sewers and water mains therein, shall
not issue a license to any one to connect with the sewers or with the
water mains of such cities, unless such person has obtained and shall
produce a certificate of competency from the examining board of such
city.
```
