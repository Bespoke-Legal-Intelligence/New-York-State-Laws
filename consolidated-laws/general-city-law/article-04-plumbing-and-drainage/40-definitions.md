# General City Law § 40 — Definitions

[General City Law](../README.md) › [Article 4 — Plumbing and Drainage](README.md)

```text
  § 40. Definitions. When used in this article, the words "employing or
master plumber" shall mean a person having a regular place of business
and who, by himself or journeymen plumbers in his employ, performs
plumbing work.
```
