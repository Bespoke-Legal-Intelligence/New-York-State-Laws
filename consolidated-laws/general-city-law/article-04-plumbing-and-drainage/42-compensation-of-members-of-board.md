# General City Law § 42 — Compensation of members of board

[General City Law](../README.md) › [Article 4 — Plumbing and Drainage](README.md)

```text
  § 42. Compensation of members of board. The master and journeymen
plumbers serving as members of such board shall receive such
compensation for their services as shall be determined by the board of
estimate and apportionment or other similar body authorized by law to
fix and determine the compensation of city officers and employees.
```
