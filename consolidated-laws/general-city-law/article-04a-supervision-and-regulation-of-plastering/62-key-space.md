# General City Law § 62 — Key space

[General City Law](../README.md) › [Article 4-A — Supervision and Regulation of Plastering](README.md)

```text
  § 62. Key space. All ceilings, stud partitions and furred walls in
tenements, apartments, hospitals, schools, and other public buildings
where plastered with lime on wood lath shall have not less than
three-eighths space between lath. All grounds and jambs shall mean not
less than seven-eighths from the stud.
```
