# General City Law § 64 — Second coat

[General City Law](../README.md) › [Article 4-A — Supervision and Regulation of Plastering](README.md)

```text
  § 64. Second coat. Second coat or brown mortar shall be of first
quality. All browning must be straight, true with no unevenness or
irregularity of surface.
```
