# General City Law § 65 — Finishing

[General City Law](../README.md) › [Article 4-A — Supervision and Regulation of Plastering](README.md)

```text
  § 65. Finishing. When white mortar, or any other material of a like
character is used for finish coat, it shall be laid on regular and
troweled to a smooth surface showing neither deficiencies or brush
marks.
```
