# General City Law § 63 — First coat or scratch coat

[General City Law](../README.md) › [Article 4-A — Supervision and Regulation of Plastering](README.md)

```text
  § 63. First coat or scratch coat. First or scratch coat shall be of
first quality to be scratched thoroughly to make a key to retain second
coat; and shall be thoroughly dry or set before applying second coat.
```
