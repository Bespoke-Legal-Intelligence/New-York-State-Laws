# General City Law § 61 — Three coat work required on lath

[General City Law](../README.md) › [Article 4-A — Supervision and Regulation of Plastering](README.md)

```text
  § 61. Three coat work required on lath. All plastering in tenements,
apartments, hospitals, schools and other public buildings when on lath
shall be known as three coat work, namely, scratch coat, brown coat and
finish.
```
