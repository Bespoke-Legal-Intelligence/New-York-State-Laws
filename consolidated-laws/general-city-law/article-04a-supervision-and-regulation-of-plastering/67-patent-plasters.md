# General City Law § 67 — Patent plasters

[General City Law](../README.md) › [Article 4-A — Supervision and Regulation of Plastering](README.md)

```text
  § 67. Patent plasters. When patent plasters, such as ivory, acme,
windsor, et cetera, are used, lathing, if of wood lath, shall not be
less than one-quarter inch key space. First coat shall be thoroughly
scratched to make key to retain second coat, and shall be set before
second coat is applied.
```
