# General City Law § 7 — Summoning witnesses

[General City Law](../README.md) › [Article 2 — General Provisions](README.md)

```text
  § 7. Summoning witnesses. The chairman or any other member of any such
committee in his name shall have power to subpoena witnesses.
```
