# General City Law § 2-B — Definition

[General City Law](../README.md) › [Article 2 — General Provisions](README.md)

```text
  § 2-b. Definition. The term "infant" or "minor", as used in this
chapter, shall mean a person who has not attained the age of eighteen
years.
```
