# General City Law § 15 — Firemen moving from one city to another

[General City Law](../README.md) › [Article 2 — General Provisions](README.md)

```text
  § 15. Firemen moving from one city to another. The firemen of the
different cities of this state, in case of removal from one city to
another, shall be allowed the time which they have served as such
firemen in the city they left, in the city to which they have removed,
upon producing a certificate of such service, signed by the chief of the
city so left, and being appointed firemen in the city to which they have
removed.
```
