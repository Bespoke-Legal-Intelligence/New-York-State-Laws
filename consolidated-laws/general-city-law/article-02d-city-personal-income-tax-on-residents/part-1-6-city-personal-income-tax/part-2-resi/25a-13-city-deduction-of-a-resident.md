# General City Law § 25-A-13 — City deduction of a resident individual

[General City Law](../../../README.md) › [Article 2-D — City Personal Income Tax On Residents](../../README.md) › [Part 1-6 — City Personal Income Tax On Residents](../README.md) › [Part 2 — Residents](README.md)

```text
  § 13. City deduction of a resident individual.-- The city deduction of
a resident individual shall be his city standard deduction unless he
elects to deduct his city itemized deduction under the conditions set
forth in section fifteen.
```
