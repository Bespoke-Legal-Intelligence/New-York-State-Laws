# General City Law § 25-A-41 — Declaration of estimated tax for taxable years beginning prior to the date this local law becomes effective

[General City Law](../../../README.md) › [Article 2-D — City Personal Income Tax On Residents](../../README.md) › [Part 1-6 — City Personal Income Tax On Residents](../README.md) › [Part 3 — Returns, Declarations and Payment of Tax](README.md)

```text
  § 41. Declaration of estimated tax for taxable years beginning prior
to the date this local law becomes effective.-- Notwithstanding
subdivision (d) of section thirty-five, no declaration of estimated tax
required by subdivision (a) of such section need be filed until sixty
days after the date this local law becomes effective.
```
