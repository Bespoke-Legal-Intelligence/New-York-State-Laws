# General City Law § 25-A-40 — Change of election

[General City Law](../../../README.md) › [Article 2-D — City Personal Income Tax On Residents](../../README.md) › [Part 1-6 — City Personal Income Tax On Residents](../README.md) › [Part 3 — Returns, Declarations and Payment of Tax](README.md)

```text
  § 40. Change of election.-- Any election expressly authorized by this
local law, other than the election authorized by subdivision (g) of
section twelve, may be changed on such terms and conditions as the
administrator may prescribe by regulation.
```
