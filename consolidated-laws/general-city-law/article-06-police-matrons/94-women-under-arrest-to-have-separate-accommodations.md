# General City Law § 94 — Women under arrest to have separate accommodations

[General City Law](../README.md) › [Article 6 — Police Matrons](README.md)

```text
  § 94. Women under arrest to have separate accommodations.  It shall be
the duty of the boards of commissioners of police in every city, or if
there be no board of police, then of the mayor of such city, to provide
sufficient accommodations for women held under arrest to keep them
separate and apart from the cells, corridors and apartments provided for
males under arrest, and to so arrange each station house that no
communication can be had between the men and women therein confined,
except with the consent of the matrons and officers in charge of such
station house.
```
