# General City Law § 97 — Appropriations under article, how made

[General City Law](../README.md) › [Article 6 — Police Matrons](README.md)

```text
  § 97. Appropriations under article, how made.  The proper local
authorities of each city in which a police matron has been appointed
shall appropriate annually such sum as may be needed for the separate
care and confinement in station houses of any women arrested in such
city, and for the appointment, salary and maintenance of police matrons
for the purposes of this article.
```
