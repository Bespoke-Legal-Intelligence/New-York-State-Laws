# General City Law § 81-E — Article not applicable to certain cities

[General City Law](../README.md) › [Article 5-A — Buildings and Use Districts](README.md)

```text
  § 81-e. Article not applicable to certain cities. The provisions of
this article shall not apply to any city having a population in excess
of one million except that any such city may by local law provide that
this article or any section thereof may apply to such city.
```
