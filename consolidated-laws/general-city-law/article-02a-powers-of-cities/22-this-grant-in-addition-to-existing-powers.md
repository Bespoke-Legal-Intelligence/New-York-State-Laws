# General City Law § 22 — This grant in addition to existing powers

[General City Law](../README.md) › [Article 2-A — Powers of Cities](README.md)

```text
  § 22. This grant in addition to existing powers.  The powers granted
by this article shall be in addition to and not in substitution for, all
the powers, rights, privileges and functions existing in any city
pursuant to any other provision of law.
```
