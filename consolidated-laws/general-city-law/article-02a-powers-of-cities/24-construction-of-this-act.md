# General City Law § 24 — Construction of this act

[General City Law](../README.md) › [Article 2-A — Powers of Cities](README.md)

```text
  § 24. Construction of this act. This article shall be construed, not
as an act in derogation of the powers of the state, but as one intended
to aid the state in the execution of its duties, by providing adequate
power of local government for the cities of the state.
```
