# General City Law § 140 — Establishment of hospitals

[General City Law](../README.md) › [Article 10 — Hospitals For Treatment of Pulmonary Tuberculosis](README.md)

```text
  § 140. Establishment of hospitals. A city of the first class shall
have power whenever its board of health shall deem it necessary for the
promotion of the health of its inhabitants, to establish, equip and
maintain, outside of its corporate limits, and not within the limits of
any other city or any village, a hospital or hospitals for the regular
treatment of the disease known as pulmonary tuberculosis.
```
