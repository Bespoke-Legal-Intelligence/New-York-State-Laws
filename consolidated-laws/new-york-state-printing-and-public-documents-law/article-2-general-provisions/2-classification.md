# New York State Printing and Public Documents Law § 2 — Classification

[New York State Printing and Public Documents Law](../README.md) › [Article 2 — General Provisions](README.md)

```text
  § 2. Classification.  The public printing is hereby classified into
two principal parts.  The first part shall be known as the legislative
printing. The second part shall be known as the department printing,
which shall include all printing for the various offices, institutions
and departments of the state, other than legislative, classified as
department printing by the commissioner of general services, pursuant to
this chapter.
```
