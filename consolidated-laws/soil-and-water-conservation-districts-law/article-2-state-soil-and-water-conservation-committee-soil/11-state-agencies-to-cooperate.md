# Soil and Water Conservation Districts Law § 11 — State agencies to cooperate

[Soil and Water Conservation Districts Law](../README.md) › [Article 2 — State Soil and Water Conservation Committee, Soil and Water Conservation Districts, and Other Functions](README.md)

```text
  § 11. State agencies to cooperate. Agencies of this state which shall
have jurisdiction over, or be charged with the administration of, any
state-owned lands, and of any county, or other governmental subdivision
of the state, which shall have jurisdiction over, or be charged with the
administration of, any county-owned, or other publicly owned lands,
lying within the boundaries of any district organized hereunder may
cooperate with the directors of such districts in the effectuation of
programs and operations undertaken by the directors under the provisions
of this chapter.
```
