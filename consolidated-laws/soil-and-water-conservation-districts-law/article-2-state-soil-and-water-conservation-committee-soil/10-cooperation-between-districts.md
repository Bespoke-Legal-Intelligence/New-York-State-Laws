# Soil and Water Conservation Districts Law § 10 — Cooperation between districts

[Soil and Water Conservation Districts Law](../README.md) › [Article 2 — State Soil and Water Conservation Committee, Soil and Water Conservation Districts, and Other Functions](README.md)

```text
  § 10. Cooperation between districts. The directors of any two or more
districts organized under the provisions of this chapter may cooperate
with one another in the exercise of any or all powers conferred in this
chapter.
```
