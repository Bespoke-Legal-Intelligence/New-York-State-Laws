# Soil and Water Conservation Districts Law § 13 — Separability clause

[Soil and Water Conservation Districts Law](../README.md) › [Article 3 — Separability Clause; Inconsistency With Other Acts; Effective Date](README.md)

```text
  § 13. Separability clause. If any provisions of this chapter or the
application of any provision to any person or circumstance, is held
invalid, the remainder of the chapter, and the application of such
provision to other persons or circumstances, shall not be affected
thereby.
```
