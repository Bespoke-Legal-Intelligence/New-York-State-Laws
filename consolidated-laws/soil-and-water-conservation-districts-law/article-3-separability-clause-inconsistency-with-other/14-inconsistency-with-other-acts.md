# Soil and Water Conservation Districts Law § 14 — Inconsistency with other acts

[Soil and Water Conservation Districts Law](../README.md) › [Article 3 — Separability Clause; Inconsistency With Other Acts; Effective Date](README.md)

```text
  § 14. Inconsistency with other acts. Insofar as any of the provisions
of this chapter are inconsistent with the provisions of any other law,
the provisions of this chapter shall be controlling.
```
