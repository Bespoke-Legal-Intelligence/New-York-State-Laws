# Public Authorities Law § 2857 — Actions by an authority

[Public Authorities Law](../../README.md) › [Article 9 — General Provisions](../README.md) › [Title 3-A — Business Practices of Public Authorities](README.md)

```text
  § 2857. Actions by an authority. No state or local authority shall
fire, discharge, demote, suspend, threaten, harass or discriminate
against an employee because of the employee's role as a whistleblower,
insofar as the actions taken by the employee are legal.
```
