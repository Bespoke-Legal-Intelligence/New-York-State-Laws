# Public Authorities Law § 2981 — Wrongful death; limitation period

[Public Authorities Law](../../README.md) › [Article 9 — General Provisions](../README.md) › [Title 11 — Wrongful Death Actions](README.md)

```text
  § 2981. Wrongful death; limitation period. A wrongful death action
against a public authority or public benefit corporation shall be
commenced within two years of the happening of the death.
```
