# Public Authorities Law § 2980 — Wrongful death; notice of claim

[Public Authorities Law](../../README.md) › [Article 9 — General Provisions](../README.md) › [Title 11 — Wrongful Death Actions](README.md)

```text
  § 2980. Wrongful death; notice of claim. No wrongful death action
against a public authority or public benefit corporation shall be
commenced unless a notice of claim has been served on the authority or
corporation in accordance with the provisions of section fifty-e of the
general municipal law.
```
