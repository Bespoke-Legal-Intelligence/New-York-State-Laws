# Public Authorities Law § 916 — Contracts

[Public Authorities Law](../../README.md) › [Article 4 — Market Authorities](../README.md) › [Title 5 — Long Island Market Authority](README.md)

```text
  § 916. Contracts. All contracts for the construction, reconstruction,
rehabilitation and improvement of projects authorized pursuant to this
title shall be subject to article eight of the labor law and shall also
be subject to the provisions of law applicable to county contracts,
except as otherwise provided herein.
```
