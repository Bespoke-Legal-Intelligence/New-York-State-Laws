# Public Authorities Law § 918 — Equal employment opportunity

[Public Authorities Law](../../README.md) › [Article 4 — Market Authorities](../README.md) › [Title 5 — Long Island Market Authority](README.md)

```text
  § 918. Equal employment opportunity. The authority shall ensure that
all employees or applicants for employment are afforded equal employment
opportunity without discrimination.
```
