# Public Authorities Law § 888 — Interest in claims and contracts prohibited

[Public Authorities Law](../../README.md) › [Article 4 — Market Authorities](../README.md) › [Title 4 — Genesee Valley Regional Market Authority](README.md)

```text
  § 888. Interest in claims and contracts prohibited.  No member,
officer, agent or employee of the authority shall be interested,
directly or indirectly, in any claim against or contract with the
authority except for lawful compensation and reimbursement for actual
and necessary expenses incurred in the performance of official duties;
provided that any such member, officer, agent or employee may, as a
grower or dealer, lease from the authority a stall, store or other
facility upon a market and may engage in such transactions with the
authority as are reasonably and ordinarily incidental to such leasing.
```
