# Public Authorities Law § 884 — State and counties not liable on bonds

[Public Authorities Law](../../README.md) › [Article 4 — Market Authorities](../README.md) › [Title 4 — Genesee Valley Regional Market Authority](README.md)

```text
  § 884. State and counties not liable on bonds. The bonds and other
obligations of the authority shall not be a debt of the state or of any
county or counties, and the board shall have no power to make them
payable out of any moneys except those of the authority.
```
