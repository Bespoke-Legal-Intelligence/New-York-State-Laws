# Public Authorities Law § 837 — Members not to be interested in transactions

[Public Authorities Law](../../README.md) › [Article 4 — Market Authorities](../README.md) › [Title 2 — Central New York Regional Market Authority](README.md)

```text
  § 837. Members not to be interested in transactions. No member of the
board shall be interested, directly or indirectly, in any transaction
with the authority, except the leasing as a grower of a stall or stalls
upon the market and such transactions as are ordinarily incidental
thereto.
```
