# Public Authorities Law § 834-A — Policing

[Public Authorities Law](../../README.md) › [Article 4 — Market Authorities](../README.md) › [Title 2 — Central New York Regional Market Authority](README.md)

```text
  § 834-a. Policing.  Any county, city, town or village within the
territorial limits of the district may, by ordinance or resolution of
its governing body, make covenants with the authority which shall
provide for the policing of designated property of the authority by the
police of such county, city, town or village with the same powers as
such police have in such county, city, town or village.
```
