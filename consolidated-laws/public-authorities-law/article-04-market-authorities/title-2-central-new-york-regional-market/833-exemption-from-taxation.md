# Public Authorities Law § 833 — Exemption from taxation

[Public Authorities Law](../../README.md) › [Article 4 — Market Authorities](../README.md) › [Title 2 — Central New York Regional Market Authority](README.md)

```text
  § 833. Exemption from taxation. The real and personal property of the
authority shall be exempt from all taxation, and so shall all of its
activities and operations, except that the owners of its bonds and other
obligations shall be subject to transfer and estate taxes.
```
