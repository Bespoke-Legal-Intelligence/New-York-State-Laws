# Public Authorities Law § 840 — Inconsistent provisions in other acts superseded

[Public Authorities Law](../../README.md) › [Article 4 — Market Authorities](../README.md) › [Title 2 — Central New York Regional Market Authority](README.md)

```text
  § 840. Inconsistent provisions in other acts superseded. In so far as
the provisions of this title are inconsistent with the provisions of any
other act, general, special or local, the provisions of this title shall
be controlling.
```
