# Public Authorities Law § 832 — Bonds legal investments for fiduciaries

[Public Authorities Law](../../README.md) › [Article 4 — Market Authorities](../README.md) › [Title 2 — Central New York Regional Market Authority](README.md)

```text
  § 832. Bonds legal investments for fiduciaries. The bonds of the
authority are hereby made securities in which all public officers and
bodies of this state and all municipalities and municipal subdivisions
may properly and legally invest funds in their control.
```
