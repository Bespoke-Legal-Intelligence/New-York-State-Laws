# Public Authorities Law § 831 — State and county not liable on bonds

[Public Authorities Law](../../README.md) › [Article 4 — Market Authorities](../README.md) › [Title 2 — Central New York Regional Market Authority](README.md)

```text
  § 831. State and county not liable on bonds. The bonds and other
obligations of the authority shall not be a debt of the state or of any
county or counties included within the district, and the board shall
have no power to make them payable out of any funds except those of the
authority.
```
