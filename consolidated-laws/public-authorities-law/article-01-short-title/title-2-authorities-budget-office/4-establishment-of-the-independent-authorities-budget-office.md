# Public Authorities Law § 4 — Establishment of the independent authorities budget office

[Public Authorities Law](../../README.md) › [Article 1 — Short Title](../README.md) › [Title 2 — Authorities Budget Office](README.md)

```text
  § 4. Establishment of the independent authorities budget office. There
is hereby established the independent authorities budget office as an
independent entity within the department of state, which shall have and
exercise the powers and duties provided by this title.
```
