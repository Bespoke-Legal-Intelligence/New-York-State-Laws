# Public Authorities Law § 3106 — Contributions to the corporation

[Public Authorities Law](../../README.md) › [Article 10-A — New York State Science and Technology](../README.md) › [Title 1 — New York State Science and Technology Foundation](README.md)

```text
  § 3106. Contributions to the corporation. Notwithstanding the
provisions of any general or special law or the provisions of any
certificate of incorporation, charter or other articles of organization,
any corporation, association or person is hereby authorized to make
contributions to the corporation.
```
