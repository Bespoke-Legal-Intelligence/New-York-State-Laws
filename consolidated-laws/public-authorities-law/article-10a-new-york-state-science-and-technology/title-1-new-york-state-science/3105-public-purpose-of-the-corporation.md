# Public Authorities Law § 3105 — Public purpose of the corporation

[Public Authorities Law](../../README.md) › [Article 10-A — New York State Science and Technology](../README.md) › [Title 1 — New York State Science and Technology Foundation](README.md)

```text
  § 3105. Public purpose of the corporation. The corporation is created
and shall be operated exclusively for public purposes and not for
pecuniary profit. No part of the net earnings of the corporation shall
inure to the benefit of any private individual.
```
