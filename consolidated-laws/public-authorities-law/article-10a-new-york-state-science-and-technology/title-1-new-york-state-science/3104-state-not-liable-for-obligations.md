# Public Authorities Law § 3104 — State not liable for obligations

[Public Authorities Law](../../README.md) › [Article 10-A — New York State Science and Technology](../README.md) › [Title 1 — New York State Science and Technology Foundation](README.md)

```text
  § 3104. State not liable for obligations. No obligation of the
corporation shall be a debt of the state, and the corporation shall have
no power to make its obligations payable out of any property or moneys
except those of the corporation.
```
