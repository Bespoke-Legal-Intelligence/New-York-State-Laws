# Public Authorities Law § 1550*2 — Short title

[Public Authorities Law](../../README.md) › [Article 7 — Parking Authorities](../README.md) › [Title 7* — Endicott Parking Authority](README.md)

*An asterisk in a number (for example Article 24\* or § 20-C\*2) is the Senate's mark for a number the Legislature has used more than once: the marked article or section is a different one from the unmarked one with the same number. \*2 means the second one enacted with that number.*

```text
  * § 1550. Short title. This title may be cited as the Endicott parking
authority act.
  * NB Dissolved December 31, 1977
  * NB There are 2 § 1550's
```
