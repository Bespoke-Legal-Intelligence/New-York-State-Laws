# Public Authorities Law § 1568 — Inconsistent provisions in other acts superseded

[Public Authorities Law](../../README.md) › [Article 7 — Parking Authorities](../README.md) › [Title 7* — Endicott Parking Authority](README.md)

```text
  * § 1568. Inconsistent provisions in other acts superseded. In so far
as the provisions of this title are inconsistent with the provisions of
any other act, general or special, or of any local law of the village,
the provisions of this title shall be controlling.
  * NB Dissolved December 31, 1977
```
