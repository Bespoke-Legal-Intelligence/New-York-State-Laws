# Public Authorities Law § 1596-S — Inconsistent provisions in other acts superseded

[Public Authorities Law](../../README.md) › [Article 7 — Parking Authorities](../README.md) › [Title 11 — City of Yonkers Parking Authority](README.md)

```text
  § 1596-s. Inconsistent provisions in other acts superseded. In so far
as the provisions of this title are inconsistent with the provisions of
any other act, general or special, or of any local law of the city, the
provisions of this title shall be controlling.
```
