# Public Authorities Law § 1570-S — Equal employment opportunity

[Public Authorities Law](../../README.md) › [Article 7 — Parking Authorities](../README.md) › [Title 8-A — Harrison Parking Authority](README.md)

```text
  § 1570-s. Equal employment opportunity. The authority shall ensure
that all employees or applicants for employment are afforded equal
employment opportunity without discrimination.
```
