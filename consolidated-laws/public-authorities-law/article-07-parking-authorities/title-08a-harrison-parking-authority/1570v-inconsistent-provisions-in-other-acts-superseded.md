# Public Authorities Law § 1570-V — Inconsistent provisions in other acts superseded

[Public Authorities Law](../../README.md) › [Article 7 — Parking Authorities](../README.md) › [Title 8-A — Harrison Parking Authority](README.md)

```text
  § 1570-v. Inconsistent provisions in other acts superseded. Insofar as
the provisions of this title are inconsistent with the provisions of any
other provision of law, or of any local law of the village, the
provisions of this title shall be controlling.
```
