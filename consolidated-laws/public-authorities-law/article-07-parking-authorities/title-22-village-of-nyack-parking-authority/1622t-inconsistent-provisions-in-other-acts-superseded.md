# Public Authorities Law § 1622-T — Inconsistent provisions in other acts superseded

[Public Authorities Law](../../README.md) › [Article 7 — Parking Authorities](../README.md) › [Title 22 — Village of Nyack Parking Authority](README.md)

```text
  § 1622-t. Inconsistent provisions in other acts superseded. Insofar as
the provisions of this title are inconsistent with the provisions of any
other act, general or special, or of any local law of the village, the
provisions of this title shall be controlling.
```
