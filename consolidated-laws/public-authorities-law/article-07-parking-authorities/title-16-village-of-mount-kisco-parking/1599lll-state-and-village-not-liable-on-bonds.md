# Public Authorities Law § 1599-LLL — State and village not liable on bonds

[Public Authorities Law](../../README.md) › [Article 7 — Parking Authorities](../README.md) › [Title 16 — Village of Mount Kisco Parking Authority](README.md)

```text
  * § 1599-lll. State and village not liable on bonds. The bonds and
other obligations of the authority shall not be a debt of the state of
New York or of the village, and neither the state nor the village shall
be liable thereon, nor shall they be payable out of any funds other than
those of the authority.
  * NB Authority ceased to exist 06/01/1976
  * NB There are 2 § 1599-lll's
```
