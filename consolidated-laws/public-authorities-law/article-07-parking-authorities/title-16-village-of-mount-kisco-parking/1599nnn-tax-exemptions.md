# Public Authorities Law § 1599-NNN — Tax exemptions

[Public Authorities Law](../../README.md) › [Article 7 — Parking Authorities](../README.md) › [Title 16 — Village of Mount Kisco Parking Authority](README.md)

```text
  * § 1599-nnn. Tax exemptions. Any bonds or notes issued pursuant to
this title, together with the income therefrom, as well as the property
of the authority, shall be exempt from taxation, except for transfer and
estate taxes.
  * NB Authority ceased to exist 06/01/1976
  * NB There are 2 § 1599-nnn's
```
