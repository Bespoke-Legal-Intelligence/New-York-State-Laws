# Public Authorities Law § 1599-TTT — Inconsistent provisions in other acts superseded

[Public Authorities Law](../../README.md) › [Article 7 — Parking Authorities](../README.md) › [Title 16 — Village of Mount Kisco Parking Authority](README.md)

```text
  * § 1599-ttt. Inconsistent provisions in other acts superseded.
Insofar as the provisions of this title are inconsistent with the
provisions of any other act, general or special, or of any local law of
the village, the provisions of this title shall be controlling.
  * NB Authority ceased to exist 06/01/1976
  * NB There are 2 § 1599-ttt's
```
