# Public Authorities Law § 1599-L*4 — State and city not liable on bonds

[Public Authorities Law](../../README.md) › [Article 7 — Parking Authorities](../README.md) › [Title 14*** — City of Schenectady Parking Authority](README.md)

*An asterisk in a number (for example Article 24\* or § 20-C\*2) is the Senate's mark for a number the Legislature has used more than once: the marked article or section is a different one from the unmarked one with the same number. \*2 means the second one enacted with that number.*

```text
  * § 1599-l. State and city not liable on bonds. The bonds and other
obligations of the authority shall not be a debt of the state or of the
city, and neither the state nor the city shall be liable thereon, nor
shall they be payable out of any funds other than those of the
authority.
  * NB Authority ceased to exist 08/05/2002
  * NB There are 4 § 1599-l's
```
