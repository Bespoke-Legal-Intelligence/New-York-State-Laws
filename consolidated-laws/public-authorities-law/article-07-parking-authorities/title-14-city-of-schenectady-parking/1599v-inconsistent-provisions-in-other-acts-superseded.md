# Public Authorities Law § 1599-V — Inconsistent provisions in other acts superseded

[Public Authorities Law](../../README.md) › [Article 7 — Parking Authorities](../README.md) › [Title 14*** — City of Schenectady Parking Authority](README.md)

```text
  * § 1599-v. Inconsistent provisions in other acts superseded. Insofar
as the provisions of this title are inconsistent with the provisions of
any other act, general or special, or of any local law of the city, the
provisions of this title shall be controlling.
  * NB Authority ceased to exist 08/05/2002
```
