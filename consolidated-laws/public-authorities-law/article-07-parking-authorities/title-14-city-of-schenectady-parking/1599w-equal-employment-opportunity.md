# Public Authorities Law § 1599-W — Equal employment opportunity

[Public Authorities Law](../../README.md) › [Article 7 — Parking Authorities](../README.md) › [Title 14*** — City of Schenectady Parking Authority](README.md)

```text
  * § 1599-w. Equal employment opportunity. The authority shall ensure
that, where possible, all employees or applicants for employment are
afforded equal employment opportunity without discrimination.
  * NB Authority ceased to exist 08/05/2002
```
