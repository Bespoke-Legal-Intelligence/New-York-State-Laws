# Public Authorities Law § 1470-R — Title not affected if in part unconstitutional or ineffective

[Public Authorities Law](../../README.md) › [Article 7 — Parking Authorities](../README.md) › [Title 3-A — City of Rome Parking Authority](README.md)

```text
  *§ 1470-r. Title not affected if in part unconstitutional or
ineffective.  If any section, clause or provision of this title shall be
unconstitutional or be ineffective in whole or in part, to the extent
that it is not unconstitutional or ineffective, it shall be valid and
effective and no other section, clause or provision shall on account
thereof be deemed invalid or ineffective.
  * NB City of Rome Parking Authority ceased to exist 12/31/2000 per §
1470-b, and thereafter until all liabilities have been met/discharged,
and all bonds paid/discharged
```
