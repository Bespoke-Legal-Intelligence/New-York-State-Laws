# Public Authorities Law § 1600-Q — Termination of the authority

[Public Authorities Law](../../README.md) › [Article 7 — Parking Authorities](../README.md) › [Title 18 — Village of Suffern Parking Authority](README.md)

```text
  * § 1600-q. Termination of the authority. Whenever all of the bonds
issued by the authority shall have been redeemed or canceled, the
authority shall cease to exist and all rights, titles and interest and
all obligations and liabilities thereof vested in or possessed by the
authority shall thereupon vest in and be possessed by the Village of
Suffern.
  * NB Ceases to exist December 31, 2014
  * NB There are 2 § 1600-q's
```
