# Public Authorities Law § 1600-K — State and Village not liable on bonds

[Public Authorities Law](../../README.md) › [Article 7 — Parking Authorities](../README.md) › [Title 18 — Village of Suffern Parking Authority](README.md)

```text
  * § 1600-k. State and Village not liable on bonds. The bonds and other
obligations of the authority shall not be a debt of the state of New
York or of the Village, and neither the state nor the Village shall be
liable thereon, nor shall they be payable out of any funds other than
those of the authority.
  * NB Ceases to exist December 31, 2014
  * NB There are 2 § 1600-k's
```
