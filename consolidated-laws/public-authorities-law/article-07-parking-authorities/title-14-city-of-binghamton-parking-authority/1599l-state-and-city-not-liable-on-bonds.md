# Public Authorities Law § 1599-L — State and city not liable on bonds

[Public Authorities Law](../../README.md) › [Article 7 — Parking Authorities](../README.md) › [Title 14 — City of Binghamton Parking Authority](README.md)

```text
  * § 1599-l. State and city not liable on bonds. The bonds and other
obligations of the authority shall not be a debt of the state of New
York or of the city, and neither the state nor the city shall be liable
thereon, nor shall they be payable out of any funds other than those of
the authority.
  * NB Authority ceased to exist 12/31/99
  * NB There are 4 § 1599-l's
```
