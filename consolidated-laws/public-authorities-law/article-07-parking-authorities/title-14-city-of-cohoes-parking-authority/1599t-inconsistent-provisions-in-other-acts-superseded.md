# Public Authorities Law § 1599-T — Inconsistent provisions in other acts superseded

[Public Authorities Law](../../README.md) › [Article 7 — Parking Authorities](../README.md) › [Title 14* — City of Cohoes Parking Authority](README.md)

```text
  * § 1599-t. Inconsistent provisions in other acts superseded. In so
far as the provisions of this title are inconsistent with the provisions
of any other act, general or special, or of any local law of the city,
the provisions of this title shall be controlling.
  * NB Authority terminated 07/01/1974
```
