# Public Authorities Law § 1599-S*2 — Title not affected if in part unconstitutional or ineffective

[Public Authorities Law](../../README.md) › [Article 7 — Parking Authorities](../README.md) › [Title 14* — City of Cohoes Parking Authority](README.md)

*An asterisk in a number (for example Article 24\* or § 20-C\*2) is the Senate's mark for a number the Legislature has used more than once: the marked article or section is a different one from the unmarked one with the same number. \*2 means the second one enacted with that number.*

```text
  * § 1599-s. Title not affected if in part unconstitutional or
ineffective. If any section, clause or provision of this title shall be
unconstitutional or be ineffective in whole or in part, to the extent
that it is not unconstitutional or ineffective, it shall be valid and
effective and no other section, clause or provision shall on account
thereof be deemed invalid or ineffective.
  * NB Authority terminated 07/01/1974
  * NB There are 4 § 1599-s's
```
