# Public Authorities Law § 1600-L — State and village not liable on bonds

[Public Authorities Law](../../README.md) › [Article 7 — Parking Authorities](../README.md) › [Title 18 — Tarrytown Parking Authority](README.md)

```text
  * § 1600-l. State and village not liable on bonds. The bonds, notes
and other obligations of the authority shall not be a debt of the state
of New York or of the village, and neither the state nor the village
shall be liable thereon, nor shall they be payable out of any funds
other than those of the authority.
  * NB Ceased to exist December 31, 1999
```
