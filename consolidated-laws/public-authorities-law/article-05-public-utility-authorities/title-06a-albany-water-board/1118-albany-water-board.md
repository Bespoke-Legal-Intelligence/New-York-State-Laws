# Public Authorities Law § 1118 — Albany water board

[Public Authorities Law](../../README.md) › [Article 5 — Public Utility Authorities](../README.md) › [Title 6-A — Albany Water Board](README.md)

```text
  § 1118. Albany water board. For the benefit of the city of Albany and
the inhabitants thereof, a water board to be known as the Albany water
board is hereby established for the accomplishment of any or all of the
purposes specified in title six of article five of this chapter. It
shall have the powers and duties now or hereafter conferred by title six
of article five of this chapter upon a water board. It shall be
organized in a manner prescribed by and be subject to the provisions of
title six of article five of this chapter. The water board, its members,
officers and employees and its operations and activities shall in all
respects be governed by the provisions of title six of article five of
this chapter.
```
