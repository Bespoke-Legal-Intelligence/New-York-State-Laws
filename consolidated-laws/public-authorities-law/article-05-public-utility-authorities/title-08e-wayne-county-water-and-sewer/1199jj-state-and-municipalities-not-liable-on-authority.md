# Public Authorities Law § 1199-JJ — State and municipalities not liable on authority bonds

[Public Authorities Law](../../README.md) › [Article 5 — Public Utility Authorities](../README.md) › [Title 8-E — Wayne County Water and Sewer Authority](README.md)

```text
  * § 1199-jj. State and municipalities not liable on authority bonds.
Neither the state nor any municipality shall be liable on the bonds of
the authority and such bonds shall not be a debt of the state or of any
municipality.
  * NB There are 2 § 1199-jj's
```
