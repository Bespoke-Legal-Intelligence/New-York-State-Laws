# Public Authorities Law § 1199-CC — Wayne county water and sewer authority district

[Public Authorities Law](../../README.md) › [Article 5 — Public Utility Authorities](../README.md) › [Title 8-E — Wayne County Water and Sewer Authority](README.md)

```text
  * § 1199-cc. Wayne county water and sewer authority district. There is
hereby defined and established a district to be known as the "Wayne
county water and sewer authority district" which shall embrace all of
the territory located within the county of Wayne.
  * NB There are 2 § 1199-cc's
```
