# Public Authorities Law § 1226-N — State, water board or municipality, other than the authority, not liable on authority bonds

[Public Authorities Law](../../README.md) › [Article 5 — Public Utility Authorities](../README.md) › [Title 10 — Upper Mohawk Valley Regional Water Finance Authority](README.md)

```text
  § 1226-n. State, water board or municipality, other than the
authority, not liable on authority bonds. Neither the state, the water
board nor any municipality, other than the authority, shall be liable on
the bonds of the authority and such bonds shall not be a debt of either
the state, the water board or any municipality, and each such bond shall
contain, on the face thereof, a statement to such effect.
```
