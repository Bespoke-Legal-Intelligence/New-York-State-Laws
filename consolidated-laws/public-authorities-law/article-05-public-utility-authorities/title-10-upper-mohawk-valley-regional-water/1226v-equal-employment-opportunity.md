# Public Authorities Law § 1226-V — Equal employment opportunity

[Public Authorities Law](../../README.md) › [Article 5 — Public Utility Authorities](../README.md) › [Title 10 — Upper Mohawk Valley Regional Water Finance Authority](README.md)

```text
  § 1226-v. Equal employment opportunity. The water board and the
authority shall ensure that all employees or applicants for employment
are afforded equal employment opportunity without discrimination.
```
