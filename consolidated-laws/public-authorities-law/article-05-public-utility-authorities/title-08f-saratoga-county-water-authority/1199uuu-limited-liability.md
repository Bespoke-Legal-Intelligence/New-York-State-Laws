# Public Authorities Law § 1199-UUU — Limited liability

[Public Authorities Law](../../README.md) › [Article 5 — Public Utility Authorities](../README.md) › [Title 8-F — Saratoga County Water Authority](README.md)

```text
  § 1199-uuu. Limited liability. Neither the members of the authority,
nor any municipality, officer or employee acting in its behalf, while
acting within the scope of their authority, shall be subject to any
personal liability resulting from the construction, maintenance or
operation of any of the properties of the authority or from carrying out
any of the powers expressly given in this title; provided, however, that
this section shall not be held to apply to any independent contractor.
```
