# Public Authorities Law § 1199-PPP — Interest in contracts prohibited

[Public Authorities Law](../../README.md) › [Article 5 — Public Utility Authorities](../README.md) › [Title 8-F — Saratoga County Water Authority](README.md)

```text
  § 1199-ppp. Interest in contracts prohibited. It shall be a
misdemeanor for any member or any officer, agent, servant or employee of
the authority to be in any way or manner interested, directly or
indirectly, in the furnishing of work, materials, supplies or labor, or
in any contract therefor which the authority is empowered by this title
to make.
```
