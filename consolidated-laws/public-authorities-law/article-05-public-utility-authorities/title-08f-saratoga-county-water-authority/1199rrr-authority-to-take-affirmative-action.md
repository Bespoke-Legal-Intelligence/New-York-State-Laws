# Public Authorities Law § 1199-RRR — Authority to take affirmative action

[Public Authorities Law](../../README.md) › [Article 5 — Public Utility Authorities](../README.md) › [Title 8-F — Saratoga County Water Authority](README.md)

```text
  § 1199-rrr. Authority to take affirmative action. The authority shall
ensure that all employees or applicants for employment are afforded
equal employment opportunity without discrimination.
```
