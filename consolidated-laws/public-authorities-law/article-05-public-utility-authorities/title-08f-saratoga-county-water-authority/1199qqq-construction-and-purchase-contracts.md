# Public Authorities Law § 1199-QQQ — Construction and purchase contracts

[Public Authorities Law](../../README.md) › [Article 5 — Public Utility Authorities](../README.md) › [Title 8-F — Saratoga County Water Authority](README.md)

```text
  § 1199-qqq. Construction and purchase contracts. The authority shall
let contracts for construction or purchase of supplies, materials, or
equipment pursuant to sections one hundred one and one hundred three of
the general municipal law. Nothing in this section shall be construed to
limit the power of the authority to do any construction directly by the
officers, agents and employees of the authority.
```
