# Public Authorities Law § 1078-A — Sale of water

[Public Authorities Law](../../README.md) › [Article 5 — Public Utility Authorities](../README.md) › [Title 4 — Suffolk County Water Authority](README.md)

```text
  § 1078-a. Sale of water. The authority shall not refuse to sell water
service to residential tenants for the sole reason that the premises of
such tenants receiving water service are rented.
```
