# Public Authorities Law § 1147-W — Equal employment opportunity

[Public Authorities Law](../../README.md) › [Article 5 — Public Utility Authorities](../README.md) › [Title 6-D — Alfred, Almond, Hornellsville Sewer Authority](README.md)

```text
  § 1147-w. Equal employment opportunity. The authority shall ensure
that all employees or applicants for employment are afforded equal
employment opportunity without discrimination.
```
