# Public Authorities Law § 1348 — Effect of inconsistent provisions

[Public Authorities Law](../../README.md) › [Article 5 — Public Utility Authorities](../README.md) › [Title 11-D — Central New York Regional Transportation Authority](README.md)

```text
  § 1348. Effect of inconsistent provisions. Insofar as the provisions
of this title are inconsistent with the provisions of any other law,
general, special or local, the provisions of this title shall be
controlling.
```
