# Public Authorities Law § 1015 — Title not affected if in part unconstitutional or ineffective

[Public Authorities Law](../../README.md) › [Article 5 — Public Utility Authorities](../README.md) › [Title 1 — Power Authority of the State of New York](README.md)

```text
  § 1015. Title not affected if in part unconstitutional or ineffective.
If any term or provision of this title shall be declared
unconstitutional or ineffective in whole or in part by a court of
competent jurisdiction, then to the extent that it is not
unconstitutional or ineffective such term or provision shall be enforced
and effectuated, nor shall such determination be deemed to invalidate
the remaining terms or provisions hereof.
```
