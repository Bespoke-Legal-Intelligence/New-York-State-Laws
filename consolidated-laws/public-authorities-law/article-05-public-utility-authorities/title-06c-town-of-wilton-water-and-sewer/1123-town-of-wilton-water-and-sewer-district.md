# Public Authorities Law § 1123 — Town of Wilton water and sewer district

[Public Authorities Law](../../README.md) › [Article 5 — Public Utility Authorities](../README.md) › [Title 6-C — Town of Wilton Water and Sewer Authority](README.md)

```text
  * § 1123. Town of Wilton water and sewer district. There is hereby
defined and established an area to be known as the "town of Wilton water
and sewer district" which shall embrace all the territory located within
the town.
```
