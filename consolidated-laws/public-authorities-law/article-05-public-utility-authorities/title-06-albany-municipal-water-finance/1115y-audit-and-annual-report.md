# Public Authorities Law § 1115-Y — Audit and annual report

[Public Authorities Law](../../README.md) › [Article 5 — Public Utility Authorities](../README.md) › [Title 6 — Albany Municipal Water Finance Authority](README.md)

```text
  § 1115-y. Audit and annual report. The accounts of the authority and
the water board shall be subject to the supervision of the city
comptroller. The authority shall annually submit to the governor and
comptroller and to the state legislature a detailed report pursuant to
the provisions of section two thousand five hundred of title one of
article nine of this chapter, and a copy of such report shall be filed
with the mayor. The authority shall comply with the provisions of
sections two thousand five hundred one, two thousand five hundred two,
and two thousand five hundred three of title one of article nine of this
chapter.
```
