# Public Authorities Law § 1299-XX — Effect of inconsistent provisions

[Public Authorities Law](../../README.md) › [Article 5 — Public Utility Authorities](../README.md) › [Title 11-B — Rochester-genesee Regional Transportation Authority](README.md)

```text
  § 1299-xx. Effect of inconsistent provisions. Insofar as the
provisions of this title are inconsistent with the provisions of any
other law, general, special or local, the provisions of this title shall
be controlling.
```
