# Public Authorities Law § 1299-B — Niagara Frontier transportation district

[Public Authorities Law](../../README.md) › [Article 5 — Public Utility Authorities](../README.md) › [Title 11-A — Niagara Frontier Transportation Authority](README.md)

```text
  § 1299-b. Niagara Frontier transportation district. There is hereby
created and established a transportation district to be known as the
Niagara Frontier transportation district which shall embrace the
counties of Erie and Niagara.
```
