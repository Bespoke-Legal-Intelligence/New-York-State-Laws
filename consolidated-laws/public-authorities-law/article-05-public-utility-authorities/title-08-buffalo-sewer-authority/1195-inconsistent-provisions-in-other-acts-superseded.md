# Public Authorities Law § 1195 — Inconsistent provisions in other acts superseded

[Public Authorities Law](../../README.md) › [Article 5 — Public Utility Authorities](../README.md) › [Title 8 — Buffalo Sewer Authority](README.md)

```text
  § 1195. Inconsistent provisions in other acts superseded.  Insofar as
the provisions of this title are inconsistent with the provisions of any
other act, general or special, or any local law, the provisions of this
title shall be controlling.
```
