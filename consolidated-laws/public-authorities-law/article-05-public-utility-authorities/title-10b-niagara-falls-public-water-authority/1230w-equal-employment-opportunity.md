# Public Authorities Law § 1230-W — Equal employment opportunity

[Public Authorities Law](../../README.md) › [Article 5 — Public Utility Authorities](../README.md) › [Title 10-B — Niagara Falls Public Water Authority](README.md)

```text
  § 1230-w. Equal employment opportunity. The water board and the
authority shall ensure that all employees or applicants for employment
are afforded equal employment opportunity without discrimination.
```
