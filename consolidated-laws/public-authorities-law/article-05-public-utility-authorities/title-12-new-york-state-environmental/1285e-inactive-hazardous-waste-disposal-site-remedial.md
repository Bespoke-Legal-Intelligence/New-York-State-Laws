# Public Authorities Law § 1285-E — Inactive hazardous waste disposal site remedial programs

[Public Authorities Law](../../README.md) › [Article 5 — Public Utility Authorities](../README.md) › [Title 12 — New York State Environmental Facilities Corporation](README.md)

```text
  § 1285-e. Inactive hazardous waste disposal site remedial programs. In
addition to its existing powers, the corporation is hereby empowered to
carry out inactive hazardous waste disposal site remedial programs. The
corporation may subcontract for the work included in carrying out such
programs.
```
