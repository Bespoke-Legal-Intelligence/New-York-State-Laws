# Public Authorities Law § 1298 — Title not affected if in part unconstitutional or ineffective

[Public Authorities Law](../../README.md) › [Article 5 — Public Utility Authorities](../README.md) › [Title 12 — New York State Environmental Facilities Corporation](README.md)

```text
  § 1298. Title not affected if in part unconstitutional or ineffective.
If any provision of any section of this title or the application thereof
to any person or circumstance shall be adjudged invalid by a court of
competent jurisdiction, such order or judgment shall be confined in its
operation to the controversy in which it was rendered, and shall not
affect or invalidate the remainder of any provision of any section of
this title or the application of any part thereof to any other person or
circumstance and to this end the provisions of each section of this
title are hereby declared to be severable.
```
