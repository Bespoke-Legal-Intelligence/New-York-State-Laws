# Public Authorities Law § 1046 — New York city water board

[Public Authorities Law](../../README.md) › [Article 5 — Public Utility Authorities](../README.md) › [Title 2-A* — New York City Water Board](README.md)

```text
  § 1046. New York city water board. For the benefit of the city of New
York and the inhabitants thereof, a water board, to be known as the New
York city water board, is hereby established for the accomplishment of
any or all of the purposes specified in this title. It shall have the
powers and duties now or hereafter conferred by this title upon a water
board. It shall be organized in a manner prescribed by and be subject to
the provisions of this title. The board, its members, officers and
employees and its operations and activities shall in all respects be
governed by the provisions of this title.
```
