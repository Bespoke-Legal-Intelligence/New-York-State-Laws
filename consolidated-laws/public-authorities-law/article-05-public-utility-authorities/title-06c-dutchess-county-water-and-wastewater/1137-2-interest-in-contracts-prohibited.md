# Public Authorities Law § 1137*2 — Interest in contracts prohibited

[Public Authorities Law](../../README.md) › [Article 5 — Public Utility Authorities](../README.md) › [Title 6-C* — Dutchess County Water and Wastewater Authority](README.md)

*An asterisk in a number (for example Article 24\* or § 20-C\*2) is the Senate's mark for a number the Legislature has used more than once: the marked article or section is a different one from the unmarked one with the same number. \*2 means the second one enacted with that number.*

```text
  * § 1137. Interest in contracts prohibited. It shall be a misdemeanor
for any member of the governing body or any officer, agent, servant or
employee of the authority to be in any way or manner interested,
directly or indirectly, in the furnishing or work, materials, supplies
or labor, or in any contract therefor which the authority is empowered
by this title to make.
  * NB There are 2 § 1137's
```
