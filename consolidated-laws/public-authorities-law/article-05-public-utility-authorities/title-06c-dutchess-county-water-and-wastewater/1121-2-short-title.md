# Public Authorities Law § 1121*2 — Short title

[Public Authorities Law](../../README.md) › [Article 5 — Public Utility Authorities](../README.md) › [Title 6-C* — Dutchess County Water and Wastewater Authority](README.md)

*An asterisk in a number (for example Article 24\* or § 20-C\*2) is the Senate's mark for a number the Legislature has used more than once: the marked article or section is a different one from the unmarked one with the same number. \*2 means the second one enacted with that number.*

```text
  * § 1121. Short title. This title shall be known and may be cited as
the "Dutchess county water and wastewater authority act".
  * NB There are 2 § 1121's
```
