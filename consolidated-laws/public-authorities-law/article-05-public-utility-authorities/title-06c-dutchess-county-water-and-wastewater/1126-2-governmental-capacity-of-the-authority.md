# Public Authorities Law § 1126*2 — Governmental capacity of the authority and municipalities

[Public Authorities Law](../../README.md) › [Article 5 — Public Utility Authorities](../README.md) › [Title 6-C* — Dutchess County Water and Wastewater Authority](README.md)

*An asterisk in a number (for example Article 24\* or § 20-C\*2) is the Senate's mark for a number the Legislature has used more than once: the marked article or section is a different one from the unmarked one with the same number. \*2 means the second one enacted with that number.*

```text
  * § 1126. Governmental capacity of the authority and municipalities.
The authority, the county and the other municipalities, in carrying out
their respective powers and duties under this title, shall be deemed to
be acting in a governmental capacity and in the performance of an
essential governmental function.
  * NB There are 2 § 1126's
```
