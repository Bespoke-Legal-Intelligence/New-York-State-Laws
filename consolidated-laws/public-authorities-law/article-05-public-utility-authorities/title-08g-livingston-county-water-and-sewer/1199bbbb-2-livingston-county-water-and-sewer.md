# Public Authorities Law § 1199-BBBB*2 — Livingston county water and sewer authority district

[Public Authorities Law](../../README.md) › [Article 5 — Public Utility Authorities](../README.md) › [Title 8-G* — Livingston County Water and Sewer Authority](README.md)

*An asterisk in a number (for example Article 24\* or § 20-C\*2) is the Senate's mark for a number the Legislature has used more than once: the marked article or section is a different one from the unmarked one with the same number. \*2 means the second one enacted with that number.*

```text
  * § 1199-bbbb. Livingston county water and sewer authority district.
There is hereby defined and established a district to be known as the
"Livingston county water and sewer authority district" which shall
embrace all of the territory located within the county of Livingston.
  * NB There are 2 § 1199-bbbb's
```
