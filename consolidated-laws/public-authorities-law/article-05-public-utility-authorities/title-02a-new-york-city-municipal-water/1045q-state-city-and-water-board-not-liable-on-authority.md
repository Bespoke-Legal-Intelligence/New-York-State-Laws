# Public Authorities Law § 1045-Q — State, city and water board not liable on authority bonds

[Public Authorities Law](../../README.md) › [Article 5 — Public Utility Authorities](../README.md) › [Title 2-A — New York City Municipal Water Finance Authority](README.md)

```text
  § 1045-q. State, city and water board not liable on authority bonds.
Neither the state, nor the city, nor the water board nor any subdivision
thereof shall be liable on the bonds of the authority and such bonds
shall not be a debt of the state, the city or such water board.
```
