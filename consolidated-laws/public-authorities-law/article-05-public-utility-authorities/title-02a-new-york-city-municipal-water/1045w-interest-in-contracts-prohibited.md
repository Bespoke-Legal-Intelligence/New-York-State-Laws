# Public Authorities Law § 1045-W — Interest in contracts prohibited

[Public Authorities Law](../../README.md) › [Article 5 — Public Utility Authorities](../README.md) › [Title 2-A — New York City Municipal Water Finance Authority](README.md)

```text
  § 1045-w. Interest in contracts prohibited. It shall be a misdemeanor
for any member or any officer, agent, servant or employee of the
authority or of the water board to be in any way or manner interested,
directly or indirectly in the furnishing of work, materials, supplies or
labor, or in any contract therefor which the authority or the board, as
the case may be, is empowered by this title to make.
```
