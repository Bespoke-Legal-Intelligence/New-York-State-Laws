# Public Authorities Law § 1045-E — Approval of water projects

[Public Authorities Law](../../README.md) › [Article 5 — Public Utility Authorities](../README.md) › [Title 2-A — New York City Municipal Water Finance Authority](README.md)

```text
  § 1045-e. Approval of water projects. The authority shall not enter
into any contract or agreement with the city or the water board with
respect to the financing of any water project pursuant to section one
thousand forty-five-i of this title, unless the state commissioner of
environmental conservation and the state commissioner of health shall
have completed all statutory reviews and approvals with respect to such
project which, as of the date of such contract or agreement, are
required by law. Nothing herein shall be construed to diminish the full
authority and responsibility of each department of the state for
statutory reviews and approvals.
```
