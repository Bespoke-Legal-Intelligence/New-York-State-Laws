# Public Authorities Law § 1021-S — Severability

[Public Authorities Law](../../README.md) › [Article 5 — Public Utility Authorities](../README.md) › [Title 1-B — North Country Power Authority](README.md)

```text
  § 1021-s. Severability. The provisions of this title are severable,
and if any clause, sentence, paragraph, action or part of this title, or
the application thereof to any person or circumstance, shall be adjudged
by any court of competent jurisdiction to be invalid or unenforceable,
such judgment shall not affect, impair or invalidate the remainder of
this title or the application of such provision to any other person or
circumstance, but shall be confined in its operation to the clause,
sentence, paragraph, section or part thereof or person or circumstance
directly involved in the controversy in which such judgment shall have
been rendered.
```
