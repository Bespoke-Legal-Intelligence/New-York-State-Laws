# Public Authorities Law § 1120-J — State and town not liable on authority bonds

[Public Authorities Law](../../README.md) › [Article 5 — Public Utility Authorities](../README.md) › [Title 6-B — Clifton Park Water Authority](README.md)

```text
  § 1120-j. State and town not liable on authority bonds. Neither the
state nor the town shall be liable on the bonds of the authority and
such bonds shall not be a debt of the state, or the town, and such bonds
shall contain, on the face thereof, a statement to such effect.
```
