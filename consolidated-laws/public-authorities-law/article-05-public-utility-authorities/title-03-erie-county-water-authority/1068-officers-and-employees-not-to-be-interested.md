# Public Authorities Law § 1068 — Officers and employees not to be interested in transactions

[Public Authorities Law](../../README.md) › [Article 5 — Public Utility Authorities](../README.md) › [Title 3 — Erie County Water Authority](README.md)

```text
  § 1068. Officers and employees not to be interested in transactions.
It shall be a misdemeanor for any of the members of the authority, or
any officer, agent, servant or employee thereof, employed or appointed
by them to be in any way or manner interested directly or indirectly in
the furnishing of work, materials, supplies or labor, or in any contract
therefor which the authority is empowered by this act to make.
```
