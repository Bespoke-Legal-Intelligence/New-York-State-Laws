# Public Authorities Law § 1276-E — Reporting

[Public Authorities Law](../../README.md) › [Article 5 — Public Utility Authorities](../README.md) › [Title 11 — Metropolitan Commuter Transportation Authority](README.md)

```text
  § 1276-e. Reporting. The authority shall post on its website on or
before the first of May, the law firms retained by the authority which
in the past year received payment for services in such year.
```
