# Public Authorities Law § 1268-A — Promotion of qualified transportation fringes

[Public Authorities Law](../../README.md) › [Article 5 — Public Utility Authorities](../README.md) › [Title 11 — Metropolitan Commuter Transportation Authority](README.md)

```text
  § 1268-a. Promotion of qualified transportation fringes. The authority
shall promote the broad use of qualified transportation fringes, under
section 132(f) of the internal revenue code, in order to increase the
number of participating companies and employees in such programs. The
authority may also study and report on ways in which programs may be
improved so as to increase public participation.
```
