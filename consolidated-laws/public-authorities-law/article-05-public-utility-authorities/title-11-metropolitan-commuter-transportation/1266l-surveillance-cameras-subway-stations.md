# Public Authorities Law § 1266-L — Surveillance cameras; subway stations

[Public Authorities Law](../../README.md) › [Article 5 — Public Utility Authorities](../README.md) › [Title 11 — Metropolitan Commuter Transportation Authority](README.md)

```text
  * § 1266-l. Surveillance cameras; subway stations. The authority shall
install and reasonably maintain proper operation of surveillance cameras
at all subway stations operated by the authority. Such surveillance
cameras may be placed in all areas, including but not limited to, areas
frequented by passengers and all train platforms.
  * NB There are 2 § 1266's
```
