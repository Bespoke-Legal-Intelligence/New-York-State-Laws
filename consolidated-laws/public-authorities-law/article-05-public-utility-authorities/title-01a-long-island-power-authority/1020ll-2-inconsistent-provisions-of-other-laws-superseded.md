# Public Authorities Law § 1020-LL*2 — Inconsistent provisions of other laws superseded

[Public Authorities Law](../../README.md) › [Article 5 — Public Utility Authorities](../README.md) › [Title 1-A — Long Island Power Authority](README.md)

*An asterisk in a number (for example Article 24\* or § 20-C\*2) is the Senate's mark for a number the Legislature has used more than once: the marked article or section is a different one from the unmarked one with the same number. \*2 means the second one enacted with that number.*

```text
  * § 1020-ll. Inconsistent provisions of other laws superseded. Insofar
as the provisions of this title are inconsistent with the provisions of
any other law or any part thereof, the provisions of this title shall be
controlling.
  * NB There are 2 § 1020-ll's
```
