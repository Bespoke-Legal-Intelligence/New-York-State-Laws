# Public Authorities Law § 1020-II — Energy storage deployment program

[Public Authorities Law](../../README.md) › [Article 5 — Public Utility Authorities](../README.md) › [Title 1-A — Long Island Power Authority](README.md)

```text
  § 1020-ii. Energy storage deployment program. The authority shall
encourage the installation of qualified energy storage systems in its
service territory through implementation of the energy storage
deployment program as set forth and defined in section seventy-four of
the public service law.
```
