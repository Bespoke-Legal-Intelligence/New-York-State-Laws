# Public Authorities Law § 1044 — Inconsistent provisions in other acts superseded

[Public Authorities Law](../../README.md) › [Article 5 — Public Utility Authorities](../README.md) › [Title 2 — Albany Light, Heat and Power Authority](README.md)

```text
  * § 1044. Inconsistent provisions in other acts superseded. In so far
as the provisions of this title are inconsistent with the provisions of
any other act, general or special, the provisions of this title shall be
controlling.
  * NB Terminated July 1, 1963
```
