# Public Authorities Law § 1037 — State and county not liable on bonds

[Public Authorities Law](../../README.md) › [Article 5 — Public Utility Authorities](../README.md) › [Title 2 — Albany Light, Heat and Power Authority](README.md)

```text
  * § 1037. State and county not liable on bonds. The bonds and other
obligations of the authority shall not be a debt of the state or of the
county, and, subject to section one thousand forty-one, the board shall
have no power to make them payable out of any funds except those of the
authority.
  * NB Terminated July 1, 1963
```
