# Public Authorities Law § 1204-F — Subway track safety

[Public Authorities Law](../../README.md) › [Article 5 — Public Utility Authorities](../README.md) › [Title 9 — New York City Transit Authority](README.md)

```text
  § 1204-f. Subway track safety. The authority shall include
instructions on how to react safely, timely, and effectively when a
person falls onto the subway tracks as part of its ongoing safety
education campaigns.
```
