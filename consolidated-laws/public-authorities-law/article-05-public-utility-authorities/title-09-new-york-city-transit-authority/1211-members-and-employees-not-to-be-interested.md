# Public Authorities Law § 1211 — Members and employees not to be interested in contracts

[Public Authorities Law](../../README.md) › [Article 5 — Public Utility Authorities](../README.md) › [Title 9 — New York City Transit Authority](README.md)

```text
  § 1211. Members and employees not to be interested in contracts.  It
shall be a misdemeanor for any member, officer or employee of the
authority to be in any way or manner interested, directly or indirectly,
in any contract made by the authority.
```
