# Public Authorities Law § 1220 — Separability

[Public Authorities Law](../../README.md) › [Article 5 — Public Utility Authorities](../README.md) › [Title 9 — New York City Transit Authority](README.md)

```text
  § 1220. Separability. If any section, clause or provision of this
title shall be unconstitutional or be ineffective in whole or in part,
to the extent that it is not unconstitutional or ineffective, it shall
be valid and effective and no other section, clause or provision shall
on account thereof be deemed invalid or ineffective.
```
