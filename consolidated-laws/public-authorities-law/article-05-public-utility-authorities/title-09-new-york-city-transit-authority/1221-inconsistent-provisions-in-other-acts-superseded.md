# Public Authorities Law § 1221 — Inconsistent provisions in other acts superseded

[Public Authorities Law](../../README.md) › [Article 5 — Public Utility Authorities](../README.md) › [Title 9 — New York City Transit Authority](README.md)

```text
  § 1221. Inconsistent provisions in other acts superseded. In so far as
the provisions of this title are inconsistent with the provisions of any
other act, general or special, or of any local law of the city, the
provisions of this title shall be controlling.
```
