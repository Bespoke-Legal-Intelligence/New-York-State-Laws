# Public Authorities Law § 1199-WWWW — Governmental capacity of the authority and municipalities

[Public Authorities Law](../../README.md) › [Article 5 — Public Utility Authorities](../README.md) › [Title 8-G — Cayuga County Water and Sewer Authority](README.md)

```text
  * § 1199-wwww. Governmental capacity of the authority and
municipalities. The authority, the county and the other municipalities,
in carrying out their respective powers and duties under this title,
shall be deemed to be acting in a governmental capacity and in the
performance of an essential governmental function.
  * NB There are 2 § 1199-wwww's
```
