# Public Authorities Law § 1199-YYYY — Separability clause

[Public Authorities Law](../../README.md) › [Article 5 — Public Utility Authorities](../README.md) › [Title 8-G — Cayuga County Water and Sewer Authority](README.md)

```text
  * § 1199-yyyy. Separability clause. If any section, clause or
provision in this title shall be held by a competent court to be
unconstitutional or ineffective in whole or in part, to the extent that
it is not unconstitutional or ineffective, it shall be valid and
effective, and no other section, clause or provision shall on account
thereof be deemed invalid or ineffective.
```
