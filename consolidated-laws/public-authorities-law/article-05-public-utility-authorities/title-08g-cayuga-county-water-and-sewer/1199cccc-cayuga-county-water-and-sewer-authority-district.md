# Public Authorities Law § 1199-CCCC — Cayuga county water and sewer authority district

[Public Authorities Law](../../README.md) › [Article 5 — Public Utility Authorities](../README.md) › [Title 8-G — Cayuga County Water and Sewer Authority](README.md)

```text
  * § 1199-cccc. Cayuga county water and sewer authority district. There
is hereby defined and established a district to be known as the "Cayuga
county water and sewer authority district" which shall embrace all of
the territory located within the county of Cayuga.
  * NB There are 2 § 1199-cccc's
```
