# Public Authorities Law § 1114-I — State and municipalities not liable on authority bonds

[Public Authorities Law](../../README.md) › [Article 5 — Public Utility Authorities](../README.md) › [Title 5-A — South Nassau Water Authority](README.md)

```text
  * § 1114-i. State and municipalities not liable on authority bonds.
Neither the state nor any municipality shall be liable on the bonds of
the authority and such bonds shall not be a debt of the state or of any
municipality.
  * NB There are 2 § 1114-i's
  * NB Effective February 1, 2022
```
