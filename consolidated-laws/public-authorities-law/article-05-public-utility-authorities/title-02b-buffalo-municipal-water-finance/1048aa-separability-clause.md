# Public Authorities Law § 1048-AA — Separability clause

[Public Authorities Law](../../README.md) › [Article 5 — Public Utility Authorities](../README.md) › [Title 2-B — Buffalo Municipal Water Finance Authority](README.md)

```text
§ 1048-aa. Separability clause. If any section, clause or provision in
this title shall be held by a competent court to be unconstitutional or
ineffective in whole or in part, to the extent that it is not
unconstitutional or ineffective, it shall be valid and effective, and no
other section, clause or provision shall on account thereof be deemed
invalid or ineffective.
```
