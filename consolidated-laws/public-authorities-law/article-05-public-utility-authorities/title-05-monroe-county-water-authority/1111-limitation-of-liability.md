# Public Authorities Law § 1111 — Limitation of liability

[Public Authorities Law](../../README.md) › [Article 5 — Public Utility Authorities](../README.md) › [Title 5 — Monroe County Water Authority](README.md)

```text
  § 1111. Limitation of liability. Neither the members of the authority,
nor any person or persons acting in its behalf, while acting within the
scope of their authority, shall be subject to any personal liability
resulting from the erection, construction, reconstruction, maintenance
or operation of the properties or any of the improvements or from
carrying out any of the powers expressly given in this title.
```
