# Public Authorities Law § 1149-P — Construction and purchase contracts

[Public Authorities Law](../../README.md) › [Article 5 — Public Utility Authorities](../README.md) › [Title 6-E — Seneca County Water and Sewer Authority](README.md)

```text
  § 1149-p. Construction and purchase contracts. The authority shall let
contracts for construction or purchase of supplies, materials, or
equipment pursuant to section one hundred three of the general municipal
law. Nothing in this section shall be construed to limit the power of
the authority to do any construction directly by the officers, agents
and employees of the authority.
```
