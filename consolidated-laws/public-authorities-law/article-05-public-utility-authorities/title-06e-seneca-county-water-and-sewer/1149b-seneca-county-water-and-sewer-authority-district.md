# Public Authorities Law § 1149-B — Seneca county water and sewer authority district

[Public Authorities Law](../../README.md) › [Article 5 — Public Utility Authorities](../README.md) › [Title 6-E — Seneca County Water and Sewer Authority](README.md)

```text
  § 1149-b. Seneca county water and sewer authority district. There is
hereby defined and established a district to be known as the "Seneca
county water and sewer authority district" which shall embrace all of
the territory located within the county of Seneca.
```
