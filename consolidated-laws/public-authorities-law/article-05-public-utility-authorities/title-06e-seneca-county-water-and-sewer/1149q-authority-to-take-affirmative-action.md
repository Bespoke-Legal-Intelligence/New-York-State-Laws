# Public Authorities Law § 1149-Q — Authority to take affirmative action

[Public Authorities Law](../../README.md) › [Article 5 — Public Utility Authorities](../README.md) › [Title 6-E — Seneca County Water and Sewer Authority](README.md)

```text
  § 1149-q. Authority to take affirmative action. The authority shall
ensure that, where possible, all employees or applicants for employment
are afforded equal employment opportunity without discrimination.
```
