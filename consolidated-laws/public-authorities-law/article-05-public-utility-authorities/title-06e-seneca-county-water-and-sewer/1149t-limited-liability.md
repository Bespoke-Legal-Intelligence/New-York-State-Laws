# Public Authorities Law § 1149-T — Limited liability

[Public Authorities Law](../../README.md) › [Article 5 — Public Utility Authorities](../README.md) › [Title 6-E — Seneca County Water and Sewer Authority](README.md)

```text
  § 1149-t. Limited liability. Neither the members of the authority, nor
any municipality, officer or employee acting in its behalf, while acting
within the scope of their authority, shall be subject to any personal
liability resulting from the construction, maintenance or operation of
any of the properties of the authority or from carrying out any of the
powers expressly given in this title; provided, however, that this
section shall not be held to apply to any independent contractor.
```
