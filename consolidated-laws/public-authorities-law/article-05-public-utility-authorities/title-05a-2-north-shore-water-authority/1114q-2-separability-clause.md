# Public Authorities Law § 1114-Q*2 — Separability clause

[Public Authorities Law](../../README.md) › [Article 5 — Public Utility Authorities](../README.md) › [Title 5-A*2 — North Shore Water Authority](README.md)

*An asterisk in a number (for example Article 24\* or § 20-C\*2) is the Senate's mark for a number the Legislature has used more than once: the marked article or section is a different one from the unmarked one with the same number. \*2 means the second one enacted with that number.*

```text
  * § 1114-q. Separability clause. If any section, clause or provision
in this title shall be held by a competent court to be unconstitutional
or ineffective in whole or in part, to the extent that it is not
unconstitutional or ineffective, it shall be valid and effective, and no
other section, clause or provision shall on account thereof be deemed
invalid or ineffective.
  * NB There are 2 § 1114-q's
```
