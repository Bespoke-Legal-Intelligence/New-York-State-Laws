# Public Authorities Law § 1114-P*2 — Construction and purchase contracts

[Public Authorities Law](../../README.md) › [Article 5 — Public Utility Authorities](../README.md) › [Title 5-A*2 — North Shore Water Authority](README.md)

*An asterisk in a number (for example Article 24\* or § 20-C\*2) is the Senate's mark for a number the Legislature has used more than once: the marked article or section is a different one from the unmarked one with the same number. \*2 means the second one enacted with that number.*

```text
  * § 1114-p. Construction and purchase contracts. The authority shall
let contracts for construction or purchase of supplies, materials, or
equipment pursuant to section one hundred three of the general municipal
law. Nothing in this section shall be construed to limit the power of
the authority to do any construction directly by the officers, agents
and employees of the authority or to contract with a public utility, for
a term not to exceed five years, for the operation and maintenance of a
water supply system acquired from said public utility.
  * NB There are 2 § 1114-p's
```
