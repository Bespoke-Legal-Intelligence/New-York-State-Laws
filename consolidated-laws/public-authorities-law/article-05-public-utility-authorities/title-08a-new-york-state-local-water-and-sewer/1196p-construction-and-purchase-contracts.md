# Public Authorities Law § 1196-P — Construction and purchase contracts

[Public Authorities Law](../../README.md) › [Article 5 — Public Utility Authorities](../README.md) › [Title 8-A — New York State Local Water and Sewer Authority Act](README.md)

```text
  § 1196-p. Construction and purchase contracts. An authority shall let
contracts for construction or purchase of supplies, materials, or
equipment pursuant to section one hundred three of the general municipal
law. Nothing in this section shall be construed to limit the power of an
authority to do any construction directly by the officers, agents and
employees of the authority.
```
