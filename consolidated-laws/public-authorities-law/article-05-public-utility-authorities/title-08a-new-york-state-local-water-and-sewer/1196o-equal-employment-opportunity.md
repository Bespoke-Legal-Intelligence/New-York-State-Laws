# Public Authorities Law § 1196-O — Equal employment opportunity

[Public Authorities Law](../../README.md) › [Article 5 — Public Utility Authorities](../README.md) › [Title 8-A — New York State Local Water and Sewer Authority Act](README.md)

```text
  § 1196-o. Equal employment opportunity. Authorities shall ensure that,
where possible, all employees or applicants for employment are afforded
equal employment opportunity without discrimination.
```
