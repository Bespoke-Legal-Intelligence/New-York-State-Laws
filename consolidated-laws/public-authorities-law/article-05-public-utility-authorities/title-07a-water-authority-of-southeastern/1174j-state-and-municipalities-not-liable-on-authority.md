# Public Authorities Law § 1174-J — State and municipalities not liable on authority bonds

[Public Authorities Law](../../README.md) › [Article 5 — Public Utility Authorities](../README.md) › [Title 7-A — Water Authority of Southeastern Nassau County](README.md)

```text
  § 1174-j. State and municipalities not liable on authority bonds.
Neither the state nor any municipality shall be liable on the bonds of
the authority and such bonds shall not be a debt of the state or of any
municipality.
```
