# Public Authorities Law § 1225-K — Title to property

[Public Authorities Law](../../README.md) › [Article 5 — Public Utility Authorities](../README.md) › [Title 9-A — Transit Construction Fund](README.md)

```text
  § 1225-k. Title to property. Title to any transit facility leased to
the fund by the transportation authority pursuant to this title shall,
on the expiration or termination of such lease, vest in the city.
```
