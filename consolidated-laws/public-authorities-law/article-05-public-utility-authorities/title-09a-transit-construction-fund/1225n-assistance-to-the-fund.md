# Public Authorities Law § 1225-N — Assistance to the fund

[Public Authorities Law](../../README.md) › [Article 5 — Public Utility Authorities](../README.md) › [Title 9-A — Transit Construction Fund](README.md)

```text
  § 1225-n. Assistance to the fund. The city may make grants of money or
property to the fund for the purpose of enabling it to carry out its
public purposes and for the exercise of its powers. This section shall
not be construed to limit any other power the city may have to make such
grants to the fund.
```
