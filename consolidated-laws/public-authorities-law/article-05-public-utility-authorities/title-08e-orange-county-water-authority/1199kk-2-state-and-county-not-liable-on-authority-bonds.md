# Public Authorities Law § 1199-KK*2 — State and county not liable on authority bonds

[Public Authorities Law](../../README.md) › [Article 5 — Public Utility Authorities](../README.md) › [Title 8-E* — Orange County Water Authority](README.md)

*An asterisk in a number (for example Article 24\* or § 20-C\*2) is the Senate's mark for a number the Legislature has used more than once: the marked article or section is a different one from the unmarked one with the same number. \*2 means the second one enacted with that number.*

```text
  * § 1199-kk. State and county not liable on authority bonds. Neither
the state nor the county shall be liable on the bonds of the authority
and such bonds shall not be a debt of the state, or the county, and each
such bond shall contain, on the face thereof, a statement to such
effect.
  * NB There are 2 § 1199-kk's
```
