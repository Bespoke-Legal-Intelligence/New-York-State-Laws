# Public Authorities Law § 1199-VV*2 — Separability

[Public Authorities Law](../../README.md) › [Article 5 — Public Utility Authorities](../README.md) › [Title 8-E* — Orange County Water Authority](README.md)

*An asterisk in a number (for example Article 24\* or § 20-C\*2) is the Senate's mark for a number the Legislature has used more than once: the marked article or section is a different one from the unmarked one with the same number. \*2 means the second one enacted with that number.*

```text
  * § 1199-vv. Separability. If any section, clause or provision in this
title shall be held by a court of competent jurisdiction to be
unconstitutional or ineffective in whole or in part, to the extent that
it is not unconstitutional or ineffective, it shall be valid and
effective, and no other section, clause or provision shall on account
thereof be deemed invalid or ineffective.
  * NB There are 2 § 1199-vv's
```
