# Public Authorities Law § 1199-SS*2 — Audit and annual report

[Public Authorities Law](../../README.md) › [Article 5 — Public Utility Authorities](../README.md) › [Title 8-E* — Orange County Water Authority](README.md)

*An asterisk in a number (for example Article 24\* or § 20-C\*2) is the Senate's mark for a number the Legislature has used more than once: the marked article or section is a different one from the unmarked one with the same number. \*2 means the second one enacted with that number.*

```text
  * § 1199-ss. Audit and annual report. In conformity with the
provisions of section five of article ten of the constitution, the
accounts of the authority shall be subject to the supervision of the
comptroller. The authority shall annually submit to the governor and
state comptroller and to the state legislature a detailed report
pursuant to the provisions of section two thousand five hundred of title
one of article nine of this chapter, and a copy of such report shall be
filed with the county executive. The authority shall comply with the
provisions of sections two thousand five hundred one, two thousand five
hundred two, and two thousand five hundred three of title one of article
nine of this chapter.
  * NB There are 2 § 1199-ss's
```
