# Public Authorities Law § 1232-T — Separability

[Public Authorities Law](../../README.md) › [Article 5 — Public Utility Authorities](../README.md) › [Title 10-D — Nassau County Sewer and Storm Water Finance Authority](README.md)

```text
  § 1232-t. Separability. If any section, clause or provision of this
title shall be held by a court of competent jurisdiction to be
unconstitutional or ineffective in whole or in part, to the extent that
it is not unconstitutional or ineffective, such section, clause or
provision shall be valid and effective, and no other section, clause or
provision shall on account thereof be deemed invalid or ineffective.
```
