# Public Authorities Law § 1232-I — State and county not liable on authority bonds

[Public Authorities Law](../../README.md) › [Article 5 — Public Utility Authorities](../README.md) › [Title 10-D — Nassau County Sewer and Storm Water Finance Authority](README.md)

```text
  § 1232-i. State and county not liable on authority bonds. Neither the
state nor the county shall be liable on the bonds of the authority and
such bonds shall not be a debt of the state or the county, and such
bonds shall contain, on the face thereof, a statement to such effect.
```
