# Public Authorities Law § 1020-R*2 — Officers and employees not to be interested in transactions

[Public Authorities Law](../../README.md) › [Article 5 — Public Utility Authorities](../README.md) › [Title 1-A* — Green Island Power Authority](README.md)

*An asterisk in a number (for example Article 24\* or § 20-C\*2) is the Senate's mark for a number the Legislature has used more than once: the marked article or section is a different one from the unmarked one with the same number. \*2 means the second one enacted with that number.*

```text
  * § 1020-r. Officers and employees not to be interested in
transactions.  It shall be a misdemeanor for any of the members of the
authority, or any officer, agent, servant or employee thereof, employed
or appointed by them to be in any way or manner interested directly or
indirectly in the furnishing of work, materials, supplies or labor, or
in any contract therefor which the authority is empowered by this title
to make.
  * NB There are 2 § 1020-r's
```
