# Public Authorities Law § 1020-O*2 — Payments by municipalities

[Public Authorities Law](../../README.md) › [Article 5 — Public Utility Authorities](../README.md) › [Title 1-A* — Green Island Power Authority](README.md)

*An asterisk in a number (for example Article 24\* or § 20-C\*2) is the Senate's mark for a number the Legislature has used more than once: the marked article or section is a different one from the unmarked one with the same number. \*2 means the second one enacted with that number.*

```text
  * § 1020-o. Payments by municipalities. The board of trustees of the
village and the governing body of any municipality in which surplus of
the authority is sold pursuant to section one thousand twenty-f of this
title are authorized to make payments to the authority for its support.
It is hereby determined that such payments are for a public purpose for
the benefit of the people of such village and such municipalities.
  * NB There are 2 § 1020-o's
```
