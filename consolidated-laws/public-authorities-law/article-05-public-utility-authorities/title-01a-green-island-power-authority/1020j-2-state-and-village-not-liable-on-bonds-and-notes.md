# Public Authorities Law § 1020-J*2 — State and village not liable on bonds and notes

[Public Authorities Law](../../README.md) › [Article 5 — Public Utility Authorities](../README.md) › [Title 1-A* — Green Island Power Authority](README.md)

*An asterisk in a number (for example Article 24\* or § 20-C\*2) is the Senate's mark for a number the Legislature has used more than once: the marked article or section is a different one from the unmarked one with the same number. \*2 means the second one enacted with that number.*

```text
  * § 1020-j. State and village not liable on bonds and notes. The bonds
and other obligations of the authority shall not be a debt of the state
or of the village and, subject to section one thousand twenty-o of this
title, the board shall have no power to make them payable out of any
funds except those of the authority.
  * NB There are 2 § 1020-j's
```
