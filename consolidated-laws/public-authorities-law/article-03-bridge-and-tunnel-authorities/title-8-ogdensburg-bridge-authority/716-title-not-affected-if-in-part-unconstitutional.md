# Public Authorities Law § 716 — Title not affected if in part unconstitutional

[Public Authorities Law](../../README.md) › [Article 3 — Bridge and Tunnel Authorities](../README.md) › [Title 8 — Ogdensburg Bridge Authority](README.md)

```text
  § 716. Title not affected if in part unconstitutional. If any section,
clause or provision of this title shall be unconstitutional or
ineffective in whole or in part, to the extent that it is not
unconstitutional or ineffective it shall be valid and effective and no
other section, clause or provision shall on account thereof be deemed
invalid or ineffective.
```
