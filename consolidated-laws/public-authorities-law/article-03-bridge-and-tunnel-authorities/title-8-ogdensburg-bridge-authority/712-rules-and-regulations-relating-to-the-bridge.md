# Public Authorities Law § 712 — Rules and regulations relating to the bridge

[Public Authorities Law](../../README.md) › [Article 3 — Bridge and Tunnel Authorities](../README.md) › [Title 8 — Ogdensburg Bridge Authority](README.md)

```text
  § 712. Rules and regulations relating to the bridge. The use of the
bridge herein authorized shall be subject to the rules and regulations
of the authority; provided, however, that the authority is not empowered
to do anything which will impair the security of the bondholders or
violate limitations contained in any agreement with bondholders.
```
