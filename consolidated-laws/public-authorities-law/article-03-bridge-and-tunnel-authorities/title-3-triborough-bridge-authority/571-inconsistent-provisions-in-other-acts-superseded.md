# Public Authorities Law § 571 — Inconsistent provisions in other acts superseded

[Public Authorities Law](../../README.md) › [Article 3 — Bridge and Tunnel Authorities](../README.md) › [Title 3 — Triborough Bridge Authority](README.md)

```text
  § 571. Inconsistent provisions in other acts superseded. Insofar as
the provisions of this title are inconsistent with the provisions of any
other act, general or special, or of any local law of the city, the
provisions of this title shall be controlling.
```
