# Public Authorities Law § 559 — Construction contracts

[Public Authorities Law](../../README.md) › [Article 3 — Bridge and Tunnel Authorities](../README.md) › [Title 3 — Triborough Bridge Authority](README.md)

```text
  § 559. Construction contracts. The authority shall do all construction
pursuant to a contract or contracts in the manner, so far as
practicable, provided in the charter of the city for contracts of such
city except that where the estimated expense of a contract does not
exceed ten thousand dollars such contract may be entered into without
public letting, but failure to comply with this section shall not
invalidate such contracts.
```
