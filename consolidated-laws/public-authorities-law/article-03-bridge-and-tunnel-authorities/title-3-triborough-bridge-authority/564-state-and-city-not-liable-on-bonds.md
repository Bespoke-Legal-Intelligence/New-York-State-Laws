# Public Authorities Law § 564 — State and city not liable on bonds

[Public Authorities Law](../../README.md) › [Article 3 — Bridge and Tunnel Authorities](../README.md) › [Title 3 — Triborough Bridge Authority](README.md)

```text
  § 564. State and city not liable on bonds. The bonds and other
obligations of the authority shall not be a debt of the state of New
York or city, and neither the state nor the city shall be liable
thereon, nor shall they be payable out of any funds other than those of
the authority.
```
