# Public Authorities Law § 663 — State and county not liable on notes and bonds

[Public Authorities Law](../../README.md) › [Article 3 — Bridge and Tunnel Authorities](../README.md) › [Title 7 — Nassau County Bridge Authority](README.md)

```text
  § 663. State and county not liable on notes and bonds. The notes and
bonds and other obligations of the authority shall not be a debt of the
state of New York or of the county, and neither the state nor the county
shall be liable thereon, nor shall they be payable out of any funds
other than those of the authority.
```
