# Public Authorities Law § 666-A — Limitation of liability

[Public Authorities Law](../../README.md) › [Article 3 — Bridge and Tunnel Authorities](../README.md) › [Title 7 — Nassau County Bridge Authority](README.md)

```text
  § 666-a. Limitation of liability. Neither the members of the
authority, nor any person or persons acting in its behalf, while acting
within the scope of their authority, shall be subject to any personal
liability resulting from the erection, construction, reconstruction,
maintenance or operation of the properties or any of the improvements or
from carrying out any of the powers expressly given in this title.
```
