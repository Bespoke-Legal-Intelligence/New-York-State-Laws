# Public Authorities Law § 526-A — Ceremonial designation

[Public Authorities Law](../../README.md) › [Article 3 — Bridge and Tunnel Authorities](../README.md) › [Title 2 — New York State Bridge Authority](README.md)

```text
  § 526-a. Ceremonial designation. All that portion of touring route 199
connecting Ulster and Dutchess counties which is identified and known as
the Kingston-Rhinecliff bridge shall be designated and known as the
"George Clinton Kingston-Rhinecliff bridge".
```
