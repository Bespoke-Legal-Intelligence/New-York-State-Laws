# Public Authorities Law § 534 — State not liable on bonds

[Public Authorities Law](../../README.md) › [Article 3 — Bridge and Tunnel Authorities](../README.md) › [Title 2 — New York State Bridge Authority](README.md)

```text
  § 534. State not liable on bonds. The bonds and other obligations of
the authority shall not be a debt of the state and the state shall not
be liable thereon, nor shall they be payable out of any funds other than
those of the authority.
```
