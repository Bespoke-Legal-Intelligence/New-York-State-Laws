# Public Authorities Law § 2049-JJ — State, counties and municipalities not liable on authority bonds

[Public Authorities Law](../../README.md) › [Article 8 — Miscellaneous Authorities](../README.md) › [Title 13-FF — Oneida-herkimer Solid Waste Management Authority](README.md)

```text
  § 2049-jj. State, counties and municipalities not liable on authority
bonds. Neither the state, the counties nor any other muncipality or
public corporation shall be liable on the bonds of the authority and
such bonds shall not be a debt of the state, the counties or any other
municipality or public corporation, and such bond shall contain on the
face thereof, a statement to such effect.
```
