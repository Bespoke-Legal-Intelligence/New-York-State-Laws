# Public Authorities Law § 2662 — Private donations

[Public Authorities Law](../../README.md) › [Article 8 — Miscellaneous Authorities](../README.md) › [Title 28-B — Schenectady Metroplex Development Authority](README.md)

```text
  § 2662. Private donations.  Notwithstanding the provisions of any
general or special law to the contrary, any person may donate, give,
tender or provide, and the authority may receive, private monies to
assist the authority in its successful operation and achievement of its
lawful goals, powers and purposes.
```
