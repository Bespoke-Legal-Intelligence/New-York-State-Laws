# Public Authorities Law § 2669 — State, county and municipalities not liable on bonds

[Public Authorities Law](../../README.md) › [Article 8 — Miscellaneous Authorities](../README.md) › [Title 28-B — Schenectady Metroplex Development Authority](README.md)

```text
  § 2669. State, county and municipalities not liable on bonds. Neither
the state, the county nor any municipality therein shall be liable on
the bonds or notes of the authority and such bonds or notes shall not be
a debt of the state, the county or of any municipality therein and such
bonds and notes shall contain on the face thereof a statement to such
effect.
```
