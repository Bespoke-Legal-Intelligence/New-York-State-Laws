# Public Authorities Law § 2062 — New York state and Broome county not liable on bonds

[Public Authorities Law](../../README.md) › [Article 8 — Miscellaneous Authorities](../README.md) › [Title 14 — Broome County Sports Center Authority](README.md)

```text
  * § 2062. New York state and Broome county not liable on bonds.  The
bonds and other obligations of the authority shall not be a debt of the
state of New York or of the county of Broome, and neither the state nor
the county shall be liable thereon, nor shall they be payable out of any
funds other than those of the authority.
  * NB Authority dissolved September 1, 1977
```
