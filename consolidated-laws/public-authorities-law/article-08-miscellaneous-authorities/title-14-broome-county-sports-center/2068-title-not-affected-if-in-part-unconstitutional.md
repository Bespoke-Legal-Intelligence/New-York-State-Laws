# Public Authorities Law § 2068 — Title not affected if in part unconstitutional or ineffective

[Public Authorities Law](../../README.md) › [Article 8 — Miscellaneous Authorities](../README.md) › [Title 14 — Broome County Sports Center Authority](README.md)

```text
  * § 2068. Title not affected if in part unconstitutional or
ineffective. If any section, clause or provision of this title shall be
unconstitutional or be ineffective in whole or in part, to the extent
that it is not unconstitutional or ineffective, it shall be valid and
effective and no other section, clause or provision shall on account
thereof be deemed invalid or ineffective.
  * NB Authority dissolved September 1, 1977
```
