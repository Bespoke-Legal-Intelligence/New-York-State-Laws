# Public Authorities Law § 2490-K — State, city and municipalities not liable on authority bonds

[Public Authorities Law](../../README.md) › [Article 8 — Miscellaneous Authorities](../README.md) › [Title 21 — Saratoga Springs City Center Authority](README.md)

```text
  § 2490-k. State, city and municipalities not liable on authority
bonds. Neither the state, the city nor any other municipality or public
corporation shall be liable on the bonds of the authority and such bonds
shall not be a debt of the state, the city or any other municipality or
public corporation, and such bonds shall contain on the face thereof a
statement to such effect.
```
