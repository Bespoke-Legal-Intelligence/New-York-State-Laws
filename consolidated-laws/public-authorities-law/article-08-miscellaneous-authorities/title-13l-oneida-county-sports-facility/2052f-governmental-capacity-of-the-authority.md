# Public Authorities Law § 2052-F — Governmental capacity of the authority and municipalities

[Public Authorities Law](../../README.md) › [Article 8 — Miscellaneous Authorities](../README.md) › [Title 13-L — Oneida County Sports Facility Authority Act](README.md)

```text
  § 2052-f. Governmental capacity of the authority and municipalities.
The authority, the county and the other municipalities, in carrying out
their respective powers and duties under this title, shall be deemed to
be acting in a governmental capacity and in the performance of an
essential governmental function.
```
