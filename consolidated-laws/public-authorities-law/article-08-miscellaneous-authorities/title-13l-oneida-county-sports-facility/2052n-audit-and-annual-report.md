# Public Authorities Law § 2052-N — Audit and annual report

[Public Authorities Law](../../README.md) › [Article 8 — Miscellaneous Authorities](../README.md) › [Title 13-L — Oneida County Sports Facility Authority Act](README.md)

```text
  § 2052-n. Audit and annual report. In conformity with the provisions
of section five of article ten of the constitution, the accounts of the
authority shall be subject to the supervision of the comptroller. The
authority shall annually submit to the governor and comptroller and to
the state legislature a detailed report pursuant to the provisions of
section two thousand eight hundred of title one of article nine of this
chapter, and a copy of such report shall be filed with the county
executive and with the county board of legislators. The authority shall
comply with the provisions of sections two thousand eight hundred one,
two thousand eight hundred two and two thousand eight hundred three of
title one of article nine of this chapter.
```
