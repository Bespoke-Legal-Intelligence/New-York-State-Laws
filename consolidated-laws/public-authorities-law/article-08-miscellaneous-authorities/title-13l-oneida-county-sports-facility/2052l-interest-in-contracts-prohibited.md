# Public Authorities Law § 2052-L — Interest in contracts prohibited

[Public Authorities Law](../../README.md) › [Article 8 — Miscellaneous Authorities](../README.md) › [Title 13-L — Oneida County Sports Facility Authority Act](README.md)

```text
  § 2052-l. Interest in contracts prohibited. It shall be a misdemeanor
for any member of the board or any officer, agent, servant or employee
of the authority to be in any way or manner interested, directly or
indirectly, in the furnishing of work, materials, supplies or labor or
in any contract therefor which the authority is empowered by this title
to make.
```
