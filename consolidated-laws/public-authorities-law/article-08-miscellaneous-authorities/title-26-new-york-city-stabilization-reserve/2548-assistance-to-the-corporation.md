# Public Authorities Law § 2548 — Assistance to the corporation

[Public Authorities Law](../../README.md) › [Article 8 — Miscellaneous Authorities](../README.md) › [Title 26 — New York City Stabilization Reserve Corporation](README.md)

```text
  § 2548. Assistance to the corporation. With the consent of the city,
the corporation may use agents, employees and facilities of the city,
including the corporation counsel, paying to the city its agreed
proportion of the compensation or costs.
```
