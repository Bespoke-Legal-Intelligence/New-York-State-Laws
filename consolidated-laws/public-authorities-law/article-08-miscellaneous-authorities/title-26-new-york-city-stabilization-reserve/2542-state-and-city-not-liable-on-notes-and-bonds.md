# Public Authorities Law § 2542 — State and city not liable on notes and bonds

[Public Authorities Law](../../README.md) › [Article 8 — Miscellaneous Authorities](../README.md) › [Title 26 — New York City Stabilization Reserve Corporation](README.md)

```text
  § 2542. State and city not liable on notes and bonds. The notes, bonds
or other obligations of the corporation shall not be a debt of either
the state or the city, and neither the state nor the city shall be
liable thereon, nor shall they be payable out of any funds other than
those of the corporation; and such notes and bonds shall contain on the
face thereof a statement to such effect.
```
