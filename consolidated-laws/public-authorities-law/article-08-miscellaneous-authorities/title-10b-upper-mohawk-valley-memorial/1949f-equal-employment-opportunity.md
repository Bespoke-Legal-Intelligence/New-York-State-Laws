# Public Authorities Law § 1949-F — Equal employment opportunity

[Public Authorities Law](../../README.md) › [Article 8 — Miscellaneous Authorities](../README.md) › [Title 10-B — Upper Mohawk Valley Memorial Auditorium Authority](README.md)

```text
  § 1949-f. Equal employment opportunity.  The authority shall ensure
that all employees or applicants for employment are afforded equal
employment opportunity without discrimination.
```
