# Public Authorities Law § 2050-R — Audit and annual report

[Public Authorities Law](../../README.md) › [Article 8 — Miscellaneous Authorities](../README.md) › [Title 13-G — Ulster County Resource Recovery Agency](README.md)

```text
  § 2050-r. Audit and annual report. In conformity with the provisions
of section five of article ten of the state constitution, the accounts
of the agency shall be subject to the supervision of the state
comptroller. The agency shall annually submit to the governor and state
comptroller and to the state legislature a detailed report pursuant to
the provisions of section two thousand five hundred of title one of
article nine of this chapter, and a copy of such report shall be filed
with the chairman of the county legislature. The agency shall comply
with the provisions of sections two thousand five hundred one, two
thousand five hundred two and two thousand five hundred three of title
one of article nine of this chapter.
```
