# Public Authorities Law § 2046-D — State and town not liable on bonds or notes

[Public Authorities Law](../../README.md) › [Article 8 — Miscellaneous Authorities](../README.md) › [Title 13-C — Town of Islip Resource Recovery Agency](README.md)

```text
  § 2046-d. State and town not liable on bonds or notes. Neither the
state, the town nor any other municipality or other public corporation
shall be liable on the bonds and notes of the agency and such bonds and
notes shall not be a debt of the state, the town or any other
municipality or public corporation, and such bonds and notes shall
contain on the face thereof a statement to such effect.
```
