# Public Authorities Law § 2350-FF — Filings to be made with the agency

[Public Authorities Law](../../README.md) › [Article 8 — Miscellaneous Authorities](../README.md) › [Title 16 — Suffolk County Judicial Facilities Agency](README.md)

```text
  § 2350-ff. Filings to be made with the agency. 1. Every person or
organization subject to the provisions of article one-A of the
legislative law as a lobbyist or client (as those terms are defined in
such article) because of acts relating to procurements of the agency
shall, in addition to filing one or more statements of registration and
reports with the New York temporary state commission on lobbying as
required by article one-A of the legislative law, file a copy of each
such document required to be filed under said article with the agency.
  2. The agency shall establish an office to receive and file documents
required to be filed under subdivision one of this section. The agency
shall catalogue such filings by the name of the lobbyist, the name of
the client, and by the contract which the lobbyist has attempted to
influence action on behalf of the client. The agency shall make
information furnished by lobbyists and clients available to the public
for inspection and copying.
```
