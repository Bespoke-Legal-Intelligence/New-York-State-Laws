# Public Authorities Law § 2350-I — Neither state nor county liable on agency bonds

[Public Authorities Law](../../README.md) › [Article 8 — Miscellaneous Authorities](../README.md) › [Title 16 — Suffolk County Judicial Facilities Agency](README.md)

```text
  * § 2350-i. Neither state nor county liable on agency bonds. Neither
the state nor the county shall be liable on the bonds of the agency and
such bonds shall not be a debt of either the state or the county and
each such bond shall contain, on the face thereof, a statement to such
effect.
  * NB Repealed December 31, 2023
```
