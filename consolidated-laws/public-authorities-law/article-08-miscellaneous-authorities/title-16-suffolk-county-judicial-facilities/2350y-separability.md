# Public Authorities Law § 2350-Y — Separability

[Public Authorities Law](../../README.md) › [Article 8 — Miscellaneous Authorities](../README.md) › [Title 16 — Suffolk County Judicial Facilities Agency](README.md)

**Repealed** (5 Jan 2024). The text below is what the Senate publishes for this repealed provision.

```text
  * § 2350-y. Separability. If any section, clause or provision in this
title shall be held by a competent court to be unconstitutional or
ineffective in whole or in part, to the extent that it is not
unconstitutional or ineffective, it shall be valid and effective, and no
other section, clause or provision shall on account thereof be deemed
invalid or ineffective.
  * NB Repealed December 31, 2023
```
