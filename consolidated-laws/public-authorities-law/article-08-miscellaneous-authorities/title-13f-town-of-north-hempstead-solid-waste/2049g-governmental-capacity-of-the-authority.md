# Public Authorities Law § 2049-G — Governmental capacity of the authority and municipalities

[Public Authorities Law](../../README.md) › [Article 8 — Miscellaneous Authorities](../README.md) › [Title 13-F — Town of North Hempstead Solid Waste Management Authority](README.md)

```text
  § 2049-g. Governmental capacity of the authority and municipalities.
The authority, the town and other municipalities, in carrying out their
respective powers and duties under this title, shall be deemed to be
acting in a governmental capacity. The construction, operation and
maintenance of any project financed in whole or in part by the authority
shall be deemed to be the performance of an essential governmental
function by the authority acting in its governmental capacity, whether
such project shall be owned or operated by the authority or by any
person or public corporation.
```
