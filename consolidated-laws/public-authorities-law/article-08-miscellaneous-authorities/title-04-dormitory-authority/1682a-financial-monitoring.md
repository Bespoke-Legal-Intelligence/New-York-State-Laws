# Public Authorities Law § 1682-A — Financial monitoring

[Public Authorities Law](../../README.md) › [Article 8 — Miscellaneous Authorities](../README.md) › [Title 4 — Dormitory Authority](README.md)

```text
  § 1682-a. Financial monitoring. The authority shall periodically
monitor the financial and managerial standing of each entity which has
received financing from the proceeds of bonds of either the authority or
the medical care facilities finance agency, for the term of such bonds.
```
