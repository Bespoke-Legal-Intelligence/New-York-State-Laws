# Public Authorities Law § 1684 — Bonds legal investments for fiduciaries

[Public Authorities Law](../../README.md) › [Article 8 — Miscellaneous Authorities](../README.md) › [Title 4 — Dormitory Authority](README.md)

```text
  § 1684. Bonds legal investments for fiduciaries.  The bonds are hereby
made securities in which all public officers and bodies of this state
and all municipalities and municipal subdivisions, all insurance
companies and associations, all savings banks and savings institutions,
including savings and loan associations, administrators, guardians,
executors, trustees, committees, conservators and other fiduciaries in
the state may properly and legally invest funds in their control.
```
