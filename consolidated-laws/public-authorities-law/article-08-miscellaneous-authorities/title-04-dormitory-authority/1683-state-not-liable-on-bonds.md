# Public Authorities Law § 1683 — State not liable on bonds

[Public Authorities Law](../../README.md) › [Article 8 — Miscellaneous Authorities](../README.md) › [Title 4 — Dormitory Authority](README.md)

```text
  § 1683. State not liable on bonds. The bonds and other obligations of
the authority shall not be a debt of the state of New York nor shall the
state be liable thereon, nor shall they be payable out of any funds
other than those of the authority.
```
