# Public Authorities Law § 1878 — State coordination, oversight and technology transfer

[Public Authorities Law](../../README.md) › [Article 8 — Miscellaneous Authorities](../README.md) › [Title 9 — New York State Energy Research and Development Authority](README.md)

```text
  § 1878. State coordination, oversight and technology transfer. The New
York state energy research and development authority shall exercise
oversight over such program; in consultation with Rensselaer Polytechnic
Institute, coordinate the direction of the program with other research
under the auspices of the authority and insure, where appropriate, the
expeditious transfer of technology resulting from such program to New
York state industries.
```
