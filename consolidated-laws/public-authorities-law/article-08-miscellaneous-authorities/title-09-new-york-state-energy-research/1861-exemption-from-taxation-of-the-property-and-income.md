# Public Authorities Law § 1861 — Exemption from taxation of the property and income of the authority

[Public Authorities Law](../../README.md) › [Article 8 — Miscellaneous Authorities](../README.md) › [Title 9 — New York State Energy Research and Development Authority](README.md)

```text
  § 1861. Exemption from taxation of the property and income of the
authority. The property of the authority and its income and operations
shall be exempt from taxation.
```
