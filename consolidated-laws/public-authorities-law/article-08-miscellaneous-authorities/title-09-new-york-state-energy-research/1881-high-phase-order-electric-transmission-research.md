# Public Authorities Law § 1881 — High phase order electric transmission research project

[Public Authorities Law](../../README.md) › [Article 8 — Miscellaneous Authorities](../README.md) › [Title 9 — New York State Energy Research and Development Authority](README.md)

```text
  § 1881. High phase order electric transmission research project. The
purpose of this project shall be to convert an existing transmission
line to a high phase order line, carry out test and operation procedures
and provide results of these procedures for evaluation.
```
