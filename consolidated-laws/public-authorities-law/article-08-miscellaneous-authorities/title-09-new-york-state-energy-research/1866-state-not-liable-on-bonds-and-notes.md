# Public Authorities Law § 1866 — State not liable on bonds and notes

[Public Authorities Law](../../README.md) › [Article 8 — Miscellaneous Authorities](../README.md) › [Title 9 — New York State Energy Research and Development Authority](README.md)

```text
  § 1866. State not liable on bonds and notes. The bonds and notes shall
not be a debt of the state of New York nor shall the state be liable
thereon and such bonds and notes shall contain on the face thereof a
statement to that effect.
```
