# Public Authorities Law § 1870 — Title not affected if in part unconstitutional or ineffective

[Public Authorities Law](../../README.md) › [Article 8 — Miscellaneous Authorities](../README.md) › [Title 9 — New York State Energy Research and Development Authority](README.md)

```text
  § 1870. Title not affected if in part unconstitutional or ineffective.
If any subtitle, section, subdivision, paragraph, sentence, clause or
provision of this title shall be unconstitutional or be ineffective in
whole or in part, to the extent that it is not unconstitutional or
ineffective, it shall be valid or effective and no other subtitle,
section, subdivision, paragraph, sentence, clause or provision shall on
account thereof be deemed invalid or ineffective.
```
