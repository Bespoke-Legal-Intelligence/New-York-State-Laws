# Public Authorities Law § 1883 — State oversight and funding

[Public Authorities Law](../../README.md) › [Article 8 — Miscellaneous Authorities](../README.md) › [Title 9 — New York State Energy Research and Development Authority](README.md)

```text
  § 1883. State oversight and funding. The New York state energy
research and development authority shall exercise oversight over such
program and is hereby authorized to disburse funds to the New York State
Electric and Gas Corporation to carry out the purpose of this program.
```
