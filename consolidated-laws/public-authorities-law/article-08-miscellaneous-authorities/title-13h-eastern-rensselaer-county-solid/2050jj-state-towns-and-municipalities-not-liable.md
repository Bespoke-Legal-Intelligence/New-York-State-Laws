# Public Authorities Law § 2050-JJ — State, towns and municipalities not liable on authority bonds

[Public Authorities Law](../../README.md) › [Article 8 — Miscellaneous Authorities](../README.md) › [Title 13-H — Eastern Rensselaer County Solid Waste Management Authority](README.md)

```text
  § 2050-jj. State, towns and municipalities not liable on authority
bonds. Neither the state, the towns nor any other municipality or public
corporation shall be liable on the bonds of the authority and such bonds
shall not be a debt of the state, the towns or any other municipality or
public corporation, and such bond shall contain on the face thereof, a
statement to such effect.
```
