# Public Authorities Law § 2482 — Limitation of liability

[Public Authorities Law](../../README.md) › [Article 8 — Miscellaneous Authorities](../README.md) › [Title 19 — New York State Sports Authority](README.md)

```text
  * § 2482. Limitation of liability. Neither the members of the
authority, nor any person or persons acting in its behalf, while acting
within the scope of their authority, shall be subject to any personal
liability resulting from carrying out any of the powers expressly given
in this act.
  * NB (Disbanded March, 1980)
```
