# Public Authorities Law § 2312 — Construction and purchase contracts

[Public Authorities Law](../../README.md) › [Article 8 — Miscellaneous Authorities](../README.md) › [Title 15 — Auburn Industrial Development Authority](README.md)

```text
  § 2312. Construction and purchase contracts. The provisions of section
eight hundred eighty-four of the general municipal law relating to
public bidding with respect to the construction of public facilities or
projects shall be applicable to the acquisition, construction,
reconstruction, or improvement of projects authorized by this title.
```
