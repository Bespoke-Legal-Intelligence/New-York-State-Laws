# Public Authorities Law § 1699-A — Legislative report

[Public Authorities Law](../../README.md) › [Article 8 — Miscellaneous Authorities](../README.md) › [Title 4-A — Small Business Concerns Set-aside Purchases and Contracts For Property and Services At the Hostos Community College](README.md)

```text
  * § 1699-a. Legislative report. The chairman shall annually before
December first, report in writing to the legislature concerning the
awarding of contracts, for specified commodities, equipment or services
or for the construction, reconstruction, rehabilitation or improvement
of college facilities at the Hostos Community College to small
businesses and minority businesses. The report shall include the total
value of awards made in the preceding fiscal year under the designation
of small business set-aside.
  * NB There are 2 § 1699-a's
```
