# Public Authorities Law § 1698 — Submission of purchase order or work contract to successful bidder

[Public Authorities Law](../../README.md) › [Article 8 — Miscellaneous Authorities](../README.md) › [Title 4-A — Small Business Concerns Set-aside Purchases and Contracts For Property and Services At the Hostos Community College](README.md)

```text
  * § 1698. Submission of purchase order or work contract to successful
bidder. After the successful bidder has been determined, the chairman
shall place an advertisement as provided by law indicating that all bids
submitted on a small business set-aside have been opened and reporting
the name and address of the successful bidder. The chairman shall send a
purchase order or work contract to the successful bidder no sooner than
fourteen days after the appearance of the advertisement announcing the
successful bidder.
  * NB There are 2 § 1698's
```
