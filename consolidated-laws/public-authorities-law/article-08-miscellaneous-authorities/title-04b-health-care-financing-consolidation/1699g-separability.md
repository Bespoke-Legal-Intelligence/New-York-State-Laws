# Public Authorities Law § 1699-G — Separability

[Public Authorities Law](../../README.md) › [Article 8 — Miscellaneous Authorities](../README.md) › [Title 4-B — Health Care Financing Consolidation Act](README.md)

```text
  § 1699-g. Separability. If any section, subdivision, paragraph,
sentence, clause or provision of this title shall be unconstitutional or
be ineffective in whole or in part, to the extent that it is not
unconstitutional or ineffective, it shall be valid or effective and no
other section, subdivision, paragraph, sentence, clause or provision
shall on account thereof be deemed invalid or ineffective.
```
