# Public Authorities Law § 1913 — Funding

[Public Authorities Law](../../README.md) › [Article 8 — Miscellaneous Authorities](../README.md) › [Title 9-C — Clean Energy Outreach and Community Planning Program](README.md)

```text
  * § 1913. Funding. 1. The authority may utilize fees collected by the
office of renewable energy siting pursuant to paragraph (e) of
subdivision seven of section ninety-four-c of the executive law to
administer the program created pursuant to this title.
  2. The authority shall annually notify the office of renewable energy
siting of the amount necessary to fund such program.
  * NB Repealed December 31, 2031
```
