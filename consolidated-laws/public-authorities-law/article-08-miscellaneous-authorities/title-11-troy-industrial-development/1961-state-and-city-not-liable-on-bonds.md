# Public Authorities Law § 1961 — State and city not liable on bonds

[Public Authorities Law](../../README.md) › [Article 8 — Miscellaneous Authorities](../README.md) › [Title 11 — Troy Industrial Development Authority](README.md)

```text
  § 1961. State and city not liable on bonds. The bonds and other
obligations of the authority shall not be a debt of the state or of the
city, and neither the state nor the city shall be liable thereon, nor
shall they be payable out of any funds other than those of the
authority.
```
