# Public Authorities Law § 2769 — Interest in contracts prohibited

[Public Authorities Law](../../README.md) › [Article 8 — Miscellaneous Authorities](../README.md) › [Title 31 — Monroe County Airport Authority](README.md)

```text
  § 2769. Interest in contracts prohibited. It shall be a misdemeanor
for any member, officer or employee of the authority to be in any way or
manner interested, directly or indirectly, in the furnishing of work,
materials, supplies or labor, or in any contract therefor which the
authority is empowered by this title to make.
```
