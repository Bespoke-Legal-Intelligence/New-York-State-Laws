# Public Authorities Law § 2508-A — Certain property acquired by authority

[Public Authorities Law](../../README.md) › [Article 8 — Miscellaneous Authorities](../README.md) › [Title 25 — New York City Sports Authority](README.md)

```text
  * § 2508-a. Certain property acquired by authority. In the event that
the New York state sports authority upon full amortization of any
outstanding bonds issued for the acquisition, construction or
rehabilitation of a sports facility, transfers such sports facility to
the authority pursuant to subdivision four of section twenty-four
hundred seventy-two of this chapter, such sports facility shall pass to
and be vested in the city.
  * NB (Discontinued-Board of Directors never appointed)
```
