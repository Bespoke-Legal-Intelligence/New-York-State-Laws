# Public Authorities Law § 2022 — Duty of authority to maintain and operate

[Public Authorities Law](../../README.md) › [Article 8 — Miscellaneous Authorities](../README.md) › [Title 13 — Onondaga County Solid Waste Disposal Authority](README.md)

```text
  § 2022. Duty of authority to maintain and operate. It shall be the
duty of the authority, subject to any limitation on the amount of
revenues to be expended for such purpose, to maintain and operate and
where necessary to reconstruct its properties.
```
