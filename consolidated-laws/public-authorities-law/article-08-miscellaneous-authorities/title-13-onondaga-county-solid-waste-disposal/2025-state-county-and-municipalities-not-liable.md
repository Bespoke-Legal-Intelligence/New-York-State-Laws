# Public Authorities Law § 2025 — State, county and municipalities not liable on bonds or notes

[Public Authorities Law](../../README.md) › [Article 8 — Miscellaneous Authorities](../README.md) › [Title 13 — Onondaga County Solid Waste Disposal Authority](README.md)

```text
  § 2025. State, county and municipalities not liable on bonds or notes.
The bonds or notes of the authority shall not be a debt of the state of
New York or of the county or of any municipality in the county, and
neither the state nor the county nor any municipality in the county
shall be liable thereon, nor shall they be payable out of any funds
other than those of the authority.
```
