# Public Authorities Law § 2421 — Inconsistent provisions of other laws superseded

[Public Authorities Law](../../../README.md) › [Article 8 — Miscellaneous Authorities](../../README.md) › [Title 17 — State of New York Mortgage Agency Act](../README.md) › [Part 1 — No title](README.md)

```text
  § 2421. Inconsistent provisions of other laws superseded. Insofar as
the provisions of this title are inconsistent with the provisions of any
other law, general, special or local, the provisions of this title shall
be controlling. It is the intent of the legislature that the provisions
of this title relating to mortgage commitments and laws be construed
liberally so as to effectuate the public and governmental purpose
thereof.
```
