# Public Authorities Law § 2417 — Limitation of liability

[Public Authorities Law](../../../README.md) › [Article 8 — Miscellaneous Authorities](../../README.md) › [Title 17 — State of New York Mortgage Agency Act](../README.md) › [Part 1 — No title](README.md)

```text
  § 2417. Limitation of liability. Neither the members of the agency,
nor any person or persons acting in its behalf, while acting within the
scope of their authority, shall be subject to any personal liability
resulting from carrying out any of the powers expressly given in this
act.
```
