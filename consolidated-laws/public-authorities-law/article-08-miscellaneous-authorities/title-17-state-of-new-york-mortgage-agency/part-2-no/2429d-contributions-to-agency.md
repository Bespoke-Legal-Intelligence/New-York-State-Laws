# Public Authorities Law § 2429-D — Contributions to agency

[Public Authorities Law](../../../README.md) › [Article 8 — Miscellaneous Authorities](../../README.md) › [Title 17 — State of New York Mortgage Agency Act](../README.md) › [Part 2 — No title](README.md)

```text
  § 2429-d. Contributions to agency. Notwithstanding the provisions of
any general or special law or the provisions of any certificate of
incorporation, charter or other articles of organization, all domestic
corporations or associations organized for the purpose of carrying on
business in this state, and any other persons are hereby authorized to
make contributions to the agency.
```
