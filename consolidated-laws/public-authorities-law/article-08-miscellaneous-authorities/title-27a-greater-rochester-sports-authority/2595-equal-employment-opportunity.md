# Public Authorities Law § 2595 — Equal employment opportunity

[Public Authorities Law](../../README.md) › [Article 8 — Miscellaneous Authorities](../README.md) › [Title 27-A — Greater Rochester Sports Authority](README.md)

```text
  § 2595. Equal employment opportunity. The authority shall ensure that
all employees or applicants for employment are afforded equal employment
opportunity without discrimination on any basis prohibited under
subdivision two of section forty-c of the civil rights law.
```
