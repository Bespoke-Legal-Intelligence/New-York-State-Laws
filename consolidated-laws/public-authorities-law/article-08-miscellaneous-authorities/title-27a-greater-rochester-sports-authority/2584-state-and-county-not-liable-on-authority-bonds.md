# Public Authorities Law § 2584 — State and county not liable on authority bonds

[Public Authorities Law](../../README.md) › [Article 8 — Miscellaneous Authorities](../README.md) › [Title 27-A — Greater Rochester Sports Authority](README.md)

```text
  § 2584. State and county not liable on authority bonds.  Neither the
state nor the county shall be liable on the bonds of the authority and
such bonds shall not be a debt of the state nor the county and such
bonds shall contain on the face thereof a statement to such effect.
```
