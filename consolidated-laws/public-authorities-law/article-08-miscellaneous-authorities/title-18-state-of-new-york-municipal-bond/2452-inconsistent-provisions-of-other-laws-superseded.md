# Public Authorities Law § 2452 — Inconsistent provisions of other laws superseded

[Public Authorities Law](../../README.md) › [Article 8 — Miscellaneous Authorities](../README.md) › [Title 18 — State of New York Municipal Bond Bank Agency Act](README.md)

```text
  § 2452. Inconsistent provisions of other laws superseded. Insofar as
the provisions of this title are inconsistent with the provisions of any
other law, general, special or local, the provisions of this title shall
be controlling.
```
