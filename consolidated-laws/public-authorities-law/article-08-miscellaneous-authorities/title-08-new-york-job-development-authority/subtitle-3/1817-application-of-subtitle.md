# Public Authorities Law § 1817 — Application of subtitle

[Public Authorities Law](../../../README.md) › [Article 8 — Miscellaneous Authorities](../../README.md) › [Title 8 — New York Job Development Authority](../README.md) › [Subtitle 3 — Financial Assistance From Pollution Control Funds](README.md)

```text
  § 1817. Application of subtitle. Subject to the provisions of sections
eighteen hundred twenty-two and eighteen hundred twenty-three of this
title, the authority shall have the power and is hereby authorized to
make loans with pollution control funds pursuant to this subtitle to
local development corporations for costs of projects located in any area
of the state for the acquisition, construction, rehabilitation or
improvement of pollution control facilities.
```
