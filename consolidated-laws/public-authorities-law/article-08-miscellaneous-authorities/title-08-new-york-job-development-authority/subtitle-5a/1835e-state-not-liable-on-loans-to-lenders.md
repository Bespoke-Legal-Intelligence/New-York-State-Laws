# Public Authorities Law § 1835-E — State not liable on loans to lenders bonds and notes

[Public Authorities Law](../../../README.md) › [Article 8 — Miscellaneous Authorities](../../README.md) › [Title 8 — New York Job Development Authority](../README.md) › [Subtitle 5-A — Loans to Lenders Program](README.md)

```text
  § 1835-e. State not liable on loans to lenders bonds and notes. The
state shall not be liable on loans to lenders bonds and notes of the
authority, and such bonds and notes shall contain on the face thereof a
statement to that effect.
```
