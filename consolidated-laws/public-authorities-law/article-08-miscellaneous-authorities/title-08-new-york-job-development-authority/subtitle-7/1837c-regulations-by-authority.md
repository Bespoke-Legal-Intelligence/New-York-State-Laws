# Public Authorities Law § 1837-C — Regulations by authority

[Public Authorities Law](../../../README.md) › [Article 8 — Miscellaneous Authorities](../../README.md) › [Title 8 — New York Job Development Authority](../README.md) › [Subtitle 7 — Special Project Development](README.md)

```text
  § 1837-c. Regulations by authority. The authority shall promulgate
rules and regulations to effectuate the purposes of this subtitle which
shall be subject to the approval of the director of the budget.
```
