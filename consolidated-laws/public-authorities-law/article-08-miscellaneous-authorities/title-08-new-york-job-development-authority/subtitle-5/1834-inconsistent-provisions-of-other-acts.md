# Public Authorities Law § 1834 — Inconsistent provisions of other acts superseded

[Public Authorities Law](../../../README.md) › [Article 8 — Miscellaneous Authorities](../../README.md) › [Title 8 — New York Job Development Authority](../README.md) › [Subtitle 5 — Miscellaneous Provisions](README.md)

```text
  § 1834. Inconsistent provisions of other acts superseded. Insofar as
the provisions of this title are inconsistent with the provisions of any
other act, general or special, the provisions of this title shall be
controlling.
```
