# Public Authorities Law § 2799-III — City notice required

[Public Authorities Law](../../README.md) › [Article 8 — Miscellaneous Authorities](../README.md) › [Title 34 — Syracuse Regional Airport Authority](README.md)

```text
  § 2799-iii. City notice required. Notwithstanding any inconsistent
provision of this title, no project having an aggregate cost exceeding
ten million dollars, including but not limited to the acquisition of
real property by the authority or the expansion of the authority's
aviation facilities, may be undertaken by the authority unless the
authority has provided notice of such project to the Syracuse common
council and the mayor.
```
