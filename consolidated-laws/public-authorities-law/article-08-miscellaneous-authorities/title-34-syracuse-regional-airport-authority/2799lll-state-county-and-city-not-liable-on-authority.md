# Public Authorities Law § 2799-LLL — State, county, and city not liable on authority bonds

[Public Authorities Law](../../README.md) › [Article 8 — Miscellaneous Authorities](../README.md) › [Title 34 — Syracuse Regional Airport Authority](README.md)

```text
  § 2799-lll. State, county, and city not liable on authority bonds.
Neither the state, the county, nor the city shall be liable on the bonds
of the authority and such bonds shall not be a debt of the state, the
county, or the city, and such bonds shall contain on the face thereof a
statement to such effect.
```
