# Public Authorities Law § 1987 — Separability

[Public Authorities Law](../../README.md) › [Article 8 — Miscellaneous Authorities](../README.md) › [Title 12 — No title](README.md)

```text
  § 1987. Separability. If any section, clause or provision of this
title shall be held unconstitutional, or be ineffective in whole or in
part, to the extent that it is not unconstitutional or ineffective, it
shall be valid and effective and no other section, clause or provision
shall, on account thereof, be deemed invalid or ineffective.
```
