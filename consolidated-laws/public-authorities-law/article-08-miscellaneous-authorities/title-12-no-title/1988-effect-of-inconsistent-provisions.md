# Public Authorities Law § 1988 — Effect of inconsistent provisions

[Public Authorities Law](../../README.md) › [Article 8 — Miscellaneous Authorities](../README.md) › [Title 12 — No title](README.md)

```text
  § 1988. Effect of inconsistent provisions. In so far as the provisions
of this title are inconsistent with the provisions of any other act,
general or special, the provisions of this title shall be controlling.
```
