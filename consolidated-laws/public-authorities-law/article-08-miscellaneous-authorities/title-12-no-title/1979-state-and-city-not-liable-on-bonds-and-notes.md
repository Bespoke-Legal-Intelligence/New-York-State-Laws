# Public Authorities Law § 1979 — State and city not liable on bonds and notes

[Public Authorities Law](../../README.md) › [Article 8 — Miscellaneous Authorities](../README.md) › [Title 12 — No title](README.md)

```text
  § 1979. State and city not liable on bonds and notes. The bonds, notes
and other obligations of the authority shall not be a debt of the state
of New York or of the city, and neither the state nor the city shall be
liable thereon, nor shall they be payable out of any funds other than
those of the authority.
```
