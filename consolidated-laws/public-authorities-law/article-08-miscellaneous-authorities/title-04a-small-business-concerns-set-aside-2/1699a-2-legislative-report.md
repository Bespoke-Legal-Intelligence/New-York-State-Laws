# Public Authorities Law § 1699-A*2 — Legislative report

[Public Authorities Law](../../README.md) › [Article 8 — Miscellaneous Authorities](../README.md) › [Title 4-A* — Small Business Concerns Set-aside Purchases and Contracts For Property and Services At the Bronx Community College](README.md)

*An asterisk in a number (for example Article 24\* or § 20-C\*2) is the Senate's mark for a number the Legislature has used more than once: the marked article or section is a different one from the unmarked one with the same number. \*2 means the second one enacted with that number.*

```text
  * § 1699-a. Legislative report. The chairman shall annually before
December first, report in writing to the legislature concerning the
awarding of contracts, for specified commodities, equipment or services
or for the construction, reconstruction, rehabilitation or improvement
of college facilities at the Bronx community college and York college to
small businesses and minority businesses. The report shall include the
total value of awards made in the preceding fiscal year under the
designation of small business set-asides.
  * NB There are 2 § 1699-a's
```
