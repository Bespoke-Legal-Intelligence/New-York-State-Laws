# Public Authorities Law § 1354-A — Meetings and records of authority to be public

[Public Authorities Law](../../README.md) › [Article 6 — Port Authorities](../README.md) › [Title 2 — Port of Oswego Authority](README.md)

```text
  § 1354-a. Meetings and records of authority to be public. The regular
meetings of the authority shall be public meetings open to the general
public, except for executive sessions thereof. Subject to appropriate
provisions of law and any reasonable regulations established by the
authority, the records of the authority shall be public records.
```
