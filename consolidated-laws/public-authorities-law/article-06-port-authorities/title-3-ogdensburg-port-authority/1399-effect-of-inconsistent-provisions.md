# Public Authorities Law § 1399 — Effect of inconsistent provisions

[Public Authorities Law](../../README.md) › [Article 6 — Port Authorities](../README.md) › [Title 3 — Ogdensburg Port Authority](README.md)

```text
  § 1399. Effect of inconsistent provisions. In so far as the provisions
of this title are inconsistent with the provisions of any other law,
general, special or local, the provisions of this title shall be
controlling.
```
