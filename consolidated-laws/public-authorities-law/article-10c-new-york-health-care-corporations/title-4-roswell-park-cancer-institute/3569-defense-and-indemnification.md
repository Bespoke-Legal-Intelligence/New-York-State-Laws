# Public Authorities Law § 3569 — Defense and indemnification

[Public Authorities Law](../../README.md) › [Article 10-C — New York Health Care Corporations](../README.md) › [Title 4 — Roswell Park Cancer Institute Corporation Act](README.md)

```text
  § 3569. Defense and indemnification. The provisions of sections
seventeen and nineteen of the public officers law shall be applicable to
employees of the corporation (as such term is defined in sections
seventeen and nineteen of the public officers law) pursuant to
subdivision two of such section; provided, however, that nothing
contained within this section shall be deemed to permit the corporation
to extend the provisions of sections seventeen and nineteen of the
public officers law upon any independent contractor.
```
