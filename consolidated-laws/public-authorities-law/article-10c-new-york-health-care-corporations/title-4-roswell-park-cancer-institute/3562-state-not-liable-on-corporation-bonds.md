# Public Authorities Law § 3562 — State not liable on corporation bonds

[Public Authorities Law](../../README.md) › [Article 10-C — New York Health Care Corporations](../README.md) › [Title 4 — Roswell Park Cancer Institute Corporation Act](README.md)

```text
  § 3562. State not liable on corporation bonds.  The state shall not be
liable on the bonds or notes of the corporation and such bonds or notes
shall not be a debt of the state, and such bonds and notes shall contain
on the face thereof a statement to such effect.
```
