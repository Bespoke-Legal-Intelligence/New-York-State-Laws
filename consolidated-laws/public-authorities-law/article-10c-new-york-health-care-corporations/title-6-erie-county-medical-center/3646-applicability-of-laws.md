# Public Authorities Law § 3646 — Applicability of laws

[Public Authorities Law](../../README.md) › [Article 10-C — New York Health Care Corporations](../README.md) › [Title 6 — Erie County Medical Center Corporation](README.md)

```text
  § 3646. Applicability of laws. The provisions of this title shall be
subject to the provisions of the civil practice law and rules, the
public health law, the mental hygiene law, the social services law, the
education law, and any other applicable law or regulation, including any
amendment thereto; provided, however, that nothing in this section shall
require the county or corporation to seek approval or consent for any
transfer pursuant to sections thirty-six hundred thirty-two and
thirty-six hundred forty-four of this title.
```
