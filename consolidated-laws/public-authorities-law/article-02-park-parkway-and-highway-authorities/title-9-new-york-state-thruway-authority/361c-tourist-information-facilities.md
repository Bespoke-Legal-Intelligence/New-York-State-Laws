# Public Authorities Law § 361-C — Tourist information facilities

[Public Authorities Law](../../README.md) › [Article 2 — Park, Parkway and Highway Authorities](../README.md) › [Title 9 — New York State Thruway Authority](README.md)

```text
  § 361-c. Tourist information facilities. The authority shall provide
tourist information services at all service areas where food is offered
for sale, provided that: the department of commerce and or local and
regional tourist promotion agencies shall provide the authority with
ample supplies of such literature and brochures necessary for
distribution to the traveling public.
```
