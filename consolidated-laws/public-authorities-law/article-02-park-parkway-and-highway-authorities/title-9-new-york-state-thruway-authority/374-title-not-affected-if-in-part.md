# Public Authorities Law § 374 — Title not affected if in part unconstitutional

[Public Authorities Law](../../README.md) › [Article 2 — Park, Parkway and Highway Authorities](../README.md) › [Title 9 — New York State Thruway Authority](README.md)

```text
  § 374. Title not affected if in part unconstitutional.  If any
section, clause or provision of this title shall be unconstitutional or
be ineffective in whole or in part, to the extent that it is not
unconstitutional or ineffective, it shall be valid and effective and no
other section, clause or provision shall on account thereof be deemed
invalid or ineffective.
```
