# Public Authorities Law § 369 — Liability of state

[Public Authorities Law](../../README.md) › [Article 2 — Park, Parkway and Highway Authorities](../README.md) › [Title 9 — New York State Thruway Authority](README.md)

```text
  § 369. Liability of state. The state shall be liable on notes or bonds
guaranteed hereunder but shall not be liable on notes or bonds not
guaranteed by the state which shall not be a debt of the state.
```
