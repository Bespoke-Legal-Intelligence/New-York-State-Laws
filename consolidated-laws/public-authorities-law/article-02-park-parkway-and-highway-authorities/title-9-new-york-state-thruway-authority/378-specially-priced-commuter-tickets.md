# Public Authorities Law § 378 — Specially priced commuter tickets

[Public Authorities Law](../../README.md) › [Article 2 — Park, Parkway and Highway Authorities](../README.md) › [Title 9 — New York State Thruway Authority](README.md)

```text
  § 378. Specially priced commuter tickets. In the event that the
authority offers specially priced commuter tickets for use on its Grand
Island bridges, such tickets shall be made available for any vehicle
regularly operated by a resident of Grand Island, whether such vehicle
is owned by, leased to or loaned by an employer to such resident.
```
