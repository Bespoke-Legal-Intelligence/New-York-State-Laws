# Public Authorities Law § 207 — State not liable on bonds

[Public Authorities Law](../../README.md) › [Article 2 — Park, Parkway and Highway Authorities](../README.md) › [Title 4 — Bethpage Park Authority](README.md)

```text
  * § 207. State not liable on bonds. The bonds and other obligations of
the authority shall not be a debt of the state and the state shall not
be liable thereon, nor shall they be payable out of any funds other than
those of the authority.
  * NB (Abolished in 1975)
```
