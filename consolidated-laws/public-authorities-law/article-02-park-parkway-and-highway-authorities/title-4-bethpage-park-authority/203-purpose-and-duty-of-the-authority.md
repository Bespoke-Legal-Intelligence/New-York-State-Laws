# Public Authorities Law § 203 — Purpose and duty of the authority

[Public Authorities Law](../../README.md) › [Article 2 — Park, Parkway and Highway Authorities](../README.md) › [Title 4 — Bethpage Park Authority](README.md)

```text
  * § 203. Purpose and duty of the authority.  The purpose and duty of
the authority is to acquire land in the town of Oyster Bay, Nassau
county, and in the towns of Huntington and Babylon, Suffolk county, and
to develop, improve, embellish, maintain and operate such land as a
public park and public recreation center, and to charge fees for
admission thereto and the use thereof sufficient with other revenues to
meet its expenses and pay all debts contracted by it, and the authority
shall have power to do all things necessary or convenient to carry out
such purpose and duty in addition to the express powers conferred in
this title.
  * NB (Abolished in 1975)
```
