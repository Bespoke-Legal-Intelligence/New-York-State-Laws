# Public Authorities Law § 155 — Title to additional lands acquired

[Public Authorities Law](../../README.md) › [Article 2 — Park, Parkway and Highway Authorities](../README.md) › [Title 3 — Jones Beach State Parkway Authority](README.md)

```text
  * § 155. Title to additional lands acquired. Title to any additional
lands, easements or rights in land which may be hereafter acquired for
the purposes of this act shall be vested in the state of New York.
  * NB (Authority abolished June 30, 1978)
```
