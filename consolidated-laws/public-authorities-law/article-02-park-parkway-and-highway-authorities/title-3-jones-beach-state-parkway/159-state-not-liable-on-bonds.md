# Public Authorities Law § 159 — State not liable on bonds

[Public Authorities Law](../../README.md) › [Article 2 — Park, Parkway and Highway Authorities](../README.md) › [Title 3 — Jones Beach State Parkway Authority](README.md)

```text
  * § 159. State not liable on bonds. The bonds and other obligations of
the authority shall not be a debt of the state and the state shall not
be liable thereon nor shall they be payable out of any funds other than
those of the authority.
  * NB (Authority abolished June 30, 1978)
```
