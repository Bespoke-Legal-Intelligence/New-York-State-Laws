# Insurance Law § 1406 — Policy loans

[Insurance Law](../README.md) › [Article 14 — Investments](README.md)

```text
  § 1406. Policy loans. (a) Any life insurance company may lend to any
policyholder upon the security of the value of his policy a sum not
exceeding the legal reserve which it is required to maintain thereon.
  (b) A fraternal benefit society may make similar loans subject to the
provisions of article forty-five of this chapter.
```
