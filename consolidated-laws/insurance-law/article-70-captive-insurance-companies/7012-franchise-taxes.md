# Insurance Law § 7012 — Franchise taxes

[Insurance Law](../README.md) › [Article 70 — Captive Insurance Companies](README.md)

```text
  § 7012.  Franchise taxes.  Captive insurance companies licensed to do
a captive insurance business shall be liable for the payment of
franchise taxes in accordance with section fifteen hundred two-b of the
tax law.
```
