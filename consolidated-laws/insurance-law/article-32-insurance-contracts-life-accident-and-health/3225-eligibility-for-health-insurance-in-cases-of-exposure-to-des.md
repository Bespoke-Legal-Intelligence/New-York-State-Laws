# Insurance Law § 3225 — Eligibility for health insurance in cases of exposure to DES

[Insurance Law](../README.md) › [Article 32 — Insurance Contracts - Life, Accident and Health, Annuities](README.md)

```text
  § 3225. Eligibility for health insurance in cases of exposure to DES.
No policy of accident or health insurance, or group or blanket accident
or health insurance or renewals thereof shall be denied or cancelled by
the insurer, solely because the insured person has been exposed to
diethylstilbestrol, commonly referred to as DES.
```
