# Insurance Law § 7612 — Foreign and alien insurers

[Insurance Law](../README.md) › [Article 76 — Property/casualty Security Funds](README.md)

```text
  § 7612. Foreign and alien insurers. Every foreign or alien insurer
subject to this article is deemed to have consented to the adjudication
of all claims secured by this article in a proceeding under article
seventy-four of this chapter.
```
