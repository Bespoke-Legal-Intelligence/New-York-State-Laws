# Insurance Law § 2135 — Administration of certain functions

[Insurance Law](../README.md) › [Article 21 — Agents, Brokers, Adjusters, Consultants and Intermediaries](README.md)

```text
  § 2135. Administration of certain functions. The superintendent may
designate the National Association of Insurance Commissioners (NAIC) or
any affiliates or subsidiaries that the NAIC oversees to perform
ministerial functions, including the collection of fees, related to
producer licensing under this article that the superintendent may deem
appropriate.
```
