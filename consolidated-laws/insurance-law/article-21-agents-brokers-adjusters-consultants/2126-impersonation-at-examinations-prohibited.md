# Insurance Law § 2126 — Impersonation at examinations prohibited

[Insurance Law](../README.md) › [Article 21 — Agents, Brokers, Adjusters, Consultants and Intermediaries](README.md)

```text
  § 2126. Impersonation at examinations prohibited. No person shall
impersonate any other person, or permit or aid in any manner any other
person to impersonate him, in connection with any examination held in
accordance with the provisions of this chapter.
```
