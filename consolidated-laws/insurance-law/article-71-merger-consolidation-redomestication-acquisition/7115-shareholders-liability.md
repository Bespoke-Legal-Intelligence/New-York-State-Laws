# Insurance Law § 7115 — Shareholders' liability

[Insurance Law](../README.md) › [Article 71 — Merger, Consolidation, Redomestication, Acquisition of Assets and Acquisition of Certain Shares of Insurers](README.md)

```text
  § 7115. Shareholders' liability. The shareholders of a stock company
merging or consolidating shall continue subject to all the liabilities,
claims and demands existing against them as such at or before such
merger or consolidation.
```
