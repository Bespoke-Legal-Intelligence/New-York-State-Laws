# Insurance Law § 7111 — Proof of proceedings of meeting and action of company

[Insurance Law](../README.md) › [Article 71 — Merger, Consolidation, Redomestication, Acquisition of Assets and Acquisition of Certain Shares of Insurers](README.md)

```text
  § 7111. Proof of proceedings of meeting and action of company. A
certified copy of the proceedings of any meeting made by the secretary
thereof or of the action of any company made by the secretary of such
company, subscribed and affirmed by him as true under the penalties of
perjury, shall be presumptive evidence of the holding of the meeting and
the action of such meeting and the action of such company.
```
