# Insurance Law § 5604 — Arbitration forms, brochures, and materials

[Insurance Law](../README.md) › [Article 56 — Health Care Arbitrations](README.md)

```text
  § 5604. Arbitration forms, brochures, and materials. The arbitration
administrator shall submit to the superintendent, for review and
approval, required forms of agreement and explanatory brochures and
materials to be used for the arbitrations authorized by section
forty-four hundred six-a of the public health law and article
seventy-five-A of the civil practice law and rules.
```
