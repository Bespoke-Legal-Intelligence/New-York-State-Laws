# Insurance Law § 6708 — Rules and regulations

[Insurance Law](../README.md) › [Article 67 — Nonprofit Property/casualty Insurance Companies](README.md)

```text
  § 6708. Rules and regulations. The superintendent shall promulgate
such rules and regulations as may be necessary for the implementation of
this article. Such regulation may be necessary in light of the nonprofit
property/casualty insurance company being owned by nonprofit
organizations that it insures, including the proprietary nature of the
information relating to the nonprofit property/casualty insurance
company's operations.
```
