# Insurance Law § 2207 — Transfer

[Insurance Law](../README.md) › [Article 22 — Certification of Employees of Insurers and Savings Banks Offering Life Insurance](README.md)

```text
  § 2207. Transfer. The superintendent is authorized to transfer the
certification of any person issued pursuant to this article from one
insurer or savings bank to another insurer or savings bank, without
examination.
```
