# Insurance Law § 4314 — Not to affect provisions of workers' compensation law

[Insurance Law](../README.md) › [Article 43 — Non-profit Medical and Dental Indemnity, or Health and Hospital Service Corporations](README.md)

```text
  § 4314. Not to affect provisions of workers' compensation law. No
provisions of this article or any contract issued by a corporation
subject to the provisions of this article shall in any way affect the
operation of the workers' compensation law.
```
