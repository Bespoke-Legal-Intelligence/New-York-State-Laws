# Insurance Law § 5205 — Liability of directors and members

[Insurance Law](../README.md) › [Article 52 — Motor Vehicle Accident Indemnification Corporation](README.md)

```text
  § 5205. Liability of directors and members. (a) No director shall be
individually liable for anything done or any liability incurred or
assumed by virtue of this article and any such liability shall be
collectible only from the corporation.
  (b) No member shall be subject to any liability except for unpaid
assessments.
```
