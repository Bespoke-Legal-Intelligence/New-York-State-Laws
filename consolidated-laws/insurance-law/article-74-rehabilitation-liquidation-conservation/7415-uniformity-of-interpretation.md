# Insurance Law § 7415 — Uniformity of interpretation

[Insurance Law](../README.md) › [Article 74 — Rehabilitation, Liquidation, Conservation and Dissolution of Insurers](README.md)

```text
  § 7415. Uniformity of interpretation. The uniform insurers liquidation
act shall be interpreted and construed to effectuate its general purpose
to make uniform the law of those states that enact it. To the extent
that its provisions, when applicable, conflict with other provisions of
this chapter, the provisions of this act shall control.
```
