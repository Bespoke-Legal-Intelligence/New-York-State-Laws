# Insurance Law § 2607 — Discrimination because of sex or marital status

[Insurance Law](../README.md) › [Article 26 — Unfair Claim Settlement Practices; Other Misconduct; Discrimination](README.md)

```text
  § 2607. Discrimination because of sex or marital status. No individual
or entity shall refuse to issue any policy of insurance, or cancel or
decline to renew such policy because of the sex or marital status of the
applicant or policyholder.
```
