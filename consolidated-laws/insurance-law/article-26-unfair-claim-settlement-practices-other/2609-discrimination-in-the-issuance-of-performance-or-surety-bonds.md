# Insurance Law § 2609 — Discrimination in the issuance of performance or surety bonds

[Insurance Law](../README.md) › [Article 26 — Unfair Claim Settlement Practices; Other Misconduct; Discrimination](README.md)

```text
  § 2609. Discrimination in the issuance of performance or surety bonds.
No person, firm or corporation engaged in the business of issuing
performance or surety bonds shall refuse to issue such a bond to any
person, firm or corporation, solely because of the race, creed, color,
sex, national origin, age or marital status of the applicant.
```
