# Insurance Law § 6508 — Additional powers of superintendent

[Insurance Law](../README.md) › [Article 65 — Mortgage Guaranty Insurance Companies](README.md)

```text
  § 6508. Additional powers of superintendent. The superintendent may
adopt regulations necessary to carry out this article including
restrictions on investments, giving full consideration to the
desirability of high liquidity.
```
