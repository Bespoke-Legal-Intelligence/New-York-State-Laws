# Insurance Law § 7715 — Tax exemptions

[Insurance Law](../README.md) › [Article 77 — The Life Insurance Company Guaranty Corporation of New York Act](README.md)

```text
  § 7715. Tax exemptions. The corporation shall be exempt from payment
of all fees and all taxes levied by this state or any of its
subdivisions, except taxes levied on real property.
```
