# Insurance Law § 6908 — Applicability of other laws

[Insurance Law](../README.md) › [Article 69 — Financial Guaranty Insurance Corporations](README.md)

```text
  § 6908. Applicability of other laws. An insurer issuing policies of
financial guaranty insurance shall be subject to all of the provisions
of this chapter applicable to property/casualty insurers to the extent
that such provisions are not inconsistent with the provisions of this
article.
```
