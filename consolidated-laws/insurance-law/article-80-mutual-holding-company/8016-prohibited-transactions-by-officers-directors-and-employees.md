# Insurance Law § 8016 — Prohibited transactions by officers, directors and employees

[Insurance Law](../README.md) › [Article 80 — Mutual Holding Company](README.md)

```text
  § 8016. Prohibited transactions by officers, directors and employees.
No director, officer, agent or employee of the reorganizing insurer
shall receive any fee, commission or other valuable consideration
whatsoever, other than regular salary and compensation, for in any
manner aiding, promoting or assisting in the reorganization except as
set forth in the plan approved by the superintendent.
```
