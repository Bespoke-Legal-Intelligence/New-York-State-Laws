# Insurance Law § 3108 — Reinsurance contracts excepted

[Insurance Law](../README.md) › [Article 31 — Insurance Contracts - General](README.md)

```text
  § 3108. Reinsurance contracts excepted. The provisions of this article
shall not apply to contracts of reinsurance, except as otherwise
provided by law.
```
