# Insurance Law § 6304 — Special regulations

[Insurance Law](../README.md) › [Article 63 — Special Risks; Filing Exemption](README.md)

```text
  § 6304. Special regulations. The superintendent, by regulation, shall
provide for the implementation of the provisions of this article by
establishing methods, procedures and reports for licensing and for
facilitating, monitoring and verifying compliance with this article.
```
