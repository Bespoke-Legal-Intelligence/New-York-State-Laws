# Insurance Law § 7714 — Examination of the corporation; annual report

[Insurance Law](../README.md) › [Article 77 — The Life and Health Insurance Company Guaranty Corporation of New York Act](README.md)

```text
  § 7714. Examination of the corporation; annual report. The corporation
shall be subject to examination and regulation by the superintendent.
The corporation shall submit to the superintendent, not later than May
first of each year, a financial report for the preceding calendar year
in a form approved by the superintendent and a report of its activities
during the preceding calendar year.
```
