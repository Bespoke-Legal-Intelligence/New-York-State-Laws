# Insurance Law § 102 — Declaration

[Insurance Law](../README.md) › [Article 1 — General Provisions](README.md)

```text
  § 102. Declaration. The legislature hereby declares that the purpose
of this chapter is to recodify, without substantive change, the
insurance law in effect immediately prior to the effective date of this
chapter.
```
