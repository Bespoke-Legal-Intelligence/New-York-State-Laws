# Insurance Law § 2911 — Change of address

[Insurance Law](../README.md) › [Article 29 — Pharmacy Benefit Managers](README.md)

```text
  * § 2911. Change of address. A registrant or licensee under this
article shall inform the superintendent by a means acceptable to the
superintendent of a change of address within thirty days of the change.
  * NB Effective March 31, 2022
```
