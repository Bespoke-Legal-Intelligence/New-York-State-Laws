# Insurance Law § 2901 — Definitions

[Insurance Law](../README.md) › [Article 29 — Pharmacy Benefit Managers](README.md)

```text
  § 2901. Definitions. For purposes of this article:
  (a) "Controlling person" is any person or other entity who or which
directly or indirectly has the power to direct or cause to be directed
the management, control or activities of a pharmacy benefit manager.
  (b) The terms "covered individual", "health plan ", "pharmacy benefit
manager" and "pharmacy benefit management services" have the same
meanings as defined by section two hundred eighty-a of the public health
law. The superintendent is expressly authorized to interpret these terms
as if the definitions were stated within this article.
```
