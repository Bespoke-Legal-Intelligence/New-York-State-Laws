# Insurance Law § 6612 — Rebating and discrimination; accounts and statistics; assessment corporations

[Insurance Law](../README.md) › [Article 66 — Co-operative Property/casualty Insurance Companies](README.md)

```text
  § 6612. Rebating and discrimination; accounts and statistics;
assessment corporations. Notwithstanding anything to the contrary in
this chapter, the provisions of sections two thousand three hundred
twenty-four and two thousand three hundred twenty-five of this chapter
are applicable to assessment corporations.
```
