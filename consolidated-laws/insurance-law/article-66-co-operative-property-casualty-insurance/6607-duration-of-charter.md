# Insurance Law § 6607 — Duration of charter

[Insurance Law](../README.md) › [Article 66 — Co-operative Property/casualty Insurance Companies](README.md)

```text
  § 6607. Duration of charter. Subject to the provisions of article
seventy-four of this chapter, the charter of every co-operative
property/casualty insurance company shall be perpetual.
```
