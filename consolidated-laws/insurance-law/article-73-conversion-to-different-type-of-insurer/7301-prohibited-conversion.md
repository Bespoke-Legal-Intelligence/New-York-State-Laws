# Insurance Law § 7301 — Prohibited conversion

[Insurance Law](../README.md) › [Article 73 — Conversion to Different Type of Insurer](README.md)

```text
  § 7301. Prohibited conversion. No insurer organized or licensed under
this chapter shall convert to a different type of insurer except as
provided in this article.
```
