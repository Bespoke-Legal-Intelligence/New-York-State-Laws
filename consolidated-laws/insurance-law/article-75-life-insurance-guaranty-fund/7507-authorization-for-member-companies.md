# Insurance Law § 7507 — Authorization for member companies

[Insurance Law](../README.md) › [Article 75 — Life Insurance Guaranty Fund](README.md)

```text
  § 7507. Authorization for member companies. Each member company and
its officers, directors and agents shall have authority to do and permit
to be done any act and to make any payment herein authorized or
contemplated.
```
