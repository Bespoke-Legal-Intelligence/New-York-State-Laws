# Insurance Law § 3402 — Executory contract not a change in interest, title or possession

[Insurance Law](../README.md) › [Article 34 — Insurance Contracts-property/casualty](README.md)

```text
  § 3402. Executory contract not a change in interest, title or
possession. The making of a contract to sell or to exchange real
property shall not constitute a change in the interest, title or
possession, within the meaning of the applicable provisions of any
contract of fire insurance, including any contract supplemental thereto,
covering property located in this state.
```
