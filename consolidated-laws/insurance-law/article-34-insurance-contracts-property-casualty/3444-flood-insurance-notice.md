# Insurance Law § 3444 — Flood insurance notice

[Insurance Law](../README.md) › [Article 34 — Insurance Contracts-property/casualty](README.md)

```text
  § 3444. Flood insurance notice. 1. Insurers shall provide to their
homeowners and dwelling fire personal lines insureds an annual notice
prescribed or approved by the superintendent, explaining clearly and in
plain language that: (a) such policies do not provide coverage for loss
caused by mudslide or flood; and (b) insurance is available under
separate policies issued in accordance with the National Flood Insurance
Program, 42 U.S.C. § 4001 et seq., including information regarding flood
insurance eligibility and access.
  2. The policyholder notice required by this section shall accompany
all new homeowners and dwelling fire personal lines policies and all
renewal homeowners and dwelling fire policies.
```
