# Insurance Law § 3456 — Prohibition against cancellation of policy when motor vehicle is used or operated through a transportation network company program

[Insurance Law](../README.md) › [Article 34 — Insurance Contracts-property/casualty](README.md)

```text
  * § 3456. Prohibition against cancellation of policy when motor
vehicle is used or operated through a transportation network company
program.  (a) An insurer shall not cancel an existing motor vehicle
insurance policy solely on the basis that the motor vehicle covered by
the insurance has been made available pursuant to a transportation
network company program in compliance with article forty-four-B of the
vehicle and traffic law.
  (b) The definitions set forth in section three thousand four hundred
fifty-five of this article shall apply to this section.
  * NB Effective June 29, 2017
```
