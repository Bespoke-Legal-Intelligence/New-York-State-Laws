# Insurance Law § 3439 — Reinsurance contracts excepted

[Insurance Law](../README.md) › [Article 34 — Insurance Contracts-property/casualty](README.md)

```text
  § 3439. Reinsurance contracts excepted. The provisions of this article
shall not apply to contracts of reinsurance, except as otherwise
provided by law.
```
