# Insurance Law § 3461 — Notice of claim

[Insurance Law](../README.md) › [Article 34 — Insurance Contracts-property/casualty](README.md)

```text
  * § 3461. Notice of claim. (a) For purposes of article fifty-one of
this chapter, 11 NYCRR Part 65 and general liability claims, notice of
claim to the shared vehicle owner, shared vehicle driver, peer-to-peer
car sharing program or any insurer of the shared vehicle owner, shared
vehicle driver, or peer-to-peer car sharing program shall be deemed
notice to all appropriate parties and insurers.
  (b) Any shared vehicle owner, shared vehicle driver, peer-to-peer car
sharing program or any insurer of the shared vehicle owner, shared
vehicle driver, or peer-to-peer car sharing program receiving such
notice shall provide such notice to all appropriate parties.
  * NB Effective June 20, 2022
```
