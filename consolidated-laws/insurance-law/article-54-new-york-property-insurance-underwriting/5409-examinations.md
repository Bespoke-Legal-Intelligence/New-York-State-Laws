# Insurance Law § 5409 — Examinations

[Insurance Law](../README.md) › [Article 54 — New York Property Insurance Underwriting Association](README.md)

```text
  § 5409. Examinations. The superintendent may, in accordance with
article three of this chapter, make an examination into the affairs of
the association whenever he deems it expedient. The expenses of every
such examination shall be borne and paid by the association in the
manner prescribed by section three hundred thirteen of this chapter.
```
