# Insurance Law § 322 — Prohibited referral payment to licensees by motor vehicle repairers

[Insurance Law](../README.md) › [Article 3 — Administrative and Procedural Provisions](README.md)

```text
  § 322. Prohibited referral payment to licensees by motor vehicle
repairers. No licensed insurance agent, licensed insurance broker,
licensed adjuster, authorized insurer or representative of such insurer
shall directly or indirectly request, procure or accept any payment from
a motor vehicle repairer for referring any motor vehicle repair business
to such repairer.
```
