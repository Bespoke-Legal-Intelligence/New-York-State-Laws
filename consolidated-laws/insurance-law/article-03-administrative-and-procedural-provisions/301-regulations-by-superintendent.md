# Insurance Law § 301 — Regulations by superintendent

[Insurance Law](../README.md) › [Article 3 — Administrative and Procedural Provisions](README.md)

```text
  § 301. Regulations by superintendent. The superintendent shall have
the power to prescribe and from time to time withdraw or amend, in
writing, regulations, not inconsistent with the provisions of this
chapter:
  (a) governing the duties assigned to the members of the staff of the
department;
  (b) effectuating any power, given to him under the provisions of this
chapter to prescribe forms or otherwise make regulations;
  (c) interpreting the provisions of this chapter; and
  (d) governing the procedures to be followed in the practice of the
department.
```
