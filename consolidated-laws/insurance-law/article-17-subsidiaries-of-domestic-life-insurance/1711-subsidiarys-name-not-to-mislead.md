# Insurance Law § 1711 — Subsidiary's name not to mislead

[Insurance Law](../README.md) › [Article 17 — Subsidiaries of Domestic Life Insurance Companies and Certain Other Entities](README.md)

```text
  § 1711. Subsidiary's name not to mislead. The name of any subsidiary
shall not be such as to mislead or deceive the public.
```
