# Insurance Law § 1602 — Minimum ownership of subsidiaries' shares

[Insurance Law](../README.md) › [Article 16 — Subsidiaries of Domestic Property/casualty Insurance Companies and Certain Other Entities](README.md)

```text
  § 1602. Minimum ownership of subsidiaries' shares.  A domestic insurer
shall own not less than fifty-one percent of the issued and outstanding
voting shares of each of its subsidiaries.
```
