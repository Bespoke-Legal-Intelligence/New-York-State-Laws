# Insurance Law § 4907 — Rights and remedies

[Insurance Law](../../README.md) › [Article 49 — Utilization Review and External Appeal](../README.md) › [Title 1 — Registration of Agents and Review Process](README.md)

```text
  § 4907. Rights and remedies. The rights and remedies conferred in this
article upon insureds and health care providers shall be cumulative and
in addition to and not in lieu of any other rights or remedies available
under law.
```
