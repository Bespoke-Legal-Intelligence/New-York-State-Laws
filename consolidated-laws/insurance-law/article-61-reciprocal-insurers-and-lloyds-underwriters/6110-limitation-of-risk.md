# Insurance Law § 6110 — Limitation of risk

[Insurance Law](../README.md) › [Article 61 — Reciprocal Insurers and Lloyds Underwriters](README.md)

```text
  § 6110. Limitation of risk. Every reciprocal insurer authorized to do
business in this state shall be subject to the provisions of sections
one thousand one hundred fifteen and four thousand one hundred eighteen
of this chapter.
```
