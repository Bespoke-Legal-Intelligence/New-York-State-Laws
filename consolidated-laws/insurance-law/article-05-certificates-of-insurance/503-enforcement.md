# Insurance Law § 503 — Enforcement

[Insurance Law](../README.md) › [Article 5 — Certificates of Insurance](README.md)

```text
  § 503. Enforcement. If the superintendent finds, after notice and
hearing, that any person, other than a governmental entity, has wilfully
violated this article, then the superintendent may order the person to
pay to the people of this state a penalty in a sum of one thousand
dollars for the first violation and two thousand dollars for each
subsequent violation.
```
