# Insurance Law § 2336-A — Snowmobile liability insurance rates; premium reductions in certain cases

[Insurance Law](../README.md) › [Article 23 — Property/casualty Insurance Rates](README.md)

```text
  § 2336-a. Snowmobile liability insurance rates; premium reductions in
certain cases. Any schedule of rates or rating plan for snowmobile
liability insurance submitted to the superintendent may provide for an
actuarially appropriate reduction in premium charges for any insured who
has successfully completed a snowmobile safety course which has been
approved by the commissioner of parks, recreation and historic
preservation.
```
