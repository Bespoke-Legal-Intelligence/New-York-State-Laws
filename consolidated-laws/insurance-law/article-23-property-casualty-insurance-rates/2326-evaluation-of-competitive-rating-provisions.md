# Insurance Law § 2326 — Evaluation of competitive rating provisions

[Insurance Law](../README.md) › [Article 23 — Property/casualty Insurance Rates](README.md)

```text
  * § 2326. Evaluation of competitive rating provisions. The
superintendent shall continuously monitor the operation of the
provisions of this article which are applicable to the kinds of
insurance or insurance activities the rates for which, pursuant to
subsection (a) of section two thousand three hundred five of this
article, are not subject to prior approval.
  * NB Expires July 1, 2026
```
