# Insurance Law § 2702 — Holocaust claims assistance

[Insurance Law](../README.md) › [Article 27 — Holocaust Victims Insurance Act of 1998](README.md)

```text
  § 2702.  Holocaust claims assistance. The superintendent shall arrange
for a toll-free telephone number available in English as well as other
appropriate languages, to assist any person seeking to recover proceeds
from an insurance policy issued to or covering the life or property of a
Holocaust victim.
```
