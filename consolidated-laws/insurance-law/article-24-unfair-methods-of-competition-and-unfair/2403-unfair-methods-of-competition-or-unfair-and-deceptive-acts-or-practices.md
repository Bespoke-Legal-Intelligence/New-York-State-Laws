# Insurance Law § 2403 — Unfair methods of competition or unfair and deceptive acts or practices prohibited

[Insurance Law](../README.md) › [Article 24 — Unfair Methods of Competition and Unfair and Deceptive Acts and Practices](README.md)

```text
  § 2403. Unfair methods of competition or unfair and deceptive acts or
practices prohibited. No person shall engage in this state in any trade
practice constituting a defined violation or a determined violation as
defined herein.
```
