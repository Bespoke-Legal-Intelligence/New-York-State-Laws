# State Technology Law § 309 — Use of electronic records and signatures to be voluntary

[State Technology Law](../README.md) › [Article 3 — Electronic Signatures and Records Act](README.md)

```text
  § 309. Use of electronic records and signatures to be voluntary.
Nothing in this article shall require any entity or person to use an
electronic record or an electronic signature unless otherwise provided
by law.
```
