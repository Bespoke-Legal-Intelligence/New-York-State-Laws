# Economic Development Law § 171 — Purpose of the council

[Economic Development Law](../README.md) › [Article 5-B — New York State Tourism Advisory Council](README.md)

```text
  § 171. Purpose of the council. The purposes of the council shall be to
advise the commissioner concerning tourism and to serve as liaison
between the state's tourism-related industries and the commissioner with
respect to the design and implementation of the state's tourism policies
and programs.
```
