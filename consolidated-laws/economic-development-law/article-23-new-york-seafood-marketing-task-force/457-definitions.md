# Economic Development Law § 457 — Definitions

[Economic Development Law](../README.md) › [Article 23 — New York Seafood Marketing Task Force](README.md)

```text
  § 457. Definitions. When used in this article, the following terms
shall have the following meanings:
  1. "Task force" means the New York seafood marketing task force
established under section four hundred fifty-eight of this article.
  2. "State agencies" means state entities responsible for the
implementation of New York's fishery management and economic
development, including, but not be limited to:
  (a) the department;
  (b) the department of agriculture and markets;
  (c) the department of health;
  (d) the department of environmental conservation; and
  (e) the department of state.
```
