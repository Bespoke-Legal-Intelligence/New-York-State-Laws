# Economic Development Law § 357 — Maintenance of records

[Economic Development Law](../README.md) › [Article 17 — Excelsior Jobs Program Act](README.md)

```text
  § 357. Maintenance of records. Each participant shall keep all
relevant records for their duration of program participation plus three
years.
```
