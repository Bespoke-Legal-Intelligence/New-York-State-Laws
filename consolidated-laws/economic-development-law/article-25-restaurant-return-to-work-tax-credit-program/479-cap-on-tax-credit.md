# Economic Development Law § 479 — Cap on tax credit

[Economic Development Law](../README.md) › [Article 25 — Restaurant Return-to-work Tax Credit Program](README.md)

```text
  § 479. Cap on tax credit. The total amount of tax credits listed on
certificates of tax credit issued by the commissioner pursuant to this
article may not exceed thirty-five million dollars.
```
