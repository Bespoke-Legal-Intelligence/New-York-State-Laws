# Economic Development Law § 131 — Definition of a small business

[Economic Development Law](../README.md) › [Article 4-B — Division For Small-business](README.md)

```text
  § 131. Definition of a small business. For the purposes of this
chapter, a small business shall be deemed to be one which is resident in
this state, independently owned and operated, not dominant in its field
and employs one hundred or less persons.
```
