# Economic Development Law § 195 — Reporting

[Economic Development Law](../README.md) › [Article 7 — Industrial Effectiveness Program](README.md)

```text
  § 195. Reporting. The commissioner shall, on or before October first,
nineteen hundred eighty-eight and on or before each October first
thereafter, submit a report to the governor and the legislature on the
operation and accomplishments of the industrial effectiveness program.
```
