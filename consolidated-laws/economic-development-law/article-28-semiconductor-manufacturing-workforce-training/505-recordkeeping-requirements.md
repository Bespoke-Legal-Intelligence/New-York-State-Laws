# Economic Development Law § 505 — Recordkeeping requirements

[Economic Development Law](../README.md) › [Article 28 — Semiconductor Manufacturing Workforce Training Incentive Program](README.md)

```text
  § 505. Recordkeeping requirements. Each business entity participating
in the program shall maintain all relevant records for the duration of
its program participation plus three years.
```
