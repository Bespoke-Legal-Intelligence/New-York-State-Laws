# Economic Development Law § 427 — Maintenance of records

[Economic Development Law](../README.md) › [Article 20 — Empire State Jobs Retention Program](README.md)

```text
  § 427. Maintenance of records. Each participant shall keep all
relevant records for the duration of its program participation plus
three years.
```
