# Economic Development Law § 181-F — Definitions

[Economic Development Law](../README.md) › [Article 5-F — Senior Tourism Program](README.md)

```text
  * § 181-f. Definitions. For the purposes of this article the following
terms shall have the following meanings:
  1. "Senior" shall mean persons aged sixty-five and older;
  2. "Senior friendly attraction" shall mean a tourism attraction that
optimizes opportunities for seniors in order to enhance their quality of
life.
  * NB Effective March 18, 2018
```
