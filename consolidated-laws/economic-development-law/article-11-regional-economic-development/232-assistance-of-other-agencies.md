# Economic Development Law § 232 — Assistance of other agencies

[Economic Development Law](../README.md) › [Article 11 — Regional Economic Development](README.md)

```text
  § 232. Assistance of other agencies. To effectuate the purposes of
this article, the commissioner may request from any department,
division, board, bureau, commission or other agency of the state or from
any public benefit corporation or public authority, and the same are
authorized to provide, such assistance, services and data as will enable
the department to properly carry out its functions, powers and duties
hereunder.
```
