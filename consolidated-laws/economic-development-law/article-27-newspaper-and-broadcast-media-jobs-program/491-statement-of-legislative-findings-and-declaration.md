# Economic Development Law § 491 — Statement of legislative findings and declaration

[Economic Development Law](../README.md) › [Article 27 — Newspaper and Broadcast Media Jobs Program](README.md)

```text
  § 491. Statement of legislative findings and declaration. It is hereby
found and declared that New York state needs, as a matter of public
policy, to provide financial support and incentives for businesses which
operate as newspaper and broadcast media, to sustain a productive and
effective industry.
```
