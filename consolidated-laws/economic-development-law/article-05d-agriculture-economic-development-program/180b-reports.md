# Economic Development Law § 180-B — Reports

[Economic Development Law](../README.md) › [Article 5-D — Agriculture Economic Development Program](README.md)

```text
  § 180-b. Reports. The commissioner shall make an annual report on or
before March thirty-first of each year to the governor and the
legislature setting forth the activities undertaken by the program.
```
