# Economic Development Law § 346 — Additional insureds

[Economic Development Law](../README.md) › [Article 16 — Olympic Games Facilitation Act](README.md)

```text
  § 346. Additional insureds. The local organizing committee shall list
the state and the endorsing municipality as additional insureds on any
policy of insurance purchased by the local organizing committee to be in
effect in connection with the preparation for and conduct of the olympic
games.
```
