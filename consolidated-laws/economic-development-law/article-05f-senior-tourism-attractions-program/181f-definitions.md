# Economic Development Law § 181-F — Definitions

[Economic Development Law](../README.md) › [Article 5-F — Senior Tourism Attractions Program](README.md)

```text
  § 181-f. Definitions. For the purposes of this article the following
terms shall have the following meanings:
  1. "Senior" shall mean persons of at least sixty years or older;
  2. "Senior tourism attraction" shall mean a tourism attraction as
identified by a participatory organization that specifies opportunities
for seniors.
```
