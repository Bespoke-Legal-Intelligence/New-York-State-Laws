# Vehicle and Traffic Law § 500 — Application of this title

[Vehicle and Traffic Law](../../README.md) › [Title 5 — Drivers' Licenses](../README.md) › [Article 18 — Application of This Title](README.md)

```text
  § 500. Application of this title. Except as otherwise expressly
provided in this chapter, this title shall be exclusively controlling:
  a. Upon the licensing and regulation of drivers; and
  b. On their use of public highways.
```
