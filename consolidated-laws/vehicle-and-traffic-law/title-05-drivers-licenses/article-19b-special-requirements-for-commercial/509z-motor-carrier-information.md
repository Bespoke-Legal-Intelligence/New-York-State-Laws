# Vehicle and Traffic Law § 509-Z — Motor carrier information

[Vehicle and Traffic Law](../../README.md) › [Title 5 — Drivers' Licenses](../README.md) › [Article 19-B — Special Requirements For Commercial Motor Carriers](README.md)

```text
  * § 509-z. Motor carrier information. The commissioner of
transportation, in consultation with the commissioner, shall establish
requirements for any person or entity that owns and operates one or more
altered motor vehicles, commonly referred to as "stretch limousines",
for purposes of establishing conspicuous display of valid operating
authority, inspection information, and driver qualifications at
locations where such persons or entities perform contract or common
carrier services with altered motor vehicles and within any altered
motor vehicle used to provide such services.
  * NB Effective May 12, 2019
```
