# Vehicle and Traffic Law § 509-R — Investigations and inquiries

[Vehicle and Traffic Law](../../README.md) › [Title 5 — Drivers' Licenses](../README.md) › [Article 19-B — Special Requirements For Commercial Motor Carriers](README.md)

```text
  § 509-r. Investigations and inquiries. Every commercial motor carrier
shall make an investigation and inquiry of each commercial driver it
hires on and after the effective date of this article in accordance with
rules and regulations of the commissioner of transportation.
```
