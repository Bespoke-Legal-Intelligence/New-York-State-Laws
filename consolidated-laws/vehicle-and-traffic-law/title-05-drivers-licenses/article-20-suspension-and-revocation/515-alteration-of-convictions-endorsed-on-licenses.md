# Vehicle and Traffic Law § 515 — Alteration of convictions endorsed on licenses

[Vehicle and Traffic Law](../../README.md) › [Title 5 — Drivers' Licenses](../README.md) › [Article 20 — Suspension and Revocation](README.md)

```text
  § 515. Alteration of convictions endorsed on licenses.  No person
shall wilfully erase or otherwise make illegible, the record of a
conviction which has been endorsed on a license pursuant to the
provisions of section five hundred fourteen.
```
