# Vehicle and Traffic Law § 509-O — Penalties

[Vehicle and Traffic Law](../../README.md) › [Title 5 — Drivers' Licenses](../README.md) › [Article 19-A — Special Requirements For Bus Drivers](README.md)

```text
  § 509-o. Penalties. Upon conviction for the violation of any provision
of this article the court shall impose a sentence consisting of a fine
of not less than one hundred dollars nor more than two hundred fifty
dollars.
```
