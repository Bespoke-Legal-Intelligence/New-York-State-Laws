# Vehicle and Traffic Law § 545 — Immunity

[Vehicle and Traffic Law](../../README.md) › [Title 5 — Drivers' Licenses](../README.md) › [Article 21-B — Medical Advisory Board](README.md)

```text
  § 545. Immunity. The medical advisory board and its members shall not
be held liable in any action or proceeding on account of any
recommendation or report submitted to the commissioner pursuant to this
article.
```
