# Vehicle and Traffic Law § 550 — Recertification

[Vehicle and Traffic Law](../../README.md) › [Title 5 — Drivers' Licenses](../README.md) › [Article 21-C — Certificates For Escort Vehicles](README.md)

```text
  § 550. Recertification. A person who has had his or her escort
certificate cancelled shall not be eligible to reapply for an escort
certificate for a minimum period of six months from the date of such
cancellation.
```
