# Vehicle and Traffic Law § 547 — Period of validity of escort certificates

[Vehicle and Traffic Law](../../README.md) › [Title 5 — Drivers' Licenses](../README.md) › [Article 21-C — Certificates For Escort Vehicles](README.md)

```text
  § 547. Period of validity of escort certificates.  An escort
certificate shall be valid:  (a) for a New York state licensee from its
date of issuance until the expiration of such person's driver's license,
and (b) for non-residents, a four year period from its date of issuance.
```
