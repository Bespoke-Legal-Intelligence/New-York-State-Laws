# Vehicle and Traffic Law § 210 — Highway safety powers and duties

[Vehicle and Traffic Law](../../README.md) › [Title 2 — Department of Motor Vehicles; Commissioner](../README.md) › [Article 2 — Department of Motor Vehicles; Commissioner](README.md)

```text
  § 210. Highway safety powers and duties. The commissioner of motor
vehicles shall have power to:
  1. Formulate uniform highway safety programs and coordinate the
efforts of all interested individuals and agencies engaged in
safety-education.
  2. Conduct a highway safety and driver training program, in
conjunction with any interested public or private agencies, as an aid to
the national defense.
```
