# Vehicle and Traffic Law § 211 — Continuity of authority

[Vehicle and Traffic Law](../../README.md) › [Title 2 — Department of Motor Vehicles; Commissioner](../README.md) › [Article 2 — Department of Motor Vehicles; Commissioner](README.md)

```text
  § 211. Continuity of authority. The department of motor vehicles shall
be deemed to constitute a continuation of the powers and duties of the
bureau of motor vehicles in the department of taxation and finance and
not a new authority for the purpose of succession to the rights, powers
and duties and obligations hereby assigned to the department.
```
