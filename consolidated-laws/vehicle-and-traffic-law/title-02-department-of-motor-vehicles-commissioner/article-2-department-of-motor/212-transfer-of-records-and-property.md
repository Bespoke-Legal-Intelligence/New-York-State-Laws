# Vehicle and Traffic Law § 212 — Transfer of records and property

[Vehicle and Traffic Law](../../README.md) › [Title 2 — Department of Motor Vehicles; Commissioner](../README.md) › [Article 2 — Department of Motor Vehicles; Commissioner](README.md)

```text
  § 212. Transfer of records and property. All books, papers and records
of the bureau of motor vehicles in the department of taxation and
finance shall be transmitted to the department of motor vehicles and the
commissioner shall take possession thereof.
```
