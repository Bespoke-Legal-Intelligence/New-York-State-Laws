# Vehicle and Traffic Law § 213 — Terms occurring in contracts and other documents

[Vehicle and Traffic Law](../../README.md) › [Title 2 — Department of Motor Vehicles; Commissioner](../README.md) › [Article 2 — Department of Motor Vehicles; Commissioner](README.md)

```text
  § 213. Terms occurring in contracts and other documents.  Whenever the
bureau of motor vehicles in the department of taxation and finance is
referred to or designated in any contract or document, such reference or
designation shall be deemed to mean and refer to the department of motor
vehicles.
```
