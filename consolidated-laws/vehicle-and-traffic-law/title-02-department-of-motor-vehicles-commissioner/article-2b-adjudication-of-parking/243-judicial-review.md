# Vehicle and Traffic Law § 243 — Judicial review

[Vehicle and Traffic Law](../../README.md) › [Title 2 — Department of Motor Vehicles; Commissioner](../README.md) › [Article 2-B — Adjudication of Parking Infractions](README.md)

```text
  § 243. Judicial review. The order of the appeals board shall be the
final determination of the bureau. Judicial review may be sought
pursuant to article seventy-eight of the civil practice law and rules.
```
