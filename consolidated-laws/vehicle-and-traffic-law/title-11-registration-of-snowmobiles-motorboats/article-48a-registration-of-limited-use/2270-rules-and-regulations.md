# Vehicle and Traffic Law § 2270 — Rules and regulations

[Vehicle and Traffic Law](../../README.md) › [Title 11 — Registration of Snowmobiles, Motorboats and Limited Use Vehicles](../README.md) › [Article 48-A — Registration of Limited Use Vehicles](README.md)

```text
  § 2270. Rules and regulations. The commissioner is hereby empowered to
make such rules and regulations as he may deem necessary to carry out
the provisions of this article, including procedures with respect to the
certification of maximum performance speed of limited use vehicles.
```
