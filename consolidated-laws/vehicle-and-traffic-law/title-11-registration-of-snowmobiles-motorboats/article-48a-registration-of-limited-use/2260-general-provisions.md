# Vehicle and Traffic Law § 2260 — General provisions

[Vehicle and Traffic Law](../../README.md) › [Title 11 — Registration of Snowmobiles, Motorboats and Limited Use Vehicles](../README.md) › [Article 48-A — Registration of Limited Use Vehicles](README.md)

```text
  § 2260. General provisions. Except as otherwise expressly provided
herein, all of the provisions of this chapter shall apply to a limited
use vehicle in the same manner as if such vehicle was not a limited use
vehicle.
```
