# Vehicle and Traffic Law § 2284 — Registration record

[Vehicle and Traffic Law](../../README.md) › [Title 11 — Registration of Snowmobiles, Motorboats and Limited Use Vehicles](../README.md) › [Article 48-B — Registration of All Terrain Vehicles](README.md)

```text
  § 2284. Registration record. Upon receipt of sufficient application
for registration of an ATV, as provided in this article, the
commissioner shall enter upon the records of the department the
registration of such vehicle under the distinctive number assigned to
such ATV as provided in this article.
```
