# Vehicle and Traffic Law § 2288 — Responsibility for operation by minors

[Vehicle and Traffic Law](../../README.md) › [Title 11 — Registration of Snowmobiles, Motorboats and Limited Use Vehicles](../README.md) › [Article 48-B — Registration of All Terrain Vehicles](README.md)

```text
  § 2288. Responsibility for operation by minors. No owner or other
person in possession of any ATV shall authorize or knowingly permit any
person under sixteen years of age to operate such ATV in violation of
any provision of this article.
```
