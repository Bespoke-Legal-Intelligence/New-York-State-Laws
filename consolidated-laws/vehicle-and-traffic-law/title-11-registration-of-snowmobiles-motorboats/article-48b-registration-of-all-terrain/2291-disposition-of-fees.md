# Vehicle and Traffic Law § 2291 — Disposition of fees

[Vehicle and Traffic Law](../../README.md) › [Title 11 — Registration of Snowmobiles, Motorboats and Limited Use Vehicles](../README.md) › [Article 48-B — Registration of All Terrain Vehicles](README.md)

```text
  § 2291. Disposition of fees. The commissioner shall deposit all monies
received from the registration of ATVs and all fees otherwise collected
under this article to the credit of the general fund, as prescribed by
section twenty-two hundred eighty-two of this chapter.
```
