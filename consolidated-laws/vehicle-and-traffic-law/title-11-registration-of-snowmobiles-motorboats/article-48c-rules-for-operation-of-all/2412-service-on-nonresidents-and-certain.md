# Vehicle and Traffic Law § 2412 — Service on nonresidents and certain residents, administrators or executors

[Vehicle and Traffic Law](../../README.md) › [Title 11 — Registration of Snowmobiles, Motorboats and Limited Use Vehicles](../README.md) › [Article 48-C — Rules For Operation of All Terrain Vehicles](README.md)

```text
  § 2412. Service on nonresidents and certain residents, administrators
or executors. For the purposes of section two hundred fifty-two of this
chapter, an ATV shall be a vehicle and the provisions of that section
and section two hundred fifty-three of this chapter shall apply to ATVs.
```
