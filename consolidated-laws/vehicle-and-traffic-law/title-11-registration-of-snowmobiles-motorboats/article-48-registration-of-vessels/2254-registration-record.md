# Vehicle and Traffic Law § 2254 — Registration record

[Vehicle and Traffic Law](../../README.md) › [Title 11 — Registration of Snowmobiles, Motorboats and Limited Use Vehicles](../README.md) › [Article 48 — Registration of Vessels](README.md)

```text
  § 2254. Registration record. Upon the issuance of a registration for a
vessel, as provided in this article, the commissioner shall enter upon
the records of the department the registration of such vessel under the
registration number assigned to such vessel as provided in this article,
and in any other manner which he deems appropriate.
```
