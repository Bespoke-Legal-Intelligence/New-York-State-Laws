# Vehicle and Traffic Law § 2228 — Special events

[Vehicle and Traffic Law](../../README.md) › [Title 11 — Registration of Snowmobiles, Motorboats and Limited Use Vehicles](../README.md) › [Article 47 — Registration of Snowmobiles](README.md)

```text
  § 2228. Special events. Snowmobiles operated at special events
approved by the commissioner of parks and recreation pursuant to section
25.11 of parks and recreation law shall be exempt from the provisions of
this article concerning registration.
```
