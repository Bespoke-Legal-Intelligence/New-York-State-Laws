# Vehicle and Traffic Law § 2229 — Snowmobile, not a motor vehicle

[Vehicle and Traffic Law](../../README.md) › [Title 11 — Registration of Snowmobiles, Motorboats and Limited Use Vehicles](../README.md) › [Article 47 — Registration of Snowmobiles](README.md)

```text
  § 2229. Snowmobile, not a motor vehicle. Notwithstanding its limited
use on highways, a snowmobile shall not be determined a motor vehicle
within the meaning of section one hundred twenty-five of this chapter or
any other successor statutes.
```
