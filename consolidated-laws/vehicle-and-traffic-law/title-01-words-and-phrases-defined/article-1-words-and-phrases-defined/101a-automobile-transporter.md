# Vehicle and Traffic Law § 101-A — Automobile transporter

[Vehicle and Traffic Law](../../README.md) › [Title 1 — Words and Phrases Defined](../README.md) › [Article 1 — Words and Phrases Defined](README.md)

```text
  § 101-a. Automobile transporter. Any vehicle combination designed and
used specifically for the transport of assembled (capable of being
driven) motor vehicles.
```
