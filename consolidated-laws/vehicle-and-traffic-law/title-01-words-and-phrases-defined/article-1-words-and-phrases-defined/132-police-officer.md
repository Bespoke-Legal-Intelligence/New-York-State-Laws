# Vehicle and Traffic Law § 132 — Police officer

[Vehicle and Traffic Law](../../README.md) › [Title 1 — Words and Phrases Defined](../README.md) › [Article 1 — Words and Phrases Defined](README.md)

```text
  § 132. Police officer. All police officers as defined in subdivision
thirty-four of section 1.20 of the criminal procedure law, and every
duly designated peace officer as defined in section 2.20 of such law,
when such peace officer is acting pursuant to his special duties.
```
