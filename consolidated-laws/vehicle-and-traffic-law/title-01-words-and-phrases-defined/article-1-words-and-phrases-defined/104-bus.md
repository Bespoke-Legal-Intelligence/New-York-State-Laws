# Vehicle and Traffic Law § 104 — Bus

[Vehicle and Traffic Law](../../README.md) › [Title 1 — Words and Phrases Defined](../README.md) › [Article 1 — Words and Phrases Defined](README.md)

```text
  § 104. Bus. Every motor vehicle having a seating capacity of fifteen
or more passengers in addition to the driver and used for the
transportation of persons.
```
