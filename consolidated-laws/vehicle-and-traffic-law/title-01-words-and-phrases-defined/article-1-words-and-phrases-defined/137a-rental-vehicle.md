# Vehicle and Traffic Law § 137-A — Rental vehicle

[Vehicle and Traffic Law](../../README.md) › [Title 1 — Words and Phrases Defined](../README.md) › [Article 1 — Words and Phrases Defined](README.md)

```text
  § 137-a. Rental vehicle. A vehicle owned by a person engaged in the
business of renting or leasing vehicles which is rented or leased
without a driver to a person other than the owner and is registered in
the name of such owner.
```
