# Vehicle and Traffic Law § 138 — Residence district

[Vehicle and Traffic Law](../../README.md) › [Title 1 — Words and Phrases Defined](../README.md) › [Article 1 — Words and Phrases Defined](README.md)

```text
  § 138. Residence district.  The territory contiguous to and including
a highway not comprising a business district when the property on such
highway for a distance of three hundred feet or more is in the main
improved with residences or residences and buildings in use for
business.
```
