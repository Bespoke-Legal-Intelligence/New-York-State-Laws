# Vehicle and Traffic Law § 122-A — Maximum gross weight

[Vehicle and Traffic Law](../../README.md) › [Title 1 — Words and Phrases Defined](../README.md) › [Article 1 — Words and Phrases Defined](README.md)

```text
  § 122-a. Maximum gross weight. The weight of the vehicle unladen plus
the weight of the maximum load to be carried by such vehicle during the
registration period or the maximum gross weight for which the vehicle is
registered, whichever is greater.
```
