# Vehicle and Traffic Law § 148-B — Tow truck

[Vehicle and Traffic Law](../../README.md) › [Title 1 — Words and Phrases Defined](../README.md) › [Article 1 — Words and Phrases Defined](README.md)

```text
  § 148-b. Tow truck. A motor vehicle that tows or carries a disabled,
illegally parked or abandoned motor vehicle or a motor vehicle involved
in an accident.
```
