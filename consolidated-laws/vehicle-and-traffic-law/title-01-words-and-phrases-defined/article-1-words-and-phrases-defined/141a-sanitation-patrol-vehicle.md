# Vehicle and Traffic Law § 141-A — Sanitation patrol vehicle

[Vehicle and Traffic Law](../../README.md) › [Title 1 — Words and Phrases Defined](../README.md) › [Article 1 — Words and Phrases Defined](README.md)

```text
  § 141-a. Sanitation patrol vehicle. Every vehicle operated by an
officer or member of the sanitation police of the department of
sanitation of the city of New York, duly appointed and designated as a
peace officer by such department, when such officer or member is acting
pursuant to his special duties.
```
