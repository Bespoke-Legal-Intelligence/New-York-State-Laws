# Vehicle and Traffic Law § 106 — Circulatory roadway

[Vehicle and Traffic Law](../../README.md) › [Title 1 — Words and Phrases Defined](../README.md) › [Article 1 — Words and Phrases Defined](README.md)

```text
  § 106. Circulatory roadway. The roadway within a circular intersection
on which traffic travels in a counterclockwise direction around a
central island.
```
