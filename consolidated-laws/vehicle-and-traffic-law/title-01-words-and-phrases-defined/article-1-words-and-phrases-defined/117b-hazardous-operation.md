# Vehicle and Traffic Law § 117-B — Hazardous operation

[Vehicle and Traffic Law](../../README.md) › [Title 1 — Words and Phrases Defined](../README.md) › [Article 1 — Words and Phrases Defined](README.md)

```text
  § 117-b. Hazardous operation.  The operation, or parking, of a vehicle
on or immediately adjacent to a public highway while such vehicle is
actually engaged in an operation which would restrict, impede or
interfere with the normal flow of traffic.
```
