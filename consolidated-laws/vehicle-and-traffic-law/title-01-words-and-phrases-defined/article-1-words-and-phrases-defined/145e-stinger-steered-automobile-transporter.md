# Vehicle and Traffic Law § 145-E — Stinger-steered automobile transporter

[Vehicle and Traffic Law](../../README.md) › [Title 1 — Words and Phrases Defined](../README.md) › [Article 1 — Words and Phrases Defined](README.md)

```text
  § 145-e. Stinger-steered automobile transporter. An automobile
transporter configured as a semitrailer combination wherein the fifth
wheel is located on a drop frame located behind and below the rear-most
axle of the power unit.
```
