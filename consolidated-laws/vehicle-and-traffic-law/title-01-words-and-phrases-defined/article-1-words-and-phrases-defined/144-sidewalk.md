# Vehicle and Traffic Law § 144 — Sidewalk

[Vehicle and Traffic Law](../../README.md) › [Title 1 — Words and Phrases Defined](../README.md) › [Article 1 — Words and Phrases Defined](README.md)

```text
  § 144. Sidewalk.  That portion of a street between the curb lines, or
the lateral lines of a roadway, and the adjacent property lines,
intended for the use of pedestrians.
```
