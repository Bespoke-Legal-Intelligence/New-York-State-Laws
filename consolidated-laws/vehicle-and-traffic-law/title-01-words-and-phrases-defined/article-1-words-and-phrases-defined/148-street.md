# Vehicle and Traffic Law § 148 — Street

[Vehicle and Traffic Law](../../README.md) › [Title 1 — Words and Phrases Defined](../README.md) › [Article 1 — Words and Phrases Defined](README.md)

```text
  § 148. Street.  The entire width between the boundary lines of every
way publicly maintained when any part thereof is open to the use of the
public for purposes of vehicular travel.
```
