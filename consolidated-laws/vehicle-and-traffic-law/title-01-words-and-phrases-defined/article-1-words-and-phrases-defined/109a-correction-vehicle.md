# Vehicle and Traffic Law § 109-A — Correction vehicle

[Vehicle and Traffic Law](../../README.md) › [Title 1 — Words and Phrases Defined](../README.md) › [Article 1 — Words and Phrases Defined](README.md)

```text
  § 109-a. Correction vehicle. Every vehicle operated in the city of New
York by the New York city department of correction or the New York state
department of corrections and community supervision while engaged in an
emergency operation.
```
