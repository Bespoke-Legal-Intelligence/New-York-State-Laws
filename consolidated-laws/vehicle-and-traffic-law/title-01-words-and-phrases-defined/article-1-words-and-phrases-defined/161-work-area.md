# Vehicle and Traffic Law § 161 — Work area

[Vehicle and Traffic Law](../../README.md) › [Title 1 — Words and Phrases Defined](../README.md) › [Article 1 — Words and Phrases Defined](README.md)

```text
  § 161. Work area. That part of a highway being used or occupied for
the conduct of highway work, within which workers, vehicles, equipment,
materials, supplies, excavations, or other obstructions are present.
```
