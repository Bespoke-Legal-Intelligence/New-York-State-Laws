# Vehicle and Traffic Law § 131-A — Place of public assembly

[Vehicle and Traffic Law](../../README.md) › [Title 1 — Words and Phrases Defined](../README.md) › [Article 1 — Words and Phrases Defined](README.md)

```text
  § 131-a. Place of public assembly. A place of public assembly shall
include (1) a theatre, (2) a moving picture house, or (3) an assembly
hall maintained or leased for pecuniary gain where one hundred or more
persons may assemble for amusement or recreation.
```
