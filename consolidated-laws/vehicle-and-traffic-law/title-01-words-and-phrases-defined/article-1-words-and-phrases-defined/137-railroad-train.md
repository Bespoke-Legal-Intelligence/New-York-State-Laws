# Vehicle and Traffic Law § 137 — Railroad train

[Vehicle and Traffic Law](../../README.md) › [Title 1 — Words and Phrases Defined](../README.md) › [Article 1 — Words and Phrases Defined](README.md)

```text
  § 137. Railroad train.  A steam engine, electric or other motor
locomotive or car, with or without cars coupled thereto, operated upon
rails, except streetcars.
```
