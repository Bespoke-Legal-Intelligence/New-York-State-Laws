# Vehicle and Traffic Law § 126 — Omnibus

[Vehicle and Traffic Law](../../README.md) › [Title 1 — Words and Phrases Defined](../README.md) › [Article 1 — Words and Phrases Defined](README.md)

```text
  § 126. Omnibus.  Any motor vehicle used in the business of
transporting passengers for hire, except such a motor vehicle used in
the transportation of agricultural workers to and from their employment.
```
