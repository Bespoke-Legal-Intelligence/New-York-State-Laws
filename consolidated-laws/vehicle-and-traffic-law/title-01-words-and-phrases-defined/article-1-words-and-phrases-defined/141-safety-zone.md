# Vehicle and Traffic Law § 141 — Safety zone

[Vehicle and Traffic Law](../../README.md) › [Title 1 — Words and Phrases Defined](../README.md) › [Article 1 — Words and Phrases Defined](README.md)

```text
  § 141. Safety zone.  The area or space officially set apart within a
roadway for the exclusive use of pedestrians and which is protected or
is so marked or indicated by adequate signs as to be plainly visible at
all times while set apart as a safety zone.
```
