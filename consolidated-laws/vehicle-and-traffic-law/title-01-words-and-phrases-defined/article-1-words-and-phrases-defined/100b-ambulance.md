# Vehicle and Traffic Law § 100-B — Ambulance

[Vehicle and Traffic Law](../../README.md) › [Title 1 — Words and Phrases Defined](../README.md) › [Article 1 — Words and Phrases Defined](README.md)

```text
  § 100-b. Ambulance.  Every motor vehicle designed, appropriately
equipped and used for the purpose of carrying sick or injured persons by
a person or entity registered or certified as an ambulance service by
the department of health.
```
