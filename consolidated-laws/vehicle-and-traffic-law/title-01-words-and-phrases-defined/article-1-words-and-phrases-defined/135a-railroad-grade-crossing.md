# Vehicle and Traffic Law § 135-A — Railroad grade crossing

[Vehicle and Traffic Law](../../README.md) › [Title 1 — Words and Phrases Defined](../README.md) › [Article 1 — Words and Phrases Defined](README.md)

```text
  § 135-a. Railroad grade crossing. A location where a public highway or
private road, including associated sidewalks, crosses one or more
railroad tracks at grade.
```
