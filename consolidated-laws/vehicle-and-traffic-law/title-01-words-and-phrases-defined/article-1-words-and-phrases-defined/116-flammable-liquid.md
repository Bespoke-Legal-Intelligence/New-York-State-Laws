# Vehicle and Traffic Law § 116 — Flammable liquid

[Vehicle and Traffic Law](../../README.md) › [Title 1 — Words and Phrases Defined](../README.md) › [Article 1 — Words and Phrases Defined](README.md)

```text
  § 116. Flammable liquid.  Any liquid which has a flash point of eighty
degrees Fahrenheit, or less, as determined by a tagliabue or equivalent
open-cup test device.
```
