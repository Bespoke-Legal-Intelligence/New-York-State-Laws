# Vehicle and Traffic Law § 102-A — Bicycle lane

[Vehicle and Traffic Law](../../README.md) › [Title 1 — Words and Phrases Defined](../README.md) › [Article 1 — Words and Phrases Defined](README.md)

```text
  § 102-a. Bicycle lane. A portion of the roadway which has been
designated by striping, signing and pavement markings for the
preferential or exclusive use of bicycles.
```
