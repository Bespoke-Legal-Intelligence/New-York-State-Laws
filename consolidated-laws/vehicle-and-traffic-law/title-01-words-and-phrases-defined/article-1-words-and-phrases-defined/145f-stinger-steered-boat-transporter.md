# Vehicle and Traffic Law § 145-F — Stinger-steered boat transporter

[Vehicle and Traffic Law](../../README.md) › [Title 1 — Words and Phrases Defined](../README.md) › [Article 1 — Words and Phrases Defined](README.md)

```text
  § 145-f. Stinger-steered boat transporter. A boat transporter
configured as a semitrailer combination wherein the fifth wheel is
located on a drop frame located behind and below the rear-most axle of
the power unit.
```
