# Vehicle and Traffic Law § 141-A — Sani-vans

[Vehicle and Traffic Law](../../README.md) › [Title 1 — Words and Phrases Defined](../README.md) › [Article 1 — Words and Phrases Defined](README.md)

```text
  * § 141-a. Sani-vans. Any motor vehicle which is so designed that it
contains a rear compaction unit and is intended for use in the
collection of refuse.
  * NB There are 2 § 141-a's
```
