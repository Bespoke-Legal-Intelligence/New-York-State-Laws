# Vehicle and Traffic Law § 160 — Wheel lift

[Vehicle and Traffic Law](../../README.md) › [Title 1 — Words and Phrases Defined](../README.md) › [Article 1 — Words and Phrases Defined](README.md)

```text
  § 160. Wheel lift. Any device that may be extended beyond the rear
bumper of a tow truck or car carrier for the purpose of towing another
vehicle. Such device may also be referred to as an "under lift".
```
