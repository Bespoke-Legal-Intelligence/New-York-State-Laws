# Vehicle and Traffic Law § 134-B — Radar detector

[Vehicle and Traffic Law](../../README.md) › [Title 1 — Words and Phrases Defined](../README.md) › [Article 1 — Words and Phrases Defined](README.md)

```text
  § 134-b. Radar detector. Any instrument designed to detect the
presence of microwaves at frequencies specifically assigned by the
federal communications commission for the purpose of maintaining
vehicular speed.
```
