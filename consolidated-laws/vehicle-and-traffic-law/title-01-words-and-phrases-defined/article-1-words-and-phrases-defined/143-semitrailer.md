# Vehicle and Traffic Law § 143 — Semitrailer

[Vehicle and Traffic Law](../../README.md) › [Title 1 — Words and Phrases Defined](../README.md) › [Article 1 — Words and Phrases Defined](README.md)

```text
  § 143. Semitrailer.  Any trailer which is so designed that when
operated the forward end of its body or chassis rests upon the body or
chassis of the towing vehicle.
```
