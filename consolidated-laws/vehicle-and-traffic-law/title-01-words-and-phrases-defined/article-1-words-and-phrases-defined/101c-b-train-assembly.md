# Vehicle and Traffic Law § 101-C — B-train assembly

[Vehicle and Traffic Law](../../README.md) › [Title 1 — Words and Phrases Defined](../README.md) › [Article 1 — Words and Phrases Defined](README.md)

```text
  § 101-c. B-train assembly. A rigid frame extension attached to the
rear frame of the first semitrailer, in a
tractor-semitrailer-semitrailer combination of vehicles, which allows
for a fifth wheel connection point for the second semitrailer.
```
