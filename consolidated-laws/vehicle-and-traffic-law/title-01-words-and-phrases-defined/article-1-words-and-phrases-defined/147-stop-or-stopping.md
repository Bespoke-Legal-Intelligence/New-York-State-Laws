# Vehicle and Traffic Law § 147 — Stop or stopping

[Vehicle and Traffic Law](../../README.md) › [Title 1 — Words and Phrases Defined](../README.md) › [Article 1 — Words and Phrases Defined](README.md)

```text
  § 147. Stop or stopping.  When prohibited means any halting even
momentarily of a vehicle, whether occupied or not, except when necessary
to avoid conflict with other traffic or in compliance with the
directions of a police officer or traffic-control sign or signal.
```
