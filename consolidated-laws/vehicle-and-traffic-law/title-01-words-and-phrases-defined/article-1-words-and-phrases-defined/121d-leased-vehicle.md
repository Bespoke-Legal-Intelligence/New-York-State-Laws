# Vehicle and Traffic Law § 121-D — Leased vehicle

[Vehicle and Traffic Law](../../README.md) › [Title 1 — Words and Phrases Defined](../README.md) › [Article 1 — Words and Phrases Defined](README.md)

```text
  § 121-d. Leased vehicle. A vehicle owned by a person engaged in the
business of renting or leasing vehicles which is rented or leased
without a driver to a person other than the owner and is registered
either in the name of the lessee or renter, or in the name of the owner,
in a format prescribed by the commissioner.
```
