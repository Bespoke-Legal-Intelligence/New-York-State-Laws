# Vehicle and Traffic Law § 300 — Application of this title

[Vehicle and Traffic Law](../../README.md) › [Title 3 — Safety Responsibility; Financial Security; Equipment; Inspection; Size and Weight; and Other Provisions](../README.md) › [Article 4 — Application of This Title](README.md)

```text
  § 300. Application of this title. Except as otherwise expressly
provided in this chapter, this title shall be exclusively controlling:
  (a) On the use by motor vehicles of public highways, and
  (b) On the accessories used upon motor vehicles and motorcycles and
their incidents.
```
