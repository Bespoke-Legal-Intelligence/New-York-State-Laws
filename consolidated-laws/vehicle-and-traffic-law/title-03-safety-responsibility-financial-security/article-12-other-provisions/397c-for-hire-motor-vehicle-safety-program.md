# Vehicle and Traffic Law § 397-C — For-hire motor vehicle safety program

[Vehicle and Traffic Law](../../README.md) › [Title 3 — Safety Responsibility; Financial Security; Equipment; Inspection; Size and Weight; and Other Provisions](../README.md) › [Article 12 — Other Provisions](README.md)

```text
  § 397-c. For-hire motor vehicle safety program. The commissioner shall
evaluate and approve any for-hire motor vehicle safety program created
pursuant to section two thousand three hundred fifty-three of the
insurance law within sixty days of the effective date of such section.
```
