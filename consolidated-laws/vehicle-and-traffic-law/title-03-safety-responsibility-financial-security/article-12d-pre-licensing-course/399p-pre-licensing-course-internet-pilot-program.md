# Vehicle and Traffic Law § 399-P — Pre-licensing course internet pilot program

[Vehicle and Traffic Law](../../README.md) › [Title 3 — Safety Responsibility; Financial Security; Equipment; Inspection; Size and Weight; and Other Provisions](../README.md) › [Article 12-D — Pre-licensing Course Internet Pilot Program](README.md)

```text
  * § 399-p. Pre-licensing course internet pilot program. The
commissioner shall establish and implement a comprehensive pilot program
to review and study the use of the internet for the administration and
completion of an approved pre-licensing course as required by
subparagraph (i) of paragraph (a) of subdivision four of section five
hundred two of this chapter.
  * NB Effective June 30, 2020
  * NB Repealed June 30, 2025
```
