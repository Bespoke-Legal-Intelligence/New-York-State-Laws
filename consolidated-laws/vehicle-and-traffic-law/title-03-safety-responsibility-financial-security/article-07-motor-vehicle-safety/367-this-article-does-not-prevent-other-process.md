# Vehicle and Traffic Law § 367 — This article does not prevent other process

[Vehicle and Traffic Law](../../README.md) › [Title 3 — Safety Responsibility; Financial Security; Equipment; Inspection; Size and Weight; and Other Provisions](../README.md) › [Article 7 — Motor Vehicle Safety Responsibility Act](README.md)

```text
  § 367. This article does not prevent other process. This article shall
not be construed to prevent the plaintiff in any action at law from
relying for security upon the other processes provided by law.
```
