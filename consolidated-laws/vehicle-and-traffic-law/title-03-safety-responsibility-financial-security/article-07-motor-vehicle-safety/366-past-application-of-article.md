# Vehicle and Traffic Law § 366 — Past application of article

[Vehicle and Traffic Law](../../README.md) › [Title 3 — Safety Responsibility; Financial Security; Equipment; Inspection; Size and Weight; and Other Provisions](../README.md) › [Article 7 — Motor Vehicle Safety Responsibility Act](README.md)

```text
  § 366. Past application of article. This article, except section three
hundred thirty-nine thereof, shall not apply to any judgment in a civil
action or cause of action arising out of an accident occurring prior to
the effective date of this article.
```
