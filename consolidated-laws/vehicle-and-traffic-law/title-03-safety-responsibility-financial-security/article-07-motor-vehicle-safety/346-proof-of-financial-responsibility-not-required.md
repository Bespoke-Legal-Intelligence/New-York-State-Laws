# Vehicle and Traffic Law § 346 — Proof of financial responsibility not required after February first, nineteen hundred fifty-seven

[Vehicle and Traffic Law](../../README.md) › [Title 3 — Safety Responsibility; Financial Security; Equipment; Inspection; Size and Weight; and Other Provisions](../README.md) › [Article 7 — Motor Vehicle Safety Responsibility Act](README.md)

```text
  § 346. Proof of financial responsibility not required after February
first, nineteen hundred fifty-seven.  Notwithstanding any other
provision of law, no person, on and after February first, nineteen
hundred fifty-seven, shall be required to file or maintain proof of
financial responsibility pursuant to this article.
```
