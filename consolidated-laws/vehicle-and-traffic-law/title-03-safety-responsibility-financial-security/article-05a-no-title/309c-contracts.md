# Vehicle and Traffic Law § 309-C — Contracts

[Vehicle and Traffic Law](../../README.md) › [Title 3 — Safety Responsibility; Financial Security; Equipment; Inspection; Size and Weight; and Other Provisions](../README.md) › [Article 5-A — No title](README.md)

```text
  § 309-c. Contracts. The commissioner may in his discretion enter into
contracts with other outside agencies to provide testing devices, supply
facilities, maintain and conduct experimental motor vehicle inspections
under his direction and supervision.
```
