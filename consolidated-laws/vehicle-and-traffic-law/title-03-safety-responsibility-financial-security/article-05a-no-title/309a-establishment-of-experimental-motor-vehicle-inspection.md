# Vehicle and Traffic Law § 309-A — Establishment of experimental motor vehicle inspection stations

[Vehicle and Traffic Law](../../README.md) › [Title 3 — Safety Responsibility; Financial Security; Equipment; Inspection; Size and Weight; and Other Provisions](../README.md) › [Article 5-A — No title](README.md)

```text
  § 309-a. Establishment of experimental motor vehicle inspection
stations.  The commissioner of motor vehicles is hereby directed to
establish, maintain and/or conduct experimental motor vehicle inspection
stations, by contract or pursuant to an appropriation made therefor, for
the purpose of making the inspection prescribed by article five of the
vehicle and traffic law and for the purpose of issuing certificates of
inspection.
```
