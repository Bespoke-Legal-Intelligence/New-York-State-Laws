# Vehicle and Traffic Law § 2135 — Severability

[Vehicle and Traffic Law](../../README.md) › [Title 10 — Uniform Vehicle Certificate of Title Act](../README.md) › [Article 46 — Uniform Vehicle Certificate of Title Act](README.md)

```text
  § 2135. Severability. If any provision of this article or the
application thereof to any person or circumstances is held invalid, the
invalidity shall not affect other provisions or applications of the
article which can be given effect without the invalid provision or
application, and to this end the provisions of this article are declared
to be severable.
```
