# Vehicle and Traffic Law § 2106 — Examination of records

[Vehicle and Traffic Law](../../README.md) › [Title 10 — Uniform Vehicle Certificate of Title Act](../README.md) › [Article 46 — Uniform Vehicle Certificate of Title Act](README.md)

```text
  § 2106. Examination of records. The commissioner, upon receiving
application for a first certificate of title, shall check the
identifying number of the vehicle and the name or other identifier of
the owner shown in the application against the appropriate records of
vehicles required to be maintained.
```
