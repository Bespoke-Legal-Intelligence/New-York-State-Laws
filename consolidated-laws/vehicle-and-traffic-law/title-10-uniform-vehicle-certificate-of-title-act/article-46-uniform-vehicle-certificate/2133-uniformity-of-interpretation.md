# Vehicle and Traffic Law § 2133 — Uniformity of interpretation

[Vehicle and Traffic Law](../../README.md) › [Title 10 — Uniform Vehicle Certificate of Title Act](../README.md) › [Article 46 — Uniform Vehicle Certificate of Title Act](README.md)

```text
  § 2133. Uniformity of interpretation. This article shall be so
interpreted and construed as to effectuate its general purpose to make
uniform the law of those states which enact it.
```
