# Vehicle and Traffic Law § 2128 — Court review

[Vehicle and Traffic Law](../../README.md) › [Title 10 — Uniform Vehicle Certificate of Title Act](../README.md) › [Article 46 — Uniform Vehicle Certificate of Title Act](README.md)

```text
  § 2128. Court review. An act or omission to act of the commissioner
under this article shall be subject to review by the supreme court in
the manner provided in article seventy-eight of the civil practice law
and rules.
```
