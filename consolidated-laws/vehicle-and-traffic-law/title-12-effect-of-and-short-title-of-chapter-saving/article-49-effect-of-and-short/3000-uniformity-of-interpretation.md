# Vehicle and Traffic Law § 3000 — Uniformity of interpretation

[Vehicle and Traffic Law](../../README.md) › [Title 12 — Effect of and Short Title of Chapter; Saving Clauses; Laws Repealed; Time to Take Effect](../README.md) › [Article 49 — Effect of and Short Title of Chapter](README.md)

```text
  § 3000. Uniformity of interpretation.  Titles one, seven and
succeeding titles shall be so interpreted and construed as to effectuate
their general purpose to make uniform the law of those states which
enact them.
```
