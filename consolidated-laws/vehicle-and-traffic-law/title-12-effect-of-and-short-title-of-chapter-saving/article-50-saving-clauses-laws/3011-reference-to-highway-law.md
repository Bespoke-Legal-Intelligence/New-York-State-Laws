# Vehicle and Traffic Law § 3011 — Reference to highway law

[Vehicle and Traffic Law](../../README.md) › [Title 12 — Effect of and Short Title of Chapter; Saving Clauses; Laws Repealed; Time to Take Effect](../README.md) › [Article 50 — Saving Clauses; Laws Repealed; Time to Take Effect](README.md)

```text
  § 3011. Reference to highway law.  The reference by any law, general
or special, to any section of articles eleven or eleven-a of the highway
law shall be deemed a reference to the corresponding section of the
vehicle and traffic law as modified or changed by this chapter.
```
