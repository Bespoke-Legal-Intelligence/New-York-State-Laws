# Vehicle and Traffic Law § 405-D — Registration period

[Vehicle and Traffic Law](../../README.md) › [Title 4 — Registration of Vehicles](../README.md) › [Article 14-A — Apportionment or Allocation of Registration Fee Agreement](README.md)

```text
  § 405-d. Registration period. The registration period for any vehicle
registered pursuant to this article shall take effect and expire on
dates determined by the commissioner after consultation with appropriate
representatives of the commercial trucking industry in New York state.
```
