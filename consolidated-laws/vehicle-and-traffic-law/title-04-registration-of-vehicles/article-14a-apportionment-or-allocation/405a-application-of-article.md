# Vehicle and Traffic Law § 405-A — Application of article

[Vehicle and Traffic Law](../../README.md) › [Title 4 — Registration of Vehicles](../README.md) › [Article 14-A — Apportionment or Allocation of Registration Fee Agreement](README.md)

```text
  § 405-a. Application of article. The provisions of the succeeding
sections of this article shall take effect when this state enters into
an agreement pursuant to the authority granted in section four hundred
five of this article and shall remain in effect only so long as this
state is a party to such an agreement.
```
