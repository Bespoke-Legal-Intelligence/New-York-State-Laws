# Vehicle and Traffic Law § 409 — Notice of withdrawal

[Vehicle and Traffic Law](../../README.md) › [Title 4 — Registration of Vehicles](../README.md) › [Article 14-B — Bus Taxation Proration and Reciprocity Agreement](README.md)

```text
  § 409. Notice of withdrawal. Unless otherwise provided in any statute
withdrawing this state from participation in the agreement, the governor
shall be the officer to give notice of withdrawal therefrom.
```
