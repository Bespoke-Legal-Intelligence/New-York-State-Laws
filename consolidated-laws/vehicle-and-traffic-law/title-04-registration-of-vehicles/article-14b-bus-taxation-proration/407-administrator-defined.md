# Vehicle and Traffic Law § 407 — Administrator defined

[Vehicle and Traffic Law](../../README.md) › [Title 4 — Registration of Vehicles](../README.md) › [Article 14-B — Bus Taxation Proration and Reciprocity Agreement](README.md)

```text
  § 407. Administrator defined. As used in this agreement, with
reference to this state, the term administrator shall mean the
commissioner of motor vehicles.
```
