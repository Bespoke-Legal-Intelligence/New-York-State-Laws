# Vehicle and Traffic Law § 499 — Definition

[Vehicle and Traffic Law](../../README.md) › [Title 4 — Registration of Vehicles](../README.md) › [Article 17-C — Metropolitan Commuter Transportation District Supplemental Registration Fee](README.md)

```text
  § 499. Definition. For the purposes of this article "metropolitan
commuter transportation district" shall mean the area of the state
included in the district created and governed by section twelve hundred
sixty-two of the public authorities law.
```
