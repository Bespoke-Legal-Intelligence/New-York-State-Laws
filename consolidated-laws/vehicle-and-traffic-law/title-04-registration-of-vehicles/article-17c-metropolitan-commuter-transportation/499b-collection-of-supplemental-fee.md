# Vehicle and Traffic Law § 499-B — Collection of supplemental fee

[Vehicle and Traffic Law](../../README.md) › [Title 4 — Registration of Vehicles](../README.md) › [Article 17-C — Metropolitan Commuter Transportation District Supplemental Registration Fee](README.md)

```text
  § 499-b. Collection of supplemental fee. All registrants of motor
vehicles who reside in the metropolitan commuter transportation district
shall pay to the commissioner or his or her agent the supplemental
registration fee provided for in this article upon registration or
renewal of motor vehicles subject to registration fees pursuant to the
following sections of this chapter: paragraph a of subdivision six of
section four hundred one; schedules A, B, C, E, F, G, I and K of
subdivision seven of section four hundred one; paragraph a of
subdivision eight of section four hundred one; paragraph a of
subdivision five of section four hundred ten; and section four hundred
eleven-b.
```
