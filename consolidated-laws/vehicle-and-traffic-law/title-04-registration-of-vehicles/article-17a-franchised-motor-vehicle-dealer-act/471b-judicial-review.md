# Vehicle and Traffic Law § 471-B — Judicial review

[Vehicle and Traffic Law](../../README.md) › [Title 4 — Registration of Vehicles](../README.md) › [Article 17-A — Franchised Motor Vehicle Dealer Act](README.md)

```text
  § 471-b. Judicial review. A decision of the presiding officer under
section four hundred seventy-one-a of this article shall be subject to
review by the supreme court in the manner provided by article
seventy-eight of the civil practice law and rules.
```
