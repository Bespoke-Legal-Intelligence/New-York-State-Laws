# Vehicle and Traffic Law § 1232 — Riding on bicycles

[Vehicle and Traffic Law](../../README.md) › [Title 7 — Rules of the Road](../README.md) › [Article 34 — Operation of Bicycles and Play Devices](README.md)

```text
  § 1232. Riding on bicycles. (a) A person propelling a bicycle shall
not ride other than upon or astride a permanent and regular seat
attached thereto, nor shall he ride with his feet removed from the
pedals.
  (b) No bicycle shall be used to carry more persons at one time than
the number for which it is designed and equipped.
```
