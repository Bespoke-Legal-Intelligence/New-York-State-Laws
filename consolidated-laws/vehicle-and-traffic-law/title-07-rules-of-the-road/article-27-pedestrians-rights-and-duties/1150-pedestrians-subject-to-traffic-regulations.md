# Vehicle and Traffic Law § 1150 — Pedestrians subject to traffic regulations

[Vehicle and Traffic Law](../../README.md) › [Title 7 — Rules of the Road](../README.md) › [Article 27 — Pedestrians' Rights and Duties](README.md)

```text
  § 1150. Pedestrians subject to traffic regulations.  Pedestrians shall
be subject to traffic-control signals as provided in section eleven
hundred eleven of this title, but at all other places pedestrians shall
be accorded the privileges and shall be subject to the restrictions
stated in this article.
```
