# Vehicle and Traffic Law § 1151-A — Pedestrians' right of way on sidewalks

[Vehicle and Traffic Law](../../README.md) › [Title 7 — Rules of the Road](../README.md) › [Article 27 — Pedestrians' Rights and Duties](README.md)

```text
  § 1151-a. Pedestrians' right of way on sidewalks.  The driver of a
vehicle emerging from or entering an alleyway, building, private road or
driveway shall yield the right of way to any pedestrian approaching on
any sidewalk extending across such alleyway, building entrance, road or
driveway.
```
