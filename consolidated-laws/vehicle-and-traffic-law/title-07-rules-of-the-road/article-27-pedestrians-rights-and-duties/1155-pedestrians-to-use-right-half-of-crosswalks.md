# Vehicle and Traffic Law § 1155 — Pedestrians to use right half of crosswalks

[Vehicle and Traffic Law](../../README.md) › [Title 7 — Rules of the Road](../README.md) › [Article 27 — Pedestrians' Rights and Duties](README.md)

```text
  § 1155. Pedestrians to use right half of crosswalks.  Pedestrians
shall move, whenever practicable, upon the right half of crosswalks.
```
