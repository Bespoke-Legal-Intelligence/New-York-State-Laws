# Vehicle and Traffic Law § 1143 — Vehicle entering roadway

[Vehicle and Traffic Law](../../README.md) › [Title 7 — Rules of the Road](../README.md) › [Article 26 — Right of Way](README.md)

```text
  § 1143. Vehicle entering roadway.  The driver of a vehicle about to
enter or cross a roadway from any place other than another roadway shall
yield the right of way to all vehicles approaching on the roadway to be
entered or crossed.
```
