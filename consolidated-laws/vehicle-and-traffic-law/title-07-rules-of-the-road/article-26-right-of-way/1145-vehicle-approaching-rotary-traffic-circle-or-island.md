# Vehicle and Traffic Law § 1145 — Vehicle approaching rotary traffic circle or island

[Vehicle and Traffic Law](../../README.md) › [Title 7 — Rules of the Road](../README.md) › [Article 26 — Right of Way](README.md)

```text
  § 1145. Vehicle approaching rotary traffic circle or island.  Except
where a traffic control device directs otherwise, the driver of a
vehicle approaching or about to enter a rotary traffic circle or island
shall yield the right of way to any vehicle already traveling on such
circle or around such island.
```
