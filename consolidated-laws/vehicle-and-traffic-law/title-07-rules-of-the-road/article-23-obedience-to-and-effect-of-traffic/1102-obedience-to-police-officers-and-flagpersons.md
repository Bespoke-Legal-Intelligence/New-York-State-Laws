# Vehicle and Traffic Law § 1102 — Obedience to police officers and flagpersons

[Vehicle and Traffic Law](../../README.md) › [Title 7 — Rules of the Road](../README.md) › [Article 23 — Obedience to and Effect of Traffic Laws](README.md)

```text
  § 1102. Obedience to police officers and flagpersons. No person shall
fail or refuse to comply with any lawful order or direction of any
police officer or flagperson or other person duly empowered to regulate
traffic.
```
