# Vehicle and Traffic Law § 1263 — Carrying articles

[Vehicle and Traffic Law](../../README.md) › [Title 7 — Rules of the Road](../README.md) › [Article 34-B — Riding Horses](README.md)

```text
  § 1263. Carrying articles. No person riding or leading a horse shall
carry any package, bundle, or article which prevents such person from
keeping at least one hand upon the reins.
```
