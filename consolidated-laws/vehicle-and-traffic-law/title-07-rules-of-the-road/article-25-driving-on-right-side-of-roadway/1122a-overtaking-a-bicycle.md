# Vehicle and Traffic Law § 1122-A — Overtaking a bicycle

[Vehicle and Traffic Law](../../README.md) › [Title 7 — Rules of the Road](../README.md) › [Article 25 — Driving On Right Side of Roadway, Overtaking and Passing, Etc](README.md)

```text
  § 1122-a. Overtaking a bicycle. The operator of a vehicle overtaking,
from behind, a bicycle proceeding on the same side of a roadway shall
pass to the left of such bicycle at a safe distance until safely clear
thereof.
```
