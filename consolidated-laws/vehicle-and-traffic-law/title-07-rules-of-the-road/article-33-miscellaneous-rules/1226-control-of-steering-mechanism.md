# Vehicle and Traffic Law § 1226 — Control of steering mechanism

[Vehicle and Traffic Law](../../README.md) › [Title 7 — Rules of the Road](../README.md) › [Article 33 — Miscellaneous Rules](README.md)

```text
  § 1226. Control of steering mechanism. No person shall operate a motor
vehicle without having at least one hand or, in the case of a physically
handicapped person, at least one prosthetic device or aid on the
steering mechanism at all times when the motor vehicle is in motion.
```
