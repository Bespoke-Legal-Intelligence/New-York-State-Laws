# Vehicle and Traffic Law § 1220-C — Work permits for work on state highways

[Vehicle and Traffic Law](../../README.md) › [Title 7 — Rules of the Road](../README.md) › [Article 33 — Miscellaneous Rules](README.md)

```text
  § 1220-c. Work permits for work on state highways. Except in
connection with the construction, reconstruction, maintenance, or
improvement of a state highway, no person shall work on a state highway
without a work permit issued by the state commissioner of
transportation.
```
