# Vehicle and Traffic Law § 1228 — Riding in house coach trailers

[Vehicle and Traffic Law](../../README.md) › [Title 7 — Rules of the Road](../README.md) › [Article 33 — Miscellaneous Rules](README.md)

```text
  § 1228. Riding in house coach trailers. No person shall drive a
vehicle on a public highway while drawing a house coach trailer occupied
by any person and no person shall occupy a house coach trailer while it
is being drawn upon a public highway.
```
