# Vehicle and Traffic Law § 1218 — Crossing fire hose

[Vehicle and Traffic Law](../../README.md) › [Title 7 — Rules of the Road](../README.md) › [Article 33 — Miscellaneous Rules](README.md)

```text
  § 1218. Crossing fire hose. No vehicle shall be driven over any
unprotected hose of a fire department when laid down on any street or
private driveway, to be used at any fire or alarm of fire, without the
consent of the fire department official in command.
```
