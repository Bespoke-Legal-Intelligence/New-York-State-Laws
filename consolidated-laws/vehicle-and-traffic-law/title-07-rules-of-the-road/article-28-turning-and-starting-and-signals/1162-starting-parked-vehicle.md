# Vehicle and Traffic Law § 1162 — Starting parked vehicle

[Vehicle and Traffic Law](../../README.md) › [Title 7 — Rules of the Road](../README.md) › [Article 28 — Turning and Starting and Signals On Stopping and Turning](README.md)

```text
  § 1162. Starting parked vehicle. No person shall move a vehicle which
is stopped, standing, or parked unless and until such movement can be
made with reasonable safety.
```
