# Vehicle and Traffic Law § 1677 — County purpose and charges

[Vehicle and Traffic Law](../../README.md) › [Title 8 — Respective Powers of State and Local Authorities](../README.md) › [Article 43 — Traffic Safety Boards](README.md)

```text
  § 1677.  County purpose and charges.  For any county not wholly
included within a city, the provisions of this article and expenditures
made hereunder shall be deemed a county purpose and charge.
```
