# Vehicle and Traffic Law § 1672 — Traffic safety board

[Vehicle and Traffic Law](../../README.md) › [Title 8 — Respective Powers of State and Local Authorities](../README.md) › [Article 43 — Traffic Safety Boards](README.md)

```text
  § 1672.  Traffic safety board.  Any city, any town, or any county not
wholly included within a city may by local law establish a traffic
safety board.  The borough president of any county wholly included
within a city may establish a traffic safety board for such county.
```
