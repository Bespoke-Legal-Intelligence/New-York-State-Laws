# Vehicle and Traffic Law § 1632 — Application of article

[Vehicle and Traffic Law](../../README.md) › [Title 8 — Respective Powers of State and Local Authorities](../README.md) › [Article 38 — Regulation of Traffic By Public Authorities and Commissions](README.md)

```text
  § 1632. Application of article. This article shall not apply with
respect to any portion of the New York state thruway located within a
city having a population in excess of one million.
```
