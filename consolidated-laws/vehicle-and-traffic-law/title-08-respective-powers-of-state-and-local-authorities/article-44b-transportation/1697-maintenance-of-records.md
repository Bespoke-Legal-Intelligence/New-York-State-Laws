# Vehicle and Traffic Law § 1697 — Maintenance of records

[Vehicle and Traffic Law](../../README.md) › [Title 8 — Respective Powers of State and Local Authorities](../README.md) › [Article 44-B — Transportation Network Company Services](README.md)

```text
  * § 1697. Maintenance of records. A TNC shall maintain the following
records:
  1. individual trip records for at least six years from the date each
trip was provided; and
  2. individual records of TNC drivers at least until the six year
anniversary of the date on which a TNC driver's relationship with the
TNC has ended.
  * NB Effective July 9, 2017
```
