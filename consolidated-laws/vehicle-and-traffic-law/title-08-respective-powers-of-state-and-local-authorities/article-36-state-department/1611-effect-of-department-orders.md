# Vehicle and Traffic Law § 1611 — Effect of department orders

[Vehicle and Traffic Law](../../README.md) › [Title 8 — Respective Powers of State and Local Authorities](../README.md) › [Article 36 — State Department of Transportation](README.md)

```text
  § 1611. Effect of department orders.  Orders issued by the department
of transportation pursuant to the provisions of this chapter shall be
deemed to be rules and regulations for the purpose of taking judicial
notice thereof.
```
