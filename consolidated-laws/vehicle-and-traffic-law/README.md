*An asterisk in a number (for example Article 24\* or § 20-C\*2) is the Senate's mark for a number the Legislature has used more than once: the marked article or section is a different one from the unmarked one with the same number. \*2 means the second one enacted with that number.*

**Short title (from the law itself):** § 365. Short title. This article may be cited as the New York motor vehicle safety-responsibility act.

## Chapter text as published

```text
                               CHAPTER 775
AN ACT relating to motor vehicles, motor cycles, and highway traffic,
    constituting chapter seventy-one of the consolidated laws
Became a law April 23, 1959, with the approval of the Governor.  Passed,
    by a majority vote, three-fifths being present
```
