# Real Property Actions and Proceedings Law § 1051 — Fees and expenses

[Real Property Actions and Proceedings Law](../README.md) › [Article 10 — Action For Dower](README.md)

```text
  § 1051. Fees and expenses. The fees and expenses of the commissioners,
or of the referee, including the expense of a survey, when it is made,
must be taxed under the direction of the court; and the amount thereof
must be paid by the plaintiff, and allowed to her, upon the taxation of
her costs.
```
