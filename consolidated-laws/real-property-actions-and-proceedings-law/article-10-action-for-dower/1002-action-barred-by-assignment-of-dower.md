# Real Property Actions and Proceedings Law § 1002 — Action barred by assignment of dower

[Real Property Actions and Proceedings Law](../README.md) › [Article 10 — Action For Dower](README.md)

```text
  § 1002. Action barred by assignment of dower. The acceptance by a
widow of an assignment of dower in satisfaction of her claim upon the
property in question bars an action for dower and may be pleaded by any
defendant.
```
