# Real Property Actions and Proceedings Law § 1023 — Damages where several parcels are affected

[Real Property Actions and Proceedings Law](../README.md) › [Article 10 — Action For Dower](README.md)

```text
  § 1023. Damages where several parcels are affected. Sections 1021 and
1022 do not authorize the recovery, against a defendant who is joined
with others, of damages for withholding dower, in any portion of the
property not occupied or claimed by him.
```
