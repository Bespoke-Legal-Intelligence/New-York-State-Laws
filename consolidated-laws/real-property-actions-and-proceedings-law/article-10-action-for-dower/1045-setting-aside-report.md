# Real Property Actions and Proceedings Law § 1045 — Setting aside report

[Real Property Actions and Proceedings Law](../README.md) › [Article 10 — Action For Dower](README.md)

```text
  § 1045. Setting aside report. Upon the application of any party to the
action, and upon good cause shown, the court may set aside the report,
and, if necessary, may appoint new commissioners, or a new referee, who
must proceed, as prescribed in this article, with respect to those first
appointed.
```
