# Real Property Actions and Proceedings Law § 757 — Eviction as the result of foreclosure

[Real Property Actions and Proceedings Law](../README.md) › [Article 7 — Summary Proceeding to Recover Possession of Real Property](README.md)

```text
  § 757. Eviction as the result of foreclosure. In the event that a
lessee is removed from real property pursuant to this article, and the
leased real property was the subject of a foreclosure proceeding
pursuant to this chapter or the subject of a tax foreclosure proceeding,
the court records relating to any such lessee shall be sealed and be
deemed confidential. No disclosure or use of such information relating
to any such lessee shall be authorized, and the use of such information
shall be prohibited.
```
