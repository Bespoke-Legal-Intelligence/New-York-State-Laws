# Real Property Actions and Proceedings Law § 749-A — New York city marshals

[Real Property Actions and Proceedings Law](../README.md) › [Article 7 — Summary Proceeding to Recover Possession of Real Property](README.md)

```text
  * § 749-a. New York city marshals. A marshal of the city of New York
shall provide notice of execution of a warrant of eviction by physical
posting of the notice and by an electronic filing. For purposes of this
section, "electronic filing" shall mean an electronic filing as
described in section twenty-one hundred three-a of the New York city
civil court act.
  * NB Repealed June 30, 2028
```
