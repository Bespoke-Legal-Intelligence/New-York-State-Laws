# Real Property Actions and Proceedings Law § 931 — Contents of judgment after sale

[Real Property Actions and Proceedings Law](../README.md) › [Article 9 — Action For Partition](README.md)

```text
  § 931. Contents of judgment after sale. A final judgment confirming a
sale shall direct the officer making it to execute the proper
conveyances and take the proper securities pursuant to the sale, and
also direct application of the proceeds.
```
