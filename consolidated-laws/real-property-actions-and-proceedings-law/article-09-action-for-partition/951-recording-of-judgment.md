# Real Property Actions and Proceedings Law § 951 — Recording of judgment

[Real Property Actions and Proceedings Law](../README.md) › [Article 9 — Action For Partition](README.md)

```text
  § 951. Recording of judgment. A copy, which is certified as correct,
of the final judgment may be recorded in the office for recording deeds
in each county in which any real property affected is situated.
```
