# Real Property Actions and Proceedings Law § 945 — Judgment adjusting rents and profits

[Real Property Actions and Proceedings Law](../README.md) › [Article 9 — Action For Partition](README.md)

```text
  § 945. Judgment adjusting rents and profits. The court may adjust the
rights of a party as against any other party by reason of the receipt by
the latter of more than his proper proportion of the rents or profits of
a share.
```
