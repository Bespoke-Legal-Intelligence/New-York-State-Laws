# Real Property Actions and Proceedings Law § 919 — Interlocutory judgment directing credit on sale

[Real Property Actions and Proceedings Law](../README.md) › [Article 9 — Action For Partition](README.md)

```text
  § 919. Interlocutory judgment directing credit on sale. The court, in
the interlocutory judgment for a sale, shall direct the terms of credit
which may be allowed for any portion of the purchase-money.
```
