# Real Property Actions and Proceedings Law § 404 — Evidence as to ancestry, health and habits of individual In all valuations made under this article, no significance shall be given to the...

[Real Property Actions and Proceedings Law](../README.md) › [Article 4 — Valuing Interests In Real Property](README.md)

```text
  § 404. Evidence as to ancestry, health and habits of individual In all
valuations made under this article, no significance shall be given to
the ancestry, health or habits of the person whose life is involved.
Each valuation shall be based exclusively on the actuarial data.
```
