# Real Property Actions and Proceedings Law § 402 — Interest rate

[Real Property Actions and Proceedings Law](../README.md) › [Article 4 — Valuing Interests In Real Property](README.md)

```text
  § 402. Interest rate. In all valuations made under this article, the
interest rate of four per centum, compounded annually, shall be employed
for all purposes.
```
