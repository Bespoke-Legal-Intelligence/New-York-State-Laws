# Real Property Actions and Proceedings Law § 633 — Action against co-tenant; ouster to be proved

[Real Property Actions and Proceedings Law](../README.md) › [Article 6 — Action to Recover Real Property](README.md)

```text
  § 633. Action against co-tenant; ouster to be proved. Where the action
is brought by a tenant in common or a joint tenant against his
co-tenant, the plaintiff, besides proving his right, shall also prove
that the defendant actually ousted him or did some other act amounting
to a total denial of his right.
```
