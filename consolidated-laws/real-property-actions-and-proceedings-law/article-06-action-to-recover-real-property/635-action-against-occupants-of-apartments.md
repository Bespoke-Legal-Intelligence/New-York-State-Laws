# Real Property Actions and Proceedings Law § 635 — Action against occupants of apartments

[Real Property Actions and Proceedings Law](../README.md) › [Article 6 — Action to Recover Real Property](README.md)

```text
  § 635. Action against occupants of apartments. In a case where two or
more defendants occupy different apartments in a building the plaintiff
is entitled to judgment jointly against all the defendants who are
liable to him.
```
