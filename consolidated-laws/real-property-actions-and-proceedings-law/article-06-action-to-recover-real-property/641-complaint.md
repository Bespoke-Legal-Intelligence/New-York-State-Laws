# Real Property Actions and Proceedings Law § 641 — Complaint

[Real Property Actions and Proceedings Law](../README.md) › [Article 6 — Action to Recover Real Property](README.md)

```text
  § 641. Complaint. The complaint shall state the plaintiff's interest
in the property and describe it with reasonable certainty in such manner
that, from the description, possession of the property claimed may be
delivered.
```
