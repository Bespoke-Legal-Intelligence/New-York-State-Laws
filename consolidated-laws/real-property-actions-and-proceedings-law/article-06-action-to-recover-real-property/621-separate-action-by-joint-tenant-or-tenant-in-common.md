# Real Property Actions and Proceedings Law § 621 — Separate action by joint tenant or tenant in common

[Real Property Actions and Proceedings Law](../README.md) › [Article 6 — Action to Recover Real Property](README.md)

```text
  § 621. Separate action by joint tenant or tenant in common.  Where two
or more persons are entitled to the possession of real property as joint
tenants or tenants in common, one or more of them may maintain the
action to recover his or their undivided shares in the property in any
case where such an action might be maintained by all.
```
