# Real Property Actions and Proceedings Law § 1103 — Service of petition and notice

[Real Property Actions and Proceedings Law](../README.md) › [Article 11 — Proceeding to Discover the Death of a Tenant For Life](README.md)

```text
  § 1103. Service of petition and notice. A copy of the petition,
including the affidavit, together with notice of the time and place at
which the petition will be presented, must be personally served, at
least fourteen days before its presentation, upon the person required,
by the prayer thereof, to produce the tenant for life.
```
