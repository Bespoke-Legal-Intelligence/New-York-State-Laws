# Real Property Actions and Proceedings Law § 1125 — Proceedings on return of commission

[Real Property Actions and Proceedings Law](../README.md) › [Article 11 — Proceeding to Discover the Death of a Tenant For Life](README.md)

```text
  § 1125. Proceedings on return of commission. Upon the return of the
commission, the proceedings are the same as upon the report of a
referee, as prescribed in sections 1111 and 1112; but the court may, in
its discretion, receive additional proofs from either party.
```
