# Real Property Actions and Proceedings Law § 797-I — Trial

[Real Property Actions and Proceedings Law](../README.md) › [Article 7-C* — Special Proceedings By Tenants For Judgment Directing Repairs of Conditions and Other Relief In Residential Real Property Constituting Violation of Applicable Local or State Housing Standards or Real Property Law §](README.md)

```text
  * § 797-i. Trial. Where triable issues of fact are raised, they shall
be tried by the court unless, at the time the petition is noticed to be
heard, a party demands a trial by jury, in which case trial shall be by
jury. At the time when issue is joined, the court, at the request of
either party shall adjourn the trial of the issue, not less than
fourteen days, except by consent of all parties. A party's second or
subsequent request for adjournment shall be granted in the court's sole
discretion.
  * NB Effective June 28, 2023
```
