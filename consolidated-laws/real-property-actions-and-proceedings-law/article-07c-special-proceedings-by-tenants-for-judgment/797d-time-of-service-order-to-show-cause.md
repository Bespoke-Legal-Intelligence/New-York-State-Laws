# Real Property Actions and Proceedings Law § 797-D — Time of service; order to show cause

[Real Property Actions and Proceedings Law](../README.md) › [Article 7-C* — Special Proceedings By Tenants For Judgment Directing Repairs of Conditions and Other Relief In Residential Real Property Constituting Violation of Applicable Local or State Housing Standards or Real Property Law §](README.md)

```text
  * § 797-d. Time of service; order to show cause. 1. The notice of
petition and petition shall be served at least ten and not more than
seventeen days before the time at which the petition is to be heard.
  2. The court may grant an order to show cause to be served in lieu of
a notice of petition at a time and in a manner specified therein.
  * NB Effective June 28, 2023
```
