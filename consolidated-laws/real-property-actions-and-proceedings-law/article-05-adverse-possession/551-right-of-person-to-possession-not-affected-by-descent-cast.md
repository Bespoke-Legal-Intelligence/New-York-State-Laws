# Real Property Actions and Proceedings Law § 551 — Right of person to possession not affected by descent cast

[Real Property Actions and Proceedings Law](../README.md) › [Article 5 — Adverse Possession](README.md)

```text
  § 551. Right of person to possession not affected by descent cast. The
right of a person to the possession of real property is not impaired or
affected by a descent being cast in consequence of the death of a person
in possession of the property.
```
