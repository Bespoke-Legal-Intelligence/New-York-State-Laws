# Real Property Actions and Proceedings Law § 812 — Action for waste by ward against guardian

[Real Property Actions and Proceedings Law](../README.md) › [Article 8 — Waste and Other Actions and Rights of Action For Injury to Real Property](README.md)

```text
  § 812. Action for waste by ward against guardian. Such an action may
also be maintained against a guardian by his ward, either before or
after the termination of the guardianship, for waste committed upon the
real property of the ward during the guardianship.
```
