# Real Property Actions and Proceedings Law § 882 — Severability

[Real Property Actions and Proceedings Law](../README.md) › [Article 8 — Waste and Other Actions and Rights of Action For Injury to Real Property](README.md)

```text
  § 882. Severability. If any provision of this article or the
application thereof to any person or circumstances is held invalid, the
remainder of the article and the application of such provision to other
persons or circumstances shall not be affected thereby.
```
