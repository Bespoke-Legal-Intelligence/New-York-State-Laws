# Real Property Actions and Proceedings Law § 1612 — Report of agreement for confirmation

[Real Property Actions and Proceedings Law](../README.md) › [Article 16 — Judicial Authorization of Sale, Lease, Mortgage, Acquisition, Exchange or Voluntary Partition](README.md)

```text
  § 1612. Report of agreement for confirmation. Before a mortgage, lease
or sale is made pursuant to a final order described in section 1609, the
trustee or referee must enter into an agreement therefor, subject to the
approval of the court, and must report this agreement to the court under
oath.
```
