# Real Property Actions and Proceedings Law § 1603 — Court to which application is to be made

[Real Property Actions and Proceedings Law](../README.md) › [Article 16 — Judicial Authorization of Sale, Lease, Mortgage, Acquisition, Exchange or Voluntary Partition](README.md)

```text
  § 1603. Court to which application is to be made. An application made
pursuant to the provisions of either section 1601 or section 1602 shall
be made to a term of the supreme court held within the judicial district
in which the real property, or a part thereof, is situated.
```
