# Real Property Actions and Proceedings Law § 794 — Answer

[Real Property Actions and Proceedings Law](../README.md) › [Article 7-B — Special Proceeding For the Removal of Abandoned Manufactured Homes](README.md)

```text
  § 794. Answer. At the time when the petition is to be heard, the owner
shall answer in writing. If the notice of petition was served at least
thirty days before the time at which it was noticed to be heard and it
so demands, the answer shall be served at least three days before the
time the petition is noticed to be heard and any reply shall be served
at least one day before such time.
```
