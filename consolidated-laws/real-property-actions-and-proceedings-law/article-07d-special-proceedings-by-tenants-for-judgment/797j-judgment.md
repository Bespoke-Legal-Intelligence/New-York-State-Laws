# Real Property Actions and Proceedings Law § 797-J — Judgment

[Real Property Actions and Proceedings Law](../README.md) › [Article 7-D — Special Proceedings By Tenants For Judgment Directing Repairs of Conditions and Other Relief In Residential Real Property Constituting Violation of Applicable Local or State Housing Standards or Real Property Law §](README.md)

```text
  § 797-j. Judgment. 1. The court shall direct that final judgment be
entered determining the rights of the parties.
  2. The judgment may include:
  a. An order to repair conditions constituting violation of applicable
local and state housing standards or section two hundred thirty-five-b
of the real property law; and
  b. Any other relief that the court may deem just.
```
