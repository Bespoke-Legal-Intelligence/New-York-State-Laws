# Transportation Law § 16 — Delegation of powers and duties

[Transportation Law](../README.md) › [Article 2 — Powers, Duties and Jurisdiction of the Department of Transportation](README.md)

```text
  § 16. Delegation of powers and duties. The commissioner may, by
official order filed with the department of transportation, delegate any
of his powers to or direct any of his duties to be performed by any
deputy, director, assistant, officer or employee of such department.
```
