# Transportation Law § 37 — Inconsistent provisions of other acts superseded

[Transportation Law](../README.md) › [Article 2-C — Niagara Thruway Toll Removal Fund](README.md)

```text
  § 37. Inconsistent provisions of other acts superseded. Insofar as the
provisions of this article are inconsistent with the provisions of any
other act, general or special, the provisions of this article shall be
controlling.
```
