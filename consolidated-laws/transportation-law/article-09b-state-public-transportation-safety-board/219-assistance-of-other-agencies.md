# Transportation Law § 219 — Assistance of other agencies

[Transportation Law](../README.md) › [Article 9-B — State Public Transportation Safety Board](README.md)

```text
  § 219. Assistance of other agencies. 1. To effectuate the purposes of
this article, the board may request and receive from any department,
division, board, bureau, commission or other agency of the state or any
political subdivision thereof or any public authority such assistance,
information and data as will enable the office properly to carry out its
powers and duties hereunder.
  2. The board shall cooperate, consult and coordinate with the
metropolitan transportation authority inspector general with regard to
any activity concerning the operations of the metropolitan
transportation authority. With respect to any accident on the facilities
of the metropolitan transportation authority, the primary responsibility
for investigation shall be that of the board which shall share its
findings with the metropolitan transportation authority inspector
general.
```
