# Transportation Law § 219-A — Studies; surveys

[Transportation Law](../README.md) › [Article 9-B — State Public Transportation Safety Board](README.md)

```text
  § 219-a. Studies; surveys. In the accomplishment of the purposes of
this article, the board may undertake research and studies through its
own personnel or in cooperation with any public or private agencies,
including educational, safety research organizations, colleges,
universities, institutes or foundations.
```
