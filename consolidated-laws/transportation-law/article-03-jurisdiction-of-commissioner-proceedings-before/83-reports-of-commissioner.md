# Transportation Law § 83 — Reports of commissioner

[Transportation Law](../README.md) › [Article 3 — Jurisdiction of Commissioner; Proceedings Before Commissioner; Recovery of Penalties; Court Preferences; Fees](README.md)

```text
  § 83. Reports of commissioner. All proceedings of the commissioner in
relation to functions and powers possessed by and all obligations and
duties transferred to the commissioner from the public service
commission and the department of public service by the provisions of
this chapter, and records in his possession relating thereto shall be
public records.
```
