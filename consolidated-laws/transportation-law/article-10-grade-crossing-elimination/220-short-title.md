# Transportation Law § 220 — Short title

[Transportation Law](../README.md) › [Article 10 — Grade Crossing Elimination](README.md)

```text
  § 220. Short title. This article shall be known as the "grade crossing
elimination act of nineteen hundred seventy." However, sections of the
transportation law falling within this article may be cited either as
sections of the transportation law or as sections of the "grade crossing
elimination act of nineteen hundred seventy."
```
