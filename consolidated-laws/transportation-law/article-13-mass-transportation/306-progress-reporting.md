# Transportation Law § 306 — Progress reporting

[Transportation Law](../README.md) › [Article 13 — Mass Transportation](README.md)

```text
  § 306. Progress reporting. The commissioner shall prepare and file
annually on or about the first of December with the governor, the office
of planning services, and the director of the budget a report on the
scope and results of construction undertaken in accordance with this
article.
```
