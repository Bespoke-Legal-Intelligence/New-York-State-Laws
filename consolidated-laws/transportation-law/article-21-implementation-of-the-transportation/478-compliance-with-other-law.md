# Transportation Law § 478 — Compliance with other law

[Transportation Law](../README.md) › [Article 21 — Implementation of the Transportation Infrastructure Bond Act of 2000](README.md)

```text
  * § 478. Compliance with other law. Every recipient of funds to be
made available pursuant to this article shall comply with all applicable
state, federal and local laws.
  * NB Not effective due to defeat of the Transportation Bond Act of
2000
```
