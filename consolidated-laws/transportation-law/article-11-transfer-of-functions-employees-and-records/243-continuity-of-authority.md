# Transportation Law § 243 — Continuity of authority

[Transportation Law](../README.md) › [Article 11 — Transfer of Functions, Employees and Records; Continuity of Authority](README.md)

```text
  § 243. Continuity of authority. For the purpose of succession to all
functions, powers, duties and obligations transferred pursuant to this
article, the department shall constitute the continuation of the public
service commission and the department of public service, and not a
different agency or authority.
```
