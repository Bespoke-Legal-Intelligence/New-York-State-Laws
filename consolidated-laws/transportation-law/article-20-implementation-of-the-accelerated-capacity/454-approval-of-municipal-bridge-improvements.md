# Transportation Law § 454 — Approval of municipal bridge improvements

[Transportation Law](../README.md) › [Article 20 — Implementation of the Accelerated Capacity and Transportation Improvements of the Nineties Bond Act](README.md)

```text
  § 454. Approval of municipal bridge improvements. When the funds for
municipal bridge improvements are appropriated to the department, such
improvements shall be undertaken only pursuant to contract with the
commissioner entered into following submission to and approval by the
commissioner of a municipal bridge improvements application therefor.
```
