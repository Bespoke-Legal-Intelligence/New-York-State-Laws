# Transportation Law § 260 — Department of public works abolished

[Transportation Law](../README.md) › [Article 12 — Laws Repealed; Transfer of Functions; Interim Functions; Time to Take Effect](README.md)

```text
  § 260. Department of public works abolished. The department of public
works in the state government, created, established and continued by
section five of the public works law, as last amended by chapter three
hundred fifty-eight of the laws of nineteen hundred sixty-one, is hereby
abolished.
```
