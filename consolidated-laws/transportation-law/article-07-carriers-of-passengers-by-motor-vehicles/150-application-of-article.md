# Transportation Law § 150 — Application of article

[Transportation Law](../README.md) › [Article 7 — Carriers of Passengers By Motor Vehicles](README.md)

```text
  § 150. Application of article. This article shall apply to common
carriers of passengers by motor vehicle and contract carriers of
passengers by motor vehicle.
```
