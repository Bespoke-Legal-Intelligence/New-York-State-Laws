# Transportation Law § 230 — Expenditure of monies

[Transportation Law](../README.md) › [Article 10-A — Implementation of Rail Preservation Bond Act of Nineteen Hundred Seventy-four and the Energy Conservation Through Improved Transportation Bond Act of Nineteen Hundred Seventy-nine](README.md)

```text
  § 230. Expenditure of monies. The monies received by the state from
the sale of bonds pursuant to the rail preservation bond act of nineteen
hundred seventy-four and the energy conservation through improved
transportation bond act of nineteen hundred seventy-nine shall be
expended pursuant to appropriation for any rail preservation project or
local street or highway project approved hereunder.
```
