# Transportation Law § 146 — Adequacy of service

[Transportation Law](../README.md) › [Article 6 — Matters Relating to All Motor Carriers](README.md)

```text
  § 146. Adequacy of service. It shall be the duty of every motor
carrier to provide adequate service, equipment and facilities under such
rules and regulations as the commissioner may prescribe.
```
