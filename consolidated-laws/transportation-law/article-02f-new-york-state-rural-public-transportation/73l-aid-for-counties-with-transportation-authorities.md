# Transportation Law § 73-L — Aid for counties with transportation authorities

[Transportation Law](../README.md) › [Article 2-F — New York State Rural Public Transportation Coordination Assistance Program](README.md)

```text
  § 73-l. Aid for counties with transportation authorities. In the event
a transportation authority encompasses one or more rural counties, each
rural county may make application for aid under this article. Any such
aid may be given to such authority provided such aid is used within the
rural county to carry out the purposes of this section.
```
