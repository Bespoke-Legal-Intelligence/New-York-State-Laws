# Transportation Law § 170 — Application of article

[Transportation Law](../README.md) › [Article 8 — Carriers of Property By Motor Vehicle](README.md)

```text
  § 170. Application of article. This article shall apply to common and
contract carriers of property by motor vehicle except common carriers
providing transportation of household goods as defined in paragraphs (a)
and (b) of subdivision fifteen of section two of this chapter.
```
