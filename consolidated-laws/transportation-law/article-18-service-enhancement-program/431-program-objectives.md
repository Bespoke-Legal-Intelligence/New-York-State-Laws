# Transportation Law § 431 — Program objectives

[Transportation Law](../README.md) › [Article 18 — Service Enhancement Program](README.md)

```text
  § 431. Program objectives. The objectives of the service enhancement
program, are:
  1. the continuation of essential rail freight and passenger service;
  2. the preservation and improvement of railroad infrastructure; and
  3. the improvement of the quality and quantity of railroad freight and
passenger service.
```
