# Transportation Law § 95 — Application of article

[Transportation Law](../README.md) › [Article 4 — Provisions Relating to Common Carriers](README.md)

```text
  § 95. Application of article. The provisions of this article shall
apply to the transportation of passengers or property from one point to
another within the state of New York, and to any common carrier
performing such service.
```
