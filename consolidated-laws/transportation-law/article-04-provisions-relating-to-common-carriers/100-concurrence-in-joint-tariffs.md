# Transportation Law § 100 — Concurrence in joint tariffs

[Transportation Law](../README.md) › [Article 4 — Provisions Relating to Common Carriers](README.md)

```text
  § 100. Concurrence in joint tariffs. The names of the several carriers
which are parties to any joint tariff shall be specified therein, and
each of the parties thereto, other than the one filing the same, shall
file with the commissioner such evidence of concurrence therein or
acceptance thereof as may be required or approved by the commissioner;
and where such evidence of concurrence or acceptance is filed, it shall
not be necessary for the carriers filing the same also to file copies of
the tariffs in which they are named as parties.
```
