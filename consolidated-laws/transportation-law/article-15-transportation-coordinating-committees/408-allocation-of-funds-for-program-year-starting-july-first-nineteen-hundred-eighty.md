# Transportation Law § 408 — Allocation of funds for program year starting July first, nineteen hundred eighty-two

[Transportation Law](../README.md) › [Article 15* — Transportation Coordinating Committees](README.md)

```text
  * § 408. Allocation of funds for program year starting July first,
nineteen hundred eighty-two. For the program year starting July first,
nineteen hundred eighty-two, the allocation of funds to be made for the
above major program elements except for special studies, shall be
determined by the council.
```
