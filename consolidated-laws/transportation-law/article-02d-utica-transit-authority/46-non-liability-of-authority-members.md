# Transportation Law § 46 — Non-liability of authority members

[Transportation Law](../README.md) › [Article 2-D — Utica Transit Authority](README.md)

```text
  § 46. Non-liability of authority members. No personal liability or
accountability shall attach to any member of the Utica transit authority
or to any person executing bonds of such authority by reason of the
execution or issuance thereof.
```
