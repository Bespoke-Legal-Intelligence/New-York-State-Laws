# Transportation Law § 57 — Signature on bonds

[Transportation Law](../README.md) › [Article 2-D — Utica Transit Authority](README.md)

```text
  § 57. Signature on bonds. In case any of the members or officers of
the Utica transit authority whose signatures appear on any bonds or
coupons shall cease to be such members or officers before the delivery
of such bonds, such signatures shall, nevertheless, be valid and
sufficient for all purposes, the same as if they had remained in office
until such delivery.
```
