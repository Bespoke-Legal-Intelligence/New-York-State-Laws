# Social Services Law § 459-E — Technical assistance

[Social Services Law](../README.md) › [Article 6-A — Domestic Violence Prevention Act](README.md)

```text
  § 459-e. Technical assistance. To the extent that funds are available,
the department shall arrange for or provide technical assistance to
residential and non-residential programs for victims of domestic
violence.  Technical assistance shall include, but shall not be limited
to, budgeting techniques, fund raising and program management.
```
