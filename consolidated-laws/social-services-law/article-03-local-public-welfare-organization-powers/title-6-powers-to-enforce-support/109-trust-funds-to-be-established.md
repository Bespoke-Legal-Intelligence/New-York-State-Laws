# Social Services Law § 109 — Trust funds to be established

[Social Services Law](../../README.md) › [Article 3 — Local Public Welfare Organization; Powers and Duties](../README.md) › [Title 6 — Powers to Enforce Support](README.md)

```text
  § 109.  Trust funds to be established.  A public welfare official
shall deposit any funds received from the seizure of property or
collection of penalties for the support of a living person.  Such fund
shall, as provided in section eighty-seven, be held and used upon such
terms as the court shall direct for the benefit of such person.  If the
beneficiary of the fund is a minor, the court may require the public
welfare official to give security and to report.
```
