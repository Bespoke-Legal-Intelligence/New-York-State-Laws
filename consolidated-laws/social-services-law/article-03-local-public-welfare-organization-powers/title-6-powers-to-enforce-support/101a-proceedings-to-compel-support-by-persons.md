# Social Services Law § 101-A — Proceedings to compel support by persons in need thereof

[Social Services Law](../../README.md) › [Article 3 — Local Public Welfare Organization; Powers and Duties](../README.md) › [Title 6 — Powers to Enforce Support](README.md)

```text
  § 101-a.  Proceedings to compel support by persons in need thereof.  A
recipient of public assistance or care or a person liable to become in
need thereof may bring proceedings in a court of competent jurisdiction
against any person responsible for his or her support as provided in
section one hundred one of this chapter to compel any such person so
responsible to provide for or contribute to such support.
```
