# Social Services Law § 82 — Accounts

[Social Services Law](../../README.md) › [Article 3 — Local Public Welfare Organization; Powers and Duties](../README.md) › [Title 5 — Records, Reports, Funds and Appropriations](README.md)

```text
  § 82.  Accounts.  All social services officials shall keep such
accounts regarding the receipt and disbursements of the social services
funds, and of any trust funds, as may be required by law or directed by
the department.
```
