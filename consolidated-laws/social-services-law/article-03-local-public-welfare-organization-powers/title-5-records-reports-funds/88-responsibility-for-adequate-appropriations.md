# Social Services Law § 88 — Responsibility for adequate appropriations

[Social Services Law](../../README.md) › [Article 3 — Local Public Welfare Organization; Powers and Duties](../README.md) › [Title 5 — Records, Reports, Funds and Appropriations](README.md)

```text
  § 88.  Responsibility for adequate appropriations.  It shall be the
duty of the board of supervisors of a county, the town board of a town
and the appropriating body of a city to make adequate appropriations and
to take such action as may be necessary to provide the public assistance
and care required by this chapter.
```
