# Social Services Law § 85 — Monthly reports of county commissioner to county treasurer

[Social Services Law](../../README.md) › [Article 3 — Local Public Welfare Organization; Powers and Duties](../README.md) › [Title 5 — Records, Reports, Funds and Appropriations](README.md)

```text
  § 85.  Monthly reports of county commissioner to county treasurer.
The county commissioner shall pay over to the county treasurer on or
before the tenth day of each month all money received by him in
connection with the work of his public welfare district and make a
report giving the source of all such receipts during the preceding
calendar month.
```
