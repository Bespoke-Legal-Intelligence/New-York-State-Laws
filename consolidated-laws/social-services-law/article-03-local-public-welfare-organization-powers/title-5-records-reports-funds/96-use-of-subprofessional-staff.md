# Social Services Law § 96 — Use of subprofessional staff

[Social Services Law](../../README.md) › [Article 3 — Local Public Welfare Organization; Powers and Duties](../README.md) › [Title 5 — Records, Reports, Funds and Appropriations](README.md)

```text
  § 96.  Use of subprofessional staff.  Any inconsistent provision of
law, general, special or local notwithstanding, a local social services
district shall provide for the training and use of subprofessional
staff, with particular emphasis on the use of public assistance
recipients in accordance with state and federal requirements.
```
