# Social Services Law § 90-A — Taxes for county public welfare districts

[Social Services Law](../../README.md) › [Article 3 — Local Public Welfare Organization; Powers and Duties](../README.md) › [Title 5 — Records, Reports, Funds and Appropriations](README.md)

```text
  § 90-a.  Taxes for county public welfare districts.  Taxes for each
form of assistance or care administered by the county public welfare
district shall be levied only in the territory in which the county
public welfare district is responsible for the administration of such
form thereof.
```
