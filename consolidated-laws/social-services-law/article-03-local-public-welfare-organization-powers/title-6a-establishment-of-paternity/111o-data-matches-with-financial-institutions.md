# Social Services Law § 111-O — Data matches with financial institutions

[Social Services Law](../../README.md) › [Article 3 — Local Public Welfare Organization; Powers and Duties](../README.md) › [Title 6-A — Establishment of Paternity and Enforcement of Support](README.md)

```text
  § 111-o.  Data matches with financial institutions.  The department or
a social services district, through the commissioner, is authorized to
enter into agreements with financial institutions as provided for in
subdivision two of section four of the banking law and subsection (e) of
section three hundred twenty of the insurance law, and is authorized to
furnish to and receive from those and any other financial institutions,
as defined in paragraph one of subdivision (d) of section four hundred
sixty-nine A of the federal social security act, such information as may
be necessary to carry out the agreements provided for in section four of
the banking law and section three hundred twenty of the insurance law,
for the enforcement of child support orders.
```
