# Social Services Law § 78 — Citizens advisory committees

[Social Services Law](../../README.md) › [Article 3 — Local Public Welfare Organization; Powers and Duties](../README.md) › [Title 4 — City Public Welfare Districts](README.md)

```text
  § 78.  Citizens advisory committees.  In accordance with federal and
state regulations, each city social services commissioner shall appoint
a citizens advisory committee or committees to exercise and perform such
functions, powers and duties as the regulations of the department may
require.
```
