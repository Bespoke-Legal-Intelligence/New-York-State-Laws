# Social Services Law § 56 — City social services districts

[Social Services Law](../../README.md) › [Article 3 — Local Public Welfare Organization; Powers and Duties](../README.md) › [Title 1 — Application](README.md)

```text
  § 56.  City social services districts.   The city of New York shall
have all the powers and duties of a social services district insofar as
consistent with the provisions of the special and local laws relating to
such city.  The officers thereof charged with the administration of
public assistance and care shall have additional powers and duties of a
commissioner of social services not inconsistent with the laws relating
to said city.
```
