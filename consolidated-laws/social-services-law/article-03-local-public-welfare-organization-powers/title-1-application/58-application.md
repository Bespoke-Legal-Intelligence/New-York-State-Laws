# Social Services Law § 58 — Application

[Social Services Law](../../README.md) › [Article 3 — Local Public Welfare Organization; Powers and Duties](../README.md) › [Title 1 — Application](README.md)

```text
  § 58.  Application.  Nothing in this chapter shall be deemed to take
away the jurisdiction or any power or duty of the family court, the
department, the state department of education or the state department of
health.
```
