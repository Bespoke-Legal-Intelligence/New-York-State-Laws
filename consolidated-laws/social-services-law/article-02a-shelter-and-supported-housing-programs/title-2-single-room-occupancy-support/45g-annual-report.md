# Social Services Law § 45-G — Annual report

[Social Services Law](../../README.md) › [Article 2-A — Shelter and Supported Housing Programs](../README.md) › [Title 2 — Single Room Occupancy Support Services Program](README.md)

```text
  § 45-g. Annual report. Beginning October first, nineteen hundred
eighty-eight, and each year thereafter, the commissioner shall report to
the governor and legislature on the success of the single room occupancy
support services program in assisting eligible residents to live
independently.
```
