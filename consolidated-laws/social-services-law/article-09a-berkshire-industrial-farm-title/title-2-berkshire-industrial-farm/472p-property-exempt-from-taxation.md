# Social Services Law § 472-P — Property exempt from taxation

[Social Services Law](../../README.md) › [Article 9-A — Berkshire Industrial Farm Title](../README.md) › [Title 2 — Berkshire Industrial Farm](README.md)

```text
  § 472-p.  Property exempt from taxation.  So long as the property of
said corporation shall be used for charitable purposes only, such
property, both real and personal, shall be exempt from taxation.
```
