# Social Services Law § 472-O — Reports

[Social Services Law](../../README.md) › [Article 9-A — Berkshire Industrial Farm Title](../README.md) › [Title 2 — Berkshire Industrial Farm](README.md)

```text
  § 472-o.  Reports.  The said corporation shall annually, on or before
the fifteenth day of January, report to the legislature the number and
names of the children in its custody or under its guardianship, their
age, residence, occupation, state of education, together with the
changes in these particulars during the preceding year; the receipts and
expenditures and the financial condition of the corporation, and an
account of its general operations.
```
