# Social Services Law § 472-K — Placement of children in care of corporation; effect of placement

[Social Services Law](../../README.md) › [Article 9-A — Berkshire Industrial Farm Title](../README.md) › [Title 2 — Berkshire Industrial Farm](README.md)

```text
  § 472-k.  Placement of children in care of corporation; effect of
placement.  The family court may commit or place in said corporation,
with its consent, any child adjudicated to be a juvenile delinquent or a
person in need of supervision.  Such commitment or placement in said
corporation shall be to the custody and control thereof for the period
provided in the order of commitment or placement.
```
