# Social Services Law § 472-L — Transfer of certain children by agencies

[Social Services Law](../../README.md) › [Article 9-A — Berkshire Industrial Farm Title](../README.md) › [Title 2 — Berkshire Industrial Farm](README.md)

```text
  § 472-l. Transfer of certain children by agencies. The corporate
authorities of any agency now or hereafter having the lawful custody and
care of any child not less than six years of age, and not awaiting trial
nor under sentence for a term of years for crime, may, with the consent
of said corporation, transfer and assign such custody and care to this
corporation upon such terms as the directors of such institution and
said corporation may agree.
```
