# Social Services Law § 461-Q — Temperature standards in adult homes, enriched housing programs and residences for adults

[Social Services Law](../../README.md) › [Article 7 — Residential Care Programs For Adults and Children](../README.md) › [Title 2 — Residential Programs For Adults](README.md)

```text
  § 461-q. Temperature standards in adult homes, enriched housing
programs and residences for adults. The commissioner of health shall
promulgate rules and regulations with respect to an allowable
temperature in all areas occupied by residents of an adult home,
enriched housing program and residence for adults, including any areas
intended for use by its residents in common including auditoriums,
meeting rooms and cafeterias.  One common room in such adult home,
enriched housing program and residence for adults shall be required to
be air conditioned.
```
