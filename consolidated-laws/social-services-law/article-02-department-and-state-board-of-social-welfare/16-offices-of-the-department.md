# Social Services Law § 16 — Offices of the Department

[Social Services Law](../README.md) › [Article 2 — Department and State Board of Social Welfare](README.md)

```text
  § 16.  Offices of the Department.  The department shall be provided
with suitably furnished rooms for its offices.  The principal office of
the department shall be in the county of Albany.
```
