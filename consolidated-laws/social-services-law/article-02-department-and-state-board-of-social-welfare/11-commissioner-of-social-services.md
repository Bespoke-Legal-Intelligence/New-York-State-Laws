# Social Services Law § 11 — Commissioner of social services

[Social Services Law](../README.md) › [Article 2 — Department and State Board of Social Welfare](README.md)

```text
  § 11.  Commissioner of social services. The chief executive and
administrative officer of the department shall be the commissioner of
social services, who shall be appointed by the governor with the advice
and consent of the senate.
```
