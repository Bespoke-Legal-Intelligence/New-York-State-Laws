# Social Services Law § 38 — Commission for the blind

[Social Services Law](../README.md) › [Article 2 — Department and State Board of Social Welfare](README.md)

```text
  § 38. Commission for the blind. The New York state commission for the
blind shall continue to exercise and perform its duties, as prescribed
by law, and the regulations of the office of children and family
services subject to the supervision and control of the commissioner; and
such commission shall be a bureau of the office of children and family
services.
```
