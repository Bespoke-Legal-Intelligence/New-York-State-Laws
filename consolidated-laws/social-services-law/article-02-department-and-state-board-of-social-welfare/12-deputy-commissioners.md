# Social Services Law § 12 — Deputy commissioners

[Social Services Law](../README.md) › [Article 2 — Department and State Board of Social Welfare](README.md)

```text
  § 12.  Deputy commissioners.  The Commissioner shall appoint and
remove at pleasure a first deputy commissioner, and such other deputy
commissioners as he shall deem necessary for the proper organization and
general classification of the work of the department, each of whom shall
be in the exempt class of the civil service.
```
