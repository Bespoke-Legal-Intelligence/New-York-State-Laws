# Social Services Law § 37 — Reports of and to department

[Social Services Law](../README.md) › [Article 2 — Department and State Board of Social Welfare](README.md)

```text
  § 37.  Reports of and to department.  The department may collect and,
so far as it shall deem advantageous, embody in its annual reports
information relating to the best manner of dealing with those who
require assistance from public funds, or who receive aid from private
charity and represent its views as to the best methods of caring for
dependent children and such other matters as the commissioner may deem
advisable.
```
