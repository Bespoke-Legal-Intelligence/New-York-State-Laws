# Social Services Law § 483-A — Utilization of other agency assistance

[Social Services Law](../README.md) › [Article 10-C — State Council On Children and Families](README.md)

```text
  § 483-a. Utilization of other agency assistance. To effectuate the
purposes of this article, any department, division, board, bureau,
commission or agency of the state or of any political subdivision
thereof shall, at the request of the chair, provide to the council such
facilities, assistance and data as will enable the council properly to
carry out its powers and duties and those of the chair.
```
