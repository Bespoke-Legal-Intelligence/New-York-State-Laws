# Social Services Law § 118-C — Family court cases; state charges

[Social Services Law](../README.md) › [Article 4 — Residence and Removal](README.md)

```text
  § 118-c. Family court cases; state charges. In the event a child who
is residing on any Native American reservation within the state is
committed or placed by a court of competent jurisdiction as a
delinquent, abused, neglected, or abandoned child, or person in need of
supervision, or upon any ground specified in section three hundred
eighty-four-b of this chapter, the state, shall reimburse the social
services district from which the child was committed for the full cost
of care and maintenance of the child.
```
