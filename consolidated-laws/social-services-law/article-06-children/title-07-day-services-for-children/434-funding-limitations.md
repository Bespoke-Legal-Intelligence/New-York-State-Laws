# Social Services Law § 434 — Funding limitations

[Social Services Law](../../README.md) › [Article 6 — Children](../README.md) › [Title 7 — Day Services For Children and Families](README.md)

```text
  § 434.  Funding limitations.  Notwithstanding any other provisions of
this chapter the total amount of reimbursement to social services
districts pursuant to this title shall be limited to the amount of the
annual appropriation made by the legislature for preventive services.
```
