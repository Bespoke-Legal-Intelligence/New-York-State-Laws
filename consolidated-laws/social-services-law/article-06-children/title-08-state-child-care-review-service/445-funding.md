# Social Services Law § 445 — Funding

[Social Services Law](../../README.md) › [Article 6 — Children](../README.md) › [Title 8 — State Child Care Review Service](README.md)

```text
  § 445.  Funding.  The department shall explore the possibility of, and
is authorized to take steps necessary to qualify for any available
funding from any private source or from the federal government, and is
authorized to use any funds for which the state qualifies for the
purposes of the design, establishment or operation of the service.
```
