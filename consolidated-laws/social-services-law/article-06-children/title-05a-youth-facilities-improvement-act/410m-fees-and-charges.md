# Social Services Law § 410-M — Fees and charges

[Social Services Law](../../README.md) › [Article 6 — Children](../README.md) › [Title 5-A — Youth Facilities Improvement Act](README.md)

```text
  § 410-m.  Fees and charges.  The commissioner may, by regulation,
establish and charge to eligible borrowers such fees and charges for
inspection, regulation, supervision and audit as to the commissioner may
appear just and reasonable in order to recover the departmental costs in
performing these functions.
```
