# Social Services Law § 371-A — Procedure

[Social Services Law](../../README.md) › [Article 6 — Children](../README.md) › [Title 1 — Care and Protection of Children](README.md)

```text
  § 371-a.  Procedure.  In any proceeding commenced pursuant to this
chapter in which the family court has exercised jurisdiction, the
provisions of articles one, two and eleven of the family court act shall
apply to the extent that they do not conflict with the specific
provisions of the social services law.
```
