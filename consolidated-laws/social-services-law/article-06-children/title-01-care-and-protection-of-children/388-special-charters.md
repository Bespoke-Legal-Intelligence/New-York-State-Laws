# Social Services Law § 388 — Special charters

[Social Services Law](../../README.md) › [Article 6 — Children](../README.md) › [Title 1 — Care and Protection of Children](README.md)

```text
  § 388.  Special charters.  The power and authority given to agencies,
associations, corporations, institutions and societies in their charters
shall not be abrogated or nullified, except as the same are in conflict
with this title.
```
