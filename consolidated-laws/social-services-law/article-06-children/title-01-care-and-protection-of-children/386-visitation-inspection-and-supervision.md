# Social Services Law § 386 — Visitation; inspection and supervision

[Social Services Law](../../README.md) › [Article 6 — Children](../README.md) › [Title 1 — Care and Protection of Children](README.md)

```text
  § 386. Visitation; inspection and supervision. 1. The board or the
department is authorized to visit, in its discretion, any minor under
the age of twenty-one years committed, placed out or boarded out and not
legally adopted or in the custody of a legal guardian.
  2. The board or the department is authorized to visit, in its
discretion, any home or place where a child or children are received,
boarded or kept under a license or certificate whether or not such
children are maintained as public charges. Every licensed home shall, if
practicable, be visited by the department at least four times in each
year.
```
