# Social Services Law § 380 — Boarding and free homes; records

[Social Services Law](../../README.md) › [Article 6 — Children](../README.md) › [Title 1 — Care and Protection of Children](README.md)

```text
  § 380.  Boarding and free homes; records.  Every person who receives,
boards or keeps a child and/or minor under a license or certificate
shall keep a record in a register to be provided by the department
showing the name, date of birth and religious faith of each child and/or
minor received, the names and addresses of his parents or guardian or of
the authorized agency from whom received and of the person by whom
placed and by whom removed, the dates of reception and removal and such
other information as may be required by the department.
```
