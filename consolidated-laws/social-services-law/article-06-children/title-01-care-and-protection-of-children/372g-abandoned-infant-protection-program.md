# Social Services Law § 372-G — Abandoned infant protection program

[Social Services Law](../../README.md) › [Article 6 — Children](../README.md) › [Title 1 — Care and Protection of Children](README.md)

```text
  § 372-g. Abandoned infant protection program. The office of children
and family services shall develop and implement a public information
program to inform the general public of the provisions of the abandoned
infant protection act. The program may include but not be limited to the
following elements:
  1. educational and informational materials in print, audio, video,
electronic, or other media;
  2. public service announcements and advertisements; and
  3. establishment of toll-free telephone hotlines to provide
information.
```
