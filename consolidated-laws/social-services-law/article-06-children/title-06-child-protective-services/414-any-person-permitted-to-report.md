# Social Services Law § 414 — Any person permitted to report

[Social Services Law](../../README.md) › [Article 6 — Children](../README.md) › [Title 6 — Child Protective Services](README.md)

```text
  § 414.  Any person permitted to report.  In addition to those persons
and officials required to report suspected child abuse or maltreatment,
any person may make such a report if such person has reasonable cause to
suspect that a child is an abused or maltreated child.
```
