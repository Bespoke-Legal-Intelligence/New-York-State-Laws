# Social Services Law § 428 — Separability

[Social Services Law](../../README.md) › [Article 6 — Children](../README.md) › [Title 6 — Child Protective Services](README.md)

```text
  § 428.  Separability.  If any provision of this title or the
application thereof to any person or circumstances is held to be
invalid, the remainder of the act and the application of such provision
to other persons or circumstances shall not be affected thereby.
```
