# Social Services Law § 409-L — Advisory board

[Social Services Law](../../README.md) › [Article 6 — Children](../README.md) › [Title 4-B — Services; Pregnant Adolescents](README.md)

```text
  § 409-l. Advisory board. An advisory board shall be established by the
commissioner to assist in the development and implementation of the case
management provisions. It will be the responsibility of this board to
assist in the development of various service models that local social
services districts shall employ, assist in the formulation of
appropriate procedures for evaluating such case management services, and
determine the fiscal impact of such services as defined in section four
hundred nine-j of this title.  Appointments to the advisory board shall
be obtained from public and private organizations providing teenage and
child welfare services.
```
