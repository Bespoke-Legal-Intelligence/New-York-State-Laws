# Social Services Law § 355 — Rules, regulations and forms

[Social Services Law](../../README.md) › [Article 5 — Assistance and Care](../README.md) › [Title 10 — Aid to Dependent Children](README.md)

```text
  § 355.  Rules, regulations and forms.  The department shall  1.
supervise the administration of family assistance,
  2.  prescribe the form of and print and supply to the public welfare
officials blanks for applications, reports, affidavits and such other
forms as it may deem advisable,
  3.  make regulations necessary for the carrying out of the provisions
of this title to the end that allowances may be granted and the
provisions of this title administered uniformly throughout the state.
```
