# Social Services Law § 360 — Real property of legally responsible relatives

[Social Services Law](../../README.md) › [Article 5 — Assistance and Care](../README.md) › [Title 10 — Aid to Dependent Children](README.md)

```text
  § 360. Real property of legally responsible relatives. The ownership
of real property by an applicant or applicants, recipient or recipients
who is or are legally responsible relatives of the child or children for
whose benefit the application is made or the aid is granted, whether
such ownership be individual or joint as tenants in common, tenants by
the entirety or joint tenants, shall not preclude the granting of family
assistance or the continuance thereof if he or they are without the
necessary funds to maintain himself, herself or themselves and such
child or children.
```
