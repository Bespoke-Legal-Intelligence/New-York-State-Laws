# Social Services Law § 357 — Quarterly estimates

[Social Services Law](../../README.md) › [Article 5 — Assistance and Care](../README.md) › [Title 10 — Aid to Dependent Children](README.md)

```text
  § 357. Quarterly estimates. Each social services district shall submit
to the department quarterly estimates of its anticipated  expenditures
for family assistance and administrative expenses not less than thirty
days before  the first day of each of the quarters beginning on the
first day of the months of July, October, January and April, in such
form and together with such other information as the department may
require.
```
