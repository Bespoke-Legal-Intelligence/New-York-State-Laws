# Social Services Law § 365-O — Provision and coverage of services for living organ donors

[Social Services Law](../../README.md) › [Article 5 — Assistance and Care](../README.md) › [Title 11 — Medical Assistance For Needy Persons](README.md)

```text
  * § 365-o. Provision and coverage of services for living organ donors.
This section applies in the case of a living donor under section
forty-three hundred seventy-one of the public health law who is
otherwise eligible for medical assistance under this article. Services
for that person covered under that section that would otherwise be
health care services under this article shall be paid for under this
article and not under such section, provided federal financial
participation is available.
  * NB Effective April 1, 2023
```
