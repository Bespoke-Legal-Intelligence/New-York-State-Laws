# Social Services Law § 152-C — Feminine hygiene products

[Social Services Law](../../README.md) › [Article 5 — Assistance and Care](../README.md) › [Title 1 — General Provisions](README.md)

```text
  § 152-c. Feminine hygiene products. Any provider of temporary housing
assistance, which shall include but not be limited to, a family shelter,
a shelter for adults, a hotel, an emergency apartment, a domestic
violence shelter, a runaway and homeless youth shelter, or a safe house
for refugees, shall be required to provide feminine hygiene products,
including, but not limited to, sanitary napkins, tampons and panty
liners, at no cost to menstruating individuals.
```
