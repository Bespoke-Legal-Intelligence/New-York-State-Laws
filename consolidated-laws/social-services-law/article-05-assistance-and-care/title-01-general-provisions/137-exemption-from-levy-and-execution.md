# Social Services Law § 137 — Exemption from levy and execution

[Social Services Law](../../README.md) › [Article 5 — Assistance and Care](../README.md) › [Title 1 — General Provisions](README.md)

```text
  § 137. Exemption from levy and execution.  All moneys or orders
granted to persons as public assistance or care pursuant to this chapter
shall be inalienable by any assignment or transfer and shall be exempt
from levy and execution under the laws of this state.
```
