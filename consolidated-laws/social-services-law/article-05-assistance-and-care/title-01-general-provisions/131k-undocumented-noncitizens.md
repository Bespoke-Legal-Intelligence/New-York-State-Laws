# Social Services Law § 131-K — Undocumented noncitizens

[Social Services Law](../../README.md) › [Article 5 — Assistance and Care](../README.md) › [Title 1 — General Provisions](README.md)

```text
  § 131-k. Undocumented noncitizens. An otherwise eligible applicant or
recipient who has been determined to be ineligible for aid to dependent
children, home relief or medical assistance because such individual is a
noncitizen unlawfully residing in the United States or because such
individual failed to furnish evidence that such individual is lawfully
residing in the United States shall be immediately referred to the
United States immigration and naturalization service, or the nearest
consulate of the country of the applicant or the recipient for such
service or consulate to take appropriate action or furnish assistance.
```
