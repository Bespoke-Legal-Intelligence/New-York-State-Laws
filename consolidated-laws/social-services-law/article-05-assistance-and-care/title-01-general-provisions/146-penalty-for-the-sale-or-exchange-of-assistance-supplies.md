# Social Services Law § 146 — Penalty for the sale or exchange of assistance supplies

[Social Services Law](../../README.md) › [Article 5 — Assistance and Care](../README.md) › [Title 1 — General Provisions](README.md)

```text
  § 146. Penalty for the sale or exchange of assistance supplies.  1.
Any person who shall sell or exchange supplies or articles furnished him
as assistance or care by any public welfare official or employee of the
public welfare district, or dispose of them in any other way than as
directed, shall be guilty of a misdemeanor.
  2. Any person who purchases any article knowing it to have been
furnished to any person as assistance or care shall also be guilty of a
misdemeanor.
```
