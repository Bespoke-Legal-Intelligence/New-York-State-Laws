# Social Services Law § 131-K — Illegal aliens

[Social Services Law](../../README.md) › [Article 5 — Assistance and Care](../README.md) › [Title 1 — General Provisions](README.md)

```text
  § 131-k. Illegal aliens.  2. An otherwise eligible applicant or
recipient who has been determined to be ineligible for aid to dependent
children, home relief or medical assistance because he is an alien
unlawfully residing in the United States or because he failed to furnish
evidence that he is lawfully residing in the United States shall be
immediately referred to the United States immigration and naturalization
service, or the nearest consulate of the country of the applicant or the
recipient for such service or consulate to take appropriate action or
furnish assistance.
```
