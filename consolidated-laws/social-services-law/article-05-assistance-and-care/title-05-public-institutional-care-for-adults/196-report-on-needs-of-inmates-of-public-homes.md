# Social Services Law § 196 — Report on needs of inmates of public homes

[Social Services Law](../../README.md) › [Article 5 — Assistance and Care](../README.md) › [Title 5 — Public Institutional Care For Adults](README.md)

```text
  § 196. Report on needs of inmates of public homes. It shall be the
duty of the commissioner of public welfare to report to the legislative
body as to the needs of the home and to make recommendations of any
changes, improvements, additional equipment or other provision which he
may consider necessary to provide adequate care for the inmates.
```
