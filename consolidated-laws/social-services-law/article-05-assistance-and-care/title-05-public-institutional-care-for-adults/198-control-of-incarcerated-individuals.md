# Social Services Law § 198 — Control of incarcerated individuals

[Social Services Law](../../README.md) › [Article 5 — Assistance and Care](../README.md) › [Title 5 — Public Institutional Care For Adults](README.md)

```text
  § 198. Control of incarcerated individuals. If any incarcerated
individual shall wilfully disobey the rules of the home in such a way as
to be detrimental to the welfare of the other incarcerated individuals,
the commissioner may institute a proceeding in a court of competent
jurisdiction against such incarcerated individual for disorderly
conduct.
```
