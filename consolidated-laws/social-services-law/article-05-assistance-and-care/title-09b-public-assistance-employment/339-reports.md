# Social Services Law § 339 — Reports

[Social Services Law](../../README.md) › [Article 5 — Assistance and Care](../README.md) › [Title 9-B — Public Assistance Employment Programs](README.md)

```text
  § 339. Reports.  The commissioner shall require, from each social
services district, information and reports sufficient and timely to
enable the state to meet all federal reporting requirements.
```
