# Social Services Law § 340 — Technical assistance

[Social Services Law](../../README.md) › [Article 5 — Assistance and Care](../README.md) › [Title 9-B — Public Assistance Employment Programs](README.md)

```text
  § 340. Technical assistance.  The commissioner shall provide technical
assistance to social services districts in the development,
implementation and administration of plans described in section three
hundred thirty-three of this title.
  The department shall monitor the implementation and ongoing operation
of the programs pursuant to this title.  Such monitoring shall include
review of sample cases to assess compliance with applicable state and
federal statutes and regulations.
```
