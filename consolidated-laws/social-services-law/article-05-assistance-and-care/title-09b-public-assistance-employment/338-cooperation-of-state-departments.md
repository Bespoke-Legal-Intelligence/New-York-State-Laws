# Social Services Law § 338 — Cooperation of state departments

[Social Services Law](../../README.md) › [Article 5 — Assistance and Care](../README.md) › [Title 9-B — Public Assistance Employment Programs](README.md)

```text
  § 338. Cooperation of state departments.  There shall be such
cooperative agreements and arrangements between and among the
department, the state department of social services or its successor
agency, the state department of education and other appropriate state
departments as shall be necessary to assure compliance with federal and
state requirements or requirements of this title relating to employment
programs for applicants of and recipients of public assistance and to
assure that the purposes of such program will be effectively
accomplished.  The purpose of such arrangements is to promote
interagency planning, coordinate service delivery and identify needed
resources for the implementation of such employment programs.
```
