# Social Services Law § 178 — Veteran assistance financing; state reimbursement

[Social Services Law](../../README.md) › [Article 5 — Assistance and Care](../README.md) › [Title 4 — Veteran Assistance](README.md)

```text
  § 178. Veteran assistance financing; state reimbursement.  All
provisions of this chapter or of any other law relating to the financing
of safety net assistance by counties, cities and towns, or reimbursement
by the state for safety net assistance expenditures made by counties,
cities and towns, shall apply with the same force and effect to veteran
assistance.
```
