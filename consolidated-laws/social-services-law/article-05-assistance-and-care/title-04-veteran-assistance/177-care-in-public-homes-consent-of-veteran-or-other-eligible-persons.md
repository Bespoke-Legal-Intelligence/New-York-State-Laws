# Social Services Law § 177 — Care in public homes; consent of veteran or other eligible persons

[Social Services Law](../../README.md) › [Article 5 — Assistance and Care](../README.md) › [Title 4 — Veteran Assistance](README.md)

```text
  § 177. Care in public homes; consent of veteran or other eligible
persons. Any person eligible for veteran assistance may receive care in
a public home with his consent.
```
