# Social Services Law § 163 — Application

[Social Services Law](../../README.md) › [Article 5 — Assistance and Care](../README.md) › [Title 3 — Home Relief](README.md)

```text
  § 163. Application. The provisions of any city charter or other local
or special law forbidding the granting of outdoor relief shall not apply
to the granting of home relief under this chapter.
```
