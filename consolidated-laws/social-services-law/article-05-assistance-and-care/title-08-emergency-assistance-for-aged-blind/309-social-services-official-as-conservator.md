# Social Services Law § 309 — Social services official as conservator

[Social Services Law](../../README.md) › [Article 5 — Assistance and Care](../README.md) › [Title 8 — Emergency Assistance For Aged, Blind and Disabled Persons](README.md)

```text
  § 309. Social services official as conservator. In appropriate cases,
a social services official shall initiate a special proceeding for the
appointment of a conservator pursuant to section 77.03 of the mental
hygiene law.
```
