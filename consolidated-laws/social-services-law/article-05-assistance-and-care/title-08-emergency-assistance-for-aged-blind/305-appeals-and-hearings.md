# Social Services Law § 305 — Appeals and hearings

[Social Services Law](../../README.md) › [Article 5 — Assistance and Care](../README.md) › [Title 8 — Emergency Assistance For Aged, Blind and Disabled Persons](README.md)

```text
  § 305. Appeals and hearings. If an application for assistance under
this title is not promptly acted upon or is denied or the assistance
granted is deemed inadequate by the applicant or recipient, he may
appeal to the department in accordance with the provisions of section
twenty-two of this chapter. In scheduling investigations, local social
services districts shall give priority to applications for assistance
under this title.
```
