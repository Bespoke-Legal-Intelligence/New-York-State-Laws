# Social Services Law § 308 — Services

[Social Services Law](../../README.md) › [Article 5 — Assistance and Care](../README.md) › [Title 8 — Emergency Assistance For Aged, Blind and Disabled Persons](README.md)

```text
  § 308. Services. An application for emergency assistance under this
title shall be deemed an application for services pursuant to section
two hundred fifty-three of this chapter, and services shall be provided
according to the person's eligibility therefor in accordance with title
seven of article five of this chapter.
```
