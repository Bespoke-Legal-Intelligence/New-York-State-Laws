# Social Services Law § 306 — Records and reports

[Social Services Law](../../README.md) › [Article 5 — Assistance and Care](../README.md) › [Title 8 — Emergency Assistance For Aged, Blind and Disabled Persons](README.md)

```text
  § 306. Records and reports. Each social services official shall keep
such records and make such reports to the department at such times and
in such manner as may be required by department regulations.
```
