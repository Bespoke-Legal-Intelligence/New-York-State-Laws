# Social Services Law § 465 — Nutrition outreach and public education program; establishment

[Social Services Law](../README.md) › [Article 8-A — Nutrition Outreach and Public Education Program](README.md)

```text
  § 465. Nutrition outreach and public education program; establishment.
The nutrition outreach and public education program is established
within the office of temporary and disability assistance. Such program
is established to ensure maximum participation by eligible persons in
federal and state food assistance programs. The program shall be
structured so as to increase participation statewide but with particular
attention to high risk areas with a focus on certain at risk
populations.
```
