# Lien Law § 162 — Copy of statement and certificate to be posted

[Lien Law](../README.md) › [Article 7 — Liens For Service of Stallions or Bulls](README.md)

```text
  § 162. Copy of statement and certificate to be posted. The person
having the custody and control of such stallion or bull, shall post a
written or printed copy of such statement and certificate in a
conspicuous place in each locality in which said stallion or bull is
kept for service.
```
