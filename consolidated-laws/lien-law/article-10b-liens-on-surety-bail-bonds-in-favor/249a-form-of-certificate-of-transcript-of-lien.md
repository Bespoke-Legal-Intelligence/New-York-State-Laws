# Lien Law § 249-A — Form of certificate of transcript of lien

[Lien Law](../README.md) › [Article 10-B — Liens On Surety Bail Bonds In Favor of the People of the State of New York](README.md)

```text
  § 249-a. Form of certificate of transcript of lien. The recording
officer shall upon request issue a certificate or transcript of any
criminal surety bond lien on file in his office on the following or
substantially similar form, and the fee to be paid to the recording
officer for such certificate or transcript shall be fifty cents, except
that this fee shall not apply to state or county officers as related in
section two hundred forty-nine of the lien law. The certificate or
transcript shall be substantially in the following form:
```
