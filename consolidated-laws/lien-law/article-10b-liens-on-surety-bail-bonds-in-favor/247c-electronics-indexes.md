# Lien Law § 247-C — Electronics indexes

[Lien Law](../README.md) › [Article 10-B — Liens On Surety Bail Bonds In Favor of the People of the State of New York](README.md)

```text
  § 247-c. Electronics indexes. A county clerk may adopt a new indexing
system utilizing electro-mechanical, electronic or any other method he
deems suitable for maintaining the indexes.
```
