# Lien Law § 249-B — Certificate or transcript of discharge of lien

[Lien Law](../README.md) › [Article 10-B — Liens On Surety Bail Bonds In Favor of the People of the State of New York](README.md)

```text
  § 249-b. Certificate or transcript of discharge of lien. The recording
officer shall upon request issue a certificate or transcript of a
certificate of discharge of a criminal bail surety bond lien on file in
his office on the following or substantially similar form, and the fee
to be paid to the recording officer for such certificate or transcript
of discharge shall be fifty cents, except that this fee shall not apply
to state or county officers as related in section two hundred forty-nine
of the lien law. The certificate or transcript form shall be
substantially in the following form:
No. ......
STATE OF NEW YORK,  )
                    ) ss:
COUNTY OF.......... )
  I,  ................. Register of the county of ............ DO HEREBY
CERTIFY, that a certain Criminal Bail Bond Lien No. ........ dated  this
......  day  of  .........  19...., made by ............ Sureties, to the
People of the State of New York as a lien against Real Property, located
in  Sections,  ........  Blocks  ........,  known  as  No.  ............
Street/Avenue,  in the County of ............., as bail in the amount of
........... Dollars, and signed by ..................  District  Attorney
of  the  County  of  ................  and  filed  in  my  office on the
...... day of ........... 19..., IS DISCHARGED OF RECORD IN THIS OFFICE.
  Given  under  my  hand  and  Official  Seal,   this   .....   day   of
.................... 19....,
                     ..................................  Register.
                     ..................................  Dep'y Register.
```
