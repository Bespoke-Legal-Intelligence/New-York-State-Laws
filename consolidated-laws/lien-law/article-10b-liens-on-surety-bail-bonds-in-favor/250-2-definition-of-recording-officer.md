# Lien Law § 250*2 — Definition of recording officer

[Lien Law](../README.md) › [Article 10-B — Liens On Surety Bail Bonds In Favor of the People of the State of New York](README.md)

*An asterisk in a number (for example Article 24\* or § 20-C\*2) is the Senate's mark for a number the Legislature has used more than once: the marked article or section is a different one from the unmarked one with the same number. \*2 means the second one enacted with that number.*

```text
  * § 250. Definition of recording officer. Wherever in this article,
the term "recording officer" is used, it shall mean the county clerk of
the county, except that in a county having a register, it shall mean the
register.
  * NB There are 2 § 250's
```
