# Lien Law § 245 — Purpose of article

[Lien Law](../README.md) › [Article 10-A — Liens For Taxes Payable to the United States of America and Other Federal Liens](README.md)

```text
  § 245. Purpose of article. This article is passed for the purpose of
authorizing the filing of notices of liens in accordance with the
provisions of the statutes of the United States of America applicable
thereto.
```
