# Lien Law § 243 — Fees

[Lien Law](../README.md) › [Article 10-A — Liens For Taxes Payable to the United States of America and Other Federal Liens](README.md)

```text
  § 243. Fees. The fee to be paid to the secretary of state or a clerk
or register for filing and indexing each notice of lien or certificate
or notice affecting any such lien shall be determined in accordance with
the provisions of section ninety-six-a of the executive law. Such
officers shall bill the district directors of internal revenue or other
appropriate federal officers on a monthly basis for fees for documents
filed by them.
```
