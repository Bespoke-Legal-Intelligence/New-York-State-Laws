# Lien Law § 23 — Construction of article

[Lien Law](../README.md) › [Article 2 — Mechanics' Liens](README.md)

```text
  § 23. Construction of article. This article is to be construed
liberally to secure the beneficial interests and purposes thereof. A
substantial compliance with its several provisions shall be sufficient
for the validity of a lien and to give jurisdiction to the courts to
enforce the same.
```
