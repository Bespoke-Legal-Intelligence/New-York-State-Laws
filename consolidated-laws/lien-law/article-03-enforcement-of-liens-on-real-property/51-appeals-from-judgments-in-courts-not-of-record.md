# Lien Law § 51 — Appeals from judgments in courts not of record

[Lien Law](../README.md) › [Article 3 — Enforcement of Liens On Real Property](README.md)

```text
  § 51. Appeals from judgments in courts not of record. An appeal may be
taken from such judgment rendered in a court not of record, according to
the provisions of law regulating appeals from judgments in actions on
contract in such courts.
```
