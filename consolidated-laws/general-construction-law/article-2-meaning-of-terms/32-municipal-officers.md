# General Construction Law § 32 — Municipal officers

[General Construction Law](../README.md) › [Article 2 — Meaning of Terms](README.md)

```text
  § 32. Municipal officers. A reference to several officers of a
municipal corporation holding the same office, or to a board of such
officers, shall be deemed to refer to the single officer holding such
office, when but one person is chosen to fill such office in pursuance
of law.
```
