# General Construction Law § 28-A — Mandate

[General Construction Law](../README.md) › [Article 2 — Meaning of Terms](README.md)

```text
  § 28-a. Mandate. "Mandate" includes a writ, process or other written
direction, issued pursuant to law, out of a court, or made pursuant to
law, by a court, a judge or person acting as a judicial officer, and
commanding a court, board or other body, or an officer or other person,
named or otherwise designated therein, to do or to refrain from doing an
act therein specified.
```
