# General Construction Law § 47 — State

[General Construction Law](../README.md) › [Article 2 — Meaning of Terms](README.md)

```text
  § 47. State. The term state, when used generally to include every
state of the United States, includes also every territory of the United
States and the District of Columbia.
```
