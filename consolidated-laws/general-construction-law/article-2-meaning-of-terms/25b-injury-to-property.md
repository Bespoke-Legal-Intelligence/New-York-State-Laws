# General Construction Law § 25-B — Injury to property

[General Construction Law](../README.md) › [Article 2 — Meaning of Terms](README.md)

```text
  § 25-b. Injury to property. "Injury to property" is an actionable act,
whereby the estate of another is lessened, other than a personal injury,
or the breach of a contract.
```
