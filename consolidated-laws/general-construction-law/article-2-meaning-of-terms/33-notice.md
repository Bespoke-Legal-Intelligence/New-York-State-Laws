# General Construction Law § 33 — Notice

[General Construction Law](../README.md) › [Article 2 — Meaning of Terms](README.md)

```text
  § 33. Notice. When a notice is required to be given to a board or
body, service of such notice upon the clerk or chairman thereof shall be
sufficient.
```
