# General Construction Law § 11-A — Acquisition

[General Construction Law](../README.md) › [Article 2 — Meaning of Terms](README.md)

```text
§ 11-a.  Acquisition.  The term "acquisition" means the act of acquiring
any title to, right or interest in, real property through the exercise
of the power of eminent domain pursuant to the provisions of the eminent
domain procedure law.
```
