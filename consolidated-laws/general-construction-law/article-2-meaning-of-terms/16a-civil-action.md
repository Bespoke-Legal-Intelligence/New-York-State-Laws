# General Construction Law § 16-A — Civil action

[General Construction Law](../README.md) › [Article 2 — Meaning of Terms](README.md)

```text
§ 16-a. Civil action. The term "civil action" means any action except
when prosecuted in the name of the people of the state of New York, as
plaintiffs against a party charged with crime.
```
