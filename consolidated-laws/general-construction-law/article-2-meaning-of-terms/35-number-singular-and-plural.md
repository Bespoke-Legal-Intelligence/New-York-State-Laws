# General Construction Law § 35 — Number, singular and plural

[General Construction Law](../README.md) › [Article 2 — Meaning of Terms](README.md)

```text
  § 35. Number, singular and plural. Words in the singular number
include the plural, and in the plural number include the singular.
```
