# General Construction Law § 18 — Consolidated laws

[General Construction Law](../README.md) › [Article 2 — Meaning of Terms](README.md)

```text
  § 18. Consolidated laws. The term Consolidated Laws shall mean the
compilation of the statutes prepared by the board of statutory
consolidation and the amendments thereof.
```
