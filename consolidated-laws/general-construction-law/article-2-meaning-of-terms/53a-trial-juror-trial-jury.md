# General Construction Law § 53-A — Trial juror; trial jury

[General Construction Law](../README.md) › [Article 2 — Meaning of Terms](README.md)

```text
  § 53-a. Trial juror; trial jury. The terms "trial juror" and "trial
jury", are respectively equivalent to the terms "petit juror", and
"petit jury", as used in the constitution and laws of the state.
```
