# General Construction Law § 12 — Affidavit

[General Construction Law](../README.md) › [Article 2 — Meaning of Terms](README.md)

```text
§ 12. Affidavit. When an affidavit is authorized or required it may be
sworn to before any officer authorized by law to take the acknowledgment
of deeds in this state, unless a particular officer is specified before
whom it is to be taken.
```
