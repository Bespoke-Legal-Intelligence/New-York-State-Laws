# General Construction Law § 33-A — Notify

[General Construction Law](../README.md) › [Article 2 — Meaning of Terms](README.md)

```text
  § 33-a. Notify. "Notify" is used with respect to procuring the
attendance of a juror, is equivalent to the word "summon" as used in the
like connection, in the constitution and laws of the state.
```
