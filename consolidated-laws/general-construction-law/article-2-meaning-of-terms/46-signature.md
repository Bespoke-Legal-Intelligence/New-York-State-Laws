# General Construction Law § 46 — Signature

[General Construction Law](../README.md) › [Article 2 — Meaning of Terms](README.md)

```text
  § 46. Signature. The term signature includes any memorandum, mark or
sign, written, printed, stamped, photographed, engraved or otherwise
placed upon any instrument or writing with intent to execute or
authenticate such instrument or writing.
```
