# General Construction Law § 50 — Time, computation

[General Construction Law](../README.md) › [Article 2 — Meaning of Terms](README.md)

```text
  § 50. Time, computation. Time shall continue to be computed in this
state according to the Gregorian or new style. The first day of each
year after the year seventeen hundred and fifty-two is the first day of
January, according to such style.
```
