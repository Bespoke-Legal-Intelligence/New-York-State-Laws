# General Construction Law § 27 — Last, preceding, next and following

[General Construction Law](../README.md) › [Article 2 — Meaning of Terms](README.md)

```text
  § 27. Last, preceding, next and following. A reference to the last or
preceding section, or other provision of a statute, means the section or
other division immediately preceding, and a reference to the next or
following section or other division of a statute means the section or
other division immediately following.
```
