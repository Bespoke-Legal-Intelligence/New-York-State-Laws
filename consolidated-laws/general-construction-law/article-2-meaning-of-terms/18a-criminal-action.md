# General Construction Law § 18-A — Criminal action

[General Construction Law](../README.md) › [Article 2 — Meaning of Terms](README.md)

```text
  § 18-a. Criminal action. A "criminal action" is prosecuted in the name
of the people of the state of New York, as plaintiffs, against a party
charged with crime.
```
