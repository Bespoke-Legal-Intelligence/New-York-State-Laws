# General Construction Law § 53 — Time, use of standard

[General Construction Law](../README.md) › [Article 2 — Meaning of Terms](README.md)

```text
  § 53. Time, use of standard. Any act required by or in pursuance of
law to be performed at or within a prescribed time, shall be performed
according to the standard time.
```
