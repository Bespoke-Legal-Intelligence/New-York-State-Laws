# General Construction Law § 37-A — Personal injury

[General Construction Law](../README.md) › [Article 2 — Meaning of Terms](README.md)

```text
  § 37-a. Personal injury. "Personal injury" includes libel, slander and
malicious prosecution; also an assault, battery, false imprisonment, or
other actionable injury to the person either of the plaintiff, or of
another.
```
