# General Construction Law § 31 — Month in statute, contract and public or private instrument

[General Construction Law](../README.md) › [Article 2 — Meaning of Terms](README.md)

```text
  § 31. Month in statute, contract and public or private instrument.  In
a statute, contract or public or private instrument, unless otherwise
provided in such contract or instrument or by law, the term month means
a calendar month and not a lunar month.
```
