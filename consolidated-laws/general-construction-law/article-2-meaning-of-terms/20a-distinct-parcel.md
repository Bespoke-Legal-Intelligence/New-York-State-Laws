# General Construction Law § 20-A — Distinct parcel

[General Construction Law](../README.md) › [Article 2 — Meaning of Terms](README.md)

```text
  § 20-a. Distinct parcel. "Distinct parcel" of real property is a part
of the property which is or may be set off by boundary lines as
distinguished from an undivided share or interest therein.
```
