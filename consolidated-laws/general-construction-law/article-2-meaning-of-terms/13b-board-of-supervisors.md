# General Construction Law § 13-B — Board of supervisors

[General Construction Law](../README.md) › [Article 2 — Meaning of Terms](README.md)

```text
§ 13-b. Board of supervisors. The term "board of supervisors" means the
elective governing body of a county, by whatever name designated.
```
