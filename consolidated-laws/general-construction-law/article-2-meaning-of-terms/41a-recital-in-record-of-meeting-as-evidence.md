# General Construction Law § 41-A — Recital in record of meeting as evidence

[General Construction Law](../README.md) › [Article 2 — Meaning of Terms](README.md)

```text
  § 41-a. Recital in record of meeting as evidence. A recital in any
order, resolution or other record of any proceeding of a meeting
referred to in section forty-one of this chapter that such meeting had
been held or adjourned as provided in said section, or that it had been
held upon notice to the members, as therein provided, is prima facie
evidence thereof.
```
