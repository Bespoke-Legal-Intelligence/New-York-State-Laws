# General Construction Law § 11-B — Appropriation

[General Construction Law](../README.md) › [Article 2 — Meaning of Terms](README.md)

```text
§ 11-b.  Appropriation.  The term "appropriation" when used in reference
to the acquiring of any title to, right or interest in real property
through the exercise of the power of eminent domain, shall be deemed to
mean acquisition.
```
