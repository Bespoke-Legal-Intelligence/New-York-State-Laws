# General Construction Law § 40 — Property, real

[General Construction Law](../README.md) › [Article 2 — Meaning of Terms](README.md)

```text
  § 40. Property, real. The term real property includes real estate,
lands, tenements and hereditaments, corporeal and incorporeal.
```
