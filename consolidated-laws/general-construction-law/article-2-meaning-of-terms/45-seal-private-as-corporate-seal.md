# General Construction Law § 45 — Seal, private as corporate seal

[General Construction Law](../README.md) › [Article 2 — Meaning of Terms](README.md)

```text
  § 45. Seal, private as corporate seal. An instrument or writing duly
executed, in the corporate name of a corporation, which shall not have
adopted a corporate seal, by the proper officers of the corporation
under their private seals, shall be deemed to have been executed under
the corporate seal.
```
