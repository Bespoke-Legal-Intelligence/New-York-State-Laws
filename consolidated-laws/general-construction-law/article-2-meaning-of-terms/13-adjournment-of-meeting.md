# General Construction Law § 13 — Adjournment of meeting

[General Construction Law](../README.md) › [Article 2 — Meaning of Terms](README.md)

```text
§ 13. Adjournment of meeting. Any meeting referred to in section
forty-one of this chapter may be adjourned by a less number than a
quorum.
```
