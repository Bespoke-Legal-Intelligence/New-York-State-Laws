# General Construction Law § 26-A — Judgment creditor

[General Construction Law](../README.md) › [Article 2 — Meaning of Terms](README.md)

```text
  § 26-a. Judgment creditor. "Judgment creditor" signifies the person
who is entitled to collect or otherwise enforce, in his own right, a
judgment for a sum of money, or directing the payment of a sum of money.
```
