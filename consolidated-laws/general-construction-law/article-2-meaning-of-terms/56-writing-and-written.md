# General Construction Law § 56 — Writing and written

[General Construction Law](../README.md) › [Article 2 — Meaning of Terms](README.md)

```text
  § 56. Writing and written. The terms writing and written include every
legible representation of letters upon a material substance, except when
applied to the signature of an instrument.
```
