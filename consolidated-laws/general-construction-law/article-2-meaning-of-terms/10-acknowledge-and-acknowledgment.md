# General Construction Law § 10 — Acknowledge and acknowledgment

[General Construction Law](../README.md) › [Article 2 — Meaning of Terms](README.md)

```text
§ 10. Acknowledge and acknowledgment. The terms acknowledge and
acknowledgment, when used with reference to the execution of an
instrument or writing other than a deed of real property, include a
compliance with the provisions of the next section by either such proof
or acknowledgment.
```
