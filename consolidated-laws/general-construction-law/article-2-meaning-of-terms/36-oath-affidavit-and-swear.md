# General Construction Law § 36 — Oath, affidavit and swear

[General Construction Law](../README.md) › [Article 2 — Meaning of Terms](README.md)

```text
  § 36. Oath, affidavit and swear. The terms oath and affidavit include
every mode authorized by law of attesting the truth of that which is
stated. The term swear includes every mode authorized by law for
administering an oath.
```
