# General Construction Law § 70 — Statutes of England and Great Britain inoperative in this state

[General Construction Law](../README.md) › [Article 3 — Ancient Statutes and Resolutions](README.md)

```text
  § 70. Statutes of England and Great Britain inoperative in this state.
A statute of England or Great Britain shall not be deemed to have had
any force or effect in this state since May first, seventeen hundred and
eighty-eight.
```
