# General Construction Law § 71 — Acts of the legislature of the colony of New York inoperative

[General Construction Law](../README.md) › [Article 3 — Ancient Statutes and Resolutions](README.md)

```text
  § 71. Acts of the legislature of the colony of New York inoperative.
Acts of the legislature of the colony of New York shall not be deemed to
have had any force or effect in this state since December twenty-ninth,
eighteen hundred and twenty-eight.
```
