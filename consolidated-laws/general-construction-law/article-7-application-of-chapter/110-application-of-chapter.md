# General Construction Law § 110 — Application of chapter

[General Construction Law](../README.md) › [Article 7 — Application of Chapter](README.md)

```text
  § 110. Application of chapter. This chapter is applicable to every
statute unless its general object, or the context of the language
construed, or other provisions of law indicate that a different meaning
or application was intended from that required to be given by this
chapter.
```
