# General Construction Law § 80 — References to repealed provisions

[General Construction Law](../README.md) › [Article 4 — References, Titles and Head Notes](README.md)

```text
  § 80. References to repealed provisions. If any provision of a law be
repealed and, in substance, re-enacted, a reference in any law to such
repealed provision shall be deemed a reference to such re-enacted
provision.
```
