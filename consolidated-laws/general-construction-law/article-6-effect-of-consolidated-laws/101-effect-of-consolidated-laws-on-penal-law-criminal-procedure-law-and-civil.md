# General Construction Law § 101 — Effect of consolidated laws on penal law, criminal procedure law and civil practice law and rules

[General Construction Law](../README.md) › [Article 6 — Effect of Consolidated Laws](README.md)

```text
  § 101. Effect of consolidated laws on penal law, criminal procedure
law and civil practice law and rules. The consolidated laws shall not be
construed to amend, repeal or otherwise affect any provision of the
penal law, civil practice law and rules or criminal procedure law unless
expressly so stated.
```
