# General Construction Law § 67 — Names of corporation laws

[General Construction Law](../README.md) › [Article 2-A — Classification of Corporations and Definitions](README.md)

```text
  § 67. Names of corporation laws. The cooperative corporations law,
religious corporations law and transportation corporations law may be
cited or referred to as, respectively, the cooperative corporation law,
religious corporation law and transportation corporation law.
```
