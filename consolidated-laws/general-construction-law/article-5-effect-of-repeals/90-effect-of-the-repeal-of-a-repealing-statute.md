# General Construction Law § 90 — Effect of the repeal of a repealing statute

[General Construction Law](../README.md) › [Article 5 — Effect of Repeals](README.md)

```text
  § 90. Effect of the repeal of a repealing statute. The repeal
hereafter or by this chapter of any provision of a statute, which
repeals any provision of a prior statute, does not revive such prior
provision.
```
