# General Construction Law § 91 — Effect of the repeal of a statute upon amendments thereof

[General Construction Law](../README.md) › [Article 5 — Effect of Repeals](README.md)

```text
  § 91. Effect of the repeal of a statute upon amendments thereof.  The
repeal by the Consolidated Laws of a statute includes a statute
amendatory of the statute repealed.
```
