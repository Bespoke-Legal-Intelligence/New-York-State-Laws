# General Construction Law § 92 — Effect of the repeal of an amending statute

[General Construction Law](../README.md) › [Article 5 — Effect of Repeals](README.md)

```text
  § 92. Effect of the repeal of an amending statute. The repeal
hereafter or by this chapter of any provision of a statute, which amends
a provision of a prior statute, leaves such prior provision in force
unless the amendatory statute be a substantial re-enactment of the
statute amended.
```
