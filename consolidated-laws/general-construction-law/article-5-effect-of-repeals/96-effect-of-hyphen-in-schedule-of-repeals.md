# General Construction Law § 96 — Effect of hyphen in schedule of repeals

[General Construction Law](../README.md) › [Article 5 — Effect of Repeals](README.md)

```text
  § 96. Effect of hyphen in schedule of repeals. When two numbers in a
schedule of repeals of the consolidated laws are connected by a hyphen
both such numbers are included as well as all intermediate numbers.
```
