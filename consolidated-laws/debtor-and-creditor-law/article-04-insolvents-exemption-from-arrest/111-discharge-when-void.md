# Debtor and Creditor Law § 111 — Discharge, when void

[Debtor and Creditor Law](../README.md) › [Article 4 — Insolvent's Exemption From Arrest and Imprisonment](README.md)

```text
  § 111. Discharge, when void. A discharge, granted to an insolvent as
prescribed in this article, is void, in the same cases, so far as they
are applicable, in which a discharge, granted as prescribed in article
third of this chapter, is therein declared to be void; and the validity
of such a discharge may be tested in the same manner.
```
