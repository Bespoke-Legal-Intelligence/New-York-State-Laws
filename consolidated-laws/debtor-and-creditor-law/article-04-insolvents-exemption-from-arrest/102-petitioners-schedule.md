# Debtor and Creditor Law § 102 — Petitioner's schedule

[Debtor and Creditor Law](../README.md) › [Article 4 — Insolvent's Exemption From Arrest and Imprisonment](README.md)

```text
  § 102. Petitioner's schedule. The petitioner must annex to his
petition, a schedule, in all respects similar to that required of an
insolvent, as prescribed in section sixty-three of this chapter.
```
