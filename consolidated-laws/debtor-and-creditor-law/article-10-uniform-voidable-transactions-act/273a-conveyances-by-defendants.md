# Debtor and Creditor Law § 273-A — Conveyances by defendants

[Debtor and Creditor Law](../README.md) › [Article 10 — Uniform Voidable Transactions Act](README.md)

**Repealed** (10 Apr 2020). The text below is what the Senate publishes for this repealed provision.

```text
  * § 273-a. Conveyances by defendants. Every conveyance made without
fair consideration when the person making it is a defendant in an action
for money damages or a judgment in such an action has been docketed
against him, is fraudulent as to the plaintiff in that action without
regard to the actual intent of the defendant if, after final judgment
for the plaintiff, the defendant fails to satisfy the judgment.
  * NB Repealed April 4, 2020
```
