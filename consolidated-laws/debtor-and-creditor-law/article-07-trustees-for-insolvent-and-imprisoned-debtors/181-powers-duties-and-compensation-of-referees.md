# Debtor and Creditor Law § 181 — Powers, duties and compensation of referees

[Debtor and Creditor Law](../README.md) › [Article 7 — Trustees For Insolvent and Imprisoned Debtors](README.md)

```text
  § 181. Powers, duties and compensation of referees. Such referees
shall have the same powers, and be subject to the like duties and
obligations, and shall receive the same compensation, as referees
appointed by the supreme court, in actions pending therein.
```
