# Debtor and Creditor Law § 168 — Power to sue notwithstanding notice

[Debtor and Creditor Law](../README.md) › [Article 7 — Trustees For Insolvent and Imprisoned Debtors](README.md)

```text
  § 168. Power to sue notwithstanding notice. Notwithstanding any such
notice, the trustees may sue for and recover, any property or effects of
the debtor, and any debts due to him, at any time, before the day
appointed for the delivery or payment thereof.
```
