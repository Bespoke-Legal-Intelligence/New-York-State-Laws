# Debtor and Creditor Law § 195 — Penalties recovered

[Debtor and Creditor Law](../README.md) › [Article 7 — Trustees For Insolvent and Imprisoned Debtors](README.md)

```text
  § 195. Penalties recovered. All penalties which shall be recovered by
any trustees, pursuant to the provisions of this article, shall be
deemed a part of the estate of the debtor, and shall be distributed as
such among his creditors.
```
