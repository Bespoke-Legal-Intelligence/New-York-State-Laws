# Debtor and Creditor Law § 187 — Payment of disbursements and commissions of trustees

[Debtor and Creditor Law](../README.md) › [Article 7 — Trustees For Insolvent and Imprisoned Debtors](README.md)

```text
  § 187. Payment of disbursements and commissions of trustees. Out of
the moneys in their hands, the trustees may first deduct all the
necessary disbursements made by them in the discharge of their duty, and
a commission at the rate of five per centum on the whole sum which shall
have come into their hands.
```
