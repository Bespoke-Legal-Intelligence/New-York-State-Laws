# Debtor and Creditor Law § 197 — Provision for neglectful creditors

[Debtor and Creditor Law](../README.md) › [Article 7 — Trustees For Insolvent and Imprisoned Debtors](README.md)

```text
  § 197. Provision for neglectful creditors. Any creditor who shall have
neglected to deliver to the trustees an account of his demand, before
the first, second, third or other dividend, and who shall deliver his
account to them before the second, or other subsequent dividend, shall
receive the sum he would have been entitled to, on any former dividend,
before any distribution be made to other creditors.
```
