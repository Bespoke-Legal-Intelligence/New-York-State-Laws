# Debtor and Creditor Law § 161 — Sole trustee

[Debtor and Creditor Law](../README.md) › [Article 7 — Trustees For Insolvent and Imprisoned Debtors](README.md)

```text
  § 161. Sole trustee.  When one trustee only shall be appointed, all
the provisions herein contained, in reference to several trustees, shall
apply to him.
```
