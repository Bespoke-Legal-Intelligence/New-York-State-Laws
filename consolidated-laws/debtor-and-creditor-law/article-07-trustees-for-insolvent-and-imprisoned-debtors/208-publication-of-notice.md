# Debtor and Creditor Law § 208 — Publication of notice

[Debtor and Creditor Law](../README.md) › [Article 7 — Trustees For Insolvent and Imprisoned Debtors](README.md)

```text
  § 208. Publication of notice. Such notice shall be published, once in
each week, for four weeks successively, in such newspapers, as such
court shall direct.
```
