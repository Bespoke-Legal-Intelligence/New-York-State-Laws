# Debtor and Creditor Law § 188 — Preferred debts

[Debtor and Creditor Law](../README.md) › [Article 7 — Trustees For Insolvent and Imprisoned Debtors](README.md)

```text
  § 188. Preferred debts. They shall pay all debts due by such debtor to
the United States, and all debts due by him to persons who, by the laws
of the United States, have a preference in consequence of having paid
money as sureties of such debtor.
```
