# Debtor and Creditor Law § 198 — Unclaimed dividends

[Debtor and Creditor Law](../README.md) › [Article 7 — Trustees For Insolvent and Imprisoned Debtors](README.md)

```text
  § 198. Unclaimed dividends. If any dividend that shall have been
declared, shall remain unclaimed by the person entitled thereto for one
year after the same was declared, the trustees shall consider it as
relinquished, and shall distribute it, on any subsequent dividend, among
the other creditors.
```
