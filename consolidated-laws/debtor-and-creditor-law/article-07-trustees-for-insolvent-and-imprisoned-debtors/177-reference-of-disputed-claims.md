# Debtor and Creditor Law § 177 — Reference of disputed claims

[Debtor and Creditor Law](../README.md) › [Article 7 — Trustees For Insolvent and Imprisoned Debtors](README.md)

```text
  § 177. Reference of disputed claims. If any controversy shall arise
between the trustees and any other person in the settlement of any
demands against such debtor, or of debts due his estate, the same may be
referred to one or more indifferent persons, who may be agreed upon by
the trustees and the party with whom such controversy shall exist, by a
writing to that effect signed by them.
```
