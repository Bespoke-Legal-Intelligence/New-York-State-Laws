# Debtor and Creditor Law § 216 — Payment of expense of renunciation

[Debtor and Creditor Law](../README.md) › [Article 7 — Trustees For Insolvent and Imprisoned Debtors](README.md)

```text
  § 216. Payment of expense of renunciation. The expense of all
proceedings in effecting such renunciation and assignment, shall be paid
by the trustee making the application.
```
