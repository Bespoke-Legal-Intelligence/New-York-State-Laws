# Debtor and Creditor Law § 191 — Payment of debts before maturity

[Debtor and Creditor Law](../README.md) › [Article 7 — Trustees For Insolvent and Imprisoned Debtors](README.md)

```text
  § 191. Payment of debts before maturity. Every person to whom a
debtor, except one proceeding under the fifth article, shall be indebted
on a valuable consideration, for any sum of money not due at the time of
such distribution, but payable afterwards, shall receive his proportion
with other creditors, after deducting a rebate of legal interest upon
the sum distributed, for the time unexpired of such credit.
```
