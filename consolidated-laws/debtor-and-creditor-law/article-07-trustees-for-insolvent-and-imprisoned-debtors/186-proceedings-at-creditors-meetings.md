# Debtor and Creditor Law § 186 — Proceedings at creditors' meetings

[Debtor and Creditor Law](../README.md) › [Article 7 — Trustees For Insolvent and Imprisoned Debtors](README.md)

```text
  § 186. Proceedings at creditors' meetings. At such meeting, or other
adjourned meeting thereafter, all accounts and demands, for and against
the estate of such debtor, shall be fairly adjusted, as far as the same
can be ascertained, and the amount of moneys in the hands of the
trustees declared.
```
