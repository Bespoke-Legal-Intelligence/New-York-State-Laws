# Debtor and Creditor Law § 206 — Verification of account

[Debtor and Creditor Law](../README.md) › [Article 7 — Trustees For Insolvent and Imprisoned Debtors](README.md)

```text
  § 206. Verification of account. To such account shall be annexed the
affidavit of the trustee, that the said account is in all respects just
and true, according to the best of his knowledge and belief.
```
