# Debtor and Creditor Law § 162 — Two or more trustees

[Debtor and Creditor Law](../README.md) › [Article 7 — Trustees For Insolvent and Imprisoned Debtors](README.md)

```text
  § 162. Two or more trustees. When there are more trustees than one
appointed, the debts and property of the debtor may be collected and
received by any one of them; and when there are more than two trustees
appointed, every power and authority conferred by this chapter on the
trustees, may be exercised by any two of them.
```
