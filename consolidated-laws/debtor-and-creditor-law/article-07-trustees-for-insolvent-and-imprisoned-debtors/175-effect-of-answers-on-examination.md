# Debtor and Creditor Law § 175 — Effect of answers on examination

[Debtor and Creditor Law](../README.md) › [Article 7 — Trustees For Insolvent and Imprisoned Debtors](README.md)

```text
  § 175. Effect of answers on examination. The person so examined, and
answering to the satisfaction of the court, shall not be liable to any
penalty imposed for concealing and not delivering any property, or
paying any debt; but his answers on such examination, may be given in
evidence in the same manner, and with the like effect, as if he had been
examined by such trustees in an action brought by them against him for
the recovery of such property or debt.
```
