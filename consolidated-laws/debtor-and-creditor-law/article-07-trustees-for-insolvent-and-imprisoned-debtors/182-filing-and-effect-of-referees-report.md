# Debtor and Creditor Law § 182 — Filing and effect of referees' report

[Debtor and Creditor Law](../README.md) › [Article 7 — Trustees For Insolvent and Imprisoned Debtors](README.md)

```text
  § 182. Filing and effect of referees' report. The report of the
referees shall be filed in the same office where the order for their
appointment was entered, and shall be conclusive on the rights of the
parties, if not set aside by the court.
```
