# Debtor and Creditor Law § 199 — Disposition of surplus

[Debtor and Creditor Law](../README.md) › [Article 7 — Trustees For Insolvent and Imprisoned Debtors](README.md)

```text
  § 199. Disposition of surplus. If after settling the estate of any
debtor, and after discharging his debts, entitled to a dividend, any
surplus shall remain in the hands of his trustees, the same shall be
paid to such debtor or his legal representatives.
```
