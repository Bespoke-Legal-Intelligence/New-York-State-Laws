# Debtor and Creditor Law § 178 — Application for appointment of referee

[Debtor and Creditor Law](../README.md) › [Article 7 — Trustees For Insolvent and Imprisoned Debtors](README.md)

```text
  § 178. Application for appointment of referee. If such referee or
referees be not selected by agreement, then the trustees or the other
party to the controversy, provided no action at law is pending arising
out of any such debts or demands, may serve a notice of their intention
to apply to a judge of the court which appointed said trustees, or to
any justice of the supreme court at chambers, residing in the same
district with said trustees, for the appointment of one or more
referees, specifying the time and place when such application will be
made, which notice shall be served at least ten days before the time so
therein specified.
```
