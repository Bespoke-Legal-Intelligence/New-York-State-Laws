# Debtor and Creditor Law § 17 — Invalid claims

[Debtor and Creditor Law](../README.md) › [Article 2 — General Assignments For the Benefit of Creditors](README.md)

```text
  § 17. Invalid claims. Claims which for want of record or for other
reasons would not have been valid as against the claims of creditors of
the assignor shall not be liens against his estate.
```
