# Debtor and Creditor Law § 9 — Failure to file bond

[Debtor and Creditor Law](../README.md) › [Article 2 — General Assignments For the Benefit of Creditors](README.md)

```text
  § 9. Failure to file bond. A failure to file any bond required by or
under this article, within the specified time will not deprive the judge
of his power over the assignee or the trust estate.
```
