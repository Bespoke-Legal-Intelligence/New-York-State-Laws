# Debtor and Creditor Law § 10 — Action on bond; application of recovery

[Debtor and Creditor Law](../README.md) › [Article 2 — General Assignments For the Benefit of Creditors](README.md)

```text
  § 10. Action on bond; application of recovery. Any action brought upon
an assignee's bond may be prosecuted by a party in interest by leave of
the court; and all moneys realized thereon shall be applied by direction
of the judge in satisfaction of the debts of the assignor in the same
manner as the same ought to have been applied by such assignee.
```
