# Debtor and Creditor Law § 255 — Proceedings on return of citation

[Debtor and Creditor Law](../README.md) › [Article 9 — Payment of Debts of Incompetent Person or Conservatee](README.md)

```text
  § 255. Proceedings on return of citation. Upon the return of such
citation properly served, such court shall have the powers in relation
to such estates, claims, property and committee, which devolve on
courts, by virtue of section twenty-one, in relation to assignments and
assignees for benefit of creditors and such assigned estates.
```
