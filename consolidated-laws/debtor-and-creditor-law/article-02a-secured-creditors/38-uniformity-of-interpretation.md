# Debtor and Creditor Law § 38 — Uniformity of interpretation

[Debtor and Creditor Law](../README.md) › [Article 2-A — Secured Creditors](README.md)

```text
  § 38. Uniformity of interpretation. This article shall be so
interpreted and construed as to effectuate its general purpose to make
uniform the law of those states which enact it.
```
