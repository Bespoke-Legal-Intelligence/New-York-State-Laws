# Debtor and Creditor Law § 37 — Constitutionality

[Debtor and Creditor Law](../README.md) › [Article 2-A — Secured Creditors](README.md)

```text
  § 37. Constitutionality. If any provision of this article or the
application thereof to any person or circumstances is held invalid, the
invalidity shall not affect other provisions or applications thereof
which can be given effect without the invalid provision or application,
and to this end the provisions of this article are declared to be
severable.
```
