# Debtor and Creditor Law § 129 — Adjournment

[Debtor and Creditor Law](../README.md) › [Article 5 — Judgment Debtor's Discharge From Imprisonment](README.md)

```text
  § 129. Adjournment. Upon sufficient cause being shown by a creditor,
the court may, from time to time, adjourn the hearing; but not to a day
later than three months after the presentation of the petition.
```
