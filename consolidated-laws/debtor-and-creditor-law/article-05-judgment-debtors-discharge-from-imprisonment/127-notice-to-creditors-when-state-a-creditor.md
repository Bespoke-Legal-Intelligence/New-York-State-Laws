# Debtor and Creditor Law § 127 — Notice to creditors; when state a creditor

[Debtor and Creditor Law](../README.md) › [Article 5 — Judgment Debtor's Discharge From Imprisonment](README.md)

```text
  § 127. Notice to creditors; when state a creditor. Where the state is
a creditor, the papers must be served upon the attorney-general, who
must represent the state in the proceedings.
```
