# Debtor and Creditor Law § 121 — To what court application to be made

[Debtor and Creditor Law](../README.md) › [Article 5 — Judgment Debtor's Discharge From Imprisonment](README.md)

```text
  § 121. To what court application to be made. Application for such a
discharge must be made by petition, addressed to the court from which
the execution issued; or to the county court of the county in which he
is imprisoned; or, if he is imprisoned in the city of New York, to the
supreme court.
```
