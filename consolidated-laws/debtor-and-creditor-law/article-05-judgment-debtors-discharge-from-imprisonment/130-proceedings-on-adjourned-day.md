# Debtor and Creditor Law § 130 — Proceedings on adjourned day

[Debtor and Creditor Law](../README.md) › [Article 5 — Judgment Debtor's Discharge From Imprisonment](README.md)

```text
  § 130. Proceedings on adjourned day. An objection to a matter of form
shall not be received upon an adjourned day; and, unless the opposing
creditor satisfies the court that the proceedings on the part of the
petitioner are not just and fair, the court must direct an assignment,
as prescribed in the last section but one, and must grant a discharge,
as prescribed in the following sections of this article.
```
