# Debtor and Creditor Law § 273-A — Conveyances by defendants

[Debtor and Creditor Law](../README.md) › [Article 10 — Fraudulent Conveyances](README.md)

```text
  § 273-a. Conveyances by defendants. Every conveyance made without fair
consideration when the person making it is a defendant in an action for
money damages or a judgment in such an action has been docketed against
him, is fraudulent as to the plaintiff in that action without regard to
the actual intent of the defendant if, after final judgment for the
plaintiff, the defendant fails to satisfy the judgment.
```
