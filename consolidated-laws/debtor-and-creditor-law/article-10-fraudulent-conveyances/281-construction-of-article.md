# Debtor and Creditor Law § 281 — Construction of article

[Debtor and Creditor Law](../README.md) › [Article 10 — Fraudulent Conveyances](README.md)

```text
  * § 281. Construction of article. This article shall be so interpreted
and construed as to effectuate its general purpose to make uniform the
laws of those states which enact it.
  * NB Effective until April 4, 2020
  * § 281. Uniformity of application and construction. This article
shall be applied and construed to effectuate its general purpose to make
uniform the law with respect to the subject of this article among states
enacting it.
  * NB Effective April 4, 2020
```
