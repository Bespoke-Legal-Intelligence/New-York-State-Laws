# Debtor and Creditor Law § 280 — Cases not provided for in article

[Debtor and Creditor Law](../README.md) › [Article 10 — Fraudulent Conveyances](README.md)

```text
  § 280. Cases not provided for in article. In any case not provided for
in this article the rules of law and equity including the law merchant,
and in particular the rules relating to the law of principal and agent,
and the effect of fraud, misrepresentation, duress or coercion, mistake,
bankruptcy or other invalidating cause shall govern.
```
