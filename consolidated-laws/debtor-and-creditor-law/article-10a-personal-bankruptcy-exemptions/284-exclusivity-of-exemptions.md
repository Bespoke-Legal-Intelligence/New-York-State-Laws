# Debtor and Creditor Law § 284 — Exclusivity of exemptions

[Debtor and Creditor Law](../README.md) › [Article 10-A — Personal Bankruptcy Exemptions](README.md)

```text
  § 284. Exclusivity of exemptions. In accordance with the provisions of
section five hundred twenty-two (b) of title eleven of the United States
Code, debtors domiciled in this state are not authorized to exempt from
the estate property that is specified under subsection (d) of such
section.
```
