# Debtor and Creditor Law § 51 — To what court application to be made

[Debtor and Creditor Law](../README.md) › [Article 3 — Insolvent's Discharge From Debts](README.md)

```text
  § 51. To what court application to be made. Application for such a
discharge must be made, by the petition of the insolvent, addressed to
the county court of the county in which he resides; or, if he resides in
the city of New York, to the supreme court.
```
