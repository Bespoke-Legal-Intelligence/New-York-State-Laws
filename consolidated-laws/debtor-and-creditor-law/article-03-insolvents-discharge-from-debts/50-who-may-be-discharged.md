# Debtor and Creditor Law § 50 — Who may be discharged

[Debtor and Creditor Law](../README.md) › [Article 3 — Insolvent's Discharge From Debts](README.md)

```text
  § 50. Who may be discharged. An insolvent debtor, who is a resident of
the state at the time of presenting his petition, may be discharged from
his debts, as prescribed in this article.
```
