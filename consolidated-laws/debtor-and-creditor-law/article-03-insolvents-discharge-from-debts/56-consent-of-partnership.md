# Debtor and Creditor Law § 56 — Consent of partnership

[Debtor and Creditor Law](../README.md) › [Article 3 — Insolvent's Discharge From Debts](README.md)

```text
  § 56. Consent of partnership. Where a partnership becomes a consenting
creditor, the consent may be executed in its behalf, and any affidavit,
required of a creditor in the proceedings, may be made, by either of the
partners.
```
