# Judiciary Law § 155 — Supreme court seal

[Judiciary Law](../README.md) › [Article 5 — Supreme Court](README.md)

```text
  § 155. Supreme court seal.  The seal kept by the county clerk of each
county shall continue to be the seal of the supreme court, in that
county.
  The provisions of this section shall not be applicable in those cases
where special provisions are otherwise made by law.
```
