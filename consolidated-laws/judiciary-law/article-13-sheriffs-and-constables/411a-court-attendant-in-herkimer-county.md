# Judiciary Law § 411-A — Court attendant in Herkimer county

[Judiciary Law](../README.md) › [Article 13 — Sheriffs and Constables](README.md)

```text
  § 411-a. Court attendant in Herkimer county. The county judge and
surrogate of the county of Herkimer is hereby authorized by and with the
consent and approval of the trial justice of the supreme court, residing
in Herkimer county, to appoint a court attendant in and for the supreme
and county courts in Herkimer county, whose duty it shall be to attend
the various terms of such courts held in and for the county of Herkimer
and act as attendant and court crier thereat and to perform such other
services as shall be required and designated by said justice and judge.
Said court officer shall possess all the power of an officer designated
by the sheriff to attend upon the court and shall receive such salary as
shall be fixed from time to time by the board of supervisors of Herkimer
county.
```
