# Judiciary Law § 407 — Deputy sheriff must attend court as notified

[Judiciary Law](../README.md) › [Article 13 — Sheriffs and Constables](README.md)

```text
  § 407. Deputy sheriff must attend court as notified. Each constable or
deputy sheriff, seasonably notified, as prescribed in section four
hundred and three of this chapter, must attend the term accordingly; and
for each day's neglect he may be fined by the court, at the term at
which he was notified to attend, a sum not exceeding five dollars.
```
