# Judiciary Law § 849-A — Definitions

[Judiciary Law](../README.md) › [Article 21-A — Community Dispute Resolution Centers Program](README.md)

```text
  § 849-a. Definitions. For the purposes of this article:
  1. "Center" means a community dispute center which provides
conciliation, mediation, arbitration or other forms and techniques of
dispute resolution.
  2. "Mediator" means an impartial person who assists in the resolution
of a dispute.
  3. "Grant recipient" means any nonprofit organization that administers
a community dispute resolution center pursuant to this article, and is
organized for the resolution of disputes or for religious, charitable or
educational purposes.
```
