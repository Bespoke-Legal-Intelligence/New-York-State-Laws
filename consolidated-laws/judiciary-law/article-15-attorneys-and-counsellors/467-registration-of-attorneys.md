# Judiciary Law § 467 — Registration of attorneys

[Judiciary Law](../README.md) › [Article 15 — Attorneys and Counsellors](README.md)

```text
  § 467. Registration of attorneys. The clerks of each appellate
division shall forward to the clerk of the court of appeals and the
chief administrator of the courts a list of attorneys newly admitted to
practice. Such list shall be forwarded at such times and in such form as
the clerk of the court of appeals and the chief administrator shall
provide.
```
