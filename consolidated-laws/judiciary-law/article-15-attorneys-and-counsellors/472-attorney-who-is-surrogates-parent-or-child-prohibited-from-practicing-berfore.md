# Judiciary Law § 472 — Attorney who is surrogate's parent or child prohibited from practicing berfore him

[Judiciary Law](../README.md) › [Article 15 — Attorneys and Counsellors](README.md)

```text
  § 472. Attorney who is surrogate's parent or child prohibited from
practicing berfore him. A surrogate's parent or child shall not practice
or be employed as attorney or counsel, in any case, in which his partner
or clerk is prohibited by law from so practicing, or being employed.
```
