# Judiciary Law § 462 — Annual account by state board of law examiners

[Judiciary Law](../README.md) › [Article 15 — Attorneys and Counsellors](README.md)

```text
  § 462. Annual account by state board of law examiners. The state board
of law examiners shall render an annual account of all its receipts and
disbursements to the court of appeals.
```
