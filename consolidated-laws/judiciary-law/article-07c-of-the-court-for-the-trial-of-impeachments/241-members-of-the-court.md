# Judiciary Law § 241 — Members of the court

[Judiciary Law](../README.md) › [Article 7-C — Of the Court For the Trial of Impeachments](README.md)

```text
  § 241. Members of the court. The court is composed of the president of
the senate, the senators, or a majority of them, and the judges of the
court of appeals, or a majority of them, but on the trial of an
impeachment against the governor or lieutenant-governor, the
lieutenant-governor can not act as a member of the court.
```
