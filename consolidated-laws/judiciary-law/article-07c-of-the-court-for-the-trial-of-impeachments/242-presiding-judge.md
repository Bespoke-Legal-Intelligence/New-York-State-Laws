# Judiciary Law § 242 — Presiding judge

[Judiciary Law](../README.md) › [Article 7-C — Of the Court For the Trial of Impeachments](README.md)

```text
  § 242. Presiding judge. The president of the senate, or in case of his
impeachment, death or absence, the chief judge of the court of appeals,
or in the absence of both, such other member as the court may elect, is
the presiding judge of the court.
```
