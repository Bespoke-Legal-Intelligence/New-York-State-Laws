# Judiciary Law § 240 — Its jurisdiction

[Judiciary Law](../README.md) › [Article 7-C — Of the Court For the Trial of Impeachments](README.md)

```text
  § 240. Its jurisdiction. The court for the trial of impeachments has
power to try impeachments, when presented by the assembly, of all civil
officers of the state, except justices of the peace, justices of
justices' courts, police justices, and their clerks, for willful and
corrupt misconduct in office.
```
