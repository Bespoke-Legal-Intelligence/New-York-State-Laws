# Judiciary Law § 244 — Seal of the court

[Judiciary Law](../README.md) › [Article 7-C — Of the Court For the Trial of Impeachments](README.md)

```text
  § 244. Seal of the court. The seal of the court for the trial of
impeachments now deposited and recorded in the office of the secretary
of state shall continue to be the seal of this court and must be kept in
the custody of the secretary of the senate.
```
