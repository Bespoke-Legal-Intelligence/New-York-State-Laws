# Judiciary Law § 518 — Discharge by the court

[Judiciary Law](../README.md) › [Article 16 — Selection of Jurors](README.md)

```text
  § 518. Discharge by the court. The court shall discharge a person from
serving as a trial or a grand juror whenever it satisfactorily appears
that he or she is not qualified.
```
