# Judiciary Law § 508 — Number to be selected

[Judiciary Law](../README.md) › [Article 16 — Selection of Jurors](README.md)

```text
  § 508. Number to be selected. The commissioner of jurors shall draw at
random for each term and each separate part of a term of court at which
issues are triable by jury, such number of petit jurors and such number
of grand jurors as he believes necessary, unless otherwise specified by
order of the appellate division or by written order of the judge
appointed to hold the part or term of court. Jurors shall be drawn in
such manner, and serve for such period of time, as the appropriate
appellate division shall prescribe by rule.
```
