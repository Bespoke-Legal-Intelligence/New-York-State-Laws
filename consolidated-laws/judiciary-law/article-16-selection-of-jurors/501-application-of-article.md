# Judiciary Law § 501 — Application of article

[Judiciary Law](../README.md) › [Article 16 — Selection of Jurors](README.md)

```text
  § 501. Application of article. Except as otherwise provided, the
provisions of this article shall apply to grand and petit jurors in all
courts of the unified court system in which a jury may be drawn, whether
such courts are of record or not of record.
```
