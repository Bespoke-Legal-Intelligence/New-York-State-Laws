# Judiciary Law § 507 — Random selection

[Judiciary Law](../README.md) › [Article 16 — Selection of Jurors](README.md)

```text
  § 507. Random selection. The commissioner of jurors shall select the
names of prospective jurors, or cause them to be selected, at random
from the sources provided in section five hundred six. The selection may
be accomplished by mechanical means or by any other method designed to
implement the purposes of this article.
```
