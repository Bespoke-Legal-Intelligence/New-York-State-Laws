# Judiciary Law § 505 — Oath of office

[Judiciary Law](../README.md) › [Article 16 — Selection of Jurors](README.md)

```text
  § 505. Oath of office. Before entering upon the duties of his office a
commissioner appointed under this article, and each deputy, assistant or
employee, shall take the oath of office prescribed by law and file the
same with the office of court administration and in the office of the
county clerk without charge.
```
