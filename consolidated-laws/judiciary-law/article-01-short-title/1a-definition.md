# Judiciary Law § 1-A — Definition

[Judiciary Law](../README.md) › [Article 1 — Short Title](README.md)

```text
  § 1-a. Definition. As used in this chapter, the word "minor" or
"infant" shall mean any person who has not attained the age of eighteen
years.
```
