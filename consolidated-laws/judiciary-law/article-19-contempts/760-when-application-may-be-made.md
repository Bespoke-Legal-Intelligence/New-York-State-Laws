# Judiciary Law § 760 — When application may be made

[Judiciary Law](../README.md) › [Article 19 — Contempts](README.md)

```text
  § 760. When application may be made. An application may be made,
either before or after the final judgment in the action, or the final
order in the special proceeding.
```
