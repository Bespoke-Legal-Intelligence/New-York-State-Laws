# Judiciary Law § 776 — Offender liable to indictment

[Judiciary Law](../README.md) › [Article 19 — Contempts](README.md)

```text
  § 776. Offender liable to indictment. A person, punished as prescribed
in this article, may, notwithstanding, be indicted for the same
misconduct, if it is an indictable offense; but the court, before which
he is convicted, must, in forming its sentence, take into consideration
the previous punishment.
```
