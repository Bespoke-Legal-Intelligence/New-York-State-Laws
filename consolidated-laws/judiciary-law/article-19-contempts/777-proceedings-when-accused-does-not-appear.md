# Judiciary Law § 777 — Proceedings when accused does not appear

[Judiciary Law](../README.md) › [Article 19 — Contempts](README.md)

```text
  § 777. Proceedings when accused does not appear. Where a person has
given an undertaking for his appearance, as prescribed in this article
and fails to appear, on the return day of the application, the court may
either issue a warrant of commitment, or make an order, directing the
undertaking to be prosecuted; or both.
```
