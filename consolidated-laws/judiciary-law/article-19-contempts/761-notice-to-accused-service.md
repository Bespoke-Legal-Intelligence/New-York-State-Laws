# Judiciary Law § 761 — Notice to accused; service

[Judiciary Law](../README.md) › [Article 19 — Contempts](README.md)

```text
  § 761. Notice to accused; service. An application to punish for
contempt in a civil contempt proceeding shall be served upon the
accused, unless service upon the attorney for the accused be ordered by
the court or judge.
```
