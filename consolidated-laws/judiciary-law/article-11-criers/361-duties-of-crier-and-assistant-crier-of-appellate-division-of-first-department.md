# Judiciary Law § 361 — Duties of crier and assistant crier of appellate division of first department

[Judiciary Law](../README.md) › [Article 11 — Criers](README.md)

```text
  § 361. Duties of crier and assistant crier of appellate division of
first department.  The duties of the crier and assistant crier of the
appellate division in the first department, shall be such as may be
prescribed by the rules made by the said appellate division.
```
