# Judiciary Law § 360 — Salary of crier of court of appeals

[Judiciary Law](../README.md) › [Article 11 — Criers](README.md)

```text
  § 360. Salary of crier of court of appeals.  The crier of the court of
appeals shall receive an annual salary of twenty-eight hundred dollars
to be paid in equal monthly instalments and the comptroller is directed
to draw his warrant for the same.
```
