# Judiciary Law § 78 — Justices of appellate division in first department may make rules for management of law libraries and court-houses of appellate division ...

[Judiciary Law](../README.md) › [Article 4 — Appellate Division](README.md)

```text
  § 78. Justices of appellate division in first department may make
rules for management of law libraries and court-houses of appellate
division and supreme court. The justices of the appellate division in
the first department shall from time to time make such rules as they may
deem necessary for the management and protection of the law libraries
and for the protection and management of the court-houses and
court-rooms of the appellate division and of the supreme court.
```
