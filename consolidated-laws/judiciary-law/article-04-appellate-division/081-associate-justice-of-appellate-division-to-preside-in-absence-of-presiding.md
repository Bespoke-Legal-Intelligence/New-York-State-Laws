# Judiciary Law § 81 — Associate justice of appellate division to preside in absence of presiding justice

[Judiciary Law](../README.md) › [Article 4 — Appellate Division](README.md)

```text
  § 81. Associate justice of appellate division to preside in absence of
presiding justice. If the presiding justice is not present at the
sitting of the appellate division, the associate justice presiding in
the department having served the longest time as such, or, if two are
present who have served the same length of time, the elder of them must
act as presiding justice until a presiding justice attends.
```
