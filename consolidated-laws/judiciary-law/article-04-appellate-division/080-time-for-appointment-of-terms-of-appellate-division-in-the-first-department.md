# Judiciary Law § 80 — Time for appointment of terms of appellate division in the first department

[Judiciary Law](../README.md) › [Article 4 — Appellate Division](README.md)

```text
  § 80. Time for appointment of terms of appellate division in the first
department. The justices of the appellate division in the first
department shall fix a time and place for holding the terms of the
appellate division in the first department on or before the first day of
December in each year.
```
