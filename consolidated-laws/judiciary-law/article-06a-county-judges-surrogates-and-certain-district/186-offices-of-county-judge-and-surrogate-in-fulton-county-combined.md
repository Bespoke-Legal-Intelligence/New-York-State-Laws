# Judiciary Law § 186 — Offices of county judge and surrogate in Fulton county combined

[Judiciary Law](../README.md) › [Article 6-A — County Judges, Surrogates and Certain District Attorneys](README.md)

```text
  § 186. Offices of county judge and surrogate in Fulton county
combined. The office of county judge and the office of surrogate in
Fulton county are hereby combined and on and after the effective date of
this act all the functions, powers and duties of such surrogate shall be
exercised and performed by each county judge of such county.
```
