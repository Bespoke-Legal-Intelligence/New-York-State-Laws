# Judiciary Law § 260 — Salary of clerk of court of appeals

[Judiciary Law](../README.md) › [Article 8 — Clerks](README.md)

```text
  § 260. Salary of clerk of court of appeals.  The clerk of the court of
appeals shall receive a salary of ten thousand dollars per annum.
```
