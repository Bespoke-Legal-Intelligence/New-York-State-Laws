# Judiciary Law § 265-A — Deputy clerk or attendant of appellate division in third department to act as librarian

[Judiciary Law](../README.md) › [Article 8 — Clerks](README.md)

```text
  § 265-a. Deputy clerk or attendant of appellate division in third
department to act as librarian.  The deputy clerk of the appellate
division of the supreme court in the third judicial department or any
attendant of said court shall also act under direction of said court as
librarian and have charge of the library in use by said court.
```
