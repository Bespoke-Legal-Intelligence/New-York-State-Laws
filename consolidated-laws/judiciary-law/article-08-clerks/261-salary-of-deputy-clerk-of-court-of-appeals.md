# Judiciary Law § 261 — Salary of deputy clerk of court of appeals

[Judiciary Law](../README.md) › [Article 8 — Clerks](README.md)

```text
  § 261. Salary of deputy clerk of court of appeals.  The deputy clerk
of the court of appeals shall receive a salary of five thousand two
hundred dollars per annum.
```
