# Judiciary Law § 252 — Clerks not to receive fees except as prescribed by law

[Judiciary Law](../README.md) › [Article 8 — Clerks](README.md)

```text
  § 252. Clerks not to receive fees except as prescribed by law.  Each
clerk of a court must perform all the duties required of him, in the
course and practice of the court, without fee or reward, except as
expressly prescribed by law.
```
