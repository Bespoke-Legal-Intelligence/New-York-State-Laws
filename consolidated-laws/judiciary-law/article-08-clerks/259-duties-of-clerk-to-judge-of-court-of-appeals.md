# Judiciary Law § 259 — Duties of clerk to judge of court of appeals

[Judiciary Law](../README.md) › [Article 8 — Clerks](README.md)

```text
  § 259. Duties of clerk to judge of court of appeals.  The clerk
appointed by each judge of the court of appeals shall perform such
services as the judge appointing him may require.
```
