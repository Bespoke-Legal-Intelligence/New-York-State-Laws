# Judiciary Law § 263 — Salary of law clerk of court of appeals

[Judiciary Law](../README.md) › [Article 8 — Clerks](README.md)

```text
  § 263. Salary of law clerk of court of appeals.  The law clerk of the
court of appeals shall receive a salary of not to exceed five thousand
dollars a year.
```
