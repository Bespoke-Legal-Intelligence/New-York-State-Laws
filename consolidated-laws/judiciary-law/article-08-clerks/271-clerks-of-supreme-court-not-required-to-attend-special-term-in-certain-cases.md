# Judiciary Law § 271 — Clerks of supreme court not required to attend special term in certain cases

[Judiciary Law](../README.md) › [Article 8 — Clerks](README.md)

```text
  § 271. Clerks of supreme court not required to attend special term in
certain cases.  Where a special term of the supreme court is adjourned
to the chambers of a justice of the court, pursuant to section one
hundred and forty-seven of this chapter, the attendance of the clerk is
not required, unless the justice directs him to attend.
```
