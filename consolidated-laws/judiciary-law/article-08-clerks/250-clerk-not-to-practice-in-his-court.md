# Judiciary Law § 250 — Clerk not to practice in his court

[Judiciary Law](../README.md) › [Article 8 — Clerks](README.md)

```text
  § 250. Clerk not to practice in his court.  The clerk, deputy clerk,
or special deputy clerk of a court shall not, during his continuance in
office, practice as attorney or counsellor in that court.
```
