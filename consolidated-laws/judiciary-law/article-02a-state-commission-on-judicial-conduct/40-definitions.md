# Judiciary Law § 40 — Definitions

[Judiciary Law](../README.md) › [Article 2-A — State Commission On Judicial Conduct](README.md)

```text
  § 40. Definitions. For the purposes of this article the following
terms have the following meanings:
  1. "Commission" means the state commission on judicial conduct.
  2. "Judge" means a judge or justice of any court of the unified court
system.
  3. "Hearing" means a proceeding under subdivision four of section
forty-four of this article.
  4. "Member of the bar" means a person admitted to the practice of law
in this state for at least five years.
```
