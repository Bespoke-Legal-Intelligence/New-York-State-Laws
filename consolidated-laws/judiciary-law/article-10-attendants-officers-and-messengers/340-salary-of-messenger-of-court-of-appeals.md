# Judiciary Law § 340 — Salary of messenger of court of appeals

[Judiciary Law](../README.md) › [Article 10 — Attendants, Officers and Messengers](README.md)

```text
  § 340. Salary of messenger of court of appeals. The messenger
appointed to attend on the judges of the court of appeals shall receive
a salary of one thousand six hundred dollars per annum, to be paid in
monthly instalments.
```
