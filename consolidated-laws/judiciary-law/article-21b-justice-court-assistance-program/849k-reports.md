# Judiciary Law § 849-K — Reports

[Judiciary Law](../README.md) › [Article 21-B — Justice Court Assistance Program](README.md)

```text
  § 849-k. Reports. The chief administrator of the courts shall report
annually to the governor and the legislature regarding the operation and
success of the program established by this article.
```
