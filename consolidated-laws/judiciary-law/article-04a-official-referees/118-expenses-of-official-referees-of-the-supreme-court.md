# Judiciary Law § 118 — Expenses of official referees of the supreme court

[Judiciary Law](../README.md) › [Article 4-A — Official Referees](README.md)

```text
  § 118. Expenses of official referees of the supreme court.  Each
official referee of the supreme court hereby continued in office shall
be furnished with all necessary office room and supplies, which shall be
a charge upon the county in which he resides.
```
