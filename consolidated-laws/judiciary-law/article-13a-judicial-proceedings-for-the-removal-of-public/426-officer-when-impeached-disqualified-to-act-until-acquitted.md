# Judiciary Law § 426 — Officer, when impeached, disqualified to act until acquitted

[Judiciary Law](../README.md) › [Article 13-A — Judicial Proceedings For the Removal of Public Officers By Impeachment](README.md)

```text
  § 426.  Officer, when impeached, disqualified to act until acquitted.
No officer shall exercise his office, after articles of impeachment
against him shall have been delivered to the senate, until he is
acquitted.
```
