# Judiciary Law § 425 — Nature of the judgment

[Judiciary Law](../README.md) › [Article 13-A — Judicial Proceedings For the Removal of Public Officers By Impeachment](README.md)

```text
  § 425. Nature of the judgment. Upon conviction, the judgment must be
either:
  1. That the defendant be removed from office; or
  2. That he be removed from office and disqualified to hold and enjoy a
particular office or class of offices, or any office of profit, trust or
honor whatever under this state.
```
