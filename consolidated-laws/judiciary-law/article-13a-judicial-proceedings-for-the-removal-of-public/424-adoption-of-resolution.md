# Judiciary Law § 424 — Adoption of resolution

[Judiciary Law](../README.md) › [Article 13-A — Judicial Proceedings For the Removal of Public Officers By Impeachment](README.md)

```text
  § 424. Adoption of resolution. On the adoption of the resolution, by a
majority of the members present, who voted on the question of acquittal
or conviction, it becomes the judgment of the court.
```
