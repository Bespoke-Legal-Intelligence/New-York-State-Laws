# Judiciary Law § 428 — Impeachment, not a bar to indictment

[Judiciary Law](../README.md) › [Article 13-A — Judicial Proceedings For the Removal of Public Officers By Impeachment](README.md)

```text
  § 428. Impeachment, not a bar to indictment. If the offense for which
the defendant is impeached be a crime, the prosecution thereof is not
barred by the impeachment.
```
