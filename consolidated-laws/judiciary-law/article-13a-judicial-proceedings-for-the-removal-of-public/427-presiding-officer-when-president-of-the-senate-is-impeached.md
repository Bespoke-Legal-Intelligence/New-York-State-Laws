# Judiciary Law § 427 — Presiding officer, when president of the senate is impeached

[Judiciary Law](../README.md) › [Article 13-A — Judicial Proceedings For the Removal of Public Officers By Impeachment](README.md)

```text
  § 427. Presiding officer, when president of the senate is impeached.
If the president of the senate be impeached, notice of the impeachment
must be immediately given to the senate by the assembly, that another
president may be chosen.
```
