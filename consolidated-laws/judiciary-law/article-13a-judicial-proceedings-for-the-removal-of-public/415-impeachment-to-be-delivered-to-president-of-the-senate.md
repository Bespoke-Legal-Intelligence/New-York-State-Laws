# Judiciary Law § 415 — Impeachment to be delivered to president of the senate

[Judiciary Law](../README.md) › [Article 13-A — Judicial Proceedings For the Removal of Public Officers By Impeachment](README.md)

```text
  § 415. Impeachment to be delivered to president of the senate.  When
an officer of the state is impeached by the assembly, the articles of
impeachment must be delivered to the president of the senate.
```
