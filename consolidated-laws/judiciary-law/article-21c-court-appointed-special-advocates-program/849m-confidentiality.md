# Judiciary Law § 849-M — Confidentiality

[Judiciary Law](../README.md) › [Article 21-C — Court-appointed Special Advocates Program](README.md)

```text
  § 849-m. Confidentiality. Each CASA program shall safeguard the
confidentiality of all information and material in accordance with
applicable state and federal laws, rules and regulations and, to this
end, shall ensure that all of its board members, officers, employees and
volunteers are trained in, and comply with, such laws, rules and
regulations.
```
