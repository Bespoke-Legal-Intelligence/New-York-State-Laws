# Judiciary Law § 291 — Qualifications of stenographers

[Judiciary Law](../README.md) › [Article 9 — Stenographers](README.md)

```text
  § 291. Qualifications of stenographers. A person shall not be
appointed to the office of stenographer, unless he is skilled in the
stenographic art.
```
