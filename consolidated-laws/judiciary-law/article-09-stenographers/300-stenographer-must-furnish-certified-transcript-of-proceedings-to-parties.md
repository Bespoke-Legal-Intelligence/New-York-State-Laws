# Judiciary Law § 300 — Stenographer must furnish certified transcript of proceedings to parties on payment of fees

[Judiciary Law](../README.md) › [Article 9 — Stenographers](README.md)

```text
  § 300. Stenographer must furnish certified transcript of proceedings
to parties on payment of fees. The stenographer shall, upon the payment
of his fees allowed by law therefor, furnish a certified transcript of
the whole or any part of his minutes, in any case reported by him, to
any party to the action requiring the same.
```
