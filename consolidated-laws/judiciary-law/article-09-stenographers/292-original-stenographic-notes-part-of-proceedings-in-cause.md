# Judiciary Law § 292 — Original stenographic notes part of proceedings in cause

[Judiciary Law](../README.md) › [Article 9 — Stenographers](README.md)

```text
  § 292. Original stenographic notes part of proceedings in cause. The
original stenographic notes, taken by a stenographer, are part of the
proceedings in the cause.
```
