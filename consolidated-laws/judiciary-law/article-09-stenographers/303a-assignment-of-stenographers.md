# Judiciary Law § 303-A — Assignment of stenographers

[Judiciary Law](../README.md) › [Article 9 — Stenographers](README.md)

```text
  § 303-a. Assignment of stenographers. Stenographers of the county
court or surrogate's court may serve in either of said courts with the
consent and under the direction of the respective judges thereof.
```
