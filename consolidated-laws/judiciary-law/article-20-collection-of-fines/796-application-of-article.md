# Judiciary Law § 796 — Application of article

[Judiciary Law](../README.md) › [Article 20 — Collection of Fines](README.md)

```text
  § 796. Application of article. This article does not apply to a case,
where special provision for the collection of a fine is otherwise made
by law.
```
