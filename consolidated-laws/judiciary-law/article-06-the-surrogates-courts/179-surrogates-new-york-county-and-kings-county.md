# Judiciary Law § 179 — Surrogates, New York county and Kings county

[Judiciary Law](../README.md) › [Article 6 — The Surrogates' Courts](README.md)

```text
  § 179. Surrogates, New York county and Kings county. The surrogate's
courts of New York county and Kings county shall each consist of two
surrogates.
```
