# Judiciary Law § 195 — County court seal

[Judiciary Law](../README.md) › [Article 7 — County Court](README.md)

```text
  § 195. County court seal.  The seal kept by the county clerk of each
county except in the county of New York, shall continue to be the seal
of the county court in that county.
  The provisions of this section shall not be applicable in those cases
where special provisions are otherwise made by law.
```
