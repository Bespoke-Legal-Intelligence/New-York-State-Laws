# Judiciary Law § 190-E — Appointment of term to be filed with county clerk

[Judiciary Law](../README.md) › [Article 7 — County Court](README.md)

```text
  § 190-e. Appointment of term to be filed with county clerk.  Each
appointment, made as prescribed in section one hundred and ninety and
one hundred and ninety-one of this chapter, must be filed in the county
clerk's office.
```
