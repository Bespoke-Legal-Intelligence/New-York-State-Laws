# Judiciary Law § 202 — Power of county judge of Erie county to appoint court attendant

[Judiciary Law](../README.md) › [Article 7 — County Court](README.md)

```text
  § 202. Power of county judge of Erie county to appoint court
attendant.  The county judge of Erie county may appoint, and at pleasure
remove, one court officer to attend his court in said county, and to
perform such duties in respect thereto as the said county judge may
prescribe.
```
