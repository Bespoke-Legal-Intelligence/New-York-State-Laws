# Judiciary Law § 191 — Power of county judge to hear special proceeding that supreme court justice can hear at chambers

[Judiciary Law](../README.md) › [Article 7 — County Court](README.md)

```text
  § 191. Power of county judge to hear special proceeding that supreme
court justice can hear at chambers.  A county judge within his county
has the power to, and upon proper application shall, at a reasonable
time, hear and determine any special proceeding which a supreme court
justice is authorized to hear and determine at chambers.
```
