# Judiciary Law § 221-C — Salary of judges of the court of claims

[Judiciary Law](../README.md) › [Article 7-B — Compensation of Judges and Justices of the Unified Court System](README.md)

```text
  § 221-c. Salary of judges of the court of claims. The annual salaries
of the presiding judge and judges of the court of claims shall be as
follows:
Presiding Judge        144,000
Judge                  136,700
```
