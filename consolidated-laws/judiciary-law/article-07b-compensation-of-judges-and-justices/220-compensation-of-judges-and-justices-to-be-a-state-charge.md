# Judiciary Law § 220 — Compensation of judges and justices to be a state charge

[Judiciary Law](../README.md) › [Article 7-B — Compensation of Judges and Justices of the Unified Court System](README.md)

```text
  § 220. Compensation of judges and justices to be a state charge. The
salaries and expenses of judges and justices of the unified court
system, except justices of town and village courts, shall be paid by the
state subject to the provisions of section thirty-nine of this chapter.
Salaries shall be paid in twenty-six bi-weekly installments.
```
