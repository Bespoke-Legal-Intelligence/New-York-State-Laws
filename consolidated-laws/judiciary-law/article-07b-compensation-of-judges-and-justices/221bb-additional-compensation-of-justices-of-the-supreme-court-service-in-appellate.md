# Judiciary Law § 221-BB — Additional compensation of justices of the supreme court; service in appellate term

[Judiciary Law](../README.md) › [Article 7-B — Compensation of Judges and Justices of the Unified Court System](README.md)

```text
  § 221-bb. Additional compensation of justices of the supreme court;
service in appellate term. A justice of the supreme court who shall be
designated by the appellate division of the supreme court to serve in an
appellate term in the manner provided in section eight of article six of
the constitution shall receive, in addition to his or her judicial
salary as established by law, the sum of three thousand dollars annually
as compensation for his or her services in appellate term.
```
