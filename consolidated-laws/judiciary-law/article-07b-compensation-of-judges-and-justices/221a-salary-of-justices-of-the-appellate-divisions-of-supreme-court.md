# Judiciary Law § 221-A — Salary of justices of the appellate divisions of supreme court

[Judiciary Law](../README.md) › [Article 7-B — Compensation of Judges and Justices of the Unified Court System](README.md)

```text
  § 221-a. Salary of justices of the appellate divisions of supreme
court.  The annual salaries of the presiding justices and associate
justices of the appellate divisions of the supreme court shall be as
follows:
Presiding Justice      147,600
Associate Justice      144,000
```
