# Judiciary Law § 221 — Salary of judges of the court of appeals

[Judiciary Law](../README.md) › [Article 7-B — Compensation of Judges and Justices of the Unified Court System](README.md)

```text
  § 221. Salary of judges of the court of appeals. The annual salaries
of the chief judge and associate judges of the court of appeals shall be
as follows:
Chief Judge            156,000
Associate Judge        151,200
```
