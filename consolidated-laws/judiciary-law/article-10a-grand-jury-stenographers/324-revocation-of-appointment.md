# Judiciary Law § 324 — Revocation of appointment

[Judiciary Law](../README.md) › [Article 10-A — Grand Jury Stenographers](README.md)

```text
  § 324. Revocation of appointment. Any appointment made under the
provisions of this article may be revoked by the district attorney,
which revocation must be in writing and be filed in the office of the
clerk of the county in which such appointment was filed.
```
