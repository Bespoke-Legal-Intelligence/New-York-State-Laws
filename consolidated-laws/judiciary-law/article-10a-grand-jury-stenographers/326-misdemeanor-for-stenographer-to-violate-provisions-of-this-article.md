# Judiciary Law § 326 — Misdemeanor for stenographer to violate provisions of this article

[Judiciary Law](../README.md) › [Article 10-A — Grand Jury Stenographers](README.md)

```text
  § 326. Misdemeanor for stenographer to violate provisions of this
article.  Every stenographer appointed as aforesaid, who violates any
provision of this article is guilty of a misdemeanor.
```
