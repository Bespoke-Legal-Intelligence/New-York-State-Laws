# Judiciary Law § 430 — Law reporting bureau; state reporter

[Judiciary Law](../README.md) › [Article 14 — Law Reporting](README.md)

```text
  § 430. Law reporting bureau; state reporter. There is hereby created
and established the law reporting bureau of the state of New York. The
bureau shall be under the direction and control of a state reporter, who
shall be appointed and be removable by the court of appeals by an order
entered in its minutes. The state reporter shall be assisted by a first
deputy state reporter and such other deputy state reporters and such
staff as may be necessary, all of whom shall be appointed and be
removable by the court of appeals.
```
