# Judiciary Law § 812 — Appellate division libraries

[Judiciary Law](../README.md) › [Article 21 — Court Libraries](README.md)

```text
  § 812. Appellate division libraries. The libraries heretofore
established for the appellate divisions of the supreme court are
continued. They are under exclusive supervision of the respective
appellate divisions. The justices of the court shall be trustees thereof
who shall continue to be vested with all the powers with regard thereto
now possessed by those justices.
```
