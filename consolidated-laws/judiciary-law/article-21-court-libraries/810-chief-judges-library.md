# Judiciary Law § 810 — Chief judge's library

[Judiciary Law](../README.md) › [Article 21 — Court Libraries](README.md)

```text
  § 810. Chief judge's library. The consultation library of the court of
appeals is continued. This library shall be under the exclusive
supervision of that court and the chief judge may add thereto from any
funds available.
```
