# Judiciary Law § 854 — Chief administrator to make rules

[Judiciary Law](../README.md) › [Article 22 — Judicial Hearing Officers](README.md)

```text
  § 854. Chief administrator to make rules. The chief administrator of
the courts shall promulgate rules in order to effectuate the provisions
of this article.
```
