# Judiciary Law § 51 — Court of appeals may make rules of practice for its court

[Judiciary Law](../README.md) › [Article 3 — Court of Appeals](README.md)

```text
  § 51. Court of appeals may make rules of practice for its court.  The
court of appeals may from time to time adopt, amend, or rescind rules,
not inconsistent with the constitution or statutes of the state,
regulating the practice and proceedings in the court.
```
