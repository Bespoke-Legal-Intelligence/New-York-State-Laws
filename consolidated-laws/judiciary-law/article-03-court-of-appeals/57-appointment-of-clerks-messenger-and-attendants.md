# Judiciary Law § 57 — Appointment of clerks, messenger and attendants

[Judiciary Law](../README.md) › [Article 3 — Court of Appeals](README.md)

```text
  § 57. Appointment of clerks, messenger and attendants. The court of
appeals may, from time to time, by an order entered in its minutes,
appoint and remove its clerk, a law clerk, a messenger, and such
attendants or other clerks as it deems necessary.
```
