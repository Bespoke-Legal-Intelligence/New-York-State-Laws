# Judiciary Law § 52 — General rule or order of court of appeals must be published

[Judiciary Law](../README.md) › [Article 3 — Court of Appeals](README.md)

```text
  § 52. General rule or order of court of appeals must be published. A
general rule or order of the court of appeals does not take effect until
it has been published in the state register.
```
