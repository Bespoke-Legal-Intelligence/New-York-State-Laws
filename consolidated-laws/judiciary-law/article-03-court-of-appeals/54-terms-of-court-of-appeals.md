# Judiciary Law § 54 — Terms of court of appeals

[Judiciary Law](../README.md) › [Article 3 — Court of Appeals](README.md)

```text
  § 54. Terms of court of appeals. The terms of the court of appeals
must be appointed to be held at such times and places as the court
thinks proper, and continued as long as the public interest requires.
  A term of the court may be appointed to be held in a building other
than that designated by law for holding courts.
  A term may be adjourned from the place where it is appointed to be
held, to another place in the same city.
  One or more of the judges may adjourn a term, without day, or to a day
certain.
```
