# Judiciary Law § 58 — Appointment of clerks to judges of court of appeals

[Judiciary Law](../README.md) › [Article 3 — Court of Appeals](README.md)

```text
  § 58. Appointment of clerks to judges of court of appeals. Each judge
of the court of appeals may appoint and at pleasure remove a clerk. The
chief judge, in addition thereto, is authorized to appoint a
confidential clerk.
```
