# Judiciary Law § 13-B — Oath of referee, receiver, commissioner or appraiser

[Judiciary Law](../README.md) › [Article 2 — General Provisions Relating to Courts and Judges](README.md)

```text
  § 13-b. Oath of referee, receiver, commissioner or appraiser. A
referee, receiver, commissioner or appraiser appointed by a court or
judge, before entering upon his duties, shall be sworn faithfully and
fairly to discharge the trust committed to him. The oath may be waived
upon consent of all parties.
```
