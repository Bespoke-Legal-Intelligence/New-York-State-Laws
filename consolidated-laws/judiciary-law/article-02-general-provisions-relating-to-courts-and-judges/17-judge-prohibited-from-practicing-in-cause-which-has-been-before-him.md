# Judiciary Law § 17 — Judge prohibited from practicing in cause which has been before him

[Judiciary Law](../README.md) › [Article 2 — General Provisions Relating to Courts and Judges](README.md)

```text
  § 17. Judge prohibited from practicing in cause which has been before
him. A judge or surrogate or former judge or surrogate shall not act as
attorney or counsellor in any action, claim, matter, motion or
proceeding, which has been before him in his official character.
```
