# Judiciary Law § 29 — Seal of court of record

[Judiciary Law](../README.md) › [Article 2 — General Provisions Relating to Courts and Judges](README.md)

```text
  § 29. Seal of court of record. The seal of each court of record in the
state, now in use, shall continue to be the seal of the court in which
it is in use. A description of each of such seals, must be deposited and
recorded in the office of court administration, unless it has already
been done; and must remain of record.
  The provisions of this section shall not be applicable in those cases
where special provisions are otherwise made by law.
```
