# Judiciary Law § 16 — Judge prohibited from practicing law in his court

[Judiciary Law](../README.md) › [Article 2 — General Provisions Relating to Courts and Judges](README.md)

```text
  § 16. Judge prohibited from practicing law in his court. A judge shall
not practice or act as an attorney or counsellor in a court of which he
is, or is entitled to act as a member, or in an action, claim, matter,
motion or proceeding originating in that court.
```
