# Judiciary Law § 15 — Judge of court of record not disqualified because a resident or a taxpayer

[Judiciary Law](../README.md) › [Article 2 — General Provisions Relating to Courts and Judges](README.md)

```text
  § 15. Judge of court of record not disqualified because a resident or
a taxpayer. A judge of a court of record is not disqualified from
hearing or deciding an action, claim, matter, motion or proceeding, by
reason of his being a resident or taxpayer of a town, village, city, or
county, interested therein.
```
