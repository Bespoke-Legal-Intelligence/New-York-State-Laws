# Judiciary Law § 13-A — Power to remove certain officers and to appoint successors

[Judiciary Law](../README.md) › [Article 2 — General Provisions Relating to Courts and Judges](README.md)

```text
  § 13-a. Power to remove certain officers and to appoint successors. A
referee, receiver, commissioner or appraiser appointed by a court or
judge may be removed by the same court or judge. In case of the death,
resignation, removal, or neglect or refusal to serve of any such
officer, another person may be appointed in his stead.
```
