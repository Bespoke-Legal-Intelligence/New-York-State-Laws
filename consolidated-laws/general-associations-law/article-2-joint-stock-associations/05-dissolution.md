# General Associations Law § 5 — Dissolution

[General Associations Law](../README.md) › [Article 2 — Joint-stock Associations](README.md)

```text
  § 5.  Dissolution.  A joint-stock association shall not be dissolved
except in pursuance of its articles of association, or by consent of all
its stockholders, or by judgment of a court for fraud in its management,
or for good cause shown.
```
