# General Associations Law § 3 — Contents of articles of association

[General Associations Law](../README.md) › [Article 2 — Joint-stock Associations](README.md)

```text
  § 3.  Contents of articles of association. The articles of association
of a joint-stock association may:
  1.  Provide that the death of a stockholder thereof, or the transfer
of his shares of stock therein, shall not work a dissolution of the
association;
  2.  Prescribe the number of its directors, not less than three, to
have the sole management of its affairs;
  3.  Contain any other provision for the management of its affairs not
inconsistent with law.
```
