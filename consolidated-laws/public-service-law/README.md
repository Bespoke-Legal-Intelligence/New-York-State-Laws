*An asterisk in a number (for example Article 24\* or § 20-C\*2) is the Senate's mark for a number the Legislature has used more than once: the marked article or section is a different one from the unmarked one with the same number. \*2 means the second one enacted with that number.*

**Short title (from the law itself):** § 1. Short title. This chapter shall be known as the "Public Service Law." Reference in any law to the public service commission law shall mean the public service law, and amendments, in form, to the public service commission law enacted by the legislature of nineteen hundred thirty shall be treated as amendments to the public service law.
