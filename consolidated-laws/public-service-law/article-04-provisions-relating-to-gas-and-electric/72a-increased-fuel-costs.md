# Public Service Law § 72-A — Increased fuel costs

[Public Service Law](../README.md) › [Article 4 — Provisions Relating to Gas and Electric Corporations; Regulation of Price of Gas and Electricity](README.md)

```text
  § 72-a. Increased fuel costs. Every gas and electric corporation
subject to the jurisdiction of the commission shall file with the
commission, on a monthly basis, the following information regarding
increased fuel costs:
  (a) what services are affected by the fuel increase;
  (b) base cost of the fuel;
  (c) name and address of supplier;
  (d) point of delivery of all fuel purchased;
  (e) present average cost of fuel to said corporation;
  (f) dates used to determine average;
  (g) amount per unit of consumption affected; and
  (h) amount of fuel in storage at the end of each monthly period.
  (i) and such other relevant information as the commission may require.
```
