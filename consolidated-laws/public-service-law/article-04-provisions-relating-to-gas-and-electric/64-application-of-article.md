# Public Service Law § 64 — Application of article

[Public Service Law](../README.md) › [Article 4 — Provisions Relating to Gas and Electric Corporations; Regulation of Price of Gas and Electricity](README.md)

```text
  § 64. Application of article.  This article shall apply to the
manufacture, conveying, transportation and furnishing of gas (natural or
manufactured or mixture of both) for light, heat or power and the
generation, furnishing and transmission of electricity for light, heat
or power. Within the meaning of any provision of this article, each of
the terms "gas corporation" and "electric corporation" also shall
include a gas and electric corporation, that is, such a corporation as
is organized for the objects and purposes of both a gas corporation and
an electric corporation, as the context of such provisions and the
powers actually exercised by such gas and electric corporation may
require.
```
