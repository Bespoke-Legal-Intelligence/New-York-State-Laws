# Public Service Law § 68-A — Statements of nature and extent of interests to be filed upon request

[Public Service Law](../README.md) › [Article 4 — Provisions Relating to Gas and Electric Corporations; Regulation of Price of Gas and Electricity](README.md)

```text
  § 68-a. Statements of nature and extent of interests to be filed upon
request.  Whenever any person or corporation signs or certifies any
estimate, appraisal, report, or other statement, to be submitted to the
public service commission in relation to any public utility company, in
which such person or corporation is or has been financially interested,
directly or indirectly, or is or has been employed in any manner or
capacity, or of which such person is or has been an officer or employee,
the commission in its discretion, may require that such signature or
certification shall be accompanied by a specific statement setting forth
the nature and extent of such interest or engagement.
```
