# Public Service Law § 150 — Board of railroad commissioners abolished; effect thereof

[Public Service Law](../README.md) › [Article 9 — Commissions and Offices Abolished; Saving Clause; Repeal](README.md)

```text
  § 150. Board of railroad commissioners abolished; effect thereof.  The
board of railroad commissioners was abolished on July first, nineteen
hundred and seven. All the powers and duties of such board conferred and
imposed by any statute of this state shall hereafter be exercised and
performed by the public service commission.
```
