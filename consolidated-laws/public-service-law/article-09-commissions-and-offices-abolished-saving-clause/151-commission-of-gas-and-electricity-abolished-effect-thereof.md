# Public Service Law § 151 — Commission of gas and electricity abolished; effect thereof

[Public Service Law](../README.md) › [Article 9 — Commissions and Offices Abolished; Saving Clause; Repeal](README.md)

```text
  § 151. Commission of gas and electricity abolished; effect thereof.
The commission of gas and electricity was abolished on July first,
nineteen hundred and seven. All the powers and duties of such commission
conferred and imposed by any statute of this state shall hereafter be
exercised and performed by the public service commission.
```
