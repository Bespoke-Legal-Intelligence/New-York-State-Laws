# Public Service Law § 135-I — Default

[Public Service Law](../README.md) › [Article 7-A — Home Insulation and Conservation](README.md)

```text
  § 135-i. Default. In the event that a participating customer defaults
in respect to payment under a financing agreement and security contract
entered into pursuant to this article, the utility shall employ
reasonable efforts to collect all amounts due from the customer.
```
