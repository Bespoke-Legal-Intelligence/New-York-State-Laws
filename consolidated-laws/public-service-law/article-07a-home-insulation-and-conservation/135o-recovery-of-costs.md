# Public Service Law § 135-O — Recovery of costs

[Public Service Law](../README.md) › [Article 7-A — Home Insulation and Conservation](README.md)

```text
  § 135-o. Recovery of costs. A utility shall be allowed to recover as
normal operating expenses through rates the just and reasonable costs of
carrying out its responsibilities and home conservation plan under this
article, as determined by the commission after public hearing upon
reasonable notice.
```
