# Public Service Law § 49 — Residential steam service

[Public Service Law](../README.md) › [Article 2 — Residential Gas, Electric and Steam Utility Service](README.md)

```text
  § 49. Residential steam service. The rights and responsibilities of
customers receiving residential steam service from steam corporations
shall be substantially comparable to those of gas and electric customers
under this article. The commission shall take such actions as it deems
necessary and proper to achieve this objective.
```
