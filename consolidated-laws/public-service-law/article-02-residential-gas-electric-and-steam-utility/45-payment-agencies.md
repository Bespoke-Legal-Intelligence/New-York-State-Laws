# Public Service Law § 45 — Payment agencies

[Public Service Law](../README.md) › [Article 2 — Residential Gas, Electric and Steam Utility Service](README.md)

```text
  § 45. Payment agencies. A utility corporation or municipality may
permit its customers to pay their bills to a payment agent. The date of
payment to any such agent shall be regarded as the date of payment to
the utility or municipality.
```
