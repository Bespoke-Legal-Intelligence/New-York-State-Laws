# Public Service Law § 119-D — Maintenance of utility poles

[Public Service Law](../README.md) › [Article 6 — Provisions Affecting Two or More Kinds of the Public Service and the Persons and Corporations Furnishing Such Service](README.md)

```text
  § 119-d. Maintenance of utility poles. On or before April first, two
thousand twenty-four, the commission shall promulgate rules and
regulations to require, in addition to other maintenance procedures, the
trimming of vines on utility poles throughout the state by the utility
company or cable television company that owns such utility pole where
such vine growth is likely to disrupt safe and reliable service.
```
