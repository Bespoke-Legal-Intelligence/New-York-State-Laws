# Public Service Law § 17 — Certified copies of papers filed to be evidence

[Public Service Law](../README.md) › [Article 1 — The Department of Public Service](README.md)

```text
  § 17. Certified copies of papers filed to be evidence.  Copies of all
official documents and orders filed or deposited according to law in the
office of the commission, certified by a commissioner or by the
secretary or assistant secretary of the commission to be true copies of
the originals, under the official seal of the division, shall be
evidence in like manner as the originals.
```
