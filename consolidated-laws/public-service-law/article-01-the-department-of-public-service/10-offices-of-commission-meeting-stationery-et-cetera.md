# Public Service Law § 10 — Offices of commission; meeting; stationery, et cetera

[Public Service Law](../README.md) › [Article 1 — The Department of Public Service](README.md)

```text
  § 10. Offices of commission; meeting; stationery, et cetera. The
principal office of the public service commission shall be in the city
of Albany, in rooms designated by the commissioner of general services.
The public service commission shall also maintain a branch office in the
city of New York. The commission shall hold stated meetings at least
once a month during the year at its office. The offices shall be
supplied with all necessary books, maps, charts, stationery, office
furniture, telephone and telegraph connections and all other necessary
appliances, to be paid for in the same manner as other expenses
authorized by this chapter.
```
