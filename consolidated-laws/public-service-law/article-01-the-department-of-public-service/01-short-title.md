# Public Service Law § 1 — Short title

[Public Service Law](../README.md) › [Article 1 — The Department of Public Service](README.md)

```text
  § 1. Short title. This chapter shall be known as the "Public Service
Law." Reference in any law to the public service commission law shall
mean the public service law, and amendments, in form, to the public
service commission law enacted by the legislature of nineteen hundred
thirty shall be treated as amendments to the public service law.
```
