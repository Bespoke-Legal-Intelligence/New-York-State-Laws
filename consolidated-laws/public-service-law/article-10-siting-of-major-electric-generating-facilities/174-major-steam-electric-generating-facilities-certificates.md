# Public Service Law § 174 — Major steam electric generating facilities certificates

[Public Service Law](../README.md) › [Article 10 — Siting of Major Electric Generating Facilities](README.md)

```text
  * § 174. Major steam electric generating facilities certificates. Any
certificate of environmental compatibility and public need issued to a
major steam electric generating facility under the former article VIII
of this chapter shall be treated for purposes of compliance and
enforcement as if such certificate was issued under this article.
  * NB Repealed December 31, 2040
```
