# Public Service Law § 89-A — Application of article

[Public Service Law](../README.md) › [Article 4-B — Provisions Relating to Water](README.md)

```text
  § 89-a. Application of article.  This article shall apply to the sale,
furnishing and distribution of water for domestic, commercial and public
purposes, not including bottled water.
```
