# General Municipal Law § 554-A — Agency contracts

[General Municipal Law](../README.md) › [Article 15-A — Municipal Urban Renewal Agencies, Organization and Powers](README.md)

```text
  § 554-a. Agency contracts. Except as otherwise expressly provided by
an act of the legislature, provisions of state and local law applicable
to the letting of public works and purchase contracts by the
municipality for which an agency is established shall apply to the same
degree and extent to such agency.
```
