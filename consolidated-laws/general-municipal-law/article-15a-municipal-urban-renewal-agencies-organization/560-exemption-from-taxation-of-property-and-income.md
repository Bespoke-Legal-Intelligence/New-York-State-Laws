# General Municipal Law § 560 — Exemption from taxation of property and income

[General Municipal Law](../README.md) › [Article 15-A — Municipal Urban Renewal Agencies, Organization and Powers](README.md)

```text
  § 560. Exemption from taxation of property and income.  The property
of an agency and its income and operations shall be exempt from
taxation.
```
