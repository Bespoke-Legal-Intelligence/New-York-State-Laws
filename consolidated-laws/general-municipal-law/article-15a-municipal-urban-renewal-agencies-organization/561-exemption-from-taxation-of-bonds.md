# General Municipal Law § 561 — Exemption from taxation of bonds

[General Municipal Law](../README.md) › [Article 15-A — Municipal Urban Renewal Agencies, Organization and Powers](README.md)

```text
  § 561. Exemption from taxation of bonds.  Bonds, mortgages, notes and
other obligations of an agency are declared to be issued for a public
purpose and to be public instrumentalities and together with interest
thereon, shall be exempt from taxation.
```
