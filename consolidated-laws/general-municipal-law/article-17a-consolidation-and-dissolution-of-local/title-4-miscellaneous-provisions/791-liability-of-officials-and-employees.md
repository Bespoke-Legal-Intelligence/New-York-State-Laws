# General Municipal Law § 791 — Liability of officials and employees

[General Municipal Law](../../README.md) › [Article 17-A — Consolidation and Dissolution of Local Government](../README.md) › [Title 4 — Miscellaneous Provisions](README.md)

```text
  § 791. Liability of officials and employees. In the absence of fraud,
gross negligence or willful misfeasance, no officer or employee of a
local government entity shall be held personally liable upon any claim
arising from the consolidation or dissolution of a local government
entity pursuant to this article or any circumstances connected with such
consolidation or dissolution.
```
