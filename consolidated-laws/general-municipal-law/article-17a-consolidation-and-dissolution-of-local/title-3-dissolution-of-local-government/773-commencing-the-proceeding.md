# General Municipal Law § 773 — Commencing the proceeding

[General Municipal Law](../../README.md) › [Article 17-A — Consolidation and Dissolution of Local Government](../README.md) › [Title 3 — Dissolution of Local Government Entities](README.md)

```text
  § 773. Commencing the proceeding. 1. A local government entity other
than a town may be dissolved and terminated by the procedure described
in this title.
  2. Dissolution proceedings may be commenced by:
  (a) a resolution of the governing body of the local government entity
to be dissolved endorsing a proposed dissolution plan; or
  (b) elector initiative.
```
