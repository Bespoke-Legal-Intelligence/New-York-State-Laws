# General Municipal Law § 778 — Effective date of dissolution plan

[General Municipal Law](../../README.md) › [Article 17-A — Consolidation and Dissolution of Local Government](../README.md) › [Title 3 — Dissolution of Local Government Entities](README.md)

```text
  § 778. Effective date of dissolution plan. A local government entity
dissolved pursuant to a dissolution plan shall continue to be governed
as before dissolution until the effective date of the dissolution
specified in the dissolution plan; provided, however, that no
dissolution plan for a village shall take effect unless approved by a
majority of electors of the village at a referendum called through a
resolution enacted pursuant to section seven hundred seventy-seven of
this title.
```
