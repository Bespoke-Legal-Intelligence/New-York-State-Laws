# General Municipal Law § 610 — Amsterdam urban renewal agency

[General Municipal Law](../../README.md) › [Article 15-B — Municipal Urban Renewal Agencies, Created](../README.md) › [Title 41 — Amsterdam Urban Renewal Agency](README.md)

```text
  § 610. Amsterdam urban renewal agency. An urban renewal agency, to be
known as the Amsterdam urban renewal agency, is hereby established for
the accomplishment of any or all of the purposes specified in articles
fifteen and fifteen-A of this chapter and in accordance with article
eighteen of the constitution of the state of New York. It shall
constitute a body corporate and politic, and be perpetual in duration.
It shall have the powers and duties now or hereafter conferred by
article fifteen-A of this chapter upon municipal renewal agencies. It
shall be organized in the manner prescribed by and be subject to the
provisions of article fifteen-A of this chapter and the agency, its
members, officers and employees and its operations and activities shall
in all respects be governed by the provisions of such article.
```
