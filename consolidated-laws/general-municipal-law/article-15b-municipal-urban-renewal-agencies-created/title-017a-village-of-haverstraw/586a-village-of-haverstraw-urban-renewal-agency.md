# General Municipal Law § 586-A — Village of Haverstraw urban renewal agency

[General Municipal Law](../../README.md) › [Article 15-B — Municipal Urban Renewal Agencies, Created](../README.md) › [Title 17-A — Village of Haverstraw Urban Renewal Agency](README.md)

**Repealed** (26 Dec 2014). The text below is what the Senate publishes for this repealed provision.

```text
  * § 586-a. Village of Haverstraw urban renewal agency. An urban
renewal agency, to be known as the village of Haverstraw urban renewal
agency, is hereby established for the accomplishment of any or all of
the purposes specified in articles fifteen and fifteen-A of this chapter
and in accordance with article eighteen of the constitution of the state
of New York. It shall constitute a body corporate and politic, be
perpetual in duration and consist of five members, appointed by the
mayor. Each member shall serve a term of five years. It shall have the
powers and duties now or hereafter conferred by article fifteen-A of
this chapter upon municipal renewal agencies. It shall be organized in
the manner prescribed by and be subject to the provisions of article
fifteen-A of this chapter and the agency, its members, officers and
employees and its operations and activities shall in all respects be
governed by the provisions of such article.
  * NB Repealed December 19, 2014
```
