# General Municipal Law § 670 — Village of Albion urban renewal agency

[General Municipal Law](../../README.md) › [Article 15-B — Municipal Urban Renewal Agencies, Created](../README.md) › [Title 102 — Village of Albion Urban Renewal Agency](README.md)

**Repealed** (26 Dec 2014). The text below is what the Senate publishes for this repealed provision.

```text
  * § 670. Village of Albion urban renewal agency. An urban renewal
agency, to be known as the village of Albion urban renewal agency, is
hereby established for the accomplishment of any or all of the purposes
specified in articles fifteen and fifteen-A of this chapter and in
accordance with article eighteen of the constitution of the state of New
York. It shall constitute a body corporate and politic, and be perpetual
in duration and consist of five members, the village mayor, who shall be
its chairman, and four other members, any or all of which may be a
member of the board of trustees. It shall have the powers and duties now
or hereafter conferred by article fifteen-A of this chapter upon
municipal renewal agencies. It shall be organized in the manner
prescribed by and be subject to the provisions of article fifteen-A of
this chapter and the agency, its members, officers and employees and its
operations and activities shall in all respects be governed by the
provisions of such article.
  * NB Repealed December 19, 2014
```
