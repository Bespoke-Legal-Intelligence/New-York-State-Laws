# General Municipal Law § 622 — Village of Johnson City urban renewal agency

[General Municipal Law](../../README.md) › [Article 15-B — Municipal Urban Renewal Agencies, Created](../README.md) › [Title 53 — Village of Johnson City Urban Renewal Agency](README.md)

```text
  * § 622. Village of Johnson City urban renewal agency. An urban
renewal agency, to be known as the village of Johnson City urban renewal
agency, is hereby established for the accomplishment of any or all of
the purposes specified in articles fifteen and fifteen-A of this chapter
and in accordance with article eighteen of the constitution of the state
of New York. It shall constitute a body corporate and politic, shall be
perpetual in duration, and shall consist of five members who shall be
appointed by and serve at the pleasure of the village board of trustees.
It shall have the powers and duties now or hereafter conferred by
article fifteen-A of this chapter upon municipal renewal agencies. It
shall be organized in the manner prescribed by and be subject to the
provisions of article fifteen-A of this chapter and the agency, its
members, officers and employees and its operations and activities shall
in all respects be governed by the provisions of such article.
  * NB Repealed December 19, 2014
```
