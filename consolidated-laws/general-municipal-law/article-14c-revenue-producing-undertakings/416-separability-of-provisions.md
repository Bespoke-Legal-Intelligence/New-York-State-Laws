# General Municipal Law § 416 — Separability of provisions

[General Municipal Law](../README.md) › [Article 14-C — Revenue-producing Undertakings](README.md)

```text
  § 416. Separability of provisions. If any provision of this article or
the application of such provision to any person, body, or circumstance
shall be held invalid, the remainder of this article or the application
of such provision to persons, bodies, or circumstances other than those
as to which it shall have been held invalid, shall not be affected
thereby.
```
