# General Municipal Law § 411 — Undertaking exempt from taxation

[General Municipal Law](../README.md) › [Article 14-C — Revenue-producing Undertakings](README.md)

```text
  § 411. Undertaking exempt from taxation. So long as a municipality
shall own any undertaking, the property, within the municipality, and
all revenue of such undertaking shall be exempt from taxation.
```
