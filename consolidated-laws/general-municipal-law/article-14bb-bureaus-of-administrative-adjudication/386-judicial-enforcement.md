# General Municipal Law § 386 — Judicial enforcement

[General Municipal Law](../README.md) › [Article 14-BB — Bureaus of Administrative Adjudication](README.md)

```text
  § 386. Judicial enforcement. Any order or subpoena or any final
decision or determination rendered by the bureau shall be subject to
enforcement by the judiciary in an action or proceeding commenced in a
court of competent jurisdiction by the prevailing party including the
municipality.
```
