# General Municipal Law § 387 — Restriction on collateral use

[General Municipal Law](../README.md) › [Article 14-BB — Bureaus of Administrative Adjudication](README.md)

```text
  § 387. Restriction on collateral use. Decisions, determinations and
orders issued by the bureau shall not be cited, and shall not be
considered precedent nor be given any force or effect in any criminal
proceeding.
```
