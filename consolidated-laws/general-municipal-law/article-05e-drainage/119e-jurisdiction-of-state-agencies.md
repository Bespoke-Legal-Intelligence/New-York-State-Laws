# General Municipal Law § 119-E — Jurisdiction of state agencies

[General Municipal Law](../README.md) › [Article 5-E — Drainage](README.md)

```text
  § 119-e. Jurisdiction of state agencies. Nothing contained in this
article shall be held to alter or abridge the powers and duties of any
state department or agency in relation to drainage matters.
```
