# General Municipal Law § 696-C — Site preparation

[General Municipal Law](../README.md) › [Article 16 — Urban Development Action Area Act](README.md)

```text
  § 696-c. Site preparation. A municipality shall be authorized to
undertake site preparation for each eligible area prior to its
disposition. Such site preparation may include, but need not be limited
to, demolition, site clearance and the curing of problems caused by
abnormal site conditions.
```
