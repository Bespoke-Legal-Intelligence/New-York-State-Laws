# General Municipal Law § 165-A — Voluntary municipal assistance to public cemetery corporations

[General Municipal Law](../README.md) › [Article 8 — Cemeteries](README.md)

```text
  § 165-a. Voluntary municipal assistance to public cemetery
corporations.  Any municipal corporation may appropriate and provide
funding to a public cemetery corporation as defined in article fifteen
of the not-for-profit corporation law. In lieu of or in addition to
providing funding to a public cemetery corporation, any municipal
corporation may provide goods and/or services to a public cemetery
corporation as defined in article fifteen of the not-for-profit
corporation law.
```
