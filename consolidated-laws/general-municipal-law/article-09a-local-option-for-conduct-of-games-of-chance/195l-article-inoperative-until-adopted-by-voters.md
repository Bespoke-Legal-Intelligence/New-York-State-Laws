# General Municipal Law § 195-L — Article inoperative until adopted by voters

[General Municipal Law](../README.md) › [Article 9-A — Local Option For Conduct of Games of Chance By Certain Organizations](README.md)

```text
  § 195-l. Article inoperative until adopted by voters. Except as
provided in section one hundred ninety-five-k of this article, the
provisions of this article shall remain inoperative in any municipality
unless and until a proposition therefor submitted at a general or
special election in such municipality shall be approved by a vote of the
majority of the qualified electors in such municipality voting thereon.
```
