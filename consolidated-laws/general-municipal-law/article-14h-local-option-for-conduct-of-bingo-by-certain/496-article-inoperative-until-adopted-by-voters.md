# General Municipal Law § 496 — Article inoperative until adopted by voters

[General Municipal Law](../README.md) › [Article 14-H — Local Option For Conduct of Bingo By Certain Organizations](README.md)

```text
  § 496. Article inoperative until adopted by voters.  Except as
provided in section 495-a, the provisions of this article shall remain
inoperative in any municipality unless and until a proposition therefor
submitted at a general or special election in such municipality shall be
approved by a vote of the majority of the qualified electors in such
municipality voting thereon.
```
