# General Municipal Law § 498 — Delegation of authority

[General Municipal Law](../README.md) › [Article 14-H — Local Option For Conduct of Bingo By Certain Organizations](README.md)

```text
  § 498. Delegation of authority.  The governing body of a municipality
may delegate to an officer or officers thereof designated by it for that
purpose any of the authority granted to it hereby in relation to the
issuance, amendment and cancellation of licenses, the conduct of
investigations and hearings, the supervision of the operation of the
games and the collection and transmission of fees.
```
