# General Municipal Law § 353 — Approval of board of estimate required

[General Municipal Law](../README.md) › [Article 14 — Airports and Landing Fields](README.md)

```text
  § 353. Approval of board of estimate required. Wherever under this
article the local legislative body of a city is authorized to take any
action, the separate approval of such action by the board of estimate
and apportionment or similar body of such city, if there be such a board
or body, shall also be required, unless such board of estimate and
apportionment or similar body is a branch of the local legislative body.
```
