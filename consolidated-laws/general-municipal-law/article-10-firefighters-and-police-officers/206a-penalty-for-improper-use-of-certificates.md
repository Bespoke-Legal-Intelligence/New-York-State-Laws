# General Municipal Law § 206-A — Penalty for improper use of certificates

[General Municipal Law](../README.md) › [Article 10 — Firefighters and Police Officers](README.md)

```text
  § 206-a. Penalty for improper use of certificates. Every police
officer and firefighter who shall permit any other person to use the
certificate issued to him or her as provided by the last section, or
shall present or make use of the same, except while acting in the course
of the performance of his or her official duties, or who shall use such
certificate after the expiration of his or her term of office or his
resignation or removal therefrom, shall be deemed guilty of a
misdemeanor.
```
