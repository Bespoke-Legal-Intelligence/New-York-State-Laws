# General Municipal Law § 512 — Approval of program by commissioner

[General Municipal Law](../README.md) › [Article 15 — Urban Renewal](README.md)

```text
  § 512. Approval of program by commissioner. No contract for a state
loan and periodic subsidy or for a state capital grant shall be executed
until the program for which such aid is sought has been approved by the
commissioner.
```
