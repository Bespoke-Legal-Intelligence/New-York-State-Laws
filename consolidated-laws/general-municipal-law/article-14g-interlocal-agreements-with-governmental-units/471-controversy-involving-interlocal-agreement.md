# General Municipal Law § 471 — Controversy involving interlocal agreement

[General Municipal Law](../README.md) › [Article 14-G — Interlocal Agreements With Governmental Units of Other States](README.md)

```text
  § 471. Controversy involving interlocal agreement. In any case or
controversy involving the performance or interpretation of the
interlocal agreement or liability thereunder, the contracting public
agencies shall be the real parties in interest.
```
