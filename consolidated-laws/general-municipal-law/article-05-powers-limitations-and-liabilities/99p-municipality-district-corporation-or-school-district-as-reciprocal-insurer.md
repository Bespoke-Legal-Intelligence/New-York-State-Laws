# General Municipal Law § 99-P — Municipality, district corporation or school district as reciprocal insurer

[General Municipal Law](../README.md) › [Article 5 — Powers, Limitations and Liabilities](README.md)

```text
  § 99-p. Municipality, district corporation or school district as
reciprocal insurer. Any county, town, city, village, district
corporation (as defined in paragraph three of section 2.00 of the local
finance law), or school district and board of cooperative educational
services, may become a subscriber to a municipal reciprocal insurer
formed under article sixty-one of the insurance law.
```
