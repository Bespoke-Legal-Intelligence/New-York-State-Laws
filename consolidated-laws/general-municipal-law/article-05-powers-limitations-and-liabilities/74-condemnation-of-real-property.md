# General Municipal Law § 74 — Condemnation of real property

[General Municipal Law](../README.md) › [Article 5 — Powers, Limitations and Liabilities](README.md)

```text
  § 74. Condemnation of real property. A municipal corporation
authorized by law to take and hold real property for the uses and
purposes of the corporation, may, if it is unable to agree with the
owners for the purchase thereof, acquire title to such property by
condemnation.
```
