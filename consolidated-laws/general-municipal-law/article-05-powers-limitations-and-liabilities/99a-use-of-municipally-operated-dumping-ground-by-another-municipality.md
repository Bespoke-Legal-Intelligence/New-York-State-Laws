# General Municipal Law § 99-A — Use of municipally operated dumping ground by another municipality

[General Municipal Law](../README.md) › [Article 5 — Powers, Limitations and Liabilities](README.md)

```text
  § 99-a. Use of municipally operated dumping ground by another
municipality. One municipality may contract with any other municipality
for the use of a municipally operated public dump or dumping ground on
such terms as may be agreed upon.
```
