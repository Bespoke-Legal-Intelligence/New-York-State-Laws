# General Municipal Law § 99-C — Agreements for joint building inspectors by municipal corporations

[General Municipal Law](../README.md) › [Article 5 — Powers, Limitations and Liabilities](README.md)

```text
  § 99-c. Agreements for joint building inspectors by municipal
corporations.  It shall be lawful for two or more municipal corporations
to engage jointly one building inspector and make an agreement
specifying how such inspector shall be paid for his services.
```
