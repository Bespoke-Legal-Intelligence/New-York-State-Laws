# General Municipal Law § 78 — Insurance of property

[General Municipal Law](../README.md) › [Article 5 — Powers, Limitations and Liabilities](README.md)

```text
  § 78. Insurance of property. Public officers having by law the care
and custody of the public buildings and other property of a municipal
corporation, may insure the same at the expense and for the benefit of
such corporation.
```
