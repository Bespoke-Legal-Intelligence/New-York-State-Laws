# General Municipal Law § 144 — Parks, playgrounds and libraries to be free

[General Municipal Law](../README.md) › [Article 7 — Trusts For Parks and Libraries In Villages and Towns](README.md)

```text
  § 144. Parks, playgrounds and libraries to be free. All parks,
playgrounds and libraries existing under this article shall be free and
open to the public for use and enjoyment, subject only to such
reasonable rules and regulations as the trustees from time to time shall
adopt and promulgate.
```
