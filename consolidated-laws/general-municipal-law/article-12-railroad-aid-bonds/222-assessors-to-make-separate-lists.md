# General Municipal Law § 222 — Assessors to make separate lists

[General Municipal Law](../README.md) › [Article 12 — Railroad Aid Bonds](README.md)

```text
  § 222. Assessors to make separate lists. The assessors of the town to
which shall have been added a part of another town, shall yearly, until
such bonds be paid, make a separate and distinct list of the taxable
inhabitants and lands contained in the territory so annexed in the
assessment-roll of the said town, in all respects similar in form and
manner to the assessment-roll as now made.  Said list shall be
designated in such roll "List of annexed lands and inhabitants."
```
