# General Municipal Law § 230 — Compensation of supervisor

[General Municipal Law](../README.md) › [Article 12 — Railroad Aid Bonds](README.md)

**Repealed** (20 Sep 2024). The text below is what the Senate publishes for this repealed provision.

```text
  § 230. Compensation of supervisor. For the performance of the
additional duties devolved upon him under the provisions of this
article, such supervisor shall be entitled to reasonable compensation,
to be fixed by the board of town auditors of such town.
```
