# General Municipal Law § 699-C — Mortgage counseling for the Long Island workforce housing program

[General Municipal Law](../README.md) › [Article 16-A — Long Island Workforce Housing Act](README.md)

```text
  § 699-c. Mortgage counseling for the Long Island workforce housing
program. Persons purchasing affordable workforce housing created
pursuant to this article, or who receive downpayment assistance pursuant
to this article, must attend homebuyer education and mortgage counseling
provided free of charge through the Long Island Housing Partnership.
```
