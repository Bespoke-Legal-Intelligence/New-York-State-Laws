# General Municipal Law § 246 — Annual appropriation

[General Municipal Law](../README.md) › [Article 13 — Playgrounds and Neighborhood Recreation Centers](README.md)

```text
  § 246. Annual appropriation. The local authorities of a municipality
having power to appropriate money therein may annually appropriate and
cause to be raised by taxation in such municipality a sum sufficient to
carry out the provisions of this article.
```
