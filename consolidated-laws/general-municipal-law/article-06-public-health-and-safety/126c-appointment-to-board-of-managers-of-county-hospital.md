# General Municipal Law § 126-C — Appointment to board of managers of county hospital

[General Municipal Law](../README.md) › [Article 6 — Public Health and Safety](README.md)

```text
  § 126-c. Appointment to board of managers of county hospital.  When a
county operates a county hospital, the local governing body of such
county may appoint one of its own members to the board of managers of
such county hospital.
```
