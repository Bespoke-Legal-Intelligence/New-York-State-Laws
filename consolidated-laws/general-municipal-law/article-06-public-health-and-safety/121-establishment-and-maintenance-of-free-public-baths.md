# General Municipal Law § 121 — Establishment and maintenance of free public baths

[General Municipal Law](../README.md) › [Article 6 — Public Health and Safety](README.md)

```text
  § 121. Establishment and maintenance of free public baths. Any city,
village or town may establish and maintain free public baths, and any
city, village or town may appropriate of its funds for the purpose of
establishing such free public baths.
```
