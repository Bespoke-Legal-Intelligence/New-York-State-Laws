# General Municipal Law § 120-Q — Rules and regulations

[General Municipal Law](../README.md) › [Article 6 — Public Health and Safety](README.md)

```text
  § 120-q. Rules and regulations. Such person, persons or corporation
operating and maintaining such system or contracting for the removal of
sewage as herein provided shall be subject to such rules, ordinances and
regulations as said municipalities may establish, not inconsistent with
any contract made therefor.
```
