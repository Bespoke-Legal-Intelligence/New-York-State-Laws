# General Municipal Law § 365 — Effect of unconstitutionality in part

[General Municipal Law](../README.md) › [Article 14-A — Gas and Electric Service](README.md)

```text
  § 365. Effect of unconstitutionality in part. If any section, clause
or provision of this article shall be unconstitutional or invalid or
ineffective in whole or in part, to the extent that it is not
unconstitutional or invalid or ineffective it shall be valid and
effective; and no other section, clause or provision shall on account
thereof be deemed invalid or ineffective.
```
