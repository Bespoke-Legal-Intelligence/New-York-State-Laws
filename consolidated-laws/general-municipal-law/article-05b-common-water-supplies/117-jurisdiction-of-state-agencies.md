# General Municipal Law § 117 — Jurisdiction of state agencies

[General Municipal Law](../README.md) › [Article 5-B — Common Water Supplies](README.md)

```text
  § 117. Jurisdiction of state agencies.  Nothing contained in this
article shall be held to alter or abridge the powers and duties of the
state department of health or of the water power and control commission
over water supply matters.
```
