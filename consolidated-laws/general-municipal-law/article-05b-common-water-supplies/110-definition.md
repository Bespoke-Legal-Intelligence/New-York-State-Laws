# General Municipal Law § 110 — Definition

[General Municipal Law](../README.md) › [Article 5-B — Common Water Supplies](README.md)

```text
  § 110. Definition.  The term "municipality" as used in this article
shall mean a city, a village, a county on behalf of a county water
district and a town acting on behalf of a water district or water
storage and distribution district, including a water district governed
by a separate board of commissioners.
```
