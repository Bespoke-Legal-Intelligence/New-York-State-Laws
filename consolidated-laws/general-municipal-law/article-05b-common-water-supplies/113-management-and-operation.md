# General Municipal Law § 113 — Management and operation

[General Municipal Law](../README.md) › [Article 5-B — Common Water Supplies](README.md)

```text
  § 113. Management and operation.  The joint water works system may be
managed and operated by a joint operating agency, by one of the
contracting municipalities or by contract with a public authority as the
contracting municipalities shall provide. A contract with a public
authority shall provide for periodic reports of operations to the
participating municipalities.
```
