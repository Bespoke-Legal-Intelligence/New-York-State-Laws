# General Municipal Law § 430 — Application of article

[General Municipal Law](../README.md) › [Article 14-D — Jails](README.md)

```text
  § 430. Application of article. This article shall apply to all
counties outside the city of New York and to the several municipal
corporations within such counties.
```
