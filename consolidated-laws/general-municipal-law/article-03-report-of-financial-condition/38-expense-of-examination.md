# General Municipal Law § 38 — Expense of examination

[General Municipal Law](../README.md) › [Article 3 — Report of Financial Condition](README.md)

```text
  § 38. Expense of examination. The expenses of examining the accounts
of any municipal corporation, industrial development agency, district,
agency or activity shall be paid out of such appropriation as shall be
made to carry the provisions of this article into effect.
```
