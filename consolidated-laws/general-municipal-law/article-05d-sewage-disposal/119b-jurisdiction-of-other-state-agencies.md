# General Municipal Law § 119-B — Jurisdiction of other state agencies

[General Municipal Law](../README.md) › [Article 5-D — Sewage Disposal](README.md)

```text
  § 119-b. Jurisdiction of other state agencies. Nothing contained in
this article shall be held to alter or abridge the powers and duties of
the state department of health, the state conservation department or the
water pollution control board over sewage treatment and disposal
matters.
```
