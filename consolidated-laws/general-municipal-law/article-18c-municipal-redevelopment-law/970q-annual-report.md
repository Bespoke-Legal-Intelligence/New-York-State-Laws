# General Municipal Law § 970-Q — Annual report

[General Municipal Law](../README.md) › [Article 18-C — Municipal Redevelopment Law](README.md)

```text
  § 970-q. Annual report. The municipality shall submit to the state
comptroller, on or before March thirty-first of each year, a progress
report and financial statement for the preceding calendar year for each
redevelopment project undertaken pursuant to this article.
```
