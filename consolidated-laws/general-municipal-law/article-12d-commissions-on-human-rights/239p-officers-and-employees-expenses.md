# General Municipal Law § 239-P — Officers and employees; expenses

[General Municipal Law](../README.md) › [Article 12-D — Commissions On Human Rights](README.md)

```text
  § 239-p. Officers and employees; expenses. The commission shall have
the power and authority to employ an executive director, a secretary and
such attorneys, experts and other employees as may be necessary, within
the amount made available by the local governing board. Each county,
city, village and town is hereby authorized and empowered to make such
appropriation for such expenses and for the compensation, if any, to be
paid to the members of the commission.
```
