# General Municipal Law § 275 — Picnic parks and/or roadside rest areas

[General Municipal Law](../README.md) › [Article 13-B — Picnic Parks And/or Roadside Rest Areas](README.md)

```text
  § 275. Picnic parks and/or roadside rest areas. The governing body of
any county, city, town or village is hereby authorized and empowered to
authorize and direct such of its officers or agencies as it shall
designate to enter into agreements with the state superintendent of
public works for the maintenance of a picnic park and/or roadside rest
area as authorized by section twenty of the highway law and to
appropriate and spend moneys of the county, city, town or village for
that purpose.
```
