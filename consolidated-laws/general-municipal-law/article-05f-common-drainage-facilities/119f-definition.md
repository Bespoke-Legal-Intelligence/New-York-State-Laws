# General Municipal Law § 119-F — Definition

[General Municipal Law](../README.md) › [Article 5-F — Common Drainage Facilities](README.md)

```text
  § 119-f. Definition. The term "municipality" as used in this article
shall mean a city, a village, a county on behalf of a county drainage
district, a town and a town acting on behalf of a town drainage
district.
```
