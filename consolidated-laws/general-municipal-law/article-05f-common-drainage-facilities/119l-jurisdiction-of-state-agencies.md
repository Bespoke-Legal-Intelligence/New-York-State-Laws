# General Municipal Law § 119-L — Jurisdiction of state agencies

[General Municipal Law](../README.md) › [Article 5-F — Common Drainage Facilities](README.md)

```text
  § 119-l. Jurisdiction of state agencies. Nothing contained in this
article shall be held to alter or abridge the powers and duties of the
state conservation department or any other state department or agency
over drainage matters.
```
