# General Municipal Law § 888 — Inconsistent provisions in other acts superseded

[General Municipal Law](../../README.md) › [Article 18-A — Industrial Development](../README.md) › [Title 1 — Agencies, Organization and Powers](README.md)

```text
  § 888. Inconsistent provisions in other acts superseded. Insofar as
the provisions of this title are inconsistent with the provisions of any
other act, general or special, or of any local laws of the municipality,
the provisions of this title shall be controlling except in cases of
inconsistency with the Indian law.
```
