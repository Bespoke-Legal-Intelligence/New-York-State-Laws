# General Municipal Law § 870 — State and municipality not liable on bonds or notes

[General Municipal Law](../../README.md) › [Article 18-A — Industrial Development](../README.md) › [Title 1 — Agencies, Organization and Powers](README.md)

```text
  § 870. State and municipality not liable on bonds or notes. The bonds
or notes and other obligations of the authority shall not be a debt of
the state or of the municipality, and neither the state nor the
municipality shall be liable thereon, nor shall they be payable out of
any funds other than those of the agency.
```
