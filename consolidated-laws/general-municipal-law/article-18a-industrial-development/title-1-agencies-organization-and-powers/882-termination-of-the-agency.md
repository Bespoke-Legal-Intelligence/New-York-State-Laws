# General Municipal Law § 882 — Termination of the agency

[General Municipal Law](../../README.md) › [Article 18-A — Industrial Development](../README.md) › [Title 1 — Agencies, Organization and Powers](README.md)

```text
  § 882. Termination of the agency. Whenever all of the bonds or notes
issued by the agency shall have been redeemed or cancelled, and all
straight-lease transactions have been terminated, the agency shall cease
to exist and all rights, titles, and interest and all obligations and
liabilities thereof vested in or possessed by the agency shall thereupon
vest in and be possessed by the municipality.
```
