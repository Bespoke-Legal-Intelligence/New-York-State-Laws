# General Municipal Law § 883 — Conflicts of interest

[General Municipal Law](../../README.md) › [Article 18-A — Industrial Development](../README.md) › [Title 1 — Agencies, Organization and Powers](README.md)

```text
  § 883. Conflicts of interest. All members, officers, and employees of
an agency or industrial development authority established by this
chapter or created by the public authorities law shall be subject to the
provisions of article eighteen of this chapter.
```
