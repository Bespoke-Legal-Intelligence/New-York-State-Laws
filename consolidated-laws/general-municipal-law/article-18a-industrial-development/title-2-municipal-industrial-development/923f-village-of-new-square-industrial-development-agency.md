# General Municipal Law § 923-F — Village of New Square industrial development agency

[General Municipal Law](../../README.md) › [Article 18-A — Industrial Development](../README.md) › [Title 2 — Municipal Industrial Development Agencies](README.md)

```text
  * § 923-f. Village of New Square industrial development agency. For
the benefit of the Village of New Square and the inhabitants thereof, an
industrial development agency, to be known as the VILLAGE OF NEW SQUARE
INDUSTRIAL DEVELOPMENT AGENCY, is hereby established for the
accomplishment of any or all of the purposes specified in title one of
article eighteen-A of this chapter. It shall constitute a body corporate
and politic, and be perpetual in duration. It shall have the powers and
duties now conferred by title one of article eighteen-A of this chapter
upon industrial development agencies except that it shall not have the
power of condemnation and, provided however, that the exercise of the
powers by such agency with respect to the acquisition of real property
whether by purchase or otherwise, shall be limited to lands within the
confines of the incorporated area of the village of New Square and shall
be subject to the local zoning and planning regulations and shall take
into consideration the regional and local comprehensive land use plans.
It shall be organized in a manner prescribed by and be subject to the
provisions of title one of article eighteen-A of this chapter. Its
members shall be appointed by the governing body of the Village of New
Square. The agency, its members, officers and employees and its
operations and activities shall in all respects be governed by the
provisions of title one of article eighteen-A of this chapter.
  * NB Repealed December 19, 2014
```
