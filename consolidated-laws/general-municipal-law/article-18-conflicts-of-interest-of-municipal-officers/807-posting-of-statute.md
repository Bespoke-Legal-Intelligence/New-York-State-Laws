# General Municipal Law § 807 — Posting of statute

[General Municipal Law](../README.md) › [Article 18 — Conflicts of Interest of Municipal Officers and Employees](README.md)

```text
  § 807. Posting of statute. The chief executive officer of each
municipality shall cause a copy of sections eight hundred through eight
hundred nine of this article to be kept posted in each public building
under the jurisdiction of his or her municipality in a place conspicuous
to its officers and employees. Failure to post any such copy shall have
no effect on the duty of compliance with this article, nor with the
enforcement of the provisions thereof.
```
