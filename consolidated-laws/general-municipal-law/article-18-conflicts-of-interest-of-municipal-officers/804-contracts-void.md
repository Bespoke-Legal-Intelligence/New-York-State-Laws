# General Municipal Law § 804 — Contracts void

[General Municipal Law](../README.md) › [Article 18 — Conflicts of Interest of Municipal Officers and Employees](README.md)

```text
  § 804. Contracts void. Any contract willfully entered into by or with
a municipality in which there is an interest prohibited by this article
shall be null, void and wholly unenforceable.
```
