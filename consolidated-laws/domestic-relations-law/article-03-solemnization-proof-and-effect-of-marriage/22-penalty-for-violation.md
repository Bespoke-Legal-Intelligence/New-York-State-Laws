# Domestic Relations Law § 22 — Penalty for violation

[Domestic Relations Law](../README.md) › [Article 3 — Solemnization, Proof and Effect of Marriage](README.md)

```text
  § 22. Penalty for violation. Any town or city clerk who shall violate
any of the provisions of this article or shall fail to comply therewith
shall be deemed guilty of a misdemeanor and shall pay a fine not
exceeding the sum of one hundred dollars on conviction thereof.
```
