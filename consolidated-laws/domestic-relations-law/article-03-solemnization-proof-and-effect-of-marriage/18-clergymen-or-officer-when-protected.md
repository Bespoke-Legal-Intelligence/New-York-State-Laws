# Domestic Relations Law § 18 — Clergymen or officer, when protected

[Domestic Relations Law](../README.md) › [Article 3 — Solemnization, Proof and Effect of Marriage](README.md)

```text
  § 18. Clergymen or officer, when protected. Any such clergymen or
officer as aforesaid to whom any such license duly issued may come and
not having personal knowledge of the incompetency of either party
therein named to contract matrimony, may lawfully solemnize matrimony
between them.
```
