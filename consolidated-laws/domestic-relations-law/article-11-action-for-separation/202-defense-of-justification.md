# Domestic Relations Law § 202 — Defense of justification

[Domestic Relations Law](../README.md) › [Article 11 — Action For Separation](README.md)

```text
  § 202. Defense of justification. The defendant in an action for
separation from bed and board may set up, in justification, the
misconduct of the plaintiff; and if that defense is established to the
satisfaction of the court, the defendant is entitled to judgment.
```
