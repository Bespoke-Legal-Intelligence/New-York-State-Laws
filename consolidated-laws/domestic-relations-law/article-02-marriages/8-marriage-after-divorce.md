# Domestic Relations Law § 8 — Marriage after divorce

[Domestic Relations Law](../README.md) › [Article 2 — Marriages](README.md)

```text
  § 8. Marriage after divorce. Whenever, and whether prior or subsequent
to September first, nineteen hundred sixty-seven, a marriage has been
dissolved by divorce, either party may marry again.
```
