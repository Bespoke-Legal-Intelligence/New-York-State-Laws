# Domestic Relations Law § 2 — Definitions

[Domestic Relations Law](../README.md) › [Article 1 — Short Title; Definitions](README.md)

```text
  § 2. Definitions.  A "minor" or "infant", as used in this chapter, is
a person under the age of eighteen years.
```
