# Domestic Relations Law § 122 — Public policy

[Domestic Relations Law](../README.md) › [Article 8 — Genetic Surrogate Parenting Contracts](README.md)

```text
  § 122. Public policy. Genetic surrogate parenting agreements are
hereby declared contrary to the public policy of this state, and are
void and unenforceable.
```
