# Domestic Relations Law § 77-A — Enforcement under Hague Convention

[Domestic Relations Law](../../README.md) › [Article 5-A — Uniform Child Custody Jurisdiction and Enforcement Act](../README.md) › [Title 3 — Enforcement](README.md)

```text
  § 77-a. Enforcement under Hague Convention. Under this act, a court of
this state may enforce an order for the return of the child made under
the Hague Convention on the Civil Aspects of International Child
Abduction as if it were a child custody determination.
```
