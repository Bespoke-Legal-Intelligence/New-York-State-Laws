# Domestic Relations Law § 75-F — Priority

[Domestic Relations Law](../../README.md) › [Article 5-A — Uniform Child Custody Jurisdiction and Enforcement Act](../README.md) › [Title 1 — General Provisions](README.md)

```text
  § 75-f. Priority. If a question of existence or exercise of
jurisdiction under this article is raised in a child custody proceeding,
the question, upon request of a party, child or child's attorney must be
given priority on the calendar and handled expeditiously.
```
