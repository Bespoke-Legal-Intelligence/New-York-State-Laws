# Domestic Relations Law § 78-A — Severability clause

[Domestic Relations Law](../../README.md) › [Article 5-A — Uniform Child Custody Jurisdiction and Enforcement Act](../README.md) › [Title 4 — Miscellaneous Provisions](README.md)

```text
  § 78-a. Severability clause. If any provision of this article or its
application to any person or circumstance is held invalid, the
invalidity does not affect other provisions or applications of this
article which can be given effect without the invalid provision or
application, and to this end the provisions of this article are
severable.
```
