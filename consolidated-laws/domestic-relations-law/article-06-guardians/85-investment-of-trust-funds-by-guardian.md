# Domestic Relations Law § 85 — Investment of trust funds by guardian

[Domestic Relations Law](../README.md) › [Article 6 — Guardians](README.md)

```text
  § 85. Investment of trust funds by guardian. A guardian holding funds
for investment has the powers provided by section twenty-one of the
personal property law and must not invest the funds in any other
securities or manner.
```
