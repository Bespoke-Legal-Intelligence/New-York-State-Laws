# Domestic Relations Law § 58 — Pardon not to restore marital rights

[Domestic Relations Law](../README.md) › [Article 4 — Certain Rights and Liabilities of Husband and Wife](README.md)

```text
  § 58. Pardon not to restore marital rights. A pardon granted to a
person sentenced to imprisonment for life within this state does not
restore that person to the rights of a previous marriage or to the
guardianship of a child, the issue of such a marriage.
```
