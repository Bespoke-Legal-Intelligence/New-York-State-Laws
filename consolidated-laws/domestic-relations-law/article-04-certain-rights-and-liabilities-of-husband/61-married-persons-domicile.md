# Domestic Relations Law § 61 — Married person's domicile

[Domestic Relations Law](../README.md) › [Article 4 — Certain Rights and Liabilities of Husband and Wife](README.md)

```text
  § 61. Married person's domicile. The domicile of a married man or
woman shall be established for all purposes without regard to sex.
```
