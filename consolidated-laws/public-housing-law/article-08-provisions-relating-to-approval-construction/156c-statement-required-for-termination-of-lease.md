# Public Housing Law § 156-C — Statement required for termination of lease

[Public Housing Law](../README.md) › [Article 8 — Provisions Relating to Approval, Construction, Management and Operation of Projects](README.md)

```text
  § 156-c. Statement required for termination of lease.  Notwithstanding
any other provision of law, an authority must incorporate into a written
notice of termination of a lease the specific grounds upon which it has
made its determination for termination.
```
