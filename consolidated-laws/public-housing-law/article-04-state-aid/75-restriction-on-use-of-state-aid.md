# Public Housing Law § 75 — Restriction on use of state aid

[Public Housing Law](../README.md) › [Article 4 — State Aid](README.md)

```text
  § 75. Restriction on use of state aid.  No state moneys allocated to
an authority or a municipality hereunder shall be mingled or commingled
with financial aid or assistance from the federal government or any
agency thereof, except with the approval of the commissioner.
```
