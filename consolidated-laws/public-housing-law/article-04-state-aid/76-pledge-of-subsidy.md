# Public Housing Law § 76 — Pledge of subsidy

[Public Housing Law](../README.md) › [Article 4 — State Aid](README.md)

```text
  § 76. Pledge of subsidy.  Payments under any contracts for periodic
subsidies by the state may be pledged by an authority as security for
loans obtained from any source for the project to which such periodic
subsidies relate.
```
