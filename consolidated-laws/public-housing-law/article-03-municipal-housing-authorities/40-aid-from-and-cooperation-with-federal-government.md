# Public Housing Law § 40 — Aid from and cooperation with federal government

[Public Housing Law](../README.md) › [Article 3 — Municipal Housing Authorities](README.md)

```text
  § 40. Aid from and cooperation with federal government.  An authority
is empowered to include in agreements with the federal government
provisions that such government shall have the right to supervise and
approve the construction, maintenance and operation of any project to
which such government may render any assistance, and subject to the
restrictions contained in this chapter, any other terms, covenants and
provisions which the authority may deem necessary or appropriate.
```
