# Public Housing Law § 48 — Guaranteed indebtedness of an authority

[Public Housing Law](../README.md) › [Article 3 — Municipal Housing Authorities](README.md)

```text
  § 48. Guaranteed indebtedness of an authority.  An authority may
contract indebtedness and authorize and issue bonds guaranteed by a
municipality as to principal and interest, or only as to the interest.
```
