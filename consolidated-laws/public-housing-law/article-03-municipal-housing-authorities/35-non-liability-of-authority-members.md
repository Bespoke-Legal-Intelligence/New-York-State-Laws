# Public Housing Law § 35 — Non-liability of authority members

[Public Housing Law](../README.md) › [Article 3 — Municipal Housing Authorities](README.md)

```text
  § 35. Non-liability of authority members.  No personal liability or
accountability shall attach to any member of an authority or to any
person executing bonds of such authority by reason of the execution or
issuance thereof.
```
