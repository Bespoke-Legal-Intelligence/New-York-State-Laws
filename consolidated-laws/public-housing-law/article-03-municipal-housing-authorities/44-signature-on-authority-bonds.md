# Public Housing Law § 44 — Signature on authority bonds

[Public Housing Law](../README.md) › [Article 3 — Municipal Housing Authorities](README.md)

```text
  § 44. Signature on authority bonds.  In case any of the members or
officers of the authority whose signatures appear on any bonds or
coupons shall cease to be such members or officers before the delivery
of such bonds, such signatures shall, nevertheless, be valid and
sufficient for all purposes, the same as if they had remained in office
until such delivery.
```
