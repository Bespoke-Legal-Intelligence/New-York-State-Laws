# Public Housing Law § 45 — Repurchase of authority bonds

[Public Housing Law](../README.md) › [Article 3 — Municipal Housing Authorities](README.md)

```text
  § 45. Repurchase of authority bonds.  An authority shall have power to
purchase any bonds issued by it for the purpose of retirement or
investment. An authority shall not purchase such bonds at a price in
excess of the face value thereof plus a premium of four per centum. All
bonds purchased for the purpose of retirement shall be cancelled.
```
