# Public Housing Law § 56 — Authorities created prior to the enactment of this chapter

[Public Housing Law](../README.md) › [Article 3 — Municipal Housing Authorities](README.md)

```text
  § 56. Authorities created prior to the enactment of this chapter.  Any
authority created prior to the enactment of this chapter pursuant to the
provisions of the municipal housing authorities law shall continue, as
now constituted, in existence as an authority under the provisions of
this chapter.
```
