# Public Housing Law § 223-B — Discrimination against a person who is deaf or hard of hearing who has a hearing dog

[Public Housing Law](../README.md) › [Article 11 — Miscellaneous](README.md)

```text
  § 223-b. Discrimination against a person who is deaf or hard of
hearing who has a hearing dog. No person who is deaf or hard of hearing
shall be denied occupancy in a dwelling in any project or be subjected
to eviction from any such dwelling on the sole ground that such person
owns a hearing dog as defined in section forty-seven-b of the civil
rights law, provided, however, that if after occupancy a health hazard
results on account of such dog, the public health officer having
jurisdiction may take such corrective measures as may be appropriate.
```
