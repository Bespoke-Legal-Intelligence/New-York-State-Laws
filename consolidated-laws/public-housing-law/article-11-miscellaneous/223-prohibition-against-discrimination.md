# Public Housing Law § 223 — Prohibition against discrimination

[Public Housing Law](../README.md) › [Article 11 — Miscellaneous](README.md)

```text
  § 223. Prohibition against discrimination.  For all the purposes of
this chapter, no person shall, because of race, creed, color or national
origin, be subjected to any discrimination.
```
