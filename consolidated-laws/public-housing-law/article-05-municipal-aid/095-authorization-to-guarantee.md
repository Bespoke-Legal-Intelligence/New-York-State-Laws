# Public Housing Law § 95 — Authorization to guarantee

[Public Housing Law](../README.md) › [Article 5 — Municipal Aid](README.md)

```text
  § 95. Authorization to guarantee.  A municipality is authorized to
guarantee for a period not exceeding the probable life of a project, the
principal of and interest on, or only the interest on, indebtedness
contracted by an authority operating within the territorial limits of
such municipality in connection with such project.
```
