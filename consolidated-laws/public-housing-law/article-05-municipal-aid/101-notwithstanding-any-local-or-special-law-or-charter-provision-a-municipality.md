# Public Housing Law § 101 — Notwithstanding any local or special law or charter provision, a municipality shall, irrespective of the boundaries of a project, provide...

[Public Housing Law](../README.md) › [Article 5 — Municipal Aid](README.md)

```text
  § 101. Notwithstanding any local or special law or charter provision,
a municipality shall, irrespective of the boundaries of a project,
provide for the tenants of such project, police, fire and health
protection services of the same character and to the same extent as
those provided for other residents of the municipality.
```
