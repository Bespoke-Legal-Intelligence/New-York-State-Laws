# Public Housing Law § 97 — Procedure for municipal aid

[Public Housing Law](../README.md) › [Article 5 — Municipal Aid](README.md)

```text
  § 97. Procedure for municipal aid.  Contracts for municipal loans or
subsidies to an authority or contracts for loans to a housing company
shall be approved in the same manner as is required for approval and
final authorization of capital projects or permanent improvements in
such municipality. The provisions of this section shall not apply to any
project commenced prior to the date of enactment of this chapter.
```
