# Public Housing Law § 17 — Occupancy preference

[Public Housing Law](../README.md) › [Article 2 — Division of Housing](README.md)

```text
  § 17. Occupancy preference.  The occupancy of any state project shall
be restricted to persons of low income and preference shall be given to
persons who live or shall have lived in the substandard or insanitary
area or areas which have been or will be cleared, replanned,
reconstructed or rehabilitated pursuant to a plan.
```
