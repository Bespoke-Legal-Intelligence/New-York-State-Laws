# Public Housing Law § 12 — Officers and employees

[Public Housing Law](../README.md) › [Article 2 — Division of Housing](README.md)

```text
  § 12. Officers and employees.  Subject to approval by the governor, or
pursuant to rules prescribed by him, the commissioner may appoint such
officers and employees as may be required for the performance of the
powers and duties of the division, determine their qualifications and
fix their compensation within the amount provided by appropriation.
```
