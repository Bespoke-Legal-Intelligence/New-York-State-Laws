# Public Housing Law § 122 — Acquisition by a government

[Public Housing Law](../README.md) › [Article 7 — Acquisition of Property](README.md)

```text
  § 122. Acquisition by a government.  Real property acquired for an
authority by a government subsequent to the approval of a project may be
conveyed by such government to the authority with or without
consideration and upon such terms as may be agreed upon by such
government and the authority.
```
