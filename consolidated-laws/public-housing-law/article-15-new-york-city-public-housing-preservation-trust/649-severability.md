# Public Housing Law § 649 — Severability

[Public Housing Law](../README.md) › [Article 15 — New York City Public Housing Preservation Trust](README.md)

```text
  * § 649. Severability. If any provision of this article or its
application to any person or circumstance is held unconstitutional or
invalid, in whole or in part, by any court, such holding of
unconstitutionality or invalidity shall in no way affect or impair any
other provision of this article or the application of any such provision
to any other person or circumstance, and to this end the provisions of
this article are severable.
  * NB Effective August 15, 2022
```
