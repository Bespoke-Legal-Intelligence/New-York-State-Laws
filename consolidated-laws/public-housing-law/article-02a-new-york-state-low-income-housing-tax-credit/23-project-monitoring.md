# Public Housing Law § 23 — Project monitoring

[Public Housing Law](../README.md) › [Article 2-A — New York State Low Income Housing Tax Credit Program](README.md)

```text
  * § 23. Project monitoring. The commissioner shall establish such
procedures as he deems necessary for monitoring compliance of an
eligible low-income building with the provisions of this article, and
for notifying the commissioner of taxation and finance of any such
noncompliance of which he becomes aware.
  * NB Effective until May 12, 2018
  * § 23. Project monitoring. The commissioner shall establish such
procedures deemed necessary for monitoring compliance of an eligible
low-income building with the provisions of this article, and for
notifying the commissioner of taxation and finance of any such
noncompliance.
  * NB Effective May 12, 2018
```
