# Public Housing Law § 622 — Housing choice

[Public Housing Law](../README.md) › [Article 14-A — Housing Access Voucher Pilot Program](README.md)

```text
  * § 622. Housing choice. 1. The commissioner shall administer the
housing access voucher pilot program under this article to promote
housing choice for voucher holders. The commissioner shall affirmatively
promote fair housing to the extent possible under this pilot program.
  2. Nothing in this article shall lessen or abridge any fair housing
obligations promulgated by municipalities, localities, or any other
applicable jurisdiction.
  * NB Effective until May 1, 2030
```
