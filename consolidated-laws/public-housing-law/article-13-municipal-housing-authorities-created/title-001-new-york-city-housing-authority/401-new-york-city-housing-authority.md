# Public Housing Law § 401 — New York City Housing Authority

[Public Housing Law](../../README.md) › [Article 13 — Municipal Housing Authorities, Created](../README.md) › [Title 1 — New York City Housing Authority](README.md)

```text
  § 401. New York City Housing Authority.  The New York City Housing
Authority is hereby constituted and declared to be a body corporate and
politic with all the powers, rights and duties set forth in article five
of the former state housing law.
```
