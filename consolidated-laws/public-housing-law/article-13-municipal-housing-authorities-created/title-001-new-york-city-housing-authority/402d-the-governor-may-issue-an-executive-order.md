# Public Housing Law § 402-D — The governor may issue an executive order, pursuant to article two-B of the executive law and subject to the availability of a state appr...

[Public Housing Law](../../README.md) › [Article 13 — Municipal Housing Authorities, Created](../README.md) › [Title 1 — New York City Housing Authority](README.md)

```text
  § 402-d. The governor may issue an executive order, pursuant to
article two-B of the executive law and subject to the availability of a
state appropriation, which shall govern the examination and remediation
of conditions, including the construction or reconstruction as may be
required, of residential properties owned by the authority and the
development and execution of a plan to remediate such conditions.
```
