# Public Housing Law § 416 — Rotterdam Municipal Housing Authority

[Public Housing Law](../../README.md) › [Article 13 — Municipal Housing Authorities, Created](../README.md) › [Title 13 — Rotterdam Municipal Housing Authority](README.md)

```text
  § 416. Rotterdam Municipal Housing Authority.  A municipal housing
authority, to be known as the Rotterdam Municipal Housing Authority, is
hereby created and established for the town of Rotterdam in the county
of Schenectady, for the accomplishment of any or all of the purposes
specified in article eighteen of the constitution of the state of New
York. It shall constitute a body corporate and politic, be perpetual in
duration and consist of five members. It shall have the power and duties
now or hereafter conferred by this chapter upon municipal housing
authorities. It shall be organized in the manner prescribed by and
subject to the provisions of this chapter, and the authority, its
members, officers and employees and its operations and activities shall
in all respects be governed by the provisions of this chapter.
```
