# Public Housing Law § 469 — Village of Nyack housing authority

[Public Housing Law](../../README.md) › [Article 13 — Municipal Housing Authorities, Created](../README.md) › [Title 66 — Village of Nyack Housing Authority](README.md)

```text
  § 469. Village of Nyack housing authority.  A municipal housing
authority, to be known as the village of Nyack housing authority, is
hereby created and established for the village of Nyack in the county of
Rockland, for the accomplishment of any or all of the purposes specified
in article eighteen of the constitution of the state of New York. It
shall constitute a body corporate and politic, be perpetual in duration
and consist of five members. It shall have the powers and duties now or
hereafter conferred by this chapter upon municipal housing authorities.
It shall be organized in the manner prescribed by and subject to the
provisions of this chapter, and the authority, its members, officers and
employees and its operations and activities shall in all respects be
governed by the provisions of this chapter.
```
