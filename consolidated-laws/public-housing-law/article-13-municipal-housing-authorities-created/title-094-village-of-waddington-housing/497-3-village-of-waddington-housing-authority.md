# Public Housing Law § 497*3 — Village of Waddington housing authority

[Public Housing Law](../../README.md) › [Article 13 — Municipal Housing Authorities, Created](../README.md) › [Title 94** — Village of Waddington Housing Authority](README.md)

*An asterisk in a number (for example Article 24\* or § 20-C\*2) is the Senate's mark for a number the Legislature has used more than once: the marked article or section is a different one from the unmarked one with the same number. \*2 means the second one enacted with that number.*

```text
  * § 497. Village of Waddington housing authority. A municipal housing
authority to be known as the Village of Waddington housing authority is
hereby created/and established for the Village of Waddington in the
County of St. Lawrence for the accomplishment of any or all of the
purposes specified in article eighteen of the constitution of the state
of New York. It shall constitute a body corporate and politic, be
perpetual in duration and consist of five members. It shall have the
powers and duties now or hereafter conferred by this chapter upon
municipal housing authorities. It shall be organized in the manner
prescribed by and subject to the provisions of this chapter, and the
authority, its members, officers and employees and its operations and
activities shall in all respects be governed by the provisions of this
chapter.
  * NB There are 3 § 497's
```
