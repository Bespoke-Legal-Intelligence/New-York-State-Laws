# Public Housing Law § 505*2 — City of Oneida housing authority

[Public Housing Law](../../README.md) › [Article 13 — Municipal Housing Authorities, Created](../README.md) › [Title 102* — City of Oneida Housing Authority](README.md)

*An asterisk in a number (for example Article 24\* or § 20-C\*2) is the Senate's mark for a number the Legislature has used more than once: the marked article or section is a different one from the unmarked one with the same number. \*2 means the second one enacted with that number.*

```text
  * § 505. City of Oneida housing authority. A municipal housing
authority, to be known as the city of Oneida housing authority, is
hereby created and established for the city of Oneida in the county of
Madison, for the accomplishment of any or all the purposes specified in
article eighteen of the constitution of the State of New York. It shall
constitute a body corporate and politic, be perpetual in duration and
consist of not less than three nor more than five members. It shall have
the powers and duties now or hereafter conferred by this chapter upon
municipal housing authorities. It shall be organized in the manner
prescribed by and subject to the provisions of this chapter, and the
authority, its members, officers and employees and its operations and
activities shall in all respects be governed by the provisions of this
chapter.
  * NB There are 3 § 505's
```
