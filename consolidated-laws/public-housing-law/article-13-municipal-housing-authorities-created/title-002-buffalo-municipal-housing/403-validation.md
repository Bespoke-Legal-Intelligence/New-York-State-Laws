# Public Housing Law § 403 — Validation

[Public Housing Law](../../README.md) › [Article 13 — Municipal Housing Authorities, Created](../README.md) › [Title 2 — Buffalo Municipal Housing Authority](README.md)

```text
  § 403. Validation.  The creation and establishment of the Buffalo
Municipal Housing Authority under the provisions of article five of the
former state housing law, together with all proceedings, acts and things
undertaken, performed or done with reference thereto, are hereby
validated, ratified, confirmed, approved and declared legal in all
respects, notwithstanding any want of statutory authority or any defect
or irregularity in such acts or proceedings.
```
