# Public Housing Law § 489 — Town of Wallkill housing authority

[Public Housing Law](../../README.md) › [Article 13 — Municipal Housing Authorities, Created](../README.md) › [Title 86 — Town of Wallkill Housing Authority](README.md)

```text
  § 489. Town of Wallkill housing authority.  A municipal housing
authority to be known as the town of Wallkill housing authority is
hereby created and established for the town of Wallkill in the county of
Orange, for the accomplishment of any or all of the purposes specified
in article eighteen of the constitution of the state of New York. It
shall constitute a body corporate and politic, be perpetual in duration
and consist of five members. It shall have all the powers and duties now
or hereafter conferred by this chapter upon municipal housing
authorities. It shall be organized in the manner prescribed by and
subject to the provisions of this chapter, and the authority, its
members, officers and employees and its operations and activities shall
in all respects be governed by the provisions of this chapter.
```
