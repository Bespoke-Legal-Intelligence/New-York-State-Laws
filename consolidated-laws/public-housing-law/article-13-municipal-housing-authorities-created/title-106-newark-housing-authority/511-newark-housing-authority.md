# Public Housing Law § 511 — Newark housing authority

[Public Housing Law](../../README.md) › [Article 13 — Municipal Housing Authorities, Created](../README.md) › [Title 106 — Newark Housing Authority](README.md)

```text
  § 511. Newark housing authority.  A municipal housing authority to be
known as the Newark housing authority is hereby created and established
for the village of Newark in the county of Wayne for the accomplishment
of any or all of the purposes specified in article eighteen of the
constitution of the state of New York. It shall constitute a body
corporate and politic, be perpetual in duration and consist of five
members. It shall have the powers and duties now or hereafter conferred
by this chapter upon municipal housing authorities. It shall be
organized in the manner prescribed by and subject to the provisions of
this chapter, and the authority, its members, officers and employees and
its operations and activities shall in all respects be governed by the
provisions of this chapter.
```
