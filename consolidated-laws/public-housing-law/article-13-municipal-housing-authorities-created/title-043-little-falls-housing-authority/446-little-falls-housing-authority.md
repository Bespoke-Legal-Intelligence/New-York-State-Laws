# Public Housing Law § 446 — Little Falls Housing Authority

[Public Housing Law](../../README.md) › [Article 13 — Municipal Housing Authorities, Created](../README.md) › [Title 43 — Little Falls Housing Authority](README.md)

```text
  § 446. Little Falls Housing Authority.  A municipal housing authority,
to be known as the Little Falls Housing Authority, is hereby created and
established for the city of Little Falls in the county of Herkimer, New
York, for the accomplishment of any or all of the purposes specified in
article eighteen of the constitution of the state of New York. It shall
constitute a body corporate and politic, be perpetual in duration and
consist of five members. It shall have the powers and duties now or
hereafter conferred by this chapter upon municipal housing authorities.
It shall be organized in the manner prescribed by and be subject to the
provisions of this chapter, and the authority, its members, officers and
employees and its operations and activities shall in all respects be
governed by the provisions of this chapter.
```
