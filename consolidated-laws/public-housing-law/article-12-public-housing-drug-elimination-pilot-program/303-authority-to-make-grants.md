# Public Housing Law § 303 — Authority to make grants

[Public Housing Law](../README.md) › [Article 12 — Public Housing Drug Elimination Pilot Program](README.md)

```text
  § 303. Authority to make grants. The commissioner, in accordance with
the provisions of this article, may make grants to public housing
authorities or agencies for use in eliminating drug-related crime in
public housing projects.
```
