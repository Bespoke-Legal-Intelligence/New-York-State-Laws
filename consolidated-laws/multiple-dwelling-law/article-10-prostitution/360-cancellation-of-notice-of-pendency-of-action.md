# Multiple Dwelling Law § 360 — Cancellation of notice of pendency of action

[Multiple Dwelling Law](../README.md) › [Article 10 — Prostitution](README.md)

```text
  § 360. Cancellation of notice of pendency of action.  If an action or
proceeding to establish a lien upon such premises terminates otherwise
than in a judgment establishing such a lien, or if the judgment be fully
paid, such notice of pendency of action or proceeding may be cancelled.
Prior to the termination of such action or proceeding the notice may be
cancelled by giving an undertaking.
```
