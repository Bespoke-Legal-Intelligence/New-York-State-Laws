# Multiple Dwelling Law § 351 — Lien

[Multiple Dwelling Law](../README.md) › [Article 10 — Prostitution](README.md)

```text
  § 351. Lien.  A multiple dwelling shall be subject to a penalty of one
thousand dollars if it or any part of it shall be used as a house of
prostitution or assignation with the permission of the owner, and such
penalty shall be a lien upon the dwelling and lot upon which it is
situated.
```
