# Multiple Dwelling Law § 305 — Violation of local laws and regulations

[Multiple Dwelling Law](../README.md) › [Article 8 — Requirements and Remedies](README.md)

```text
  § 305. Violation of local laws and regulations. Any owner, architect,
builder, contractor, sub-contractor, construction superintendent or
their agents who shall, in the construction or alteration of any
building or structure intended to be occupied as a multiple dwelling,
knowingly violate any of the provisions of local laws, ordinances, rules
or regulations shall be guilty of a misdemeanor.
```
