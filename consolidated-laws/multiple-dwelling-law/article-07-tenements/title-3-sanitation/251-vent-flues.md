# Multiple Dwelling Law § 251 — Vent flues

[Multiple Dwelling Law](../../README.md) › [Article 7 — Tenements](../README.md) › [Title 3 — Sanitation](README.md)

```text
  § 251. Vent flues. Supplementary water-closet compartments and
bathrooms in fireproof tenements shall be ventilated and lighted in
accordance with the applicable provisions of section seventy-six.
```
