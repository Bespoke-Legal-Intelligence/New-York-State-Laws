# Multiple Dwelling Law § 245 — Cellar entrance

[Multiple Dwelling Law](../../README.md) › [Article 7 — Tenements](../README.md) › [Title 2 — Fire Protection](README.md)

```text
  § 245. Cellar entrance. In tenements erected after April twelfth,
nineteen hundred one, there shall be an entrance to the cellar, or to
the basement or other lowest story if there be no cellar, from the
outside of the tenement.
```
