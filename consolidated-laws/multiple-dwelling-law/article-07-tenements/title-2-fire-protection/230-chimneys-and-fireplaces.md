# Multiple Dwelling Law § 230 — Chimneys and fireplaces

[Multiple Dwelling Law](../../README.md) › [Article 7 — Tenements](../README.md) › [Title 2 — Fire Protection](README.md)

```text
  § 230. Chimneys and fireplaces.  In every existing tenement which is
not heated from a central heating plant there shall be adequate flues or
chimneys through every floor with a fireplace or place for a stove
properly connected with one of such flues or chimneys for every
apartment.
```
