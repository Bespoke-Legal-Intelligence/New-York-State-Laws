# Multiple Dwelling Law § 244 — Spaces under stairs

[Multiple Dwelling Law](../../README.md) › [Article 7 — Tenements](../README.md) › [Title 2 — Fire Protection](README.md)

```text
  § 244. Spaces under stairs. In non-fireproof tenements erected after
April twelfth, nineteen hundred one, no closet of any kind shall be
constructed or maintained under any stair leading from the entrance
story to the upper stories, and such space shall be kept entirely open
and clear of any encumbrance.
```
