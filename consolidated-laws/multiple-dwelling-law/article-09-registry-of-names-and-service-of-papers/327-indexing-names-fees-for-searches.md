# Multiple Dwelling Law § 327 — Indexing names; fees for searches

[Multiple Dwelling Law](../README.md) › [Article 9 — Registry of Names and Service of Papers](README.md)

```text
  § 327. Indexing names; fees for searches. 1. The names and addresses
filed in accordance with section three hundred twenty-five shall be
indexed under the direction of the registrar of records of the
department in such a manner that all of those filed in relation to each
dwelling shall be together and readily ascertainable. The department
shall provide the necessary books and clerical assistance for that
purpose, and the expense thereof shall be paid by the city. Such indices
shall be public records.
  2. The department shall have power to charge and collect fees for
searches, and to make rules governing charges for certification of
pending violations.
```
