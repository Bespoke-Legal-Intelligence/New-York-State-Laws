# Private Housing Finance Law § 26-A — Findings for municipally-aided projects

[Private Housing Finance Law](../README.md) › [Article 2 — Limited-profit Housing Companies](README.md)

```text
  § 26-a. Findings for municipally-aided projects.  No municipally-aided
project aided solely by tax exemption shall be approved by the
supervising agency unless the agency shall have made the findings set
forth in subdivision one of section twenty-six.
```
