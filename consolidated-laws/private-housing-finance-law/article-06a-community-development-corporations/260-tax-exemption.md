# Private Housing Finance Law § 260 — Tax exemption

[Private Housing Finance Law](../README.md) › [Article 6-A — Community Development Corporations](README.md)

```text
  § 260. Tax exemption. The property of a community development
corporation and its income and operations shall be exempt from taxation.
```
