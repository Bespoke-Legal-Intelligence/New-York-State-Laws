# Cooperative Corporations Law § 83 — Status as profit-making corporation

[Cooperative Corporations Law](../README.md) › [Article 5-A — Worker Cooperative Corporations](README.md)

```text
  § 83. Status as profit-making corporation. Notwithstanding any
inconsistent provisions of this chapter, no worker cooperative shall be
classed as a non-profit or not-for-profit corporation.
```
