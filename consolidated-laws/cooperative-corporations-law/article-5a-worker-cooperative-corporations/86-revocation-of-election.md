# Cooperative Corporations Law § 86 — Revocation of election

[Cooperative Corporations Law](../README.md) › [Article 5-A — Worker Cooperative Corporations](README.md)

```text
  § 86. Revocation of election. A worker cooperative may revoke its
election under section eighty-two of this article by a vote of
two-thirds of the members and through a certificate of amendment filed
in accordance with article eight of the business corporation law.
```
