# Cooperative Corporations Law § 87 — Corporate name

[Cooperative Corporations Law](../README.md) › [Article 5-A — Worker Cooperative Corporations](README.md)

```text
  § 87. Corporate name. A worker cooperative shall include the word
"cooperative", "corporation", "incorporated" or "limited" in its
corporate name, or an abbreviation of one of such words, notwithstanding
inconsistent provisions in this chapter or in article three of the
business corporation law.
```
