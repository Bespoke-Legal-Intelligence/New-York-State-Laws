# Cooperative Corporations Law § 131 — Laws repealed

[Cooperative Corporations Law](../README.md) › [Article 8 — Repeals and Savings Clauses](README.md)

```text
  § 131. Laws repealed. Chapter two hundred and thirty-one of the laws
of nineteen hundred and twenty-six, entitled "An act relating to
cooperative corporations, constituting chapter seventy-seven of the
consolidated laws," and all acts amendatory thereof, are hereby
repealed.
```
