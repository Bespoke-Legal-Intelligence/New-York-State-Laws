# Cooperative Corporations Law § 132 — Existing actions

[Cooperative Corporations Law](../README.md) › [Article 8 — Repeals and Savings Clauses](README.md)

```text
  § 132. Existing actions. Any action or proceeding begun before this
chapter takes effect, under or pursuant to any section, article or act
repealed by this chapter, may be conducted and completed in the manner
provided thereby.
```
