# Cooperative Corporations Law § 41 — Representation of members

[Cooperative Corporations Law](../README.md) › [Article 3 — Members and Meetings](README.md)

```text
  § 41. Representation of members. If a member be other than a natural
person, such member may be represented by any individual duly authorized
in writing filed with the corporation.
```
