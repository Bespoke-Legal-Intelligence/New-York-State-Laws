# Cooperative Corporations Law § 43 — Resignation of members

[Cooperative Corporations Law](../README.md) › [Article 3 — Members and Meetings](README.md)

```text
  § 43. Resignation of members. Any member of a non-stock corporation
may, subject to fulfilling the liability, contractural or otherwise,
then incurred by him as a member of such corporation, resign and
withdraw from such corporation, in accordance with provisions therefor
in the by-laws.
```
