# Cooperative Corporations Law § 64 — Officers

[Cooperative Corporations Law](../README.md) › [Article 4 — Directors, Officers and Employees](README.md)

```text
  § 64. Officers. The officers of every corporation shall include a
president, one or more vice-presidents, secretary and treasurer who
shall be elected annually by the board of directors. The president and a
first vice-president shall be members of the corporation or of a member
corporation, and shall be elected from among the directors. Other
officers need not be directors or members of the corporation. The
offices of secretary and treasurer may be combined.
```
