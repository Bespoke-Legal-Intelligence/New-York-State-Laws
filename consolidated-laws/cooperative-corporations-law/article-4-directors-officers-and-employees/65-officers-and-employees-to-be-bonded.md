# Cooperative Corporations Law § 65 — Officers and employees to be bonded

[Cooperative Corporations Law](../README.md) › [Article 4 — Directors, Officers and Employees](README.md)

```text
  § 65. Officers and employees to be bonded. Before handling funds or
securities of the corporation, amounting to one thousand dollars or more
in any one year, the officer or employee shall be covered by an adequate
bond to be approved by the board of directors.
```
