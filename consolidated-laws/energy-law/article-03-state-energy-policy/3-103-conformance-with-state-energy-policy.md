# Energy Law § 3-103 — Conformance with state energy policy

[Energy Law](../README.md) › [Article 3 — State Energy Policy](README.md)

```text
  § 3-103. Conformance with state energy policy.  Every agency of the
state shall conduct its affairs so as to conform to the state energy
policy expressed in this chapter.
```
