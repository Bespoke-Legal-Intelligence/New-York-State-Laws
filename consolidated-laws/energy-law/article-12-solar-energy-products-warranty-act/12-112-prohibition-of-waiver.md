# Energy Law § 12-112 — Prohibition of waiver

[Energy Law](../README.md) › [Article 12 — Solar Energy Products Warranty Act](README.md)

```text
  § 12-112. Prohibition of waiver.  Any waiver by the owner of a solar
thermal system of the provisions of this article shall be deemed
contrary to public policy and shall be unenforceable and void.
```
