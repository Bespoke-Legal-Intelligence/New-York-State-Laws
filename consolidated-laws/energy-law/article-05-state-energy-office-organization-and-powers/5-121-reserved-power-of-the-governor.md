# Energy Law § 5-121 — Reserved power of the governor

[Energy Law](../README.md) › [Article 5 — State Energy Office; Organization and Powers, Functions and Duties](README.md)

```text
  § 5-121. Reserved power of the governor.  Nothing contained in this
chapter shall be construed to limit, curtail, abolish or terminate any
function or power of the governor which he had prior to the effective
date hereof.
```
