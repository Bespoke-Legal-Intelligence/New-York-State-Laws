# Energy Law § 5-113 — Coordination with environmental conservation law

[Energy Law](../README.md) › [Article 5 — State Energy Office; Organization and Powers, Functions and Duties](README.md)

```text
  § 5-113. Coordination with environmental conservation law.  The office
may review and comment in writing upon the statements of effects on the
use and conservation of energy resources made pursuant to section 8-0109
of the environmental conservation law. Such written comment shall be
made public.
```
