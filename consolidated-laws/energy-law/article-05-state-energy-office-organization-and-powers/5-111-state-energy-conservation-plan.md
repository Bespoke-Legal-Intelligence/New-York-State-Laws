# Energy Law § 5-111 — State energy conservation plan

[Energy Law](../README.md) › [Article 5 — State Energy Office; Organization and Powers, Functions and Duties](README.md)

```text
  § 5-111. State energy conservation plan.  The office shall formulate
and from time to time revise a state energy conservation plan to be
submitted pursuant to the federal Energy Policy and Conservation Act of
1975 or any acts amendatory or supplemental thereto.
```
