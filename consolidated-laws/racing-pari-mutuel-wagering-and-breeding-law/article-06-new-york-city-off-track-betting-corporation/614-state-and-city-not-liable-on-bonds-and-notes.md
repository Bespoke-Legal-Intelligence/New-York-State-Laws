# Racing, Pari-Mutuel Wagering and Breeding Law § 614 — State and city not liable on bonds and notes

[Racing, Pari-Mutuel Wagering and Breeding Law](../README.md) › [Article 6 — New York City Off-track Betting Corporation](README.md)

```text
  § 614. State and city not liable on bonds and notes. The bonds, notes
or other obligations of the corporation shall not be a debt of either
the state or the city, and neither the state nor the city shall be
liable thereon, nor shall they be payable out of any funds other than
those of the corporation.
```
