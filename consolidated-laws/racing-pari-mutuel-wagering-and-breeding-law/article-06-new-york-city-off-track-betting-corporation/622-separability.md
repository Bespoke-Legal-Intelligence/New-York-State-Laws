# Racing, Pari-Mutuel Wagering and Breeding Law § 622 — Separability

[Racing, Pari-Mutuel Wagering and Breeding Law](../README.md) › [Article 6 — New York City Off-track Betting Corporation](README.md)

```text
  § 622. Separability. If any section, provision or part of this
article, or the application thereof to any person or circumstances shall
be held invalid by any court of competent jurisdiction, the remainder
thereof or the application of such section, provision or part to any
other person or circumstances shall not be affected thereby.
```
