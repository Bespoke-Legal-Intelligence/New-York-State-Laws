# Racing, Pari-Mutuel Wagering and Breeding Law § 623 — Inconsistent provisions of other laws superseded

[Racing, Pari-Mutuel Wagering and Breeding Law](../README.md) › [Article 6 — New York City Off-track Betting Corporation](README.md)

```text
  § 623. Inconsistent provisions of other laws superseded. Insofar as
the provisions of this article are inconsistent with the provisions of
any other law, general, special or local, the provisions of this article
shall be controlling.
```
