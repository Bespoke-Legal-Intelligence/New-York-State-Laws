# Racing, Pari-Mutuel Wagering and Breeding Law § 1319 — Hearings

[Racing, Pari-Mutuel Wagering and Breeding Law](../../README.md) › [Article 13 — Destination Resort Gaming](../README.md) › [Title 2 — Facility Determination and Licensing](README.md)

```text
  § 1319. Hearings. The commission and the board shall have the
independent authority to conduct hearings concerning the conduct of
gaming and applicants for gaming facility licenses in accordance with
any procedures set forth in this article and any applicable implementing
regulations.
```
