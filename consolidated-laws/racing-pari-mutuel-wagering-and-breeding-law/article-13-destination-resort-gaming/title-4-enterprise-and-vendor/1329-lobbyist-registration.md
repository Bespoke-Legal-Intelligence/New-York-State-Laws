# Racing, Pari-Mutuel Wagering and Breeding Law § 1329 — Lobbyist registration

[Racing, Pari-Mutuel Wagering and Breeding Law](../../README.md) › [Article 13 — Destination Resort Gaming](../README.md) › [Title 4 — Enterprise and Vendor Licensing and Registration](README.md)

```text
  § 1329. Lobbyist registration. 1. For purposes of this section, the
terms "lobbyist", "lobbying", "lobbying activities" and "client" shall
have the same meaning as those terms are defined by section one-c of the
legislative law.
  2. In addition to any other registration and reporting required by
law, each lobbyist seeking to engage in lobbying activity on behalf of a
client or a client's interest before the commission shall first register
with the secretary of the commission. The secretary shall cause a
registration to be available on the commission's website within five
days of submission.
```
