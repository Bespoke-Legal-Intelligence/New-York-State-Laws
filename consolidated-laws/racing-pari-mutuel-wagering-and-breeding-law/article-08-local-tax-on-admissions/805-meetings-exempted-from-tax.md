# Racing, Pari-Mutuel Wagering and Breeding Law § 805 — Meetings exempted from tax

[Racing, Pari-Mutuel Wagering and Breeding Law](../README.md) › [Article 8 — Local Tax On Admissions](README.md)

```text
  § 805. Meetings exempted from tax. The provisions of this article
shall not apply to a harness horse race meeting conducted by any state,
county or other agricultural association except as provided in paragraph
d of subdivision one of section eight hundred one of this chapter.
```
