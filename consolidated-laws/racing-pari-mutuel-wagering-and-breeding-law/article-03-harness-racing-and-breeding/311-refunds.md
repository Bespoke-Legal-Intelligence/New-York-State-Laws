# Racing, Pari-Mutuel Wagering and Breeding Law § 311 — Refunds

[Racing, Pari-Mutuel Wagering and Breeding Law](../README.md) › [Article 3 — Harness Racing and Breeding](README.md)

```text
  § 311. Refunds.  Moneys received by the board pursuant to this article
may within one year from the receipt thereof be refunded to the party
for whose account the same were received on proof satisfactory to the
board that:
  1. Such moneys were in excess of the amount required by law.
  2. The license for which application was made has been refused by the
board.
  3. Such moneys were received as a fine and the board has after review
reduced the amount of such fine.
  4. Upon appeal, the court reduced or remitted the fine imposed.
  Such refunds shall, upon approval by the board and after audit by the
comptroller, be paid from any moneys in the custody of the department
received pursuant to this article.
```
