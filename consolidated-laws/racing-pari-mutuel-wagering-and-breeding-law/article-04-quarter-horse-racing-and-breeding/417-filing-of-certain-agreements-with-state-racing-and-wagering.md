# Racing, Pari-Mutuel Wagering and Breeding Law § 417 — Filing of certain agreements with state racing and wagering board

[Racing, Pari-Mutuel Wagering and Breeding Law](../README.md) › [Article 4 — Quarter Horse Racing and Breeding](README.md)

```text
  § 417. Filing of certain agreements with state racing and wagering
board. Every association or corporation licensed to conduct quarter
horse race meetings at which pari-mutuel betting is permitted shall
promptly after entering into any lease, agreement concerning any
concession, labor management relations, the hiring of designated classes
of officers, employees or contractors specified by the board or any such
other contract, agreement or arrangement as the board may from time to
time prescribe file with the board a true and correct copy, or an
accurate summary, if oral, thereof.
```
