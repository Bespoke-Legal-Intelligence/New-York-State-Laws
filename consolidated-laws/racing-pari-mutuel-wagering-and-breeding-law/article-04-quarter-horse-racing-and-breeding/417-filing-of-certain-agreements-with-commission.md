# Racing, Pari-Mutuel Wagering and Breeding Law § 417 — Filing of certain agreements with commission

[Racing, Pari-Mutuel Wagering and Breeding Law](../README.md) › [Article 4 — Quarter Horse Racing and Breeding](README.md)

```text
  § 417. Filing of certain agreements with commission. Every association
or corporation licensed to conduct quarter horse race meetings at which
pari-mutuel betting is permitted shall promptly after entering into any
lease, agreement concerning any concession, labor management relations,
the hiring of designated classes of officers, employees or contractors
specified by the commission or any such other contract, agreement or
arrangement as the commission may from time to time prescribe file with
the commission a true and correct copy, or an accurate summary, if oral,
thereof.
```
