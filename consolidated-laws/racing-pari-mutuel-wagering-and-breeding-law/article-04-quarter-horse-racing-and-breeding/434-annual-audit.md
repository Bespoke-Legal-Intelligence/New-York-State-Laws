# Racing, Pari-Mutuel Wagering and Breeding Law § 434 — Annual audit

[Racing, Pari-Mutuel Wagering and Breeding Law](../README.md) › [Article 4 — Quarter Horse Racing and Breeding](README.md)

```text
  § 434. Annual audit. The comptroller or his legally authorized
representative is authorized to and shall from time to time but at least
annually examine the accounts and books of the fund, including its
receipts and disbursements.
```
