# Racing, Pari-Mutuel Wagering and Breeding Law § 414 — Construction

[Racing, Pari-Mutuel Wagering and Breeding Law](../README.md) › [Article 4 — Quarter Horse Racing and Breeding](README.md)

```text
  § 414. Construction. Nothing in sections two hundred twenty-two
through seven hundred five of this chapter shall be construed to
preclude the American Quarter Horse Association from independently
licensing or disciplining any of its members or performing any function
heretofore performed by that organization relative to its membership in
this state.
```
