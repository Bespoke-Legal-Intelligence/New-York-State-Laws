# Racing, Pari-Mutuel Wagering and Breeding Law § 435 — Annual report

[Racing, Pari-Mutuel Wagering and Breeding Law](../README.md) › [Article 4 — Quarter Horse Racing and Breeding](README.md)

```text
  § 435. Annual report. The fund shall annually make during the month of
January a report to the governor setting forth in detail:
  1. Its operations and accomplishments during the preceding year; and
  2. Its receipts and disbursements during the preceding year.
```
