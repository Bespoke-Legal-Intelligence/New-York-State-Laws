# Racing, Pari-Mutuel Wagering and Breeding Law § 1109 — Administration and management

[Racing, Pari-Mutuel Wagering and Breeding Law](../README.md) › [Article 11 — Interstate Compact On Licensure of Participants In Live Horse Racing With Pari-mutuel Wagering](README.md)

```text
  § 1109. Administration and management. The compact committee shall:
  a. annually elect, from its members, a chair, vice-chair and
secretary/treasurer;
  b. adopt (and amend or rescind) by-laws for the conduct of its
business, publish them in a convenient form, and file a copy of them
(including any amendments) with the secretary of state, or its
equivalent, of each party state; and
  c. delegate day-to-day management and administration of its duties, as
needed, to an executive director and support staff, who shall all be
considered governmental employees.
```
