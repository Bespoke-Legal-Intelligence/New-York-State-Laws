# Racing, Pari-Mutuel Wagering and Breeding Law § 1103 — Entry into force, eligible parties, and withdrawal

[Racing, Pari-Mutuel Wagering and Breeding Law](../README.md) › [Article 11 — Interstate Compact On Licensure of Participants In Live Horse Racing With Pari-mutuel Wagering](README.md)

```text
  § 1103. Entry into force, eligible parties, and withdrawal. This
compact shall come into force when enacted by any four states.
Thereafter, this compact shall become effective, as to any other party
state that enacts this compact, upon the affirmative vote of a majority
of the officials on the compact committee.
```
