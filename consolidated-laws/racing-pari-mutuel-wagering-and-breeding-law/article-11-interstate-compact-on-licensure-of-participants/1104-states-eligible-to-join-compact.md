# Racing, Pari-Mutuel Wagering and Breeding Law § 1104 — States eligible to join compact

[Racing, Pari-Mutuel Wagering and Breeding Law](../README.md) › [Article 11 — Interstate Compact On Licensure of Participants In Live Horse Racing With Pari-mutuel Wagering](README.md)

```text
  § 1104. States eligible to join compact. Any state that has adopted or
authorized horse racing with pari-mutuel wagering shall be eligible to
become a party to this compact.
```
