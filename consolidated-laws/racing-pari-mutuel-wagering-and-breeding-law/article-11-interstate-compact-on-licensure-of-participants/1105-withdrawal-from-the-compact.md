# Racing, Pari-Mutuel Wagering and Breeding Law § 1105 — Withdrawal from the compact

[Racing, Pari-Mutuel Wagering and Breeding Law](../README.md) › [Article 11 — Interstate Compact On Licensure of Participants In Live Horse Racing With Pari-mutuel Wagering](README.md)

```text
  § 1105. Withdrawal from the compact. Any party state may withdraw from
this compact by enacting a statute repealing this compact, but no such
withdrawal shall become effective until the head of the executive branch
of the withdrawing state has given notice in writing of such withdrawal
to the head of the executive branch of all other party states. If
withdrawals reduce participation in this compact to less than three
party states, this compact no longer shall be in effect until there are
three or more party states again participating in this compact.
```
