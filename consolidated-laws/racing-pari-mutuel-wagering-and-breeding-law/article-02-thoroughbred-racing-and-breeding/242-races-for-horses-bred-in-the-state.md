# Racing, Pari-Mutuel Wagering and Breeding Law § 242 — Races for horses bred in the state

[Racing, Pari-Mutuel Wagering and Breeding Law](../README.md) › [Article 2 — Thoroughbred Racing and Breeding](README.md)

```text
  § 242. Races for horses bred in the state. The commission in granting
a license to a corporation to conduct running or steeplechase races at
its racetrack, if the commission deems such requirement practicable, may
require such corporation to provide for at least one race during the
racing season in which the entries shall be exclusively horses foaled in
this state.
```
