# Racing, Pari-Mutuel Wagering and Breeding Law § 220-A — Corporations continued

[Racing, Pari-Mutuel Wagering and Breeding Law](../README.md) › [Article 2 — Thoroughbred Racing and Breeding](README.md)

```text
  § 220-a. Corporations continued. Corporations heretofore formed under
chapter five hundred seventy of the laws of eighteen hundred
ninety-five, chapter four hundred forty of the laws of nineteen hundred
twenty-six, or under article twenty of the membership corporations law,
are hereby continued without change of corporate status, power or
obligation, under the provisions of this article.
```
