# Racing, Pari-Mutuel Wagering and Breeding Law § 202-B — No debt of the state

[Racing, Pari-Mutuel Wagering and Breeding Law](../README.md) › [Article 2 — Thoroughbred Racing and Breeding](README.md)

```text
  § 202-b. No debt of the state. Nothing in this article shall be
construed to accept a liability to or create a debt of the state within
the meaning of any constitutional or statutory provision.
```
