# Racing, Pari-Mutuel Wagering and Breeding Law § 229 — Backstretch employees drug and alcohol rehabilitation eligibility

[Racing, Pari-Mutuel Wagering and Breeding Law](../README.md) › [Article 2 — Thoroughbred Racing and Breeding](README.md)

```text
  § 229. Backstretch employees drug and alcohol rehabilitation
eligibility. Any licensed not-for-profit organization providing drug and
alcohol rehabilitation services to backstretch employees shall receive
information concerning available funding and existing programs from a
coordinated effort of the commission and division of substance abuse
services.
```
