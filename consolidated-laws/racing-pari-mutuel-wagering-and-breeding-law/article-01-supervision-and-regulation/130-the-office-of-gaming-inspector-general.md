# Racing, Pari-Mutuel Wagering and Breeding Law § 130 — The office of gaming inspector general

[Racing, Pari-Mutuel Wagering and Breeding Law](../README.md) › [Article 1 — Supervision and Regulation](README.md)

```text
  § 130. The office of gaming inspector general. The duties and
responsibilities of the former office of the gaming inspector general
are transferred to and encompassed by the office of the state inspector
general as expressly referenced in article four-A of the executive law.
```
