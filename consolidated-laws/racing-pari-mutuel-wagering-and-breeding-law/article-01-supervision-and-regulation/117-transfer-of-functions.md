# Racing, Pari-Mutuel Wagering and Breeding Law § 117 — Transfer of functions

[Racing, Pari-Mutuel Wagering and Breeding Law](../README.md) › [Article 1 — Supervision and Regulation](README.md)

```text
  § 117. Transfer of functions. All of the functions and powers
possessed by and the obligations and duties of the former racing and
wagering board and its predecessors and the division of the lottery and
its predecessors are hereby transferred to the commission.
```
