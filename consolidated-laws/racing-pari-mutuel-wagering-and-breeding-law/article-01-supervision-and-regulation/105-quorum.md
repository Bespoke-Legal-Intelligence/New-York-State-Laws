# Racing, Pari-Mutuel Wagering and Breeding Law § 105 — Quorum

[Racing, Pari-Mutuel Wagering and Breeding Law](../README.md) › [Article 1 — Supervision and Regulation](README.md)

```text
  § 105. Quorum. A majority of the duly appointed members of the
commission shall constitute a quorum and not less than a majority of
such quorum may transact any business, perform any duty or exercise any
power of the commission.
```
