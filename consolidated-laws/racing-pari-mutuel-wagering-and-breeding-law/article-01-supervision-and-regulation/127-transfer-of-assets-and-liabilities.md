# Racing, Pari-Mutuel Wagering and Breeding Law § 127 — Transfer of assets and liabilities

[Racing, Pari-Mutuel Wagering and Breeding Law](../README.md) › [Article 1 — Supervision and Regulation](README.md)

```text
  § 127. Transfer of assets and liabilities. All assets and liabilities
of the former division of the lottery and former racing and wagering
board are hereby transferred to and assumed by the commission.
```
