# Racing, Pari-Mutuel Wagering and Breeding Law § 106 — Salary and expenses

[Racing, Pari-Mutuel Wagering and Breeding Law](../README.md) › [Article 1 — Supervision and Regulation](README.md)

```text
  § 106. Salary and expenses. 1. The commission shall fix the
compensation for its officers and employees within the amounts
appropriated therefor.
  2. The members, officers and employees of the commission shall be
reimbursed for all actual and necessary traveling and other expenses and
disbursements incurred or made by them in the discharge of their
official duties.
```
