# Racing, Pari-Mutuel Wagering and Breeding Law § 128 — Promulgation of rules and regulations

[Racing, Pari-Mutuel Wagering and Breeding Law](../README.md) › [Article 1 — Supervision and Regulation](README.md)

```text
  § 128. Promulgation of rules and regulations. Notwithstanding any
inconsistent provision of the state administrative procedure act, the
commission shall be authorized to promulgate regulations on an emergency
basis to ensure the implementation of this article.
```
