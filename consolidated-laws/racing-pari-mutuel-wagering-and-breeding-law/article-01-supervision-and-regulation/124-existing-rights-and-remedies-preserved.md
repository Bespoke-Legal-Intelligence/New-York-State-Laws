# Racing, Pari-Mutuel Wagering and Breeding Law § 124 — Existing rights and remedies preserved

[Racing, Pari-Mutuel Wagering and Breeding Law](../README.md) › [Article 1 — Supervision and Regulation](README.md)

```text
  § 124. Existing rights and remedies preserved. No existing right or
remedy of any character shall be lost, impaired or affected by reason of
the transfer or assignment of functions, powers, obligations and duties
from the former division of the lottery and former racing and wagering
board to the commission.
```
