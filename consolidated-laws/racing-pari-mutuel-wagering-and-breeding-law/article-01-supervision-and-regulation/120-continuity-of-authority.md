# Racing, Pari-Mutuel Wagering and Breeding Law § 120 — Continuity of authority

[Racing, Pari-Mutuel Wagering and Breeding Law](../README.md) › [Article 1 — Supervision and Regulation](README.md)

```text
  § 120. Continuity of authority. For the purpose of succession to all
functions, powers, duties and obligations of the former division of the
lottery and former racing and wagering board transferred to and assumed
by the commission, such commission shall be deemed to and held to
constitute the continuation of such functions, powers, duties and
obligations, and not a different agency or authority.
```
