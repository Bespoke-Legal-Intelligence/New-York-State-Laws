# Racing, Pari-Mutuel Wagering and Breeding Law § 123 — Terms occurring in laws, contracts and other documents

[Racing, Pari-Mutuel Wagering and Breeding Law](../README.md) › [Article 1 — Supervision and Regulation](README.md)

```text
  § 123. Terms occurring in laws, contracts and other documents. Unless
the context shall otherwise require, whenever the "racing and wagering
board" or "board", "state racing commission", "state harness racing
commission", "state quarter horse racing commission", or "division of
the lottery" are referred to or designated in any law, contract or
document pertaining to the functions, powers, obligations and duties
transferred and assigned to the commission, such reference or
designation shall be deemed to refer to the "state gaming commission".
```
