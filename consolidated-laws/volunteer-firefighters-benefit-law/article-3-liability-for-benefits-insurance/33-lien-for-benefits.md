# Volunteer Firefighters' Benefit Law § 33 — Lien for benefits

[Volunteer Firefighters' Benefit Law](../README.md) › [Article 3 — Liability For Benefits; Insurance](README.md)

```text
  § 33. Lien for benefits. Benefits to be paid and provided under this
chapter shall be a lien against the assets of the stock corporation,
mutual corporation or reciprocal insurer liable therefor, without limit
of amount, subordinate, however, to claims for unpaid wages and prior
recorded liens.
```
