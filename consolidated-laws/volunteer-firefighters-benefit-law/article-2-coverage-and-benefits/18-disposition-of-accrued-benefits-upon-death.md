# Volunteer Firefighters' Benefit Law § 18 — Disposition of accrued benefits upon death

[Volunteer Firefighters' Benefit Law](../README.md) › [Article 2 — Coverage and Benefits](README.md)

```text
  § 18. Disposition of accrued benefits upon death. Except as otherwise
provided in section ten of this chapter, in the case of the death of an
injured volunteer fireman to whom there was due at the time of his death
any benefits under the provisions of this chapter, the amount of such
benefits shall be payable to the surviving spouse, if there be one, or,
if none, to the surviving child or children of the deceased under the
age of eighteen years, and if there be no surviving spouse or children,
then to the dependents of such deceased or to any of them as the
workmen's compensation board may direct, and if there be no surviving
spouse, children or dependents of such deceased, then to his estate. An
award for disability may be made after the death of an injured volunteer
fireman.
```
