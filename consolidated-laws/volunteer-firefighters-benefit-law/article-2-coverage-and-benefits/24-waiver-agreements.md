# Volunteer Firefighters' Benefit Law § 24 — Waiver agreements

[Volunteer Firefighters' Benefit Law](../README.md) › [Article 2 — Coverage and Benefits](README.md)

```text
  § 24. Waiver agreements. No agreement or release by a volunteer
firefighter, or, in the case of death, the dependents of a deceased
volunteer firefighter, to waive his or her right to benefits under this
chapter shall be valid, except as provided in section thirty-two of the
workers' compensation law or otherwise in this chapter.
```
