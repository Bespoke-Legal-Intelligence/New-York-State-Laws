# Volunteer Firefighters' Benefit Law § 25 — Limitation of time

[Volunteer Firefighters' Benefit Law](../README.md) › [Article 2 — Coverage and Benefits](README.md)

```text
  § 25. Limitation of time. No limitation of time provided in this
chapter shall run as against any person who is mentally incompetent or a
minor so long as he has no committee or guardian.
```
