# Volunteer Firefighters' Benefit Law § 43 — Determination of claims for benefits

[Volunteer Firefighters' Benefit Law](../README.md) › [Article 4 — Procedure](README.md)

```text
  § 43. Determination of claims for benefits. The provisions of section
twenty of the workmen's compensation law shall be applicable as fully as
if set forth in this chapter, except that the waiting period of seven
days for the presentation of claims for benefits shall not apply to the
presentation of claims for benefits under this chapter.
```
