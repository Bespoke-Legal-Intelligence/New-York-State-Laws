# Volunteer Firefighters' Benefit Law § 46 — Appeals

[Volunteer Firefighters' Benefit Law](../README.md) › [Article 4 — Procedure](README.md)

```text
  § 46. Appeals. The provisions of section twenty-three of the workmen's
compensation law shall be applicable as fully as if set forth in this
chapter except that reimbursement following modification or rescission
upon appeal shall be paid from administration expenses as provided by
section sixty of this chapter.
```
