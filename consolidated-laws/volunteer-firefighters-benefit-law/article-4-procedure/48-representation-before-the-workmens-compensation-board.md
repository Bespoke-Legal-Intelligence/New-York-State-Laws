# Volunteer Firefighters' Benefit Law § 48 — Representation before the workmen's compensation board

[Volunteer Firefighters' Benefit Law](../README.md) › [Article 4 — Procedure](README.md)

```text
  § 48. Representation before the workmen's compensation board.  The
provisions of section twenty-four-a of the workmen's compensation law
which are not inconsistent with the provisions of this chapter shall be
applicable as fully as if set forth in this chapter.
```
