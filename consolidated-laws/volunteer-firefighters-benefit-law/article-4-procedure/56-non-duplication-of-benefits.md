# Volunteer Firefighters' Benefit Law § 56 — Non-duplication of benefits

[Volunteer Firefighters' Benefit Law](../README.md) › [Article 4 — Procedure](README.md)

```text
  § 56. Non-duplication of benefits. If benefits are required to be paid
under this chapter in the event of injury to or death of a volunteer
fireman, the volunteer fireman or other persons entitled to such
benefits shall not receive workmen's compensation under the provisions
of the workmen's compensation law in relation to such injury or death.
```
