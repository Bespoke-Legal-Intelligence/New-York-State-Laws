# Volunteer Firefighters' Benefit Law § 1 — Short title

[Volunteer Firefighters' Benefit Law](../README.md) › [Article 1 — Short Title; Purpose; Definitions; Miscellaneous](README.md)

```text
  § 1. Short title. a. This chapter shall be known and may be cited as
"volunteer firefighters' benefit law".
  b. Reference in this chapter or in any other law to the volunteer
firemen's benefit law shall be deemed to mean and refer to the volunteer
firefighters' benefit law. The terms "volunteer fireman" or "volunteer
firemen" appearing in this chapter shall be deemed to mean "volunteer
firefighter" or "volunteer firefighters," respectively. The terms "he,"
"his," or "him," appearing in this chapter shall be deemed to mean "he
or she," "his or her," and "him or her."
```
