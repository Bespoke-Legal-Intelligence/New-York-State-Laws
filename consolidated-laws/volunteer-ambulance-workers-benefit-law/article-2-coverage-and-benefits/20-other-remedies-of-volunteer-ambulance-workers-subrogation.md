# Volunteer Ambulance Workers' Benefit Law § 20 — Other remedies of volunteer ambulance workers; subrogation

[Volunteer Ambulance Workers' Benefit Law](../README.md) › [Article 2 — Coverage and Benefits](README.md)

```text
  § 20. Other remedies of volunteer ambulance workers; subrogation.  The
provisions of section twenty-nine of the workers' compensation law to
the extent that such provisions are not inconsistent with the provisions
of this chapter, shall be applicable as fully as if set forth in this
chapter.
```
