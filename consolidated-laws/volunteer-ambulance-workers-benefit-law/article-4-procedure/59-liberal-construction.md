# Volunteer Ambulance Workers' Benefit Law § 59 — Liberal construction

[Volunteer Ambulance Workers' Benefit Law](../README.md) › [Article 4 — Procedure](README.md)

```text
  § 59. Liberal construction. The provisions of this article relating to
giving notice of injury and filing of claim, and to the contents of any
such notice or claim, shall be construed liberally in order to
effectuate the objects and purposes of this chapter.
```
