# Volunteer Ambulance Workers' Benefit Law § 54 — Aggregate trust fund

[Volunteer Ambulance Workers' Benefit Law](../README.md) › [Article 4 — Procedure](README.md)

```text
  § 54. Aggregate trust fund. The provisions of section twenty-seven of
the workers' compensation law which are not inconsistent with the
provisions of this section, shall be applicable as fully as if set forth
in this chapter with respect to claims for benefits under this chapter.
```
