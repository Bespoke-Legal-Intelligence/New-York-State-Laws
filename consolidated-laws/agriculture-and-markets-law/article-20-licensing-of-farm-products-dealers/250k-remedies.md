# Agriculture and Markets Law § 250-K — Remedies

[Agriculture and Markets Law](../README.md) › [Article 20 — Licensing of Farm Products Dealers](README.md)

```text
  § 250-k. Remedies. The commissioner may institute such action at law
or in equity as may be necessary to enforce compliance with any
provisions of this article or of any rule or regulation promulgated
hereunder. In addition to any other remedy prescribed in article three
of this chapter, or otherwise, if necessary to protect the producers or
dealers of this state, the commissioner may apply for relief by
injunction without alleging or proving that an adequate remedy at law
does not exist. Such application may be made to the supreme court in any
district or county, as provided by the civil practice law and rules, or
to the supreme court in the third department.
```
