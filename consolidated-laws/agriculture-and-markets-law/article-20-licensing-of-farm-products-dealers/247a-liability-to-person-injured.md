# Agriculture and Markets Law § 247-A — Liability to person injured

[Agriculture and Markets Law](../README.md) › [Article 20 — Licensing of Farm Products Dealers](README.md)

```text
  § 247-a. Liability to person injured. Any dealer, who violates any
provisions of section two hundred forty-six of this article, shall be
liable to the person injured thereby for the full amount of damages
resulting from such violation.
```
