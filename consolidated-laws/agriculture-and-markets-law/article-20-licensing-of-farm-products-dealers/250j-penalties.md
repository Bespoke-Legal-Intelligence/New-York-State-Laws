# Agriculture and Markets Law § 250-J — Penalties

[Agriculture and Markets Law](../README.md) › [Article 20 — Licensing of Farm Products Dealers](README.md)

```text
  § 250-j. Penalties. Each transaction by a dealer may be considered a
separate violation subjecting the dealer to liability pursuant to
section thirty-nine of this chapter, in addition to remedies provided in
this article.
```
