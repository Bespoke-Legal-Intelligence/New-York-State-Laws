# Agriculture and Markets Law § 96-Z-4 — Review

[Agriculture and Markets Law](../README.md) › [Article 5-C — Licensing of Rendering Plants](README.md)

```text
  § 96-z-4. Review.  The action of the commissioner in refusing to grant
a license, or in suspending or revoking a license may be reviewed in the
manner provided by article seventy-eight of the civil practice law and
rules, and the decision of the commissioner shall be final unless within
thirty days from this date of service thereof on the applicant or
licensee a proceeding is instituted to review such action.
```
