# Agriculture and Markets Law § 96-Z-10 — Criminal sale of inedible meat

[Agriculture and Markets Law](../README.md) › [Article 5-C — Licensing of Rendering Plants](README.md)

```text
  § 96-z-10. Criminal sale of inedible meat.  A person is guilty of
criminal sale of inedible meat when he knowingly and unlawfully sells
such meat for the purpose of human consumption. Criminal sale of
inedible meat is a class E felony.
```
