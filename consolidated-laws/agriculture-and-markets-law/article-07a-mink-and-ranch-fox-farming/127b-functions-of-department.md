# Agriculture and Markets Law § 127-B — Functions of department

[Agriculture and Markets Law](../README.md) › [Article 7-A — Mink and Ranch Fox Farming](README.md)

```text
  § 127-b. Functions of department.  All the functions of the
conservation department which affect the breeding, raising, producing,
marketing, or any other phase of the production or distribution, of
domestically raised mink and ranch fox, or products thereof, are hereby
transferred to and vested in the department of agriculture and markets.
```
