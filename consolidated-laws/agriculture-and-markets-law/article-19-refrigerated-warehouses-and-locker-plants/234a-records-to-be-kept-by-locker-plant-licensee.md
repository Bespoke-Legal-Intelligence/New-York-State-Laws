# Agriculture and Markets Law § 234-A — Records to be kept by locker plant licensee

[Agriculture and Markets Law](../README.md) › [Article 19 — Refrigerated Warehouses and Locker Plants](README.md)

```text
  § 234-a. Records to be kept by locker plant licensee. The locker plant
licensee shall assign a designating number to each locker and keep an
accurate record of the name and address of lessee of each locker, by its
designated number.
```
