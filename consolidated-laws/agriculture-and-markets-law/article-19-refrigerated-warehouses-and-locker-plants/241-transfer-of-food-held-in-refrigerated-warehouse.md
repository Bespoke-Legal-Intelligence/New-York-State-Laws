# Agriculture and Markets Law § 241 — Transfer of food held in refrigerated warehouse

[Agriculture and Markets Law](../README.md) › [Article 19 — Refrigerated Warehouses and Locker Plants](README.md)

```text
  § 241. Transfer of food held in refrigerated warehouse. The transfer
of any food from one refrigerated warehouse or locker plant to another
for the purpose of evading any provisions of this article is hereby
prohibited.
```
