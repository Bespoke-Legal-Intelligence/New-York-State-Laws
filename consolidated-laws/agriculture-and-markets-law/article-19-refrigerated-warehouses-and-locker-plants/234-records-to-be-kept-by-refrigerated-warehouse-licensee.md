# Agriculture and Markets Law § 234 — Records to be kept by refrigerated warehouse licensee

[Agriculture and Markets Law](../README.md) › [Article 19 — Refrigerated Warehouses and Locker Plants](README.md)

```text
  § 234. Records to be kept by refrigerated warehouse licensee.  The
refrigerated warehouse licensee shall assign to each lot of food, when
received for storage in a refrigerated warehouse, a distinguishing lot
number for the purpose of identification, and shall keep an accurate
record of such lot number, and shall also make and keep a record of the
date of the receipt and of the date of removal of each lot of food.
```
