# Agriculture and Markets Law § 139-A — Tolerances

[Agriculture and Markets Law](../README.md) › [Article 9 — Inspection and Sale of Seeds](README.md)

```text
  § 139-a. Tolerances.  On account of the unavoidable variations which
occur between two analyses or tests and likewise between label
statements and the results of subsequent analyses and tests, recognized
tolerances shall be employed in the enforcement of the provisions of
this article.
```
