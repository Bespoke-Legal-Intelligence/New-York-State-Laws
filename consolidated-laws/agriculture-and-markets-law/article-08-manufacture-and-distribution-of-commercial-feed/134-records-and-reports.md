# Agriculture and Markets Law § 134 — Records and reports

[Agriculture and Markets Law](../README.md) › [Article 8 — Manufacture and Distribution of Commercial Feed](README.md)

```text
  § 134. Records and reports. Every distributor, manufacturer and
transporter of a commercial feed in this state shall maintain such
records and submit such reports to the commissioner as the commissioner
may require pertaining to the origin, manufacture, movement, storage,
distribution or other disposition of commercial feed. Such records shall
be open to inspection by the commissioner or his authorized agent at any
reasonable time.
```
