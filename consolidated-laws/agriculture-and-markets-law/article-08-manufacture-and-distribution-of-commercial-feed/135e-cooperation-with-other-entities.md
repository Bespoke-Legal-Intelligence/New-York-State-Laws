# Agriculture and Markets Law § 135-E — Cooperation with other entities

[Agriculture and Markets Law](../README.md) › [Article 8 — Manufacture and Distribution of Commercial Feed](README.md)

```text
  § 135-e. Cooperation with other entities. The commissioner may
cooperate with and enter into agreements with governmental agencies of
this state, or other states, agencies of the federal government, and
private associations in order to carry out the purpose and provisions of
this article.
```
