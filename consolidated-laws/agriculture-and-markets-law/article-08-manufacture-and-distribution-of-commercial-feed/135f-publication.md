# Agriculture and Markets Law § 135-F — Publication

[Agriculture and Markets Law](../README.md) › [Article 8 — Manufacture and Distribution of Commercial Feed](README.md)

```text
  § 135-f. Publication. The commissioner shall publish at least
annually, in such forms as he may deem proper, a report of the results
of the analyses of official samples of commercial feed distributed
within the state and any violations established as a result thereof.
```
