# Agriculture and Markets Law § 514 — Records

[Agriculture and Markets Law](../README.md) › [Article 29 — Growth of Hemp](README.md)

```text
  § 514. Records. Every licensee shall keep, in such form as the
commissioner may direct, such records as may be required pursuant to
rules and regulations promulgated pursuant to this article.
```
