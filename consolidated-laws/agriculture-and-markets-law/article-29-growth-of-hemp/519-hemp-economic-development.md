# Agriculture and Markets Law § 519 — Hemp economic development

[Agriculture and Markets Law](../README.md) › [Article 29 — Growth of Hemp](README.md)

```text
  § 519. Hemp economic development. To the extent the commissioner
believes it to be necessary, the commissioner shall consult and
cooperate with the New York state urban development corporation with
respect to the funding and support of research concerning hemp and
businesses involved in growing, cultivating, and processing hemp for
food, fiber, cannabinoid content, construction materials and other uses.
```
