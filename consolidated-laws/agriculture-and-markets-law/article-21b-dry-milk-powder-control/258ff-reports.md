# Agriculture and Markets Law § 258-FF — Reports

[Agriculture and Markets Law](../README.md) › [Article 21-B — Dry Milk Powder Control](README.md)

```text
  § 258-ff. Reports. Each person who sells or distributes, other than
exclusively at retail, dry milk powder originating from any area outside
the continental United States including Alaska and Hawaii shall, from
time to time, as required by rule or order of the commissioner, make and
file a verified report on forms prescribed by the commissioner of all
matters on account of which a record is required to be kept, together
with such other information or facts as the commissioner may deem
pertinent and material within the scope of the purpose and intent of
this article.
```
