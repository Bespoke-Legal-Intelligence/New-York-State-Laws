# Agriculture and Markets Law § 258-BB — Definitions

[Agriculture and Markets Law](../README.md) › [Article 21-B — Dry Milk Powder Control](README.md)

```text
  § 258-bb. Definitions. As used in this article unless otherwise
expressly stated, or unless the context or subject matter otherwise
requires:
  1. "Commissioner" means the commissioner of agriculture and markets.
  2. "Person" means any person, firm, corporation, co-partnership,
association, co-operative corporation or unincorporated co-operative
association.
```
