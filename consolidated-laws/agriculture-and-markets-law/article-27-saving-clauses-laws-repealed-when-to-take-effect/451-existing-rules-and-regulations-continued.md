# Agriculture and Markets Law § 451 — Existing rules and regulations continued

[Agriculture and Markets Law](../README.md) › [Article 27 — Saving Clauses; Laws Repealed; When to Take Effect](README.md)

```text
  § 451. Existing rules and regulations continued.  The rules and
regulations now in force and effect, pertaining to any matter within the
jurisdiction of the department, adopted in pursuance of any law, the
duty of enforcing which is imposed upon the department as provided
herein, shall continue in full force and effect until they are modified,
amended or repealed as provided by this chapter.
```
