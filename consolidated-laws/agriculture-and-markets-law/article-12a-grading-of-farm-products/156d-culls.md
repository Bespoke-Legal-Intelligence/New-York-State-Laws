# Agriculture and Markets Law § 156-D — Culls

[Agriculture and Markets Law](../README.md) › [Article 12-A — Grading of Farm Products](README.md)

```text
  § 156-d. Culls.  The commissioner is hereby authorized and empowered,
after investigation and public hearing, to establish and promulgate
definitions of "culls" as applied to farm products, or any of them; and
to change such definitions from time to time.
```
