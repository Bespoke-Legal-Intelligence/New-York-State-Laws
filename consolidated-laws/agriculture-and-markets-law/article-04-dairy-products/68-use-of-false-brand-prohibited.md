# Agriculture and Markets Law § 68 — Use of false brand prohibited

[Agriculture and Markets Law](../README.md) › [Article 4 — Dairy Products](README.md)

```text
  § 68. Use of false brand prohibited.  No person shall offer, sell or
expose for sale, in any package, butter or cheese which is falsely
branded or labeled.
```
