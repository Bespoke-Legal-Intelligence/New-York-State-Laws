# Agriculture and Markets Law § 434 — Waiving of requirements authorized

[Agriculture and Markets Law](../README.md) › [Article 26-C — Regulation of Animal Shelters](README.md)

```text
  * § 434. Waiving of requirements authorized. The commissioner is
hereby authorized to waive the requirements established pursuant to this
article during a disaster emergency, provided that an official
declaration of such disaster emergency has been made by the governor or
his or her delegated state official pursuant to section twenty-eight of
the executive law.
  * NB Effective December 15, 2025
```
