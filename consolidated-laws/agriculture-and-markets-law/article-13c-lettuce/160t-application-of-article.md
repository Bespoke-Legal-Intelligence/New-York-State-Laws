# Agriculture and Markets Law § 160-T — Application of article

[Agriculture and Markets Law](../README.md) › [Article 13-C — Lettuce](README.md)

```text
  § 160-t. Application of article. This article shall not apply to
lettuce which lettuce is ultimately grown outside of the state of New
York and only transported through or within this state for sale outside
New York.
```
