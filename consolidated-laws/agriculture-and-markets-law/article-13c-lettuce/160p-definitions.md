# Agriculture and Markets Law § 160-P — Definitions

[Agriculture and Markets Law](../README.md) › [Article 13-C — Lettuce](README.md)

```text
  § 160-p. Definitions.  As used in this article:
  1. The words "closed package" mean a crate, box, carton or other
container, the contents of which cannot be adequately inspected without
opening.
  2. The word "lettuce" means iceberg type lettuce.
```
