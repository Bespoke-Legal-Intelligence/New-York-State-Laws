# Agriculture and Markets Law § 160-S — Rules and regulations

[Agriculture and Markets Law](../README.md) › [Article 13-C — Lettuce](README.md)

```text
  § 160-s. Rules and regulations.  The commissioner is hereby
authorized, after public hearing, to adopt and promulgate such rules and
regulations to supplement and give full effect to the provisions of this
article as he may deem necessary.
```
