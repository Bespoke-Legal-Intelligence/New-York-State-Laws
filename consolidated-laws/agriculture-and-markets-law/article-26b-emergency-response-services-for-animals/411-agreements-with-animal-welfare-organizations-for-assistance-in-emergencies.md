# Agriculture and Markets Law § 411 — Agreements with animal welfare organizations for assistance in emergencies and disasters

[Agriculture and Markets Law](../README.md) › [Article 26-B — Emergency Response Services For Animals](README.md)

```text
  § 411. Agreements with animal welfare organizations for assistance in
emergencies and disasters. The commissioner is hereby authorized to
enter into agreements with animal welfare organizations at no direct
cost to the state to support the prevention of, preparedness for,
response to, and recovery from emergencies and disasters affecting
animals in New York state.
```
