# Agriculture and Markets Law § 258-O — The commissioner is hereby authorized and directed to make a study of the sanitary regulations, codes and rules applicable to milk and mi...

[Agriculture and Markets Law](../README.md) › [Article 21 — Milk Control](README.md)

```text
  § 258-o. The commissioner is hereby authorized and directed to make a
study of the sanitary regulations, codes and rules applicable to milk
and milk products produced in the state of New York, and to study and
report to the governor the possible economic impact of a national
sanitation act upon New York milk production and New York dairy farmers.
```
