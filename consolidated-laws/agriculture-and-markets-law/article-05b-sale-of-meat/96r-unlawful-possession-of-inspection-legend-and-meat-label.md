# Agriculture and Markets Law § 96-R — Unlawful possession of inspection legend and meat label

[Agriculture and Markets Law](../README.md) › [Article 5-B — Sale of Meat](README.md)

```text
  § 96-r. Unlawful possession of inspection legend and meat label.  It
shall be unlawful for any person to possess, keep, or use an inspection
legend or meat label or to possess, keep or use any stamp or label
simulating the inspection legend and meat label unless authorized
pursuant to the federal meat inspection act.
```
