# Agriculture and Markets Law § 146-D — Rules and regulations

[Agriculture and Markets Law](../README.md) › [Article 10 — Sale and Analysis of Commercial Fertilizer](README.md)

```text
  § 146-d. Rules and regulations. The commissioner is hereby authorized,
after public hearing, to adopt, promulgate and issue such rules and
regulations as he may deem necessary to supplement, carry out and give
full force and effect to the provisions of this article.
```
