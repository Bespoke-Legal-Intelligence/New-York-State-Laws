# Agriculture and Markets Law § 251-Z-7 — Records to be kept by licensee

[Agriculture and Markets Law](../README.md) › [Article 20-C — Licensing of Food Processing Establishments](README.md)

```text
  § 251-z-7. Records to be kept by licensee. Every operator of a food
processing establishment shall keep, in such form as the commissioner
shall approve, such records as may be required by the commissioner
pursuant to rules and regulations promulgated pursuant to this article.
```
