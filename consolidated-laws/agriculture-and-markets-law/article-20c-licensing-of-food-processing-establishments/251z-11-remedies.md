# Agriculture and Markets Law § 251-Z-11 — Remedies

[Agriculture and Markets Law](../README.md) › [Article 20-C — Licensing of Food Processing Establishments](README.md)

```text
  § 251-z-11. Remedies. The commissioner may institute such action at
law or in equity as may be necessary to enforce compliance with any
provision of this article or of any rule or regulation applicable
thereto or promulgated thereunder. In addition to any other remedy
prescribed in article three of this chapter, or otherwise, he may apply
for relief by injunction without alleging or proving that an adequate
remedy at law does not exist. Such application may be made to the
supreme court in any district or county, as provided by the civil
practice law and rules, or to the supreme court in the third judicial
district.
```
