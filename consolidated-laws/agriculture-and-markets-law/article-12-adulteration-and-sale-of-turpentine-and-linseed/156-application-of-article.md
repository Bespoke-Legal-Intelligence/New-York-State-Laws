# Agriculture and Markets Law § 156 — Application of article

[Agriculture and Markets Law](../README.md) › [Article 12 — Adulteration and Sale of Turpentine and Linseed Oil](README.md)

```text
  § 156. Application of article.  The provisions of this article shall
not be construed as prohibiting the manufacture or sale of any compounds
or imitations mentioned in this article providing the requirements of
this article are otherwise observed.
```
