# Agriculture and Markets Law § 535 — Inspections and ongoing requirements

[Agriculture and Markets Law](../README.md) › [Article 29-A — Regulation of Hemp Extract](README.md)

```text
  * § 535. Inspections and ongoing requirements. All licensees shall be
subject to reasonable inspection by the department, in consultation with
the department of health, and a person who holds a license must make
himself or herself, or an agent thereof, available and present for any
inspection required by the department. The department shall make
reasonable accommodations so that ordinary business is not interrupted
and safety and security procedures are not compromised by the
inspection.
  * NB Effective and Repealed March 8, 2020
```
