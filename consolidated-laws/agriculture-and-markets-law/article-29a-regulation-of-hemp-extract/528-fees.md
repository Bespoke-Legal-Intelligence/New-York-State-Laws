# Agriculture and Markets Law § 528 — Fees

[Agriculture and Markets Law](../README.md) › [Article 29-A — Regulation of Hemp Extract](README.md)

**Repealed** (13 Mar 2020). The text below is what the Senate publishes for this repealed provision.

```text
  * § 528. Fees. The department shall have the authority to charge
licensees a biennial license fee. Such fee may be based on the amount of
hemp extract to be grown, processed, manufactured or extracted by the
licensee, the gross annual receipts of the licensee for the previous
license period, or any other factors deemed appropriate by the
department.
  * NB Effective and Repealed March 8, 2020
```
