# Agriculture and Markets Law § 539 — Advertising

[Agriculture and Markets Law](../README.md) › [Article 29-A — Regulation of Hemp Extract](README.md)

```text
  * § 539. Advertising. The department shall promulgate rules and
regulations governing the advertising of hemp extract and any other
related products or services as determined by the commissioner.
  * NB Effective March 8, 2020
```
