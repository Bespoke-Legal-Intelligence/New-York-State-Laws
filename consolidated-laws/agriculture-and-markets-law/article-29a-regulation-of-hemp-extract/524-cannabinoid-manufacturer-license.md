# Agriculture and Markets Law § 524 — Cannabinoid manufacturer license

[Agriculture and Markets Law](../README.md) › [Article 29-A — Regulation of Hemp Extract](README.md)

**Repealed** (13 Mar 2020). The text below is what the Senate publishes for this repealed provision.

```text
  * § 524. Cannabinoid manufacturer license. 1. A cannabinoid
manufacturer license authorizes the licensee's acquisition, possession,
and manufacture of hemp extract from a licensed cannabinoid grower or
cannabinoid extractor for the processing of hemp extract or the
production of hemp extract products marketed, distributed or sold for
cannabinoid content and used or intended for human or animal consumption
or use.
  2. Notwithstanding subdivision one of this section, nothing shall
prevent a cannabinoid manufacturer from manufacturing industrial hemp
products not used or intended for human or animal consumption or use.
  * NB Effective and Repealed March 8, 2020
```
