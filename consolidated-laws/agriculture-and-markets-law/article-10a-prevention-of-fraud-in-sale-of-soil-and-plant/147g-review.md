# Agriculture and Markets Law § 147-G — Review

[Agriculture and Markets Law](../README.md) › [Article 10-A — Prevention of Fraud In Sale of Soil and Plant Inoculants](README.md)

```text
  § 147-g. Review.  The action of the commissioner in refusing to grant
a license, or in revoking a license, shall be subject to review in the
manner provided by article seventy-eight of the civil practice law and
rules.
```
