# Agriculture and Markets Law § 147-C — Label requirements

[Agriculture and Markets Law](../README.md) › [Article 10-A — Prevention of Fraud In Sale of Soil and Plant Inoculants](README.md)

```text
  § 147-c. Label requirements.  Each soil or plant inoculant, when sold
or offered or exposed for sale within this state, shall be clearly and
plainly labeled to show whether the inoculant is represented as
effective for inoculating legumes or for some other purpose, and if
represented as effective for the inoculation of legumes, for which
legume or legumes it is so represented; and the date to which the
inoculant is represented to produce effective inoculation.
```
