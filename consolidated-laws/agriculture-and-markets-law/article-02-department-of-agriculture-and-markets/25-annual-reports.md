# Agriculture and Markets Law § 25 — Annual reports

[Agriculture and Markets Law](../README.md) › [Article 2 — Department of Agriculture and Markets; Jurisdiction; General Powers and Duties](README.md)

```text
  § 25. Annual reports.  The commissioner may require all agricultural
societies receiving money from the state to make reports to the
department, and may prescribe the form of such reports.
```
