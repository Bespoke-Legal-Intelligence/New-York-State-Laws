# Agriculture and Markets Law § 10 — Confidential positions

[Agriculture and Markets Law](../README.md) › [Article 2 — Department of Agriculture and Markets; Jurisdiction; General Powers and Duties](README.md)

```text
  § 10. Confidential positions. The deputy commissioners, secretary and
counsel shall be deemed to occupy confidential positions to the
commissioner, and may be appointed without competitive examination.
```
