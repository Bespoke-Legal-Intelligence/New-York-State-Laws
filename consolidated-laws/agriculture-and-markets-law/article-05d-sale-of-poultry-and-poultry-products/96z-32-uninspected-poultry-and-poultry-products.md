# Agriculture and Markets Law § 96-Z-32 — Uninspected poultry and poultry products

[Agriculture and Markets Law](../README.md) › [Article 5-D — Sale of Poultry and Poultry Products](README.md)

```text
  § 96-z-32. Uninspected poultry and poultry products.  The commissioner
shall seize and destroy for food purposes any poultry or poultry product
subject to federal inspection that does not bear the official inspection
legend affixed pursuant to federal inspection.
```
