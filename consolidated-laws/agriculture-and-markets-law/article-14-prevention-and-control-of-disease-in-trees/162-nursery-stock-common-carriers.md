# Agriculture and Markets Law § 162 — Nursery stock; common carriers

[Agriculture and Markets Law](../README.md) › [Article 14 — Prevention and Control of Disease In Trees and Plants; Insect Pests; Sale of Fruit-bearing Trees](README.md)

```text
  § 162. Nursery stock; common carriers.  No nursery stock shall be
received by a common carrier for shipment into or within the state
unless accompanied by a copy of an unexpired certificate of inspection
issued as provided in the subsequent sections of this article.
```
