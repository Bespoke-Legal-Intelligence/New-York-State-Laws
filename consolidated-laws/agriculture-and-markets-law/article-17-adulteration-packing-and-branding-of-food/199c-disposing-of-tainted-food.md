# Agriculture and Markets Law § 199-C — Disposing of tainted food

[Agriculture and Markets Law](../README.md) › [Article 17 — Adulteration, Packing, and Branding of Food and Food Products](README.md)

```text
  § 199-c. Disposing of tainted food. A person who with intent that the
same be used as food, drink, or medicine, sells, or offers or exposes
for sale, any article whatever which, to his knowledge, is tainted or
spoiled, or for any cause unfit to be used as such food, drink or
medicine, is guilty of a misdemeanor.
```
