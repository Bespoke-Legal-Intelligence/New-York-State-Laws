# Agriculture and Markets Law § 204-C — Prepared horseradish

[Agriculture and Markets Law](../README.md) › [Article 17 — Adulteration, Packing, and Branding of Food and Food Products](README.md)

```text
  § 204-c. Prepared horseradish.  The term "prepared horseradish" means
a clean, wholesome food product made with pure ground horseradish root,
vinegar and salt, with or without the addition of oil of mustard. Sugar,
spices and other flavorings, and either cream or vegetable oil may be
used as optional ingredients provided they are listed on the label.
```
