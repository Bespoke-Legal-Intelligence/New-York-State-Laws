# Agriculture and Markets Law § 214-A — Samples

[Agriculture and Markets Law](../README.md) › [Article 17 — Adulteration, Packing, and Branding of Food and Food Products](README.md)

```text
  § 214-a. Samples.  The commissioner is hereby empowered to enact,
amend and repeal rules prescribing the number, nature and size of
samples of food to be taken in the enforcement of this article.
```
