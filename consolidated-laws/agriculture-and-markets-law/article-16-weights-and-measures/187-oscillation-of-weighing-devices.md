# Agriculture and Markets Law § 187 — Oscillation of weighing devices

[Agriculture and Markets Law](../README.md) › [Article 16 — Weights and Measures](README.md)

```text
  § 187. Oscillation of weighing devices. No person selling, or offering
for sale a commodity by net weight shall quote or state the net weight
or the total selling price of such commodity unless the weight indicator
of the weighing device on which it is being weighed is at rest.
```
