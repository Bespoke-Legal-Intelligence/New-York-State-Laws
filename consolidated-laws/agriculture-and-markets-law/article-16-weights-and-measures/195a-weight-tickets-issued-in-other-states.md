# Agriculture and Markets Law § 195-A — Weight tickets issued in other states

[Agriculture and Markets Law](../README.md) › [Article 16 — Weights and Measures](README.md)

```text
  § 195-a. Weight tickets issued in other states. Whenever in any state
requiring weighing licenses or weight tickets, the administrative
officer in charge thereof is authorized by statute to recognize and
accept weighing licenses and weight tickets of this state, the
commissioner in his discretion is authorized to recognize and accept
such licenses and tickets provided such tickets have been issued by a
licensed weighmaster.
```
