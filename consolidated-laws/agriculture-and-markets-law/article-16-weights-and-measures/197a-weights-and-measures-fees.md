# Agriculture and Markets Law § 197-A — Weights and measures fees

[Agriculture and Markets Law](../README.md) › [Article 16 — Weights and Measures](README.md)

```text
  § 197-a. Weights and measures fees. Notwithstanding any other
provision of law to the contrary, the commissioner is hereby authorized
and directed to deposit all money received in payment of fees under this
article in an account within the miscellaneous special revenue fund.
```
