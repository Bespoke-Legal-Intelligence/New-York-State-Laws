# Agriculture and Markets Law § 222 — Rules and regulations

[Agriculture and Markets Law](../README.md) › [Article 17-B — Licensing of Food Salvagers](README.md)

```text
  § 222. Rules and regulations.  The commissioner is hereby authorized,
after public hearing, to adopt and promulgate such rules and regulations
to supplement and give full effect to the provisions of this article as
he may deem necessary.
```
