# Agriculture and Markets Law § 216 — Application of article

[Agriculture and Markets Law](../README.md) › [Article 17-B — Licensing of Food Salvagers](README.md)

```text
  § 216. Application of article.  The general purpose of this article is
to protect the public safety and health by controlling the salvaging of
damaged or contaminated food, food products and devices which come into
intimate contact with food or food products.
```
