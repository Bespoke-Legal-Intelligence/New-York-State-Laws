# Agriculture and Markets Law § 172-C — Penalties for violations

[Agriculture and Markets Law](../README.md) › [Article 14-A — Sale and Distribution of Lye and Other Caustic Substances](README.md)

```text
  § 172-c. Penalties for violations.  Any person violating the
provisions of this article shall be guilty of a misdemeanor and, upon
conviction thereof, be punished by a fine of not more than two hundred
dollars, or by imprisonment for not more than ninety days, or by both
such fine and imprisonment, in the discretion of the court.
```
