# Agriculture and Markets Law § 42 — Act of officer or agent deemed act of principal

[Agriculture and Markets Law](../README.md) › [Article 3 — Investigation; Practice and Procedure; Violations; Penalties](README.md)

```text
  § 42. Act of officer or agent deemed act of principal.  In construing
and enforcing the provisions of this chapter relating to penalties, the
act of a director, officer, agent or other person acting for or employed
by a person, association or corporation subject to the provisions of
this chapter and acting within the scope of his employment, shall be
deemed the act of such person, association or corporation.
```
