# Agriculture and Markets Law § 33 — Immunity of witnesses

[Agriculture and Markets Law](../README.md) › [Article 3 — Investigation; Practice and Procedure; Violations; Penalties](README.md)

```text
  § 33. Immunity of witnesses.  In any investigation, hearing or
inquiry, conducted pursuant to this chapter or the rules of the
department, the commissioner, or his deputy or other officer presiding
at such investigation, hearing or inquiry, may confer immunity in
accordance with the provisions of section 50.20 of the criminal
procedure law.
```
