# Agriculture and Markets Law § 105-C — Temporary emergency

[Agriculture and Markets Law](../README.md) › [Article 6-A — Indemnification For Rabies](README.md)

```text
  § 105-c. Temporary emergency.  The present losses to livestock in
certain areas of the state resulting from the disease in animals known
as rabies threaten to impair the agricultural economy of the communities
affected. This article is enacted in the exercise of the police power
and its purposes generally are to protect the public welfare. The
provisions of this article shall not apply to the city of New York.
```
