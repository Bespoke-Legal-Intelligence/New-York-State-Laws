# Personal Property Law § 38 — Transfers or charges without consideration

[Personal Property Law](../README.md) › [Article 3 — Agreements In Writing; Without Consideration; Fraudulent; Factors](README.md)

```text
  § 38. Transfers or charges without consideration. A transfer or charge
shall not be adjudged fraudulent as against creditors or purchasers,
solely on the ground that it was not founded on a valuable
consideration.
```
