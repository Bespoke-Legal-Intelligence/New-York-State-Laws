# Penal Law § 180.10 — Bribery of labor official; definition of term

[Penal Law](../../../README.md) › [Part 3 — Specific Offenses](../../README.md) › [Title K — Offenses Involving Fraud](../README.md) › [Article 180 — Bribery Not Involving Public Servants, and Related Offenses](README.md)

```text
§ 180.10 Bribery of labor official; definition of term.
  As used in this article, "labor official" means any duly appointed
representative of a labor organization or any duly appointed trustee or
representative of an employee welfare trust fund.
```
