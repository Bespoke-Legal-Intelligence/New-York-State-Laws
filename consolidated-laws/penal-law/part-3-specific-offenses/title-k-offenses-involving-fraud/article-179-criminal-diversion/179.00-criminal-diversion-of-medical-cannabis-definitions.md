# Penal Law § 179.00 — Criminal diversion of medical cannabis; definitions

[Penal Law](../../../README.md) › [Part 3 — Specific Offenses](../../README.md) › [Title K — Offenses Involving Fraud](../README.md) › [Article 179 — Criminal Diversion of Medical Marihuana](README.md)

```text
* § 179.00 Criminal diversion of medical cannabis; definitions.
  The following definitions are applicable to this article:
  1. "Medical cannabis" means medical cannabis as defined in section
three of the cannabis law.
  2. "Certification" means a certification, made under section thirty of
the cannabis law.
  * NB Repealed July 5, 2028
```
