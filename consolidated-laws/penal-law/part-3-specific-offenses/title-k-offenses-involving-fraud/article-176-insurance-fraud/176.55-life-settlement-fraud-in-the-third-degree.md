# Penal Law § 176.55 — Life settlement fraud in the third degree

[Penal Law](../../../README.md) › [Part 3 — Specific Offenses](../../README.md) › [Title K — Offenses Involving Fraud](../README.md) › [Article 176 — Insurance Fraud](README.md)

```text
§ 176.55 Life settlement fraud in the third degree.
  A person is guilty of life settlement fraud in the third degree when
he or she commits a fraudulent life settlement act and thereby
wrongfully takes, obtains or withholds, or attempts to wrongfully take,
obtain or withhold property with a value in excess of fifty thousand
dollars.
  Life settlement fraud in the third degree is a class D felony.
```
