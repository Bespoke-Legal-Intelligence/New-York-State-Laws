# Penal Law § 187.05 — Residential mortgage fraud in the fifth degree

[Penal Law](../../../README.md) › [Part 3 — Specific Offenses](../../README.md) › [Title K — Offenses Involving Fraud](../README.md) › [Article 187 — Residential Mortgage Fraud](README.md)

```text
§ 187.05 Residential mortgage fraud in the fifth degree.
  A person is guilty of residential mortgage fraud in the fifth degree
when he or she commits residential mortgage fraud.
  Residential mortgage fraud in the fifth degree is a class A
misdemeanor.
```
