# Penal Law § 270.30 — Unlawful fleeing a police officer in a motor vehicle in the second degree

[Penal Law](../../../README.md) › [Part 3 — Specific Offenses](../../README.md) › [Title P — Offenses Against Public Safety](../README.md) › [Article 270 — Other Offenses Relating to Public Safety](README.md)

```text
§ 270.30 Unlawful fleeing a police officer in a motor vehicle in the
            second degree.
  A person is guilty of unlawful fleeing a police officer in a motor
vehicle in the second degree when he or she commits the offense of
unlawful fleeing a police officer in a motor vehicle in the third
degree, as defined in section 270.25 of this article, and as a result of
such conduct a police officer or a third person suffers serious physical
injury.
  Unlawful fleeing a police officer in a motor vehicle in the second
degree is a class E felony.
```
