# Penal Law § 265.12 — Criminal sale of a firearm in the second degree

[Penal Law](../../../README.md) › [Part 3 — Specific Offenses](../../README.md) › [Title P — Offenses Against Public Safety](../README.md) › [Article 265 — Firearms and Other Dangerous Weapons](README.md)

```text
* § 265.12 Criminal sale of a firearm in the second degree.
  A person is guilty of criminal sale of a firearm in the second degree
when such person:
  (1) unlawfully sells, exchanges, gives or disposes of to another five
or more firearms; or
  (2) unlawfully sells, exchanges, gives or disposes of to another
person or persons a total of five or more firearms in a period of not
more than one year.
  Criminal sale of a firearm in the second degree is a class C felony.
  * NB Effective until May 9, 2022
* § 265.12 Criminal sale of a firearm in the second degree.
  A person is guilty of criminal sale of a firearm in the second degree
when such person:
  (1) unlawfully sells, exchanges, gives or disposes of to another five
or more firearms; or
  (2) unlawfully sells, exchanges, gives or disposes of to another
person or persons a total of two or more firearms in a period of not
more than one year.
  Criminal sale of a firearm in the second degree is a class C felony.
  * NB Effective May 9, 2022
```
