# Penal Law § 265.01-C — Criminal possession of a rapid-fire modification device

[Penal Law](../../../README.md) › [Part 3 — Specific Offenses](../../README.md) › [Title P — Offenses Against Public Safety](../README.md) › [Article 265 — Firearms and Other Dangerous Weapons](README.md)

```text
* § 265.01-c Criminal possession of a rapid-fire modification device.
  A person is guilty of criminal possession of a rapid-fire modification
device when he or she knowingly possesses any rapid-fire modification
device.
  Criminal possession of a rapid-fire modification device is a class A
misdemeanor.
  * NB Effective November 26, 2019
```
