# Penal Law § 280.05 — Unlawful use of a drone

[Penal Law](../../../README.md) › [Part 3 — Specific Offenses](../../README.md) › [Title P — Offenses Against Public Safety](../README.md) › [Article 280 — Offenses Relating to Unlawful Use of a Drone](README.md)

```text
§ 280.05 Unlawful use of a drone.
  A person commits unlawful use of a drone when such person:
  1. operates a drone in a nefarious manner; or
  2. operates a drone over prohibited space without express prior
approval from someone the person reasonably believes has the authority
to grant such approval.
  Unlawful use of a drone is a class A misdemeanor.
```
