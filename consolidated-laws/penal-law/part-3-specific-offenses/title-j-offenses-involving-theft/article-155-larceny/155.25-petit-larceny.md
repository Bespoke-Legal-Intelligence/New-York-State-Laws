# Penal Law § 155.25 — Petit larceny

[Penal Law](../../../README.md) › [Part 3 — Specific Offenses](../../README.md) › [Title J — Offenses Involving Theft](../README.md) › [Article 155 — Larceny](README.md)

```text
§ 155.25 Petit larceny.
  A person is guilty of petit larceny when he steals property.
  Petit larceny is a class A misdemeanor.
```
