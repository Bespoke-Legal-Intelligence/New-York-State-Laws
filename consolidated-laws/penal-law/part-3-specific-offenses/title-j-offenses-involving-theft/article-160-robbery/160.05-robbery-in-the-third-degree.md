# Penal Law § 160.05 — Robbery in the third degree

[Penal Law](../../../README.md) › [Part 3 — Specific Offenses](../../README.md) › [Title J — Offenses Involving Theft](../README.md) › [Article 160 — Robbery](README.md)

```text
§ 160.05 Robbery in the third degree.
  A person is guilty of robbery in the third degree when he forcibly
steals property.
  Robbery in the third degree is a class D felony.
```
