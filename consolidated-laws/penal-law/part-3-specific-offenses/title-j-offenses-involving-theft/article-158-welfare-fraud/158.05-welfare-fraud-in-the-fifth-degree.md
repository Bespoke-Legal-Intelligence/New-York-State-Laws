# Penal Law § 158.05 — Welfare fraud in the fifth degree

[Penal Law](../../../README.md) › [Part 3 — Specific Offenses](../../README.md) › [Title J — Offenses Involving Theft](../README.md) › [Article 158 — Welfare Fraud](README.md)

```text
§ 158.05 Welfare fraud in the fifth degree.
  A person is guilty of welfare fraud in the fifth degree when he or she
commits a fraudulent welfare act and thereby takes or obtains public
assistance benefits.
  Welfare fraud in the fifth degree is a class A misdemeanor.
```
