# Penal Law § 140.20 — Burglary in the third degree

[Penal Law](../../../README.md) › [Part 3 — Specific Offenses](../../README.md) › [Title I — Offenses Involving Damage to and Intrusion Upon Property](../README.md) › [Article 140 — Burglary and Related Offenses](README.md)

```text
§ 140.20 Burglary in the third degree.
  A person is guilty of burglary in the third degree when he knowingly
enters or remains unlawfully in a building with intent to commit a crime
therein.
  Burglary in the third degree is a class D felony.
```
