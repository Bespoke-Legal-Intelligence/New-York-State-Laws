# Penal Law § 140.05 — Trespass

[Penal Law](../../../README.md) › [Part 3 — Specific Offenses](../../README.md) › [Title I — Offenses Involving Damage to and Intrusion Upon Property](../README.md) › [Article 140 — Burglary and Related Offenses](README.md)

```text
§ 140.05. Trespass.
  A person is guilty of trespass when he knowingly enters or remains
unlawfully in or upon premises.
  Trespass is a violation.
```
