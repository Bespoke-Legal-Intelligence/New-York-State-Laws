# Penal Law § 242.10 — Harming a service animal in the second degree

[Penal Law](../../../README.md) › [Part 3 — Specific Offenses](../../README.md) › [Title N — Offenses Against Public Order, Public Sensibilities and the Right to Privacy](../README.md) › [Article 242 — Offenses Against Service Animals and Handlers](README.md)

```text
§ 242.10 Harming a service animal in the second degree.
  A person is guilty of harming a service animal in the second degree
when, with the intent to do so, he or she causes physical injury, or
causes such injury that results in the death, of a service animal.
  Harming a service animal in the second degree is a class A
misdemeanor.
```
