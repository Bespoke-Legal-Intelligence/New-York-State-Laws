# Penal Law § 242.15 — Harming a service animal in the first degree

[Penal Law](../../../README.md) › [Part 3 — Specific Offenses](../../README.md) › [Title N — Offenses Against Public Order, Public Sensibilities and the Right to Privacy](../README.md) › [Article 242 — Offenses Against Service Animals and Handlers](README.md)

```text
§ 242.15 Harming a service animal in the first degree.
  A person is guilty of harming a service animal in the first degree
when, he or she commits the crime of harming a service animal in the
second degree, and has been convicted of harming a service animal in the
first or second degree within the prior five years.
  Harming a service animal in the first degree is a class E felony.
```
