# Penal Law § 250.05 — Eavesdropping

[Penal Law](../../../README.md) › [Part 3 — Specific Offenses](../../README.md) › [Title N — Offenses Against Public Order, Public Sensibilities and the Right to Privacy](../README.md) › [Article 250 — Offenses Against the Right to Privacy](README.md)

```text
§ 250.05 Eavesdropping.
  A person is guilty of eavesdropping when he unlawfully engages in
wiretapping, mechanical overhearing of a conversation, or intercepting
or accessing of an electronic communication.
  Eavesdropping is a class E felony.
```
