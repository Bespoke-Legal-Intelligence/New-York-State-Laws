# Penal Law § 240.20 — Disorderly conduct

[Penal Law](../../../README.md) › [Part 3 — Specific Offenses](../../README.md) › [Title N — Offenses Against Public Order, Public Sensibilities and the Right to Privacy](../README.md) › [Article 240 — Offenses Against Public Order](README.md)

```text
§ 240.20 Disorderly conduct.
  A person is guilty of disorderly conduct when, with intent to cause
public inconvenience, annoyance or alarm, or recklessly creating a risk
thereof:
  1. He engages in fighting or in violent, tumultuous or threatening
behavior; or
  2. He makes unreasonable noise; or
  3. In a public place, he uses abusive or obscene language, or makes an
obscene gesture; or
  4. Without lawful authority, he disturbs any lawful assembly or
meeting of persons; or
  5. He obstructs vehicular or pedestrian traffic; or
  6. He congregates with other persons in a public place and refuses to
comply with a lawful order of the police to disperse; or
  7. He creates a hazardous or physically offensive condition by any act
which serves no legitimate purpose.
  Disorderly conduct is a violation.
```
