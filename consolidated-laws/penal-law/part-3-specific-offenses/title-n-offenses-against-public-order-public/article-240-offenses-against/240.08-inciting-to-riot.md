# Penal Law § 240.08 — Inciting to riot

[Penal Law](../../../README.md) › [Part 3 — Specific Offenses](../../README.md) › [Title N — Offenses Against Public Order, Public Sensibilities and the Right to Privacy](../README.md) › [Article 240 — Offenses Against Public Order](README.md)

```text
§ 240.08 Inciting to riot.
  A person is guilty of inciting to riot when he urges ten or more
persons to engage in tumultuous and violent conduct of a kind likely to
create public alarm.
  Inciting to riot is a class A misdemeanor.
```
