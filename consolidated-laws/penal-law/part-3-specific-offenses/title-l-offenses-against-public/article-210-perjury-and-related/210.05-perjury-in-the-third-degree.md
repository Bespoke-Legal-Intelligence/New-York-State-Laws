# Penal Law § 210.05 — Perjury in the third degree

[Penal Law](../../../README.md) › [Part 3 — Specific Offenses](../../README.md) › [Title L — Offenses Against Public Administration](../README.md) › [Article 210 — Perjury and Related Offenses](README.md)

```text
§ 210.05 Perjury in the third degree.
  A person is guilty of perjury in the third degree when he swears
falsely.
  Perjury in the third degree is a class A misdemeanor.
```
