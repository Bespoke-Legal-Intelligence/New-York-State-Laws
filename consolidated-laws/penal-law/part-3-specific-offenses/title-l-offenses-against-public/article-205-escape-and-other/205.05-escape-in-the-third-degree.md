# Penal Law § 205.05 — Escape in the third degree

[Penal Law](../../../README.md) › [Part 3 — Specific Offenses](../../README.md) › [Title L — Offenses Against Public Administration](../README.md) › [Article 205 — Escape and Other Offenses Relating to Custody](README.md)

```text
§ 205.05 Escape in the third degree.
  A person is guilty of escape in the third degree when he escapes from
custody.
  Escape in the third degree is a class A misdemeanor.
```
