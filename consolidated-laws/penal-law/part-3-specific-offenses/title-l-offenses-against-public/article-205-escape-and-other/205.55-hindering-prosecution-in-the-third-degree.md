# Penal Law § 205.55 — Hindering prosecution in the third degree

[Penal Law](../../../README.md) › [Part 3 — Specific Offenses](../../README.md) › [Title L — Offenses Against Public Administration](../README.md) › [Article 205 — Escape and Other Offenses Relating to Custody](README.md)

```text
§ 205.55 Hindering prosecution in the third degree.
  A person is guilty of hindering prosecution in the third degree when
he renders criminal assistance to a person who has committed a felony.
  Hindering prosecution in the third degree is a class A misdemeanor.
```
