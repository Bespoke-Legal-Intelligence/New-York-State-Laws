# Penal Law § 200.25 — Receiving reward for official misconduct in the second degree

[Penal Law](../../../README.md) › [Part 3 — Specific Offenses](../../README.md) › [Title L — Offenses Against Public Administration](../README.md) › [Article 200 — Bribery Involving Public Servants and Related Offenses](README.md)

```text
§ 200.25 Receiving reward for official misconduct in the second degree.
  A public servant is guilty of receiving reward for official misconduct
in the second degree when he solicits, accepts or agrees to accept any
benefit from another person for having violated his duty as a public
servant.
  Receiving reward for official misconduct in the second degree is a
class E felony.
```
