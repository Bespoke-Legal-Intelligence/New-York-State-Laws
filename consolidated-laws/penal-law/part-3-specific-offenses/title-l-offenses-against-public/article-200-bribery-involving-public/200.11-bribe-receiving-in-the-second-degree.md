# Penal Law § 200.11 — Bribe receiving in the second degree

[Penal Law](../../../README.md) › [Part 3 — Specific Offenses](../../README.md) › [Title L — Offenses Against Public Administration](../README.md) › [Article 200 — Bribery Involving Public Servants and Related Offenses](README.md)

```text
§ 200.11 Bribe receiving in the second degree.
  A public servant is guilty of bribe receiving in the second degree
when he or she solicits, accepts or agrees to accept any benefit valued
in excess of five thousand dollars from another person upon an agreement
or understanding that his or her vote, opinion, judgment, action,
decision or exercise of discretion as a public servant will thereby be
influenced.
  Bribe receiving in the second degree is a class C felony.
```
