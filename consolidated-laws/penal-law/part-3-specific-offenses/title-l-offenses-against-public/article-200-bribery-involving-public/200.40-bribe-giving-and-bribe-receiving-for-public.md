# Penal Law § 200.40 — Bribe giving and bribe receiving for public office; definition of term

[Penal Law](../../../README.md) › [Part 3 — Specific Offenses](../../README.md) › [Title L — Offenses Against Public Administration](../README.md) › [Article 200 — Bribery Involving Public Servants and Related Offenses](README.md)

```text
§ 200.40 Bribe giving and bribe receiving for public office; definition
           of term.
  As used in sections 200.45 and 200.50, "party officer" means a person
who holds any position or office in a political party, whether by
election, appointment or otherwise.
```
