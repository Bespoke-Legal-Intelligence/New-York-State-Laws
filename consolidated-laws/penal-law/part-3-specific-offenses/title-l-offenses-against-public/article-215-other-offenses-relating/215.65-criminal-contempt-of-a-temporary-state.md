# Penal Law § 215.65 — Criminal contempt of a temporary state commission

[Penal Law](../../../README.md) › [Part 3 — Specific Offenses](../../README.md) › [Title L — Offenses Against Public Administration](../README.md) › [Article 215 — Other Offenses Relating to Judicial and Other Proceedings](README.md)

```text
§ 215.65 Criminal contempt of a temporary state commission.
  A person is guilty of criminal contempt of a temporary state
commission when, having been duly subpoenaed to attend as a witness at
an investigation or hearing before a temporary state commission, he
fails or refuses to attend without lawful excuse.
  Criminal contempt of a temporary state commission is a class A
misdemeanor.
```
