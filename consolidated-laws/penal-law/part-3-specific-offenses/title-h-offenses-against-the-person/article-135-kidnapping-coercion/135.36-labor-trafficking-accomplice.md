# Penal Law § 135.36 — Labor trafficking; accomplice

[Penal Law](../../../README.md) › [Part 3 — Specific Offenses](../../README.md) › [Title H — Offenses Against the Person Involving Physical Injury, Sexual Conduct, Restraint and Intimidation](../README.md) › [Article 135 — Kidnapping, Coercion and Related Offenses](README.md)

```text
§ 135.36 Labor trafficking; accomplice.
  In a prosecution for labor trafficking, a person who has been
compelled or induced or recruited, enticed, harbored or transported to
engage in labor shall not be deemed to be an accomplice.
```
