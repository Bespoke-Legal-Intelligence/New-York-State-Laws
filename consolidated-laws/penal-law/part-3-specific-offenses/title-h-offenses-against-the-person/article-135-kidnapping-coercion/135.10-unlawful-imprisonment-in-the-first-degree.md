# Penal Law § 135.10 — Unlawful imprisonment in the first degree

[Penal Law](../../../README.md) › [Part 3 — Specific Offenses](../../README.md) › [Title H — Offenses Against the Person Involving Physical Injury, Sexual Conduct, Restraint and Intimidation](../README.md) › [Article 135 — Kidnapping, Coercion and Related Offenses](README.md)

```text
§ 135.10 Unlawful imprisonment in the first degree.
  A person is guilty of unlawful imprisonment in the first degree when
he restrains another person under circumstances which expose the latter
to a risk of serious physical injury.
  Unlawful imprisonment in the first degree is a class E felony.
```
