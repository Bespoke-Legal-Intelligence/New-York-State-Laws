# Penal Law § 135.20 — Kidnapping in the second degree

[Penal Law](../../../README.md) › [Part 3 — Specific Offenses](../../README.md) › [Title H — Offenses Against the Person Involving Physical Injury, Sexual Conduct, Restraint and Intimidation](../README.md) › [Article 135 — Kidnapping, Coercion and Related Offenses](README.md)

```text
§ 135.20 Kidnapping in the second degree.
  A person is guilty of kidnapping in the second degree when he abducts
another person.
  Kidnapping in the second degree is a class B felony.
```
