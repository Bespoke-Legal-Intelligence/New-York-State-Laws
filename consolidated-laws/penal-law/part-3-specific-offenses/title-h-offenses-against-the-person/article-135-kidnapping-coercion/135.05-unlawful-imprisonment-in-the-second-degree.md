# Penal Law § 135.05 — Unlawful imprisonment in the second degree

[Penal Law](../../../README.md) › [Part 3 — Specific Offenses](../../README.md) › [Title H — Offenses Against the Person Involving Physical Injury, Sexual Conduct, Restraint and Intimidation](../README.md) › [Article 135 — Kidnapping, Coercion and Related Offenses](README.md)

```text
§ 135.05 Unlawful imprisonment in the second degree.
  A person is guilty of unlawful imprisonment in the second degree when
he restrains another person.
  Unlawful imprisonment in the second degree is a class A misdemeanor.
```
