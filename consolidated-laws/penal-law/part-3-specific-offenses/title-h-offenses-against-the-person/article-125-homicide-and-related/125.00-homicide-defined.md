# Penal Law § 125.00 — Homicide defined

[Penal Law](../../../README.md) › [Part 3 — Specific Offenses](../../README.md) › [Title H — Offenses Against the Person Involving Physical Injury, Sexual Conduct, Restraint and Intimidation](../README.md) › [Article 125 — Homicide and Related Offenses](README.md)

```text
§ 125.00 Homicide defined.
  Homicide means conduct which causes the death of a person under
circumstances constituting murder, manslaughter in the first degree,
manslaughter in the second degree, or criminally negligent homicide.
```
