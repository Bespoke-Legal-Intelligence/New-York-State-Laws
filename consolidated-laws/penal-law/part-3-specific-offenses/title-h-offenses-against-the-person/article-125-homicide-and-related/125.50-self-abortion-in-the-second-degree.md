# Penal Law § 125.50 — Self-abortion in the second degree

[Penal Law](../../../README.md) › [Part 3 — Specific Offenses](../../README.md) › [Title H — Offenses Against the Person Involving Physical Injury, Sexual Conduct, Restraint and Intimidation](../README.md) › [Article 125 — Homicide and Related Offenses](README.md)

**Repealed** (25 Jan 2019). The text below is what the Senate publishes for this repealed provision.

```text
§ 125.50 Self-abortion in the second degree.
  A female is guilty of self-abortion in the second degree when, being
pregnant, she commits or submits to an abortional act upon herself,
unless such abortional act is justifiable pursuant to subdivision three
of section 125.05.
  Self-abortion in the second degree is a class B misdemeanor.
```
