# Penal Law § 105.20 — Conspiracy; pleading and proof; necessity of overt act

[Penal Law](../../../README.md) › [Part 3 — Specific Offenses](../../README.md) › [Title G — Anticipatory Offenses](../README.md) › [Article 105 — Conspiracy](README.md)

```text
§ 105.20 Conspiracy; pleading and proof; necessity of overt act.
  A person shall not be convicted of conspiracy unless an overt act is
alleged and proved to have been committed by one of the conspirators in
furtherance of the conspiracy.
```
