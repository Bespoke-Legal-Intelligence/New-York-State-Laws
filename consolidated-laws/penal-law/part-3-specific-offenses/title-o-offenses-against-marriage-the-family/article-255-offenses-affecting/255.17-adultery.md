# Penal Law § 255.17 — Adultery

[Penal Law](../../../README.md) › [Part 3 — Specific Offenses](../../README.md) › [Title O — Offenses Against Marriage, the Family, and the Welfare of Children and Incompetents](../README.md) › [Article 255 — Offenses Affecting the Marital Relationship](README.md)

```text
§ 255.17 Adultery.
  A person is guilty of adultery when he engages in sexual intercourse
with another person at a time when he has a living spouse, or the other
person has a living spouse.
  Adultery is a class B misdemeanor.
```
