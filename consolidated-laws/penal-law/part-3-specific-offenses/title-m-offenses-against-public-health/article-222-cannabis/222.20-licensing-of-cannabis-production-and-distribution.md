# Penal Law § 222.20 — Licensing of cannabis production and distribution; defense

[Penal Law](../../../README.md) › [Part 3 — Specific Offenses](../../README.md) › [Title M — Offenses Against Public Health and Morals](../README.md) › [Article 222 — Cannabis](README.md)

```text
§ 222.20 Licensing of cannabis production and distribution; defense.
  In any prosecution for an offense involving cannabis under this
article or an authorized local law, it is a defense that the defendant
was engaged in such activity in compliance with the cannabis law.
```
