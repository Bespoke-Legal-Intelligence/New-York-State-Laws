# Penal Law § 222.45 — Unlawful sale of cannabis

[Penal Law](../../../README.md) › [Part 3 — Specific Offenses](../../README.md) › [Title M — Offenses Against Public Health and Morals](../README.md) › [Article 222 — Cannabis](README.md)

```text
§ 222.45 Unlawful sale of cannabis.
  A person is guilty of unlawful sale of cannabis when he or she
knowingly and unlawfully sells cannabis or concentrated cannabis.
  Unlawful sale of cannabis is a violation punishable by a fine of not
more than two hundred fifty dollars.
```
