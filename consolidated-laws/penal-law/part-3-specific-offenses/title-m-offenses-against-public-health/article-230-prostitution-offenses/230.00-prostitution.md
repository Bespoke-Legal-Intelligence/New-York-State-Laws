# Penal Law § 230.00 — Prostitution

[Penal Law](../../../README.md) › [Part 3 — Specific Offenses](../../README.md) › [Title M — Offenses Against Public Health and Morals](../README.md) › [Article 230 — Prostitution Offenses](README.md)

```text
§ 230.00 Prostitution.
  A person is guilty of prostitution when such person engages or agrees
or offers to engage in sexual conduct with another person in return for
a fee.
  Prostitution is a class B Misdemeanor.
```
