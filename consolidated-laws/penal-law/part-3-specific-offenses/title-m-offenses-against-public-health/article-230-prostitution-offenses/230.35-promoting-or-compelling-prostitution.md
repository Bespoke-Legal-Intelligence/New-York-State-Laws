# Penal Law § 230.35 — Promoting or compelling prostitution; accomplice

[Penal Law](../../../README.md) › [Part 3 — Specific Offenses](../../README.md) › [Title M — Offenses Against Public Health and Morals](../README.md) › [Article 230 — Prostitution Offenses](README.md)

```text
* § 230.35 Promoting or compelling prostitution; accomplice.
  In a prosecution for promoting prostitution or compelling
prostitution, a person less than seventeen years of age from whose
prostitution activity another person is alleged to have advanced or
attempted to advance or profited or attempted to profit shall not be
deemed to be an accomplice.
  * NB Effective until January 19, 2016
* § 230.35 Promoting or compelling prostitution; accomplice.
  In a prosecution for promoting prostitution or compelling
prostitution, a person less than eighteen years old from whose
prostitution activity another person is alleged to have advanced or
attempted to advance or profited or attempted to profit shall not be
deemed to be an accomplice.
  * NB Effective January 19, 2016
```
