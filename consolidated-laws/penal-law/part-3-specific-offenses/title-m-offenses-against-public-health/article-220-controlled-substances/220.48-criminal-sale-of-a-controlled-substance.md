# Penal Law § 220.48 — Criminal sale of a controlled substance to a child

[Penal Law](../../../README.md) › [Part 3 — Specific Offenses](../../README.md) › [Title M — Offenses Against Public Health and Morals](../README.md) › [Article 220 — Controlled Substances Offenses](README.md)

```text
§ 220.48 Criminal sale of a controlled substance to a child.
  A person is guilty of criminal sale of a controlled substance to a
child when, being over twenty-one years old, he or she knowingly and
unlawfully sells a controlled substance in violation of section 220.34
or 220.39 of this article to a person less than seventeen years old.
  Criminal sale of a controlled substance to a child is a class B
felony.
```
