# Penal Law § 221.05 — Unlawful possession of marihuana in the second degree

[Penal Law](../../../README.md) › [Part 3 — Specific Offenses](../../README.md) › [Title M — Offenses Against Public Health and Morals](../README.md) › [Article 221 — Offenses Involving Marihuana](README.md)

```text
§ 221.05 Unlawful possession of marihuana in the second degree.
  A person is guilty of unlawful possession of marihuana in the second
degree when he knowingly and unlawfully possesses marihuana.
  Unlawful possession of marihuana in the second degree is a violation
punishable only by a fine of not more than fifty dollars.
```
