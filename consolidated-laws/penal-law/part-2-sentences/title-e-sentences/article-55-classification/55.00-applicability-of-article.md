# Penal Law § 55.00 — Applicability of article

[Penal Law](../../../README.md) › [Part 2 — Sentences](../../README.md) › [Title E — Sentences](../README.md) › [Article 55 — Classification and Designation of Offenses](README.md)

```text
§ 55.00 Applicability of article.
  The provisions of this article govern the classification and
designation of every offense, whether defined within or outside of this
chapter.
```
