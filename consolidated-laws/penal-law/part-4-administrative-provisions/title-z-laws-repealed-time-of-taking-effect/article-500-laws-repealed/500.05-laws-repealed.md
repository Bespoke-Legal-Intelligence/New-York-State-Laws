# Penal Law § 500.05 — Laws repealed

[Penal Law](../../../README.md) › [Part 4 — Administrative Provisions](../../README.md) › [Title Z — Laws Repealed; Time of Taking Effect](../README.md) › [Article 500 — Laws Repealed; Time of Taking Effect](README.md)

```text
§ 500.05 Laws repealed.
  Chapter eighty-eight of the laws of nineteen hundred nine, entitled
"An act providing for the punishment of crime, constituting chapter
forty of the consolidated laws," and all acts amendatory thereof and
supplemental thereto, constituting the penal law as heretofore in force,
are hereby repealed.
```
