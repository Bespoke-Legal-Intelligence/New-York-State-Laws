# State Law § 59-J — Acquisition of land

[State Law](../README.md) › [Article 4-B — Acquisition of Land By the United Nations](README.md)

```text
  § 59-j. Acquisition of land. The United Nations may take by gift,
grant or devise, acquire by purchase, but not by condemnation, any land
necessary, useful or convenient in carrying on the functions of such
organization within the state and hold, transmit and dispose of the
same.
```
