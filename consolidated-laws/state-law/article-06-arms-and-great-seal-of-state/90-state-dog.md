# State Law § 90 — State dog

[State Law](../README.md) › [Article 6 — Arms and Great Seal of State](README.md)

```text
  § 90. State dog. The service dog shall be the official dog of the
state.  "Service dog" shall have the same meaning as set forth in
section one hundred eight of the agriculture and markets law.
```
