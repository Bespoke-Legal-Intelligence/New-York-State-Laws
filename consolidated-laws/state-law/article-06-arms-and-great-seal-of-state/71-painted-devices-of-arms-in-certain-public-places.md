# State Law § 71 — Painted devices of arms in certain public places

[State Law](../README.md) › [Article 6 — Arms and Great Seal of State](README.md)

```text
  § 71. Painted devices of arms in certain public places. The device of
arms of the state, corresponding to the blazon hereinbefore given, shall
be painted in colors upon wood or canvass, and hung upon the walls of
the executive chamber, the court of appeals, the office of the secretary
of state, and the senate and assembly chambers.
```
