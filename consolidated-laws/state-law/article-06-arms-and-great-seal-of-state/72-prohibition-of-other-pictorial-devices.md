# State Law § 72 — Prohibition of other pictorial devices

[State Law](../README.md) › [Article 6 — Arms and Great Seal of State](README.md)

```text
  § 72. Prohibition of other pictorial devices. No pictorial devices
other than the arms of the state shall be used in the public offices at
the capitol for letter headings and envelopes used for official
business. Persons printing and circulating public documents under the
authority of the state, when they use a vignette, shall place upon the
title pages of the documents the standard device of the state arms
without alterations or additions.
```
