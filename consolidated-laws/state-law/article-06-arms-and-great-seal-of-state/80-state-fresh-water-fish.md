# State Law § 80 — State fresh-water fish

[State Law](../README.md) › [Article 6 — Arms and Great Seal of State](README.md)

```text
  § 80. State fresh-water fish. The brook or speckled trout (Salvelinus
Fontinalis) shall be the official fresh-water fish of the state of New
York.
```
