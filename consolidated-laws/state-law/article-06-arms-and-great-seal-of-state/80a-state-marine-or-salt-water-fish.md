# State Law § 80-A — State marine or salt water fish

[State Law](../README.md) › [Article 6 — Arms and Great Seal of State](README.md)

```text
  § 80-a. State marine or salt water fish. The striped bass (Morone
Saxatilis) shall be the official marine or salt water fish of the state
of New York.
```
