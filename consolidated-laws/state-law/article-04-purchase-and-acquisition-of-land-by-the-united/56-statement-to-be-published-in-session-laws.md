# State Law § 56 — Statement to be published in session laws

[State Law](../README.md) › [Article 4 — Purchase and Acquisition of Land By the United States](README.md)

```text
  § 56. Statement to be published in session laws. The secretary of
state shall cause to be printed in the session laws of the year
succeeding the filing in his office of said deed, a statement of the
date of the application of the United States for said deed and a copy of
the description of the lands so conveyed or ceded, together with the
date of the recording of said deed in the office of the said secretary
of state.
```
