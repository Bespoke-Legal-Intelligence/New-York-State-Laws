# State Law § 57-A — Application of article to Adirondack park

[State Law](../README.md) › [Article 4 — Purchase and Acquisition of Land By the United States](README.md)

```text
  § 57-a. Application of article to Adirondack park. Nothing in this
article shall be construed to give the consent of the state of New York
to the purchase or acquisition by the United States of any tract, piece
or parcel of land within the Adirondack park, except under circumstances
described in subdivision two of section fifty-one of this article or
pursuant to article eleven of the general municipal law or section
thirty-four-a of the public lands law.
```
