# State Law § 126 — Application

[State Law](../../README.md) › [Article 8 — Assembly and Senate Districts of the State](../README.md) › [Title 3 — Miscellaneous](README.md)

```text
  § 126. Application. For the purpose of filling vacancies occurring
other than by the expiration of term in the offices of members of the
senate and assembly elected at the general election in two thousand
twelve, the senate and assembly districts of this state, as described
and set out in a chapter of the laws of two thousand twelve as added by
senate bill number 6696 or assembly bill number 9525, shall continue to
be respectively, the senate districts and the assembly districts of the
state.
```
