# State Law § 126 — Application

[State Law](../../README.md) › [Article 8 — Assembly and Senate Districts of the State](../README.md) › [Title 3 — Miscellaneous](README.md)

```text
  * § 126. Application. For the purpose of filling vacancies occurring
other than by the expiration of term in the offices of members of the
senate and assembly elected at a general election in two thousand
twenty-two, the senate and assembly districts of this state, as
described and set out in a chapter of the laws of two thousand
twenty-two, amending the state law relating to establishing assembly and
senate districts, shall continue to be respectively, the senate
districts and the assembly districts of the state.
  * NB Effective until January 1, 2025
  * § 126. Application; senate. For the purpose of filling vacancies
occurring other than by the expiration of term in the offices of members
of the senate elected at a general election in two thousand twenty-two,
the senate districts of this state, as described and set out in a
chapter of the laws of two thousand twenty-two, amending the state law
relating to establishing senate districts, shall continue to be
respectively, the senate districts of the state.
  * NB Effective January 1, 2025
```
