# State Law § 120 — Number of members of the assembly

[State Law](../../README.md) › [Article 8 — Assembly and Senate Districts of the State](../README.md) › [Title 1 — Assembly Districts](README.md)

```text
  § 120. Number of members of the assembly. The assembly shall consist
of one hundred fifty members chosen from the districts described within
and apportioned among the counties on the basis of the number of
inhabitants of the state based on the Federal Census of two thousand
ten, as adjusted pursuant to the provisions of part XX of chapter
fifty-seven of the laws of two thousand ten.
```
