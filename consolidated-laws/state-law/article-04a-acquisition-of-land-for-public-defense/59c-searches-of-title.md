# State Law § 59-C — Searches of title

[State Law](../README.md) › [Article 4-A — Acquisition of Land For Public Defense](README.md)

```text
  § 59-c. Searches of title. The attorney-general shall furnish to the
commission all searches necessary to prove the title to the lands taken
as provided in this article. The expense of making such searches shall
be paid from the treasury out of the funds appropriated therefor, on the
audit and warrant of the comptroller.
```
