# State Law § 9 — Saving clause

[State Law](../README.md) › [Article 2 — State Boundaries](README.md)

```text
  § 9. Saving clause. This article shall not be construed as a
relinquishment by the state of New York of any territory to which it now
has title, or over which it now has jurisdiction.
```
