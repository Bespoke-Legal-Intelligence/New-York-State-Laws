# Railroad Law § 207 — Railroads in parks in New York city, by whom constructed

[Railroad Law](../README.md) › [Article 5 — Street Surface Railroads](README.md)

```text
  § 207. Railroads in parks in New York city, by whom constructed.
Whenever any railroads shall be constructed in the city of New York,
under the provisions of the preceding six sections, such railroads shall
be constructed by the department of public parks of said city with the
concurrence of the sinking fund commissioners of said city.
```
