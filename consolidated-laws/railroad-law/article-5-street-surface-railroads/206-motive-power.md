# Railroad Law § 206 — Motive power

[Railroad Law](../README.md) › [Article 5 — Street Surface Railroads](README.md)

```text
  § 206. Motive power.  Any portion of any railroad constructed,
equipped and operated under section two hundred and one may be operated
by cable.
```
