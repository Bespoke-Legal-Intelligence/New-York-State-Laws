                               CHAPTER 481
  AN ACT in relation to railroads, constituting chapter forty-nine of
the consolidated laws.
  Became a law June 14, 1910, with the approval of the Governor.
    Passed, three-fifths being present.
  The People of the State of New York, represented in Senate and
Assembly, do enact as follows:
                   CHAPTER 49 OF THE CONSOLIDATED LAWS
                              RAILROAD LAW
Article  1. Short title (§§ 1, 2).
         2. Organization, general powers and location (§§ 5-34).
         3. Construction, operation and management (§§ 50-108).
         4. Consolidation, lease, sale and reorganization (§§ 140-162).
         5. Street surface railroads (§§ 170-210).
         6. Rapid transit act of 1875 (§§ 220-244).
         7. Railroad redevelopment corporations (§§ 300-313).
         8. Laws repealed; when to take effect (§§ 450-451).
