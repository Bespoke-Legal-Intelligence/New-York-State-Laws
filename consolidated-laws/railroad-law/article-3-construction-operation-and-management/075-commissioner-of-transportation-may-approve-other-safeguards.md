# Railroad Law § 75 — commissioner of transportation may approve other safeguards

[Railroad Law](../README.md) › [Article 3 — Construction, Operation and Management](README.md)

```text
  § 75. commissioner of transportation may approve other safeguards.
The commissioner of transportation may, on the application of any
railroad corporation, authorize it to use any other safeguard or device
approved by the commissioner of transportation, in place of any
safeguard or device hereinbefore required by this article, which shall
thereafter be used in lieu thereof, and the same penalties for neglect
or refusal to use the same shall be incurred and imposed as for a
failure to use the safeguard or device hereinbefore required, in lieu of
which the same is to be used.
```
