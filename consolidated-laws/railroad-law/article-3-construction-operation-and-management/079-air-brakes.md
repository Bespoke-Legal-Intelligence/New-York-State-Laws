# Railroad Law § 79 — Air-brakes

[Railroad Law](../README.md) › [Article 3 — Construction, Operation and Management](README.md)

```text
  § 79. Air-brakes. It shall be unlawful for any railroad or other
company to haul or permit to be hauled or used on its line or lines
within this state any freight train that has not a sufficient number of
cars in it so equipped with continuous power or air-brakes that the
engineer on the locomotive drawing such train can control its speed
without requiring brakemen to use the common hand brake for that
purpose.
```
