# Railroad Law § 20 — Railroads through Chautauqua assembly grounds

[Railroad Law](../README.md) › [Article 2 — Organization, General Powers and Location](README.md)

```text
  § 20. Railroads through Chautauqua assembly grounds. No railroad
corporation shall build, construct or operate any railroad in, upon,
over or through the grounds, lands or premises owned by the Chautauqua
assembly corporation in the town and county of Chautauqua, without the
written consent of a majority of the board of trustees of such assembly
corporation.
```
