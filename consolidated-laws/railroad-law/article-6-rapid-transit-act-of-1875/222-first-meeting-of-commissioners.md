# Railroad Law § 222 — First meeting of commissioners

[Railroad Law](../README.md) › [Article 6 — Rapid Transit Act of 1875](README.md)

```text
  § 222. First meeting of commissioners. Within fifteen days after their
appointment, the commissioners shall meet in some convenient place in
the county or city and organize themselves as a board with appropriate
officers.
```
