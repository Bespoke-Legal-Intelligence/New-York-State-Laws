# Railroad Law § 242 — Sections to be printed and posted

[Railroad Law](../README.md) › [Article 6 — Rapid Transit Act of 1875](README.md)

```text
  § 242. Sections to be printed and posted. The officers and board of
directors of such railroad corporations shall cause copies of sections
two hundred and thirty-nine, two hundred and forty and two hundred and
forty-one to be printed conspicuously and posted in the depots or
stations and in each car belonging to them.
```
