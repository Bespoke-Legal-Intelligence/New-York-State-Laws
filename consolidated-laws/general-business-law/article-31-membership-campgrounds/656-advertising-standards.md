# General Business Law § 656 — Advertising standards

[General Business Law](../README.md) › [Article 31 — Membership Campgrounds](README.md)

```text
  § 656. Advertising standards. Membership campground operators, their
employees and agents shall be subject to all relevant provisions of this
chapter, including, but not limited to, sections three hundred
forty-nine, three hundred fifty, and three hundred sixty-nine-ee in the
marketing, promotion, and advertising of membership camping contracts.
```
