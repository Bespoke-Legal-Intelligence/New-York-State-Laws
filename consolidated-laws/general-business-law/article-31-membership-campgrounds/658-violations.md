# General Business Law § 658 — Violations

[General Business Law](../README.md) › [Article 31 — Membership Campgrounds](README.md)

```text
  § 658. Violations. Any membership campground operator or his agents,
employees or assignees who violate any provision of this article shall
be liable for a civil penalty of not more than ten thousand dollars for
each such violation.
```
