# General Business Law § 659 — Contracts void and unenforceable

[General Business Law](../README.md) › [Article 31 — Membership Campgrounds](README.md)

```text
  § 659. Contracts void and unenforceable. 1. Any membership camping
contract which does not comply with the applicable provisions of this
article shall be void and unenforceable as contrary to public policy.
  2. Any waiver by the purchaser of the provisions of this article shall
be deemed void and unenforceable by the seller and contrary to public
policy.
```
