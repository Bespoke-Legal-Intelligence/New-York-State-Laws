# General Business Law § 89-HHH — Enforcement of article; investigations

[General Business Law](../README.md) › [Article 8-B — Licensing of Armored Car Carriers](README.md)

```text
  § 89-hhh. Enforcement of article; investigations.  All armored car
carriers shall be subject to the enforcement provisions contained in
article seven of this chapter, provided that such provisions shall be
read in such a manner as to apply to armored car carriers or armored car
guards, as the context requires.
```
