# General Business Law § 785 — Waiver prohibited

[General Business Law](../README.md) › [Article 37 — Deposits On Construction of New Homes](README.md)

```text
  § 785. Waiver prohibited. Any agreement entered into by a vendee which
waives, limits or disclaims the rights or obligations set forth in this
article shall be void as contrary to public policy.
```
