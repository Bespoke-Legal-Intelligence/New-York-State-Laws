# General Business Law § 274 — Assistance of policeman or constable

[General Business Law](../README.md) › [Article 17 — Milk Cans](README.md)

```text
  § 274. Assistance of policeman or constable. Any person authorized by
this article to seize and take into his possession any such cans may, in
case of resistance, call to his aid any police officer or constable of
the town, village or city who shall when so called on assist him in
seizing or taking possession of such cans.
```
