# General Business Law § 111 — Issue of non-negotiable warehouse receipt not so marked

[General Business Law](../README.md) › [Article 9 — Bills of Lading, Warehouse Receipts, Other Receipts and Vouchers](README.md)

```text
  § 111. Issue of non-negotiable warehouse receipt not so marked.  Any
person who with intent to defraud issues or aids in issuing a
non-negotiable warehouse receipt without the words "not negotiable"
placed plainly upon the face thereof, shall be guilty of a misdemeanor.
```
