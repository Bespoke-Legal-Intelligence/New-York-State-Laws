# General Business Law § 106 — Issue of warehouse receipt containing false statement

[General Business Law](../README.md) › [Article 9 — Bills of Lading, Warehouse Receipts, Other Receipts and Vouchers](README.md)

```text
  § 106. Issue of warehouse receipt containing false statement.  A
warehouseman, or any officer, agent or servant of a warehouseman, who
fraudulently issues or aids in fraudulently issuing a warehouse receipts
for goods knowing that it contains any false statement, shall be guilty
of a misdemeanor.
```
