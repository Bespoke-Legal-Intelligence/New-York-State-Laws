# General Business Law § 96 — Property demanded by process of law

[General Business Law](../README.md) › [Article 9 — Bills of Lading, Warehouse Receipts, Other Receipts and Vouchers](README.md)

```text
  § 96. Property demanded by process of law. The last two sections do
not apply to any case where property is demanded by virtue of legal
process.
```
