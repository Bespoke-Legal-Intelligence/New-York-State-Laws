# General Business Law § 104 — Issue of a non-negotiable bill of lading not so marked

[General Business Law](../README.md) › [Article 9 — Bills of Lading, Warehouse Receipts, Other Receipts and Vouchers](README.md)

```text
  § 104. Issue of a non-negotiable bill of lading not so marked.  Any
person who with intent to defraud issues or aids in issuing a
non-negotiable bill of lading without the words "not negotiable" placed
plainly upon the face thereof, shall be guilty of a misdemeanor.
```
