# General Business Law § 238 — Effect of invalidity of any part of this article

[General Business Law](../README.md) › [Article 13-A — Platinum Stamping](README.md)

```text
  § 238. Effect of invalidity of any part of this article. If any part
of this article, or the application thereof to any particular situation,
is held by any court of competent jurisdiction to be invalid on account
of unconstitutionality, such adjudication shall not affect the remainder
of this article, or the application of such first-mentioned part of this
article to any other situation.
```
