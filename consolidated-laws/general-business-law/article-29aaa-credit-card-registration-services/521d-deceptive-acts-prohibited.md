# General Business Law § 521-D — Deceptive acts prohibited

[General Business Law](../README.md) › [Article 29-AAA — Credit Card Registration Services](README.md)

```text
  § 521-d. Deceptive acts prohibited. It is hereby declared to be a
deceptive trade practice and unlawful for a credit card registration
service to misrepresent in its advertising, promotional materials, sales
materials, or in any other manner the nature of the services to be
performed, the identity of the provider of the services, or the
consequences of failing to subscribe to the services.
```
