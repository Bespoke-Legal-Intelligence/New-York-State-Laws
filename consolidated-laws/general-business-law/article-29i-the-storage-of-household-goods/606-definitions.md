# General Business Law § 606 — Definitions

[General Business Law](../README.md) › [Article 29-I — The Storage of Household Goods](README.md)

```text
  § 606. Definitions. Whenever used in this article:
  (a) Warehouseman means a person engaged in the business of storing
goods for hire.
  (b) Storage of household goods means goods stored by a consumer bailor
which are used or bought for use primarily for personal, family or
household purposes.
  (c) Consumer bailor means a natural person.
```
