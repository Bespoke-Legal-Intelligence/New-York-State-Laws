# General Business Law § 239-A — Certain appraisals; penalty

[General Business Law](../README.md) › [Article 13-B — Appraisers of Jewelry, Works of Art, Watches and Objects Made From or Containing Precious Stones or Metals](README.md)

```text
  § 239-a. Certain appraisals; penalty. Any appraiser who knowingly
gives a written appraisal of property with intent to defraud, deceive,
or injure another person, firm, partnership, corporation, or association
or any officer, director, employee, or agent thereof shall be guilty of
a misdemeanor.
```
