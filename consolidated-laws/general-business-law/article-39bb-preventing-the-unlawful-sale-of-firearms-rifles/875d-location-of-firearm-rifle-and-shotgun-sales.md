# General Business Law § 875-D — Location of firearm, rifle, and shotgun sales

[General Business Law](../README.md) › [Article 39-BB — Preventing the Unlawful Sale of Firearms, Rifles, and Shotguns to Individuals With a Criminal Record](README.md)

```text
  * § 875-d. Location of firearm, rifle, and shotgun sales. Every dealer
shall sell or otherwise dispose of firearms, rifles, and shotguns only
at the location listed on the dealer's federal firearms license or at
gun shows.
  * NB Effective December 3, 2022
```
