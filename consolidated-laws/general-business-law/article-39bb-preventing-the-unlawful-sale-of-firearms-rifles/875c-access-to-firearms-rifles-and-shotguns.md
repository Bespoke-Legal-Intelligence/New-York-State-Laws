# General Business Law § 875-C — Access to firearms, rifles, and shotguns

[General Business Law](../README.md) › [Article 39-BB — Preventing the Unlawful Sale of Firearms, Rifles, and Shotguns to Individuals With a Criminal Record](README.md)

```text
  * § 875-c. Access to firearms, rifles, and shotguns. Every retail
dealer shall exclude all persons under eighteen years of age from those
portions of its premises where firearms, rifles, shotguns, or ammunition
are stocked or sold, unless such person is accompanied by a parent or
guardian.
  * NB Effective December 3, 2022
```
