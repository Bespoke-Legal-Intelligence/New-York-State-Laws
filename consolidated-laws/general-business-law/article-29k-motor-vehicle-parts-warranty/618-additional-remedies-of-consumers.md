# General Business Law § 618 — Additional remedies of consumers

[General Business Law](../README.md) › [Article 29-K — Motor Vehicle Parts Warranty](README.md)

```text
  § 618. Additional remedies of consumers. Nothing in this article shall
in any way contravene or limit the rights, remedies or privileges which
are otherwise available to a consumer under any other law.
```
