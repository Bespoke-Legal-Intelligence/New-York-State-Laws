# General Business Law § 619 — Prohibition against waiver of rights

[General Business Law](../README.md) › [Article 29-K — Motor Vehicle Parts Warranty](README.md)

```text
  § 619. Prohibition against waiver of rights. Waiver of any rights by
the consumer under this article shall be deemed contrary to public
policy and shall be unenforceable and void.
```
