# General Business Law § 350 — False advertising unlawful

[General Business Law](../README.md) › [Article 22-A — Consumer Protection From Deceptive Acts and Practices](README.md)

```text
  § 350. False advertising unlawful. False advertising in the conduct of
any business, trade or commerce or in the furnishing of any service in
this state is hereby declared unlawful.
```
