# General Business Law § 428 — Regulations

[General Business Law](../README.md) › [Article 27-A — Licensing of Coin Processors](README.md)

```text
  § 428. Regulations. The secretary is hereby authorized and empowered
to make such rules and regulations necessary for the proper conduct of
the business authorized under this article, and not inconsistent
herewith.
```
