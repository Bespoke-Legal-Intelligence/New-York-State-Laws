# General Business Law § 1423 — Loss of equity

[General Business Law](../README.md) › [Article 44-B — Responsible Ai Safety and Education (raise) Act](README.md)

```text
  * § 1423. Loss of equity. The loss of value of equity shall not count
as damage to or loss of property for the purposes of this article.
```
