# General Business Law § 1423 — Duties and obligations

[General Business Law](../README.md) › [Article 44-B — Responsible Ai Safety and Education (raise) Act](README.md)

```text
  * § 1423. Duties and obligations. The duties and obligations imposed
by this article are cumulative with any other duties or obligations
imposed under other law and shall not be construed to relieve any party
from any duties or obligations imposed under other law and do not limit
any rights or remedies under existing law.
  * NB Effective March 19, 2026
```
