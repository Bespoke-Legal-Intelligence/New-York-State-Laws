# General Business Law § 1413 — Discrimination prohibited

[General Business Law](../README.md) › [Article 44-A — Freelance Isn't Free Act](README.md)

```text
  * § 1413. Discrimination prohibited. No hiring party, as defined in
this article, shall threaten, intimidate, discipline, harass, deny a
work opportunity to, or discriminate against a freelance worker, or take
any other action that penalizes a freelance worker for, or is reasonably
likely to deter a freelance worker from, exercising or attempting to
exercise any right guaranteed under this article, or from obtaining any
future work opportunity because the freelance worker has done so.
  * NB Effective August 28, 2024
```
