# General Business Law § 899-JJJ — Effect of communication on privileges

[General Business Law](../README.md) › [Article 39-H — Third Party Litigation Financing](README.md)

```text
  * § 899-jjj. Effect of communication on privileges. All communication
between the consumer's attorney in the legal claim and the consumer
legal funding company as it pertains to the consumer legal funding shall
fall within the scope of the attorney client privilege, including,
without limitation, the work-product doctrine.
  * NB Effective June 17, 2026
```
