# General Business Law § 899-MMM — Severability

[General Business Law](../README.md) › [Article 39-H — Third Party Litigation Financing](README.md)

```text
  * § 899-mmm. Severability. If any provision of this article is, for
any reason, declared unconstitutional or invalid, in whole or in part,
by any court of competent jurisdiction, such portion shall be deemed
severable, and such unconstitutionality or invalidity shall not affect
the validity of the remaining portions of this article, which remaining
portions shall continue in full force and effect.
  * NB Effective and Repealed June 17, 2026
```
