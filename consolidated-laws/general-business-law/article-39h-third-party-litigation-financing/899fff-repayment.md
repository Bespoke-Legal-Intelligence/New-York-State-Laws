# General Business Law § 899-FFF — Repayment

[General Business Law](../README.md) › [Article 39-H — Third Party Litigation Financing](README.md)

```text
  * § 899-fff. Repayment. The repayment of the funded amount plus
charges to be paid to the consumer litigation funding company by the
consumer shall be a predetermined amount based upon intervals of time
from the funding date through the resolution date, and shall not be
determined as a percentage of the recovery from the legal claim.
  * NB Effective June 17, 2026
```
