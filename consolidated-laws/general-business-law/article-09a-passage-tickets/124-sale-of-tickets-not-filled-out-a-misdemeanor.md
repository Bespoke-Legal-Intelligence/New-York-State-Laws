# General Business Law § 124 — Sale of tickets not filled out, a misdemeanor

[General Business Law](../README.md) › [Article 9-A — Passage Tickets](README.md)

```text
  § 124. Sale of tickets not filled out, a misdemeanor.  A person who
issues, sells or delivers to another, any ticket, instrument,
certificate, order or receipt, which is not made or filled out as
prescribed in the last section, is guilty of a misdemeanor.
```
