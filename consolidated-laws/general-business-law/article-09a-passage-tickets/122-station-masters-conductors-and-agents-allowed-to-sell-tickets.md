# General Business Law § 122 — Station masters, conductors and agents allowed to sell tickets

[General Business Law](../README.md) › [Article 9-A — Passage Tickets](README.md)

```text
  § 122. Station masters, conductors and agents allowed to sell tickets.
The provisions of this article do not prevent the station master or
other ticket agent upon any railway, from selling in his office at any
station on such railway, any passage tickets upon such railway; nor do
they prevent any conductor upon a railway from selling such tickets upon
the trains of such railway.
```
