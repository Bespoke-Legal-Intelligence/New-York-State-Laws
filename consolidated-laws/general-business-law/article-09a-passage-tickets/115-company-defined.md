# General Business Law § 115 — Company defined

[General Business Law](../README.md) › [Article 9-A — Passage Tickets](README.md)

```text
  § 115. Company defined.  The term "company," as used in this article,
includes all corporations, whether created under the laws of this state,
or of the United States, or of those of any other state or nation.
```
