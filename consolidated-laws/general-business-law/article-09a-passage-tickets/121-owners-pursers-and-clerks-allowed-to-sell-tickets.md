# General Business Law § 121 — Owners, pursers and clerks allowed to sell tickets

[General Business Law](../README.md) › [Article 9-A — Passage Tickets](README.md)

```text
  § 121. Owners, pursers and clerks allowed to sell tickets.  The
provisions of this article do not prevent the actual owners or
consignees of any vessel, from selling passage tickets thereon; nor do
they prevent the purser or clerk of any vessel from selling in his
office on board of such vessel, any passage ticket upon such vessel.
```
