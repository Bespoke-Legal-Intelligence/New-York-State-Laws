# General Business Law § 1012 — Temporary training facilities

[General Business Law](../README.md) › [Article 41 — Combative Sports](README.md)

```text
  * § 1012. Temporary training facilities. The commission in its
judgment may exempt from licensing under this article any training
facility providing contact sparring established and maintained on a
temporary basis for the purpose of preparing professionals for a
specific authorized combative sport to be conducted, held or given
within the state of New York.
  * NB Effective September 1, 2016
```
