# General Business Law § 89-R — Preemption

[General Business Law](../README.md) › [Article 7-A — Security Guard Act](README.md)

```text
  § 89-r. Preemption. The provisions of this article shall govern
notwithstanding any other law to the contrary and further, no local law
shall be enacted which shall require any fee or license for the
licensure of registration or security guards.
```
