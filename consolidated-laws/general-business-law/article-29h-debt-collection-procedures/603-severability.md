# General Business Law § 603 — Severability

[General Business Law](../README.md) › [Article 29-H — Debt Collection Procedures](README.md)

```text
  § 603. Severability. If any provision of this article or the
application thereof to any person or circumstances is held invalid the
invalidity thereof shall not affect other provisions or applications of
the article which can be given effect without the invalid provision or
application, and to this and the provisions of this article are
severable.
```
