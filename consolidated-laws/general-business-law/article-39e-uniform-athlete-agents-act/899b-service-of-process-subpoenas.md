# General Business Law § 899-B — Service of process; subpoenas

[General Business Law](../README.md) › [Article 39-E — Uniform Athlete Agents Act](README.md)

```text
  § 899-b. Service of process; subpoenas. 1. By acting as an athlete
agent in this state, a nonresident individual appoints the secretary of
state as the individual's agent for service of process in any civil
action in this state related to the individual's acting as an athlete
agent in this state.
  2. The secretary of state may issue subpoenas for any material that is
relevant to the administration of this article.
```
