# General Business Law § 899-O — Administrative penalty

[General Business Law](../README.md) › [Article 39-E — Uniform Athlete Agents Act](README.md)

```text
  § 899-o. Administrative penalty. The secretary of state may assess a
civil penalty against an athlete agent not to exceed twenty-five
thousand dollars for a violation of section eight hundred ninety-nine-l
of this article.
```
