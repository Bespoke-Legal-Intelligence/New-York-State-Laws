# General Business Law § 899-P — Uniformity of application and construction

[General Business Law](../README.md) › [Article 39-E — Uniform Athlete Agents Act](README.md)

```text
  § 899-p. Uniformity of application and construction. In applying and
construing this article in this state, consideration shall be given to
the need to promote uniformity of the law with respect to its subject
matter among states that enact it.
```
