# General Business Law § 899-G — Registration and renewal fees

[General Business Law](../README.md) › [Article 39-E — Uniform Athlete Agents Act](README.md)

```text
  § 899-g. Registration and renewal fees. An application for
registration or renewal of registration shall be accompanied by a fee in
the following amount:
  1. three hundred dollars for an initial application for registration;
or
  2. one hundred fifty dollars for an application for renewal of
registration.
```
