# General Business Law § 389-T — Labeling of solder

[General Business Law](../README.md) › [Article 25-C — Products Containing Lead](README.md)

```text
  § 389-t. Labeling of solder. 1. No person, firm, or corporation shall
sell or offer for sale any solder unless there is printed on the package
in which such solder is sold or offered for sale a conspicuous notice
stating the percentage of lead contained in such solder. Any solder
containing more than two-tenths of one percent lead shall contain a
warning label, prominently displayed, which states, "Contains lead which
may be harmful to your health. Not to be used for joints on potable
water service piping."
  2. Any violation of this section shall be punishable by a civil
penalty not to exceed five hundred dollars.
```
