# General Business Law § 69-D — Saving clause with respect to other laws

[General Business Law](../README.md) › [Article 6-B — Sale of Goods Produced With Child Labor](README.md)

```text
  § 69-d. Saving clause with respect to other laws. Nothing in this
article shall be deemed to modify, alter, repeal or amend any provision
affecting terms, requirements, conditions, hours or wages of employment,
or any definitions applicable thereto, contained in the labor law, the
education law or any other law, general, special or local.
```
