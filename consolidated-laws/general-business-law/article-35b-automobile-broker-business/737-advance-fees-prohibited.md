# General Business Law § 737 — Advance fees prohibited

[General Business Law](../README.md) › [Article 35-B — Automobile Broker Business](README.md)

```text
  § 737. Advance fees prohibited. No automobile broker business shall
solicit, receive or collect from a consumer any fee, or commission, in
advance of the performance of those services specified in the contract
as required by section seven hundred thirty-eight of this article.
```
