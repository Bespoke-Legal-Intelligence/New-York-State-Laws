# General Business Law § 628 — Private right of action

[General Business Law](../README.md) › [Article 30 — Health Club Services](README.md)

```text
  § 628. Private right of action. 1. Any buyer damaged by a violation of
this article may bring an action for recovery of damages. Judgment may
be entered in an amount not to exceed three times the actual damages
plus reasonable attorney fees.
  2. Nothing in this article shall be construed so as to nullify or
impair any right or rights which a buyer may have against a seller at
common law, by statute, or otherwise.
```
