# General Business Law § 199 — Penalties

[General Business Law](../README.md) › [Article 11-A — Motor Vehicle Manufacturers](README.md)

```text
  § 199. Penalties. Any person, firm, or corporation violating the
provisions of section one hundred ninety-six of this article shall be
liable in an action brought on behalf of the people of the state of New
York in the sum of one thousand dollars for each of such violations.
```
