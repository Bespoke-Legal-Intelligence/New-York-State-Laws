# General Business Law § 351-D — Definitions

[General Business Law](../README.md) › [Article 23 — Bucket Shops](README.md)

```text
  § 351-d. Definitions. "Bucket shop" shall mean any building, or any
room, apartment, booth, office or store therein or any other place where
any contract prohibited by this article is made or offered to be made.
```
