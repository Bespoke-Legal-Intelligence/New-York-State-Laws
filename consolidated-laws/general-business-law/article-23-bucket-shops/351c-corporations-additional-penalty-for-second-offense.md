# General Business Law § 351-C — Corporations; additional penalty for second offense

[General Business Law](../README.md) › [Article 23 — Bucket Shops](README.md)

```text
  § 351-c. Corporations; additional penalty for second offense. If a
domestic corporation shall be convicted of a second offense hereunder
the supreme court shall have jurisdiction upon an action brought by the
attorney-general, in the name of the people, for that purpose, to
dissolve such corporation; and if a foreign corporation shall be
convicted of a second offense, such court shall have jurisdiction in an
action brought in like manner to restrain such corporation from doing
business in this state.
```
