# General Business Law § 351-E — Witnesses' immunity

[General Business Law](../README.md) › [Article 23 — Bucket Shops](README.md)

```text
  § 351-e. Witnesses' immunity. In any criminal proceeding before any
court or grand jury for a violation of any of the provisions of this
article, the court or grand jury may confer immunity in accordance with
the provisions of section 50.20 or l90.40 of the criminal procedure law.
```
