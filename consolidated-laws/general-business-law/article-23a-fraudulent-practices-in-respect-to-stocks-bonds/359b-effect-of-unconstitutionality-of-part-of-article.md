# General Business Law § 359-B — Effect of unconstitutionality of part of article

[General Business Law](../README.md) › [Article 23-A — Fraudulent Practices In Respect to Stocks, Bonds and Other Securities](README.md)

```text
  § 359-b. Effect of unconstitutionality of part of article. Should any
section or provision of this article be declared unconstitutional, by
the decision of any court, such decision shall affect the section or
provision so declared unconstitutional and shall not affect any other
section or provision of the article.
```
