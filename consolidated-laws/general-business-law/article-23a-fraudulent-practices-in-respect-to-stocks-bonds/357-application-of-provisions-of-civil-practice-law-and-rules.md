# General Business Law § 357 — Application of provisions of civil practice law and rules

[General Business Law](../README.md) › [Article 23-A — Fraudulent Practices In Respect to Stocks, Bonds and Other Securities](README.md)

```text
  § 357. Application of provisions of civil practice law and rules. The
provisions of the civil practice law and rules shall apply to all
actions brought under this article except as herein otherwise provided.
```
