# General Business Law § 805 — Separability

[General Business Law](../README.md) › [Article 37-A — Registration of Hearing Aid Dispensers](README.md)

```text
  § 805. Separability. If any section or provision of this article shall
be adjudged by any court of competent jurisdiction to be invalid or
inapplicable to any person or situation, such judgment shall not affect,
impair or invalidate any other section or provision of this article or
the applicability of such section or provision to other persons or other
situations.
```
