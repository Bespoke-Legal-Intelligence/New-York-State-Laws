# General Business Law § 28 — Limitation

[General Business Law](../README.md) › [Article 3 — Auctions and Auctioneers](README.md)

```text
  § 28. Limitation.  Sections twenty-five, twenty-six and twenty-seven
hereof shall apply only to cities of the first class and do not repeal
or supersede the provisions of chapter thirty-two, title B, article
twenty-one of the administrative code of the city of New York.
```
