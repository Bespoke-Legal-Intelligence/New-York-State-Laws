# General Business Law § 593 — Injunctions

[General Business Law](../README.md) › [Article 29-F — Going Out of Business Sales](README.md)

```text
  § 593. Injunctions. Upon application by the licensing authority of the
appropriate city, town or village in which a "closing out sale," "a sale
of goods damaged by fire, smoke or water," or a "defunct business sale"
is being held, the supreme court of the county wherein a violation of
this act occurred may restrain and enjoin any act forbidden or declared
illegal by any provision of this article.
```
