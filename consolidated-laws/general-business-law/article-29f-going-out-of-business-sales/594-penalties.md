# General Business Law § 594 — Penalties

[General Business Law](../README.md) › [Article 29-F — Going Out of Business Sales](README.md)

```text
  § 594. Penalties. Any person violating any provision of this article
shall be guilty of an unclassified misdemeanor punishable by a fine of
two hundred fifty dollars or imprisonment for one year or both, and each
day upon which any sale is conducted in violation of this article shall
be a separate violation.
```
