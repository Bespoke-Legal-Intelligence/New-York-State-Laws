# General Business Law § 596 — Saving clause

[General Business Law](../README.md) › [Article 29-F — Going Out of Business Sales](README.md)

```text
  § 596. Saving clause. If any section or provision of this article
shall at any time be declared to be unconstitutional, it is the
expressed legislative intent that no other section or provision hereof
be thereby affected.
```
