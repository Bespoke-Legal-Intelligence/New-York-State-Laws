# General Business Law § 415 — Disposition of moneys

[General Business Law](../README.md) › [Article 27 — Licensing of Nail Specialty, Natural Hair Styling, Esthetics and Cosmetology](README.md)

```text
  § 415. Disposition of moneys. All moneys derived from the operation of
this article shall be deposited to the credit of the business and
licensing services account established pursuant to section
ninety-seven-y of the state finance law.
```
