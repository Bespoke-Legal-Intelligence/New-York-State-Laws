# General Business Law § 413 — Judicial review

[General Business Law](../README.md) › [Article 27 — Licensing of Nail Specialty, Natural Hair Styling, Esthetics and Cosmetology](README.md)

```text
  § 413. Judicial review. The action of the secretary in suspending,
revoking or refusing to issue or renew a license, or issuing an order
directing the cessation of unlicensed activity or imposing a fine or
reprimand may be appealed by a proceeding brought under and pursuant to
article seventy-eight of the civil practice law and rules.
```
