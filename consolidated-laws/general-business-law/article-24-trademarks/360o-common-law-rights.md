# General Business Law § 360-O — Common law rights

[General Business Law](../README.md) › [Article 24 — Trademarks](README.md)

```text
  § 360-o. Common law rights. Nothing herein shall adversely affect the
rights or the enforcement of rights in marks acquired in good faith at
any time at common law.
```
