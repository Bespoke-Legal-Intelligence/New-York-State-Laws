# General Business Law § 360-J — Fraudulent registration

[General Business Law](../README.md) › [Article 24 — Trademarks](README.md)

```text
  § 360-j. Fraudulent registration. Any person who shall for himself or
herself, or on behalf of any other person, procure the filing or
registration of any mark in the office of the secretary under the
provisions hereof, by knowingly making any false or fraudulent
representation or declaration, orally or in writing, or by any other
fraudulent means, shall be liable to pay all damages sustained in
consequence of such filing or registration, to be recovered by or on
behalf of the party injured thereby in any court of competent
jurisdiction.
```
