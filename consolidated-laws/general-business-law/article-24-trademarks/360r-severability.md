# General Business Law § 360-R — Severability

[General Business Law](../README.md) › [Article 24 — Trademarks](README.md)

```text
  § 360-r. Severability. If any provision hereof, or the application of
such provision to any person or circumstance is held invalid, the
remainder of this article shall not be affected thereby.
```
