# General Business Law § 456 — Budget planning prohibited

[General Business Law](../README.md) › [Article 28-B — Budget Planning](README.md)

```text
  § 456. Budget planning prohibited. No person or entity shall engage in
the business of budget planning as defined in section four hundred
fifty-five of this article, except as authorized in article twelve-C of
the banking law.
```
