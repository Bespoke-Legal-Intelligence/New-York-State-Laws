# General Business Law § 457 — Penalty

[General Business Law](../README.md) › [Article 28-B — Budget Planning](README.md)

```text
  § 457. Penalty. Whoever either individually or as officer, director or
employee of any person, firm, association or corporation, violates any
of the provisions of the preceding section shall be guilty of a
misdemeanor for each such violation.
```
