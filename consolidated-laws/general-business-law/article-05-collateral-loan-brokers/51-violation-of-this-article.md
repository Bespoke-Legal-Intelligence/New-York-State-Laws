# General Business Law § 51 — Violation of this article

[General Business Law](../README.md) › [Article 5 — Collateral Loan Brokers](README.md)

```text
  § 51. Violation of this article. The mayor or local licensing
authority so licensing such collateral loan broker shall have full power
and authority after a hearing to impose fines and penalties of not less
than twenty-five dollars nor more than one hundred dollars upon persons
offending against any of the foregoing provisions of this article for
each and every offense, excepting sections forty and forty-one, and may
also suspend or revoke the license of the collateral loan broker
willfully violating any of the aforesaid provisions.
```
