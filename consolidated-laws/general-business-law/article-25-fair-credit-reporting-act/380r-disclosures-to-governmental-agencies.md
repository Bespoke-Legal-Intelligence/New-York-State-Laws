# General Business Law § 380-R — Disclosures to governmental agencies

[General Business Law](../README.md) › [Article 25 — Fair Credit Reporting Act](README.md)

```text
  § 380-r. Disclosures to governmental agencies. Notwithstanding the
provisions of section three hundred eighty-b of this article, a consumer
reporting agency may furnish identifying information respecting any
consumer, limited to his name, address, former addresses, places of
employment, or former places of employment to a governmental agency.
```
