# General Business Law § 380-Q — Disclosure of medical information

[General Business Law](../README.md) › [Article 25 — Fair Credit Reporting Act](README.md)

```text
  § 380-q. Disclosure of medical information. Whenever any provision of
this article requires disclosure of medical information, or the
disclosure of a reason for adverse action which involves medical
information, such information or reason shall be disclosed only to a
physician designated by the consumer for such purpose.
```
