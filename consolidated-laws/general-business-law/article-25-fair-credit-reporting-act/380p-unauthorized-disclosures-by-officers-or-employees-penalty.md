# General Business Law § 380-P — Unauthorized disclosures by officers or employees; penalty

[General Business Law](../README.md) › [Article 25 — Fair Credit Reporting Act](README.md)

```text
  § 380-p. Unauthorized disclosures by officers or employees; penalty.
Any officer or employee of a consumer reporting agency who knowingly and
willfully provides information concerning an individual from the
agency's files to a person not authorized to receive that information
shall, upon conviction, be fined not more than five thousand dollars or
imprisoned not more than one year, or both.
```
