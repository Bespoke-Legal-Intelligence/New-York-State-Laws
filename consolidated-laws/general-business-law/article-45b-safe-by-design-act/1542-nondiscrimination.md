# General Business Law § 1542 — Nondiscrimination

[General Business Law](../README.md) › [Article 45-B — Safe By Design Act](README.md)

```text
  * § 1542. Nondiscrimination. An operator shall not withhold, degrade,
lower the quality of, or increase the price of any product, service, or
feature of a covered platform, other than as necessary for compliance
with the provisions of this article or any rules or regulations
promulgated pursuant to this article, to a user due to such operator
being required to comply with this article.
  * NB Effective January 1, 2027
```
