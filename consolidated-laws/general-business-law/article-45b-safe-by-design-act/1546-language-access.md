# General Business Law § 1546 — Language access

[General Business Law](../README.md) › [Article 45-B — Safe By Design Act](README.md)

```text
  * § 1546. Language access. 1. Instructions to parents on how to
provide parental consent and to exercise parental controls, overrides,
settings, and other permissions required by this article shall clearly
and conspicuously be made available in no fewer than the twelve most
commonly spoken languages in New York state consistent with section two
hundred two-a of the executive law and as further defined by regulations
that may be promulgated by the attorney general.
  2. The attorney general shall ensure that any public information or
guidance that it may provide concerning this article is available in the
twelve most commonly spoken languages in New York state consistent with
section two hundred two-a of the executive law.
  * NB Effective January 1, 2027
```
