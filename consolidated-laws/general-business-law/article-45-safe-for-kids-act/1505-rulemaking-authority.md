# General Business Law § 1505 — Rulemaking authority

[General Business Law](../README.md) › [Article 45 — Safe For Kids Act](README.md)

```text
  * § 1505. Rulemaking authority. The attorney general shall promulgate
such rules and regulations as are necessary to effectuate and enforce
the provisions of this article.
  * NB Effective on the 180th day after the office of the attorney
general promulgates rules and regulations necessary (see chapter 120 of
2024 § 5)
```
