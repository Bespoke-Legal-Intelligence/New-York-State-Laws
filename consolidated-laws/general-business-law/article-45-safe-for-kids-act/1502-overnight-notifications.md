# General Business Law § 1502 — Overnight notifications

[General Business Law](../README.md) › [Article 45 — Safe For Kids Act](README.md)

```text
  * § 1502. Overnight notifications. It shall be unlawful for the
covered operator of an addictive social media platform to, between the
hours of 12 AM Eastern and 6 AM Eastern, send notifications concerning
an addictive feed to a covered minor unless the operator has obtained
verifiable parental consent to send such nighttime notifications.
  * NB Effective on the 180th day after the office of the attorney
general promulgates rules and regulations necessary (see chapter 120 of
2024 § 5)
```
