# General Business Law § 598 — Penalty for violation

[General Business Law](../README.md) › [Article 29-G — Receipts For Personal Property](README.md)

```text
  § 598. Penalty for violation. A wilfull refusal to issue such receipt
when requested by said customer, shall constitute an offense punishable
by a fine not to exceed twenty-five dollars.
```
