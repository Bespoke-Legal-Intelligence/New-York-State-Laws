# General Business Law § 89-II — Preservation of private remedies

[General Business Law](../README.md) › [Article 8-A — Process Servers and Process Serving Agencies In Cities Having a Population of One Million or More](README.md)

```text
  § 89-ii. Preservation of private remedies. Nothing in this article
shall be construed to nullify or impair any right or rights which an
individual may have against a process server or process serving agency
at common law, by statute or otherwise.
```
