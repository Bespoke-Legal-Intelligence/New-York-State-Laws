# General Business Law § 89-KK — Severability

[General Business Law](../README.md) › [Article 8-A — Process Servers and Process Serving Agencies In Cities Having a Population of One Million or More](README.md)

```text
  § 89-kk. Severability. If any provision of this article or if any
application thereof to any person or circumstance is held invalid, the
remainder of this article and the application of the provision to other
persons and circumstances shall not be affected thereby.
```
