# General Business Law § 861 — Penalty

[General Business Law](../README.md) › [Article 39-A — Merchants of Torah Scrolls](README.md)

```text
  § 861. Penalty. Any merchant who fails to comply with the requirements
of section eight hundred fifty-seven or eight hundred sixty or who
knowingly provides false or misleading information otherwise required by
this article shall be guilty of a violation punishable by a fine not to
exceed two hundred fifty dollars.
```
