# General Business Law § 864 — Severability

[General Business Law](../README.md) › [Article 39-A — Merchants of Torah Scrolls](README.md)

```text
  § 864. Severability. If any sentence, paragraph, section or part of
this article shall be adjudged invalid by a court of competent
jurisdiction such judgment shall not impair or invalidate the remainder
thereof but shall be confined to that part.
```
