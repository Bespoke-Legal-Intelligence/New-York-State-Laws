# General Business Law § 860 — Records

[General Business Law](../README.md) › [Article 39-A — Merchants of Torah Scrolls](README.md)

```text
  § 860. Records. Each merchant subject to the requirements of this
article shall maintain, in chronological order, a copy of all disclosure
statements issued by him for a period of ten years.
```
