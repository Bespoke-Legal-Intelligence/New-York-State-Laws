# General Business Law § 69-U — Judicial review

[General Business Law](../README.md) › [Article 6-D — Business of Installing Security or Fire Alarm Systems](README.md)

```text
  § 69-u. Judicial review.  The action of the secretary of state in
suspending, revoking or refusing to issue or renew a license, or
imposing a fine or reprimand on the holder thereof may be reviewed by a
proceeding brought under and pursuant to article seventy-eight of the
civil practice law and rules.
```
