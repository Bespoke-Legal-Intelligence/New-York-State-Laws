# General Business Law § 369-D — Effect of partial invalidity

[General Business Law](../README.md) › [Article 24-A — Fair Trade Law](README.md)

```text
  § 369-d. Effect of partial invalidity. If any provision of this
article is declared unconstitutional it is the intent of the legislature
that the remaining portions thereof shall not be affected but that such
remaining portions remain in full force and effect.
```
