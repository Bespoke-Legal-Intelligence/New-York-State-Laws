# General Business Law § 369-C — Definitions

[General Business Law](../README.md) › [Article 24-A — Fair Trade Law](README.md)

```text
  § 369-c. Definitions. The following terms, as used in this article,
are hereby defined as follows: "Producer" means grower, baker, maker,
manufacturer or publisher. "Commodity" means any subject of commerce.
```
