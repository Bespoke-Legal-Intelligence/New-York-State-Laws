# General Business Law § 369-A — Price-fixing prohibited

[General Business Law](../README.md) › [Article 24-A — Fair Trade Law](README.md)

```text
  § 369-a. Price-fixing prohibited. Any contract provision that purports
to restrain a vendee of a commodity from reselling such commodity at
less than the price stipulated by the vendor or producer shall not be
enforceable or actionable at law.
```
