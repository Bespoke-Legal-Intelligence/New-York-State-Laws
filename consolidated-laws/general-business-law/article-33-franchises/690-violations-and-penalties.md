# General Business Law § 690 — Violations and penalties

[General Business Law](../README.md) › [Article 33 — Franchises](README.md)

```text
  § 690. Violations and penalties. Any person who knowingly violates any
of the provisions of this article or any rule or regulation issued
thereunder shall be guilty of a class A misdemeanor punishable by a fine
of not more than one thousand dollars, or imprisonment for not more than
one year, or both.
```
