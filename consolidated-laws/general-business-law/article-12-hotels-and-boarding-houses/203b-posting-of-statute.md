# General Business Law § 203-B — Posting of statute

[General Business Law](../README.md) › [Article 12 — Hotels and Boarding Houses](README.md)

```text
  § 203-b. Posting of statute. Every keeper of a hotel or motel or inn
shall post in a public and conspicuous place and manner in the
registration office and in the public rooms of such hotel or motel or
inn a printed copy of this section and section two hundred three-a.
```
