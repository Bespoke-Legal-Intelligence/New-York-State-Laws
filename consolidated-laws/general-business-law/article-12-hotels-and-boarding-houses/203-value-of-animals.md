# General Business Law § 203 — Value of animals

[General Business Law](../README.md) › [Article 12 — Hotels and Boarding Houses](README.md)

```text
  § 203. Value of animals. No animal belonging to a guest and destroyed
by fire while on the premises of any inn keeper shall be deemed of
greater value than three hundred dollars, unless an agreement shall be
proved between such guest and inn keeper that a higher estimate shall be
made of the same.
```
