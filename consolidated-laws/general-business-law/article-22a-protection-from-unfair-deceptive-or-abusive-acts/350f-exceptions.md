# General Business Law § 350-F — Exceptions

[General Business Law](../README.md) › [Article 22-A — Protection From Unfair, Deceptive, or Abusive Acts and Practices](README.md)

```text
  § 350-f. Exceptions. Nothing in this article shall apply to any
television or sound radio broadcasting station or to any publisher or
printer of a newspaper, magazine, or other form of printed advertising,
who broadcasts, publishes, or prints such advertisement.
```
