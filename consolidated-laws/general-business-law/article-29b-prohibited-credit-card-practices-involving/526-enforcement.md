# General Business Law § 526 — Enforcement

[General Business Law](../README.md) › [Article 29-B — Prohibited Credit Card Practices Involving Providers of Travel Services](README.md)

```text
  § 526. Enforcement. The attorney general may bring an action in the
name of the people of the state to restrain or prevent any violation of
this article or any continuance of any such violation.
```
