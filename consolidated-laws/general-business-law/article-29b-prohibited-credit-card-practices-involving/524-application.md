# General Business Law § 524 — Application

[General Business Law](../README.md) › [Article 29-B — Prohibited Credit Card Practices Involving Providers of Travel Services](README.md)

```text
  § 524. Application. This article shall apply with respect to any
transaction involving the furnishing of travel services to a consumer
where a credit card is used to obtain credit and where the provider of
travel services is a person other than the credit card issuer.
```
