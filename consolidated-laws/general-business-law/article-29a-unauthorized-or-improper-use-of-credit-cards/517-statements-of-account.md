# General Business Law § 517 — Statements of account

[General Business Law](../README.md) › [Article 29-A — Unauthorized or Improper Use of Credit Cards and Debit Cards](README.md)

```text
  § 517. Statements of account. No agreement between the issuer and the
holder shall contain any provision that a statement sent by the issuer
to the holder shall be deemed correct unless objected to within a
specified period of time. Any such provision is against public policy
and shall be of no force or effect.
```
