# General Business Law § 443 — Judicial review

[General Business Law](../README.md) › [Article 28 — Practice of Barbering](README.md)

```text
  § 443. Judicial review. The action of the secretary of state in
suspending, revoking or refusing to issue or renew a license or
certificate or imposing any fine or reprimand upon the holder thereof
may be reviewed by a proceeding brought under and pursuant to article
seventy-eight of the civil practice law and rules.
```
