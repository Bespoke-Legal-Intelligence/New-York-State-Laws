# General Business Law § 445 — Disposition of moneys derived from operation of article

[General Business Law](../README.md) › [Article 28 — Practice of Barbering](README.md)

```text
  § 445. Disposition of moneys derived from operation of article. All
moneys derived from the operation of this article shall be deposited to
the credit of the business and licensing services account established
pursuant to section ninety-seven-y of the state finance law.
```
