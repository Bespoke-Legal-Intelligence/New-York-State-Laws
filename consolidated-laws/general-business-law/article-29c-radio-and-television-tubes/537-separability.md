# General Business Law § 537 — Separability

[General Business Law](../README.md) › [Article 29-C — Radio and Television Tubes](README.md)

```text
  § 537. Separability. If any section of this article or any part
thereof shall be adjudged by any court of competent jurisdiction to be
invalid, such judgment shall not affect, impair or invalidate the
remainder or any other section or part thereof.
```
