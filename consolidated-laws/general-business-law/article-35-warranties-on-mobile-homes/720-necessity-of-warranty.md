# General Business Law § 720 — Necessity of warranty

[General Business Law](../README.md) › [Article 35 — Warranties On Mobile Homes](README.md)

```text
  § 720. Necessity of warranty. After the effective date of this
article, all new mobile homes sold by a mobile home dealer situated in
the state of New York shall be covered by the warranty set forth in this
article.
```
