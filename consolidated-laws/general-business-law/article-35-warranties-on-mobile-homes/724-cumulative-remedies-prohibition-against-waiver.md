# General Business Law § 724 — Cumulative remedies; prohibition against waiver

[General Business Law](../README.md) › [Article 35 — Warranties On Mobile Homes](README.md)

```text
  § 724. Cumulative remedies; prohibition against waiver. The warranty
under this article shall be in addition to and not in derogation of all
other rights and privileges which such consumer may have under any other
law or instrument. Waiver of any rights by the buyer under this chapter
shall be deemed contrary to public policy and shall be unenforceable and
void.
```
