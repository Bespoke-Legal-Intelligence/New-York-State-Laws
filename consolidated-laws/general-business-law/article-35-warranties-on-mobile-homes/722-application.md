# General Business Law § 722 — Application

[General Business Law](../README.md) › [Article 35 — Warranties On Mobile Homes](README.md)

```text
  § 722. Application. The warranty provided for in this article shall
apply to the manufacturer of the mobile home as well as to the dealer
who sells the mobile home to the buyer.
```
