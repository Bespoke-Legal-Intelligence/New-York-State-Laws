# General Business Law § 750-UU — Small pet cemetery exclusion

[General Business Law](../README.md) › [Article 35-C — Operation of Pet Cemeteries and Pet Crematoriums](README.md)

```text
  § 750-uu. Small pet cemetery exclusion. Any pet cemetery which buries
less than five animals a year, makes no representation that the pet
graves will be cared for or that the land is dedicated, and does not
charge a maintenance fee for the care of the pet graves shall be exempt
from the provisions of sections seven hundred fifty-n, seven hundred
fifty-p and seven hundred fifty-r of this article.
```
