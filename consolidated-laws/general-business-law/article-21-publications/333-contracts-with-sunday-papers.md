# General Business Law § 333 — Contracts with Sunday papers

[General Business Law](../README.md) › [Article 21 — Publications](README.md)

```text
  § 333. Contracts with Sunday papers. All contracts or agreements of
any nature made with the publishers or proprietors of any paper dated,
published or issued on the first day of the week shall be as valid,
legal and binding, as contracts made with newspapers dated or published
on any other day of the week.
```
