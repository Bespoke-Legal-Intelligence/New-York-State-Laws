# General Business Law § 334 — Misrepresentation of circulation of newspapers or periodicals

[General Business Law](../README.md) › [Article 21 — Publications](README.md)

```text
  § 334. Misrepresentation of circulation of newspapers or periodicals.
Every proprietor or publisher of any newspaper or periodicals who shall
wilfully or knowingly misrepresent the circulation of such newspaper or
periodical for the purpose of securing advertising or other patronage
shall be deemed guilty of a misdemanor.
```
