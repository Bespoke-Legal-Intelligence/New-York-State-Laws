# General Business Law § 501 — Prohibitions

[General Business Law](../README.md) › [Article 29 — Manufacture, Sale and Introduction or Movement In Commerce of Flammable Wearing Apparel, Fabrics, Related Material and Interior Furnishings Prohibited](README.md)

```text
  § 501. Prohibitions. The manufacture and/or sale, or offering for
sale, or the introduction, delivery for introduction, transportation or
causing to be transported in commerce or for the purpose of sale or
delivery after sale in commerce, of any product, fabric or related
material which under the provisions of section five hundred two of this
article is so highly flammable as to be dangerous when worn or used by
individuals, shall be unlawful.
```
