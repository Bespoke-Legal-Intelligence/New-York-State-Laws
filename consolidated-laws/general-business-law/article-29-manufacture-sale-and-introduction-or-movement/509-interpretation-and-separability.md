# General Business Law § 509 — Interpretation and separability

[General Business Law](../README.md) › [Article 29 — Manufacture, Sale and Introduction or Movement In Commerce of Flammable Wearing Apparel, Fabrics, Related Material and Interior Furnishings Prohibited](README.md)

```text
  § 509. Interpretation and separability. The provisions of this article
shall be held to be in addition to, and not in substitution for or
limitation of, the provisions of any other law of this state. If any
provision of this article or the application thereof to any person or
circumstances is held invalid the remainder of the article and the
application of such provision to any other person or circumstance shall
not be affected thereby.
```
