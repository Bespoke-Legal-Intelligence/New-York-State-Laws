# General Business Law § 507 — Violations

[General Business Law](../README.md) › [Article 29 — Manufacture, Sale and Introduction or Movement In Commerce of Flammable Wearing Apparel, Fabrics, Related Material and Interior Furnishings Prohibited](README.md)

```text
  § 507. Violations. A violation of any provision of this article or any
rule or regulation of the commissioner promulgated hereunder is a
misdemeanor. Each product, fabric or related material made, sold or
exposed for sale, shall constitute a separate violation.
```
