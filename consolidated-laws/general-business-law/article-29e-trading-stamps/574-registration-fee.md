# General Business Law § 574 — Registration fee

[General Business Law](../README.md) › [Article 29-E — Trading Stamps](README.md)

```text
  § 574. Registration fee. The trading stamp company shall pay an annual
registration fee of one hundred dollars to the secretary of state at the
time of filing each registration statement.
```
