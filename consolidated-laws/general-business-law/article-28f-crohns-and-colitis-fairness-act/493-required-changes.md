# General Business Law § 493 — Required changes

[General Business Law](../README.md) › [Article 28-F — Crohn's and Colitis Fairness Act](README.md)

```text
  § 493. Required changes. Nothing in section four hundred ninety-two of
this article shall be construed as requiring a place of business open to
the general public for the sale of goods or services that has a toilet
facility for its employees to make any physical changes to an employee
toilet facility.
```
