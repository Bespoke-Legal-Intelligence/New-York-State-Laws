# General Business Law § 460-J — Other remedies

[General Business Law](../README.md) › [Article 28-C — Immigrant Assistance Services](README.md)

```text
  § 460-j. Other remedies. The civil and criminal remedies set forth in
this article shall not preclude any individual or entity or government
authority from seeking relief under any other statutory or common law
right to relief.
```
