# General Business Law § 279-I — Saving clause

[General Business Law](../README.md) › [Article 17-A — Filing of Names, Marks and Devices Used On Certain Vessels, Receptacles and Utensils](README.md)

```text
  § 279-i. Saving clause. No suit, proceeding or appeal pending on the
effective date of this article shall abate or be affected in any way by
reason of the enactment of this article.
```
