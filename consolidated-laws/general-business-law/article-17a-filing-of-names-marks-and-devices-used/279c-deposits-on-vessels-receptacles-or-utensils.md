# General Business Law § 279-C — Deposits on vessels, receptacles or utensils

[General Business Law](../README.md) › [Article 17-A — Filing of Names, Marks and Devices Used On Certain Vessels, Receptacles and Utensils](README.md)

```text
  § 279-c. Deposits on vessels, receptacles or utensils. The requiring,
taking, paying or accepting of any deposit for any purpose upon any
vessel, receptacle or utensil shall not be deemed to constitute a sale
of such property, either optional or otherwise, for the purposes of this
article.
```
