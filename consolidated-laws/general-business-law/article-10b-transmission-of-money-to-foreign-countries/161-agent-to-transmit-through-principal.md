# General Business Law § 161 — Agent to transmit through principal

[General Business Law](../README.md) › [Article 10-B — Transmission of Money to Foreign Countries](README.md)

```text
  § 161. Agent to transmit through principal. No such agent so
authorized shall transmit any money received for transmission by him
through any person, firm or corporation other than through the principal
from whom such agent has duly received authority.
```
