# General Business Law § 89-A — Disposition of fees and other revenue

[General Business Law](../README.md) › [Article 7 — Private Investigators, Bail Enforcement Agents and Watch, Guard and Patrol Agencies](README.md)

```text
  § 89-a. Disposition of fees and other revenue. All fees and other
moneys derived from the operation of this article shall on the fifth day
of each month be paid by the department of state into the state
treasury.
```
