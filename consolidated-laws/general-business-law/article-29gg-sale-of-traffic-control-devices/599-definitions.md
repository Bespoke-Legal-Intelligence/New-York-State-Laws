# General Business Law § 599 — Definitions

[General Business Law](../README.md) › [Article 29-GG — Sale of Traffic Control Devices](README.md)

```text
  § 599. Definitions. Traffic control devices mean all signs, signals,
markings and devices not inconsistent with the vehicle and traffic law
placed or erected by authority of a public body or official having
jurisdiction for the purpose of regulating, warning or guiding traffic.
```
