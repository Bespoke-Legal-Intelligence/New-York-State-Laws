# General Business Law § 599-C — Penalties

[General Business Law](../README.md) › [Article 29-GG — Sale of Traffic Control Devices](README.md)

```text
  § 599-c. Penalties. A violation of the provisions of section five
hundred ninety-nine-a of this chapter shall be punishable by a fine of
not less than one hundred dollars and not to exceed one thousand
dollars.
```
