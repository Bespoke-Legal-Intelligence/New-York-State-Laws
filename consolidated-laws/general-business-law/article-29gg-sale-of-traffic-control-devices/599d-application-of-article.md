# General Business Law § 599-D — Application of article

[General Business Law](../README.md) › [Article 29-GG — Sale of Traffic Control Devices](README.md)

```text
  § 599-d. Application of article. This article shall not apply to the
sale, lease or offer for sale or hire to a city having a population in
excess of one million.
```
