# General Business Law § 199-E — Waiver provisions

[General Business Law](../README.md) › [Article 11-B — Franchises For the Sale of Motor Fuels](README.md)

```text
  § 199-e. Waiver provisions. Any provision of any franchise agreement
purporting to waive any provision of this article shall be null and
void.
```
