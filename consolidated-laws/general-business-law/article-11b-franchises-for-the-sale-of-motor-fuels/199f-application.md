# General Business Law § 199-F — Application

[General Business Law](../README.md) › [Article 11-B — Franchises For the Sale of Motor Fuels](README.md)

```text
  § 199-f. Application. This act shall not apply to a franchise granted
prior to the effective date of this article provided that a renewal or
extension of such a franchise shall not be excluded from the application
of this article.
```
