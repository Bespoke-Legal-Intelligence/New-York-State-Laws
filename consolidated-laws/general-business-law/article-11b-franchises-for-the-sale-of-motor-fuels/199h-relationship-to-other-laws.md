# General Business Law § 199-H — Relationship to other laws

[General Business Law](../README.md) › [Article 11-B — Franchises For the Sale of Motor Fuels](README.md)

```text
  § 199-h. Relationship to other laws. This article shall not be held to
invalidate any provisions of the laws of this state unless there is a
direct conflict between the provisions of this article and the
provisions of such state law, in which case this article shall prevail.
```
