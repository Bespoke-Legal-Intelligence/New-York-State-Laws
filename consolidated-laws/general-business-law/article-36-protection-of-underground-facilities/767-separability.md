# General Business Law § 767 — Separability

[General Business Law](../README.md) › [Article 36 — Protection of Underground Facilities](README.md)

```text
  § 767. Separability. If any provision of this article or the
application thereof to any person or circumstances is held invalid, the
remainder of the article and the application of such provision to other
persons or circumstance shall not be affected thereby.
```
