# General Business Law § 389-Q — Penalty

[General Business Law](../README.md) › [Article 25-B — Use of Safety Glazing Materials](README.md)

```text
  § 389-q. Penalty. Violation of or noncompliance with the provisions of
this article shall be a misdemeanor punishable by a fine of not less
than five hundred dollars nor more than ten thousand dollars or by
imprisonment for one year or by both.
```
