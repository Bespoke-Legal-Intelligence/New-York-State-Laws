# General Business Law § 1402 — Assisted reproduction service providers regulated under this article

[General Business Law](../README.md) › [Article 44 — Regulation of Surrogacy Programs and Assisted Reproduction Service Providers](README.md)

```text
  § 1402. Assisted reproduction service providers regulated under this
article. The provisions of this article apply to agents, gamete banks,
fertility clinics, and other entities if:
  1. The agent, gamete bank, fertility clinic, or other entity does
business in this state; or
  2. Any health care services performed, provided or otherwise arranged
by the entity are performed in this state.
```
