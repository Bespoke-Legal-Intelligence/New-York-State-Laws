# General Business Law § 730 — Definitions

[General Business Law](../README.md) › [Article 35-A — Aftermarket Rustproofing Warranties of New Motor Vehicles](README.md)

```text
  § 730. Definitions. Whenever used in this article: 1. "Aftermarket
rustproofing" means the application of rust-inhibiting material to
various areas of a new motor vehicle after the vehicle has been
assembled.
  2. "Buyer" means a natural person who buys or receives aftermarket
rustproofing on a new motor vehicle if such vehicle is bought for use
primarily for personal, family or household purposes.
  3. "Person" means an individual, partnership, corporation, association
or any other legal entity.
  4. "Warrantor" means any person who issues a written warranty pursuant
to section seven hundred thirty-one of this article.
```
