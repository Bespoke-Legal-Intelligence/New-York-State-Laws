# General Business Law § 731 — Written warranty required

[General Business Law](../README.md) › [Article 35-A — Aftermarket Rustproofing Warranties of New Motor Vehicles](README.md)

```text
  § 731. Written warranty required. A person who applies aftermarket
rustproofing to motor vehicles sold in this state shall provide the
buyer with a written warranty which complies with the requirements of
this article.
```
