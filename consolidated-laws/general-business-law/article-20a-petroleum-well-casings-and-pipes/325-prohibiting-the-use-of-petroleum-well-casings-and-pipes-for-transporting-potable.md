# General Business Law § 325 — Prohibiting the use of petroleum well casings and pipes for transporting potable water

[General Business Law](../README.md) › [Article 20-A — Petroleum Well Casings and Pipes](README.md)

```text
  § 325. Prohibiting the use of petroleum well casings and pipes for
transporting potable water. No person shall construct a water well which
is to be used to convey or transport a potable water supply using
petroleum well casings and pipes.
```
