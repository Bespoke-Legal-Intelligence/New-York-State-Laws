# General Business Law § 326 — Private right of action

[General Business Law](../README.md) › [Article 20-A — Petroleum Well Casings and Pipes](README.md)

```text
  § 326. Private right of action. A consumer injured by a violation of
this section may bring an action to recover damages. Judgment may be
entered for three times the actual damages suffered by a consumer or
five hundred dollars, whichever is greater. A court may also award
attorneys' fees to a prevailing plaintiff consumer.
```
