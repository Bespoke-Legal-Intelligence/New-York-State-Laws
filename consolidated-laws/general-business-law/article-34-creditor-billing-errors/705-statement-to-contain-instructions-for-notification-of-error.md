# General Business Law § 705 — Statement to contain instructions for notification of error

[General Business Law](../README.md) › [Article 34 — Creditor Billing Errors](README.md)

```text
  § 705. Statement to contain instructions for notification of error.
Every statement of account rendered by a creditor to a consumer shall
include the address to be used by the consumer in making inquiries
concerning his statement.
```
