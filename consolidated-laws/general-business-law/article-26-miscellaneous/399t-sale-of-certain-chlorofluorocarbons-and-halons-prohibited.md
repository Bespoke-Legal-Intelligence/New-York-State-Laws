# General Business Law § 399-T — Sale of certain chlorofluorocarbons and halons prohibited

[General Business Law](../README.md) › [Article 26 — Miscellaneous](README.md)

```text
  * § 399-t. Sale of certain chlorofluorocarbons and halons prohibited.
1.  On and after January first, nineteen hundred ninety-one, no
containers of chlorofluorocarbon compounds commonly used as air horn
propellants, for blow cleaning photographic products, or any similar
use, or containers devised as toys or for amusement which use
chlorofluorocarbon compounds as a foaming agent shall be sold within the
state.
  2. On and after January first, nineteen hundred ninety-one, no hand
held fire extinguisher containing five pounds or less of halon compounds
as defined in section 38-0103 of the environmental conservation law
shall be sold within the state.
  3. A violation of this section shall be punishable by a civil penalty
not to exceed one hundred dollars.
  * NB There are 2 § 399-t's
```
