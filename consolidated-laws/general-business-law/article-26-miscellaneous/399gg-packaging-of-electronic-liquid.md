# General Business Law § 399-GG — Packaging of electronic liquid

[General Business Law](../README.md) › [Article 26 — Miscellaneous](README.md)

```text
  § 399-gg. Packaging of electronic liquid. 1. No person, firm or
corporation shall sell or offer for sale any electronic liquid, as
defined in paragraph (e) of subdivision one of section thirteen hundred
ninety-nine-cc of the public health law, unless the electronic liquid is
sold or offered for sale in a child resistant bottle which is designed
to prevent accidental exposure of children to electronic liquids.
  2. Any violation of this section shall be punishable by a civil
penalty not to exceed one thousand dollars.
```
