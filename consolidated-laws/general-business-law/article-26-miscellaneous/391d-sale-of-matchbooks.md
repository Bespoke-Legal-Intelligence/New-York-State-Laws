# General Business Law § 391-D — Sale of matchbooks

[General Business Law](../README.md) › [Article 26 — Miscellaneous](README.md)

```text
  § 391-d. Sale of matchbooks. All one piece matchbooks sold or
distributed within the state must have the striking area on the side
other than the side which the matchbook opens. Any person in the
ordinary course of their business who violates the provisions of this
act shall be subject to a fine of not more than two hundred and fifty
dollars.
```
