# General Business Law § 391-P — Prohibit the rental of clothing articles previously worn

[General Business Law](../README.md) › [Article 26 — Miscellaneous](README.md)

```text
  § 391-p. Prohibit the rental of clothing articles previously worn. 1.
No person shall rent articles of clothing which have been previously
worn unless such articles of clothing are cleaned prior to such rental.
  2. A knowing violation of this section shall be punishable by a civil
penalty not to exceed two hundred fifty dollars for the first violation
and not to exceed one thousand dollars for each subsequent violation.
```
