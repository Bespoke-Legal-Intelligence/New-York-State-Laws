# Municipal Housing Authorities Law § 60 — Short title

[Municipal Housing Authorities Law](../README.md) › [Article 5 — Of the Former State Housing Law (cited Herein As the "municipal Housing Authorities Law"), As Such Article Existed Immediately Prior to Its Repeal Pursuant to Section 227 of Chapter 808 of the Laws of 1939, Is Provided Here For Ease of Reference and Historical Purposes As Such Text Continues to Be Applicable For the New York City Housing Authority Pursuant to the Provisions of Section 401 of the Current Public Housing Law](README.md)

```text
  * § 60. Short title. This article shall be known and may be cited and
referred to as the "municipal housing authorities law."
  * NB The text of Article 5 of the former State Housing Law (cited
herein as the "Municipal Housing Authorities Law"), as such article
existed immediately prior to its repeal pursuant to section 227 of
Chapter 808 of the Laws of 1939, is provided here for ease of reference
and historical purposes as such text continues to be applicable for the
New York City Housing Authority pursuant to the provisions of section
401 of the current Public Housing Law.
```
