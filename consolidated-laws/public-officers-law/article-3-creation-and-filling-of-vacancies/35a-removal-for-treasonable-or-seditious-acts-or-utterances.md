# Public Officers Law § 35-A — Removal for treasonable or seditious acts or utterances

[Public Officers Law](../README.md) › [Article 3 — Creation and Filling of Vacancies](README.md)

```text
  § 35-a. Removal for treasonable or seditious acts or utterances.  A
person holding any public office shall be removable therefrom, in the
manner provided by law, for the utterance of any treasonable or
seditious word or words or the doing of any treasonable or seditious act
or acts during his term.
```
