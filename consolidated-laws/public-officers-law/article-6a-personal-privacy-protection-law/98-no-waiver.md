# Public Officers Law § 98 — No waiver

[Public Officers Law](../README.md) › [Article 6-A — Personal Privacy Protection Law](README.md)

```text
  § 98. No waiver. Any agreement purporting to waive a data subject's
rights under this article is hereby declared to be void as against
public policy.
```
