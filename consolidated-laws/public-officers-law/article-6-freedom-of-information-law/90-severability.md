# Public Officers Law § 90 — Severability

[Public Officers Law](../README.md) › [Article 6 — Freedom of Information Law](README.md)

```text
  § 90. Severability. If any provision of this article or the
application thereof to any person or circumstances is adjudged invalid
by a court of competent jurisdiction, such judgment shall not affect or
impair the validity of the other provisions of the article or the
application thereof to other persons and circumstances.
```
