# Public Officers Law § 79 — Fine in certain cases

[Public Officers Law](../README.md) › [Article 4 — Powers and Duties of Public Officers](README.md)

```text
  § 79. Fine in certain cases. Where an officer or a member of a board
or other body has without just cause refused or neglected to perform a
public duty enjoined upon him by a special provision of law, a court may
impose a fine, not exceeding two hundred fifty dollars, upon the officer
or member who has so refused or neglected, to be paid into the treasury
of the state.
```
