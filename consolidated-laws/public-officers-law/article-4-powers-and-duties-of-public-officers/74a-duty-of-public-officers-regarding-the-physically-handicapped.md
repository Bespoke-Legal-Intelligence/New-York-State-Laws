# Public Officers Law § 74-A — Duty of public officers regarding the physically handicapped

[Public Officers Law](../README.md) › [Article 4 — Powers and Duties of Public Officers](README.md)

```text
  § 74-a. Duty of public officers regarding the physically handicapped.
It shall be the duty of each public officer responsible for the
scheduling or siting of any public hearing to make reasonable efforts to
ensure that such hearings are held in facilities that permit
barrier-free physical access to the physically handicapped, as defined
in subdivision five of section fifty of the public buildings law.
```
