# Public Officers Law § 68-A — Fees for oath or acknowledgment

[Public Officers Law](../README.md) › [Article 4 — Powers and Duties of Public Officers](README.md)

```text
  § 68-a. Fees for oath or acknowledgment. Any officer, authorized to
perform the services specified in this section, and to receive fees
therefor, is entitled to the following fees:
  1. For administering an oath or affirmation, and certifying the same
when required, except where another fee is specially prescribed by
statute, two dollars.
  2. For taking and certifying the acknowledgment or proof of the
execution of a written instrument; by one person, two dollars; and by
each additional person, two dollars; for swearing each witness thereto,
two dollars.
```
