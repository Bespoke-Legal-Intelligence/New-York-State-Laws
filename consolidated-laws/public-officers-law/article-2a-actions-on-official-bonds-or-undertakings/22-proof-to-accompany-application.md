# Public Officers Law § 22 — Proof to accompany application

[Public Officers Law](../README.md) › [Article 2-A — Actions On Official Bonds or Undertakings](README.md)

```text
  § 22. Proof to accompany application.  The application must be
accompanied with
  1. A certified copy of the official bond or undertaking;
  2. Proof by affidavit of the default or misconduct complained of, and
that satisfaction of the same has not been received.
  3. If the default consists of the non-payment of money, and the
applicant has not recovered judgment against the officer, or special
provision is not otherwise made by law, proof of a demand for the money
from the officer, or that a demand cannot be made with due diligence.
```
