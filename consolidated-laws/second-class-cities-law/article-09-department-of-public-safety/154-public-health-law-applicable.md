# Second Class Cities Law § 154 — Public health law applicable

[Second Class Cities Law](../README.md) › [Article 9 — Department of Public Safety](README.md)

```text
  § 154. Public health law applicable. The public health law, so far as
it pertains to cities, shall be applicable to cities of the second
class, except as herein expressly modified.
```
