# Second Class Cities Law § 139 — Exemptions

[Second Class Cities Law](../README.md) › [Article 9 — Department of Public Safety](README.md)

```text
  § 139. Exemptions. No member of the police or fire department shall be
liable to military or jury duty or to arrest on criminal or civil
process while on duty.
```
