# Second Class Cities Law § 140 — Pension funds

[Second Class Cities Law](../README.md) › [Article 9 — Department of Public Safety](README.md)

```text
  § 140. Pension funds. The provisions of law governing the
establishment and maintenance of pension funds for the benefit of
members of the police and fire departments shall be unimpaired by this
chapter. The mayor, comptroller and commissioner of public safety and
their successors in office shall constitute the trustees of each such
fund and shall have the care, control, management and distribution
thereof.
```
