# Second Class Cities Law § 155 — Subordinates

[Second Class Cities Law](../README.md) › [Article 9 — Department of Public Safety](README.md)

```text
  § 155. Subordinates. The superintendent of buildings may appoint such
subordinates as may be prescribed by the board of estimate and
apportionment to hold office during his pleasure, and who shall receive
such compensation as shall be fixed by said board.
```
