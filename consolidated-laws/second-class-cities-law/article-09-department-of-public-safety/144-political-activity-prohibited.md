# Second Class Cities Law § 144 — Political activity prohibited

[Second Class Cities Law](../README.md) › [Article 9 — Department of Public Safety](README.md)

```text
  § 144. Political activity prohibited. Any officer or member of the
police department who violates any provision of section 17-110 of the
election law shall be dismissed from office.
```
