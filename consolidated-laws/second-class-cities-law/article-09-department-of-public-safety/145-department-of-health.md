# Second Class Cities Law § 145 — Department of health

[Second Class Cities Law](../README.md) › [Article 9 — Department of Public Safety](README.md)

```text
  § 145. Department of health. The commissioner of public safety shall
exercise all the powers and be charged with all the duties conferred
upon or required of local boards of health by the laws of this state, so
far as the same pertain to cities, with the exceptions, limitations and
additions herein contained.
```
