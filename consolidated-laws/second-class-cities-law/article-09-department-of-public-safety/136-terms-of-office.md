# Second Class Cities Law § 136 — Terms of office

[Second Class Cities Law](../README.md) › [Article 9 — Department of Public Safety](README.md)

```text
  § 136. Terms of office. All members of the police and fire
departments, subject to the power of removal hereinafter specified,
shall hold their respective offices during good behavior or until by age
or disease they shall become permanently incapacitated to discharge
their duties.
```
