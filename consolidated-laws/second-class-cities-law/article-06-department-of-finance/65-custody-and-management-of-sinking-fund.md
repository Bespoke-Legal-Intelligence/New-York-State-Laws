# Second Class Cities Law § 65 — Custody and management of sinking fund

[Second Class Cities Law](../README.md) › [Article 6 — Department of Finance](README.md)

```text
  § 65. Custody and management of sinking fund. The comptroller shall
have, under the direction of the board of estimate and apportionment,
the custody, investment and management of any sinking fund provided for
the payment or redemption of city debts.
```
