# Second Class Cities Law § 80 — Penalties for violation of preceding section

[Second Class Cities Law](../README.md) › [Article 6 — Department of Finance](README.md)

```text
  § 80. Penalties for violation of preceding section. Any officer or
member of any board or department of the city, making or voting for any
contract prohibited by the preceding section, or auditing any account or
claim under any such contract, shall be deemed guilty of a misdemeanor.
```
