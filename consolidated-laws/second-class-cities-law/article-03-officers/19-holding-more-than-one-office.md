# Second Class Cities Law § 19 — Holding more than one office

[Second Class Cities Law](../README.md) › [Article 3 — Officers](README.md)

```text
  § 19. Holding more than one office. No person shall, at the same time,
hold more than one city office.  Upon the acceptance by a city officer
of a second office the office first held by him shall thereupon become
vacant. The term "city officer" as used herein, however, shall not be
deemed to include a commissioner of deeds.
```
