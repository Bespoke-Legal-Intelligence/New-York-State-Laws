# Second Class Cities Law § 10 — Officers

[Second Class Cities Law](../README.md) › [Article 3 — Officers](README.md)

```text
  § 10. Officers. City officers, within the meaning of this chapter,
include all persons elected or appointed to any office of the city
created or authorized by this chapter or otherwise by law.
```
