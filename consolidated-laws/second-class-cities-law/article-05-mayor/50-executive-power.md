# Second Class Cities Law § 50 — Executive power

[Second Class Cities Law](../README.md) › [Article 5 — Mayor](README.md)

```text
  § 50. Executive power. The executive power of the city is vested in
the mayor, and in such executive officers and departments as are or may
be created by law, or by ordinance of the common council.
```
