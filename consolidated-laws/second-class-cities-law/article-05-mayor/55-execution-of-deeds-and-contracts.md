# Second Class Cities Law § 55 — Execution of deeds and contracts

[Second Class Cities Law](../README.md) › [Article 5 — Mayor](README.md)

```text
  § 55. Execution of deeds and contracts. The mayor shall, on behalf of
the city, execute all deeds and contracts made by it and shall cause to
be affixed thereto the city seal.
```
