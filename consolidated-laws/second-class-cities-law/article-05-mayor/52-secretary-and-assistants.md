# Second Class Cities Law § 52 — Secretary and assistants

[Second Class Cities Law](../README.md) › [Article 5 — Mayor](README.md)

```text
  § 52. Secretary and assistants. The mayor shall appoint a secretary
and such other assistants as may be prescribed by the board of estimate
and apportionment.
```
