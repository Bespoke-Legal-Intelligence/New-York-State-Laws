# Second Class Cities Law § 210 — Supervisors

[Second Class Cities Law](../README.md) › [Article 14 — Supervisors; Sealer of Weights and Measures](README.md)

```text
  § 210. Supervisors. Supervisors shall have the powers and perform the
duties of supervisors of towns under the general laws of the state, and
other laws applicable thereto.
```
