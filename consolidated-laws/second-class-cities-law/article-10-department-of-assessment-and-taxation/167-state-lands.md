# Second Class Cities Law § 167 — State lands

[Second Class Cities Law](../README.md) › [Article 10 — Department of Assessment and Taxation](README.md)

```text
  § 167. State lands. Nothing herein contained shall affect any
assessment upon lands owned by the state nor be deemed to repeal or
modify any of the provisions of section twenty-one of the public lands
law.
```
