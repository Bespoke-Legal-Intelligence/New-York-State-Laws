# Second Class Cities Law § 125 — Power to purchase and contract limited

[Second Class Cities Law](../README.md) › [Article 8 — Department of Contract and Supply](README.md)

```text
  § 125. Power to purchase and contract limited. No person shall have
power to make any purchase or contract any debt for which the city shall
be liable unless authorized by the provisions of this chapter or the
local finance law.
```
