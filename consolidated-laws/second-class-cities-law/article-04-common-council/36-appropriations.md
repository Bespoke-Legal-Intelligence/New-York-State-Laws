# Second Class Cities Law § 36 — Appropriations

[Second Class Cities Law](../README.md) › [Article 4 — Common Council](README.md)

```text
  § 36. Appropriations.  No appropriation of money shall be made for any
purpose except by ordinance specifying each item, the amount thereof,
and the department or specific purpose for which the appropriation is
made.
```
