# Second Class Cities Law § 102 — Streets by prescription

[Second Class Cities Law](../README.md) › [Article 7 — Department of Public Works](README.md)

```text
  § 102. Streets by prescription. All lands which shall have been used
by the public as a street for twenty years or more continuously shall be
a street with the same force and effect as if it had been duly laid out
and recorded as such.
```
