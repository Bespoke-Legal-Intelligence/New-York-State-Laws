# Second Class Cities Law § 241 — Books and papers to be public records

[Second Class Cities Law](../README.md) › [Article 16 — Miscellaneous Provisions](README.md)

```text
  § 241. Books and papers to be public records. All books, papers and
documents filed with or constituting a part of the records or
proceedings of any officer, board or department of the city, shall be
deemed to be public records and shall, during office hours, be open to
public inspection.
```
