# Second Class Cities Law § 174 — Liability of city

[Second Class Cities Law](../README.md) › [Article 11 — Department of Public Welfare](README.md)

```text
  § 174. Liability of city. Nothing contained in this chapter shall be
deemed to make the city liable for the support or relief of any poor
person when it is not otherwise so liable.
```
