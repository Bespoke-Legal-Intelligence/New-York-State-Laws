# Second Class Cities Law § 203 — Account of moneys collected

[Second Class Cities Law](../README.md) › [Article 13 — Department of Law](README.md)

```text
  § 203. Account of moneys collected. The corporation counsel shall pay
over at once to the city treasurer, for and on behalf of the city, all
moneys collected by him for and on behalf of the city, including fines
and penalties.
```
