# Second Class Cities Law § 202 — Costs

[Second Class Cities Law](../README.md) › [Article 13 — Department of Law](README.md)

```text
  § 202. Costs. Neither the corporation counsel, deputy, clerk, or any
other subordinate or assistant, shall receive any fee or compensation of
any kind, other than the salary fixed by law or by the board of estimate
and apportionment.
```
