# Second Class Cities Law § 188 — Trial by jury

[Second Class Cities Law](../README.md) › [Article 12 — Judiciary](README.md)

```text
  § 188. Trial by jury. In the police court, at the time of interposing
any plea which forms an issue of fact, the defendant may demand a trial
by jury, and unless so demanded then a trial by jury is waived.
```
