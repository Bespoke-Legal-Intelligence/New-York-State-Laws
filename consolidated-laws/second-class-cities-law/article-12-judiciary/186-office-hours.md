# Second Class Cities Law § 186 — Office hours

[Second Class Cities Law](../README.md) › [Article 12 — Judiciary](README.md)

```text
  § 186. Office hours. It shall be the duty of the police justice to be
present at the police court rooms at such times and during such hours as
the public interest may require, unless necessarily detained therefrom.
```
