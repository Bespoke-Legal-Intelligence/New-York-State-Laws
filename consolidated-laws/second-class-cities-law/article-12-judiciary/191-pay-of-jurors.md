# Second Class Cities Law § 191 — Pay of jurors

[Second Class Cities Law](../README.md) › [Article 12 — Judiciary](README.md)

```text
  § 191. Pay of jurors. Jurors in the police court shall receive the
same compensation as jurors in justice's court held by justices of the
peace except that in the police court of the city of Albany jurors shall
receive such compensation as may be provided by the board of estimate
and apportionment of the city of Albany.
```
