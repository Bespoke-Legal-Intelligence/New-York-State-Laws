# Second Class Cities Law § 180 — Jurisdiction of police court

[Second Class Cities Law](../README.md) › [Article 12 — Judiciary](README.md)

```text
  § 180. Jurisdiction of police court. If, under the general or local
laws, there now exists or shall hereafter be established in the city a
court of criminal jurisdiction known as the police court, it shall have
the jurisdiction and powers hereinafter provided.
```
