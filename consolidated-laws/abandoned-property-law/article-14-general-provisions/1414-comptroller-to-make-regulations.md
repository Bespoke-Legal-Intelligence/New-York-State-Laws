# Abandoned Property Law § 1414 — Comptroller to make regulations

[Abandoned Property Law](../README.md) › [Article 14 — General Provisions](README.md)

```text
  § 1414. Comptroller to make regulations. The state comptroller is
hereby authorized to make such rules and regulations as he may deem
necessary to enforce the provisions of this chapter.
```
