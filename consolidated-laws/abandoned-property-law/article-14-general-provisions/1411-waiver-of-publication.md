# Abandoned Property Law § 1411 — Waiver of publication

[Abandoned Property Law](../README.md) › [Article 14 — General Provisions](README.md)

```text
  § 1411. Waiver of publication. The state comptroller may waive the
publication of any notice required by this chapter, except a notice
required by section fourteen hundred two, whenever in his opinion the
cost of publishing such notice would be unreasonable in relation to the
amount of abandoned property.
```
