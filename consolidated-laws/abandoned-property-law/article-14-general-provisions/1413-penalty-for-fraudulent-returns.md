# Abandoned Property Law § 1413 — Penalty for fraudulent returns

[Abandoned Property Law](../README.md) › [Article 14 — General Provisions](README.md)

```text
  § 1413. Penalty for fraudulent returns. The making of a willful false
oath in any report required under the provisions of this chapter shall
be perjury and punishable as such according to law.
```
