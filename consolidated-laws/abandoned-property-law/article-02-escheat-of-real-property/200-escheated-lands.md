# Abandoned Property Law § 200 — Escheated lands

[Abandoned Property Law](../README.md) › [Article 2 — Escheat of Real Property](README.md)

```text
  § 200.  Escheated lands.  All lands the title of which shall fail from
a defect of heirs, shall revert, or escheat, to the people.
```
