# Abandoned Property Law § 205 — Report by attorney-general

[Abandoned Property Law](../README.md) › [Article 2 — Escheat of Real Property](README.md)

```text
  § 205. Report by attorney-general. The attorney-general shall report
to the commissioner of general services all the real property recovered
by the people in any action brought pursuant to this article.
```
