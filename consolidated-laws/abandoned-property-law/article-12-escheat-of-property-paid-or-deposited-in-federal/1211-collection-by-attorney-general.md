# Abandoned Property Law § 1211 — Collection by attorney-general

[Abandoned Property Law](../README.md) › [Article 12 — Escheat of Property Paid or Deposited In Federal Courts](README.md)

```text
  § 1211. Collection by attorney-general. The attorney-general shall
take appropriate action, by obtaining an order of the court of the
United States, or otherwise, to collect and receive such funds or
property.
```
