# Abandoned Property Law § 704 — Life insurance departments of savings and insurance banks

[Abandoned Property Law](../README.md) › [Article 7 — Unclaimed Life Insurance Funds](README.md)

```text
  § 704. Life insurance departments of savings and insurance banks.  The
life insurance department of any savings and insurance bank shall be
regarded as a domestic life insurance corporation for the purposes of
this article and such savings and insurance banks shall comply with and
be subject to all the provisions of this article with respect to the
actions and transactions of such life insurance department.
```
