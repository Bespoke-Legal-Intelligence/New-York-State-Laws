# Rapid Transit Law § 12 — Record of proceedings

[Rapid Transit Law](../README.md) › [Article 2 — Board of Transportation](README.md)

```text
  § 12. Record of proceedings.  The secretary of the board of
transportation, or other officer or employee designated by it, shall
keep a full and true record of all its proceedings. The record of
proceedings shall be printed as the minutes of the transactions of the
board.
```
