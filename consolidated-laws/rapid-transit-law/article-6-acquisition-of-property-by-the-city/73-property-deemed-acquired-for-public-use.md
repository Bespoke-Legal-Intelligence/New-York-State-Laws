# Rapid Transit Law § 73 — Property deemed acquired for public use

[Rapid Transit Law](../README.md) › [Article 6 — Acquisition of Property By the City](README.md)

```text
  § 73. Property deemed acquired for public use.  All property acquired
under the provisions of this chapter shall be and shall be deemed to
have been acquired for public uses and purposes, and for the purpose of
affording increased facilities for rapid transit between points within
the city acquiring such property.
```
