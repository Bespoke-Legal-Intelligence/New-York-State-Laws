# Rapid Transit Law § 36 — Rates of fare

[Rapid Transit Law](../README.md) › [Article 4 — Construction and Operation By the Board of Transportation](README.md)

```text
  § 36. Rates of fare. The board of transportation, with the approval of
the mayor, is hereby empowered to fix and adjust from time to time the
rate of fare that may be charged any passenger traveling on any railroad
publicly or municipally operated pursuant to the authority of this
chapter, provided, however, that any change in such rate of fare shall
become effective on the first day of the next ensuing fiscal year of the
city.
```
