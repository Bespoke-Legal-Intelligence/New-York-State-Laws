# Rapid Transit Law § 108 — Continuity of jurisdiction

[Rapid Transit Law](../README.md) › [Article 9 — Miscellaneous](README.md)

```text
  § 108. Continuity of jurisdiction.  The board of transportation shall
be deemed and held to constitute a continuation, as to matters within
its jurisdiction, of the transit commission, and not as a new
commission, for the purpose of succession to all such of the rights,
powers, duties and obligations of the transit commission transferred to
such board of transportation.
```
