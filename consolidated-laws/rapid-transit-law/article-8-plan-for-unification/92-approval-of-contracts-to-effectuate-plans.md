# Rapid Transit Law § 92 — Approval of contracts to effectuate plans

[Rapid Transit Law](../README.md) › [Article 8 — Plan For Unification](README.md)

```text
  § 92. Approval of contracts to effectuate plans.  Anything contained
in this article to the contrary notwithstanding, the commission shall
not execute and deliver any plan, or any lease thereunder, without
obtaining the consent and approval of the board of estimate.
```
