# Banking Law § 147 — Separability of provisions

[Banking Law](../README.md) › [Article 3-A — Bank Holding Companies; Control of Banking Institutions](README.md)

```text
  § 147. Separability of provisions. If any provision of this article,
or the application of such provision to any individual, company or
circumstance, shall be held invalid, the remainder of the article, and
the application of such provision to individuals, companies or
circumstances other than those to which it is held invalid, shall not be
affected thereby.
```
