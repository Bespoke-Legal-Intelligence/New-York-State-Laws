# Banking Law § 9012 — Corporate seal as evidence

[Banking Law](../README.md) › [Article 16 — General Provisions Applicable to Banking Non-stock Corporations](README.md)

```text
  § 9012. Corporate seal as evidence. The presence of the corporate seal
on a written instrument purporting to be executed by authority of a
corporation or a foreign corporation shall be prima facie evidence that
the instrument was so executed.
```
