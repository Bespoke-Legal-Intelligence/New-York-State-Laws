# Banking Law § 626 — Judgments recovered shall not be liens

[Banking Law](../README.md) › [Article 13 — Merger; Voluntary Dissolution; Superintendent's Taking Possession; Reorganization; Liquidation](README.md)

```text
  § 626. Judgments recovered shall not be liens. A lien shall not attach
to any of the property or assets of any banking organization by reason
of the entry of any judgment recovered against such banking organization
after the superintendent has taken possession of its property and
business and so long as such possession continues.
```
