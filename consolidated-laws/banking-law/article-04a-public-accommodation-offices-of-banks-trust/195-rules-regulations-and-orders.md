# Banking Law § 195 — Rules, regulations and orders

[Banking Law](../README.md) › [Article 4-A — Public Accommodation Offices of Banks, Trust Companies, Savings Banks, Savings and Loan Associations and Foreign Banking Corporations](README.md)

```text
  § 195. Rules, regulations and orders. The superintendent shall have
power to adopt, amend and enforce such rules, regulations and orders as
the superintendent may deem necessary to enable the superintendent to
administer and carry out the provisions of this article and to prevent
evasions thereof.
```
