# Banking Law § 496 — Judicial review

[Banking Law](../README.md) › [Article 11-B — Sales Finance Companies](README.md)

```text
  § 496. Judicial review. The refusal, suspension or revocation of a
license by the superintendent shall be subject to review in the manner
provided by article seventy-eight of the civil practice law and rules.
```
