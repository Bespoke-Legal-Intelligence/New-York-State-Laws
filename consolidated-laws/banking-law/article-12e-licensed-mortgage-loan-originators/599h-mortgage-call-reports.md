# Banking Law § 599-H — Mortgage call reports

[Banking Law](../README.md) › [Article 12-E — Licensed Mortgage Loan Originators](README.md)

```text
  § 599-h. Mortgage call reports. Each originating entity shall submit
to the NMLSR reports of condition, which shall be in such form and shall
contain such information as the NMLSR may require.
```
