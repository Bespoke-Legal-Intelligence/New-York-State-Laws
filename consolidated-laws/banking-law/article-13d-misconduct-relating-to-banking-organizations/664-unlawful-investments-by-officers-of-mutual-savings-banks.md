# Banking Law § 664 — Unlawful investments by officers of mutual savings banks

[Banking Law](../README.md) › [Article 13-D — Misconduct Relating to Banking Organizations](README.md)

```text
  § 664. Unlawful investments by officers of mutual savings banks.  Any
officer or trustee of a mutual savings bank authorizing or making any
investment of the funds of the bank in securities not authorized by law,
is guilty of a misdemeanor.
```
