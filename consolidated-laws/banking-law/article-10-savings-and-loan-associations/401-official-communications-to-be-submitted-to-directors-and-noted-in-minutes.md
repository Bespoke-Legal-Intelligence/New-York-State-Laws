# Banking Law § 401 — Official communications to be submitted to directors and noted in minutes

[Banking Law](../README.md) › [Article 10 — Savings and Loan Associations](README.md)

```text
  § 401. Official communications to be submitted to directors and noted
in minutes. Every official communication, as defined in article two of
this chapter, shall be submitted, by the officer receiving it, to the
board of directors at the next meeting of such board and noted in the
minutes.
```
