# Banking Law § 383-A — Rental of safe deposit boxes

[Banking Law](../README.md) › [Article 10 — Savings and Loan Associations](README.md)

```text
  § 383-a. Rental of safe deposit boxes. Any savings and loan
association may rent to its members safe deposit boxes in which to keep
personal property and papers of any kind.
```
