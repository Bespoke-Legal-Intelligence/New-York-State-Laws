# Banking Law § 36-A — Reports of lending by banking organizations

[Banking Law](../README.md) › [Article 2 — Department of Financial Services; Superintendent of Financial Services; Supervisory and Regulatory Powers](README.md)

```text
  § 36-a. Reports of lending by banking organizations. The
superintendent may require every banking organization to submit from
time to time data on its mortgage loans, home improvement loans, or
other loans and data on its deposits.
```
