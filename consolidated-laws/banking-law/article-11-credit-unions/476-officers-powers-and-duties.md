# Banking Law § 476 — Officers; powers and duties

[Banking Law](../README.md) › [Article 11 — Credit Unions](README.md)

```text
  § 476. Officers; powers and duties.  The powers and duties of the
officers of any credit union shall be such as are prescribed in the
bylaws.
```
