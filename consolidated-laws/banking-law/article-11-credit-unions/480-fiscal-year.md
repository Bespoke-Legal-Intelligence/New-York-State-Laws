# Banking Law § 480 — Fiscal year

[Banking Law](../README.md) › [Article 11 — Credit Unions](README.md)

```text
  § 480. Fiscal year.  The fiscal year of every credit union shall end
at the close of business on the thirty-first day of December.
```
