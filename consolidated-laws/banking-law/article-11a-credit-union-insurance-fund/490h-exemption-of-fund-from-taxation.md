# Banking Law § 490-H — Exemption of fund from taxation

[Banking Law](../README.md) › [Article 11-A — Credit Union Insurance Fund](README.md)

```text
  § 490-h. Exemption of fund from taxation. The fund provided for by
this article and the income thereof shall be exempt from all taxation
now or hereafter imposed by the state of New York or by any county,
municipality or local authority or subdivision.
```
