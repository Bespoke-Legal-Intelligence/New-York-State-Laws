# Banking Law § 701 — Construction

[Banking Law](../README.md) › [Article 14 — Laws Repealed; Construction; When to Take Effect](README.md)

```text
  § 701. Construction. The provisions of this chapter shall be construed
as a continuation of the provisions of chapter ten of the laws of
nineteen hundred nine, as amended, modified or amended according to the
language employed, and not as a new enactment.
```
