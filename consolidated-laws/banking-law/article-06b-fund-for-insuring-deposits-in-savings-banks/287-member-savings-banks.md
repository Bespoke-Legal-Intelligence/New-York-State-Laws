# Banking Law § 287 — Member savings banks

[Banking Law](../README.md) › [Article 6-B — Fund For Insuring Deposits In Savings Banks And/or Otherwise Protecting Depositors](README.md)

```text
  § 287. Member savings banks. Any savings bank incorporated in the
state of New York may become a member of the fund upon the terms and
conditions set forth in the agreement by executing the agreement
authorized by this article, or a counterpart thereof.
```
