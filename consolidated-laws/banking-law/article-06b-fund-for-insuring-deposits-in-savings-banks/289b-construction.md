# Banking Law § 289-B — Construction

[Banking Law](../README.md) › [Article 6-B — Fund For Insuring Deposits In Savings Banks And/or Otherwise Protecting Depositors](README.md)

```text
  § 289-b. Construction. The provisions of this article shall be

savings banks.
```
