# Banking Law § 289 — Confirmation of powers

[Banking Law](../README.md) › [Article 6-B — Fund For Insuring Deposits In Savings Banks And/or Otherwise Protecting Depositors](README.md)

```text
  § 289. Confirmation of powers. Any savings bank shall have the
authority to do and permit to be done any of the acts and things and
make any of the payments and contributions herein authorized.
```
