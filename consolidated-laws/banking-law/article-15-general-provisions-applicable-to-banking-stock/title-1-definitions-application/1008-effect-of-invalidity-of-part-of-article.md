# Banking Law § 1008 — Effect of invalidity of part of article; severability

[Banking Law](../../README.md) › [Article 15 — General Provisions Applicable to Banking Stock Corporations, Limited Liability Investment Companies, and Limited Liability Trust Companies](../README.md) › [Title 1 — Definitions; Application; Certificates; Miscellaneous](README.md)

```text
  § 1008. Effect of invalidity of part of article; severability.  If any
provision of this article or application thereof to any person or
circumstances is held invalid, such invalidity shall not affect other
provisions or applications of this article which can be given effect
without the invalid provision or application, and to this end the
provisions of this article are declared severable.
```
