# Banking Law § 1006 — Corporate seal as evidence

[Banking Law](../../README.md) › [Article 15 — General Provisions Applicable to Banking Stock Corporations, Limited Liability Investment Companies, and Limited Liability Trust Companies](../README.md) › [Title 1 — Definitions; Application; Certificates; Miscellaneous](README.md)

```text
  § 1006. Corporate seal as evidence. The presence of the corporate seal
on a written instrument purporting to be executed by authority of a
corporation or a foreign corporation shall be prima facie evidence that
the instrument was so executed.
```
