# Banking Law § 680-I — Injunction or similar relief

[Banking Law](../README.md) › [Article 13-F — Uniform Special Deposits Act](README.md)

```text
  § 680-i. Injunction or similar relief. A court may enjoin, or grant
similar relief that would have the effect of enjoining, a bank from
paying a depositor or beneficiary only if payment would constitute a
material fraud or facilitate a material fraud with respect to a special
deposit.
```
