# Banking Law § 572 — Notice of assignment; payments

[Banking Law](../README.md) › [Article 12-B — Insurance Premium Finance Agencies](README.md)

```text
  § 572. Notice of assignment; payments. Unless the insured has notice
of actual or intended assignment of a premium finance agreement, payment
thereunder by him to the last known holder of the agreement shall be
binding upon all subsequent holders or assignees.
```
