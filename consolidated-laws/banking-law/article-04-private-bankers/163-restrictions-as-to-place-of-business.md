# Banking Law § 163 — Restrictions as to place of business

[Banking Law](../README.md) › [Article 4 — Private Bankers](README.md)

```text
  § 163. Restrictions as to place of business. The office of a private
banker shall not be located in the same room with, or in a room
connecting with, any bank, trust company, savings bank, or national
bank.
```
