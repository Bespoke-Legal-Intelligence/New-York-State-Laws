# Public Health Law § 2600 — Helen Hayes Hospital; hospital care and treatment

[Public Health Law](../README.md) › [Article 26 — Helen Hayes Hospital](README.md)

```text
  § 2600. Helen Hayes Hospital; hospital care and treatment.  1. The
Helen Hayes Hospital is continued for the care and treatment of persons
who are physically disabled, deformed or are suffering from disease from
which they are likely to become physically disabled or deformed.
  2. The patients in such hospital shall be permitted to exercise and
enjoy religious profession and worship freely and without discrimination
or preference.
```
