# Public Health Law § 3652 — Licensure required

[Public Health Law](../README.md) › [Article 36-A — Medical Equipment Service Agencies](README.md)

```text
  § 3652. Licensure required. No medical equipment service agency
located in New York state, or located in another state and providing
home medical equipment services in this state may provide home medical
equipment services unless a valid license has been issued as provided in
this article.
```
