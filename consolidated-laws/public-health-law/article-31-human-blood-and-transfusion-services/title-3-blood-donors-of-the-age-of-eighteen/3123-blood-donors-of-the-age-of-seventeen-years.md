# Public Health Law § 3123 — Blood donors of the age of seventeen years or over

[Public Health Law](../../README.md) › [Article 31 — Human Blood and Transfusion Services](../README.md) › [Title 3 — Blood Donors of the Age of Eighteen Years or Over](README.md)

```text
  § 3123. Blood donors of the age of seventeen years or over. Any person
of the age of seventeen years or over shall be eligible to donate blood
in any voluntary and noncompensatory blood program without the necessity
of obtaining parental permission or authorization.
```
