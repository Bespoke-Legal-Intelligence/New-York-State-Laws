# Public Health Law § 3120 — Blood collection; permit required; procedure

[Public Health Law](../../README.md) › [Article 31 — Human Blood and Transfusion Services](../README.md) › [Title 2 — Regulation of the Business of Blood Donating](README.md)

```text
  § 3120. Blood collection; permit required; procedure.
  No person, firm or corporation shall collect human blood for the
purpose of transfusion into another human being unless a permit has been
issued pursuant to the provisions of title five of article five of this
chapter.
```
