# Public Health Law § 2988 — Requiring or prohibiting execution of proxy

[Public Health Law](../README.md) › [Article 29-C — Health Care Agents and Proxies](README.md)

```text
  § 2988. Requiring or prohibiting execution of proxy. No person may
require or prohibit the execution of a health care proxy by an
individual as a condition for providing health care services or
insurance to such individual.
```
