# Public Health Law § 2987 — Liability for health care costs

[Public Health Law](../README.md) › [Article 29-C — Health Care Agents and Proxies](README.md)

```text
  § 2987. Liability for health care costs. Liability for the cost of
health care provided pursuant to an agent's decision shall be the same
as if the health care were provided pursuant to the principal's
decision.
```
