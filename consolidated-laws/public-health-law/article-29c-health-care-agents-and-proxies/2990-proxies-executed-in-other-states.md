# Public Health Law § 2990 — Proxies executed in other states

[Public Health Law](../README.md) › [Article 29-C — Health Care Agents and Proxies](README.md)

```text
  § 2990. Proxies executed in other states. A health care proxy or
similar instrument executed in another state or jurisdiction in
compliance with the law of that state or jurisdiction shall be
considered validly executed for purposes of this article.
```
