# Public Health Law § 3397-G — Report

[Public Health Law](../README.md) › [Article 33-A — Controlled Substances Therapeutic Research Act](README.md)

**Repealed** (4 Aug 2017). The text below is what the Senate publishes for this repealed provision.

```text
  § 3397-g. Report.  The commissioner, in conjunction with the state
patient qualification review board, shall each year report his findings
and recommendations to the governor and the legislature regarding the
effectiveness of the Antonio G. Olivieri controlled substances
therapeutic research program.
```
