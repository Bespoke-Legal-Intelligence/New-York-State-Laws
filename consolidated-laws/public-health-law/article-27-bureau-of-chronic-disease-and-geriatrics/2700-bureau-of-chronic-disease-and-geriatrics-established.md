# Public Health Law § 2700 — Bureau of chronic disease and geriatrics established

[Public Health Law](../README.md) › [Article 27 — Bureau of Chronic Disease and Geriatrics](README.md)

```text
  § 2700. Bureau of chronic disease and geriatrics established.  The
state department of health shall establish a bureau of chronic disease
and geriatrics, which shall develop a program designed to improve and
protect the health and vitality of middle-aged and elderly citizens of
the state.
```
