# Public Health Law § 2702 — Powers and duties

[Public Health Law](../README.md) › [Article 27 — Bureau of Chronic Disease and Geriatrics](README.md)

```text
  § 2702. Powers and duties.  The commissioner shall have the power and
it shall be his duty to employ such assistants and personnel, within the
amount of the appropriation, as is necessary to carry out the provisions
of this article.
```
