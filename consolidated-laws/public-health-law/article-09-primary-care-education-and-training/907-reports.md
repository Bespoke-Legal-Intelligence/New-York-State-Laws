# Public Health Law § 907 — Reports

[Public Health Law](../README.md) › [Article 9 — Primary Care Education and Training](README.md)

```text
  § 907. Reports. The commissioner, in conjunction with the commissioner
of education, shall prepare and submit a report to the governor and the
legislature, due on February first, nineteen hundred ninety-five,
reporting the results and evaluating the effectiveness of the New York
state primary care education and training act, including recommendations
for the placement of primary care physicians and primary care
practitioners in underserved areas.
```
