# Public Health Law § 3618 — Continuation of existing home health agencies

[Public Health Law](../README.md) › [Article 36 — Home Care Services](README.md)

```text
  § 3618. Continuation of existing home health agencies. Any home health
agency which, on March thirty-first, nineteen hundred seventy-eight,
possessed a valid certificate of approval issued under the provisions of
article twenty-eight of this chapter shall be deemed a certified home
health agency and such certificate of approval shall be deemed valid
under the provisions of this article.
```
