# Public Health Law § 3621 — Payment of claims

[Public Health Law](../README.md) › [Article 36 — Home Care Services](README.md)

```text
  § 3621. Payment of claims. Notwithstanding any law to the contrary,
the provisions of section thirty-two hundred twenty-four-a of the
insurance law, and regulations thereunder, shall apply to claims for
payment submitted by a licensed home care services agency, certified
home health agency, long term home health care program, or fiscal
intermediary operating under section three hundred sixty-five-f of the
social services law, pursuant to a contract with a payor under section
forty-four hundred three-f of this chapter or section three hundred
sixty-four-j of the social services law, and such claims shall be
subject to and settled in compliance with the standards set forth in
such section.
```
