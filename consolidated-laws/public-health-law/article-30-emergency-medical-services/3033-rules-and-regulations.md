# Public Health Law § 3033 — Rules and regulations

[Public Health Law](../README.md) › [Article 30 — Emergency Medical Services](README.md)

```text
  § 3033. Rules and regulations. The commissioner, upon approval of the
state emergency medical services council, may promulgate rules and
regulations to effectuate the purposes of this article, provided,
however, that sections three thousand thirty and three thousand
thirty-one of this article shall be subject to the provisions of section
three thousand thirty-four of this article.
```
