# Public Health Law § 3032 — Rules and regulations

[Public Health Law](../README.md) › [Article 30 — Emergency Medical Services](README.md)

```text
  § 3032. Rules and regulations.  The state council, with the approval
of the commissioner, shall promulgate rules and regulations to
effectuate the purposes of sections three thousand thirty and three
thousand thirty-one of this article.
```
