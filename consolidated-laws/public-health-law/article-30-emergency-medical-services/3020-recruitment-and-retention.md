# Public Health Law § 3020 — Recruitment and retention

[Public Health Law](../README.md) › [Article 30 — Emergency Medical Services](README.md)

```text
  § 3020. Recruitment and retention. 1. The commissioner shall establish
and fund within amounts appropriated, a public service campaign to
recruit additional personnel into the emergency medical system fields.
  2. The commissioner shall establish and fund within amounts
appropriated an emergency medical system mental health and wellness
program that provides resources to emergency medical service
practitioners to retain personnel in the emergency medical system
fields.
```
