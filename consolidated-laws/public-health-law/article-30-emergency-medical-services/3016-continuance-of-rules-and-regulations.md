# Public Health Law § 3016 — Continuance of rules and regulations

[Public Health Law](../README.md) › [Article 30 — Emergency Medical Services](README.md)

```text
  § 3016. Continuance of rules and regulations.  All rules and
regulations heretofore adopted by the commissioner pertaining to all
ambulance services shall continue in full force and effect as rules and
regulations until duly modified or superseded by rules and regulations
hereafter adopted and enacted by the state council pursuant to section
three thousand two of this article.
```
