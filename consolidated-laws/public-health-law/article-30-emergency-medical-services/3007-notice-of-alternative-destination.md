# Public Health Law § 3007 — Notice of alternative destination

[Public Health Law](../README.md) › [Article 30 — Emergency Medical Services](README.md)

```text
  § 3007. Notice of alternative destination.  In any city having a
population of one million or more, whenever an individual is transported
by ambulance from one facility to a second facility and the destination
is changed, it shall be the responsibility of the senior ambulance
dispatcher to inform the facility of origin by telephone of the ultimate
destination immediately upon arrival thereat.
```
