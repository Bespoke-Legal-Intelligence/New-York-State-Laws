# Public Health Law § 5002 — Laws repealed

[Public Health Law](../README.md) › [Article 50 — Separability Clause; Saving Clause; Construction; Laws Repealed; Time of Taking Effect](README.md)

```text
  § 5002. Laws repealed. Chapter forty-nine of the laws of nineteen
hundred nine, entitled "An act in relation to the public health,
constituting chapter forty-five of the consolidated laws, " and all acts
amendatory thereof and supplemental thereto, constituting the public
health law as heretofore in force, are hereby repealed, except as
otherwise provided herein.
```
