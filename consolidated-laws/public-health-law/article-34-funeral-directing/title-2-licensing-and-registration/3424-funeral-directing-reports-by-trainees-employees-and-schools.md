# Public Health Law § 3424 — Funeral directing; reports by trainees, employees and schools

[Public Health Law](../../README.md) › [Article 34 — Funeral Directing](../README.md) › [Title 2 — Licensing and Registration](README.md)

```text
  § 3424. Funeral directing; reports by trainees, employees and schools.
1. Each trainee and the employer and funeral director school shall:
  (a) maintain and file such periodic records and reports as the
department shall require; and
  (b) notify the department promptly of any change in school
registration or change of employment.
```
