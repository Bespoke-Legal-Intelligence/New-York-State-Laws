# Public Health Law § 3427 — Funeral directing; issuance of duplicate licenses

[Public Health Law](../../README.md) › [Article 34 — Funeral Directing](../README.md) › [Title 2 — Licensing and Registration](README.md)

```text
  § 3427. Funeral directing; issuance of duplicate licenses. Upon
receipt of satisfactory evidence that a license or certificate has been
lost, mutilated or destroyed, the department may issue a duplicate
license or certificate upon such terms and conditions as the
commissioner shall prescribe, and upon payment of a fee of twenty
dollars.
```
