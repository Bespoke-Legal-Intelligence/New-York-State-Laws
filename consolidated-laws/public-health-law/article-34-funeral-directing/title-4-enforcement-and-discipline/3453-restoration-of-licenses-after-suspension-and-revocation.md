# Public Health Law § 3453 — Restoration of licenses after suspension and revocation

[Public Health Law](../../README.md) › [Article 34 — Funeral Directing](../README.md) › [Title 4 — Enforcement and Discipline; Violations and Penalties](README.md)

```text
  § 3453. Restoration of licenses after suspension and revocation.  1.
The commissioner may, in his discretion, reissue a license to any person
whose license has been revoked.
  2. Application for the reissuance of a license shall not be made prior
to one year after revocation and shall be made in such manner as the
commissioner may direct.
```
