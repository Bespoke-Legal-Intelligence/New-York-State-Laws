# Public Health Law § 3440 — Funeral directors; duty to use prescribed tests of death

[Public Health Law](../../README.md) › [Article 34 — Funeral Directing](../README.md) › [Title 3 — Practice of Funeral Directing; Funeral Establishments](README.md)

```text
  § 3440. Funeral directors; duty to use prescribed tests of death.
Before removing the body of a deceased person from the place of death or
preparing it for burial by injection of fluid or otherwise, every
funeral director, undertaker or embalmer shall apply such tests as are
prescribed by the commissioner for determining whether life is extinct.
If the body is not to be embalmed the same tests shall be made before it
is prepared for burial.
```
