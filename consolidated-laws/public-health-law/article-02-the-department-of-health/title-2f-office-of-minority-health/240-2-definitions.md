# Public Health Law § 240*2 — Definitions

[Public Health Law](../../README.md) › [Article 2 — The Department of Health](../README.md) › [Title 2-F — Office of Minority Health](README.md)

*An asterisk in a number (for example Article 24\* or § 20-C\*2) is the Senate's mark for a number the Legislature has used more than once: the marked article or section is a different one from the unmarked one with the same number. \*2 means the second one enacted with that number.*

```text
  * § 240. Definitions. For the purposes of this article:
  1. "Minority area" shall mean a county with a non-white population of
forty percent or more, or the service area of an agency, corporation,
facility or individual providing medical and/or health services whose
non-white population is forty percent or more.
  2. "Minority health care provider" or "minority provider" shall mean
any agency, corporation, facility, or individual providing medical
and/or health care services to residents of a minority area.
  3. "Office" shall mean the office of minority health, as created
pursuant to section two hundred thirty-eight-a of this article.
  4. "Minority health council" shall mean that advisory body to the
commissioner, created pursuant to the provisions of section two hundred
thirty-eight-c of this article.
  * NB There are 2 § 240's
```
