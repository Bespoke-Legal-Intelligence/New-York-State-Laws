# Public Health Law § 241 — Regional health directors; powers and duties

[Public Health Law](../../README.md) › [Article 2 — The Department of Health](../README.md) › [Title 3 — State Health Areas](README.md)

```text
  * § 241. Regional health directors; powers and duties. Each regional
health director, subject to the provisions of this chapter and the
sanitary code, shall act as the representative of the commissioner in
all matters affecting public health within his region and shall perform
such duties and exercise such powers as may be assigned to him by the
commissioner.
  * NB There are 2 § 241's
```
