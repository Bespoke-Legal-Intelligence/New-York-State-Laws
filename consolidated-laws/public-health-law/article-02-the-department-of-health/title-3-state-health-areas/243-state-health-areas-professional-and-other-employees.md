# Public Health Law § 243 — State health areas; professional and other employees

[Public Health Law](../../README.md) › [Article 2 — The Department of Health](../README.md) › [Title 3 — State Health Areas](README.md)

```text
  * § 243. State health areas; professional and other employees.
Whenever he may deem it expedient so to do, the commissioner may employ
such professional or other assistants or employees as he shall consider
necessary within the limits of his appropriation, and may assign them
from time to time to regional and district health areas and in such
manner as in his judgment will best aid in the control of communicable
diseases and in the promotion of public health.
  * NB There are 2 § 243's
```
