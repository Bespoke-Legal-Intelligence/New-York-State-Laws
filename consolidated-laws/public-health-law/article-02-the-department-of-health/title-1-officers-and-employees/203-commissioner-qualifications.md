# Public Health Law § 203 — Commissioner; qualifications

[Public Health Law](../../README.md) › [Article 2 — The Department of Health](../README.md) › [Title 1 — Officers and Employees](README.md)

```text
  § 203. Commissioner; qualifications.  The commissioner shall be a
physician, a graduate of an incorporated medical college, of at least
ten years' experience in the actual practice of his profession, and of
skill and experience in public health duties and sanitary science.
```
