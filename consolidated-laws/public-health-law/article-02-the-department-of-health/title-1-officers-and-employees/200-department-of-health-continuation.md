# Public Health Law § 200 — Department of health; continuation

[Public Health Law](../../README.md) › [Article 2 — The Department of Health](../README.md) › [Title 1 — Officers and Employees](README.md)

```text
  § 200. Department of health; continuation.  There shall continue to be
in the state government a department of health. The head of the
department shall be the commissioner of health of the state of New York.
```
