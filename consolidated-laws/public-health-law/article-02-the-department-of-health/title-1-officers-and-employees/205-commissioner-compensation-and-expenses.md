# Public Health Law § 205 — Commissioner; compensation and expenses

[Public Health Law](../../README.md) › [Article 2 — The Department of Health](../README.md) › [Title 1 — Officers and Employees](README.md)

```text
  § 205. Commissioner; compensation and expenses.  The commissioner
shall receive an annual salary not to exceed an amount appropriated
therefor by the legislature and his expenses actually and necessarily
incurred in the performance of his official duties, unless otherwise
provided by the legislature.
```
