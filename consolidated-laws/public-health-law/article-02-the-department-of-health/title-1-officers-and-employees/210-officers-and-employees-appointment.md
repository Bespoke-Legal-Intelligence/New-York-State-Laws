# Public Health Law § 210 — Officers and employees; appointment

[Public Health Law](../../README.md) › [Article 2 — The Department of Health](../README.md) › [Title 1 — Officers and Employees](README.md)

```text
  § 210. Officers and employees; appointment.  The commissioner shall
appoint such assistant commissioners, officers and other assistants as
are necessary for the proper performance of the powers and duties of the
department.
```
