# Public Health Law § 211 — Training

[Public Health Law](../../README.md) › [Article 2 — The Department of Health](../README.md) › [Title 1 — Officers and Employees](README.md)

```text
  § 211. Training.  The commissioner may, within the appropriation
allowed therefor, provide suitable training for personnel engaged in or
to be engaged in the maintenance or improvement of public health in the
state.
```
