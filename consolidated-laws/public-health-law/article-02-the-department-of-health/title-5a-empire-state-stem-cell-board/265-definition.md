# Public Health Law § 265 — Definition

[Public Health Law](../../README.md) › [Article 2 — The Department of Health](../README.md) › [Title 5-A — Empire State Stem Cell Board](README.md)

```text
  * § 265. Definition. As used or referred to in this title, unless a
different meaning clearly appears from the context "stem cell" means
stem or progenitor cells that divide and are capable of generating one
or more different types of progeny. Stem cells and their progeny can
potentially repair or replace specific tissues or be used to develop
disease models.
  * NB Repealed December 31, 2025
```
