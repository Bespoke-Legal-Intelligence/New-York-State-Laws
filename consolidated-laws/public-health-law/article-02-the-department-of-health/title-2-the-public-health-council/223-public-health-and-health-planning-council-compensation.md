# Public Health Law § 223 — Public health and health planning council; compensation and expenses

[Public Health Law](../../README.md) › [Article 2 — The Department of Health](../README.md) › [Title 2 — The Public Health Council](README.md)

```text
  § 223. Public health and health planning council; compensation and
expenses. The members of the public health and health planning council
other than the commissioner of health shall receive no compensation for
their services, but shall be reimbursed for expenses actually and
necessarily incurred in the performance of their duties.
```
