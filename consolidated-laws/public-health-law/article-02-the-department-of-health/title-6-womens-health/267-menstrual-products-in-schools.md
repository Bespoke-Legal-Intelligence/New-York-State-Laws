# Public Health Law § 267 — Menstrual products in schools

[Public Health Law](../../README.md) › [Article 2 — The Department of Health](../README.md) › [Title 6 — Women's Health](README.md)

```text
  * § 267. Menstrual products in schools. All elementary and secondary
public schools in the state serving students in any grade from grade six
through grade twelve shall provide menstrual products in the restrooms
of such school building or buildings. Such products shall be provided at
no charge to students.
  * NB Effective until July 1, 2024
  * § 267. Menstrual products in schools. All elementary and secondary
public schools and non-public schools in the state serving students in
any grade from grade six through grade twelve shall provide menstrual
products in the restrooms of such school building or buildings. Such
products shall be provided at no charge to students.
  * NB Effective July 1, 2024
```
