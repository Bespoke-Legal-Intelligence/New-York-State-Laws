# Public Health Law § 466 — Judicial review

[Public Health Law](../README.md) › [Article 4-A — Regulation of Body Piercing and Tattooing](README.md)

```text
  § 466. Judicial review. The action of the commissioner in suspending,
revoking or refusing to issue or renew a permit or imposing any fine or
reprimand upon the holder thereof may be reviewed by a proceeding
brought under and pursuant to article seventy-eight of the civil
practice law and rules.
```
