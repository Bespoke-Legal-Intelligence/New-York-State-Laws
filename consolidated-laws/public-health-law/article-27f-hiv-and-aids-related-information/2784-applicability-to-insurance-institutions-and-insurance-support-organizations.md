# Public Health Law § 2784 — Applicability to insurance institutions and insurance support organizations

[Public Health Law](../README.md) › [Article 27-F — Hiv and Aids Related Information](README.md)

```text
  § 2784. Applicability to insurance institutions and insurance support
organizations.  Except for disclosure to third party reimbursers and
insurance institutions pursuant to paragraphs (i) and (j) of subdivision
one of section twenty-seven hundred eighty-two of this article and
except for disclosures pursuant to section twenty-seven hundred
eighty-five of this article, the provisions of this article shall not
apply to insurance institutions and insurance support organizations,
except that health care providers associated with or under contract to a
health maintenance organization or other medical services plan shall be
subject to the provisions of this article.
```
