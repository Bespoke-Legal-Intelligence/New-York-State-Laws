# Public Health Law § 2775 — The acquired immune deficiency syndrome institute

[Public Health Law](../README.md) › [Article 27-E — The Acquired Immune Deficiency Syndrome Institute](README.md)

```text
  § 2775. The acquired immune deficiency syndrome institute.  1. There
is hereby established within the department of health the acquired
immune deficiency syndrome institute. The institute shall have the
central responsibility for administering the provisions of this article
and otherwise coordinating the state's policies with respect to acquired
immune deficiency syndrome.
  2. The commissioner shall appoint a director of the institute and may
assign such personnel within the amounts appropriated as is necessary to
carry out the provisions of this article.
```
