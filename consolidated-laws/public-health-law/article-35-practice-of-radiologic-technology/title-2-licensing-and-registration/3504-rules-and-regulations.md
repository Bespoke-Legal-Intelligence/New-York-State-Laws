# Public Health Law § 3504 — Rules and regulations

[Public Health Law](../../README.md) › [Article 35 — Practice of Radiologic Technology](../README.md) › [Title 2 — Licensing and Registration](README.md)

```text
  § 3504. Rules and regulations. The commissioner shall have power to
make such rules and regulations, not inconsistent with law, as may be
necessary to carry out the provisions of this article, including but not
limited to, the availability of emergency equipment appropriate to
provide treatment in the event of an unanticipated reaction to the
administration of contrast media.
```
