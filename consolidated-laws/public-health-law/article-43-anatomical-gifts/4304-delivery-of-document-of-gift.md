# Public Health Law § 4304 — Delivery of document of gift

[Public Health Law](../README.md) › [Article 43 — Anatomical Gifts](README.md)

```text
  § 4304. Delivery of document of gift. If the gift is made by the donor
to a specified donee, the will, card or other document or a copy
thereof, may be delivered to him or her to expedite the appropriate
procedures immediately after death; however delivery is not necessary to
the validity of the gift. On request of an interested party upon or
after the donor's death, the person in possession shall produce the
document for examination.
```
