# Public Health Law § 4309 — Application

[Public Health Law](../README.md) › [Article 43 — Anatomical Gifts](README.md)

```text
  § 4309. Application. The provisions of this article shall not be
deemed to supersede or affect the provisions of the public health law
relating to the functions, powers and duties of coroners, coroner's
physicians or medical examiners.
```
