# Public Health Law § 2954 — Limitation on liability of network participants

[Public Health Law](../../README.md) › [Article 29-A — Access to Community Health Care Services In Rural Areas](../README.md) › [Title 1 — Rural Health Care Access](README.md)

```text
  §  2954.  Limitation  on liability of network participants.  Neither a
rural health network, nor any provider participating therein, whether by
agreement or through membership or common directorates, shall be  liable
for claims against or for the debt or obligations of any other affiliate
solely  by  reason  of  affiliation  or,  in  the absence of substantial
evidence of fraud or willful, wanton or criminal  activity  attributable
to  the  incurring  of such claim, debt or obligation on the part of the
entity from whom recovery is sought.
```
