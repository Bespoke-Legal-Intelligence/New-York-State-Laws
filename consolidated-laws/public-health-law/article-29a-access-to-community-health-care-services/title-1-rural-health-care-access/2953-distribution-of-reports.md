# Public Health Law § 2953 — Distribution of reports

[Public Health Law](../../README.md) › [Article 29-A — Access to Community Health Care Services In Rural Areas](../README.md) › [Title 1 — Rural Health Care Access](README.md)

**Repealed** (4 Aug 2017). The text below is what the Senate publishes for this repealed provision.

```text
  § 2953. Distribution of reports. The commissioner shall prepare a
report by December thirty-first, nineteen hundred ninety-five, and every
two years thereafter which shall include a status report of each network
in conjunction with the legislative commission on the development of
rural resources, which shall contain a description of the various
networks, their effect on cost, delivery, continuity, quality, and
accessibility of health care services in rural areas, the effect of each
network on existing providers and consumers of such services, as well as
the effect on each community served. The commissioner may arrange by
contract with an independent entity for the preparation of such reports
including evaluation activities necessary to produce such reports. Such
reports shall also identify laws, rules and regulations that should be
adopted, repealed or amended in order to make it possible to properly
and efficiently implement such networks in rural areas of the state.
Such reports shall be made available and distributed to all network
participants in the program, as well as to the governor, the
legislature, the chair of the New York state rural health council and
the chair of the legislative commission on the development of rural
resources.
```
