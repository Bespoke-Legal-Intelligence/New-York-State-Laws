# Public Health Law § 2002 — Contents of report

[Public Health Law](../README.md) › [Article 20 — Reporting of Alzheimer's Disease](README.md)

```text
  § 2002. Contents of report.  The report as set forth in section two
thousand one of this article shall be designated an Alzheimer's disease
report and shall contain information which the commissioner establishes
as necessary to identify, locate and investigate the occurrence,
frequency, incidence, cause, effect and prognosis of Alzheimer's
disease, and other relevant data and findings with respect thereto.
```
