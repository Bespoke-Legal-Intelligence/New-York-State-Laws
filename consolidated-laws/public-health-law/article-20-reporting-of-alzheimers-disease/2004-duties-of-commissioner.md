# Public Health Law § 2004 — Duties of commissioner

[Public Health Law](../README.md) › [Article 20 — Reporting of Alzheimer's Disease](README.md)

```text
  § 2004. Duties of commissioner.  The commissioner shall promulgate
rules and regulations to implement the provisions of this article,
including but not limited to:
  1. defining a person representing or employed by a facility and
defining a person with a valid scientific interest;
  2. establishing the form, content and manner of filing the Alzheimer's
disease report;
  3. prescribing the permissible uses for the information made available
pursuant to this article; and
  4. establishing procedures to maintain the confidentiality of
information provided to persons pursuant to this article.
```
