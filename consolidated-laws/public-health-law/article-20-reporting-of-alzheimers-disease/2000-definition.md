# Public Health Law § 2000 — Definition

[Public Health Law](../README.md) › [Article 20 — Reporting of Alzheimer's Disease](README.md)

```text
  § 2000. Definition.  As used in this article, "facility" shall mean
any governmental or private agency, department, institution, clinic,
laboratory, hospital, nursing care facility, health maintenance
organization, association or other similar entity that provides medical
care.
```
