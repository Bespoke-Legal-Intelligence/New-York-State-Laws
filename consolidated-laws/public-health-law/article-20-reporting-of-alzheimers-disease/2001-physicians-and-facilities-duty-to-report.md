# Public Health Law § 2001 — Physicians and facilities; duty to report

[Public Health Law](../README.md) › [Article 20 — Reporting of Alzheimer's Disease](README.md)

```text
  § 2001. Physicians and facilities; duty to report.  An attending
physician or a person representing or employed by a facility as defined
by the commissioner shall report the existence of Alzheimer's disease to
the department when the physician or other person diagnoses or confirms
the presence of that illness.
```
