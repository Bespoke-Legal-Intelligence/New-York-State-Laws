# Public Health Law § 372 — Certain cities; existing regulations and sanitary codes

[Public Health Law](../../README.md) › [Article 3 — Local Health Organization](../README.md) › [Title 4 — Certain Cities](README.md)

```text
  § 372. Certain cities; existing regulations and sanitary codes.  The
existing sanitary codes of cities having a population of less than one
hundred seventy-five thousand, and all rules, regulations and special
directions heretofore duly formulated, adopted and promulgated by the
commissioner of public safety, the board of health or the health
officer, shall continue in full force and effect, except as the same may
be revised, altered, amended or annulled from time to time as provided
by law.
```
