# Public Health Law § 364 — Certain cities; compensation of commissioner and others

[Public Health Law](../../README.md) › [Article 3 — Local Health Organization](../README.md) › [Title 4 — Certain Cities](README.md)

```text
  § 364. Certain cities; compensation of commissioner and others.  In
cities having a population of less than one hundred seventy-five
thousand, the salary of the commissioner of health, deputy commissioner
of health, health officer and all subordinates shall be fixed by the
board of estimate and apportionment, or by the board or authority
authorized by charter to fix salaries.
```
