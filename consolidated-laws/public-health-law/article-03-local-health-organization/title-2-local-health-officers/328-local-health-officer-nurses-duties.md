# Public Health Law § 328 — Local health officer; nurses; duties

[Public Health Law](../../README.md) › [Article 3 — Local Health Organization](../README.md) › [Title 2 — Local Health Officers](README.md)

```text
  § 328. Local health officer; nurses; duties.  Subject to the
provisions of this chapter and the sanitary code, public health nurses
appointed pursuant to this article, shall work under the direction of
the local health officer and shall perform such duties as may be
assigned by him.
```
