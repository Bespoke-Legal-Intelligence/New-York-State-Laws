# Public Health Law § 302 — Local boards of health; villages and towns; organization

[Public Health Law](../../README.md) › [Article 3 — Local Health Organization](../README.md) › [Title 1 — Local Boards of Health; General Provisions](README.md)

```text
  § 302. Local boards of health; villages and towns; organization.  1.
In villages the board of health shall consist of the board of trustees
of the village.
  2. In towns the board of health shall consist of the town board.
```
