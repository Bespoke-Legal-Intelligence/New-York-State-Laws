# Public Health Law § 312 — City of New York; exceptions and limitations

[Public Health Law](../../README.md) › [Article 3 — Local Health Organization](../README.md) › [Title 1 — Local Boards of Health; General Provisions](README.md)

```text
  § 312. City of New York; exceptions and limitations.  Unless otherwise
expressly provided, the provisions of this article except section three
hundred ten of this chapter shall not apply to the city of New York.
```
