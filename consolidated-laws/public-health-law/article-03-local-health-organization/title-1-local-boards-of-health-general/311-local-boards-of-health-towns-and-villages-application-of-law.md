# Public Health Law § 311 — Local boards of health; towns and villages; application of law

[Public Health Law](../../README.md) › [Article 3 — Local Health Organization](../README.md) › [Title 1 — Local Boards of Health; General Provisions](README.md)

```text
  § 311. Local boards of health; towns and villages; application of law.
The provisions contained in this article as to the local boards of
health, and for the appointment of local health officers shall apply to
all towns and villages, whether such villages are organized under
general or special laws.
```
