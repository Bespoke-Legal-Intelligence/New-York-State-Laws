[Public Health Law](../../README.md) › [Article 3 — Local Health Organization](../README.md) › [Title 1 — Local Boards of Health; General Provisions](README.md)

```text
  § 303. Local boards of health; notice of organization.  Notice of the
membership and organization of every local board of health shall be
given forthwith by such board to the department.
```
