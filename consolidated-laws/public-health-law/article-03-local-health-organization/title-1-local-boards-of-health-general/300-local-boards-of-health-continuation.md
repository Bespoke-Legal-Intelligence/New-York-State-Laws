# Public Health Law § 300 — Local boards of health; continuation

[Public Health Law](../../README.md) › [Article 3 — Local Health Organization](../README.md) › [Title 1 — Local Boards of Health; General Provisions](README.md)

```text
  § 300. Local boards of health; continuation. There shall continue to
be local boards and departments of health and health officers in the
several counties, cities, villages and towns of the state except as
otherwise provided by law.
```
