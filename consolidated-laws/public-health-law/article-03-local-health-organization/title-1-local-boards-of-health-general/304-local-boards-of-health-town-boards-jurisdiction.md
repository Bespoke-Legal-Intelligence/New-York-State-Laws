# Public Health Law § 304 — Local boards of health; town boards; jurisdiction; appropriations

[Public Health Law](../../README.md) › [Article 3 — Local Health Organization](../README.md) › [Title 1 — Local Boards of Health; General Provisions](README.md)

```text
  § 304. Local boards of health; town boards; jurisdiction;
appropriations.  A town board of health shall not have jurisdiction over
any city or incorporated village or part of such city or village in such
town unless otherwise provided by law. Appropriations for a town board
of health in a town in which there are one or more incorporated villages
shall be a charge upon the taxable property of that part of the town
outside of any incorporated village and shall be assessed, levied and
collected therefrom in the same manner as other town charges levied on
property outside of incorporated villages.
```
