# Public Health Law § 310 — Local boards of health; modification of orders by commissioner

[Public Health Law](../../README.md) › [Article 3 — Local Health Organization](../README.md) › [Title 1 — Local Boards of Health; General Provisions](README.md)

```text
  § 310. Local boards of health; modification of orders by commissioner.
The commissioner may annul or modify an order, regulation, by-law or
ordinance of a local board of health concerning a matter which in his
judgment affects the public health beyond the territory over which such
local board of health has jurisdiction.
```
