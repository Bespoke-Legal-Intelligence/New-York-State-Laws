# Public Health Law § 350 — County or part-county boards of health; officers

[Public Health Law](../../README.md) › [Article 3 — Local Health Organization](../README.md) › [Title 3 — County and Part-county Health Districts](README.md)

```text
  § 350. County or part-county boards of health; officers.  The board of
health of a county or part-county health district shall elect annually
one of its members as president and another as vice-president. The board
of health may designate the county health commissioner to act as its
secretary without extra compensation.
```
