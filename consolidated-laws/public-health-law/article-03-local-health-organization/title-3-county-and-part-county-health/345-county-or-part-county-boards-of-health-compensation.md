# Public Health Law § 345 — County or part-county boards of health; compensation and expenses of members

[Public Health Law](../../README.md) › [Article 3 — Local Health Organization](../README.md) › [Title 3 — County and Part-county Health Districts](README.md)

```text
  § 345. County or part-county boards of health; compensation and
expenses of members. The members of the board of health of a county or
part-county health district shall receive for attendance at meetings of
the board, and, when authorized by the president of the board, within
appropriations made therefor, for meetings of standing committees, a per
diem compensation which shall be fixed by the board of supervisors, and
in addition thereto, they shall be allowed actual and necessary
traveling expenses, to be audited and paid in the same manner as other
expenses of such board of health.
```
