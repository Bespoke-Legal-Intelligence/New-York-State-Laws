# Public Health Law § 394 — Consolidated health district; board of health; officers

[Public Health Law](../../README.md) › [Article 3 — Local Health Organization](../README.md) › [Title 6 — Consolidated Health Districts](README.md)

```text
  § 394. Consolidated health district; board of health; officers.  The
board of health of a consolidated health district shall from time to
time elect a president from among its members. The health officer of a
consolidated health district shall serve as the secretary of the board
of health thereof without additional remuneration therefor.
```
