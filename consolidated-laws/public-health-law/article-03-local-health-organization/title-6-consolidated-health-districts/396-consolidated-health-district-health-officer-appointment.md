# Public Health Law § 396 — Consolidated health district; health officer; appointment

[Public Health Law](../../README.md) › [Article 3 — Local Health Organization](../README.md) › [Title 6 — Consolidated Health Districts](README.md)

```text
  § 396. Consolidated health district; health officer; appointment.  In
each consolidated health district the board of health shall appoint a
health officer qualified as provided in the sanitary code.
```
