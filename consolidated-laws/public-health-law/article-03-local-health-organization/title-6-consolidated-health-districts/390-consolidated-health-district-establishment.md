# Public Health Law § 390 — Consolidated health district; establishment

[Public Health Law](../../README.md) › [Article 3 — Local Health Organization](../README.md) › [Title 6 — Consolidated Health Districts](README.md)

```text
  § 390. Consolidated health district; establishment. The commissioner,
on the written request of the town board of any town and the board of
trustees of any village and the common council or other like authority
of any city, may combine into one health district, hereinafter referred
to as a consolidated health district, any two or more of such towns,
villages or cities and may on the written request of the town board of
any town, board of trustees of any village or common council or other
like authority of any city at any time thereafter set apart such town,
village or city as a separate health district.
```
