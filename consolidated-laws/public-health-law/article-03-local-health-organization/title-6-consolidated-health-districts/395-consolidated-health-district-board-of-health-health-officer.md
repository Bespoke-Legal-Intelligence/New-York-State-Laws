# Public Health Law § 395 — Consolidated health district; board of health; health officer; powers and duties

[Public Health Law](../../README.md) › [Article 3 — Local Health Organization](../README.md) › [Title 6 — Consolidated Health Districts](README.md)

```text
  § 395. Consolidated health district; board of health; health officer;
powers and duties. Each board of health and each health officer of a
consolidated health district shall have all the rights, powers, duties
and obligations conferred and imposed by law upon boards of health and
health officers respectively.
```
