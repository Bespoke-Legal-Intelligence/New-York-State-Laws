# Public Health Law § 382 — State park health district; board of health; powers and duties

[Public Health Law](../../README.md) › [Article 3 — Local Health Organization](../README.md) › [Title 5 — State Park Health Districts](README.md)

```text
  § 382. State park health district; board of health; powers and duties.
The board of health of a state park health district shall have all the
powers and duties of local boards of health and shall appoint a health
officer qualified as provided by the sanitary code.
```
