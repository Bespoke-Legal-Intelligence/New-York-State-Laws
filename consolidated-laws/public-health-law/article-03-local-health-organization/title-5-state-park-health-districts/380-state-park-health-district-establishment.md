# Public Health Law § 380 — State park health district; establishment

[Public Health Law](../../README.md) › [Article 3 — Local Health Organization](../README.md) › [Title 5 — State Park Health Districts](README.md)

```text
  § 380. State park health district; establishment. The commissioner may
in his discretion, from time to time, create health districts comprised
exclusively of lands lying within the boundaries of a state park by
filing in the office of the secretary of state an order defining
generally the boundaries of such district or districts.
```
