# Public Health Law § 381 — State park health district; board of health; membership

[Public Health Law](../../README.md) › [Article 3 — Local Health Organization](../README.md) › [Title 5 — State Park Health Districts](README.md)

```text
  § 381. State park health district; board of health; membership.  Upon
the making and filing of an order by the commissioner creating and
establishing a state park health district pursuant to section three
hundred eighty of this chapter, the local board of health of such
district shall consist of the park commission, and ex officio, the state
commissioner of health or his duly authorized representative.
```
