# Public Health Law § 2711 — New York state kidney disease institute; organization

[Public Health Law](../README.md) › [Article 27-AA — New York State Kidney Disease Institute](README.md)

```text
  § 2711. New York state kidney disease institute; organization.  1. The
New York state kidney disease institute is hereby created within the
department of health.
  2. The commissioner shall appoint a director of the institute and may
employ such assistants and personnel, within the amounts appropriated,
as is necessary to carry out the provisions of this article.
```
