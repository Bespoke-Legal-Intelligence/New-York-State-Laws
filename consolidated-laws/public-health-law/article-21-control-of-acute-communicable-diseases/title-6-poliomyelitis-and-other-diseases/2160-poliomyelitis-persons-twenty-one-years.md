# Public Health Law § 2160 — Poliomyelitis; persons twenty-one years of age and over; medical inspection

[Public Health Law](../../README.md) › [Article 21 — Control of Acute Communicable Diseases](../README.md) › [Title 6 — Poliomyelitis and Other Diseases](README.md)

```text
  § 2160. Poliomyelitis; persons twenty-one years of age and over;
medical inspection.  In addition to the powers and duties prescribed by
section two thousand one hundred of this chapter, every local board of
health and every health officer shall exercise proper and vigilant
medical inspection of all persons twenty-one years of age or over,
infected or who have been infected with poliomyelitis.
```
