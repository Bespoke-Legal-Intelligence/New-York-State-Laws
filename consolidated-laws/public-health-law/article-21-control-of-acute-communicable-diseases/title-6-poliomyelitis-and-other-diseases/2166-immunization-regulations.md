# Public Health Law § 2166 — Immunization; regulations

[Public Health Law](../../README.md) › [Article 21 — Control of Acute Communicable Diseases](../README.md) › [Title 6 — Poliomyelitis and Other Diseases](README.md)

```text
  § 2166. Immunization; regulations. The commissioner may promulgate
regulations with respect to immunization conducted by a home care
services agency licensed pursuant to section thirty-six hundred five of
this chapter or certified pursuant to section thirty-six hundred eight
of this chapter, facilities or institutions established under article
twenty-eight of this chapter, and other health care providers and
practitioners, including, but not limited to, standards for the quality
of care and services provided, consistent with the education law.
```
