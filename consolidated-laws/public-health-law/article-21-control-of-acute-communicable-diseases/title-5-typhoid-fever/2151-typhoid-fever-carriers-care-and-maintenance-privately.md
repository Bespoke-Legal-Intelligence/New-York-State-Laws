# Public Health Law § 2151 — Typhoid fever; carriers; care and maintenance, privately

[Public Health Law](../../README.md) › [Article 21 — Control of Acute Communicable Diseases](../README.md) › [Title 5 — Typhoid Fever](README.md)

```text
  § 2151. Typhoid fever; carriers; care and maintenance, privately.
When no hospital or institution is available for the care of a carrier
of typhoid fever bacilli in accordance with the provisions of section
two thousand one hundred fifty of this chapter, and when in the opinion
of the commissioner such person may be cared for at home or in a private
family with due regard to the protection of the public health, the
commissioner may furnish necessary medical attendance and maintenance
and designate the agency or person through which such medical attendance
and maintenance shall be furnished.
```
