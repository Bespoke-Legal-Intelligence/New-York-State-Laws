# Public Health Law § 2134 — Disclosure of medical information

[Public Health Law](../../README.md) › [Article 21 — Control of Acute Communicable Diseases](../README.md) › [Title 3 — Human Immunodeficiency Virus](README.md)

```text
  § 2134. Disclosure of medical information. Disclosure of medical
information obtained in accordance with this title, including a
diagnosis of HIV infection, may be made only to (a) the protected
individual, (b) the municipal health commissioner or district health
officer if such commissioner or officer is not the examining physician,
and (c) without specifically revealing the identity of the protected
individual, contacts as defined pursuant to subdivision ten of section
twenty-seven hundred eighty of this chapter.
```
