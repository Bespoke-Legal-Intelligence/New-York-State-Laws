# Public Health Law § 2137 — Domestic violence recognition

[Public Health Law](../../README.md) › [Article 21 — Control of Acute Communicable Diseases](../README.md) › [Title 3 — Human Immunodeficiency Virus](README.md)

```text
  § 2137.  Domestic violence recognition. The department shall, in
consultation with the office for the prevention of domestic violence and
statewide organizations and community based organizations, develop a
protocol for the identification and screening of victims of domestic
violence who may either be a protected individual or a contact as used
in this title.
```
