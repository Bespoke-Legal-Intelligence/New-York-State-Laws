# Public Health Law § 2110 — City of New York; exceptions

[Public Health Law](../../README.md) › [Article 21 — Control of Acute Communicable Diseases](../README.md) › [Title 1 — General Provisions](README.md)

```text
  § 2110. City of New York; exceptions.  The provisions of sections two
thousand one hundred to two thousand one hundred eight of this chapter,
inclusive, shall not apply to the city of New York.
```
