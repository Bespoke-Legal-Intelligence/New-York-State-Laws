# Public Health Law § 2105 — Communicable diseases; state institutions; duty to report; duty of commissioner

[Public Health Law](../../README.md) › [Article 21 — Control of Acute Communicable Diseases](../README.md) › [Title 1 — General Provisions](README.md)

```text
  § 2105. Communicable diseases; state institutions; duty to report;
duty of commissioner.  1. It shall be the duty of the director or person
in charge of each state institution to report immediately an outbreak of
a communicable disease in such institution to the commissioner and as
may otherwise be provided in the sanitary code.
  2. Upon receipt of a report from a state institution of the outbreak
of a communicable disease in said institution, the commissioner shall
advise the director or person in charge of the institution as to the
best means to control said disease effectually.
```
