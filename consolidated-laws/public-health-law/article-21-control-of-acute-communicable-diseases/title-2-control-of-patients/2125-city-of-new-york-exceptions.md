# Public Health Law § 2125 — City of New York; exceptions

[Public Health Law](../../README.md) › [Article 21 — Control of Acute Communicable Diseases](../README.md) › [Title 2 — Control of Patients](README.md)

```text
  § 2125. City of New York; exceptions.  The provisions of sections two
thousand one hundred twenty to two thousand one hundred twenty-four of
this chapter, inclusive, shall not apply to the city of New York.
```
