# Public Health Law § 2124 — Communicable diseases; appeal by committed patient

[Public Health Law](../../README.md) › [Article 21 — Control of Acute Communicable Diseases](../README.md) › [Title 2 — Control of Patients](README.md)

```text
  § 2124. Communicable diseases; appeal by committed patient.  Nothing
contained in this article shall be construed to prohibit any person
committed to any institution pursuant to its provisions from appealing
to any court having jurisdiction, for a review of the evidence on which
commitment was made.
```
