# Public Health Law § 2891 — Nurse manpower center

[Public Health Law](../README.md) › [Article 28-C — Nurse Manpower Center](README.md)

```text
  § 2891. Nurse manpower center. The commissioner shall establish within
the department a nurse manpower center for the purposes of instituting
and conducting nurse manpower studies and surveys and the making of
recommendations for the improvement of nursing service.
```
