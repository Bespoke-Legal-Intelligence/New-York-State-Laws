# Public Health Law § 2446 — Rules and regulations

[Public Health Law](../README.md) › [Article 24-A — Protection of Human Subjects](README.md)

```text
  § 2446. Rules and regulations.  The commissioner shall have the power
to promulgate such rules and regulations as shall be necessary and
proper to effectuate the purposes of this article.
```
