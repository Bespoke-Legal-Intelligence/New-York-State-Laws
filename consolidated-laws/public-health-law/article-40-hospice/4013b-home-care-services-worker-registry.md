# Public Health Law § 4013-B — Home care services worker registry

[Public Health Law](../README.md) › [Article 40 — Hospice](README.md)

```text
  § 4013-b. Home care services worker registry. A hospice program
providing hospice care in a patient's home shall comply with the home
care services worker registry under section thirty-six hundred thirteen
of this chapter.
```
