# Public Health Law § 2207 — Tuberculosis; application of law to city of New York

[Public Health Law](../../README.md) › [Article 22 — Control of Tuberculosis](../README.md) › [Title 1 — General Provisions](README.md)

```text
  § 2207. Tuberculosis; application of law to city of New York.  All of
the provisions of sections two thousand two hundred to two thousand two
hundred seven, inclusive, of this chapter, shall apply throughout the
entire state and to each county and city thereof, including the city of
New York.
```
