# Public Health Law § 2225 — Tuberculosis; recovery of patient; duty to report

[Public Health Law](../../README.md) › [Article 22 — Control of Tuberculosis](../README.md) › [Title 2 — Control](README.md)

```text
  § 2225. Tuberculosis; recovery of patient; duty to report.  Upon the
recovery of any person having tuberculosis, it shall be the duty of the
attending physician to make a report of this fact to the proper health
officer, who shall record the same in the records of his office, and
shall relieve said person from further liability to any requirements
imposed by this article.
```
