# Public Health Law § 2227 — Tuberculosis; duty of public health council

[Public Health Law](../../README.md) › [Article 22 — Control of Tuberculosis](../README.md) › [Title 2 — Control](README.md)

```text
  § 2227. Tuberculosis; duty of public health council.  The public
health council shall include in the sanitary code regulations defining
the methods and precautions to be observed in disinfecting, cleansing,
or renovating premises under the provisions of this article.
```
