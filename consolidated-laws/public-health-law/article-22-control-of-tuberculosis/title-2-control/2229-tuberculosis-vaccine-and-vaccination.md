# Public Health Law § 2229 — Tuberculosis; vaccine and vaccination

[Public Health Law](../../README.md) › [Article 22 — Control of Tuberculosis](../README.md) › [Title 2 — Control](README.md)

```text
  § 2229. Tuberculosis; vaccine and vaccination.  1. No vaccine shall be
used for the purpose of inducing immunity against tuberculosis in humans
in this state unless produced in accordance with regulations established
or approved by the commissioner.
  2. Vaccination with such approved vaccine shall be performed only in
accordance with regulations established by the commissioner.
```
