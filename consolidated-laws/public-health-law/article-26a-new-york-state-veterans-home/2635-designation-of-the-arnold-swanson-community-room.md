# Public Health Law § 2635 — Designation of the "Arnold Swanson Community Room"

[Public Health Law](../README.md) › [Article 26-A — New York State Veterans' Home](README.md)

```text
  § 2635. Designation of the "Arnold Swanson Community Room".  The
communal area multi-purpose room of the New York state home for veterans
in the city of Batavia shall be designated and known as the "Arnold
Swanson Community Room" in honor of Arnold Swanson.
```
