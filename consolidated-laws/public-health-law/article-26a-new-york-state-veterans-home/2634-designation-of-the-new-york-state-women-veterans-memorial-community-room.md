# Public Health Law § 2634 — Designation of the "New York State Women Veterans Memorial Community Room"

[Public Health Law](../README.md) › [Article 26-A — New York State Veterans' Home](README.md)

```text
  § 2634. Designation of the "New York State Women Veterans Memorial
Community Room".  The communal area multi-purpose room of the New York
state home for veterans in the city of New York, such room as referred
to as area one hundred ninety-four in the architectural plans and
drawings of such home for veterans, shall be designated and known as the
"New York State Women Veterans Memorial Community Room" in honor of all
women veterans of this state.
```
