# Public Health Law § 4163 — Penalties

[Public Health Law](../../README.md) › [Article 41 — Vital Statistics](../README.md) › [Title 5 — Reporting of Pregnancy Loss](README.md)

```text
  § 4163. Penalties. Any person who shall release information which
might disclose the identity of the pregnant person in connection with a
certificate of pregnancy loss or report of pregnancy loss in violation
of the provisions of this title shall be subject to a civil penalty not
to exceed five thousand dollars for each such release. Such penalty may
be recovered in the same manner as the penalty provided in section
twelve of this chapter.
```
