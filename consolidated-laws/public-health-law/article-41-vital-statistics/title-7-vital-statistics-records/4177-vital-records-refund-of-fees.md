# Public Health Law § 4177 — Vital records; refund of fees

[Public Health Law](../../README.md) › [Article 41 — Vital Statistics](../README.md) › [Title 7 — Vital Statistics Records](README.md)

```text
  § 4177. Vital records; refund of fees. Moneys received pursuant to
this article may within one year from the receipt thereof be refunded on
proof satisfactory to the commissioner that such moneys were in excess
of amounts required by law. Such refund shall after audit by the
comptroller be paid by the commissioner from any moneys in the custody
of the commissioner received pursuant to this article.
```
