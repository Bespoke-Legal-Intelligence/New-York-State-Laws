# Public Health Law § 4135-A — Birth certificate; child born in prison

[Public Health Law](../../README.md) › [Article 41 — Vital Statistics](../README.md) › [Title 3 — Registration of Births](README.md)

```text
  § 4135-a. Birth certificate; child born in prison. There shall be no
specific statement on the birth certificate as to the fact that a child
was born in prison.
```
