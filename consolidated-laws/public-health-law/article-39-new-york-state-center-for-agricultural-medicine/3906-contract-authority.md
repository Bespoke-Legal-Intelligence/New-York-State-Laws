# Public Health Law § 3906 — Contract authority

[Public Health Law](../README.md) › [Article 39 — New York State Center For Agricultural Medicine and Health](README.md)

```text
  § 3906. Contract authority. The center is hereby authorized to enter
into agreements or contracts with any public or private agency,
corporation, or individual in order to carry out the provisions of this
article. Funds made available by the center pursuant to this article
shall be provided pursuant to the terms of such contracts.
```
