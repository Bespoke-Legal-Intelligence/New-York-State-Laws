# Public Health Law § 3902 — New York state center for agricultural medicine and health

[Public Health Law](../README.md) › [Article 39 — New York State Center For Agricultural Medicine and Health](README.md)

```text
  § 3902. New York state center for agricultural medicine and health.
There is hereby established a center for agricultural medicine and
health to operate in conjunction with the Mary Imogene Bassett Hospital,
Cooperstown, New York. Such center shall operate under the guidance of a
director, who shall be appointed by the board of trustees of the Mary
Imogene Bassett Hospital, Cooperstown, New York upon the advice and
consent of the president of such hospital.
```
