# Public Health Law § 1150 — Definitions

[Public Health Law](../../README.md) › [Article 11 — Public Water Supplies; Sewerage and Sewage Control](../README.md) › [Title 3 — Annual Water Supply Statement](README.md)

```text
  § 1150. Definitions.  As used in this title the term "water works
corporation" shall mean any entity that has one thousand or more water
service connections, and comes within the definition in section two of
the public service law or paragraph (a) of subdivision one of section
one hundred twenty-u of the general municipal law.
```
