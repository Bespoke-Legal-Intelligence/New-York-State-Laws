# Public Health Law § 1152-A — New York state public water supply report

[Public Health Law](../../README.md) › [Article 11 — Public Water Supplies; Sewerage and Sewage Control](../README.md) › [Title 3 — Annual Water Supply Statement](README.md)

```text
  § 1152-a. New York state public water supply report. The department,
shall prepare an annual New York state public water supply report. Such
report shall consist of a compilation and analysis of the information
and data submitted by water suppliers pursuant to the provisions of this
article.
```
