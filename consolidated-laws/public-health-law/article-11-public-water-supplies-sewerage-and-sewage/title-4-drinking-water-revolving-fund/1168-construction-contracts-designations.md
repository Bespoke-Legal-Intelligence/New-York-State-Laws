# Public Health Law § 1168 — Construction contracts; designations

[Public Health Law](../../README.md) › [Article 11 — Public Water Supplies; Sewerage and Sewage Control](../README.md) › [Title 4 — Drinking Water Revolving Fund](README.md)

```text
  § 1168.  Construction contracts; designations.  The contracts for the
construction of eligible projects constructed and financed pursuant to a
financing agreement shall be subject to the requirements and provisions
of article fifteen-A of the executive law and, for such purposes, any
such contract shall be considered a "state contract" and the department
shall be the "contracting agency" for each such contract.
```
