# Public Health Law § 1100-A — Fluoridation

[Public Health Law](../../README.md) › [Article 11 — Public Water Supplies; Sewerage and Sewage Control](../README.md) › [Title 1 — Potable Waters](README.md)

```text
  § 1100-a. Fluoridation. 1. Notwithstanding any contrary provision of
law, rule, regulation or code, any county, city, town or village that
owns both its public water system and the water supply for such system
may by local law provide whether a fluoride compound shall or shall not
be added to such public water supply.
  2. Any county, wherein a public authority owns both its public water
system and the water supply for such system, may by local law provide
whether a fluoride compound shall or shall not be added to such public
water supply.
```
