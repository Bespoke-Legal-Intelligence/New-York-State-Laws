# Public Health Law § 1108 — Powers and duties with respect to comprehensive public water supply planning

[Public Health Law](../../README.md) › [Article 11 — Public Water Supplies; Sewerage and Sewage Control](../README.md) › [Title 1 — Potable Waters](README.md)

```text
  § 1108. Powers and duties with respect to comprehensive public water
supply planning.  The commissioner shall perform such duties and
functions with respect to comprehensive public water supply planning as
are provided in part five-A of article five of the conservation law, and
shall have such powers and authority as may be necessary in that regard.
```
