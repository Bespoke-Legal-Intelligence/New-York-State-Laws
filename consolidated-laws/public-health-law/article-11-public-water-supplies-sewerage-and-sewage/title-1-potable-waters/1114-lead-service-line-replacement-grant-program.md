# Public Health Law § 1114 — Lead service line replacement grant program

[Public Health Law](../../README.md) › [Article 11 — Public Water Supplies; Sewerage and Sewage Control](../README.md) › [Title 1 — Potable Waters](README.md)

```text
  § 1114. Lead service line replacement grant program. 1. To the extent
practicable, the department shall allocate appropriated funds equitably
among regions of the state. Within each region, the department shall
give priority to municipalities that have a high percentage of elevated
childhood blood lead levels, based on the most recent available data. In
distributing the awards allocated for each region to such priority
municipalities, the department shall also consider whether the community
is low income and the number of lead service lines in need of
replacement. The department may request that such municipalities provide
such documentation as the department may require to confirm award
eligibility.
  2. The department shall publish information, application forms,
procedures and guidelines relating to the program on its website and in
a manner that is accessible to the public and all potential award
recipients.
```
