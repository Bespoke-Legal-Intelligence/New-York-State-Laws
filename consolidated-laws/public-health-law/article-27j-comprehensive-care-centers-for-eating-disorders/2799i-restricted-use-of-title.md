# Public Health Law § 2799-I — Restricted use of title

[Public Health Law](../README.md) › [Article 27-J — Comprehensive Care Centers For Eating Disorders](README.md)

```text
  § 2799-i. Restricted use of title. No person or entity shall claim,
advertise or imply to consumers, health plans or other health care
providers that such provider or practitioner is a state-identified
comprehensive care center for eating disorders unless it is qualified
pursuant to section twenty-seven hundred ninety-nine-h of this article.
```
