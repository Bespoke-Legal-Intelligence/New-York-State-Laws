# Public Health Law § 1350 — Food places; powers of the commissioner

[Public Health Law](../../README.md) › [Article 13 — Nuisances and Sanitation](../README.md) › [Title 8 — Food Handling](README.md)

```text
  § 1350. Food places; powers of the commissioner.  1. The commissioner
shall have full power and authority to inspect and supervise all public
places in this state in which food is prepared, sold or served.
  2. The commissioner may appoint and designate, from time to time,
persons to make the inspections authorized by this section and section
thirteen hundred fifty-one, of this chapter.
```
