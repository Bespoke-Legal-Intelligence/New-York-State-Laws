# Public Health Law § 1389 — Reports

[Public Health Law](../../README.md) › [Article 13 — Nuisances and Sanitation](../README.md) › [Title 12 — Toxic Substances](README.md)

```text
  § 1389. Reports.  The commissioner of health shall make an initial
report to the governor and the legislature on or before September
fifteenth, nineteen hundred seventy-eight of his progress and a further
report to the governor and the legislature on or before April first,
nineteen hundred eighty-one.
```
