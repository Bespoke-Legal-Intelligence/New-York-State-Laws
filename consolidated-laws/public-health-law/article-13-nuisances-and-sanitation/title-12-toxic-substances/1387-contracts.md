# Public Health Law § 1387 — Contracts

[Public Health Law](../../README.md) › [Article 13 — Nuisances and Sanitation](../README.md) › [Title 12 — Toxic Substances](README.md)

```text
  § 1387. Contracts.  The commissioner of health is authorized to enter
into contracts and agreements with individuals, corporations and
municipalities to perform the study herein directed to alleviate the
specific hazard to which the general public or members thereto may be
exposed as the result of toxic substances emanating from landfills.
```
