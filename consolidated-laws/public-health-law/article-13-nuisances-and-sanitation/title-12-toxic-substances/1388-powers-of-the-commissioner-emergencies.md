# Public Health Law § 1388 — Powers of the commissioner; emergencies

[Public Health Law](../../README.md) › [Article 13 — Nuisances and Sanitation](../README.md) › [Title 12 — Toxic Substances](README.md)

```text
  § 1388. Powers of the commissioner; emergencies.  In case of great and
imminent peril to the health of the general public from such hazards as
may be identified as resulting from exposure to toxic substances
emanating from landfills, the commissioner may declare the existence of
an emergency and take such measures and do such acts as he may deem
reasonably necessary and proper for the preservation and protection of
the public health.
```
