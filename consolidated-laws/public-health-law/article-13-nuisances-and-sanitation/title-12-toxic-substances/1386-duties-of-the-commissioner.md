# Public Health Law § 1386 — Duties of the commissioner

[Public Health Law](../../README.md) › [Article 13 — Nuisances and Sanitation](../README.md) › [Title 12 — Toxic Substances](README.md)

```text
  § 1386. Duties of the commissioner.  The commissioner of health shall
conduct a study of both the long and the short term effects of health
hazards associated with exposure to toxic substances emanating from
certain landfills.
```
