# Public Health Law § 1348 — Hotel sanitation; application of article

[Public Health Law](../../README.md) › [Article 13 — Nuisances and Sanitation](../README.md) › [Title 7 — Hotel Sanitation](README.md)

```text
  § 1348. Hotel sanitation; application of article.  The provisions of
sections thirteen hundred forty-five to thirteen hundred forty-seven,
inclusive, of this chapter, shall not apply to cities having a
population of one million inhabitants or over.
```
