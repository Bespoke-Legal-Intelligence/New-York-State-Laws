# Public Health Law § 1304 — Nuisances; local health officers; general powers

[Public Health Law](../../README.md) › [Article 13 — Nuisances and Sanitation](../README.md) › [Title 1 — General Provisions; Control and Abatement](README.md)

```text
  § 1304. Nuisances; local health officers; general powers.  The local
health officer of a health district having no local board of health and
each county health commissioner shall have authority equal to a local
board of health to investigate and abate public nuisances which may
affect health.
```
