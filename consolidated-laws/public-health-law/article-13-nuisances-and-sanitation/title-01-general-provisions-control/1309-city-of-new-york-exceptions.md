# Public Health Law § 1309 — City of New York; exceptions

[Public Health Law](../../README.md) › [Article 13 — Nuisances and Sanitation](../README.md) › [Title 1 — General Provisions; Control and Abatement](README.md)

```text
  § 1309. City of New York; exceptions.  The provisions of sections one
thousand three hundred three to one thousand three hundred seven of this
chapter, inclusive, shall not apply to the city of New York.
```
