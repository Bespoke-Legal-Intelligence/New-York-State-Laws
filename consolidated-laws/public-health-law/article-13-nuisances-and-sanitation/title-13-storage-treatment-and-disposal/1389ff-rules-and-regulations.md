# Public Health Law § 1389-FF — Rules and regulations

[Public Health Law](../../README.md) › [Article 13 — Nuisances and Sanitation](../README.md) › [Title 13 — Storage, Treatment and Disposal of Regulated Medical Waste](README.md)

```text
  § 1389-ff. Rules and regulations.  The commissioner shall promulgate
rules and regulations in conformity with the standards for generators of
regulated medical waste, and the storage, containment, treatment and
disposal of regulated medical waste found in this title. Nothing in this
title shall authorize the commissioner to adopt or amend any rule or
regulation in a manner inconsistent with the provisions of title fifteen
of article twenty-seven of the environmental conservation law.
```
