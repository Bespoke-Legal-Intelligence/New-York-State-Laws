# Public Health Law § 1336 — Certain businesses prohibited; enforcement; violation

[Public Health Law](../../README.md) › [Article 13 — Nuisances and Sanitation](../README.md) › [Title 5 — Suppression of Certain Businesses](README.md)

```text
  § 1336. Certain businesses prohibited; enforcement; violation.  1. All
departments of health or the commissioner or commissioners thereof in
any incorporated city of this state shall have power to enforce the
provisions of section thirteen hundred thirty-five of this chapter.
  2. Any person violating the provisions of section thirteen hundred
thirty-five of this chapter shall, upon conviction thereof, be guilty of
a misdemeanor.
```
