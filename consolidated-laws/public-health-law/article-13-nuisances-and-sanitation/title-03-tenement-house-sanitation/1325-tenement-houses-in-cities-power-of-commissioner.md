# Public Health Law § 1325 — Tenement houses in cities; power of commissioner

[Public Health Law](../../README.md) › [Article 13 — Nuisances and Sanitation](../README.md) › [Title 3 — Tenement House Sanitation](README.md)

```text
  § 1325. Tenement houses in cities; power of commissioner. 1. The
commissioner shall have power to examine into the enforcement of the
laws relating to tenement houses in any city.
  2. Whenever required by the governor, the commissioner shall make such
an examination and shall report the results thereof to the governor
within the time prescribed by him therefor.
```
