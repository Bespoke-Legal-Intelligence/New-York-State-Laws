# Public Health Law § 2402 — Cancer; reports confidential

[Public Health Law](../../README.md) › [Article 24 — Control of Malignant Diseases](../README.md) › [Title 1 — General Provisions](README.md)

```text
  § 2402. Cancer; reports confidential.  The reports of cancer cases
made pursuant to the provisions of this article shall not be divulged or
made public so as to disclose the identity of any person to whom they
relate, by any person, except in so far as may be authorized in the
sanitary code.
```
