# Public Health Law § 2403 — Cancer; compulsory submission for supervision or inspection not required

[Public Health Law](../../README.md) › [Article 24 — Control of Malignant Diseases](../README.md) › [Title 1 — General Provisions](README.md)

```text
  § 2403. Cancer; compulsory submission for supervision or inspection
not required.  Nothing in this article shall be construed to compel any
individual to submit to medical or health department supervision or
inspection.
```
