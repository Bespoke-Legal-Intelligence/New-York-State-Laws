# Public Health Law § 2404-B — Breast cancer; duty of providers of mammography services to ascertain and provide clinical breast examination information

[Public Health Law](../../README.md) › [Article 24 — Control of Malignant Diseases](../README.md) › [Title 1 — General Provisions](README.md)

```text
  § 2404-b. Breast cancer; duty of providers of mammography services to
ascertain and provide clinical breast examination information. Every
provider who provides mammography services shall ascertain when, or if,
their patients have had a clinical breast examination prior to providing
such service and shall include such information with any mammography
results or reports to alert healthcare providers if a clinical breast
examination has not recently been performed. Criminal penalties for
violation pursuant to subdivisions one and two of section twelve-b of
this chapter shall not apply to this section.
```
