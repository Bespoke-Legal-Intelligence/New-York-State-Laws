# Public Health Law § 2422 — Cancer institute; commissioner's duty to publish reports

[Public Health Law](../../README.md) › [Article 24 — Control of Malignant Diseases](../README.md) › [Title 2 — State Institute For the Study of Malignant Diseases](README.md)

```text
  § 2422. Cancer institute; commissioner's duty to publish reports.  The
commissioner shall publish from time to time the result of the
investigations by the state institute for the study of malignant
diseases for the benefit of humanity, and from time to time, he shall
collate its publications in a scientific report for distribution to
scientific organizations and to medical scientists and qualified members
of the medical profession.
```
