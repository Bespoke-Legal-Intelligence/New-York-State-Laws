# Public Health Law § 1391 — Declaration of public policy

[Public Health Law](../README.md) › [Article 13-B — Regulation of Overnight, Summer Day, and Traveling Summer Day Camps For Children](README.md)

```text
  § 1391. Declaration of public policy. It is the declared policy of the
state of New York to protect the health, safety and welfare of children
attending what are commonly called children's overnight, summer day, and
traveling summer day camps.
```
