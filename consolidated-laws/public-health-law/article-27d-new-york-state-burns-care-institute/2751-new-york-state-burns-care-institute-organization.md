# Public Health Law § 2751 — New York state burns care institute; organization

[Public Health Law](../README.md) › [Article 27-D — New York State Burns Care Institute](README.md)

```text
  § 2751. New York state burns care institute; organization.  1. The New
York state burns care institute is hereby created within the department
of health.
  2. The commissioner shall appoint a director of the institute and may
employ such assistants and personnel as are necessary to carry out the
provisions of this article.
```
