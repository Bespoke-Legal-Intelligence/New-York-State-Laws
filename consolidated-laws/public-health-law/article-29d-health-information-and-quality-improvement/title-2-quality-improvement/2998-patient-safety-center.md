# Public Health Law § 2998 — Patient safety center

[Public Health Law](../../README.md) › [Article 29-D — Health Information and Quality Improvement](../README.md) › [Title 2 — Quality Improvement](README.md)

```text
  § 2998. Patient safety center. 1. There is established within the
department a patient safety center, referred to in this article as "the
center".
  2. The purpose of the center is to maximize patient safety, reduce
medical errors, and improve the quality of health care by improving
systems of data reporting, collection, analysis and dissemination, and
to improve public access to health care information not otherwise
restricted.
```
