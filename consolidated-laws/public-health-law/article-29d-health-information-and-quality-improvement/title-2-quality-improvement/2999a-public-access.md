# Public Health Law § 2999-A — Public access

[Public Health Law](../../README.md) › [Article 29-D — Health Information and Quality Improvement](../README.md) › [Title 2 — Quality Improvement](README.md)

```text
  § 2999-a. Public access. Nothing in this title shall abridge public
access to information currently available or permitted by any other
provision or rule of law. Nothing in this title shall authorize the
disclosure of otherwise confidential information, except pursuant to the
express terms of this title.
```
