# Public Health Law § 2998-D — Voluntary and collaborative reporting system

[Public Health Law](../../README.md) › [Article 29-D — Health Information and Quality Improvement](../README.md) › [Title 2 — Quality Improvement](README.md)

```text
  § 2998-d. Voluntary and collaborative reporting system. The center, in
collaboration with health care providers, shall develop a voluntary and
collaborative reporting system, for the purposes of obtaining
information useful for maximizing patient safety, reducing medical
errors, and improving the quality of health care pursuant to this title.
```
