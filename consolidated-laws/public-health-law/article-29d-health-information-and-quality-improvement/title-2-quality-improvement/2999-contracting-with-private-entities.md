# Public Health Law § 2999 — Contracting with private entities

[Public Health Law](../../README.md) › [Article 29-D — Health Information and Quality Improvement](../README.md) › [Title 2 — Quality Improvement](README.md)

```text
  § 2999. Contracting with private entities. The center may contract
with one or more private entities to undertake the duties delineated in
this title. Such entities shall remain subject to existing
confidentiality protections.
```
