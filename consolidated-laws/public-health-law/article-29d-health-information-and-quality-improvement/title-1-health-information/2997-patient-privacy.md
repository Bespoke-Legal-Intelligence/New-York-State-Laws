# Public Health Law § 2997 — Patient privacy

[Public Health Law](../../README.md) › [Article 29-D — Health Information and Quality Improvement](../README.md) › [Title 1 — Health Information](README.md)

```text
  § 2997. Patient privacy. 1. The department shall examine state laws
protecting the privacy of individual patient data, and determine the
necessity of supplementing existing protections.
  2. The department shall examine federal initiatives protecting the
privacy of individual patient data, and such federal initiatives'
potential preemptive effect on state law protections.
  3. The department shall provide a report of its findings to the
governor and the legislature within eighteen months of the effective
date of this section.
```
