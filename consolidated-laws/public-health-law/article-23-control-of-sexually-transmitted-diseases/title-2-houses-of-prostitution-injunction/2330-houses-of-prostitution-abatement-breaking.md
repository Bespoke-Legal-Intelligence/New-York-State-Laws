# Public Health Law § 2330 — Houses of prostitution; abatement; breaking, entering or using closed building

[Public Health Law](../../README.md) › [Article 23 — Control of Sexually Transmitted Diseases](../README.md) › [Title 2 — Houses of Prostitution: Injunction and Abatement](README.md)

```text
  § 2330. Houses of prostitution; abatement; breaking, entering or using
closed building. If any person shall break and enter, or use a building,
erection, or place directed to be closed pursuant to the provisions of
this article, he shall be punished as for contempt as provided in this
article.
```
