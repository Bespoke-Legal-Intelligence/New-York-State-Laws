# Public Health Law § 2334 — Houses of prostitution; criminal proceedings; duty of district attorney

[Public Health Law](../../README.md) › [Article 23 — Control of Sexually Transmitted Diseases](../README.md) › [Title 2 — Houses of Prostitution: Injunction and Abatement](README.md)

```text
  § 2334. Houses of prostitution; criminal proceedings; duty of district
attorney.  1. In case the existence of a nuisance as defined in this
article, is established, in a criminal action in a court other than the
supreme court it shall be the duty of the district attorney to proceed
promptly under the provisions of this article to enforce the provisions
and penalties thereof.
  2. The conviction of the defendant in such criminal action, unless
reversed or set aside, shall be conclusive as against such defendant as
to the existence of the nuisance.
```
