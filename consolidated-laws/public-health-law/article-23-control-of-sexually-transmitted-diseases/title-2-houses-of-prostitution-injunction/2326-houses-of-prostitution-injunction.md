# Public Health Law § 2326 — Houses of prostitution; injunction; dismissal; costs against plaintiff

[Public Health Law](../../README.md) › [Article 23 — Control of Sexually Transmitted Diseases](../README.md) › [Title 2 — Houses of Prostitution: Injunction and Abatement](README.md)

```text
  § 2326. Houses of prostitution; injunction; dismissal; costs against
plaintiff.  If an action instituted pursuant to the provisions of this
article, is brought by any person or association and the court finds
there were not reasonable grounds or cause for said action the costs may
be taxed against such person or association.
```
