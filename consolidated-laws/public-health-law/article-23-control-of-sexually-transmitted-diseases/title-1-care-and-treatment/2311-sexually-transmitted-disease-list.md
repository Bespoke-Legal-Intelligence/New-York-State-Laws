# Public Health Law § 2311 — Sexually transmitted disease list

[Public Health Law](../../README.md) › [Article 23 — Control of Sexually Transmitted Diseases](../README.md) › [Title 1 — Care and Treatment](README.md)

```text
  § 2311. Sexually transmitted disease list. The commissioner shall
promulgate a list of sexually transmitted diseases, such as gonorrhea
and syphilis, for the purposes of this article. The commissioner, in
determining the diseases to be included in such list, shall consider
those conditions principally transmitted by sexual contact, other
sections of this chapter addressing communicable diseases and the impact
of particular diseases on individual morbidity and the health of
newborns.
```
