# Public Health Law § 2307 — Venereal disease; person knowing himself to be infected

[Public Health Law](../../README.md) › [Article 23 — Control of Sexually Transmitted Diseases](../README.md) › [Title 1 — Care and Treatment](README.md)

```text
  § 2307. Venereal disease; person knowing himself to be infected.  Any
person who, knowing himself or herself to be infected with an infectious
venereal disease, has sexual intercourse with another shall be guilty of
a misdemeanor.
```
