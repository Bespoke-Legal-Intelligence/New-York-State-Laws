# Public Health Law § 2721 — Mary Lasker Heart and Hypertension Institute; organization

[Public Health Law](../../README.md) › [Article 27-B — Treatment of Hypertension](../README.md) › [Title 1 — Mary Lasker Heart and Hypertension Institute](README.md)

```text
  § 2721. Mary Lasker Heart and Hypertension Institute; organization.
The commissioner shall establish within the department a heart and
hypertension institute to be named the Mary Lasker Heart and
Hypertension Institute for the purpose of promoting the screening,
evaluation and methods of treatment of hypertension.
```
