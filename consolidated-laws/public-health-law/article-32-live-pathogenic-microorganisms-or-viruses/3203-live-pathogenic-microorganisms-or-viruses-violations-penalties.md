# Public Health Law § 3203 — Live pathogenic microorganisms or viruses; violations; penalties

[Public Health Law](../README.md) › [Article 32 — Live Pathogenic Microorganisms or Viruses](README.md)

```text
  § 3203. Live pathogenic microorganisms or viruses; violations;
penalties.  Any violation of this article shall be deemed a misdemeanor
punishable by a fine of two hundred dollars, six months' imprisonment or
both.
```
