# Public Health Law § 2594-C — Criteria for admittance into the New York state community doula directory

[Public Health Law](../../README.md) › [Article 25 — Maternal and Child Health](../README.md) › [Title 6-A — Doulas For Medicaid](README.md)

```text
  * § 2594-c. Criteria for admittance into the New York state community
doula directory. 1. Each doula seeking Medicaid reimbursement shall
apply to be registered in the New York state community doula directory.
  2. A doula shall submit to the department a certified copy of such
doula's certification from a doula educational organization or by
demonstration of satisfactory proficiency pursuant to this section, and
his, her, or their full name, address, phone number, email address, and
if applicable, their national provider identification number.
  * NB Effective and Repealed August 6, 2024
```
