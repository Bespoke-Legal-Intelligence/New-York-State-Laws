# Public Health Law § 2594-A — Definitions

[Public Health Law](../../README.md) › [Article 25 — Maternal and Child Health](../README.md) › [Title 6-A — Doulas For Medicaid](README.md)

```text
  * § 2594-a. Definitions. 1. For the purposes of this title, "doula"
means a trained person who provides continuous physical, emotional, and
informational support to a pregnant person and the family of such
pregnant person before, during or a reasonable time after childbirth.
  2. A doula may provide services in hospitals, birthing centers, at
home deliveries and other community settings.
  * NB Effective August 6, 2024
```
