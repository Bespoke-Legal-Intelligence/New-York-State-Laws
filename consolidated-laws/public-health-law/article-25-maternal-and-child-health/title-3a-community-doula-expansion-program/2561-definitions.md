# Public Health Law § 2561 — Definitions

[Public Health Law](../../README.md) › [Article 25 — Maternal and Child Health](../README.md) › [Title 3-A — Community Doula Expansion Program](README.md)

```text
  * § 2561. Definitions. As used in this title:
  1. "Eligible providers" shall mean community-based organizations
providing for the recruitment, training, certification, supporting,
and/or mentoring of community-based doulas.
  2. "Community-based doula" shall mean a certified doula that provides
culturally sensitive pregnancy and childbirth education, early linkage
to health care, and aids birthing persons in navigating other services
and supports that they may need to be healthy.
  * NB Repealed March 31, 2025
```
