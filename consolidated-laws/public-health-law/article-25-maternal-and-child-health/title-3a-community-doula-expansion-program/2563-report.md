# Public Health Law § 2563 — Report

[Public Health Law](../../README.md) › [Article 25 — Maternal and Child Health](../README.md) › [Title 3-A — Community Doula Expansion Program](README.md)

```text
  * § 2563. Report. Upon expiration of the program, the commissioner
shall post a final report on the department's website outlining the
total number of grants awarded, the names of eligible providers awarded
funds pursuant to the program, and the amount of funding received by
each.
```
