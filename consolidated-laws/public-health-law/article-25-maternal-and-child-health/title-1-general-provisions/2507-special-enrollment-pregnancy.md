# Public Health Law § 2507 — Special enrollment; pregnancy

[Public Health Law](../../README.md) › [Article 25 — Maternal and Child Health](../README.md) › [Title 1 — General Provisions](README.md)

```text
  § 2507. Special enrollment; pregnancy. An insurer, subject to the
provisions of article thirty-two of the insurance law, a corporation
organized pursuant to article forty-three of the insurance law, or a
licensed maintenance organization under article forty-four of this
chapter, that issues a health insurance policy or contract that provides
eligibility for a special enrollment period shall allow for the
enrollment of a pregnant individual at any time after the commencement
of the pregnancy, as certified by a health care practitioner licensed
pursuant to title eight of the education law, acting within his or her
scope of practice. Coverage shall be deemed effective as of the first of
the month in which the individual receives certification of the
pregnancy.
```
