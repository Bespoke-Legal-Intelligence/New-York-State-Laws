# Public Health Law § 2509-A — Corded window blind leaflet

[Public Health Law](../../README.md) › [Article 25 — Maternal and Child Health](../README.md) › [Title 1 — General Provisions](README.md)

```text
  § 2509-a. Corded window blind leaflet. The commissioner, in
consultation with the secretary of state, shall create a leaflet
regarding the danger that corded window blinds pose to children
including proper installation of blinds, examples of current standards
and the potential fatal consequences from strangulation posed by corded
window blinds. Such leaflet shall be posted on the department's website
in a printable format to allow each pediatric primary care provider to
provide the leaflet to parents at a child's six month well child visit.
```
