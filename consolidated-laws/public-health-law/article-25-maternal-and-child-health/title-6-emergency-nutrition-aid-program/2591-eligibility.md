# Public Health Law § 2591 — Eligibility

[Public Health Law](../../README.md) › [Article 25 — Maternal and Child Health](../README.md) › [Title 6 — Emergency Nutrition Aid Program For Pregnant Women and Adolescents](README.md)

```text
  * § 2591. Eligibility.  Eligibility for the program shall be limited
to pregnant women and adolescents who, because of low income and dietary
deficiencies, are at high risk endangering the nutritional well-being of
their fetuses and risking the delivery of low birth weight infants.
  * NB Expired March 31, 1986
```
