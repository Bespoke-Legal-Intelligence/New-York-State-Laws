# Public Health Law § 2580 — Children with physical disabilities; policy

[Public Health Law](../../README.md) › [Article 25 — Maternal and Child Health](../README.md) › [Title 5 — Children With Physical Disabilities](README.md)

```text
  § 2580. Children with physical disabilities; policy.  It is the policy
of the state of New York to provide medical service for the treatment
and rehabilitation of children with physical disabilities.
```
