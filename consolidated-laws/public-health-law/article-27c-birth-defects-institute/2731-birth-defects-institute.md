# Public Health Law § 2731 — Birth defects institute

[Public Health Law](../README.md) › [Article 27-C — Birth Defects Institute](README.md)

```text
  § 2731. Birth defects institute.  The commissioner shall establish
within the department a birth defects institute for the purposes of
initiating and conducting investigations of the causes, mortality,
methods of treatment, prevention and cure of birth defects and genetic
and allied diseases.
```
