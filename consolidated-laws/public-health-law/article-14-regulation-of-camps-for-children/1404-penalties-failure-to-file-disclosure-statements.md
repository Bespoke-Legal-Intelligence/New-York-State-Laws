# Public Health Law § 1404 — Penalties; failure to file disclosure statements

[Public Health Law](../README.md) › [Article 14 — Regulation of Camps For Children](README.md)

```text
  § 1404. Penalties; failure to file disclosure statements.  The
attorney general may seek an injunction against any children's camp,
camp owner, operator, director or officer enjoining the solicitation of
enrollments for children in such camps who reside within the state of
New York for failure to file a disclosure statement or repeated failure
to comply with the provisions of section fourteen hundred two of this
article.
```
