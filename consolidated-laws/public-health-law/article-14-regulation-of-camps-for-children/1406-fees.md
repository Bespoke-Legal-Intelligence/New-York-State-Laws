# Public Health Law § 1406 — Fees

[Public Health Law](../README.md) › [Article 14 — Regulation of Camps For Children](README.md)

```text
  § 1406. Fees.  Upon filing the initial disclosure statement with the
department, the fee shall be fifty dollars, except that no fee shall be
charged in the case of camps operated by a person, firm, corporation or
association for charitable, philanthropic, or religious purposes.
  No filing fee shall be required for the filing of any amended
disclosure statement or for the filing of subsequent disclosure
statements pursuant to the annual filing requirements of section
fourteen hundred one of this article.
```
