# Public Health Law § 1403 — Designation of secretary of state as agent for serving process

[Public Health Law](../README.md) › [Article 14 — Regulation of Camps For Children](README.md)

```text
  § 1403. Designation of secretary of state as agent for serving
process.  Every children's camp, operator and owner of such camp shall
be deemed to have designated the secretary of state of the state of New
York for service of process.  Service shall be made by delivering the
summons to the secretary of state or his designated representative at an
office of the secretary of state within the state.
```
