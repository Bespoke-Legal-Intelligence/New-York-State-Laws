# Public Health Law § 2968 — Effect of order not to resuscitate on other treatment

[Public Health Law](../README.md) › [Article 29-B — Orders Not to Resuscitate For Residents of Mental Hygiene Facilities](README.md)

**Repealed** (29 Mar 2024). The text below is what the Senate publishes for this repealed provision.

```text
  * § 2968. Effect of order not to resuscitate on other treatment.
Consent to the issuance of an order not to resuscitate shall not
constitute consent to withhold or withdraw medical treatment other than
cardiopulmonary resuscitation.
  * NB Repealed March 21, 2024
```
