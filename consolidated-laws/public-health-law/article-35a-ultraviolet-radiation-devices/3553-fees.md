# Public Health Law § 3553 — Fees

[Public Health Law](../README.md) › [Article 35-A — Ultraviolet Radiation Devices](README.md)

```text
  § 3553. Fees. All monies collected by the department pursuant to
sections thirty-five hundred fifty-one and thirty-five hundred
fifty-four of this article shall be deposited into the ultraviolet
radiation devices program account within the special revenue
fund--other, which is hereby established. Monies in such account may be
used to offset costs to the state of registering, inspecting and
regulating tanning facilities.
```
