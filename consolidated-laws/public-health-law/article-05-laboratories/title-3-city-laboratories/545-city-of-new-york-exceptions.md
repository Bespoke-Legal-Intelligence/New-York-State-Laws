# Public Health Law § 545 — City of New York; exceptions

[Public Health Law](../../README.md) › [Article 5 — Laboratories](../README.md) › [Title 3 — City Laboratories](README.md)

```text
  § 545. City of New York; exceptions. Unless otherwise expressly
provided, the provisions of sections five hundred forty to five hundred
forty-four of this chapter, inclusive, shall not apply to the city of
New York.
```
