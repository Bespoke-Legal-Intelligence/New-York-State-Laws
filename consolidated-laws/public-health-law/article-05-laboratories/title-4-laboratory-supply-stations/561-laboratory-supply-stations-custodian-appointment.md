# Public Health Law § 561 — Laboratory supply stations; custodian; appointment

[Public Health Law](../../README.md) › [Article 5 — Laboratories](../README.md) › [Title 4 — Laboratory Supply Stations](README.md)

```text
  § 561. Laboratory supply stations; custodian; appointment. The
commissioner may appoint the health officer of any municipality or
county, the director or person in charge of any public health
laboratory, or other competent person, to serve as the custodian of the
district laboratory supply station.
```
