# Public Health Law § 2854 — Consent of commissioner

[Public Health Law](../README.md) › [Article 28-A — Nursing Home Companies](README.md)

```text
  § 2854. Consent of commissioner. Whenever any such certificate shall
be presented to the secretary of state, he shall not file such
certificate unless there shall accompany the same a certificate of the
commissioner that he consents to the filing of such certificate; nor
shall any amendment to the certificate of incorporation be filed unless
it is accompanied by a certificate of the commissioner consenting
thereto.
```
