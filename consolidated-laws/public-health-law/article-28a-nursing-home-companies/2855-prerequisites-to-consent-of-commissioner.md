# Public Health Law § 2855 — Prerequisites to consent of commissioner

[Public Health Law](../README.md) › [Article 28-A — Nursing Home Companies](README.md)

```text
  § 2855. Prerequisites to consent of commissioner. The commissioner
shall not approve any such certificate or amendment thereto unless and
until there is endorsed thereon or annexed thereto the written approval
of the public health council in accordance with section two thousand
eight hundred one-a of the public health law.
```
