# Public Health Law § 2498 — Provision of summary by physician

[Public Health Law](../README.md) › [Article 24-E — Information About Hysterectomy](README.md)

```text
  § 2498. Provision of summary by physician.  The summary shall be
provided by a physician to each person under such physician's care, when
a hysterectomy is under consideration for that person.
```
