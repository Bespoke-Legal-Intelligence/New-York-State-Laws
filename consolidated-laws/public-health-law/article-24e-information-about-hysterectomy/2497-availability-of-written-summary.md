# Public Health Law § 2497 — Availability of written summary

[Public Health Law](../README.md) › [Article 24-E — Information About Hysterectomy](README.md)

```text
  § 2497. Availability of written summary.  The commissioner shall make
the summary provided for in section twenty-four hundred ninety-six of
this article available free of charge to all clinics, health maintenance
organizations, hospitals, and physician offices.
```
