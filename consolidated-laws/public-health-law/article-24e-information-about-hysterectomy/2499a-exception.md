# Public Health Law § 2499-A — Exception

[Public Health Law](../README.md) › [Article 24-E — Information About Hysterectomy](README.md)

```text
  § 2499-a. Exception.  The dispensing of the standard written summary
shall not pertain when the hysterectomy is performed in a situation of
imminent danger in which the physician determines prior dispensing is
not possible or feasible.
```
