# Public Health Law § 2803-Y — Provision of residency agreement

[Public Health Law](../README.md) › [Article 28 — Hospitals](README.md)

```text
  * § 2803-y. Provision of residency agreement. The operator of a
residential health care facility shall provide to prospective residents
who inquire about admission, and post on its website, a copy of the
facility's entire approved residency agreement, including the
non-governmental rates charged to residents.
  * NB Effective April 14, 2020
```
