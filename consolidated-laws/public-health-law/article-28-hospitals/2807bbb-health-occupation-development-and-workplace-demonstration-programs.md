# Public Health Law § 2807-BBB — Health occupation development and workplace demonstration programs

[Public Health Law](../README.md) › [Article 28 — Hospitals](README.md)

```text
  § 2807-bbb. Health occupation development and workplace demonstration
programs. Subject to the availability of funds, the provisions of clause
(B) of subparagraph (iii) of paragraph (e) of subdivision one of section
twenty-eight hundred seven-c of this article shall apply to diagnostic
and treatment centers.
```
