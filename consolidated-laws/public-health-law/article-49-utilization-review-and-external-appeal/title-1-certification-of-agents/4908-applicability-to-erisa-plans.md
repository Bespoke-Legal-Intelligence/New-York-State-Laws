# Public Health Law § 4908 — Applicability to ERISA Plans

[Public Health Law](../../README.md) › [Article 49 — Utilization Review and External Appeal](../README.md) › [Title 1 — Certification of Agents and Utilization Review Process](README.md)

```text
  § 4908. Applicability to ERISA Plans. Notwithstanding the foregoing,
the provisions of this article shall not apply to any utilization review
conducted by, or on behalf of, a self-insured employee welfare benefit
plan, as defined in the employee retirement income security act of 1974,
as amended.
```
