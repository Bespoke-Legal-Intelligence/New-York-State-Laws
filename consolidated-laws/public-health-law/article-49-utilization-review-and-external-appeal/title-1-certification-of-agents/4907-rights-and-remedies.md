# Public Health Law § 4907 — Rights and remedies

[Public Health Law](../../README.md) › [Article 49 — Utilization Review and External Appeal](../README.md) › [Title 1 — Certification of Agents and Utilization Review Process](README.md)

```text
  § 4907. Rights and remedies. The rights and remedies conferred in this
article upon enrollees and health care providers shall be cumulative and
in addition to and not in lieu of any other rights or remedies available
under law.
```
