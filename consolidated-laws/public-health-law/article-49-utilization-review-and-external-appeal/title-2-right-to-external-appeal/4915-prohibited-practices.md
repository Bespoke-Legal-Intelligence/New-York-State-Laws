# Public Health Law § 4915 — Prohibited practices

[Public Health Law](../../README.md) › [Article 49 — Utilization Review and External Appeal](../README.md) › [Title 2 — Right to External Appeal](README.md)

```text
  § 4915. Prohibited practices. An external appeal agent shall not, with
respect to external appeal activities, permit or provide compensation or
anything of value to its employees, agents, or contractors based on:
  1. either a percentage of the amount by which a claim is reduced for
payment or the number of claims or the cost of services for which the
person has denied authorization or payment; or
  2. any other method that encourages the upholding of an adverse
determination.
```
