# Public Health Law § 3350 — Dispensing prohibition

[Public Health Law](../../README.md) › [Article 33 — Controlled Substances](../README.md) › [Title 5 — Dispensing to Persons With Substance Use Disorder and Habitual Users](README.md)

```text
  § 3350. Dispensing prohibition. Controlled substances may not be
prescribed for, or administered or dispensed to persons with substance
use disorder or habitual users of controlled substances, except as
provided by this title or title III.
```
