# Public Health Law § 3384 — Information program for retailers

[Public Health Law](../../README.md) › [Article 33 — Controlled Substances](../README.md) › [Title 7 — Offenses, Violations and Enforcement](README.md)

```text
  § 3384. Information program for retailers. The department shall
develop and maintain a program to inform retailers about the
methamphetamine problem in New York state.
```
