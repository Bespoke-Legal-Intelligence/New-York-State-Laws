# Public Health Law § 3369-C — Suspend; terminate

[Public Health Law](../../README.md) › [Article 33 — Controlled Substances](../README.md) › [Title 5-A — Medical Use of Marihuana](README.md)

```text
  * § 3369-c. Suspend; terminate. Based upon the recommendation of the
commissioner and/or the superintendent of state police that there is a
risk to the public health or safety, the governor may immediately
terminate all licenses issued to registered organizations.
  * NB Repealed July 5, 2021
```
