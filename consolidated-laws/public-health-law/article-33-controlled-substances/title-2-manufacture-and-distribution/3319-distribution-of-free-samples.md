# Public Health Law § 3319 — Distribution of free samples

[Public Health Law](../../README.md) › [Article 33 — Controlled Substances](../README.md) › [Title 2 — Manufacture and Distribution of Controlled Substances](README.md)

```text
  § 3319. Distribution of free samples.  It shall be unlawful to
distribute free samples of controlled substances, except to persons
licensed pursuant to title III of this article.
```
