# Public Health Law § 4806 — Public inquiries

[Public Health Law](../README.md) › [Article 48 — Toxic Substances](README.md)

```text
  § 4806. Public inquiries. Within the limits of any moneys appropriated
therefor, the department shall process all inquiries it receives from
manufacturers, importers and employers regarding the toxic effects and
circumstances under which these effects are produced for toxic
substances found in the workplace.
```
