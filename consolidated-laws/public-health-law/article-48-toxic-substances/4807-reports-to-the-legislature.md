# Public Health Law § 4807 — Reports to the legislature

[Public Health Law](../README.md) › [Article 48 — Toxic Substances](README.md)

**Repealed** (4 Aug 2017). The text below is what the Senate publishes for this repealed provision.

```text
  § 4807. Reports to the legislature. The commissioner, in consultation
with the industrial commissioner, shall submit an annual evaluation
report on the program outlined in this article to the governor and to
the members of the legislature. The report shall include a statement of
the scope, status and quality of the program, the costs associated with
the program, recommendations for continuation of the program and any
other pertinent information.
```
