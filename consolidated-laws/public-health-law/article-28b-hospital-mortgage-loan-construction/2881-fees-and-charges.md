# Public Health Law § 2881 — Fees and charges

[Public Health Law](../README.md) › [Article 28-B — Hospital Mortgage Loan Construction](README.md)

```text
  § 2881. Fees and charges. The commissioner may, by regulation,
establish and charge to eligible borrowers, for the period from
occupancy date to mortgage discharge, a fee for inspection, regulation,
supervision and audit not to annually exceed two-tenths of one percent
of the mortgage loan to recover the departmental costs in performing
these functions in relation to any hospital project financed or
refinanced by a loan made under this article prior to April first, two
thousand fourteen.  Notwithstanding the foregoing, no such fee shall be
charged or payable pursuant to this section with respect to a hospital
project financed or refinanced with bonds issued on or after April
first, two thousand fourteen.
```
