# Public Health Law § 4210-A — Unlawful dissection of the body of a human being

[Public Health Law](../../README.md) › [Article 42 — Cadavers](../README.md) › [Title 2 — Autopsy and Dissection](README.md)

```text
  § 4210-a. Unlawful dissection of the body of a human being. A person
who makes, or causes or procures to be made, any dissection of the body
of a human being, except by authority of law, or in pursuance of a
permission given by the deceased, is guilty of a class E felony.
```
