# Public Health Law § 4220 — Disturbing funerals

[Public Health Law](../../README.md) › [Article 42 — Cadavers](../README.md) › [Title 2 — Autopsy and Dissection](README.md)

```text
  § 4220. Disturbing funerals. A person who, without authority of law,
obstructs or detains any persons engaged in carrying or accompanying the
dead body of a human being to a place of burial, is guilty of a
misdemeanor.
```
