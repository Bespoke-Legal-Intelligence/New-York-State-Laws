# Public Health Law § 2799-Q — Participation in New York wellness works

[Public Health Law](../README.md) › [Article 27-K — New York Wellness Works](README.md)

```text
  § 2799-q. Participation in New York wellness works. The commissioner,
in consultation with the advisory panel, shall establish criteria for
implementation and operation of the New York wellness works program,
which at a minimum shall include:
  (a) applicant eligibility and selection criteria;
  (b) program operating requirements and standards;
  (c) employee safeguards.
```
