# Public Health Law § 615 — State aid; form

[Public Health Law](../../README.md) › [Article 6 — State Aid to Cities and Counties](../README.md) › [Title 3 — State Aid; General Provisions](README.md)

```text
  § 615. State aid; form. The appropriations made or to be made for the
purposes of carrying out the provisions of this article shall be
available, in accordance with certificates of approval issued or to be
issued by the director of the budget, to the commissioner for the
payment of state aid to municipalities.
```
