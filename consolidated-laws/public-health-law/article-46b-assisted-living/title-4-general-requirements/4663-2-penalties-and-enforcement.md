# Public Health Law § 4663*2 — Penalties and enforcement

[Public Health Law](../../README.md) › [Article 46-B — Assisted Living](../README.md) › [Title 4 — General Requirements](README.md)

*An asterisk in a number (for example Article 24\* or § 20-C\*2) is the Senate's mark for a number the Legislature has used more than once: the marked article or section is a different one from the unmarked one with the same number. \*2 means the second one enacted with that number.*

```text
  * § 4663. Penalties and enforcement. Any person who violates any
provision of this article or any rule or regulation promulgated by the
department, or the terms or conditions of any order or permit issued by
the department pursuant to this article, shall be subject to the maximum
penalties which may be levied against a licensed adult care facility.
  * NB There are 2 § 4663's
```
