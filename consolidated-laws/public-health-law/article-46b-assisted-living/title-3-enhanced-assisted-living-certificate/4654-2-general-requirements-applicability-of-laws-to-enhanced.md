# Public Health Law § 4654*2 — General requirements; applicability of laws to enhanced assisted living

[Public Health Law](../../README.md) › [Article 46-B — Assisted Living](../README.md) › [Title 3 — Enhanced Assisted Living Certificate](README.md)

*An asterisk in a number (for example Article 24\* or § 20-C\*2) is the Senate's mark for a number the Legislature has used more than once: the marked article or section is a different one from the unmarked one with the same number. \*2 means the second one enacted with that number.*

```text
  * § 4654. General requirements; applicability of laws to enhanced
assisted living. Nothing in this article shall require a residence to
obtain an enhanced assisted living certificate pursuant to this title
unless such residence elects to provide aging in place by retaining
residents described in subdivision fifteen of section four thousand six
hundred fifty-one of this article.
  * NB There are 2 § 4654's
```
