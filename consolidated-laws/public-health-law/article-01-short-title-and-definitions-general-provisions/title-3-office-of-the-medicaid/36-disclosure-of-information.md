# Public Health Law § 36 — Disclosure of information

[Public Health Law](../../README.md) › [Article 1 — Short Title and Definitions: General Provisions](../README.md) › [Title 3 — Office of the Medicaid Inspector General](README.md)

```text
  § 36. Disclosure of information. The inspector shall not disclose
information which is prohibited from disclosure by any other provision
of law.
```
