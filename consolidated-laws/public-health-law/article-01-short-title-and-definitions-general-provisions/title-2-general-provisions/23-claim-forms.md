# Public Health Law § 23 — Claim forms

[Public Health Law](../../README.md) › [Article 1 — Short Title and Definitions: General Provisions](../README.md) › [Title 2 — General Provisions](README.md)

```text
  * § 23. Claim forms. A non-participating physician shall include a
claim form for a third-party payor with a patient bill for health care
services, other than a bill for the patient's co-payment, coinsurance or
deductible.
  * NB Effective March 31, 2015
```
