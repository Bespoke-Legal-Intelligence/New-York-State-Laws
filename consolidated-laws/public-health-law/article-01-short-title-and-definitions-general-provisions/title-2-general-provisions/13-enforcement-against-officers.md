# Public Health Law § 13 — Enforcement: against officers

[Public Health Law](../../README.md) › [Article 1 — Short Title and Definitions: General Provisions](../README.md) › [Title 2 — General Provisions](README.md)

```text
  § 13. Enforcement: against officers.  The performance of any duty or
the doing of any act enjoined, prescribed or required by this chapter,
may be enforced by a proceeding pursuant to article seventy-eight of the
civil practice law and rules at the instance of the department or of a
local board of health, or of any citizen of full age resident of the
municipality where the duty should be performed or the act done.
```
