# Public Health Law § 12-C — Obstructing health officer in performance of his duty

[Public Health Law](../../README.md) › [Article 1 — Short Title and Definitions: General Provisions](../README.md) › [Title 2 — General Provisions](README.md)

```text
  § 12-c. Obstructing health officer in performance of his duty.  A
person who wilfully opposes or obstructs a health officer or physician
charged with enforcement of the health laws, in performing any legal
duty, is guilty of a misdemeanor.
```
