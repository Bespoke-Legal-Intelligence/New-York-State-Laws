# Public Health Law § 1605 — Meetings

[Public Health Law](../README.md) › [Article 16 — Pesticide Control Board](README.md)

```text
  § 1605. Meetings.  1. The board shall meet at least once every three
months.  Special meetings shall be called by the chairman on his own
initiative or upon the written request of two voting members. Ten days'
written notice of the time, place and purpose of each meeting shall be
given to each member of the board.
  2. Four voting members of the board shall constitute a quorum to
transact the business of the board. A majority vote shall be necessary
for any action taken by the board except as provided in subdivision one
of section sixteen hundred six of this article.
```
