# Public Health Law § 1609 — Conflicting laws

[Public Health Law](../README.md) › [Article 16 — Pesticide Control Board](README.md)

```text
  § 1609. Conflicting laws.  Nothing in this article shall be deemed to
derogate or detract from the functions, powers and duties of any
department or agency of the state prescribed by law.
```
