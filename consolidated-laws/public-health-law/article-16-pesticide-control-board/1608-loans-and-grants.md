# Public Health Law § 1608 — Loans and grants

[Public Health Law](../README.md) › [Article 16 — Pesticide Control Board](README.md)

```text
  § 1608. Loans and grants.  The board shall have the power to accept
and administer loans and grants from the federal government and other
sources, public or private, for carrying out its functions under this
article.
```
