# Public Health Law § 1602 — Definition

[Public Health Law](../README.md) › [Article 16 — Pesticide Control Board](README.md)

```text
  § 1602. Definition.  When used in this article, the term "pesticide"
means any substance or mixture of substances intended for preventing,
destroying, repelling, or mitigating any insect, rodent, fungi, weeds,
or other forms of plant or animal life or viruses, except viruses on or
in living man or animals.
```
