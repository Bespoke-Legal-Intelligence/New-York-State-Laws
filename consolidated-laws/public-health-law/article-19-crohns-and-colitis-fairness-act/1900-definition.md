# Public Health Law § 1900 — Definition

[Public Health Law](../README.md) › [Article 19 — Crohn's and Colitis Fairness Act](README.md)

```text
  * § 1900. Definition. As used in this article, "eligible medical
condition" means Crohn's disease, ulcerative colitis, any other
inflammatory bowel disease, irritable bowel syndrome or any other
medical condition that requires immediate access to a toilet facility.
  * NB Effective April 17, 2018
```
