# Public Health Law § 280-D — Patient notification of prescription label change

[Public Health Law](../../README.md) › [Article 2-A — Prescription Drugs](../README.md) › [Title 2 — Prescription Drugs; Various Provisions](README.md)

```text
  § 280-d. Patient notification of prescription label change. A health
care prescriber shall inform the patient if such prescriber has included
or has requested to include the name or address of the prescribing
health care practice instead of the name of such prescriber on the
prescription label when prescribing mifepristone or misoprostol.
```
