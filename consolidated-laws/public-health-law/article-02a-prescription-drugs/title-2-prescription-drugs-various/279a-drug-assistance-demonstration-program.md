# Public Health Law § 279-A — Drug assistance demonstration program

[Public Health Law](../../README.md) › [Article 2-A — Prescription Drugs](../README.md) › [Title 2 — Prescription Drugs; Various Provisions](README.md)

```text
  § 279-a. Drug assistance demonstration program. 1. The commissioner
shall develop a demonstration program (the "program") to provide access
to insulin and other life sustaining maintenance prescription drugs
identified by the commissioner for residents of the state who are
without health coverage, are ineligible for Medicaid or other publicly
funded health coverage, or are otherwise determined to be eligible by
the commissioner and depend upon such medication for their survival. In
developing the program the commissioner shall:
  (a) consider modeling the program on the state's HIV/AIDS drug
assistance program providing access to eligible individuals at certain
income thresholds above the federal poverty level; and
  (b) engage with pharmaceutical manufacturers to explore a public
private partnership to bring affordable drugs through the program to
eligible individuals.
  2. The commissioner shall report to the governor, the temporary
president of the senate, the speaker of the assembly, and the chairs of
the senate and assembly health committees on the available options to
establish the program, various cost sharing models for eligible
participants and the related costs to the state associated with
implementing the program. The report shall be a public document posted
on the department's website. The report shall be issued no later than
three months after the effective date of this section.
```
