# Public Health Law § 422 — Boards of visitors; compensation and expenses of members

[Public Health Law](../../README.md) › [Article 4 — Property and Hospitals In the Department of Health](../README.md) › [Title 2 — Hospitals: Boards of Visitors](README.md)

```text
  § 422. Boards of visitors; compensation and expenses of members.
Members of the board of visitors of each hospital in the department
shall receive no compensation, but each shall be entitled to receive his
expenses actually and necessarily incurred in the performance of his
duties.
```
