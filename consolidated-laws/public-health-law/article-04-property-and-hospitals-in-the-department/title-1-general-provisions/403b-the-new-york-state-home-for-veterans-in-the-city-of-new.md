# Public Health Law § 403-B — The New York state home for veterans in the city of New York

[Public Health Law](../../README.md) › [Article 4 — Property and Hospitals In the Department of Health](../README.md) › [Title 1 — General Provisions](README.md)

```text
  § 403-b. The New York state home for veterans in the city of New York.
There is hereby established in the city of New York a New York state
home for veterans. Such home for veterans shall be subject to the
provisions of this article except when such provisions are inconsistent
with the provisions of article twenty-six-A of this chapter.
```
