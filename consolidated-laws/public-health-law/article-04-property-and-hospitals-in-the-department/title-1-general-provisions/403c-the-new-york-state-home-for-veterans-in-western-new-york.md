# Public Health Law § 403-C — The New York state home for veterans in western New York

[Public Health Law](../../README.md) › [Article 4 — Property and Hospitals In the Department of Health](../README.md) › [Title 1 — General Provisions](README.md)

```text
  § 403-c. The New York state home for veterans in western New York.
There is hereby established a state home for veterans in western New
York. Such home for veterans shall be subject to the provisions of this
article except when such provisions are inconsistent with the provisions
of article twenty-six-A of this chapter.
```
