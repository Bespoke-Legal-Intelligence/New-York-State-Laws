# Public Health Law § 403-D — The New York state home for veterans in the lower-Hudson Valley

[Public Health Law](../../README.md) › [Article 4 — Property and Hospitals In the Department of Health](../README.md) › [Title 1 — General Provisions](README.md)

```text
  § 403-d. The New York state home for veterans in the lower-Hudson
Valley. There is hereby established a state home for veterans in the
lower-Hudson Valley. Such home for veterans shall be subject to the
provisions of this article except when such provisions are inconsistent
with the provisions of article twenty-six-A of this chapter.
```
