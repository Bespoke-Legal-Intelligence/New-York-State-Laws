# Public Health Law § 403-A — The New York state home for veterans and their dependents at Oxford

[Public Health Law](../../README.md) › [Article 4 — Property and Hospitals In the Department of Health](../README.md) › [Title 1 — General Provisions](README.md)

```text
  § 403-a. The New York state home for veterans and their dependents at
Oxford.  The New York state home for veterans and their dependents at
Oxford shall be subject to the provisions of this article except when
such provisions are inconsistent with the provisions of article
twenty-six-A of this chapter.
```
