# Public Health Law § 405 — Hospitals; visitors and officers; oaths and bonds

[Public Health Law](../../README.md) › [Article 4 — Property and Hospitals In the Department of Health](../README.md) › [Title 1 — General Provisions](README.md)

```text
  § 405. Hospitals; visitors and officers; oaths and bonds. 1.  Each
director, member of the board of visitors, and treasurer of the several
state hospitals in the department shall take the constitutional oath of
office.
```
