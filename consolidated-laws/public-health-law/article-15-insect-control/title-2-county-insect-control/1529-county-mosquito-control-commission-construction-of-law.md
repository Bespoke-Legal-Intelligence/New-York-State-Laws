# Public Health Law § 1529 — County mosquito control commission; construction of law

[Public Health Law](../../README.md) › [Article 15 — Insect Control](../README.md) › [Title 2 — County Insect Control](README.md)

```text
  § 1529. County mosquito control commission; construction of law.
Nothing in this article shall be construed to alter, amend, modify or
repeal any of the provisions of article thirteen of this chapter, or any
of the provisions of the conservation law except to the extent that the
provisions of this article are inconsistent therewith.
```
