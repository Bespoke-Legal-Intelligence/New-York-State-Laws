# Public Health Law § 1527 — County mosquito control commission; obstructions; interferences; violations

[Public Health Law](../../README.md) › [Article 15 — Insect Control](../README.md) › [Title 2 — County Insect Control](README.md)

```text
  § 1527. County mosquito control commission; obstructions;
interferences; violations. Any person who obstructs or interferes with
the entry of the county mosquito control commission or its employees
upon land, or who obstructs or interferes with, molests, or damages any
of the work performed by the said commission shall be guilty of a
misdemeanor.
```
