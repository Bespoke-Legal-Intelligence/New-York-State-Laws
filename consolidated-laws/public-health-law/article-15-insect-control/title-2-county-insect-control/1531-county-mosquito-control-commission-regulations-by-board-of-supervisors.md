# Public Health Law § 1531 — County mosquito control commission; regulations by board of supervisors as to purchases, contracts and expenditures

[Public Health Law](../../README.md) › [Article 15 — Insect Control](../README.md) › [Title 2 — County Insect Control](README.md)

```text
  § 1531. County mosquito control commission; regulations by board of
supervisors as to purchases, contracts and expenditures. Such mosquito
extermination commission shall not pay any accounts or claims, make any
contracts or expenditures or purchase any materials, supplies or
equipment except after compliance with such regulations as may be
adopted by the board of supervisors with respect to audit, advertising
or otherwise.
```
