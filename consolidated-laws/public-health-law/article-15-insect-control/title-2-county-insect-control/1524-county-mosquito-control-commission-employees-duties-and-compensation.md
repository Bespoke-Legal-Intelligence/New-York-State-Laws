# Public Health Law § 1524 — County mosquito control commission; employees; duties and compensation

[Public Health Law](../../README.md) › [Article 15 — Insect Control](../README.md) › [Title 2 — County Insect Control](README.md)

```text
  § 1524. County mosquito control commission; employees; duties and
compensation.  1. Each county mosquito control commission, may, with the
approval of the board of supervisors of the county, appoint and employ
such clerks, assistants, inspectors and day laborers as may be necessary
to carry out the provisions of this article.
  2. The compensation of such employees shall be fixed by the board of
supervisors of the county.
  3. The commission shall prescribe the duties and hours of employment
of clerks and assistants and make all rules and regulations respecting
the same and furnish them with necessary and proper facilities.
```
