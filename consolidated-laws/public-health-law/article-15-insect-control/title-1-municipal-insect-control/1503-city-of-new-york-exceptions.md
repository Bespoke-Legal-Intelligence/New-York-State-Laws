# Public Health Law § 1503 — City of New York; exceptions

[Public Health Law](../../README.md) › [Article 15 — Insect Control](../README.md) › [Title 1 — Municipal Insect Control](README.md)

```text
  § 1503. City of New York; exceptions.  The provision of sections one
thousand five hundred to one thousand five hundred two of this chapter,
inclusive, shall not apply to the city of New York.
```
