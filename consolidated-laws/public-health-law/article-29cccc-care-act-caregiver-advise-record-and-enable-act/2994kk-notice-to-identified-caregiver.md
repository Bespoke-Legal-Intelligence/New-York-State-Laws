# Public Health Law § 2994-KK — Notice to identified caregiver

[Public Health Law](../README.md) › [Article 29-CCCC — Care Act (caregiver Advise, Record and Enable Act)](README.md)

```text
  * § 2994-kk. Notice to identified caregiver. A hospital shall notify
the patient's identified caregiver of the patient's discharge or
transfer to another hospital or facility licensed by the department or
the office of mental health as soon as the date and time of discharge or
transfer can be anticipated prior to the patient's actual discharge or
transfer to such facility. In the event the hospital is unable to
contact the designated caregiver, the lack of contact shall not
interfere with, delay, or otherwise affect the medical care provided to
the patient or an appropriate discharge of the patient. The hospital
shall promptly document the attempt in the patient's medical record.
  * NB Effective April 23, 2016
```
