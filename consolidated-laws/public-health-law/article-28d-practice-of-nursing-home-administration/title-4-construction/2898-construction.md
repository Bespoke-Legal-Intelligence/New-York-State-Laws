# Public Health Law § 2898 — Construction

[Public Health Law](../../README.md) › [Article 28-D — Practice of Nursing Home Administration](../README.md) › [Title 4 — Construction](README.md)

```text
  § 2898. Construction. Nothing in this article shall be construed to
limit the activities, services, and use of official title on the part of
any person, provided that no title shall be used which includes the term
or words "licensed nursing home administrator" by any person not
qualified to do so under the provisions of this article.
```
