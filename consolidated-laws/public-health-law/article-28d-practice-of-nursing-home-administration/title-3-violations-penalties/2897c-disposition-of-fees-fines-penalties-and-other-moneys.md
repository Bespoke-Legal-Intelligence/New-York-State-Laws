# Public Health Law § 2897-C — Disposition of fees, fines, penalties and other moneys

[Public Health Law](../../README.md) › [Article 28-D — Practice of Nursing Home Administration](../README.md) › [Title 3 — Violations; Penalties](README.md)

```text
  § 2897-c. Disposition of fees, fines, penalties and other moneys. All
fees, fines, penalties and other moneys derived from the operation of
this article shall be paid to the department and shall be paid by the
department to the state treasury, as provided in the state finance law.
```
