# Public Health Law § 2895 — Declaration of policy and statement of purpose

[Public Health Law](../../README.md) › [Article 28-D — Practice of Nursing Home Administration](../README.md) › [Title 1 — General Provisions and Public Policy](README.md)

```text
  § 2895. Declaration of policy and statement of purpose. It is hereby
declared and found that the health and safety of the people of the state
of New York require that the administration of medical facilities
providing nursing home accommodations be adequate and proper, and that
the quality of such administrative services is related to the calibre of
training and experience of the persons administering such facilities. It
is the purpose of this article to establish standards of education,
training and experience and provide for the examination, licensure and
registration of nursing home administrators.
```
