# Public Health Law § 2907 — Review of hospital operations

[Public Health Law](../README.md) › [Article 29 — Hospital Survey, Planning and Review](README.md)

```text
  § 2907. Review of hospital operations. The department shall continue
to exercise all of the functions, powers and duties prescribed by this
chapter in connection with hospital operations within the state with the
objective of promoting the highest quality of hospital care for the
citizens of the state.
```
