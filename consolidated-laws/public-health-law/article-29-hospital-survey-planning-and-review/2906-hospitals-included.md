# Public Health Law § 2906 — Hospitals included

[Public Health Law](../README.md) › [Article 29 — Hospital Survey, Planning and Review](README.md)

```text
  § 2906. Hospitals included. As used in this article, the term
"hospital" shall include public health centers, diagnostic or treatment
centers, rehabilitation facilities, nursing homes and chronic disease
hospitals as defined in section six hundred thirty-one (i) of the
federal public health service act, as amended, tuberculosis, mental,
chronic disease, and other types of hospitals, including laboratories,
out-patient departments, nurses' homes and training facilities, and
central service facilities operated in connection with hospitals, but
does not include any hospital furnishing primarily domiciliary care.
```
