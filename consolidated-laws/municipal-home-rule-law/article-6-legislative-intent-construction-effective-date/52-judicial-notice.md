# Municipal Home Rule Law § 52 — Judicial notice

[Municipal Home Rule Law](../README.md) › [Article 6 — Legislative Intent; Construction; Effective Date](README.md)

```text
  § 52. Judicial notice. Courts shall take judicial notice of all local
laws and of rules and regulations adopted pursuant thereto.
```
