# Municipal Home Rule Law § 53 — No repeal by implication

[Municipal Home Rule Law](../README.md) › [Article 6 — Legislative Intent; Construction; Effective Date](README.md)

```text
  § 53. No repeal by implication. It is not intended by this chapter to
repeal by implication any existing provision of law and no law shall be
deemed repealed thereby unless expressly provided for therein.
```
