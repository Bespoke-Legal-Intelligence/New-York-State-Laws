# Navigation Law § 1 — Short title

[Navigation Law](../README.md) › [Article 1 — Short Title and Definitions](README.md)

```text
  § 1. Short title. This chapter shall be known as the navigation law,
and shall apply to navigation and the use of navigable waters of the
state and regulations hereby created.
```
