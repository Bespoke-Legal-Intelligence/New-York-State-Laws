# Navigation Law § 77 — Rules and regulations

[Navigation Law](../../README.md) › [Article 4 — Vessels](../README.md) › [Part 5 — Boating Safety Education](README.md)

```text
  § 77. Rules and regulations. The commissioner may make rules and
regulations designed to result in the further knowledge and observance
of the principles of safe boat operation.
```
