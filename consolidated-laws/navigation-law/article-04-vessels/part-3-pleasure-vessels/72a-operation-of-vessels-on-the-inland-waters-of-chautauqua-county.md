# Navigation Law § 72-A — Operation of vessels on the inland waters of Chautauqua county

[Navigation Law](../../README.md) › [Article 4 — Vessels](../README.md) › [Part 3 — Pleasure Vessels](README.md)

```text
  § 72-a. Operation of vessels on the inland waters of Chautauqua
county. No water craft shall pass the stern of a boat being used for
trolling less than two hundred feet from the stern of such boat on the
inland lakes of Chautauqua county.
```
