# Navigation Law § 175 — Notification by persons responsible for discharge

[Navigation Law](../../README.md) › [Article 12 — Oil Spill Prevention, Control, and Compensation](../README.md) › [Part 2 — Oil Spill Cleanup and Removal; Licenses](README.md)

```text
  § 175. Notification by persons responsible for discharge. Any person
responsible for causing a discharge shall immediately notify the
department pursuant to rules and regulations established by the
department, but in no case later than two hours after the discharge.
Failure to so notify shall make persons liable to the penalty provisions
of section 192 of this article.  Notwithstanding the provisions of any
other law, such notification to the department shall be deemed to
fulfill the notification requirements of any other state or local law.
```
