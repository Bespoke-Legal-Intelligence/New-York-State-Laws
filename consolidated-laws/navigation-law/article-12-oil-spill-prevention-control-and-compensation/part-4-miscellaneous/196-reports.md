# Navigation Law § 196 — Reports

[Navigation Law](../../README.md) › [Article 12 — Oil Spill Prevention, Control, and Compensation](../README.md) › [Part 4 — Miscellaneous](README.md)

```text
  § 196. Reports. The commissioner and the administrator shall make an
annual report to the legislature and the governor which shall describe
the quality and quantity of spills of petroleum, the costs and damages
paid by and recovered for the fund, and the economic and environmental
impact on the state as a result of the administration of this article.
```
