# Navigation Law § 194 — Severability

[Navigation Law](../../README.md) › [Article 12 — Oil Spill Prevention, Control, and Compensation](../README.md) › [Part 4 — Miscellaneous](README.md)

```text
  § 194. Severability. If any section, subdivision, provision, clause or
portion of this article is adjudged unconstitutional or invalid by a
court of competent jurisdiction, the remainder of this article shall not
be affected thereby.
```
