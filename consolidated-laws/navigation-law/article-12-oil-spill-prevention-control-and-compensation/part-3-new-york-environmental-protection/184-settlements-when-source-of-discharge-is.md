# Navigation Law § 184 — Settlements when source of discharge is unknown

[Navigation Law](../../README.md) › [Article 12 — Oil Spill Prevention, Control, and Compensation](../README.md) › [Part 3 — New York Environmental Protection and Spill Compensation Fund; Liability; Third Party Compensation](README.md)

```text
  § 184. Settlements when source of discharge is unknown. If the source
of the discharge is unknown or cannot be determined, the claimant and
the administrator shall attempt to arrange a settlement of any claim
against the fund.  The administrator is authorized to enter and certify
payment of such settlement subject to such proof and procedures
contained in regulations promulgated by the administrator.
```
