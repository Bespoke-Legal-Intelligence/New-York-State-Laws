# Navigation Law § 181-E — Amounts received to satisfy lien

[Navigation Law](../../README.md) › [Article 12 — Oil Spill Prevention, Control, and Compensation](../README.md) › [Part 3 — New York Environmental Protection and Spill Compensation Fund; Liability; Third Party Compensation](README.md)

```text
  § 181-e. Amounts received to satisfy lien. Amounts received by the
administrator to satisfy all or part of an environmental lien shall be
deposited in the state treasury and credited to the environmental
protection and spill compensation fund.
```
