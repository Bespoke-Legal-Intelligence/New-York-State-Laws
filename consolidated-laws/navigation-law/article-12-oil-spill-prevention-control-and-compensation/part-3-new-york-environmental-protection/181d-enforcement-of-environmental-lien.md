# Navigation Law § 181-D — Enforcement of environmental lien

[Navigation Law](../../README.md) › [Article 12 — Oil Spill Prevention, Control, and Compensation](../README.md) › [Part 3 — New York Environmental Protection and Spill Compensation Fund; Liability; Third Party Compensation](README.md)

```text
  § 181-d. Enforcement of environmental lien. An environmental lien may
be enforced against the property specified in the notice of
environmental lien, and an environmental lien may be vacated or
discharged, as prescribed in article three of the lien law; provided,
however, that nothing in this article or in article three of the lien
law shall affect the right of the fund to bring an action to recover
cleanup and removal costs and damage costs under section one hundred
eighty-one, one hundred eighty-seven, one hundred eighty-eight or one
hundred ninety of this part.
```
