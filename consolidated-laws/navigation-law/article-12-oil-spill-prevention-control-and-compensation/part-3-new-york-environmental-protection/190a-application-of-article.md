# Navigation Law § 190-A — Application of article

[Navigation Law](../../README.md) › [Article 12 — Oil Spill Prevention, Control, and Compensation](../README.md) › [Part 3 — New York Environmental Protection and Spill Compensation Fund; Liability; Third Party Compensation](README.md)

```text
  § 190-a. Application of article. For purposes of cleanup and removal
of any public or private ground water supply system contaminated by a
discharge occurring either before or after the effective date of article
twelve of this chapter, all relevant provisions of article twelve of
this chapter shall apply.
```
