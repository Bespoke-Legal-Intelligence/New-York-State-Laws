# Navigation Law § 190 — Claims against insurers

[Navigation Law](../../README.md) › [Article 12 — Oil Spill Prevention, Control, and Compensation](../README.md) › [Part 3 — New York Environmental Protection and Spill Compensation Fund; Liability; Third Party Compensation](README.md)

```text
  § 190. Claims against insurers. Any claims for costs of cleanup and
removal, civil penalties or damages by the state and any claim for
damages by any injured person, may be brought directly against the bond,
the insurer, or any other person providing evidence of financial
responsibility.
```
