# Navigation Law § 32-B — Lights upon swing bridges

[Navigation Law](../README.md) › [Article 3 — Navigable Waters of the State](README.md)

```text
  § 32-b. Lights upon swing bridges. A corporation, company, or
individual, owning, maintaining or operating a swing bridge across the
Hudson river, who during the navigation season between sundown and
sunrise, neglects to keep and maintain upon every such bridge the lights
required by law, is guilty of a misdemeanor.
```
