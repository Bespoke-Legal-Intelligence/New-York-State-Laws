# Navigation Law § 32-A — Using net or weir unlawfully in Hudson river

[Navigation Law](../README.md) › [Article 3 — Navigable Waters of the State](README.md)

```text
  § 32-a. Using net or weir unlawfully in Hudson river. A person, who
uses any net or weir for setting or attaching nets, or a pole or other
fixture in any part of the Hudson river, except as permitted by statute,
is guilty of a misdemeanor.
```
