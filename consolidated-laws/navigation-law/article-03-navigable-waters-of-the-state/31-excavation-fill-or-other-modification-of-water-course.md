# Navigation Law § 31 — Excavation, fill or other modification of water course

[Navigation Law](../README.md) › [Article 3 — Navigable Waters of the State](README.md)

```text
  § 31. Excavation, fill or other modification of water course. No
person or local public corporation shall excavate or place fill in the
navigable waters of the state without first obtaining a permit therefor
in conformity with the provisions of section 15-0505 of the
environmental conservation law.
```
