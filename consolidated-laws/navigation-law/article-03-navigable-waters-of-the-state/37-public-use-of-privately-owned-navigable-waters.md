# Navigation Law § 37 — Public use of privately owned navigable waters

[Navigation Law](../README.md) › [Article 3 — Navigable Waters of the State](README.md)

```text
  § 37. Public use of privately owned navigable waters. The provisions
of this chapter shall apply to privately owned navigable waters to which
the public has or is granted access, for compensation or otherwise, for
boating, bathing, swimming or other recreational uses or purposes.
```
