# Navigation Law § 124 — Persons prohibited from landing logs, timber or lumber

[Navigation Law](../README.md) › [Article 9 — Rivers and Streams As Public Highways](README.md)

```text
  § 124. Persons prohibited from landing logs, timber or lumber.  No
person shall stop, take up or draw to, or lodge on the shore of any
river or stream used for floating logs, timber or lumber, or on any
island therein, any lumber, logs, timber, boards or planks floating in
such river or stream, without the consent of the owner thereof.  Any
person violating the provisions of this section shall for each violation
forfeit to the person aggrieved thereby the sum of ten dollars, and in
addition thereto shall be liable to the owner of such logs, timber or
lumber for all damages sustained thereby.
```
