# Navigation Law § 143 — State marine facilities

[Navigation Law](../README.md) › [Article 11 — Improvement and Preservation of Waterways](README.md)

```text
  § 143. State marine facilities. The commissioner is hereby authorized,
within amounts appropriated therefor as allocated by the director of the
budget, to construct, reconstruct, improve, operate and maintain state
marine facilities, including state marine facilities incidental to a
harbor of refuge.
```
