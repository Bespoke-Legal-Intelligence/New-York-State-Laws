# Navigation Law § 139-C — Officer unlawfully detaining wrecked property

[Navigation Law](../README.md) › [Article 10 — Wrecks](README.md)

```text
  § 139-c. Officer unlawfully detaining wrecked property. An officer,
whose duties pertain in any way to wrecked property, who, without
authority of law, detains such property or proceeds thereof, after the
salvage and expenses chargeable thereon have been paid or offered to
him, or who is guilty of any fraud in the discharge of such duties, is
guilty of a misdemeanor.
```
