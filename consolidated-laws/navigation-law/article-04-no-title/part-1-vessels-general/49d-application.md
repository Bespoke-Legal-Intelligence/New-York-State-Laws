# Navigation Law § 49-D — Application

[Navigation Law](../../README.md) › [Article 4 — No title](../README.md) › [Part 1 — Vessels, General](README.md)

```text
  § 49-d. Application. Sections forty-seven, forty-seven-b, forty-eight,
forty-nine-a and forty-nine-b of this chapter shall apply to any vessel
operated on privately or publicly owned underground non-navigable
waterways with no navigable inlet or outlet that carry passengers for
compensation, either received directly or where provided as an
accommodation, advantage, facility or privilege at any place of public
accommodation, resort or amusement.
```
