# Navigation Law § 76 — Information

[Navigation Law](../../README.md) › [Article 4 — No title](../README.md) › [Part 5 — Boating Safety Education](README.md)

```text
  § 76. Information. Such program shall include the preparation and
dissemination of water safety information to the public, and
particularly to the owners and operators of pleasure boats.
```
