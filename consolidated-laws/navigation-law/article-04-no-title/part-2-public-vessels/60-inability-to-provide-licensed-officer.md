# Navigation Law § 60 — Inability to provide licensed officer

[Navigation Law](../../README.md) › [Article 4 — No title](../README.md) › [Part 2 — Public Vessels](README.md)

```text
  § 60. Inability to provide licensed officer. If the owner or master of
a public vessel is unable to obtain the services of a licensed officer,
the inspector shall be notified and the deficiency may be temporarily
supplied, if the inspector approves, until the services of a licensed
officer can be obtained.
```
