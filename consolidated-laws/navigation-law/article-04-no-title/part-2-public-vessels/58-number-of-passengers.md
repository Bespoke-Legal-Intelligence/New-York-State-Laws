# Navigation Law § 58 — Number of passengers

[Navigation Law](../../README.md) › [Article 4 — No title](../README.md) › [Part 2 — Public Vessels](README.md)

```text
  § 58. Number of passengers. It shall not be lawful to take on board of
any public vessel a greater number of passengers than the number allowed
in the certificate of inspection and for every violation of this
provision, the master, pilot, joint pilot and engineer or owner shall be
guilty of a misdemeanor punishable as set forth in section
seventy-three-b of this article.
```
