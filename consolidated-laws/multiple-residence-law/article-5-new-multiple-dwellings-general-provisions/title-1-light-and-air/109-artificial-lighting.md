# Multiple Residence Law § 109 — Artificial lighting

[Multiple Residence Law](../../README.md) › [Article 5 — New Multiple Dwellings--general Provisions](../README.md) › [Title 1 — Light and Air](README.md)

```text
  § 109. Artificial lighting. The owner shall provide a light or lights
in every public hall, stair and fire-stair on every floor so that every
such space shall be lighted adequately.
```
