# Multiple Residence Law § 251 — Height limitation

[Multiple Residence Law](../../README.md) › [Article 7 — Non-fireproof New Multiple Dwellings](../README.md) › [Title 1 — Fire Protection](README.md)

```text
  § 251. Height limitation.  A dwelling not exceeding seventy-five feet
or six stories in height may be non-fireproof construction.
```
