# Multiple Residence Law § 253 — Construction of first floor

[Multiple Residence Law](../../README.md) › [Article 7 — Non-fireproof New Multiple Dwellings](../README.md) › [Title 1 — Fire Protection](README.md)

```text
  § 253. Construction of first floor.  The first floor above the lowest
cellar, or, if there be no cellar, above the lowest story, shall be
fireproof or, if the dwelling be three stories or less in height,
fire-retarded.
```
