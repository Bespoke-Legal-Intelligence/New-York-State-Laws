# Transportation Corporations Law § 108 — Regulation

[Transportation Corporations Law](../README.md) › [Article 8 — Freight Terminal Corporations](README.md)

```text
  § 108. Regulation. In construction and operation of its plant and
equipment and in the transaction of its business, every such corporation
shall be subject to all regulations as to construction, fire, health and
safety imposed by law, ordinance or local authority.
```
