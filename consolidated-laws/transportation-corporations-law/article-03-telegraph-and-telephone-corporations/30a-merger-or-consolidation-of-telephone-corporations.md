# Transportation Corporations Law § 30-A — Merger or consolidation of telephone corporations

[Transportation Corporations Law](../README.md) › [Article 3 — Telegraph and Telephone Corporations](README.md)

```text
  § 30-a. Merger or consolidation of telephone corporations. Any two or
more domestic telephone corporations may merge or consolidate with each
other, in accordance with the procedure and with the effect set forth in
article nine of the business corporation law.
```
