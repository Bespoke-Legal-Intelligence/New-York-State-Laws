# Transportation Corporations Law § 110 — Definition

[Transportation Corporations Law](../README.md) › [Article 9 — District Steam Corporations](README.md)

```text
  § 110. Definition. A district steam corporation is a corporation
organized to supply steam to consumers from a central station or
stations through pipes laid wholly or partly in the public streets.
```
