# Transportation Corporations Law § 70 — Definition

[Transportation Corporations Law](../README.md) › [Article 6 — Ferry Corporations](README.md)

```text
  § 70. Definition. A ferry corporation is a corporation organized to
operate a ferry, wholly within or partly without this state.
```
