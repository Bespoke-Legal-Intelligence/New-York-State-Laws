# Transportation Corporations Law § 72 — Posting schedule of rates

[Transportation Corporations Law](../README.md) › [Article 6 — Ferry Corporations](README.md)

```text
  § 72. Posting schedule of rates. A corporation operating a ferry in
this state, or between this state and any other state, shall post in a
conspicuous and accessible place in each of its ferry houses, in plain
view of the passengers, a schedule plainly printed in the English
language, of its rates.
```
