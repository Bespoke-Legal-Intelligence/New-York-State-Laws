# Transportation Corporations Law § 117 — Approval by department of health

[Transportation Corporations Law](../README.md) › [Article 10 — Sewage-works Corporations](README.md)

```text
  § 117. Approval by department of health. A local governing body shall
not consent to the establishment of a sewage-works corporation in any
municipality unless there shall first be filed with the department of
health, or city, county, or part-county department of health having
jurisdiction, maps and specifications of the proposed system and such
department shall have given its approval thereof.
```
