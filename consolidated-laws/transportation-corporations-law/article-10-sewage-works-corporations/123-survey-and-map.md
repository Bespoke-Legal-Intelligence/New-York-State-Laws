# Transportation Corporations Law § 123 — Survey and map

[Transportation Corporations Law](../README.md) › [Article 10 — Sewage-works Corporations](README.md)

```text
  § 123. Survey and map. Before taking or using any land, for its
corporate purposes such corporation shall cause a survey and map to be
made of the lands intended to be taken designating the land of the
several owners or occupants thereof, which map shall be signed by the
president and the secretary, and filed in the office of the clerk of the
county in which such lands are situated.
```
