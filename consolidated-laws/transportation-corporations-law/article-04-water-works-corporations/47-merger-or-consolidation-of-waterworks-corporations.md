# Transportation Corporations Law § 47 — Merger or consolidation of waterworks corporations

[Transportation Corporations Law](../README.md) › [Article 4 — Water-works Corporations](README.md)

```text
  § 47. Merger or consolidation of waterworks corporations. Any two or
more domestic waterworks corporations may merge or consolidate with each
other, in accordance with the procedure and with the effect set forth in
article nine of the business corporation law.
```
