# Transportation Corporations Law § 40 — Definition

[Transportation Corporations Law](../README.md) › [Article 4 — Water-works Corporations](README.md)

```text
  § 40. Definition. A water-works corporation is a corporation organized
to supply water by mains or pipes to any of the cities, towns or
villages in this state, and the inhabitants thereof.
```
