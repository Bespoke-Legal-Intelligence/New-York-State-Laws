# Transportation Corporations Law § 44 — Survey and map

[Transportation Corporations Law](../README.md) › [Article 4 — Water-works Corporations](README.md)

```text
  § 44. Survey and map. Before taking or using any land, for its
corporate purposes such corporation shall cause a survey and map to be
made of the lands intended to be taken designating the land of the
several owners or occupants thereof, which map shall be signed by the
president and its secretary, and filed in the office of the county clerk
of the county in which such lands are situated.
```
