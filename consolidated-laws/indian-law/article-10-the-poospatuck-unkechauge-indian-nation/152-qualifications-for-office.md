# Indian Law § 152 — Qualifications for office

[Indian Law](../README.md) › [Article 10 — The Poospatuck (unkechauge) Indian Nation](README.md)

```text
  § 152. Qualifications for office.  All officers shall qualify for
office and perform their respective duties in accordance with the tribal
rules, customs and regulations of the Poospatuck Indian nation and, in
addition, shall have been actually residing on the reservation for six
months immediately preceding election.
```
