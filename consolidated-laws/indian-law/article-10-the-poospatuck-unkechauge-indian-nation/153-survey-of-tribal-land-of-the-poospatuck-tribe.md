# Indian Law § 153 — Survey of tribal land of the Poospatuck tribe

[Indian Law](../README.md) › [Article 10 — The Poospatuck (unkechauge) Indian Nation](README.md)

```text
  § 153. Survey of tribal land of the Poospatuck tribe.  The Poospatuck
Indian tribe is hereby authorized to have an accurate survey and
description made of its tribal lands for the purposes of determining its
reservation boundaries. Such survey shall, however, not affect the
claims of the tribe to other lands, nor shall such survey be made more
than once every ten years.
```
