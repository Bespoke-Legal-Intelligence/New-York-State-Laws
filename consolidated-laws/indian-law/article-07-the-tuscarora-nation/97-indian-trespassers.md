# Indian Law § 97 — Indian trespassers

[Indian Law](../README.md) › [Article 7 — The Tuscarora Nation](README.md)

```text
  § 97. Indian trespassers.  Any Indian who shall cut or destroy timber
or trees on any of the timbered lands of such nation, or without the
consent of such chiefs, shall be liable to a penalty of twice the value
of the timber so cut down or destroyed, recoverable by such chiefs, in
the name of the nation.
```
