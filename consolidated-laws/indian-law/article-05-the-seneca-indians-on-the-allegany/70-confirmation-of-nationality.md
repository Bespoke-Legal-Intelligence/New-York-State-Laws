# Indian Law § 70 — Confirmation of nationality

[Indian Law](../README.md) › [Article 5 — The Seneca Indians On the Allegany and Cattaraugus Reservations](README.md)

```text
  § 70. Confirmation of nationality.  The Seneca Indians residing on the
Allegany and Cattaraugus reservations shall, subject to the limitations
provided by law, hold and possess such reservations as a distinct
community.
```
