# Indian Law § 75 — Vacancies in elective offices

[Indian Law](../README.md) › [Article 5 — The Seneca Indians On the Allegany and Cattaraugus Reservations](README.md)

```text
  § 75. Vacancies in elective offices.  If a vacancy occur in the office
of the president of the Seneca nation, such office shall be filled by
the council by a majority vote thereof, and a meeting of the council for
that purpose shall be called by the clerk of the nation upon a written
petition presented to him, signed by at least ten electors of such
nation. If a vacancy occur in any other office of such nation, such
office shall be filled by appointment by the president of the nation,
under his hand and the seal of the nation.
```
