# Indian Law § 108 — Qualifications of voters

[Indian Law](../README.md) › [Article 8 — The Saint Regis Tribe](README.md)

```text
  § 108. Qualifications of voters.  All Indians of the Saint Regis
tribe, who are eighteen years of age or upwards, who reside on the
American side of the line dividing the United States from Canada, and
who are entitled to draw the yearly annuity money, shall be entitled to
vote for the chiefs or headmen, the subchiefs and clerk of the tribe at
the elections of such tribe provided for by this article.
```
