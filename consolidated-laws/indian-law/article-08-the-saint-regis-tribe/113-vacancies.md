# Indian Law § 113 — Vacancies

[Indian Law](../README.md) › [Article 8 — The Saint Regis Tribe](README.md)

```text
  § 113. Vacancies.  If a vacancy shall occur for any reason in the
office of chief or headman, subchief or clerk of such tribe, the chiefs
or headmen shall appoint a person to fill such vacancy, who shall hold
office until the next succeeding annual election, at which a person
shall be elected to such office to hold office until the expiration of
the term for which his predecessor was elected.
```
