# Indian Law § 6 — Exemption of reservation lands from taxation

[Indian Law](../README.md) › [Article 2 — General Provisions](README.md)

```text
  § 6. Exemption of reservation lands from taxation.  No taxes shall be
assessed, for any purpose whatever, upon any Indian reservation in this
state, so long as the land of such reservation shall remain the property
of the nation, tribe or band occupying the same.
```
