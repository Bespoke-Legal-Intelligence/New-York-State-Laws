# Indian Law § 5 — Actions in state courts

[Indian Law](../README.md) › [Article 2 — General Provisions](README.md)

```text
  § 5. Actions in state courts.  Any action or special proceeding
between Indians or between one or more Indians and any other person or
persons may be prosecuted and enforced in any court of the state to the
same extent as provided by law for other actions and special
proceedings.
```
