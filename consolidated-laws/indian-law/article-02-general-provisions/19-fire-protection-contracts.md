# Indian Law § 19 — Fire Protection Contracts

[Indian Law](../README.md) › [Article 2 — General Provisions](README.md)

```text
  § 19. Fire Protection Contracts.  Notwithstanding any other provisions
of law, the Seneca Nation of Indians and any city, town, village, fire
district or incorporated fire company may enter into a contract or
contracts whereby such city, town, village, fire district or
incorporated fire company for a stated consideration, agrees to provide
fire protection upon all or any part of the lands of the Seneca Nation
of Indians.
```
