# Indian Law § 26 — Plank-road on reservation

[Indian Law](../README.md) › [Article 3 — The Onondaga Tribe](README.md)

```text
  § 26. Plank-road on reservation.  All Indians residing on the Onondaga
reservation, or belonging to the Onondaga tribe, shall, as to the
portion of the Syracuse and Tully plank-road constructed upon such
reservation, and as to all gates erected within the bounds thereof, pass
free of any charge or toll.
```
