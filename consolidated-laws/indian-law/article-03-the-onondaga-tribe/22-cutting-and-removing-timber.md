# Indian Law § 22 — Cutting and removing timber

[Indian Law](../README.md) › [Article 3 — The Onondaga Tribe](README.md)

```text
  § 22. Cutting and removing timber.  No person other than an Onondaga
Indian shall cut or remove from the Onondaga reservation any tree,
timber, wood, bark or poles; and no Indian shall cut any tree, timber,
wood, bark or poles for the purpose of sale or removal from such
reservation, nor shall sell, remove, cause to be removed or aid in the
removal from such reservation of any trees, timber, wood, bark or poles,
except upon the written permission of a majority of the chiefs of the
Onondaga tribe, particularly specifying the quantity and kind of trees,
timber, wood, bark or poles to be cut or removed.
```
