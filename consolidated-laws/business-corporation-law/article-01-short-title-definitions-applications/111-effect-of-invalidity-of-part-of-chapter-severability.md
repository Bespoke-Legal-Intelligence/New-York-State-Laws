# Business Corporation Law § 111 — Effect of invalidity of part of chapter; severability

[Business Corporation Law](../README.md) › [Article 1 — Short Title; Definitions; Applications; Certificates, Miscellaneous](README.md)

```text
§ 111. Effect of invalidity of part of chapter; severability.
  If any provision of this chapter or application thereof to any person
or circumstances is held invalid, such invalidity shall not affect other
provisions or applications of this chapter which can be given effect
without the invalid provision or application, and to this end the
provisions of this chapter are declared severable.
```
