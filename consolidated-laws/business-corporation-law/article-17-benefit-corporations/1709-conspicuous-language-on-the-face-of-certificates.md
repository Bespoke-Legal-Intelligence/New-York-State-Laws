# Business Corporation Law § 1709 — Conspicuous language on the face of certificates

[Business Corporation Law](../README.md) › [Article 17 — Benefit Corporations](README.md)

```text
§ 1709. Conspicuous language on the face of certificates.
  All certificates representing shares of a benefit corporation shall
contain, in addition to any other statements required by the business
corporation law, the following conspicuous language on the face of the
certificate:
  "This entity is a benefit corporation organized under article
seventeen of the New York business corporation law."
```
