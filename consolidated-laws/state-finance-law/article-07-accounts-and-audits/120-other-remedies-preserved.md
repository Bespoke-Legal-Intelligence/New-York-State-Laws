# State Finance Law § 120 — Other remedies preserved

[State Finance Law](../README.md) › [Article 7 — Accounts and Audits](README.md)

```text
  § 120. Other remedies preserved. This article does not preclude the
state from the enforcement of any other remedy, for the recovery of any
debt due or to become due to the state.
```
