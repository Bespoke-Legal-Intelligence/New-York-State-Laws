# State Finance Law § 224 — Rules and regulations

[State Finance Law](../README.md) › [Article 15 — Excelsior Linked Deposit Act](README.md)

```text
  § 224. Rules and regulations. The commissioner of economic development
shall, in consultation with the comptroller, the commissioner of
taxation and finance and the superintendent of financial services,
promulgate rules and regulations necessary and reasonable for the
operation of the program.
```
