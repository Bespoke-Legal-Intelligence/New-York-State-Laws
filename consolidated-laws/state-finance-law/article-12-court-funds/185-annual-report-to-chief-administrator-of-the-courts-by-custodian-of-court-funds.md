# State Finance Law § 185 — Annual report to chief administrator of the courts by custodian of court funds

[State Finance Law](../README.md) › [Article 12 — Court Funds](README.md)

```text
  § 185. Annual report to chief administrator of the courts by custodian
of court funds. On or before the first day of February in each year the
county treasurer in each county and the commissioner of finance of the
city of New York shall file a report duly verified with the chief
administrator of the courts containing a statement of all moneys in his
or her hands on the first day of January preceding that have been paid
into court or received by him or her to the credit of any action or
proceeding, specifically stating the securities in which the moneys so
paid into court are invested or the depositories in which such moneys
are deposited.
```
