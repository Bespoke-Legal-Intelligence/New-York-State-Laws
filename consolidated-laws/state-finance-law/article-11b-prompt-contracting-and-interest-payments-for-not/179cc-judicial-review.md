# State Finance Law § 179-CC — Judicial review

[State Finance Law](../README.md) › [Article 11-B — Prompt Contracting and Interest Payments For Not-for-profit Organizations](README.md)

```text
  § 179-cc. Judicial review. Any state agency action or failure to act
with respect to the contract approval process in implementation of a
program under the provisions of this article shall be subject to
judicial review in an action pursuant to article seventy-eight of the
civil practice law and rules brought by a not-for-profit organization
which provides services that would or could assist a state agency in
carrying out activities with respect to such program.
```
