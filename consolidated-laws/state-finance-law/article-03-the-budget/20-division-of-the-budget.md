# State Finance Law § 20 — Division of the budget

[State Finance Law](../README.md) › [Article 3 — The Budget](README.md)

```text
  § 20. Division of the budget. Functions, powers and duties of the
governor, under this article, shall belong to the executive department,
and shall be exercised and performed by the governor, or under his
direction, through the division of the budget.
```
