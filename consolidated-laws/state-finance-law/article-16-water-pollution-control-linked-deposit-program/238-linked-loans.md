# State Finance Law § 238 — Linked loans

[State Finance Law](../README.md) › [Article 16 — Water Pollution Control Linked Deposit Program Act](README.md)

```text
  § 238. Linked loans. Linked loans shall be made by lenders pursuant to
the program only to eligible borrowers in connection with eligible
projects. No such loans shall be made after September thirtieth, two
thousand eleven. The credit decisions for making a linked loan shall be
made solely by the lender.
```
