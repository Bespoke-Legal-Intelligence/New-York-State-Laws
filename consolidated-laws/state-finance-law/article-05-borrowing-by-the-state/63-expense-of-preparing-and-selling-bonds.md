# State Finance Law § 63 — Expense of preparing and selling bonds

[State Finance Law](../README.md) › [Article 5 — Borrowing By the State](README.md)

```text
  § 63. Expense of preparing and selling bonds. From the moneys realized
from the sale of bonds heretofore or hereafter authorized the
comptroller shall apportion the expense of preparing and selling such
bonds against the appropriation or appropriations, and shall use such
apportioned funds in paying such expenses.
```
