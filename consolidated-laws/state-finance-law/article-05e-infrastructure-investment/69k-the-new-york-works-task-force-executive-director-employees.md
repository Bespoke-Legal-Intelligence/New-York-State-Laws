# State Finance Law § 69-K — The New York works task force; executive director, employees

[State Finance Law](../README.md) › [Article 5-E — Infrastructure Investment](README.md)

```text
  § 69-k. The New York works task force; executive director, employees.
The governor may designate an individual to act as executive director of
the New York works task force and may assign from time to time such
other employees as the New York works task force may require.
```
