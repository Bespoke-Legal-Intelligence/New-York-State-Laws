# State Finance Law § 123-F — Termination of action

[State Finance Law](../README.md) › [Article 7-A — Citizen-taxpayer Actions](README.md)

```text
  § 123-f. Termination of action. No action brought pursuant to this
article shall be compromised, discontinued or dismissed by consent,
default, or neglect to prosecute, except with approval of the court.
```
