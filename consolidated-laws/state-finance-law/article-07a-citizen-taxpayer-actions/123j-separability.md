# State Finance Law § 123-J — Separability

[State Finance Law](../README.md) › [Article 7-A — Citizen-taxpayer Actions](README.md)

```text
  § 123-j. Separability. If any provision of this article is held
invalid, such invalidity shall not affect other provisions which can be
given effect without the invalid provision.
```
