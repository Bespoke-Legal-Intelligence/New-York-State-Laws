# State Finance Law § 179-K — Acceptance of payment

[State Finance Law](../README.md) › [Article 11-A — Interest Payments On Certain Amounts Owed By State](README.md)

```text
  § 179-k. Acceptance of payment. The acceptance of payment, in whole or
in part, by a contractor shall not be deemed to constitute a waiver of
interest otherwise due under the provisions of this article nor to
supersede the provisions of section one hundred forty-five of this
chapter.
```
