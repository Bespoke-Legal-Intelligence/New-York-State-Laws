# State Finance Law § 179-I — Exclusion of interest from reimbursement rates

[State Finance Law](../README.md) › [Article 11-A — Interest Payments On Certain Amounts Owed By State](README.md)

```text
  § 179-i. Exclusion of interest from reimbursement rates. Any interest
paid to a contractor pursuant to the provisions of this article shall
not be included as revenue for the purposes of determining any
reimbursement rates applicable to such contractor.
```
