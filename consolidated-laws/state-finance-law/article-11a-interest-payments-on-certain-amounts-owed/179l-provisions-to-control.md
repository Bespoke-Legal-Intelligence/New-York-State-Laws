# State Finance Law § 179-L — Provisions to control

[State Finance Law](../README.md) › [Article 11-A — Interest Payments On Certain Amounts Owed By State](README.md)

```text
  § 179-l. Provisions to control. No contract entered into after July
first, nineteen hundred eighty-four shall contain any provision
requiring the payment of interest in a manner inconsistent with this
article, and any provision contained in such contract which waives the
right to the payment of interest is hereby declared to be contrary to
public policy and wholly void.
```
