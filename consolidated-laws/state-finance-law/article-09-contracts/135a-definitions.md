# State Finance Law § 135-A — Definitions

[State Finance Law](../README.md) › [Article 9 — Contracts](README.md)

```text
  § 135-a. Definitions. As used in this chapter, unless otherwise
indicated, the term "small business concern" shall mean a business which
is resident in this state, independently owned and operated, not
dominant in its field and employs one hundred or less persons.
```
