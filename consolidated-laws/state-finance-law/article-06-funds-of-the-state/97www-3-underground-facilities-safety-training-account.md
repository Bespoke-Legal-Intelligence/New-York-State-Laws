# State Finance Law § 97-WWW*3 — Underground facilities safety training account

[State Finance Law](../README.md) › [Article 6 — Funds of the State](README.md)

*An asterisk in a number (for example Article 24\* or § 20-C\*2) is the Senate's mark for a number the Legislature has used more than once: the marked article or section is a different one from the unmarked one with the same number. \*2 means the second one enacted with that number.*

```text
  * § 97-www. Underground facilities safety training account. 1. There
is hereby established in the joint custody of the state comptroller and
the commissioner of taxation and finance an account of the miscellaneous
special revenue fund to be known as the "underground facilities safety
training account".
  2. Such account shall consist of all moneys received by the state
pursuant to section seven hundred sixty-five of the general business law
and all other moneys credited, appropriated, or transferred from any
other fund or source.
  3. The moneys of the underground facilities safety training account,
following appropriation by the legislature and allocation by the
director of the budget, shall be made available for programs to provide
training and education on the requirements and procedures for protection
of underground facilities conducted by any one-call notification systems
registered with and certified by the public service commission.
  4. Such moneys deposited in the underground facilities safety training
account shall be payable to any one-call notification system registered
with and certified by the public service commission upon the audit and
warrant of the comptroller on vouchers approved by the department of
public service.
  * NB Repealed October 1, 2020
  * NB There are 5 § 97-www's
```
