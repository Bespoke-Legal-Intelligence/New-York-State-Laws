# State Finance Law § 97-VV — Senate recyclable materials, information services and conference fund

[State Finance Law](../README.md) › [Article 6 — Funds of the State](README.md)

```text
  § 97-vv. Senate recyclable materials, information services and
conference fund. 1. There is hereby established in the joint custody of
the state comptroller and the commissioner of taxation and finance a
fund to be known as the senate recyclable materials, information
services and conference fund.
  2. Such fund shall consist of all revenues received from the sale of
recyclable materials, fees received for the distribution of senate
documents, materials, computerized information and conference fees.
  3. Moneys of the fund, following appropriation by the legislature, may
be expended for services and expenses including but not limited to waste
disposal, production and distribution of senate documents, materials and
computerized information, or expenses related to conferences sponsored
by the senate.
```
