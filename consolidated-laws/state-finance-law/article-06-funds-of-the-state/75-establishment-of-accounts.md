# State Finance Law § 75 — Establishment of accounts

[State Finance Law](../README.md) › [Article 6 — Funds of the State](README.md)

```text
  § 75. Establishment of accounts. The comptroller is authorized to
establish whatever accounts he deems necessary in conformance with
generally accepted accounting principles for the discharge of his
duties.
```
