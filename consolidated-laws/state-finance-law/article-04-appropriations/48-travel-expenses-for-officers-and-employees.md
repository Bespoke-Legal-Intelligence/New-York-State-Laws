# State Finance Law § 48 — Travel expenses for officers and employees

[State Finance Law](../README.md) › [Article 4 — Appropriations](README.md)

```text
  § 48. Travel expenses for officers and employees. Except as otherwise
provided by law, payments for the travel expenses of officers or
employees shall be only for actual and necessary expenses incurred in
the performance of official duties.
```
