# State Finance Law § 47 — Substitute employees

[State Finance Law](../README.md) › [Article 4 — Appropriations](README.md)

```text
  § 47. Substitute employees.  Where in any act a line item
appropriation is made for the salary of an employee and the employee is
on leave of absence on less than full pay, the balance of such
appropriation shall be available for the payment of the salary of a
substitute employee.
```
