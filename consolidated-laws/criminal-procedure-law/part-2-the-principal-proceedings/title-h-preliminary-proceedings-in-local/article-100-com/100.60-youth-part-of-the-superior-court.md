# Criminal Procedure Law § 100.60 — Youth part of the superior court accusatory instruments; in what courts filed

[Criminal Procedure Law](../../../README.md) › [Part 2 — The Principal Proceedings](../../README.md) › [Title H — Preliminary Proceedings In Local Criminal Court](../README.md) › [Article 100 — Commencement of Action In Local Criminal Court or Youth Part of a Superior Court--accusatory Instruments](README.md)

```text
§ 100.60 Youth part of the superior court accusatory instruments; in
             what courts filed.
  Any youth part of the superior court accusatory instrument may be
filed with the youth part of the superior court of a particular county
when an offense charged therein was allegedly committed in such county
or that part thereof over which such court has jurisdiction.
```
