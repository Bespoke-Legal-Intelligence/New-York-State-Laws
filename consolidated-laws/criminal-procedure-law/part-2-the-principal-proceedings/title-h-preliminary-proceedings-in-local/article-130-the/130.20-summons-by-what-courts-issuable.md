# Criminal Procedure Law § 130.20 — Summons; by what courts issuable and in what courts returnable

[Criminal Procedure Law](../../../README.md) › [Part 2 — The Principal Proceedings](../../README.md) › [Title H — Preliminary Proceedings In Local Criminal Court](../README.md) › [Article 130 — The Summons](README.md)

```text
§ 130.20 Summons; by what courts issuable and in what courts returnable.
  A summons may be issued only by the local criminal court or superior
court with which the accusatory instrument underlying it has been filed,
and it may be made returnable in such issuing court only.
```
