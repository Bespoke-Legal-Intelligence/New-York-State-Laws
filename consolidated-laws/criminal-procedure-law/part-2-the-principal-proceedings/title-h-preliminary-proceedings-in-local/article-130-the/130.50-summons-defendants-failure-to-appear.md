# Criminal Procedure Law § 130.50 — Summons; defendant's failure to appear

[Criminal Procedure Law](../../../README.md) › [Part 2 — The Principal Proceedings](../../README.md) › [Title H — Preliminary Proceedings In Local Criminal Court](../README.md) › [Article 130 — The Summons](README.md)

```text
§ 130.50  Summons; defendant's failure to appear.
  If after the service of a summons the defendant does not appear in the
designated local criminal court or superior court at the time such
summons is returnable, the court may issue a warrant of arrest.
```
