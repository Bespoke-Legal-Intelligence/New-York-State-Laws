# Criminal Procedure Law § 360.05 — Jury trial; order of trial

[Criminal Procedure Law](../../../README.md) › [Part 2 — The Principal Proceedings](../../README.md) › [Title K — Prosecution of Informations In Local Criminal Courts-plea to Sentence](../README.md) › [Article 360 — Jury Trial](README.md)

```text
§ 360.05  Jury trial; order of trial.
  The provisions of section 260.30, governing the order of proceedings
of a jury trial of an indictment in a superior court, are applicable to
a jury trial of an information in a local criminal court.
```
