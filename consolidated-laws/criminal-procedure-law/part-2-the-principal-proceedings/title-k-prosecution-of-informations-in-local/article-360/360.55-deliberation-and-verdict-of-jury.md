# Criminal Procedure Law § 360.55 — Deliberation and verdict of jury

[Criminal Procedure Law](../../../README.md) › [Part 2 — The Principal Proceedings](../../README.md) › [Title K — Prosecution of Informations In Local Criminal Courts-plea to Sentence](../README.md) › [Article 360 — Jury Trial](README.md)

```text
§ 360.55  Deliberation and verdict of jury.
  The provisions of article three hundred ten, governing the
deliberation and verdict of a jury upon a jury trial of an indictment in
a superior court, are applicable to a jury trial of an information in a
local criminal court.
```
