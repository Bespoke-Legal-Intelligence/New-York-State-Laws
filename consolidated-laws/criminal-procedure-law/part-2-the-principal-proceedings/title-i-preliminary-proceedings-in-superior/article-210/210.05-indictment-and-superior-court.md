# Criminal Procedure Law § 210.05 — Indictment and superior court information exclusive methods of prosecution

[Criminal Procedure Law](../../../README.md) › [Part 2 — The Principal Proceedings](../../README.md) › [Title I — Preliminary Proceedings In Superior Court](../README.md) › [Article 210 — Proceedings In Superior Court From Filing of Indictment to Plea](README.md)

```text
§ 210.05  Indictment and superior court information exclusive methods of
        prosecution.
  The only methods of prosecuting an offense in a superior court are by
an indictment filed therewith by a grand jury or by a superior court
information filed therewith by a district attorney.
```
