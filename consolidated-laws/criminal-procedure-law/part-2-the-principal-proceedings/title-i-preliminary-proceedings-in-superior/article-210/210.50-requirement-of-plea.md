# Criminal Procedure Law § 210.50 — Requirement of plea

[Criminal Procedure Law](../../../README.md) › [Part 2 — The Principal Proceedings](../../README.md) › [Title I — Preliminary Proceedings In Superior Court](../README.md) › [Article 210 — Proceedings In Superior Court From Filing of Indictment to Plea](README.md)

```text
§ 210.50  Requirement of plea.
  Unless an indictment is dismissed or the criminal action thereon
terminated or abated pursuant to the provisions of this article or some
other provision of law, the defendant must be required to enter a plea
thereto.
```
