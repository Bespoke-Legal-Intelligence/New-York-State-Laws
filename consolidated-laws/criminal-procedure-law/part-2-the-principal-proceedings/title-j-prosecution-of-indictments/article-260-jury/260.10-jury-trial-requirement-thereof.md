# Criminal Procedure Law § 260.10 — Jury trial; requirement thereof

[Criminal Procedure Law](../../../README.md) › [Part 2 — The Principal Proceedings](../../README.md) › [Title J — Prosecution of Indictments In Superior Courts--plea to Sentence](../README.md) › [Article 260 — Jury Trial--generally](README.md)

```text
§ 260.10  Jury trial; requirement thereof.
  Except as otherwise provided in section 320.10, every trial of an
indictment must be a jury trial.
```
