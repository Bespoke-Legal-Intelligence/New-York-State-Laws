# Criminal Procedure Law § 715.05 — Dangerous drugs; definition

[Criminal Procedure Law](../../../README.md) › [Part 3 — Special Proceedings and Miscellaneous Procedures](../../README.md) › [Title T — Procedures For Securing Evidence By Means of Court Order and For Sup- Pressing Evidence Unlawfully or Improperly Obtained](../README.md) › [Article 715 — Destruction of Dangerous Drugs](README.md)

```text
§ 715.05 Dangerous drugs; definition.
  "Dangerous drugs" means any substance listed in schedule I, II, III,
IV or V of section thirty-three hundred six of the public health law.
```
