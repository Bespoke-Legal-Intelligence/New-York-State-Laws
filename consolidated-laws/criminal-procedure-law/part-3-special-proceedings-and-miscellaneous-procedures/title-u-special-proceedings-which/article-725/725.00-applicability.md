# Criminal Procedure Law § 725.00 — Applicability

[Criminal Procedure Law](../../../README.md) › [Part 3 — Special Proceedings and Miscellaneous Procedures](../../README.md) › [Title U — Special Proceedings Which Replace, Suspend or Abate Criminal Actions](../README.md) › [Article 725 — Removal of Proceeding Against Juvenile Offender to Family Court](README.md)

```text
§ 725.00  Applicability.
  The provisions of this article apply in any case where a court directs
that an action or charge is to be removed to the family court under
section 180.75, 190.71, 210.43, 220.10, 310.85 or 330.25 of this
chapter.
```
