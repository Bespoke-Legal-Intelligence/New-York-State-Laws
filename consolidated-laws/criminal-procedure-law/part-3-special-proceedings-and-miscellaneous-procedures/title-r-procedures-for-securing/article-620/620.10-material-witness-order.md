# Criminal Procedure Law § 620.10 — Material witness order; defined

[Criminal Procedure Law](../../../README.md) › [Part 3 — Special Proceedings and Miscellaneous Procedures](../../README.md) › [Title R — Procedures For Securing Attendance of Witnesses In Criminal Actions](../README.md) › [Article 620 — Securing Attendance of Witnesses By Material Witness Order](README.md)

```text
§ 620.10  Material witness order; defined.
  A material witness order is a court order (a) adjudging a person a
material witness in a pending criminal action and (b) fixing bail to
secure his future attendance thereat.
```
