# Criminal Procedure Law § 610.40 — Securing attendance of witnesses by subpoena; how and by whom subpoena may be served

[Criminal Procedure Law](../../../README.md) › [Part 3 — Special Proceedings and Miscellaneous Procedures](../../README.md) › [Title R — Procedures For Securing Attendance of Witnesses In Criminal Actions](../README.md) › [Article 610 — Securing Attendance of Witnesses By Subpoena](README.md)

```text
§ 610.40  Securing attendance of witnesses by subpoena; how and by whom
             subpoena may be served.
  A subpoena may be served by any person more than eighteen years old.
Service must be made in the manner provided by the civil practice law
and rules for the service of subpoenas in civil cases.
```
