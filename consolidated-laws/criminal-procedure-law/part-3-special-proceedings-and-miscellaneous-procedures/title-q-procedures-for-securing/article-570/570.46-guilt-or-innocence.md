# Criminal Procedure Law § 570.46 — Guilt or innocence of accused; when inquired into

[Criminal Procedure Law](../../../README.md) › [Part 3 — Special Proceedings and Miscellaneous Procedures](../../README.md) › [Title Q — Procedures For Securing Attendance At Criminal Actions and Proceedings of Defendants Not Securable By Conventional Means--and Related Matters](../README.md) › [Article 570 — Securing Attendance of Defendants Who Are Outside the State But Within the United States--rendition to Other Jurisdictions of Defendants Within the State--uniform Criminal Extradition Act](README.md)

```text
§ 570.46  Guilt or innocence of accused; when inquired into.
  The guilt or innocence of the accused as to the crime with which he is
charged may not be inquired into by the governor, or in any proceeding
after the demand for extradition accompanied by a charge of crime in
legal form as above provided shall have been presented to the governor,
except as it may be involved in identifying the person held as the
person charged with the crime.
```
