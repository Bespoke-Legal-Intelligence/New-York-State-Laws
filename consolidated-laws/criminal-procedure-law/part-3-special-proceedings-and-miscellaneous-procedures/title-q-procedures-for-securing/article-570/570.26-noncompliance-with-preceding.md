# Criminal Procedure Law § 570.26 — Noncompliance with preceding section; penalties for violation

[Criminal Procedure Law](../../../README.md) › [Part 3 — Special Proceedings and Miscellaneous Procedures](../../README.md) › [Title Q — Procedures For Securing Attendance At Criminal Actions and Proceedings of Defendants Not Securable By Conventional Means--and Related Matters](../README.md) › [Article 570 — Securing Attendance of Defendants Who Are Outside the State But Within the United States--rendition to Other Jurisdictions of Defendants Within the State--uniform Criminal Extradition Act](README.md)

```text
§ 570.26  Noncompliance with preceding section; penalties for violation.
  Any officer who shall deliver to the agent for extradition of the
demanding state a person in his custody under the governor's warrant, in
disobedience of the preceding section, shall be guilty of a felony.
```
