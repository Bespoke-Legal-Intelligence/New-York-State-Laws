# Criminal Procedure Law § 570.20 — Execution of warrant; manner and place thereof

[Criminal Procedure Law](../../../README.md) › [Part 3 — Special Proceedings and Miscellaneous Procedures](../../README.md) › [Title Q — Procedures For Securing Attendance At Criminal Actions and Proceedings of Defendants Not Securable By Conventional Means--and Related Matters](../README.md) › [Article 570 — Securing Attendance of Defendants Who Are Outside the State But Within the United States--rendition to Other Jurisdictions of Defendants Within the State--uniform Criminal Extradition Act](README.md)

```text
§ 570.20  Execution of warrant; manner and place thereof.
  Such warrant shall authorize the police officer or other person to
whom directed to arrest the accused at any time and any place where he
may be found within the state and to command the aid of all police
officers or other persons in the execution of the warrant, and to
deliver the accused, subject to the provisions of this article to the
duly authorized agent of the demanding state.
```
