# Criminal Procedure Law § 570.14 — Extradition of persons who left the demanding state under compulsion

[Criminal Procedure Law](../../../README.md) › [Part 3 — Special Proceedings and Miscellaneous Procedures](../../README.md) › [Title Q — Procedures For Securing Attendance At Criminal Actions and Proceedings of Defendants Not Securable By Conventional Means--and Related Matters](../README.md) › [Article 570 — Securing Attendance of Defendants Who Are Outside the State But Within the United States--rendition to Other Jurisdictions of Defendants Within the State--uniform Criminal Extradition Act](README.md)

```text
§ 570.14  Extradition of persons who left the demanding state under
             compulsion.
  The governor of this state may also surrender, on demand of the
executive authority of any other state, any person in this state who is
charged in the manner provided in section 570.08 with having violated
the laws of the state whose executive authority is making the demand,
even though such person left the demanding state involuntarily.
```
