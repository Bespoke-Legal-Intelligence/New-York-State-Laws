# Criminal Procedure Law § 600.20 — Corporate defendants; prosecution thereof

[Criminal Procedure Law](../../../README.md) › [Part 3 — Special Proceedings and Miscellaneous Procedures](../../README.md) › [Title Q — Procedures For Securing Attendance At Criminal Actions and Proceedings of Defendants Not Securable By Conventional Means--and Related Matters](../README.md) › [Article 600 — Securing Attendance of Corporate Defendants and Related Matters](README.md)

```text
§ 600.20  Corporate defendants; prosecution thereof.
  At all stages of a criminal action, from the commencement thereof
through sentence, a corporate defendant must appear by counsel.  Upon
failure of appearance at the time such defendant is required to enter a
plea to the accusatory instrument, the court may enter a plea of guilty
and impose sentence.
```
