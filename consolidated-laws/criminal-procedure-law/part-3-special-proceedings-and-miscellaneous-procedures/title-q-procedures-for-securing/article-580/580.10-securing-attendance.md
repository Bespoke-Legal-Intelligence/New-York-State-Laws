# Criminal Procedure Law § 580.10 — Securing attendance of defendants confined as prisoners in institutions of other jurisdictions of the United States; methods

[Criminal Procedure Law](../../../README.md) › [Part 3 — Special Proceedings and Miscellaneous Procedures](../../README.md) › [Title Q — Procedures For Securing Attendance At Criminal Actions and Proceedings of Defendants Not Securable By Conventional Means--and Related Matters](../README.md) › [Article 580 — Securing Attendance of Defendants Confined As Prisoners In Institutions of Other Jurisdictions of the United States--rendition to Other Jurisdictions of Persons Confined As Prisoners In This State--agreement On Detainers](README.md)

```text
§ 580.10  Securing attendance of defendants confined as prisoners in
             institutions of other jurisdictions of the United States;
             methods.
  The attendance in a criminal action pending in a court of this state
of a defendant confined as a prisoner in an institution of another
jurisdiction of the United States may, under prescribed circumstances,
be secured pursuant to:
  1.  Section 570.12 of article five hundred seventy, known as the
uniform criminal extradition act; or
  2.  Section 580.20, known as the agreement on detainers; or
  3.  Section 580.30.
```
