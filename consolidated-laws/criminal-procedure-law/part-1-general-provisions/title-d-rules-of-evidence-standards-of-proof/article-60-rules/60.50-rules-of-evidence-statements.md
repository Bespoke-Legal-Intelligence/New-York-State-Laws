# Criminal Procedure Law § 60.50 — Rules of evidence; statements of defendants; corroboration

[Criminal Procedure Law](../../../README.md) › [Part 1 — General Provisions](../../README.md) › [Title D — Rules of Evidence, Standards of Proof and Related Matters](../README.md) › [Article 60 — Rules of Evidence and Related Matters](README.md)

```text
§ 60.50  Rules of evidence; statements of defendants; corroboration.
  A person may not be convicted of any offense solely upon evidence of a
confession or admission made by him without additional proof that the
offense charged has been committed.
```
