# Criminal Procedure Law § 60.10 — Rules of evidence; in general

[Criminal Procedure Law](../../../README.md) › [Part 1 — General Provisions](../../README.md) › [Title D — Rules of Evidence, Standards of Proof and Related Matters](../README.md) › [Article 60 — Rules of Evidence and Related Matters](README.md)

```text
§ 60.10  Rules of evidence; in general.
  Unless otherwise provided by statute or by judicially established
rules of evidence applicable to criminal cases, the rules of evidence
applicable to civil cases are, where appropriate, also applicable to
criminal proceedings.
```
