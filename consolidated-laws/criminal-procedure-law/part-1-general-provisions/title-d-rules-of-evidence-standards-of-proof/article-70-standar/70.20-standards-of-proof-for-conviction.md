# Criminal Procedure Law § 70.20 — Standards of proof for conviction

[Criminal Procedure Law](../../../README.md) › [Part 1 — General Provisions](../../README.md) › [Title D — Rules of Evidence, Standards of Proof and Related Matters](../README.md) › [Article 70 — Standards of Proof](README.md)

```text
§ 70.20  Standards of proof for conviction.
  No conviction of an offense by verdict is valid unless based upon
trial evidence which is legally sufficient and which establishes beyond
a reasonable doubt every element of such offense and the defendant's
commission thereof.
```
