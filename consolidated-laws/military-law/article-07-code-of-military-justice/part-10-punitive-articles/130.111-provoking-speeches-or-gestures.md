# Military Law § 130.111 — Provoking speeches or gestures

[Military Law](../../README.md) › [Article 7 — Code of Military Justice](../README.md) › [Part 10 — Punitive Articles](README.md)

```text
  § 130.111. Provoking speeches or gestures.  Any person subject to this
code who, while on active state duty or in a duty status other than
active state duty, uses provoking or reproachful words or gestures
towards any other person subject to this code shall be punished as a
court-martial may direct.
```
