# Military Law § 130.86 — Assaulting or willfully disobeying officer

[Military Law](../../README.md) › [Article 7 — Code of Military Justice](../README.md) › [Part 10 — Punitive Articles](README.md)

```text
  § 130.86. Assaulting or willfully disobeying officer. Any person
subject to this code who--
  (1) strikes his superior officer or draws or lifts up any weapon or
offers any violence against him while he is in the execution of his
office; or
  (2) wilfully disobeys a lawful command of his superior officer;
shall be punished as a court-martial may direct.
```
