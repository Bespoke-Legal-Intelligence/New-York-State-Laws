# Military Law § 130.106 — Drunken or reckless driving

[Military Law](../../README.md) › [Article 7 — Code of Military Justice](../README.md) › [Part 10 — Punitive Articles](README.md)

```text
  § 130.106. Drunken or reckless driving.  Any person subject to this
code who, while on active state duty or in a duty status other than
active state duty, operates any vehicle while drunk, or in a reckless or
wanton manner, shall be punished as a court-martial may direct.
```
