# Military Law § 130.94 — Noncompliance with procedural rules

[Military Law](../../README.md) › [Article 7 — Code of Military Justice](../README.md) › [Part 10 — Punitive Articles](README.md)

```text
  § 130.94. Noncompliance with procedural rules. Any person subject to
this code who--
  (1) is responsible for unnecessary delay in the disposition of any
case of a person accused of an offense under this code; or
  (2) knowingly and intentionally fails to enforce or comply with any
provision of this code regulating the proceedings before, during, or
after trial of an accused;
shall be punished as a court-martial may direct.
```
