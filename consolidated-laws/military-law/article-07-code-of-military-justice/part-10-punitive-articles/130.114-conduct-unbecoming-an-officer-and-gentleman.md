# Military Law § 130.114 — Conduct unbecoming an officer and gentleman

[Military Law](../../README.md) › [Article 7 — Code of Military Justice](../README.md) › [Part 10 — Punitive Articles](README.md)

```text
  § 130.114. Conduct unbecoming an officer and gentleman.  Any officer
who is convicted of conduct unbecoming an officer and a gentleman shall
be punished as a court-martial may direct.
```
