# Military Law § 130.80 — Unlawful enlistment, appointment, or separation

[Military Law](../../README.md) › [Article 7 — Code of Military Justice](../README.md) › [Part 10 — Punitive Articles](README.md)

```text
  § 130.80. Unlawful enlistment, appointment, or separation.  Any person
subject to this code who effects an enlistment or appointment in or a
separation from the organized militia of any person who is known to him
to be ineligible for such enlistment, appointment, or separation because
it is prohibited by law, regulation, or order shall be punished as a
court-martial may direct.
```
