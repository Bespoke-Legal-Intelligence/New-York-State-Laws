# Military Law § 130.98 — Forcing a safeguard

[Military Law](../../README.md) › [Article 7 — Code of Military Justice](../README.md) › [Part 10 — Punitive Articles](README.md)

```text
  § 130.98. Forcing a safeguard.  Any person subject to this code who
forces a safeguard shall be punished as a court-martial may direct.
```
