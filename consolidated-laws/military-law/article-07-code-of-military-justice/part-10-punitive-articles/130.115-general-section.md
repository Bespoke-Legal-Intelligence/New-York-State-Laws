# Military Law § 130.115 — General section

[Military Law](../../README.md) › [Article 7 — Code of Military Justice](../README.md) › [Part 10 — Punitive Articles](README.md)

```text
  § 130.115. General section.  Though not specifically mentioned in this
code, all disorders and neglects to the prejudice of good order and
discipline in the organized militia and all conduct of a nature to bring
discredit upon the organized militia or a force thereof, of which
persons subject to this code may be guilty, shall be taken cognizance of
by a general or special or summary court-martial, according to the
nature and degree of the offense, and punished at the discretion of such
court.
```
