# Military Law § 130.75 — Conviction of lesser included offense

[Military Law](../../README.md) › [Article 7 — Code of Military Justice](../README.md) › [Part 10 — Punitive Articles](README.md)

```text
  § 130.75. Conviction of lesser included offense.  An accused may be
found guilty of an offense necessarily included in the offense charged
or of an attempt to commit either the offense charged or of an offense
necessarily included therein.
```
