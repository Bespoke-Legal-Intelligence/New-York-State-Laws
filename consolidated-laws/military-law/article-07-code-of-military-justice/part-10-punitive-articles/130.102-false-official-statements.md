# Military Law § 130.102 — False official statements

[Military Law](../../README.md) › [Article 7 — Code of Military Justice](../README.md) › [Part 10 — Punitive Articles](README.md)

```text
  § 130.102. False official statements.  Any person subject to this code
who, with intent to deceive, signs any false record, return, regulation,
order, or other official document, knowing the same to be false, or
makes any other false official statement knowing the same to be false,
shall be punished as a court-martial may direct.
```
