# Military Law § 130.88 — Failure to obey order or regulations

[Military Law](../../README.md) › [Article 7 — Code of Military Justice](../README.md) › [Part 10 — Punitive Articles](README.md)

```text
  § 130.88. Failure to obey order or regulations. Any person subject to
this code who--
  (1) violates or fails to obey any lawful general order or regulation;
or
  (2) having knowledge of any other lawful order issued by a member of
the armed forces, which it is his duty to obey, fails to obey the same;
or
  (3) is derelict in the performance of his duties;
shall be punished as a court-martial may direct.
```
