# Military Law § 130.109 — Malingering

[Military Law](../../README.md) › [Article 7 — Code of Military Justice](../README.md) › [Part 10 — Punitive Articles](README.md)

```text
  § 130.109. Malingering. Any person subject to this code who for the
purpose of avoiding work, duty, or service in the militia--
  (1) feigns illness, physical disablement, mental lapse or derangement;
or
  (2) intentionally inflicts self-injury;
shall be punished as a court-martial may direct.
```
