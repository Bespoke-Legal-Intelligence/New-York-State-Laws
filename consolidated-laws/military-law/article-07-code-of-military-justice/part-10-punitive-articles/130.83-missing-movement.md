# Military Law § 130.83 — Missing movement

[Military Law](../../README.md) › [Article 7 — Code of Military Justice](../README.md) › [Part 10 — Punitive Articles](README.md)

```text
  § 130.83. Missing movement.  Any person subject to this code who
through neglect or design misses the movement of a ship, aircraft, or
unit with which he is required in the course of duty to move shall be
punished as a court-martial may direct.
```
