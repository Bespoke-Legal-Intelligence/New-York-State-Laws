# Military Law § 130.84 — Contempt towards officials

[Military Law](../../README.md) › [Article 7 — Code of Military Justice](../README.md) › [Part 10 — Punitive Articles](README.md)

```text
  § 130.84. Contempt towards officials.  Any person subject to this code
who uses contemptuous words against the president, the governor or the
legislature shall be punished as a court-martial may direct.
```
