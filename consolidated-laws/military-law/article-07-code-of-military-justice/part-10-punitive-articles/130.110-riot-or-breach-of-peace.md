# Military Law § 130.110 — Riot or breach of peace

[Military Law](../../README.md) › [Article 7 — Code of Military Justice](../README.md) › [Part 10 — Punitive Articles](README.md)

```text
  § 130.110. Riot or breach of peace.  Any person subject to this code
who, while on active state duty or in a duty status other than active
state duty, causes or participates in any riot or breach of the peace
shall be punished as a court-martial may direct.
```
