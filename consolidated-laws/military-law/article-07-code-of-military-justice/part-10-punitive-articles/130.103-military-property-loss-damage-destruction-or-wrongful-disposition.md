# Military Law § 130.103 — Military property--loss, damage, destruction, or wrongful disposition

[Military Law](../../README.md) › [Article 7 — Code of Military Justice](../README.md) › [Part 10 — Punitive Articles](README.md)

```text
  § 130.103. Military property--loss, damage, destruction, or wrongful
disposition. Any person subject to this code who, without proper
authority--
  (1) sells or otherwise disposes of; or
  (2) willfully or through neglect damages, destroys, or loses; or
  (3) willfully or through neglect suffers to be lost, damaged,
destroyed, sold or wrongfully disposed of;
any military property of the United States or of the state, shall be
punished as a court-martial may direct.
```
