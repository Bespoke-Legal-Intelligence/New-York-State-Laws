# Military Law § 130.91 — Arrest and confinement

[Military Law](../../README.md) › [Article 7 — Code of Military Justice](../README.md) › [Part 10 — Punitive Articles](README.md)

```text
  § 130.91. Arrest and confinement.  Any person subject to this code who
resists apprehension or breaks arrest or who escapes from custody or
confinement shall be punished as a court-martial may direct.
```
