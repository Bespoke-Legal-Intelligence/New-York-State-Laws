# Military Law § 130.85 — Disrespect towards superior officer

[Military Law](../../README.md) › [Article 7 — Code of Military Justice](../README.md) › [Part 10 — Punitive Articles](README.md)

```text
  § 130.85. Disrespect towards superior officer.  Any person subject to
this code who behaves with disrespect towards his superior officer shall
be punished as a court-martial may direct.
```
