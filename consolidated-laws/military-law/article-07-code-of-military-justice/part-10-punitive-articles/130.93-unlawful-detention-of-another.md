# Military Law § 130.93 — Unlawful detention of another

[Military Law](../../README.md) › [Article 7 — Code of Military Justice](../README.md) › [Part 10 — Punitive Articles](README.md)

```text
  § 130.93. Unlawful detention of another.  Any person subject to this
code who, except as provided by law or regulations, apprehends, arrests,
or confines any person shall be punished as a court-martial may direct.
```
