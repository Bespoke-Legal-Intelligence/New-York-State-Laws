# Military Law § 130.74 — Accessory after the fact

[Military Law](../../README.md) › [Article 7 — Code of Military Justice](../README.md) › [Part 10 — Punitive Articles](README.md)

```text
  § 130.74. Accessory after the fact.  Any person subject to this code
who, knowing that an offense punishable by this code has been committed,
receives, comforts, or assists the offender in order to hinder or
prevent his apprehension, trial, or punishment shall be punished as a
court-martial may direct.
```
