# Military Law § 130.108 — Dueling

[Military Law](../../README.md) › [Article 7 — Code of Military Justice](../README.md) › [Part 10 — Punitive Articles](README.md)

```text
  § 130.108. Dueling.  Any person subject to this code who, while on
active state duty or in a duty status other than active state duty,
fights or promotes, or is concerned in or connives at fighting a duel,
or who, having knowledge of a challenge sent or about to be sent, fails
to report the fact promptly or the proper authority, shall be punished
as a court-martial may direct.
```
