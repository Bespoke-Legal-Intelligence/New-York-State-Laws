# Military Law § 130.77 — Conspiracy

[Military Law](../../README.md) › [Article 7 — Code of Military Justice](../README.md) › [Part 10 — Punitive Articles](README.md)

```text
  § 130.77. Conspiracy.  Any person subject to this code who conspires
with any other person or persons to commit an offense under this code
shall, if one or more of the conspirators does an act to effect the
object of the conspiracy, be punished as a court-martial may direct.
```
