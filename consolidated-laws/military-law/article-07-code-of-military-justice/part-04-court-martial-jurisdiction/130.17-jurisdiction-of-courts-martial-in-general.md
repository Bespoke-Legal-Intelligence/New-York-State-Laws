# Military Law § 130.17 — Jurisdiction of courts-martial in general

[Military Law](../../README.md) › [Article 7 — Code of Military Justice](../README.md) › [Part 4 — Court-martial Jurisdiction](README.md)

```text
  § 130.17. Jurisdiction of courts-martial in general.  Each force of
the organized militia shall have court-martial jurisdiction over all
persons subject to this code.  The exercise of jurisdiction by one force
over personnel of another force shall be in accordance with regulations
issued pursuant to this chapter.
```
