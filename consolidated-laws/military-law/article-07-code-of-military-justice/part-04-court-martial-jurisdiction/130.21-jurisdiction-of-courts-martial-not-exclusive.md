# Military Law § 130.21 — Jurisdiction of courts-martial not exclusive

[Military Law](../../README.md) › [Article 7 — Code of Military Justice](../README.md) › [Part 4 — Court-martial Jurisdiction](README.md)

```text
  § 130.21. Jurisdiction of courts-martial not exclusive.  The
provisions of this code conferring jurisdiction upon courts-martial
shall not be construed as depriving provost courts or other military
tribunals of concurrent jurisdiction in respect to offenders or offenses
that by statute or by the law of war may be tried by such provost courts
or other military tribunals.
```
