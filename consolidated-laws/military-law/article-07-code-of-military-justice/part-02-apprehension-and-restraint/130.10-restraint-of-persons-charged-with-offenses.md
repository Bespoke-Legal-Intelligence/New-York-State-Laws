# Military Law § 130.10 — Restraint of persons charged with offenses

[Military Law](../../README.md) › [Article 7 — Code of Military Justice](../README.md) › [Part 2 — Apprehension and Restraint](README.md)

```text
  § 130.10. Restraint of persons charged with offenses.  Any person
subject to this code charged with an offense under this code shall be
ordered apprehended or into arrest or confinement, as circumstances may
require.  When any person subject to this code is placed in arrest or
confinement prior to trial, immediate steps shall be taken to inform him
of the specific wrong of which he is accused and to try him or to
dismiss the charges and release him.
```
