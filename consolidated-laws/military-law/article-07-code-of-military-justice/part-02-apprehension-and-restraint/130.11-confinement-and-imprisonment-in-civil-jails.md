# Military Law § 130.11 — Confinement and imprisonment in civil jails

[Military Law](../../README.md) › [Article 7 — Code of Military Justice](../README.md) › [Part 2 — Apprehension and Restraint](README.md)

```text
  § 130.11. Confinement and imprisonment in civil jails.  Confinement
and imprisonment other than in guard house, whether prior to, during or
after trial by a military court, shall be executed in jails,
penitentiaries or prisons designated by the governor or by the adjutant
general for that purpose.
```
