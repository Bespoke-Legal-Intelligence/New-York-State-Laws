# Military Law § 130.8 — Apprehension of deserters

[Military Law](../../README.md) › [Article 7 — Code of Military Justice](../README.md) › [Part 2 — Apprehension and Restraint](README.md)

```text
  § 130.8. Apprehension of deserters.  It shall be lawful for any civil
officer having authority to apprehend offenders under the laws of the
United States or of any state, district, territory or possession of the
United States summarily to apprehend a deserter from the organized
militia and deliver him into the custody of the organized militia.
```
