# Military Law § 131.10 — Presumption of jurisdiction

[Military Law](../../README.md) › [Article 7 — Code of Military Justice](../README.md) › [Part 11 — Miscellaneous Provisions](README.md)

```text
  § 131.10. Presumption of jurisdiction.  The jurisdiction of the
military courts and boards established by this chapter shall be presumed
and the burden of proof shall rest on any person seeking to oust such
courts or boards of jurisdiction in any action or proceeding.
```
