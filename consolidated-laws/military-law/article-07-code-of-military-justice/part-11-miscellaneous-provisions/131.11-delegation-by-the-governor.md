# Military Law § 131.11 — Delegation by the governor

[Military Law](../../README.md) › [Article 7 — Code of Military Justice](../README.md) › [Part 11 — Miscellaneous Provisions](README.md)

```text
  § 131.11. Delegation by the governor.  The governor is authorized to
delegate any authority vested in him under this code, and to provide for
the subdelegation of any such authority, except with respect to the
power vested in him under section 130.22 of this code.
```
