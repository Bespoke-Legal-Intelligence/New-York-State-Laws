# Military Law § 130.40 — Continuances

[Military Law](../../README.md) › [Article 7 — Code of Military Justice](../README.md) › [Part 7 — Trial Procedure](README.md)

```text
  § 130.40. Continuances.  The military judge or a court-martial without
a military judge may, for reasonable cause, grant a continuance to any
party for such time, and as often, as may appear to be just.
```
