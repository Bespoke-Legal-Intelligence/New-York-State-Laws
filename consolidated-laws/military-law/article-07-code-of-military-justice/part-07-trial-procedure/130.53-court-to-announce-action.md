# Military Law § 130.53 — Court to announce action

[Military Law](../../README.md) › [Article 7 — Code of Military Justice](../README.md) › [Part 7 — Trial Procedure](README.md)

```text
  § 130.53. Court to announce action.  Every court-martial shall
announce its findings and sentence to the parties as soon as determined.
```
