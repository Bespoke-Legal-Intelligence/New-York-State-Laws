# Military Law § 16 — Audit of military accounts

[Military Law](../README.md) › [Article 1 — The Militia of the State](README.md)

```text
  § 16. Audit of military accounts.  The comptroller of the state shall
be the auditor of all military accounts payable by the state.  Such
accounts shall be approved by the adjutant general prior to submission
to the comptroller.
```
