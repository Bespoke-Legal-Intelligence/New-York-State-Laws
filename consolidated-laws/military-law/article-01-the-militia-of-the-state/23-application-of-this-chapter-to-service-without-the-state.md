# Military Law § 23 — Application of this chapter to service without the state

[Military Law](../README.md) › [Article 1 — The Militia of the State](README.md)

```text
  § 23. Application of this chapter to service without the state.  The
provisions of this chapter shall apply to the members of the organized
militia while serving without the state and while going to and returning
from such service without the state in like manner and to the same
extent as while serving within the state.
```
