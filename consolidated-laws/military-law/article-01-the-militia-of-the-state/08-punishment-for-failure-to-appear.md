# Military Law § 8 — Punishment for failure to appear

[Military Law](../README.md) › [Article 1 — The Militia of the State](README.md)

```text
  § 8. Punishment for failure to appear.  Any member of the unorganized
militia who is ordered to register or to be drafted into the organized
militia under the provision of this chapter and who fails to appear at
the time and place designated in such order shall be guilty of a
misdemeanor.
```
