# Military Law § 15 — The attorney general

[Military Law](../README.md) › [Article 1 — The Militia of the State](README.md)

```text
  § 15. The attorney general.  The attorney general of the state shall
give such legal advice and assistance to the adjutant general as he may
request.
```
