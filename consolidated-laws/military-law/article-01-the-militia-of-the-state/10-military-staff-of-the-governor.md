# Military Law § 10 — Military staff of the governor

[Military Law](../README.md) › [Article 1 — The Militia of the State](README.md)

```text
  § 10. Military staff of the governor.  The military staff of the
governor shall consist of the adjutant general and such aides as the
governor shall deem necessary who shall be commissioned officers
detailed by the governor from the organized militia.  Officers detailed
under this section shall not be relieved from their ordinary duties
except when actually on duty with the governor.  The military staff of
the governor shall perform such ceremonial functions and duties as the
governor may prescribe.
```
