# Military Law § 94 — Dropping from the rolls

[Military Law](../README.md) › [Article 4 — Enlisted Personnel of the Organized Militia](README.md)

```text
  § 94. Dropping from the rolls.  When an enlisted person of the
organized militia absents himself without leave and there is reason to
believe that he does not intend to return, he may be dropped from the
rolls, under such regulations as may be prescribed pursuant to this
chapter.
```
