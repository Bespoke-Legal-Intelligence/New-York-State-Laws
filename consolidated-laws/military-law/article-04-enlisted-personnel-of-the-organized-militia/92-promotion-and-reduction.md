# Military Law § 92 — Promotion and reduction

[Military Law](../README.md) › [Article 4 — Enlisted Personnel of the Organized Militia](README.md)

```text
  § 92. Promotion and reduction.  Enlisted personnel of the organized
militia shall be promoted and reduced in grade or rate, as prescribed by
regulations issued pursuant to this chapter.
```
