# Military Law § 238-B — Seizing military stores belonging to the state

[Military Law](../README.md) › [Article 11 — Privileges, Prohibitions and Penalties](README.md)

```text
  § 238-b. Seizing military stores belonging to the state.  A person who
enters any fort, magazine, arsenal, armory, arsenal yard or encampment,
and seizes or takes away any arms, ammunition, military stores or
supplies belonging to the people of this state, is guilty of a felony.
```
