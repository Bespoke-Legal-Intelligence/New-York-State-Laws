# Military Law § 255 — Benefit information for national guard members and families

[Military Law](../README.md) › [Article 11 — Privileges, Prohibitions and Penalties](README.md)

```text
  * § 255. Benefit information for national guard members and families.
  1. For the purpose of this section, "military service" shall have the
same meaning as defined in section three hundred one of this chapter.
  2. The division of military and naval affairs shall make available
state and federal benefit information for national guard members and
their spouses, domestic partners, children, and parents, including but
not limited to, information from the state family support services
program.  Such benefit information shall be made available in a printed
version and posted electronically on such division's website.
  * NB Effective October 7, 2022
```
