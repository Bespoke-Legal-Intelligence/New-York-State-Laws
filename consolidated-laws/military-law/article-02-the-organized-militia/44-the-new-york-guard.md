# Military Law § 44 — The New York guard

[Military Law](../README.md) › [Article 2 — The Organized Militia](README.md)

```text
  § 44. The New York guard.  The New York guard, whenever a state force
shall be duly organized under the constitution and laws of the United
States and of the state, shall be a reserve land force of the organized
militia, and shall be commanded by a general officer.
```
