# Military Law § 261 — Formation of state association

[Military Law](../README.md) › [Article 12 — Miscellaneous Provisions](README.md)

```text
  § 261. Formation of state association.  The officers of the organized
militia may organize themselves into an association.  Such association
may adopt by-laws not inconsistent with the statutes of the state and
regulations issued pursuant to this chapter and may alter and amend the
same and may take and hold such real and personal property as may be
necessary for the purposes of the association.
```
