# Military Law § 185 — Disposal of useless property

[Military Law](../README.md) › [Article 9 — Armories](README.md)

```text
  § 185. Disposal of useless property.  Whenever state property issued
for the use of the organized militia shall become obsolete, useless or
superfluous, the adjutant general may cause the same to be sold or
otherwise disposed of in accordance with the provisions of the state
finance law.
```
