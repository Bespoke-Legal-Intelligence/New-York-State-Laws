# Military Law § 74 — Uniforms

[Military Law](../README.md) › [Article 3 — Officers of the Organized Militia](README.md)

```text
  § 74. Uniforms.  Every commissioned officer shall provide himself with
such uniforms and articles of equipment as may be prescribed by
regulations issued pursuant to this chapter.
```
