# Military Law § 78 — Absence without leave; discharge

[Military Law](../README.md) › [Article 3 — Officers of the Organized Militia](README.md)

```text
  § 78. Absence without leave; discharge.  The governor may discharge
any commissioned officer who absents himself without leave for three
months.  Such officer shall receive a discharge in writing in such form
and of such type or classification as may be prescribed by applicable
laws and regulations of the United States and by regulations issued
pursuant to this chapter.
```
