# Military Law § 322 — Interlocutory orders

[Military Law](../README.md) › [Article 13 — New York State Soldiers' and Sailors' Civil Relief Act](README.md)

```text
  § 322. Interlocutory orders.  Any interlocutory order made by any
court under the provisions of this act may, upon the court's own motion
or otherwise, be revoked, modified, or extended by it upon such notice
to the parties affected as it may require.
```
