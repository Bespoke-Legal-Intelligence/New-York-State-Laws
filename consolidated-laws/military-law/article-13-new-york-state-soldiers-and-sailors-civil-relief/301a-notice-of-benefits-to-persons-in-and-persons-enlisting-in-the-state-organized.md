# Military Law § 301-A — Notice of benefits to persons in and persons enlisting in the state organized militia

[Military Law](../README.md) › [Article 13 — New York State Soldiers' and Sailors' Civil Relief Act](README.md)

```text
  § 301-a. Notice of benefits to persons in and persons enlisting in the
state organized militia. The adjutant general shall ensure the giving of
notice of the benefits accorded by this article and accorded by the laws
of this state to persons in and to persons enlisting in the state
organized militia.
```
