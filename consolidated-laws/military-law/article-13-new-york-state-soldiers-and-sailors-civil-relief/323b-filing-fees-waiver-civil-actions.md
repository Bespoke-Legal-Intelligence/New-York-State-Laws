# Military Law § 323-B — Filing fees waiver; civil actions

[Military Law](../README.md) › [Article 13 — New York State Soldiers' and Sailors' Civil Relief Act](README.md)

```text
  § 323-b. Filing fees waiver; civil actions. In any civil action or
proceeding commenced in any court by a person who is an active member of
the force of the organized militia, as defined by subdivision nine of
section one of this chapter, where the dispute or controversy is related
to such person's service in the organized militia or the reserves, and
the claims are based in whole or in part on this chapter, or on the
human rights law relating to military status, or on title 38 or title 50
of the United States code, all court costs or filing fees for the
commencement of the civil action or proceeding shall be waived.
```
