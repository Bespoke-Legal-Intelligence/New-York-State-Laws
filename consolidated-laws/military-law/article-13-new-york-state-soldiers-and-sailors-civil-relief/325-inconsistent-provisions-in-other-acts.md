# Military Law § 325 — Inconsistent provisions in other acts

[Military Law](../README.md) › [Article 13 — New York State Soldiers' and Sailors' Civil Relief Act](README.md)

```text
  § 325. Inconsistent provisions in other acts.  Insofar as the
provisions of this act are inconsistent with the provisions of any other
act, general or special, or of any local law, the provisions of this act
shall be controlling.
```
