# Village Law § 17-1734 — Town assessment-roll official

[Village Law](../README.md) › [Article 17 — Provisions Applicable to a Village Embracing the Entire Territory of a Town](README.md)

```text
  § 17-1734 Town assessment-roll official. The assessment-roll prepared
by the board of assessors of such a town shall be the official
assessment-roll for the town and each special tax district and school
district or portion thereof, so far as it relates to property lying
within the limits of such town. The assessment-roll shall be prepared in
duplicate and after revision and correction both rolls shall be
delivered to the supervisor of the town. The town board may publish or
cause to be published the assessment-roll in whole or in part.
```
