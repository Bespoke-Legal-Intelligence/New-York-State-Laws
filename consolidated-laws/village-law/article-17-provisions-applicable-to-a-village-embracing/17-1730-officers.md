# Village Law § 17-1730 — Officers

[Village Law](../README.md) › [Article 17 — Provisions Applicable to a Village Embracing the Entire Territory of a Town](README.md)

```text
  § 17-1730 Officers. In any such village a person otherwise qualified
to hold a village office shall not be disqualified by reason of holding
a town office, whether elective or appointive.
```
