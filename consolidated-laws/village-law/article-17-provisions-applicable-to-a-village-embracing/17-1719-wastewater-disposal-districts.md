# Village Law § 17-1719 — Wastewater disposal districts

[Village Law](../README.md) › [Article 17 — Provisions Applicable to a Village Embracing the Entire Territory of a Town](README.md)

```text
  § 17-1719 Wastewater disposal districts. All of the provisions of
section 17-1718 of this article, relating to the continuance of sewer
districts, shall apply to the continuance of wastewater disposal
districts.
```
