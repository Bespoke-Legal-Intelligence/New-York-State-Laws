# Village Law § 17-1700 — Application

[Village Law](../README.md) › [Article 17 — Provisions Applicable to a Village Embracing the Entire Territory of a Town](README.md)

```text
  § 17-1700 Application. This article shall apply to any village which
has been or may hereafter be incorporated to embrace the entire
territory of a town, to any village which has annexed or may hereinafter
annex territory to embrace the entire territory of a town, and to any
town which has been or may hereafter be created to be coterminous with
any village.
```
