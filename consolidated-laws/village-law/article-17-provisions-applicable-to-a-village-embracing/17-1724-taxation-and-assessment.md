# Village Law § 17-1724 — Taxation and assessment

[Village Law](../README.md) › [Article 17 — Provisions Applicable to a Village Embracing the Entire Territory of a Town](README.md)

```text
  § 17-1724 Taxation and assessment. In every such village all property
shall be assessed for taxation for state, county, town, village and
district purposes, in the manner provided by the laws applicable to the
county in which such village may be situated.
```
