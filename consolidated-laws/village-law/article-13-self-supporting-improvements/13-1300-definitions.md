# Village Law § 13-1300 — Definitions

[Village Law](../README.md) › [Article 13 — Self-supporting Improvements](README.md)

```text
  § 13-1300 Definitions. As used in this article:
  The term, "self-supporting improvement" shall mean any recreational
facility, and parking areas in connection therewith, established
pursuant to this article from which revenues are obtained by the
imposition and collection of rates, fees, tolls or admissions.
  The term "charges" shall mean any fees, tolls, rates or admissions.
```
