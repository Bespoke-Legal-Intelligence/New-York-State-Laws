# Village Law § 6-602 — Separate highway district

[Village Law](../README.md) › [Article 6 — Streets, Sidewalks and Public Grounds](README.md)

```text
  § 6-602 Separate highway district. The streets and public grounds of a
village constitute a separate highway district and are under the
exclusive control and supervision of the board of trustees or other
officers of the village when such control is delegated to them by such
board.
```
