# Village Law § 6-626 — Streets by prescription

[Village Law](../README.md) › [Article 6 — Streets, Sidewalks and Public Grounds](README.md)

```text
  § 6-626 Streets by prescription. All lands within the village which
have been used by the public as a street for ten years or more
continuously, shall be a street with the same force and effect as if it
had been duly laid out and recorded as such.
```
