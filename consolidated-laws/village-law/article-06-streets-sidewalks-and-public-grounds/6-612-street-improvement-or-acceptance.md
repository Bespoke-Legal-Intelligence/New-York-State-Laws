# Village Law § 6-612 — Street improvement or acceptance

[Village Law](../README.md) › [Article 6 — Streets, Sidewalks and Public Grounds](README.md)

```text
  § 6-612 Street improvement or acceptance. The board of trustees may by
resolution provide for laying out, altering, widening, narrowing,
discontinuing or accepting the dedication of a street in the village.
```
