# Village Law § 6-600 — Definitions

[Village Law](../README.md) › [Article 6 — Streets, Sidewalks and Public Grounds](README.md)

```text
  § 6-600 Definitions. The term "street" as used in this article also
includes a highway, road, avenue, lane or alley which the public have a
right to use; and the term "pavement" includes a macadam, asphalt,
brick, concrete or other similarly improved roadbed, and is only applied
to the portion of the street between the sidewalks or established curb
lines.
```
