# Village Law § 4-404 — Taking of affidavits by village officers

[Village Law](../README.md) › [Article 4 — Powers, Duties and Compensation of Officers](README.md)

```text
  § 4-404 Taking of affidavits by village officers. The village clerk or
any member of the board of trustees may administer oaths and take
affidavits upon any claim or account against the village. The village
clerk may also administer oaths and take affidavits upon matters in
connection with village business.
```
