# Village Law § 18-1824 — Form of proposition

[Village Law](../README.md) › [Article 18 — Alterations and Form of Government](README.md)

```text
  § 18-1824  Form of proposition.  The proposition submitting such local
law to the electors of the village shall be in substantially the
following form: Shall local law number..........of
nineteen............creating a commission to prepare a local law
establishing the position of village manager and defining the duties and
responsibilities thereof for the village of...................be
approved?
```
