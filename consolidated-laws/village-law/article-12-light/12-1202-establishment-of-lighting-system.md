# Village Law § 12-1202 — Establishment of lighting system

[Village Law](../README.md) › [Article 12 — Light](README.md)

```text
  § 12-1202 Establishment of lighting system. The board of trustees may,
by resolution, provide for the establishment of a system for supplying
the village and its inhabitants with light by any approved method, or
for the acquisition of an existing private system, at an expense in
either case not exceeding the sum stated in the resolution.
```
