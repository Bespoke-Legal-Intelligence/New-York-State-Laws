# Village Law § 12-1208 — Establishment of light rents

[Village Law](../README.md) › [Article 12 — Light](README.md)

```text
  § 12-1208 Establishment of light rents. The board of light
commissioners shall establish a scale of rents for the use of light, to
be called "light rents" and to be paid at such times and in such manner
as the board may prescribe.
```
