# Village Law § 20-2004 — Proof of ordinance adopted prior to September first, nineteen hundred seventy-four

[Village Law](../README.md) › [Article 20 — Actions Involving the Village; Ordinances](README.md)

```text
  § 20-2004 Proof of ordinance adopted prior to September first,
nineteen hundred seventy-four. The certificate of the village clerk
setting forth the records of the village showing the adoption of one or
more ordinances and the publishing of the notice of adoption shall be
presumptive evidence of such adoption and publication in any action or
special proceeding in any court or otherwise.
```
