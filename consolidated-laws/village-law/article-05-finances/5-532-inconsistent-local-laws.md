# Village Law § 5-532 — Inconsistent local laws

[Village Law](../README.md) › [Article 5 — Finances](README.md)

```text
  § 5-532 Inconsistent local laws. No local laws shall be adopted
changing, amending or superseding any of the provisions of this article.
```
