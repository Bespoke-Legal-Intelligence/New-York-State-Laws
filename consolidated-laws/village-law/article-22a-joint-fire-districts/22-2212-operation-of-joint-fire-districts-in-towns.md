# Village Law § 22-2212 — Operation of joint fire districts in towns

[Village Law](../README.md) › [Article 22-A — Joint Fire Districts](README.md)

```text
  § 22-2212 Operation of joint fire districts in towns. In all respects
the provisions of article eleven-A of the town law shall apply to the
establishment, extension, operation and management of any joint fire
district provided for by this article.
```
