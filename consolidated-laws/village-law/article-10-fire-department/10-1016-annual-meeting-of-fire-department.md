# Village Law § 10-1016 — Annual meeting of fire department

[Village Law](../README.md) › [Article 10 — Fire Department](README.md)

```text
  § 10-1016 Annual meeting of fire department. The members of the
several companies constituting the fire department shall hold an annual
meeting at such time and place as the council may direct.
```
