# Village Law § 10-1008 — Incorporation of fire department

[Village Law](../README.md) › [Article 10 — Fire Department](README.md)

```text
  § 10-1008 Incorporation of fire department. The members of all the
fire, hose, protective and hook and ladder companies of a village,
organized and maintained in pursuance of law, constitute a corporation
by the name of the "fire department of.........." The term, fire
department of a village, as used in this chapter, refers to such a
corporation.
```
