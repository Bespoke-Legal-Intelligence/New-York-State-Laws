# Village Law § 2-248 — First meeting of the board of trustees

[Village Law](../README.md) › [Article 2 — Incorporation](README.md)

```text
  § 2-248 First meeting of the board of trustees. As soon as is
practicable after such first election the mayor and trustees elected
thereat shall meet and appoint a clerk and treasurer and such other
officers as are authorized by law.
```
