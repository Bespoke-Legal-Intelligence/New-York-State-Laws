# Village Law § 2-226 — Notice of proceeding for review of election

[Village Law](../README.md) › [Article 2 — Incorporation](README.md)

```text
  § 2-226 Notice of proceeding for review of election. A person
instituting a proceeding under section 2-224 of this article shall serve
a copy of the verified petition on which same is to be heard on each
town clerk with whom the original or a copy of the certificate of
election was filed and also upon each person designated in the petition
for incorporation, or designated in written objections to said petition,
to receive service of papers in connection with the proceeding for
incorporation.  In addition the person instituting such proceedings
shall cause copies of such petition to be posted in five conspicuous
places in such territory.
```
