# Village Law § 14-1408 — Establishment or enlargement of sewage treatment plant

[Village Law](../README.md) › [Article 14 — Sewers](README.md)

```text
  § 14-1408 Establishment or enlargement of sewage treatment plant.  The
board of trustees of any village may establish a sewage treatment plant
or enlarge or remodel an existing sewage treatment plant at the expense
of the village by resolution adopted at a regular meeting. The
resolution shall state the maximum amount to be expended for such work.
```
