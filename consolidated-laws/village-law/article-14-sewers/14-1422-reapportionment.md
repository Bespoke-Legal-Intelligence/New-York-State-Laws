# Village Law § 14-1422 — Reapportionment

[Village Law](../README.md) › [Article 14 — Sewers](README.md)

```text
  § 14-1422 Reapportionment. A reapportionment shall be made in the
following cases:
  1. By the commissioners appointed by the county court, where the
original apportionment is reversed on the ground that it is erroneous,
unequal or inequitable.
  2. By the board of sewer commissioners where the original
apportionment is reversed upon any other ground. A reapportionment under
this subdivision shall be made in like manner as the original.
```
