# Village Law § 14-1418 — Appeal from apportionment

[Village Law](../README.md) › [Article 14 — Sewers](README.md)

```text
  § 14-1418 Appeal from apportionment. A person aggrieved by an
apportionment may, within fifteen days after the filing thereof, appeal
therefrom to the county court of a county in which any part of the
village is situated. Such appeal shall be taken by a notice, stating the
grounds thereof, addressed to the board of sewer commissioners, and
filed with the village clerk.
```
