# Village Law § 11-1116 — Rules, regulations, and local laws

[Village Law](../README.md) › [Article 11 — Water](README.md)

```text
  § 11-1116 Rules, regulations, and local laws. The board of water
commissioners may adopt rules, regulations and local laws not
inconsistent with law, for enforcing the collection of water rents and
relating to the use of the water, and may enforce observance thereof by
cutting off the supply of water.
```
