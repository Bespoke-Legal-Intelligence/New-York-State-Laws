# Village Law § 11-1104 — Acquisition of existing system

[Village Law](../README.md) › [Article 11 — Water](README.md)

```text
  § 11-1104. Acquisition of existing system. If a resolution be adopted
for the acquisition of an existing system of water works, the board of
water commissioners may purchase the same at a price not exceeding the
sum specified therein. If the board cannot agree with the owners of the
system for its purchase, proceedings may be taken to acquire the same
pursuant to the provisions of the eminent domain procedure law.
```
