# Village Law § 9-906 — Reconsideration of act or resolution upon submission to referendum

[Village Law](../README.md) › [Article 9 — Permissive Referendum](README.md)

```text
  § 9-906 Reconsideration of act or resolution upon submission to
referendum. At any time after the adoption of an act or resolution and
not later than fifteen days prior to the election, the board of trustees
of the village may reconsider its action thereon and if upon such
reconsideration the said act or resolution is repealed, the proposition
for its approval shall not be submitted at such election or if
submitted, the vote of the electors thereon shall be without effect.
```
