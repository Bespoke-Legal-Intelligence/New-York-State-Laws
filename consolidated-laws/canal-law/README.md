## Chapter text as published

```text
                               CHAPTER 542
AN ACT relating to canals, constituting chapter five of the consolidated
                                  laws
      Became a law May 26, 1939, with the approval of the Governor.
                   Passed, three-fifths being present
  The People of the State of New York, represented in Senate and
Assembly, do enact as follows:
                                CANAL LAW
```
