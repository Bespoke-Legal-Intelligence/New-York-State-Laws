# Canal Law § 73 — Registered owner to be advised of assessments and penalties

[Canal Law](../README.md) › [Article 8 — Canal Navigation](README.md)

```text
  § 73. Registered owner to be advised of assessments and penalties. The
corporation shall advise the person whose name appears on the latest
application for registry on file in its office of any assessments,
penalties or other charges levied against a float or its crew for acts
or omissions occurring while the float is on the canal system. Should
the registered owner fail to make prompt payment of such assessments,
penalties or charges, the corporation may refuse clearance to the float
and action shall be instigated as provided under section eighty-three of
this article.
```
