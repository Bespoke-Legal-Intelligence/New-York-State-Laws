# Canal Law § 77 — Copy of clearance

[Canal Law](../README.md) › [Article 8 — Canal Navigation](README.md)

```text
  § 77. Copy of clearance. The corporation, or its representative
issuing a clearance or with whom a clearance is filed, shall, when
requested, furnish a copy thereof, with any additional cargo entered
thereupon and the several indorsements, if any, which copy shall have
the same validity and effect as the original clearance of which it is a
copy.
```
