# Canal Law § 44 — When applied

[Canal Law](../README.md) › [Article 5 — Acquisition of Property For the Canal System](README.md)

```text
  § 44. When applied. The provisions of this chapter shall not affect
any proceeding or appropriation now pending for the acquisition of any
property for the purposes of the canal system of the state of New York,
and any such proceeding or appropriation shall be continued to
termination pursuant to the statute under which such proceeding or
appropriation was instituted.
```
