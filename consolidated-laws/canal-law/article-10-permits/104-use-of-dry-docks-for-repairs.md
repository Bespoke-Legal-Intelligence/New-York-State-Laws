# Canal Law § 104 — Use of dry docks for repairs

[Canal Law](../README.md) › [Article 10 — Permits](README.md)

```text
  § 104. Use of dry docks for repairs. The corporation may grant
permission to owners of vessels operating upon the canals to use the
state dry docks to the extent space is not required for the needs of
canal maintenance vessels, and the corporation shall collect from such
owners equitable charges for the use thereof. All sums collected for
such use shall be paid into the canal fund.
```
