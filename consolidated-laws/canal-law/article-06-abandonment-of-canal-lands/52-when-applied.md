# Canal Law § 52 — When applied

[Canal Law](../README.md) › [Article 6 — Abandonment of Canal Lands](README.md)

```text
  § 52. When applied.  Notwithstanding the provisions of any existing
general or special acts, the procedure in abandonment of canal lands
shall hereafter be in accordance with the provisions set forth in this
chapter.
```
