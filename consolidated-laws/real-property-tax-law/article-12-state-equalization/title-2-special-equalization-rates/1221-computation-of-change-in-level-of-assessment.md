# Real Property Tax Law § 1221 — Computation of change in level of assessment

[Real Property Tax Law](../../README.md) › [Article 12 — State Equalization](../README.md) › [Title 2 — Special Equalization Rates](README.md)

```text
  § 1221. Computation of change in level of assessment. Upon the receipt
of an assessor's annual report, filed pursuant to section five hundred
seventy-five of this chapter, which complies with the rules of the
commissioner, the commissioner shall compute the change in level of
assessment that has occurred, if any.
```
