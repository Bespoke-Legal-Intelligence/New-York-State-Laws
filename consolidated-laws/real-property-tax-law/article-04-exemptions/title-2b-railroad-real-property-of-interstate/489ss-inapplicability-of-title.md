# Real Property Tax Law § 489-SS — Inapplicability of title

[Real Property Tax Law](../../README.md) › [Article 4 — Exemptions](../README.md) › [Title 2-B — Railroad Real Property of Interstate Railroad Companies](README.md)

```text
  § 489-ss. Inapplicability of title. The provisions of this title shall
not apply to railroad real property which is owned by or under the
jurisdiction, supervision or control of either an industrial development
agency or a municipal corporation.
```
