# Real Property Tax Law § 454 — Indians

[Real Property Tax Law](../../README.md) › [Article 4 — Exemptions](../README.md) › [Title 2 — Private Property](README.md)

```text
  § 454.  Indians. The real property in any Indian reservation owned by
the Indian nation, tribe or band occupying them shall be exempt from
taxation and exempt from special ad valorem levies and special
assessments to the extent provided in section four hundred ninety of
this chapter.
```
