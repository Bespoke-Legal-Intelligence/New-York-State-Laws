# Real Property Tax Law § 486-A — Non-profit corporations operating as health maintenance organizations

[Real Property Tax Law](../../README.md) › [Article 4 — Exemptions](../README.md) › [Title 2 — Private Property](README.md)

```text
  § 486-a.  Non-profit corporations operating as health maintenance
organizations.  Real property owned by a not-for-profit corporation
operating as a health maintenance organization subject to the provisions
of article forty-four of the public health law and used exclusively for
its corporate purposes shall be exempt from taxation.
```
