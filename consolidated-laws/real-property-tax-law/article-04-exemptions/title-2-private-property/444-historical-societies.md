# Real Property Tax Law § 444 — Historical societies

[Real Property Tax Law](../../README.md) › [Article 4 — Exemptions](../README.md) › [Title 2 — Private Property](README.md)

```text
  § 444.  Historical societies.  Real property owned by a historical
society acquired for the purpose of inclosure, preservation and the
erection of monuments shall be entitled to the exemption provided in the
not-for-profit corporation law.
```
