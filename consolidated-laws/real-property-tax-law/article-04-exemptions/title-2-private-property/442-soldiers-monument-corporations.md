# Real Property Tax Law § 442 — Soldiers monument corporations

[Real Property Tax Law](../../README.md) › [Article 4 — Exemptions](../README.md) › [Title 2 — Private Property](README.md)

```text
  § 442.  Soldiers monument corporations.  Real property owned by a
soldiers monument corporation shall be entitled to the exemption
provided in the not-for-profit corporation law.
```
