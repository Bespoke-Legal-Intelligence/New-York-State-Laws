# Real Property Tax Law § 489-I — Earnings ratio

[Real Property Tax Law](../../README.md) › [Article 4 — Exemptions](../README.md) › [Title 2-A — Railroad Real Property of Intrastate Railroad Companies](README.md)

```text
  § 489-i. Earnings ratio. The commissioner shall determine the earnings
ratio for each railroad company by ascertaining the ratio of its average
railway earnings to its system reproduction cost, computed to the
nearest hundredth of one per cent.
```
