# Real Property Tax Law § 412 — Public authorities

[Real Property Tax Law](../../README.md) › [Article 4 — Exemptions](../README.md) › [Title 1 — Public Property](README.md)

```text
  § 412.  Public authorities.  Real property owned by public authorities
enumerated in the public authorities law shall be entitled to such
exemption as may be provided therein.
```
