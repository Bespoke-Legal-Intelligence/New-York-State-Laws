# Real Property Tax Law § 1132 — Preference over other proceedings and actions

[Real Property Tax Law](../../README.md) › [Article 11 — Procedures For Enforcement of Collection of Delinquent Taxes](../README.md) › [Title 3 — Foreclosure of Tax Lien By Proceeding In Rem](README.md)

```text
  § 1132. Preference over other proceedings and actions. Any proceeding
brought pursuant to this article shall be given preference over all
other proceedings and actions.
```
