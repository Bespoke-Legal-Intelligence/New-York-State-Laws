# Real Property Tax Law § 1135 — Application for surplus

[Real Property Tax Law](../../README.md) › [Article 11 — Procedures For Enforcement of Collection of Delinquent Taxes](../README.md) › [Title 3 — Foreclosure of Tax Lien By Proceeding In Rem](README.md)

```text
  § 1135. Application for surplus. In lieu of filing an answer to the
foreclosure proceeding, any person claiming surplus arising from a tax
district's enforcement of delinquent property taxes shall have the right
to file with the clerk in whose office the report of sale is filed at
any time before the confirmation of the report of sale, a written notice
of such claim, stating the nature and extent of their claim and the
address of the claimant or the claimant's attorney.
```
