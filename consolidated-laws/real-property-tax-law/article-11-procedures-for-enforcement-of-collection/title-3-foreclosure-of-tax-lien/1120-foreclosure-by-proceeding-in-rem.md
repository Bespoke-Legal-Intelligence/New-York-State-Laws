# Real Property Tax Law § 1120 — Foreclosure by proceeding in rem

[Real Property Tax Law](../../README.md) › [Article 11 — Procedures For Enforcement of Collection of Delinquent Taxes](../README.md) › [Title 3 — Foreclosure of Tax Lien By Proceeding In Rem](README.md)

```text
  § 1120. Foreclosure by proceeding in rem. 1.  A proceeding to
foreclose a tax lien shall be commenced in the manner provided in this
title.
  2. The supreme court and the county court shall have concurrent
jurisdiction over such proceedings.
```
