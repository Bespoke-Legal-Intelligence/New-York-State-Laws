# Real Property Tax Law § 1156 — Publication of notices; distress and sale

[Real Property Tax Law](../../README.md) › [Article 11 — Procedures For Enforcement of Collection of Delinquent Taxes](../README.md) › [Title 4 — General Provisions](README.md)

```text
  § 1156. Publication of notices; distress and sale. 1. Before
proceeding to enforce the collection of tax liens pursuant to this
article, the enforcing officer shall publish such notices as the
general, special or local laws relating to his or her tax district
requires.
  2. Proceedings to enforce collection by distress and sale of personal
property or other means of compulsory collection shall not be a
condition precedent to the remedies provided in this article.
```
