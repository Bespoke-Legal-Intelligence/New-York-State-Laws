# Real Property Tax Law § 1164 — Consolidation of actions or proceedings

[Real Property Tax Law](../../README.md) › [Article 11 — Procedures For Enforcement of Collection of Delinquent Taxes](../README.md) › [Title 4 — General Provisions](README.md)

```text
  § 1164. Consolidation of actions or proceedings. Actions or
proceedings pending in the courts, or otherwise, to cancel a lien which
is being foreclosed by a proceeding under this article, shall be
terminated upon the institution of a foreclosure proceeding pursuant to
this article, and the rights and remedies of the parties in interest to
such pending actions or proceedings shall be determined by the court in
such foreclosure proceeding.
```
