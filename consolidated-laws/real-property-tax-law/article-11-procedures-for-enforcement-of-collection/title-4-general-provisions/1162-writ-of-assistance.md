# Real Property Tax Law § 1162 — Writ of assistance

[Real Property Tax Law](../../README.md) › [Article 11 — Procedures For Enforcement of Collection of Delinquent Taxes](../README.md) › [Title 4 — General Provisions](README.md)

```text
  § 1162. Writ of assistance. Any party or tax district acquiring title
to a parcel of real property under and pursuant to the terms and
provisions of this article shall be entitled to a writ of assistance,
with the same force and effect as if such party or tax district had
acquired the property by virtue of a mortgage foreclosure.
```
