# Real Property Tax Law § 724 — Appeals

[Real Property Tax Law](../../README.md) › [Article 7 — Judicial Review](../README.md) › [Title 1 — General Provisions](README.md)

```text
  § 724.  Appeals.  An appeal may be taken by either party from an
order, judgment or determination under this article as from an order of
the supreme court, and it shall be heard and determined in like manner
as appeals from such orders.
```
