# Real Property Tax Law § 589-A — Authority to promulgate rules

[Real Property Tax Law](../../README.md) › [Article 5 — Assessment Procedure](../README.md) › [Title 4-A — Assessment and Taxation of Watershed Conservation Easements and Watershed Agricultural Easements Acquired By or On Behalf of the City of New York For Watershed Protection Purposes](README.md)

```text
  § 589-a. Authority to promulgate rules. In addition to any other
authority conferred upon the commissioner by statute, the commissioner
is hereby authorized to promulgate rules and mandate the use of forms to
implement the provisions of this title.
```
