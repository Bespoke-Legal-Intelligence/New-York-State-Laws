# Real Property Tax Law § 575 — Assessor's annual reports

[Real Property Tax Law](../../README.md) › [Article 5 — Assessment Procedure](../README.md) › [Title 4 — Miscellaneous Provisions](README.md)

```text
  § 575. Assessor's annual reports. The assessor shall annually file
with the commissioner reports which shall include information relating
to changes in levels of assessed valuation, changes in the condition and
ownership of real property and other information as the commissioner may
require by rule and regulation.
```
