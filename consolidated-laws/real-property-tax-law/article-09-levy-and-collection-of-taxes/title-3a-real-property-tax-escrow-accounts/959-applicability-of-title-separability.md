# Real Property Tax Law § 959 — Applicability of title; separability

[Real Property Tax Law](../../README.md) › [Article 9 — Levy and Collection of Taxes](../README.md) › [Title 3-A — Real Property Tax Escrow Accounts](README.md)

```text
  § 959. Applicability of title; separability. 1. This title shall apply
to all municipal corporations in the state which collect taxes.
  2. Separability. If any provision of this title or the application of
such provision in certain circumstances shall be held invalid, the
validity of the remainder of this title and its applicability to other
circumstances shall not be affected.
```
