# Real Property Tax Law § 942 — Payment of taxes to county treasurer after return of collecting officer

[Real Property Tax Law](../../README.md) › [Article 9 — Levy and Collection of Taxes](../README.md) › [Title 3 — Collection of Taxes; Return of Collecting Officer](README.md)

```text
  § 942. Payment of taxes to county treasurer after return of collecting
officer. The amount of any unpaid tax on any parcel of real property
returned to the county treasurer by the collecting officer in accordance
with section nine hundred thirty-six of this chapter, with interest and
any charges imposed by law thereon may be paid to the county treasurer
at any time prior to the expiration of the applicable redemption period.
```
