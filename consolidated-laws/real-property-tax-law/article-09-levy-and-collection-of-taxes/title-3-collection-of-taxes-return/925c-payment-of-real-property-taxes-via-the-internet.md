# Real Property Tax Law § 925-C — Payment of real property taxes via the internet

[Real Property Tax Law](../../README.md) › [Article 9 — Levy and Collection of Taxes](../README.md) › [Title 3 — Collection of Taxes; Return of Collecting Officer](README.md)

```text
  § 925-c. Payment of real property taxes via the internet.
Notwithstanding any contrary provision of this chapter, or of any
general, special or local law, code or charter, real property taxes may
be paid via the internet under the terms and conditions set forth in
section five-b of the general municipal law.
```
