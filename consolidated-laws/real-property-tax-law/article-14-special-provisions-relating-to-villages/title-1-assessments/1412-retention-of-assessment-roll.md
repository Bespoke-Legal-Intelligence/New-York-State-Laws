# Real Property Tax Law § 1412 — Retention of assessment roll

[Real Property Tax Law](../../README.md) › [Article 14 — Special Provisions Relating to Villages](../README.md) › [Title 1 — Assessments](README.md)

```text
  § 1412. Retention of assessment roll. A copy of the final assessment
roll shall be retained in the office of the village clerk as a public
record for a minimum of ten years from the date of completion thereof.
```
