# Real Property Tax Law § 1400 — Date of taxable status

[Real Property Tax Law](../../README.md) › [Article 14 — Special Provisions Relating to Villages](../README.md) › [Title 1 — Assessments](README.md)

```text
  § 1400. Date of taxable status. Real property shall be assessed for
village purposes according to its condition and ownership as of the
first day of January or such other date as may be applicable pursuant to
section 5-510 of the village law.
```
