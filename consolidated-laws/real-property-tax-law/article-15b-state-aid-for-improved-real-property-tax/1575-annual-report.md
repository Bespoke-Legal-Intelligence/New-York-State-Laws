# Real Property Tax Law § 1575 — Annual report

[Real Property Tax Law](../README.md) › [Article 15-B — State Aid For Improved Real Property Tax Administration](README.md)

```text
  § 1575. Annual report. The commissioner shall submit a comprehensive
report to the governor, the president pro tem of the senate and the
speaker of the assembly annually beginning March thirty-first, nineteen
hundred ninety-seven, concerning the effectiveness of all financial,
administrative and technical incentives and assistance provided by the
state for the improvement of property tax administration and the
commissioner's recommendations relating to such incentives and
assistance.
```
