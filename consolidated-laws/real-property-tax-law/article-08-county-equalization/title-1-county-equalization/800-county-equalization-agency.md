# Real Property Tax Law § 800 — County equalization agency

[Real Property Tax Law](../../README.md) › [Article 8 — County Equalization](../README.md) › [Title 1 — County Equalization](README.md)

```text
  § 800.  County equalization agency.  The board of supervisors of each
county shall be the county equalization agency except that the board of
supervisors of any county may by resolution determine that commissioners
of equalization, appointed as provided in section eight hundred two of
this chapter, shall be the county equalization agency.
```
