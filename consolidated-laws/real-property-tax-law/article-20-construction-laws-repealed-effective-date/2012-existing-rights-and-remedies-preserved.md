# Real Property Tax Law § 2012 — Existing rights and remedies preserved

[Real Property Tax Law](../README.md) › [Article 20 — Construction; Laws Repealed; Effective Date](README.md)

```text
  § 2012.  Existing rights and remedies preserved.  No existing right or
remedy of any character shall be lost, impaired or affected by reason of
this chapter, nor shall the validity of any action taken by any public
official under the law in force immediately prior to the time this
chapter shall take effect be affected by the enactment of this chapter.
```
