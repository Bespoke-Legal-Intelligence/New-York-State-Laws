# Real Property Tax Law § 2010 — Pending actions and proceedings

[Real Property Tax Law](../README.md) › [Article 20 — Construction; Laws Repealed; Effective Date](README.md)

```text
  § 2010.  Pending actions and proceedings.  No action or proceeding
pending at the time when this chapter shall take effect shall be
affected by any provision of this chapter, but the same may be
prosecuted, defended or enforced as if this chapter had not been
enacted.
```
