# Real Property Tax Law § 2006 — Exceptions

[Real Property Tax Law](../README.md) › [Article 20 — Construction; Laws Repealed; Effective Date](README.md)

```text
  § 2006.  Exceptions.  This chapter shall not be deemed to repeal or
otherwise affect the provisions of any special or local law or ordinance
or of any county, city or village charter, or other special form of
government, it being the intention of the legislature that the same
shall continue in full force and effect until and unless otherwise duly
amended, repealed or affected.
```
