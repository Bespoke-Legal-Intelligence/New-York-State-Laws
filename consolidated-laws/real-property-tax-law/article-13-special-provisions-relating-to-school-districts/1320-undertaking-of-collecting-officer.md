# Real Property Tax Law § 1320 — Undertaking of collecting officer

[Real Property Tax Law](../README.md) › [Article 13 — Special Provisions Relating to School Districts](README.md)

```text
  § 1320. Undertaking of collecting officer. The undertaking or sureties
of any collecting officer, given for the faithful performance of his
official duties, shall be liable for any moneys received or collected on
any school tax roll and warrant delivered to such collecting officer.
```
