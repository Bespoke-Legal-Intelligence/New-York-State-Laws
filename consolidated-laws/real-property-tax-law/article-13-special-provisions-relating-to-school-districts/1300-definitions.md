# Real Property Tax Law § 1300 — Definitions

[Real Property Tax Law](../README.md) › [Article 13 — Special Provisions Relating to School Districts](README.md)

```text
  § 1300. Definitions. As used in this article:
  1. The term "city school district" shall mean a school district to
which article fifty-one of the education law is applicable.
  2. The term "city" shall mean a city (a) which is co-terminous with a
city school district or (b) in which a city school district is wholly or
partly located.
```
