# Real Property Tax Law § 1600 — Statement of purpose

[Real Property Tax Law](../README.md) › [Article 16 — Consolidated Assessing Units](README.md)

```text
  § 1600. Statement of purpose. The purpose of this article is to
establish a mechanism by which two or more assessing units may
consolidate their assessment functions and thereby provide quality
assessment services to all their taxpayers at a reasonable cost.
```
