# Real Property Tax Law § 1616 — Local options not relinquished

[Real Property Tax Law](../README.md) › [Article 16 — Consolidated Assessing Units](README.md)

```text
  § 1616. Local options not relinquished. Nothing in this article shall
be construed so as to remove from the governing body of a constituent
municipality the right to exercise any option which this chapter or any
other law confers upon a governing body of a city or town in relation to
the taxable status of real property.
```
