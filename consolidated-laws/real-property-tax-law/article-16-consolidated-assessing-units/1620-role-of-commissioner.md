# Real Property Tax Law § 1620 — Role of commissioner

[Real Property Tax Law](../README.md) › [Article 16 — Consolidated Assessing Units](README.md)

```text
  § 1620. Role of commissioner. The commissioner shall facilitate the
establishment and operation of a consolidated assessing unit, and shall
prepare manuals relating to the procedures and duties of a consolidated
assessing unit.
```
