# Real Property Tax Law § 350 — Applicability

[Real Property Tax Law](../../README.md) › [Article 3 — Taxable Real Property and Standard of Assessment](../README.md) › [Title 3 — Assessors and Assessor Training In Cities With a Population of Five Million or More](README.md)

```text
  § 350. Applicability. The provisions of this title shall apply to any
city having a population of five million or more inhabitants. The term
"city", as used in this title, shall mean any such city.
```
