# Real Property Tax Law § 364 — Inconsistent laws inapplicable

[Real Property Tax Law](../../README.md) › [Article 3 — Taxable Real Property and Standard of Assessment](../README.md) › [Title 3 — Assessors and Assessor Training In Cities With a Population of Five Million or More](README.md)

```text
  § 364. Inconsistent laws inapplicable. Provisions of all general,
special, local or other laws which are inconsistent with the provisions
of this title shall be inapplicable to cities to which this title
applies, but if not inconsistent shall apply to such cities.
```
