# Real Property Tax Law § 308 — Local government defined

[Real Property Tax Law](../../README.md) › [Article 3 — Taxable Real Property and Standard of Assessment](../README.md) › [Title 2 — Assessors and Assessor Training](README.md)

```text
§ 308. Local government defined.  "Local government" when used in this
title, unless otherwise expressly stated or unless the context otherwise
requires, means a county, city or town with the power to assess real
property for the purpose of taxation.
```
