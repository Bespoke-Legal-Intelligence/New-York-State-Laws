# Real Property Tax Law § 332 — Inconsistent laws inapplicable

[Real Property Tax Law](../../README.md) › [Article 3 — Taxable Real Property and Standard of Assessment](../README.md) › [Title 2 — Assessors and Assessor Training](README.md)

```text
  § 332. Inconsistent laws inapplicable. Provisions of all general,
special, local or other laws which are inconsistent with the provisions
of this title shall be inapplicable to counties, cities and towns to
which this title applies but if not inconsistent shall apply to such
counties, cities and towns.
```
