# Real Property Tax Law § 1558 — Adoption of local laws

[Real Property Tax Law](../../README.md) › [Article 15-A — County and State Assessment Services](../README.md) › [Title 4 — Application of Article; Adoption of Local Laws](README.md)

```text
  § 1558. Adoption of local laws.  No "charter law," as such term is
defined in section thirty-two of the municipal home rule law, nor local
law shall be adopted which is inconsistent with the provisions of this
article. No local law adopted pursuant to the provisions of this article
shall be subject to a mandatory or permissive referendum.
```
