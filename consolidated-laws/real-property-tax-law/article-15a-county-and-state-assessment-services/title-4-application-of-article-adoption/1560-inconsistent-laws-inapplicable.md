# Real Property Tax Law § 1560 — Inconsistent laws inapplicable

[Real Property Tax Law](../../README.md) › [Article 15-A — County and State Assessment Services](../README.md) › [Title 4 — Application of Article; Adoption of Local Laws](README.md)

```text
  § 1560. Inconsistent laws inapplicable.  Provisions of all general,
special, local or other laws which are inconsistent with the provisions
of this article shall be inapplicable to counties, cities and towns to
which this article applies but if not inconsistent shall apply to such
counties, cities and towns.
```
