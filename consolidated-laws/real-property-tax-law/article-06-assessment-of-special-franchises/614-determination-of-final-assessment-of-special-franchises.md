# Real Property Tax Law § 614 — Determination of final assessment of special franchises

[Real Property Tax Law](../README.md) › [Article 6 — Assessment of Special Franchises](README.md)

```text
  § 614. Determination of final assessment of special franchises. After
receiving the commissioner's report regarding any complaint filed
pursuant to section six hundred twelve of this article, the state board
of real property tax services shall determine the final assessment of
each special franchise.
```
