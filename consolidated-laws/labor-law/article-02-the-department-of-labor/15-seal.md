# Labor Law § 15 — Seal

[Labor Law](../README.md) › [Article 2 — The Department of Labor](README.md)

```text
  § 15. Seal. The commissioner may adopt a seal of the department and
require that it be used for the authentication of orders and proceedings
and for such other purpose as he may prescribe.
```
