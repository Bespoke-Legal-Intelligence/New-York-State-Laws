# Labor Law § 36 — Destruction of old records

[Labor Law](../README.md) › [Article 2 — The Department of Labor](README.md)

```text
  § 36. Destruction of old records. All statistics and other documentary
matter filed with the department may be destroyed by the commissioner
after the expiration of six years from the filing thereof.
```
