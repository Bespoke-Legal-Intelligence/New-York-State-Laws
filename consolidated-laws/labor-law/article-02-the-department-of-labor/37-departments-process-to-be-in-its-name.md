# Labor Law § 37 — Department's process to be in its name

[Labor Law](../README.md) › [Article 2 — The Department of Labor](README.md)

```text
  § 37. Department's process to be in its name. All notices or orders
shall be given by and in the name of the department by the commissioner,
by the industrial board of appeals or a member thereof, or by the deputy
commissioner or other officer or employee thereunto duly authorized.
```
