# Labor Law § 32 — Interference with officer or employee of department prohibited

[Labor Law](../README.md) › [Article 2 — The Department of Labor](README.md)

```text
  § 32. Interference with officer or employee of department prohibited.
No person shall interfere with, obstruct or otherwise hinder any officer
or employee of the department in the performance of his duties.
```
