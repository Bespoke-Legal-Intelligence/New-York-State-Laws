# Labor Law § 38 — Oaths and affidavits

[Labor Law](../README.md) › [Article 2 — The Department of Labor](README.md)

```text
  § 38. Oaths and affidavits. The commissioner, a member of the
unemployment insurance appeal board, the deputy commissioner, a referee
and any other officer or employee of the department if duly authorized
by the commissioner, may administer oaths and take affidavits in matters
relating to the provisions of this chapter.
```
