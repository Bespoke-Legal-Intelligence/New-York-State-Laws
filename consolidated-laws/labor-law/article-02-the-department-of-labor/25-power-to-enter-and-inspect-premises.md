# Labor Law § 25 — Power to enter and inspect premises

[Labor Law](../README.md) › [Article 2 — The Department of Labor](README.md)

```text
  § 25. Power to enter and inspect premises. The commissioner or the
officers and employees of the department shall inspect every place which
is, or which they have reasonable cause to believe is, affected by the
provisions of this chapter, and they may in the discharge of their
duties enter any such places.
```
