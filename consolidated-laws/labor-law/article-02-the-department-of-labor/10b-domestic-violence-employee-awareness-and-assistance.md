# Labor Law § 10-B — Domestic violence employee awareness and assistance

[Labor Law](../README.md) › [Article 2 — The Department of Labor](README.md)

```text
  § 10-b. Domestic violence employee awareness and assistance.  The
commissioner shall assist the office for the prevention of domestic
violence in the creation, approval and dissemination of the model
domestic violence employee awareness and assistance policy as further
defined in subdivision nine of section five hundred seventy-five of the
executive law. Upon completion and approval of the model plan as
outlined in subdivision nine of section five hundred seventy-five of the
executive law, the commissioner shall assist in the promotion of the
model policy to businesses in New York state.
```
