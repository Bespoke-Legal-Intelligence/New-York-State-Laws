# Labor Law § 24 — Delegation of powers by commissioner

[Labor Law](../README.md) › [Article 2 — The Department of Labor](README.md)

```text
  § 24. Delegation of powers by commissioner. The commissioner may by
order filed in the department delegate any of his powers to or direct
any of his duties to be performed by the deputy commissioner or a head
of a division or bureau of such department.
```
