# Labor Law § 41 — Rules governing hearings

[Labor Law](../README.md) › [Article 2 — The Department of Labor](README.md)

```text
  § 41. Rules governing hearings. The commissioner shall not be bound by
technical rules of evidence and shall conduct all hearings according to
procedure prescribed by him.
```
