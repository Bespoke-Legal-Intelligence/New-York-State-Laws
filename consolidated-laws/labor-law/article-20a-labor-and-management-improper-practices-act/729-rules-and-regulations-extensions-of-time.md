# Labor Law § 729 — Rules and regulations; extensions of time

[Labor Law](../README.md) › [Article 20-A — Labor and Management Improper Practices Act](README.md)

```text
  § 729. Rules and regulations; extensions of time. 1. The industrial
commissioner may, from time to time, promulgate, amend and rescind
appropriate rules and regulations designed to carry out the express
provisions and purposes of this article.
```
