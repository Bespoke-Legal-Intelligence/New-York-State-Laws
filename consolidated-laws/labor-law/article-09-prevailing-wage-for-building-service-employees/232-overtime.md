# Labor Law § 232 — Overtime

[Labor Law](../README.md) › [Article 9 — Prevailing Wage For Building Service Employees and Covered Airport Workers](README.md)

```text
  § 232. Overtime. An employee, employed by a contractor, who works more
than eight hours in any one day or more than forty hours in any workweek
shall be paid wages for such overtime at a rate not less than
one-and-one-half times his prevailing basic cash hourly rate.
```
