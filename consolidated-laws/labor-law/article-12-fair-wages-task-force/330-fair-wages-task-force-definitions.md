# Labor Law § 330 — Fair wages task force; definitions

[Labor Law](../README.md) › [Article 12 — Fair Wages Task Force](README.md)

```text
  § 330. Fair wages task force; definitions. As used in this article,
the following terms shall have the following meanings:
  (a) "Commissioner" shall mean the commissioner of labor;
  (b) "Department" shall mean the department of labor; and
  (c) "Task force" shall mean the fair wages task force within the
department.
```
