# Labor Law § 818 — Separability

[Labor Law](../README.md) › [Article 23 — Apprenticeship Training](README.md)

```text
  § 818. Separability.  If any provision of this article or the
application thereof to any person or circumstances, is held invalid, the
remainder of the article, and the application of such provision to other
persons and circumstances, shall not be affected thereby.
```
