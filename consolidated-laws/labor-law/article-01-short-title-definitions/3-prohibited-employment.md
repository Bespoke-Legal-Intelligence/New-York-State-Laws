# Labor Law § 3 — Prohibited employment

[Labor Law](../README.md) › [Article 1 — Short Title: Definitions](README.md)

```text
  § 3. Prohibited employment. Whenever the provisions of this chapter
prohibit the employment of a person in certain work or under certain
conditions the employer shall not permit such person to so work, with or
without compensation, and in a prosecution or action therefor lack of
consent by the employer shall be no defense.
```
