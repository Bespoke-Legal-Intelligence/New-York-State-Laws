# Labor Law § 737 — Supplemental provisions

[Labor Law](../README.md) › [Article 20-B — Psychological Stress Evaluators and Employment](README.md)

```text
  § 737. Supplemental provisions. No individual shall administer or
participate in the administration of a psychological stress evaluator
examination within the state to any individual seeking employment
outside the state of New York or for the purpose of continuing
employment outside the state of New York.  Any individual violating the
provisions of this section shall be guilty of a class B misdemeanor upon
first conviction and upon any subsequent convictions shall be guilty of
a class A misdemeanor.
```
