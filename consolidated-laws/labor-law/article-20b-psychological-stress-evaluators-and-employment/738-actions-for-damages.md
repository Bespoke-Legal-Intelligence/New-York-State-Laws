# Labor Law § 738 — Actions for damages

[Labor Law](../README.md) › [Article 20-B — Psychological Stress Evaluators and Employment](README.md)

```text
  § 738. Actions for damages. Any employee or prospective employee
damaged as the result of a violation of any of the provisions of this
article shall be entitled to file an action for damages in the supreme
court of this state.
```
