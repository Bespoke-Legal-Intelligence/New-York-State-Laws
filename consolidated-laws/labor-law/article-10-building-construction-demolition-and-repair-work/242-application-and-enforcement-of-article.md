# Labor Law § 242 — Application and enforcement of article

[Labor Law](../README.md) › [Article 10 — Building Construction, Demolition and Repair Work](README.md)

```text
  § 242. Application and enforcement of article.  Notwithstanding any
other law or regulation, local or general, the provisions of this
article and the rules issued thereunder shall be applicable exclusively
throughout the state and the commissioner shall have exclusive authority
to enforce this article and the rules issued thereunder.
```
