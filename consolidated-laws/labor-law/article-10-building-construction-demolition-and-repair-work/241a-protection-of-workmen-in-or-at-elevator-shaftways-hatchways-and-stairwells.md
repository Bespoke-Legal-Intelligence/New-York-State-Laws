# Labor Law § 241-A — Protection of workmen in or at elevator shaftways, hatchways and stairwells

[Labor Law](../README.md) › [Article 10 — Building Construction, Demolition and Repair Work](README.md)

```text
  § 241-a. Protection of workmen in or at elevator shaftways, hatchways
and stairwells.  Any men working in or at elevator shaftways, hatchways
and stairwells of buildings in course of construction or demolition
shall be protected by sound planking at least two inches thick laid
across the opening at levels not more than two stories above and not
more than one story below such men, or by other means specified in the
rules of the board.
```
