# Labor Law § 775 — No private right of action

[Labor Law](../README.md) › [Article 21 — New York Call Center Jobs Act](README.md)

```text
  § 775. No private right of action. Nothing set forth in this article
shall be construed as creating, establishing, or authorizing a private
cause of action by an aggrieved person against a call center employer
who has violated, or is alleged to have violated, any provision of this
article.
```
