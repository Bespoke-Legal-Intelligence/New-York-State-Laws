# Labor Law § 907 — Investigations and complaints

[Labor Law](../README.md) › [Article 30 — Asbestos or Products Containing Asbestos; Licensing](README.md)

```text
  § 907. Investigations and complaints. The commissioner shall have the
authority to inspect ongoing or completed asbestos projects and to
conduct an investigation upon his or her own initiation or upon receipt
of a complaint by any person or entity.
```
