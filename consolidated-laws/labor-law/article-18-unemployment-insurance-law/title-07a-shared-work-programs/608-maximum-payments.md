# Labor Law § 608 — Maximum payments

[Labor Law](../../README.md) › [Article 18 — Unemployment Insurance Law](../README.md) › [Title 7-A — Shared Work Programs](README.md)

```text
  § 608. Maximum payments. In no event shall total benefits paid in any
benefit year, either under this title, the other titles of this article,
or both, exceed the maximum amount for which a claimant would be
eligible under the other titles of this article alone.
```
