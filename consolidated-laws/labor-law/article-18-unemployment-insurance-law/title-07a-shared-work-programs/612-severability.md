# Labor Law § 612 — Severability

[Labor Law](../../README.md) › [Article 18 — Unemployment Insurance Law](../README.md) › [Title 7-A — Shared Work Programs](README.md)

```text
  § 612. Severability. If any amendment contained in a clause, sentence,
paragraph, section or part of this title shall be adjudged by the United
States Department of Labor to violate requirements for maintaining
benefit standards required of the state in order to be eligible for any
financial benefit offered through federal law or regulation including,
but not limited to, the waiver of interest on advances or the waiver of
obligations to repay such advances to the state unemployment insurance
fund, such amendments shall be severed from this act and shall not
affect, impair or invalidate the remainder thereof.
```
