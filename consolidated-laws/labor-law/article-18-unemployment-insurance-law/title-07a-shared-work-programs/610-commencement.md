# Labor Law § 610 — Commencement

[Labor Law](../../README.md) › [Article 18 — Unemployment Insurance Law](../README.md) › [Title 7-A — Shared Work Programs](README.md)

```text
  § 610. Commencement. A shared work program and payment of benefits to
claimants thereunder shall begin with the first week following approval
of an application by the commissioner or the first week specified by the
employer, whichever is later.
```
