# Labor Law § 630 — Penalties

[Labor Law](../../README.md) › [Article 18 — Unemployment Insurance Law](../README.md) › [Title 9 — Penalities and Misdemeanors](README.md)

```text
  § 630. Penalties. Any misdemeanor defined in this title shall be
punishable by a fine of not more than five hundred dollars or
imprisonment for not more than one year, or both.
  The penalties and misdemeanors imposed by this title are in addition
to those otherwise prescribed in this entire article.
```
