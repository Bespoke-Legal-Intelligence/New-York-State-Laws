# Labor Law § 635 — Deductions from wages

[Labor Law](../../README.md) › [Article 18 — Unemployment Insurance Law](../README.md) › [Title 9 — Penalities and Misdemeanors](README.md)

```text
  § 635. Deductions from wages. Any employer who shall make a deduction
from the remuneration of any employee to pay any portion of the
contribution which the employer is required to make shall be guilty of a
misdemeanor.
```
