# Labor Law § 633 — Wilful failure to pay contributions

[Labor Law](../../README.md) › [Article 18 — Unemployment Insurance Law](../README.md) › [Title 9 — Penalities and Misdemeanors](README.md)

```text
  § 633. Wilful failure to pay contributions. Any person who wilfully
refuses or fails to pay a contribution to the fund shall be guilty of a
misdemeanor.
```
