# Labor Law § 636 — No fees to be charged for services rendered by free public employment bureaus

[Labor Law](../../README.md) › [Article 18 — Unemployment Insurance Law](../README.md) › [Title 9 — Penalities and Misdemeanors](README.md)

```text
  § 636. No fees to be charged for services rendered by free public
employment bureaus.  A person connected with or employed in a free
public employment bureau, who shall charge or receive directly or
indirectly any fee or compensation from any person applying to such
bureau for help or employment, is guilty of a misdemeanor.
```
