# Labor Law § 634 — Refusal to permit inspections of records

[Labor Law](../../README.md) › [Article 18 — Unemployment Insurance Law](../README.md) › [Title 9 — Penalities and Misdemeanors](README.md)

```text
  § 634. Refusal to permit inspections of records. Any person who
refuses to allow the commissioner or his authorized representative to
inspect his payroll or other records or documents relative to the
enforcement of this article shall be guilty of a misdemeanor.
```
