# Labor Law § 516 — Paid

[Labor Law](../../README.md) › [Article 18 — Unemployment Insurance Law](../README.md) › [Title 2 — Definitions](README.md)

```text
  § 516. Paid.  The term "wages paid" or "remuneration paid" shall, for
the purposes of this article, be deemed paid on the date such payments
are made.
```
