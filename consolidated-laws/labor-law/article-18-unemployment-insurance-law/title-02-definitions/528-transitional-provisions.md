# Labor Law § 528 — Transitional provisions

[Labor Law](../../README.md) › [Article 18 — Unemployment Insurance Law](../README.md) › [Title 2 — Definitions](README.md)

```text
  § 528. Transitional provisions.  Remuneration used to establish a
valid original claim prior to the first day of April, nineteen hundred
ninety-nine may be used to establish a subsequent valid original claim.
Under such circumstances, the general account rather than the affected
employer's account shall be charged for the portion of experience rating
charges attributable to such remuneration.
```
