# Labor Law § 521 — Benefit year

[Labor Law](../../README.md) › [Article 18 — Unemployment Insurance Law](../README.md) › [Title 2 — Definitions](README.md)

```text
  § 521. Benefit year.  A claimant's "benefit year" means the period of
fifty-two consecutive weeks beginning with the first Monday after he
files a valid original claim.
```
