# Labor Law § 626 — Exclusive procedure

[Labor Law](../../README.md) › [Article 18 — Unemployment Insurance Law](../README.md) › [Title 8 — Hearings and Appeals](README.md)

```text
  § 626. Exclusive procedure. The procedure herein provided for hearings
before referees with respect to any determination, rule, or order of the
commissioner, and for decisions thereon and for appeals therefrom, first
to the appeal board and thereafter to the courts, shall be the sole and
exclusive procedure notwithstanding any other provision of law.
```
