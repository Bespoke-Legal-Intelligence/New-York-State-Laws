# Labor Law § 572 — Notice of liability

[Labor Law](../../README.md) › [Article 18 — Unemployment Insurance Law](../README.md) › [Title 6 — Contributions to Fund](README.md)

```text
  § 572. Notice of liability. Any employer who has become liable for
contributions shall notify the commissioner of such fact immediately and
shall give information concerning his operations and persons employed by
him.
```
