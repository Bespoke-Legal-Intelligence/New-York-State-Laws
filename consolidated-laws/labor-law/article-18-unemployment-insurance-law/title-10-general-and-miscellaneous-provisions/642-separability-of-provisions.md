# Labor Law § 642 — Separability of provisions

[Labor Law](../../README.md) › [Article 18 — Unemployment Insurance Law](../README.md) › [Title 10 — General and Miscellaneous Provisions](README.md)

```text
  § 642. Separability of provisions.  If any provision of this article
or the application thereof to any person or circumstance is held
invalid, the remainder of the article and the application of such
provision to other persons or circumstances shall not be affected
thereby.
```
