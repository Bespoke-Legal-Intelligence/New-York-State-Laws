# Labor Law § 801 — Appointment; qualification

[Labor Law](../README.md) › [Article 22 — Boards of Inquiry In Labor Disputes](README.md)

```text
  § 801. Appointment; qualification.  A board of inquiry for the
purposes of this article shall consist of a chairman and such other
persons as the commissioner shall from time to time appoint, The
chairman and each appointive member of the board shall be exempt from
civil service examination and the provisions of the civil service law
and rules.
```
