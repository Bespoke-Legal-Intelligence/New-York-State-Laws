# Labor Law § 823 — Applicability of other provisions

[Labor Law](../README.md) › [Article 23-A — New York State Manpower Training Act](README.md)

```text
  § 823. Applicability of other provisions.  The provisions of section
five hundred ninety-seven and of title eight of article eighteen of this
chapter shall, insofar as appropriate, apply to payments authorized by
this article.
```
