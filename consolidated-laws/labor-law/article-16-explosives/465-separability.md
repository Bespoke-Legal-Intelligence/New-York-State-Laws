# Labor Law § 465 — Separability

[Labor Law](../README.md) › [Article 16 — Explosives](README.md)

```text
  § 465. Separability. If any provision of this article or the
application thereof to any person or circumstances is held invalid, the
remainder of the article and the application of such provision to other
persons or circumstances shall not be affected thereby.
```
