# Labor Law § 452 — Packing and labeling

[Labor Law](../README.md) › [Article 16 — Explosives](README.md)

```text
  § 452. Packing and labeling. No person shall own, possess, store, deal
in, sell, give or purchase explosives unless the packing, or encasement,
and the marking and labeling of such explosives shall comply with the
regulations promulgated pursuant to this article.
```
