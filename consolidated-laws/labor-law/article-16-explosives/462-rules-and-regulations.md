# Labor Law § 462 — Rules and regulations

[Labor Law](../README.md) › [Article 16 — Explosives](README.md)

```text
  § 462. Rules and regulations. The commissioner may make rules
supplemental to this article as deemed necessary or desirable to assure
the public safety as well as to provide reasonable and adequate
protection of the lives, health and safety of persons employed in the
manufacture, storage, handling, use, purchase, sale, disposition and
ownership of explosives. The commissioner may prescribe such regulations
as are deemed necessary and proper for the administration of this
article. The commissioner shall by rule adopt the codes, standards and
recommended practices promulgated by the most recent edition of National
Fire Protection Association, 1123 and 1126 Standards on Fireworks
Displays and Use of Pyrotechnics Before a Proximate Audience, in
accordance with the provisions of this article and article
twenty-eight-D of the general business law.
```
