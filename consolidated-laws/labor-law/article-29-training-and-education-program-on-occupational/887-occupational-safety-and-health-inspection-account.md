# Labor Law § 887 — Occupational safety and health inspection account

[Labor Law](../README.md) › [Article 29 — Training and Education Program On Occupational Safety and Health](README.md)

```text
  § 887.  Occupational safety and health inspection account.
Notwithstanding any other law, rule or regulation to the contrary,
expenditures by the department made pursuant to appropriations from the
miscellaneous special revenue fund, occupational safety and health
inspection account shall be deemed an assessable expense of the workers'
compensation board within the meaning of section one hundred fifty-one
of the workers' compensation law.
```
