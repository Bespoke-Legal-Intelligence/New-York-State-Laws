# Labor Law § 292 — Drinking water

[Labor Law](../../README.md) › [Article 11 — Factories](../README.md) › [Title 4 — Sanitation](README.md)

```text
  § 292. Drinking water. There shall be provided in every factory at all
times for the use of employees a sufficient supply of clean and pure
drinking water, and if placed in receptacles the same shall be properly
covered and kept clean.
```
