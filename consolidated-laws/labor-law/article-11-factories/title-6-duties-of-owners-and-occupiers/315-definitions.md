# Labor Law § 315 — Definitions

[Labor Law](../../README.md) › [Article 11 — Factories](../README.md) › [Title 6 — Duties of Owners and Occupiers](README.md)

```text
  § 315. Definitions. Whenever used in this article:
  1. "Owner" means the owner of the premises, or the lessee of the whole
thereof, or the agent in charge of the property.
  2. "Tenant-factory building" means a building, separate parts of which
are occupied and used by different persons and one or more of which
parts is used as a factory.
```
