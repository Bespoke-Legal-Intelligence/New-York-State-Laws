# Labor Law § 277 — Notice of issue of local construction permit

[Labor Law](../../README.md) › [Article 11 — Factories](../README.md) › [Title 3 — Fire Hazard](README.md)

```text
  § 277. Notice of issue of local construction permit. The officer of
any city, village or town having power to approve plans for the
construction and alteration of buildings shall, immediately upon the
issuance of a permit for the construction or alteration of a building to
be used for a factory or mercantile establishment as defined by this
chapter, forward to the commissioner on forms provided by him a notice
of the issue of such permit and such other information as he may
require. The provisions of this section shall not apply to the city of
New York.
```
