# Labor Law § 255 — Elevators and hoistways

[Labor Law](../../README.md) › [Article 11 — Factories](../README.md) › [Title 2 — Accident Prevention](README.md)

```text
  § 255. Elevators and hoistways.  Every elevator used in connection
with a factory, the elevator opening and the machinery connected
therewith, and every hoistway, hatchway and well-hole shall be so
constructed, guarded, equipped, maintained and operated as to be safe
for all persons. The board shall adopt rules to carry into effect the
provisions of this section.
```
