# Labor Law § 474 — Revocation of certificate of compliance

[Labor Law](../README.md) › [Article 17 — Public Safety](README.md)

```text
  § 474. Revocation of certificate of compliance.  No place of public
assembly shall be conducted for pecuniary gain unless a certificate of
compliance shall have been issued and conspicuously posted therein. The
enforcing authority granting the certificate of compliance may revoke
the same if premises are not maintained according to the provisions of
law and the rules adopted thereunder.
```
