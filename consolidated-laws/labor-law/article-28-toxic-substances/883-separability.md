# Labor Law § 883 — Separability

[Labor Law](../README.md) › [Article 28 — Toxic Substances](README.md)

```text
  § 883. Separability. If any section, clause or provision of this
article shall be unconstitutional or be ineffective in whole or in part,
to the extent that it is not unconstitutional or ineffective, it shall
be valid and effective and no other section, clause or provision shall
on account thereof be deemed invalid or ineffective.
```
