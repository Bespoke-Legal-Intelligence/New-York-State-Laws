# Labor Law § 881 — Powers of the industrial commissioner

[Labor Law](../README.md) › [Article 28 — Toxic Substances](README.md)

```text
  § 881. Powers of the industrial commissioner. The industrial
commissioner may promulgate such regulations as he shall consider
necessary and proper to effectuate the purposes and provisions of this
article.
```
