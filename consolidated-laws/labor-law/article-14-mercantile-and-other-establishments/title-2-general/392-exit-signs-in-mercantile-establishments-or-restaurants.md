# Labor Law § 392 — Exit signs in mercantile establishments or restaurants

[Labor Law](../../README.md) › [Article 14 — Mercantile and Other Establishments](../README.md) › [Title 2 — General](README.md)

```text
  § 392. Exit signs in mercantile establishments or restaurants. An
employer having custody and control of a mercantile establishment or
restaurant shall be required to post a legible sign marked "EXIT" over
doors to be used for egress in the event of a fire emergency.
```
