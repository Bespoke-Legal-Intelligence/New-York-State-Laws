# Labor Law § 206-A — Physical examinations of females

[Labor Law](../README.md) › [Article 7 — General Provisions](README.md)

```text
  § 206-a. Physical examinations of females. Whenever an employer shall
require a physical examination of a female by a physician or a surgeon
she shall be entitled to have the examination made by one of her sex or
to have another female present if a male physician or surgeon makes the
examination. The employer requiring the examination shall post a notice
informing the party to be examined of her rights under this section.
```
