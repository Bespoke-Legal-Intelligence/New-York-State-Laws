# Labor Law § 212 — Drinking water for farm laborers

[Labor Law](../README.md) › [Article 7 — General Provisions](README.md)

```text
  § 212. Drinking water for farm laborers. Every grower or processor who
employs or uses paid farm or food processing workers, whether or not he
uses the services of a farm labor contractor, shall, at his own expense,
provide safe drinking water, which may be in a portable container
reasonably accessible to each and every site whereat laborers are
working.
```
