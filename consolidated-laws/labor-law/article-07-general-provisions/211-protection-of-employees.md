# Labor Law § 211 — Protection of employees

[Labor Law](../README.md) › [Article 7 — General Provisions](README.md)

```text
  § 211. Protection of employees. The commissioner shall cooperate with
any employee in the enforcement of a just claim against his employer and
for his protection against frauds and other improper practices on the
part of any person public or private.
```
