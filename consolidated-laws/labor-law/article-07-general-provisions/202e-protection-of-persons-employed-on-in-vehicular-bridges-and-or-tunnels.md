# Labor Law § 202-E — Protection of persons employed on/in vehicular bridges and/or tunnels

[Labor Law](../README.md) › [Article 7 — General Provisions](README.md)

```text
  § 202-e. Protection of persons employed on/in vehicular bridges and/or
tunnels. The board of standards and appeals may make such rules as may
be necessary for the protection of the lives, health and safety of
persons employed on/in vehicular bridges and/or tunnels by bridge and
tunnel authorities subject to the public authorities law of this state.
```
