# Labor Law § 201-E — Maintenance of employee-patient records at occupational health service centers

[Labor Law](../README.md) › [Article 7 — General Provisions](README.md)

```text
  § 201-e. Maintenance of employee-patient records at occupational
health service centers. No person charged with the custody and care of
the health records of employees treated at an on-site employer sponsored
occupational health service facility shall release or disclose any
employee-patient record to an employer, except upon the express
authorization of the employee-patient, or as otherwise authorized by
law.
```
