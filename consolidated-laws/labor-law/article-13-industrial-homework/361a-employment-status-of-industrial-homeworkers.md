# Labor Law § 361-A — Employment status of industrial homeworkers

[Labor Law](../README.md) › [Article 13 — Industrial Homework](README.md)

```text
  § 361-a. Employment status of industrial homeworkers. All industrial
homeworkers shall be presumed to be employees of their employer and not
independent contractors.
```
