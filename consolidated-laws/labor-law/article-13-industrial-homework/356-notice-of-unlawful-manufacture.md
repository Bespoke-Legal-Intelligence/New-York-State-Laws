# Labor Law § 356 — Notice of unlawful manufacture

[Labor Law](../README.md) › [Article 13 — Industrial Homework](README.md)

```text
  § 356. Notice of unlawful manufacture. If the commissioner discovers
any manufacture in a house contrary to the provisions of this article,
he shall serve notice of such unlawful manufacture upon the employer.
```
