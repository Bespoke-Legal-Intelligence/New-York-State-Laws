# Labor Law § 360 — Notice and hearing

[Labor Law](../README.md) › [Article 13 — Industrial Homework](README.md)

```text
  § 360. Notice and hearing. No permit or certificate shall be refused,
revoked or suspended unless the holder previously have reasonable
notice, and an opportunity to be heard.
```
