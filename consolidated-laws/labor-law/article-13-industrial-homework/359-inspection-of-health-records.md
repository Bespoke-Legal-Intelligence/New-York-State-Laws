# Labor Law § 359 — Inspection of health records

[Labor Law](../README.md) › [Article 13 — Industrial Homework](README.md)

```text
  § 359. Inspection of health records. The commissioner may inspect such
records of any department of health or of any health officer or any
county board of health as will aid him in carrying out his duties under
this article. Any health officer or officer of any such department or
county board shall, at the commissioner's request, aid him in such
inspection.
```
