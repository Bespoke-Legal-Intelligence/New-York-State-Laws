# Labor Law § 363 — Construction

[Labor Law](../README.md) › [Article 13 — Industrial Homework](README.md)

```text
  § 363. Construction. If any provision of this article or the
application thereof to any person or circumstance is held invalid, the
remainder of the article and the application of such provision to other
persons or circumstances shall not be affected thereby.
```
