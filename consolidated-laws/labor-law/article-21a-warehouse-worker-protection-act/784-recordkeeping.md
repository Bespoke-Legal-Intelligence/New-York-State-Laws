# Labor Law § 784 — Recordkeeping

[Labor Law](../README.md) › [Article 21-A — Warehouse Worker Protection Act](README.md)

```text
  § 784. Recordkeeping. 1. Each employer shall establish, maintain, and
preserve for three years contemporaneous, true, and accurate records to
ensure compliance with employee or commissioner requests for data.
  * NB Effective June 19, 2023
```
