# Labor Law § 788 — Other powers

[Labor Law](../README.md) › [Article 21-A — Warehouse Worker Protection Act](README.md)

```text
  § 788. Other powers. The attorney general, either upon his or her own
complaint or the complaint of any person acting for themselves or the
general public, has the authority to prosecute actions, either civil or
criminal, for violations of this article, or to enforce the provisions
thereof independently and without specific direction of the
commissioner.
```
