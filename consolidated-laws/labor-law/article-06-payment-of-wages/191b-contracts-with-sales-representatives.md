# Labor Law § 191-B — Contracts with sales representatives

[Labor Law](../README.md) › [Article 6 — Payment of Wages](README.md)

```text
  § 191-b. Contracts with sales representatives. 1. When a principal
contracts with a sales representative to solicit wholesale orders within
this state, the contract shall be in writing and shall set forth the
method by which the commission is to be computed and paid.
  2. The principal shall provide each sales representative with a signed
copy of the contract.  The principal shall obtain a signed receipt for
the contract from each sales representative.
  3. A sales representative during the course of the contract, shall be
paid the earned commission and all other monies earned or payable in
accordance with the agreed terms of the contract, but not later than
five business days after the commission has become earned.
```
