# Labor Law § 849 — Reporting and evaluation

[Labor Law](../README.md) › [Article 24 — Worker Adjustment Act](README.md)

```text
  § 849. Reporting and evaluation. Reporting and evaluation requirements
prescribed by this article shall conform with those stipulated in
article forty-nine of the executive law, the workforce preparation
evaluation act.
```
