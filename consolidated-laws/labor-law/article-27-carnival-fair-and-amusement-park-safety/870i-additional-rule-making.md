# Labor Law § 870-I — Additional rule making

[Labor Law](../README.md) › [Article 27 — Carnival, Fair and Amusement Park Safety](README.md)

```text
  § 870-i. Additional rule making. The commissioner may make additional
rules consistent with this article guarding against personal injuries in
the assembly, disassembly and use of amusement devices, viewing stands
and tents at carnivals, fairs and amusement parks to persons employed at
or to persons attending the carnivals, fairs and amusement parks. Where
the carnivals, fairs and amusement parks are located within the city of
New York, the department of buildings of the city of New York may also
make and enforce such additional rules.
```
