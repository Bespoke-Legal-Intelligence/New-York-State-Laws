# Labor Law § 870-H — Notice to owners

[Labor Law](../README.md) › [Article 27 — Carnival, Fair and Amusement Park Safety](README.md)

```text
  § 870-h. Notice to owners. The commissioner shall furnish to all
owners, lessees and operators of amusement devices, viewing stands and
tents notice of all rights and obligations prescribed under this article
upon receipt of permit applications.
```
