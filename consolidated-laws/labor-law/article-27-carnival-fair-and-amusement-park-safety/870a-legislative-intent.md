# Labor Law § 870-A — Legislative intent

[Labor Law](../README.md) › [Article 27 — Carnival, Fair and Amusement Park Safety](README.md)

```text
  § 870-a. Legislative intent. 1. The purpose of this article is to
guard against personal injuries in the assembly, disassembly and use of
amusement devices, viewing stands and tents at carnivals, fairs and
amusement parks to persons employed at or attending the same, and, in
the event of a personal injury, to insure to the injured party the
possibility of financial recovery as against the owner of the carnival,
fair or amusement park where the injury occurred.
  2. It is the intent of this article that such devices, viewing stands
and tents shall be so designed, constructed, assembled or disassembled,
maintained and operated as to prevent such injuries.
```
