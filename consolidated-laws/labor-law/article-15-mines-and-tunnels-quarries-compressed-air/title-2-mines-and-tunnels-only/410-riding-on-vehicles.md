# Labor Law § 410 — Riding on vehicles

[Labor Law](../../README.md) › [Article 15 — Mines and Tunnels; Quarries; Compressed Air](../README.md) › [Title 2 — Mines and Tunnels Only](README.md)

```text
  § 410. Riding on vehicles. No person shall ride upon, or be permitted
to ride upon, any loaded car, cage or bucket into or out of any mine or
tunnel in process of construction.
```
