# Labor Law § 417 — Ventilation

[Labor Law](../../README.md) › [Article 15 — Mines and Tunnels; Quarries; Compressed Air](../README.md) › [Title 2 — Mines and Tunnels Only](README.md)

```text
  § 417. Ventilation. An air current sufficient to remove smoke and
noxious gases and to insure the safety of every employee shall be
conducted along every passageway and working place.
```
