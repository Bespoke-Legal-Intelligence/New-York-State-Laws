# Labor Law § 416 — Timbering

[Labor Law](../../README.md) › [Article 15 — Mines and Tunnels; Quarries; Compressed Air](../README.md) › [Title 2 — Mines and Tunnels Only](README.md)

```text
  § 416. Timbering. The roofs and sides of each passageway and working
place in every mine and tunnel shall be properly timbered and shall be
kept secure.  No person shall work in an unsafe place except to make it
safe.
```
