# Labor Law § 403 — Storage of inflammable supplies

[Labor Law](../../README.md) › [Article 15 — Mines and Tunnels; Quarries; Compressed Air](../README.md) › [Title 1 — Mines, Tunnels and Quarries](README.md)

```text
  § 403. Storage of inflammable supplies. No powder or oils shall be
stored in any mine, tunnel or quarry, or in or around any shaft, engine
or boiler house. All inflammable or destructive supplies shall be stored
at safe distances from mine or tunnel openings.
```
