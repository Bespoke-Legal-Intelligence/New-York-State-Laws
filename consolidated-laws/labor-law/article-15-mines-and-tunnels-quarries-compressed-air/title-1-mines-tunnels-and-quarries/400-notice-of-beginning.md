# Labor Law § 400 — Notice of beginning

[Labor Law](../../README.md) › [Article 15 — Mines and Tunnels; Quarries; Compressed Air](../README.md) › [Title 1 — Mines, Tunnels and Quarries](README.md)

```text
  § 400. Notice of beginning. Every operator opening a new shaft,
incline, tunnel or quarry shall report in writing its location and the
name of its owner to the commissioner before the excavation reaches a
depth of twenty-five feet.
```
