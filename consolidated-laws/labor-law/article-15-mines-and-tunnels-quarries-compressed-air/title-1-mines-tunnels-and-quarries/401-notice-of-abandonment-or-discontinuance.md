# Labor Law § 401 — Notice of abandonment or discontinuance

[Labor Law](../../README.md) › [Article 15 — Mines and Tunnels; Quarries; Compressed Air](../README.md) › [Title 1 — Mines, Tunnels and Quarries](README.md)

```text
  § 401. Notice of abandonment or discontinuance. Every operator
abandoning or permanently discontinuing any shaft, incline, tunnel or
quarry shall immediately notify the commission in writing of such
abandonment or discontinuance.
```
