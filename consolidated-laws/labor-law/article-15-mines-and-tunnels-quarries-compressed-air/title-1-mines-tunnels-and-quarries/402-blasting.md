# Labor Law § 402 — Blasting

[Labor Law](../../README.md) › [Article 15 — Mines and Tunnels; Quarries; Compressed Air](../README.md) › [Title 1 — Mines, Tunnels and Quarries](README.md)

```text
  § 402. Blasting. When explosives are used in a mine, tunnel or quarry,
the manner of storing, keeping, moving, charging and firing, or in any
manner using such explosives, shall be in accordance with the rules
prescribed by the board.
```
