# Labor Law § 436 — Penalties

[Labor Law](../../README.md) › [Article 15 — Mines and Tunnels; Quarries; Compressed Air](../README.md) › [Title 4 — Enforcement of Article](README.md)

```text
  § 436. Penalties. Violation of or noncompliance with any provision of
this article shall be a misdemeanor punishable by a fine of not less
than two hundred and fifty dollars or by imprisonment for one year or by
both.
```
