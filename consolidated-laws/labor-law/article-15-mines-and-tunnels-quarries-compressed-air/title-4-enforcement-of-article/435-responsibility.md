# Labor Law § 435 — Responsibility

[Labor Law](../../README.md) › [Article 15 — Mines and Tunnels; Quarries; Compressed Air](../README.md) › [Title 4 — Enforcement of Article](README.md)

```text
  § 435. Responsibility. Except as otherwise provided the owner, agent,
lessee, manager, operator and superintendent shall be responsible for
the observance of the provisions of this article.
```
