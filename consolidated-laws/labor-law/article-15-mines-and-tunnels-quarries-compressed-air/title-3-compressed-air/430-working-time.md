# Labor Law § 430 — Working time

[Labor Law](../../README.md) › [Article 15 — Mines and Tunnels; Quarries; Compressed Air](../README.md) › [Title 3 — Compressed Air](README.md)

```text
  § 430. Working time. The working time in compressed air in any
twenty-four hours shall be specified by the board in its rules. No
person shall be subjected to pressure exceeding fifty pounds except in
emergency. The maximum number of hours for any pressure, shall be as
specified by the board in its rules.
```
