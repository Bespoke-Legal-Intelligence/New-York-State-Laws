# Labor Law § 431 — Decompression

[Labor Law](../../README.md) › [Article 15 — Mines and Tunnels; Quarries; Compressed Air](../README.md) › [Title 3 — Compressed Air](README.md)

```text
  § 431. Decompression. The employer or person in charge shall not
permit any person to pass from compressed air to normal pressure without
passing through a period of decompression. Such periods of decompression
shall be as specified by the board in its rules.
```
