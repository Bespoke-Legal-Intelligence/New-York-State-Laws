# Labor Law § 711 — Cooperation

[Labor Law](../README.md) › [Article 20 — New York State Labor Relations Act](README.md)

```text
  § 711. Cooperation. The department and the other departments of the
state and the officers and employees thereof shall render such
assistance to the board as it may request in connection with its work
hereunder. The board shall at all times have the cooperation of and
shall cooperate with the department and the several boards, divisions
and officers thereof in carrying out the policy declared in section
seven hundred of this article.
```
