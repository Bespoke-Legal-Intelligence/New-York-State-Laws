# Labor Law § 712 — Repeal of inconsistent provisions

[Labor Law](../README.md) › [Article 20 — New York State Labor Relations Act](README.md)

```text
  § 712. Repeal of inconsistent provisions. Insofar as the provisions of
this article are inconsistent with the provisions of any other general,
special or local law, the provisions of this article shall be
controlling.
```
