# Labor Law § 163-A — Farm laborers

[Labor Law](../../README.md) › [Article 5 — Hours of Labor](../README.md) › [Title 1 — General](README.md)

```text
  § 163-a. Farm laborers. No person or corporation operating a farm
shall require any farm laborer to work more than sixty hours in any
calendar week; provided, however, that any overtime work performed by a
farm laborer shall be at a rate which is at least one and one-half times
the farm laborer's regular rate of pay.
```
