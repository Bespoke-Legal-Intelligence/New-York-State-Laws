# Labor Law § 163 — Hours of labor in brickyards

[Labor Law](../../README.md) › [Article 5 — Hours of Labor](../README.md) › [Title 1 — General](README.md)

```text
  § 163. Hours of labor in brickyards. No corporation owning or
operating a brickyard shall require employees to work more than ten
hours in any day, or to commence work before seven o'clock in the
morning. But overwork and work prior to seven o'clock in the morning for
extra compensation may be performed by agreement between employer and
employee.
```
