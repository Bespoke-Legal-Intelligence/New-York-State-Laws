# Labor Law § 220-C — Contractor or subcontractor verifying false statement guilty of perjury

[Labor Law](../README.md) › [Article 8 — Public Work](README.md)

```text
  § 220-c. Contractor or subcontractor verifying false statement guilty
of perjury.
  Any contractor or subcontractor who shall upon his oath verify any
statement required to be filed under this act which is known by him to
be false shall be guilty of perjury and punishable as provided by the
penal law.
```
