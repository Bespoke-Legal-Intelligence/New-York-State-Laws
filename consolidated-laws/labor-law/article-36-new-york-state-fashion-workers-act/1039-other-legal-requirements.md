# Labor Law § 1039 — Other legal requirements

[Labor Law](../README.md) › [Article 36 — New York State Fashion Workers Act](README.md)

```text
  § 1039. Other legal requirements. Nothing in this article shall
diminish the rights, privileges, wages, working conditions or remedies
of any employee under any applicable collective bargaining agreement.
```
