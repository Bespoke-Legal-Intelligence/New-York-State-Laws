# Real Property Law § 239-E — Portable kerosene heaters prohibited in multiple dwellings

[Real Property Law](../README.md) › [Article 7A — Portable Kerosene Heaters](README.md)

```text
  § 239-e. Portable kerosene heaters prohibited in multiple dwellings.
It shall be unlawful to use any portable kerosene heater in a multiple
dwelling.
```
