# Real Property Law § 601 — Construction

[Real Property Law](../README.md) › [Article 20 — Laws Repealed; Construction; When to Take Effect](README.md)

```text
  § 601. Construction. This chapter does not alter or impair any vested
estate, interest or right, or alter or affect the construction of any
conveyance, will or other instrument which has taken effect at any time
before this chapter becomes a law.
```
