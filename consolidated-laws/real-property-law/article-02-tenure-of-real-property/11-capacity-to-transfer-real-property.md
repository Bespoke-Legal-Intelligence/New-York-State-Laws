# Real Property Law § 11 — Capacity to transfer real property

[Real Property Law](../README.md) › [Article 2 — Tenure of Real Property](README.md)

```text
  § 11. Capacity to transfer real property. A person other than a minor,
a mentally retarded person, or person of unsound mind, seized of or
entitled to an estate or interest in real property, may transfer such
estate or interest.
```
