# Real Property Law § 241 — Ancient conveyances abolished

[Real Property Law](../README.md) › [Article 8 — Conveyances and Mortgages](README.md)

```text
  § 241. Ancient conveyances abolished. The conveyance of real property
by feoffment, with livery of seizin, or by fines, or common recoveries,
is abolished.
```
