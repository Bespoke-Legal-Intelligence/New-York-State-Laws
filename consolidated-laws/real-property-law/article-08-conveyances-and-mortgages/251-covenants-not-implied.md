# Real Property Law § 251 — Covenants not implied

[Real Property Law](../README.md) › [Article 8 — Conveyances and Mortgages](README.md)

```text
  § 251. Covenants not implied. A covenant is not implied in a
conveyance of real property, whether the conveyance contains any special
covenant or not.
```
