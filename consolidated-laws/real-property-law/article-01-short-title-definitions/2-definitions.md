# Real Property Law § 2 — Definitions

[Real Property Law](../README.md) › [Article 1 — Short Title; Definitions](README.md)

```text
  § 2. Definitions. 1. The terms "real property" and "lands" as used in
the first eight articles of this chapter are co-extensive in meaning
with lands, tenements and hereditaments.
  2. The term "infant" or "minor" as used in this chapter means a person
who has not attained the age of eighteen years.
```
