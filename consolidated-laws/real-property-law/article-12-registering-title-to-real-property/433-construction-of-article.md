# Real Property Law § 433 — Construction of article

[Real Property Law](../README.md) › [Article 12 — Registering Title to Real Property](README.md)

```text
  § 433. Construction of article. This article shall be construed
liberally, so far as may be necessary for the purpose of effecting its
general intent.
```
