# Real Property Law § 205 — Widow may bequeath a crop

[Real Property Law](../README.md) › [Article 6 — Dower and Curtesy](README.md)

```text
  § 205. Widow may bequeath a crop. A widow may bequeath a crop in the
ground of land held by her in dower.
```
