# Real Property Law § 344-K — Impairment of rights

[Real Property Law](../README.md) › [Article 9-E — Conveyance and Encumbrance of Manufactured Homes As Real Property](README.md)

```text
  * § 344-k. Impairment of rights. Nothing in this section shall impair
any rights existing under law prior to the effective date of this
section of anyone claiming an interest in a manufactured home.
  * NB Effective December 12, 2026
```
