# Legislative Law § 1-Q — Record of appearances

[Legislative Law](../README.md) › [Article 1-A — Lobbying Act](README.md)

```text
  § 1-q. Record of appearances. The commission shall promulgate all
rules or regulations and any procedures, forms, or instructions
necessary to implement the provisions of section one hundred sixty-six
of the executive law relating to the quarterly filing of the record of
appearances before regulatory agencies.
```
