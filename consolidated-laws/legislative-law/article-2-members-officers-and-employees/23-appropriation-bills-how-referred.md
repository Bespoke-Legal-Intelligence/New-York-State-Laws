# Legislative Law § 23 — Appropriation bills, how referred

[Legislative Law](../README.md) › [Article 2 — Members, Officers and Employees of the Legislature](README.md)

```text
  § 23. Appropriation bills, how referred. All bills that involve any
appropriation from the treasury of the state, when introduced in the
senate, shall be referred to the committee on finance, and when
introduced in the assembly, shall be referred to the committee on ways
and means.
```
