# Legislative Law § 3 — Expulsion of members

[Legislative Law](../README.md) › [Article 2 — Members, Officers and Employees of the Legislature](README.md)

```text
  § 3. Expulsion of members. Each house has the power to expel any of
its members, after the report of a committee to inquire into the charges
against him shall have been made.
```
