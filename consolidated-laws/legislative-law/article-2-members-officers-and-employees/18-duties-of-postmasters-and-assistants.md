# Legislative Law § 18 — Duties of postmasters and assistants

[Legislative Law](../README.md) › [Article 2 — Members, Officers and Employees of the Legislature](README.md)

```text
  § 18. Duties of postmasters and assistants. The postmasters and
assistant postmasters and post-office messengers shall perform all the
labors in the post-offices of their respective houses.
```
