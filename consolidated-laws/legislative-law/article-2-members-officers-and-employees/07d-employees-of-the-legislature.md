# Legislative Law § 7-D — Employees of the legislature

[Legislative Law](../README.md) › [Article 2 — Members, Officers and Employees of the Legislature](README.md)

```text
  § 7-d. Employees of the legislature. Employees of any legislative
committee, commission, task force, council or similar body, where all
the members of such body are members of the legislature, shall be
considered to be employees of the legislature for all purposes.
```
