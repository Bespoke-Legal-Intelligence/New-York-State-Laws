# Legislative Law § 9 — Additional employees

[Legislative Law](../README.md) › [Article 2 — Members, Officers and Employees of the Legislature](README.md)

```text
  § 9. Additional employees. A committee of investigation or other
special committee of the legislature or of either house thereof may
employ needed assistants and fix their compensation within the amount
available to the committee by appropriation.
```
