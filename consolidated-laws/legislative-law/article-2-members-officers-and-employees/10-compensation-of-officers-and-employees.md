# Legislative Law § 10 — Compensation of officers and employees

[Legislative Law](../README.md) › [Article 2 — Members, Officers and Employees of the Legislature](README.md)

```text
  § 10. Compensation of officers and employees. The secretary of the
senate, the clerk of the assembly, and all other officers and employees
of the senate and assembly, shall be paid the compensation fixed by the
appointing officer within the amount provided by appropriation.
```
