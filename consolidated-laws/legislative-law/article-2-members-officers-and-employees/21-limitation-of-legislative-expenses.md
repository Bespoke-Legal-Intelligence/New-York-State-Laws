# Legislative Law § 21 — Limitation of legislative expenses

[Legislative Law](../README.md) › [Article 2 — Members, Officers and Employees of the Legislature](README.md)

```text
  § 21. Limitation of legislative expenses. Neither house shall, without
the consent of the other, order to be printed more than ten thousand
copies of any paper, bill or document, the aggregate cost of which shall
exceed one thousand dollars; or appoint any committee of its own members
or others at the public expense, except in case of contested elections;
or incur any expense whatever except as provided by this chapter.
```
