# Legislative Law § 19 — Duties of official stenographers

[Legislative Law](../README.md) › [Article 2 — Members, Officers and Employees of the Legislature](README.md)

```text
  § 19. Duties of official stenographers. The official stenographers of
each house shall attend at every session of the body for which they are
elected and take stenographic notes of the debates of such body and in
the committee of the whole thereof.
```
