# Legislative Law § 54-B — Reorganization plan

[Legislative Law](../README.md) › [Article 3 — The Enactment and Publication of Laws](README.md)

```text
  * § 54-b. Reorganization plan. The legislature may by concurrent
resolution prescribe rules for the consideration and disposition of a
reorganization plan, as defined in article three-A of the executive law.
```
