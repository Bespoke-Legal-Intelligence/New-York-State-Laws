# Legislative Law § 43 — Time of taking effect of laws

[Legislative Law](../README.md) › [Article 3 — The Enactment and Publication of Laws](README.md)

```text
  § 43. Time of taking effect of laws. Every law, unless a different
time shall be prescribed therein, shall take effect on the twentieth day
after it shall have become a law.
```
