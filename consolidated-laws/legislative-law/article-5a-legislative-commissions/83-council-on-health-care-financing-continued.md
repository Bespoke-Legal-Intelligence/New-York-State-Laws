# Legislative Law § 83 — Council on health care financing; continued

[Legislative Law](../README.md) › [Article 5-A — Legislative Commissions](README.md)

```text
  * § 83. Council on health care financing; continued. Notwithstanding
any other provision of law, the council on health care financing as
established by chapter five hundred twenty of the laws of nineteen
hundred seventy-eight, as last amended and continued by chapter
fifty-one of the laws of nineteen hundred ninety-three, is hereby
continued.
```
