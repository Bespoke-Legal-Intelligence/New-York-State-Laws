# Legislative Law § 56 — Effect of requests from local governments

[Legislative Law](../README.md) › [Article 3-A — Requests From Local Governments For Enactment of Special Laws](README.md)

```text
  § 56. Effect of requests from local governments. The enactment of any
law under paragraph two of subdivision (b) of section two of article
nine of the constitution shall be conclusive as to the facts that (1) a
necessity as set forth in the request existed at the time of enactment
and (2) that sufficient facts were set forth in such request to
establish such necessity.
```
