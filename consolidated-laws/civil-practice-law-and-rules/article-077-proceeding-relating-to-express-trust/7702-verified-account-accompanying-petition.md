# Civil Practice Law and Rules § 7702 — Verified account accompanying petition

[Civil Practice Law and Rules](../README.md) › [Article 77 — Proceeding Relating to Express Trust](README.md)

```text
  § 7702. Verified account accompanying petition. A petition by a
trustee praying that his intermediate or final account be judicially
settled shall be accompanied by an account verified in the form required
by section twenty-two hundred nine of the surrogate's court procedure
act.
```
