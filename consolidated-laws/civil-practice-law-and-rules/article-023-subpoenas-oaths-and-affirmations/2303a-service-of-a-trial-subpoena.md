# Civil Practice Law and Rules § 2303-A — Service of a trial subpoena

[Civil Practice Law and Rules](../README.md) › [Article 23 — Subpoenas, Oaths and Affirmations](README.md)

```text
  § 2303-a. Service of a trial subpoena. Where the attendance at trial
of a party or person within the party's control can be compelled by a
trial subpoena, that subpoena may be served by delivery in accordance
with subdivision (b) of rule 2103 to the party's attorney of record.
```
