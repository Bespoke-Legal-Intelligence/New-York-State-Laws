# Civil Practice Law and Rules § 603 — Severance and separate trials

[Civil Practice Law and Rules](../README.md) › [Article 6 — Joinder of Claims, Consolidation and Severance](README.md)

```text
  § 603. Severance and separate trials. In furtherance of convenience or
to avoid prejudice the court may order a severance of claims, or may
order a separate trial of any claim, or of any separate issue. The court
may order the trial of any claim or issue prior to the trial of the
others.
```
