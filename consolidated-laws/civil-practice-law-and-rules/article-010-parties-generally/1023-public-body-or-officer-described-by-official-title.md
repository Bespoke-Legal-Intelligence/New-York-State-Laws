# Civil Practice Law and Rules § 1023 — Public body or officer described by official title

[Civil Practice Law and Rules](../README.md) › [Article 10 — Parties Generally](README.md)

```text
  § 1023. Public body or officer described by official title. When a
public officer, body, board, commission or other public agency may sue
or be sued in its official capacity, it may be designated by its
official title, subject to the power of the court to require names to be
added.
```
