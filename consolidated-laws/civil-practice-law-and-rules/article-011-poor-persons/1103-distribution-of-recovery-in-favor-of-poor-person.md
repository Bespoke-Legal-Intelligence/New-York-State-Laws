# Civil Practice Law and Rules § 1103 — Distribution of recovery in favor of poor person

[Civil Practice Law and Rules](../README.md) › [Article 11 — Poor Persons](README.md)

```text
  § 1103. Distribution of recovery in favor of poor person. Any recovery
by judgment or by settlement had in favor of a poor person, shall be
paid to the clerk of the court in which the order permitting the person
to proceed as a poor person was entered, to await distribution pursuant
to court order.
```
