# Civil Practice Law and Rules § 5107 — Conveyance by sheriff

[Civil Practice Law and Rules](../README.md) › [Article 51 — Enforcement of Judgments and Orders Generally](README.md)

```text
  § 5107. Conveyance by sheriff. The court may require the sheriff to
convey real property in conformity with its directions.
```
