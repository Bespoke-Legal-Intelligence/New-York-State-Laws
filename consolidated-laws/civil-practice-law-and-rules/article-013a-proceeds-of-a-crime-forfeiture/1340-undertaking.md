# Civil Practice Law and Rules § 1340 — Undertaking

[Civil Practice Law and Rules](../README.md) › [Article 13-A — Proceeds of a Crime-forfeiture](README.md)

```text
  § 1340. Undertaking. A temporary receiver shall give an undertaking in
an amount to be fixed by the court making the appointment, that he or
she will faithfully discharge his or her duties.
```
