# Civil Practice Law and Rules § 1350 — Rules of procedure; in general

[Civil Practice Law and Rules](../README.md) › [Article 13-A — Proceeds of a Crime-forfeiture](README.md)

```text
  § 1350. Rules of procedure; in general. The civil practice law and
rules shall govern the procedure in proceedings and actions commenced
under this article, except where the procedure is regulated by any
inconsistent provisions herein.
```
