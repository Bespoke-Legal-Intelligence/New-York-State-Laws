# Civil Practice Law and Rules § 1342 — Removal

[Civil Practice Law and Rules](../README.md) › [Article 13-A — Proceeds of a Crime-forfeiture](README.md)

```text
  § 1342. Removal. Upon motion of any party or upon its own initiative,
the court which appointed a receiver may remove him or her at any time.
```
