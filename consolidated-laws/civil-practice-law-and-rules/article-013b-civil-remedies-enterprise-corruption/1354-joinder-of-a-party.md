# Civil Practice Law and Rules § 1354 — Joinder of a party

[Civil Practice Law and Rules](../README.md) › [Article 13-B — Civil Remedies; Enterprise Corruption](README.md)

```text
  § 1354. Joinder of a party. A person or enterprise not convicted of
the crime of enterprise corruption may be made a party to a civil action
under this article, whenever joinder of such person or enterprise is
necessary pursuant to section 1001 of this chapter.
```
