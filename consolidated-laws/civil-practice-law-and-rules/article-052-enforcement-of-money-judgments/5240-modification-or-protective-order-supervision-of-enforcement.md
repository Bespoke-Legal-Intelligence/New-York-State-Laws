# Civil Practice Law and Rules § 5240 — Modification or protective order; supervision of enforcement

[Civil Practice Law and Rules](../README.md) › [Article 52 — Enforcement of Money Judgments](README.md)

```text
  § 5240. Modification or protective order; supervision of enforcement.
The court may at any time, on its own initiative or the motion of any
interested person, and upon such notice as it may require, make an order
denying, limiting, conditioning, regulating, extending or modifying the
use of any enforcement procedure. Section 3104 is applicable to
procedures under this article.
```
