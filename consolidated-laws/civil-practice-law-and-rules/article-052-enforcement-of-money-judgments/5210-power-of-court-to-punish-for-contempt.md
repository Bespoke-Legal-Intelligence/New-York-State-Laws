# Civil Practice Law and Rules § 5210 — Power of court to punish for contempt

[Civil Practice Law and Rules](../README.md) › [Article 52 — Enforcement of Money Judgments](README.md)

```text
  § 5210. Power of court to punish for contempt.  Every court in which a
special proceeding to enforce a money judgment may be commenced, shall
have power to punish a contempt of court committed with respect to an
enforcement procedure.
```
