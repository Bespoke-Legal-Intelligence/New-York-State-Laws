# Civil Practice Law and Rules § 1404 — Rights of persons entitled to damages not affected; Rights of indemnity or subrogation preserved

[Civil Practice Law and Rules](../README.md) › [Article 14 — Contribution](README.md)

```text
  § 1404. Rights of persons entitled to damages not affected; Rights of
indemnity or subrogation preserved. (a) Nothing contained in this
article shall impair the rights of any person entitled to damages under
existing law.
  (b) Nothing contained in this article shall impair any right of
indemnity or subrogation under existing law.
```
