# Civil Practice Law and Rules § 350 — Legally protected health activities

[Civil Practice Law and Rules](../README.md) › [Article 3-A — Conflict of Law In Actions Related to Legally Protected Health Activity](README.md)

```text
  § 350. Legally protected health activities. Notwithstanding any
general or special law, common law, or conflict of law rule to the
contrary, the laws of this state shall govern in any case or controversy
heard in this state related to legally protected health activity, as
defined by section 570.17 of the criminal procedure law.
```
