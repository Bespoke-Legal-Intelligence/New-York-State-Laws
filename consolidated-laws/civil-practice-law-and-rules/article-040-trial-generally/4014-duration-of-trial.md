# Civil Practice Law and Rules § 4014 — Duration of trial

[Civil Practice Law and Rules](../README.md) › [Article 40 — Trial Generally](README.md)

```text
  Rule 4014. Duration of trial. Notwithstanding the expiration of the
term at which it was commenced, a trial shall continue until it is
completed.
```
