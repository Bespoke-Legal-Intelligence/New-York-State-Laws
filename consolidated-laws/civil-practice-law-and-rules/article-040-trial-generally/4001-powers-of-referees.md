# Civil Practice Law and Rules § 4001 — Powers of referees

[Civil Practice Law and Rules](../README.md) › [Article 40 — Trial Generally](README.md)

```text
  § 4001.  Powers of referees. A court may appoint a referee to
determine an issue, perform an act, or inquire and report in any case
where this power was heretofore exercised and as may be hereafter
authorized by law.
```
