# Civil Practice Law and Rules § 4201 — Powers of referees to report

[Civil Practice Law and Rules](../README.md) › [Article 42 — Trial By the Court](README.md)

```text
  § 4201. Powers of referees to report. A referee to inquire and report
shall have the power to issue subpoenas, to administer oaths and to
direct the parties to engage in and permit such disclosure proceedings
as will expedite the disposition of the issues.
```
