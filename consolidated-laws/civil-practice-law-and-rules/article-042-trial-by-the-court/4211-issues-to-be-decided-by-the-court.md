# Civil Practice Law and Rules § 4211 — Issues to be decided by the court

[Civil Practice Law and Rules](../README.md) › [Article 42 — Trial By the Court](README.md)

```text
  Rule 4211. Issues to be decided by the court. The court shall decide
any issue not required to be tried by a jury unless it is referred to a
referee to determine pursuant to section 4317.
```
