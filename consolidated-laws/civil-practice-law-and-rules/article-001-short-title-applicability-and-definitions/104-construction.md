# Civil Practice Law and Rules § 104 — Construction

[Civil Practice Law and Rules](../README.md) › [Article 1 — Short Title; Applicability and Definitions](README.md)

```text
  § 104. Construction. The civil practice law and rules shall be
liberally construed to secure the just, speedy and inexpensive
determination of every civil judicial proceeding.
```
