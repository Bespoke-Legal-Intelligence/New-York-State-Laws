# Civil Practice Law and Rules § 106 — Civil and criminal prosecutions not merged

[Civil Practice Law and Rules](../README.md) › [Article 1 — Short Title; Applicability and Definitions](README.md)

```text
  § 106. Civil and criminal prosecutions not merged. Where the violation
of a right admits of both a civil and criminal prosecution, the one is
not merged in the other.
```
