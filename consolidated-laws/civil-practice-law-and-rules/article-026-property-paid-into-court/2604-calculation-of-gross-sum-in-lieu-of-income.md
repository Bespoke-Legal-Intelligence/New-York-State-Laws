# Civil Practice Law and Rules § 2604 — Calculation of gross sum in lieu of income

[Civil Practice Law and Rules](../README.md) › [Article 26 — Property Paid Into Court](README.md)

```text
  § 2604. Calculation of gross sum in lieu of income. A gross sum
payable to a party in lieu of the income of a sum of money paid into
court for his benefit shall be calculated according to article four of
the real property actions and proceedings law.
```
