# Civil Practice Law and Rules § 2608 — Liability of custodian

[Civil Practice Law and Rules](../README.md) › [Article 26 — Property Paid Into Court](README.md)

```text
  § 2608. Liability of custodian. No liability shall attach to a
custodian of property paid into court because of a payment made by him
in good faith in accordance with the direction of an order of the court
or as provided in rule 2607.
```
