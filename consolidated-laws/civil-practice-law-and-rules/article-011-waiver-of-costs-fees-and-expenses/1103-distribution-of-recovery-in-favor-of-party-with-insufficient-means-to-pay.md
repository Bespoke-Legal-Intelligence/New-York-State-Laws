# Civil Practice Law and Rules § 1103 — Distribution of recovery in favor of party with insufficient means to pay costs, fees, and expenses

[Civil Practice Law and Rules](../README.md) › [Article 11 — Waiver of Costs, Fees, and Expenses](README.md)

```text
  § 1103. Distribution of recovery in favor of party with insufficient
means to pay costs, fees, and expenses. Where a court grants a motion
pursuant to subdivision (a) of section one thousand one hundred one of
this article, recovery by judgment or by settlement had in favor of the
moving party shall be paid to the clerk of the court in which the order
determining the motion was entered, to await distribution pursuant to
court order.
```
