# Civil Practice Law and Rules § 4107 — Judge present at examination of jurors

[Civil Practice Law and Rules](../README.md) › [Article 41 — Trial By a Jury](README.md)

```text
  Rule 4107. Judge present at examination of jurors. On application of
any party, a judge shall be present at the examination of the jurors.
```
