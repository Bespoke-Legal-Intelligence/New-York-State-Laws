# Civil Practice Law and Rules § 7557 — Reparation offers; denials of liability

[Civil Practice Law and Rules](../README.md) › [Article 75-A — Health Care Arbitration](README.md)

```text
  § 7557. Reparation offers; denials of liability. All communications
incidental to settlement made orally or in writing by any party shall
not be disclosed to the arbitration panel, unless all parties consent to
such disclosure.
```
