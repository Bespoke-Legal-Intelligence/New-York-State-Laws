# Civil Practice Law and Rules § 7565 — Modification and judicial review of decision

[Civil Practice Law and Rules](../README.md) › [Article 75-A — Health Care Arbitration](README.md)

```text
  § 7565. Modification and judicial review of decision. A decision of a
panel of arbitrators shall be binding on all parties, unless modified or
vacated pursuant to section seven thousand five hundred nine or seven
thousand five hundred eleven of this chapter.
```
