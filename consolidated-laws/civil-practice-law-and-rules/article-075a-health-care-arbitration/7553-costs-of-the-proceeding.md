# Civil Practice Law and Rules § 7553 — Costs of the proceeding

[Civil Practice Law and Rules](../README.md) › [Article 75-A — Health Care Arbitration](README.md)

```text
  § 7553. Costs of the proceeding. The administrative expense of
arbitrations shall be paid from the arbitration administration fund
established pursuant to section five thousand six hundred three of the
insurance law.
```
