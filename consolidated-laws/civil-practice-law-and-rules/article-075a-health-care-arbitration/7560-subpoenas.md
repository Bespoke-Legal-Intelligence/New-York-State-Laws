# Civil Practice Law and Rules § 7560 — Subpoenas

[Civil Practice Law and Rules](../README.md) › [Article 75-A — Health Care Arbitration](README.md)

```text
  § 7560. Subpoenas. The chairperson of the panel and any attorney of
record in the proceeding has the power to issue subpoenas, in accordance
with section seventy-five hundred five of this chapter.
```
