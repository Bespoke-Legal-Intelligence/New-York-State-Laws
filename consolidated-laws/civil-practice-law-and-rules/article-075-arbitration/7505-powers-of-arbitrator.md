# Civil Practice Law and Rules § 7505 — Powers of arbitrator

[Civil Practice Law and Rules](../README.md) › [Article 75 — Arbitration](README.md)

```text
  § 7505. Powers of arbitrator. An arbitrator and any attorney of record
in the arbitration proceeding has the power to issue subpoenas. An
arbitrator has the power to administer oaths.
```
