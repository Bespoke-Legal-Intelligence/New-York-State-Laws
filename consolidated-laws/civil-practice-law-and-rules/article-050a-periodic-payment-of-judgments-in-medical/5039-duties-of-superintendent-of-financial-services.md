# Civil Practice Law and Rules § 5039 — Duties of superintendent of financial services

[Civil Practice Law and Rules](../README.md) › [Article 50-A — Periodic Payment of Judgments In Medical and Dental Malpractice Actions](README.md)

```text
  § 5039. Duties of superintendent of financial services. The
superintendent of financial services shall establish rules and
procedures for determining which insurers, self-insurers, plans or
arrangements are financially qualified to provide the security required
under this article and to be designated as qualified insurers.
```
