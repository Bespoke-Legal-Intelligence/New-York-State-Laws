# Civil Practice Law and Rules § 5511 — Permissible appellant and respondent

[Civil Practice Law and Rules](../README.md) › [Article 55 — Appeals Generally](README.md)

```text
  § 5511. Permissible appellant and respondent.  An aggrieved party or a
person substituted for him may appeal from any appealable judgment or
order except one entered upon the default of the aggrieved party. He
shall be designated as the appellant and the adverse party as the
respondent.
```
