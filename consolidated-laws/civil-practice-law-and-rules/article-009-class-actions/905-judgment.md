# Civil Practice Law and Rules § 905 — Judgment

[Civil Practice Law and Rules](../README.md) › [Article 9 — Class Actions](README.md)

```text
  § 905. Judgment. The judgment in an action maintained as a class
action, whether or not favorable to the class, shall include and
describe those whom the court finds to be members of the class.
```
