# Civil Practice Law and Rules § 906 — Actions conducted partially as class actions

[Civil Practice Law and Rules](../README.md) › [Article 9 — Class Actions](README.md)

```text
  § 906. Actions conducted partially as class actions. When appropriate,
  1. an action may be brought or maintained as a class action with
respect to particular issues, or
  2. a class may be divided into subclasses and each subclass treated as
a class.
  The provisions of this article shall then be construed and applied
accordingly.
```
