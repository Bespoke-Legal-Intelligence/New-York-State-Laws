# Civil Practice Law and Rules § 213-D — Actions to be commenced within three years; medical debt

[Civil Practice Law and Rules](../README.md) › [Article 2 — Limitations of Time](README.md)

```text
  § 213-d. Actions to be commenced within three years; medical debt. An
action on a medical debt by a hospital licensed under article
twenty-eight of the public health law or a health care professional
authorized under title eight of the education law shall be commenced
within three years of treatment.
```
