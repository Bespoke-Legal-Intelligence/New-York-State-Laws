# Civil Practice Law and Rules § 508 — Actions to recover a chattel

[Civil Practice Law and Rules](../README.md) › [Article 5 — Venue](README.md)

```text
  § 508. Actions to recover a chattel. The place of trial of an action
to recover a chattel may be in the county in which any part of the
subject of the action is situated at the time of the commencement of the
action.
```
