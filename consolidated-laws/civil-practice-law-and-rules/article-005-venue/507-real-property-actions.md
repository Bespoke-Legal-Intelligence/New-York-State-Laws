# Civil Practice Law and Rules § 507 — Real property actions

[Civil Practice Law and Rules](../README.md) › [Article 5 — Venue](README.md)

```text
  § 507. Real property actions. The place of trial of an action in which
the judgment demanded would affect the title to, or the possession, use
or enjoyment of, real property shall be in the county in which any part
of the subject of the action is situated.
```
