# Civil Practice Law and Rules § 501 — Contractual provisions fixing venue

[Civil Practice Law and Rules](../README.md) › [Article 5 — Venue](README.md)

```text
  * § 501. Contractual provisions fixing venue. Subject to the
provisions of subdivision two of section 510, written agreement fixing
place of trial, made before an action is commenced, shall be enforced
upon a motion for change of place of trial.
  * NB Effective until December 3, 2021
  * § 501. Contractual provisions fixing venue. Subject to the
provisions of subdivision two of section five hundred ten and section
five hundred fourteen of this article, written agreement fixing place of
trial, made before an action is commenced, shall be enforced upon a
motion for change of place of trial.
  * NB Effective December 3, 2021
```
