# Civil Practice Law and Rules § 8202 — Amount of costs on motion

[Civil Practice Law and Rules](../README.md) › [Article 82 — Amount of Costs](README.md)

```text
  § 8202. Amount of costs on motion. Costs awarded on a motion shall be
in an amount fixed by the court, not exceeding one hundred dollars.
```
