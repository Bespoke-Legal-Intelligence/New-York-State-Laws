# Civil Practice Law and Rules § 8204 — Amount of costs on appeal to the court of appeals

[Civil Practice Law and Rules](../README.md) › [Article 82 — Amount of Costs](README.md)

```text
  § 8204. Amount of costs on appeal to the court of appeals. Unless the
court awards a lesser amount, costs awarded on an appeal to the court of
appeals shall be in the amount of five hundred dollars.
```
