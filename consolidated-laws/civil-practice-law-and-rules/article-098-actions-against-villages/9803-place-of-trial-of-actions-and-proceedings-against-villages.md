# Civil Practice Law and Rules § 9803 — Place of trial of actions and proceedings against villages

[Civil Practice Law and Rules](../README.md) › [Article 98 — Actions Against Villages](README.md)

```text
  § 9803. Place of trial of actions and proceedings against villages.
The place of trial of all actions and proceedings against a village or
any of its officers or boards shall be the county in which the village
is situated.
```
