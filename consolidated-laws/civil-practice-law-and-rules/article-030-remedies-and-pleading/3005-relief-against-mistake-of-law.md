# Civil Practice Law and Rules § 3005 — Relief against mistake of law

[Civil Practice Law and Rules](../README.md) › [Article 30 — Remedies and Pleading](README.md)

```text
  § 3005. Relief against mistake of law. When relief against a mistake
is sought in an action or by way of defense or counterclaim, relief
shall not be denied merely because the mistake is one of law rather than
one of fact.
```
