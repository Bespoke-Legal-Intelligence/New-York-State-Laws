# Civil Practice Law and Rules § 9703 — Form of records

[Civil Practice Law and Rules](../README.md) › [Article 97 — Records of Clerks of the Courts](README.md)

```text
  Rule 9703. Form of records. A clerk shall keep books and records in
such form and style as may be prescribed by the chief administrator of
the courts.
```
