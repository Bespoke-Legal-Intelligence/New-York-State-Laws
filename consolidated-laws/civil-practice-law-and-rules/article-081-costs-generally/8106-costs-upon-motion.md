# Civil Practice Law and Rules § 8106 — Costs upon motion

[Civil Practice Law and Rules](../README.md) › [Article 81 — Costs Generally](README.md)

```text
  § 8106. Costs upon motion. Costs upon a motion may be awarded to any
party, in the discretion of the court, and absolutely or to abide the
event of the action.
```
