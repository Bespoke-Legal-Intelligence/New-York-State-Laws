# Civil Practice Law and Rules § 8105 — Costs where more than one plaintiff or defendant

[Civil Practice Law and Rules](../README.md) › [Article 81 — Costs Generally](README.md)

```text
  § 8105. Costs where more than one plaintiff or defendant. Where a
judgment is entered in favor of two or more parties, they shall be
entitled, in all, to the same costs in the action as a single party,
unless the court otherwise orders.
```
