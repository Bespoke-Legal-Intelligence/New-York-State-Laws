# Civil Practice Law and Rules § 5711 — Where appeal heard

[Civil Practice Law and Rules](../README.md) › [Article 57 — Appeals to the Appellate Division](README.md)

```text
  § 5711. Where appeal heard. Except as provided in subdivision (d) of
rule 511, an appeal to the appellate division shall be brought in the
department embracing the county in which the judgment or order appealed
from is entered and there heard and determined unless, in furtherance of
justice, the appeal is sent to another department.
```
