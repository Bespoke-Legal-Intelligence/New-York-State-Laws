# Civil Practice Law and Rules § 5406 — Optional procedure

[Civil Practice Law and Rules](../README.md) › [Article 54 — Enforcement of Judgments Entitled to Full Faith and Credit Summary of Article](README.md)

```text
  § 5406. Optional procedure. The right of a judgment creditor to
proceed by an action on the judgment or a motion for summary judgment in
lieu of complaint, instead of proceeding under this article, remains
unimpaired.
```
