# Civil Practice Law and Rules § 5405 — Fees

[Civil Practice Law and Rules](../README.md) › [Article 54 — Enforcement of Judgments Entitled to Full Faith and Credit Summary of Article](README.md)

```text
  § 5405. Fees. When a foreign judgment is filed pursuant to this
article, an index number shall be assigned in accordance with the
provisions of subdivision (a) of section 8018 and the fee shall be as
prescribed therein.
```
