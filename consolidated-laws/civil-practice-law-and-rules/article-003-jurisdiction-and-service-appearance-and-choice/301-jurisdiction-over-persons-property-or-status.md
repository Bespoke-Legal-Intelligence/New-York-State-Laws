# Civil Practice Law and Rules § 301 — Jurisdiction over persons, property or status

[Civil Practice Law and Rules](../README.md) › [Article 3 — Jurisdiction and Service, Appearance and Choice of Court](README.md)

```text
  § 301. Jurisdiction over persons, property or status. A court may
exercise such jurisdiction over persons, property, or status as might
have been exercised heretofore.
```
