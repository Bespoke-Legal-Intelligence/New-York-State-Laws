# Civil Practice Law and Rules § 5004 — Rate of interest

[Civil Practice Law and Rules](../README.md) › [Article 50 — Judgments Generally](README.md)

```text
  § 5004. Rate of interest. Interest shall be at the rate of nine per
centum per annum, except where otherwise provided by statute.
```
