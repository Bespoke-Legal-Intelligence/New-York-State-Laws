# Civil Practice Law and Rules § 5003 — Interest upon judgment

[Civil Practice Law and Rules](../README.md) › [Article 50 — Judgments Generally](README.md)

```text
  § 5003. Interest upon judgment.  Every money judgment shall bear
interest from the date of its entry.  Every order directing the payment
of money which has been docketed as a judgment shall bear interest from
the date of such docketing.
```
