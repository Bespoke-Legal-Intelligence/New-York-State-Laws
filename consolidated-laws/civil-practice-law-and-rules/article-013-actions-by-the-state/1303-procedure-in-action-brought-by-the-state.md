# Civil Practice Law and Rules § 1303 — Procedure in action brought by the state

[Civil Practice Law and Rules](../README.md) › [Article 13 — Actions By the State](README.md)

```text
  § 1303. Procedure in action brought by the state. Except as otherwise
specially prescribed by statute or rule the proceedings in an action
brought by the state shall be the same as in an action by a private
person.
```
