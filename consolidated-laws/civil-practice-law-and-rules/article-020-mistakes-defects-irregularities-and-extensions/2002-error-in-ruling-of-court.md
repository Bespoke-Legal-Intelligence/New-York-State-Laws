# Civil Practice Law and Rules § 2002 — Error in ruling of court

[Civil Practice Law and Rules](../README.md) › [Article 20 — Mistakes, Defects, Irregularities and Extensions of Time](README.md)

```text
  § 2002. Error in ruling of court. An error in a ruling of the court
shall be disregarded if a substantial right of a party is not
prejudiced.
```
