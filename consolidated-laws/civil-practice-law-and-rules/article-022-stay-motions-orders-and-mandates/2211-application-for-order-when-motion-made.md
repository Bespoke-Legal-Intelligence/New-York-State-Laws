# Civil Practice Law and Rules § 2211 — Application for order; when motion made

[Civil Practice Law and Rules](../README.md) › [Article 22 — Stay, Motions, Orders and Mandates](README.md)

```text
  § 2211. Application for order; when motion made. A motion is an
application for an order. A motion on notice is made when a notice of
the motion or an order to show cause is served.
```
