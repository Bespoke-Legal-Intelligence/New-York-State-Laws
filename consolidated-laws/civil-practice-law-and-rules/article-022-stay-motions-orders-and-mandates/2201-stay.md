# Civil Practice Law and Rules § 2201 — Stay

[Civil Practice Law and Rules](../README.md) › [Article 22 — Stay, Motions, Orders and Mandates](README.md)

```text
  § 2201. Stay. Except where otherwise prescribed by law, the court in
which an action is pending may grant a stay of proceedings in a proper
case, upon such terms as may be just.
```
