# Civil Practice Law and Rules § 7012 — Redetention after discharge

[Civil Practice Law and Rules](../README.md) › [Article 70 — Habeas Corpus](README.md)

```text
  § 7012. Redetention after discharge. A person discharged upon the
return of a writ of habeas corpus shall not be detained for the same
cause, except by virtue of a subsequent lawful mandate.
```
