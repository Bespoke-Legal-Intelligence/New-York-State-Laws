# Civil Practice Law and Rules § 5042 — Form of security

[Civil Practice Law and Rules](../README.md) › [Article 50-B — Periodic Payment of Judgments In Personal Injury, Injury to Property and Wrongful Death Actions](README.md)

```text
  § 5042. Form of security. Security authorized or required for payment
of a judgment for periodic installments entered in accordance with this
article must be in the form of an annuity contract, executed by a
qualified insurer and approved by the superintendent of financial
services pursuant to section five thousand forty-nine of this article,
and approved by the court.
```
