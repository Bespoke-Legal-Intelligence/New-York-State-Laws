# Civil Practice Law and Rules § 5302 — Applicability

[Civil Practice Law and Rules](../README.md) › [Article 53 — Recognition of Foreign Country Money Judgments Summary of Article](README.md)

```text
  § 5302. Applicability. This article applies to any foreign country
judgment which is final, conclusive and enforceable where rendered even
though an appeal therefrom is pending or it is subject to appeal.
```
