# Civil Practice Law and Rules § 5307 — Recognition in other situations

[Civil Practice Law and Rules](../README.md) › [Article 53 — Recognition of Foreign Country Money Judgments Summary of Article](README.md)

```text
  § 5307. Recognition in other situations. This article does not prevent
```
