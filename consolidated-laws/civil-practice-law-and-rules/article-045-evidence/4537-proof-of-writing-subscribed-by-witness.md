# Civil Practice Law and Rules § 4537 — Proof of writing subscribed by witness

[Civil Practice Law and Rules](../README.md) › [Article 45 — Evidence](README.md)

```text
  Rule 4537. Proof of writing subscribed by witness. Unless a writing
requires a subscribing witness for its validity, it may be proved as if
there was no subscribing witness.
```
