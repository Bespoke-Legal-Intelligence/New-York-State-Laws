# Civil Practice Law and Rules § 4528 — Weather conditions

[Civil Practice Law and Rules](../README.md) › [Article 45 — Evidence](README.md)

```text
  Rule 4528. Weather conditions. Any record of the observations of the
weather, taken under the direction of the United States weather bureau,
is prima facie evidence of the facts stated.
```
