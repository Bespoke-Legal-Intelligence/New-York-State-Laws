# Civil Practice Law and Rules § 4512 — Competency of interested witness or spouse

[Civil Practice Law and Rules](../README.md) › [Article 45 — Evidence](README.md)

```text
  § 4512. Competency of interested witness or spouse. Except as
otherwise expressly prescribed, a person shall not be excluded or
excused from being a witness, by reason of his interest in the event or
because he is a party or the spouse of a party.
```
