# Civil Practice Law and Rules § 4529 — Inspection certificate issued by United States department of agriculture

[Civil Practice Law and Rules](../README.md) › [Article 45 — Evidence](README.md)

```text
  Rule 4529. Inspection certificate issued by United States department
of agriculture. An inspection certificate issued by the authorized
agents of the United States department of agriculture on file with the
United States secretary of agriculture is prima facie evidence of the
facts stated.
```
