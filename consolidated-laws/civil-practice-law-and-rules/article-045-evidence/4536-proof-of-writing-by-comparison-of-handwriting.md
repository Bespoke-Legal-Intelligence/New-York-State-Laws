# Civil Practice Law and Rules § 4536 — Proof of writing by comparison of handwriting

[Civil Practice Law and Rules](../README.md) › [Article 45 — Evidence](README.md)

```text
  Rule 4536. Proof of writing by comparison of handwriting.  Comparison
of a disputed writing with any writing proved to the satisfaction of the
court to be the handwriting of the person claimed to have made the
disputed writing shall be permitted.
```
