# Civil Practice Law and Rules § 4516 — Proof of age of child

[Civil Practice Law and Rules](../README.md) › [Article 45 — Evidence](README.md)

```text
  Rule 4516. Proof of age of child. Whenever it becomes necessary to
determine the age of a child, he may be produced and exhibited to enable
the court or jury to determine his age by a personal inspection.
```
