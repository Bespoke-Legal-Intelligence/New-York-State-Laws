# Civil Practice Law and Rules § 6405 — Removal

[Civil Practice Law and Rules](../README.md) › [Article 64 — Receivership](README.md)

```text
  § 6405. Removal. Upon motion of any party or upon its own initiative,
the court which appointed a receiver may remove him at any time.
```
