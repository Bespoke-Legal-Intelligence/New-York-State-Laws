# Civil Practice Law and Rules § 6403 — Undertaking

[Civil Practice Law and Rules](../README.md) › [Article 64 — Receivership](README.md)

```text
  § 6403. Undertaking. A temporary receiver shall give an undertaking in
an amount to be fixed by the court making the appointment, that he will
faithfully discharge his duties.
```
