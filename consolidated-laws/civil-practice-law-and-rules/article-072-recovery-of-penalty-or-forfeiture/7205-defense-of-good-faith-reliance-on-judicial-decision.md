# Civil Practice Law and Rules § 7205 — Defense of good faith reliance on judicial decision

[Civil Practice Law and Rules](../README.md) › [Article 72 — Recovery of Penalty or Forfeiture](README.md)

```text
  § 7205. Defense of good faith reliance on judicial decision. No action
for a penalty or forfeiture may be brought for an act done in good faith
and pursuant to a construction given to a statute by a decision of an
appellate court and adjudged lawful thereby, where such act was done
prior to a reversal or the overruling of such decision.
```
