# Civil Practice Law and Rules § 7204 — Recovery of part of penalty or forfeiture

[Civil Practice Law and Rules](../README.md) › [Article 72 — Recovery of Penalty or Forfeiture](README.md)

```text
  § 7204. Recovery of part of penalty or forfeiture. Where a statute
gives a pecuniary penalty or forfeiture not exceeding a specified sum,
the whole sum or a part proportionate to the offense may be awarded.
```
