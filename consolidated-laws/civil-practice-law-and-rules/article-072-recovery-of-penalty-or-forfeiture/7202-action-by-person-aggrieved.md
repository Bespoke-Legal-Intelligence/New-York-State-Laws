# Civil Practice Law and Rules § 7202 — Action by person aggrieved

[Civil Practice Law and Rules](../README.md) › [Article 72 — Recovery of Penalty or Forfeiture](README.md)

```text
  § 7202. Action by person aggrieved. Where a penalty or forfeiture is
given by a statute to a person aggrieved by the act or omission of
another, the person aggrieved may commence an action to recover it.
```
