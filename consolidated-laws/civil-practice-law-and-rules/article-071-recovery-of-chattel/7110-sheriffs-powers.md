# Civil Practice Law and Rules § 7110 — Sheriff's powers

[Civil Practice Law and Rules](../README.md) › [Article 71 — Recovery of Chattel](README.md)

```text
  § 7110. Sheriff's powers. If the order of seizure so provides, the
sheriff, in accordance with the order of seizure, may break open, enter
and search for the chattel in the place where the chattel may be and
take the chattel into his possession.
```
