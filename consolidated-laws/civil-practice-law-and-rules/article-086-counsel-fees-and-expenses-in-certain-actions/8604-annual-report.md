# Civil Practice Law and Rules § 8604 — Annual report

[Civil Practice Law and Rules](../README.md) › [Article 86 — Counsel Fees and Expenses In Certain Actions Against the State](README.md)

```text
  § 8604. Annual report. The department of law shall file with the
governor, the speaker of the assembly and the temporary president of the
senate an annual report describing the number, nature and amount of each
award in the previous fiscal year including the agency involved in each
action, and other relevant information which might aid the legislature
and the governor in evaluating the scope and impact of such awards.
```
