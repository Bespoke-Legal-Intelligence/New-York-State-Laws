# Civil Practice Law and Rules § 8603 — Interest

[Civil Practice Law and Rules](../README.md) › [Article 86 — Counsel Fees and Expenses In Certain Actions Against the State](README.md)

```text
  § 8603. Interest. If the state appeals an award made pursuant to this
section and the award is affirmed in whole or in part, interest shall be
paid on the amount of the award. Such interest shall run from the date
of the award through the day before the date of the affirmance.
```
