# Eminent Domain Procedure Law § 706 — Pending actions and proceedings

[Eminent Domain Procedure Law](../README.md) › [Article 7 — Miscellaneous Provisions](README.md)

```text
  § 706. Pending actions and proceedings. No action or proceeding
pending at the time when this chapter takes effect, shall be affected by
any provision of this chapter, and the same may be prosecuted as if this
chapter had not been enacted.
```
