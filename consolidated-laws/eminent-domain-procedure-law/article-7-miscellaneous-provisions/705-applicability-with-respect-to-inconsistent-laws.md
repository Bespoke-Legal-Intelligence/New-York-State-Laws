# Eminent Domain Procedure Law § 705 — Applicability with respect to inconsistent laws

[Eminent Domain Procedure Law](../README.md) › [Article 7 — Miscellaneous Provisions](README.md)

```text
  § 705. Applicability with respect to inconsistent laws.
Notwithstanding any inconsistent provisions of law, general or special,
the provisions of this law shall be controlling and on and after the
effective date of this law, any interest in real property subject to
acquisition shall be acquired pursuant to the provisions of this law.
```
