# Eminent Domain Procedure Law § 708 — Acquisition of other than real property

[Eminent Domain Procedure Law](../README.md) › [Article 7 — Miscellaneous Provisions](README.md)

```text
  § 708. Acquisition of other than real property. Whenever any condemnor
is authorized to acquire for a public use, title to property other than
real property, the acquisition of such property shall be in the manner
and procedure prescribed for the acquisition of real property under this
chapter.
```
