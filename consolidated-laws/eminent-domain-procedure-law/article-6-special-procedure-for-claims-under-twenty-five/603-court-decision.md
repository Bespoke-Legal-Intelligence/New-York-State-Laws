# Eminent Domain Procedure Law § 603 — Court decision

[Eminent Domain Procedure Law](../README.md) › [Article 6 — Special Procedure For Claims Under Twenty-five Thousand Dollars](README.md)

```text
  § 603. Court decision. The court shall file its decision with the
clerk of the court. The decision shall direct the clerk of the court to
enter the decision together with an appropriate entry for lawful
interest as the final judgment. The clerk shall serve a copy of the
judgment with notice of entry on the condemnee and the condemnor.
```
