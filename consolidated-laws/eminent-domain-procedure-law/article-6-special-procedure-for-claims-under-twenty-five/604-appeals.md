# Eminent Domain Procedure Law § 604 — Appeals

[Eminent Domain Procedure Law](../README.md) › [Article 6 — Special Procedure For Claims Under Twenty-five Thousand Dollars](README.md)

```text
  § 604. Appeals. Any party to an action pursuant to this article may
appeal on the sole ground that substantial justice has not been
accomplished between the parties according to the rules and principles
of substantive law.
```
