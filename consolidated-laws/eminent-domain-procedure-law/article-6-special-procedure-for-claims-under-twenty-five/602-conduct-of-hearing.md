# Eminent Domain Procedure Law § 602 — Conduct of hearing

[Eminent Domain Procedure Law](../README.md) › [Article 6 — Special Procedure For Claims Under Twenty-five Thousand Dollars](README.md)

```text
  § 602. Conduct of hearing. At the hearing, a stenographic record shall
be made and the respective parties shall present to the court evidence
to support their positions. The parties need not be represented by an
attorney nor present expert witnesses, nor shall the rules of evidence
apply. The courts shall adopt rules governing the conduct of hearings
under this article.
```
