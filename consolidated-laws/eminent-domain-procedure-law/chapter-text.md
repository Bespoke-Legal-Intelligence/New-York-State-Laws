                               CHAPTER 839
  AN ACT to establish the eminent domain procedure law, constituting
           chapter seventy-three of the consolidated laws, and to repeal
           the condemnation law, relating to the acquisition, by
           condemnation, of real property for a public use
  Became a law August 11, l977, with the approval of the Governor.
        Passed by a majority vote, three-fifths being present.
  The People of the State of New York, represented in Senate and

                    CHAPTER 73 OF THE CONSOLIDATED LAWS
                      EMINENT DOMAIN PROCEDURE LAW
Article 1. Purpose; short title; definitions; applicability (Secs.
             101-104)
        2. Determination of the need and location of a public project
             prior to acquisition (Secs. 201-208)
        3. Offer and negotiations (Secs. 301-305)
        4. Vesting; possession; abandonment (Secs. 401-406)
        5. Procedure for determining just compensation (Secs. 501-514)
        6. Special procedure for claims under twenty-five thousand
             dollars (Secs. 601-604)
        7. Miscellaneous provisions (Secs. 701-709)
