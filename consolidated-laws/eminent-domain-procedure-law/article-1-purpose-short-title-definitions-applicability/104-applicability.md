# Eminent Domain Procedure Law § 104 — Applicability

[Eminent Domain Procedure Law](../README.md) › [Article 1 — Purpose; Short Title; Definitions; Applicability](README.md)

```text
  § 104. Applicability. The eminent domain procedure law shall be
uniformly applied to any and all acquisitions by eminent domain of real
property within the state of New York.
```
