# Eminent Domain Procedure Law § 512 — Decision of the court

[Eminent Domain Procedure Law](../README.md) › [Article 5 — Procedure For Determining Just Compensation](README.md)

```text
  § 512.  Decision of the court.  The court, after hearing the testimony
and weighing the evidence, shall determine the compensation due the
condemnees for damages as the result of the acquisition.  The court's
decision shall be in compliance with section four thousand two hundred
thirteen of the civil practice law and rules.
```
