# Eminent Domain Procedure Law § 507 — Answer; affirmative defenses

[Eminent Domain Procedure Law](../README.md) › [Article 5 — Procedure For Determining Just Compensation](README.md)

```text
  § 507. Answer; affirmative defenses. In all acquisitions under
subdivision (A) of section five hundred one the condemnor shall not be
required to interpose an answer or plead affirmative defenses.
```
