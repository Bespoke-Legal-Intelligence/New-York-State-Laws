# Eminent Domain Procedure Law § 301 — Policy

[Eminent Domain Procedure Law](../README.md) › [Article 3 — Offer and Negotiations](README.md)

```text
  § 301. Policy. The condemnor, at all stages prior to or subsequent to
an acquisition by eminent domain of real property necessary for a
proposed public project shall make every reasonable and expeditious
effort to justly compensate persons for such real property by
negotiation and agreement.
```
