# New York State Constitution, Article Xiv, § 5 — Violations of article; how restrained

[New York State Constitution](../README.md) › [Article XIV — Conservation](README.md)

```text
  § 5. A violation of any of the provisions of this article may be
restrained at the suit of the people or, with the consent of the supreme
court in appellate division, on notice to the attorney-general at the
suit of any citizen.
```
