# New York State Constitution, Article Vi, § 31 —   Peacemakers and other Indian courts continued

[New York State Constitution](../README.md) › [Article VI — Judiciary](README.md)

```text
  § 31. This article does not apply to the peacemakers courts or other
Indian courts, the existence and operation of which shall continue as
may be provided by law.
```
