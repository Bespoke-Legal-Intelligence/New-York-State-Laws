# New York State Constitution, Article Xx, § 1 — Time of taking effect

[New York State Constitution](../README.md) › [Article XX — When to Take Effect](README.md)

```text
                               ARTICLE XX
                           When to Take Effect
  Section 1. This constitution shall be in force from and including the
first day of January, one thousand nine hundred thirty-nine, except as
herein otherwise provided.
```
