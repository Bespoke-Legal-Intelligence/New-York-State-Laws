# New York State Constitution, Article I, § 19 — Environmental rights

[New York State Constitution](../README.md) › [Article I — Bill of Rights](README.md)

```text
§ 19. Environmental rights. Each person shall have a right to clean air
and water, and a healthful environment.
```
