# New York State Constitution, Article I, § 4 — Habeas corpus

[New York State Constitution](../README.md) › [Article I — Bill of Rights](README.md)

```text
  § 4. The privilege of a writ or order of habeas corpus shall not be
suspended, unless, in case of rebellion or invasion, the public safety
requires it.
```
