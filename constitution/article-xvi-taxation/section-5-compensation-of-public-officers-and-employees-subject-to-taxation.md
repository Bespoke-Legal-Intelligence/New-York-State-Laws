# New York State Constitution, Article Xvi, § 5 — Compensation of public officers and employees subject to            taxation

[New York State Constitution](../README.md) › [Article XVI — Taxation](README.md)

```text
  § 5. All salaries, wages and other compensation, except pensions, paid
to officers and employees of the state and its subdivisions and agencies
shall be subject to taxation.
```
