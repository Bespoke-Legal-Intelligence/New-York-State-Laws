# New York State Constitution, Article Xvi, § 4 — Certain corporations not to be discriminated against

[New York State Constitution](../README.md) › [Article XVI — Taxation](README.md)

```text
  § 4. Where the state has power to tax corporations incorporated under
the laws of the United States there shall be no discrimination in the
rates and method of taxation between such corporations and other
corporations exercising substantially similar functions and engaged in
substantially similar business within the state.
```
