# New York State Constitution, Article Iii, § 9 —   Powers of each house

[New York State Constitution](../README.md) › [Article III — Legislature](README.md)

```text
  § 9. A majority of each house shall constitute a quorum to do
business.  Each house shall determine the rules of its own proceedings,
and be the judge of the elections, returns and qualifications of its own
members; shall choose its own officers; and the senate shall choose a
temporary president and the assembly shall choose a speaker.
```
