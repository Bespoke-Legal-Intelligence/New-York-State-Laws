# New York State Constitution, Article Iii, § 12 —   Bills may originate in either house; may be amended by the              other

[New York State Constitution](../README.md) › [Article III — Legislature](README.md)

```text
  § 12. Any bill may originate in either house of the legislature, and
all bills passed by one house may be amended by the other.
```
