# New York State Constitution, Article Iii, § 3 —   Senate districts

[New York State Constitution](../README.md) › [Article III — Legislature](README.md)

```text
  § 3. The senate districts described in section three of article three
of this constitution as adopted by the people on November sixth,
eighteen hundred ninety-four are hereby continued for all of the
purposes of future reapportionments of senate districts pursuant to
section four of this article.
```
