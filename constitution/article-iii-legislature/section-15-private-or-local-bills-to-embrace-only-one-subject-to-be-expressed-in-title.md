# New York State Constitution, Article Iii, § 15 —   Private or local bills to embrace only one subject to be              expressed in title

[New York State Constitution](../README.md) › [Article III — Legislature](README.md)

```text
  § 15. No private or local bill, which may be passed by the
legislature, shall embrace more than one subject, and that shall be
expressed in the title.
```
