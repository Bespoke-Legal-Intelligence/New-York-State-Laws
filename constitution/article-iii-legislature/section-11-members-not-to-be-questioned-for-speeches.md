# New York State Constitution, Article Iii, § 11 —   Members not to be questioned for speeches

[New York State Constitution](../README.md) › [Article III — Legislature](README.md)

```text
  § 11. For any speech or debate in either house of the legislature, the
members shall not be questioned in any other place.
```
