# New York State Constitution, Article Iii, § 13 —   Enacting clause of bills; no law to be enacted except by              bill

[New York State Constitution](../README.md) › [Article III — Legislature](README.md)

```text
  § 13. The enacting clause of all bills shall be "The People of the
State of New York, represented in Senate and Assembly, do enact as
follows," and no law shall be enacted except by bill.
```
