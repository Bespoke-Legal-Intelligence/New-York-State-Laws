# New York State Constitution, Article Iv, § 2 — Qualifications of governor and lieutenant-governor

[New York State Constitution](../README.md) › [Article IV — Executive](README.md)

```text
  § 2. No person shall be eligible to the office of governor or
lieutenant-governor, except a citizen of the United States, of the age
of not less than thirty years, and who shall have been five years next
preceding the election a resident of this state.
```
