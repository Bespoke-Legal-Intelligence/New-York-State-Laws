# New York State Constitution, Article Xix, § 3 — Amendments simultaneously submitted by convention and            legislature

[New York State Constitution](../README.md) › [Article XIX — Amendments to Constitution](README.md)

```text
  § 3. Any amendment proposed by a constitutional convention relating to
the same subject as an amendment proposed by the legislature,
coincidently submitted to the people for approval shall, if approved, be
deemed to supersede the amendment so proposed by the legislature.
```
