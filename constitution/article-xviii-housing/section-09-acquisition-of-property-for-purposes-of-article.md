# New York State Constitution, Article Xviii, § 9 — Acquisition of property for purposes of article

[New York State Constitution](../README.md) › [Article XVIII — Housing](README.md)

```text
  § 9. Subject to any limitation imposed by the legislature, the state,
or any city, town, village or public corporation, may acquire by
purchase, gift, eminent domain or otherwise, such property as it may
deem ultimately necessary or proper to effectuate the purposes of this
article, or any of them, although temporarily not required for such
purposes.
```
