# New York State Constitution, Article V, § 7 — Membership in retirement systems; benefits not to be diminished            nor impaired

[New York State Constitution](../README.md) › [Article V — Officers and Civil Departments](README.md)

```text
  § 7. After July first, nineteen hundred forty, membership in any
pension or retirement system of the state or of a civil division thereof
shall be a contractual relationship, the benefits of which shall not be
diminished or impaired.
```
