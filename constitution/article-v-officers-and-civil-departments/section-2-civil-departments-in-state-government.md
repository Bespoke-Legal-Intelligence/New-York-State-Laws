# New York State Constitution, Article V, § 2 — Civil departments in state government

[New York State Constitution](../README.md) › [Article V — Officers and Civil Departments](README.md)

```text
  § 2. There shall be not more than twenty civil departments in the
state government, including those referred to in this constitution. The
legislature may by law change the names of the departments referred to
in this constitution.
```
