# New York State Constitution, Article Xi, § 1 — Common schools

[New York State Constitution](../README.md) › [Article XI — Education](README.md)

```text
                               ARTICLE XI
                                Education
  Section 1. The legislature shall provide for the maintenance and
support of a system of free common schools, wherein all the children of
this state may be educated.
```
