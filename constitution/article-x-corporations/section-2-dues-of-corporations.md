# New York State Constitution, Article X, § 2 — Dues of corporations

[New York State Constitution](../README.md) › [Article X — Corporations](README.md)

```text
  § 2. Dues from corporations shall be secured by such individual
liability of the corporators and other means as may be prescribed by
law.
```
