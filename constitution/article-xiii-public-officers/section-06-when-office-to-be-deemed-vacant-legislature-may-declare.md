# New York State Constitution, Article Xiii, § 6 —    When office to be deemed vacant; legislature may declare

[New York State Constitution](../README.md) › [Article XIII — Public Officers](README.md)

```text
  § 6. The legislature may declare the cases in which any office shall
be deemed vacant when no provision is made for that purpose in this
constitution.
```
