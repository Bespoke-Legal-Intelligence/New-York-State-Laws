# New York State Constitution, Article Xiii, § 4 —    Political year and legislative term

[New York State Constitution](../README.md) › [Article XIII — Public Officers](README.md)

```text
  § 4. The political year and legislative term shall begin on the first
day of January; and the legislature shall, every year, assemble on the
first Wednesday after the first Monday in January.
```
