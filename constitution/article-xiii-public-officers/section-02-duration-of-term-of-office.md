# New York State Constitution, Article Xiii, § 2 —    Duration of term of office

[New York State Constitution](../README.md) › [Article XIII — Public Officers](README.md)

```text
  § 2. When the duration of any office is not provided by this
constitution it may be declared by law, and if not so declared, such
office shall be held during the pleasure of the authority making the
appointment.
```
