# New York State Constitution, Article Xiii, § 5 —    Removal from office for misconduct

[New York State Constitution](../README.md) › [Article XIII — Public Officers](README.md)

```text
  § 5. Provision shall be made by law for the removal for misconduct or
malversation in office of all officers, except judicial, whose powers
and duties are not local or legislative and who shall be elected at
general elections, and also for supplying vacancies created by such
removal.
```
