# Notice

**What this is.** An unofficial, machine-generated history of the New York Consolidated and Unconsolidated Laws, court acts, legislative rules and the State Constitution, arranged as the printed laws are, with one commit per weekly publication. It is built from the New York State Senate's Open Legislation service (legislation.nysenate.gov). It is not an official publication of the State of New York, the Senate, the Assembly, or the Legislative Bill Drafting Commission. Consult the official text for any legal proceeding. A publication date is the date the text was published, not the date a change took legal effect.

**How the text is shown.** Statute text appears line for line as the Senate publishes it. The only change is that the line-break markers in the Senate's data are shown as actual line breaks, and blank space at the very end of a text is trimmed.

**Statute text.** The laws are the law of New York. Text written by legislators in the course of their official duties is not copyrightable under the government edicts doctrine (*Georgia v. Public.Resource.Org, Inc.*, 590 U.S. 255 (2020)). No copyright is claimed in it here.
